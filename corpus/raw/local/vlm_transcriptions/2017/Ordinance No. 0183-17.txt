Ord. No. 0183 - 17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF THE ZONING REQUIREMENT FOR THE ALLOWABLE USE OF A CERTAIN PARCEL OF LAND WITH LOT NO. 146-201304442, TCT NO. 146-201301297, TCT NO. T-401471 AND TCT NO. T-401469, WITH AN AGGREGATE TOTAL AREA OF 166,512 SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PERTHENT PROVISIONS OF THE DAVAO CITY COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF 2013 FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF LYCEUM OF THE PHILIPPINES UNIVERSITY-MINDANAO CAMPUS LOCATED IN BARANGAYS PANACAN/SASA, DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES FOR DEVELOPMENT - Article XII, Section 1, of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City provides: "Request for Additional Allowable Use". The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general and all sub-zones are exhaustive but not inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of project, proposal, and upon favorable recommendation of the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment, may grant an Allowable Use not enumerated therein if they find it to be deemed fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3/4 majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolutions and ordinances.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED May 2, 2017, by three-fourths (3/4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
