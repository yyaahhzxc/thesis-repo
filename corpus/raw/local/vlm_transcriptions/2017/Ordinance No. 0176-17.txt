Ord. No. 0176-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ALONG A PORTION OF MABINIG STREET (IN BETWEEN AURORA AND ARTAGA STREETS) ON APRIL 29, 2017 FROM 8:00 A.M. TO 11:00 A.M. FOR THE 38TH ARAW NG BARANGAY AND GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF BARANGAY 33-D, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY;"
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.- Section 2(c) of R.A. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs...
SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE.- A portion of Mabini Street (in between Aurora and Artaga Streets) shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on April 29, 2017 from 8:00 A.M. to 11:00 P.M. for the 38th Araw ng Barangay and General Assembly of Barangay 33-D, Poblacion, Davao City;
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.- If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 20th day of April, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
