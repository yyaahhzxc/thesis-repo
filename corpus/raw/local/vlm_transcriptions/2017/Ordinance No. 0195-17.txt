Ord. No. 0195-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT THE DONATION AND TO SIGN THE DEED TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH-DAVAO REGION, RELATIVE TO THE MOBILE DENTAL CLINICS";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. DEED OF DONATION: This is a Deed of Donation between the Department of Health-Davao Region and the City Government of Davao whereby DOH-Davao Region will give the City Government of Davao mobile dental clinics including all its accessories and equipment;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY: The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation between the Department of Health-Davao Region and the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, this 9th day of May, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

The Deed of Donation entered into on the 17th day of June 2016 in Manila, Philippines, by the DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH, a National Government entity duly organized and existing under the laws of the republic of the Philippines, hereinafter referred to as "DOH," with office addresses at San Lazaro Compound, Rizal Avenue, Sta. Cruz, Manila, Philippines, herein represented by PAULYN JEAN B. ROSELL-UBIAL, MD, MPH, CESO II, Secretary of Health as DONOR.

In favor of

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit duly recognized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, hereinafter referred to as DONEE, with the office address at San Pedro St., Davao city, Philippines, herein represented by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, as the DONEE.

In this agreement, DOH and the City Government of Davao are individually referred to as "Party" and collectively as "Parties."

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the state recognizes its mandate to provide equitable and accessible quality health for all Filipinos;

WHEREAS, the Health Facility Enhancement Program (HFEP) is one of the major pillars of "Kalusugan Pangkalatahan";

WHEREAS, "Kalusugan Pangkalatahan" envisions quality health care to be accessible to the poor and vulnerable population by ensuring the availability of functional static and mobile health facilities, including mobile dental clinics;

WHEREAS, studies have shown that the ratio of government dentist to population is one(1) dentist for every 48,000 to 50,000, and that the prevalence of dental diseases continue to be high starting at childhood;

WHEREAS, to address the lack of dentists who will serve the dental needs of all the municipalities and cities in the country, the DOH is investing in the acquisition of mobile dental clinics, including equipment, to cover the unserved communities;

WHEREAS, in CY 2015, the DOH is committed to provide mobile dental clinics, including equipment, to cover the unserved communities;

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao plays a critical role in ensuring administrative support to augment public dental health services in the country; and

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao shall provide the necessary health human resource and logistics support for the operation and maintenance of the mobile dental clinic.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and subject to the conditions stipulated hereunder, the Department of Health hereby donates to the City Government of Davao the following unit of mobile dental clinic including equipment.

--- [Page 11] ---
The Donor undertakes to:

1. Ensure that the Mobile Dental Clinic is utilized in accordance with its purpose.
2. Ensure the availability of dentists and other health personnel, drugs, medicines, and supplies, utilities, and other operational expenses for the operation of the mobile dental equipment.
3. Ensure the rational use of the mobile dental clinic within the city to maximize its benefit to the residents needing dental service.
4. Ensure the proper maintenance, out of its own resources, of the aforesaid mobile dental clinic, including the equipment.
5. Designate the City Health Officer as the focal who will be coordinating with the DOH Regional Office for purposes of program monitoring and evaluation.

The Donor reserves the right to revoke this Deed of Donation in case any of the aforesaid conditions are not faithfully complied with by the Donee. In case this Deed of Donation is revoked, the Donor shall surrender the mobile dental clinic, including all its accessories and the equipment, to the Donor within fifteen (15) days from receipt of the Notice of Revocation. Ownership of the said mobile dental equipment shall then vest to the Donor effective upon receipt by the Donor of the Notice of Revocation. And the Donor shall have seven (7) days from receipt of the Notice of Revocation to transfer the possession of the mobile dental clinic, including all its accessories and the equipment, to the Donor.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties here under set their hand on the date and place above mentioned.
