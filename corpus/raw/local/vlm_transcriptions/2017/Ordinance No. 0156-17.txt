Ord. No. 0156-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND DAVAO LIGHT AND POWER COMPANY, FOR THE ELECTRIFICATION OF PUROK 6, MATINA BIAO, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"For sufficient, effective and economical governance the purposes of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent, by virtue of his authority, all functions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Davao Light and Power Company, for the electrification of Purok 6, Matina Biao, Tugbok District, this City;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval; ENACTED, February 14, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement, made and entered into by and between:

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, represented by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, with office address at City Hall Drive, San Pedro, Davao City hereinafter referred to as THE FIRST PARTY;

and

THE DAVAO LIGHT & POWER CO., INC., represented in this act by its Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer, RODGER S. VELASCO, likewise of law, married, and a resident of Davao City, Philippines hereinafter referred to as THE SECOND PARTY.

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY and the SECOND PARTY have similar visions and are committed to the improvement of the quality of life of the residents of Purok 6, Barangay Matina Biao, Davao City, through the extension of basic services by providing adequate electricity to the said area;

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY and the SECOND PARTY have jointly committed to install light and power in the aforementioned area as one of their existing projects in Davao City;

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY and the SECOND PARTY hereby agree to undertake the ELECTRIFICATION OF PUROK 6 BARANGAY MATINA BIAO, DAVAO CITY (the Project), subject to the following terms and conditions, to wit:

1. Enclosures. The following enclosures are integral parts of this Agreement:
   1.1 Certification of Availability Funds by the City Budget Office, City of Davao, attached as Annex "A";
   1.2 Work Plans and Specifications attached as Annex "B" and "B-1".

2. The Project.
   2.1 The FIRST PARTY, with a Certification of Availability Funds by the City Budget Office of City of Davao, shall pay to the SECOND PARTY the Project cost in the amount of the TREE HUNDRED NINETY EIGHT THOUSAND SEVEN HUNDRED FIFTY SEVEN & 34/100 (P398,757.34) Pesos from ADY, CFDY 2014. Copy of the Certification of Funds is herein attached as Annex "A".
   2.2 The SECOND PARTY shall implement the Project in accordance with the plans and specifications hereto attached and approved by the FIRST PARTY. Payment shall be made by the SECOND PARTY upon full payment of the Project Cost by the FIRST PARTY.
   2.3 The FIRST PARTY shall grant and furnish the SECOND PARTY the necessary right-of-ways and permits for the implementation of the Project.

3. The electrical equipment and facilities constructed and/or installed by the SECOND PARTY, including those described in Annex "B" and "B-1", shall at all times remain the property of the SECOND PARTY notwithstanding that the electrical equipment, facilities or any of its parts may now be, or hereafter become, the property of the FIRST PARTY.
4. Refund of Advances. The SECOND PARTY shall refund/waive the FIRST PARTY without interest the appropriated amount at the rate of SEVENTY FIVE PERCENT (75%) of its annual gross distribution revenue derived from the electric billing of the consumers served from the electrical facilities until such time that the total amount appropriated shall have been liquidated or refunded to the FIRST PARTY.

5. Effectivity. This Agreement shall take effect on the day when this Agreement and its Ammexes shall have been executed.

6. Confidentiality.
   6.1 This Agreement shall be kept confidential and shall not be disclosed to any person unless the prior written approval from the Parties, as the case may be, except for the following instances:
   a. Disclosure as may be required by law or by a judicial decree or order, or required by the rules and regulations of any agency, including disclosures required by any laws and regulations, in the Philippines or otherwise, relating to the sale and/or exchange of securities;
   b. Disclosure made by SECOND PARTY to its affiliates or related companies, lenders, professional advisors, employees, agents or authorized representatives on a need-to-know basis and provided each person is under a duty of confidentiality in the SECOND PARTY.

--- [Page 11] ---
e. Disclosure made by the FIRST PARTY to its lenders, professional advisors, employees, agents or authorized representatives on a need to know basis and provided such persons are under a duty of confidentiality to the FIRST PARTY.

6.2 Each of FIRST PARTY and SECOND PARTY further agrees that all information and documents (whether financial, technical or otherwise) obtained by a Party or its agents from the other Party or such Party's agents, which are not generally publicly available, shall be kept confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior written approval of the other Party. The FIRST PARTY or SECOND PARTY, as the case may be, except to the extent (i) the disclosure of such information is required by applicable law or by any court or like tribunal of competent jurisdiction pursuant to a ruling or decree binding on the First or Second Party, as the case may be, which has already been published and/or (ii) disclosure is required or necessary for compliance with any applicable rules of the stock exchange, the Philippine Stock Exchange Commission or any other regulatory body having jurisdiction over the Parties and their respective affiliates.

6.3 The provisions contained in this Paragraph 6 shall follow following the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

6.4 Press Release. Each of the FIRST PARTY or SECOND PARTY may issue from time to time press releases containing non-sensitive information in relation to this Agreement, subject to the prior written approval of the other party which approval shall be unreasonably withheld.

7. Entire Agreement. On the Effective Date, this Agreement, its Schedules and/or Annexes supercede any previous agreements, arrangements or representations between the Parties, whether oral or written, in respect of the subject matter hereof and shall constitute the entire agreement between the Parties in relation thereto.

8. Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is declared invalid by competent courts, the other provisions or parts shall not thereby be affected or impaired. In the event any provision of this Agreement is declared invalid, the Parties shall meet in good faith to agree, to the fullest extent allowed by law, on a substitute provision that would most closely approximate the intention of the invalid provision, and amend this Agreement accordingly.

9. Remedies. The remedies provided herein provided, the remedies under this Agreement shall be cumulative and not exclusive, and the exercise of one remedy shall not exclude the exercise or shall not be construed as a waiver of the right to exercise the other remedies.

10. Amendments. No changes or modifications of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing signed by both Parties.

11. Non-waiver. None of the provisions of this Agreement shall be considered waived by either Party except when such waiver is given in writing. The failure of either Party to insist in, in any one or more instances, upon strict performance of any of the provisions of this Agreement or to take advantage of any rights granted herunder shall not be construed as a waiver of any such provisions or the relinquishment of any such rights in the future, but the same shall continue and remain in full force and effect.

12. Accrued Liability. The termination or assignment by a Party of this Agreement shall not release such Party from any liability under this Agreement which at the time of termination or assignment has already accrued to it in favor of the other Party.

13. Governing Law; Interpretation; Venue. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, and shall be consistent with the rules and regulations of the ERC. In case of conflict between the provisions of the Metered Service Contract and this Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail.

In case of any dispute, controversy, or disagreement between the Parties arising from or in relation to this Agreement, the same shall be settled and/or litigated in the proper courts of the City of Davao, to the exclusion of all other courts.
