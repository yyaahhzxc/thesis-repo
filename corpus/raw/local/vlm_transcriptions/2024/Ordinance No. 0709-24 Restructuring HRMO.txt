SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "AN ORDINANCE RESTRUCTURING THE HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT OFFICE (HRMO) AND AMENDING SECTION 1 ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO) OF ORDINANCE NO. 2374, SERIES OF 1994."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES - Civil Service Commission (CSC) Memorandum Circular (MC) Number 12, Series of 2022, issued the 2022 Guidelines and Standards in the Establishment of Organizational Structures and Staffing Patterns in Local Governments pursuant to CSC Resolution No. 2200373. Titles of positions in Local Government Units corresponding to the same or similar functional description as those established in the latest DBM circular shall be used and adopted:

A. Letter of Atty. Francis Mark H. Layog, Acting City Administrator, addressed to Hon. J. Melchor B. Quitan, Jr., Vice-Mayor of the City of Davao, thru Hon. Alberto T. Ungab, chairperson of the Committee on Appointments and Government Reorganization of the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City

The letter requested for legislative authority relative to the creation of positions and organizational restructuring of the Human Resource Management Office (HRMO). This is a vital component for the aspiration of the said department for Level III accreditation. Included in the letter are the Proposed Organizational Structure, Proposed Organizational Functions per Position, Budgetary Requirements for Creation of Positions, and Proposed Plantilla Schedule.

B. Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994

Section 1 of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, entitled "An Ordinance Approving the Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City of Davao, and For Other Purposes" approved the attached volumes of staffing pattern and organizational structure of the City;

C. Section 6, Republic Act No. 7160

"Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern - Every local government unit shall design and implement its own organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil Service Commission.";

D. CSC Memorandum Circular No. 12 series of 2022

CSC MC No. 12, Series of 2022, states the adoption of the 2022 Guidelines and Standards in the Establishment of Organizational Structures and Staffing Patterns (OSSPs) in Local Government Units (LGUs) pursuant to CSC Resolution No. 2200373 promulgated on September 20, 2022:

1. Appropriate OSSPs of LGUs shall be determined and established in accordance with Section 17 (Basic Services and Facilities) of R.A. No. 7160;

SECTION 4. CREATION OF HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM-OPERATIONS AND MAINTENANCE DIVISION (HRMISMD) - The Human Resource Management Information System-Operations and Maintenance Division shall be created as a new division of the Human Resource Management Office:
A. The division shall be headed by a Supervising Administrative Officer with Salary Grade 22;
B. The position title of the Assistant Division Head shall be an Administrative Officer V with Salary Grade 18;
C. The sections under Human Resource Management Information System- Operations and Maintenance Division shall be the Systems Operations and Data Security Section, and Onboarding, Client Support and Documentation Section:
1. The Systems Operations and Data Security Section (SODSS) shall be composed of:
a. One (1) Administrative Officer IV (HRMO II) with Salary Grade 15 shall be the Section Head;
b. Three (3) Administrative Officer II (HRMO I) with Salary Grade 11;
2. The Onboarding, Client Support and Documentation Section (OCDSCS) shall be composed of two (2) Administrative Officer II (HRMO I) with Salary Grade 11.

SECTION 5. AMEND THE ADMINISTRATIVE DIVISION (AD) OF HRMO IN SECTION 1 (ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO) OF ORDINANCE NO. 2374 SERIES OF 1994 - Section 1 (Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City Government of Davao) of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, specifically the Administrative Division of HRMO shall be amended.

SECTION 7. CONVERSION OF POSITION - Conversion of the Computer Programmer III (SG 18) position to Administrative Officer V (SG 18) position shall be accomplished pursuant to DBM Memorandum or Budget Circulars, CSC rules and regulations, Republic Act No. 7160, and all other existing laws, rules and regulations.  
A. The position of Computer Programmer III (SG-18) shall be abolished for the purpose of conversion.  
1. Section 11.h of the Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Personnel Actions (OROAPA) revised July 2018, in part states "... In LGUs, no reclassification shall be allowed except when the position is actually vacant";  
2. DBM Budget Circular 2018-3 states "Any additional funding shall be sourced out of the abolition of vacant positions".

--- [Page 7] ---
B. The position Administrative Officer V (SG-18) shall be created in lieu of the abolished Computer Programmer III (SG-18).

1. Section 325.5 of Republic Act No. 7160 states "In cases of abolition of positions and the creation of new ones resulting from the abolition of existing positions in the career service, such abolition or creation shall be made in accordance with pertinent provisions of this Code and the civil service law, rules and regulations".

2. Section 325.7 of R.A. No. 7160 states "No changes in designation or nomenclature of positions resulting in a promotion or demotion in rank or in increased or decreased compensation shall be allowed, except when the position is actually vacant, and the filling of such positions shall be strictly made in accordance with the civil service law, rules, and regulations".

3. Section 2.5 of CSC Memorandum Circular No. 12, Series of 2022, states "In cases of the abolition of positions and the creation of new ones resulting from the abolition of existing positions in the career service, such abolition or creation shall be made in accordance with pertinent provisions of R.A. No. 7160 and the civil service laws, rules, and regulations. (RA No. 7160 Sec. 325.d)".
