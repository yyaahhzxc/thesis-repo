Ord. No. 0552-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "DABAWENYO DCPLINADO FOR THE FUTURE GENERATION".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Pursuant to Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991", the City Government of Davao shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of general welfare; and shall ensure and support preservation and enrichment of culture, promote safety, improve public morals, enhance social justice, maintain peace and order and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

Republic Act No. 10833 - otherwise known as "An Act Enhancing the Philippine Basic Education System by Strengthening the Curriculum and Increasing the Number of Years for Basic Education and Appropriating Funds Therefor and Other Purposes" - Section 5 (b) Curriculum Development states that: "The Department shall formulate the design and details of the enhanced basic education curriculum. The curriculum shall be flexible enough to enable and allow schools to localize, indigenize and enhance the same based on their respective educational and social contexts. The production and development of locally produced teaching materials shall be encouraged and approval of these materials shall devolve to the regional and division education units."

Likewise, the City Government of Davao aims to ensure that the people of Davao are empowered, protected, secure, gender and culturally sensitive to attain the best quality of life. Davao City aims to cultivate a disciplined and law-abiding citizenry, starting from the youngest member of society.

SECTION 3. OBJECTIVES

The objectives of this Ordinance are as follows:

a. To develop among children and the youth an authentic appreciation of their sense of moral responsibility to their fellow citizens, democratic institutions, and human society.

b. To empower the children and youth as future adults for them to understand, protect and help promote the common good as law-abiding citizens through self-discipline.

c. To enable the children and youth to understand, reflect, and fully discern the role that social media and modern technology play in their lives.

d. To guide the children and youth in realizing their duties as stewards of the environment and the good of the future generation.

e. To form the children and youth into individuals who do not discriminate against others, especially the poor, and respect women, the elderly, Indigenous Peoples and the LGBTQ community.

--- [Page 3] ---
**SECTION 4. SCOPE AND COVERAGE**

This Ordinance shall apply to all educational institutions within Davao City, covering all levels of basic education.

**SECTION 5. PROGRAM INTEGRATION**

a. Subject to the approved curricula, laws, and regulations of the Department of Education, the integration of the Dabawenyo DCPlinado modules into the Values Education (VE), Araling Panlipunan, Makabayan, and Good Manners and Right Conduct (GMRC) subjects within the Department of Education Curriculum shall be implemented in accordance with national standards.

b. The program shall include modules on:

- Civic Responsibility and Community Engagement
- Respect for Laws and Regulations
- Cultural Sensitivity and Gender Equality
- Personal Discipline and Ethical Behavior
- Information and Digital Literacies
- Environmental Stewardship
- Culture of Security
- Financial Literacy

**SECTION 6. IMPLEMENTATION**

The City Mayor's Office, in coordination with the Department of Education, shall be responsible for the:

a. Development, implementation, and assessment of Dabawenyo DCPlinado Curriculum Integration Program.

b. Regular training and workshops to be conducted for educators to effectively develop, improve, and deliver the curriculum.

**SECTION 7. MONITORING AND EVALUATION**

The City Government of Davao shall create a Monitoring and Evaluation Team.

a. The members of the Monitoring and Evaluation Team shall be appointed by the City Mayor and shall come from the various stakeholders of the Program.

b. Periodic assessments will be carried out to evaluate the program's impact on students. However, these assessments will not affect the academic standing of the learners.

**SECTION 8. APPROPRIATIONS**

The City Government of Davao shall allocate the necessary funds for the implementation of this Ordinance. Requesting further the Local School Board (LSB) to allocate sufficient funds through the Special Education Fund (SEF).

**SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE**

Any Ordinance, order, memorandum, rule, and regulation which is inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance is hereby modified or repealed accordingly.
