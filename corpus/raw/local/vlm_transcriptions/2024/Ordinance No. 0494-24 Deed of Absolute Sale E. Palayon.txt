Ord. No. 0494-24

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE EXECUTED BY THE GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE PURCHASE OF LAND REGISTERED UNDER THE NAME OF EVELYN SIASLA PALAYON, COVERED BY TRANSFER CERTIFICATE OF TITLE NO. T-386741, LOCATED AT SOBRECAREY STREET, BARANGAY 17-B, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY, TO BE UTILIZED AS A PERMANENT SITE FOR THE BARANGAY FACILITIES, INFRASTRUCTURES, OR OTHER PUBLIC PURPOSES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of the Local Government Code of 1991 empowers every local government unit to exercise powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Sale executed by the City Government of Davao for the purchase of land registered under the name of Evelyn Sialsa Palayon, covered by Transfer Certificate of Title No. T-386741, located at Sobrecarey St., Barangay 17-B, this City, to be utilized as a permanent site for the barangay facilities, infrastructures, or other public purposes.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 14th day of May 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
