SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Usufruct to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Barangay 27-C for the use of a parcel of land as the site of their Barangay Hall.
