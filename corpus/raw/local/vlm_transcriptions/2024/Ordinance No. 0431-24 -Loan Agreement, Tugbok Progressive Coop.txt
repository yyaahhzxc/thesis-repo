Ord. No. 043-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND TUGBOK PROGRESSIVE COCONUT FARMERS MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE."

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Tugbok Progressive Coconut Farmers Multipurpose Cooperative;

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 27th day of February 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
