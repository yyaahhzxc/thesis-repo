No. 0587-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC PARTICULARLY THE TWO (2) WAY LANE DIRECTION OF DAVAO COASTAL ROAD FROM THE INTERSECTION OF DAVAO-COTABATO ROAD (BAGO APLAYA) AND TULIP STREET, NEAR COASTAL ROAD ROUNDABOUT ECOLAND, MATINA, DAVAO CITY FROM AUGUST 23 (MIDNIGHT) UNTIL 9:00 AM ON AUGUST 24, 2024 IN CONNECTION WITH THE FUN RUN EVENT OF EASTINCOM 18TH FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY AND COMMEMORATION OF NATIONAL HEROES DAY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the abovementioned request, Mr. Ricardo Ray B. Garcia, LTC PAF (GSC) ACUCs for RRA of Eastern Mindanao Command requested for a road closure of the abovementioned roads.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 6th day of August 2024, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
