Ord. No. 0544-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION TO ALL EXHIBITORS IN THE MICRO, SMALL, AND MEDIUM ENTERPRISES DEVELOPMENT (MSMED) WEEK 2024 FROM THE PAYMENT OF SPECIAL MAYOR'S PERMIT FEES COVERING THE PERIOD JULY 12-14, 2024".

SECTION 2. BASIS - The Sangguniang Panlungsod, as the legislative body of the City, may grant tax exemptions, incentives, or reliefs in accordance with Section 458, (a) (2) (ii) of the Local Government Code of 1991. Further, Section 236, Article 28 of Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, or the 2017 Revenue Code provides that exemption from the payment of Special Mayor's Permit may also be granted to programs conducted by educational, charitable, religious, and governmental institutions, NGOs duly registered/accredited with the City Government.

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - All exhibitors that will participate in the Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Week 2024 are hereby exempted from the payment of special permit fees covering the period July 12-14, 2024.

SECTION 4. LIMITATIONS - The grant of exemption shall be limited only to the special permit fees imposed by the City. Consequently, all other fees, impositions, and taxes shall be paid in accordance with law.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, June 25, 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang, there being a quorum.
