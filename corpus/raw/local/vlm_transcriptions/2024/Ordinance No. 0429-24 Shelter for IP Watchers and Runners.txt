Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING THE SHELTER FOR IP WATCHERS AND RUNNERS OF PATIENTS FROM THE INDIGENOUS PEOPLES AND MUSLIMS BELONGING TO THE ELEVEN TRIBES OF DAVAO CITY NAMELY: KLATA, ATA, MATIGASALUG, OBU-MANUVU, TAGABAWA, TAUSIG, IRANUN, KAGAN, SAMA, MARANAO, AND MAGUNDANAO (KAMOTT-IKASAMMA")."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The State shall take measures, with the participation of the Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs) and Muslim Cultural Tribes concerned, to protect their rights and guarantee respect for their cultural integrity, and to ensure that members of the ICCs/IPs benefit on an equal footing from the rights and opportunities which national laws and regulations grant to other members of the population. The State also recognizes its obligations to respond to the strong expression of the ICCs/IPs for cultural integrity by assuming maximum ICC/IP participation in the direction of education, health, as well as other services of ICCs/IPs, in order to render such services more responsive to the needs and desires of these communities.

SECTION 3. OBJECTIVES - Qualified Indigenous Peoples (IPs) and Muslim tribe beneficiaries residing in far-flung areas of Davao City may avail of the services offered by the Ordinance. In establishing an operational inpatient shelter facility for Indigenous Peoples and Muslims (IPs/Muslims), the following are considerable objectives to prioritize the well-being, cultural sensitivity, and specific needs of this community:

1. Ensure that the facility respects and incorporates Indigenous cultural practices, traditions, and languages to create a culturally safe and welcoming environment;

2. Provide access to healthcare services that are sensitive to the unique health challenges faced by Indigenous Cultural Communities (ICCs), including addressing issues related to historical trauma and disparities in healthcare access by offering a range of services that address not only physical health but also mental, emotional, and spiritual well-being, reflecting indigenous concepts of holistic health;

3. Implement trauma-informed care principles to create a safe and supportive environment for Indigenous and Muslim individuals who may have experienced historical or personal trauma by providing counseling services that is culturally relevant to the individuals seeking help;

4. Involve Indigenous and Muslim community members in the planning, operation, and decision-making processes to ensure that the facility meets their specific needs and preferences;

5. Collaborate with Indigenous and Muslim-led organizations and tribal authorities to strengthen the network of support and resources available to the community;

6. Ensure that the facility's physical environment, design, and artwork are culturally appropriate and reflect Indigenous and Muslim aesthetics and values;

7. Develop a sustainable operational and funding plan that ensures the facility's long-term existence and effectiveness in meeting the needs of Indigenous and Muslim individuals;

8. To supplement other government services to the eleven (11) major tribes not mentioned in this Ordinance.

**SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS**

a) Inpatient Indigenous People and Muslims Shelter - This is a facility or program designed to provide culturally sensitive, community-based support and services to Indigenous and Muslim individuals who do not prefer or prefer not to stay overnight in a residential or inpatient shelter. These shelters are often established to address the specific needs of Indigenous populations, recognizing their unique cultural, historical, and social circumstances;

b) Shelter - A facility of the City Government of Davao providing cover or protection from weather and danger to the member of the Indigenous Peoples and Muslim Tribes belonging to the eleven (11) major tribes of Davao City.

**SECTION 5. ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SHELTER**

There shall be established an IP-Muslims KAMOTT- IKASAMMA Shelter to be constructed within the Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC) compound covering an area of Two Hundred (200) Square meters, more or less. The City Government of Davao shall allocate budget therefor under the Annual Development Fund (ADF) 2024.

**SECTION 6. WHO MAY AVALI**

Any member of the eleven (11) major tribes of Davao City, namely: KLATA, ATA, MATIGSALIGU, OBU-MANUVJU, TAGABAWA, TAUSUN, IRANUN, KAGAN, SAMA, MARANAQO, and MAGUINDANAO who are watchers and runners of IPs and Muslim patients may avail of the benefits, trainings, capacity building programs of the KAMOTT-IKASAMMA Shelter Ordinance.

**SECTION 7. SEPARABILITLTY CLAUSE**

If for any reason, any part or provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, any part or provision not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

**SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY**

This Ordinance shall take effect FIFTEEN (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao.

**ENACTED**, on the 27th day of February 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian Panlungsod (City Government Department Head).
