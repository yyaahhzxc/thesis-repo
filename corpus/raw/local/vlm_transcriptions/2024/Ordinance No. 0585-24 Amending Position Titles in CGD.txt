Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be referred to as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE POSITION TITLES EMBODIED IN ORDINANCE NO. 2374, SERIES OF 1994, PARTICULARLY IN SECTION 1- ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES - Civil Service Commission (CSC) Memorandum Circular (MC) Number 12, Series of 2022, issued the CY 2022 guidelines and standards in the establishment of organizational structures and staffing patterns in local governments pursuant to CSC Resolution No. 2200373. Titles of positions in Local Government Units (LGUs) corresponding to the same or similar functional description as those established in the latest DBM circular shall be used and adopted.

A. Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994 Section 1 of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, entitled "An Ordinance Approving the Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City of Davao, and for Other Purposes" approved the attached volumes of staffing pattern and organizational structure of the city;

B. Section 76 of Republic Act No. 7160 "Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern - Every local government unit shall design and implement its own organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil Service Commission.";

C. CSC Memorandum Circular No. 12, Series of 2022 CSC MC No. 12, Series of 2022, states the adoption of the 2022 Guidelines and Standards in the Establishment of Organizational Structures and Staffing Patterns (OSSPs) in Local Government Units (LGUs) pursuant to CSC Resolution No. 2200373, promulgated on September 20, 2022:

1. "Appropriate OSSPs of LGUs shall be determined and established in accordance with Section 17 (Basic Services and Facilities) of Republic Act No. 7160";

2. "The titles of positions in LGUs corresponding to the same or similar functional description as those established under the latest DBM Local Budget Circular on the Index of Occupational Services, Position Titles and Salary Grades (DBM IOS) in the Local Government, CY 2021 Edition, shall be used and adopted";

3. "All existing CSC policies inconsistent with these Guidelines shall be deemed repealed and/or amended accordingly";

D. CSC Resolution No. 2200373
The CSC resolution promulgated on September 20, 2022 adopts the "Guidelines and Standards in the Establishment of Organizational Structures and Staffing Patterns in Local Government Units";

E. DBM Local Budget Circular No. 137 issued on July 13, 2021 - Index of Occupational Services, Position Titles and Salary Grades in the Local Government (IOS-LGU), CY 2021 Edition
The IOS-LGU, CY 2021 Edition, contains the updated list of classes or position titles existing in the Local Government Units (LGUs) that are grouped by occupation;

F. Budget Circular No. 2022-2, issued on April 5, 2022
"The DBM shall issue advisories whenever there are modifications in the IOS as a result of the approved creation, abolition, rettling or reallocation of classes or position titles". DBM IOS CY 2022 is already available;

G. DBM Manual on Position Classification and Compensation (PCC)
This is the manual covering the principles governing the position classification and compensation system in the Philippines.
