Ord. No. 0433-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROADS TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON THE SPECIFIED DATE AND TIME HEREIN INDICATED IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE 87TH ARAW NG DABAW".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs."

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the yearly celebration of Araw ng Dabaw, the City Tourism Office requested for the closure of roads to vehicular traffic on specified date and time herein indicated.
