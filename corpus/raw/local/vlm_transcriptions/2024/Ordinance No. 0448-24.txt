Series of 2024

An Ordinance Authorizing the City Mayor to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao the deed of donation by and between the Office of the President and the City Government of Davao relative to the donation of twenty million pesos (P20,000,000.00) in favor of the latter, as financial assistance to its constituents affected by the Northeast Monsoon and Trough of Low-Pressure Area

Ord. No. 0448-24  
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN REHAF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO THE DEED OF DONATION BY AND BETWEEN THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF TWENTY MILLION PESOS (P20,000,000.00) IN FAVOR OF THE LATTER, AS FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO ITS CONSTITUENTS AFFECTED BY THE NORTHEAST MONSOON AND TROUGH OF LOW-PRESSURE AREA."  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22 (a) (5) and (c) and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that:  

"Section 22. Corporate Powers  
(a) Every Local Government Unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:  
(5) To enter into contracts; and  
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the Local Chief Executive in behalf of the Local Government Unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A table copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."  

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.  
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:  
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the City Government, and in this connection, shall:  
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."  

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign for and in behalf of the City of Davao the Deed of Donation by and between the Office of the President and the City Government of Davao relative to the donation of Twenty Million Pesos (P20,000,000.00) in favor of the latter, as financial assistance to its constituents affected by the Northeast Monsoon and trough of Low-Pressure Area.  

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Deed of Donation made and entered into this day of 20 , by and between:

The OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT, with office address at Malacañang, J.P. Laurel St., San Miguel, Manila, represented herein by Undersecretary ADRIAN CARLOS A. BERSAMIN and Undersecretary AL- AZREE J. MOHAMMADSAli and hereinafter referred to as the DONOR;

and

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at City Hall, Poblacion District, Davao City, represented herein by MAYOR SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, and hereinafter referred to as the DONEE,

WITNESSETH

1. GRANT. That the DONOR hereby donates to the DONEE the total amount of Twenty Million Pesos (P20,000,000.00), PHILIPPINE CURRENCY, from the Socio-Civic Projects Fund (SCPF), by virtue of OP Office Order No. 02 dated 29 December 2016, and approved Memorandum by the Office of the Executive Secretary dated 19 December 2023.

2. That the DONEE hereby accepts and agrees to use the said amount to provide the immediate needs and concerns of the DONEE's constituents affected by the combined effects of the Northeast Monsoon and trough of Low Pressure Area.

3. That the DONOR shall release the total amount of P20,000,000.00 SCPF financial assistance for the immediate needs and concerns of the DONEE's constituents affected by the combined effects of the Northeast Monsoon and trough of Low Pressure Area.

4. That the DONEE hereby states, for the purpose of giving full effect and validity to this donation, the following:

a. That the Grant shall be utilized to provide the immediate needs and concerns of the DONEE's constituents who were affected by the Northeast Monsoon and trough of Low Pressure Area, such as but not limited to the following forms of assistance, subject to pertinent laws, and government accounting and auditing guidelines, rules and regulations.
- Food;
- Medicines and other medical supplies;
- Cash Assistance not exceeding Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) per household for immediate household needs. Provided that the total cash assistance to households shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the total grant provided to the LGU; and
- Non-food items such as but not limited to the following:

--- [Page 8] ---
o Additional toilet and bath in the evacuation centers and repair of the existing ones;

o Materials needed for the shelters/evacuation centers such as blankets, towels, bed mats, and tents;

o Livelihood assistance in the form of providing seedlings, fishnets, fishing gears, and livestock;

o Construction materials for the repair of houses damaged by the flooding the amount of which shall depend on the extent of damage, but not exceeding Thirty Thousand Pesos (P30,000.00) per household.

o Construction materials needed for the construction of temporary learning shelters for the affected students;

o Other special or extraordinary expenses incurred related to the calamity such as but not limited to: fuel to transport the evacuees, food for and during the medical missions in the different evacuation centers/areas, and any other incidental expenses, and

o Other similar non-food expense items;

b. That the DONOR shall utilize the grant for a period of one (1) year reckoned from the date of the actual transfer of the fund.

c. That the DONOR shall submit to the DONOR, a Fund Utilization Report showing the expenses incurred and the disbursement of the Grant, duly stamped and received by the DONOR's Resident Auditor.

d. That the DONOR - Office of the Deputy Executive Secretary for Finance and Administration may conduct a Financial and Physical Performance Evaluation of the utilization of the Grant to ensure that programs, activities, and projects are properly implemented, and funds are fully liquidated, documented, and effectively utilized for the intended purpose.

e. That if any provisions hereof is prohibited or made unenforceable under any applicable laws or by a competent court of authority, the same shall not affect any provisions which are otherwise valid and enforceable.

f. That no terms and conditions of this agreement shall be deemed waived and no breach or default be excused unless such waiver or excuse be in writing and signed by the DONOR or DONEE.
