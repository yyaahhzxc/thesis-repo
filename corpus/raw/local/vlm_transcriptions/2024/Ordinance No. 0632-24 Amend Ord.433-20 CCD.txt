AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0433-20, SERIES OF 2020, PARTICULARLY SECTION 2 THEREOF, TO INCLUDE IN ITS COVERAGE THE CANTEEN IN THE CITY COLLEGE OF DAVAO AND THE COFFEE SHOP IN THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOOD BUILDING

Ord No. 0632-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0433-20, SERIES OF 2020, PARTICULARLY SECTION 2 THEREOF, TO INCLUDE IN ITS COVERAGE THE CANTEEN IN THE CITY COLLEGE OF DAVAO AND THE COFFEE SHOP IN THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOOD BUILDING".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 2 of Ordinance No. 0433-20, Series 2020, shall be amended as follows:

From:

Article Three. - Fees for Library and Information Center

Section 256. Imposition of Fees. - There shall be collected the following fees for the Davao City Library and Information Center:

xxx

d. Rental Fee for Use of Space in the City Library Building, Canteen in the City College of Davao Building, and the Coffee Shop in the Sangguniang Panlungsod Building:

1. Airconditioned - PhP 25/sq.m/day
2. Non-Airconditioned - PhP 16.50/sq.m/day

To:

Article Three. - Fees for Library and Information Center, City College of Davao, and Sangguniang Panlungsod

Section 256. Imposition of Fees. - There shall be collected the following fees for the Davao City Library and Information Center, City College of Davao, and Sangguniang Panlungsod:

xxx

d. Rental Fee for Use of Space in the City Library Building, City College of Davao Building, and Sangguniang Panlungsod Building:

1. Airconditioned - PhP 25/sq.m/day
2. Non-Airconditioned - PhP 16.50/sq.m/day

SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE. - All Ordinances or portions of Ordinances in conflict with this Ordinance, or inconsistent with the regulations of this Ordinance, are hereby repealed to the extent necessary to give this Ordinance full force and effect.
