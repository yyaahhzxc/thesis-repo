Ord. No. 0584-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE RENAMING THE LYING-IN CENTERS, REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND WELLNESS CENTER (RHWC), AND THE DISPENSARY SECTION OF THE CITY HEALTH OFFICE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The Local Government of Davao acknowledges the policy enunciated in Chapter II, Section 13, Paragraph (b) of Republic Act No. 7160, or the Local Government Code of 1991, which provides that the Sangguniang Panlungsod of highly urbanized cities may, in consultation with the Philippine Historical Commission, change the name of facilities within its territorial jurisdiction, among others, city hospitals, health centers, and other health facilities.

Chapter II, Section 13, paragraph (f) of the Local Government Code of 1991, likewise provides that the changing of names of public hospitals, health centers, and other health facilities shall be made only upon the recommendation of the concerned Local Health Board.

Further, the renaming of the facilities mentioned in this Ordinance was recommended by virtue of Resolution No. 02, Series of 2024, of the Local Health Board of Davao City, with the concurrence of the Philippine Historical Commission.

SECTION 3. RENAMING OF THE LYING-IN CENTERS, REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND WELLNESS CENTER (RHWC), AND THE DISPENSARY SECTION OF THE CITY HEALTH OFFICE - This Ordinance shall hereby rename the remaining birthing homes with the name "Lying-in" to "Birthing Homes".

It shall likewise rename the "Dispensary (Main)" of the City Health Office (CHO)" into "City Health Office - Dispensary Section".

Furthermore, the acronym in parenthesis "(RHWC)" shall be added to the name of the Reproductive Health and Wellness Center.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, August 6, 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
