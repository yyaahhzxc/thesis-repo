Ord. No. 0430-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND PHILIPPINE DIESEL CALIBRATION COOPERATIVE."

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Philippine Diesel Corporation.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on February 27, 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
