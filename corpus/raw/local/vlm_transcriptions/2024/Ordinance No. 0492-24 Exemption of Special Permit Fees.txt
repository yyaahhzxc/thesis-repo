Ord. No. 0492-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION TO ALL EXHIBITORS IN THE 9TH PHILIPPINE HALAL TRADE AND TOURISM EXPO (PHTE) FROM THE PAYMENT OF SPECIAL PERMIT FEES COVERING THE PERIOD MAY 23 TO 25, 2024".

SECTION 2. BASIS: The Sangguniang Panlungsod, as the legislative body of the City, may grant tax exemptions, incentives, or reliefs in accordance with Section 458 (a) (2) (ii) of the Local Government Code of 1991.

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION: All exhibitors that will participate in the 9th Philippine Halal Trade and Tourism Expo (PHTE) are hereby exempted from the payment of special permit fees covering the period May 23 to 25, 2024.

SECTION 4. LIMITATION: The grant of exemption shall be limited only to the special permit fees imposed by the City. Consequently, all other fees, impositions, and taxes shall be paid in accordance with law.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 7th day of May 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
