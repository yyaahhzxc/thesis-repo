Ord. No. 0390-24

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF SPECIFIED STREETS ON THE TIME AND SET-UP LOCATION MENTIONED HEREIN IN CONNECTION WITH THE UPCOMING MASS LAUNCHING AND PRAYER RALLY AT SAN PEDRO SQUARE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7169, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the upcoming mass launching and prayer rally, the ZEJEN MANAGEMENT GROUP requested for a temporary road closure of specified streets on the time and set-up location as follows:
> January 26, 2024, Friday, 10PM, until January 29, 2024, Monday, 5AM:  
San Pedro Street from corner Bolton to corner CM Recto;  
San Pedro Street from corner Bolton to corner Magallanes; and  
Bolton Street from corner Rizal to corner San Pedro Street.

> January 27, 2024, Saturday, from 8AM to January 29, 2024, 5AM:  
Anda Street from corner Magallanes Street to corner Rizal Street; and  
Pondiano Street (Crooked Road) from corner Rizal Street to corner San Pedro Square.

> January 27, 2024, Saturday, from 8PM to January 29, 2024, 5AM:  
San Pedro corner Anda to San Pedro corner Quirino; and  
Legaspi Street from corner Rizal to corner Magallanes.

> January 28, 2024, Sunday, from 1PM to 8PM:  
CM Recto corner Marco Polo to corner San Pedro Street;  
Entire Length of Roxas Boulevard (one lane-Aldevinco Side); and  
San Pedro Extension from corner Quezon Boulevard to corner CM Recto.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 23rd day of January 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
