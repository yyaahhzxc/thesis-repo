Ordinance No. 0694-19  
Series of 2019  

An Ordinance authorizing the City Mayor to utilize a portion of the thirty thousand pesos (P30,000.00) from the City Government's five percent (5%) Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund (Calamity Fund) for the purpose of providing relief to the victims of the typhoon "Amang" and heavy rains which affected the Province of Davao Oriental, which were declared under a state of calamity due to tropical depression "Amang" and heavy rains; and the municipality of Tagum, the municipalities of Carmen, Sto. Tomas, and San Isidro in the province of Davao del Norte, which was declared under a state of calamity due to the effects of the dry spell experienced in some parts of Davao del Sur, subject to existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

Ordinance No. 0694-19  
Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:  

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY GOVERNMENT TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OR OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (DRMF) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2019, IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF THREE MILLION NINE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P3,900,000.00), TO BE GIVEN AT THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P300,000.00) EACH, TO THE CITY OF TAGUM; THE MUNICIPALITIES OF CARMEN, STO. TOMAS, ASUNCION, BRAULIO E. DUALI AND KAPALONG, ALL OF THE PROVINCE OF DAVAO NORTE; THE MUNICIPALITIES OF BOSTON, CATEEL, CARAGA, TARRAGONA AND BAGANGA, ALL OF THE PROVINCE OF DAVAO ORIENTAL, WHICH WERE DECLARED UNDER A STATE OF CALAMITY DUE TO TROPICAL DEPRESSION "AMANG" AND HEAVY RAINS; AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF BANSALAN, PROVINCE OF DAVAO DEL SUR, WHICH WAS DECLARED UNDER A STATE OF CALAMITY DUE TO THE EFFECTS OF THE DRY SPELL EXPERIENCED IN SOME PARTS OF DAVAO DEL SUR, SUBJECT TO EXISTING GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS.  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.  

SECTION 3. BENEFICIARIES. The following areas are hereby designated as beneficiaries of the assistance, to wit:  
1. CITY OF TAGUM, DAVAO DEL NORTE  
2. MUNICIPALITY OF CARMEN, DAVAO DEL NORTE  
3. MUNICIPALITY OF STO. TOMAS, DAVAO DEL NORTE  
4. MUNICIPALITY OF ASUNCION, DAVAO DEL NORTE  
5. MUNICIPALITY OF BRAULIO E. DUALI, DAVAO DEL NORTE  
6. MUNICIPALITY OF KAPALONG, DAVAO DEL NORTE  
7. MUNICIPALITY OF BOSTON, DAVAO ORIENTAL  
8. MUNICIPALITY OF CATEEL, DAVAO ORIENTAL  
9. MUNICIPALITY OF CARAGA, DAVAO ORIENTAL  
10. MUNICIPALITY OF TARRAGONA, DAVAO ORIENTAL  
11. MUNICIPALITY OF BAGANGA, DAVAO ORIENTAL  
12. MUNICIPALITY OF BANSALAN, DAVAO DEL SUR  
TOTAL  
P 3,900,000.00  

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASIS. Pursuant to the provisions of Section 324 (d) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, as amended by Republic Act 8185, which states: "Five Percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as annual lump sum appropriations for relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction and other works or services in connection with calamities which may occur during the budget year. Provided, however, that such fund shall be used only in the area, or a portion thereof, of the local government unit or other areas affected by a disaster or calamity, as determined and declared by the local sanggunian concerned."

Further, Section 5 of the Rules and Regulations implementing Republic Act 8185, states that:

"Section 5. Allocation and Utilization of Five Percent (5%) Lumpsum Appropriations for Calamity Fund

a) Allocation xxx xxx xxx
"LGUs may also allocate/use a portion of the five percent (5%) Calamity Fund to other affected areas on condition that the said areas are declared as under a State of Calamity by the Sanggunian concerned";

b) Utilization
(2) A portion of the calamity fund may also be authorized to be used by the LGU concerned to provide financial assistance to other LGUs whose area or portion thereof had been declared under a state of calamity by its Sanggunian".

SECTION 5. USE OF FUNDS - The amounts herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sanggunian Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
