(DLPC), ABOTIZ FOUNDATION, INC., AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A RESCUE CENTER FACILITY FOR MARINE TURTLES AND OTHER MARINE LIFE SPECIES AT THE ABOTIZ CLEANERY PARK, WHICH SHALL BE KNOWN AS THE "PAWIKAN CENTER" IN DAVAO CITY

Ord. No. 0681-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR, TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL RESOURCES (DENR) REGIONAL OFFICE XI, DAVAO LIGHT AND POWER COMPANY, INC. (DLPC), ABOTTIZ FOUNDATION, INC., AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A RESCUE CENTER FOR ENDANGERED TURTLES AND A MARINE HATCHERY AT THE ABOTTIZ CLEANERGY PARK, WHICH SHALL BE KNOWN AS THE "PAWIKAN CENTER" IN DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and among the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) Regional Office XI, Davao Light and Power Company Inc. (DLPC), Abottiz Foundation, Inc., and the City Government of Davao, relative to the establishment of a Rescue Center for Endangered Turtles and a Marine Hatchery at the Abottiz Cleanergy Park, which shall be known as the "Pawikan Center" in Davao City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 8th day of January, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this day of 2018, at , by and among:

DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL RESOURCES-REGION XI, a government agency duly established and operating under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at Lanang, Davao City, represented herein by its Regional Executive Directors, RUTH M. TAWANTAWAN, hereinafter referred to as the "DENR-XI";

-and-

CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit duly organized and existing under and by virtue of its charter under Republic Act No. 7160, with seat of government at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented herein by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as "LGU-Davao City";

-and-

DAVAO LIGHT & POWER COMPANY, INC., a corporation duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at Abotiz Corporate Center, Governor Manuel A. Cuenco Avenue, Kasambagan, Cebu City, represented herein by its President and Chief Executive Officer, JAIME JOSE Y. ABOITIZ, hereinafter referred to as "DLPC";

-and-

ABOITIZ FOUNDATION, INC., a non-stock, non-profit corporation duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at Abotiz Corporate Center, Governor Manuel A. Cuenco Avenue, Kasambagan, Cebu City, represented herein by its Trustee, SABIN M. ABOITIZ, hereinafter referred to as the "Foundation".

[Each of the DENR-XI, LGU-Davao City, DLPC and the Foundation shall be referred to individually as a "Party," and collectively as the "Parties."] 

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ("DENR"), by virtue of Executive Order No. 132, is the government agency mandated to conserve, protect, preserve and ensure the sustainable utilization of wildlife resources for the benefit of the nation's present and future generations;

WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of Republic Act 9147 otherwise known as the Wildlife Conservation and Protection Act of 2001, the DENR-XI exercises management authority over wildlife resources, including marine turtles and their habitats, within the territorial jurisdiction of Region XI which includes Davao City;

WHEREAS, the DENR-XI endeavors to cooperate and collaborate with local government units, the private sector, non-governmental organizations, people's organizations and other interested and capable entities in the conservation of marine turtles within the territorial jurisdiction of Region XI;

--- [Page 10] ---
WHEREAS, DENR-XI field surveys confirm that marine turtles or "pawikan" are found within the territorial waters of Davao City;
WHEREAS, LGU-Davao City has expressed its willingness to assist and collaborate with the DENR-XI in pursuing marine turtle conservation and protection activities within its area of responsibility;
WHEREAS, the Sangguniang Panglungsod of Davao City has, by virtue of Sangguniang Panglungsod Ordinance No. 075-07, Series of 2007, declared the shoreline extending up to a distance of around 300 meters seaward in Punta Dumalag as a "Marine Protected Area" for the purpose of, among others, protection and conservation of endangered, rare and threatened species such as marine turtles;
WHEREAS, the Foundation has been granted by Ronesgo Development Corporation, the registered owner of the property located in Punta Dumalag, Matina, Davao City, the right to access and use the same for the establishment of Abotiz Cleanery Park therein;
WHEREAS, DLPC and the Foundation, in pursuit of their commitment to significantly contribute in environmental protection as part of their commitment to corporate social responsibility, are also willing to cooperate and collaborate with the DENR-XI and other stakeholders in the conservation and protection of marine turtles;
WHEREAS, the DENR-XI is willing to foster partnerships with LGU-Davao City, DLPC and the Foundation to strengthen conservation efforts for marine turtles that may be found at the Abotiz Cleanery Park located in Punta Dumalag;
WHEREAS, for the purpose of protecting and conserving endangered species, specifically marine turtles,aspart of the overall multi-sectorial effort of pursuing sustainable development within the context of a balanced ecology, the Parties herein agree to initiate a Project (as defined below) and enter into this Agreement;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of the mutual covenants herein set forth, the Parties hereby agree to the following terms and conditions:
ARTICLE 1. THE PROJECT
The project (hereinafter referred to as the "Project") involves the following:
1.1 The establishment of a rescue center facility for marine turtles and a marine turtle hatchery called the Abotiz Cleanery Park which shall be known as the "Pawikan Center" For purposes of this Agreement, rescue center facility or the Pawikan Center refers to the temporary shelter, clinic, laboratory, observation deck, boardwalks and any other facilities necessary or convenient for the rescue, rehabilitation, conservation, protection and care of marine turtles in Punta Dumalag areas; and
1.2 The enhancement of an ecosystem-based protection for the endangered marine turtleand other endangered species that may be found in the Abotiz Cleanery Park, through planting of endemic trees and implementation of other measures for the conservation and, where necessary and appropriate, protection of marine turtles at all stages of their life cycle,
ARTICLE 2. OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT
The Project shall have the following objectives:
2.1 Conserve and protect the nesting area of marine turtles in Abotiz Cleanery Park located in Punta Dumalag;
2.2 Identify, document, and address threats to marine turtles and their habitats in Punta Dumalag.

--- [Page 11] ---
2.3 Improve understanding of marine turtle ecology and population in Punta Dumalag through research, monitoring and information exchange;
2.4 Enhance public awareness and participation in the conservation and protection of marine turtles and their habitats in Punta Dumalag; and
2.5 Enhance cooperation for the long-term recovery of the marine turtle population in Punta Dumalag.
ARTICLE 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE DENR-XI
The DENR-XI:
3.1 Shall provide technical assistance in the implementation of the Project by way of, but not necessarily limited to, the following:
3.1.1 Conduct of training on marine turtle conservation and management, including rescue protocol for the partner-agencies/organizations under this Agreement;
3.1.2 Provision of technical services, including data gathering and analysis of relevant information on marine turtles that may be encountered in Punta Dumalag;
3.1.3 Provision of marine turtle response manual and data sheets necessary for marine turtle handling, tagging and release, such as, but not limited to:
MT01 Marine Turtle Tagging Data Form
MT02 Marine Turtle Stranding Report Form
MT03 Marine Turtle Rehabilitation Health Record
MT04 Marine Turtle Necropsy Form
MT05 Marine Turtle Hatchery Data Form
MT06 Marine Turtle Nest Evaluation Form
3.2 Shall initiate or assist in the establishment of the Pawikan Center within the Abotitz Cleanenergy Park in Punta Dumalag, Davao City that has/have been identified and delineated by the Parties;
3.3 Shall take the lead in the conduct of information and education campaign on marine turtle conservation and relevant laws, rules and regulations, specifically in coastal communities, and to create awareness on marine turtle conservation in the City of Davao;
3.4 Shall serve as the repository of data and information generated through the Project and disseminate such information/data in the form of annual status reports to partner-agencies/organizations under this Agreement;
3.5 Hereby allows DLPC and/or the Foundation to establish or build the Pawikan Center or rescue center facility in the foreshore, seashore, underwater or any part of the littoral zone of the Abotitz Cleanergy Park, as may be necessary for the conduct of Project-related activities and the security of the Pawikan Center, and the authority herein granted to DLPC and/or the Foundation to do so shall be subject to the condition that the same shall not be destructive of the marine turtle habitat in the area, provided further that no permanent structures are built in the foreshore, seashore and underwater areas;
3.6 Shall delegate interested and qualified employees, agents, and members of LGU Davao City and DLPC as Wildlife Enforcement Officer(s) ("WEO/s") in accordance with existing policies, rules and regulations;
3.7 Shall undertake to establish Punta Dumalag as a critical habitat for marine turtles.

--- [Page 12] ---
pursuant to DENR Memorandum Circular No. 2007-02 dated February 28, 2007, as appropriate, and Republic Act No. 9147.

ARTICLE 4. OBLIGATIONS OF LGU-DAVAO CITY

The LGU-Davao City:

4.1 Shall pass local ordinances/ to support and/or strengthen the enforcement of existing laws, rules and regulations for the conservation of marine turtles and the protection of their habitats in Punta Dumalag;

4.2 Shall assist in the establishment of Punta Dumalag as a critical habitat for marine turtles in accordance with Republic Act No. 9147, its implementing rules and regulations and DENR Memorandum Circular No. 2007-02, as may be appropriate;

4.3 Shall allow the use of LGU-Davao City facilities and/or provide manpower support, if available, in pursuit of Project-related activitiesand in the implementation of this Agreement, upon prior notification-request from the DENR-XI;

4.4 Furnish monitoring reports to the DENR- XI on activities implemented by LGU-Davao City under this Agreement, if applicable; and

4.5 Hereby allows DLPC and/or the Foundation to establish or build the Pawikan Center or rescue center facility in the Punta Dumalag Marine Protected Area, as may be necessary for the conduct of Project-related activities and the security of the Pawikan Center.

ARTICLE 5. OBLIGATIONS OF DLPC

DLPC:

5.1 Shall educate or train its employees and personnel using data forms/materials provided by the DENR-Xlor the purpose of:

5.1.1 Providing assistance in sustaining marine turtle rescue, tagging, nesting, incidence monitoring and nest protection activities in collaboration with LGU-Davao City and other concerned parties; and

5.1.2 Providing assistance in capacity-building efforts for all stakeholders to strengthen conservation and management of marine turtles and their habitat;

5.2 Shall allow the use of the Pawikan Center and any other available facilities in the Abotiz Cleanergy Park for the conduct of Project-related activities upon prior notification-request by the DENR-XI;

5.3 Shall identify interested and qualified employees who maybe deputized by the DENR- XI as WEDOs;

5.4 Shall assist in the enforcement of wildlife laws, policies and regulations governing marine turtle and habitat conservation when properly deputized therefor; and

5.5 Shall furnish DENR-XI with reports on activities undertaken pursuant to this Agreement.

ARTICLE 6. OBLIGATIONS OF THE FOUNDATION

The Foundation:

6.1 Shall closely coordinate with the other Parties for the efficient and effective implementation of the Project.

--- [Page 13] ---
6.2 Shall provide monetary assistance as may be necessary for the implementation of the Project; and

6.3 Shall provide set of materials and supplies that are necessary for the conduct of an efficient response during marine turtle rescues.

ARTICLE 7. JOINT OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES

The Parties further agree to jointly undertake the following:

7.1 Assign/designate a focal person to be directly responsible in coordinating with the Parties and overseeing the implementation of the Project in accordance with the Agreement;

7.2 Organize a multi-sectoral Pawikan Conservation Team ("PCT") that will handle the implementation of activities under the Project, to include, but not necessarily be limited to, the following:

7.2.1 Establishment, monitoring, and management of the Pawikan Center;

7.2.2 Conduct of field research, including assessment, monitoring, and documentation of marine turtle population and habitat, survival threats, and turtle mortalities in Punta Dumagat;

7.2.3 Rehabilitation of marine turtle habitats to enhance recovery of degraded seagrass, mangrove and coral reef areas in Punta Dumagat;

7.3 As may be necessary, source out funds from foreign and/or local institutions/organizations to sustain the implementation of the Project and/or prepare project proposals on marine turtles and to make possible submission to outside funding sources; provided, that funds generated by any of the Parties from the sale of marine turtles and their products shall be shared equally among the other Parties by giving them notice in writing within a reasonable period from the receipt of such funds; provided further, that utilization of such funds shall be subject to a prior detailed work and financial plan mutually approved by all the Parties; and provided further, that should the funds be generated by DLPC and/or the Foundation, such funds shall remain private funds;

7.4 Monitor illegal activities on marine turtles and their habitats and strengthen linkages among the barangays, other concerned local government units, other government agencies, academic institutions, non-government organizations, people's organization and other stakeholders to facilitate communication and exchange of information concerning illegal hunting, killing or capture, trafficking, trade, sale, possession, consumption of marine turtles, and/or any of their parts (e.g. meat) or by-products (e.g. carapace), and for long-term research, conservation and management of marine turtles and their habitat in Punta Dumagat;

7.5 Assess the implementation of this Agreement prior to the expiration of its original term and each of its renewed term;

7.6 Annually analyze data generated through this Agreement;

7.7 Identify and implement other forms of mechanisms towards meeting the objectives of this Agreement;

7.8 Provide, execute, or otherwise deliver all necessary documents, authorizations, certifications, contracts, or deeds as may be required by the Foundation or DLPC for purposes of the Foundations' or DLPC's compliance with applicable laws, ordinances, rules and regulations in connection with the implementation of the Project;

7.9 Ensure that the project operation and development shall be confined only within the

--- [Page 14] ---
terms and conditions of the Agreement, the Marine Turtle Conservation Action Plan, if any, and in line with the objectives of this Agreement.

ARTICLE B. LIMITATIONS

8.1 LGU-Davao City, the Foundation, D.P.C., or any of their employees, staff, members or agents shall not rear or maintain marine turtles in captivity except when geared for rehabilita- tion purposes of specimens turned-over, retrieved, rescued, or confiscated pursuant to this Agreement; Provided, that details with regard to rescue operations involving marine turtles in captivity by any of the Parties shall be made known to the other Parties before any information would be released to the media and the public; Provided further, that WEOs, enforcement groups, and deputized employees shall maintain confidentiality with regard to those rescue opera- tions and shall not post any details on such rescue operations in their personal social media accounts;

8.2 All information and data that could be generated through the Project shall be jointly owned by the Parties and shall only be published upon unanimous consent of the Parties; and

8.3 No Party shall enter into any contract or agreement with, nor assume any obligation on behalf of, or in the name of the other Parties, in the course of, or during the implementation of this Agreement without the prior written consent of such Parties.

ARTICLE 9. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Each of the Parties represents and warrants to the other Parties that:

9.1 It is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines;

9.2 It has the requisite capacity, corporate power and/or authority to enter into this Agreement and to carry out its obligations hereunder and that this Agreement has been duly and validly executed and constitutes its valid and binding obligation, enforceable against each Party in accordance with the terms hereof;

9.3 The execution of this Agreement or the performance of the terms hereof or the transactions contemplated hereby or the fulfillment of any of the terms hereof will not violate any provision of, or result in the breach of, or accelerate or permit the acceleration of the performance required by the terms, of any applicable law, rule or regulation or any governmental body having jurisdiction in the premises, or the Party's Articles of Incorporation, By-laws or constitutive documents, or conflict with, as a result, in a breach of, or result in the termination of, any license, permit, certificate, bond, undertaking, agreement or comparable order issued by it or any of its properties or assets may be bound or affected thereby or constitute a default or encumbrance of any nature whatsoever upon any of its properties or assets under such agreement or instrument, or in any way affect the rights of the Parties to the benefits of the same, including, but not limited to, revocation, suspension, or cancellation of, any licenses, approvals, certificates, permits or authorizations held by it; and

9.4 It has not paid, agreed to pay, or caused or permitted to be paid, either directly or indirectly, in any form, to any trustee, officer, employee, or agent of the other Parties any commission, percentage, or contingent fee payment or other benefit of any kind in connection with the entering into or performance of this Agreement;

All notices required under this Agreement shall be given by personal delivery, by registered mail or reputable courier services (postage prepaid and properly addressed), or electronic mail. Notices shall be addressed to:
