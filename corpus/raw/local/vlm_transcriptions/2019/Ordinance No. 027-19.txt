Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ON BUILDING HEIGHT LIMITS AMENDING ARTICLE VIII SECTIONS 3 TO 6 OF THE AMENDED 2013 COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. PURPOSE: This Ordinance is enacted for the following purposes:
1) Guide, control and regulate future growth and development of the City in accordance with pertinent laws on building height regulations and on allowable urban development mechanisms responsive to the challenges and opportunities of urbanization;
2) Provide an orderly pattern of development that is both transit-oriented and transit responsive;
3) Conform to flight safety regulations and improve disaster-resilience;
4) Preserve view of cultural and heritage sites;
5) Promote and improve urban livability index such as better air quality, sufficient access to sunlight, open and green spaces and urban connectivity and mobility comforts;
6) Provide incentives to the private sector in improving the built environment.

SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF POLICY: It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to manage efficiently the urban landscape where mobility potentials are maximized, greening programs actualized through innovative space provision, and discrimination against the visually impaired through building height restrictions. The City shall continue to devise zoning and land use mechanisms responsive to the challenges and opportunities as may be presented by urban growth and development. The scheme for Additional floor-space credits for qualified individuals or corporations which have allocated open spaces for the public through the Privately Owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS) program and the premium required from developers opting for additional floor areas over and above the regular height limit being imposed by the city are strictly enforced. The construction of buildings that may be permitted in a zone for a specified fee shall provide the City an avenue for revenue generation that may be devoted to road improvement and greening programs.

It shall be further declared to be the policy of the City to strictly enforce measures in accordance with existing and relevant land use provisions.

SECTION 4. SCOPE OF APPLICATION: This Ordinance shall prescribe the rules applicable to any building construction activity within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City. This Ordinance shall prescribe the guidelines on the applicable permissible height in relation to the distance from the flight path and with reference to the desired urban densities and in setting relative order to the City's skyline and preservation efforts. Variance or additional premium height over and above the regular height limit set by the City shall be allowed and permitted provided that it shall not be higher than the limitations set by the Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAA).

--- [Page 6] ---
**SECTION 5. DEFINITION OF TERMS-**

For the purpose of the Rules and Regulations, the following terms and phrases shall mean as follows:

1) Obstacle Limitation Surface (OLS) - is an imaginary surface above ground that sets the height limit for building structures to ensure flight navigational safety;

2) Floor Area Ratio (FAR) - is the ratio of the total plot area to the total building area and is determined by the size of the plot and the width of the road in front of the plot;

3) "Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP)" shall refer to the document embodying specific proposal for guiding, regulating growth and/or development;

4) HLLRB - Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board;

5) Conforming Use - a use, which is in accordance with the zone classification as provided for in the Ordinance;

6) Green Architecture - building and structure design philosophy that aims at minimal use of non-renewable and/or polluting materials and resources/energy in construction and use of a facility when completed;

7) Green Space - community space consisting of land (as parks) rather than buildings;

8) Privately-Owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS) - are physical spaces which are privately owned but legally required to be open to the public under a land-use law and arrangement with the City Government of Davao such as but not limited to plazas, terraces, atriums, small parks, view decks, playground, food park, and alleyways that connects two structures without passing through a regular street.

9) Zoning Map - a duly authenticated map delineating the different zone classifications into which the whole city is divided.

10) Mean Sea Level - the zero reference point from which the limits shall be measured from.

**SECTION 6. AMENDMENT TO ARTICLE VIII SECTIONS 3 TO 6 OF THE AMENDED 2013 COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY - For purposes of providing the amendment and supplemental provisions of Article VIII Sections 3 to 6 of the Amended 2013 Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City, the following revisions and amendments are depicted as follows:**

**ARTICLE VIII COMMON REGULATIONS FOR MAJOR URBAN SUB-ZONES**

"SECTION 3. To provide specific height limits for buildings and structures in the City intended to achieve desired levels of urban densities, respond to transport and traffic concerns with due consideration to the rising demand for water, power, and other utilities, provide precautionary and proactive approach to urban mobility and efficiency and create an environment conducive to investment to improve overall quality of life. Height regulations must conform to the restrictions and requirements as set forth in this Ordinance which are generally set lower than the prescribed limits set by the Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP), and providing further that the requirements are compliant with the National Building Code, the Structural Code as well as all laws, ordinances, and regulations related to land development and building construction and the various safety codes."

3.3.10 Tallest Landmark Structure Limitation - The City Government of Davao may allow the construction of a structure which can reach up to 140 meters above mean sea level and which will serve as the City's tallest landmark and postcard image provided it is to be built in any of the following Height Zones: TO-E; TO-F; AP-D; AP-E; and CHS.
Provided, further, that only one tallest structure shall be allowed to be built within Davao City.
3.3.10.1 Criteria for Permitting Construction of the tallest structure - For a structure to be considered and awarded with the privilege of becoming the tallest landmark in the city, the project must meet the following criteria:
1. The building project's design is unique and should bear semblance to Davao City's cultural heritage;
2. It must devolve at least 50 percent of its gross floor area accessible to the public. The summit must also be made accessible to the public for tourism and viewing purposes either for free or for a fee; and
3. The usable floor spaces must be at least 70% non-residential development.

SECTION 4. HEIGHT REGULATIONS IN LOW DENSITY RESIDENTIAL SUB-ZONE (R-1). In R-1 Sub-zone, buildings or structures for human occupancy whether public or private shall not exceed nine (9) meters in height or the limitation that will be set by the CAAP, whichever is lower.

SECTION 5. HEIGHT REGULATIONS IN MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTAL SUB-ZONE (R-2). In R-2 Sub-zone, buildings or structures for human occupancy whether public or private shall not exceed twelve (12) meters in height or the limitation that will be set by the CAAP, whichever is lower.

SECTION 6. ALL OTHER SUB-ZONES. Within these districts, building heights shall be based on the prescribed Floor Area Ratio (FAR) or as enumerated in Article VIII, Section 3, whichever is lower, applicable, and permitted by the Buildings & Structures Height Regulation Committee of Davao City.

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. The provisions of these Rules are hereby declared to be separable, and in the event that one or more of the provisions hereof are held illegal or unconstitutional, the validity of the other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY. This ordinance shall take effect thirty (30) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City and posting for three (3) consecutive weeks at the entrance of the City Hall of Davao and in at least two (2) other conspicuous places.
