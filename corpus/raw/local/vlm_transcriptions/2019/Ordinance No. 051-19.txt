ORDINANCE NO. 051-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, THROUGH ITS REGIONAL OFFICE XI, RELATIVE TO THE FINANCIAL ASSURANCE OF THE OPERATING REQUIREMENTS AND VARIOUS EQUIPMENT OF AGDAO FARMER'S MARKET

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 051-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR OR COUNCIL TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, THROUGH ITS REGIONAL OFFICE XI, RELATIVE TO THE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO PURPOSELY FOR THE OPERATING REQUIREMENTS AND VARIOUS EQUIPMENT OF AGDAO FARMER'S MARKET."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states:

"(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Agriculture, through its Regional Office XI, relative to the financial assistance to the City Government of Davao purposely for the operating requirements and various equipment of Agdao Farmer's Market.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions thereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 24th day of September 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

For the Release of Financial Assistance to the City Government of Davao purposely for the Operating Requirements and Various Equipment of Agdao Farmer's Market

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement made and entered into by and between:

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government instrumentality existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at Room 4, City Hall, Davao City, represented by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUERTEIRA-CARPIO, herein after referred to as "CC-GDAVAO," in her capacity as City Mayor, duly empowered and authorized to enter into, and execute this Memorandum of Agreement in behalf of the City of Davao, through Sangguniang Panlungsod Resolusyon/Uriinahan Nuno, series of 2019 dated ______.

-and-

THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE through its REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, a government agency created and existing under the Republic of the Philippines with office address at F. Bangoy St., Davao City, represented by its Regional Executive Director, ENGR. RICARDO M. ONATE, JR., hereinafter referred to as "DA-RFO XI";

Together hereunder referred to as the "Parties"

WHEREAS, the Department Of Agriculture (DA), through its mandate and under RA 8435-Agriculture and Fisheries Modernization Act (AFMA) of 1997 provides postharvest infrastructure and facilities to enable small farmers and fisherfolk to have access to technologies in order to improve the handling, storage, processing and marketing of agricultural and fishery products;

WHEREAS, the DA is the government agency in-charge of food security and making food accessible to all specifically the marginalized sector engaged in agriculture and fisheries;

WHEREAS, RA No. 7160-Local Government Code of 1991 provides that the LGU shall assume the primary responsibility for the provision of basic services and facilities and the improvement of quality of life of their constituents;

WHEREAS, there is a need to support the City Government of Davao for their operational and equipment expenses in order to realize a more increased livelihood opportunities and marketing service;

WHEREAS, the Agdao Farmer's Market seeks to provide support to farmers and fisherfolk to enable them to sell their produce directly, earn incomes and make basic food affordable and accessible to the public;

WHEREAS, DA-RFO XI and DPWH RO XI previously entered and executed a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) on September 21, 2018 for the Project entitled "Construction of Agdao Farmer's Market" in the amount of Two Hundred Million Pesos Only (Php 200,000,000.00) located at Brgy. Agdao Proper, Davao City;

--- [Page 8] ---
WHEREAS, as per approved General Appropriations Act (GAA) FY 2019, RA No. 11260, the DA-RFO XI has allocated and intends to release a fund in the amount of One Hundred Forty Nine Million Seven Hundred Thirty Six Thousand Pesos (PhP 149,736,000.00) under maintenance and other operating expenses as financial assistance to CG-DAVAO for the operating requirements and various equipments of Agdao Farmers Market;

WHEREAS, another remaining Four Hundred Fifty Million Two Hundred Sixty Four Thousand Pesos (PhP 450,264,000.00) will be downloaded directly to the Department of Public Works and Highways pursuant to the MOEE and GAA purposely for the completion of the Agdao Farmer's Market Building and its premises;

WHEREAS, the DA-RFO XI and CG-DAVAO shall be working closely as to the establishment of the Agdao Farmer's Market to ensure viability of the parties' intentions and capabilities to pursue its goals and objectives;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto agree to enter into this agreement under the following terms and conditions:

ARTICLE I
THE PROJECT

The parties agree to and signify their intentions to the following:

1. Undertake the operation of Agdao Farmer's Market to be located at Brgy. Agdao, Davao City with a budget allocation of One Hundred Forty Nine Million Seven Hundred Thirty Six Thousand and 00/100 Pesos (PhP 149,736,000.00) with all the equipment and provisions shall be complete within available budgetary allocations; and,

2. DA-RFO XI will allocate and disburse the same fund as GRANT under fund transfer arrangement to CG-DAVAO for the establishment of the Farmers Market in accordance with government accounting rules and regulations.

ARTICLE II
ROLES AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES

A. RESPONSIBILITIES OF DA-RFO XI

The DA-RFO XI shall:

1. Transfer the funds in the total amount of One Hundred Forty Nine Million Seven Hundred Thirty Six Thousand and 00/100 Pesos (PhP 149,736,000.00) as per approved allocation;

2. Review the completeness and validity of all the documentary requirements for the release of funds submitted by CG-DAVAO;

--- [Page 9] ---
3. Provide the necessary technical support and assistance to the CG-DAVAO to ensure that the project objectives are met;

4. Monitor the utilization of funds in accordance with the purpose and objectives and implementation of the project;

5. Enforce and adhere strictly to the provision of COA Circular No. 94-103 and COA Circular No. 2012-001 on the liquidation of fund transfer;

6. Issue a certification by the Chief Accounting Section of the DA RFO XI, stating that the funds transferred to the CG-DAVAO has been liquidated, post-audited and accounted based on the DA books whenever applicable as per COA rules.

B. RESPONSIBILITIES OF CG DAVAOCG-DAVAO shall:

1. Submit to DA RFO XI the following documentary requirements for the release of the funds:
a. approved program of work;
b. List of necessary equipment to be used during the operation of farmers market pursuant to the specifications of the National Meat Inspection Service Office as provided in Checklist No. 32-D, Good Operating Practices (GOP);
c. approved project expenditures / estimated expenses indicating the project objective and expected output;
d. Sangguniang Panglungsod Resolution authorizing the Local Chief Executive to enter into Memorandum of Agreement;
e. Certification by the Accountant that funds previously transferred from DA-RFO XI has been liquidated, post audited and accounted for in the books.

2. Issue Official Receipt (OR) in the name of the DA RFO XI corresponding to the amount received;

3. Maintain a separate account of the fund received for the Farmer's Market from the DA RFO XI;

4. Record all transactions which are related to the utilization of the funds and other information relevant to the monitoring and evaluation of the project and keep the same open for inspection by the DA-RFO XI or its duly authorized representatives;

5. Submit to DA RFO XI the full liquidation report duly certified by the resident auditor within sixty (60) days after the completion of the Project;

6. Return to DA RFO XI any amount that remains unused after the completion of the PROJECT;

ARTICLE III

Effectively, Termination and Modification

--- [Page 10] ---
This Agreement shall take effect upon signing hereof and shall continue to be in full force and effect for a period of two years or until written notice is given by either party sixty (60) days prior to the date of intended termination, or shall be terminated upon the satisfactory fulfillment of all terms and conditions embodied herein.

No modifications or amendments to this agreement and the approved project documents shall be allowed without the written consent of both parties involved. All amendment(s) that will appended to this agreement shall be in writing and must be delivered to the address of the parties indicated herein.

ARTICLE IV
OTHER PROVISIONS

All parties hereby agree that in all matters relating to this MOA, whether during its subsistence or after its termination, including all matters concerning these provisions where any questions, dispute or difference may arise, all parties shall opt to settle these issued and concerns on mutually beneficial arrangements, more so, in good faith.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties, through their duly authorized representatives, have hereto aliased their signatures on this day of , Davao City.
