Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF STO. NIÑO SEASHORE HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION, INC., FOR THE RECLASSIFICATION OF THE 23,610-SQUARE METER PARCEL OF LAND COVERED BY OCT P-14843 AND OCT P-14840 FROM PRIME AGRICULTURAL LAND SUB-ZONE (APR) AND WATER RESOURCE ZONE (WRZ) TO SOCIALIZED HOUSING ZONE (SHZ), AS THEIR RELOCATION SITE

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 031-19

Be it ordained by the Sanguguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF STO. NIÑO SEASHORE HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION, INC., FOR THE RECLASSIFICATION OF THE 23,610-
SQUARE METER PARCEL OF LAND COVERED BY OCT P-14843 AND OCT P-
14840 FROM PRIME AGRICULTURAL LAND SUB-ZONE (APR) AND WATER RESOURCE ZONE (WRZ) TO SOCIALIZED HOUSING ZONE (SHZ), AS THEIR RELOCATION SITE".

SECTION 2. COMMON REGULATIONS FOR GENERAL ZONES - Article V of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022.) provides Common Regulations for General Zones:

Section 1. REVIEW OF GENERAL ZONES: General zones are subject to review by the Zoning Review Committee every five (5) years from the enactment of this ordinance. Any amendment thereof as recommended by committee shall be in accordance with the provision of Article XIV Section 14.

Section 2. RECLASSIFICATION AND CHANGE OF ZONE REQUIREMENT: xxx Reclassification from agricultural zone to non-agricultural use and any change from one general zone to another general zone must be approved by three-fourths (3/4) vote of all the members of the Sanguguniang Panlungsod through a resolution and an ordinance. Any change of general zone shall be considered as amendment of the zoning ordinance and must comply with the provision of Article XIV, Section 14 hereof.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the grant of the application of Sto. Niño Seashore Homeowners Association, Inc., for the Reclassification of the 23,610-square meter parcel of land covered by OCT P-14843 and OCT P-14840 from Prime Agricultural Land Sub-Zone (APR) and Water Resource Zone (WRZ) to SocIALIZED Housing Zone (SHZ), as their relocation site.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, September 3, 2019, by 3/4 (three-fourths) majority vote of all the Members of the Sanguguniang, there being a quorum.
