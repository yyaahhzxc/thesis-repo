Ord. No. 073-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REPEALING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0419-83, SERIES OF 1983".

SECTION 2. REPEALING CAUSE - City Ordinance No. 0419-83, Series of 1983, known as "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS TO PROVIDE REFLECTORIZED FIRE EXIT SIGNS TO BE PLACED IN CONSPICUOUS PLACES OF SAID BUILDINGS," is hereby repealed as the same has been overtaken by the national law.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) consecutive public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, October 15, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
