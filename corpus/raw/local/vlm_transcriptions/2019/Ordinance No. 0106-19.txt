ORDINANCE NO. 0106-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 381-82, SERIES OF 1982, REGULATING THE PUBLIC SHOWING OF MOTION PICTURES AND CINEMATOGRAPHIC WORKS IN PUBLIC ESTABLISHMENTS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO

--- [Page 5] ---
SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be otherwise known as "AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE PUBLIC SHOWING OF MOTION PICTURES AND CINEMATOGRAPHIC FILMS IN PUBLIC ESTABLISHMENTS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO," AS AMENDED.

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Pursuant to the constitutional guarantee on freedom of expression, the State shall promote and support the development and growth of the local film industry as a medium for the upliftment aesthetic, cultural and social values or the better understanding and appreciation of the Filipino Identity. (Section 1 of R.A. 9167 known as AN ACT CREATING THE FILM DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL OF THE PHILIPPINES, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFORE, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES)

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS

a. Cinematographic films - are those audiovisual work consisting of a series of related images which, when shown in succession, impart an impression of motion, together with accompanying sounds, if any. (Sec. 2(e) of R.A. 10088 or the Anti-Camcorading Act of 2010)

b. Motion pictures - includes those completed composite print of any silent and sound motion film or tape, either black and white or in color, that can form visual moving images on the projection screen or television screen through the mechanical application of the projection equipment. (Sec. 10(1) of R.A. 3680 or AN ACT CREATING THE BOARD OF LENSERS FOR MOTION PICTURES);

c. Public Establishments - cover those persons, establishments, entities, institutions, sole proprietorship or similar establishments which are involved in the public exhibition of cinematographic films, whether or not for profit, or whether the said public exhibition be their primary or secondary business or merely to increase the value of their establishment such as public showing of cinematographic films in restaurants, bars, pubs, coffee shops, schools, public utility vehicles, and any other public places to which a copyrighted cinematographic films is being publicly shown.

d. Storage Devices - cover those USB Flash Drives or Thumb Drives, Secure Digital (SD) cards, Network Attached Storage (NAS), External/Internal Hard Disk Drives (HDD), Solid State Drives (SSD), Cellular Phones, Tablet Computers, Portable Music Players, and Internet-Based File Storage and Synchronization Service pursuant to Republic Act No. 9239, in relation to Optical Media Board Memorandum Circular No. 2018-002.

SECTION 4. REGISTRATION. - Public establishments are required, before engaging in the public exhibition of motion pictures and cinematographic films, to secure a Mayor's Permit from the City Government of Davao subject to the following requirements:

4.a. If the cinematographic film or motion picture is contained in a storage device, the said storage device must be registered and/or licensed before the Optical Media Board, in accordance with R.A. 9239 or the Optical Media Act of 2003, in compliance with the Implementing Rules and Regulations of the same.

4.b. The public showing or exhibition of cinematographic films or motion pictures shall be with prior consent, permission, and approval by the copyright owner and shall be subject to the provisions and penalties provided for under Republic Act No. 10088 or

--- [Page 6] ---
**The "Anti-Camcording Act of 2010" and/or Republic Act No. 8293 or the "Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines".**

**SECTION 5 PROHIBITED ACT - Any public establishment publicly showing motion pictures and cinematographic films contained in storage devices, DVDs, CDs, and tapes without the necessary Mayor's Permit shall hereby be penalized.**

**SECTION 6 PENALTIES - Any persons or entity violating this Ordinance shall suffer the following penalties:**

a. First Offense - a fine of Phip 3,000.00

b. Second Offense - a fine of Phip 4,000.00

c. Third Offense - a fine of Phip 5,000.00

**SECTION 7 REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance or Executive Order, or any provision therein, inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby modified or repealed accordingly.**

**SECTION 8 SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any provision of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance is declared invalid, the remainder of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected by such declaration.**

**SECTION 9 EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days from publication hereof.**

ADOPTED, November 19, 2019, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
