Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "MANDATORY 14-DAY HOME QUARANTINE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.- The Local Government Code of 1991 or Republic Act No. 7160 allows every local government to exercise specific powers for the promotion of the general welfare. Towards this end, the City of Davao enacts an Ordinance to promote health and safety, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of its constituents.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.
a) Public Health Authority - refers to the Department of Health (DOH), IATF-EID, the City Health Office, or any person directly authorized to act in behalf of the aforementioned.
b) COVID-19 - a potentially severe respiratory illness caused by a coronavirus and characterized by fever, coughing, and shortness of breath, the spread of which is the reason the World Health Organization has declared a worldwide pandemic.
c) Quarantine - a state or place of isolation for a person or groups of persons who may have come in contact with contagious diseases, and is not reserved for sick people only.
d) Fourteen (14)-day home quarantine - a quarantine protocol aligned with the 14-day period of incubation of the COVID-19 virus. As per the World Health Organization (WHO), the guidelines are as follows:
1) Allocate a separate room with adequate ventilation at home if possible. Household members should stay in another room or should be separated from the returnee;
2) Maintain at least one meter distance from family members.
3) Other household members should use a separate bathroom. If sharing the same bathroom, cleaning of taps, doorknobs and utensils with soap and water are a requirement.
4) Minimize visitors in the house. The person under isolation should not interact with any visitors.
5) Frequent handwashing with soap and water for at least twenty (20) seconds at a time and maintain alcohol-based hygiene in instances where handwashing facilities are inadequate.
6) Avoid touching eyes, nose and mouth with unwashed hand.
7) The home quarantined person is expected to monitor body temperature using a thermometer twice a day. If he/she develops fever, cough, difficulty in breathing, sore throat, body aches and pain, including flu-like symptoms, immediately inform local health authorities.

8) After use, disposable facemasks should be properly discarded.

9) Assign separate dishes, drinking glasses, cups, eating utensils, towels, bedding and other items for the quarantined person.

10) Used utensils, bed linen and clothes should be washed with soap and water.

e) Rapid Antibody Test – also known as Rapid Test (RT), is an Antibody Test that qualitatively detects the presence of IgM and IgG antibodies against the COVID-19 virus in human serum, plasma and venous whole blood specimen for the auxiliary diagnosis of COVID-19 infection.

f) Reverse Transcription Polymerase Chain Reaction (RT-PCR): A COVID-19 test using actual swabs from patients taken from the nose or throat. This test determines the actual presence of the coronavirus and if a person is currently infected.

g) Asymptomatic – when a disease exists in a person without noticeable symptoms.

h) Permanent Resident Visa: a document granted to foreigners, granting them the right to reside in the Philippines on a permanent basis.

i) Locally Stranded Individuals (LSI): Foreign nationals or Filipino citizens in a specific locality within the Philippines who have expressed intention to return to their place of residence/home origin. LSIs may be further classified as:

1) Workers;

2) Students;

3) Tourists; and

4) Other individuals stranded in various localities while in transit.

j) Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs): overseas Filipinos whose primary reason for being outside the country or for leaving the country is due to a contract of employment in a foreign nation or a vessel flying another nation's flag.

k) Non-Overseas Filipino Workers (Non-OFWs): overseas Filipinos whose primary reason for being abroad is not due to a contract of employment in a foreign nation or vessel.

l) Returning Overseas Filipinos (ROFs): Filipino citizens who are returning to the Philippines from abroad. They are categorized into:

1) OFWs, whether documented or non-documented

2) Non-Overseas Filipino Workers (Non-OFWs):

i. Returning students

ii. 31 visa holders under exchange visitor's program

iii. Returning Filipino Diplomats

iv. Returning Filipino Tourists

v. Participants of Philippine government-sponsored programs

vi. Dependents and accompanying foreign spouses

SECTION 4. SCOPE AND COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall apply to all residents of Davao City, and non-residents who are transiting, or are being held in the City for mandatory quarantine due to COVID-19, whether undergoing the same in a government facility, a private facility, or at one's place of residence.

SECTION 5. MANDATORY QUARANTINE - The policy on mandatory 14-day home quarantine shall apply to the following, subject to modification and revision by the IATF, the DOH, and/or other Public Health Authorities:

a) All asymptomatic returning OFWs, whether sea-based or land based, subject to the arrangements, and rules and regulations imposed by the governing authority;

b) Asymptomatic Returning Overseas Filipinos and holder of permanent resident visas,

c) Asymptomatic Locally Stranded Individuals

d) Asymptomatic healthcare workers with unprotected exposure to a confirmed Covid-19 patient without appropriate PPEs, whether RT-PCR test results are positive or negative;

e) Asymptomatic non-healthcare workers who test IgM positive in rapid tests, are required to undergo isolation at home or at a community quarantine facility for 14 days; and

f) Other such cases which require mandatory 14-day home quarantine as prescribed by law.

SECTION 6. FINES AND PENALTIES - Any person caught in violation of the Ordinance by leaving the place of residence or barangay quarantine or isolation center before the required fourteen (14) days shall be imposed the fixed penalty of administrative fine of Five Thousand Pesos (Php5,000.00) and/or imprisonment for a maximum of six (6) months, or both, at the discretion of the court.

SECTION 7. NO CONTEST PROVISION - Any person apprehended for violation of this Ordinance, who does not wish to contest the violation and is willing to voluntarily pay the fine imposed upon him/her prior to the filing of a formal complaint with the Office of the City Prosecutor, may be allowed to pay the said fine with the City Treasurer's Office to avoid being criminally prosecuted.

SECTION 8. IMPLEMENTATION - The DOH Center for Health Development, Davao City Health Office, and Davao City Police Office, shall take the lead in the implementation of the Ordinance. At the barangay level, the Office of the Punong Barangay and the respective Barangay Health Emergency Response Teams (BHERT) shall ensure the enforcement of the Ordinance.

SECTION 9. APPROPRIATIONS - The City Government of Davao shall allocate funds for the implementation of the various undertakings relative to this Ordinance.

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 11. REPEALING CLAUSE – All Ordinances, Resolutions, Executive Orders, Memoranda and Administrative Regulations or part thereof in conflict or inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are thereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.**

EFFECTIVITY – This Ordinance shall take effect immediately, and shall remain in full force and effect until the declaration of the existence of a Public Health Emergency of International Concern is introduced by the World Health Organization, or when the State of Public Health Emergency due to COVID-19 in the country is lifted.**
