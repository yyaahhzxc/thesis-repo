Ord. No. 0310-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE CHARITY SWEETSTAKES OFFICE (PCSO) RELATIVE TO THE GRANT OF CALAMITY ASSISTANCE FUND BY PCSO TO DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 25 and Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides that:

"SECTION 25. National Supervision over Local Government Units-
(b) National agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation of said national projects.

Section 455 (b) (1) (vi):
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement by and between the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), relative to the grant of calamity assistance fund by PCSO to Davao City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR THE GRANT OF CALAMITY ASSISTANCE FUND BY THE PCSO TO DAVAO CITY

THE PUBLIC IS INFORMED:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT entered into between:

PHILIPPINE CHARITY SWEEPSTAKES OFFICE (PCSO), a government-owned and controlled corporation existing by virtue of Republic Act No. 1169, as amended, holding office at Sun Plaza Building, No. 1507 Shaw Boulevard cor. Princeton St., Mandaluyong City, Philippines, represented by its Vice-Chairperson and General Manager, ROYINA M. GARMA, duly authorized for this purpose under Board Resolution No. 2018-011 dated 11th July 2018, is shown in Secretary's Certificate No. 346 dated 06 October 2018, a copy of which is attached as Annex "A".

and

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO (BENEFICIARY), a Local Government Unit existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with principal office at City Hall Drive, San Pedro St., Davao City, herein represented by the City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE.

ANTECEDENTS:

The PCSO is the principal government agency for raising and providing funds for health programs, medical assistance and services and charities of national character.

On 15 January 2020, a fire incident occurred in Barangay 5-A Quiñin Avenue, Bankerohan, Davao City affecting Four Hundred Seventy-Six (476) families in the area.

Pursuant to the Calamity Assistance Program of PCSO, and due to the urgency of the need for the financial assistance, the Charity Sector recommended to the OIC-General Manager, approval and authority for the immediate release of the assistance, in the amount of Seven Hundred Fourteen Thousand Pesos (Php714,000.00) for the Four Hundred Seventy-Six (476) affected families. Dr. Larry Cedro, Assistant General Manager for Administrative Sector, in his capacity as then OIC-General Manager, approved the same.

During its meeting on 3[1]1[2], the PCSO Board ratified and confirmed the approval by then OIC-General Manager, Dr. Larry Cedro, thru Board Resolution No. 494 Series of 2020, as contained in Secretary's Certificate No. 32[2][3] (a copy of which is attached as Annex "B"), of the amount of Seven Hundred Fourteen Thousand Pesos (Php714,000.00) as calamity assistance for the Affected Four Hundred Seventy Six (476) families of Barangay 5-A Quiñin Avenue, Bankerohan, Davao City, to be charged to the Calamity Assistance Program allocation for CY 2020 and for the PCSO Charities Fund, to be deposited in the account of the City Government Unit and the beneficiaries, in coordination with the Office of the President, subject to liquidation and subject further to applicable laws, rules and regulations.

ACCORDINGLY, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereby agree as follows:

1. The calamity assistance is in the amount of SEVEN HUNDRED FOURTEEN THOUSAND PESOS (Php714,000.00) payable in one-time release to the

--- [Page 11] ---
BENEFICIARY. For this purpose: BENEFICIARY shall issue an Official Receipt in favor of PCSO to confirm receipt of the calamity assistance.

2. The BENEFICIARY shall perform the following:

a. Utilize the calamity assistance for the Four Hundred Seventy Six (476) families affected by the fire incident on 15 January 2020 in Barangay 5-A Quirino Avenue, Bankerohan, Davao City for any of the following purposes:
   a) Food necessities, and/or feeding projects for displaced families in evacuation centers and/or temporary shelters;
   b) Procurement of medicines and/or medical supplies necessary for the conduct of health-related activities for disease prevention and/or treatment among the affected population including water and hygiene/sanitation requirements.

b. Ensure the prudent administration and utilization of the calamity assistance by adopting applicable government accounting and auditing rules and regulations otherwise, it shall be principally and directly liable to the PCSO.

c. Upon full utilization of the calamity assistance or within sixty (60) days from receipt of the same, whichever comes earlier, submit to PCSO a Credit Notice or appropriate document issued by the COA Resident Auditor or Authorized Accountant's Report, accompanied by an Accomplishment and Liquidation Report, supported by the following:

i. For financial assistance utilized to provide for needs of feeding projects for displaced families in evacuation centers: Certified List of individuals/families served;

ii. For financial assistance utilized to purchase of various medicines and/or medical supplies necessary for the conduct of health-related activities for disease prevention and/or treatment among the affected population: Certified List of Patients Served;

iii. For financial assistance distributed to affected families for the procurement of food, water, sanitation, and medicine requirements: Official Payroll of Financial Assistance Disbursements to Affected Families or Qualified Beneficiaries;

iv. Copies of the photo (and video, if applicable) documentation of the distribution of the PCSO assistance to the affected families

Failure to submit such report and its supporting documents within the prescribed period will result in the denial of subsequent requests under any and all PCSO institutional assistance programs.

d. Keep proper books of accounts pertaining to the calamity assistance in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles and as may be required by PCSO and/or COA.

e. Make readily available its books of accounts, other records and documents pertaining to the appropriation and use of the calamity assistance for inspection by PCSO or its duly authorized representatives, the Internal Audit Service (IAS) and the COA at any time during regular office hours.

--- [Page 12] ---
f. Support all PCSO programs and help enhance PCSO's corporate image by performing the following:

1. Issue a Certification confirming the conduct of distribution of the calamity assistance;
2. Post photos and videos of the activity in the official website and social media sites of the local government unit, if available, with the proper captions and acknowledgments; and
3. Provide PCSO copies of the photo (and video) documentation.

The BENEFICIARY warrants the following:

a. That it disclosed all material information relative to this MOA;
b. That all such information disclosed are true and correct;
c. That it has entered into the MOA with the PCSO without the help or interference of any party who has or might be financially benefited, directly or indirectly, as a result of the grant; and
d. That this MOA is entered into not for the purpose of fraud or for any cause/manner contrary to the PCSO Charter and the Philippine laws in general.

4. Violation of, or default in, any of the obligations of the BENEFICIARY shall be sufficient basis for the reversion of the calamity assistance. The PCSO shall serve written notice upon the BENEFICIARY stating the details and incidents of the breach committed, and giving the latter a non-extendible period of fifteen (15) days from receipt of notice within which to cure the defective performance of its obligations. If the BENEFICIARY fails to cure the defect within the said period, the PCSO shall serve a written Notice of Reversion upon the BENEFICIARY, without need of further demand or judicial action or declaration.

5. A dispute between the Parties shall be first settled amicably; otherwise, it shall be resolved in accordance with the rules on Alternative Dispute Resolution for government entities.
