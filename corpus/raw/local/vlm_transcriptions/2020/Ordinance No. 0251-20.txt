AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO EXECUTE THE DEED OF ACCEPTANCE OF DONATION IN RELATION TO THE DEED OF DONATION DATED MARCH 31, 2020, EXECUTED BY PANHUA GROUP CO., LTD., REPRESENTED BY ITS PRESIDENT, LI XINHUA AND TO RECEIVE, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE VARIOUS PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) IN RELATION THEREOF

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0251-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO EXECUTE THE DEED OF ACCEPTANCE OF DONATION IN RELATION TO THE DEED OF DONATION DATED MARCH 31, 2020, EXECUTED BY PANHUA GROUP CO., LTD., REPRESENTED BY ITS PRESIDENT, LI XINHUA AND TO RECEIVE, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE VARIOUS PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) IN RELATION THEREOF."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICIES - Section 455, paragraph (b) sub-paragraph (vi) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE of 1991, provides, to wit:

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

xxxxxxx

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to execute the Deed of Acceptance of Donation in relation to the Deed of Donation dated March 31, 2020, executed by PANHUA GROUP CO., LTD., represented by its President, LI XINHUA, and to receive, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the following Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), viz:

1. Disposable Face Masks - three hundred thousand (300,000) pieces;
2. Protective Clothing - two thousand (2,000) pieces;
3. Protective Goggles - five thousand (5,000) pairs;
4. PVC Gloves - fifty thousand (50,000) pairs.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

DEED OF ACCEPTANCE OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This DEED OF ACCEPTANCE OF DONATION is executed by CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, represented herein by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, with office address at City Hall of Davao, San Pedro St., 8000 Davao City, Philippines hereinafter referred to as the DONOR;

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, PANHUA GROUP CO., LTD., represented by its President, MR. LI XINHUA, with office address at Zhangjiagang City Panhua International Plaza, Zhangjiagang City, Jiangsu Province, China, hereinafter referred to as the DONOR;

WHEREAS, in a Deed of Donation dated March 31, 2020, DONOR has freely and voluntarily given, transferred and conveyed by way of DONATION to the DONEE, its successors and assigns, from all liens and encumbrances, the following:
1. Disposable Face Masks - three hundred thousand (300,000) pieces;
2. Protective Clothing - two thousand (2,000) pieces;
3. Protective Goggles - five thousand (5,000) pairs;
4. PVC Gloves - fifty thousand (50,000) pairs.

WHEREAS, the DONOR has undertaken this donation for the sole purpose of helping the people of Davao City, through its local government, in its fight against the spread of COVID-19, without any consideration whatsoever;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the DONOR'S act of kindness and liberality, the DONEE hereby accepts this DONATION and further expresses its profoundest appreciation and thanks for the kindness and generosity shown by the DONOR;

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the DONEE has heretofore affixed its signature on at Davao City, Philippines.

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO
represented by:

SARA Z. DUTERTE
SIGNED IN THE PRESENCE OF: ATTY. IZULEKAT T. LOPEZ City Administrator
