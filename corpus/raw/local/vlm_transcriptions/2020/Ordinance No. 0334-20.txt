AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF DR. Ord. No. 0334-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF DR. NOEL BACASNOT, PRESIDENT OF MAGALLANES RESIDENCES CONDOMINIUM CORPORATION (MRC), BOLTON EXTENSION, THIS CITY, FOR THE WAIVER OF PAYMENT OF THE REAL PROPERTY TAX FOR THE YEAR 2020, BECAUSE THEY WERE NOT ABLE TO UTILIZE THEIR PROPERTY DUE TO STRUCTURAL DAMAGE AND THE SAME WAS DECLARED "UNSAFE" BY THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES DUE TO SERIES OF EARTHQUAKES, CONSIDERED AS A NATURAL CALAMITY, THAT STRUCK DAHAO CITY AND THE REST OF DAVAO REGION IN 2019".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 192 of the Local Government Code states that "Local Government Units may, through ordinances duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs under such terms and conditions as they may deem necessary".

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Section 276 of the Local Government Code states that:

Section 276: Condonation or Reduction of Real Property Tax and Interest - In case of general failure of crops or substantial decrease in the price of agricultural or agri-based products, or calamity in any province, city or municipality, the sanggunian concerned, by ordinance passed prior to the first day of January of any year and upon the recommendation of the Local Disaster Coordinating Council, may Condone or Reduce, wholly or partially, the taxes and the interest thereon for the succeeding year or years in any city or municipality affected by the calamity.

SECTION 4. PERIOD - This waiver of payment of the real property tax will be for the year 2020 only, and the Tax Condonation shall only be applied proportionately to be computed by the Office of the City Building Official (OCBO), City Assessor's Office, City Treasurer's Office, all of this City, and shall not apply to tax penalties and surcharges previously incurred.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVENESS - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval and valid only for Calendar Year 2020 Real Property Tax.

ENACTED, August 25, 2020, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
