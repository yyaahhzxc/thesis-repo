October 6, 2020
Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the attached Ordinance No. 0330-20 entitled "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SCHOOLS IN ALL LEVELS IN DAVAO CITY, TO INCLUDE IN THEIR CURRICULUM THE TEACHING OF THE HISTORY OF THE CITY OF DAVAO", with the herein findings and recommendation.
As aptly noted, the intent of the ordinance is the integration of the history of Davao City in the school curricula both in private and public school as shown in the title of the Ordinance and that of Resolution No. 01388-20, Series of 2020.
However, the ordinance failed to deliver or effectuate the intent of the framers. It simply directed the creation of the Davao History Book Council and its budgetary operation. While it appears in the Declaration of Policy directing all the schools offering elementary, secondary and tertiary education to include as part of their curriculum, the History of Davao City, the same does not serve the purpose.
At any rate, resort to the title is applicable if the ordinance is vague. In the case at bar, the text of the ordinance is clear and free from doubt.
VIEWED from the foregoing, it is recommended that the Ordinance be approved.

ORDINANCE NO. 0330-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SCHOOLS IN ALL LEVELS IN DAVAO CITY, TO INCLUDE IN THEIR CURRICULUM THE TEACHING OF THE HISTORY OF THE CITY OF DAVAO

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0330-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "DAVAO HISTORY ORDINANCE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City of Davao to promote character formation and wholesome personality development by requiring schools at the elementary, secondary and tertiary levels to inculcate patriotism and nationalism, foster love of humanity, respect for human rights, appreciation of the role of national heroes in the historical development of the country, teach the rights and duties of citizenship, strengthen ethical and spiritual values, and develop moral character and personal discipline. Thus, culture and tradition found in the history of the City will now be an integral part of the educational system of the City.

All public and private schools in the city offering elementary, secondary and tertiary education are hereby mandated to include, as part of their curriculum, the History of Davao City.

SECTION 3. CREATION OF DAVAO HISTORY BOOK COUNCIL - A Davao History Book Council shall be created to be headed by the City Mayor, for the purpose of managing and implementing the project.

SECTION 4. COMPOSITION DAVAO HISTORY BOOK COUNCIL - The Davao History Book Council shall be composed of:

a. Chairperson - City Mayor
b. Vice Chairperson - Vice Mayor
c. Members:
- Chairperson, Committee on Education, Science and Technology, Arts and Culture
- Head, City Planning and Development Office
- Superintendent, Division of City Schools
- Director, Commission of Higher Education
- President, Davao Historical Society

SECTION 5. TECHNICAL WORKING GROUP (TWG) - The Technical Working Group shall be created for the purpose of undertaking, researches, write-ups, preparing modules and other learning materials. The members of this group shall be remunerated based on reasonable compensation allowed by law. The members of the Technical Working Group/Research Team are as follows:

1. Two (2) Members of the City Council
2. Four (4) representatives from the Division of City Schools
3. One (1) representative from the City Planning and Development Office
4. One (1) representative from the City Library
5. One (1) representative from Museo Dabawenyo
6. Three (3) authors of Davao History Books
7. Two (2) representatives from Davao Historical Society
8. Two (2) representatives from the Department of Education (DepEd) Regional Office

The Technical Working Group/Research Team is allowed to organize consultation meetings with prominent persons of Davao (old families), private schools/universities, and other sectors of the community (public officials, businessmen, indigenous people, media, artists, etc.), for evaluation and validation purposes. Expenses for consultations will be shouldered by the City.

SECTION 6. EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS - In order to carry out the purpose of this Ordinance, the City Government of Davao shall cause the publication and distribution, free of charge, of the "History of Davao" as its official textbook to all public and private educational institutions in the City. Copies of the book shall also be given to all local and national government offices in the City.

SECTION 7. ACADEMIC FREEDOM - Academic Freedom shall be allowed for private and public colleges and universities for other supplemental learning materials provided, however, that their learning materials are faithful to the official Davao History Book.

SECTION 8. BUDGET - An annual appropriation shall be appropriated for purposes of this Ordinance. The City shall appropriate funds for the first year in constituting the Technical Working Group for the research/workshops on the development and production of the book.

Thereafter, the cost of publication, updating of the materials and other related expenses shall be included in the Local School Board Funds.

SECTION 9. TIMETABLE - The Teaching of the History of Davao City shall officially commence in 2022, or earlier if feasible.

SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE - All ordinances, resolutions, circulars, memoranda, or rules and regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed and modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any portion or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 12. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a local newspaper.

ENACTED, August 18, 2020, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
