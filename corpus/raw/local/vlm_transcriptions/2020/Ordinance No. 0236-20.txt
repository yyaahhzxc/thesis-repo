Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO AND BETWEEN THE DANGEROUS DRUGS BOARD AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE UTILIZATION OF THE SAVINGS IN THE AMOUNT OF ONE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND SIXTY-SIX PESOS AND TWELVE CENTS (P1,576,066.12) FROM THE SIX MILLION SIX HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P6,600,000.00) DDB FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF AN OPEN MEAL HALL WITH KITCHEN, SUBJECT TO APPLICABLE ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING RULES AND PROCEDURES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991" provides that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

Section 36. Assistance to People's and Non-governmental Organizations. - A local government unit may, through its local chief executive and with the concurrence of the sanggunian concerned, provide assistance, financial or otherwise, to such people's and non-governmental organizations for economic, socially-oriented, environmental, or cultural projects to be implemented within its territorial jurisdiction.

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(a) The City Mayor, as chief executive of the city government, shall exercise such powers and perform such duties and functions as provided by this Code and other laws.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS

This Memorandum of Agreement ("MOA") was made and entered into by the following Parties:

THE DANGEROUS DRUGS BOARD (hereinafter referred to as the "BOARD"); A GOVERNMENT AGENCY CREATED PURSUANT TO Republic Act No. 9165 or the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002, as amended, with office address at 3rd Floor DDS-PDEA Building, NIA Road, Quezon City represented herein by Secretary CATALINO S. CUY;

And

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO (hereinafter referred to as the "CITY GOVERNMENT"), with office address at Davao City Hall, San Pedro St., Davao City represented herein by Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE.

WITNESSETH: That --

WHEREAS, the BOARD is the policy making and strategy-formulating body in the planning and formulation of policies and programs on drug prevention and control which shall develop and adopt a comprehensive integrated, unified and balanced national drug abuse prevention and control strategy;

WHEREAS, the BOARD is mandated by Republic Act No. 9165 or the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002, as amended, to extend assistance in the establishment and maintenance of drug treatment and rehabilitation centers in the country;

WHEREAS, the BOARD and the CITY GOVERNMENT entered into a MOA dated 21 May 2015 for the provision of financial assistance by the former to the latter in the amount of SIX MILLION AND SIX HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P 6,600,000.00) to support the construction of a two-storey building which shall house the facility staff and patients of the Davao City Treatment and Rehabilitation Center for Drug Dependents (DCTRCCD);

WHEREAS, after the construction of the aforesaid two-storey building, the CITY GOVERNMENT was able to realize savings from the financial assistance provided by the BOARD in the amount of ONE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED SEVENTY-SIX THOUSAND AND SIXTY-SIX PESOS AND TWELVE CENTAVOS (P 1,578,066.12);

WHEREAS, in a letter dated 05 August 2019, Dr. Gene L. Gulanes, Center Manager of DCTRCCD, formally requested Secretary CATALINO S. CUY for the utilization of the aforesaid savings in the construction of an open mess hall with kitchen adjacent to the female dormitory of DCTRCCD;

WHEREAS, the request was discussed and approved by the BOARD during its 185th REGULAR MEETING ON 29 October 2019.

--- [Page 15] ---
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the, foregoing premises, the PARTIES hereby agree as follows:

1. That the savings realized from the financial assistance granted by the BOARD to the CITY GOVERNMENT in the amount of ONE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED SEVENTY-SIX THOUSAND SIXTY-SEVEN PESOS AND TWELVE CENTAVOS (P 1,576,066) be utilized by the latter for the construction of an open mess hall with kitchen adjacent to the female dormitory of DCTRCCD.

2. That the CITY GOVERNMENT initiate the process leading to the construction of the open mess hall with kitchen within thirty (30) days from the signing of this MOA and conclude within one (1) year from the issuance of the Notice of Award to the winning bidder or service provider, and

3. That the CITY GOVERNMENT, under applicable existing accounting and auditing procedures, be responsible for the liquidation of the aforementioned financial assistance from the BOARD. The Liquidation Report shall be duly certified by the Auditor assigned by the Commission on Audit to the CITY GOVERNMENT, and said papers and documents shall be submitted by the CITY GOVERNMENT to the BOARD within sixty (60) days from full utilization of the financial assistance.

This MOA shall take effect immediately upon its signing by the PARTIES hereto. Either PARTY may pre-terminate this MOA in case of breach by the other Party, by giving notice to the PARTY guilty of breach at least thirty (30) days prior to pre-termination. The PARTY guilty of breach shall be held liable for violation of any applicable administrative issuances and / or penal laws.

IN WITNESS THEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their affirmation on the day of 19 OCT 2019 in Davao City, Philippines.
