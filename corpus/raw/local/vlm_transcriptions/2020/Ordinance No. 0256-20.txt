1st INDERESEMENT
May 7, 2020

Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject Ordinance No. 0256-20 Series of 2020, with the observation that since the subject Ordinance has been enacted earlier than the Omnibus Guidelines on the Implementation of Community Quarantine in the Philippines (Omnibus Guidelines), which were issued by the Department of Health and supplemented by the latter, and there may have been mandatory provisions in the latter which is not included in the former. These minor flaws though will not affect the validity of the ordinance.

In view thereof, this office recommends the approval of the ordinance.

ORDINANCE NO. 0256-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTING THE DAVAO CITY COVID-19
ENHANCED COMMUNITY QUARANTINE POLICIES

--- [Page 5] ---

Ord. No. 0256-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTING THE DAVAO CITY COVID-19 ENHANCED COMMUNITY QUARANTINE POLICIES".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE: - This Ordinance shall be imposed upon all persons, entities and establishments within the City of Davao, until the existing Enhanced Community Quarantine is lifted, or unless any part therein is earlier modified, withdrawn or extended.

SECTION 3. ENHANCED COMMUNITY QUARANTINE: The entire City of Davao is placed under an Enhanced Community Quarantine which shall have the effect of imposing the following rules:

1. No person can come out from his/her residence, dwelling, and immediate surroundings except those who will buy food or medicine, go to the hospital or in an emergency situation, and except as herein provided;

2. Imposing a social distancing measure of at least two (2) meters between all persons in public areas;

3. Face masks are mandatory when going out in public;

4. Suspension of all mass gatherings, including those which are religious, educational, or recreational in nature;

5. All government offices must work from home and leave minimal employees to continue work in offices except those involved in security, health, social services, sanitation, and disaster, who must continue to work on full operation;

6. Individuals are allowed to exercise or go out into the sun or get some fresh air, provided that they are within a few meters from their residence or dwelling;

7. All beach resorts and inland water resorts must be closed until further notice.

SECTION 4. FOOD AND MEDICINE (FM) PASS

1. The food and medicine pass shall be used to buy food, medicines or go to the hospital;

2. Two (2) individuals can be named on the pass but only one (1) named person therein can use it at any given time;

3. FM pass can only be used from the hours of 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM;

4. All individuals on the streets must carry and, whenever asked by law enforcement, must present their employee ID OR food and medicine pass (FM pass) with valid ID, whichever is applicable;

5. Any individual in public without the proper employment IDs, FM passes or not responding to an emergency shall be in violation of this Ordinance.
6. The abuse and un sanctioned use of FM Pass shall be subject to its revocation. For this purpose, the authorities should record the data of usage and shall check the food and/or medicine actually acquired or purchased upon re-entry of the individual to his/her barangay address when allowed by the circumstances;
SECTION 5. LAND TRAVEL
A. Non-essential travel - All non-essential travel within Davao City is suspended. Travel within Davao City is allowed only for the following:
1. Going to and from work for sectors allowed to operate, provided they have Identification Cards;
2. Delivery of essential goods and services;
3. Buying food and medicine, provided the person has an FM Pass;
4. Going to the hospital, dentist, pharmacy or for medical emergencies;
5. Law enforcement officers and those officially assisting in the fight against COVID-19.
B. Suspension of travel into and out of Davao City - All private vehicles and any mode of public transportation shall be prohibited from crossing into and out of Davao City borders, except for cargo, transport vehicles of COVID-19 frontline workers and skeleton workforces of establishments that are allowed to operate during ECQ. No mass transport of individuals is allowed.
C. Jeepneys - All jeepneys must stop their operation except those hired to work for the government, offices and businesses that are open during the ECQ and those hauling cargoes.
D. These forms of transportation are allowed:
a. Private vehicles and taxis with one (1) driver and one (1) passenger in front and one (1) at the back, observing physical distancing;
b. Tricycle with one (1) driver and one (1) passenger in front and one (1) at the back, observing physical distancing;
c. Single rider motorcycle or bicycle;
d. Free bus rides from the government, or ECQ exempt establishments, provided that they create a system with physical distance between passengers of at least one meter;

E.Unrestricted movement of Frontline Workers - The following workers are allowed unrestricted movement from their residence to their place of work and shall be prioritized in any transportation arrangements:

a. All DOH officials and staff;
b. All health and safety personnel stationed at LGU health facilities;
c. All Philippine Red Cross vehicles bearing the Red Cross Logo and staff with Red Cross Identification;
d. Core Staff from the World Health Organization Country and Regional Offices;
e. All medical officials, hospital administrative staff, and aides from private facilities, as well as their respective transport service providers.

F.Truck Ban - The schedule of the ban of trucks along the highways is hereby lifted, until later restored;

G. Cargo - All cargoes (food and non-food) within, from and to Davao City shall not be delayed, except for necessary health screening and safety measures, decontamination and disinfection stations and identification verification.

SECTION 6. SEA TRAVEL - All sea travel into Davao City is hereby suspended, except for sea cargo and government vessels. Vessels can unload cargo in any port in the City but all personnel and crew of the vessels are prohibited to come down from the vessels.

SECTION 7. AIR TRAVEL - Domestic and Foreign Air travel to and from Davao City at the Davao International Airport shall be suspended until the same is lifted by the City Government of Davao.

The following are exempted from suspension:
1. Cargo flights;
2. Medical Evacuations;
3. Weather Mitigation flights;
4. Maintenance flights;
5. Government and Military flights;
6. Flights specifically allowed by the City Government of Davao;

7. Emergency landings:
a. As much as possible, no crew or passenger may disembark without prior notice;
b. Passengers or crew who disembark must be placed under isolation upon landing;

8. Recovery flights for expatriates, subject to prior clearance with the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 8. ESTABLISHMENTS -
A. All private establishments and offices must close EXCEPT:
1. groceries, supermarkets;
2. wet markets/palengeks;
3. food commissaries, wholesale food outlets;
4. food processing or manufacturing;
5. food delivery services;
6. convenience and sari-sari stores;
7. hospitals, medical laboratories;
8. pharmacies, drugstores;
9. other health services/personnel;
10. banks and ATMs;
11. savings and credit cooperatives;
12. money-transfer services and bayad centers;
13. pawnshops;
14. courier services;
15. other delivery services;
16. doctor's and dentist's clinics;
17. gas stations;
18. water refilling stations;
19. LPG stations;
20. business process outsourcing/call centers;
21. funeral parlors, cemeteries; and
22. mass media outlets.

Provided,
1. Whenever possible, skeletal workforce arrangement must be implemented and, at all times, physical distancing between employees must be observed.

2. Sari-sari stores must not allow people to stay and loiter, and must be closed during the curfew hours of 6:00 P.M. to 6:00 A.M. of the next day.

3. All wet markets/palenegks must have an entrance, exit, and one-way direction for people to go through. People cannot turn around and go back and cannot roam around the wet markets/palenegks. Public Markets must be closed by 3pm each day.

4. All restaurants, cafes, bakeries, and other similar food establishments should close down their dining area and open only for take-out, in-house delivery or food delivery services;

Essential Sectors – All other business establishments and private offices not mentioned but are considered essential sectors, including but not limited to energy, water, telecommunications, cargo, must work only for preventive maintenance and during emergencies.

Agri-business and farms should reduce workload and extend their deadlines to significantly reduce laborers in the job site and to create a two-meter distance between laborers.

C. Other Sectors – Manufacturing, fabrication, and construction work that is not pertinent to the food sector, the state of public health emergency and state of calamity response should immediately cease operation. All other business establishments and private offices not mentioned are required to close and shift to online work and implement a work at home scheme.

D. Food Vendors – Ambulant food vendors who are using different modes of transportation to sell basic food commodities in the barangays shall be allowed. Basic food commodities shall mean rice, bread, meat, poultry, fish, fruits and vegetables, cooking oil and other food condiments.

There must be a maximum of two (2) workers, one (1) driver and one (1) assistant who shall wear face masks and shall implement proper food handling.

SECTION 9. CURFEW – The curfew hours shall be from 6:00PM to 6:00AM of the next day. Within the curfew hours, people cannot go out of their houses, except in cases of emergencies or if going to work in the exempted sectors/frontline offices. There is no need for any ID or pass in the event of an emergency.

The following workers are exempted from curfew:

1. Employed in government offices related to security, health, social services, sanitation, and disaster;

2. 24-hour groceries/convenience stores;

3. Wet markets/palenegks;

4. Food commissaries;

5. Food processing or manufacturing;

6. Food delivery services;
7. Hospitals, medical laboratories;
8. Pharmacies, drugstores;
9. Other health services/personnel;
10. Doctor's and dentist's clinics;
11. Gas stations;
12. Funeral parlors;
13. Mass media outlets; and
14. Persons officially assisting in the fight against COVID-19.

Any person or persons not covered by the exemption who are outside their residences during the curfew hours are in violation of this Ordinance and shall be reprimanded and sanctioned accordingly.

SECTION 10. SPECIAL CURFEW FOR VULNERABLE SECTORS- No minor, senior citizen, pregnant woman, sick, and immune-compromised individual can go out of their residence or dwelling except to go to the hospital, medical, or allied medical institutions.

Those who live alone may get an FM pass or, whenever possible and if appropriate, ask a neighbor or a friend to help them buy food and medicines. Whenever possible, employers must assign their senior citizens to work from home.

In cases of minors, parents shall be held liable under this Ordinance for neglect of minors.

SECTION 11. LIQUOR BAN - No liquor and any other alcoholic or intoxicating drinks shall be sold, served, or consumed in public 24 hours of the day during the state of public emergency.

SECTION 12. FUNERALS - Funeral parlors and private and public cemeteries are mandated to impose a "family members only" wake and burial and at all times demand compliance and observance of two-meter distance between family members and frequent handwashing with soap and water.

SECTION 13. PATIENT - Any person classified as suspected, probable or confirmed case, or patients classified by the Department of Health as falling under any category for purposes of COVID-19 case investigation and monitoring, shall comply with the existing DOH guidelines for their treatment, home quarantine, isolation or confinement.

Any patient who:
1. Refuses to comply with the home quarantine or isolation;
2. Fails to fully comply with the terms of the home quarantine or isolation;

--- [Page 11] ---
3. Escapes or avoids home quarantine or isolation after being defined as a PUM;

shall be in violation of this Ordinance and shall be subject to government intervention, without prejudice to other criminal actions.

SECTION 14. FALSIFICATION OF FOOD AND MEDICINE PASSES OR EMPLOYEE IDENTIFICATION – Any person who shall make, fabricate, forge, imitate or in any manner reproduce an FM pass without the authority of the City Mayor’s Office, or shall forge, counterfeit or fabricate an identification card or employee certificate, or simulate or falsely represent themselves to be an employee of a sector exempt from the curfew, establishment, closure or travel suspension, shall be liable under this Ordinance.

SECTION 15. FAILURE TO DISCLOSE – Any person, who fails to immediately disclose the fact of their recent travel history to Covid-19 areas with local transmission, or exposure to known confirmed, suspect or probable cases of COVID-19 cases shall be in violation of this Ordinance and shall be subject to government intervention, without prejudice to other criminal actions.

SECTION 16. FALSE INFORMATION – No person shall create, spread, announce, or claim any false or unverified statement in the guise of being an official statement from the national or local government or any office, agency or official connected with the fight against COVID - 19.

SECTION 17. PENALTY CLAUSE

General Penalty Clause – Any violation of this Ordinance shall be punished by the following penalties, without prejudice to the prosecution of other criminal actions.
• 1st Offense – a Fine of Three Thousand Pesos (P3,000)
• 2nd Offense – a Fine of Five Thousand Pesos (5,000) and/or imprisonment of not more than 1 year, at the discretion of the court

SECTION 18. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE – If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 19. REPEALING CLAUSE – Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 20. EFFECTIVITY – This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval and publication.

ENACTED, on the 16th day of April 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
