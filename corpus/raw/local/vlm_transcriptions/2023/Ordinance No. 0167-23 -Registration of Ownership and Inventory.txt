Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTING AND IMPLEMENTING THE REGISTRATION OF OWNERSHIP AND INVENTORY OF AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERIES MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY

Section 19 of Republic Act No. 10601 mandates all owners of agricultural and fishery machinery and equipment to register their agricultural and fishery machinery and equipment with the agriculture offices of municipal and city government units.

The Department of Agriculture (DA), Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) and Civil Service Commission (CSC), pursuant to Section 29 of RA No. 10601 issued Joint Memorandum Circular No. 02, Series of 2020, mandating all LGUs to create a division within the City Agriculturist's Office to undertake, among others, the registration of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment.

It is also hereby declared the policy of the State pursuant to RA No. 10601 to ensure the quality and safety of machines and equipment that are either locally produced or imported by strict observance of regulations through the creation and enforcement of performance standards for machinery and equipment, regular testing and evaluation, registration, and the accreditation and classification of suppliers, assemblers, and manufacturers to ensure compliance with set quality standards;

Further, it is hereby declared the policy of the City's policy pursuant to Article 3, Section 459.2 of the Local Government Code to generate and optimize the use of funds and resources for the City's development plans, programs, goals and priorities as specified in Section 18 of the Code, with particular emphasis on the expansion and advancement of the countryside and agro-industrial development.

SECTION 3. SCOPE/COVERAGE: The Ordinance shall cover a one-time registration of ownership of both new and currently used agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment of any individual owner, registered farmer-cooperatives and farmer-associations, and private entities, which are registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) or Cooperative Development Authority (CDA). The agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment cover those which are used for crop production, livestock raising, poultry farming, aquaculture, seed production, preserving, transporting and distribution, research, extension and instruction which include, but are not limited to, the list of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment official listed by DA-RAED and BFAAR as mentioned in Section 4(h) of this Ordinance. Furthermore, agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment owned and operated by government entities are likewise covered under this Ordinance.

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS: For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terminologies are used and defined as follows:

a) Agricultural Machinery and Equipment - refers to machinery and equipment for the production, harvesting, processing, storage, manufacture, preserving, transporting and distribution of agricultural products. These include,

but not limited to, tractors and their attachments, power tillers, seeders,
transplanters, windmills, harvesting machines, crop protection and maintenance equipment, irrigation equipment and accessories, greenhouses and other thermal conditioning equipment, livestock equipment, slaughtering equipment, meat and crop processing equipment, post-harvest machines such as milling machines, dryers, thresher, grain and other strippers, and agricultural transport machinery. New agricultural and fishery machinery includes newly imported as well as one that has not been used since its date of manufacture.

b) Fisheries Machinery and Equipment - refers to machinery and equipment for the production, harvesting, processing, storage, manufacture, preserving, transporting and distribution of fisheries and aquaculture products. These include, but not limited to, Aerator, Chiller, Cold Storage/Stationary Reefer Container and Quick Storage.

c) Fishing Boat - refers to any boat, ship or other watercraft equipped to be used for taking of fishery species or aiding or assisting one (1) or more vessels in the performance of any activity relating to fishing including, but not limited to, preservation, supply, storage, refrigeration, transportation and/or processing.

1. Fishing Boat three (3) gross tons and less - refers to a fishing boat which is already registered under the City Fisheries Section under the Davao City Fisheries Code.

2. Fishing boat three (3) gross tons and above - refers to a fishing boat registered under BFAAR. No registration of this type of vessel is required with the City.

d) Reefer Van - refers to a refrigerated van, stationary or mounted, used to store, and/or transport fishery commodities from the fish landing centers to local markets.

1. Stationary Reefer Container/Cold Storage - refers to a reefer van or a room which incorporates a refrigeration system to maintain the desired room environment for the commodities to be stored. Registration of this type of container is required under this Ordinance.

2. Mounted Container Reefer Van - refers to a reefer van mounted to a vehicle registered under the Land Transportation Office (LTO). No registration of this type of vehicle is required under this Ordinance.

e) Processing Facilities - refer to an integrated system of equipment and facilities for processing of agricultural and fisheries products.

f) RAFMES - refers to Registration of Agricultural and Fisheries Machinery and Equipment System.

g) BAFE - refers to Bureau of Agriculture and Fisheries Engineering.

h) Annex "A" - a list officially prepared by the Department of Agriculture Regional Agricultural Engineering Division (DA-RAED) and the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAAR), in coordination with the City Agriculturist's Office and the City Fisheries Section.

**SECTION 5. REGISTRY OF AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERIES MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT** - The City Agriculturist's Office (CAGR'O) shall create, maintain and update a registry of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment. These functions shall be transferred to and assumed by the City Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering Division the moment it will be created and fully operational, pursuant to Section 29 of RA No. 10601 and Joint DA-DBM-CSC Memorandum Circular No. 02, Series of 2000, otherwise known as "Implementing Guidelines on the Strengthening and Establishment of the Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering Groups of the Local Government Units".

Section 5.1. The City Agriculturist's Office (CAGR'O) shall:

1) Train Agricultural and Biosystems Engineers/ (ABE) to undertake the evaluation and verification of documents submitted. In the absence of trained ABES, CAGR'O shall hire at least one ABE.

2) One QR printer.

Section 5.2. The lists of newly registered agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment with complete information shall be uploaded monthly to the server of Philippine Center for Post-Harvest Development and Mechanization-Agriculture and Fisheries Mechanization and Engineering Resource Network (PhilMech-AF Mech'RN). Likewise, this shall be submitted quarterly by CAGR'O to the Agricultural Engineering Division for consolidation. The consolidated report shall be endorsed to DA-RAED, copy furnished the Barangay Offices.

Section 5.3. In cases when the above-mentioned machinery and equipment are no longer operational, the owner shall report directly to the Municipal Agriculturist's Office (MAO) with a written notice/report and likewise furnish the Barangay Office. These requirements are condition sine qua non to the removal of the subject machinery and equipment from the registries of CAGR'O.

**SECTION 6. CONSOLIDATION OF RECORDS OF AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERIES MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT PRESENTLY REGISTERED BY OTHER CONCERNED NATIONAL GOVERNMENT AGENCIES** - The registration of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment presently being undertaken by concerned government agencies, such as the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) for fishing boats three (3) gross tons and above; Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA) on oil mills and; Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA) and other concerned national government agencies shall be consolidated by DA through BAFE.

**SECTION 7. GENERAL GUIDELINES FOR THE ISSUANCE OF CERTIFICATE OF AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERIES MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT REGISTRATION (CAPMER)**

Section 7.1. The owner/user of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment shall submit to the Municipal Agriculturist's Office (MAO) the following documents:

a) Certificate of Residency of the owner of the subject Machinery/Equipment from the Barangay where the owner is residing. In cases when the certificate is not available, the owner shall provide a copy or copies of duly notarized Affidavit of Residency.

b) Duly accomplished prescribed Registration Application Form as per Annex "B"; and  

c) Evidence of acquisition of ownership or possession of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment includes, but not limited to, Delivery Receipt (DR), Sales Invoice (SI), Deed of Sale, Notarized Extradition Settlement, Court-approved Judicial Partition, Official Certificate (OC), Court Order, Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) or Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). In cases that these documents are not available, the owner shall execute an affidavit attesting to the lawful ownership of the machinery or equipment or have a Department of Agriculture ownership certification.  

Section 7.2 The MAO shall forward the requirements to the Agricultural Engineering Division (ABE) of CAgRo. Upon receipt, the ABE shall verify and evaluate the documents submitted. Upon finding that the owner of the machinery/equipment has complied with all the documentary requirements and has paid the registration fee with MAO, CAgRo shall process and recommend the issuance of CAFMER (Annex "C") for approval by the City Agriculturist or his duly authorized representative.  

Section 7.3 The registration sticker shall be issued by CAgRo after the application form has been approved. CAgRo shall keep a copy of the approved CAFMER and ensure that the registration is uploaded to PhilMech-AFMechENR server upon registration.  

The data of the registered agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment will be synced/linked to the PhilMech-AFMechENR server upon registration.  

SECTION 8. PROCEDURE IN CASE OF AMENDMENT TO THE CERTIFICATE OF AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERIES MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT REGISTRATION (CAFMER) - The Office of the City Mayor shall issue a Certificate of Clearance and an amended CAFMER in the following instances:  

a) Transfer of Ownership by way of sale, donation, transfers inter vivos or mortis cause of the agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment. The documents stated in the preceding section, the Original CAFMER and Certificate of Clearance shall be required in the application for an amended CAFMER.  

b) Permanent Transfer of Location to another city/municipality - A new registration form shall be secured which shall be approved by the city where the owner has transferred.  

Section 9.1. The Office of the City Mayor through CAgRo shall issue a Certificate of Clearance to the owner for submission to the city/municipality where the owner has transferred within 30 days from the date of issuance of the clearance. The portion in the application form which states "For Deletion/Cancellation of Agricultural and Fisheries Machinery and Equipment Registration" should be filled out.  

Section 9.2. CAgRo of the city of origin shall remove the agricultural and/or fisheries machinery and equipment in the list of registration. The issuance of the amended CAFMER will require the owner to undergo another registration process. Thus, the owner will pay the registration fee as provided for in this Ordinance.

Section 8.3. In the event that such machinery and equipment are involved in any criminal or civil case regarding the question of ownership as reported by the Barangay Officials, no Certificate of Clearance shall be issued by CAgRo.

SECTION 9. REGISTRATION FEES - The registration fee of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment per establishment/owner shall be One Hundred Fifty Pesos (P150.00), inclusive of the cost of the registration sticker to be paid to the district office of the City. Upon payment of the registration sticker, a certificate of clearance shall be issued by CAgRo to the owner upon showing of the receipt of payment.

The agricultural and fishery machineries and equipment owned by the government, by cooperatives duly registered under R.A. No. 6810 (Magna Carta for Countryside and Barangay Business Enterprises (Kalakalan 2000) and R.A. No. 6938 (Cooperative Code of the Philippines), shall be exempted from the payment of registration fees. Likewise, agricultural and fishery machinery and equipment owned by associations and individual farmer/s and fisherfolk/s shall be exempted.

SECTION 10. INCENTIVES FOR THE REGISTRATION OF AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERIES MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT

a) Registration may be used in the availedment of government incentives/subsidies;
b) Registration will facilitate the location of lost agricultural and fisheries machinery units;
c) Availment of Common Service Facilities (CSF) services in the city;
d) Provision of insurance (e.g., Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture) for registered agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment.

CAGRo shall coordinate with the Philippine Crop Insurance (PCIC) for the most appropriate insurance package for agricultural and fisheries implements, in reference to the Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RBSBA) of DA-ICTS.

SECTION 11. PUBLIC INFORMATION CAMPAIGN - The CAgRo in collaboration with the City Information Office and Liga ng mga Barangay shall undertake information campaign on the implementation of the registration of ownership of agricultural and fishery machinery and equipment.

SECTION 12. FUNDING - Funding for the implementation of the registration of agricultural and fisheries machinery and equipment, which includes the hiring of ABES, purchase of registration equipment (QR Printer, etc.), and other activities related to the registration of ownership of agricultural and fishery machinery and equipment shall be incorporated in the annual work and financial plan of the City Agriculturist's Office.

SECTION 13. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS - The implementing unit shall formulate and adopt the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) within 90 days after the approval of this Ordinance.

SECTION 14. REPEALING CLAUSE - All existing ordinances, rules and regulations, as well as orders inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 15. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - Should any of these clauses or parts of this Ordinance be declared unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction, all other remaining portions not so declared shall remain valid and effective.
