Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USURPT TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE BARANGAY COUNCIL OF VICENTE HIZON SR. RELATIVE TO THE USE BY THE LATTER OF THREE HUNDRED FORTY-SIX SQUARE METERS (346 SQ.M.) OF LAND, A PORTION OF THE PARCEL OF LAND OWNED BY THE FORMER, LOCATED AT LOT 559-A, Bldd-10622, BARANGAY VICENTE HIZON SR., BUHANGIN DISTRICT, DAVAO CITY, COVERED BY TRANSFER CERTIFICATE OF TITLE (TCT) NO. T-8333".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES - The Mandate of the City of Davao Citizen's Charter of 2023 states "The priority thrusts and program of the City Government of Davao is anchored on the basic principles of sustainable development that responds to the increasing needs of the public and challenge of effectively and efficiently maintaining a dynamic administrative machinery that will realize the goals and objectives of the local government and the aspirations of the constituents". This Ordinance is based on the following policies:

A. Section 22 of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:
"SECTION 22. Corporate Powers - (a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
1. To have continuous succession in its corporate name;
2. To sue and be sued;
3. To have and use a corporate seal;
4. To acquire and convey real or personal property;
5. To enter into contracts; and
6. To exercise such other powers as are granted to corporations, subject to the limitations provided in this Code and other laws";

B. Section 455 of Republic Act No. 7160 provides:
"SECTION 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation - (a) The city mayor, as chief executive of the city government, shall exercise such powers and perform such duties and functions as provided by this Code and other laws.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

C. Office of the City Legal Officer Legal Opinion No. 619, Series of 2023, dated August 29, 2023 states "It is respectfully recommended that the Deed of Usurft be considered by the City Mayor by endorsing the same to the Sanggunian Panlungsod for appropriate action";

D. Transfer Certificate Title No. T-8333 is registered under the name of the "City Government of Davao", the area of which includes Lot 559-A of subdivision plan Bsd-10622 and Lot 559-B of subdivision plan Bsd-10622.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - The following words, terms, phrases and acronyms shall be defined as follows:

Bsd It is a subdivision survey on government titled properties conducted by a Government Geodetic Engineer. If a private titled land is being surveyed by a Government Geodetic Engineer thru court order, the resulting survey is a Bsd.

Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) The document that provides proof of ownership of a piece of property in the Philippines. The certificate is issued by the Register of Deeds in the province where the property is located.

R.A No. 7160 Republic Act Number 7160, otherwise known as "The Local Government Code of 1991"

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Usurft by and between the City of Davao and the Barangay Council of Vicente Hizon Sr., represented by the Punong Barangay, relative to the exclusive and uninterrupted use of land containing and area of Three Hundred Forty-six Square Meters (346 sq.m.), a portion of the land described as Lot 559-A, Bsd-10622, Barangay Vicente Hizon Sr., Buhangin District, this City covered by Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) No. T-8333.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 17th day of October 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
