AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND MAG-UUMANG NAGKAHUSA SA MACATABO (MANAGSAMA) MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE

Ord. No. 0301-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND MAG-UUMANG NAGKAIHUSA SA MACATABO (MANAGSAMAS) MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE."

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Mag-Uumang Nagkaihusa sa Macatabo (Managsamas) Multipurpose Cooperative.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVENESS - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 26th day of September 2023, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
