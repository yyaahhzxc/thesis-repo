**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY -** The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Department of Public Works and Highways and the City of Davao for the installation of roadway lighting at Davao-Cotabato Road (Davao City-JC-Digos Section).

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE -** This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
