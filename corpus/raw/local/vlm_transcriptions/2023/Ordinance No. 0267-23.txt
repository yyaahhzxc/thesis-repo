AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY THE CITY OF DAVAO IN FAVOR OF SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER (SPMC) RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF TWENTY-THREE (23) UNITS OF WATER DISPENSER AND THIRTY (30) UNITS OF STAND FAN FOR SPMC

Ord. No. 0267-23

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF TWENTY-THREE (23) UNITS OF WATER DISPENSER AND THIRTY (30) UNITS OF STAND FAN FOR SPMC".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by the City of Davao in favor of Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC), relative to the donation of twenty-three (23) units of water dispenser and thirty (30) units of stand fan for SPMC.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 25th day of July 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
