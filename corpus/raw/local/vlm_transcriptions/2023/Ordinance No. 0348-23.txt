Ord. No. 0348-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE FOLLOWING STREETS: 1. SAN PEDRO SQUARE FROM 10:00 PM TO 12:00 MN ON DECEMBER 18, 2023 UNTIL DECEMBER 22, 2023; 2. MARCO POLO GROUND - 2 PARALLEL STREETS OF ROXAS AVENUE IN BETWEEN MARCO POLO AND CITY TRIANGLE FROM 10:00 PM TO 12:00 MN ON DECEMBER 20, 2023 UNTIL DECEMBER 22, 2023; 3. ROXAS STREET AND ALL INTERSEC TIONS (P. ZAMORA STREET, FAIRA & AURORA STREET, MANDALIYA STREET) FROM 10:00 PM TO 12:00 MN ON DECEMBER 22, 2023; 4. MARCO POLO GROUND TO CM RECTO STREET RIGHT TO SAN PEDRO LEFT TO PELAYO STREET LEFT TO MAGALLANES B oulevard to San Pedro Left to FelciS Centrale Quimpo Boulevard to San Pedro Left to Chico Triangle and Half Lane Closure to CM RECTO FROM 10:00 PM TO 12:00 N OON JUNE 28, 2024 UNTIL JANUARY 1, 2024; 5. 2 PARALLEL STREETS BETWEEN MARCO POLO AND CITY TRIANGLE/ROXAS AVENUE UNTIL PADRE ZAMORA STREET AND PADRE GOMEZ STREET, ON DECEMBER 31, 2023 UNTIL JANUARY 1, 2024; BOLTON STREET BETWEEN RIZAL PARK AND SP BUILDING FROM 12:00 MN TO 7:00 AM, ON DECEMBER 16, 2023 UNTIL DECEMBER 24, 2023, IN CONNECTION WITH THE ANNUAL CELEBRATION OF PASKO FIESTA."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national holiday, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fairs:

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The aforementioned streets shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on the time and date hereinafter specified in connection with the annual celebration of Pasko Fiesta.

SECTION 4. SEPARAPBLITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 28th day of November, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
