Ord. No. 0169-23

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROADS TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC DURING THE CONDUCT OF THE ACTIVITIES IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE 86TH ARAW NG DAVAO ON THE DATES AND TIME REQUESTED".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fair.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the yearly celebration of "Araw ng Davao", Mr. Jennifer R. Romero, Officer-In-Charge of the City Tourism Operations Office, requested for the temporary road closure on the following dates and time to serve as venue for the activities in connection with the celebration of the 86th Araw ng Davao, to wit:
