AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON THE FOLLOWING SPECIFIED DATES AND TIME IN CONNECTION WITH THE HOLY WEEK AND EASTER CELEBRATIONS, TO WHICH:
1. FROM APRIL 2, 5:00 AM UP TO APRIL 9, 2023 9:00 PM (FRONTING SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL ONLY) IN BETWEEN BOLTON ST. AND C.M. RECTO AVENUE SPECIFICALLY FRONTING THE SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL ONLY (INSTALLATION AND RESTORATION OF STEEL STRUCTURE FOR SUGA/FORMATO); AND
ON APRIL 2, 2023 (PALM SUNDAY) 5:00 AM - FOR PROCESSION GOING TO THE CATHEDRAL AFTER THE BLESSING OF PALMS FROM RIZAL PARK PASSING THROUGH MAGALLANES ST. - BONIFACIO ROTUNDA;
2. ON APRIL 6, 2023 (GOOD FRIDAY - TRADITIONAL WAY OF THE CROSS) FROM 3:00 AM UNTIL 7:00 AM STARTING FROM FELCRIS CENTRE TO THE SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL PASSING THROUGH QUINTO ST. AND C.M. RECTO AVENUE (ALONG RED CROSS) - PONCIANO ST. - BONIFACIO ST. - LEGAZPI ST. - MAGALLANES ST. - BONIFACIO ROTUNDA - SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL;
FROM 3:00 PM UNTIL 10:00 PM (PROCESSION OF THE SANTO ENTERO) FROM SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL PASSING THROUGH C.M. RECTO AVENUE - ROXAS EXTENSION (ALONG RED CROSS) - PONCIANO ST. - LEGAZPI ST. - MAGALLANES ST. - BONIFACIO ROTUNDA - SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL.

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON THE FOLLOWING SPECIFIED DATES AND TIME IN CONNECTION WITH THE HOLY WEEK AND EASTER CELEBRATIONS, TO WIT:
1. FROM APRIL 2, 5:00 AM UP TO APRIL 9, 2023 9:00 PM, (FRONTING SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL ONLY) IN BETWEEN BOLTON ST. AND C.M. RECTO AVENUE SPECIFICALLY FRONTING THE SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL ONLY (INSTALLATION AND RESTORATION OF STEEL STRUCTURE FOR SUGAT/SALUBONG AND BLESSING OF FIRE)

ON APRIL 2, 2023 (PALM SUNDAY) 5:00 AM - FOR PROCESSION GOING TO THE CATHEDRAL AFTER THE BLESSING OF PALMS FROM RIZAL PARK PASSING THROUGH MAGALLANES ST. - BONIFACIO ROTUNDA

2. ON APRIL 6, 2023 (GOOD FRIDAY - TRADITIONAL WAY OF THE CROSS) FROM 3:00 AM UNTIL 7:00 AM STARTING FROM FELCRIS CENTRALE TO THE SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL PASSING THROUGH QUIIMPO BOULEVARD - RIZAL EXTENSION - C. DE JESUS (UJ) - BONIFACIO ST. - CM RECTO AVENUE - ROXAS EXTENSION (ALONG RED CROSS) - PONCIANO ST. - BONIFACIO ST. - LEGAZPI ST. - MAGALLANES ST. - BONIFACIO ROTUNDA - SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL;

FROM 3:00 PM UNTIL 10:00 PM (PROCESSION OF THE SANTO ENTERO) FROM SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL PARISH PASSING THROUGH CM RECTO AVENUE - ROXAS EXTENSION (ALONG RED CROSS) - PONCIANO ST. - BONIFACIO ST. - LEGAZPI ST. - MAGALLANES ST. - BONIFACIO ROTUNDA - SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21(c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fair or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and water works projects.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The San Pedro Cathedral Parish Pastoral Council requested for a temporary road closure to vehicular traffic on the following abovementioned specified streets and time in connection with the upcoming Holy Week and Easter Celebrations.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
