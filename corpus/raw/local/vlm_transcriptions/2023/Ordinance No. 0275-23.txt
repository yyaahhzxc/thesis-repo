Ord. No. 0275-23

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF CERTAIN ROADS TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON SPECIFIED DATES AND TIME AS HEREINAFTER PROVIDED IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE 38TH KADAYAWAN FESTIVAL."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that any national or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency or fiesta celebration, public rallies, agricultural and industrial fairs.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the yearly celebration of Kadayawan Festival, the City Tourism Operations Office requested for the closure of certain roads to vehicular traffic on specified dates and time attached herewith.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on August 15, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
