ORDINANCE NO. 499-49
Series of 2008

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING THE
INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS BOARD OF
DAVAO CITY

Be it Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "The International
Relations Board of Davao City";

SECTION II. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Local government exchange and cooperation is now a global concern, promoting transnational understanding, facilitating economic development, and improving people's lives. It is an effective way for which provinces, cities, and municipalities can enhance local governance by sharing ideas, experiences, and even resources with their partner Local Government Unit.

Through inter-local technical partnerships, Philippines local authorities are now more ready to assume critical roles in the promotion of culture and techniques in the areas of local government and to enhance economy, tourism, agriculture, environmental management, culture and arts, information and communications technology, education, trade, and industry cooperation. Active participation of our local authorities in international relations and cooperation initiatives is part of the overall strategies to prepare themselves for the challenges of the next century.

SECTION III. CREATION OF THE INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS BOARD OF DAVAO CITY - In order to strengthen Davao City's international relations, the International Relations Board of Davao City is hereby created composed of the following:

1. The City Mayor, as Chairman;
2. Chair, Committee on International Relations;
3. Chair, Committee on Trade and Industry;
4. Chair, Committee on Information and Communications Technology;
5. Office-In-Charge, Davao City Investment Promotion Center (DCIPC);
6. City Planning and Development Coordinator;
7. Governor, City Tourism Council;
8. City Information Officer;
9. City Agriculturist;
10. Regional Director, Department of Science and Technology-XI (DOST-XI);
11. City Director, Department of Trade and Industry-XI (DTTIXI);
12. President, Mindanao Economic Development Council (MEDC);
13. President, Davao City Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Inc. (DCCCI);
14. Regional Director, Department of Foreign Affairs-XI (DFA-XI);
15. Provincial Director, Technical Skills and Development Authority-XI (TSDA-XI);

SECTION IV. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD - The Board shall have the following duties and functions, to wit:

a. Act as advisory group for the Committee on International Relations which is ad hoc in nature and "on-call" basis engagement;
b. The Board shall serve as "think tank" for the Committee on International Relations and matters pertaining to international engagements and the like;
c. The Board together with the Committee on International Relations shall review proposals on sister cities;
d. The Board shall come up with proposed strategic agenda for Davao City on matters pertaining to international engagements.

e. The Board shall provide substantive inputs to the Committee on International Relations especially in coming up with priority legislation, programs and projects of the said committee;
f. Assist in the review of the committee's mandate and come up with relevant initiatives to strengthen and sustain the efforts that will be initiated by the committee;
g. Inform the Committee on International Relations on relevant international events or prospective initiatives beneficial and strategic to the city whereby creating a significant impact to the city's development;

SECTION V. RULE MAKING POWER - The Board shall issue implementing Rules and Regulations as may be necessary for the effective implementation of the provisions of this Ordinance within ninety (90) days from its effectivity thereof;

SECTION VI. SUPPLEMENTARY EFFECT OF OTHER LAWS AND DECREES - The provisions of this Ordinance shall be without prejudice to other existing laws and decrees;

SECTION VII. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other City Ordinances, or part(s) thereof, which are inconsistent with this Ordinance, are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION VIII. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, May 20, 2006, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
