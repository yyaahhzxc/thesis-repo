AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE CITY TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT OFFICE (CTTMO)

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance Creating the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted to it, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare;

Subparagraph (a) of Section 17 thereof also provides that local government units shall likewise exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective provisions of the basic services and facilities enumerated under the Local Government Code;

SECTION 3. CREATION OF DEPARTMENT: The City Transport and Traffic Management Office is hereby created under the direct supervision and control of the City Mayor's Office. It shall be headed by a Department Head and a City Government Assistant Department Head. For this purpose, the following divisions shall be created:

1. Administrative Division;
2. Transport Planning & Management Division;
3. Traffic Engineering & Infrastructure Division;
4. Facilities Management & Operations Division;
5. Traffic Enforcement & Street Management Division;
6. Motorized Vehicle Franchising & Regulatory Division.

SECTION 4. THE CITY TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT OFFICE:

a. The Administrative Division shall:

1. Provide administrative and logistical support and services to all the divisions of the office;
2. Provide support in the management of database including collection of data and other information relative to the operation and maintenance of the department;
3. Provide administrative support in the maintenance, updating, conduct of inventories of all data, records, supplies and other equipment of the department;
4. Adopt/maintain computerized transport and traffic management systems;
5. Do other functions in providing support to other divisions.

b. The Transport Planning & Management Division shall:

1. Provide technical inputs into the preparation and updating of the city's Land Use Plan, particularly the long-term road network plan of the city;
2. Conduct studies and researches in both transport and traffic administration appropriate to the local setting of the city;

3. Update and analyze the public transport routes and services covering motorized tricycles, buses, jeepneys, and other public transport modes;
4. Identify and recommend possible "missionary routes";
5. Collect traffic data and statistics such as vehicular counts, road layout and dimensions, etc., and analyze the same for use in policy formulation;
6. Attend hearings of the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFR) to ensure that the issuance of permits of public convenience is consistent with the comprehensive zoning ordinance of the City;
7. Evaluate requests, in coordination with the City Planning and Development Office, for variance from the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City that are likely to have significant traffic impact;
8. Formulate and advocate programs that will promote and encourage walking and trips by public transport and higher-capacity modes;
9. Perform other duties and functions as directed by the Department Head.

c. The Traffic Engineering & Facilities Management Division shall:

1. Identify traffic bottleneck points and establish priorities in implementing remedial measures;
2. Formulate traffic engineering schemes, such as banning of turning movements, creation of one-way streets, prohibition of parking, designation of loading/unloading zones, installation of traffic signs, regulating speeds and streamlining traffic in accordance with its approved priority lists;
3. Install and maintain traffic signs, road and/or pavement markings and other traffic control devices or cause the preparation and installation of the same;
4. Recommend to the Transport and Traffic Management Advisory Board (TTMAB) to support traffic management schemes;
5. Review major property development proposals, especially those located in the Central Business District, along major thoroughfares and national roads and highways within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City, to its impact to traffic generation;
6. Identify private subdivision roads that should be opened to improve traffic overall circulation and initiate moves for the full or partial integration into the road network;
7. Review and collect data and statistics such as vehicular counts, road layouts and dimensions, and other relevant data which would be of importance for appropriate transport and traffic policy formulation;
8. Review and recommend requests for approval of road diggings, road constructions, temporary closures, production and installation of traffic-related signage from individuals, private entities and barangay councils, parades, and conduct of extra ordinary events that would reduce road capacity;
9. Perform other duties and functions as directed by the Department Head.

d. The Traffic Enforcement & Street Management Division shall:

1. Assign personnel to direct traffic at intersections and other locations requiring such intervention;
2. Execute the enforcement component of any traffic schemes devised or considered by the Traffic Engineering Division as approved by Transport and Traffic Management Advisory Board (TTMAB).

3. Enforce applicable traffic rules and regulations, including apprehensions and issuance of city traffic citation tickets and penal summons to drivers found in violation of any provision in this Code in coordination with the regional offices of the Department of Transportation (DOTR);
4. Assist the Davao City Police Office (DCPO) in the conduct of investigations of any verbal accident that occurred within the limits of the City;
5. Coordinate with the Davao City Police Office (DCPO), traffic enforcers and Land Transportation Office (LTO) traffic enforcers to harmonize personnel deployment and other field operations within the city;
6. Initiate the towing or removal of all illegally parked, stopped/stalled or disabled vehicle obstructing traffic including abandoned vehicles;
7. Suggest changes in any traffic scheme, including provision of traffic signals;
8. Cause the removal of any obstruction on pedestrian sidewalks, alleys, streets and no throughfare which impede vehicular or pedestrian traffic; in the event that no one admits ownership of any object or thing found impeding vehicular or pedestrian traffic, take possession of the same in the city impoundment area for later disposition;
9. Cause the removal of unauthorized traffic signs and take possession of the same in the city impoundment area for later disposition;
10. Cause the filing of a complaint or information and secure the issuance of a warrant of arrest against violators who fail or refuse to obey the city traffic citation tickets or penal summons in coordination with Davao City Police Office (DCPO);
11. Prescribe and recommend rates of administrative fines or penalties to be imposed on violators in admission of a violation, and willing to settle the same without having the case filed in court; and
12. Perform other duties and functions as directed by the Department Head;

e. Motorized Vehicle Franchising & Regulatory Division shall:

1. Manage the operations and registration of motorized tricycle for hire;
2. Hear and decide uncontested MTOP application;
3. Issue, amend, revise, drop, cancel and revoke MTOPs after due notice and hearings;
4. Conduct investigations and hearings of complaints on violations of this Code, other traffic laws, rules and regulations, and other issuances of MTRC;
5. Summon operators/drivers and witnesses to appear in hearings conducted by the MTRC;
6. Determine, fix and prescribe summonses or MTC citation tickets;
7. Recommend proposals for the location of MTH terminals in the barangays;
8. Formulate and recommend appropriate policies, subject to approval of MTH;
9. Keep records of all MTH registered and/or operating in Davao City which shall include the names of registered owners, operators, drivers, users and their addresses and other information relating the physical identification of MTH;
10. Conduct public information campaigns in barangays with MTH operations in support to enhance the implementation and enforcement of traffic rules and regulations;
11. Perform other duties and functions as directed by the Department Head.

For Motorized and Non-Motorized Vessel:
1. Manage the conduct of registration of motorized fishing vessels, three (3) tons and below, using the City of Dorav as homeport and other commercial operations within the municipal waters of the City of Dorav;
2. Prescribe rules and regulations that are acceptable to stakeholders;
3. Implement the enforcement component devised or considered as approved by the TTMB;
4. Perform other duties and functions as directed by the Department Head.

SECTION 5. BUDGET ALLOCATION: The City Budget Officer shall annually allocate funds for the operation of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTM) which will be sourced from the General Fund.

**SECTION 7. AUTOMATIC TRANSFER OF BUDGETARY ALLOCATION:**
All budgetary allocations of every position transferred in or detailed from various offices are deemed transferred to the City Transport and Traffic Management Office from the effectiveness of this Ordinance.

**SECTION 8. PERSONNEL TRANSITION:**
To effect the transfer of personnel from other departments to the CTTMO, affected employees shall be reappointed by the Local Chief Executive pursuant to Civil Service Rules and Regulations.

All personnel movements shall conform to Section 235 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991.

**SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE:**
All ordinances, resolutions, orders, and decrees or parts thereof which are inconsistent with any provision of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:**
If, for any reason/s, any part or provision of this Ordinance is held to be unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof that are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.
