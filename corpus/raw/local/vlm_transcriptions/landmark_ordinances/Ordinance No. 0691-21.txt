ORDINANCE NO. 0691-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITTING THE MANUFACTURE, SALE,
DISTRIBUTION OR USE OF HYDROGEN FILLED BALLOONS IN
THE CITY OF DAVAO

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0691-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "HYDROGEN-FILLED BALLOONS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao hereby declares the following as its policies to wit:
a) To eliminate the practice of using hydrogen-filled balloons in all business, leisure, entertainment, sports, and social activities to ensure the protection of public health and the environment;
b) To raise awareness that hydrogen is an unstable and flammable gas which can easily explode and catch fire;
c) To promote responsibility and accountability in the use of hydrogen in balloons;
d) To ensure proper monitoring/control of hydrogen balloons within the City, as a means for maintaining the ecological balance.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS -
a) Balloon - a nonporous bag of light material that is inflated especially with gas; such as a bag that is filled with heated air or a gas lighter than air so as to rise and float in the atmosphere;
b) Hydrogen - a nonmetallic gaseous chemical element with atomic number 1 that is the simplest and lightest of the elements and is used especially in the processing of fossil fuels and the synthesis of ammonia.

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover all hydrogen-filled balloons within the territorial jurisdiction and airspace of Davao City with the exception of those used for research and development purposes in the field of science and technology, laboratory analysis, and atmospheric monitoring.

SECTION 5. PROHIBITED ACTS - It shall be unlawful for any person or business establishment to sell or offer for sale, distribute, or use hydrogen-filled/inflated balloons within the territory of Davao City.

SECTION 6. PENALTY CLAUSE - Any violation of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be punished as follows, without prejudice to the prosecution of other civil and criminal actions:
• 1st Offense - a fine of One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00)
• 2nd Offense - a fine of Three Thousand Pesos (P3,000.00)
• 3rd Offense - a fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) and one year suspension of business permit

If the violation is committed by a business establishment, the President, General Manager, or the person acting in behalf of either the President, Owner, or General Manager shall be held liable in the case of a corporation or partnership, or the Owner, Proprietor, or the person acting in his behalf shall be held responsible in the case of a single proprietorship.

**SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE**  
Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**  
If, for any reason, any Section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY**  
This Ordinance shall take effect 15 days upon approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation.
