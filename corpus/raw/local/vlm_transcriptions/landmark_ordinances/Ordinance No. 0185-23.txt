Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE UPDATING THE ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE BY CONVERSION, AND CREATION OF DIVISIONS, SECTIONS, AND POSITIONS IN THE CITY PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT OFFICE (CPDO)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES. - Presented to the Honorable Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod for review and consideration, is a letter from Atty. Francis Mark H. Layog, Acting City Administrator, proposing for the conversion of vacant positions and organizational structuring of position titles in the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO), in order to address the gaps in the hierarchy of positions which is already affecting the delivery of services, competence and morale of the department:

Section 16 of Republic Act 7160 provides that "every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants";

Section 76 of Republic Act 7160 empowered local government units to design and implement its organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil Service Commission (CSC);

Section 12 (2), Chapter 3, Title I (A), Book V of the Administrative Code of 1987 provides that the Civil Service Commission shall prescribe, amend and enforce rules and regulations for carrying into effect the provisions of the Civil Service Law and other pertinent laws;

Civil Service Commission (CSC) Resolution No. 1701009 dated June 16, 2017, which was published in the Philippine Star on August 2, 2017 and took effect on August 18, 2017, circulated through CSC Memorandum Circular No. 24, Series of 2017, adopts and promulgates the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resources Actions (ORAOHRA) to govern the preparation, submission of, and actions to be taken on appointments and other human resource movements;

Section 11 of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions (Revised July 2018) Nature of Appointment

"Reclassification - A form of staffing modification and/or position classification action which is applied only when there is substantial change in the regular duties and responsibilities of the position. This may result in a change in any or all of the position attributes: position title, level and/or salary grade. It is the generic term for changes in staff/position classification, which includes upgrading, downgrading, and reclassification";

Department of Budget and Management (DBM) Budget Circular No. 2018-3, dated July 30, 2018, was issued to "amend the guidelines on reclassification of positions as part of the mandate of DBM to administer and maintain the Compensation and Position Classification System (CPCS) in the government";

Section 4.2 of DBM Budget Circular No. 2018-3 dated July 30, 2018 states:

"4.2 Reclassification shall be applied in the following cases:
4.2.1 There are changes in the level/category of the government entity which will bear on the level of key positions;
4.2.2 There are changes in the quantitative and other factors used in determining the appropriate position and level in accordance with existing position classification standards;
4.2.3 The reclassification of a position is provided by law or Presidential authority; or
4.2.4 Other meritorious cases as may be determined by the DBM";

Section 4.4 of DBM Budget Circular No. 2018-3, dated July 30, 2018, states "Accordingly, the previous policy which allows reclassification to a different class category is hereby rescinded";

Section 4.9 of DBM Budget Circular No. 2018-3 Guidelines on the Reclassification of Positions, dated July 30, 2018, states "Reclassification shall not apply to vacant regular positions which necessitate changes in their position titles for being under any of the instances cited in item 4.2 of this Circular. Instead, the conversion of positions shall be effected i.e., the items shall be abolished and in their stead, the appropriate positions shall be created";

Section 12 of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions allows adjustments or movement of human resource without need of issuance of an appointment;

Section 1 of Republic Act Number 1080, entitled "An Act Declaring the Bar and Board Examinations as Civil Service Examinations," states "The bar examinations and the examinations given by the various boards of examiners of the Government are declared as civil service examinations, and shall, for purposes of appointment to positions in the classified service, the duties of which"

SECTION 4. SUBDIVIDING THE ADMINISTRATIVE DIVISION OF CPPD INTO TWO (2) SECTIONS - The Administrative Division of the City Planning and Development Office shall have two (2) sections namely: Administration and Operations Section, and Communications and Auxiliary Section.
A. The functions of the Administration and Operations Section shall include:
1. Administrative and general services for the department;
2. Provides general clerical, records, and property management services;
3. Assists in the preparation of the office's annual budget, annual reports and personnel services records;
4. Prepares and facilitates the transmission of communications and related services;
5. Performs such other duties and functions as may be directed by the head of office and/or as may be prescribed by law or ordinances.
B. The functions of the Communications and Auxiliary Section shall include:
1. Supports the technical function of the department;
2. Leads the team of technical writers and committee secretaries;
3. Organizes consolidated office schedule and proof-reading of write-ups;
4. Oversees the communications efforts of the department including public relations and social media engagement;
5. Works closely with the department head in delivering highly technical tasks like reports and presentations that are highly confidential in nature;
6. Performs such other duties and functions as may be directed by the head of office and/or as may be prescribed by law or ordinances.
C. The Administrative Division shall be headed by a Supervising Administrative Officer VI with Salary Grade 22;

SECTION 5. CREATION OF THE GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEM DIVISION IN THE CPDO -- The Geographic Information System (GIS) Division shall be created as a division under the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO).  

A. The functions of the GIS Division include:  

1. Creates results through digital maps, printed maps, tables, and graphs;  
2. Designs, develops, and manages Geographic Information System and their procedures to incorporate mapping in GIS-related functions for CPDO in coordination with CTFE;  
3. Collects and captures geographic data from remote sensing images, existing maps, tables, aerial ground surveys, technical descriptions, reports, and tabular data from various government agencies and private entities as input data for the GIS process of creating digital data, through digitization process using GIS software and hardware;  
4. Stores and manages data into different media, such as hard disk, spatial database, online drive, and on cloud for easy updating and to avoid data redundancy;  
5. Integrates data through GIS making possible to link information collected from different sources and GIS is used in combination to map variables to create and analyze new variables and factors;  
6. Converts data collected from different sources, such as the conversion of projection through geo-referencing, depending upon the user's requirements and facilitates analysis of the same;

8. Simulates models on data to create useful geographic information for various sectors;

10. Performs such other duties and functions as may be directed by the head of office and/or as may be prescribed by law or ordinances.

SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances, resolutions, executive orders, memoranda and other issuances in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
