Series of 2024

AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE WATER-BREAK TIME
IN THE PRIVATE AND PUBLIC SCHOOLS OFFERING DAY CARE,
KINDERGARTEN AND ELEMENTARY CLASSES IN THE CITY OF
DAVAO

Ord. No. 0548-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "A MINUTE WATER-BREAK OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote and encourage the development of adequate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover private and public schools offering day care, kindergarten and elementary classes in the City of Davao.

SECTION 4. MANNER OF ADMINISTRATION - Water-Break Time shall be observed before the start of the second subject and at the end of the last subject. Pursuant thereto, this Ordinance shall not deter nor prohibit the students from drinking water at any given time, provided that they will not in any way disrupt the classes.

SECTION 5. ROLE OF THE SCHOOL ADMINISTRATOR - The School Administrator in all public and private schools are hereby directed to strictly implement the mandate of this Ordinance in their respective schools by conducting an orientation to all concerned for its effective implementation in coordination with the Parent-Teacher Association (PTA). The School Administrator shall also ensure that adequate and only potable water is accessible to children and ensure further that water sold inside the school premises is in sealed water bottles and underwents the water quality and potability test from a laboratory that is accredited by the Department of Health (DOH).

SECTION 6. ROLE OF THE PARENTS - Parents are encouraged to support this Ordinance by ensuring that their children have sufficient supply of drinking water contained in a not single-use plastic bottles or canisters.

SECTION 7. ROLE OF THE CITY HEALTH OFFICE - The City Health Office is required by this Ordinance to conduct periodic monitoring to ensure that only potable water sources inside the schools are accessible to children and further secure that all water sold inside the school premises is in sealed water bottles and has undergone the water quality and potability test through accredited laboratories only.

SECTION 8. INFORMATION CAMPAIGN - The School Administrator is hereby mandated to post informational materials with inscription detailing matters relating to "A Minute Water-Break" posted in conspicuous places within the school's vicinity.

SECTION 9. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
