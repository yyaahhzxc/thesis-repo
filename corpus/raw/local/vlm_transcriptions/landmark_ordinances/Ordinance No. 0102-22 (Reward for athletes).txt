ORDINANCE NO. 0102-22
Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING A REWARD SYSTEM FOR ATHLETES AND COACHES WHO BRING PRESTIGE AND HONOR TO THE CITY OF DAVAO IN THEIR OUTSTANDING PERFORMANCES IN REGIONAL, NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL YOUTH SPORTS COMPETITIONS AND TOURNAMENTS, APPROPRIATING FUNDS IN RELATION THERETO AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Page 2 of 7
Ord. No. 0102-22

Be it enacted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "DAVAO CITY SPORTS EXCELLENCE INCENTIVES PROGRAM" Ordinance.

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao recognizes that sports is a lasting social investment for building a physically fit and healthy society and that a successful Sports Excellence Incentives Program of the city is needed for the development of National and International caliber athletes. Towards this end, a reward-and incentive program for the exemplary performance of an athlete is hereby declared as a valuable tool with the following objectives:
a) To develop, upgrade and uplift the overall standard of sports in Davao City in order to achieve excellence in the arena of sports;
b) To achieve recognition in sports and to promote Davao City through sports;
c) To motivate and manage the City's sports talents so as to realize their maximum potential;
d) To play an active role in the social development aspect in the city through sports;
e) To help build a healthy, cultured and disciplined society through sports and various development programs.

SECTION 3. ESTABLISHMENT OF THE DAVAO CITY SPORTS EXCELLENCE INCENTIVES PROGRAM - It is hereby established in Davao City a Sports Excellence Incentives Program under the City Sports Office.

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean as follows:
"Athlete" shall be construed as any person trained or skilled in sports, or games usually requiring skill, physical strength, agility, stamina, who must be a Filipino citizen and a resident of the City of Davao. The term shall include all members of the official delegates of the City of Davao to a particular tournament or competition. Their names should be included in the official list of players prepared by the City Sports Office, in coordination with the Philippine Olympic Committee and the Philippine Sports Commission.
"Coach" shall refer to a person involved in the direction, instruction and training of the operations of a sports team or of individual sports. The coach must be a Filipino citizen, bonafide resident of the City of Davao, member of the athlete and coaches' pool, recognized or endorsed by the City Sports Office and has represented the City of Davao as an official coach to a Davao City athlete or team in a particular sporting event/competition.

"International Competition" refers to sports competitions held in an international level, and can be categorized into regular major competitions, world-level championships, Asian-level competitions and qualifying competitions for world-level games.  

"National Competition" refers to sports competition held in the national level, categorized into regular major competitions and qualifying competitions for national-level games.  

"Regional Competition" refers to sports competition made within Mindanao and/or within Davao Region.  

SECTION 5. BENEFICIARIES OF DAVAO CITY SPORTS EXCELLENCE INCENTIVES PROGRAM - All duly recognized athletes, trainers and coaches from Davao City who are winners in any Regional, National or International competition or tournament sanctioned/acknowledged by the Philippine Olympic Committee and the Philippine Sports Commission, as the case may be, are hereby qualified under the Davao City Sports Excellence Incentives Program under the following guidelines:  

a) An athlete, trainer or coach should represent Davao City as their LGU in the tournament or competition;  

b) Only a one-time incentive per event/competition shall be given to the entitled athlete or coach regardless of the number of medals won; the incentive given is equivalent to the highest award won.  

SECTION 6. REGIONAL SPORTS COMPETITION - Regional sports competition shall refer to regular major competitions within Davao Region and Mindanao-wide competitions, such as the Batang Pinoy and other similar competitions, sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission.  

SECTION 7. NATIONAL SPORTS COMPETITION - National sports competition shall refer to regular major competition such as: Philippine National Games, PhilSpada National Games and Batang Pinoy National Championships held every year or any event sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission.  

SECTION 8. INTERNATIONAL SPORTS COMPETITION - International sports competition shall refer to regular major international competitions such as: the Olympic Games, Asian Games, Southeast Asian (SEA) Games, Youth Olympic Games, Paralympic Games, Asian Park Games, Asian Indoor and Martial Arts Games, Asian Beach Games, and Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) Para Games, ASEAN School Games, ARAPURA Games and the BIMPIT-EAGA Games or any event sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission and/or the Philippine Olympic Committee.

SECTION 9.1. Aside from the foregoing cash incentives, the Davao City Athlete shall also be given a medal of valor to be issued by the City Mayor.  

SECTION 9.2. Only those competition or tournaments sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission for regional and national sports competition and the Philippine Sports Commission and/or the Philippine Olympic Committee for international sports competition, shall be qualified for the cash incentives program of this Ordinance.  

SECTION 9.3. All cash incentives herein stated may be adjusted by the City Mayor, through the Sangguniang Panlungsod, based on studies, consultations, and public hearings with relevant agencies to reflect the economic needs of athletes, not later than three (3) years after the effectiveness of this Ordinance, and every three (3) years thereafter.

SECTION 9.4. For team competitions, the team shall receive the same cash incentives to be divided equally among the team members actually present during the competition.

SECTION 9.5. At the discretion of the City Mayor, an additional cash incentive can be given to the Davao City Athlete, either individual or as a team, depending on the prestige brought to Davao City, the available budget, as well as taking into consideration other circumstances. For this, the City Sports Office can make a recommendation.

SECTION 10. CASH INCENTIVES FOR COACHES - Coaches of athletes, who are endorsed by the City Sports Office, shall be entitled to cash incentives and Medal of Valor if they have trained and rendered service to the athletes or teams who win in national and/or international competitions. The incentives for the coaches shall be equivalent to the highest award won by their athlete of the cash incentives for gold, silver or bronze medallists.

SECTION 11. QUALIFICATION FOR REWARD AND RECOGNITION - To be qualified for the Davao City Sports Excellence Incentives Program under this Ordinance, the following qualifications should be satisfied:

SECTION 11.1 FOR ATHLETES
a. Must be a Filipino citizen
b. Must be a bonafide resident of the City of Davao for at least two (2) years and must have duly represented the City of Davao in the sports competitions participated.
c. Must present a recommendatory letter or certification that the athlete was invited to join the event/competition.
d. Has brought pride and honor for the City of Davao because of the athlete's achievement.
e. Must win a gold, silver and bronze medal in any sports event or competition recognized and sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission, the Philippine Olympic Committee and the International Olympic Committee.

SECTION 11.2 FOR COACHES
a. Must be a Filipino Citizen.
b. Must be a bonafide resident of the City of Davao for at least two (2) years.
c. Must be a member of the athletes' pool/roster/list, recognized by the City Sports Office.
d. Must have represented the City of Davao as the official coach.
e. Must be endorsed by the City Sports Office.

SECTION 12. DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS - The concerned athletes and their coaches shall also submit the following documents to the Davao City Sports Office to qualify for the cash award and incentive as contained in this Ordinance:

SECTION 12.1 For Athlete:
a. Certified true copy of Birth Certificate issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).
b. Barangay Certification of residency or voter's affidavit or ID of the winner, or if in case a minor, voter's affidavit or ID of parents or guardians showing that the athlete is a resident of Davao City.
c. Certificate of award, medal or trophy showing that the athlete has indeed won in the Regional, National or International competition/tournament.
d. Invitation Letter or Certificate inviting the athlete to join the competition/tournament that the athlete has won.
e. Certification from the City Sports Office that the coach is the official coach and has personally trained or rendered service to the winning Davao City athlete or team. Such certification must be in writing.
f. Other documents that may be required by the City Sports Office and the City Accounting Office.

SECTION 13. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS - The City Sports Office under the Office of the City Mayor shall formulate other implementing rules and regulations in granting rewards to the qualified athletes of Davao City.

SECTION 14. APPROPRIATION - The appropriation necessary for the implementation of this Ordinance shall be included in the proposed Annual Budget of the City Government, under the Sports Development Program of the City Sports Office under the City Mayor's Office, subject to the approval of the Local Finance Committee as well as auditing rules and regulations.

SECTION 15. REPEALING CLAUSE - All local legislative measures, rules, and regulations inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 16. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

ENACTED, November 22, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
