**Ordinance No. 088-13**

**Series of 2013**

**An Ordinance Granting Authority to the City Mayor to Release the Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) Based on Resolution No. 012-13, Series of 2013, of the Local Finance Committee (LFC), City Government of Davao entitled, "RECOMMENDING APPROVAL OF THE IMPLEMENTING GUIDELINES ON THE GRANT OF INCENTIVE FOR PLANTILLU PERSONNEL, CONSULTANTS, JOB ORDINANCE OFFICERS, AND OTHERS OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, IN RECOGNITION OF THEIR VALUABLE SUPPORT IN THE PERFORMANCE OF ACTUAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES TOWARDS THE ATTAINMENT AND SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF THE CITY'S VISION, MISSION AND GOAL, CHARGEBEAG AGAINST ANY SAVINGS OF 2013 APPROPRIATIONS," in accordance with the Local Government Code of 1991, and the Implementing Guidelines (IG) Subject to Commission on Audit (COA) Auditing Rules and Regulations.**

Ord. No. 088-13

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panglungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE. AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO RELEASE THE PRODUCTIVITY ENHANCEMENT INCENTIVE (PEI) BASED ON RESOLUTION NO. 012-13, SERIES OF 2013, OF THE LOCAL FINANCE COMMITTEE (LFC), CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, ENTITLED, "RECOMMENDATION FOR THE GRANTING OF PRODUCTIVITY ENHANCEMENT INCENTIVE (PEI) TO ALL PLANTILLA PERSONNEL CONSULTANTS, JOB ORDER/CONTRACT OF SERVICE, INCLUDING GRANT-IN-AID, DAY CARE WORKERS, BARANGAY HEALTH WORKERS, BARANGAY NUTRITION SCHOLARS, AND AUXILIARY PERSONNEL OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, IN RECOGNITION OF THEIR VALUABLE SUPPORT IN THE PERFORMANCE OF ACTUAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES TOWARDS THE ATTAINMENT OF THE GOAL OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO TO REDUCE THE STATE CHARGEABLE AGAINST ANY SAVINGS OF 2013 APPROPRIATIONS," IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT (DBM) GUIDELINES, SUBJECT TO COMMISSION ON AUDIT (COA) AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS;

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use;

SECTION 3. LEGAL BASIS. The Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) for Plantilla Personnel Consultants, Job Order/Contract of Service, including Grant-In-Aid, Day Care Workers, Barangay Health Workers, Barangay Nutrition Scholars, and Auxiliary Personnel of the City Government of Davao, shall be granted in accordance with the Department of Budget Management (DBM) Circular No. 0313-3;

SECTION 4. SOURCE OF FUND. The granting Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) to all Plantilla Personnel Consultants, Job Order/Contract of Service, including Grant-In-Aid, Day Care Workers, Barangay Health Workers, Barangay Nutrition Scholars, and Auxiliary Personnel of the City Government of Davao, shall be sourced out of the savings from the current Operating Expenditure of all city departments and offices for Calendar Year 2013;

SECTION 5. IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PRODUCTIVITY ENHANCEMENT INCENTIVE (PEI). The Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) shall be granted to each personnel of the City Government of Davao, based on Resolution No. 012-13, Series of 2013, of the Local Finance Committee, City Government of Davao, as approved by Resolution No. 0313-13, Series of 2013, implementing therein;

SECTION 6. LIMITATION. The amount provided for the purpose shall be exclusively used for the granting of Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) to all Plantilla Personnel Consultants, Job Order/Contract of Service, including Grant-In-Aid, Day Care Workers, Barangay Health Workers, Barangay Nutrition Scholars, and Auxiliary Personnel of the City Government of Davao, the release of which must conform with the existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulation;

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;
