Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE SPECIAL OFFICE FOR CHILDREN'S CONCERNS (SOCO) UNDER THE CITY MAYOR'S OFFICE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - For purposes of the implementation of Ordinance No. 0293-06, Series of 2006, entitled "Comprehensive Children and Family Support System in Davao City, As Amended," the Special Office for Children's Concerns (SOCO) shall be created to beef up the capacity of all implementing agencies in realizing the provision of the Ordinance. It shall be the special coordinating and implementing arm of the Local Government of Davao City Local Council for the Protection of Children (LCPC). The LCPC shall be composed of children's organizations from the entire district from the direct service function of the Davao City's social welfare arm, the City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO). In addition, SOCO shall serve as the secretariat of the Local Council for the Protection of Children.

SECTION 3. FUNCTIONS OF THE SPECIAL OFFICE FOR CHILDREN'S CONCERNS

a) The line function office shall be created by the Local Government of Davao City responsible for the implementation of this Ordinance with regulatory, executive, coordinative, monitoring, and evaluative functions.
b) Ensure, uphold, promote, and fulfill the four fundamental rights of children namely: survival, protection, development, and participation through plans, programs, and policy making.
c) Coordinate with networks of organizations, both GOs and CSOs, in the preparation and assessment as well as updating of Davao City Development Plan for Children to ensure its implementation at all levels through meetings, workshops, and consultations.
d) Establish and manage a database on children in Davao City.
e) Formulate or recommend mechanisms, standards, and guidelines in public or private entities to ensure the effective implementation of the objectives of the LCPC and other laws involving children.
f) Oversee and assess policies and programs of public and private entities whether there is faithfulness to the DDCVC and other laws and ordinances involving children and coordination with various organizations towards improving its implementation and effectiveness.

A. REGULATORY

1.1 Ensure the implementation of standards and guidelines for all children and development mechanisms instituted by all establishments, local government agencies and instrumentalities, such as but not limited to, the Department of Education, Philippine National Police, Department of Social Welfare and Development, Department of Health, and the Local Barangays, for effective implementation of the Barangay Council for the Protection of Children (BCPC) programs/projects including proper coordination and effective referral system.
1.2 Review existing policies and guidelines in terms of adherence to the local children's code.
1.3 Act on complaints involving alleged violations against children by an implementing agency.

B. COORDINATING, MONITORING AND EVALUATION

1.1 Formulate, review, update, and consolidate tools for the monitoring and evaluation of the performance of implementing agencies pertinent to children's concerns.
1.2 Monitor and evaluate existing policies and programs of barangays, departments, and government offices in terms of its implementation and effectiveness.

**SECTION 7. JOB DESCRIPTION OF POSITIONS AND FUNCTIONS**

**Development Management Officer IV (SOCC HEAD)**

1. Oversee the overall operation of the Special Office for Children's Concerns.
2. Represent SOCC in other Bodies during meetings and consultations which involve children's welfare.
3. Formulate and submit the annual operation plan, Local Development Investment Program (LDIP), and the State of Children's report of SOCC to LCPC for approval.
4. Document best practices for possible policy formulation and adoption of LCPC for the implementation of DCWC and other programs related to children.
5. Provide regular updates to LCPC regarding laws, policies, and programs for children.
6. Ensure the execution and implementation of plans as approved by LCPC.
7. Ensure that the Local Development Plan for Children is reviewed and assessed by LCPC annually.
8. Act as the official spokesperson of Davao City regarding issues involving children.
9. Consolidate and submit program reports on programs for children every quarter or as may be deemed necessary.
10. Coordinate with the national and local department heads and/or offices towards effective implementation of the ordinances and other related programs for, by, and with the children.
11. Ensure effective implementation of children's programs of the City by strengthening collaboration among BPCs in Barangay levels and other mechanisms.
12. Perform such other functions as may be directed by LCPC and/or the City Mayor.

**Development Management Officer III**

1. Collaborate with Non-Government Organizations, Faith-based Organizations, and other Civil Society Organizations for feedbacks and recommendations on maximizing resources for children's plans and programs.
2. Ensure the creation and management of the data bank on the situation and status of children in Davao City.
3. Monitor the performance of all implementing offices and services in line with DCCWC.
4. Shall be responsible for the codification of all laws relating to children.
5. Shall serve as custodian of all files, minutes, communications, and other papers relating to SOCC.
6. Assist in the execution and implementation of the plan as approved by LCPC.
7. Assist in ensuring that the Local Development Plan for Children is reviewed and approved by LCPC.
8. Assist in reviewing the City Development Plan, LDIP, and the Local State of Children's report of SOCC to LCPC for approval.

**Development Management Officer II (Coordinating, Monitoring and Evaluation Section)**

1. Conduct biannual review on programs and implementations including, but not limited to, the programs of SOCC and other offices in line with DCVCW.
2. Recommend to LCPC and other departments and offices for advocacy works and utilization of the research results.
3. Initiate consultations with CSOs, barangays, and other concerned agencies for feedbacks and assessments pertaining to protocols and standards on handling cases and issues involving children.
4. Prepare accomplishment reports in all activities undertaken by the Coordinating, Monitoring and Evaluation Section as well as other monitoring and evaluation reports to be submitted to the immediate supervisor for review and recommendation.
5. Perform other functions as may be directed from time to time by the immediate supervisor.

**Development Management Officer I (Coordinating, Monitoring and Evaluation Section)**

1. Assist in the identification, transportation, and interpretation of data essential to the execution of the operation plan, and other office development by way of team coaching approach and by data collation and tabulation.
2. Assist in monitoring and evaluating programs and policies in offices in line with children's concerns and in preparing the biannual review.
3. Assist in preparing accomplishment reports in all activities undertaken by SOCC and in monitoring and evaluating programs and policies in offices in line with children's concerns and in preparing the biannual review.
4. Follow up administrative requirements of the activities, projects and program advocacies of SOCC through the establishment of linkages and facilitation of documents to ensure the necessary support services are met.
5. Perform staff functions and other related duties that may, from time to time be assigned by the superiors.

**Development Management Officer II (Regulatory Section)**

1. Design and prepare proposals of mechanisms and appropriate intervention models attending to the social context of children specific problems and needs specifically in situations of discrimination, abuse and exploitation for the guidance of all children centered programs and services whether government or private establishments or agencies.
3. Submit consolidated reports, findings and feedbacks on changes and enhancement in policies, strategies and programs.
4. Perform such functions as may be directed from time to time by the immediate supervisor.

**SECTION 8. QUALIFICATION**

A person who will be appointed in the career service of the Special Office for Children's Concerns (SOCC) should meet the qualification standards as approved by the Civil Service Commission.

In determining the relevance of education and experience requirement, reference shall be made to the duties and responsibilities attached to the positions and the occupational groupings including experience in children's work and should be a child right's advocate.

Furthermore, selection of employees for appointment in this newly created division shall be open to all qualified men and women according to the principle of merit and fitness. There shall be no discrimination in the selection of employees on account of gender, civil status, disability, religion, ethnicity, or political affiliation.

**SECTION 9. BUDGET**

Sufficient funds shall be allocated for the creation of the Special Office for Children's Concerns (SOCC) under the City Mayor's Office and for the eight (8) positions under it.

**SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**

If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is held unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions heredof shall not be affected thereby.

**SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE**

This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after fifteen (15) days from the date of its complete publication in a newspaper of local circulation or in the absence thereof, the posting of this Ordinance in at least three (3) conspicuous places in the City of Davao.

ENACTED, on the 27th day of November, 2018, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
