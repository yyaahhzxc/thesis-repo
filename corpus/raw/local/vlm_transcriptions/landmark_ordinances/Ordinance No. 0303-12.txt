AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT CREATING THE PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND, SPECIFICALLY SECTION 8.2 THEREOF, TO BE READ AS FOLLOWS:

FROM: "A SEPARATE BOOK OF ACCOUNTS SHALL BE MAINTAINED BY THE CITY FOR THE PURPOSE"

Ord. No. 0303-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT CREATING THE PHILHEALTH
CAPITATION FUND, SPECIFICALLY SECTION 8.2 THEREOF, TO READ AS
FOLLOWS:
FROM: "A SEPARATE BOOK OF ACCOUNTS SHALL BE MAINTAINED
BY THE CITY FOR THE PURPOSE"
TO: "A SEPARATE DEPOSITARY ACCOUNT UNDER TRUST FUND
BOOK OF ACCOUNTS SHALL BE MAINTAINED BY THE CITY
FOR THE PURPOSE".
SECTION 2. AMENDMENT TO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT-
The Memorandum of Agreement for the Creation of the Philhealth Capitration
Fund specifically Section 8.2 thereof, is hereby amended to read as follows:
FROM: 8.2 A separate book of accounts shall be maintained by the
City for the purpose;
TO: 8.2 A separate depositary account under Trust Fund Book of
Accounts shall be maintained by the City for the purpose;
SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval;
ERACTED, June 5, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present.
