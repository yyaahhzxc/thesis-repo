Ord. No. 0396-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE CITY ARCHIVES AND RECORDS OFFICE, AS AMENDED".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of the Local Government Code of the Philippines provides that "every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare."

Section 17 thereof also provides that "local government units shall likewise exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective provision of the basic services and facilities enumerated herein."

SECTION 3. CREATION OF OFFICE - The City Archives and Records Office is hereby created under the direct supervision and control of the City Mayor's Office. It shall be headed by a City Government Department Head II to be assisted by a City Government Assistant Department Head II. The Office shall be composed of four (4) divisions, as follows:

a. Administrative Division;
b. Records Management Division;
c. Archives Collection & Access Division; and
d. Archives Preservation Division.

SECTION 4. THE CITY ARCHIVES AND RECORDS OFFICE

a. The City Government Department Head II shall oversee the operation of the Office and its employees in planning, formulating, and implementing a records management and archival program for the efficient creation, utilization, maintenance, retention, storage, preservation, conservation, protection, and disposal of all City public records. He/she shall perform other related tasks as may be necessary from time to time.

b. The City Government Assistant Department Head II shall assist the City Government Department Head II in overseeing the operation of the Office and its employees. He/she shall perform other related tasks as may be necessary from time to time.

c. The Administrative Division shall:

1. Take charge and provide administrative support functions for the smooth, efficient and effective implementation of the Office's mandate;

2. Disseminate and implement rules, laws, policies and other legal issuances pertaining to personnel, budgeting, accounting, and such similar areas affecting the Office, its employees and activities;

--- [Page 3] ---
3. Provide coordinative action with other offices, agencies and the general public; and

4. Do other related activities as may be necessary or required from time to time.

d. The Records Management Division shall:

1. Assist in planning, formulating and implementing a records management program and coordinate with City Departments/Offices for the efficient creation, utilization, maintenance, retention, storage, protection and disposal of all temporary City public records;

2. Do other related tasks as may be necessary or required from time to time.

e. The Archives Collection & Accession Division shall:

1. Undertake and provide technical assistance in the gathering, proper storage, protection and availability of all permanent City public records;

2. Do other related tasks as may be necessary or required from time to time.

f. The Archives Preservation Division shall:

1. Undertake conservation and restoration of all permanent City public records;

2. Render technical assistance, training, and repair/rehabilitation of damaged records;

3. Perform other related tasks as may be necessary or required from time to time.

SECTION 5. BUDGET ALLOCATION - The City Budget Officer shall annually allocate funds for the operation of the City Archives and Records Office which will be sourced from the General Fund;

SECTION 6. PERSONNEL COMPLEMENT - The Department shall be composed of a total of ninety-one (91) positions to support its manpower requirements, as follows:

(a) Seven (7) positions to be newly created, to wit:

| No. of Employees | Positions | Salary Grade |
|------------------|------------|-------------|
| 1                | City Government Department Head II | 26/1 |
| 1                | City Government Assistant Department Head II | 24/1 |
| 1                | Supervising Administrative Officer (Records Officer IV) | 22/1 |
| 1                | Administrative Officer V (Records Officer III) | 18/1 |
| 1                | Administrative Officer VI (Records Officer II) | 14/1 |
| 2                | Administrative Aide III (Utility Worker II) | 3/1 |

--- [Page 4] ---
(b) POSITIONS TO BE TRANSFERRED

Eighty four (84) positions from other City Departments and Offices shall be transferred to the newly-created office, as certified by the City Human Resource Management Officer, attached hereto as Annex "A."

Section 7. AUTOMATIC TRANSFER OF BUDGETARY ALLOCATION. All budgetary allocations pertaining to Personnel Services for the positions to be transferred to the City Archives and Records Office from the different City Departments and Offices as mentioned in Section 6(b) hereof shall be deemed automatically transferred to the said Office from the effectivity of this Ordinance.

Section 8. PERSONNEL TRANSITION. To effect the transfer of personnel from other Departments to the City Archives and Records Office (CARO), affected employees shall be given due and appropriate notice by the Local Chief Executive pursuant to Civil Service Rules and Regulations.

Section 9. REPEALING CLAUSE. All ordinances, resolution, orders, and decrees or parts thereof which are inconsistent with any provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

Section 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If, for any reason/s any part or provision of this Ordinance is held to be unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof that are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its posting in the bulletin board of the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, and at the entrance of City Hall and at least one other conspicuous place in the City of Davao;

ENACTED, February 6, 2018, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
