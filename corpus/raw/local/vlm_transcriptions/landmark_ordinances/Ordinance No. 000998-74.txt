**ORDINANCE NO. 598**  

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE ESTABLISHMENT, MAINTENANCE AND OPERATIONS OF BILLIARDS AND POOL HALL AND BOWLING ALLEYS IN THE CITY OF DENVER.  

**BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL, CITY OF DENVER, THAT:**  

**SECTION 1.** - No person shall conduct or operate a billiards or pool hall, or a bowling alley, where persons are permitted to play on a compensation or for hire basis, without first securing a permit from the City Mayor and paying the license fees for such purposes to the City Treasurer.  

**SECTION 2.** - No billiards or pool hall, or bowling alley shall be allowed to be maintained or operated within a radius of fifty (50) linear meters from any public building, school, public library, church or hospital. This provision as to location shall not apply to those already licensed and operating at the time of the approval of this ordinance nor will the opening of any public building or other premises prejudice any billiards or pool hall or bowling alley already then licensed and operating; Provided, however, that the noise from such billiards or pool hall or bowling alley established within fifty (50) linear meters shall not disturb those in the school, hospital, church and, if such noise causes such disturbance than such billiards or pool hall or bowling alley shall not operate during school hours when near a school, or at night when near a hospital or when there are religious services when near a church.  

**SECTION 3.** - No billiards or pool hall, or bowling alley shall be open to the public, and no billiards, pool, or bowling games shall be allowed therein, except during the hours from 8:00 o'clock A.M. to 11:00 o'clock midnight daily; Provided, however, That on Christmas Continued on Page 2-
