Ord. No. 06-11

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE GRANTING THE CITY MAYOR SARA Z. DUERTE LEGISLATIVE
AUTHORITY TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF
DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT
OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION
OF THE PROJECT OF THE CITY CIVIL REGISTRAR ENTITLED "SA PAARALAN
KO, PWDE NG MAGREHISTRO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Section 455 (b)(1)(vi) of the
Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the
purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its
inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City
Mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over
all projects, programs, services and activities of the city
government and in this connection shall:
Represent the city in all its business transactions and
sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations; and
such other documents upon authority of the Sanggunian
Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement [hereinafter referred to as "MOA"] to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Education [hereinafter referred to as "Department"] for the implementation of the project of the City Civil Registrar entitled, "Sa Paaralan Ko, Pwede ng Magrehistro";

SECTION 4. APPLICABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section
or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid,
the other sections or provisions shall not be affected thereby;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon its approval.

ENACTED, February 8, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of
the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

MARGARITO C. DELA CRUZ
Secretary to the Sanggunian Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head II)
