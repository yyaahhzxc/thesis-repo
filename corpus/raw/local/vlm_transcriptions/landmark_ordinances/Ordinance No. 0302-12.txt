Ord. No. 0302-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0220-11, SERIES OF 2011, SPECIFICALLY
SECTION 6 (C) THEREOF, TO READ AS FOLLOWS:
FROM: "A SEPARATE BOOK OF ACCOUNTS SHALL BE MAINTAINED
BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT"
TO: "A SEPARATE DEPOSITORY ACCOUNT UNDER TRUST FUND
BOOK OF ACCOUNTS SHALL BE MAINTAINED BY THE CITY
FOR THE PURPOSE",
SECTION 2. AMENDMENT TO THE ORDINANCE - Section 6 (c) of
Ordinance No. 0220-11 is hereby amended to read as follows:
FROM: "A SEPARATE BOOK OF ACCOUNTS SHALL BE MAINTAINED
BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT"
TO: "A SEPARATE DEPOSITORY ACCOUNT UNDER TRUST FUND
BOOK OF ACCOUNTS SHALL BE MAINTAINED BY THE CITY
FOR THE PURPOSE",
SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval;
ENACTED, June 5, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present.
