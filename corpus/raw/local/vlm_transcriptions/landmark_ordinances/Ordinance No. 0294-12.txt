Ord. 0294-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RESOLUTION NO. 0196E-12 WITH ITS CORRESPONDING ORDINANCE NO. 0287-12, BOTH SERIES OF 2012, BY CHANGING THE NAME OF THIS EVENT FROM "DAVAO CITY INTERNATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL" TO "SINENG PAMBansa NATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL" AND THE DATE FROM JUNE 25-29, 2012 TO JUNE 29- JULY 3, 2012".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS - Ordinance No. 0287-12 is hereby amended:

From:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE FILM DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL OF THE PHILIPPINES (FDCP) AND THE DAVAO CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY INC. [DCCCI] RELATIVE TO THE DAVAO CITY INTERNATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL FROM JUNE 25 TO JUNE 29, 2012"

To:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE FILM DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL OF THE PHILIPPINES (FDCP) AND THE DAVAO CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY INC. [DCCCI] RELATIVE TO THE "SINENG PAMBansa NATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL" FROM JUNE 29 TO JULY 3, 2012"

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, May 15, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

CERTIFIED CORRECT: 
CHARITO N. SANTOS
