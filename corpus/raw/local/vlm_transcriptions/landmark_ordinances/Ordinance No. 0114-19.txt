AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE SMOKE-FREE DECLARATION OF SUPPORT AND MEMBERSHIP TO THE SMOKER FREE CITY ASIA-PACIFIC NETWORK AND EXPRESSING ITS CONFORMITY TO THE SANGUNIANG PANLUNGSOOD ON THE CITY'S MEMBERSHIP TO THE SMOKE-FREE CITIES ASIA-PACIFIC NETWORK

Ord. No. 0114-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session, assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE SMOKE-FREE DECLARATION OF SUPPORT AND MEMBERSHIP TO THE SMOKE-FREE CITIES ASIA PACIFIC NETWORK."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared to be the policy of the State to take a proactive role in the preventive health care of its citizens. Towards this end, the City Council of Davao shall enact this Ordinance as an acknowledgment and reiteration of its commitment to a smoke-free Davao and ultimately a smoke-free Philippines.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Smoke-free Declaration of Support and Membership to the Smoke-free Cities Asia Pacific Network (SCAN) of the Southeast Asia Tobacco Control Alliance.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, November 19, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

Smoke-Free Declaration
Pledge of Commitment
Smoke-Free Cities/Municipalities/Provinces

We acknowledge that:
- All tobacco products are harmful; scientific evidence has clearly and unequivocally established that tobacco use and exposure to tobacco smoke cause serious diseases, disabilities, and premature deaths in our communities.
- Tobacco is an illegal product that kills two thirds of all its regular users with six million tobacco-caused deaths globally plus 600,000 deaths from exposure to secondhand smoke.
- Tobacco use is increasing worldwide (1.3 billion smokers) with 10% of the world's smokers (121 million) in the U.S. alone. The tobacco epidemic is an addiction largely taken up by children and young people and has a negative social and economic impact on our communities. The burden of smoking in our communities significantly increases household incomes and benefits the community and local economy.
- Addressing the tobacco epidemic must be an essential priority in the context of reducing the burden of non-communicable diseases.
- Smoking is an epidemic created and sustained by the tobacco industry; because the tobacco industry is the vector of this epidemic, there is a need to be alert to any efforts by this industry to undermine tobacco control efforts.
- Designating indoor smoking areas or smoking rooms do not provide adequate protection, confuse the public's perception of safety, and increase the economic burden of smoking.
- The federal and state legislatures from tobacco states corresponded to an obligation of governments to enact and enforce legislations to protect individuals against threats to their fundamental rights and freedoms.

We welcome the:
- Commitment from local governments to lead local action to respond to the tobacco epidemic and secure the general welfare, health, social, economic, and environmental benefits that come from reducing smoking prevalence by enacting and enforcing 100% smoke-free laws/regulations;
- Endorsement of the statement by the national government, Health Department, and other government agencies, and partners.

Our Local Government commits to:
- Actively support national government action and local level initiatives to reduce smoking prevalence and health inequalities in our communities and to communicate continuously and effectively the evidence on the harms of tobacco use and exposure to tobacco smoke and the positive impact of a smoke-free environment;
- Work with our partners and local communities to address the causes and hazards of tobacco use; take immediate steps to ban emerging tobacco products that will lure the youth into the habit of smoking;
- Act now and recognize that the only way to end the tobacco epidemic is through legislation that we have to strengthen or enact 100% smoke-free laws/regulations and ensure its sustained enforcement;
- Implement programs and strategies in order to reduce smoking in our communities;
- Protect our communities from the financial and human resources of the tobacco industry;
- Ensure that our officials, personnel, and work will not accept any partnerships, payments, services, shares in the profits, and donations, monetary or in kind, offered by the tobacco industry;
- Publicly declare our commitment to being smoke-free in our communities by joining as a guest member of the Smoke-Free Cities ASEAN Network (SCAN), the Alliance of Local Government Units working together to protect the lives of our constituents; and
- Actively participate in the SCAN and pledge ourselves to work together, share experiences, and support each other towards our vision of a 100% smoke-free ASEAN.
