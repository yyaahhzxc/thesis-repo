ORDINANCE NO. 0120-16
Series of 2016

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY TREASURER TO OPEN TRUST ACCOUNTS FOR I-REAP SUBPROJECT ENTITLED "CACAO PRODUCTION AND MARKETING OF DRY FERMENTED BEANS" UNDER THE PHILIPPINE RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECT (PRDP) OF THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE (DA)

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0120-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. - TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY TREASURER TO OPEN TRUST ACCOUNTS FOR I-REAP SUBPROJECT ENTITLED "CACAO PRODUCTION AND MARKETING OF DRY FERMENTED BEANS" UNDER THE PHILIPPINE RURAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECT (PRDP) OF THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE (DA);"

SECTION 2. - DECLARATION OF POLICY. Pursuant to Section 309 (b) of R.A 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, there shall be maintained in every provincial city, or municipal treasurer's fund which shall consist of private and public moneys, which have been offered into the possession of the local government treasurer or other government officer that is an agent or administrator, or which have been received as a guarantee for the fulfillment of some obligation. A trust fund shall only be used for the specific purpose for which it was created or for which it came into the possession of the local government unit;

SECTION 3. - AUTHORITY. The City Treasurer is hereby authorized to open trust accounts for I-REAP subproject entitled "CACAO PRODUCTION AND MARKETING OF DRY FERMENTED BEANS" under the Philippine Rural Development Project (PRDP) as required in Article IV, Section 4.01 of the Implementation Management Agreement (IMA)/Enterprise Development and the Department of Agriculture;

SECTION 4. - SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. - EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, December 12, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
