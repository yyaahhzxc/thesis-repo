AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE PRICING SCHEME FOR TIGATTO HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT AS ENDORSED BY THE CITY PRICING COMMITTEE, OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR, AS THE MOST APPROPRIATE AND RESPONSIVE TO THE BENEFICIARIES OF TIGATTO HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT, AS AMENDED

Ord. No. 0720-19  

Be it ordained by the Sanguinian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING SCHEME FOR TIGATTO HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT AS ENDORSED BY THE CITY PRICING COMMITTEE, OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR, AS THE MOST APPROPRIATE AND RESPONSIVE TO THE BENEFICIARIES OF TIGATTO HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT, AS AMENDED".  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 2 of Executive Order No. 45, Series of 2013, entitled "RECONSTITUTING THE MEMBERSHIP OF PRICING COMMITTEE FOR THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT PROGRAM (SIR) PROJECT AND URBAN LAND REFORM PROGRAM (ULRP) OF THE CITY", as amended by Executive Order No. 20, Series of 2014, states that the committee shall have the power to determine the fair market value/cost, interest and surcharges, payment scheme of residential and commercial lots under the housing program of the City of Davao, such as, but not limited to, Slum Improvement and Resettlement Program, Urban Land Reform Program, Relocation Sites, for sale or resale to the awardees/qualified buyers", as anched.  

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the approval of the Pricing Scheme for the Tigatto Homes Resettement Project.
