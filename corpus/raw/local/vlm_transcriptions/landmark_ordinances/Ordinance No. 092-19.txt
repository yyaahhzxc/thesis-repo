AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 192-04, SERIES OF 2004, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL BUSINESS OPERATIONS WITH FIXED PLACES OF BUSINESS OPERATIONS OR ADDRESSES TO REGISTER AND SECURE A BUSINESS REGISTRATION AND IDENTIFICATION PLATES AND YEARLY VALIDATION STICKERS BY INCREASING THE PENALTIES TO THE MAXIMUM

Ord. No. 092-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 192-04, SERIES OF 2004, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL BUSINESS OPERATIONS OR FIXED PLACES OF BUSINESS OPERATIONS OR ADDRESSES TO REGISTER AND SECURE A BUSINESS REGISTRATION AND IDENTIFICATION PLATES AND YEARLY VALIDATION STICKERS" BY INCREASING THE PENALTIES TO THE MAXIMUM".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT Section 8 of City Ordinance No. 192-04, Series of 2004, shall be amended as follows:

1. For the first offense P3,000.00
2. For the second offense P4,000.00
3. For the third offense P5,000.00 and revocation or cancellation of the business permit, and withdrawal of the registration and identification plate and validation sticker

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting it in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, on the 5th day of November 2019, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
