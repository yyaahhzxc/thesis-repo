ORDINANCE NO. 0703-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING A MANDATORY PUBLIC
SAFETY AND SECURITY ANNOUNCEMENT SYSTEM IN
ESTABLISHMENTS IN DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No. 0703-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "PUBLIC SAFETY AND SECURITY ANNOUNCEMENT ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. PURPOSE - The purpose of this Ordinance is to ensure public safety by raising awareness on disaster prevention protocols in order to take a more proactive stance on the matter.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover all establishments with a total floor area of at least 2,790 square meters which are:
1. Assembly establishments such as cinemas; conference rooms; exhibition halls; places of worship; passenger stations and terminals of air, surface, underground, and marine public transportation facilities; and recreational facilities such as gyms, bowling alleys, pool rooms, cockpits, and the like.
2. Educational institutions such as schools; child day care facilities; museums; theaters; libraries; and places of public gathering.
3. Medical establishments such as hospitals; laboratories; clinics; nursing homes; birth centers; and residential custodial care centers such as nurseries, home for the aged and the like.
4. Commercial establishments such as malls; supermarkets; department stores; shopping centers; hardware/construction supplies; showrooms; auction rooms; and the like.

SECTION 4. MANDATORY PUBLIC SAFETY AND SECURITY ANNOUNCEMENT - All establishments covered under this Ordinance shall announce in an audible and persuasive manner, through public address systems, speakers, or other audio-visual means, the emergency safety and security protocol of the establishment.

The safety and security protocol shall be announced three (3) times throughout the operational hours of the establishment.

SECTION 5. PENALTY CLAUSE - Owners of establishments who do not comply with the above Ordinance shall be penalized as such:
• 1st Offense - A fine of P3,000.00
• 2nd Offense - A fine of P5,000.00 and/or imprisonment of three months at the discretion of the court
• 3rd Offense - A fine of P5,000.00 and/or imprisonment of six months at the discretion of the court and revocation of business permit.

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance, local issuances, or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days upon approval, posting, and publication in a newspaper of local circulation.

ENACTED, on the 19th day of February, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
