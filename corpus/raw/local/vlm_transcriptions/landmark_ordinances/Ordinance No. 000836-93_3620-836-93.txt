REQUISITION NO. 2620

As a corollary to Resolution No. 3619 with its corresponding Ordinance No. 851, Series of 1993, Councillor Aristote V.H. Albany proposed the passage of a corollary resolution adopting the established rates imposed by the Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board (HLURB) for the processing fees and other charges imposed on applicants in accordance with the provisions and requirements under FP No. 357 and HP No. 220 for consideration and appropriate action of the Body.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councillor Aristote V.H. Albany, duly seconded by Councillors Paz C. Mata and Pilar C. Braga, it was RESOLVED, AS IN HEREIN RESOLVED, To approve an ordinance entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 856

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:
