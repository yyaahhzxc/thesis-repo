AN ORDINANCE REPEALING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0452-73, SERIES OF 1973, KNOWN AS "AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE LIGHTING FACILITIES AND THE PLACING OF PARTITIONS IN MASSAGE CENTERS OR CLINIC WITHIN THE PREMISES OR BARBERSHOPS AND/OR MASSAGE CENTERS OR CLINICS ESTABLISHED INDIVIDUALLY IN BARBERSHOPS OR MASSAGE CENTERS, OR BOTH, THEREBY PUTTING UP OF A BAR OR RESTAURANT INSIDE A BARBERSHOP WITH A MASSAGE CENTER OR CLINIC AND PROVIDING FOR PENALTIES"

--- [Page 5] ---

Ord. No. 074-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REPEALING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0452-73, SERIES OF 1973."

SECTION 2. REPEALING CLAUSE - City Ordinance No. 0452-73, Series of 1973, known as "AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE LIGHTING FACILITIES AND THE PLACING OF PARTITIONS IN MASSAGE CENTERS OR CLINIC WITHIN THE PREMISES OR BARBERSHOPS AND/OR MASSAGE CENTERS OR CLINICS ESTABLISHED INDEPENDENTLY OF BARBERSHOPS AND PROHIBITTING FURTHER THE PUTTING UP OF A BAR OR RESTAURANT INSIDE A BARBERSHOP WITH A MASSAGE CENTER OR CLINIC AND PROVIDING FOR PENALTIES," is hereby repealed, in view of the issuance of Presidential Decree No. 856.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, October 15, 2019, by unanimous vote of the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
