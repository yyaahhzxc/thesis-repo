ORDINANCE NO. 0361-17
Series of 2017

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE UNAUTHORIZED
PRODUCTION, MANUFACTURE, STORAGE, SALE,
DISTRIBUTION, POSSESSION OR USE OF UNIFORMS AND
OTHER ACCOUTREMENTS OF THE ARMY, THE PHILIPPINE
NATIONAL POLICE AND OTHER UNIFORMED LAW ENFORCEMENT
AGENCIES IN DAVAO CITY

Page 2 of 5
Ord. No. 0361-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The Davao City Government is mandated to protect life, liberty, and property and to ensure the safety, welfare, and interest of each individual residing and sojourning within its territorial jurisdiction through the prevention of the commission of terrorist and illegal activities.

R.A. No. 7160, An Act Providing for a Local Government Code of 1991, provides in Section 16 - General Welfare that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:

a. Accreditation certificate - a document issued by the Armed Forces of the Philippines, the Philippine National Police, or respective uniformed law enforcement agency as a requirement prior to the issuance of a Mayor's permit to persons or establishments wishing to engage in the business of production, manufacture, sale, or distribution of uniforms and other accoutrements within the City of Davao;

b. Authorization for purchase - a document issued by the Armed Forces of the Philippines, the Philippine National Police, or respective uniformed law enforcement agency to a prospective buyer, upon written application, fully citing thereof the purpose for which the uniforms and other accoutrements shall be used;

c. Authorization for use - a document issued by the Armed Forces of the Philippines, the Philippine National Police, or respective uniformed law enforcement agency to a prospective wearer, upon written application, fully citing thereof the purposes authorized under R.A. No. 493;

d. Uniforms and other accoutrements - This include authorized uniforms, badges, insignias, and other accoutrements of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, the Philippine National Police, and uniformed law enforcement agencies. This also covers imitations which have close semblance to the color, design and pattern of official uniforms and accoutrements;

e. Unauthorized use - This includes the use of uniforms and other accoutrements without the proper authorization for use by any individual or organization who are not members or part of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, the Philippine National Police, or any uniformed law enforcement agencies, or any deviation from the granted use in the authorization for use;

f. Other uniformed law enforcement agencies - This shall include members of the

1. Bureau of Fire Protection;
2. Bureau of Jail Management and Penology;
3. Bureau of Corrections;
4. National Bureau of Investigation;

--- [Page 3] ---
6. Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency;
7. Philippine Ports Authority;
8. Philippine Coast Guard;
9. Land Transportation Office;
10. Office of Transportation Security;
11. Bureau of Customs;
12. City Transportation and Traffic Management Office;
13. And other uniformed law enforcement agencies;

SECTION 3. ACCREDITATION CERTIFICATE - The establishment shall post their accreditation certificate in a public and conspicuous area within the establishment, and shall record all transactions involving uniforms and accoutrements by documenting the details of the authorization certificate and identification provided;

SECTION 4. AUTHORIZATION FOR PURCHASE - All accredited establishments shall require members of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, Philippine national Police, and other uniformed law enforcement agencies to present their official and valid Armed Forces of the Philippines, Philippine National Police or law enforcement agency identification card prior to any purchase;

Any person, other than members of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, Philippine National Police, or other uniformed law enforcement agencies, who shall purchase uniforms or accoutrements, shall be required to show the authorization for purchasers from the immediate superior and show a photocopy of the official and valid identification card of the principal buyer and person authorized to purchase on his behalf, prior to any transaction;

SECTION 5. AUTHORIZATION FOR USE - Any person, who shall use uniforms or accoutrement for purpose authorized under R.A. 4/3, is required to submit an Affidavit of Undertaking stating the purpose and duration of use thereof, to the local or regional head of office of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, Philippine National Police, or respective law enforcement agency for the issuance of an authorization for use;

SECTION 6. PROHIBITED ACTS - The following acts shall be prohibited and punishable under this Ordinance, to wit:

a. The purchase or use of uniforms or other accoutrements by individuals who are not members of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, Philippine National Police, or other uniformed law enforcement agencies, except when duly authorized;

b. The production, manufacture, storage, sale, distribution, or possession of uniforms or other accoutrements without the proper accreditation certificate issued by the concerned agency and the business or mayor's permit issued by the Business Bureau;

c. The failure to record, by any person or establishment, every transaction, including the details of the authorization for purchase issued by the concerned agency and proof of identity, involving the sale or distribution of the uniforms and other accoutrements;

d. The use of any uniform or accoutrement for purposes other than what was covered by the issued authorization for use;

**SECTION 7. PENALTY CAUSE -** The imposition of the following penalties for violations of this Ordinance shall be without prejudice to the prosecution of other criminal, civil and administrative actions:

**A. Individuals -**
- **1st Offense:** Subject to confiscation of the item, and an administrative fine of P 1,500;
- **2nd Offense:** Subject to confiscation of the item, a penalty of P 3,000.00 and imprisonment of not more than 6 months at the discretion of the court;
- **3rd Offense:** Subject to confiscation of the item, a penalty of P 5,000.00 and imprisonment of not more than 1 year at the discretion of the court;

**B. Establishments and Judicial Persons -** In case a violation is committed by an establishment or judicial person, it will result in immediate confiscation of items, revocation of its business license or permit, immediate closure of the establishment, including its offices, buildings, warehouses, stockrooms, or any other place where the uniforms and other accoutrements are being produced, manufactured, stored, sold, and/or distributed, and forfeiture at all paraphernalia used in the same, without prejudice to the imposition of administrative fines;

**C. The owner/proprietor, partners, managers and members of the board of directors of such establishment or judicial person shall be liable individually under Paragraph A;**

**SECTION 8. LIMITATION CLAUSE -** The penalties stated in paragraph A of Section 7 shall not apply to the violations covered by R.A. 493 and E.O. 297-2000;

**SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE -** Any ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

**SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If for any reason any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect;

**SECTION 11. EFFECTIVE CLAUSE -** This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days upon approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation in the City of Davao;

**ENACTED, December 12, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panglungsod (City Government Department Head II).**
