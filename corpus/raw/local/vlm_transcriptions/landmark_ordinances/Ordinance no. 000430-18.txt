AN ORDINANCE DECLARING DATU BAGO AS HERO OF DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No. 0430-18  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE DECLARING DATU BAGO AS HERO OF DAVAO CITY".  

SECTION 2. PARAMOUNT WARRIOR - Also called "Bawangin" by the local tribes), Datu Bago was of Kagan, Tausug and Maguindanawan ancestry who settled in Bagobo land (now known as Davao). He fought long and hard in keeping Davao Gulf free from foreign control as the whole area around the Gulf of Davao was under his control. In 1839, Datu Bago joined by the Bagobo and Kagan Tribes, struck his greatest blow against foreign invaders. Davao was the last unoccupied territory in the Philippines and Datu Bago heroically defended it against the Spaniards.  

SECTION 3. COMMEMORATION - A date shall be set for its annual commemoration to be determined by the Office of the City Mayor in coordination with the Davao Historical Society.  

SECTION 4. EDUCATION - All schools in the City of Davao, public and private, shall instill in its curriculum the life and time of Datu Bago's reign as a fitting tribute to a local hero.  

SECTION 5. CONSTRUCTION OF MONUMENT - In order for the people of the City of Davao, particularly the youth, to regard Datu Bago with respect and to remember and appreciate his heroism, a monument of Datu Bago shall be erected at a site most suitable to honor him.
