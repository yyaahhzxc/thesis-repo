ORDINANCE NO. 0190-09  
Series of 2009  
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING MOTORCADE  
WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF  
DAVAO CITY  
By it ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  
SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN  
ORDINANCE REGULATING MOTORCADE WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL  
JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Pursuant to Section 16 of the Local Government Code, it states that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate or incidental for its efficient and effective governance and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare."

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS: As used in the Ordinance, the following terms shall mean as follows:

a. Caravan - A group of vehicles traveling in a file to promote a certain advocacy;

b. Fiesta Celebration - Any jocund occasion to celebrate a religious festival, saint's day, holidays, commemoration of anniversary and other similar occasions;

c. Motorcade - A procession of cars or other vehicles, especially one forming an escort for somebody important or during a parade in connection with fiesta celebrations, commemoration of anniversary and other similar activities;

d. Public Congregation - A group of people using vehicles to promote advocacies and/or religious undertakings;

e. Applicant/s - A person/s, event organizer or representative of an organization, entity or corporation, having the capacity to represent such juridical entity, who applies for a permit to conduct a motorcade;

f. Police Escorts - Refers to police or military personnel using gasoline/diesel-fueled mode of transportation, whose services are rendered during motorcades in connection with fiesta celebrations, caravans, saint's day, holidays, commemoration of anniversary and other activities of similar nature for the purpose of maintaining orderliness;

g. Promotional Events - Refer to activities such as, but not limited to, advertising of products and services, movies, business undertakings, etc.;

h. Rush Hours
-between the hours of seven (7) to nine (9) in the morning and four (4) to eight (8) in the afternoon,
i. Foot Parade or Procession
-refers to the parade of persons on foot;

SECTION 4. PROHIBITED ACTS: In the conduct of motorcades held in connection with public gatherings, caravans and other promotional events, the following acts are prohibited:
a. Non-payment of permit fee to conduct a motorcade which must be secured from the City Mayor's Office;
b. Throwing of goods, candies, confetti and similar materials during the motorcade;
c. No foot parade shall be conducted simultaneously with motorcade except during funeral processions and other purely religious activities;

SECTION 5. LIMITATION: Vehicles participating in a motorcade held in connection with public congregation, caravans, and other promotional events, shall observe the following limitations:
a. There shall only be a maximum of ten (10) four-wheeled participating vehicles, not including the police escorts, which shall be properly marked by the organizers;
b. Motorcades shall not be conducted during rush hours;
c. The motorcade shall commence at Magaysay Park, up to a distance of four (4) kilometers therefrom, which shall be coordinated with the Traffic Management Center (TMC) to ensure undisrupted flow of traffic;
d. During the motorcade, the participants shall observe and follow the directional signal lights of the traffic;
e. The participants shall not make a U-turn but rather, they shall follow the city route;

SECTION 6. EXEMPTION: Motorcades held during the "Araw ng Dabaw", "Kadayawan sa Dabaw" celebrations, Davao Chinese New Year Festival, "Araw ng Distrito", "Araw ng Barangay" funeral processions and purely religious activities shall be exempted from the provisions of this Ordinance;

SECTION 7. PAYMENT OF FEES AND CHARGES - In cases where the holding of motorcycles in connection with public gatherings, caravans and other promotional events is granted, the corresponding fees in the amount of Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00) shall be paid;

SECTION 8. PENAL PROVISION: Applicant's violating this Ordinance shall be fined in the amount of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) Pesos and/or Imprisonment of not less than Six (6) months or both;

In case of corporations, its Manager or Persons Responsible for the violation of the Ordinance shall be fined in the amount of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) Pesos and/or Imprisonment of not less than Six (6) months or both;

SECTION 9. SEPARAPARITY CLAUSE: If a provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, other provisions hereof shall continue to be in full force or effect;

SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City;

ENACTED, March 17, 2009 by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
