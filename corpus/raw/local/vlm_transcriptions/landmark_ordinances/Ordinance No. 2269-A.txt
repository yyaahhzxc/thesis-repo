SECTION 1. - The Rules and Regulations Implementing the Davao City Investment Incentive Code of 1999 is hereby approved, to wit:

RULE I.

POLICY APPLICATION

Article 1. Title. - These Rules shall be known and cited as the Rules and Regulations Implementing the Davao City Investment In-
C. X. TIVE CODE of 1999.

Article 2. Purpose. - These Rules are promulgated to prescribe the procedure and guidelines for the implementation of the Davao City Investment Incentive Code of 1999 in order to facilitate compliance therewith.

Article 3. Declaration of Policy. - To accelerate the sound development of Davao City in accordance with the updated city development plan and the national development plan for the year 2000, it is hereby declared to be the policy of the City of Davao to encourage new investments and to promote and develop certain activities like vacuum, dehydrated and spray-dried fruitprocessing, canned specialty vegetables, etc.; tourism facilities like mid-range hotels, and restaurants; light manufacturing activities such as clothing but not limited to, port infrastructure and common bonded warehouses; miscellaneous light manufacturing activities such as garments, footwear, bulky housewares, kitchenware, etc.; property development such as the establishment of residential areas, industrial parks, and other preferred areas of investments as may be determined from time to time which will provide employment opportunities, raise the standard of living of the people of Davao City, and provide for an equitable distribution of wealth.

The City of Davao welcomes and encourages domestic and foreign capital to establish enterprises which would utilize substantial amount of labor, raw materials and natural resources of the city.

It is further declared to be the policy of the City of promote the establishment and operation of non-government organizations to serve as active partners in achieving local autonomy.

Article 4. Scope of Application. - These Rules shall apply to all persons, entities or enterprises, government and non-government organizations and instrumentalities to the extent provided in the Code.

RULE IV.
PREFERENCES/PRIORITY INVESTMENT AREAS/ACTIVITIES AND APPROPRIATE INCENTIVES AND SUPPORT MEASURES
Article 13. Preferred/Priority Investment Areas - Incentives will be provided to new, expansion and diversification projects in the following investment areas:
1. Agri-business Sector such as but not limited to:
a. Vacuum, dehydrated and spray-dried fruit processing;
b. Canned specialty vegetables;
c. Extruded snack food;
d. Cutflower production;
2. Tourism Facilities such as but not limited to:
a. Mid-range Business hotels;
b. Beach and mountain resorts;
c. Theme Parks, restaurants and marinas;
3. Transportation facilities such as but not limited to:
a. Port infrastructure;
b. Common bonded warehouses;
4. Miscellaneous light manufacturing activities such as but not limited to:
a. Garments;
b. Footwear;
c. Bulk housewares;
d. Toys;
5. Property Development such as but not limited to:
a. Private Industrial estate;
b. Commercial/Office Buildings;
c. Office parks;
d. Convention Center Development.

Article 15. Appropriate Incentives and Support Measures. The Board, upon the recommendation of the Center shall identify and recommend for the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod appropriate incentives and support measures which it shall be willing to extend to promote priority investment areas and activities, to target investors.
Article 16. Effectivity of Appropriate Incentives and Support Measures. The Center shall review the package of appropriate incentives and support measures and the investment areas or activity, every two years, and remove an investment area or activity from the list when it becomes clear that sufficient investments in the area or activity have been attained and where continued extension of incentives or support measures for the expansion of said areas or activity may place the interest of the City and the public in adverse position. The Center may also recommend to the Board to remove an investment area or activity from the list where such area or activity in the City cannot attract investors within reasonable time and cost or may result in unfavorable business climate.
Article 17. Removal/Deletion of a Preferred/Priority Investment Area. Upon the recommendation of the Center, the Board may remove an area or activity from the list of preferred/priority investments. Basis for the removal of a Preferred Investment Area are as follows:
a. Sufficient investments in the areas or activity have been attained;
b. The continued extension of incentives or support measures for the specific investment area is no longer to the interest of Davao City; and
c. The investment area or activity cannot attract investors within reasonable time and cost or may result in unfavorable business climate.

Article 27. Registration and Approval Procedures

A. Registration Procedure

1. Applications shall be submitted to the Center together with the required documents;
2. The applicant pays the non-refundable filing fee of One Thousand Pesos ($1,000.00) to form part of the operating expenses of the Center;
3. Upon all required documents are submitted, the project will be evaluated by the Center;
4. If found not qualified, the Center informs the applicant;
5. If found qualified, the Center forwards the application to the Chairman of the Board for approval;
6. Once approved by the Chairman, the Center informs the applicant in writing and delivers the Certificate of Registration;
7. The Center, then, informs all concerned agencies, entities of such approval for their information, guidance and appropriate action;

B. Evaluation Criteria

The following criteria will be used in the evaluation of applications for registration under the Code:

1. Capitalization for new enterprises, or investments for expansion or diversification must be as follows:
a. At least One Million Pesos ($1,000,000.00) but not more than Two Million Pesos ($2,000,000.00), in case of a small-scale enterprise;
b. At least Ten Million Pesos ($10,000,000.00) but not more than Forty Million Pesos ($40,000,000.00), in case of a medium-scale enterprise;
c. And more than Forty Million Pesos ($40,000,000.00) in case of a large-scale industry.

Article 24. Tax Incentives to Registered Enterprises.
In addition to the incentives provided by law and the national government as well, any enterprise registered under the Code shall be fully exempt from the payment of:
1. Building permit fees and other fees and charges;
2. Mayor's permit fees, business sales taxes, fee and charges within three (3) years from the start of commercial operations;
3. Exemption from the basis real property tax, accruing to the general fund, imposed under the Tax Code of Davao City for a period of two (2) years from the date of approval of registration by the Board.

Rule IX. Appropriations
Article 25. Initial Funding to Defray Expenses Necessary for Implementation of Provisions of This Code.
An initial funding to defray the expenses necessary for or incidental to the implementation of the provisions of this Code and of the Davao City Ordinance No. 2263-A, which shall be appropriated annually by Government Fund as contained, and hereby adopted, in the Memorandum of Agreement between the Davao City Government and the Philippine Exporters Association, shall be appropriated annually based on a budget presented by the Board, the necessary funding requirements which it deemed necessary for the continued implementation of the provisions of the Code, subject to the usual government accounting auditing rules and regulations.
