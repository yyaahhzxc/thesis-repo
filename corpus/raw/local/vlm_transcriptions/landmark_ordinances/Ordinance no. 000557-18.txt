Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0546-13 OR THE COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY PARTICULARLY ARTICLE IX, SECTION 3 - URBAN ECOLOGICAL ENHANCEMENT SUB-ZONE".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Article IX, Section 3 of City Ordinance No. 0546-13, Series of 2013, is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION 3. URBAN ECOLOGICAL ENHANCEMENT SUB-ZONE

It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to manage, protect, conserve, and implement greening programs in areas determined as prone to natural hazards and deemed critical in providing the ecological balance and enhancement in the city's urban zones.

The City shall promote environmental stability and biodiversity conservation, and enhance climate change mitigation and adaptation in the Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone such as the entire Shrine Hills area and other similar areas that may be declared as such in the future;

The City shall promote and establish Privately owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS) in the areas considered as Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone as may be recommended by the Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone Committee, and upon approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

The City encourages the consolidated effort of private landowners and all stakeholders in the Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone in jointly achieving an ecologically balanced Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone through massive greening programs and limited development with the barest minimum alteration of the top soil and the overall physical characteristics of the area.

3.1 Definition: This Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone (UEESZ) refers to areas intended for massive greening program for ecological enhancement in major urban zone as precautionary and proactive approach to climate change adaptation and part of risk reduction management program against flooding, landslides and inundation as the edges and slopes of the ridge or hill must be left as possible to landslides and landslides are hereinafter defined as any development in the area must strictly comply with the following provisions:

The Allowable Uses/Activities in Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone:

3.1.1 All uses/activities in Forest Zone (FZ)

3.1.2 Improvement and maintenance of all waterway easement

3.1.3 Reforestation Development Projects

3.1.4 Other greening programs which includes enhancement of biodiversity and those under POPOS or Privately-owned Public Open Spaces projects.

3.2 The following are the Compatible Uses in an UEESZ which is also identified by DENR-EMB as a Landslide Mitigation Sub-zone

3.2.1 Uses in Agricultural Non-village Zone

3.2.2 Eco-tourism projects Nature and eco-tourism attractions and activities shall be allowed with the lowest minimum alteration of the site. For example, this Ordinance refers to the use of forested or developing, visiting fishing ponds and relatively undisturbed areas, intended as a low impact and often small scale alternative to standard commercial (mass) tourism that is designed to be environmentally friendly.

3.2.2A Developing the Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone as an Eco-Tourism Park For revenue generation eco-tourism projects, the City Government will provide incentives to landowners for the establishment of an Urban Eco-Tourism Park within the sub-zone.

3.2.2B Construction area limitation for the Eco-Tourism Park The construction area limitation for the Eco-Tourism Park shall be up to a maximum of twenty-five percent (25%) of the total lot area so that the remaining seventy-five percent (75%) will be devoted to greening. Construction area determination and computations shall be based on the actual area occupied by foundations, poles and all roof-covered areas. Extreme slope protection measures that may require more than planting vegetation may not be counted as part of the construction area limitation provided that the techniques involved are duly presented and approved by the Urban Ecological Enhancement Committee as the best approach for the specific site/slope. Amenities and facilities that can be included and allowed inside an Eco-Tourism Park in the UEESZ Sub-zone shall be, but not limited to, the following:

a) Refreshment Parlor;

b) Gadgets/Toys Retail/Rental Shop;

c) Elevated Walkway/Traill;

d) Hanging bridges;

e) View Deck/Observatory spaces;

f) Rock Climbing Facilities;

g) Botanical Garden;

h) Endemic Animal sanctuary;

i) Function Hall;

j) Wellness Facility;

k) Chapel.

**3.4.3. Powers and Functions of the UEEES Committee**

The Committee shall have the following functions and responsibilities:

A. To review all Preliminary Approval and Location Clearance (PALC) granted to subdivision developers for subdivision projects within the UEF Sub-Zone and submit its recommendation to the Sangunian Panglaood.

B. To conduct comprehensive research and studies and adopt long-term measures and programs aimed at environmental promotion and development of the UEF Sub-Zone.

C. To identify and determine areas in the city appropriate for rezoning as Urban Ecological Enhancement Zones, or classified as Privately Owned Public Open Spaces (POOPS), and propose changes of land use to the City Council.

D. To grant Credit Certificate for Additional Allowable Floor Space applicable to Floor Area Ratio or Height Limits set by the City.

E. To review and deliberate on the pertinent provisions of this IRR and recommend revisions or amendments thereto.

F. To submit recommendations and resolutions to the Sangunian Panglaood.

G. To perform such other functions necessary and proper in carrying out the provisions of this amendment.

**3.4.4. Meetings**

The Committee shall meet at least once a month or as often as deemed necessary.

**3.4.5. Appointment of Permanent Representatives**

At the beginning of every year, a member of the Committee may name in writing his/her permanent authorized representative.

**3.4.6. Secretariat**

Employees/Officers occupying positions up to the 3rd Level from the City Administrator's Office, CPDD, CENRO, Business Bureau, and CEO shall be selected and appointed by the Committee Chairperson as members of the secretariat. The Secretariat shall accept and process applications under Sections 3.2 and 3.5 and in general perform secretarial functions for the UEEES Committee.

**3.4.7. Monitoring Team**

The Secretariat together with the Private Sector and NGO Representatives of the Committee shall serve as the Monitoring Team tasked to inspect, monitor, and evaluate compliance with or violations of this IRR, and apprise the Committee as to the state or condition of all Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zones in the city. The monitoring team shall conduct regular inspections without need of notice to see if compliance and limitations are being observed and followed.

3.6 LANDSLIDE PROTECTION AND SURFACE EROSION CONTROL MEASURES

3.6.1 Slope Maintenance: Given the high susceptibility to landslides and surface erosion of the hingles of the Shrine Hills, a Slope Protection Measures Program must be established by the City in partnership with the landowners. In all cases, slope maintenance responsibility belongs to the owner of the land.

3.6.2. Slope Maintenance Inspection: Regular inspection should be carried out to determine the conditions of a slope. These inspections can be subdivided into three levels/categories:

1. Routine inspection - by NGOs and POs based on some general visual guidelines;

2. Engineering inspection - by qualified geotechnical engineer and the City Engineer in accordance with engineering standards;

3. Regular monitoring process - by a qualified engineering firm/consultant with special expertise and may involve the use of some monitoring devices or analyses.

3.7 DESIGNATION OF PRIVATELY-OWNED PUBLIC OPEN SPACES (POPOS): are physical spaces which are privately-owned but legally required to be open to the public under land use law and arrangement with the City Government of Davao.

3.7.1 POPOS INCENTIVES: A private landowner devoting his/her lot as Privately-Owned Public Open Space (POPOS) shall be eligible to the following incentives, as may be recommended by the Committee and upon approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod:

3.7.2. ADDITIONAL FLOOR-SPACE CREDIT (FSC) ABOVE THE REGULAR HEIGHT LIMIT - POPOS complying landowners shall earn Floor Space Credit equivalent to a maximum of three (3x) times the size of their lot area devoted to POPOS:

1) To be applied as an additional floor space for future projects outside the UEF Sub-Zone. The FSC shall in no case be applied in any building project inside the UEF Sub Zone.

2) Transferrable to real-estate developers or anyone needing FSC for a consideration or otherwise.

A landowner who will apply or transfer the FSC shall be barred from altering the POPOS or withdrawing the POPOS from which an FSC was earned. Deliberate alteration of the POPOS in this case renders the landowner liable to refund to the City the computed value of FSC and other applicable penalties under Article XIV, Section 17 of the Amended Zoning Ordinance.
