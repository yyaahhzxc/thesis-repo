**ORDINANCE NO. 0179-03**  
**Series of 2003**

---

**AN ORDINANCE**  
**CREATING AND**  
**CONSTITUTING THE DAVAO CITY**  
**CHINATOWN DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL,**  
**DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS AND**  
**FOR OTHER PURPOSES**

---

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "The Davao City Chinatown Development Council Ordinance";

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0179-03

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Undeniably, the Chinese-
Filipino Community has been contributing considerably to the Philippine economy
and to its culture. As a people, the Chinese-Filipinos have, with their knack in
business, helped in the development of our city. Today, they have regarded this
City as their home, and the country as their land;
As the City Government is faced with enormous responsibilities, the
concerns of the Chinese-Filipino community in Davao City is most often
neglected. There is, thus, a need to enjoin all segments of the local community in
identifying and solving their own problems thereby encouraging the people affected
to participate in the development process;
Participatory democracy gives added meaning to people empowerment, the
corrosiveness of the current administration's effort to accelerate urban renewal by
localizing solutions of problems;
As a major city in the world, there is an equal need to develop
Chinatown in Davao City into a world-class destination for local and foreign tourists;
The City Government of Davao sees the need to create the DAVAO CITY
CHINATOWN DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL to assist in the active formulation
and implementation of policies in an area that has great commercial, historical,
economic and social significance to the City of Davao;
SECTION 3 TERRITORIAL BOUNDARIES. The boundaries covered by the
Davao City Chinatown shall include portions of Barangays 27-C and 30-C,
both of the City of Davao, particularly the areas encompassing the areas bounded
by Rizaln Magaysay Ave. (formerly Uyaguro), Leon Garcia Street, Tomas
Mendezave Ave., and Sta. Ann Ave.
SECTION 4. CREATION OF THE COUNCIL. There is hereby created a
multi-sectoral body to be known as the Davao City Chinatown Development
Council which shall actively participate in the formulation and implementation
of development plans for Chinatown;
SECTION 5. COMPOSITION OF MEMBERSHIP. The Davao City
Chinatown Development Council shall be governed by an Executive Committee
which shall be, composed of seventeen (17) members, with the City Mayor sitting
as Chairperson:
The said Chairperson shall appoint the other sixteen (16) members of the
said committee, eight (8) of whom shall come from the local government of the
City of Davao and the other eight (8) shall come from the private sector
representing the Davao Filipino-Chinese constituents of Davao City, upon favorable
recommendation of the Davao Filipino-Chinese Community, as follows:
Government Sector:
Chairperson, Committee on Tourism and
Beautification of the Sangguniang Panlungsod
City Planning Development Coordinator, City
Planning Department
City Tourism Officer, City Tourism Operations
Office

Head, City Environment and Natural Resources Office

The Chairperson shall sit in such capacity for the duration of his term as City Mayor of Davao and his appointees shall hold office with the Davao City Chinatown Development Council, co-terminus with the tenure of the appointing City Mayor.

SECTION 6. FUNCTIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES - The Davao City Chinatown Development Council shall be tasked with the following functions and responsibilities:

a. Plan, conceptualize, and draft the Davao City Chinatown Development Plan in the context of ensuring the sustainable development of Chinatown;
b. Identify the developed goals, objectives, programs, and projects of the Chinatown Development Plan, and conduct feasibility studies;
c. Provide overall direction, coordination, and supervision in the planning and implementation of the Davao City Chinatown Development Plan;
d. Enlist the support of the different divisions, departments, branches, and bureaus of the City of Davao, as well as the Non-Governmental Organizations and other components of the private sector involved with the development of Chinatown;
e. Define and promulgate its Implementing Rules and Regulations for the effective administration of its internal operations;
f. Conduct itself and its operations in a manner consistent with national and local laws, as well as the policies and programs of the City Government of Davao.

SECTION 6. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS - The implementing Rules and Regulations of this Ordinance shall be formulated by the Davao City Chinatown Development Council, through detailed consultation with the Government of the City of Davao, the National Commission on Local Government, and other relevant agencies. The IRR shall be formulated within ninety (90) days after the effectiveness of this Ordinance.

SECTION 7. OPERATING EXPENSES - The Davao City Chinatown Development Council shall be self-sustaining, its overhead expenditures covered by its own funds which may be sourced from private foundations, non-government organizations (NGOs), and other similar organizations.

SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other Ordinances and executive orders, or parts thereof inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions thereof, which are not affected, shall continue to remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
