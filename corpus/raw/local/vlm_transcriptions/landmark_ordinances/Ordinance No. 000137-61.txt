ORDINANCE NO. 137
AN ORDINANCE DECLARING JUNE 29TH OF EVERY YEAR, A SPECIAL PUBLIC HOLIDAY
IN THE CITY OF DAVAO, THIS DAY BEING ITS TOWN FIESTA.
Be it ordained by the City Council, City of Davao, that:
SECTION 1. - Pursuant to Republic Act No. 2264, otherwise known as the Autonomy Act, the City Council of Davao hereby declares June 29th of every year a Special Public Holiday in the City of Davao, in order that government officials and employees as well as those working in the different private firms in this City will be able to enjoy the celebration of their town fiesta, in honor of Saint Peter, which falls on that date.
SEC. 2. - This Ordinance shall take effect on June 29, 1962.
