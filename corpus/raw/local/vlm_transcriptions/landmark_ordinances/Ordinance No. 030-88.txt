RESOLUTION NO. 395  

The 3rd Indorsement, dated May 19, 1988, of the Committee on Ways and Means forwarding to the Sanggunian, this City, Resolution No. 191, series of 1988, requesting the Committee on Laws and Ordinances and Ways and Means to study the possibility of amending Section 4 of the "Anti-Malicious Ordinance", otherwise known as the "Anti-Malicious Ordinance", specifically Section 4 thereof, by increasing the penalty imposed thereon, was presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body.  

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councilor Shane L. Dolar, seconded by Councilor Liborio V. Lumain, jointly sponsored by Councilors Leonel M. Bautista, Jr., Leonard R. Avila, III, Liborio V. Lumain, Tomas Monteverde, Jr., Gerardo C. Nogales, Cesar G. Robillo, Jaime Q. Rodriguez, and Antonio H. Vergara, as amended by Councilor Victorio S. Advincula, it was RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To amend and revise Ordinance No. 42, series of 1959, otherwise known as "THE ANTI- MALICIOUS ORDINANCE".

**Section 4:** Any person, company, corporation, or establishment violating this Ordinance shall be punished as follows:

(a) For the first offense, by a fine of not less than P 200.00 but not more than P 500.00, or by imprisonment of not less than 7 days but not more than three months or both, such fine and imprisonment at the discretion of the court.

(b) For the second and subsequent offenses, by a fine of not less than P 500.00 but not more than P 1,000.00, or by imprisonment of not less than three months or both, such fine and imprisonment at the discretion of the court.

In case the violation has been committed by a juridical entity, the person in charge of the management and who has knowledge thereof, shall be responsible therefor.

**Section 5:** Any Ordinance or ordinances which are contrary or inconsistent with any provision of this Ordinance are deemed amended or repealed accordingly.

**Section 6:** This Ordinance shall take effect 30 days from publication thereof.
