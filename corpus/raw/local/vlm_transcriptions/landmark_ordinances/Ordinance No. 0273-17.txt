ORDINANCE NO. 0273-17
Series of 2017

AN ORDINANCE CONDONING THE IMPOSITION OF REAL PROPERTY TAX AND TRANSFER TAX, FEES AND SURCHARGES FOR REAL PROPERTIES DONATED TO AND ACCEPTED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0273-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE CONDENDING THE IMPOSITION OF REAL PROPERTY TAX AND TRANSFER TAX, FEES, AND SURCHARGES FOR REAL PROPERTIES DONATED TO AND ACCEPTED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It shall be the policy of the Local Government to act promptly on donations to the City of Davao and facilitate the transfer of titles in its name for all validly donated and accepted real properties;

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall govern the condonation of real property taxes, fees, charges and other impositions on real properties donated and accepted by the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY TO GRANT EXEMPTION PRIVILEGES - Local government units may, through ordinances duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs under such terms and conditions, as they may be necessary;

SECTION 5. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following shall be construed to mean as follows:

(a) Beneficial Use - refers to the right to use and enjoy property according to one's own liking or so as to derive a profit or benefit from it, including all that makes it desirable or habitable; as distinguished from a mere right of occupancy or possession;

(b) Condonation - is an act of liberality by the City Government of Davao, which, without receiving any equivalent, renounces the enforcement of the donor's obligation resulting in its partial or total extinguishment;

(c) Real Property - that which consist of land and all rights and profits arising from and annexed to land, of a permanent or immovable nature;

(d) Donation - refers to an act of liberality whereby a person disposes gratuitously of a thing or right in favor of another, who accepts it;

(e) Deed of Donation - refers to the donation of an immovable property made in a public instrument, executed, subscribed and sworn to by the donor conveying the ownership of the real property to the City Government of Davao, specifying therein the property donated and the value of the charges, which the donor must satisfy;

(f) Donor - is a private person or entity who owns a real property and transfers the ownership of such property to the City Government of Davao as an act of liberality and generosity;

(g) Donee - refers to the City Government of Davao which accepts the real property donated by a private person or entity;

--- [Page 3] ---
(h) Perfected/Accepted Donation - a donation that has been validly executed by the donor and accepted by the City Mayor upon grant of legislative authority by the Sangguniang Panlungsod;

(i) Appropriate Government Agencies - are the offices which shall be either from the Local or National Government enumerated as follows:

i. City Mayor's Office - is an office which shall cause the signing of the Deed of Donation upon legislative authority granted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod and the execution of the Deed of Acceptance on the donations made;

ii. City Assessor's Office - is an office which shall cause the dropping of the real property from the assessment roll for tax purposes;

iii. Registry of Deeds - is an office who shall cause the issuance of the Transfer Certificate Title to the City Government of Davao upon presenting the Deed of Donation and Deed of Acceptance and upon payment of fees and charges provided by law;

iv. City Legal Office - is an office under the Executive Department which shall cause the review of the public instrument and recommend the necessary action to be taken for protection and preservation of the real property;

v. Task Force on the Inventory of Real Properties of the City of Davao - is a Task Force group created pursuant to Executive Order No. 7, Series of 2016 who is tasked to preserve and protect the real properties of the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 6. PROTECTION AND PRESERVATION OF THE REAL PROPERTY - For purposes of protecting from legal infirmities and preserving the real property validly donated to the City in accordance with Article 749 in relation to Article 1356 of the New Civil Code, all liabilities for real property tax on property, actually and directly used by the City of Davao and/or under its administration, are hereby condoned. All fines, penalties and interests on such deficiency real property tax liabilities and transfer fees are also hereby condoned and relieved from payment thereof.

The City Treasurer shall issue the necessary Tax Clearances and Transfer Tax to facilitate the transfer in the name of the City of Davao. The Task Force on the Inventory of the Real Properties shall be responsible for the necessary actions to be taken for titling of the real property in the name of the City.

The Task Force on the Inventory of Real Properties of the City Government of Davao and/or the City Legal Office, City Assessor's Office and Secretariat of the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall submit the complete list of properties of donated properties which shall be subject to the condonation upon the effectiveness of this Ordinance.

--- [Page 4] ---
**SECTION 7. LOCAL TAX LIABILITY OF THE DONOR:**
The real property tax liabilities and transfer tax liabilities of donors of real properties validly donated to and accepted by the City Government of Davao are herein contained, provided that such property has been used for the benefit of the public and/or under the administration of the City Government of Davao even if the title remains with the donor's name.

**SECTION 8. DEED OF DONATIONS PERFECTED AFTER THE EFFECTIVITY OF THIS ORDINANCE:**
Any Deed of Donation in favor of the City Government of Davao after the effectivity of this Ordinance shall strictly conform to the requirements set forth in Ordinance No. 0189-14, Series of 2014 and the Local Government Code requiring the donors to pay Real Property Taxes and Transfer Taxes of the donated properties and submit the necessary Real Property Tax Clearance before acceptance be made by the City Government of Davao;

**SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:**
If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other sections or provisions shall be affected thereby;

**SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY:**
This Ordinance shall take effect after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao;

**ENACTED, September 5, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II)**
