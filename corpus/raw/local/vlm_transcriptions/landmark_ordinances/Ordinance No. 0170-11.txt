**ORDINANCE NO. 0170-11**
**Series of 2011**

**AN ORDINANCE FOR THE MANDATORY SUPERVISED DAILY TOOTHBRUSH DRILL FOR CHILDREN UNDER THE EARLY CHILDHOOD CARE AND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM OF DAVAO CITY**

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0170-11

Article I
General Provision

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as the "TOOTHRUSH DRILL ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. It is hereby declared the policy of the City to:

a. Ensure the protection of public health of our children;
b. Reduce, if not eliminate, the prevalence of dental caries and periodontal diseases among the general population;
c. Mandate day care centers to adopt a program that will help children practice the proper way of tooth brushing;
d. Promote awareness on the importance of daily tooth brushing and health consciousness among day care children.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. This Ordinance is enacted to mandate all government-run day care centers in Davao City to conduct a supervised daily toothbrush drill for children under the Early Childhood Care and Development Program of the City.

SECTION 4. DECLARATION OF ORAL HEALTH WEEK. The last week of June is hereby declared as the Oral Health Week, which aims to promote oral hygiene among children in all schools and day care centers;

Article II
Scope

SECTION 5. SCOPE. This Ordinance shall apply to all government-run day care centers, under the Early Childhood Care and Development Program of the City of Davao;

Article III
Definition of Terms

SECTION 6. DEFINITION OF TERMS. The words and phrases used in this Ordinance shall mean as follows;

a. Dental Carries - also known as tooth decay or a cavity, is a disease where bacterial processes damage hard tooth structure;

b. Early Childhood Care and Development Program - is a program of the City Government in compliance with R.A. 8980 covering all students enrolled in home based and center based City Government Run Day Care Centers. It refers to the full range of health, nutrition, early education and social services programs that provide for the basic holistic needs of young children from birth to age six (6) to promote their optimum growth and development;

c. Oral Health - is a state of being free from chronic mouth and facial pain, oral and throat cancer, oral sores, birth defects such as periodontal (gum) disease, tooth decay, tooth loss and other diseases and disorders that affect the oral cavity;

d. Periodontal disease - a chronic bacterial infection that affects the gums and bone supporting the teeth;

e. Toothbrush Drill - covers daily supervised tooth brushing among children attending government run day care centers.

Article IV
Monitoring/Enforcement

The City Government of Davao, in coordination with the City Health Office through the Dental Division, shall closely monitor all the activities related to the implementation of this Ordinance;

Article V
Miscellaneous Provision

SECTION 1. REPEALING CLAUSE. Any existing Ordinance or any provision thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance shall be repealed;

SECTION 2. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any part or provision of this Ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, the other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect within thirty (30) days from its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City;
