ORDINANCE NO. 072-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 166-81, SERIES OF 1981, KNOWN AS "AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE SALE OF LPG IN DAVAO CITY, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES"

--- [Page 6] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 072-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 166-81, SERIES OF 1981, KNOWN AS 'AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE SALE OF LPG IN DAVAO CITY, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - The amendment shall be as follows:

Section 1. All sellers and dealers of Liquid Petroleum Gas (LPG) in Davao City contained in cylinders of all weight are required to indicate in the cylinder the weight of the LPG contained therein and the weight of the cylinder itself.

Section 2. All LPG sellers and dealers in Davao City are required to have a weighing scale in their places of business, daily inspected and scaled twice a year by the City Treasurer's Office, before they can be issued any license or business permit to do business in Davao City.

Section 3. Any LPG sold which is contained in cylinders must be weighed in the presence of the customers before any sale is made. This shall also cover those sales made through delivery.

Section 4. Any person or entity violating this Ordinance shall suffer the following penalties:
a) First Offense: a fine of P5,000.00 and/or imprisonment of not less than three (3) months, or both, at the discretion of the Court.
b) Second Offense: a fine of P10,000.00 and/or imprisonment of not less than six (6) months, or both, at the discretion of the Court.
c) Third Offense: a fine of P20,000.00 and/or imprisonment of not less than one (1) year, or both, at the discretion of the Court.

Violation under this Ordinance shall be a ground for the cancellation of the license and business permit to sell LPG in Davao City.

In case of corporation, partnership, and association, the officers, managers, or partners responsible therefor shall be held liable.

Section 5. The Business Bureau and the City Treasurer's Office shall ensure the implementation of this Ordinance and when warranted, investigate and cause the institution of criminal and administrative legal actions against persons and entities found violating this Ordinance.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain valid and subsisting.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.
