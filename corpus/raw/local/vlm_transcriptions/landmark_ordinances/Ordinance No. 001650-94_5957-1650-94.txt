RESOLUTION No. 597  
A proposed ordinance regulating the operation of small scale fishing and commercial fishing within Davao City water and for other purposes, for which the Committee on Rules and Privileges recommended that it be treated by the Body as a whole was presented for consideration and discussion at the regular session of the Body by Councillor Bonifacio E. Militar, Chairman of the Committee on Rules and Privileges.  
Councillor Rene V. Galope proposed an amendment to the effect that Barangay Officials whose Barangay are situated along the shore lines of Davao City should also be one of those to be deputized by the Mayor to make arrest.  
The proponent accepted his proposals and therefore proposed for the addition of another paragraph which will be denominated as Barangay Z, under Section 7, thereof.

The following resolution was passed:

WHEREAS: There is an urgent need to improve the standard of living in the rural fishing communities of the City of Davao and to protect the City's fishery resources against the heavy exploitation of fish and aquatic resources within the entire waters of the City of Davao, including the commercial trawl and purse seine in marine water areas within a distance of 7 kilometers from the shoreline of the City of Davao in order to regulate the operation of fishing boats within a wide area within which to operate fishing boats with a gross weight of 5 tons or less and to increase their catch per unit effort as prescribed under Fisheries Administrative Order 156, Series of 1986 implementing LOI No. 132;

WHEREAS, Section 149 (a) of RA 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that municipalities shall have the exclusive authority to grant licenses for the use of fishing rods and lines in the municipal waters and further, section 149 (b) empowers the Sangguniang Bayan to issue licenses for the operation of fishing vessels of 3 tons weight of less;

WHEREAS, the operation of commercial trawl and purse seine is prohibited in marine water areas within a distance of 7 kilometers from the shoreline of the city;

WHEREAS, the present issue license for commercial fishing boat for fishing operations within 15 kilometers jurisdiction in municipal water is lodged with the Local Government Unit to 5 tons gross weight or less;

WHEREAS, municipal water as defined in Section 131 of RA 7160 includes marine waters included between two lines drawn perpendicularly to the general coastline from points where boundary lines of the municipality meet the sea and a third line parallel with the general coastline and 15 kilometers from it.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, AND IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED FOR purposes of implementing the provision of Section 131, Section 149 (a) and Section 149 (b) of RA 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 and the provisions of LOI No. 132:

To approve an Ordinance entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 1650

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE OPERATION OF SMALL SCALE FISHING AND COMMERCIAL FISHING WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY WATERS AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

1) Definition of Terms: For purposes of this Ordinance the following terms shall mean:

a) Commercial trawl - a fishing gear operated by a fishing boat of more than 3 gross tons consisting of nets made in the form of a conical bag with the mouth kept open by various devices (as other boards/dors, wooden or iron beams, two boats and extra long ropes) and the entire gear towed, trailed or trawled, to capture fish or fishery/ aquatic produces.

b) Commercial purse seine - a fishing gear operated by a fishing boat of more than 3 gross tons consisting of a net with the bunt or landing piece located in any side of the net, and the whole net provided with a pursing device which consist of a series of purse rings attached to the footrope by straps or ring bridles with a pursing line rove thru the ring that closes the bottom of the seine when pulled thereby forming a trap or purse, the net of which is hauled by means of a power block.

c) Ring net - Has a regular pursing device and are used in small and medium fishing boats.

SEC. 2. Range of Prohibition. - The operation of commercial trawl and purse seine is prohibited in marine water areas within a distance of seven (7) kilometers from the shoreline; Provided, further, that in coastal areas / fathoms deep or more which are not reached by sustenance fishermen, by the operation of commercial trawl and purse seine may be allowed by the Secretary of Agriculture and Food upon the recommendation of the Director of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources, only after such areas have been defined and designated as such by the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources on a yearly or seasonal basis;

SEC. 3. Persons authorized to enforce this ordinance.
The following are authorized to enforce the provisions of this ordinance after having been deputized by the City Mayor of Davao City; to wit:

a) Philippine National Police (PNP)
b) Philippine Coast Guard (PCG)
c) Deputy Fish Warden
d) City Agriculturist Office
e) Barangay Officials whose barangays are situated along the shorelines

SEC. 4. Under no circumstances that fishing boats regardless of sizes or tonnages be allowed to operate as a public utility or to carry commercial cargoes and/or passengers other than those for its exclusive use/consumption and/or crew, except on emergency cases or as authorized by the Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA), or other lawful orders from other Government agencies.

SEC. 5. Fishery Rental or Fees. The City of Davao in the exercise of its authority to grant exclusive fishery right and license to individual fishing gears with the use of 12 knots or more in the City waters may levy or fix rental or fees or licenses thereof in accordance with the provisions of Section 1, Article 19, Ordinance No. 230, Series of 1991 as amended, to wit:
