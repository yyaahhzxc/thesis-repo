Ord. No. 0223-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 089-22, SERIES OF 2022, AND AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGAL AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, A MUTUAL AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION INCORPORATED-DAVAO CHAPTER RELATIVE TO THEIR PROGRAMS AND SERVICES CATERING THE MENTAL HEALTH OF CHILDREN AND WOMEN IN DAVAO CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY -
(1) Sections 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states:
Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
xxx xxx xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx xxx xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."
(2) Section 34 of RA No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, recognizes local government units in promoting the establishment and operation of people's and non-government organizations as active partners in the pursuit of local autonomy;
and
(3) Section 35 of RA No. 7160, authorizes local government units to enter into joint ventures and such other cooperative arrangement with people's and non-government organizations to engage in the delivery of certain basic services, capacity-building and livelihood projects, and to develop local enterprises designed to improve agriculture, promote diversified agriculture, spur rural industrialization, promote ecological balance, and enhance the economic and social well-being of the people.

**Ord. No. 0223-23**

**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY -** The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Philippine Mental Health Association Incorporated-Davao City Chapter relative to their programs and services catering the mental health of Women and Children in Davao City.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY -** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED, May 23, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all Members of the Sanggunian.**
