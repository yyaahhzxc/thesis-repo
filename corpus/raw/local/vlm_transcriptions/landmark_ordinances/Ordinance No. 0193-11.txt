**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 514, SERIES OF 1955, RECONSTITUTING THE MEMBERSHIP OF THE DAVAO CITY RECREATION CENTER (DCRC) BOARD OF TRUSTEES, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS**

Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0193-11

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 514, SERIES OF 1985, REDEFINING THE MEMBERSHIP OF THE DAVAO CITY RECREATION CENTER (DCRC) BOARD OF TRUSTEES, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 455 (b) (1) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the City Government."

SECTION 3. AMENDMENTS. Section 3 of Ordinance No. 514, Series of 1985, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"SECTION 3. ADMINISTRATION AND MANAGEMENT. The Center shall be managed by a Board of Trustees designated by the City Mayor whose term of office shall be at the pleasure of the designating official".

The following shall compose the Center's Board of Trustees:

1. The City Mayor or her duly authorized representative
2. Manager, City Economic Enterprise
3. City Legal Officer
4. Chairperson, SP Committee on Economic Enterprise and Privatization
5. Chairperson, SP Committee on Youth Development and Sports
6. City Treasurer or his duly authorized representative
7. City Engineer or his duly authorized representative
8. NGO Representative

A. Powers and Functions - The Board shall be vested with the following powers, functions and responsibilities;
i. To adopt requisite policy guidelines and standards, rules and regulations in the operation, management, administration and maintenance of the Center;

--- [Page 3] ---

ii. To recommend to the City Mayor and provide for the collection of the prevailing rentals, charges and fees for the use of similar recreational facilities, subject to the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod and the City Mayor.

iii. To recommend to the Sangguniang Panlungsod through the City Mayor, the appropriate measures for the development and improvement of the Center, its budget and plantilla, the Center's manager and his support staff, and define their respective duties and responsibilities.

iv. To undertake and provide for the repair, construction, maintenance, improvement and/or expansion of the facilities of the Center, in order that the center can more effectively render and deliver the public services for which it has been constructed;

v. To program and disburse any funds or income resulting from its operations;

vi. To organize and form its Technical and Secretariat Support Staff, enlist the assistance of national agencies, Non-Government Organizations (NGOs) other City Offices and individuals, whenever necessary;

vii. To determine the honorarium of the Members of the Board of Trustees, the amount of which is subject to the availability of funds, provided that the same shall not exceed fifty percent (50%) of the salary of the officers;

viii. To exercise such other functions and formulate regulating measures as may be necessary or incidental to the effective administration, maintenance and operation of the Center;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other section or provision shall be affected thereby;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED. October 25, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
