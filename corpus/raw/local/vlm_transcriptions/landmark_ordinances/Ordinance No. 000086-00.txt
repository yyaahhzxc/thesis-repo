ORDINANCE NO. 086
Series of 2000

AN ORDINANCE ENACTING THE RED TIDE
MONITORING AND QUARANTINE ORDINANCE OF
DAVAO CITY

Re it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod
in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE RED TIDE MONITORING AND QUARANTINE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The periodic presence of red tide causing organisms called dinoflagellates in the territorial waters of Davao City and its neighboring provinces has caused death to red tide poisoning. In line with the policy of the City Government of Davao to ensure the health and safety of the consuming public, it is necessary that regular monitoring and quarantine of shellfish products be provided. Shipment of shellfish products coming from the territorial waters of Davao City, especially those coming from outside the territory of the City, shall be closely monitored and quarantined at all times.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following terms are defined as follows:

(a.) "Red Tide" - as defined by the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources:
1.) "is the name given to the phenomenon of red discoloration of marine and estuarine waters caused by a bloom of single-celled plants. The color is usually lighter than red blood in orange or brown in daylight and is often visible as phosphorescence at night".
2.) "seawater discolored by proliferation of plankton that kills large number of fish and other marine life. The proliferation is sometimes stimulated by the addition of nutrients".

(b.) Shellfish or shellfish products - refer to both univalves and bivalves such as mussels and oysters prone to absorbing red tide toxin.

(c.) Seeds of Spats - refer to juvenile shellfish used for seeding or propagation of brood stock.

(d.) Person - shall be construed to mean any individual, contractor, agent, owner, administrator, manager, representing a firm or establishment, institution or association, either partnership or corporation.

(e.) City Red Tide Task Force (CRTTF) - refers to an inter-agency task force composed of the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), as lead agency, with the City Health Office (CHO), the City Agriculturist's Office (CAO), the Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG), the Department of Science and Technology (DOST), as members, that closely monitors red tide toxicity levels within all territorial waters of Davao City, in addition to the National Red Tide Task Force (NRTTF) and the Regional Inter-Agency Committee on Environmental Health (RICEAH).

(f.) Auxiliary Invoice - refers to a supplemental document accompanying the shipment of shellfish and/or shellfish products, stating therein the kind, quantity, and value of the product sold, the quantity and value sold, the source thereof, the fee paid, and the recipient of the corresponding official receipt issued from the point of origin and its final destination.

(g.) Certificate of Health - refers to a document issued by the City or Municipal Health Officer from the point of origin allowing the taking of shellfish and/or shellfish products from the fishing grounds.

SECTION 4. ISSUANCE OF AUXILIARY INVOICE - The issuance of auxiliary invoice for shellfish and/or shellfish products shall be as follows:

(a.) Only auxiliary invoice which conform with all conditions provided for in Section 3 (f) of this Ordinance and issued from the source or point of origin shall be honored.

(b.) No auxiliary invoice shall be honored for shellfish and/or shellfish products taken from areas outside of Davao City that are placed under ban by the National Red Tide Task Force (NRTTF).

SECTION 5. PROHIBITIONS - The following prohibitions shall be enforced within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City:

(a.) No person, natural or juridical, shall sell, ship or transport to or outside Davao City any shellfish and/or shellfish products intended for human consumption, taken from the waters placed under ban by the National Red Tide Task Force (NRTTF) and the City Red Tide Task Force (CRTTF).

(b) No person, natural or juridical, shall sell, ship
or transport to or outside Davao City, any
shellfish seeds of spats from any waters within or
outside of Davao City, with red tide history, for
seeding or propagation purposes.

SECTION 6. CONFISCACTION AND DISPOSAL OF CONFISCATED
SHELLFISH OR SHELLFISH PRODUCTS. - Any shellfish or shellfish
products coming from sources or waters under ban by the
National Red Tide Task Force (NRTTF) or by the City Red
Tide Task Force (CRTTF), found have entered Davao City shall be
confiscated and disposed of by the Bureau of
Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR).

SECTION 7. PENAL PROVISION. - Any person found
violating Sections 4 and 5 of this Ordinance shall be
penalized as follows:

a) First Offense - a fine of not less than five
hundred pesos ($500.00) but not more than two
thousand pesos ($2,000.00) or an imprisonment of
not less than seven (7) days but not more than one
(1) month, or both, at the discretion of the
court;

b) Second Offense - a fine of not less than two
thousand pesos ($2,000.00) but not more than five
thousand pesos ($5,000.00) or an imprisonment of
not less than one (1) month but not more than
three (3) months, or both, at the discretion of
the court and revocation of the person's business
permit;

SECTION 8. SEPARATIVITY CLAUSE. - Any Court
declaration that a provision of this Ordinance is unlawful or
unconstitutional, shall not affect all other provisions
thereof which shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE. - Any provision of other
ordinances, rules and regulations inconsistent herewith, are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take
effect upon its publication in a newspaper of general
circulation.
