**RESOLUTION NO. 1193**

**A Proposed Resolution for the banning of distribution of Bandera Magasinas presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body by Councilor C. Domingirang, Chairman, was referred to the Censorship of Moviehouses/Clubs and Other Amusement Places and shows.**

Councilor Danilo C. Domingirang informed the members of the Body that pursuant to the legal opinion of the City Legal Officer in his 2nd Indorsement, dated September 17, 1991, stated among others that under Batas Pampana No. 357, otherwise known as the Local Government Code, Chapter 4, Title 1, Section 1, it provides that:

"(4) The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall prohibit the printing, circulation, sale, distribution or exhibition of immoral and obscene pictures, films, articles, books or any other kind of pornographic publications!"

--- [Page 2] ---
**Resolution No. 1192, Ordinance**

**2L, Series of 1991, cont'd:**

In view thereof, Councillor Dayanghirang proposed for the passage of an Ordinance banning the distribution of Bandera magazine in Davao City based on the legal opinion of the City Legal Officer as afore- sighted.

Councillor Gerardo G. Nogales amended to include all other papers, magazines, or printed materials displaying obscene pictures, the amendment was accepted by the proponent.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councillor Danilo D. Dayanghirang, jointly seconded by Councillors Antonio R. Vergara and Antonio V. Vergara, and co-sponsored by Councillors Antonio V. Vergara and Agosto T. Huertas, it was

RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an Ordinance entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 212

AN ORDINANCE BANNING THE DISTRIBUTION OF BANDERA MAGAZINE AND OTHER PAPERS, MAGAZINE, AND ALL PRINTED MATERIALS DISPLAYING OBSCENE PICTURES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.

Be it Ordered that the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

SEC. 1. It is hereby banned the distribution or exhibition of Bandera magazine and other papers, printing materials, magazines, articles, books and any other kind of pornographic publication in the City of Davao;

SEC. 2. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED: September 30, 1991.

CARRIED, By a majority of nineteen (19) affirmative votes. (Note: Councillors Almendra, Banzon, Bonjuyan, Delor, Halayman, Navarro, Jr. were out when the votes were taken.)
