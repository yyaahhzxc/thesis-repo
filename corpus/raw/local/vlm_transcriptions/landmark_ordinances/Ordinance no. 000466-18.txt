AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE OPENING OF ROAD LOTS 1-4 AND 10-B-17-B-2-D-2, FORMING PART OF H. TORRES EXTENSION ST., BARANGAY 20-B, OBRERO, THIS CITY, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE CITY TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT OFFICE (CTITRO) AND THE CITY LEGAL OFFICE

Ord.No. 0466-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE OPENING OF ROAD LOTS 14 AND 15 B, 17 AND 20-B, OBERRO, THIS CITY, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE CITY TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT OFFICE (CTTMO) AND THE CITY LEGAL OFFICE".

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVE - This Ordinance aims to decongest the traffic situation along J.P. Laurel Avenue, this City. It also aims to maintain the opening of those road lots for public safety and welfare.

SECTION 3. LEGAL BASES - Pursuant to Section 21 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, it provides:

Section 21. Closure and Opening of Roads - (a) A local government unit may, pursuant to an Ordinance, permanently or temporarily close or open any local road, alley, park, or square falling within its jurisdiction; Provided, however, that in case of permanent closure, such Ordinance must be approved by at least two-thirds (2/3's) of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod when necessary, an adequate substitute for the public facility that is subject to closure is provided.

and paragraphs a and b of Section 186 of the Comprehensive Transport and Traffic Code of Davao City, it states:

Section 186. Access Restriction on Some Roads.

(a) It shall be unlawful for any person, natural or juridical, to close, obstruct, prevent, or otherwise refuse to the public or vehicle traffic the use of free access to any subdivision or community street owned by the government and within the jurisdiction of the City Government of Davao, xxx.

(b) Private subdivision roads and/or private roads not turned over to the City may be opened to the public under the following conditions:

(i) The road will strengthen the road network and provide traffic relief to nearby roads that are congested as may be determined by the CTTMO;

(ii) The Sangguniang Panlungsod has approved its opening, under such terms and conditions that will minimize or obviate any disturbances, pollution, or inconvenience to the residents in the area.
