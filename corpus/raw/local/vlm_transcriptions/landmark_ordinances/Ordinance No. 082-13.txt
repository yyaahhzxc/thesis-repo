AN ORDINANCE RENAMING MAGSAYSAY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL LOCATED AT DALIAO, TORIL DISTRICT, THIS CITY, TO RAMON F. MAGSAYSAY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
