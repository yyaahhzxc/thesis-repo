RESOLUTION NO. 5869
A proposed resolution/ordinance regulating the operation on business establishments selling liquors, coconut wine and other nature wine and other alcoholic beverages in the city of Davao, was presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body by Councilor Bonifacio E. Militar, Chairman, Committee on Rules and Privileges.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councilor Bonifacio E. Militar, duly seconded by Councilor Frances A. Robillo, it was RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an Ordinance, entitled: ORDINANCE NO. 1527
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE OPERATION ON BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS SELLING LIQUORS, COCONUT WINE AND OTHER NATURE WINE AND OTHER ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2

Resolution No. 5669, Ordinance
No. 10/74, series of 1994, cont'd.

Be it Ordered by the Samggunjang Paalungeod, City of Davao, that:

SEC. 1.- All persons below legal age are hereby prohibited to drink and/or order liquors, alcoholic beverages in hotels, beer houses, disco houses, fun houses, motels, restaurants, food houses and coconut wines (tubs) and other nature wine on stores and the like, either inside or brought outside of the business establishment premises, within 24 hours a day;

SEC. 2.- All business establishments selling liquors, alcoholic beverages and the like that causes intoxications, stores selling coconut wine (tubs) and other native wines are hereby prohibited to sell the same after 2:00 o'clock in the morning;

SEC. 3.- Any violations to the provisions of the preceding Sections 1 and 2 of this Ordinance shall be punished by a fine of not less than P5,000.00 pesos or an imprisonment of three (3) months or both upon the discretion of the court. The following shall be held liable upon violation to the preceding Section 1 and 2 of this Ordinance to wit:

1. The president or manager in case the business establishment is a corporation, partnership or cooperative;

2. The owner and/or proprietor of the business establishment if single proprietorship;

SEC. 4.- Any provisions of any existing Ordinance which is inconsistent with any provision of this Ordinance is hereby modified and/or amended accordingly to this effect;

SEC. 5.- This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and after due publication to a newspaper of general circulation in the locality for three (3) consecutive times.
