**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 253 OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 158-05, SERIES OF 2005, AS AMENDED, OTHERWISE Known As "THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO"**  

Be it ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 253 OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 158-05, SERIES OF 2005, AS AMENDED, OTHERWISE Known As "THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO".**  

**SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY** - Under the General Welfare Clause or Section 16 of the Local Government Code, "Every Local Government Unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, that necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare,"

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other ordinances and executive orders, or parts thereof previously enacted which are inconsistent herewith are deemed amended, repealed or modified accordingly;
