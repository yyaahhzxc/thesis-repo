Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE MANDATING THE REGISTRATION AND SECURING OF A PERMIT TO OPERATE BY ALL PRIVATE CHILD DEVELOPMENT CENTERS/LEARNING CENTERS OFFERING EARLY CHILDHOOD CARE AND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS FOR CHILDREN ZERO TO FOUR (0-4) YEARS OF AGE

Ord. No. 0782-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE MANDATING THE REGISTRATION AND SECURING OF A PERMIT TO OPERATE BY ALL PRIVATE CHILD DEVELOPMENT CENTERS/LEARNING CENTERS OFFERING EARLY CHILDHOOD CARE AND DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS FOR CHILDREN ZERO TO FOUR (0-4) YEARS OF AGE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote the rights of children for survival, development, and special protection with full recognition of the nature of childhood as well as the need to provide developmentally appropriate experiences to address their needs and to support parents in their roles as primary caregivers and as their children's first teachers;

Further, the State hereby recognizes the age of children from zero (0) to eight (8) years as the first crucial stage of educational development of which the age from zero (0) to four (4) shall be the responsibility of the Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) Council;

Thus, this Ordinance seeks to promote quality early childhood education in Private Child Development Centers/Learning Centers offering services to young children 0 to 4 years old in order to (i) guide the ongoing development and improvement of center-based early childhood programs; (ii) encourage self-monitoring and regular evaluation of the centers by their staff and partners; and (iii) serve as reference in the assessment of the Centers for the granting of permit and recognition by local government authorities and the ECCD Council.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS:

Early Childhood Care & Development Program - refers to the various services of health and sanitation, nutrition, early education, child protection, social services, and human development that may be conducted as center-based programs or home-based programs by various ECCD service providers which include:

a. Center-based Programs - refer to the programs such as the day care service established under Republic Act No. 6972, otherwise known as the "Barangay Level Total Development Protection of Children Act" and herein after referred to as the child development service, community or church-based early childhood development education programs initiated by non-government organizations or people's organization, workplace-related childcare and education programs, child mindling centers, and health centers and students;

b. Home-based Programs - refer to programs which utilize parents as the primary service providers for ECCD such as the neighborhood-based play groups, family child care program, parent education, and home visiting program;

Child Development Center/Learning Center - refers to an early learning center, public or private, that offers early education and/or child care program for children age zero to four who are taught by Child Development Teacher/s or Worker/s;

Curriculum - refers to what everything that the staff do and use for the
children's learning and development. It also refers to appropriate
developmental activities and materials in CDCs/LCs that will ensure the
holistic development and learning of children age zero to four years old;
Guidelines - refer to statements which determine the courses of action
to be taken that aim to streamline particular processes according to sound
practices;
Parent - refers to a child's father, mother, person acting as parent of the
child, or legal guardian;
Permit - refers to the authorization granted by the Local Government Unit
at the city level for the Child Development Center/Learning Center to
conduct early childhood programs for children zero to four years old for a
period of three (3) years based on requirements set in the Standards and
Guidelines for Early Childhood Programs for the 0 to 4 years old Filipino
Children issued by the ECCD Council;
Private Child Development Centers/Learning Centers - refer to
three established and owned by Corporations, Associations, Religious
Groups, Mission Boards, Non-Government Organizations and People's
Organizations. They are managed and supervised by an
Administrator/Director/Principal of the Center;
Program - refers to a center-based early education/child care program for
children zero to four years old;
Recognition - refers to the authorization granted by the ECCD Council
for the Child Development Center/Learning Center to conduct early
childhood programs for the zero to four years old based on the Guidelines
on Registration and Granting of Permit and Recognition issued by the ECCD
Council;
Standards - refer to written general statements of actions, behavior,
characteristics and conditions agreed by the stakeholders against whom
the Child Development Centers/Learning Centers are judged or measured;
Systematic Assessment - refers to the use of various tools that help
determine young children's strengths, weaknesses, and progress in the
areas of development and utilization of results in planning and modifying
instruction;
Volunteer - refers to an external partner who is not being paid to work
in the program and who assists in the care and education of the young
children.
SECTION 4. EVALUATION - All Private Child Development Centers/Learning
Centers shall be required to register and undergo an evaluation process for the issuance
of a permit to operate and thereafter be recognized by the ECCD Council to ensure quality
care in early childhood education programs.
SECTION 5. REGISTRATION - All Private CDCs/LCs shall be required to
register with the Office of the City Mayor through the City Social Services and
Development Office six months before it can offer early childhood programs for children
0-4 years old.

**SECTION 6. PERMIT TO OPERATE - All Private CDCs/LCs shall also secure a permit to operate during the period of its registration or six months prior to its operation from the City Mayor through the City Social Services and Development Officer;**

The application and inspection fee in the amount of Two Hundred Fifty Pesos (P250.00) shall thereafter be paid to the City Treasurer's Office. The application and inspection fee may be increased subject however to a prior notice to the public of the newly imposed fee.

**SECTION 7. INSPECTION - All Private CDCs/LCs shall be subject to a visit/inspection by the City Social Services and Development Officer to determine whether there is faithful compliance with all the requirements laid down;**

The visit/inspection shall be conducted within a period of thirty (30) days after the application for permit to operate is filed.

**SECTION 8. CERTIFICATE TO OPERATE - When the applicant has complied with all the requirements, a Certificate to Operate shall thereafter be given to all qualified Private CDCs/LCs which shall be valid for three (3) years;**

If the applicant is unable to comply with all the said requirements, a Letter to Comply with the Requirements shall be served to unqualified Private CDCs/LCs with the Report of Inspection attached thereto. The Center thereafter shall have a period of five (5) months to one (1) year to comply with all the lacking requirements. However, they may still be given a temporary Permit to Operate for one (1) year only and a recommendation for closure if failure to comply with the requirements persist.

**SECTION 9. PROCESS OF RECOGNITION - A Letter of Intent for Recognition addressed to the City Mayor through the City Social Services and Development Officer shall be submitted by the Administrator/Principal/Director of a CDC/LC seeking for recognition;**

An Internal Assessment shall thereafter be made followed by an External Assessment. If CDC/LC complies with the required score, the Evaluator shall recommend to the ECCD Council through the DSWD Regional Office for the issuance of a Certificate of Recognition (Level), one week after the assessment;

A Certificate of Recognition Level 1 (satisfactory rating) valid for three (3) years shall be issued if CDC/LC has demonstrated mandatory compliance with the Standards and Guidelines showing quality service implementation. A Certificate of Recognition Level 2 (very satisfactory rating) valid for four (4) years shall be issued if CDC/LC has demonstrated optimal compliance with the Standards and Guidelines that increases the effectiveness of the program. A Certificate of Recognition Level 3 (outstanding rating) valid for five (5) years shall be issued if CDC/LC has demonstrated the highest compliance with the Standards and Guidelines that makes CDC/LC a center of excellence;

The CDC/LC that has been granted recognition is encouraged to maintain its status. When the number of years for its recognition has lapsed, CDC/LC has to file again a Letter of Intent for Recognition.

**SECTION 10. GUIDELINES - The Guidelines on the Registration and Granting of Permit and Recognition to Public and Private Child Development Centers/Learning Centers Offering Early Childhood Programs for all 0 to 4 Years Old Children of the ECCD Council shall be followed by the local authorities in granting registration, permit to operate and recognition.**

SECTION 11. PENALTY - All private CDCs/LCs shall comply with the requirements of registration and permit to operate before it can offer early childhood programs to the public. Any private CDC/LC who shall operate without the required registration and permit to operate shall be immediately closed by the Business Bureau within five (5) days. In case a Private CDC/LC has complied with all the requirements under this Ordinance after its closure, the Business Bureau shall lift its order of closure and the Private CDC/LC shall thereafter be allowed to operate. In the event that an order for closure is issued by the Business Bureau against a CDC/LC and the latter shall continue to operate, the Private CDC/LC shall be permanently barred from registering and securing a permit to operate.

SECTION 12. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 13. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication.

ENACTED, May 23, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
