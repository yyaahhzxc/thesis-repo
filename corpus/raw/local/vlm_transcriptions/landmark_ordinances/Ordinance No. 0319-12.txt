Ord No. 0319-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE HONORABLE CITY MAYOR OF DAVAO CITY, TO ENTER
INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE
MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BY AND AMONG THE CITY
GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, BANCO SENTRAL NG PILIPINAS (BSP),
COOPERATIVES AND GOVERNMENT FUNDS ACCOUNTS OF THE DEVELOPMENT
BANK OF THE PHILIPPINES (DBP) AND OTHER CONCERNED STAKEHOLDERS FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE
DAVAO CITY CREDIT SURETY FUND (DCCSF) PROGRAM";

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter
into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the
Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and among the City
Government of Davao, Banco Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), the concerned
Cooperatives, Land Bank of the Philippines, Development Bank of the
Philippines and other concerned stakeholders for the establishment of the
Davao City Credit Surety Fund (DCCSF) Program;

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon
approval;

ENACTED, July 3, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present.
