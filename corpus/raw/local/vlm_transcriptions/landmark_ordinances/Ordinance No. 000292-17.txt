Ord. No. 0292-17  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND CONCURRENCING THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE DAVAO CITY BULK WATER SUPPLY PROJECT OF APO AGUA INFRASTRUCTURA, INC. (AII) AND DAVAO CITY WATER DISTRICT (DCWD)".  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Article 9 (a) of Ordinance No. 0310-07, Series of 2007, known as the Watershed Protection, Conservation and Management Ordinance as amended by Ordinance No. 0467-15, Series of 2015, states that:  

Article 9. PROHIBITED ACTS  

(a) CONSERVATION AREAS  

"x" Constructing or maintaining any kind of structure, fence or enclosure and conducting any business enterprise. Provided, however, that the construction of any structure, fence, or enclosure or the conduct of any business enterprise shall be allowed with the prior approval and concurrence of the Sangguniang Panlungsod by a majority vote of all the members present constituting a quorum, upon proof and showing that the said structure, fence, or enclosure or the said conduct of business shall be beneficial to the watershed areas and recharge areas as defined under this Ordinance.  

Likewise, Resolution No. 02754-16, Series of 2016, entitled "Granting approval and concurrence to Davao City Bulk Water Supply Project of Apo Agua Infrastructure Inc. and the Davao City Water District (DCWD), without prejudice to the passage of subsequent Ordinance for the collection of local taxes, fees and charges due to the City Government" was taken into consideration in granting approval and concurrence to the project;  

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The Apo Agua Infrastructure Inc. and the Davao City Water District (DCWD) are hereby authorized to construct the Davao City Bulk Water Supply Project in Barangay Gumalang, this City;  

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;  

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.  

ENACTED, on the 9th day of October, 2017, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.
