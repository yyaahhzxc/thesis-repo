ORDINANCE NO. 0599-21
Series of 2021
AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL ACCREDITED MOTORCYCLE
DEALERS AND DISTRIBUTORS IN DAVAO CITY TO SECURE
FIRST A CERTIFICATE OF COMPLIANCE OF BASIC
MOTORCYCLE TRAINING COURSE FROM THEIR PROSPECTIVE
CLIENTS BEFORE RELEASING THE PURCHASED MOTORCYCLES

Ord. No. 0599-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be referred to as "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL ACCREDITED MOTORCYCLE DEALERS AND DISTRIBUTORS IN DAVAO CITY TO SECURE FIRST A CERTIFICATE OF COMPLIANCE OF BASIC MOTORCYCLE TRAINING COURSE FROM THEIR PROSPECTIVE CLIENTS BEFORE RELEASING THE PURCHASED MOTORCYCLES".

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF APPLICATION: This Ordinance shall guarantee the motorcycle drivers with road worthiness and safety driving habits. It is essential to understand the benefits of every motorcycle driver undergoing a mandatory basic driving training course to ensure an efficient, safe, unhampered, and orderly economic growth and social vitality of the city. As an end result, they will be equipped with the proper knowledge on traffic rules which eventually will lessen the possibility of fatal crashes.

SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF POLICY

1. Article II, Section 5 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that the maintenance of peace and order, the protection of life, liberty, and property, and the promotion of the general welfare are essential for the enjoyment by all the people of the blessings of democracy. It is hereby declared the policy of the State to help curb road mishaps caused by motorcycles road accidents.

2. Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991" states in part that every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare;

3. The city's theme "Life is Here" signifies that the safety of its people is the utmost concern, that there is always a need to curtail the incidence and prevalence of death and injuries, damage to properties and socio-economic losses caused by related road accidents and in recognition to the imminent need to promote a proactive road safety program.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS: As used in this Ordinance, the following terms are defined and shall be referred to as follows:

1. City Traffic Citation Ticket - refers to complaint or notice upon which the city traffic enforcement officer shall record an occurrence involving one or more vehicle traffic law violations by the driver or person cited.

2. Driver - any person with a valid driver's license driving or controlling a motorcycle.

3. Highways - shall mean every public thoroughfare, public boulevard, driveway, avenue, park, alley and Callejon but shall not include roadways upon grounds owned by private persons, colleges, universities, or other similar institutions.

4. Motorcycle - a powered two (2) or three (3)-wheeled motor vehicle, including, but not limited to, scooters, mopeds, and motorcycles with appendages such as sidecars, tricycles, or trikes. This shall include government-owned vehicles.

5. Motorcycle Dealers/Distributors - shall mean every person, association, partnership, or corporation making, manufacturing, constructing, assembling, repairing, modifying, setting up motorcycle vehicles and any kind of motorcycle vehicles, dealing in motorcycle vehicles, keeping the same in stock or selling the same or handling with a view to trading the same.

6. Motorcycle Owner - refers to any person who owns or is the registered owner of a motorcycle as appearing in the LTO registration. Under this Ordinance, dealer refers to any person who has actual control and possession of a motorcycle, whether registered or not.

7. Motorcycle Training Center - a test track training facility where they teach motorcycle riders the basic driving skills and education needed to be equipped for riding on public roads. This includes the theoretical and practical examinations about basic road signs and warning.

8. Motor Vehicle - shall mean any vehicle propelled by any power other than muscular power using public highways.

9. Registration by the Owner - the owner of a motorcycle shall register his or her motorcycle with the Land Transportation Office (LTO) within five (5) days from such acquisition of ownership. The owner of a motorcycle shall also immediately report any sale or disposition of his or her motorcycle to LTO.

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover:

1. All prospective motorcycle drivers of newly purchased motorcycle units from accredited motorcycle dealers and distributors within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City, either for personal use or for hire purposes.

2. This will cover all motorcycle drivers, either beginners, intermediate, or advance skill.

SECTION 5. PURPOSE:

1. To require an individual motorcycle driver to complete a One (1) day mandatory motorcycle safety training course prior to the release of their purchased motorcycle;

2. To require the buyer to secure first a Certificate of Completion of a training program for riders, particularly focused on motorcycle riding safety, driving rules and regulations, road courtesy and hazards, among others.

3. In order to lessen, if not totally avoid, accidents to motorcycle riders steps should be taken to train them in the proper way of riding their steel horses and safely travel on roads without inflicting injury or damage to themselves or others.

SECTION 6. PROHIBITION - MOTORCYCLE DEALERS AND DISTRIBUTORS

1. It is prohibited for the motorcycle dealer and distributor to sell motorcycles to buyers without requiring the buyers to show their valid driver's license in reference with the restriction code issued by the Land Transportation Office (LTO);

2. It is prohibited for the motorcycle dealer and distributor to release the purchased motorcycle unit to their buyer without securing first a Certificate of Compliance of basic motorcycle training course.

SECTION 7. MANDATORY TRAINING

1. Mandatory Motorcycle Training Course shall be completed within One (1) day and will be conducted at accredited motorcycle training centers.

2. Motorcycle Dealers and Distributors shall cover the minimal fee of Two Hundred Pesos (P200.00) for the said training.

3. Technical person from the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) - Regional Office XI and the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO) will conduct the training through the course.

4. CTTMO will be the one to issue the Certificate of Compliance of Basic Motorcycle Training Course since they will be the official implementing office as regards the training.

5. As to the accreditation of the motorcycle training center, TESDA R-XI will accredit the same considering its facility and training capacity.

SECTION 8. TECHNICAL WORKING GROUP

The following agencies shall form part of the Technical Working Group (TWG) who will work out the details of the training mechanics of the program, to wit:

• Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA)- Regional Office XI
• City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO)
• Land Transportation Office (LTO)
• Highway Patrol Group- Region XI
• Davao Crocodile Park Inc.
• Public Safety and Security Command Center (PSSCC)

The aforementioned agencies who form part of the Technical Working Group (TWG) shall also take part in the crafting of the Implementing Rules and Regulation (IRR) of the Ordinance.

SECTION 9. PENALTIES. - Any motorcycle dealer or distributor who is in violation of this Ordinance shall be penalized as follows:

(i) First Offense - One Thousand Pesos (Php1,000.00);

(ii) Second Offense - Three Thousand Pesos (Php3,000.00);

(iii) Third Offense - Five Thousand Pesos (Php5,000.00) and revocation of business permit

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any provision of this Ordinance shall be declared invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the validity, legality and enforceability of the remaining provisions of the Ordinance shall not in any way be affected or impaired thereby.

SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen days (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao.

ENACTED, May 18, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
