SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN LTS MALLS, INC. SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22 (a) (5) and (c); and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, provide:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
xxx
(5) To enter into contracts; and xxx
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sangguniang panlungsod. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation - xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between LTS Malls, Inc. (NCCC) and the City of Davao, relative to the free movie access to Persons with Disabilities (PWFDS) in Davao City, pursuant to Davao City Ordinance No. 155-23, Series of 2023, also known as "The Persons with Disability Free Movie Privilege Ordinance".

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is entered into this day of by and between:

LTS MALLS, INC., a company duly organized and existing under the laws of the Philippines, with office address at NHQ Bldg., Gempesaw Ext., Davao City represented by its President, SHARLENE FAYE A. LIM (hereinafter referred to as NCCC)

AND

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at the Davao City Hall, San Pedro Street, Davao City, represented by its City Mayor, Hon. SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE (hereinafter referred to as "LGU").

WHEREAS, LGU issued the City Ordinance No. 0155-23 Series of 2023, entitled as "Granting the Persons with Disability, with duly Issued Identification Cards by the City Government of Davao, Free Movie Privileges to All Cinemas in the City of Davao."

WHEREAS, NCCC offers its cinema facility in cognizance with the aforementioned ordinance by the LGU which is to recognize and give privilege to Persons with Disability (PWD) by means of regulated free access to cinema;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties have agreed to bind themselves as follows:

A. City Government of Davao shall:

1. Collaborate with NCCC for the implementation of the ordinance;
2. Provide any necessary assistance and support to uphold the smooth implementation of the ordinance.

B. LTS Malls, Inc. shall:

1. Provide free access to NCCC Cinemas to Persons with Disability (PWD) that are bonafide residents of Davao City;
2. Require the availing PWDs to present their PWD Identification Card and movie pass booklet issued by Person with Disabilities Affairs Office (PDAO) - Davao City upon entry. Free access is strictly non-transferable;

--- [Page 9] ---
3. Implement a monitoring sheet for registration or recording of PWD who availed the free access;
4. Designate Monday of every week for PWDs to avail free movie pass during the 1st screening only. This is applicable to one screening per week only, however the PWDs can avail 20% PWD discount for other movie schedules;
5. Allow free movie passes to regular movie screenings only, to exclude ATMOS, 3D, Premium, Special Movie Premier and pay-per view shows. However, blockbuster movies can only be availed on its second week;
6. Allocate 5% (10 seats per theater) of the total seats for availing PWDs on wheelchair, each Cinema can accommodate a maximum of six;
7. The free movie privilege is not applicable to other existing promotions. PWDs may choose either to avail their free movie or the existing promotion;
8. The free movie privilege is also not applicable during declared National/Legal Holidays such as but not limited to:
1. New Year
2. Maundy Thursday and Good Friday
3. Araw ng Kagitingan
4. Araw ng Bagong Ilog
5. Independence Day
6. National Heroes' Day
7. Bonifacio Day
8. All Saints Day
9. Christmas Day
10. Rizal Day
9. For festivals such as Metro Manila Film Festival and the like, PWDs shall follow festival guidelines and policies.
C. TERM. This Agreement shall be effective upon approval of the IRR unless otherwise terminated by either party through written notice to the other.
D. MUTUAL TRUST. The Parties agree to act in good faith and fidelity in exercising their rights and obligations and to mutually lend to one another maximum assistance and cooperation to the end that the purpose and objectives of this Agreement are achieved expeditiously and harmoniously. The Parties further agree to execute such other implementing agreements and instruments, and do such other acts as necessary to implement the intent of this Agreement.
