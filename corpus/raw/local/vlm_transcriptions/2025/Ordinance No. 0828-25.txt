**Ord. No. 0825-25**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

**SECTION 1. TITLE:** This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE NAMING THE ROAD AT PUROKS 1, 2, AND 3 BALITE, CALINAN POBLACION, CALINAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY, AS SIMEON V. LATAYAN STREET."

**SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY** Section 13 (b) (1), (2), and (5) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:

"Section 13. Naming of Local Government Units and Public Places, Streets and Structures.

(b) The sanggunian of highly urbanized cities and of component cities whose charters prohibit their voters from voting for provincial elective officials hereinafter referred to in this Code as independent component cities, upon consultation with the Philippine Historical Commission, change the name of the following within its territorial jurisdiction:

(1) City Barangays, upon recommendation of the sanggunian concerned;

(2) City roads, avenues, boulevards, thoroughfares, and bridges;

xxx

xxx

xxx

(5) Any other public place or building owned by the city government."

**SECTION 3. NEW NAME** The road at Puroks 1, 2, and 3 Balite, Calinan Poblacion, Calinán District, this City, is hereby named as "SIMEON V. LATAYAN STREET."

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE** If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions heretofore not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 23rd day of May 2025, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
