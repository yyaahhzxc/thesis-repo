"SECTION 21. Closure and Opening of Roads. (a) A local government unit may, pursuant to an ordinance, partially or temporarily close or open any road or roads in its square falling within its jurisdiction; Provided, However, That in case of permanent closure, such ordinance must be approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of all the members of the sanggunian, and when necessary, an adequate substitute for the public facility that is subject to closure is provided.
Xxx
(c) Any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs, or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications, and waterworks projects, the duration of which shall be specified by the local chief executive concerned in a written order; Provided, However, That no national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed for athletic, cultural, or civic activities not officially sponsored, recognized, or approved by the local government unit concerned."
In view thereof, this office recommends the approval of the subject ordinance.

Order No. 0791-25

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE P. VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF CERTAIN STREETS SPECIFIED HEREON, ON SPECIFIED AHEAD TIME, IN CONNECTION WITH THE REQUEST OF REV. FATHER VOLTAIRE P. DIMOL, PARISH ADMINISTRATOR OF SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL PARISH, PERTAINING TO THEIR SCHEDULE IN THE UPCOMING HOLY WEEK ACTIVITIES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs xxx".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the upcoming Holy Week activities, Rev. Father Voltaire P. Dimol, Parish Administrator, San Pedro Cathedral Parish, requested for a temporary road closure along the specified streets on the dates specified herein;

1. April 13 - 20, 2025: Palm Sunday - Easter Sunday
The temporary closure of the road in between Bolton St. and C.M. Recto Ave. (fronting the cathedral only) on the whole duration of the Holy Week and Easter celebration that is from 12am on April 13 - 20, 2025 up to 11:59 pm on April 20, 2025 for the Blessing of Palms, installation and restoration of Steel Structures (Sustan); for the Blessing of Fire and the traditional re-enactment of the Sugat/Elencuerto/Salubong.

2. April 18, 2025: Good Friday
The permission to use the major thoroughfares of the city for the traditional Way of the Cross procession @ 3:00 o'clock, early morning onwards, starting from Felcius Centrale to the Cathedral passing through Brion Quirino Avenue, Roxas Extension (along Red Cross), Legaspi St. (U/C), Ponciano St., Bonifacio St., Legaspi St., Magallanes St., Bonifacio Rotunda, and,

For the procession of the Santo Entierro @ 3:00 o'clock in the afternoon onwards on Good Friday, from the Cathedral passing through C.M. Recto Ave., Roxas Extension (along Red Cross), Ponciano St., Bonifacio St., Legaspi St., Magallanes St., Bonifacio Rotunda and back to the cathedral.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
