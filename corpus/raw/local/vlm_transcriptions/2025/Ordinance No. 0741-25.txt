Ord. No. 0741-25

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE SPORTS COMMISSION (PSC) RELATIVE TO THE 2023 PHILIPPINE NATIONAL GAMES CASH INCENTIVES GRANTED TO THE FORMER, FOR WINNING 4TH PLACE IN THE SAID NATIONAL COMPETITION".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22 (a) (5) and (c) and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provide that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers-
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and

(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation-
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY**  
The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC) relative to the 2023 Philippine National Games Cash Incentives granted to the former, for winning 4th place in the said national competition.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**  
If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY**  
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) made and entered into this 28th day of October 2023 in the City of Manila, Philippines, by and between:

The PHILIPPINE SPORTS COMMISSION (PSC), a national government agency created and existing by virtue of Republic Act No. 6847, with principal address at Administration Building, Rizal Memorial Sports Complex, Pablo Ocampo, Sr., St., Malate, Manila, represented by its Chairman, RICHARD E. BACHMANN, hereinafter referred to as the PSC;

-and-

CITY OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit (LGU) duly-organized and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall Bldg., San Pedro St., Davao City, represented herein by its City Mayor, SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the CD;

WITNESSETH, That:

WHEREAS, the PSC by virtue of R.A. No. 6847, encourages wide participation of all sectors, government and private, in amateur sports promotion and development, and provides incentives, recognitions and awards to deserving associations, athletes, referees, game officials, coaches, trainers and other persons or entities involved in or supporting sports development as may be permissible under the rules of amateurism;

WHEREAS, Executive Order (EO) No. 163 of 1994 established the Philippine National Games (PNG) as a comprehensive sports development program and forms part of the national policy for sports development at the grassroots level which also serves as a recruitment base for sports specialization;

WHEREAS, the PSC hosted and organized the Philippine National Games (PNG) 2023 held in Metro Manila from December 17-22, 2023;

WHEREAS, the PSC grants rewards and incentives to the LGUs who placed within the top 5 based on the over-all official medal tally during the PNG Championships 2023 to be used for the LGU's grassroots sports promotion and development and talent identification programs;

WHEREAS, the CD participated in the Philippine National Games (PNG) 2023 and won 4th place in the over-all official medal tally;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises, their mutual consent heretofore set forth, the parties have agreed to the following terms and conditions:

ARTICLE I

CONDITIONS OF THE GRANT AND DUTIES OF THE PARTIES

A. PSC shall:

1. Grant the CD the total amount of TWO MILLION PESOS (P2,000,000.00) as cash incentive for winning 4th place in the over-all official medal tally during the Philippine National Games (PNG) Championships 2023;

2. Monitor the utilization of the cash incentive by the CD and ensure that the incentive is used exclusively for the LGU's grassroots sports promotions and development and talent identification programs;

--- [Page 10] ---
3. Provide the necessary technical support to the CD in the planning, identification, establishment, coordination and implementation of its grassroots sports development and talent identification programs, which should be aligned with PSC's sports development policy and framework to ensure proper coordination and implementation.

B. The City of Davao shall:

1. Utilize the cash incentive provided by PSC exclusively for its grassroots sports promotion and development and talent identification programs which should be aligned with the grassroots sports development policy and framework of PSC,

2. Submit to PSC the CD's specific sports promotion and talent identification programs for which the cash incentive shall be utilized, as well as its proposed plans, projects and activities, the manner of execution and program implementation, within the timeframe provided by PSC.

ARTICLE II EFFECTIVITY

This MOA shall effect upon its signing, provided, however, that as far as PSC is concerned, such signing is contingent upon the prior approval of its Board of Commissioners.

This Agreement is subject to the usual government accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

ARTICLE III AMENDMENTS/PARTIAL INVALIDITY

All amendments or revisions of any provision of this MOA shall be upon the prior written mutual consent of all Parties subject to the execution of an appropriate instrument for the purpose.

If any of the provisions of this MOA is declared by a competent court or quasi-judicial body, shall supersede all other previous communications or contracts, oral or written, between and among the parties with respect to the subject matter thereof.

ARTICLE IV GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE

This Agreement shall be construed, interpreted, and implemented in accordance with Philippine laws. The venue of any suit or action arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be in the City of Manila only to the exclusion of all other courts.

ARTICLE V ENTIRE CONTRACT

The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between them and shall supersede all other previous communications or contracts, oral or written, between and among the Parties with respect to the subject matter thereof.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have caused this Agreement to be signed on the date and place first above-mentioned.
