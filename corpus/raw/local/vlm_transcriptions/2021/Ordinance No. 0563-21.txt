MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

Know all Men by These Presents:

This Memorandum of Understanding is made and entered into and between:

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit, established by virtue of Republic Act No. 4354 series of 1965, located at City Hall Drive, San Pedro St., Davao City represented herein by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the "CG of Davao.

and

The DEVELOPMENT ACADEMY OF THE PHILIPPINES, a government institution duly organized and existing under and in the virtue of Presidential Decree No. 205, as amended, with principal office address at the DAP Building, San Miguel Avenue, Ortigas Center, Pasig City, represented herein by its President and CEO, ATTY. ENGELBERT C. CARONAN, JR., MNSA, hereinafter referred to as the "DAP."

Hereinafter, collectively referred to as "Parties."

- WITNESSETH, that -

WHEREAS, there is a need for Philippine government agencies to be responsive to the needs of their intended beneficiaries by improving productivity in line with the Administration's thrust for seamless delivery of service;

WHEREAS, the Administration is committed to enhancing the social fabric through ensuring people-centered, clean, and efficient governance as cited in the Philippine Development Plan 2017-2022;

WHEREAS, this clear and compelling direction of the Administration further strengthens the need for government agencies to comply with Executive Order No. 605, "Institutionalizing the Structure, Mechanisms, and Standards to Implement the Government Quality Management Program (GQMP)," which was issued to strengthen branch departments and agencies, government-owned and controlled corporations, and government financial institutions, to effect improvement in public sector performance;

WHEREAS, the DAP, as a member of the Technical Working Group on Advocacy and Capability-Building of the Government Quality Management Committee (GQMC), and as the National Productivity Organization, spearheads the development of productivity consciousness and promotes the principles, techniques, and practice of productivity and quality in key sectors of the economy;

WHEREAS, the DAP, through the General Appropriations Act of 2021, has funding to strengthen and expand the implementation of the Government Quality Management Program through the development, expansion, integration, and leveling-up of the Quality Management System (QMS) in other beneficiary Agencies;

--- [Page 19] ---
WHEREAS, the DAP has developed the Policies and Guidelines on the Provision of Technical Assistance on QMS for GQMP Beneficiary Agencies, which details the scope and coverage of the technical assistance, eligibility requirements for program participation, including the screening and selection process;
WHEREAS, the CG OF DAVAO, in line with its mandate to ensure and support among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants, aims to continually improve the delivery of its services and transform its current management system to an ISO 9001:2015-certified QMS;
WHEREAS, the CG OF DAVAO has signified its interest and commitment to undertake the project, and has been selected as one of the GQMP Beneficiary Agencies that will receive technical assistance from the DAP for the establishment of a QMS certifiable status eligible to ISO 9001:2015 standard.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties agree as follows:
Section 1. SCOPE OF WORK/DESCRIPTION OF THE PROJECT
The Parties hereby undertake to perform the project entitled: "Development of a Quality Management System Certifiable to ISO 9001:2015 Standard for the City Government of Davao," more particularly described in the Terms of Reference attached hereto and made an integral part hereof as Annex "A."
Section 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE DAP
a. Organize a team that will conduct the project within the agreed timeframe;
b. Prepare the program and course designs for all the orientation, training, seminar, and workshop activities;
c. Provide one set of orientation, training, and seminar materials for reproduction/distribution by the CG OF DAVAO, for each course to be conducted;
d. Provide the resource persons and facilitators;
e. Provide the certificates to qualified participants of training courses and seminars conducted;
f. Review the draft documented information and recommend necessary revisions to ensure their alignment with ISO 9001:2015 standard;
g. Provide technical advice and guidance to counterpart team in the establishment and implementation of the CG OF DAVAO's QMS.

--- [Page 20] ---
h. Conduct assessments of the CG OF DAVAO's initial quality performance prior to ISO 9001:2015 QMS implementation to identify areas for improvement and on the effectiveness of the established QMS to recommend final preparations for the ISO 9001:2015 certification process.

Section 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CG OF DAVAO

a. Designate a counterpart support team who will work closely with the DAP Project Team regarding technical and administrative requirements of the project, including the monitoring of the progress of various project activities;

b. Provide logistical requirements of the project face-to-face or virtual activities, such as orientation/training/workshop/seminar/meeting venue, accommodation (when necessary), equipment (LCD projector with desktop or laptop), training supplies, reproduction of orientation, training, and seminar materials for participants, and meals and articles of participants and requisite persons, including housing of virtual activities, as applicable;

c. Provide the transportation for the resource persons and the DAP Project Team, from the DAP Office, Pasig City to orientation/training/workshop/ seminar/meeting venue and vice-versa, as applicable;

d. Draft the necessary documented information, including specific QMS-related forms and tools, and make the necessary revisions to ensure their alignment to ISO 9001:2015 standard with guidance from the DAP Project Team;

e. Ensure the implementation of relevant project activities, including improvement action plans, and provide policy support for the implementation of quality systems and procedures adopted by the CG OF DAVAO;

f. Ensure the availability and participation of staff/participants concerned during the conduct of training courses and other activities necessary in the course of the project;

g. Ensure that the project is completed within the agreed duration;

h. Procure a third-party certifying body within three months upon completion of DAP's technical assistance;

i. Pursue the certification of the established QMS within six months upon completion of DAP's technical assistance;

j. Provide the DAP Project Team progress reports on the status of certification and implementation of improvement action plans upon completion of the DAP's technical assistance;

k. Share the benefits and impact of establishing QMS with other government agencies during any event that may be organized by the DAP and/or through a report submitted to DAP to advocate QMS in the government.

--- [Page 21] ---
**Section 4: STATEMENT OF CONSIDERATION (FEES)**

The CG of Davao, as the selected beneficiary agency of the GQMP, shall not pay the services of the DAP for the technical assistance. Nothing in this MOU obligates the Parties to transfer any funds. Specific activities that involve transfer or payment of funds for additional activities outside the technical assistance package described in Annex "A" will require the execution of a separate written contract. Such written contract must be independently authorized by the Parties' respective authorized representatives.

**Section 5: CONFIDENTIALITY**

Each party undertakes not to divulge at any time to any third person any confidential information relating to the other Party, except upon prior written consent of the other Party or where required under the law or regulation or by a valid order of a court or other governmental authority with competent jurisdiction.

**Section 6: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS**

All materials and work provided by the DAP shall belong exclusively to the DAP and shall not be used for any purpose other than the implementation of this MOU. Any use, reproduction, publication, sale, or distribution of these materials and work shall be subject to the prior written consent of the DAP and such terms as the DAP shall require. Any original work created, arising out of or in connection with this MOU, shall belong to the DAP, except where the original was licensed to the DAP under an agreement with the CG of Davao. In such case, the work shall be owned jointly by the Parties, with each party having royalty-free, non-exclusive, and irrevocable license to use, publish, copy, reproduce, or distribute the work for the government or public purposes.

1. Ownership of and intellectual property rights in all materials and work provided by a party shall remain with the said party and shall not be used by the other party for any purpose without the prior written consent of the owner of the said materials and work.
2. Intellectual property rights created in connection with this Memorandum of Understanding shall be owned jointly by the parties herein.

**Section 7: LIABILITY**

The DAP shall not be liable to the CG of Davao for loss or damage to the CG of Davao's property, unless due to gross negligence or breach of this MOU. The DAP shall not be liable to the CG of Davao for any indirect, special, or consequential loss arising out of or in connection with the provision of the services pursuant to this MOU.

**Section 8: INDEMNITY**

The CG of Davao shall indemnify and hold the DAP free and harmless from any and all claims or causes of action of third parties arising from a negligent or otherwise wrongful act or omission by the CG of Davao or its employees or representatives.

--- [Page 22] ---
Each party shall indemnify and hold the other party free and harmless from any and all claims or causes of action against the other party arising from a negligent or otherwise wrongful act or omission by the first party or its employees or representatives relating to or in connection with the said first party's performance of its responsibilities under this Memorandum of Understanding.

Section 9. DURATION OF TERM OF THE MOU

This MOU shall become effective upon execution thereof and shall be valid and subsisting until 31 December 2021.

I. BACKGROUND / RATIONALE

The need for government agencies to improve quality in their operations and service delivery has never been more urgent in the light of the current Administration's thrust to enhance the social fabric through ensuring people-centered, clean, and efficient governance, as cited in the "Malasakit" pillar of the Philippine Development Plan 2017-2022. To this goal, the government is leveraging its priorities of its Results-Based Performance Management System, and its performance-based Performance Incentive System under Executive Order No. 80, s. 2012. This is further complemented by the Public Procurement Act No. 11032, otherwise known as the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018, which aims to establish transparency, efficiency, and quality in the delivery of government services and ensure citizen satisfaction, thereby improving the competitiveness of the country. There is also an urgent call to improve the citizens' faith and trust in the government's capacity to make the Filipino people better, safer, and healthier.

This clear and compelling direction of the Administration further intensifies the need for government agencies to comply with Executive Order No. 605, Institutionalizing the "Structure, Mechanisms, and Standard to Implement the Government Performance Management System," which seeks to achieve improvement in public sector performance by ensuring the consistency of products and services through quality processes. Effective management and good governance are essential elements of quality that can be achieved through an effective Quality Management System (QMS). Aligning an organization's planning, service delivery, and evaluation processes to these standards provides a holistic approach in establishing a QMS. This methodology provides a clear perspective of the quality system requirements and the areas of control to ensure consistency in the quality of services and continuous process improvement intended to generate approaches to improve citizen satisfaction.

The City Government of Davao (CG of Davao), established through Republic Act No. 4354 series of 1965, was mandated to ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, provide adequate housing, promote economic growth, ensure justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants. In line with its mission to be a globally livable City and a center of excellence in governance, investment, tourism, climate change, adaptation, disaster resilience, and sustainable growth driven by the empowered citizenry, the CG of Davao seeks to continuously improve the efficiency, effectiveness, and transformation of its current management system to an ISO 9001:2015 certified QMS. The CG of Davao signified its interest and commitment and was selected to become one of the QGMP Beneficiary Agencies that will receive technical assistance from the Development Academy of the Philippines (DAP) for the establishment of a QMS certifiable to ISO 9001:2015 standard.

In this regard, the DAP is implementing the project entitled, "Development of a Quality Management System Certifiable to ISO 9001:2015 Standard for the City Government of Davao."
