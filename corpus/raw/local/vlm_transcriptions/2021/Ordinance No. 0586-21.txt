RENEWAL OF CONTRACT OF LEASE

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Renewal of Contract of Lease executed and entered into by and between:

METRO DAVAO DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION, represented by GEORGE U. LIM, of legal age, married, Filipino, and residing at San Pedro St., Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR," and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, represented by MAYOR SARA DUTERTE, as authorized by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod in its Resolution No. ______, with principal address at City Hall Building, Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE."

THAT, for and in consideration of the payment of the rent and compliance of all conditions and covenants hereinafter contained, the LESSOR has agreed to lease, and demise, unto the LESSEE, and the latter has agreed to accept as he/it does according to the terms of the lease, the above-described commercial building situated at City Hall Drive, Davao City, with a floor area of 300 square meters, more or less, under the following terms and conditions:

1. TERM - The lease period for three (3) year commencing February 16, 2021, to February 15, 2024, based on the original terms and conditions as agreed upon by both parties.

In the event that the LESSEE desires to renew this contract under this paragraph, the LESSOR shall be notified at least three (3) months before the expiration of the lease.

2. RENTAL - The LESSEE shall pay the LESSOR the amount of PESOS: FORTY-FIVE THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED EIGHTY-SEVEN CENTS only ($ 45,580.37) for year 2021, FORTY SEVEN THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED NINETY-SEVEN CENTS only ($ 47,859.37) for year 2022, FIFTY THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED FIFTY TWO PESOS AND THIRTY-SIX CENTS only ($ 50,252.36) for year 2023, per month, inclusive of twelve percent (12%) value added tax (VAT), reflecting an increase of five percent (5%) on the previous rental rates of PESOS: FORTY-THREE THOUSAND THREE HUNDRED NINE PESOS AND EIGHTY-EIGHT CENTS only ($ 43,409.88) as stipulated in the previous Contract of Lease hereto attached as Annex "A" and made part of this document. A five percent (5%) per year escalation on the rental fees shall continue to be effective in any subsequent renewal of this Lease Contract.

Without prejudice to the exercise by the LESSOR of its rights in Paragraph 17 and 18 herein, the LESSEE shall pay to the LESSOR interest at the rate of twenty four percent (24%) per annum on any monthly rental not paid, to be computed from the date of the delinquency.

--- [Page 15] ---
PAGE 2 - Contract of Lease

3. USES OF THE PREMISES - The LESSEE shall not use the leased premises for dwelling or sleeping purposes. The premises hereby leased shall be used exclusively by the LESSEE for office and stockroom purposes and the latter shall not divert the premises to any other uses without the prior consent of the LESSOR, it being expressly agreed that if, at any time during the existence of this lease and without the previous consent of the LESSOR, the said premises is used for any other purposes, the LESSOR reserves the right to rescind this contract in accordance with Section 27; (b) increase the rental; or (c) compel the LESSEE to stop the new activities.

4. IMPROVEMENT AND ALTERATIONS - The LESSEE shall not make any alterations, installations, additions or improvements of the leased premises, such as partitions, shades, shades, blinds, window guards, etc., without the consent of the LESSOR. It is further agreed that all such alterations, installations, additions or improvements shall be made at the expense of the LESSOR. If the LESSEE, becomes property and shall remain, and be surrendered with the premises as part thereof, upon termination of the lease, without compensation to the LESSEE. If the LESSOR does not elect to retain such alteration, installations, additions or improvements, the premises shall be surrendered to their original condition at the expense of the LESSEE. The LESSOR reserves the right to refuse any alteration, installation, addition or improvement requested by the LESSEE if in the LESSOR's opinion, there is just cause to warrant such refusal.

5. POWER CONSUMPTION AND CHARGES FOR UTILITIES - All electrical consumption and charges, fees and costs for telephone, water, and other utilities used by the LESSEE in connection with the premises, shall be for its account. The LESSEE may, at its discretion, install a separate meter and convenience outlets and all electrical connections as it may deem necessary.

6. SIGNS AND ADVERTISEMENTS - To insure uniformity of design, the LESSEE shall not cause to be affixed, inscribed or painted, any notice, sign or other advertising medium, on any part of the building without the consent of the LESSOR.

The LESSEE shall not permit or give consent to any other person or entity to advertise, as if said person or entity uses, holds office, or is otherwise established, at the premises leased, or any part thereof, and unless the LESSOR agrees to the contrary, only the name and nomenclature of the LESSEE as written in the contract of lease shall be placed in the building directory and advertised at or near the main door of the premises leased. No other sign or advertisement may be placed in the leased premises other than the place herein above indicated.

--- [Page 16] ---
7. CARE OF THE LEASED PREMISES - The LESSEE shall maintain the leased premises in a clean and sanitary condition, free from noxious odors and disturbing noise, and upon expiration of the lease, shall surrender and return the leased premises and fixtures in as good condition, except ordinary wear and tear, as the leased premises were actually found at the beginning of the lease.

8. RULES, REGULATIONS, ETC. - The LESSEE will comply with any and all reasonable rules and safety regulations which may be promulgated from time to time by the LESSOR and made known to the LESSEE in writing, for the administration/use/occupancy of the leased premises, and with all the rules, regulations, ordinances and laws made by the health and duly constituted authorities of the City National Government, arising from or regarding the use, occupancy and sanitation of leased premises.

The LESSEE shall indemnify and hold harmless the said LESSOR against all actions, suits, damages and claims by whosoever they may be brought or made by reason of the non-observance or non-performance of the said rules, regulations, ordinances, or laws, or of any covenants of this section, without prejudice to the right of the LESSOR to cancel this lease in accordance with the penal provisions hereinafter contained.

9. INSPECTION OF PREMISES - The LESSOR or its authorized agent shall, by previous arrangement with the LESSEE, and after presenting his/her authority, have the right to enter the leased premises at any reasonable time, to examine the same, subject to security requirements of the LESSEE, and during the last three (3) months of the term of the lease, to exhibit the leased premises to prospective tenants, except when this lease has been renewed as herein provided.

10. REPAIRS - The LESSEE shall make necessary repairs on the leased premises and maintain the same in good and tenableable condition.

During the existence of this lease, the LESSEE shall pay for the cost of repairs, replacements, and/or restoration of damages caused on the leased premises by the herein LESSEE, its employees, representatives, agents, clients and visitors.

The LESSEE shall at all times, subject to prior arrangement, allow and extend access to the LESSOR to the leased premises for the purpose of making repairs, remodeling or repainting or to undertake all works necessary for the preservation, improvement or decoration of the building or any part thereof. No compensation or claims shall be allowed against the LESSOR by reason of inconvenience, annoyance or injury to the LESSEE's business that may arise by virtue of undertaking any work under this provision.

--- [Page 17] ---
Page 4 - Contract of Lease

11. TERMINATION OF LEASE - Should the LESSEE opt not to renew the lease, LESSEE agrees to return and surrender to the LESSOR the leased premises at the end of the term of this lease in as good condition as reasonable wear and tear will permit and without delay whatsoever; devolve of all occupants, furniture, articles and effects of any kind other than such alterations, additions or improvement which shall be subject to the provisions of Paragraph 5.

12. SUB-LEASE, TRANSFER OR LEASE - The LESSEE shall not assign or transfer its right in this contract nor sublease or sublet all or any part of the leased premises, without the prior written consent of the LESSOR and no right, title or interest thereto shall be conferred or vested in anyone other than the LESSEE without such written consent.

13. OCCUPATION OF THE LEASED PREMISES - After this lease has been terminated for any reason whatsoever, if the LESSEE continues the occupation of the leased premises with the consent of the LESSOR, said extension of lease shall be understood as running from month to month only, under the same terms and conditions herein stipulated and may be terminated by either party by means of a written notice served upon the other party at least 30 days prior to the date of such termination.

14. INFLAMMABLE AND EXPLOSIVE MATERIALS - The LESSEE shall not bring into or store, nor shall allow third persons to bring into or store, in the leased premises anything of a highly inflammable nature or explosive materials, nor cook or install any apparatus, machinery or equipment which may expose the leased premises to fire or increase the fire hazard of the property or change the insurance rate of the property or any other article which the LESSOR may reasonably prohibit, it being understood that should the LESSEE do so, such violation may cause the LESSOR to order for the immediate removal of the abovementioned articles from the premises.

15. INSURANCE - The LESSOR shall insure the property up to the extent of its insurable interest and against all perils it may choose to be covered; the Lessee may also insure at its expense its property/improvement brought into the leased premises against perils it may want insured against.

16. NON-WAIVER - The failure of the LESSOR to insist upon a strict performance of any of the terms, conditions and covenants hereof shall not be deemed a relinquishment or waiver of any rights or remedy that said lessor may own, nor shall it be construed as a waiver of any subsequent breach or default of the terms, conditions and covenants hereof, which shall continue to be in full force and effect. No waiver by the LESSOR of any of its rights under this contract shall be deemed to have been made unless expressed in writing and signed by the LESSOR.

--- [Page 18] ---
17. PAYMENT OF REALTY TAXES - It shall be the duty of the LESSOR to pay the realty taxes on the land where the property is constructed as well as the property itself.

18. DAMAGE TO LEASED PREMISES - In case of damage to the leased premises or its appurtenance by fire, earthquake, war, or any other unforeseen cause, the LESSEE shall give immediate notice thereof to the LESSOR. If the lease premises shall be damaged by fire or other cause without the fault or negligence of the LESSEE or its agents, employees, servants, or visitors, the injury shall be repaired at the expense of the LESSOR within the reasonable time after such notice, unless the building of the LESSOR has been rendered so dilapidated as to make it untenantable without the fault or neglect of the LESSEE. The LESSOR shall be fully liable to the LESSOR in all cases where damage of any nature or kind is caused by the fault or negligence of the said LESSEE, his employees, agents, servants, or visitors. No compensation or claim shall be allowed against the LESSOR by reason of any inconvenience, annoyance, or injury to the LESSEE's business arising out of the necessity of repairing any portion of the building, in whatever way such necessity may arise.

19. LOAD - No portion of the floor in said premises shall be loaded in excess of 32 kilos (70 lbs.) per square foot, nor shall any safe or other article weighing in excess of 200 kilos (+40 lbs.) be placed in or removed from the premises without the knowledge and consent of the LESSOR, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

20.
