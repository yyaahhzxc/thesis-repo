Ord. No. 0709-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING AND GIVING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT IN THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROJECT, NAMELY: PIAPI UPGRAADING AREA, IN FAVOR OF THE AWARDEE ROSARIO M. CAFETE LOCATED AT BLOCK 11, LOT 6, PIAPI UPGRAADING AREA, BARANGAY 22-C, THIS CITY, CONSISTING OF SIXTY (60) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Sale for the lot in the SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROGRAM, NAMELY: PIAPI UPGRAADING AREA AND PIAPI COMMERCIAL AREA, in favor of the awardee, Rosario M. Cafefe.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, September 14, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

CHARLOTTE N. SANTOS
