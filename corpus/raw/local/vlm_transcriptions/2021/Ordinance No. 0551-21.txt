**SECTION 5. PURCHASE AGREEMENT -** In the negotiation with the representatives of the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation, the City of Davao requested for a three-year payment scheme with the following details:

1. The full purchase price shall be divided into three (3) installments;
2. Each installment shall be paid annually at the end of March;
3. The vendor, herein the City of Davao, is granted zero-interest rate by the vendor, PDCO;
4. All expenses such as the Capital Gains Tax, Documentary Tax, Transfer Tax, Registration Expenses, and other related expenses of the sale shall be borne by the City of Davao.

As assurance that the City of Davao will be able to settle its financial obligation, the City Budget Office shall issue the availability of a continued appropriation and source of budget.

**SECTION 6. COVERAGE -** This Ordinance shall cover the approval of the Purchase/Sale Agreement between the City of Davao and the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation in the acquisition of the above-mentioned nine (9) properties until the purchase agreement and payments shall have been completed.

**SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, such sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY -** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED, April 20, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
