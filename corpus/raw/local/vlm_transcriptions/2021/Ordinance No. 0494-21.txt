Ord. No. 0494-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF MS. CARMELITA SATOR FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF COMMERCIAL SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY LOCATED ALONG DAVAO RIVER, BARANGAY LAMPIANAO AND BARANGAY TALANDAG, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Article XII, Section 1 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City provides for requests of Additional Allowable Use which reads:

"Section 1. Request for Additional Allowable Use - The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general and all sub-zones are not exclusive nor all inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponents and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board and Affidavit of No Objection (LZBA) may allow other uses not enumerated therein as it may deem fit. All projects which are not permitted to, the following projects which are socio-economic and environmental significance and/or of national interest by a 3/4 majority vote of all Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance."

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST - The application for Additional Allowable Use of Ms. Carmelita Sator for the operation of commercial sand and gravel quarry located on the northwest portion of Davao City along Davao River between Barangay Lampianao and Barangay Talandag, with a total area of Ten Thousand (10,000) square meters, particularly described as:

**SECTION 4. OBLIGATION OF PERMIT HOLDER**  
The grantee of any permit for the operation of a Sand and Gravel Quarry has the obligation to comply with existing rules, laws, and Ordinances. The environment shall at all time be protected. Adverse environmental impact of any use of the property shall be avoided. Non-compliance with existing rules, laws, and ordinances shall be a cause for the repeal of this Ordinance.

**SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**  
If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY**  
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED**  
on the 15th day of February 2021, by 3/4 majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
