Ord. No. 0653-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION BY AND BETWEEN
THE CITY OF DAVAO AND COSMIC TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED
RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF FIVE HUNDRED (500) UNITS OF CHERRY
FLARE TABLETS TO THE CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of the Local
Government Code of 1991 provides:

"Every local government unit shall exercise the powers
expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as
well as power necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its
effective and efficient performance of its functions which are
essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within
their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government
units shall ensure and support, among other things, the
preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health
and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced
ecology, encourage and support the development of
appropriate self-help programs, improve their technological
capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic
prosperity and social justice, promote full employment
among their resident, maintain peace and order, and
preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation by and between the City of Davao
and Cosmic Technologies Incorporated relative to the donation of the Five Hundred
(500) Units of Cherry Flare tablets to the City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 13th day of July 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
City Government Department Head

DEED OF DONATION AND ACCEPTANCE

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Deed of Donation and Acceptance, made and executed by COSMIC TECH NOLOGIES INCORPORATED, a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at 901 Apable St., Corner Leon Guintra St., Ermita, Manila, represented by its Director, MILTON S. NGU (herein referred to as the "Donor"), and by the City of Davao, represented by its Mayor, HON. RODRIGUEZ, M.D. (herein referred to as the "Dovee"), hereinafter referred to as the "Donor" and the "Dovee," respectively.

WITNESSETH

In consideration of the Donor's advocacy of promoting quality education through the use of electronic gadgets, the Donor hereby voluntarily gives, transfers, and conveys by way of donation, unto the Dovee, free from liens, encumbrances and charges of whatever form the following personal property:

| QTY | BRAND/ MODEL | UNIT PRICE | ASSET DESCRIPTION | TOTAL COST |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 500 | CHERRY Flare Tablet | Php/10,000.00 | 10" Tablet with Bluetooth Keyboard | Php/5,000,000.00 |

That, The Donor and Dovee agree as follows:

1. The Dovee shall allocate One Hundred (100) tablets to the students of Davao City High School ("DCHS") as identified by DCHS Batch 95 to be led by the Honorable Sara Duterte-Carpio and Joanne D. Domingo-Latayan.

2. The remaining Four Hundred (400) tablets may be disposed of by the Dovee upon its sole discretion.

3. Upon delivery and acceptance, all risk of loss and/or damage shall pass unto the Dovee.

4. The Donor hereby indemnifies and shall keep as indemnified the Dovee, its Directors, Officers, and Employees from and against any expenses, cost, claim, suit demand, loss or injury owing to third parties in respect to third party claims arising out of Dovee's ownership, possession or use of the said donation to the extent that such losses relate to, arise out of or result from the Donor's breach of this Deed of Donation, negligence, fraud, bad faith, or willful misconduct.

--- [Page 9] ---
5. Donor will conduct inspection and recording in the book of accounts the above-mentioned donations in compliance with government auditing and accounting rules and regulations.

6. This Donation is not made for any consideration that is contrary to law, morals or good customs, or with the object of defrauding the Donors' creditors, if there are any, having reserved to themselves property sufficient to answer for any debts that may have been contracted prior to the execution of this instrument.

That, the Donor accepts with profound gratitude on the donations of the above-mentioned tablets by the Donor.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, both Parties have hereunder subscribed their names this 21 SEP 2021 , in DAVAO CITY , Philippines.
