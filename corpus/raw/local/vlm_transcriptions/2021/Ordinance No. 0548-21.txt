AN ORDINANCE RECLASSIFYING THREE (3) POSITIONS OF COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ASSISTANT II (SG-9), ONE (1) POSITION OF MINING CLAIMS EXAMINER I (SG-10), AND ONE (1) POSITION OF COMPUTER PROGRAMMER III (SG-18) UNDER THE ENGINEERING AND INFORMATION RESOURCES OFFICE (ENRO). CONSISTENT WITH THE 2017 OMNIBUS RULES ON APPOINTMENTS AND OTHER HUMAN RESOURCE ACTIONS (REVISED JULY 2018) OF THE CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be referred to as "AN ORDINANCE REORGANIZING THREE (3) POSITIONS OF COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ASSISTANT II (CDA II), ONE (1) POSITION OF MINING CLAIMS EXAMINER (SG-10), AND ONE (1) POSITION OF COMPUTER PROGRAMMER III (SG-18) UNDER THE CITY ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL RESOURCES OFFICE (CENRO), CONSISTENT WITH THE 2017 OMNIBUS RULES ON APPOINTMENTS AND OTHER HUMAN RESOURCE ACTIONS (REVISED JULY 2018) OF THE CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES. Presented to the Honorable Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod for review and consideration, is a letter from Atty. Zulekita L. Lopez, City Administrator, proposing for the redesignation of three (3) positions of Community Development Assistant II - Salary Grade 9 (SG-9), one (1) position of Mining Claims Examiner - Salary Grade 10 (SG-10), and one (1) position of Computer Programmer III - Salary Grade 18 (SG-18), under the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) to one (1) position of Administrative Officer IV (Administrative Officer II) - Salary Grade 15 (SG-15), one (1) position of Environmental Management Specialist I - Salary Grade 11 (SG-11), one (1) position of Administrative Officer III (Administrative Officer II) - Salary Grade 16 (SG-16), one (1) position of Engineering Assistant - Salary Grade 8 (SG-8), and one (1) position of Administrative Assistant II (Clerk IV) - Salary Grade 8 (SG-8), under the same office.

Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7169 provides that "every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessary therefor, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are necessary to the performance of the general welfare within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants".

Section 76 of Republic Act No. 7160 empowers local government units to design and implement its organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil Service Commission (CSC);

Section 12 (2) of Chapter 3, Title 1(A), Book V of the Administrative Code of 1987 provides that the Civil Service Commission shall prescribe, amend and enforce rules and regulations for carrying into effect the provision of the Civil Service Law and other pertinent laws;

Civil Service Commission (CSC) Resolution No. 1701009 dated June 16, 2017, which was published in the Philippine Star on August 2, 2017 and took effect on August 18, 2017 circulated through CSC Memorandum Circular No. 24, Series of 2017, otherwise known as the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resources Actions (ORAOHRA).

Section 11 of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions provides the Nature of Appointment:

"Reclassification - a form of staffing modification and/or position classification action which is applied only when there is substantial change in the regular duties and responsibilities of the position. This may result in a change in any or all of the position attributes: position, title, level and/or salary grade. It is the generic term for changes in staff/position classification which includes upgrading, downgrading, and reclassification."

Section 12 of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions allows adjustments or movement of human resource without need of issuance of an appointment;

SECTION 4. RECLASSIFICATION OF POSITIONS UNDER CENRO. The three (3) positions of Community Development Assistant II Salary Grade 9, one (1) position of Mining Claims Examiner I Salary Grade 10, and one (1) position of Computer Programmer III Salary Grade 18 under the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) shall be reclassified into:
One (1) Administrative Officer IV (Administrative Officer I) Salary Grade 15
One (1) Environmental Management Specialist I Salary Grade 11;
One (1) Administrative Assistant III (Computer Operator II) Salary Grade 9;
One (1) Engineering Assistant Salary Grade 8;
One (1) Administrative Assistant II (Clerk IV) Salary Grade 8;
SECTION 5. MINIMUM QUALIFICATION STANDARDS. The minimum qualification standards for the reclassified positions shall be the following:
A. Minimum Qualification Standards pursuant to CSC MC No. 5, Series of 2016, and COA Resolution No. 2013-19 for Administrative Officer IV (Administrative Officer II) Salary Grade 15 (Updated Qualification Standards for Certain Positions per CSC and COA Issuances)
1. Bachelor's degree relevant to the job;
2. Four (4) hours of relevant training;
3. One (1) year of relevant experience
4. Career Service (Professional) Second Level Eligibility;
B. Minimum Qualification Standards for Environmental Management Specialist I Salary Grade 11:
1. Career Service (Professional) Second Level Eligibility;
2. Bachelor's degree relevant to the job;
C. Minimum Qualification Standards for Administrative Assistant III (Computer Operator II) SG-9:
1. Data Encoder (MC 6, s.2010) Career Service (Sub-Professional) First Level Eligibility;
2. Completion of two (2) years of studies in College or High School graduate with relevant vocational/trade course;
3. One (1) year of relevant experience;
4. Four (4) hours of relevant training.

SECTION 6. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

A. Administrative Officer IV (Administrative Officer II) SG-15:
1. Assist in supervising the administrative functions of the office relative to records management, personnel administration, supply and property utilization, budgeting, janitorial services, including maintenance and repair;
2. Undertake and coordinate in the administration of fringe benefits and other employee services;
3. Undertake the dissemination of latest issuances relative to the functions of the office; and
4. Do other functions as may be directed by the Department Head.

B. Environmental Management Specialist I SG-11:
1. Assist the Section Chief in the provision of technical assistance for the implementation of the Upland Community-Based Project and other related income generating projects;
2. Assist the Section Chief in the formulation and implementation of plans, programs related to the management of Upland Community-Based Projects and effective Forest Management System;
3. Assist the Section Chief in the supervision and management of Established/Developed Upland Community-based Forest Management Project;
4. Conduct regular monitoring of all activities involved in Upland Community Development; and
5. Do other related functions as may be directed by the Department Head.

C. Administrative Assistant III (Computer Operator II) SG-9:
1. Perform various computer operations, encode and print data such as reports, transmittals, financial statements, etc.;

3. Prepare draft of routine official letters and endorsements; prepare official correspondence upon oral or written instructions of superior;  
4. Perform clerical tasks such as, preparation and computation of leave credits, preparation of certification of records and correspondence;  
5. Assist in performing general office functions such as doing frontline services to clients, sorting of official documents, mails, etc.; and  
6. Do other functions as may be directed by the Department Head.  
SECTION 7. APPROPRIATION - The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall appropriate the necessary funds to cover the benefits and salaries of the positions stated in this Ordinance.  
SECTION 8. AUTOMATIC REVIEW - The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall automatically review this Ordinance once every three (3) years after its approval or whenever the need for such review arises.  
SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances, resolutions, executive orders, memoranda and other such issuances in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
