AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION (BFP) REGION XI, RELATIVE TO THE ASSESSMENT AND COLLECTION OF FIRE SAFETY INSPECTION FEES

Ord. No. 0701-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

Section 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION (BFP) REGION XI, RELATIVE TO THE ASSESSMENT AND COLLECTION OF FIRE SAFETY INSPECTION FEES."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 16 and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provide, to wit:

Section 16. General Welfare. Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient, effective, and economical governance which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of the economy through the utilization of natural and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity, promote full employment among their resident, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

Section 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.
xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:
xxx
(2) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Region XI, relative to the assessment and collection of Fire Safety Inspection Fees.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
ENACTED, on the 7th day of September 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
