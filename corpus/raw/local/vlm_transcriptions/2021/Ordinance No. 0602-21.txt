AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUPTO TO BE SUBMITTED INTO, BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION-REGION XI, RELATIVE TO THE USE OF THE PROPERTY SITUATED IN CATIGAN, TORIL DISTRICT, THIS CITY, CONTAINING AN AREA OF FIVE THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED TWENTY-FOUR (5,124) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, TO SERVE AS THE SITE OF CATIGAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Ord. No. 0602-21  

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUCT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION-REGION XI, RELATIVE TO THE USE OF THE PROPERTY SITUATED IN CATIGAN, TORIL DISTRICT, THIS CITY, CONTAINING AN AREA OF FIVE THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED TWENTY-FOUR (5,124) SQUARE METERS MORE OR LESS, TO SERVE AS THE SITE OF CATIGAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL."  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16, General Welfare, of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991" provides:  

"... local government units shall ensure and support among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, protect the environment, safeguard their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."  

SECTION 3. DEED OF USUFRUCT - The Deed of Usufruct refers to the instrument to be executed by and between the City of Davao and the Department of Education-Catigan National High School.  

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Usufruct by and between the City of Davao and the Department of Education - Region XI, relative to the use of the property situated in Catigan, Toril District, this City, containing an area of Five Thousand One Hundred Twenty-Four (5,124) square meters more or less, to serve as the site of Catigan National High School.  

SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.  

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.  

ENACTED, on the 25th day of May 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

TERM: That this Contract of Usufruct shall be effective for a period of TWENTY FIVE (25) years from the time of the signing of this contract renewable upon the consent of both parties. However, in the event that the USUFRUCTUARY ceases to use or operate the property for which it is intended the Usufruct is automatically terminated and all the improvements theron shall be forfeited in favor of the Owner.

PURPOSE: The property shall be used solely and exclusively as CATIGAN HIGH SCHOOL.

MAINTENANCE: The USUFRUCTUARY shall maintain the land in accordance with the existing rules and regulation and preserve the same in habitable condition during the existence of Usufruct.

The USUFRUCTUARY is authorized to construct new buildings, make repairs, including renovations, provided that the same shall conform to the standard prescribed by law.

LIABILITY: The USUFRUCTUARY shall be solely liable for damages or criminal liability arising from its negligence while in possession of the lot subject of this usufruct.

MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

1. During the existence of Usufruct, the USUFRUCTUARY is absolutely prohibited from alienating its rights on the usufruct, nor leases the same to any third person.

2. That any violation of the terms and conditions of this Usufruct is a valid ground for its termination.
