Ord. No. 0614-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY THE CITY OF DAVAO IN FAVOR OF SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER (SPMC), RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF FIVE (5) UNITS SHINE POWER AIR PURIFYING RESPIRATORS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 25 (b) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:

"SECTION 25. (b). National agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation of said national projects."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation executed by the City of Davao in favor of Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC), relative to the donation of five (5) units Shine Power Air Purifying Respirators.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 15th day of June 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head)
