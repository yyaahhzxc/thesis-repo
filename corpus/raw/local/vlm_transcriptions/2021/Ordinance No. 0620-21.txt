**SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION ARRANGEMENTS for the KALAIHI CIDSS-KKB**

This is a supplementary agreement to the Memorandum of Agreement signed between the DSWD, and City Government of DAVAO. It details the specific implementation arrangements for the KALAIHI CIDSS-KKB in the Local Government Unit (LGU).

**Article I ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES**

**Section 1. Roles and Responsibilities of the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)**

The DSWD shall:

1. Assign an Area Coordinating Team (ACT) at the City, composed of an Area Coordinator, Technical Facilitator, City Financial Analyst, and Community Empowerment Facilitators.

The ACT shall be tasked to coordinate with the Inter-agency Task Force on Emerging Infectious Diseases (IATF-III) and the Local Disaster Risk Reduction Management Council (LDRRMC) to facilitate the proper implementation of the Program's Disaster Response Operations Manual/Procedures (DROM/P). The ACT shall conduct monitoring, technical assistance provision, and capability-building interventions to the City Coordinating Team (CCT) and the Community Voluteurs (CVs), to ensure that participatory development processes are correctly designed, implemented, monitored, evaluated, and operated, and maintenance of sub-project are compliant to procedural, technical and financial standards set by the Program as reflected in its various manuals and implementation guidelines;

2. Provide grants for community sub-projects identified through a participatory, transparent, and open process of community prioritization, following Program guidelines for the purpose. The total amount of indicative community grants shall depend on the initial category of city under the Program. This grant for community projects shall be disbursed directly to respective bank accounts opened for the prioritized barangays.

3. Provide grants to barangays, upon request and subject to Program guidelines, for the preparation of community sub-project proposals and/or to support specific aspects of sub-project implementation, for and of selective types of sub-projects;

4. Promote, through the ACT and Regional Program Management Office, in partnership with the Regional and Provincial Department of Interior and Local Government and the City Local Government Operations Officer (CLGOO), the active participation of provincial, city, and barangay local government units in Program implementation, facilitate enhanced functionality of City and Barangay Development Plans, and ensure wide acceptance and ownership of KALAIHI CIDSS principles and processes by the Provincial, City, and Barangay LGUs.

--- [Page 13] ---
5. Establish linkages with other National Government Agencies (NGA), Donor and other Development Agencies, Private Foundations, and Civil Society Organizations, to leverage development assistance resources, technical assistance, and other forms of support for Program cities and communities;

Section 2. Roles and Responsibilities of the Area Coordinating Team of DSWD

The ACT of DSWD shall:

1. Coordinate with the IATF-EID and LDRRMC on the introduction, formulation, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of KALAAHI-CIDSS KKB programs to be undertaken in the City in response to COVID-19;

2. The ACT shall ensure that all the Disbursement Vouchers and supporting documents of implementing Barangay Local Government Units (BLGUs) are reviewed and conforms with existing government accounting and auditing laws, rules and regulations prior to any payment that will be made by the BLGU;

3. For wholly-QFP Funded Sub-projects, DSWD through the Area Coordinating Team, ensure the submission of the following reports by the BLGU to the DSWD Field Office in conformance to the existing government rules:

a. Reports of Checks Issued and Report of Disbursement/Utilization prepared by the Barangay Treasurer, approved by the Punong Barangay, certified by the City Accountant, noted by the City Local Government Unit (CLGU) Local Chief Executive and duly received by the auditor of the LGU. This shall be submitted within ten (10) days after the end of each month and finally at the end of the agreed period for the Sub-Project Implementation.

b. Upon completion of the sub-project, Report of 100% physical accomplishment prepared by the BSPMC Chairperson duly approved by the Punong Barangay, and noted by the City Local Chief Executive.

c. If any, copy of the Official Receipt issued by the DSWD for the refund of unexpended balance.

Section 3. Roles and Responsibilities of the CITY Government Unit

The CITY Government of DAVAO shall:

1. Pass relevant ordinances and/or resolutions in support of community-driven development and participatory governance, and implementation of the KALAAHI CIDSS- KKB DROF in the City, to include but shall not be limited to the following:

a. Institutionalization of People's Participation, Transparency, and Social Accountability, and development and implementation of strategies to mainstream the same in LGU development planning and poverty reduction projects, activities and programs (PAPs), that includes the DRKOM/P;

--- [Page 14] ---
b. Formation of City Inter-Agency Committee (CIAC) and City Coordinating Team (CCT) & designation of members. The CIAC and CCT shall plan the roles in the implementation of the KALAAHI CIDSS- KKB DROP. The city shall form the CCT with the same number of staff to that of the ACT. The CCT, especially the Community Empowerment Facilitators (CEFs), shall be full time staff to KALAAHI CIDSS - KKB;

c. Assigning Gender and Development (GAD) focal person who will ensure integration of Gender Sensitivity and Gender Responsiveness in the Project Implementation;

d. NGO and PO accreditation;

e. Support to Barangay Development Plan (BDP) formulation and BDP-City Development Plan integration;

f. Resolution allowing the participation/involvement and defining the roles of the CDRRMC and the BDRRMC in the implementation of the DROM/P; and

g. Resolution on the inclusion of the KALAAHI CIDSS Area Coordinator and (3) CVs as members of the CDRRMC for the duration of the project implementation;

Facilitate the convergence of the various National Government Agency (NGA) poverty reduction initiatives that are being implemented in the City;

3. Ensure the active involvement of the CLGOO and the City Health Officer (CHO) in the implementation of the KALAAHI CIDSS - KKB DROP;

4. Monitoring and evaluation activities of the LGU staff and other M&E conferences conducted at the city level;

5. Make accessible relevant information on the state of the City and the LGU, as well as on LGU plans, programs, and activities including, but not necessarily limited to, the following:

a. Income and expenditure (including city assets that may be tapped to support Program implementation, as well as liabilities that may hinder delivery of counterpart commitments - e.g., loans and commitments to other NGAs and institutions;

b. Budgets, including data and information used for formulating budgets;

c. LGU Plans and activities to address local poverty and development, including data and information used for formulating city plans; and

d. LGU plans and activities to assess the state of competency of LGU staff, and address competency gaps.

b. Increase PO - NGO representation in the CDC, and ensure PO - NGO representation in Local Special Bodies (LSB) at the city and barangay level;
