AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MUTUAL PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT BY AND AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT OFFICE XII, THE CITY OF DAVAO, BARANGAY MARILOG PROPER, MARILOG DISTRICT; GUILON AND LAPINIG PARENTS AND TEACHERS ASSOCIATION AND THE IP COUNCIL OF ELDERS RELATIVE TO THE MANAGEMENT AND OPERATION OF THE COMPLETELY CONSTRUCTED CLASSROOM FOR LUMAD IN BARANGAY MARILOG PROPER, MARILLOG DISTRICT, THIS CITY

Ord. No. 0683-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MUTUAL PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT, FIELD OFFICE XI, THE CITY OF DAVAO, BARANGAY MARILOG PROPER, MARILOG DISTRICT, GULION, LAPING AND LAPING PARENTS AND TEACHERS ASSOCIATION AND THE IP COUNCIL OF ELDER RELATIVE TO THE MANAGEMENT AND OPERATION OF THE COMPLETELY CONSTRUCTED CLASSROOM FOR LUMAD IN BARANGAY MARILOG PROPER, MARILOG DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, or the Local Government Code of 1991 states:

Section 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall;

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Mutual Partnership Agreement by and among the Department of Social Welfare and Development, Field Office XI, the City of Davao, Barangay Marilog Proper, Marilog District, Gulion and Laping Parents and Teachers Association and the IP Council of Elders relative to the management and operation of the completely constructed classroom for Lumad in Barangay Marilog Proper, Marilog District, this City.

SECTION 4. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, August 17, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MUTUAL PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT For the Operation and Maintenance of CONSTRUCTION OF CLASSROOM FOR LUMADS KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENT: This Agreement made and executed this day of April 2021 at Barangay Marilog Proper City of Davao, Davao Del Sur Province, Philippines by and between: The Department of Social Welfare and Development – Field Office XI, a National Government agency existing under the Republic of the Philippines with its main office at Ramon Magaysan Street, Corr. Damaso Suazo Street, Davao City, and represented by its KALAHI-CIDSS Regional Program Coordinator, LANI E. CUDINO, RSW, herein referred as the "DWSD"; The City Local Government Unit, a local government unit existing under the law of the Republic of the Philippines, located in the City of Davao, Province of Davao Del Sur, and represented by its Mayor HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE CARPIO hereinafter referred as "CLGU"; The Barangay Local Government Unit of Sitio Gulion and Sitio Lapinig, Barangay Marilog Proper in the City of Davao and represented by its Barangay Captain, HON. PURIFICACION A. SUYKO, hereinafter referred as "BLGU"; The Gulion and Lapinig Parents and Teachers Association an interim body established to operate and manage the KALAHI – CIDSS Construction of Classrooms for Lumads (CCLS) sub-project as part of Sitio Gulion and Lapinig Community Development Project of City of Davao, Davao Del Sur, Philippines, and by its President of Association, ARLYN M. PANGAYA and MARIO BALINA, hereinafter referred as the "ASSOCIATION"; -and- The IP Council of Elders represented by their Brgy. IPMR HON COSME A. NONGCAL with their office address and their Head of offices at Barangay Marilog Proper, City of Davao, herein referred to as the "Tribal Council". WITNESSETH THAT WHEREAS the DWSD through its KALAHI – CIDSS Program funded the sub-project intervention identified and funded by the Department of Education in consultation with the Indigenous People of Lumads; WHEREAS, the community with the leadership of the B5GPMC and the assistance provided by the local government units, BLGU, CLGU and other stakeholders was able to satisfactorily complete their sub – project, CONSTRUCTION OF ONE (1) UNIT TWO (2) CLASSROOM SCHOOL BUILDING; WHEREAS, the Program and the Community intends to sustain the delivery of services provided by the completed sub – project through proper and timely operation and maintenance activities; WHEREAS, in consultation and coordination with Department of Education (DepEd) and other stakeholders and beneficiaries, an Operation and Maintenance Plan for the sub – project was prepared and agreed to be implemented by the Project Management Committee responsible; WHEREAS, amenities and software needed to maximize the services are agreed to be shouldered by DepEd, other stakeholders and beneficiaries during the operation and maintenance period; NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the PARTIES do hereby mutually agree and bind themselves as follows:

--- [Page 5] ---
1. General Policies and Principles

1.1 That the Parents and Teachers Association (PTA) will ensure implementation of Operation and Maintenance Fund of the completed sub-project under the supervision of the Department of Education (DepEd).

1.2 That the provision of resources needed for the operation and maintenance will be the responsibility of the Department of Education, and as such shall be reflected on their Annual Budget for Maintenance and Other Operating Expense (MOOE);

1.3 That DSWD as the implementing agency of the KALAHI - CIDISS Program shall be responsible in coordinating and monitoring the compliance of the parties to the specifications of this agreement. Regular monitoring of O and M activities through Sustainability Evaluation Test (SET) shall be conducted once a year.

2. Roles

2.1 The following are the agreed commitments; roles and functions of the major stakeholders of the sub-project:

2.1.1 The Parents and Teachers Association of Sitio Gullon and Lapining Barangay Maring Proper shall efficiently/effectively manage and operate the completed CONSTRUCTION OF ONE (1) UNIT TWO (2) CLASSROOM SCHOOL BUILDING with support and supervision from the Department of Education (DepEd). They shall also determine the dates and the day to open operation and maintenance of the completed sub-project;

2.1.2 In case of unutilized O and M funds, the DepEd in consultation with the Parents & Teachers Association shall plan its usage for the improvement of the facility in place;

2.1.3 The Gullon and Lapining Parents & Teachers Association shall endeavor to establish appropriate mechanisms to fully manage and operate and thus, become a self-sustaining organization;

2.1.4 The Gullon and Lapining Parents & Teachers Association shall establish partnership with DEPED and other sectoral bodies on relevant technical, administrative, and operational matters related to operation and maintenance;

2.1.5 The Gullon and Lapining Parents & Teachers Association members and officers shall abide by their own policies, by-laws, as well as the applicable statutes of the Republic of the Philippines, local school policies promulgated by DEPED, and Local ordinances legislated by the LGU;

2.2 The Indigenous Peoples Tribal Council of Elders shall ensure that the completed CONSTRUCTION OF ONE (1) UNIT TWO (2) CLASSROOM SCHOOL BUILDING are used in accordance to its purpose and objectives:

2.2.1 The Indigenous Peoples Tribal Council of Elders in partnership with DepEd, the Parents and Teachers Association (PTA), and other stakeholders shall promote IP education to its constituents;

2.2.2 The Indigenous Peoples Tribal Council of Elders shall commit to encourage children from the Indigenous Peoples community to patronize the school and participate in extracurricular activities;

2.2.3 The Indigenous Peoples Tribal Council of Elders shall commit to participate in the Operations and Maintenance activities through the annual "Brigada Eskwela" and other related activities spearheaded by the Department of Education.

--- [Page 6] ---
2.3 The Barangay Local Government Unit (BLGU) shall provide the necessary support to ensure the efficient and effective management and operation of the completed CONSTRUCTION OF ONE (1) UNIT TWO (2) CLASSROOM SCHOOL BUILDING SUB - PROJECT.
2.3.1 The BLGU shall exercise oversight function on the planning and proper usage of unutilized Q and M funds for the improvement of the facility in place;
2.3.2 The BLGU shall ensure the compliance of the Association to their formulated policies and by - laws;
2.3.3 The BLGU shall ensure the protection, conservation and management of facilities of the completed CONSTRUCTION OF ONE (1) UNIT TWO (2) CLASSROOM SCHOOL BUILDING SUB - project and ;
2.3.4 The BLGU shall ensure the regular submission of physical and financial reports to all monitoring bodies.
2.4 The City Local Government Unit (. CLGU) in general shall assist the DepEd and the Parents and Teachers Association be responsible for mobilizing other external for the conduct of major repair and maintenance works of the completed CONSTRUCTION OF ONE (1) UNIT TWO (2) CLASSROOM SCHOOL BUILDING Sub - Project and other works requiring the use of special equipment.
2.4.1 The CLGU and Other Stakeholders will provide technical and administrative assistance in the implementation and monitoring of the Operation and Maintenance of the completed sub - project to include the conduct of Sustainability Evaluation Test (SET) in an annual basis;
2.4.2 The CLGU shall ensure that the City Accountant certification that the funds from the SETs be transferred by DSWD to the BLGU has been liquidated and taken up in the books;
2.4.3 The CLGU shall provide assistance in the establishment of networks to different agencies and sectoral bodies on relevant technical, administrative and operative matters on operation and maintenance as indicated in the approved O and M Plan.
2.5 The Department of Education (DepEd) shall ensure functionality of the completed sub-projects by assuring that Operations and Maintenance (O & M) Plan are in place;
2.5.1 The DepEd shall ensure that the completed sub-projects are officially registered in its book of assets and properties;
2.5.2 The DepEd shall ensure that Q&M financial requirements are incorporated to the annual maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOEES);
2.5.3 The DepEd shall supervise and coordinate with the Parents and Teachers Association (PTA) and other stakeholders to mobilize for other resources for the Operations and Maintenance purposes.
2.6 The Department of Social Welfare and Development shall conduct continuing sub - project post - implementation evaluation to determine Project's effectiveness and sustainability. Document success stories and good practices for adoption and replication;
2.6.1 The DSWD shall continue to provide technical assistance in the operation and maintenance of the completed Sub - project that would lead to the pursuit and attainment of the Project goal.
3. OTHER PROVISIONS

--- [Page 7] ---
3.1 By mutual consent, this Agreement or any part thereof may be changed, modified and amended or supplemented for the purpose of effective, quality and sustainable O and M.
3.2 Provided, however, that the modifications or revisions are in conformity to the requirements set forth in O and M in the Philippines and that all provisions of the Mutual Partnership Agreement are still met.
4. EFFECTIVITY
This Mutual Partnership Agreement shall take effect immediately after being signed by the parties herein.
