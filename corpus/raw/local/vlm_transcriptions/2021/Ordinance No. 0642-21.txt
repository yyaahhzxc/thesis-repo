Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH), DAVAO CITY 2nd DISTRICT ENGINEERING OFFICE, AND THE BARANGAY COUNCILS OF BIAO JOAQUIN, DOMINGA, INAYANGAN, LACSON, MEKAGAWAN, RIVERSIDE, SIRIB, AND TAMAYONG, ALL IN CALNAIN DISTRICT, RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION AND IMPLEMENTATION OF INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS UNDER THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT DEVELOPMENT PLAN (LGDP) AND THE BARANGAY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (BLGP-DP) OF THE NATIONAL TASK FORCE TO END LOCAL COMMUNIST ARMED CONFLICT (NTF-ELAC) PURSUANT TO EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 70, SUBJECT TO GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING, AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES, THE PROFESSIONAL CODE OF ETHICS AND CONDUCT ON AUDIT, THE PROCUREMENT LAW AS WELL AS OTHER APPLICABLE LAWS AND REGULATIONS.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN THE NAME OF THE CITY OF DAVAO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH) - DAVAO CITY 2ND DISTRICT ENGINEERING OFFICE, AND THE BARANGAY COUNCILS OF BIAO JOAQUIN, DOMINGA, INAYANGAN, LACSON, MEGKAWAYAN, RIVERSIDE, SIRIB, AND TAMAYONG, ALL IN CALINAN DISTRICT, RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION AND IMPLEMENTATION OF INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS UNDER THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT SUPPORT FUND TO THE BARANGAY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (LGSD-FBDP) OF THE NATIONAL TASK FORCE TO END LOCAL COMMUNIST ARMED CONFLICT (NATTF-LCAC), INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO ACTIVITIES RELATED TO GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING, AND AUDITTING RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT, THE COMMISSION ON AUDIT, THE PROCUREMENT LAW AS WELL AS OTHER APPLICABLE LAWS."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 22 (a) (5) (c) and Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, state as follows:

Section 22. Corporate Powers. (a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers: xxx

(5) To enter into contracts; and xxx

(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the Sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation. xxx

(b). For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall: xxx

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement by and among the City of Davao, the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) – Davao City 2nd District Engineering Office, and the Barangay Councils of Bao Joaquin, Dominga, Inayangan, Lacson, Megkawayan, Riverside, Sirib, and Tamayong, All in Calinan District, relative to the implementation of the Local Government Support Fund to the Barangay Development Program (LGGSF-BDP) of the National Task Force 18, and Local Communist Armed Conflict (NTF-ELAC) pursuant to Executive Order No. 70, subject to Government Budgeting, Accounting, and Auditing rules and Regulations of the Department of Budget and Management, the Commission on Audit, the Procurement Law as well as other applicable laws.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 6th day of July 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
