Be it Ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 1, OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2022, CONSISTING OF THE GENERAL FUND PROPER, THE DEVELOPMENT FUND AND THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES, IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF FOUR HUNDRED NINETEEN MILLION SEVEN HUNDRED EIGHTY-SEVEN THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED SEVENTY-FOUR PESOS AND 76/100 (P419,787,174.76)."  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time, ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use.  

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS. The following amounts or so much thereof, as may be necessary, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Treasurer of Davao City, not otherwise appropriated from the operation of the City Government of Davao from January 1 to December 31, 2022, except as otherwise provided therein.  

SECTION 4. AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES. The Supplemental Budget No. 1 of the City Government of Davao, for Calendar Year 2022, covers the total Sources of Funds in the total amount of FOUR HUNDRED NINETEEN MILLION SEVEN HUNDRED EIGHTY-SEVEN THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED SEVENTY-FOUR PESOS AND 76/100 (P419,787,174.76), broken down as follows:

SECTION 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS. The amount herein appropriated for the projects indicated therein shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sanggunian Panglungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget must conform with existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules, regulations, policies and guidelines of the Commission on Audit (COA), Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.  

SECTION 7. USE OF FUNDS. The use of funds under Supplemental Budget No. 1 of the City Government of Davao, for Calendar Year 2022, utilized in violation of Title 5, Local Fiscal Administration, as embodied in RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, shall subject the erring officials and employees to disciplinary action or to appropriate legal measures under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget must conform with existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules, regulations, policies and guidelines of the Commission on Audit (COA), Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.  

SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any Section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof, which are not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.  

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY. The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.  

ENACTED, on February 22, 2022, by an unanimous vote of the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
