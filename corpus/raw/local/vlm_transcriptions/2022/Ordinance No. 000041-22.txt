ORDINANCE NO. 041-22
SERIES OF 2022

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (DRRMF) QUICK RESPONSE FUND OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2022, IN THE AMOUNT OF THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P300,000.00) AS FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE MUNICIPALITY OF CABANGAN, ZAMBALES, WHICH WAS DECLARED UNDER A STATE OF CALAMITY DUE TO SUPER TYPHOON KARDING

Ord. No. 041-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE — This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE CALENDAR YEAR 2022, IN THE AMOUNT OF THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P300,000.00) FOR FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE MUNICIPALITY OF CABANGAN, ZAMBALES, WHICH WAS DECLARED UNDER A STATE OF CALAMITY BUT IS SUBJECT TO FURTHER NARRATION."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY — It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to adopt measures and adhere to the national principles and standards of humanitarian assistance in response to risk reduction and declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.

SECTION 3. BENEFICIARIES — The Municipality of Cabangan, Zamboanga, which was placed under the State of Calamity, is hereby declared as a beneficiary of the financial assistance and shall receive an amount of Three Hundred Thousand Pesos (P300,000.00).

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASIS — Section 324 (d) of Republic Act No. 7160 and the Local Government Code of 1991, as amended by Republic Act No. 8185, states that "five percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as annual lump sum appropriations for relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction, and other works or services in connection with calamities which may occur during the budget year. Provided, however, that such fund shall be used only in the area, or a portion thereof, of the local government concerned which is declared a disaster or calamity, as determined and declared by the local sanggunian concerned."

Section 5 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 8185 likewise states that "LGUs may also allocate/use a portion of the five percent (5%) Calamity Fund to other affected areas on condition that the said areas are declared as under a State of Calamity by the Sanggunian concerned." It further provides that "a portion of the calamity fund may also be authorized to be used by the LGU concerned to provide financial assistance to the municipality whose area or portion thereof has been declared under a state of calamity by its Sanggunian."

SECTION 5. UTILIZATION OF FUNDS — The amount herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such item and expenditure approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.
