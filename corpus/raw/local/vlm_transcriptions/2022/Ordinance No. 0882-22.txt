Ord. No. 0882-22.
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY
GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY
AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND SUBASTA INTEGRATED FARMERS MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE".
SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into
and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Subasta Integrated Farmers Multipurpose Cooperative.
SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, on the 15th day of February, 2022, by an unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
