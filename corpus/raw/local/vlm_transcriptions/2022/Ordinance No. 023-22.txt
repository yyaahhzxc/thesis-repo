Ord. No. 023-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ALONG JUNCTION VELOSO ST. -- NICASIO TORRES STREET GOING TO ABREZZA MALL FROM SEPTEMBER 14 UP TO OCTOBER 30, 2022, TO HASTEN THE COMPLETION OF THE REMAINING 16-METER REINFORCED CONCRETE BOX CULVERT (DPWH 1 PROJECT)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21(c) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:
"Any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, and agricultural or industrial fair".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The road along Junction Veloso St. -- Nicasio Torres St., going to Abrezzo Mall will be temporarily closed from September 14 up to October 30, 2022, in order to hasten the completion of the remaining unfinished reinforcement of concrete box culvert.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 13th day of September 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT;
