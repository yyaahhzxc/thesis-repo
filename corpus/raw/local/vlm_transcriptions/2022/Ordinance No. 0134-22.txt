Ordinance No. 0134-22
Series of 2022

An Ordinance for the Temporary Closure of Road to Vehicular Traffic on December 13, 2022 until June 30, 2022, Particularly the Portion of Bukidnon Road (Talomo Section) - Davao - Bukidnon Road (Catalunan Pequeño - Mintal Section - Calinan Section) by Pass Road (Viaduct) (Partial Substructure), Brgy. Talomo Proper and Brgy. Catalunan Pequeño relative to the request of Rey H., Sucatana Construction, Gamma Construction Supply Inc. in connection with the DPWH XI Project with Contract ID 21LO-0148

Ord. No. 0134-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON DECEMBER 13, 2022 UNTIL JUNE 30, 2023 PARTIALLY THE PORTION OF COASTAL ROAD (TALOMO SECTION) - DAVAO - BUKIDNON ROAD (Catalunan Pequeno - MINTAL SECTION - Calinan Section) BY PASS ROAD (VIADUCT) (PARTIAL SUBSTRUCTURE), BRGY. TALOMO PROPER AND BRGY. CATALUNAN PEQUENO RELATIVE TO THE REQUEST OF REY H. SUCAYAN, DIRECTOR, JEMMA CONSTRUCTION SUPPLY INC., IN CONNECTION WITH THE DPWH XI PROJECT WITH CONTRACT ID 2110- 5248".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21(c) of Republic Act No. 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, a fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and water works projects.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - JEMMA CONSTRUCTION INC. requested for the extension of the road closure affecting their project site construction in the specified area on the abovementioned details.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

CHARITO N. SANTOS
