Ord. No. 091-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON NOVEMBER 21, 2022 (12 MN) UP TO NOVEMBER 23, 2022 (12 MN) IN MANDALAY STREET BOUNDED BY A. PICHON STREET AND DATU BAGO EXTENSION CONNECTION BETWEEN THE PRE AND POST LAUNCHING OF PALENGKE QR-PH PROGRAM BY BANGKOK SENTRAL NG PILIPINAS (BSP) AND THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT REGION XI (DILG-XI) TO ADOPT DIGITAL PAYMENTS IN MARKETS, SARI-SARI STORES, TRICYCLE DRIVERS, CONSUMERS AND COMMUTERS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, a fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and waterworks projects".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The street along Marfori bounded by A. Pichon Street and Datu Bago Extension will be temporarily closed on November 21, 2022 (12 MN) up to November 23, 2022 (12 MN) in connection with the Pre and Post Launching of the Palengke QR-PH Program.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 15th day of November 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
