Ord. No. 0122-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR A LOAN FACILITY TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND CSSDO EMPLOYEES MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement for a Loan Facility to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and CSSDO Employees Multipurpose Cooperative.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, December 07, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
