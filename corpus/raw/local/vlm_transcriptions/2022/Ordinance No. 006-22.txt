Ord. No. 006-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON THE INCLUSIVE DATES AND TIME HEREIN LISTED IN CONNECTION WITH THE REQUEST OF THE CITY TOURISM OPERATIONS OFFICE (CTOO) FOR THE CELEBRATION OF THE 37TH KADAYAWAN SA DAVAO FESTIVAL".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21(c) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebration, public rallies, and agricultural or industrial fair".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The following roads herein mentioned will be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic along specified routes inclusive of dates and time listed in connection with the 37th Kadayawan sa Davao Festival.
