AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE - REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, THE CITY OF DAVAO AND FARDECO AGRICULTURAL MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE, RELATING TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE INTEGRATED NATIONAL SWINE PRODUCTION INITIATIVE TO RECOVER AND EXPANSION (INSPIRE) PROGRAM - SWINE CLUSTERING AMOUNTING TO FIVE MILLION FIVE THOUSAND PESOS (P 5,500,000.00)

Ord. No. 01005-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE - REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, THE CITY OF DAVAO, AND TAREDO AGRICULTURAL MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE, RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE INTEGRATED NATIONAL SWINE PRODUCTION INITIATIVE TO RECOVERY AND EXPANSION (INSPIRE) PROGRAM – SWINE CLUSTERING AMOUNTING TO FIVE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (PHP 5,500,000.00).

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 25 (b) and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provide, to wit:

Section 25. National supervision over Local Government Units-
XXX
XXX
XXX
(b) National agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation of said national projects."

Section 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, Memorandum of Agreement entered into by and among the Department of Agriculture Regional Field Office XI, TAREDO Agricultural Multipurpose Cooperative, and the City of Davao, relative to the implementation of the Integrated National Swine Production Initiative to Recovery and Expansion (INSPIRE) Program – Swine Clustering amounting to Five Million Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (Php 5,500,000.00).

SECTION 4. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect
