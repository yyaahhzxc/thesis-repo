AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND
SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF
DONATION (DDD) TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY
OF DAVAO AND THE CHINESE REPUBLIC OF CHINA IN DAVAO CITY,
AND THE OFFICE OF DAVAO CITY'S FIRST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT REPRESENTED BY HON. PAOLO Z. DUTERTE, AS DONORS, RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF 320 UNITS OF
HOUSEHOLD SEWING MACHINES, IDENTIFIED AS JAV-2 SEWING
MACHINE WITH PORTABLE PLASTIC CASE AND MOTOR, IN
RECOGNITION AND SUPPORT OF THE DIGNITY, IMPROVEMENT OF THE
INDIGENOUS CULTURAL COMMUNITIES/INDIGENOUS PEOPLES
(ICC/S/IPS) AND ENHANCING THEIR QUALITY OF LIFE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN REHABILITATION OF THE INDIGENOUS CULTURAL HERITAGE (ICC) ACTIVITIES TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE CONSULATE GENERAL OF THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA IN DAVAO CITY, AND THE OFFICE OF DAVAO CITY'S FIRST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT REPRESENTED BY HON. PAOLO Z. DUTERTE, AS DONORS, RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF 320 UNITS OF HOUSEHOLD SEWING MACHINES, IDENTIFIED AS JAZ-2 SEWING MACHINE WITH PORTABLE PLASTIC CASE AND MOTOR, IN RECOGNITION AND SUPPORT OF THE DONOR IN EMPOWERING THE INDIGENOUS CULTURAL HERITAGE ACTIVITIES/INDIGENOUS CULTURAL HERITAGE ACTIVITIES (ICC/ICS) AND ENHANCING THEIR QUALITY OF LIFE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into, sign, for, and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation (DOD) to be executed by and between the City of Davao and the Consulate General of the People's Republic of China in Davao City, and the Office of Davao City's First Congressional District represented by Hon. Paolo Z. Duterte, as Donors, relative to the donation of 320 units of Household Sewing Machines, identified as JAZ-2 Sewing Machine with Portable Electric Case and Motor, in recognition and support of the Donee in empowering and aiding the Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (CCS/IPs) and enhancing their quality of life and economic development.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
