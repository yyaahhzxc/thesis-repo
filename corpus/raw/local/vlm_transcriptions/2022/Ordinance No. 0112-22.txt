Ord. No. 0112-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE TOTAL ROAD CLOSURE OF THE DAVAO CITY DIVERSION ROAD, THIS CITY, ON DECEMBER 4, 2022 AT 1:00 AM FOR THE INSTALLATION OF GIRDS AS PART OF THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE PEDESTRIAN OVERPASS".

SECTION 2. PERTINENT PROVISIONS UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160 OR THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991

Section 25. National Supervision over Local Government Unit.-xxx

(h) National agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation of said government projects.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the approval of the total road closure of the Davao City Diversion Road, this City, on December 4, 2022 at 1:00 AM for the installation of girders as part of the construction of the pedestrian overpass.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 29th day of November 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
