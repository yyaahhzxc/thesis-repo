AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF ATENEO DE DAVAO UNIVERSITY GS '93 HS '97, INC., A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION, FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUND-RAISING ACTIVITY/EXHIBITION ON DECEMBER 30, 2022, AND AN ENDORSEMENT BY THE CITY CONSIDERING THAT A PORTION OF THE PROCEEDS OF SAID EVENT WILL BE DONATED TO CHosen BENEFICIARIES OF THE ORGANIZATION

Ord. No. 0128-22

'Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE REQUEST OF ATENEO DE DAVAO UNIVERSITY GS '93 HS '97,
ENGINEERS AND ARCHITECTS ASSOCIATION OF THE PHILIPPINES TO
EXEMPT FROM AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUND RAISING
ACTIVITY/CONCERT ON DECEMBER 30, 2022 AT THE ENDERLE TENT,
AZUELA COVE OF THIS CITY CONSIDERING THAT A PORTION OF THE
PROCEEDS OF SAID EVENT WILL BE DONATED TO CHOSEN BENEFICIARIES
OF THE ORGANIZATION".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act 7160,
otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines, provides that,
"Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those
necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental
for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the
promotion of the general welfare.xxx".

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Section 66(b) of the 2017 Revenue Code of
Davao City provides that, "(b) where the admission fees are collected in connection with
the holding of the operas, concerts, dramas, recitals, paintings and art exhibitions,
flowers, shows, musical programs, literary and oratorical presentations except pop rock,
or similar concerts not intended for profit and furthermore, not including film
exhibition".

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other
sections or provisions shall be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after
approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head II)
