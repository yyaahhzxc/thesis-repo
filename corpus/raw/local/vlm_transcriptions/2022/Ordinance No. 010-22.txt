AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC (FULL ROAD CLOSURE) ON AUGUST 17, 2022 UNTIL SEPTEMBER 17, 2022 PARTICULARLY THE STREETS ALONG THE BYPASS ROAD AT JUNCTION DAVAO-COTABATO ROAD- CENTRAL PARK (CORDILLERA ROAD) - JUNCTION MATINA APLAYA ROAD FOR THE DPWH R-XI PROJECT

Ord. No. 010-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC (P.M. ROAD CLOSING) AND AUGUST 20, 2022 UNTIL SEPTEMBER 17, 2022 PARTICULARLY THE STREETS ALONG THE BYPASS ROAD AT JUNCTION DAVAO-COTABATO ROAD-CENTRAL PARK (CORDILLERA ROAD) - JUNCTION MATINA APIAYA ROAD FOR THE DPWH R-XI PROJECT".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act 7159, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebration, public rallies, and agricultural or industrial fair for an underlying of public works and highways, telecommunication and water works projects".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The streets along the ByPass Road at Junction Davao-Cotabato Road-Central Park (Cordillera Road) - Junction Matina Apiaya Road will be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on August 17, 2022 up to September 17, 2022 for the DPWH R-XI Project.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 16th day of August 2022, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
