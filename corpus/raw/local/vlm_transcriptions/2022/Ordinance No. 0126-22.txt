ORDINANCE NO. 0126-22
Series of 2022.

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF THE UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES MINDANAO FOUNDATION, INC. (UPMFI), A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION, FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX FOR THEIR UPCOMING FUNDRAISING CONCERT TO BE HELD AT THE STADIUM AND CULTURAL CENTER, THIRD ST. BO, OBRERO ON DECEMBER 20, 2022, CONSIDERING THAT A PORTION OF SAID CONCERT WILL BE DONATED TO THE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM OF THE UPMFI

Ord. No. 0126-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF THE UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES MINORITIES AND INDIGENOUS PEOPLE (UPMMP), A NON-PROFIT CORPORATION, FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX FOR THEIR UPCOMING FUNDRAISING CONCERT TO BE HELD AT USP SPORTS AND CULTURAL CENTER, INIGO ST., BO. OBRERO ON DECEMBER 20, 2022 CONSIDERING THAT A PORTION OF THE PROCEEDS OF SAID CONCERT WILL BE DONATED TO THE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM OF THE UPMMP".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines provides that, "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare...".

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Section 66(a) and (b) of the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City provides that, "(a) where the admission fees are collected for and in behalf of the charitable, educational, or religious institutions or associations who are declared by law or presidential proclamation as exempted from the payment of amusement tax on paid admission, provided further, that such exhibition, show, performance and the like shall be limited only to three (3) days in a calendar year; (b) where admission fees are collected in connection with the holding of the operas, concerts, dramas, recitals, paintings and art exhibitions, flowers, shows, musical programs, literary and oratorical presentations except pop rock, or similar concerts not intended for profit and furthermore, not including film exhibition";

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other sections or provisions shall be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after approval.

ENACTED, on the 9th day of December 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II)
