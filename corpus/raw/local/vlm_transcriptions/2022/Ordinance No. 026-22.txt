An Ordinance Partially Approving the Request of Johndorf Ventures Corporation for the Rezoning of the Parcels of Land from Residential-2 (Medium Density Residential Sub-Zone) to Residential-3 (High Density Residential Sub-Zone), Located at Barangay Mulig, Toril District, Davao City, Particularly the 18,288.41 square meters portion of the property covered by TCT No. 146-2022, and the 17,538.41 square meters portion of the property covered by TCT No. 2000026645, subject to the compliance of the applicant of the required concrete barriers as mitigating measure to protect the property from flooding

Be it ordained by the Sanggunan Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "AN ORDINANCE PARTIALLY APPROVING THE REQUEST OF JOHNDORF VENTURES CORPORATION FOR THE REZONING OF THE PARCELS OF LAND FROM RESIDENTIAL-2 (MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL SUB-ZONE) TO RESIDENTIAL-3 (HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL SUB-ZONE), LOCATED AT BARANGAY MULIG, TORIL DISTRICT, DAVAO CITY, PARTICULARLY THE 18,268.41 SQUARE METERS PORTION OF THE PROPERTY COVERED BY TCT NO. 146-2020022645 AND THE 18,360.98 SQUARE METERS PORTION OF THE PROPERTY COVERED BY TCT NO. 146-2020022645, SUBJECT TO THE COMPLIANCE OF THE APPLICANT WITH THE REQUIRED CONCRETE BARRIERS AS MITIGATING MEASURE TO PROTECT THE PROPERTY FROM FLOODING."

SECTION 2. COMMON REGULATIONS FOR GENERAL ZONES - Article V of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2014) provides for the following Common Regulations for General Zones, to wit:

Section 1 of Article V is entitled REVIEW OF GENERAL ZONES which states that general zones are subject to review by the Zoning Review Committee every five (5) years from the enactment of this ordinance. Any amendment thereof shall be in accordance with the provision of Article XIV Section 14.

Section 2 of Article V is entitled RECLASSIFICATION AND CHANGE OF ZONE REQUIREMENT which states that reclassification from agricultural zone to non-agricultural use and any change from one general zone to another general zone must be approved by three-fourths (3/4) vote of all the members of the Sanggunan Panlungsod through a resolution and ordinance. Any change of general zone to another shall be considered as amendment if the zoning ordinance and must comply with the provision of Article XIV, Section 14 hereof.

SECTION 3. AMENDMENTS TO THE ZONING ORDINANCE - Article XIV, Section 14 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City provides that changes in the Zoning Ordinance as a result of the review by the Local Zoning Review Committee shall be treated as amendments provided that any amendment to the Zoning Ordinance or provisions thereof shall be subject to public hearing and sectoral consultation to be conducted by the Local Zoning Review Committee and review evaluation of the Zoning Review Committee and shall be carried out through a resolution/ordinance upon 3/4 majority votes of all the members of the Sanggunan Panlungsod.

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the partial grant of the application of Johndorf Ventures Corporation for partial approval of the rezoning of the parcels of land from Residential-2 (Medium Density Residential Sub-Zone) to Residential-3 (High Density Residential Sub-Zone), located at Barangay Mulig, Toril District, Davao City, particularly the 18,268.41 square meters portion of the property covered by TCT No. 146-2020022645 and the 18,360.98 square meters portion of the property covered by TCT No. 146-2020022645 subject to the compliance of the applicant with the required concrete barriers as mitigating measure to protect the property from flooding.
