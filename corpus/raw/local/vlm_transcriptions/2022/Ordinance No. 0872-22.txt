Ord. No. 0872-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND NATIONAL BOOK DEVELOPMENT BOARD (NBBD) RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PROJECT ENTITLED "THE BOOK NOOK PROJECT."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 22 (a) (5) and (c), and Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, or the Local Government Code of 1991, provide as follows:

Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers: xxx
(5) To enter into contracts; and xxx
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation. xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor, hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the National Book Development Board (NBBD) relative to the implementation of the project entitled "The Book Nook Project."

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY**  
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT**

**NATIONAL BOOK DEVELOPMENT BOARD AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY**

This Memorandum of Agreement is made and entered into this 1st day of June, 2021, in Quezon City, by and between:

The **NATIONAL BOOK DEVELOPMENT BOARD (NBDB)**, a government agency created and organized under Republic Act No. 8047, with office and postal address at 3rd & 4th Floors, Regalado Hive Building, Regalado Avenue, Fairview, Quezon City, Philippines, represented herein by its Executive Director, MARIBISES AQUINO-TU MADERO, hereinafter referred to as the "NBDB";

and

THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY, a local government unit organized and existing under the laws of the Philippines, with address at the City Hall, San Pedro St., Davao City, herein represented by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "DAVAO CITY",

together herinafter referred to as the "Parties,"

WHEREAS, the NBDB promotes the continuing development of the book publishing industry in all regions of the country as embodied in Republic Act No. 8047 or the "Book Publishing Industry Development Act";

WHEREAS, the local government of Davao City represents Davao City Library and Information Center, a division under the Office of the City Mayor established to promote the intellectual well-being of the people, elevate the literacy level of Davaoños, and recognize the vital role of knowledge and information of the locale in line with RA 7743;

WHEREAS, the Mayor of Davao City is the Chief Executive who represents the City in all its business transactions and signs on behalf of all contracts;

WHEREAS, Davao City Library and Information Center has expressed its willingness to partner and provide the necessary assistance in establishing a book nook corner within its library premises through a donation of books for the Book Nook Project;

WHEREAS, the Parties agree to establish a framework for cooperation to ensure the effective implementation of this project, hereinafter referred to as "The Book Nook Project", having agreed upon the following:

Article I - Objective

a) provide Book Nook package composed of Filipino-authored quality books and other reading materials
b) supplement the Filipiniana Collection of the public libraries through the Book Nook package;

d) facilitate the conduct of monitoring and evaluation activities geared towards ensuring the sustainability of the Book Nook Project.
2.Davao City has agreed and affirmed to:
a. acknowledge NDBB as one of its partners at its official website;
b. appoint a coordinator to represent Davao City Library and Information Center;
c. assist the NDBB in carrying out monitoring and evaluation activities to assess the impact of the project;
d. maintain accountability on the safekeeping of the reading materials;
e. coordinate and receive the Book Nook package through a courier from the NDBB office in Quezon City, and furnish the NDBB with an acknowledgement report and inventory of reading materials, duly signed by the authorized persons.
Article III - Term
This Memorandum of Agreement shall enter into force on the date the representatives of the Parties affix their signatures. It will be valid and effective until December 31, 2021, unless earlier revoked by any of the parties.
Article IV - Working Relationship
For purposes of the performance of the obligations under this M0A, no provision shall be deemed or construed to create an employer-employee relationship, joint venture, agency, or partnership between M0BB and its trustees, officers, agents, students and interns on one hand, and the Foundation, its directors, officers, agents, and agents, on the other hand. Neither Party shall have any authority to incur any obligation on behalf of the other Party or to make any undertaking, representation, or agreement of any nature on behalf of the other party.
Article V - Data Privacy
It is expressly understood by both Parties that in the course of this agreement, the Parties may be exposed to technical and/or personal information, and other information and data deemed as classified by either Party. In view of this, all parties shall ensure that such information and data remain confidential and shall undertake to prevent its disclosure or transfer, consciously or unconsciously, to any party outside of this agreement without the knowledge and written consent of all parties.
Article VI - Free and Harmless Clause
Each party shall hold each other free and harmless from any and all liabilities
