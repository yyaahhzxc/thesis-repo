ORDINANCE NO. 0486-16  
Series of 2016  

AN ORDINANCE ENJOINING ALL INDIVIDUALS, PRIVATE AND PUBLIC OFFICES OR ENTITIES TO USE "KAGAN" NOT "KALAGAN" IN THEIR FILES, DATA, RECORDS AND OTHER DOCUMENTS WHEN REFERRING TO THE "KAGAN", THOSE DEPTHS OF THE TRIBE FOR PUBLICATION, PUBLIC DISPLAY, PUBLIC USE, AND OTHER PURPOSES AND TO CORRECT ALL THEIR EXISTING FILES, DATA, RECORDS, AND OTHER DOCUMENTS TO REFLECT THE TRIBE'S NAME AS "KAGAN" NOT "KALAGAN"

Page 2 of 4  
Ord. No. 0486-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

ARTICLE I  
GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ENJOINING ALL INDIVIDUALS, PRIVATE AND PUBLIC OFFICES OR ENTITIES TO USE 'KAGAN' NOT 'KALAGAN' IN THEIR FILES, DATA, RECORDS AND OTHER DOCUMENTS WHEN REFERRING TO THE 'KAGAN' TRIBE AS DEFINED IN THIS ACT, FOR PUBLICATION, PUBLIC DISPLAY, PUBLIC USE, AND OTHER PURPOSES, TO CORRECT ALL THEIR EXISTING FILES, DATA, RECORDS AND OTHER DOCUMENTS TO REFLECT THE TRIBES NAME AS 'KAGAN' NOT 'KALAGAN'

SECTION 2. PURPOSE. The purpose of this Ordinance is to recognize and give due regard to the "Kagan" tribe, by informing the people and create public awareness that "Kagan" not "Kalagan" is the correct and proper name for their tribe. It also aims to use the term "Kagan" not "Kalagan" when referring to the Kagan Tribe, as well as to correct all existing files, data, records and other documents to achieve the purpose of this Ordinance.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.
a) Kagen - refers to indigenous group who originally embraced Islam that has distinct practices, culture and tradition, based in Davao City and other neighboring municipalities, cities and provinces all over Region XI.
b) Kalagan - is the unrefined term used by some when referring to the Kagan tribe as defined in the preceding paragraph.
c) Public display/Public use - the act or manner by which a thing, object, file, data, record and other documents are made available and accessible to the public, whether for a fee or not.

ARTICLE II  
PROHIBITED ACTS

SECTION 4. PROHIBITED ACTS. It shall be unlawful for any person, entity or establishment, whether in private or public:
a) To put/insert, include, print, or encode, the term "kalagan" not "kagan" in their files, data, records and other documents for public use, public display and for publication purposes, when referring to "kagan" as defined in this Ordinance;
b) To fail to correct files, data, records and other documents for public use, public display and for publication purposes which contain the word "kalagan" as referring to the "kagan" tribe as defined in this Ordinance, after sixty (60) days following the effectiveness of this Act.

**ARTICLE III**
**PENAL PROVISIONS**

**SECTION 5. PENALTIES.** Any person, entity, or establishment who violates the provision of Section 4 of this Ordinance shall be penalized as follows:

a) First Offense - Fine of not less than P500.00 but not more than P1,000.00 at the discretion of the Court;

b) Second Offense - Fine of not less than P1,000.00 but not more than P2,000.00 at the discretion of the Court;

c) Third Offense - Fine of not less than P2,000.00 but not more than P5,000.00 and revocation/suspension of business permit and license to operate, at the discretion of the Court.

**ARTICLE IV**
**PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION**

**SECTION 6.** The City Mayor's Office in coordination with the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples, the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos, and other concerned agencies of the City of Davao, is hereby urged to ensure that the provisions of this Ordinance shall be strictly implemented and complied with by the constituents of this City

For this purpose, the City Mayor's Office after consultation with the NCIP and the NCMF, is permitted to perform the following functions, to wit:

1) To receive complaints in any form for any violation of this Ordinance, and to investigate the same and make necessary findings and recommendations to any concerned agencies;

2) To promulgate rules and regulations, if necessary, for the effective implementation of this Ordinance, in consultation with the proper authorities, associations and establishments.

**ARTICLE V**
**FINAL PROVISION**

**SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.** If any provision of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to any person or circumstances is declared unconstitutional, or inconsistent with national laws, the other unaffected provisions of this Ordinance shall remain in full force and effect.

**SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE.** Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules and regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
