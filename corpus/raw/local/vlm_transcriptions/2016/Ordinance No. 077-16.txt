Ord. No. 077-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF THE PRESIDENT OF PHILXPORT REGION XI FOR EXEMPTION FROM PAYMENT OF MAYOR'S PERMIT FEE UNDER PARAGRAPHS A & D, SECTION 221, ARTICLE 26, OF THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF DAVAO CITY FOR DAVAO-BASED REGISTERED EXHIBITORS IN CONNECTION WITH THE BANANA CONGRESS 2016 ON OCTOBER 6-8, 2016, AT SM LANANG PREMIER, THIS CITY";

SECTION 2. COVERAGE: The exemption solely refers to the payment of Mayor's Permit Fee under paragraphs A & D, Section 221, Article 26, of the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao for all Davao-based registered exhibitors;

SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION: The exemption shall be from the approval of this Ordinance until October 8, 2016, and that in no case shall the organizers be exempt from compliance with other provisions of the "Signage Ordinance of Davao City" and other applicable laws and ordinances;

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, October 04, 2016, by a majority vote of all the members of the Sanggunian present.
