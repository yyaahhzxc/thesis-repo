Order No. 0508-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC PORTION OF THE ROAD ALONG ROXAS AVENUE, FROM C.M. RECTO STREET TO PACIANO BANGAY STREET, PORTION OF PACIANO BANGAY STREET, FROM PALMA GIL STREET TO FREEDOM PARK AND QUIRINO AVENUE TO FREEDOM PARK; FROM MANGAYASAY AVENUE FROM C.M. RECTO AVENUE TO PACIANO BANGAY STREET, THIS ORDINANCE IS EFFECTIVE ON MARCH 16, 2016 FROM 4:00 P.M. ONWARDS, FOR THE GRAND RALLY OF PDP LABAN WITH THE THEME "KAHAYAG SA PAGBAG-O";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION POLICY.- Section 21 (e) of R.A. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, states that any national or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications, and water works projects;

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE.- To ensure the safety of the participants for the Grand Rally of PDP Laban with the theme "KAHAYAG SA PAGBAG-O", the road along Roxas Avenue, from C.M. Recto Street to Paciano Bangay Street to Paciano Bangay Street from Palma Gil Street to Freedom Park in Freedom Park Avenue to Freedom Park; and along Mangayasay Avenue from C.M. Recto Avenue to Paciano Bangay Street in Davao City is hereby temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on March 16, 2016 from 4:00 P.M. onwards;

SECTION 4. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE.- If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 8th day of March, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
