Ordinance No. 091-16

Be it Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 3 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2016, CONSISTING OF THE BUDGET UNDER THE GENERAL FUND PROPER, THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISE FUND AND THE DEVELOPMENT FUND, IN THE AMOUNT OF ONE HUNDRED TWENTY TWO MILLION TWO HUNDRED SIXTY-EIGHT THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED NINETEEN PESOS AND 39/100 (P122,268,519.39);"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and, at the same time, ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use;

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS. The following amounts or so much thereof, as maybe necessary, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Treasurer of Davao City not otherwise appropriated from the operation of the City Government of Davao from January 1 to December 31, 2016, except as otherwise provided therein;

SECTION 4. AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES. The Supplemental Budget No.3 of the City Government of Davao for Calendar Year 2016, covers a total source of funds in the amount of ONE HUNDRED TWENTY-TWO MILLION TWO HUNDRED SIXTY-EIGHT THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED NINETEEN PESOS AND 39/100 (P122,268,519.39) distributed as follows:
