Ord. No. 0494-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC
OF THAT ROAD IDENTIFIED AS SAN FRANCISCO STREET FROM CORNER
PUROK STO. NIÑO UP TO THAT PORTION OF SAN FRANCISCO ROAD IN
FRONT OF THE BARANGAY HALL AND BUHANGIN DISTRICT OFFICE,
DAVAO CITY ON FEBRUARY 5, 2016, TO FEBRUARY 18, 2016 IN LINE
WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE 21ST ARAW NG BARANGAY BUHANGIN,
DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.- Section 21(c) of R.A. 7160,
otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, states that any
national or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed during
an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies and agricultural
or industrial fairs;

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE.- In celebration of the 21st Araw
ng Buhangin, Buhangin District, portion of that road identified as San
Francisco street from corner Purok Sto. Niño up to that portion of San
Francisco road in front of the Barangay Hall and Buhangin District Office,
Davao City will be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on February 5, 2016 to
February 18, 2016 in line with the celebration of the 21st Araw ng Barangay
Buhangin, Davao City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITITY CLAUSE.- If for any reason, any section
or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other
sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to
be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE.- This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 2nd day of February, 2016, by a majority vote of all the
Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.
