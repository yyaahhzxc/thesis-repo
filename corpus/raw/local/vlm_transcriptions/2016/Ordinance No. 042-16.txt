ORDINANCE NO. 042-16
Series of 2016
AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND BINUGAO FISHERFOLK ASSOCIATION (BFA), FOR THE "FISHERY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM" OR "MUNICIPAL FISHERY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT" UNDER THE BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING PROGRAMS FOR CY 2014

--- [Page 10] ---
**Page 2 of 3**

Ord. No. 04216

**SECTION 1. TITLE:** This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND BINUGAO FISHERFOLK ASSOCIATION (BFA), FOR THE "FISHERY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM OF MUNICIPAL FISHERY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT" UNDER THE BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING PROGRAMS FOR CY 2014."

**SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY:** Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY:** The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and Binugao Fisherfolk Association (BFA), this City, for the Fishery Resource Management Program of Municipal Fishery Development Project under the Bottoms-up Budgeting Programs for CY 2014.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:** If any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE:** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED: September 13, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present.

DEED OF DONATION

ONE (1) UNIT 7.5 H.P. MOTORIZED 16 FEET X 24"X19" BANCA
10 UNITS NON MOTORIZED 12 FEET X 18"X14" BANCA AND
WEIGHING SCALE, MANUAL 10 KLS. CAPACITY

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS :

This deed, made and executed in the City of Davao, Philippines, by and between:

The City Government of Davao-Davao City, an LGU of the Republic of the Philippines with and office address at San Pedro St., Davao City, represented by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, and hereinafter referred to as DONOR;

and

The BINUGAO FISHERFOLK ASSOCIATION (BFA) located at Brgy. Binugao, Davao City, represented by its President, LEO CURAYAG, hereinafter referred to as DONEE;

WITNESSETH

1. That the DONOR is the owner of 1 unit 7.5 H.P. motorized 16 feet x 24" x 19" banca; 10 units 12x18"x14" non-motorized banca and weighing scale amounting to One hundred forty four Thousand four hundred fifty (Php 144,450.00), which is more particularly described as follows:

1 unit motorized 16"x24"x19" banca with serial no. PR16FB201508000241 Php 43,250.00 (Dura Guard Brand)
10 units non-motorized 12x18"x14" @10,000.00 each Php 100,000.00
1 unit weighing scale 10 kls. Capacity Php 1,200.00

2. That the DONEE will use the aforesaid property under the project of the DONOR entitled: "Fishery Resource Management Program" Municipal Fishery Development Project under Bottom-Up Budgeting CY 2014.

3. That the DONOR, by obligatory authority, helps the DONEE by way of donating the above- described property, thus, DONOR hereby transferand conveys, by way of donation, unto the DONEE said property for the purpose of increasing fish productivity, increasing income of Fisherfols, free and clear from all liens and encumbrances and subject to terms and conditions provided hereunder:

a. To safeguard the property subject of donation, the DONEE must insure the donated property and shall shoulder all license fees and taxes that will be imposed on the use and operation of the same;

b. The DONEE must utilize the donated property in accordance to its intended use and shall not in any way sell, lease, mortgage nor subject the same to all forms of encumbrances nor liens;

--- [Page 13] ---
c. In order to optimize the useful life of the property subject of donation, the DONOR, through its project monitoring team shall monitor the use of the donated property within the period of five (5) years from the date of the DONEE's acceptance of the donation; d. That upon acceptance of the donation, the donated property will become the property of the DONEE and not of any of the DONEE's officers, members, heirs and assigns; e. That the DONOR shall provide the DONEE with all necessary information and advice maybe of the DONEE shall respect and honor all the terms and conditions of this Deed of Donation; f. The DONEE must maintain a complete and detailed logbook of the donated property's operation including subsidiary ledger and, whenever requested, allow the DONOR's project Monitoring team and the COA Auditors (who are duly authorized to perform visitatorial audit) to access the same; g. That the DONEE shall accede to the peaceful return and surrender of ownership and possession of the donated property in case of non-compliance with any of the terms and conditions herein stipulated. Upon payment said DONEE must also pay/refund the DONOR for all the expenses of this Deed of Donation; h. The DONEE shall likewise pay/refund the DONOR for all the expenses of this Deed of Donation in case of the dissolution of the former; and i. The property donated herein shall automatically revert to the purpose for which the donation is made would cease to exist; 4. That the DONEE hereby accepts the foregoing donation, subject to all terms and conditions set forth heretofore, and at the same time expresses its profound gratitude for this act of liberality on the part of the DONOR, by these presents also takes notice of acceptance.
