Ord. No. 0497-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND DAVIES PAINTS PHILIPPINES, INC. RELATIVE TO THE PAINTING OF ROAD-SIDE AND CENTER ISLAND RAILINGS AND PEDESTRIAN OVERPASSES LOCATED WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b) [1] of the Local Government Code, provides to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all contracts, agreements, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Davies Paints Philippines, Inc. relative to the painting of roadside and center island railings and pedestrian overpasses located within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, February 9, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT entered into and executed this day of by and between:

LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY, company with office address at duly represented in this act by its Mayor, Hon. Rodrigo Duterte, herein referred to as "DAVAO CITY";

-AND-

DAVIES PAINTS PHILIPPINES, INC., a corporation duly organized and existing under Philippine Laws with office address at 1707-B Antel Global Corporate Center, Julia Vargas Avenue, Ortigas Center, Pasig City, duly represented in this agreement by its VP for Sales and Marketing, Mr. Albert Gan Lee herein referred to as "DAVIES";

WITNESSETH THAT

WHEREAS, the parties recognize the need to establish partnership and cooperation between the government and the private sector for the effective implementation of the City's development plan;

WHEREAS, DAVIES offered to re-paint the road side corner gutters; corner of center island railing, as well as pedestrian overpasses in Davao City;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto agree and stipulate:

1. DAVIES shall provide all the materials and labor necessary to complete the repainting works for the following identified road side corner gutters; corner of center island railings, and pedestrian overpasses in Davao City, to wit:

ROAD-SIDE ISLAND RAILINGS:
1. SM ECOLAND TO MACASSARAT PARK
2. MATINBA CROSSING TO MANDA
3. BANGKEROHAN TO GAISANO MALL
4. BAJADA TO BUHANGIN BRIDGE

PEDESTRIAN OVERPASSES:
1. DAVAO DIVERSION ROAD NEAR LA VERNA SUBDIVISION

--- [Page 13] ---
2. CABAGUIO AVENUE NEAR ASSUMPTION COLLEGE
3. BRGY., ILANG, BUNAWAN DISTRICT IN FRONT OF SIXTO BABAO E/S ALONG DAVAO-AGUSAN ROAD
4. QUIMIO BOULEVARD JCT SAN PEDRO EXT. NEAR ALMENDRAS GYM
5. DAVAO-AGUSAN ROAD, BRGY. TIBUNGCO, NEAR TIBUNGCO PUBLIC MARKET
6. DAVAO-AGUSAN ROAD, BRGY. SASA, NEAR SASA PUBLIC MARKET
7. DAVAO-AGUSAN ROAD, BRGY. LASANG, NEAR LASANG BRIDGE
8. BUNAWAN, NEAR BUNAWAN DISTRICT HALL
9. QUIRINO AVE., BANKEROHOR, NEAR BANKEROHOR PUBLIC MARKET
10. UPLAND AVE., BRGY. SAJADA, FRONTING GASANO MALL
11. MATINA CROSSING, BRGY. COTABATO, FRONTING COTABATO ROAD
12. DAVAO-COTABATO ROAD, TORIL, FRONTING ST. PETER'S COLLEGE OF TORIL
13. SPAC OVERPASS, JP LAUREL AVE. FRONTING DOH AND SPFMC

2. DAVIES agrees to adopt the approved design and color of DAVAO CITY to be painted on the footbridge.

3. For and in consideration of the painting works, DAVIES is likewise allowed to apply/paint/install its logo and tagline on the named railings and footbridges in a scale and location that can be visible to motorists and the public.

4. DAVAO CITY agrees to provide assistance to all personnel of DAVIES while painting works are on-going.

5. DAVAO CITY agrees and undertakes that installed logos of DAVIES shall remain untouched and not to be replaced within a period of six years upon turn-over of completed project unless there's a need to replace said material due to damage.

6. DAVIES shall re-touch and make the necessary re-painting after three years, if needed, for maintenance of the railings and pedestrian overpasses. Labor and materials for the re-painting shall be provided by Davies.

7. The parties warrant that they all have the power and authority to enter into and execute this agreement, and undertake to faithfully perform their respective obligation hereunder, as well as do everything necessary, proper, and convenient to carry out its contractual intent and objectives.

8. This MOA or any part thereof shall not be amended, altered, added, removed or otherwise modified except upon written agreement among the parties.

9. This MOA shall take effect upon its execution and shall be binding for six (6) years from 1 February 2016 to 1 February 2022, subject to extension upon written agreement of both parties prior to the termination of the Agreement. It is hereby understood that DAVIES PAINTS PHILIPPINES, INC. is given the right of first refusal in the execution of a new agreement.
