ORDINANCE NO. 0482-16
Series of 2016
AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE ROAD IDENTIFIED AS GLADIOLA STREET (IN BETWEEN CARNATION AND SAMPAQUIU STREET), BUHANGIN, DAVAO CITY, ON JANUARY 29, 2016 FROM 7:30 A.M. TO 3:00 P.M., FOR THE BAZAAR/SPORTSFEET EVENT, IN CELEBRATION OF THE 11TH FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY OF STARCHILD INNOVATIVE LEARNING ACADEMY

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 2
Ord No. 0482-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON THE ROAD BETWEEN GLADOLA STREET BETWEEN CARNATION AND SAMPAQUITA STREETS), BUHANGAN, DAVAO CITY, ON JANUARY 29, 2016 FROM 7:30 A.M. TO 3:00 P.M., FOR THE BAZAAR/SPORTSFEST EVENT IN CELEBRATION OF THE 11TH FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY OF STARCHILD INNOVATIVE LEARNING ACADEMY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fair;

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In celebration of the 11th Founding Anniversary of Starchild Innovative Learning Academy, the road identified as Gladola Street (in between Carnation and Sampaquitla Streets), Buhangan, Davao City, shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic for the Bazaar/Sportsfest Event on January 29, 2016 from 7:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

SECTION 4. SEPARAPRITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, January 19, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
