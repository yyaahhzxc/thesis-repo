ORDINANCE NO. 0135-17
Series of 2017

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH RESPECTIVELY XI AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, INCORPORATING INTO A PARTNERSHIP FOR A COLLABORATIVE APPROACH IN THE PROVISION OF SERVICES FOR THE EXPANSION OF THE REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND WELLNESS CENTER UNDER THE CITY HEALTH OFFICE

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No. 0135-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH REGION XI AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, PERTAINING TO A PARTNERSHIP FOR A COLLABORATIVE APPROACH IN THE PROVISION OF SERVICES FOR THE EXPANSION OF THE REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND WELLNESS CENTER UNDER THE CITY HEALTH OFFICE."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Pursuant to the constitutional mandate that the State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development, it is the City's policy to team up and pool resources with the national government to provide health services to all the people of Davao especially to the underprivileged, including those suffering from HIV and AIDS in order to prevent the spread of the said disease;

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Health Region XI pertaining to the collaborative approach in the provision of services for the expansion of the Reproductive Health and Wellness Center under the City Health Office;

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any existing Ordinance or any provision thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections of provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 10th of January 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

That this Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) entered into this day of 2017, by and between:

THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH (DOH): REGIONAL OFFICE XI, a regional office in Davao Region, of the executive department of the DOH Republic of the Philippines with principal office address at Bajeda Avenue, Davao City, represented by its Regional Director Dr. Abdullah Dumana Jr., hereinafter referred to as the "DOH";

and

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, duly organized and existing by virtue of the Philippine Laws, with office address at San Pedro Street represented by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as the "CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO";

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, pursuant to Administrative Order No. 18 of 1995 which standardized the clinical management of HIV and AIDS by mandating DOH-retained tertiary hospitals to establish an HIV and AIDS Core Team (HACT);

WHEREAS, the DOH, as the lead agency of government in the Davao Region on HIV and AIDS prevention and control, identifies both government and private health facilities, including provincial and local government hospitals treatment hubs to provide services related to HIV and AIDS;

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 22, Article I RA 8504, otherwise known as "Philippines AIDS Prevention and Control Act of 1998," an ACT PROMULGATING POLICIES AND PRESCRIBING MEASURES FOR THE PREVENTION AND CONTROL OF HIV/AIDS IN THE PHILIPPINES, INSTITUTING A NATIONWIDE AIDS INFORMATION AND DATA SYSTEM, STRENGTHENING THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL AIDS COUNCIL, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES, which mandates all hospitals and other appropriate health care facilities, to establish an HIV and AIDS Core Team (HACT) to oversee the implementation of policies and guidelines on all matters pertaining to HIV and AIDS in the healthcare setting, with the primary objectives of facilitating the provision of safe, comprehensive and compassionate care to persons living with HIV (PLWHIV) and AIDS by properly trained personnel; mobilizing to improve HIV infection on all patients and his/her family; and coordinating all efforts to prevent and control the transmission of HIV infection;

WHEREAS, the City Health Office- Reproductive Health Wellness Center (CHO-RHWCC), under the CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, is the city government health facility with a functional medical team whose members had undergone capacity building on the clinical management of HIV and AIDS;

WHEREAS, the DOH supports the global initiative on Universal Access that facilitates access to antiretroviral therapy and other services related to HIV and AIDS;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto agreed as follows:

--- [Page 5] ---
**SECTION 1. OBJECTIVE**

1.1 The objective of this Memorandum of Agreement is for the Department of Health (DOH) and City Health Office-Reproductive Health and Wellness Centre (CHO-RHWCC) under the City Government of Davao to enter into a partnership for the collaborative approach in the provision of services related to HIV and AIDS including access to antiretroviral drugs for people living with HIV.

1.2 For CHO-RHWCC to become one of the official DOH-designated satellite treatment hubs in the country that facilitates the provision of safe, comprehensive, and compassionate care to HIV and AIDS patients;

**SECTION 2. GENERAL PROVISION**

2.1 CHO-RHWCC shall establish a mechanism and create policies and guidelines for HIV and AIDS within its facility;

2.2 CHO-RHWCC shall designate a specific area/office/clinic for the medical team where services can be provided to patients including but not limited to HIV counseling, testing, clinical management, patient monitoring, and other care and support services to people living with HIV and their family;

2.3 CHO-RHWCC shall allocate a desktop computer or laptop to be used exclusively for recording and maintenance of database of patients accessing HIV services at the family;

2.4 CHO-RHWCC shall utilize the reporting/monitoring tools prescribed by the DOH-NEC and submit the same to the DOH on a monthly basis;

2.5 CHO-RHWCC through its pharmacist shall monitor the stock level of antiretroviral drugs provided by the DOH to prevent stock outs;

2.6 CHO-RHWCC shall be responsible in the disposal of unutilized drugs for HIV provided by the DOH;

2.7 CHO-RHWCC shall ensure that the necessary protective equipment such as gloves, goggles and gown, shall be made available to all health care workers directly involved in the provision of services to PLHIV and similarly exposed personnel at all times;

2.8 CHO-RHWCC shall employ appropriate measures for TB infection control within its premises;

2.9 CHO-RHWCC shall make available services for post-exposure management for any health care workers with occupational exposure needing appropriate intervention, including provision of antiretroviral drugs as post-exposure prophylaxis per recommendation of the HACT;

2.10 CHO-RHWCC shall be an accredited provider of the Phil Health Outpatient HIV and AIDS Treatment (OHAT) Package and implement the same in accordance with the Phil Health Circular No. 10 s. 2010;

2.11 CHO-RHWCC shall strictly adhere to the policies and guidelines of the DOH on matters pertaining to the implementation of HIV and AIDS program in the health care setting;

**Access of Antiretroviral Drugs:**

2.12 Any PLHIV in need of antiretroviral (ARV) drug upon the clinical evaluation of a trained physician in accordance with DOH Administrative Order (AO) 2014-031 or Policies and Guidelines on Use of Antiretroviral Therapy for Adults Living with Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) and HIV+ exposure, can access the said drugs from CHO-RHWCC through the HACT. The PLHIV upon need of ARV has to consult the HACT and will only be enrolled in antiretroviral therapy (ART) upon the recommendation of the HACT physician;

--- [Page 6] ---
2.13 Antiretroviral drugs shall be given for FREE subject to the availability of supply, which can be availed of anytime at the CHO-RHWC. The DOH, through the NASPCP, shall allocate these drugs to the CHO-RHWC based on the current number of patients;

2.14 The CHO-RHWC shall be responsible in reporting adverse drug reactions to DOH, NASPCP, and BFAD on monthly basis;

2.15 CHO-RHWC shall only give a maximum of three (3) month's supply of these drugs to PLHIV on ART for treatment and adherence monitoring by the physician;

2.16 Stored ARV at the CHO-RHWC can be used as post exposure prophylaxis only in case of occupational exposure of any healthcare workers, as per recommendation by the HACT. Antiretroviral drugs shall not be used as prophylaxis for any other forms of exposure;

2.17 CHO-RHWC shall store the antiretroviral drugs in a designated cabinet with lock and only a designated pharmacist's/ or Nurse-in-charge shall have access to the key. Said pharmacist or Nurse-in-charge shall be responsible in the inventory of the drugs and preparation and submission of monthly stock inventory to the DOH through its Regional HIV and STI Coordinator;

HIV AND AIDS CORE TEAM (HACT)

2.18 The CHO-RHWC HACT Chairperson shall lead the team of health professionals in the diagnostic, treatment and management of PLHIV. Appropriate referral shall be made for services not available within its health facility.

2.19 The CHO-RHWC HACT shall ensure adherence to the provisions of RA 8504 and Davao City AIDS Ordinance. It shall be responsible in ensuring confidentiality at all times. All health care providers, personnel, and other custodians of any medical record, file, data, or test result at the CHO-RHWC shall strictly observe confidentiality in handling of all medical information, particularly the identity and status of persons with HIV;

2.20 Members of the CHO-RHWC HACT shall participate in capacity building activities to further enhance their knowledge, skills, and attitude towards effective care and management of patients and for proper information dissemination and education on HIV and AIDS, by participating in continuous medical education, training, national treatment hub conferences, research and other related activities;

2.21 Any individual referred by government or other private facilities shall be properly managed and provided appropriate services by CHO-RHWC with utmost confidentiality;

2.22 The CHO-RHWC HACT shall closely coordinate with other government agencies, non-government and faith-based organizations, and other support groups in the provision of care and support services to PLHIV;

EDUCATION AND INFORMATION

2.23 HIV and AIDS education and information dissemination shall form part of the delivery of health services by the CHO-RHWC HACT;

2.24 The CHO-RHWC HACT shall make available to the public such information necessary to control the spread of HIV and AIDS and to correct common misconceptions about this disease;

2.25 CHO-RHWC HACT shall conduct orientation and training of all health workers in the facility on HIV and AIDS stigma reduction including discussions on HIV-related ethical issues such as confidentiality, informed consent and the duty to provide treatment and care;

--- [Page 7] ---
SECTION 3. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The DOH upon the signing of this MOA shall:

3.1.1 Oversee the implementation of the HIV and AIDS program at the CHO-RHWC;

3.1.2 Provide technical assistance as necessary in setting up the mechanism and creation of policies and guidelines on matters pertaining to HIV and AIDS at the CHO-RHWC;

3.1.3 Be responsible in ensuring the provision of FREE antiretroviral drugs to the CHO-RHWC from DOH Central Office based on actual number of patients enrolled and projected number of patients to be enrolled in the ART program; subject to its availability;

3.2 The CHO-RHWC under the CTF GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO shall:

3.2.1 Set up a mechanism and create policies and guidelines for the implementation of the HIV and AIDS program within the facility;

3.2.2 Implement all the related stipulations herein indicated within the facility through the leadership and initiative of the CHO-RHWC HACT;

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY

4.1 This agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing and execution thereof and full compliance by all parties of its respective obligations under this agreement;

4.2 This Agreement is made under and shall be construed according to the laws of the Republic of the Philippines;

4.3 Any amendment to this agreement shall be approved by all parties in writing and shall be made official in a Supplemental Agreement;

4.4 Any violations committed by either party to this agreement shall render this MOA void and unenforceable;

4.5 Either of the parties herein may pre-terminate this agreement by giving 30 days prior to the other party stating therein among others why this MOA should be terminated.
