Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0473-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC
OF THE ROAD FROM THE SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL PASSING THROUGH
C.M. RECTO STREET, THEN LEFT BACK TO PALMA GIL STREET, THEN
TO LEGASPI STREET, THEN CROSSING SAN PEDRO STREET, TO
MAGALLANES STREET AND BACK TO ORIGIN FOR THE CELEBRATION OF
THE FEAST DAY OF THE BLACK NAZARENE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act
No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides
that any national or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed
during an actual emergency, festive celebration, public rallies and
agricultural or industrial fair.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The following streets in
Poblacion, Davao City, mentioned in Section 1, have or shall be temporarily
closed to vehicular traffic for the Celebration of the Feast Day of the Black
Nazarene on January 9, 2016 at 4 o'clock in the afternoon onwards,

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other
sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to
be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, January 5, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sangguniang Panlungsod present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
