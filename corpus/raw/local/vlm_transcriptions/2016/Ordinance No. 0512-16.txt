ORDINANCE NO. 0512-16  
Series of 2016  

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (5% CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2016, IN THE AMOUNT OF THIRTY-ONE MILLION (31,000,000) PESOS (PHP 31,500,000.00) FOR THE PURCHASE OF FIFTEEN THOUSAND (15,000) SACKS OF RICE AS FOOD ASSISTANCE TO THE VICTIMS OF DROUGHT AND RAT INFESTATION AS A RESULT OF THE DROUGHT IN THE PROVINCE OF COTABATO, SUBJECT TO THE SUBMISSION OF THE EXEMPTION FROM THE COMMISSION ON ELECTIONS (COMELEC).

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No. 0512-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUNDS (5% CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALAMITIES." THE AMOUNT OF THIRTY-ONE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (PUP 31,500,000.00) FOR THE PURCHASE OF FIFTEEN THOUSAND (15,000) SACKS OF RICE FOOD ASSISTANCE AS A RESULT OF THE HIGHER PHENOMENON IN THE PROVINCE OF COTABATO, SUBJECT TO THE SUBMISSION OF THE EXEMPTION FROM THE COMMISSION ON ELECTIONS [COMELEC].

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use;

SECTION 3. BENEFICIARY - The Province of Cotabato is hereby designated as the beneficiary of said food support assistance;

SECTION 4. SUMMARY OF BUDGETARY ALLOCATION - A portion of the Quick Response Fund (QRF) to be sourced out of the Disaster Risk Reduction Management Fund-5% Calamity Fund of the City Government of Davao in the total amount of THIRTY-ONE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (PUP 31,500,000.00) shall be utilized to purchase fifteen thousand (15,000) sacks of rice as food assistance to the Province of Cotabato;

SECTION 5. LEGAL BASIS - Pursuant to Republic Act No. 8185, otherwise known as "An Act Amending Section 234 (d) of R.A. 7100, otherwise known as the Local Government of 1991," which states that: "Five Percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from the regular sources shall be set as annual lump sum appropriation for relief, rehabilitation, and reconstruction of areas affected by calamities and in conjunction with calamities which may occur during the budget year. Provided, however, that such fund shall be used only in the area, or a portion thereof, of the local government unit or other areas affected by a disaster or calamity, as determined and declared by the local Sanggunian concerned";

Further, pursuant to Section 5 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of R.A. 8185, which states that:

*Section 5. Allocation and Utilization of Five Percent (5%) Lumpsum Appropriations for Calamity Fund*

a) Allocation
xxx
xxx
*LGUs may also allocate/ use a portion of the five percent (5%) Calamity Fund to other affected areas on condition that the said areas are declared as under a State of Calamity by the Sanggunian concerned*;

b) Utilization

b2) A portion of the calamity fund may also be authorized to be used by the LGU concerned to provide financial assistance to other LGUs whose area or portion thereof had been declared under a state of calamity by its Sanggunian."

SECTION 6. RELEASE OF ASSISTANCE - The fifteen thousand (15,000) sacks of rice shall only be released upon the submission of the exemption from the Commission on Elections (COMELEC);

EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, April 5, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
