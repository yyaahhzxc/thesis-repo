ORDINANCE NO. 013.16
Series of 2016
GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF THE PHILIPPINE INSTITUTE OF CIVIL ENGINEERS (PICIE), DAVAO CITY ENGINEER, INCORPORATING THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 47, 48 AND 49 OF ORDINANCE QUARTET EXEMPTION FROM SECTIONS 37, 41 AND 42 OF ORDINANCE NO.092, SERIES OF 2000 (SIGNAGE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY) IN CONNECTION WITH THE 9TH REGIMEN XI TECHNICAL CONFERENCE ON AUGUST 18, 19, 2016 AT GRAND MEN SENOR HOTEL, THIS IS ORDERED:

--- [Page 6] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 013-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE LETTER REQUEST OF THE PHILIPPINE INSTITUTE OF CIVIL ENGINEERS, DAVAO CITY CHAPTER, INC., REQUESTING FOR THE PASSAGE OF AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION FROM SECTIONS 37, 41 AND 42 OF ORDINANCE NO. 092, SERIES OF 2000 [SIGNAGE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY] IN CONNECTION WITH THE 5TH REGION XI TECHNICAL CONFERENCE ON AUGUST 18-19, 2016 AT GRAND MEN SENG HOTEL, THIS CITY";

SECTION 2. COVERAGE. The exemption solely refers to the payment of applicable fee under Chapter 10, Section 37 and exemption from the coverage of restricted areas for installation and display of any advertising and/or propaganda materials under Chapter 7, Section 41 and Section 42 of Ordinance No. 092, Series of 2000 [Signage Ordinance of Davao City].

SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION. The exemption shall be from the passage of this Ordinance up to August 20, 2016 only and that in no case shall the organizers be exempt from compliance with the other provisions of the "Signage Ordinance of Davao City" and other applicable laws and ordinances.

SECTION 4. CONDITIONS.
a) All limitations and conditions set forth in the Signage Ordinance of the city and those additional conditions imposed by the City Engineer's Office (CEO), the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and the Davao Light and Power Company (DLPC), in relation to the installation and display of any advertising and/or propaganda materials within the restricted areas must be strictly complied with;
b) The City Government of Davao shall be recognized as event partner in all programs, collateral and banners;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED: August 9, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
