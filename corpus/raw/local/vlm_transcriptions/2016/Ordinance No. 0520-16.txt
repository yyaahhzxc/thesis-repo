ORDINANCE NO. 0520-16/02785-16  
Series of 2016  

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING SUPERMARKETS, GROCERY STORES AND OTHER ESTABLISHMENTS SELLING RAW FISH, SEAFOODS, MEAT, PROCESSED MEAT AND OTHER MEAT PRODUCTS, INCLUDING SUPPLIERS THERETO, TO SEGREGATE HALAL FOOD ITEMS, FROM DELIVERY TO STORAGE, DISPLAY, WEIGHING, SLICING, CARRYING THRU BARKERIES, CARTONS, UNPACKING, OR PACKING IN THE PAYMENT COUNTER THEREBY ESTABLISHING HEREIN A "HALAL LANE" FOR THIS PURPOSE

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

ARTICLE I  
GENERAL PROVISIONS  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "HALAL LANE ORDINANCE"  

SECTION 2. PURPOSE - The purpose of this Ordinance is to promote, protect and respect the religious belief, customs and traditions of our Muslim community in Davao City in their sacred concept of halal and to ensure spiritual purity and cleanliness in their food consumption. Further, this legislation is a recognition of the importance and contribution of the Muslim community in our national and local goals and to further make them active participants in nation-building.  

SECTION 3. COVERAGE  

I.  
COVERED  

1) All grocery stores, meat shops, supermarkets and other establishments selling raw fish, seafood, foods, other fish products, halal certified meat, halal certified processed meat products and at the same time selling non-halal/haram meat, such as but not limited to the following:  

a) Supermarkets, shops and stores inside malls, marts, and centers  

b) Supermarkets, shops and stores outside malls whether or not registered with proper authorities  

c) Shops, stores, stalls and marts having only one counter  

d) Other outlets, stores and other shops which have as part of their business the selling of meat, processed meat and other meat products that are both halal-certified and non-halal/haram meat products  

2) Suppliers of raw fish, seafood, halal-certified meat, halal certified processed meat who are also selling/supplying non-halal/haram meat products at the same time;  

II. NOT COVERED - The Ordinance shall not cover the following:  

1) Shops, stores, stalls, marts, outlets and other establishments which do not sell any halal food or halal-certified products;

2) Stalls, shops, or stores inside public markets;  
3) Suppliers selling purely halal food/halal certified food products or selling purely non-halal/haram food items.  

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS -  
a) Halal food - means "lawful," pertaining to raw food items for human consumption that is permitted under Islamic practice. It primarily refers to fish, seafood, and other fish products which, by its nature, does not undergo processing in a slaughterhouse.  
b) Halal-certified products - those raw meat items such as, chicken meat, beef, goat meat, and carabao meat that are certified as Halal by the proper certifying authorities and identified by a Halal logo pursuant to the Rules laid down by the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos.  
c) Non-halal/haram Food - means "unlawful," pertaining to raw food items not allowed for Muslim consumption primarily referring to PORK MEAT as well as other meat that are inherently prohibited under Islamic practice and also those meat products that are not certified as Halal.  
d) Supermarkets, stores, shops - refer to establishments selling a wide range of goods and products including selling of different types of raw meat, processed meat, and other meat products including fish, seafood, and other fish products that are both halal and non-halal/haram, whether inside or outside mall premises.  
e) Segregate - the act of separating or setting apart non-halal/haram products from halal food and halal-certified products, including any other means, to ensure non-mixture of non-halal/haram and halal food/products such as compartmentalizing and tight sealing of containers.  
f) Halal lane - refers to a place, path, or lane in a grocery store, but which, also specifically includes delivery vehicles, storage room or place for preservation of meat items, storage or place of display for sale, carts, baskets, trolleys, and other carriers, weighing scales and other tools, packing/packaging, and the counter which will be used exclusively for halal-certified products only that is free from contamination of non-halal/haram food items.

ARTICLE II
GENERAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF OWNERS,
PROPRIETORS, SUPPLIERS, TENANTS FOR THE
CREATION AND ESTABLISHMENT OF HALAL LANE

WHAT CONSTITUTES HALAL LANE -

SECTION 5. DELIVERY FROM SUPPLIERS TO THE STORAGE -
This refers to the delivery of halal food items and halal certified meat products by the covered suppliers, whether supplied by the mall itself or by independent entities, through vans, trucks and other meet and fish carriers.

Duties and Responsibilities:

5.a Suppliers, tenants, owners, owners of grocery stores, supermarkets or meat shops, owners, owners of grocery stores, supermarkets or meat shops, through their managers/staff or representatives, who are responsible for the delivery of fish, seafood and meat products shall segregate the halal food and halal-certified products from non-halal/haram products inside the vans, trucks or any other vehicles used for delivery. They shall make sure that the boxes, containers, freezers, compartments, tools and equipment used for the delivery and storage, while in transit, is exclusive for halal food items and halal-certified products only. The storage for halal food items and halal food products must be separated and tightly sealed in order to avoid physical contact or adulteration from non-halal/haram food items that are inside the delivery van, truck or carrier;

5.b In case the suppliers are separate entities from the mall or the grocery store itself, the owner of grocery store/s shall inform in writing, the suppliers when they will be receiving the shipment. Moreover, owner, owner of grocery stores, supermarkets or meat shops, through their managers/staff or representatives, shall ensure that the aforementioned food items received by them complied with the segregation requirement under this provision and shall further guarantee that the halal food items and halal certified products are not contaminated during delivery as hereto stated.

5.c Suppliers, tenants and owners of malls/supermarkets/meat shops or grocery stores are concurrently liable under this section respecting their concomitant duties and responsibilities.

5.d. The persons/entities covered in this Ordinance are not required to purchase, new freezers, containers or other tools and equipment in order to comply with this section. It is enough that they compartmentalize or subdivide their storage/freezers and segregate some of their tools and equipment, after thoroughly washing them, and thereafter use them exclusively for halal food and halal-certified products only.

**SECTION 6. STORAGE OF MEAT AND FISH PRODUCTS - This includes freezers, warehouses, or any storage place, path, or room inside the grocery stores or supermarkets which are used to store the meat and fish products for preservation or for any other purpose.**

**Duties and Responsibilities:**

**6.a. Suppliers, owners of grocery stores or supermarkets through their managers, staffs, and tenants shall provide for separate storage, container or freezer exclusively for halal food items and halal-certified meat products only. They shall adopt a structure that will prevent the mixture or absolute separation of halal food items and halal-certified meat products from non-halal/haram products. They shall make sure that the storage, container or freezer for halal food items and halal-certified products is tightly covered and sealed to avoid adulteration. They shall also use separate equipment, tools, gears, and other apparatus which are exclusive for halal food items and halal-certified products only.**

**6.b. In cases where storage of the meat products for preservation is provided by the supplier or owned by a tenant by virtue of a tenancy agreement or any other contract/agreement creating jurisdictional responsibility, the manager, staffs, and tenants shall inform in writing, the tenants, lessors or supplier's duty to segregate their meat products in accordance with this Article, or include the same as a provision in their contract. Moreover, the mail/shop or grocery store owner thru their manager, shall supervise, regulate and ensure that the segregation requirement under this provision is duly complied and implemented within the mail/shop/store premises;**

**6.c. Suppliers, tenants and owners of malls/supermarkets/mess shops or grocery stores are concurrently liable under this section respecting their concomitant duties and responsibilities.**

**6.d. The storing of different kinds of halal meat (such as chicken, beef, fish) in one storage, section or room exclusive for halal food items and halal certified meat products is allowed provided it does not cause any harm or negative effects to the meat itself.**

**6.e. The persons/entities covered in this Ordinance are not required to purchase new freezers, containers or other tools and equipment in order to comply with this section. It is enough that they compartmentalize or subdivide their storage/freezers and segregate some of their tools and equipment, after thoroughly washing them, and thereafter use them as exclusive for halal food and halal-certified products only. However, there should be only one set presently used for both kinds of meat products. The said established entities are not obliged nor are they required to establish another set of the said tools/equipment to be utilized exclusively for halal food and halal-certified products only.**

SECTION 7. PLACE OF DISPLAY OR STALLS WHERE HALAL FOOD ITEMS AND HALAL-CERTIFIED PRODUCTS ARE DISPLAYED FOR SALE - This consists of tables, freezers, stalls or any other sections which are used to display the products for sale inside grocery stores, supermarkets, meat shops and other establishments selling fish, seafood, meat, processed meat and other meat products.

Duties and Responsibilities:

7.a. Owners, thru their managers/staffs, tenants and suppliers shall segregate halal food items and halal-certified meat products at the place of display. They shall make sure that non-halal/haram products are not, in any manner mixed with any halal food and halal-certified products and completely segregated inside the freezers or stalls;

7.b. In cases where storage of the meat products in the place of display for sale are provided by the supplier or owned by a tenant by virtue of a tenancy agreement or any other contract/agreement creating juridical relations, the mall/shop or grocery store owner thru their manager, shall inform, in writing, the tenant, lessee or supplier's duty to segregate their meat products in accordance with this Article, and include this as a provision in their contract. Moreover, the mall/shop, or grocery store owner shall supervise, regulate and ensure that the segregation requirement under this provision is duly complied and implemented within the mall/shop/store premises;

7.c. Suppliers, tenants and owners of malls/supermarkets/meat shops or grocery stores are concurrently liable under this section respecting their concomitant duties and responsibilities.

SECTION 8. SLICING, WEIGHING AND PACKING OF MEAT AND FISH - This includes the slicing, weighing and packing of meat and fish at the place of display for sale inside grocery stores, meat shops, supermarkets and other establishments selling fish, seafood, meat, processed meat and other meat products.

Duties and Responsibilities:

8.a. Owners thru their managers/staffs, tenants and suppliers shall provide or allocate slicing knives, slicing board, cleaver, weighing scale, trays, tongs, packing materials and any other paraphernalia that are exclusive for halal food and halal-certified products only.

8.b. It is NOT required that a new set of the above-enumerated tools/equipment be purchased. It is enough that they segregate some of their current tools and equipment as exclusive for halal food and halal-certified products, provided, it has been thoroughly washed or cleaned properly. However, should there be only one set presently used for both kinds of meat products, the covered establishments shall be obliged to purchase/provide another set of the said tools/equipment to be utilized exclusively for halal food and halal-certified products only.

S.c. The persons or staffs in charge of slicing and packing shall wear gloves, body gears, and such other apparatus necessary for sanitation which shall also be exclusive for halal food and halal-certified products.  

S.d. In cases where there is only one person in charge or assigned for slicing, packing, and weighing of all meats, whether halal or non-halal/haram, such person shall make sure that he changes gloves and body gears when slicing halal food and halal-certified products and such gloves and other body gears shall not be used on meat that are not halal/haram.  

S.e. In cases where services and equipment for slicing, weighing and packing of meat products are provided by the supplier or owned by the tenant/lessee by virtue of a tenancy agreement or any other contractual arrangement or legal relations, the mall/shop or grocery store owner through his manager, shall inform, in writing, the tenants, lessees or suppliers' duty to segregate their meat products in accordance with this Article, or incorporate the same as a provision in their contract. Moreover, the mall/shop or grocery store owner through his manager, shall supervise, regularly check and ensure that the segregation requirement under this provision is duly complied and implemented within the mall/shop/store premises;  

8.f. Suppliers, tenants, and owners of malls/supermarkets/meat shops or grocery stores are concurrently liable under this section respecting their concomitant duties and responsibilities.  

SECTION 9. CARTS, BASKETS, TROLLEYS OR OTHER PRODUCT CARRIERS: This includes carts, push-carts, baskets, trolleys, or any other carriers used to carry or transport the goods inside the grocery store, supermarket, meat shop or other establishment selling fish, seafood, meat, processed meat and other meat products.  

Duties and Responsibilities:  

9.a Owners, proprietors thru their manager/staff of grocery stores, supermarkets, meat shops, and establishments that provide for halal food and halal-certified food products shall make sure that these carriers are labeled as exclusive for halal food and halal-certified products in such a way that the consumers/buyers can identify the carriers as such.  

9.b They shall also separate or segregate the carriers which are exclusive for halal food and halal-certified products from the other carriers in the depository section. Their staffs are duty-bound to inform the buying public of such purpose whenever the need arises;

9.c. At least ten percent (10%) of all the push carts, trollies, and other product carriers are to be used for halal food items and halal-certified products. However, such carriers may be used for non-halal/haram products that are tightly sealed or cannot, in any way, contaminate other products such as canned goods and other tightly packed products.

9.d. The persons/entities covered in this Ordinance are not required to purchase new carriers in order to comply with this section. It is enough that they segregate some of their carriers, after thoroughly washing them, and thereafter, use the carriers as exclusive for halal food and halal-certified products only.

SECTION 10, PAYMENT COUNTER: Includes the counter, table, or lane and trays for wet goods where the customers slide over their products for payment and where the consummation of the sale usually takes place.

Duties and Responsibilities:

10.a. Owners, proprietors thru their managers/staffs of grocery stores, supermarkets, meat shops and other establishments will provide/allocate at least one (1) counter which shall be labeled as HALAL LANE, provided however, that such entities must have at least two (2) counters inside, in order for this Section to apply.

10.b. Only products which are halal food and halal-certified are allowed in this lane or counter. However, other products, although not labeled as halal-certified, such as canned goods, packetized foods, and other tightly enclosed products may be placed in this lane or counter provided that such products do not or cannot, in any way, contaminate nor be mixed with other products.

10.c. The persons/entities covered in this Ordinance are not required to install new counters/lanes in order to comply with this Section. It is enough that they allocate at least one (1) of their counters/lanes to be used as Halal lane.

10.d. They shall also provide a sign indicating that such counter is considered or known as "HALAL LANE".

SECTION 11, MEAT SHOPS, GROCERY STORES, MARTS AND CENTERS HAVING ONLY ONE COUNTER: For shops, grocery stores, marts or centers with one (1) counter only, and does not opt to add another counter, shall comply with the requirement of segregation mentioned in the preceding sections and shall allocate a space in their existing payment counter that is exclusive for halal food and halal certified products only. Such persons/entities are required to ensure that halal food and halal-certified products placed on the persons/entities are not contaminated by non-halal/haram food items, such as, but not limited to, wearing of gloves or apron when handling halal food and halal certified products.

ARTICLE III
PROHIBITED ACTS AND PRACTICES

SECTION 12. It shall be unlawful for any person, entity or establishment covered in this Ordinance to commit the following, or cause the commission of the following, whether or not negligence is involved:

a) Mixing or caused to be mixed halal-certified meat products with non-halal/haram certified products inside the delivery van/truck/carrier, freezer, containers, or any other storage for preservation;

b) Improper covering or sealing of halal food items and halal certified products inside the delivery van/truck/carrier and freezer, containers, or any other storage for preservation;

c) Mixing or caused to be mixed halal-certified meat products with non-halal/haram certified products in the display area or the table where the products are displayed for sale;

d) Mixing or caused to be mixed halal-certified meat products with non-halal/haram certified products in slicing, packing, or freezing;

e) Using tools, gloves, equipment and other apparatus exclusive for halal/halal-certified products for non-halal/haram products in the delivery, storage for preservation, storage for display, carriage of goods inside grocery areas and payment counter except where such non-halal/haram products consist of canned goods, tightly sealed goods and such other tightly enclosed products which do not or cannot, in any way, contaminate or be mixed with either raw halal food and halal certified products;

f) In case of Section 7 b, failure of owners, managers, lessors to communicate in writing to the lessees, tenants or suppliers or to include in their contract a provision to comply with this Ordinance;

g) In case of Section 8 c, using of the same gloves and such other body gears for all types of meat or processed meat, whether halal or non-halal/haram;

h) Failure of the staffs to apprise the buying consumers of the said exclusivity of such carrier or counter for halal-certified products upon such failure caused the consumer's refusal of these products;

i) Failure to segregate or separate at least ten percent (10%) of all their push carts and other carriers or failure to provide a sign or indication that such segregated carriers are for halal-certified products only.

j) Failure to provide at least one (1) counter for halal products when such establishment has at least two or more counters;  
k) Failure to provide /allocate space in the payment counter if the supermarket, shop or store has only one payment counter or failure of the staff assigned to such payment counter to wear gloves/aprons or other body gears exclusive for halal products;  
l) To cause the use of halal counter for non-halal/haram products which may contaminate or be mixed with other products;  
m) Such other cases analogous to the foregoing or such other acts which constitute a violation of any provisions of this Ordinance.  

ARTICLE IV  
PENAL PROVISIONS  

SECTION 13: Owners/proprietors of grocery stores, supermarkets and other establishments as well as suppliers and tenants of the same that are covered in this Ordinance who violate any provisions of Article III in relation to Article II as well as the prohibited acts and practices under Section 14 of Article IV of this Ordinance shall be penalized as follows:  
a) First Offense: Fine of not less than P500.00 but not more than P1,000.00 at the discretion of the Court  
b) Second Offense: Fine of not less than P3,000.00 and/or suspension of business permit and license to operate for at least three (3) months but not more than six (6) months, at the discretion of the Court  
c) Third Offense: Fine of not less than P3,000.00 but not more than P5,000.00 and/or revocation of business permit and license to operate, at the discretion of the Court.  

ARTICLE V  
PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION  

SECTION 14: The City Health Office, City Veterinarian's Office and/or Business Bureau of Davao City, in close coordination with the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos and other concerned agencies of the City of Davao, are hereby mandated to ensure that the provisions of this Article shall be strictly implemented and complied with by the covered supermarkets, shops, stores, centers, suppliers and other establishments operating within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City. For this purpose, a TASK FORCE consisting of the above-enumerated government offices is hereby created and mandated to perform the following functions:

1) To conduct inspection or assessment of delivery vans/carriers, storage rooms or warehouses and premises of grocery stores, supermarkets, meat shops to ensure compliance of establishing a "HALAL LANE" (all phases) as herein provided and make the necessary recommendation on the matter;

2) To receive complaints in any form for any violation of this Ordinance, and to investigate the same and make necessary findings and recommendations to any concerned agencies;

SECTION 15. PUBLIC INFORMATION DISSEMINATION - The Davao City Information Office in coordination with other Implementing Offices shall inform the public as well as the mail-owners, and other stakeholders such as Muslim Associations, of the provisions of this Ordinance in a manner and language which can be easily understood.

ARTICLE VI
FINAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 16. PRESUMPTION OF BEING HALAL - Any meat, processed meat and other meat products labeled as "HALAL" and marked with the official logo of the proper certifying body, is presumed to be "HALAL" as referred to herein, subject to and must be consistent with Ordinance No. 0447-10, Series of 2010, and/or other applicable ordinances and national laws.
