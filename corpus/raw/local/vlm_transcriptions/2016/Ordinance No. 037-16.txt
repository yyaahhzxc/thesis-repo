Ord. No. 037-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND NAGAHUANSUAG GAGMANGYAN MANANAGAT SA BARANGAY LASANG (NAGAMBALA), FOR THE "FISHERY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM OF MUNICIPAL FISHERY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT" UNDER THE BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING PROGRAMS FOR CY 2014";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code, provides to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(v) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and Nagahuansuag Gagmangyangan Mananagat sa Barangay Lasang (Nagambala), Davao City, for the Fishery Resource Management Program of Municipal Fishery Development Project under the Bottoms-up Budgeting Programs for CY 2014";
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect;
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED: September 13, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

For and in the absence of the Secretary:

Nilda C. Magno
Acting Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(Assistant Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod)

DEED OF DONATION

ONE (1) UNIT 7.5 H.P. MOTORIZED 16 FEET X 24"X19" BANCA
15 UNITS NON MOTORIZED 12 FEET X 18"X14" BANCA AND
WEIGHING SCALE, MANUAL 10 KLS. CAPACITY

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS :

This deed, made and executed in the City of Davao, Philippines, by and between:

The City Government of Davao-Davao City, an LGU of the Republic of the Philippines with
and office address at San Pedro St., Davao City, represented by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE
and hereinafter referred to as DONOR;

and

The NAGKAHIUSANG GAGMAY MANANAGAT SA BARANGAY LASANG (NAGAMBALA) located at
Brgy. Lasang, Davao City, represented by its President, CLEMENTE NAMOC, hereinafter referred to as DONEE;

WITNESSETH

1. That the DONORS: the owner of 1 unit 7.5 H.P. motorized 16 feet x 24" x 19" banca; 15 units
12x18"x14" non-motorized banca and weighing scale amounting to One hundred ninety four
Thousand four hundred fifty (Pp 194,450.00), which is more particularly described as follows:
1 unit motorized 16x24"x19" banca with serial no. WM 15080000250
(King Eagle brand)
15 units non-motorized 12x18"x14"
@10,000.00 each Php 150,000.00
1 unit weighing scale 10 kls. Capacity
Php 1,200.00

2. That the DONEE will use the aforesaid property under the project of the DONOR entitled:
"Fishery Resource Management Program" Municipal Fishery Development Project under Bottom-Up Budgeting CY 2014.

3. That the DONOR, by obligatory authority, helps the DONEE by way of donating the above-
described property, thus, DONOR hereby transfered conveys, by way of donation, unto the
DONEE said property for the purpose of increasing fish productivity, increasing income of
Fisherfolks, free and clear from all liens and encumbrances and subject to terms and
conditions provided hereunder.
a. To safeguard the property subject of donation, the DONEE must insure the donated
property and shall shoulder all license fees and taxes that will be imposed on the use and
operation of the same;
b. The DONEE must utilize the donated property in accordance to its intended use and shall
not in any way sell, lease, mortgage nor subject the same to all forms of encumbrances
nor liens;

--- [Page 10] ---
c. In order to optimize the useful life of the property subject of donation, the DONOR, through its project monitoring team shall monitor the use of the donated property within the period of five (5) years from the date of the DONEE's acceptance of the donation;
d. That upon acceptance of the donation, the donated property will become the property of the DONEE and not of any of the DONEE's officers, members, heirs and assigns;
e. That the DONOR shall appoint a medical officer, who shall be responsible for the welfare of the people of the DONEE.
f. The DONEE shall respect and honor all the terms and conditions of this Deed of Donation.
g. The DONEE must maintain a complete and detailed logbook of the donated property's operation including subsidiary ledger and, whenever requested, allow the DONOR's project Monitoring team and the COA Auditors (who are duly authorized to perform visitatorial audit) to access the same;
h. The DONEE shall accede to the peaceful return and surrender of ownership and possession of the donated property in case of non-compliance with any of the terms and conditions herein stipulated whereupon said DONEE must also pay/refund the DONOR for all the expenses incurred by the DONOR.
i. The property donated herein will automatically revert to the purpose for which the donation is made would cease to exist;
4. That the DONEE hereby accepts the foregoing donation, subject to all terms and conditions set forth heretofore, and at the same time expresses its profound gratitude for this act of liberality on the part of the DONOR, who by these presents also takes notice of acceptance.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have set their hand at the place first above written, on this day of 07 SEP 2017 2016.
