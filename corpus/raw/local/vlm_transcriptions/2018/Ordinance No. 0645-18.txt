Ord. No. 0645-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF MARITES PALMA FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF COMMERCIAL SAND AND GRAVEL EXTRACTION LOCATED IN BARANGAY MADUG, BUHAGINN DISTRICT, AND NEW CARMEN, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES FOR DEVELOPMENT - Article XII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) more particularly Section 1 thereof, "Request for Additional Allowable Use" provides that the uses enumerated in the preceding articles of general and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponent and upon favorable recommendation of the Local Zoning Board of Appeals (LZBA), may allow uses not enumerated therewith as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by ¾ majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and Ordinance.

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST FOR RECONSIDERATION - The request of Mr. Marites Palma for Additional Allowable Use for the operation of commercial sand and gravel extraction located in Barangay Madug, Buhagin District, and Barangay New Carmen, Tugbok District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 12, 2018, by three-fourths (3/4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
