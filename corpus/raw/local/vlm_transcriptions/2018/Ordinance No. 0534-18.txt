ORDINANCE NO. 0534-18
SERIES OF 2018

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGNE FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO; THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE- NATIONAL MEAT INSPECTION SERVICE (DA-NMIS) FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF MALAGOS "AA" SLAUGHTERHOUSE WITH MEAT AND COLD STORAGE FACILITY

--- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0534-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE-NATIONAL MEAT INSPECTION SERVICE (DA-NMIS) FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF MALAGOS "AA" SLAUGHTERHOUSE WITH MEAT AND COLD STORAGE FACILITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b)(i)(vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Amended Memorandum of Agreement (MoA) to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Agriculture-National Meat Inspection Service (DA-NMIS) for the construction of Malagos "AA" slaughterhouse with meat and cold storage facility.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, September 4, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENT:

This Agreement is entered into by and between:

The Department of Agriculture - National Meat Inspection Service (DA-NMIS), a government agency, with office address at No. 4 Visayas Avenue, Brgy. Varsa, Quezon City represented by its Executive Director, DR. ERNESTO S. GONZALES, herein referred to as "DA-NMIS";

and

The City Government of Davao with office address at San Pedro St., Davao City represented by its City Mayor, ATTY. SARAZ DUERTE-CARPIO herein referred to as "LGD".

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Philippines is envisioned to be a major producer of meat and meat products in Southeast Asia with meat coming from newly established and rehabilitated meat establishments that meet the prescribed standards of NMIS;

WHEREAS, Republic Act 9296 otherwise known as the Meat Inspection Code of the Philippines provides that the Local Government Units (LGUs) shall endeavor to establish or improve existing meat facilities that complies with the national standards, and the national government shall provide financial assistance for LGUs for this purpose;

WHEREAS, the Department of Agriculture – National Livestock Program (DA - NLP) provides technical and financial assistance to livestock and poultry farmers and LGUs in strategic areas of the country to promote livestock and poultry production in these areas;

WHEREAS, the Congress allocated to the DA funds for the NLP which the DA downloaded to NMIS earmarked for the construction of meat establishments in identified strategic area to be compliant to NMIS standards;

WHEREAS, one of the LGUs identified by the DA was to be beneficiary of the financial assistance for the construction of "AA" slaughterhouse, meat cutting plant and cold storage facilities is the City Government of Davao;

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao through Sangguniang Bayan Resolution no. series of _ has agreed to be the recipient of the DA-NMIS financial assistance for the construction of slaughterhouse, meat cutting plant and cold storage facilities and has duly authorized Davao City Mayor Sara Z. Duerte-Carpio to enter into and sign a Memorandum of Agreement with the DA- NMIS for the said project;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and their mutual covenants hereinafter set forth, the parties hereby agree to undertake the above project based on the following terms and conditions:

A. The National Meat Inspection Service (NMIS) shall:

1. Allocate the amount of EIGHTY MILLION PESOS (Pmp 80,000,000.00) for the National Livestock Program (NLP) for the establishment of Malagos "AA" Slaughterhouse with Meat-Cutting Facilities And Cold Storage in Brgy. Malagos, Baguio District, Davao City, Philippines;

2. Issue a certificate of availability of funds (CAF);

3. Pay the winning bidder the aforementioned amount divided/apportioned as follows:

--- [Page 10] ---
2.a Release of 15% of the Contract Price
Note: Retention fee will be 15% of the 50% of the contract price.
For mobilization and operating expenses

3.b Three (3) Progress Billings
(35%, 50%, 75%)
Note: 15% mobilization final to be recouped every progress billing.

For completed works validated by NMIS Project Management Office (PMO), RTCOF Focal Administrator, and LGU Davao City.

3.c Final payment (100%)

After submission of (1) terminal report as per WFP and Inspection; (2) Acceptance Report duly validated by NMIS and LGU; (3) as-built plan by civil works contractor; (4) AMTEC test result for fabrication.

4. Undertake the bidding, awarding, signing of contract and issuance of Notice to Proceed, in strict adherence to Republic Act No. 9184 (2007) and its Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations (RIRR) issued by the Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB) particularly the timelines for procurement action. For this purpose, the NMIS RAC shall allow only NMIS Registered contractors and fabricators to participate pursuant to DA Memorandum Circular No. 16-2013.

5. Require the winning bidder to complete the project within one hundred twenty (120) calendar days from the day the Notice to Proceed is received but shall in no case exceed six (6) months. Failure of the winning contractor to implement the project within the stipulated time, except by reason of FortuNatus Event or Force Majeure, the Contractor shall be liable for liquidated damages at least equal to one-tenth of one percent (0.001%) of the cost of unperformed portion for every day of delay (RA No. 9184, §13).

6. Inform the LGU for the schedule of bidding:

7. Provide technical assistance in the preparation of architectural and engineering plans and design, scope and Program of Works, equipment listing, and others considering the requirements of GMP and SSIP;

8. Approve the engineering plan and design, scope and Program of Works, equipment listing and cost estimates which shall form part of the bidding documents;

9. Accredite and provide list of NMIS accredited contractors and fabricators to LGU Davao City that shall be allowed to participate in the bidding of the project;

10. Monitor and submit monthly reports to the PMO through the concerned RTCOF Focal Administrator regarding the progress of the project implementation and conformance to the standards;

11. Record all transactions which are related to the utilization of the funds and keep the books of account open for inspection by COA or its duly authorized representative;

12. Allocate five percent (5%) of the budget to cover administrative expenses incurred such as, but not limited to, consultancy fees, honoraria, traveling expenses, training expenses and other related expenses of personnel involved in the project, during project implementation;

13. Require the fabricator to get an AMTEC Test Certification for the delivered and installed slaughterhouse with cutting and cold storage facilities equipment. AMTEC Test Certification must be included in the Bidding Specifications.

14. Conduct dry/test-run operation of slaughterhouse with cutting and cold storage facilities equipment upon project completion.

--- [Page 11] ---
15. Undertake capacity building program to enable the local meat inspection service to comply with the national standards and to take the project financially and technically viable by:

a. Organizing the following mandatory seminar and workshops to be attended by LGU officials and personnel: (1) GMP/SSOP including manual preparation; (2) Financial and Economic Analysis; (3) Environmental Management Plan (EMP); and (4) Marketing and Sales;

b. Conducting trainings such as but not limited to Slaughterhouse Operation and Management Orientation Seminar (SOMOS), Butchers Orientation Training and Basic Meat Inspection Course for LGU Veterinarians and meat inspectors;

16. Evaluate, classify and issue License To Operate (LTO);

17. Turn-over the project to the LGU after the final inspection by COA Auditor;

18. Collect 25% monthly share of the ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection fees as regulatory fee, immediately upon turnover of the project;

B. The LGU shall:

1. Provide for an adequate lot area, owned by the LGU (certified true copy of OCT/TCT/Absolute Deed of Sale or Deed of Donation and Acceptance to be submitted), for the construction of the project and certified by the appropriate office to be compliant with the existing zoning ordinance and other existing laws, rules and regulations;

2. Provide the following:

a. Sanggunian Resolution stating intent of the LGU to establish Slaughterhouse with cutting and cold storage facilities;

b. Sanggunian Resolution authorizing the LCE to enter into MOA with NMIS; and

c. Barangay Sanggunian Resolution allowing the construction of Slaughterhouse with cutting and cold storage facilities in Barangay (the area of construction).

3. Provide Good Financial Housekeeping Certificate issued by DILG;

4. Provide for electrical, water and communication connections, drainage system, access road to the facility, waste treatment facility and site development;

5. Prepare the engineering plans and design that conforms to the requirement of Good Manufacturing Practices-Sanitation Standard Operation Procedure (GMP-SSOP) and/or Hazard Analyza Critical Control Point (HACCP). These documents, including the approved engineering plan, bill of quantities, scope and program of works, and such other required legal and technical documents shall form part of the bidding documents;

6. In case of delay and or suspension of work, the LGU shall notify the NMIS in writing of any changes, delay or any concern relating to the complete and effective implementation of the identified project;

7. Secure the Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) or Certificate of Non-Coverage (CNC) and assist the contractor for securing other necessary permits;

8. Be responsible for any other expenses not covered by the NMIS funding, in order to comply with GMP, SSOP, and HACCP requirements;

--- [Page 12] ---
9. Provide administrative and management support mechanism through the Local Meat Inspection Service (LMIS) necessary for the implementation of the Project;
10. The LGU shall exercise extraordinary diligence in its compliance with all the stipulations agreed upon in the MOA ;
11. Conduct daily monitoring and evaluation using appropriate engineering tools such as PERT-CPM and S-Curve of the project and submit weekly reports to NMIS RTOC to ascertain progress of the implementation of the project and compliance to the scope of works and specifications;
12. Produce digital photos/videos that will document the project from the pre-inspection until the post-implementation of the project that will show that the project was done accordingly as required by the Contract;
13. Ensure necessary access by NMIS representatives to all facilities and documents relevant to the monitoring and evaluation on the progress of the project;
14. Submit a Terminal Report and as-built plan reflecting the amount of the actual cost of the Project. The liquidation documents should be duly audited in accordance with existing accounting rules as well as other relevant COG regulations;
15. Accept the Project and record the same in the LGU Book of Accounts;
16. Apply for License to Operate (LTO) "AA" of all facilities subject of this MOA with the NMIS and continually maintain its LTO "AA" and be responsible for organizing all necessary training of meat inspectors and workers as required for LTO, GMP-SSOP and HACCP certification;
17. Remit to NMIS, every month the twenty five percent (25%) of the ante-mortem and post-mortem inspection fees (regulatory fees) allocated in accordance with Executive Order No.137 and Meat Inspection Code of the Philippines (RA No. 9296 as amended by RA No. 16536);
18. The LGU shall give priority to the proper maintenance of the meat establishment in good repair and operating condition by utilizing the 75% share of the LGU on the collected meat inspection fees (RA No. 9296 as amended by RA No. 16536);
19. The LGU shall ensure the proper usage of the meat establishment thru enactment of an ordinance which will require all meat dealers/handlers including lecheronos to dress domestic food animals in the meat establishment;
20. The LGU shall operate and maintain the meat establishment in good repair and operating condition by utilizing the 75% share of the LGU on collected meat inspection fees (RA # 9296 as amended by RA # 16536). However, after a year of operation with full compliance with NMIS requirements, the LGU may enter into a partnership agreement for the operation, maintenance and rehabilitation of the slaughterhouse with cutting and cold storage facilities. The terms and conditions of such partnership agreement, with the private entity shall be subject to the approval of the NMIS.
C. Effectivity
This Agreement shall take effect on the date of signing hereof by the parties concerned and shall continue to be enforced until the meat establishment ceased to be operational or until otherwise rescinded by the Meat Inspection Board. Any modification to this Agreement shall be mutually agreed in writing by the parties.
