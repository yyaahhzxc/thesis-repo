Ord. No. 0639-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF MR. KENT ANTHONY LAZARRAGRA FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF COMMERCIAL SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY LOCATED IN BARANGAY MANDUG, BUHANGIN DISTRICT, AND BARANGAY NEW VALENCIA, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES FOR DEVELOPMENT - Article XII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) more particularly Section 1 thereof, Request for Additional Allowable Use provides that:

"the uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general zone and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod may, upon request of the project proponent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustments and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not enumerated thereunder as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by 3/4 majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolutions and Ordinance."

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE - The application of Kent Anthony Lazarraga for the reconsideration of his application for additional allowable use for the operation of commercial sand and gravel quarry located in Barangay Mandug, Buhangin District, and Barangay New Valencia, Tugbok District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILIT Y CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 12, 2018, by a 3/4 majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
