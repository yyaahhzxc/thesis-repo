Ord. No. 0644-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panglungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF ARMANDO CATUCAG FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF EARTHFILL QUARRY LOCATED AT PUROK 8, BARANGAY WAH, BUHANGIN DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES OF DEVELOPMENT - Article XII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) more particularly Section 1 thereof, "Request for Additional Allowable Use" provides that the uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panglungsod, upon application of the project proponent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA & A), hereby approves the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by 3/4 majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panglungsod through resolution and Ordinance:

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE - The request of Mr. Armando Catucag for the reconsideration of his application for Additional Allowable Use for the operation of earthfill quarry located at Purok 8, Barangay Wah, Buhangin District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 12, 2018, by three-fourths (3/4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
