Ord. No. 0459-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN THE DEED AND DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY MONICO R. GOHETIA SR., LEONARDA G. ARENDAIN, AURELIA G. BULIG AND AGAPIITO R. GOHETIA SR. IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO PERTAINING TO THEIR 4/5 SHARE OVER A PARCEL OF LAND SITUATED IN BARANGAY FATIMA, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, THIS CITY, CONTAINING AN AREA OF 15,000 SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, COVERED BY TRANSFER CERTIFICATE OF TITLE NO. CL-8921";

SECTION 2 DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b)(1)(vi) of R.A. No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon which the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to accept and sign the Deed of Donation to be executed by Monico R. Gohetia Sr., Leonarda G. Arendain, Aurelia G. Bulig and Agapiito R. Gohetia Sr. in favor of the City Government of Davao pertaining to their 4/5 share over a parcel of land situated in Barangay Fatima, Paquibato District, the City, containing an area of 15,000 square meters, more or less, covered by Transfer Certificate of Title No. CL-8921;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, valid sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, June 5, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
