Ord. No. 0403-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 188 (PROHIBITION ON PRODUCTION OF ROAD SIGNS) OF ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, SERIES OF 2012, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE 'COMPREHENSIVE TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC CODE OF DAVAO CITY'".

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVE - This Ordinance aims to provide clearer guidance on the interpretation of the said provision;

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - Section 188 (Prohibition on Production of Road Signs) of Ordinance No. 0334-12, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive Transport and Traffic Code of Davao City" is hereby amended as:

Original Text

Section 188. Prohibition on Production of Road Sign

It is unlawful for any person, natural or juridical, or any government instrumentalities, except for the City Transport and Traffic Management Office, to manufacture, fabricate, install and erect traffic sign and/or signal and/or pavement marking on city road, thoroughfare and highway. The use of logo or emblem of a company on any unauthorized road sign shall be "prima facie" evidence that said road sign was erected by the owner of the logo or emblem;

Amendment

Section 188. Prohibition on Production of Road Signs

It is unlawful for any person, natural or juridical, or any government instrumentalities, except for the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTMO), to put up any kind of temporary or permanent structures/materials on all public, private, open spaces, roads, to manufacture, fabricate, install and erect traffic sign and/or signal, and/or pavement marking on city road, thoroughfare and highway. The use of logo or emblem of a company on any unauthorized road sign shall be "prima facie" evidence that said road sign was erected by the owners of the establishments where the logo or emblem is found;

SECTION 4. PENAL CLAUSE - For any violation committed thereof, the penalty shall be FIVE THOUSAND PESOS (P5,000.00);

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect 15 days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao;
