"SECTION 21. Closure and Opening of Roads. -(a) A local government unit may, pursuant to an ordinance, temporarily or permanently close or open any local road, alley, park, or square for public use, or for any other purpose. That in case of permanent closure, such ordinance must be approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of all the members of the sanggunian, and when necessary, an adequate substitute for the public facility that is subject to closure is provided.

"(f) Any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs, or the holding of public works, works, and highways, telecommunications, and waterworks projects. Ord. No. 0398-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ALONG DATU ABENG STREET, BARANGAY CALINAN PROPER, CALINAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY, ON MARCH 9, 2018, FROM 8:00 A.M. TO 6:00 P.M. FOR A CIRCUIT RACE EVENT AS PART OF THE 81ST ARAW NG CALINAN CELEBRATION";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:
"Any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs...";

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The road along Datu Abeng Street, Barangay Calinan Proper, Calinan District, this City, shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on March 9, 2018 from 8:00 a.m. until 6:00 p.m. for a Circuit Race Event in line with the celebration of the 81st Araw ng Calinán;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 6th day of February, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
