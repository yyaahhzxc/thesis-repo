COMMENT/OPINION
September 7, 2018
Respectfully RETURNED to the ASSISTANT CITY ADMINISTRATOR (ADMINISTRATION) ATTY. TRISTAN DWIGHT P. DOMINGO, the documents attached hereto relative to the above Subject with the information discussed hereunder.
Real estate developers are required under the Urban Development and Housing Act (RA No. 7279), as amended by RA No. 10884, to comply with the "balanced housing development" requirement. The "balanced housing development" requirement can be achieved is through Incentive Compliance as provided under the Implementing Rules and Regulations issued by the HLURB through its Resolution No. 955, Series of 2017. Section 11.1 of the said resolutions states that:
"Any developer, who shall participate in any socialized housing project approved by the HLURB xxx xxx xxx by way of constructing or contracting through a Third party for the construction or installation of any of the enumerated, shall be considered as compliance:
Xxx xxx xxx
(c) implementation of land development plans which shall include the construction of roads, drainage and open space facilities:"
Fortunately for this City, there are city-owned lands that are intended for socialized housing which remain undeveloped due to budget constraints and at present, the National Government, through the HLURB, in order to ensure the strict compliance, proposes to the City to make the city-owned lands available for land development that will be undertaken by the Developers in exchange for the Incentive Compliance.
Simply stated, this City will benefit from the land development without incurring costs on its own.
With the growing demand for socialized housing in this City, it is timely that this City gives favor to the above-said proposal as the Local Government Code of 1991 (RA No. 7160) mandates under Section 25 (b) thereof:
"SECTION 25. (b) National agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They may also coordinate with the provincial government units both in the planning and implementation of said national projects."

--- [Page 9] ---
Likewise, under Republic Act No. 7279 otherwise known as the "Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992," it has been the declared policy of the State:
"Sec. 1 Declaration of State Policy and Program Objectives. It shall be the policy of the State to undertake, in cooperation with the private sector, a comprehensive and continuing Urban Development and Housing Program hereinafter referred to as the Program, which shall:
Xxx xxx xxx
(f) Improve the capability of local government units in undertaking urban development and housing programs and projects."
Clearly from the foregoing, this City has a role to play in the Program of the State and is legally bound to do so. In addition, the proposal of HLURB, in our humble view, conforms to the mandate of the above-aid laws.
WHEREFORE, it is hereby recommended that the matter be forwarded to the Sangguniang Panlungsod for the required legislative authority in order for the City Mayor to sign the MOA.

WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, Section 18 in relation to Section 21 of Republic Act No. 7279 as amended by Republic Act No. 10884 (the "Revised UDHA"), and Board Resolution No. 965, Series of 2017 or the "Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations to Govern Sections 3, 18 and 20 of Republic Act 7279, otherwise known as the Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992, As Amended by Republic Act No. 10884, Otherwise Known as "Cleaner Cities" and "Smart City Development Program Amendments" (Revised IRR) requires the DEVELOPERS of proposed subdivisions and condominiums to comply with the balanced housing requirement of equivalent to at least 15% and 5%, respectively, or the same equivalent based on the cost of their subdivision or condominium project;
WHEREAS, Section 18(b) of the Revised UDHA, in relation to Section 11 of the Revised IRR states that the balanced housing requirement may be complied with by participating in any socialized housing project approved by the HLURB by way of construction or implementation of land development plans which shall include the construction of roads, drainage and open space facilities;
WHEREAS, the HLURB is the sole agency tasked to ensure the strict compliance aforementioned laws;
WHEREAS, the LGU is the owner of one parcel of land located at Barangay Lasang, Davao City designated as Lot 7, Pes-11-664,495, with a total area of one hundred forty-six thousand five hundred seventy (146,570) square meters, more or less, and currently covered by Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) No. T-433508 of the Registry of Deeds for the City of Davao (hereinafter referred to as the "Property"). A copy of the TCT is attached as Annex "A";
WHEREAS, SMDC, SMPHI, STRC, 102 EDSA, MSDPC and PMI (the "DEVELOPERS") undertake to construct or implement land development on the Property as incentivized compliance under the abovementioned laws;
NOW THEREFORE, in accordance with their respective mandate, the PARTIES agree as follows:
Section 1. Project Description. - The project shall consist mainly of the land development of a parcel of land, which shall include the construction of roads, drainage, and open space facilities, with an aggregate area of 146,570 sq.m. A copy of the site development plan for the Project is attached as Annex "B" ("The Project").
Section 2. Socialized Housing Compliance Component. - In accordance with the existing laws and rules and regulations, HLURB shall accord the Project as a BALAI Filipino Housing Project. This shall qualify all participations made by the DEVELOPERS for incentivized compliance of the balance housing requirement under RA 7279, as amended by RA 10884.

--- [Page 15] ---
Section 3. Participation of the DEVELOPERS with Specifications.
The DEVELOPERS shall at their exclusive expense, raise the design, engineering, and construction of the land development for the Property, which shall include the construction of roads, drainage, and open space facilities with a total value of THREE HUNDRED TWENTY-TWO MILLION FOUR HUNDRED FIFTY-FOUR THOUSAND PESOS (P222,454,000.00) for a total area of 146,570 square meters. A map with the geodetic of the specified total area is attached hereto as Annex "C".
A plan detailing the computation table is attached as Annex "D".

SMDC shall serve as the disbursing entity of the DEVELOPERS to the project contractor.

Upon the completion of the Project, the Parties shall coordinate and mutually agree on the schedule and parameters for inspection thereof. Upon the issuance of a Certificate of Completion by HLURB, the DEVELOPERS shall then turnover the Project, at no cost or right to reimbursement, to the LGU for its awarding to the identified beneficiaries.

Section 4. Development Schedule.
The completion of the Project shall be in accordance with the project timetable attached hereto as Annex "E".

Section 5. Socialized Housing Credit Commitment.
The land development shall be used as basis for computing the advance credit in favor of the DEVELOPERS in compliance with the balanced housing requirement in Section 18 of the Revised HUDHA. Section 11 of HLURBA Board Resolution No. R-965, series of 2017, a copy of which is attached hereto as Annex "F", provides for incentivized compliance on participation in the rehabilitation of calamity-stricken communities under the BALAI Programs of the HUDCC. The computation of the amount equivalent to at least twenty (20%) percent of the total project cost of the socialized housing project in order to be credited the entire socialized housing project and program shall be hereby authorized.

On the basis of the foregoing, the amount of THREE HUNDRED TWENTY-TWO MILLION FOUR HUNDRED FIFTY-THOUSAND PESOS (P222,454,000.00), participation of the DEVELOPERS can immediately be used as advance credit of ONE BILLION SIX HUNDRED TWELVE MILLION TWO HUNDRED SEVENTY THOUSAND PESOS (P1,612,270,000.00).

Notwithstanding the foregoing, HLURB recognizes the critical role of the DEVELOPERS in the Project, and hereby agrees and expressly authorizes the immediate application of the advance credit values computed above by the DEVELOPERS upon the execution of this MOA, for any of their projects located anywhere in the Philippines. HLURB warrants that the authorization granted hereunder may be used by the DEVELOPERS in lieu of a Compliance Certification to be subsequently issued as proof of compliance with Section 18 of the Revised HUDHA.

SECTION 6. Government Responsibilities.
The Parties understand and recognize the significant and critical role of the land development as a component of the Project. Thus, in addition to the development of the Project, HLURB and LGU agree to render full cooperation and fulfill the following obligations to support the DEVELOPERS' construction of the Project:

i) Authorize the DEVELOPERS and their employees, agents, contractors, and representatives to mobilize and access the Project for the purpose of

--- [Page 16] ---
conducting the necessary technical studies and surveys, as well as all works and construction in furtherance of their obligations hereunder;

ii) Process and secure all required national and local permits, consents, certificates and approvals;

iii) Provide and secure the final alignment and demarcations of the main roads, streets, and alleys, including right of way agreements with private land owners, if necessary, to enable the DEVELOPERS to lay down the necessary transmission lines and tapping points needed for the Project;

iv) Provide adequate security measures for the project development and construction personnel of the DEVELOPERS during the development of land until full turn-over, as stipulated below; and

vii) Take-over, by itself or through assignment in favor of another government instrumentality, the over-all operation, maintenance, and management of the Project, including all of its component parts, upon completion of the same.

SECTION 7. DEVELOPERS' Representations and Warranties. - The DEVELOPERS hereby represent and warrant in favor of HLUJB and LGU that:

i) They have the technical capacities and financial capabilities to undertake the construction and development of the Property;

ii) They are real estate developers in good standing, organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with legal authorities and capacities to enter into this MOA; and

iii) They have secured all necessary corporate approvals for the execution and full implementation of their obligations and responsibilities under this MOA.

SECTION 8. Force Majeure. - No Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in the performance of its obligations hereunder to the extent that such failure or delay shall be caused by or arise out of a "Force Majeure Event." For purposes of this Agreement, a "Force Majeure Event" shall include, but not be limited to: (i) declared or undeclared war, armed hostilities, revolution, rebellion, insurrection, riot, public disorder, strikes, lockouts, or other labor disturbances; (ii) acts of God, including but not limited to, earthquakes, floods, storms, hurricanes, typhoons, landslides, and volcanic eruptions; (iii) unforeseen rock formations in the geologic layers of the identified deep well sites; and (iv) extreme and unforeseen weather conditions, fire, unusual flood, earthquake, and similar calamities or acts of God.

The Party invoking the existence of a Force Majeure Event shall immediately notify the other Party, specifying the nature and details of the Force Majeure Event, how it affects the Party's ability to comply with its obligations hereunder, and a proposed mitigating action to address the same. Upon the cessation of such Force Majeure Event, the Parties shall immediately resume the performance of their obligations hereunder. However, if the Force Majeure Event extends for a period in excess of 180 continuous days, the Parties hereto shall meet to discuss the basis and terms upon which this MOA may be continued or terminated.
