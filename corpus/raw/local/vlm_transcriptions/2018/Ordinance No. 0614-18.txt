Ord. No. 0614-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION AND ACCEPTANCE BY AND BETWEEN JAPAN INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION AGENCY (JICCA) AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF ONE (1) MAX PREMIUM PLANT".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi), Section 22 (a) (5) and (c), of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

Section 22. Corporate Powers.

(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:

xxx xxx xxx

(5) To enter into contracts;

(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the Sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Donation and Acceptance by and between Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICCA) and the City Government of Davao relative to the donation of one (1) MAX Premium Plant.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

That the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) (hereinafter referred to as the DONOR), with address at the 40th Floor, Yuchengco Tower, RCBC Plaza, Sen. Gil J. Puyat Avenue corner Ayala Avenue, Makati City, represented by MR. YOSHIHO WADA, Chief Representative, of legal age, hereby freely and voluntarily give, convey and transfer by way of donation unto the City Government of Davao (hereinafter referred to as the DONEE), one (1) MAX Premium Plant, to have and to hold the same by the DONEE, absolutely and within its durable years of use, for the official use of the counterpart personnel in the conduct of the activities related to The Verification Survey with the Private Sector for Disseminating Japanese Technologies for Improving Environment Through Establishment of Used Cooking Oil Collection Systems and Biodiesel Plant, provided that the maintenance of the said Equipment will be the sole responsibility of the DONEE.

That the City Government of Davao, represented by the Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, of legal age, hereby accepts and receives the abovementioned donation with the understanding that the DONEE will maintain and keep the equipment in good working condition; and on behalf of the DONEE hereby thank JICA for their generosity and liberality.

Dated this day of 2018, in Makati City, Metro Manila Philippines.

JAPAN INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION AGENCY (JICA)
