Ord. No. 0460-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, INDIGENOUS CULTURAL COMMUNITIES AND THE NATIONAL COMMISSION ON INDIGENOUS PEOPLES (NCIP) RELATIVE TO THE RELOCATION PROJECT OF EAST MARAHAN, MARILOG DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 22 (a) (5) (c) and Section 455 (b)(1)(v) of R.A. No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:

Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

--- [Page 7] ---
**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY**  
The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Amended Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and among the City Government of Davao, Indigenous Cultural Communities and the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) relative to the Relocation Project of East Marahan, Marilao District, this City.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**  
If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY**  
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED**, June 5, 2018, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement is made and entered into this 2019 in City, Philippines, by and between:

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit (LGU) duly established under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at San Pedro Street, Davao City, Philippines, herein represented by SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARRIO, City Mayor and hereinafter known as the FIRST PARTY;

The MATIGASALOG INDIGENOUS CULTURAL COMMUNITIES through their Indigenous Political Structure (IPS) authorizing their Indigenous Peoples Organization the Matigasalog Indigenous Cultural Communities (MAMATIPCICO), Inc., Davao City, Inc. (MAMATIPCICO) represented by DATU AMADO MANASABID, Chairman Ancestral Domain Management Office, pursuant to a Community Resolution attached herewith as Annex "A," hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARTY;

-AND-

THE NATIONAL COMMISSION ON INDIGENOUS PEOPLES (NCIP), a Government agency under the Office of the President of the Republic of the Philippines, with Central Office address located at E. B. Valeroan Building, E. Lopez Street, Sandawa Plaza, S.I.R., New Manila, Davao City, represented herein by its Regional Director ATTY. GERONCIO R. AGUIO, CESO III, National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) Regional Office XI, hereinafter known as the THIRD PARTY.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, in addressing the plight of the families affected by the soil erosion, natural disaster, and homeless living within the geo-hazard areas of the mountainous range of Matigal District, Davao City, the FIRST PARTY initiated a program to provide a Relocation Project at East Marchan, Matigal District which shall give adequate, viable, and safe community for the indigenous peoples (IP) and non-indigenous peoples alike;

WHEREAS, the proposed relocation site of the FIRST PARTY is located within the Ancestral Domain of the SECOND PARTY covered by Certificate of Ancestral Domain Title (CADT) No. R-10-KT-0701-001 and the latter through Matigasalog-Manobo Tribal People Council of Elder Davao, Inc. (MAMATIPCICO) issued Resolution No. 14, Series of 2015 to allocate Ten (10) Hectares of their Ancestral Domain for the proposed relocation project locally it was occupied, and possessed by the ANSUFO Family, particularly Nangindagilang Village in Marchan, Mainan Proper, Davao City;

WHEREAS, the ANSUFO Family, possessor/occupant of the proposed relocation site belonging to the Indigenous Cultural Communities of the SECOND PARTY had previously given their consent to the FIRST PARTY, and manifested their approval through the SECOND PARTY and allocated Ten (10) Hectares thereof;

WHEREAS, the Project as proposed has been duly solicited by the SECOND PARTY thus the process provided for under Section 39 of NCIP Administrative Order No.3, Series of 2012 otherwise

--- [Page 9] ---
known as the Revised Guidelines on Free and Prior Informed Consent (FPIC) and Related Processes of 2012 that shall apply in which case the THIRD PARTY will only conduct field validation activities with the SECOND PARTY;

WHEREAS, after a series of consultative meetings of all the parties and as a result of the field validation and negotiation activities in the communities of the SECOND PARTY in accordance with their customary laws and practices, the SECOND PARTY gave their consent to the Relocation Site;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto agree, and by these presents, they do hereby agree, to be bound by the following terms and conditions, to wit:

1. COMMENCEMENT OF THE PROJECT. The Project shall commence upon signing of all parties to the agreement;

2. DURATION. The duration of this agreement shall be for a period of [ ] year from commencement, renewable for another [ ] Years; a. LOCATION AND AREA. The agreed site where the FIRST PARTY shall develop the Relocation Site will be in the Matigalug Village, East Marahan, Matigalug Proper, Davao City, specifically the portion allotted by the Arnuda Family, consisting of Ten (10) Hectares: Sketch Map including the technical metes and bounds thereof are hereo attached as Annex "A" and formed an integral part thereof; A Contract of Usufruct shall be executed by the SECOND PARTY in favor of the Individual Non-IP Identified beneficiaries.

3. CONSENT. With the signing of this Agreement, the SECOND PARTY shall be deemed to have given and hereby gives its consent to the Project. However, in the event that there are changes and alterations in the Project Plan, the FIRST PARTY undertakes to inform in writing the SECOND PARTY and the THIRD PARTY. Also, the FIRST PARTY agrees to conduct community consultations with respect to changes in their Project Plan;

4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE FIRST PARTY.

4.1 The FIRST PARTY shall develop a dirt road necessary for the Relocation Site;

4.2 The FIRST PARTY shall facilitate and coordinate with other National Government Agencies for probable sourcing of funds for housing, water distribution, livelihood among others;

4.3 The FIRST PARTY shall facilitate, manage, supervise and monitor the implementation of the project;

4.4 The FIRST PARTY shall identify a BLOCK consisting of square metres of the Relocation Site to be assigned and allocated for the Non-Indigenous People Beneficiary; The size of the block and or the number of lots allotted for the Non-IP beneficiaries shall be determine by the FIRST PARTY upon consultation with the SECOND PARTY;

--- [Page 10] ---
4.5 The FIRST PARTY shall create and lead the Screening Committee which shall screen and evaluate potential beneficiaries not previously identified. The SECOND PARTY shall be a member of the said Screening Committee. Their representative shall be selected and endorsed by the SECOND PARTY through Maligasag-Manobo Tribal Peoples Council of Elders, Inc. (MAMATRIPCEDE) and the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NClP) Davao City Provincial Office.

4.6 It shall, at all times, respect the rights, customs, culture and traditions of Maligasag Tribe and shall take reasonable endeavors to ensure that laborers and contractors working for it shall do likewise. The FIRST PARTY shall be culture sensitive in its dealings with the laborers and contractors throughout the entire duration of the agreement. For this purpose, the former must give utmost respect to the latter's rites and practices including their sacred cultural sites.

5. ORIGIATIONS OF THE SECOND PARTY: The following are the obligations of the SECOND PARTY:

5.1. The SECOND PARTY shall execute a Contract of Usufruct for the individual Non-IP household identified, screened and approved by the FIRST PARTY as beneficiary of the Relocation Site;

5.2. The SECOND PARTY shall assist the FIRST PARTY in identifying and screening qualified beneficiaries;

5.3. The SECOND PARTY shall select volunteers who will monitor the project on its progress as well as its compliance on the program of works and project specification. The volunteer shall not receive remuneration;

5.4. The SECOND PARTY shall give unimpeded access to the FIRST PARTY employees, contractors and representatives to the Relocation Site;

5.5. The SECOND PARTY shall cooperate with the FIRST PARTY and the THIRD PARTY and their representatives in the monitoring of project developments

5.6. The SECOND PARTY shall endeavor to clarify and resolve any conflict arising in relation to ancestral lands, crops and other properties affected by the Project and likewise assist in good faith to settle any and all disputes that may arise between the FIRST PARTY and the members of the community;

6. ROLE OF THE THIRD PARTY:

6.1. The THIRD PARTY shall protect and promote the interest and well-being of the Indigenous Peoples with due regard to their beliefs, customs, traditions and institutions.

6.2. To see to it that all the terms and conditions stipulated in this Agreement shall be respected and implemented.

6.3. To conduct visits in the sites/areas of the Relocation Project to secure the conditions and well-being of the ICC's/IPs;

--- [Page 11] ---
7. MODIFICATION: Any modification of this Agreement will be effected only if in writing and signed by all the parties hereto.

8. FORCE MAJEURE: The FIRST PARTY shall not be held liable to the SECOND PARTY for any failure or delay in the performance of its obligations under this Agreement due to causes beyond its reasonable control or that is not foreseeable, unavoidable, or not due to its negligence, including but not limited to acts of God, war, terrorism, strikes, lockouts, embargoes, first explosions, sabotages, floods, epidemics, quarantine restrictions, accidents, typhoons, labor disputes, strikes, orders, restraints or prohibitions by any government authority, and other causes analogous or similar to the foregoing.

9. COMPLIANCE IN GOOD FAITH: All the parties to this Agreement hereby bind themselves to exercise utmost good faith in the performance of their duties, particularly on those directly affecting the Project and other related activities.

10. RESOLUTION OF DISPUTES:
a. Any complaint involving the interpretation and implementation of this Agreement or non-compliance or violation of any provision hereof shall be raised to the NCIP Davao City Provincial Office, which shall verify the complaint, through the conduct of oicular hearing, and then proceed to the process of conciliation and mediation. If the complaint pertains to the selection of bidders, it shall be resolved by the screening committee which shall be headed by its FIRST PARTY and a representative from NAMATRIPCEDE and NCIP;
b. When there is failure of the Parties to arrive at an amicable settlement, the NCIP Provincial Office Provincial Office shall forward the case to the Council of Elders/Leaders, which shall resolve the matter by applying its customs and traditions such that if there is a clear violation of the terms and conditions of this Agreement based on the report, the Council of Elders/Leaders shall order the guilty Party to comply with its obligation and to pay a penalty as the council may reasonably determine;
c. Any Party aggrieved by the order and/or decision of the Council of Elders/Leaders may file a complaint before the NCIP Regional Hearing Officer in Region X-Davao City, who shall resolve the case in accordance with NCIP Administrative Order Circular No. 1, Series of 2014, Revised Rules of Procedure before the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP);

11. EFFECT OF VIOLATIONS ON THE PROVISIONS: Any person/partty who willfully violates or fails to comply with the duty or the obligation under the provision of this Agreement may be dealt with in accordance with the customary laws and practices of the concerned ICC/IP's and sanctions, penalties, or imposed in accordance therewith. However, any complaint involving the interpretation and implementation of this Agreement or any revision thereof shall be filed before the appropriate NCIP Regional Hearing Office for proper resolution;

12. NON-WAIVER OF RIGHTS: The failure of any Party to exercise promptly or to fulfill its one or more instances with regard to the performance of any of the responsibilities and obligations in this Agreement shall not be construed as abandonment or cancellation or waiver of the right to impose the performance of such responsibilities and obligations. No waiver by any Party shall be deemed to have been made unless expressed in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

--- [Page 12] ---
13. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE: The principle of culture sensitivity shall govern the relationship of the parties to this Agreement:

14. EXPLANATION VIA LOCAL DIALECT AND VOLUNTARINESS: This Agreement is explained in the vernacular and in their common and native dialect to all the parties and is entered into voluntarily by all the parties herein, without force and duress.
