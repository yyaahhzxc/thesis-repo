Ord. No. 0469-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panglungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, BANKEROHAN LIVESTOCK MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE, THE DEPARTMENT OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY AND THE UNIVERSITY OF THE IMMACULATE CONCEPTION RELATING TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE WASTEWATER TREATMENT SYSTEM UPGRAADING PROGRAM AND GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGNE THE SAME, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the Mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and agreements, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panglungsod pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and among the City Government of Davao, the Department of Science and Technology (DOST) Region XI, Bankerohan Livestock Multipurpose Cooperative (BLMC), and the University of the Immaculate Conception (UC) relative to the implementation of the Wastewater Treatment System Upgrading Program.

SECTION 4. CONDITION - The approval of this Ordinance is without prejudice to the proposed transfer of Ma-a Slaughterhouse in the near future, as the approval of this Ordinance is only for purpose of complying with the environmental requirements without necessarily abdicating the future decision to relocate Ma-a Slaughterhouse to another location.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, June 19, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

DECLARATION OF AGREEMENT:

WHEREAS, the “Wastewater Treatment System Upgrading Program” was funded by the DOST-PCIERD with the objective of helping local MSMEs, public facilities and small communities through the introduction of low-cost, nature based wastewater treatment;

WHEREAS, the “Wastewater Treatment System Upgrading Program” will be implemented in Davao City through the partnership among DOST XI, UIC-SRC, BLMC and City of Davao;

WHEREAS, the DOST XI is a regional arm of the DOST and is committed to serve the Filipino nation and to relate its activities to the needs of the Filipino people and their aspirations for social progress and transformation;

WHEREAS, Ms. Ludovina M. Porticos is project leader and committed in implementing a Vertical Helophyte Filter System for the Ma-a Slaughterhouse which functions as a resilient, nature-based, and low-cost wastewater treatment technology that will promote sustainable industries through innovation;

WHEREAS, the BLMC is a Cooperative which is operating at the Ma-a Slaughterhouse through a PPP agreement with the City of Davao;

WHEREAS, City of Davao is the Local Government Unit that has the area of responsibility over the current address of the Ma-a Slaughterhouse;

WHEREAS, it is the mutual and collective aim of the Parties to promote sustainable development among MSMEs and the communities by supporting sustainable and low-cost wastewater treatment technologies and to contribute to national development.

WHEREAS, DOST XI, UIC, BLMC and City of Davao jointly pledge to extend their full cooperation to one another for the effective implementation of the project;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and their mutual covenants herein set forth, DOST XI, UIC, BLMC and City of Davao hereby stipulate and agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Project Title: This project shall be known as “Wastewater Treatment System Upgrading Program (WATsUP) Project: Enhancing the Applicability of the Vertical Helophyte Filter Systems for Small Communities, Local MSMEs and Public Entities”.

--- [Page 15] ---
2. DOST XI and UIC Financial Support:

DOST and UIC shall provide financial support to the project with a grant of ONE MILLION AND SEVEN HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS ONLY (Php 1,700,000.00) for twenty-one (21) months to be expended for the construction of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System at the Ma-a Slaughterhouse.

II. SPECIFIC PROVISIONS
II.1 Responsibilities
UIC-SRC shall:
1. Serve as the monitoring institution that will supervise the project implementation output and compliance with the work plan;
2. Provide the funding support thru for the implementation of the project in accordance with the approved budgetary requirement;
3. Facilitate the procurement and/or acquisition of personnel services, necessary supplies and materials, labor, rental, laboratory tests, and other project-related requirements;
4. Administer the project funds in accordance with the approved budget, work plan and timetable;
5. Facilitate meetings among partners to monitor the progress of the project;

--- [Page 17] ---
6. Work in coordination with Mr. Ligtvvoet and BLMC for executing project plans;
7. Assist in documenting the whole process of the implementation of the project, publish articles about the process and results of the project and organize visitations and the likes for project information dissemination and promotion of the technology;
8. Conduct quarterly monitoring of the project from October 2018 to April 2020 to assess the effectiveness of the prototype and identify possible problems.

DOST XI shall:

1. Serve as the developer of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System technology;
2. Develop the design with cost estimation and implement a prototype of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System technology for the Ma-a Slaughterhouse;
3. Coordinate with BLMC for the necessary adjustments of the technology design based on the actual production capacity, plant lay-out, and other necessary considerations;
4. Administer the project funds in accordance with the approved budget, work plan and timetable;
5. Initiate meetings among partners to monitor the progress of the project;
6. Document the whole process of the implementation of the field-test project, and provide information to UIC-SRC in publishing articles about the process and results of the project;
7. Present the results of the project to the UIC-SRC, City of Davao, BLMC and to other relevant partners and stakeholders as maybe determined by parties involved in pursuit of information dissemination and promotion of the sustainable technology;
8. Submit a full technical report of the project UIC-SRC, City of Davao and BLMC upon completion of the development of the prototype.
9. Coordinate with UIC-SRC, City of Davao and BLMC during the monitoring activities from October 2018 to April 2020 to address possible problems and evaluate the effectiveness of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System.

City of Davao shall:

1. Provide an area of 650 square meter within the Ma-a Slaughterhouse compound where the low-cost, nature based wastewater treatment technology will be installed;
2. Provide necessary information and assistance (e.g. technical, infrastructure support, area preparation) to DOST XI and UIC-SRC as inputs to the

--- [Page 18] ---
development of the design, development of prototype of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System technology, and over-all effective implementation of the project;
3. Work in coordination with UIC-SRC and DOST XI for executing project plans;
4. Participate and allow DOST XI and UIC-SRC in the monitoring of the physical upkeep and sustainability of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System technology;
5. Allow project visitations as requested by DOST XI and UIC-SRC in pursuit of information dissemination and promotion of the technology.
6. Coordinate with DOST XI and UIC-SRC during the monitoring activities from October 2018 to April 2020 to address possible problems and evaluate the effectiveness of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System technology.
BLMC shall:
1. Work in coordination with UIC-SRC, DOST XI, and City of Davao in executing project plans.
2. Participate and allow DOST XI, UIC-SRC, and City of Davao in the monitoring of the physical upkeep and sustainability of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System technology;
3. Allow project visitations as requested by DOST XI, UIC-SRC and City of Davao in pursuit of information dissemination and promotion of the technology.
4. Coordinate with DOST XI, UIC-SRC, and City of Davao during the monitoring activities from October 2018 to April 2020 to address possible problems and evaluate the effectiveness of the Vertical Helophyte Filter System technology.
II.2 Duration of the Project
The project shall have a duration of twenty-one (21) months, with the prototype construction and technical report submission from August 2018 to December 2018, and the monitoring and evaluation from October 2018 to April 2020.
II.3 Termination of the Project and Project Properties
The termination of the project, and the ownership and responsibility over the properties, such as supplies, materials, equipment, and prototype and/or model acquired and/or developed using the project funds, shall be subject to the pertinent provisions of the Revised Guidelines for GIA Funds, as amended, copies of which are attached hereto as Annex I, and made integral part hereof, which is binding upon the parties hereto and must be complied with by the parties herein.
