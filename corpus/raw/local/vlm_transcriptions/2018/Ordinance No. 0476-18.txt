Ord. No. 0476-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF MR. ARTURO M. MILAN, PRESIDENT, DAVAO CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY, INC. (DCCCI), FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE COVERAGE OF SECTIONS 37, 41 AND 42 OF ORDINANCE NO. 092, SERIES OF 2000 (SIGNAGE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY), IN RELATION TO THE 50TH YEAR ANNIVERSARY OF DAVAO CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY TO BE HELD ON JULY 15-29, 2018, AT SMX CONVENTION CENTER, SM LANANG PREMIER, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption solely pertains to the (1) payment of applicable fees under Chapter 10, Section 37; and, (2) exemption from the coverage of restricted areas for the installation and display of any advertising and/or propaganda materials under Chapter 10, Sections 41 and 42 of Ordinance No. 092, Series of 2000 (Signage Ordinance of Davao City).

SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION - The exemption shall be from the passage of this Ordinance up to August 3, 2018 only and that in no case shall the organizers and exhibitors be exempt from compliance with the other provisions of the 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao and other applicable laws and ordinances.

SECTION 4. CONDITION
a. All limitations and conditions as set forth in the Signage Ordinance of the City and those additional conditions imposed by the City Engineer's Office (CEO), the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and Davao Light and Power Company (DLPC);
b. The City Government of Davao shall be recognized as an event partner in all programs, collaterals and banners.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, June 25, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
