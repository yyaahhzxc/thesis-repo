Ord. No. 0498-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, A DEED OF DONATION WHICH TO BE EXECUTED BY DATU PANILIKSEN LATIP P. AYURANDAHU AMUPAC JR., ARCH. JOSE ALVARADO, LIBRADA P. RUTO, RUBEN TUNGAPALAN, JULIUS P. BALAGAS, AND MARIA CONCEPCION RAFAK IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO A NUMBER OF ITEMS/ARTIFACTS TO BE DONATED TO MUSEO DABAWENYO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 435, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7150, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all contracts, agreements, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor hereby granted legislative authority to accept, fix, and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by Datu Paniliksen Latip P. Ayurandahu Amupac Jr., Arch. Jose Alvarado, Librada P. Ruto, Ruben Tungapalan, Julius P. Balagtas, and Maria Concepcion Raflak in favor of the City Government of Davao, relative to a number of items/artifacts to be donated to Museo Dabawenyo.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, July 17, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT,

CHRYSTO R. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head)
