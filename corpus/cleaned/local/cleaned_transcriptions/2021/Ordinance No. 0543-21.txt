Ord. No. 0543-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR AND ENDORSEMENT OF
THE CONTRACT OF USUFRUCT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF
DAVAO AND THE BARANGAY COUNCIL OF SASA, BUHANGIN DISTRICT, THIS CITY,
FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SASA HEALTH CENTER."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b)
sub-paragraph (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government
Code of 1991 provides:

"SECTION 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation

xxx
xxx
xxx

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of
which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to
Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

xxx
xxx
xxx

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its
behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents
upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or
ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to enter into and sign for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Contract of
Usufruct to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and the Barangay Council
of Sasa, Buhangin District, this city, for the establishment of the Sasa Health Center.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, April 13, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

**CONTRACT OF USUFRUCT**

Know All Men by these Presents:

This contract of usufruct is made and entered into by between:

BARANGAY COUNCIL OF SASA, a local government unit duly organized under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at Sasa, Davao City, represented by its Punong Barangay, Jerry L. Licarte, hereinafter referred to as the OWNER.

And

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a government entity of Davao City duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines in representation for and behalf of Sasa Health Center with office address at Km. 9, Sasa, Davao City represented herein by the City Mayor, Hon. Sara Z. Dulerte hereinafter referred to as USUFRUCTUARY.

Witnesseth:

Whereas, under Section 16 and 17 of Republic Act No. 7160, the Owner, is an LGU, is empowered to exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare, and to provide basic services and facilities to its inhabitants.

Whereas, the Owner is the lawful owner of a certain parcel of land located at Paradise Island Drive, Km. 9, Sasa, Davao City containing an area of Three Thousand (3,000) square meter more or less lot of land covered by FLC-11-01-000118-D located at Paradise Island Drive, Km. 9, Sasa, Davao City.

Whereas, the USUFRUCTUARY desires to use FOUR HUNDRED EIGHTY-ONE (481) square meters portion of the above mentioned parcel of Land located at Paradise Island Drive, Km. 9, Sasa, Davao City to be used as Sasa Health Center of the USUFRUCTUARY.

Whereas, the continued presence of the USUFRUCTUARY in the aforesaid property is to be beneficial to the owner, and its constituents and the Filipino people in general.

Now therefore, for and in consideration of the forging, and for the general welfare and convenience of the OWNER's constituents, the owner hereby agrees, cedes, conveys, by way of Usufruct, FOUR HUNDRED EIGHTY-ONE (481) SQUARE METERS portion of the above-mentioned parcel of land, subject to the following terms and conditions:

--- [Page 5] ---
LAND AREA: The USUFRUCTUARY shall be allowed exclusive and uninterrupted use of FOUR HUNDRED EIGHTY-ONE (481) SQUARE METERS portion of the above-designated parcel of land.

PURPOSE: The property shall be used solely and exclusively for health care programs.

TERM: This contract of usufruct shall be effective for a period of TWENTY-FIVE (25) years from the time of the signing of this document renewable upon the consent of both parties. However, in the event that USUFRUCTUARY ceases to use or operate for which it intended, this usufruct is automatically terminated and all the improvements thereon shall be forfeited in favor of the OWNER.

MAINTENANCE: The USUFRUCTUARY shall maintain the land in accordance with the existing rules and regulation and preserve the same in habitable condition during the existence of Usufruct.

The USUFRUCTUARY is authorized to construct new buildings, make repairs, including renovations, provided that the same shall conform to the standards prescribed by law.

LIABILITY: The USUFRUCTUARY shall be solely liable for damages or criminal liability arising from its negligence while in possession of the lot stated above subject of this usufruct.

MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

1. During the existence of this Usufruct, the USUFRUCTUARY is absolutely prohibited from alienating its rights on the usufruct, nor leases the same to any third person.

2. All taxes for the said property during the duration of this USUFRUCTUARY shall be borne by the USUFRUCTUARY.

3. That any violation of the terms and conditions of this usufruct is a valid ground for the termination of this contract without any Court Order.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands this 2021 at Davao City, Philippines.
