Ord. No. 0485-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUCT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND GREAT EARTH MARKETING AND DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION OVER A PROPERTY SITUATED AT NCCC VICTORIA PLAZA, NO. 37, LAUREL AVENUE, THIS CITY, ENCRUSTED IN TRANSFER CERTIFICATE ON TITLE NO. 146-20180111240, FOR THE OPERATION OF A FIRE STATION AND TOURIST POLICE STATION".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 22 (a) (5) and (c) and Section 455 (b) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the "Local Government Code of 1991" provides that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.

(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:

(5) To enter into contracts; and

(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, the Deed of Usufruct to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Great Earth Marketing and Development Corporation, over a property situated at NCCC Victoria Plaza, J.P. Laurel Avenue, this City, encrusted in Transfer Certificate of Title No. 146-20180111240, for the operation of a Fire Station and Tourist Police Station.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

DEED OF USUFRUCT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Deed of Usufruct is executed this by and between:

GREAT EARTH MARKETING AND DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION, a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office located at NHQ Building, Gempesaw Extension, Davao City, Philippines, represented in this Deed by its President, SARELUNE FAYE A. LIM, by virtue of a Secretary's Certificate herein attached as Annex "A", hereinafter referred to as the OWNER.

and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a body politic, organized and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office situated at City Hall Bldg., City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented herein by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, herein after referred to as the USUFRUCTUARY.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the OWNER is the true and registered owner of a parcel of land known as NCCC Victoria Plaza situated at J.P. Laurel Ave., Davao City, Philippines, covered under Transfer Certificate of Title No. 246-20180111240 (herein attached as Annex "B") issued by the Registry of Deeds for Davao City, containing an area of Thirty Thousand Nine Hundred Twenty-One (30,921) square meters, more or less by virtue of a Deed of Absolute Sale dated 12 March 2019 hereto attached as Annex "C";

WHEREAS, the OWNER desires to give the right of Usufruct to the USUFRUCTUARY over a portion of the said parcel of land (hereinafter referred to as the "PROPERTY") with an area of Seven Hundred Fourteen and 47/100 (704.47) Square Meters, more or less with the latter having the full right to use and enjoy the PROPERTY, the technical description of which is hereto attached as Annex "D";

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual agreements, covenants, and stipulations hereinafter set forth, the OWNER hereby transfers and conveys unto the USUFRUCTUARY by way of usufruct the Property, subject to the following terms and conditions:

1. This grant of Usufruct shall be valid and binding for a period of Fifteen (15) years from the time of the signing of this agreement, subject for renewal for a period determined by the parties and subject to the condition that the USUFRUCTUARY shall actually use the Property for the purpose for which the Usufruct is constituted, which is the operation of a Fire Station and Tourist Police Station;

2. The USUFRUCTUARY shall pay and be liable for all taxes, licenses, permits, duties, levies and such other charges as the Government or any of its instrumentalities and agencies may impose by reason of and arising out of this agreement and its implementation, including the real property taxes;

3. The USUFRUCTUARY may build and construct any building or improvement on the Property upon approval by the OWNER of the building plan, which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld. This is to ensure that the building of the USUFRUCTUARY is consistent with the overall concept and design of the Mail. The USUFRUCTUARY hereby agrees and binds

--- [Page 5] ---
Itself to insure at its expense the building, furnishing, and equipment built or placed thereon against loss by fire, typhoon, flood or other similar natural events. In no case shall the USUFRUCTUARY be liable for any loss, damage or injury that may arise by reason of its use and enjoyment of the Property.
4. The USUFRUCTUARY shall defray all expenses for building and maintenance including the cost of electricity, telephone, and other utilities attached, connected or related to the operation of or improvement introduced into the Property;
5. The USUFRUCTUARY shall, at its own expense, make all necessary repairs to keep the Property and all its improvements, equipment, and facilities, from deteriorating in value or condition during the term of the grant;
6. The USUFRUCTUARY shall not lease or alienate its right of Usufruct over any part or portion of the premises or the whole thereof, without the express written consent of the Owner;
7. The Owner hereby covenants with the USUFRUCTUARY that the latter shall peaceably hold and enjoy the full use and possession of the Property during the period of the grant. However, the Owner reserves the right to recover possession of the Property prior to the expiration of the period of the grant for purposes of redevelopment, provided that the Owner shall identify another suitable location within NCCC Victoria Plaza as replacement for the Property and shall inform USUFRUCTUARY of such decision at least Sixty (60) days prior to the transfer, provided, further that the cost of relocation shall be for the exclusive account of the Owner;
8. The USUFRUCTUARY is exempt from making an inventory of all the property which contains an appraisal of the movables and a description of the condition of the immovables and giving a security binding itself to fulfill the obligations imposed upon it;
9. The Owner may immediately recover possession of the Property, including all permanent structures and improvements introduced thereon, without obligation to reimburse the herein USUFRUCTUARY, upon expiration of the period of the Grant or upon termination of the Usufruct by the Owner should the USUFRUCTUARY violate any of the provisions of this Deed or fail to comply with its obligations as Usufructuary as provided in the New Civil Code of the Philippines, as amended, the applicable provisions of which are incorporated herein and deemed part of the provisions of this Deed.
IN WITNESS THEREOF, the parties, through their representatives, have hereunto set their hands this at Davao City, Philippines.
GREAT EARTH MARKETING AND DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION Local Government of the City of Davao Usufructuary
