AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT UNDER THE "SUMO IMPROVEREN" AND SUBLETTENT ("SIRI PROGRAM") LAND; PIAPI UPGRADING AREA; AND NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASES 1 AND 2, PARTICULARLY IDENTIFIED AS BLOCK 43, LOT 2-A, NEW MATINA UPGRADING AREA, DAVAO CITY, CONSISTING OF THREE HUNDRED TWENTY-THREE (323) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF JULIANO J. GUMPAD

Ord. No. 0714-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO ASSIGNMENT FOR THE PURPOSE OF EXECUTING THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROGRAM NAMELY: PIAPI UPGRAADING AREA AND PIAPI COMMERCIAL AREA; AND NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASES 1 AND 2, PARTICULARLY IDENTIFIED AS BLOCK 43, LOT 2-A, NEW MATINA UPGRAADING AREA, DAVAO CITY, CONSISTING OF THREE HUNDRED TWENTY-THREE (323) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF JULIANO J. GUMPAD".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, with the City of Davao City, the Deed of Sale for the lot in the Slum Improvement and Rezettlement (SIR) Program namely: Piapi Upgrading Area and Piapi Commercial Area and New Matina Sites and Services Phase 1 and 2, particularly identified as Block 43, Lot 2-A, New Matina Upgrading Area, Davao City, consisting of Three Hundred Twenty-Three (323) square meters, more or less, in favor of Juliano J. Gumpad.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 14th day of September 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
