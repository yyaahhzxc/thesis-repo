Ord. No. 0529-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR, THROUGH THE CITY LEGAL OFFICE, TO PETITION THE COURT TO CHANGE THE USE OF THE LOT IN CATIGAN EMBRACED IN TRANSFER CERTIFICATE OF TITLE NO. 29744, FROM SCHOOL PURPOSES TO MIXED-USE".

SECTION 2. SCOPE: The City Mayor of Davao, through the City Legal Office, is hereby granted authority to petition the court to change the use of the property covered by TCT No. T-29744 from School Purposes to Mixed-Use.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, April 6, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
