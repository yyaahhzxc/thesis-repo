Ord. No. 0588-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO AND BETWEEN DAVAO CITY WATER DISTRICT (DCWD) AND THE CITY OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE SEPTAGE ASPECT OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0363-10, SERIES OF 2010, OR THE SEPTAGE AND SEWERAGE MANAGEMENT ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:

"SEC. 16. General Welfare - Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the use of appropriate, advanced, and scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between Davao City Water District (DCWD) and the City of Davao, relative to the septage aspect of City Ordinance No. 0363-10, Series of 2010, or the Septage and Sewerage Management Ordinance of Davao City", in accordance with existing laws, rules and regulations of the Republic of the Philippines.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITy CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provisions of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

ENACTED, May 11, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) for Septage Management Program is made between:

The CITY OF DAVAO, a government instrumentality duly organized in accordance with the Local Government Code, with principal office address at City Hall Drive, Davao City, and represented in this act by the City Mayor, MARIANNE QUINTANILLO, hereinafter referred to as "CITY";

and

The DAVAO CITY WATER DISTRICT, a public water utility created under Presidential Decree No. 198, as amended, with principal office address at Km. 2.5 Mac Arthur Highway, Matina, Davao City, Philippines, duly represented by the Chairperson of the Board of Directors, EDUARDO A. BANGAYAN, hereinafter referred to as the "DCWD";

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the CITY is committed to protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them. It is an environmental concern of the City to have a sustainable management of waterways to meet and safeguard the recreational and life supporting capacity of water. It is committed to improving the water quality of the water resource within its territory and acknowledges that large amounts of contaminants or discharges come from untreated domestic sewage discharges and trade wastes, or industrial liquid wastes, thereby causing significant health risks to people near the river, and those in residential areas affected by floods;

WHEREAS, under Section 20 of RA 9275 or otherwise known as the Philippines Clean Water Act imposes upon local government units to share the responsibility in the management and improvement of water utility within their territorial jurisdictions;

WHEREAS, under Section 17, RA 7160 mandates local government units (LGUs) to effectively provide basic services and facilities to their constituents, including drainage and sewerage;

--- [Page 5] ---
WHEREAS, as provided for in Section 2, Article 1 of Ordinance No. 0363-10, Series of 2013 otherwise known as the Septage and Sewerage Management Ordinance, it is a declared policy of Davao City to adopt and implement a comprehensive and integrated septic and sewerage management program, which shall among others, reduce, if not eliminate, the prevalence of waterborne and excreta-related diseases and pollution in bodies of water and encourage greater public-private partnership;

WHEREAS, Section 5 of Title II of the PD 198 provides for the formation of water districts to operate water supply and distribution systems, wastewater collection, treatment, and disposal facilities and to conduct other functions and operations incidental to water resource development, utilization, and disposal;

WHEREAS, majority of the homes in Davao City use septic tanks as a way of providing partial treatment of septage and sludge;

WHEREAS, the sludge is being disposed without proper treatment due to the non-existence of an appropriate treatment facility that caters to this need. Thus, the waste is being discharged directly to the drainage canals, creeks, and other bodies of water that become polluted;

WHEREAS, the DCWD aims to establish a proper Septage Management Program which will include the construction of Septage Treatment Plant (SPTP);

WHEREAS, a Feasibility Study for the proposed Septage Management Program was conducted by DCWD;

WHEREAS, the Feasibility Study through the establishment of a Septage Management Program aims to reduce the pollution attributed to untreated septage discharge to protect our water resource, to improve water quality, sanitation and public health conditions, and provide efficient and affordable septage desludging service initially to existing DCWD customers and to all targeted areas of DCWD;

WHEREAS, the CITY enacted Ordinance No. 0363-10 and Implementing Rules and Regulations prescribing the procedures in the establishment of Septage and Sewerage Management Program; and

WHEREAS, the CITY has manifested its intent to support in the establishment of SPTP.

NOW THEREFORE, the parties have agreed as follows:

--- [Page 6] ---
I. PURPOSE

The Parties seek to enter into MOA for the establishment, operation, maintenance and management of SpTP specifically mentioned in Section 22, Rule IX, IRR of City Ordinance No. 0363-10.

II. OUTPUT

To ensure commitment for the establishment, operation, maintenance and management of SpTP.

III. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

A. The CITY shall:

1. Grant DCWD the authority to construct SpTP;

2. Grant DCWD the authority to operate and maintain the SpTP in accordance with DOH standards;

3. Support and assist DCWD in the implementation of Septage Management Program;

4. Grant DCWD the authority to desludge all residential and commercial establishment within his territorial jurisdiction;

5. Ensure administration and enforcement of the Septage and Sewerage Ordinance and its Implementing Rules and Regulations;

6. Conduct a survey of all properties and premises in the CITY in coordination with the barangay officials to determine if a septic tank is present, and if it is accessible for desludging;

7. Serve notices of non-conformance to the owners/administrators, or occupants in case of non-conformance with the provisions of the IRR;

8. Issue certificate of compliance to the property owners who are deemed to have met the minimum specifications for septic tanks as stated in the IRR;

--- [Page 7] ---
9. Keep a record of all owners/administrators of building and structures who have undug their septic tanks, those that are inaccessible, those that do not have septic tanks, and those that do not have water-sealed toilets, and other data that may deemed necessary for the purpose of this MOA;

10. Conduct a periodic survey of properties every five (5) years to verify changes in septic tank accessibility or changes in tank capacity requirements. This shall be done in coordination with the barangay officials;

11. Assist DCWD in facilitating and acquiring permits, clearances and documents required and related to the construction and establishment of the SpTP;

12. Make the necessary adjustment/update in the zonal classification of the area where the SpTP is to be constructed, when necessary, upon proper public consultation and favorable recommendation and approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod; and

13. The issuance of such authority above-enumerated is non-exclusive to DCWD and the City may enter into agreement with other entities for the implementation of a Septage and Sewerage Management Program in the City.

B. The DCWD shall:

1. Construct a SpTP.
2. Fully bear the entire costs in the construction of the SpTP.
3. Shoulder the operations and maintenance cost of the SpTP.
4. Impose the Septage Management Fee (SMF) and its mode of payment being subject further to the conditions as follows:

a. For DCWD customers, SMF shall be added up to their monthly water bill which is computed as a uniform price per cubic meter of water consumed;
b. The total monthly basic rate for the first 10 cubic meters of water consumption with the added SMF shall not exceed 5% of the average monthly income of the low-income-group families in DCWD's franchise area; and

--- [Page 8] ---
c. For non-DCWD customers, the septage collection and treatment shall be handled by DCWD and shall be done on a per job order basis. Households shall pay SMF to DCWD on a "pay-per-service" scheme.

5. Impose the Treatment Fee as payment of the accredited private desludging service providers who would avail the septage treatment services of DCWD, based on a price per cubic meter of septage for treatment, a "pay-per-service" scheme.

6. Pay the Desludging Fee to the City as per Section 40, Rule XIII, IRR of the City Ordinance No. 0363-10, or as may be adjusted by the City.

7. Determine the SMF for DCWD customers, and the Treatment Fee for private desludging service providers subject to the review and approval of the Local Water Utilities Administration.

8. Reserve the right to adjust the SMF rate and Treatment Fee for reasons such as:

a. Increase in the capacity of the Septage Treatment Plant or the implementation of the subsequent phases of the plant.

b. Any other circumstances that may warrant improvement and expansion of the SpTP and such other services.

C. BOTH PARTIES shall:

1. Support the operation and management of the Septage Management Program;

2. Extend mutual cooperation to each other to ensure smooth implementation of the Septage Management Program;

3. Exchange information and / or views with each other with respect to any matters necessary for successful completion of environmental impact study of the project;

4. Consult each other at any time on any matters of common interest to all Parties;

--- [Page 9] ---
5. Deliberate on any matter in an appropriate period in order to facilitate the coordination among concerned agencies and accomplish the objectives of this MOA; and

6. Institute and spearhead the advocacy and promotional activities for the dissemination of the information, education and communication campaign for the Septage Management Program.

IV. BINDING AND TERMINATION

The Parties are entering into this MOA while wishing to maintain their own separate and unique missions and mandates, and their own accountability. Unless specifically provided otherwise, the cooperation between the Parties as outlined in this MOA shall be binding upon them, their successors or assigns, and/or any other entity or person who is authorized by law to act on their behalf. Nothing in this MOA shall be construed as superseding or interfering in any way with other agreements or contracts entered into between the Parties, either prior to or subsequent to the signing of MOA.

This MOA shall be for a period of fifty (50) years commencing from the date of notarization, renewable for another twenty-five (25) years by giving written notice to renew at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the original fifty-year term, upon terms and conditions mutually agreed by the parties.

Any party may terminate this MOA only for valid and just cause. Within thirty (30) days following the termination of this MOA, DCWD shall remit all the moneys due to the CITY under this MOA.

V. MISCELLANEOUS

1. This MOA, including its Annexes, contains the entire stipulation between the parties, and neither party has relied on any prior or contemporaneous representation, either oral or written, that is not contained in this MOA.

2. This MOA cannot be deemed modified or amended unless such modification is in writing and signed by the party against whom enforcement of such modification is sought.

3. If any of the provision(s) of this MOA is held to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the validity, legality and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not in any way be affected or impacted.

--- [Page 10] ---
4. This MOA shall be binding upon the respective successors and assignees of the parties hereto.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands on Davao City, Philippines.
