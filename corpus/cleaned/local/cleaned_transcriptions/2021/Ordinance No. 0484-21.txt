AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0460-21, SERIES OF 2021, AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION (DOO) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF TOURISM XI AND THE CITY OF DAVAO PERTAINING TO THE DONATION OF ONE (1) MILLION DOLLARS TRANSSCRIPTION POLYMERASE CHAIN REACTION (RT-PCR) MACHINE TO REFLECT THE CORRECT NAME OF THE DONOR, FROM DEPARTMENT OF TOURISM XI TO STEELASIA MANUFACTURING CORPORATION

Ord. No. 0484-21
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0460-21, SERIES OF 2021, OR AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION (DOD) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF TOURISM-XI AND THE CITY OF DAVAO PERTAINING TO THE DONATION OF ONE (1) UNIT REVERSE TRANSCRIPTION POLYMERASE CHAIN REACTION (RT-PCR) MACHINE TO REFLECT THE CORRECT NAME OF THE DONOR, FROM DEPARTMENT OF TOURISM XI TO STEELASIA MANUFACTURING CORPORATION".
SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 1 of Ordinance No. 0460-21, Series of 2021, shall be amended, to read as follows:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION (DOD) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN STEELASIA MANUFACTURING CORPORATION AND THE CITY OF DAVAO PERTAINING TO THE DONATION OF ONE (1) UNIT REVERSE TRANSCRIPTION POLYMERASE CHAIN REACTION (RT-PCR) MACHINE."
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - Section 3 of Ordinance No. 0460-21, Series of 2021 shall be amended to read as follows:
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation (DOD) to be entered into by and between SteelAsia Manufacturing Corporation and the City of Davao pertaining to the donation of one (1) unit Reverse Transcription Polymerase Chain Reaction (RT-PCR) Machine.
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
ENACTED, February 02, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head II).
