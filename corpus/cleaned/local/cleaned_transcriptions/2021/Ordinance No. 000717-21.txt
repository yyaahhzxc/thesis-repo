AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT UNDER THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROGRAM NAMELY: PIAPI UPGRADING AREA AND PIAPI COMMERCIAL AREA AND NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASES I AND II, DAVAO CITY, LOT 10, DAVAO CITY, LOT 117, LOT 13, NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASE II, DAVAO CITY, CONSISTING OF ONE HUNDRED TWENTY (120) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF JULIO R. ARIOLA

Ord. No. 0717-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT UNDER THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROGRAM NAMELY: PIAPI UPGRAADING AREA AND PIAPI COMMERCIAL AREA AND NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASES 1 AND 2, PARTICULARLY IDENTIFIED AS BLOCCH 117 LOT 13, NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASE II, DAVAO CITY, CONSISTING OF ONE HUNDRED TWENTY (120) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF JULIO R. ARIOLA".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Sale for the lot in the Slum Improvement and Resettlement (SIR) Program namely: Piapi Upgrading Area and Piapi Commercial Area and New Matina Sites and Services Phases 1 and 2, particularly identified as Block 117 Lot 13, New Matina Sites and Services Phase II, Davao City, consisting of One Hundred Twenty (120) square meters, more or less, in favor of Julio R. Ariola.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 14th day of September 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
