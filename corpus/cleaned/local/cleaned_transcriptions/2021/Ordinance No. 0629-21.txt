Ord. No. 0629-21
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE RENAMING MATINA APLAYA ROAD TO CONG. I. VELOSO ROAD".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 13 (b) (2) (d) and (h) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, states that:
"Section 13. Naming of Local Government Units and Public Places, Streets and Structures.
(b) The sanggunians of highly urbanized cities and of component cities whose charters prohibit their voters from voting for provincial elective officials, hereinafter referred to in this Code as independent component cities, may, in consultation with the Philippine Historical Commission, change the name of the following within its territorial jurisdiction:
(2) City roads, avenues, boulevards, thoroughfares, and bridges;
(d) None of the foregoing local government units, institutions, places, or buildings shall be named after a living person, nor may a change of name be made unless for a justifiable reason and, in any case, not other than once every ten (10) years. The name of a street, avenue, boulevard, thoroughfare, or bridge which has historical, cultural, or ethnic significance shall not be changed, unless by a unanimous vote of the sanggunian concerned and in consultation with the PHC.
(h) In any change of name, the Office of the President, the representative of the legislative district concerned, and the Bureau of Posts shall be notified."
SECTION 3. ROAD TO BE RENAMED - "Matina Aplaya Road", located in Barangay Matina Aplaya, Talomo District, this City, is hereby renamed as "Cong. I. Veloso Road".
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions heretofore not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
ENACTED, June 29, 2021, by a unanimous vote of the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
