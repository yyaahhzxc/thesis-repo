Ord. No. 0527-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER, INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN REHAB OF THE CITY OF DAVAO POLICE OFFICE, THE AGREEMENTS ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DAVAO CITY POLICE OFFICE RELATIVE TO THE TRANSFER OF EXISTING RESOURCES UNDER THE K9 UNIT OF THE CITY OF DAVAO IN FAVOR OF THE DAVAO CITY POLICE OFFICE."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 18 and 381 of RA 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states, to wit:

SEC. 18. Power to Generate and Apply Resources. - Local government units shall have the power and authority to xxx acquire, develop, lease, encumber, alienate, or otherwise dispose of real or personal property held by them in their proprietary capacity and to apply their resources and assets for productive, developmental, or welfare purposes, in the exercise or furtherance of their governmental or proprietary powers and functions and thereby ensure their development into self-reliant communities and active participants in the attainment of national goals.

SEC. 381. Transfer Without Cost. - Property which has become unserviceable or is no longer needed may be transferred without cost to another office, agency, subdivision or instrumentality of the national government or another local government unit or appraised valuation may be made of such property upon its own cost. Such transfer shall be subject to the approval of the sanggunian concerned making the transfer and by the head of the office, agency, subdivision, instrumentality or local government unit receiving the property.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and on behalf of the Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and the Davao City Police Office relative to the transfer of existing resources under the K9 Unit of the City of Davao in favor of the Davao City Police Officers.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, April 6, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement is made and entered into by and between:

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, with principal office at City Hall Building, San Pedro St., Davao City, herein represented by its Local Chief Executive, SARA Z. DUTERTE, herein referred to as the "FIRST PARTY";

And

The DAVAO CITY POLICE OFFICE, with principal office at Camp Domingo Leonor, San Pedro St., Davao City, represented herein by its City Director, COL. KIRBY JOHN B. KRAFT, herein referred to as the "SECOND PARTY";

WHEREAS, terrorism remains a threat to the nation's security and welfare of the people;

WHEREAS, the Parties have implemented anti-terrorism measures and mobilized necessary resources to undertake critical and appropriate responses to counter acts of terrorism and other similar incidents;

WHEREAS, the program "Davao City Central 911" particularly the K9 Unit is created mainly to develop facilities for the confinement, breeding, and training of K9 Service dogs and/or Explosive Detector Dogs (EDDs) to provide safety and security services to the city;

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY continuously provides support to the SECOND PARTY to augment its existing institutional capability to aid law enforcement;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the PARTIES hereby agree as follows:

ARTICLE I

RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES

1. The FIRST PARTY shall

1.1 transfer its existing resources under the K9 Unit to the SECOND PARTY. The list of the resources is attached in this agreement as Annex "1";

1.2 Be responsible through the City Veterinarian's Office for all the needed dog maintenance such as dog food, health check-ups, and medicines; and

1.3 Detail its personnel who are the handlers of the dogs to the SECOND PARTY. The list of the personnel are hereto attached as Annex "2". Lastly, should the needed repair and maintenance of the vehicles and the attached equipment as mentioned in Annex "1"

--- [Page 11] ---
2. The SECOND PARTY shall:
2.1. Create a "DCPO K9 Auxiliary Team" and utilize the resources mentioned in Annex "I". The "DCPO K9 Auxiliary Team" shall provide K9 services for the residents of Davao City;
2.2. The items listed in Annex "I" shall be used for the same purpose for which they were purchased which is to respond to all bomb-related threats, calls, and incidents;
2.3. Consider the functions of the "DCPO K9 Auxiliary Team" as official police work; and
2.4. Shall execute a Receipt of Property upon the turnover of the items.
The PARTIES hereto agree that the Emergency Calls Answering Point (ECAPD) Unit under the CENTRAL 911 Program shall continue to be under the control of the FIRST PARTY and shall continue to cater calls directed to the 911 hotline. However, calls which require K9 services shall be relayed to the "DCPO K9 Auxiliary Team" for their response.
