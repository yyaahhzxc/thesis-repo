Ord. No. 0785-21
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE GIVING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND
SIGN, FOR AND IN THE NAME OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT IN THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROJECT,
NEW MATINA URBANIZING AREA, PANLUNGSOD VILLAGE, BLOCK 34, LOT 5, PHASE 1, NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES, DAVAO CITY,
CONSISTING OF SEVENTY-FIVE (75) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN
FAVOR OF ROSA CARIDAD R. ROSALES".
SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into
and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Sale for the lot in the Slum Improvement and Resettlement (SIR) Project, New Matina Sites and Services, particularly described as Block 34, Lot 5, Phase 1, New Matina Sites and Services, this City, in favor of Rosa Caridad R. Rosales.
SECTION 3. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, November 23, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHIQUIN J. SANTOS

(City Government Department Head II)
