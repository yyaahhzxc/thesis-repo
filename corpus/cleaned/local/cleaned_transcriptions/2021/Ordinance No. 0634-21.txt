AN ORDINANCE GOVERNING ABANDONED AND
DERELICT MOTOR VEHICLES WITHIN THE
TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY AND
PROVIDING PENALTIES THEREOF

Ord. No. 0634-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as the "ABANDONED AND DERELICT MOTOR VEHICLES ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY -

(a) Article II, Section 5 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that the maintenance of peace and order, the protection of life, liberty, and property, and the promotion of the general welfare are essential for the enjoyment by all the people of the blessings of democracy;

(b) It is hereby declared the policy of the city to eradicate abandoned and derelict vehicles parked unattended in public and subdivision roads or in litter public roads and pathways in order for emergency vehicles to respond freely, lessen traffic congestion, curb the number of vehicles blocking down city streets and for the pedestrians to walk freely and safe;

(c) Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines state in part that every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare;

SECTION 3. SCOPE OF APPLICATION - This Ordinance shall guarantee that public roads and private roads will be free from all abandoned and derelict vehicles obstructing the highways to give way to free and unhampered rescue of emergency vehicles during emergency situation.

SECTION 4. PURPOSE - The germane purpose of promulgating this legislative measure is to eradicate abandoned and derelict vehicles parked on a public street, road, highway or public and private properties to unclutter roads and pathways in order for emergency vehicles to respond freely, lessen traffic congestion, curb the number of vehicles blocking city streets and for pedestrians to walk freely and safe.

SECTION 5. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following terms are defined and referred as:

1. Abandoned Vehicles - refer to a motor vehicle or trailer and its contents:

a) Any unattended, wrecked, burned or partially dismantled vehicle creating a traffic hazard because of its position in relation to the street, alley or public way or its physical location causing the impeding of traffic flow;

b) Parked along the street, alley or public way or upon property owned by the city or any other governmental entity and the vehicle displays no license plate or other sign of registration;
c) Parked along the street, alley or public way or upon property owned by the city or any other governmental entity in the same place for a period of 48 hours; in a commercial or industrial area for a period of seven days;
d) Parked along real property other than the property of the owner of the vehicle without the consent of the real property owner;

2. Derelict Motor Vehicles - refer to such act:
a) Refer to any motor vehicle which has been left unattended on a private property for not less than two (2) days or in public property for not less than three (3) days without the owner or driver making any attempt to recover such vehicle or to leave a conspicuously placed note that such owner or driver intends to return for such vehicle for recovery of possession;
b) Includes any motor vehicle which, if a conspicuous note was left has been unattended for less than five (5) days and if because of damage, vandalism, theft, or fire the vehicle is damaged to the extent that its restoration to an operable condition would require the replacement of one or more major component parts or involves any structural damage that would affect the vehicle;

3. Motor Vehicle - refers to any vehicle propelled by any power other than muscular power using the public highways;

4. Driver - any person with valid driver's license driving or controlling a motor vehicle;

5. City Traffic Citation Ticket - refers to a complaint or notice upon which the city traffic enforcement officer shall record an occurrence involving one or more vehicle traffic violations by the driver or person cited;

6. Highways - shall mean every public thoroughfare, public boulevard, driveway, avenue, park, alley and Callejon but shall not include roadway upon grounds owned by private persons, colleges, universities, or other similar institutions;

7. Unattended Vehicle Check - refers to actions as are reasonably necessary to determine that the unattended vehicle does not contain an injured or incapacitated person and to determine that the unattended vehicle does not pose a threat to public health or safety;

SECTION 6. REMOVAL OF AN ABANDONED AND DERELICT MOTOR VEHICLE BY A TRAFFIC ENFORCER - In coordination with the Barangay Council and the PNP-Highway Patrol Group (HRS), the CTTMO will conduct an inventory of all abandoned and derelict motor vehicles which has been left unattended on a public street, road, highway or other public and private properties.

The Barangay Council upon recognition of the enlisted abandoned and derelict vehicle shall notify the owners to remove them from obstructing the public street, road, highway or other public and private properties.

For a period of at least seven (7) days after the on-site conducted inventory and notifications, the CTTMO shall cause such motor vehicle to be removed and transferred to an impoundment garage or other place of safety if it reasonably believes that the person who abandoned the motor vehicle does not intend to return and remove such motor vehicle.

At any given circumstances, when the motor vehicle which has been left abandoned on a public street, road, highway or other public and private property poses a threat to the public health or safety or to mitigate congestion, the CTTMO shall be authorized to cause such motor vehicle to be removed immediately for transfer to a garage or other place of safety.

SECTION 7. DUTY OF THE TRAFFIC ENFORCER TO PERFORM AN UNATTENDED VEHICLE CHECK - It shall be the duty of any traffic officer who discovers a motor vehicle which has been left abandoned on a public street, road, highway or public property to immediately perform an unattended vehicle check on such motor vehicle, unless it is displayed on such motor vehicle an unattended vehicle check card indicating that another enforcer has already performed such checking.

SECTION 8. DUTY OF THE TRAFFIC ENFORCER IF ABANDONED VEHICLE IS FOUND TO BE A STOLEN MOTOR VEHICLE - If any abandoned motor vehicle being removed is determined to be a stolen motor vehicle, the CTTMO shall immediately notify the storage facility of such determination, and shall further notify the PNP Highway Patrol Group of the location of such motor vehicle within three (3) days after receiving notice that such motor vehicle is a stolen vehicle.

SECTION 9. PROCEDURE FOR DERELICT MOTOR VEHICLES - Derelict Motor Vehicles shall be dealt accordingly. If a motor vehicle has been left unattended on a private property for not less than two (2) days or on public property for not less than three (3) days without the owner or driver making any attempt to recover such vehicle or to leave a conspicuous placed note that such owner or driver intends to return such vehicle; or if a conspicuous note was left if the motor vehicle has been left unattended for more than five (5) days and if because of damage, vandalism, theft or fire the vehicle is damaged to the extent that its restoration to an operable condition would require the replacement of one or more major component parts or involves any structural damage that would affect the safety of the vehicle; or if here is evidence that the vehicle is inoperable due to major mechanical breakdown at the time it was left on the property, or if the vehicle is severed.

**SECTION 10. DISPOSAL OF ABANDONED AND DERELICT MOTOR VEHICLES**

a) The City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO) in coordination with the PNP-Highway Patrol Group, LTO, LTFRB, Barangay Council where the vehicle is found shall recommend to the City Mayor the disposal of a vehicle that has been taken into custody provided court approval is secured for vehicle involved in litigation;

b) Such disposition shall be at public auction under such procedure, as the City Mayor shall establish with the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod. A written notice of such auction shall be sent to the last known address, at least ten (10) days prior to the date of auction, and the said auction shall be held not earlier than sixty (60) days after the date upon which such vehicle shall have been taken into custody. Any transactions pertaining to such auction upon payment at all costs and expenses relating to the towing and storage of such vehicle shall be determined by the CTTMO through the Traffic Enforcement and Street Management Office;

c) The funds derived from any such auction shall be used to defray the expenses of such auction and other expenses incurred in taking into custody, and the balance if any, if there will be no claimant, shall form part of the General Fund of the City;

d) In the event that no bid is received, the City Mayor shall offer such vehicle to any interested person under such terms favorable to the City government with the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, and if no person is interested, the City Mayor shall dispose of such vehicle at the City dump site;

**SECTION 11. TECHNICAL WORKING GROUP (TWG)**

The following agencies shall form part of the Technical Working Group who will work out the details governing abandoned and derelict motor vehicle, to wit:

- City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO);
- Land Transportation Office (LTO R-XI);
- Philippine National Police - HPG R-XI;
- Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP);
- Land Transportation Franchising Regulatory Board (LTFRB R-XI);
- Liga ng mga Barangay;
- Davao City Public Safety and Security Command Center;
- 911 Office;

The aforementioned offices shall form part in the crafting of the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).

**SECTION 12. PROHIBITED ACTS AND PENALTIES**

a) Any person who abandons a derelict motor vehicle on public or private property shall be fined an amount of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000) taking into consideration all attending circumstances and shall pay all costs of having such derelict motor vehicle removed and stored;

SECTION 12. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any provision of this Ordinance shall be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the validity, legality and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not in any way be affected or impaired thereby.
SECTION 13. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of local circulation.
