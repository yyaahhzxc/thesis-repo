AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE AMENDMENT OF THE CALENDAR YEAR 2021 LOCAL DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN (LDRMFIP) BY REVERTING THE AMOUNT OF TWELVE MILLION PESOS (P12,000,000.00) FOR MOBIL Surgical Unit, THE SAME TO BE MADE AVAILABLE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A REFRIGERANT WITH ACCESSORIES AND AWAREHOUSE IN THE AMOUNT OF ELEVEN MILLION PESOS (P11,000,000.00) AND PURCHASE OF TRAILER UNDER THE PROCUREMENT OF EVACUATION KITS PROGRAM OF THE CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT OFFICE (CDRMRO) IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF ONE MILLION PESOS (P1,000,000.00).

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1.TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE AMENDMENT OF THE CALENDAR YEAR 2021
LOCAL DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT
PLAN (LDRRMFP) BY REVERTING THE AMOUNT OF TWELVE MILLION PESOS
(P12,000,000.00) FOR MOBILE SURGICAL UNIT, THE SAME TO BE MADE
AVAILABLE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF OXYGEN PLANT WITH ACCESSORIES
AND WAREHOUSE IN THE AMOUNT OF ELEVEN MILLION PESOS
(P11,000,000.00) AND PURCHASE TRAILER VANS FOR THE PRESENTATION
EVACUATION KITS PROGRAM OF THE CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND
MANAGEMENT OFFICE (DCCRMRO) IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF ONE MILLION
PESOS (P1,000,000.00).

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - The Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Fund Investment Plan (DCDRRMFIP), for Calendar Year 2021, is hereby amended as follows:

FROM:
MOBILE SURGICAL UNIT
TO:
1. PROCUREMENT of trailer van for CDRMRO
DISASTER EVACUATION KIT
2. construction of an oxygen concentration plant
WITH A CYLINDER REFILLING SYSTEM
TOTAL
P 12,000,000.00
P 1,000,000.00
11,000,000.00
P 12,000,000.00

SECTION 3. USE OF FUNDS - The amount herein appropriated shall be used
specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission of Audit (COA), the Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances and Presidential directives.

SECTION 4. THE COMPLETE LIST OF THE AMENDED DAVAO CITY
DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN
(DCCRMFIP) FOR CY 2021, is hereto attached as ANNEX "A."

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITy CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof, which are not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full
force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
