Ord. No. 0565-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF
THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY
OF DAVAO AND SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER (SPMC)
RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF BALAY PAHULAHAN SHELTER FOR
WATCHERS, INCLUDING ITS FURNITURE AND FIXTURES, LOCATED AT THE
SPMC EMERGENCY ROOM AREA".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 16 and 17 of Republic Act
No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:
"Section 16. General Welfare. Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily
implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those
which, without such powers, would be impracticable or impossible. Therefore, within their respective territorial jurisdictions,
local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health
and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of programs which will
enhance scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morale, enhance economic prosperity, and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

Section 17. Basic Services and Facilities.
a) Local government units shall endeavor to be self-reliant and
shall continue exercising the powers and discharging the duties
and functions currently vested upon them. They shall also
discharge the functions and responsibilities of national agencies
and offices devolved to them pursuant to this Code. Local
government units shall likewise exercise such other powers
and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are
necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective
provisions of the basic services and facilities enumerated herein.
b) Such basic services and facilities include, but are not limited to
the following:

ii) Subject to the provisions of Title Five, Book I of this Code,
health services which include the implementation of programs
and projects on primary health care, maternal and child care,
and communicable and non-communicable disease control services;
access to secondary and tertiary health services; purchase of
medicines, medical supplies, and equipment needed to carry out
the services herein enumerated."

**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY**
The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to accept and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation by and between the City of Davao and Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC) relative to the donation of Balay Pahulayan Shelter for watchers, including its furniture and fixtures, located at the SPMC Emergency Room Area.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY**
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED**
April 27, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Deed of Donation is made and entered into by and between:

The CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal address at City Hall of Davao, San Pedro St., Davao City, Philippines, herein represented by SARAZ. DUTERTE, City Mayor, hereinafter called "THE CITY OF DAVAO."

In favor of

The SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER, a government hospital under the Department of Health of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal address at J.P. Laurel Ave., Bajada, Davao City, Philippines, herein represented by RICARDO B. AUDAN, MD., PFAFP, OIC-Medical Center Chief, hereinafter called the DONEE.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the State recognizes its mandate to provide equitable and accessible quality healthcare for all Filipinos;

WHEREAS, THE CITY OF DAVAO plays a critical role in ensuring administrative support to augment public health services in the country;

WHEREAS, THE CITY OF DAVAO is the owner of a building named as Balay Pahulayan, located at the Southern Philippines Medical Center Emergency Room Area, J.P. Laurel, Bajada, Davao City (hereinafter known as "Balay Pahulayan");

WHEREAS, THE CITY OF DAVAO intends to contribute to the DONEE the Balay Pahulayan shelter to serve as temporary resting areas for patients' watchers of the Southern Philippines Medical Center Emergency Room patients;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and subject to the conditions stipulated hereunder, THE CITY OF DAVAO hereby donates to the DONEE the Balay Pahulayan, including its furniture and fixtures.

The Donee undertakes to:

1. Ensure that the Balay Pahulayan is utilized in accordance with its purpose.
2. Ensure the proper maintenance, out of its own resources, of the aforementioned Balay Pahulayan, including its furniture and fixture; and its repair

That the CITY OF DAVAO reserves the right to revoke this Deed of Donation in case any of the aforesaid conditions are not faithfully complied with by the Donee.

That in case this Deed of Donation is revoked, the Donee shall surrender the the Balay Pahulayan, including its furniture and fixtures, to the CITY OF DAVAO within fifteen (15) days from receipt of the Notice of Revocation. Ownership of the said the Balay

Pahulayan, including its furniture and fixtures, shall then vest to the CITY OF DAVAO effective upon receipt by the Donee of the Notice of Revocation.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have affixed their respective signatures this 12th day of June, 2021 in Davao City, Philippines.
