ORDINANCE NO. 0584-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING THE OPERATION OF MOTORCYCLE TAXIS IN DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 5
Ord. No. 0584-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING THE OPERATION OF MOTORCYCLE TAXIS IN DAVAO CITY."

It shall be known by its short title "The Motorcycle Taxi Ordinance of 2021"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, that necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare;

Subparagraph (a) of Section 17 thereof also provides that local government units shall likewise exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective provision of the basic services and facilities enumerated under the Local Government Code;

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS AND CONSTRUCTION OF PHRASES

a. Motorcycle Taxi shall refer to a single motorcycle, which is being used to ferry passengers from one place to another for a fee.

b. Motorcycle Taxi Operator shall refer to the owner of the motorcycle taxi.

c. Motorcycle Taxi Driver shall refer to the person who actually drives a Motorcycle Taxi.

d. Route shall refer to a designated point of beginning and point of destinations, or vice versa.

e. Certificate of Registration shall mean the Certificate of Registration issued by the Land Transportation Office (LTO).

f. Current Registration Official receipt shall refer to the current O.R. Issued by the Land Transportation Office (LTD).

All other terms and phrases shall be interpreted and construed in their literal meaning.

SECTION 4. REGULATED ACTS

a) No person shall operate and drive a Motorcycle Taxi within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City unless the following conditions are concurred:

Current LTO Registration and O.R. of the Motorcycle Taxi unit;
Current driver's license of the driver; and
Payment of annual license fee to the City Treasurer's Office in the amount of Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00), exclusive of other regulatory fees and charges, provided that an operator/driver who registers in the first six months of the year, and who complies with this Ordinance shall not be collected with a license fee for the year.
Covered with the necessary insurance that secures the driver and the passengers in case of injuries or death. There must be:

o Compulsory Third Party Liability (CTPL) Insurance;
o Passenger's Common Carrier Insurance;
o Other insurance which may be prescribed by the implementing agency as necessary under the circumstances;

• Protective Motor Helmets for riders and passengers prescribed under Section 3 of R.A. 10094 (An Act Mandating All Motorcycle Riders to Wear Standard Protective Motorcycle Helmets While Driving and Providing Penalties Therefor);
• There must be a color-coded helmet depending on the route which will be determined by the City Transport Management Office (CTTMO).

b) Notwithstanding the issuance of the operator's license, no person 9 years old or below shall be allowed to ride as passenger in any motorcycle taxi. In case of persons more than 9 years of age but below 15 years, they shall only be allowed to ride when accompanied by a parent or guardian.

c) Motorcycle taxi shall be allowed to operate in points where there are other available and adequate modes of transportation. They shall only be allowed in certain routes as may be established by the Office of the City Mayor in coordination with the deputized traffic police. They shall not ply roads which are not part of their established routes except for emergency reasons, during its refuel, change oil, shop examinations and other similar cause.

SECTION 5. IMPLEMENTING OFFICE: The City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO) shall have jurisdiction to regulate the operation of motorcycle taxis within the City. The CTTMO is empowered to promulgate the necessary rules and regulations for the operation of motorcycle taxis. Furthermore, additional conditions and guidelines may be imposed by the CTTMO from time to time and good faith consultation with the City Council. It shall also be the responsibility of the CTTMO to coordinate with the appropriate national agencies to ensure seamless and orderly operation of motorcycle taxis in the City.

The Motorcycle Taxi Franchising and Regulatory Committee (MTFRC) is hereby created under the CTTMO. It is a special body composed of five (5) members:

1. The City Mayor, Chairman;
2. CTTMO Head, Executive Director;
3. Chairman of the Committee on Transportation of the City Council; and,
4. Two representatives from the motorcycle tax drivers.

The Committee shall have the following functions and responsibilities with respect to the regulation of motorcycle taxis:

a) Provide advice and policy directions, including mediation;
b) Act on complaints and conduct hearings on matters referred to it by concerned citizens, CTTMO and CTTMB;
c) Review and approve the plans and programs in the implementation and enforcement that may hereafter be drawn or prepared for the operations of motorcycle taxis;
d) Refer and recommend issues, measures, and concerns to the CTTMB whenever necessary;
e) Coordinate with drivers' associations in order to ensure that matters of operation (i.e., which lane to pass through) shall be properly implemented and disseminated.

The drivers are encouraged to organize associations in order to have a more systematic and inclusive administration regarding motorcycle taxi operations.

**SECTION 6. PENALTIES.**

1. The administrative penalties under Section 28 of Ordinance No. 0410-10, Series of 2010 or the "Tricycle For Hire Code of Davao City" shall also apply to motorcycle taxis inssofar as it is not modified by this Ordinance;
2. Any person who operates or drives a motorcycle taxi without prior registration shall be fined Php500.00 for each offense;
3. Any person shall be penalized with a fine of Php300.00 for the first offense and an additional Php100.00 for subsequent offenses for:
   a. Allowing a minor 9 years old and below as passenger;
   b. Allowing a minor more than 9 years of age but below 15 years as passengers if not accompanied by a parent or guardian;
   c. Halling passengers outside their established routes;

These penalties shall be imposed upon the driver. In case the operators are found to have consented to the commission of the violation, the same amount of fine shall be imposed upon him. Three consecutive violations for any offense under this Ordinance for a period of two (2) months shall be sufficient ground to revoke the operator's license.

Non-payment of the fine herein imposed for every violation within a period of one (1) week from apprehension shall also be a ground for the revocation of the operator's license.

In case of such non-payment of the administrative penalties herein provided, a criminal case shall be filed and processed against the violator. The criminal penalty shall be a fine of Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (P2,500.00).

**SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE.** Any and all provisions of inconsistent herewith, which appear to be inconsistent herewith, are deemed modified or repealed.

**SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY.** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately fifteen (15) days following its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao.

ENACTED, May 11, 2021, by unanimous votes of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
