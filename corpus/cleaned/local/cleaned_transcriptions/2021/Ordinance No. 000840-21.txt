SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to enter into and sign the Renewal of the Contract of Lease between Mrs.
Della Y. Pono, lessee, and the City Government of Davao, as lessee, relative to the
one (1) year term of lease of 1,500 square meters of land in Davao City, Davao
Oriente, to be used as a Warehouse Storage of Pasko Fiesta Decadent Supplies, Materials subject to Government Budgeting, Accounting, Auditing Rules and Regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law, as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances and Presidential directives.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 14th day of December 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
