SECTION 16. General Welfare. - Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, these necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the protection of the general welfare. The local governments and their constitutional jurisdictional local units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological facilities, improve public morals, enhance economic opportunities, provide social services, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

Ord. No. 058-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING AND APPROVING THE TEN-YEAR SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLAN OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Pursuant to Section 16 of Republic Act No. 9003, otherwise known as the Ecological Solid Waste Management Program, which states:

Section 16. Local Government Solid Waste Management Plans. The province, city or municipality, through its local solid waste management boards, shall prepare its respective 10-year Solid Waste Management Plans consistent with the national solid waste management framework. Provided, that the waste management plan shall be for the re-use, recycling and composting of wastes generated in their respective jurisdictions. Provided, further, that the Solid Waste Management Plan of the LGU shall ensure the efficient management of solid waste generated within its jurisdiction: Provided, that the plan shall place primary emphasis on implementation of all feasible re-use, recycling and composting programs while identifying and listing all types of solid waste that cannot be used for solid waste which cannot be re-used, recycled, or composted. The plan shall contain all the components provided in Section 17 of this Act and a timetable for the implementation of the solid waste management program in accordance with the National Framework and pursuant to the provisions of this Act: Provided, finally, that it shall be reviewed and updated every year by the provincial, city or municipal solid waste management board.

SECTION 3. APPROVAL AND ADOPTION - For purposes of this Ordinance, the TEN-YEAR SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLAN endorsed by the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO), as previously approved by the Davao City Ecological Solid Waste Management Board (DCESWMB) and submitted and preliminary approved by the National Solid Waste Management Commission (NSWMC) hereby ADOPTS and APPROVES THE TEN-YEAR SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLAN OF DAVAO CITY.

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances as well as pertinent rules and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance, are hereby repealed and amended accordingly.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any word, article, sentence, clause or paragraph of this Ordinance is declared invalid for any reason, other provisions thereof shall not be affected by such declaration and shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, July 21, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
