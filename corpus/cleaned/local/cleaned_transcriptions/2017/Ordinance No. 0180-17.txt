Ord. No. C150-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ACCEPTING THE DONATION OF TWO (2) UNITS ELECTRIC BIKES (E-BIKES) AND THE AUTHORITY OF THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY MSS CYCLE TRADING (MOTOSTAR) IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 453, (i), (ii), (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 18 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations; and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. DEED OF DONATION. - This is a Deed of Donation between MSS CYCLE TRADING (MOTOSTAR) and the City Government of Davao whereby MOTOSTAR will give the City Government of Davao two (2) units of electric bikes (e-bikes).

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation between MSS CYCLE TRADING (MOTOSTAR) and the City Government of Cagayan.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, April 25, 2017, by an unanimous vote of all Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT.
