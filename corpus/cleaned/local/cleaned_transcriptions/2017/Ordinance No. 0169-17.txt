Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the attached Ordinance No. 0169-17 Series of 2017 entitled "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 1 FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2017 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CONSISTING OF THE BUDGET UNDER THE GENERAL FUND PROPER, THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES AND THE DEVELOPMENT FUND, IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF FOUR HUNDRED TWENTY-EIGHT MILLION ONE HUNDRED SEVEN THOUSAND NINE HUNDRED TWENTY-TWO PESOS AND 70/100 (P428,171,522.70)." The information is that passage of the ordinance is well within the power of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

As a general rule, all revenue sources and programs and projects should be considered and incorporated in the annual budget. After the annual budget has been authorized by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, no ordinance shall be enacted except under the following instances: (1) Funds actually available to support the change; (2) the change is covered by new revenue sources; (3), change is due to state of default in the area (Section 321 of RA 7.100).

In the case at bar, the enactment of Supplemental Budget Number 1, which changes the annual budget is covered with sufficient funds as certified by the City Treasurer.

In view thereof, approval of the ordinance is hereby recommended.
