**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE TRUST FUND OBTAINED FROM DONATIONS IN THE AMOUNT OF THREE MILLION PESOS (P3,000,000.00) FOR MEDICAL ASSISTANCE OF THE VICTIMS OF THE SEPTEMBER 2, 2016 ROXAS NIGHT MARKET BLAST NEEDING CONTINUOUS AND LONG TERM MEDICAL CARE**

Ord. No. 0191 - 17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE TRUST FUND OBTAINED FROM DONATIONS IN THE AMOUNT OF THREE MILLION PESOS (P3,000,000.00), FOR THE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE OF THE VICTIMS OF THE SEPTEMBER 2, 2016 ROXAS NIGHT MARKET BLAST NEEDING CONTINUOUS AND LONG TERM MEDICAL CARE";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to respond to the needs of its people especially those who became helpless victims of terroristic acts committed in Davao City;

SECTION 3. MEDICAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAM. The Medical Assistance Program shall be for the benefit of the victims of the September 2, 2016 Roxas Night Market Blast needing continuous and long-term medical care. While these victims have been discharged from the hospital, some of them have not regained their full physical functions. Many of them still require regular medical check-ups, continuous administration of medicines, and assistive device and apparatus;

SECTION 4. BENEFICIARIES. The Beneficiaries of the Program are the identified victims of the September 2, 2016 Roxas Night Market Blast who still need continuing medical attention in order to assist them in their full physical recovery, namely:

1. TAGADAYA, STEVEN RAEYAN
2. TAGADAYA, PRINCESS SHEENA
3. REYES, JOAN JUANA
4. NACARIO, ERICKSON
5. MANALO, FATIMA
6. ESPORTONO, LILIBETH
7. CARTAGENAS, ROSA
8. CIRILO, JUAN B.
9. ESPORTONO, JOEL
10. TAYAO, SAMUEL
11. VILLEGAS, CANILE

SECTION 5. SOURCE OF FUNDS. The funds shall be obtained from the donations made to the City Government of Davao kept in a Trust Fund from various local and national agencies and offices as well as private individuals who quickly responded to assist the victims of the September 2, 2016 Roxas Night Market Blast;

SECTION 6. DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS. The disbursement of funds for the Medical Assistance Program to the victims of the September 2, 2016, Roxas Night Market Blast shall be made in accordance with the existing, auditing rules and regulations of the Commission on Audit (CSA) as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and executive orders;

SECTION 7. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS. Within forty-five (45) days from the effectivity of this Ordinance, the City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDD) shall formulate and adopt the Implementing Rules and Regulations to carry out the objectives of this Ordinance, in consultation with the Office of the City Mayor, the Office of the City Legal Officer, the Office of the City Treasurer and the Office of the City Budget Officer;

**SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE**
Any existing executive order, ordinance, resolution or any provisions thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are deemed repealed or modified accordingly;

**SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

**SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY**
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;
