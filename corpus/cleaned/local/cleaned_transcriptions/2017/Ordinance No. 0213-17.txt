AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE OF DAVAO, INC. --- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0213 - 17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE OF DAVAO DEL SUR, INC. FOR THE ON-THE-JOB TRAINING OF ITS NURSING, MIDWIFERY, HEALTH CARE SERVICES AND CAREGIVING STUDENTS AT THE CO SU GIAN HOME FOR THE ELDERLY"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall: Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other powers and authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the renewal of the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Polytechnic College of Davao del Sur, Inc. for the On-the-Job training of its Nursing, Midwifery, Health Care Services and Caregiving students at the Co Su Gian Home for the Elderly;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, June 20, 2017, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOWN ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This AGREEMENT made and entered into in Davao City, Philippines, this day of 2016 by and between:

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, with principal address at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented by the Honorable Mayor of Davao City, SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the FIRST PARTY,

and

POLYTECHNIC COLLEGE OF DAVAO DEL SUR, INC., a non-stock and non-profit higher education institution with address at MacArthur Highway, Barangay Klagot, Digos City, represented by its President and CEO, DR. JOSE J. ADLAWAN, hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARTY;

WITNESSETH: That

1. The FIRST PARTY supervises the operations of CO SU GIAN CENTER FOR THE ELDERLY, situated in Buhantang District, Davao City, whose facilities meet the standards for training agency, registered and accredited by the Department of Social Welfare and Development.

2. The FIRST PARTY agrees and allows the affiliation and training of Nursing, Midwifery, Health Care Services and Caringgiving students of the SECOND PARTY, subject to the Policies, Procedures, and Guidelines governing Affiliation and Training of Students of the FIRST PARTY.

3. The SECOND PARTY shall strictly comply with such policies, procedures and guidelines of the FIRST PARTY. Any violation thereof shall be a valid ground for cancellation of the contract.

4. This contract shall be valid and effective for two (2) years, and may be renewed for a similar period upon agreement of the parties.

5. The FIRST PARTY shall not assume responsibility for health care costs in cases of injuries or illnesses sustained while a student and/or clinical instructor is in the clinical area or as a result of the clinical experience.

6. The SECOND PARTY shall secure personal health coverage and/or health insurance of students and clinical/s on rotation in the area.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this Contract in Davao City, Philippines, this day of 2016.
