Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO GLOBE TELECOMMUNICATIONS, INC., TO CONSTRUCT AND MAINTAIN A 40-METER HIGH SELF-SUPPORT TOWER (SST) AT NEW MATINA SIR, PHASE II, BARANGAY 76-A, BUCANA, THIS CITY, SUBJECT TO THE COMPLIANCE OF THE REGULATORY FEE PROVIDED FOR IN ORDINANCE NO. 0463-13, SERIES OF 2013".

SECTION 2. LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY: - The Sangguniang Panlungsod hereby grants Legislative Authority to Globe Telecommunications Inc. to construct and maintain a 40-meter high Self-Support Tower (SST) at New Matina Sir, Phase II, Barangay 76-A, Bucana, this City, subject to the compliance of the regulatory fee provided for in Ordinance No. 0463-13.

SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE: - All other laws, decree, orders, issuances, rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after posting.

ENACTED, November 22, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
