**Ord. No. 023-16**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEFAL OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION - DAVAO CHAPTER RELATIVE TO THE GRANT IN THE AMOUNT OF ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P100,000.00) FOR MENTAL HEALTH PROGRAMS, AS AMENDED."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purposes of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the city in its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY: The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Mental Health Association - Davao Chapter relative to the grant of one hundred thousand pesos (P100,000.00) for mental health programs;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, August 30, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOWN ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This memorandum of agreement is entered into this 5th day of September by and between the following:

PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION, DAVAO CITY CHAPTER, a non-stock, non-profit corporation created in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at 2nd Floor Insular Life Building, C.M. Recto St., Davao City, represented by its Chairperson, MR. DOMINADOR A. LOPEZ, of legal age, Filipino Citizen and a resident of Davao City, herein referred to as the PMHA.

And

THE CITY OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit organized and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented by the City MAYOR SARA Z. DUTERTE, of legal age, Filipino Citizen, married, and resident of Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the City,

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, Section 34 of RA 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 recognized the non-government organizations as partners in the pursuit of local autonomy;

WHEREAS, Section 35 of RA 7160 authorizes the local government units to enter into joint ventures and such other cooperative arrangement with people and nongovernmental organizations to engage in the delivery of certain basic services, capacity building and livelihood projects, and to develop local enterprises designed to improve productivity and income, diversify agriculture, spur rural industrialization, promote ecological balance and enhance the economic and social well-being of the people;

WHEREAS, both PARTIES recognize the urgency to address the prevalence of emotional and psychological conditions of the citizens, children in the streets, violence against women and children, drug addiction and other related social problems agreed to jointly undertake psychosocial intervention and other mental health programs for their holistic development;

WHEREAS, the PMHA has outlined programs and services to children and youth with emotional, behavioral, psychological and psychiatric disorders;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing, the parties have hereunto agreed as they hereby agree to establish a joint undertaking in the delivery of programs and services for women and children with mental and psychological problem under the following terms and conditions:

--- [Page 10] ---
A. The CITY OF DAVAO shall:

1. Provide the P100,000.00 for Philippine Mental Health Association Davao Chapter to support the mental health related programs and services for the City of Davao.

B. The PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION DAVAO CHAPTER shall:

1. Provide psychological and psychiatric services following the standards and implementing procedures laid down by appropriate agencies to the wards of CIC-Balay Dangqupan, Paginhiwawa Drop-in Center and Landag sa Kabatana having Behavioral, Psychological, Emotional and Psychiatric disorder with free of charge as direct beneficiaries.

2. Provide seminar/training for effective Parenting to parents in every community, and victims of violence against women and children including the provision of materials required during the training session and module;

3. Provide necessary services to the public schools in implementing mental health awareness through Lusog Isip ng Kabatana (LINK)Program;

4. Provide necessary services to psychology students to different schools for mental health awareness and promotion as a leading promoter of mental health in Davao City through Psychological Society Association of Mental Health (PSAMH) Program;

5. Render services to the people of Davao City in any means of service that will enrich their minds in combating their day to day struggles.

6. Submit Quarterly the Liquidation Report reflecting the usage of funds in implementing the programs and services of Philippine Mental Health Association Davao Chapter.

C. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1. The duration of this program shall be until such time that the One Hundred Thousand Pesos (P 100,000.00) have been consumed by the Philippine Mental Health Association (PMHA), pursuant to the goals of the program.

2. The guidelines issued by the Commission on Audit in the granting, utilization, accounting and auditing of funds released to Non-Government Organizations/People's Organizations (NGOs/POS) as provided under COA Circular No. 2007-001 is hereby incorporated as an integral part of this Agreement.
