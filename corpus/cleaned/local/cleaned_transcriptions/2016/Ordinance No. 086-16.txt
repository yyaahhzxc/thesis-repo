ORDINANCE NO. 086-16
Series of 2016
AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BY AND BETWEEN THE BUREAU OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES (BFA XI) AND CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROJECT FUNDED BY BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING (BUB) FOR THE YEAR 2016 FOR FISHING GEARS AND PARAPHERNALIA.

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BY AND BETWEEN THE BUREAU OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES (BFAAR XI) AND CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROJECT FUNDED BY BOTTOM-UP BUDGETING (BUB) FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2016 FOR FISHING GEARS AND PARAPHERNALIA";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides, to wit;

"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city government in all transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY: The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN THE BUREAU OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES (BFAAR XI) AND CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PROJECT FUNDED BY BOTTOM-UP BUDGETING (BUB) FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2016 FOR FISHING GEARS AND PARAPHERNALIA;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE: This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.

ENACTED, October 14, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present.

CERTIFIED CORRECT.

CHIARITO N. SANTOS

Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head)

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
FISHING GEARS & PARAPHERNALIA'S
Davao City
Funded under Bottoms-Up Budgeting 2016

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement entered into by and between:

The BUREAU OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES through the BFAR Regional Office XI, a government agency created and existing under the Republic of Philippines with the office address at R. Masaysay Ave., Davao City and represented herein by its Regional Director, FATMA M. DRIS, CESO V, and hereinafter referred to as "BFAR XI".

and

The Local Government of the City of Davao, a local government unit with postal address at City Hall Drive, Davao City and represented herein by its Honourable Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, and hereinafter referred to as "LGU".

WITHINSEETH

WHEREAS, the Philippine Government shall pursue the Philippine Millennium Development Goal (PMDG) of reducing poverty from 33.1% in 1991 to 16.6% by 2015. The same concern is reflected in the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) for 2011-2016 or the Government's blueprint that defines the strategies and programs that will translate the Social Contract (SC) into efficient, effective and responsive actions that are achievable within the term of office;

WHEREAS, Executive Order No. 43 (Pursuing Our Social Contract with the Filipino People through the Reorganization of the Cabinet Clusters, Series of 2011) enunciates all departments and agencies to orient their programs, projects and activities towards the pursuit of Five (5) Key Result Areas that include Poverty Reduction and Empowerment of the Poor and Vulnerable;

WHEREAS, a well thought strategy to reduce poverty incidence in the country is the implementation of Bottoms-Up Budgeting (BuB) that aims to ensure the inclusion of the funding requirements for the development needs of the poorest municipalities and cities in the programs and budgets of National Government Agencies (NGAs);

WHEREAS, the BUREAU OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES (BFAR XI), through its mandate and under the Republic Act 8530 - The Philippines Fisheries Code of 1998 provides support to the fishery sector through appropriate technology and research,

--- [Page 10] ---
adequate financial resources, production, construction of post-harvest facilities, marketing assistance and other services;

WHEREAS, the Local Government of the City of Davao has signified its intent to provide the counterpart fund (cash) of not less than forty percent (40%) of the total project cost subject to their Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA), in compliance with the prescribed 2011 General Appropriations Act (GAA);

WHEREAS, the Local Government of the City of Davao submitted its proposed Local Poverty Reduction Action Plan (LPRAP) and has passed the following eligibility standards:

1. Has the technical capabilities/capacities to implement the project as determined by BFAR such as the presence of knowledgeable technical staff, availability of functional equipment and good track record or performance on project implementation;
2. Recipient of Department of the Interior and Local Government's (DILG) Good Financial Housekeeping (GFH) for FY 2015 assessment;
3. Completion of the assessment of its Public Financial Management (PFM) System required by Department of Budget and Management (DBM); and
4. No unqualified cash advance from BFAR

WHEREAS, the BUREAU OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES (BFAR X), poses no objection to the request of assistance by the LGU and shall allocate the funds for this project from General Appropriations Act, afforded to implement anti-poverty projects.

ARTICLE I The Project

Parties agree to and signify their intention to undertake the FISHING GEARS & PARAPHERNALIA'S hereinafter referred to as "PROJECT" located at the DAVAO CITY, with a project allocation from BFAR XI amounting to FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (Php500,000.00) and that all activities could be completed within available budgetary allocations;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the premises and mutual covenants hereinafter set forth, the parties hereto agree as follows.

**ARTICLE III**

**Penalties and Sanctions**

The **BFAR XI** upon consultation with RPRAT chaired by the **Regional Director of DILG** is authorized to:

1. Terminate/Cancel the implementation of project for the following bases/instances:
   - a. Recipients LGU non-compliance to prescribed project processes, standards, and requirements;
   - b. Corruption and fraudulent practices;
   - c. Situations/circumstances would make it improbable for the project to continue to be carried out; and
   - d. Upon request of the recipient LGU.

In such case the project is partially or fully cancelled due to the fault of recipient LGU, it shall be obliged to return the unexpended balance of the released portion of the funds or pertinent portion related to the cancelled component, whichever applies.

2. Suspended/Withhold the release of succeeding tranches if any if any of the following occurs:
   - a. BFAR XI finds out during the conduct of review and monitoring activities of projects of the following:
     - a.1 Procurement of any contract is in inconsistent with the provision of RA 9184;
     - a.2. LGU unsatisfactorily performance in the implementation/execution of project of irreversible or irreversible slippage in the implementation; and
     - a.3. Reported wrongdoing from stakeholders in the locality/project site.

3. Suspended project implementation and improve performance/s or remedy the situation, in case of project suspension. On the other hand, the early termination of the project will be resorted only if the recipient LGU had been duly notified.

**ARTICLE IV**

**Amendment**

Any amendment to this agreement shall be made in writing duly agreed and signed by the parties hereto.

**ARTICLE V**

**Effectivity**

This agreement shall take effect signing thereof by the parties and shall remain valid and binding for (3) year or until the completion of the project unless rescinded by the parties by giving sixty (60) days advance written notice.
