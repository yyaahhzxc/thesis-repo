Ord. No. 018-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ALONG A PORTION OF 72-2 GEMPESAW EXT., BARANGAY 15-B, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY, FROM 6:00 PM ONWARDS ON AUGUST 27, 2016, IN LINE WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE 35TH ARAW NG BARANGAY OF BARANGAY 15-B, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunication and water works projects;

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE. - In line with the celebration of the 35th Araw ng Barangay of Barangay 15-B, Poblacion District, this City, the portion of 72-2 Gempe saw Ext., Barangay 15-B, Poblacion District, this City, shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic from 6:00 pm onwards on August 27, 2016;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, August 23, 2016, by a majority vote of all the members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
