Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the attached Ordinance Number 022-16 Series of 2016 entitled "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY THE CITY LEGAL OFFICER OF THE SANGUNGAN FOUNDATION, INC. (PEPCOFI) REGARDING THE GRANT OF ONE MILLION PESOS (P1,000,000.00) AS FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE PEPCOFI" informing your end that this office interposes no objection to the enactment of the ordinance citing Section 36 of RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines, to quote:

"Section 36: Assistance to People's and Non-governmental Organizations. — A local government unit may, through its local chief executive and with the concurrence of the Sanggunian concerned, provide assistance, financial or otherwise, to such people's and non-governmental organizations for economic, socially-oriented, environmental, or cultural projects to be implemented within its territorial jurisdiction".

However, in the execution of the Memorandum of Agreement, the PEPCOFI must comply with all the requirements to support the disbursement of funds of the City of Davao.

Caveat is hereby given that in the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA), the representative of the City of Davao appears to be in the name of the former mayor, now, President of the Republic of the Philippines. This should be corrected from RODRIGO R. DUTERTE to SARA Z. DUTERTE, the incumbent City Mayor of Davao City.

Hence, it is recommended that the Ordinance be approved and the Memorandum of Agreement be executed after the necessary correction therefore on the name of the representative is duly made.

Ord. No. 022-16

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR HIM IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE EAGLE CONSERVATION PROGRAM FOUNDATION, INC. (PECFI), REGARDING THE GRANT OF ONE MILLION PESOS (P1,000,000.00) AS FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE PECFI";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - In keeping its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time, ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use. Further, Section 455, (b) (I) (v) of the Local Government Code provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purposes of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS. - The amount of One Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00) was appropriated in the Annual Budget, CY 2016, particularly under the account, AIDE TO NON-GOVERNMENT ENTITIES, as financial assistance to the Philippine Eagle Conservation Program Foundation, Inc.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Eagle Conservation Program Foundation, Inc. (PECFI) regarding the support and assistance for the conservation efforts of PECFI, especially the grant of One Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00);

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, August 30, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement is made and entered into this day of 2016 at Davao City, Philippines by and between:

The Davao City Government, duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office at the City Hall of Davao City, San Pedro Street, Davao City, Mindanao, Philippines, and represented herein in this act by its City Mayor, her honor SARA Z. DUTERTE duly authorized for this purpose;

-AND-

The PHILIPPINE EAGLE CONSERVATION PROGRAM FOUNDATION, INC., a non-stock, non-profit, private organization, duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office at the Philippine Eagle Center, Malagos, Baguio District, Davao City, Mindanao, Philippines, and herein represented in this act by its Executive Director, DENNIS JOSEPH I. SALVADOR, duly authorized for this purpose:

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, PECCPFI has embarked on a project to accelerate the captive breeding program of the Philippine Eagle to replenish wild population now threatened by extinction and acquire resources needed to accomplish this objective:

WHEREAS, the Davao City Government has committed to support PECCPFI's wildlife conservation initiatives through a grant aid in support and assistance to the conservation efforts of PECCPFI.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the PARTIES agree(s) as follows:

SECTION I. SUBJECT OF AGREEMENT

A. The Davao City Government allocated in 2016 Php 1,000,000.00 to cover the food stock expense and release said amount to the PECCPFI.

B. That the PECCPFI shall include the name of the Davao City Government in the acknowledgment erected at the Philippine Eagle Center in Malagos and PECCPFI's publications.
