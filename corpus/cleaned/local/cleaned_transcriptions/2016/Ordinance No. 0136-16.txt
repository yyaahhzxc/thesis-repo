ORDINANCE NO. 0136-17
Series of 2017

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF RED TIE CONCEPTS AND EVENTS OF DAVAO CITY FOR EXEMPTION FROM PAYMENT OF FEES FOR THE INSTALLATION OF TEN (10), 1X24M BANNERS, FOR ITS "MISS UNIVERSE ANCILLARY EVENT IN DAVAO CITY" ON JANUARY 19, 2017, AT SMX CONVENTION CENTER, LANANG DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0136-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF RED TIE CONCEPTS AND EVENTS OF DAVAO CITY FOR EXEMPTION FROM PAYMENT OF FEES FOR THE INSTALLATION OF TEN (10), 1X24M BANNERS, FOR ITS 'MISS UNIVERSE ANCILLARY EVENT IN DAVAO CITY" ON JANUARY 19, 2017, AT SMX CONVENTION CENTER, LANANG DAVAO CITY;

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption solely refers to the payment of local taxes and fees on the installation of ten (10), 1x24m banners;

SECTION 3. PERIOD - The exemption shall be on January 19, 2017 only;

SECTION 4. CONDITIONS -

a. For the safety of the pedestrians/commuters/motorist, close coordination should be done with the Department of Public Works and Highways, City Engineer's office and Davao Light and Power Company during the installation of ten (10) banners;

b. No print material shall obstruct the view of the pedestrians/commuters/motorists;

c. The City Government of Davao shall be recognized as event partner in all programs and print materials;

d. All print materials shall be removed immediately at no cost to the City Government of Davao on January 20, 2017;

e. In no case shall the organizers be exempt from compliance with other provisions of the "Signage Ordinance of Davao City" and other applicable laws and Ordinances;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 10th of January 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.
