SECTION 1. **SECTION 4. SCHEDULE OF EXPENSES**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |

| 2 | 30,000 x 70 victims | Financial aid for all 70 injured victims at P30,000.00 each |
| 3 | 2,000 x 13 victims x 10 days | Food Expenses to be disbursed by the City Social Services & Development Office in aid to the victims' families |
| 4 | 20,000 x 1 victim | Expenses for the Muslim victim's burial system |
| 5 | 100,000 x 3 meals x 2 watchers x 20 days | Food expenses for use in the confinement of victims still in the hospitals to be disbursed by CSSDO |

**SECTION 5. LEGAL BASIS**

Pursuant to Section 45B, par. (1)(iv) of the Local Government Code of 1991, it empowers the Sanggunian Pangasinod to adopt measures to protect the inhabitants of the City from the harmful effects of man-made or natural disasters and criminalities and to provide relief and assistance to the victims during and in the aftermath of said disaster or calamities and their return to productive livelihood following said events;

---

**SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS**

The amounts herein appropriated shall be used especially for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Pangasinod. All disbursement and utilization of funds shall be subject to the existing government budget and management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and Presidential directives, which must be observed strictly at all times;
