Ord. No. 076-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY LEGAL OFFICER TO REPRESENT THE CITY MAYOR IN THE EJECTMENT CASES TO BE FILED AGAINST THE POSSESSORS OF LOT 6, BLOCK 3, LOCATED AT BARRIO OBRERO, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Subparagraph 3 (vi), Paragraph (b), Section 481 of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that the City Legal Officer may:

"(vi) When directed by the governor, mayor, or sanggunian, initiate and prosecute in the interest of the local government unit concerned any civil action on any bond, lease or other contract upon any breach or violation thereof".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. The City Legal Officer is hereby granted legislative authority to represent the City Mayor in the ejectment cases to be filed against the possessors of Lot 6, Block 3, located at Barrio Obrero, Poblacion District, this City;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

EHACTION: October 4, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present.

(3) In addition to the foregoing duties and functions, the legal officer shall:
