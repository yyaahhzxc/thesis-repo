Ord. No. 0492-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR
TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO,
THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND
AMONG THERMA SOUTH INC., THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE
MUNICIPALITY OF STA. CRUZ, DAVAO DEL SUR, ON THE SHARING AND
DISTRIBUTION OF LOCAL TAXES;

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph [b] (1)
(vi) of the Local Government Code provides in part, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which
is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of
this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business
transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and
such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or
pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT - This is a Memorandum
of Agreement by and among Therma South Inc. ("T'SI"), the City of Davao and the
Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur, on the sharing and distribution of
local taxes;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to sign the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and
among Therma South Inc. ("T'SI"), the City of Davao and the Municipality of Sta.
Cruz, Davao del Sur, on the sharing and distribution of local taxes;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other
sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to
be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, February 2, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of
the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into this day of at Philippines, by and between:

THERMA SOUTH, INC., a corporation duly organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal place of business at Brgy. Binugao, Toril District, Davao City, represented by its President and Chief Operating Officer, Sebastian R. Lacsan, hereinafter referred to as "TSI";

THE CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit ("LGU") organized and existing under and by virtue of its Charter under Commonwealth Act No. 93 and Republic Act No. 7686, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 (the "LGC"), with seat of government at San Pedro St., Davao City, Philippines, represented by the City Mayor, Honorable Rodolfo R. Duterte, authorized through Sangguniang Panglungsod Ordinance No. hereinafter referred to as the "City"; and

THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. CRUZ, an LGU organized and existing by virtue of its Charter under Republic Act No. and the LGC, with seat of government at the Municipal Hall Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur, Philippines, represented by the Municipal Mayor, Honorable Joel Ray L. Lopez, authorized through Sanggunian Bayan Resolution No. hereinafter referred to as the "Municipality".

TSI, the City and the Municipality are hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Parties" and singly as a "Party."

WITNESSETH: That --

WHEREAS, TSI is venturing in the business of producing and selling energy through the development, operation, and management of a coal-fired power plant utilizing the circulating fluidized bed technology with a capacity of three hundred megawatts (300 MW) (the "Power Plant");

WHEREAS, the Power Plant being developed by TSI is physically situated in, and spanning the territorial boundaries of, Brgy. Binugao, Toril District, Davao City and Brgy. Inayawan, Sta. Cruz, Davao del Sur;

WHEREAS, pursuant to the LGC and Republic Act No. 936, otherwise known as the "Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001" (the "EPIRA Law"), TSI proposes to enter into a Memorandum of Agreement with the City and the Municipality (collectively, the "LGUs"), to clarify the extent of its obligations to the LGUs, including the proper allocation of gross receipts for the applicable local taxes under the terms and conditions herein; and

WHEREAS, a proper application of the rules on situs of taxation under the LGC and existing rules, regulations and opinions on the matter is required so that each LGU receives its rightful and equitable share in all the applicable local taxes.

--- [Page 8] ---
NOW THEREFORE, in view of the foregoing premises and for and in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements hereinafter set forth, the Parties hereto, for themselves, their successors and their assigns, mutually agree as follows:

I. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES

A. Responsibilities of TSI to the LGUs:

1. Subject to the execution of the proper agreements with the Department of Energy, TSI shall comply with all applicable laws in providing financial benefits to the host communities within the LGUs.

2. TSI hereby agrees to pay the appropriate taxes (including real property tax and local business tax, as may be applicable) imposed by the LGUs pursuant to validly enacted local tax ordinances, the LGC, its pertinent rules and regulations, and any other applicable laws. Notwithstanding the foregoing, TSI's rights to dispute any assessments and avail of the taxpayer's remedies as provided for under the LGC shall be preserved and shall not in any way be impaired by this Agreement.

3. TSI shall coordinate with the LGUs for the performance and maintenance works and repairs on the access roads, road rights-of-way, and easements, if any, granted by the LGUs in accordance with Paragraph 1.6 of this Agreement; particularly the access roads, road rights-of-way, and easements where the Power Plant is located, should the same be damaged willfully by TSI, its contractors and suppliers;

4. TSI shall extend preference in employment opportunities to bona fide residents of the LGUs for its Power Plant, provided, however, that: (i) employment vacancies exist, and (ii) such residents are qualified, competent and physically fit for the vacant positions, in accordance with the employment guidelines set forth by TSI.

B. Responsibilities of the LGUs

In recognition of this Agreement, the LGUs bind themselves to:

1. continue to observe, abide by and respect the obligations and responsibilities under any existing agreements, including this Agreement, with TSI with utmost good faith;

2. institute measures to protect and preserve natural resources within their respective territorial jurisdictions;

3. encourage the maintenance of public assistance and support for the Power Plant of TSI, its affiliates, and its subsidiaries, as well as its other plants which may be developed through future expansions located within the LGUs;

4. assist in the maintenance of peace and order in the areas within the LGUs where the Power Plant and the related facilities of TSI, its affiliates, and its subsidiaries are located;

--- [Page 9] ---
5. assist TSI with the timely securing, maintenance, and renewal of its relevant local business permits, licenses, authorizations, and clearances for the continued operations of the Power Plant and its related facilities, provided that TSI shall have complied with all material requirements therefor;

6. grant free and unfettered access, road rights-of-way, and easements through its properties to TSI, its contractors, suppliers, and other authorized representatives of TSI, or their duly authorized agents, contractors/subcontractors, as may from time to time be necessary for the execution of works in connection with the operation and maintenance of the Power Plant and its related facilities;

7. impose only local business taxes under Section 143 of the LGC following the allocation of gross receipts set forth under Section II of the Agreement; and

8. take all such actions and execute all such documents, including where the same is necessary, the appropriate ordinances and resolutions therefor, to ensure and recognize that any and all payments under this Agreement completely satisfy the legal requirements provided for under the LGC and other applicable laws, rules, and regulations.

II. SHARING AND DISTRIBUTION OF LOCAL TAXES

A. Local Business Tax

Pursuant to existing laws, rules, and regulations, and local ordinances on the subject of local business taxation effective in each of the LGUs, the Parties hereby acknowledge and agree that the following is the proper allocation of the gross receipts for purposes of computing the applicable local business tax due to each LGU:

|PARTICULARS | CITY | MUNICIPALITY |
|---|---|---|
| Principal Office (30%) | 30% | Not applicable |
| Sales Allocation (70%) | -60% of 70%= 42% | 40% of 70%= 28% |
| TOTAL | 72% | 28% |

As the City is the LGU where the principal place of business of TSI is registered, it is entitled to an allocation equivalent to thirty percent (30%) of the gross receipts recorded in the principal office pursuant to Section 140(b)(1) of the LGC. The remaining seventy percent (70%) shall be shared by the City and Municipality in the following manner: 60% of the 70% shall be allocated to the City; and 40% of the 70% shall be allocated to the Municipality.

Each LGU shall apply its respective tax rates enforceable at the time of payment to the gross receipts allocated to each of them under this Agreement.

The LGUs recognize that any and all remittances, contributions, and financial benefits timely made by TSI under this Agreement shall be in full and absolute

--- [Page 10] ---
compliance with existing laws, rules and regulations and local ordinances effective in each of the LGUs.

In consideration thereof, the LGUs hereby acknowledge that upon the timely payment of the local business taxes under this Agreement, they shall no longer have, as against TSI, any claim, demand, cause of action, complaint, case or grievance as to the subject matter hereof.

Any dispute pertaining to or in connection with the allocation of gross receipts under this Agreement shall be resolved by the LGUs themselves and shall not be used by any of them to cause prejudice or put at risk the continued operation of TSI and the Power Plant. In the event that conflicting claims are made subject of court proceedings, the LGUs shall allow TSI to deposit or consign with the proper courts the proper amounts of local business taxes following the allocation of gross receipts to each of the LGUs under this Agreement, which shall have the effect of full payment of TSI's obligations under this Agreement and existing laws, rules and regulations and local ordinances.

B. Adherence to Local Ordinance

Once executed by the Parties, they shall implement the provisions herein on local business taxation (Section II) which provisions they recognize as consistent with Ordinance No. 100 of the City and Ordinance No. 237 of the Municipality (the "Ordinances").

III. FURTHER ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

A. No franchise

The LGUs hereby acknowledge that TSI's business of generating electricity is not a public utility operation and TSI is not required to secure any franchise to operate the Power Plant from the National Government or any LGU by express mandate under Section 6 of the EPIRA, and that TSI is not subject to franchise tax imposed under the LGC by any of the LGUs.

B. Exemption

Section 533(g) of the LGC provides that the exercise of the taxing powers of provinces, cities and municipalities, and barangays shall not extend to the levy of taxes on business enterprises certified by the Board of Investments (the "BOI") with pioneer or non-pioneer statuses for a period of six (6) and four (4) years, respectively from the date of registration. By virtue of TSI's registration with the BOI with Certificate of Registration No. 2011-159 dated July 15, 2011 as a business enterprise with non-pioneer status, it shall remain exempt from local taxation for a period of four (4) years from the date of its registration, or until July 15, 2015.

IV. NOT USED

V. REPRESENTATION AND WARRANTIES

A. TSI represents and warrants that as of the date hereof:

--- [Page 11] ---
1. It is duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines;

2. It has the full power, authority, and legal right to execute this Agreement, to exercise its rights and perform its obligations hereunder, and to consummate the transactions contemplated herein;

3. The obligations expressed to be assumed by it under this Agreement are, under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, its legal, valid, and binding obligations, enforceable against it in accordance with the terms hereof; and all acts, conditions, and things required by its Articles of Incorporation to be done, fulfilled or performed in order to enable it to enter into, execute, deliver, exercise its rights, and perform its obligations under this Agreement, to constitute this Agreement legal, valid, and binding upon it, and any and all proceedings for enforcement of this Agreement admissible in evidence, have been done, fulfilled and performed.

4. It has duly authorized the official signing this Agreement on its behalf.

B. Each of the LGUs represents and warrants that as of the date hereof:

1. It has full power, authority, and legal right to execute, perform, and timely observe all the provisions of this Agreement and the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement has been duly authorized; it can enter into this Agreement and that the entering into this Agreement does not contravene or violate any agreement that it may have entered into with third parties;

2. It shall not take action of any kind that is contrary to the express intention of this Agreement, and if any such action is taken by any other person, it shall protect the integrity of this Agreement and shall hold TSI free and harmless from all types of liability;

3. It has all the required governmental resolutions, licenses, consents and authorizations necessary for the due and proper performance of all its obligations under this Agreement;

4. It has duly authorized the official signing this Agreement on its behalf.

VI. EXECUTION OF IMPLEMENTING AGREEMENTS AND GOOD FAITH UNDERTAKING

The Parties shall support each other in respect of the effective implementation of this Agreement and in the performance of their respective responsibilities in any instance or occasion as may be deemed proper.

The Parties shall coordinate and consult with each other, in the spirit of mutual goodwill, in any instance or occasion in which this Agreement is applicable or relevant.

--- [Page 12] ---
The Parties, in discharging their respective commitments hereunder, agree to observe and apply the standards of good faith and to take into consideration the main purposes of this Agreement, and as such, the Parties agree to perform all such acts and to execute and deliver such other documents or instruments as may be necessary in order to give effect to the intent underlying this Agreement and to fully implement, consummate and observe the commitments contemplated hereby.

VII. TERMINATION

The material violation of any of the terms and conditions of this Agreement shall entitle the aggrieved party to exercise all the rights as may be available to it under law or equity in order to address the same, including a mandatory judicial writ to enforce compliance with the provisions hereof or the rescission or termination of this Agreement upon notice to all the other Parties. Any termination of this Agreement shall take effect only after thirty (30) days from the receipt of the notice to such effect by all the Parties.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the termination of this Agreement shall not result in the nullification or repeal of the Ordinances and TSI may continue to perform its obligations under them unless they are expressly repealed or declared null and void.

However, any protest or appeal that may be lodged by TSI in relation to the payment of local taxes pursuant to the Ordinances and to the rules contained in this Agreement, pending resolution of such contest or appeal, shall not be considered a violation that will give the LGUs the right to terminate this Agreement.

IX. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

A. Amendments

No amendments or modifications of the Agreement shall be valid except by written agreement of all the Parties.

B. Binding Effect/Assignment

The covenants, terms, conditions, provisions and undertakings in this Agreement or any modifications, revisions or supplements thereof shall be binding on the LGUs and shall extend to and be binding upon the incumbent local officials of LGUs, including their respective incumbent local legislative councils, the current principals of appointed local government officials, and their elected or appointed successors and assigns, as if all of them were in every case named and expressed herein.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, TSI may assign its rights and obligations under this Agreement, except for the payment of the applicable taxes where TSI is the statutory taxpayer, to its subsidiary or affiliate with prior notice to the LGUs.

--- [Page 14] ---
C. Separability

If any provision or part of this Agreement shall be declared invalid or unenforceable by competent courts, the validity and enforceability of the other provisions or parts hereof shall not be affected or impaired.

In the event any provision or part of this Agreement is declared invalid in accordance with the preceding paragraph above, the Parties shall meet in good faith to agree, to the fullest extent allowed by law, on a substitute provision that most closely approximates the intention contained in the invalid provision, and amend this Agreement accordingly.

D. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations or expressions of intent, whether written or oral, with respect to the subject matter contained herein.

X. PERIOD OF THE AGREEMENT

This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect for as long as TSI or its successors-in-interest, affiliates, or related parties operate the Power Plant within the territorial jurisdiction of the LGUs except when earlier terminated under the terms provided herein.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have caused this Memorandum of Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives, this day of 2016.
