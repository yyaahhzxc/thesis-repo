RESOLUTION No. 49

A proposed Ordinance requiring persons and/or business establishments such as restaurants, confectioneries, feed stalls and other related outlets to barbeque meat such as pork, beef, chicken, fish and other feed preparations needing the use of charcoal to be painted with black paint to remove the painted stokes com-
pletely or cut their sharp edges so that they cannot be used, accordingly, was presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body by Councilor Jacinto F. Rubillar, Jr., co-sponsored by Councilor Tomas Manteverdo, Jr.

Councilor Jacinto F. Rubillar stressed the fact that used or unused painted barbecue sticks were particularly used as deadly weapons. Secondly, they could also hurt or injure people who might accidentally step on them, and thirdly, used and/or unbrushed barbecue sticks can be used again after a little washing, which is difficult to prevent.

Councilor Manteverdo stated further that to ensure public safety and sanitation, he would recommend prohibitions against these cautions violating this proposed ordinance to:

For the first offense, a fine of $90.00
For the second offense, a fine of $150.00
For the third offense, a fine of $150.00 and
For the fourth offense, a fine of $200.00 or imprisonment of one (1) year if we both upon the discretion of the court in case of indivi-
duals and two (2) years in case of business establishment and conse-
culation of permits issued therefor.

Resolution No. 419, series of 1986, contd.:

Councillor Mikaner A. Ligna manifested that in order to aid more teeth to said ordinance, he proposed amendments to the effect that for the first offense, a fine of P100.00 be imposed; for the second offense, a fine of P250.00 or an imprisonment of one (1) month or both upon the discretion of the court in case of individuals and closure of such business establishment with the cancellation of permits issued thereof. The amendments were accepted by the present.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councillor Jacinto T. Rubillary, Jr., co-sponsored by Councillors Ramon Mantuarte, Jr., duly amended and seconded by Councillor Nacho A. Magno, it was RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an Ordinance entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 113

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING PERSONS AND/OR BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS SUCH AS RESTAURANTS, CATERING, FOOD STALLS AND OTHER RELATED OUTLETS SERVING BARBECUE STICKS OR SKIVERS TO PROVIDE A FOOD RECIPE BOOKLET REGARDING THE USE OF BARBECUE STICKS OR SKIVERS TO ENSURE THAT THE BARBECUE STICKS COMPLYING OR CUT THEIR SHARP ENDS FORGE IN SERVICE TO GASTRONOMY, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREOF.

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsion, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - Any business establishment such as restaurants, cordoneries, sidewalk stalls or establishments preparing food for public consumption and display, which serves barbecued meat such as pork, beef, chicken, fish or food requiring the use of barbecue sticks or skewers shall be required to cut the sharp ends or remove the barbecue sticks or skewers completely before serving to the customer;

SECTION 2. - Any used barbecue stick or skewer shall not be used again thereafter;

SECTION 3. - Violations hereof shall subject the owners, proprietors or managers of business establishments mentioned under Section 1 hereof to the following:

for the first offense, a fine of P100.00

for the second offense, a fine of P250.00

for the third offense, a fine of P250.00 or an imprisonment of one (1) month or both upon discretion of the Court and closure of the business establishment with the cancellation of the business permit;

SECTION 4. - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
