**Ord. No. 0101-14**

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE ADDITIONAL AREA OF ONE-HALF HECTARE OUT OF THE CITY-OWNED PUBLIC CEMETERY AT MA-A, FOR THE EXCLUSIVE USE OF THE MUSLIM COMMUNITY AS SHELTER FOR FUNERALS AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ITS PERIMETER FENCE."

**SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY** - The maintenance of peace and order, the protection of life, liberty, property, and promotion of the general welfare are essential for the enjoyment by all the people of the blessings of democracy.

Under the general welfare clause, it is the obligation of the State to provide decent burial site to our fellow Filipinos, in this case, our Muslim brothers and sisters.

**SECTION 3. EXPANSION** - An additional five thousand (5,000) square meters is hereby appropriated out of the city-owned public cemetery at Ma-a, this City, for the exclusive use of the Muslims of Davao City as burial site.

**SECTION 4. APPROPRIATION** - An amount of FOUR MILLION ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P4,100,000.00) is hereby appropriated for the construction of perimeter fence and other related developments for the said area.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and after due publication to a newspaper of general circulation in the locality for three [3] consecutive times.

**ENACTED, January 14, 2014, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.**
