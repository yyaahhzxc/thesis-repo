RESOLUTION No. 2217
As a privileged matter after the suspension of the Rules of the Council, Counselor Antonio S. Castillo brought for consideration and appropriate action of the Body, the proposed amendments to the Rules of the City Council, as provided by Section 10 of the Davao City Tax Ordinance of 1974, submitted by His Honor, the City Mayor to the Body pursuant to his letter, dated November 26, 1974. Counselor Antonio S. Castillo informed that this matter was already considered and approved by the Special Rules Committee of 1974 and the Body then constituted itself as a whole to consider the proposed amendments to the City Tax Ordinance.
During the deliberations of the committee, Counselor Antonio S. Castillo introduced the amendments which were considered and taken and that he is adopting these amendments. Further, Counselor Castillo introduced amendments which were considered by the Body.
After careful deliberation, on motion of Counselor Antonio S. Castillo, duly and jointly seconded by Counselors Domingo R. Vidares and Felicidad C. Santos, amended annex properly by the pro-
ponent and by Counselor Dominador E. Zule, Jr., it was
RESOLVED, To approve, as it is hereby approved, an ordinance entitled:
ORDINANCE No. 1945
AN ORD NCE AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 1, SERIES OF 1974, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "DAVO CITY TAX ORDINANCE OF 1974."

ARTICLE 3 - In conformity with and in addition to the words and phrases as defined in Presidential Decree 231 and 426, the following terms are defined to mean under this Ordinance to wit:

(a) Advertising Agency - includes all persons who are engaged in the business of advertising for others by means of billboards, posters whether pictorial or reading matters, plaques, notices, signs, directories, pamphlets, leaflets, handbills, electric or neon lights, airplanes, balloons or other media.

(b) Agricultural Product - includes the yield of the soil such as corn, rice, wheat, rye, hay, coconuts, sugar cane, tobacco, root crops, vegetables, fruits, flowers, etc., and their by-products; ordinary salt; all kinds of fish; poultry; and live stock and animal products, whether in their original form or when preserved in a more convenient and marketable form through the simple processes of freezing, drying, salting, smoking and stringing.

(c) Amusement Place - includes theatres, cinematographs, concert halls, circuses and other places of amusement where one seeks amusement to entertain himself by seeing or viewing the show or performance. It includes those places where one seeks admission to entertain himself by direct participation. It also means a place or compound, building or portion thereof, whether open or enclosed for recreational or entertainment purposes.

Section 4. - Article 17, Chapter II of Ordinance No. I, Series of 1974, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"ARTICLE 17 - AMENDED RULES AND REGULATIONS IN THE ISSUANCE OF BUSINESS PERMITS BY THE CITY MAYOR, AND IMPOSING FEES THEREFOR.

Section 1. - Definition. - "Permit or License" is a right or permission granted in accordance with law by a competent authority to engage in some business or occupation or to engage in some transaction.

All other pertinent definitions under Article 5 of this Ordinance are hereby adopted and made applicable in this Article.

SEC. 2. - Imposition of Fee. - It shall be unlawful for any person or entity to conduct or engage in any of the businesses, trades or occupations within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao for which permit is required for the proper supervision and enforcement of existing laws and ordinances governing the same, except when the health, safety or welfare of the employees engaged in the business, trades or occupation specified in this ordinance and other ordinances, that may hereinafter be enacted without first having secured a permit therefor from the City Mayor and pay the necessary fees to the City Treasurer.

The permit fee is payable for every separate or distinct calling or occupation, whether established or not, if the business or activity is to be conducted; and one line of calling or occupation, business, trade or activity does not become exempt except by being conducted with some other occupation or calling or business, trade or activity for which the permit fee has been paid.

SEC. 3. - Time of Payment. - The fee imposed herein shall be paid to the City Treasurer upon presentation of the duly approved Mayor's Permit before any occupation or calling is purposed or any business can be lawfully begun, unless otherwise provided, the fees imposed in this article shall accrue due and payable in full with in the first twenty (20) days of January of each year.

Provided, That when the fee has been paid for the whole year and the business activity or occupation is REPEATED or ABANDONED, no refund of the fee corresponding to the unexpired quarter shall be made.

Provided further that in case of change of ownership of the business, it shall be the duty of the new owner, manager or agent of such business to secure a new permit and pay the corresponding permit fee as though it were a new business.

In case of failure to pay the permit or license fee or renew or surrender the permit, the amount of the fee due shall be increased by a surcharge of twenty-five per cent (25%) of the unpaid balance. This plus interest upon the unpaid account at the rate of five percent (1/2%) per annum from due date until the fee is fully paid.

SEC. 4. - Administrative provision.

(a) Supervision and control over establishments and places, etc., The City Mayor has authority to supervise and control all establishments and places, trades or occupations subject to the City Mayor.

However, application for renewal of Mayor's Permit shall be made three months before the expiration date of the permit, and such application shall be submitted to the Mayor not earlier than December first of each year but not later than January twentieth of the succeeding month of the following year.

(d) Posting of Permit. - Every permittee shall keep his permit posted at all times in his place of business or office in a conspicuous or if he has more than one place of business or different places of business, he shall submit it in his presence. The permit shall immediately be produced upon demand by the Mayor, the City Treasurer or their duly authorized representatives.
