Ord. No. 0245-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A LIQUOR BAN IN THE CITY OF DAVAO FROM 5:00 P.M. TO 8:00 A.M. OF THE NEXT DAY, UNTIL THE COMMUNITY QUARANTINE IS LIFTED".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE: A liquor ban from 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. of the next day shall be imposed upon all persons within the City of Davao, until the existing Community Quarantine is lifted.

SECTION 3. PROHIBITED ACTS: No person or entity shall sell or serve liquor, alcoholic beverages, coconut wine and other nature wines and the like that causes intoxication within the period provided.

SECTION 4. PENALTY CLAUSE: Any violation of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be punished as follows, without prejudice to the prosecution of other criminal actions:
- 1st Offense - A fine of Three Thousand Pesos (P3,000.00)
- 2nd Offense - A fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) and imprisonment of not more than one (1) year, at the discretion of the court.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and publication.

ENACTED, March 20, 2020, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
