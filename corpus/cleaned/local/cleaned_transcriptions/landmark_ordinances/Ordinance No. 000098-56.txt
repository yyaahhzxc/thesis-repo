CITY OF DAVAO
Office of the City Council
ORDINANCE NO. 98
AN ORDINANCE PENALIZING ACES OF LITTERING, SCATTERING OR ANY CARELESS
DISPERSE OF WASTE MATERIALS, REFUSE AND OTHER INSANITARY THINGS IN
STREETS, PARKS, PUBLIC BUILDINGS AND OTHER PUBLIC PLACES IN THE CITY
OF DAVAO,
Be It Ordained by the City Council, City of Davao, that:
SECTION 1. - This Ordinance shall be known as the "Anti-Littering
Ordinance of the City of Davao."
SEC. 2. - It shall be unlawful for any person to litter, scatter
or fall to properly dispose of any refuse, fruit peelings, pieces of
papers and other waste materials, or insanitary things in public streets,
parks, playgrounds, public buildings and other public places, except in
receptacles provided for the purpose.
SEC. 3. - It shall be the duty of the Office of the City Engineer
to provide garbage receptacles in appropriate places in public streets,
parks, playgrounds, buildings and other public places for the purposes
of this Ordinance.
SEC. 4. - Violations of this Ordinance shall be penalized by a fine
of P2.00 for the first offense and a fine of not less than P5.00 and not
more than P5.00 for each succeeding offense, in the discretion of the
court.
SEC. 5. - This Ordinance shall take effect on and after January 9,
1957.
