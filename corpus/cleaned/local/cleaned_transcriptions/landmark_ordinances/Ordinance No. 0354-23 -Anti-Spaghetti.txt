ORDINANCE NO. 0354-23
Series of 2023

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE POLE USAGE AND POLE
WIRE INSTALLATION IN THE CITY OF DAVAO

Page 2 of 9
Ord. No. 0354-23
Be it ordained by the Sanguinang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "ANTI-Spaghetti WIRE ORDINANCE OF 2023".
SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS
CLUSTERED POLES - more than one (1) pole erected at one location with a spacing of less than thirty (30) meters from each other;
DEAD WIRES - inactive wires of the utility company;
DOMINANT POLE - a pole erected and maintained in a specific location to cater to wires and other attachments. Dominant poles must be able to accommodate wires, attachments and other installations taking into account the minimum safety, elevation, technical standards as set forth under existing national laws and this Ordinance;
ONE POLE POLICY - the policy of the City Government of Davao that regulates the number of poles erected at any specific location by prohibiting more than one (1) pole erected at one location with a spacing of less than thirty (30) meters from each other, unless otherwise allowed by the City Engineer's Office;
UTILITY COMPANY - a company that provides services to the general public, such as, but not limited to, the following: electricity, telephone and internet, and cable television systems;
TELCO - a company that provides services to the general public, such as telephone, internet, and cable television systems.
SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall apply to areas within Davao City. This Ordinance shall be subject to and shall not prejudice the provisions of City Ordinance No. 0177-14, Series of 2014.
SECTION 4. POLE INSTALLATION AND TRANSFER - No pole shall obstruct and impede any road right of way, sidewalks, and alleys.
In cases of road and other infrastructure projects with affected poles, the City Engineer's Office or the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) shall notify pole owners with affected poles of the commencement of the said road and other infrastructure projects. The latter shall immediately put up additional safety notices and initiate measures for poles, attachments, and installation transfer, if necessary.
The transfer of poles and its attachments and installations after completion of a road and other infrastructure projects shall not be longer than one (1) month from receipt of a Notice of project phase completion issued by the City Engineer's Office or the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH). The pole owner shall, upon receipt of a Notice of project phase completion, immediately notify all other utility companies with pole attachments in the area and shall immediately cause the removal and transfer of affected poles.

**SECTION 5. ONE POLE POLICY**
Unless otherwise allowed under this Ordinance, clustered poles on sidewalks, roadside, parks, and any public places shall not be allowed.

**SECTION 6. EXCEPTIONS TO ONE POLE POLICY**

1. Where existing pole cannot accommodate additional electrical, internet, cable-tv, and other communication wire and/or equipment installation;

2. Where there is no existing pole on road crossing or intersection and where existing pole and/or attachments are difficult to transfer, hence necessary to erect a new pole; and

3. Where the existing pole is a sub-transmission pole or a pole in relation to, connected to or within the scope of Republic Act 11361 or the "Anti-Obstruction of Power Lines Act" ("RA 11361");

4. Other analogous cases determined by the City Engineer's Office;

In all instances, all utility companies must comply and abide by the provisions of RA 11361 with regard to protecting the integrity and reliability of the country's transmission, sub-transmission, and distribution systems by keeping the land beneath, the air spaces surrounding, and the area traversed by power lines clear of dangerous obstructions.

**SECTION 7. REMOVAL OF POLE WITH LEAST USAGE**
In all cases, the City Engineer's Office shall determine the pole of least usage and shall recommend the transfer of installations to a single dominant pole and immediately order the removal of the unused pole in accordance with this Ordinance, provided that the single dominant pole shall be upgraded to carry the weight of the attachments and meet the minimum elevation of utility company's lines, wires, and cables.

The dominant pole shall be identified by the City Engineer's Office based on pole capacity for allowable weight, tension, and height requirements of utilities for both existing and future attachments.

The utility companies affected shall be furnished with a notice of removal from the City Engineer's Office for the removal of their poles and the transfer of their installations to the dominant pole within seven (7) days from receipt of notice.

**SECTION 8. NONDISCRIMINATORY ACCESS**
The dominant pole owner shall provide nondiscriminatory access to all utility companies intending to attach their lines to the dominant pole and all related support structures and right of way in exchange for the payment of the pole attachment fee, subject to uniform applicable terms and conditions to all attaching entities, affiliates, or subsidiaries.

Provided, the dominant pole owner may validly deny in writing a pole attachment request by a utility company only by reason of recognized standards of safety, industry practices, or generally accepted engineering principles.

--- [Page 4] ---
Provided further, in case where the request for pole attachment has been denied or the attachment was removed due to an emergency, and thereafter the reason for such denial or removal ceases or additional capacity becomes available for a pole attachment, it shall be offered to the utility company whose request was denied or attachment was removed before offering it to other potential utility/telco companies, its affiliates or subsidiaries.

Any removal not due to an emergency shall require prior written notice to the attaching entity.

SECTION 9. NON-AVAILABLEITY OF UTILITY POLES, RELATED SUPPORT STRUCTURES AND RIGHTS OF WAY - In case utility poles, related support structures and/or rights of way are not available for a pole attachment, legitimate cable TV operators and telecommunications companies shall have the option to secure and use their own poles, provided the following requirements are complied with:

a. That the said utility poles, related support structures and rights of way shall be secured and used by legitimate cable TV operators and telecommunications companies at their own cost and responsibility;

b. That the said utility poles, related support structures and rights of way shall be secured and used with due reference to the recognized standards of safety, industry practices and generally accepted engineering principles;

c. That the said utility poles, related support structures and rights of way shall be subject to the same pole attachment rules and regulations imposed by this Ordinance.

Where in the future another utility company has pole requirements in the area to serve its customers, the One Pole Policy in Section 4 will apply.

SECTION 10. ELEVATION - All utility companies are hereby required to increase and adopt the minimum height for vertical clearance of all its power lines/wires, telecommunication, internet cables, and cable television cables to safe heights crossing, traversing and along the roadway of Davico City as recommended by the City Engineer's Office.

SECTION 11. TELCO BRACKET - Utility Companies that own poles within Davico City shall install brackets on their respective poles where telecommunication, internet and cable television cables are to be attached. These brackets shall be installed at a height of not less than twenty (20) feet from the ground and shall have a designated horizontal space assignment for telco cables. In the event the bracket is already full of cables, the pole owner is required to install another layer.

SECTION 12. POLE CLAMP - Pole owners shall install a pole clamp at nineteen (19) feet from the ground to hold telco drop wires/subscriber lines.

SECTION 13. REMOVAL OF UNUSED LINES AND CABLES - All telcos are required to remove their unused or inactive lines and cables. Pole owners shall remove their unused or inactive lines and cables whose ownership can no longer be traced.

All utility companies shall submit to the City Engineer's Office their Unused Telecommunication Lines and Cables Removal Plan for the year indicating important information such as pole number and/or coordinates, street name, barangay and target

completion date. Update of the activity shall be reported monthly to the City Engineer's Office and shall be submitted not later than every second Friday of the month for the previous month accomplishment. Dangling and unsafe lines and cables shall be prioritized and removed immediately.

SECTION 14. PHASING OUT OF TELCO COPPER LINES, WIRES, AND CABLES - Telcos are discouraged from using copper lines, wires and cables as pole attachments in order not to cause so much tension on utility company's poles. The Telcos may establish a complete phase out plan for copper lines as pole attachments.

SECTION 15. TELCO MAINTENANCE OR EXPANSION LOOP - There shall only be a maximum of two (2) telco maintenance or expansion loops per pole with a maximum diameter of 1.5 feet and a maximum of ten (10) turns.

SECTION 16. UPGRADING OF TELCO NETWORK ACCESS POINT (NAP) - To limit the number of Network Access Points (NAP) on poles, telcos may upgrade their existing Network Access Points (NAP) to cater more subscribers. The number of Network Access Points (NAP) boxes per pole shall be limited to four (4), unless otherwise allowed by the pole owner provided that there shall be no obstruction to the pole tags/pole numbers.

SECTION 17. COLOR CODING OF CABLES - All utility companies shall mark their cables according to their respective color assignments to facilitate easy identification particularly for removal, repairs & maintenance.

The City Engineer's Office, in consultation and coordination with the private and public utility companies, shall immediately assign color assignments for utility companies operating in the city.

SECTION 18. CABLE HANGER - Each Telco shall install a cable hanger along midspan with spacing of less than one (1) meter. Telco cables extending from pole to the next pole shall be placed in the cable hanger to avoid it from dangling.

SECTION 19. VERTICAL CLEARANCE LIMITER - The City Engineer's Office or the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) shall install vertical clearance limiters at strategic roads falling within their jurisdiction to prevent the passage of trucks with a gross height of more than the allowable gross height as provided under the Traffic Code of the City.

SECTION 20. ROAD HEIGHT - To maintain the elevation of the road thereby maintaining the vertical clearance of Telco cables and power lines from the ground, the City Engineer's Office or the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) shall scrape old asphalt overlay before application of new asphalt overlay in order to maintain the road elevation and standard vertical clearance requirement of Telco cables and power lines.

SECTION 21. CABLE SLIDER/COWLING ON TOP OF TRUCKS - Truck owners and/or operators are encouraged to install cable slider or cowling on top of the truck if the gross height of their vehicle reaches 4.5 meters to prevent dragging of low-lying lines, wires, and cables of utility companies.

**SECTION 22. PRIORITY AREAS** - Utility companies shall within six (6) months from the effectiveness of this Ordinance, prioritize the installation of pole attachment bracket and tension limiters on the following areas:

a. Along roads with low lying telco cables as determined by both power and telco companies;

b. Along roads frequently passed by big trucks as determined by the city traffic management team;

c. Along roads where main power and telco cables pass through as determined by the utility companies;

d. On major thoroughfares such as, but not limited to the following:

- Quiroto Avenue
- Maesayasay Avenue
- Quezon Boulevard
- Castillo Avenue
- Cabaguo Avenue
- Ma-a Road
- F. Torres Street
- J.P. Laurel Avenue
- Daviao-Cotabato Road
- Danao-Mahinika Highway (from Lanang to Lasang)
- Cabanatuan Road
- Indangan Road
- Bacaca Road
- MacArthur Highway
- Matina Aplaya Road
- Ecoland Drive
- Whole Downtown Area/Downtown Streets
- Toril Area
- Gallinan Area

**SECTION 23. WIRE INSTALLATION** - Except in areas where the underground cabling project has been implemented under City Ordinance No. 0177-14, there shall be a designated specific wire crossing locations in city roads and along major thoroughfares. Where intersections are followed by City Engineer's Office roads along rural areas, Telco Wire crossing along roads shall only be at intersections and shall always be perpendicular to the road. Diagonal and skewed wire crossing installations may only be allowed in the absence of a connecting pole allowing perpendicular crossing, provided that the wire shall only be attached to the nearest pole.

Laying of wires shall at all times make use of and be attached to poles along its path.

Electrical and Telco Drop wires or subscriber wires shall at all times be laid to a utility subscriber from the nearest utility pole.

Prior to removal or transfer of poles and dangling wires and cables as well as installation of new poles, power and main telco lines in their jurisdiction, there shall be coordination with the Barangay concerned.

**SECTION 24. COMPLIANCE PERIOD - All installations of utility companies are given three (3) years from the effectiveness of this Ordinance to be fully compliant with the following regulations:**

1. Elevate utility lines, wires and cables to at least EIGHTEEN (18) FEET (5.48 meters) high from the road;
2. Attach telephone, television cable systems, internet cable systems, telecommunication lines and cables on pole attachment brackets;
3. Remove the unused and inactive lines, wire and cables;
4. Correct all substandard installations; and
5. Comply with all other provisions of this Ordinance.

The foregoing period notwithstanding, low-lying utility lines posing danger to safety, security, and operation of businesses and communities shall be immediately elevated.

**SECTION 25. COMPLIANCE PLAN - Upon effectivity of this Ordinance, all utility companies shall submit to the City Engineer's Office their Ordinance Compliance Plan for the year as set out in Section 24 hereof, indicating therein important information such as pole number and/or pole coordinates, street name, barangay and target completion date. Update of the activity shall be reported monthly which will be submitted not later than every second Friday of the month for the previous month accomplishment.**

**SECTION 26. ADMINISTRATIVE PENALTIES - At the end of the Compliance Period under Section 24 hereof, all utility companies and their service contractors in violation of the provisions of this Ordinance shall immediately be served with a "NOTICE OF VIOLATION AND/OR COMPLIANCE ORDER" by the City Engineer's Office. Failure to comply with the "NOTICE OF VIOLATION AND/OR COMPLIANCE ORDER" issued by the City Engineer's Office within seven (7) days from receipt thereof shall be subject to an administrative penalty of THREE HUNDRED PESOS ($300.00) per pole per day until compliance. All Telco utilities shall be primarily liable for the payment of the administrative penalties as set forth herein. The manager of Telco utilities service contractors in violation of this Ordinance shall also be informed of the violation and shall be required to submit an explanation within seven (7) days from receipt of the "NOTICE OF VIOLATION AND/OR COMPLIANCE ORDER" issued by the City Engineer's Office. For this purpose, the utility companies shall maintain and make available the record of Telco utilities service contractors. Non-payment of administrative penalties and non-compliance of paragraph 4 as set forth under this Section shall be a ground for the non-renewal of business permits.**

**SECTION 27. IMPLEMENTATION, INSPECTION AND COMPLIANCE - A random audit and inspection shall be conducted by the City Engineer's Office to check on the Utility Company's compliance to this Ordinance. A "NOTICE OF VIOLATION"**

AND/OR COMPLIANCE ORDER" shall be served to the utility company for any failure to comply with the standards required by this Ordinance.

The City Engineer's Office shall, in coordination with power and telecommunication utilities in the city, conduct a year-end assessment and coordination meeting with the PLANNING AND DESIGN DIVISION of the District Engineering Offices and Regional Offices of the Department of Public Works and Highways.

The City Engineer's Office shall conduct the necessary meetings with power and telecommunication utilities in the city for the proper and efficient implementation of this Ordinance.

SECTION 28. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS - The City Engineer's Office shall formulate the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) for the approval and issuance of the City Mayor within sixty (60) days from the effectiveness of this Ordinance.

Failure of the concerned agencies to promulgate the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) shall not prevent the implementation of this Ordinance upon its effectiveness. This Ordinance shall be deemed in full force and effect even in the absence of an Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).

SECTION 29. APPROPRIATION - The City Government of Davao shall continuously appropriate sufficient funds for the implementation and enforcement of this Ordinance through an appropriation either in the City's annual budget or in any of its supplemental budget, as the case may be.

SECTION 30. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances, issuances, rules, and regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 31. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 32. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 1st day of December 2023, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
