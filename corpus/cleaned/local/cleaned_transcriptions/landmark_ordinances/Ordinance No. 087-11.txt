MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR SOCIAL PENSION FOR INDIGENT SENIOR CITIZENS

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement is entered into in Davao City, Philippines, this day of , 2011 and among:

THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT, a national agency existing under the laws of the Philippines with office address at R. Magaysay Avenue Corner D. Suazo St., represented by its Director Ester Acebedo-Versozá, and herein referred to as the "FIRST PARTY".

-and-

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO with principal office at San Pedro St., Davao City, represented herein by HON. Sara Z. Duterte, City Mayor and hereinafter referred to as the "SECOND PARTY".

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, it is the policy of the State to promote a just and dynamic social order that will ensure the prosperity and independence of the nation and free the people from poverty through policies that provide adequate social services, promote full employment, a rising standard of living, and an improved quality of life;

WHEREAS, in support of this State policy, Republic Act No. 9994 also known as the Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010 was passed with the objective to give full support to the improvement of the total well-being of the elderly and their full participation in society, considering that senior citizens are an integral part of Philippine society;

WHEREAS, the monthly Social Pension for indigent Senior Citizens of Five Hundred Pesos (P500) is a new benefit explicitly provided for under Republic Act No. 9994 as an additional government assistance;

WHEREAS, the SOCIAL PENSION for indigent senior citizens supports the achievement of the Millennium Development Goals on Eradicating Extreme Poverty and Hunger;

WHEREAS, the Social Pension for Indigent Senior Citizens is in line with our commitments to the Vienna and Madrid International Plan of Action on Aging, the Macau Declaration and Plan of Action on Aging for Asia and the Pacific, as well as the Shanghai Implementation Strategy, that seek to address the dependency needs of older persons, especially priority directions in the areas of social protection and security and alleviation of poverty in old age;

--- [Page 21] ---
WHEREAS, THE FIRST PARTY was tasked by Congress through RA 9994 to lead in the implementation of the monthly social pension for indigent senior citizens and to coordinate with other Government Agencies, Local Government Units, Non-Government Organizations, and/or private corporations to ensure effective implementation;
WHEREAS, pursuant to the eligibility requirements set forth in RA 9994 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and in accordance with the guidelines set forth in the DSWD Administrative Order No. 15 Series of 2010, the FIRST PARTY seeks to provide this additional government assistance to extremely poor senior citizens;
WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY has been identified through the DSWD's National Household Targeting System for Poverty Reduction (NHTS-PR) as a site/locality with indigent senior citizens eligible for the monthly stipend of P500;
WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY has agreed to be a partner of the DSWD in the implementation of the social pension for senior citizens to improve the economic well-being of its indigent elderly;
WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY is willing to acquire the additional manpower required in the implementation of the social pension through the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) and City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer (C/MSWDO);
WHEREAS, the parties of this contract have decided and hereby formally bind themselves to implement the social pension for indigent senior citizens mandated under RA 9994;
NOW THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE UNDERTAKING HEREINAFTER STATED, THE PARTIES HERETO DO HEREBY MUTUALLY AGREE AND DECLARE AS FOLLOWS:
ARTICLE 1
DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
A. THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT shall:
1. Provide the monthly stipend of P500 from its Department budget through quarterly disbursements to qualified social pensioners;
2. Identify indigent senior citizens based on existing data on poverty incidence and select the potential social beneficiaries using the NHTS-PR database;
3. Establish a Regional Social Pension Unit (RSPU) at the Field Office to serve as the focal team for social pension implementation at the regional level;
4. Provide technical assistance (i.e., case management, reporting system, grievance, etc.) to the LGU on the implementation of the social pension;
5. To provide timely and updated information to the city/municipal government, beneficiaries, and other stakeholders;
6. Conduct regular monitoring of the implementation of the Social Pension and submit report to oversight agencies such as COA, DBM as necessary;

--- [Page 22] ---
7. Review and recommend policy development and enhancement to further improve the implementation of the program;

8. Take the lead in the conduct of capacity-building and training at all levels;

9. Conduct regular consultations and dialogues with other stakeholders to facilitate feedback for policy and resource development.

B. The CITY/MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENT shall:

1. Ensure that the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) in its City or municipality is established, the OSCA Head and other necessary officials appointed to guarantee the efficient and effective implementation of the Social Pension;

2. Provide office space and other logistical support to the OSCA and C/MSWDIO such as but not limited to office supplies, equipment, and other paraphernalia for the period of implementation of the social pension;

3. Ensure the issuance of senior citizen's identification (ID) cards to all its elderly constituents;

4. Enjoin the Sangguniang Pangulong/Bayan to pass the necessary local legislation to support the Social Pension for Indigent Senior Citizens;

5. Conduct the necessary verification and home visits through the OSCA and C/MSWDIO to its indigent elderly constituents who have been identified as potential social pensioners;

6. Post, through the OSCA, the verified list of social pensioners in conspicuous or public places, and send a written notification to the confirmed beneficiaries and their families;

7. Assist and facilitate the enrollment of with the Land Bank of the Philippines (LBP);

8. Coordinate with the DSWD Field Office, through the OSCA in the facilitation of the release of the funds to the social pensioners;

9. Submit and certify a quarterly financial report by the OSCA to the RSPU to serve as basis for subsequent payments;

10. Facilitate and address, through the OSCA, complaints and request of indigent senior citizens in connection with the social pension;

11. Help promote and disseminate information on the Social Pension to different stakeholders;

12. Coordinate with the Local Civil Registrar and conduct regular monitoring of its list of Identified social pensioners;

--- [Page 23] ---
13. Submit and certify a monthly report of its social pensioners through the OSCA to the RSPU for updating and in case of death or any other change of status of the social pensioners;

14. Take the initiative to coordinate with other government agencies, Local Government Units, or private sector for fund augmentation of the Social Pension for Indigent Senior Citizens within their territorial jurisdiction.

ARTICLE II

EFFECTIVITY AND PERIOD OF AGREEMENT

This Agreement shall take effect upon signing of the parties and shall remain in full force and effect for three (3) years unless otherwise revoked by any of the parties.

ARTICLE III

AMENDMENTS

Any modification of this Agreement will not be valid except when there is a written concurrence of BOTH PARTIES.
