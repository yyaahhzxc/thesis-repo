**ORDINANCE NO. 0408-10**
**Series of 2010**

**AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE DAVAO CITY LITERACY COORDINATING COUNCIL, DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, APPROVING ITS JUDGES THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. **TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "DAVAO CITY LITERACY COORDINATING COUNCIL ORDINANCE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared a policy of the City Government of Davao to prioritize the elimination of illiteracy within its component barangays. As such, the City Government of Davao shall support all plans, programs, and activities leading to One Hundred Percent (100%) literacy rate in Davao City through the creation of a Local Literacy Coordinating Council;

SECTION 3. CREATION OF THE DAVAO CITY LITERACY COORDINATING COUNCIL - To carry out this declared policy, the Davao City Literacy Coordinating Council is hereby created, hereinafter referred to as the Council;

SECTION 4. COMPOSITION OF THE COUNCIL - The Council shall be composed of the following:

CHAIRPERSON - City Mayor
CO-CHAIRPERSON - Chairperson, Committee on Education, Sangguniang Panlungsod
MEMBERS - Schools Division Superintendent, Department of Education - Division of Davao City
- President, Liga ng mga Barangay
- President, Sangguniang Kabataan Federation
- City Director of the Department of Interior and Local Government - Davao City
- Regional Director, National Economic Development Authority Regional Office XII
- Chairman, Kapilyanang mga Brodkaster ng Pilipinas, Inc.
- President, Davao City Public School Teachers Association

SECTION 5. FUNCTIONS OF THE COUNCIL - The Council shall have the following functions:

1. To serve as the over-all advisory and coordinating body, providing policies and program directions for all literacy endeavors in the City;
2. To recommend strategies for development for the total eradication of illiteracy in the City;
3. To formulate measures on the establishments and maintenance of City Data Bank and information exchange and dissemination systems at the city and barangay levels;
4. To assist in identifying successful management schemes of literacy programs and adopt measures and boost research and development work to literacy work;
5. To recommend ways and means of raising funds in order to support the literacy programs/projects and activities;
6. To recommend measures on how to organize and strengthen support structures for literacy at the city and barangay levels;

7. To perform such other functions, as may be necessary, in furtherance of the purpose of this Ordinance;
The Council shall meet regularly once a month and may hold special meetings to consider urgent matters upon the call of the Chairperson.
SECTION 6. SECRETARIAT - The Council shall also have a secretariat which shall be composed of four (4) personnel. The Chairperson shall appoint two (2) personnel from the Office of the City Mayor as members of the secretariat, and the remaining two (2) members shall be appointed by the Co-Chairperson from any personnel in his/her office.
SECTION 7. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS - The members of the Council shall convene within sixty (60) days from the approval of this Ordinance to draft the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) relative to the full implementation of this Ordinance;
SECTION 8. FUNDING - The expenses for the implementation of this Ordinance shall be provided for in the Annual or Supplemental Budget of the City. Provided that funding thereof, may also be sourced from the Local School Board, if said Board so authorizes or allows the same by appropriate resolution;
SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any Section of the Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other Sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;
SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;
SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval and publication in a newspaper of general circulation;
ENACTED, March 30, 2010, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
