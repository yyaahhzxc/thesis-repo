**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN REHALE OF THE GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE NATIONAL STATISTICS OFFICE (NSO) CONCERNING THE PHILIPPINE CIVIL REGISTRY INFORMATION SYSTEM (PHILCRIS)**

Ord. No. 0181-11

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORIINAMENT AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE NATIONAL STATISTICS OFFICE (NSO) CONCERNING THE PHILIPPINE CIVIL REGISTRY INFORMATION SYSTEM";

SECTION 2: DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT. The Memorandum of Agreement refers to the instrument to be entered into by the City Mayor, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, and the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the National Statistics Office (NSO) concerning the Philippine Civil Registry Information System (PhilCRIIS);

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the National Statistics Office (NSO) concerning the Philippine Civil Registry Information System (PhilCRIIS);

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

EXACED. October 04, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

CERTIFIED CORRECT.
MARGARITO C. DELA CRUZ
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement is executed by and between:

THE NATIONAL STATISTICS OFFICE - OFFICE OF THE CIVIL REGISTRAR GENERAL with principal address at Solicor Bldg. I. R. Magaysay Blvd. St. Mesa, Manila, herein referred to as NBO, and represented by its Regional Director of Region XI, Davao City, HON. JAIME B. PALLER, Filipino, of legal age, married, and a resident of Davao City;

and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a municipal corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Philippines, herein represented by its City Mayor, SERRZ. DUTERTE, Filipino, of legal age, married, and a resident of Davao City, hereinafter referred to as THE LGU/INSTITUTION.

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the NBO has developed an updated software Philippine Civil Registry Information System (PhilCRIIS) to deliver civil registry information management in a new, more user friendly and efficient way;

WHEREAS, the Phil CRIIS is a software in Windows version of the Civil Registry Information System (CRIB DOS version). This software enables encoding of relevant information from civil registry documents, storage of vital data, query and retrieval of encoded records, printing and issuance of certified copies of encoded civil registry documents, generation of transmittal files, data management and maintenance of the local civil registry database;

WHEREAS, the use of PhilCRIIS will enable the Local Government Unit (LGU)/Institution to print preview and view directly into the new civil registry from;

WHEREAS, for and in consideration of the foregoing, the parties agree to the following terms and conditions:

USE OF THE SYSTEM

By entering into this agreement, the LGU/Institution is given authorization to use the PhilCRIIS free-of-charge in accordance with the rules and regulations set out for its purpose.

The LGU/Institution shall be given one Distribution CD containing the PhilCRIIS Software installer, user manual, and user forms together with the software parameters needed to use the application software on one computer.

The NBO grants license to the LGU/Institution on the use of the PhilCRIIS software on the condition that it is not disassembled, decomplied or reengineered and the Distribution CD not sold, lent or shared to entities other than the implementing institution. It is understood that intellectual property

--- [Page 5] ---
rights for the PhiCRIS software belongs to the NSO

RESPONSIBILITY OF THE NSO

Provide the complete set of parameters required to install the PhiCRIS software;

Provide the user manual and softcopy of the user forms needed by the LGU/institution.

Provide technical support to the LGU/institution subject to the provision of Warranties of NSO. Technical support includes, but not limited to, assistance in the installation of the PhiCRIS software, troubleshooting PhiCRIS software and database problems, and provision of software patches and upgrades to the PhiCRIS software. Support does not include troubleshooting computer hardware problems and installing and configuring operating systems.

Provide revisions and/or upgrades of the PhiCRIS software, free to those who entered into this agreement. In case of damage to the damaged Distribution CD, NSO will replace said CD upon a clear showing that the damage was either due to a defect attributable to the NSO (product defect) or arising from the regular and authorized use of the PhiCRIS software by the LGU/institution.

RESPONSIBILITY OF THE LGU/Institution

Provide all relevant computer hardware and software environment, including protection from viruses, necessary to be able to use the PhiCRIS software.

Designate staff that will undergo the training on the use of the system conducted by the NSO. Training fees shall be accounted for the LGU/institution.

Send a copy of encoded civil registry records to the NSO Provincial Statistics Office within the first 10 days of the reference month.

Coordinate closely with the NSO Provincial Statistics Office on the proper operation and use of the system;

Ensure the integrity of the System by, among other things, providing ample security against duplication of the Distribution CD, and unauthorized use of the System.

WARRANTY

NSO software warranty extends only to the proper usage of the PhiCRIS software and the intention for which the System was established. Any damage to the application software or Distribution CD, or inconvenience to the public arising from the unauthorized or unintended use of the System, shall be the responsibility of the NSO.

NSO will replace damaged or destroyed Distribution CDs provided they arise from authorized end-user.

NSO will repair or fix any problem in the application software provided they arise from the proper use and installation of the project;

The parties hereby warrant that neither it nor its representatives have offered any government officer any consideration or commission for the Agreement, nor has it or its representatives exerted or utilized a corrupt or unlawful influence to secure or solicit this Agreement for any consideration or commission. Should any consideration be provided by either party, the parties shall disclose the name of the person and the amount being paid. Any such provision shall not constitute a sufficient ground for cancellation of this Agreement, without prejudice to the filing of civil or criminal action under the Anti-Corruption and Corrupt Practices Act and other applicable laws against the parties and/or its representatives.

TERMINATION

This Agreement may be terminated as follows:

Voluntary termination by either party upon receipt of written notice 30 days prior to termination.

Involuntary termination, in case the LGU/institution committed the following:

Use or permit others to use PhilCRIS for commercial or profit-making purposes;

Make copies or translation of PhilCRIS and the related manuals without written permission/consent of NSO;

Make alterations in or disassemble any of the PhilCRIS programs or portions thereof, and in particular, erase, replace, modify or delete any screen formats displayed by the program during their execution;

Sublicense, assign or transfer the license, sell lease or grant any right on PhilCRIS to other parties;

Transmit PhilCRIS or portions thereof over telecommunications channels

UNDERTAKING

After termination of this MOA, the LGU/institution undertakes to destroy its copy of PhilCRIS together with all the subsequent copies it has made and that it shall no longer be authorized to receive any new copy of PhilCRIS which the NSO might develop or produce henceforth.

AMENDMENTS

No provision of this Memorandum of Agreement may be amended or modified except by written agreement signed by both parties.

EFFECTIVITY

This Memorandum of Agreement takes effect upon date of signing of both parties, provided that the implementation of the System may be done after undergoing training conducted by the NSO, and shall remain in force unless otherwise terminated or revoked by either party. Each party shall retain one signed copy of this MOA.
