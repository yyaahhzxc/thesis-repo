Ord. No. 076-19
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:
Section 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REPEALING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0171, SERIES OF 2009 KNOWN AS 'THE DAVAO CITY ANTI-LITTERING ORDINANCE OF 2009' FURTHER AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 98, SERIES OF 1956, OTHERWISE Known as the "ANTI LITTERING ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO" AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO. 105, SERIES OF 1986" BY REASON OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0361-10, SERIES OF 2010, OR THE DAVAO CITY ECOLOGICAL SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT ORDINANCE OF 2009".
Section 2. REPEALING CLAUSE - City Ordinance No. 0171-19, Series of 2009, known as "THE DAVAO CITY ANTI LITTERING ORDINANCE OF 2009" FURTHER AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 98, SERIES OF 1956, Otherwise known as the "ANTI LITTERING ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO" AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO. 105, SERIES OF 1986", is hereby repealed by reason of City Ordinance No. 0361-10, Series of 2010 or the Davao City Ecological Solid Waste Management Ordinance of 2009.
Section 3. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation.
ENACTED, October 15, 2019, by unanimous vote of the members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
