Series of 2008

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 108, SERIES OF 2000, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS, "THE PUBLIC UTILITY REROUTING SCHEME FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO"

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 108, SERIES OF 2000, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS, 'THE PUBLIC UTILITY REROUTING SCHEME FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO';

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT. Section 6 of Ordinance 108, Series of 2000, is hereby amended to include the adoption of the Malabog-Lasang-Bunawan Developmental Route, to read as follows:

MALABOG-LASANG-BUNAWAN

ORIGIN - Lasang-Malabog-Bunawan
Through - Davao-Agusan Road
Right Turn - Carlos P. Garcia Highway
Left Turn - Buangan Road
Through - Malabog River
Right Turn - Dapitan Avenue
Right Turn - Inigo Street
Right Turn - Cervantes Street
Left Turn - Lacson Street
Left Turn - Portaño Street
Right Turn - San Juan Street
Right Turn - Sta. Ana Avenue
Left Turn - Guerrero Street
Right Turn - Ponce Street
Through - Gomez Street
Right Turn - Balagtas Street
Through - Banpoy Street (Pancano)
Right Turn - Jacinto Extension
Right Turn - Quirino Avenue
Left Turn - J.P. Laurel Avenue
Right Turn - Malabago River
Through - Buangan Road
Right Turn - Carlos F. Garcia Highway
Left Turn - Davao Agusan Road
Back to Origin;

SECTION 3: APPROVAL. This Ordinance grants the request of Malabog-Lasang-Bunawan Drivers Association, Inc. [MALAEI[DAI] for the passage of a legislative measure amending Ordinance No. 108, Series of 2000, otherwise known as, "THE PUBLIC UTILITY RECOGNITI ON SCHEME FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO," in order to accommodate the inclusion of the Malabog-Lasang-Bunawan Developmental Route.

SECTION 4: EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval;

ENACTED, May 6, 2008, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod
