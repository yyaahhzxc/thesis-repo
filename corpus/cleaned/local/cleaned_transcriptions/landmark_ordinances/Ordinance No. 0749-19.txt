Ord. No. 0749-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0115-14, SERIES OF 2014, ENTITLED 'AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING POLICIES, GUIDELINES AND RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE OF THE PASALUBONG CENTER, A TOURISM ORIENTED PROJECT OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO'".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS - Section 3, No. 7; Section 8; Section 10, letters h to j; Section 11 and Section 13.

SECTION 3. POWER AND FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD

7. To establish as the need arises, in pursuance to this Ordinance, similar centers within the three (3) districts of the city, or satellite extensions within the premises of existing facilities owned or operated by the City,

SECTION 8. WHO MAY QUALIFY AS TENANTS: Any person or entity residing in Davao City who is engaged in producing, selling or distributing locally made delicacies and food products, goods or other souvenir items using domestic raw materials may apply as tenant/lessee of the Center.

National government agencies, Local Government Units (LGUs) and other entities enjoying privileges and incentives under the law may apply for permission to establish a similar space for the sale and promotion of their own products, services and advocates at the Center.

Further, in order to strengthen the Sisterhood Agreement entered into by the City Government of Davao with other LGUs, such LGU may be allowed to enter into a Lease or Memorandum of Agreement with the City Government to display for sale their locally made products at the Center.

SECTION 9. EVALUATION AND APPROVAL OF APPLICATION: The City Economic Enterprise, acting as the Center Management, shall evaluate the qualifications and the products of all interested lessee applicants based on criteria as prescribed under the Implementing Rules and Regulations to be adopted by the Board. The list of qualified applicants shall be published in the City Gazette. Subsequently, a Lease Contract or Memorandum of Agreement shall be executed between the City Government of Davao and the awardee.

SECTION 10. LOCALLY MADE PRODUCTS SHOWCASE:

h) Clothing, textiles and other related accessories;

i) Other locally made delicacies and food products, goods or other souvenir items using indigenous or domestic raw materials;

j) Any other items, goods, or services as may be approved by the Board.

SECTION 11. RENTAL RATE:

Provided however, that the Chief Executive, upon recommendation by the Board, may defer or waive the rental fee owing from those agencies or entities under Section 8 of this Ordinance. Provided further, that the said incentive is contained in a Memorandum of Agreement duly approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

**SECTION 12. AUTHORITY TO SIGN LEASE CONTRACTS**

The Pasalubong Center Board, through its Chairperson, or his/her authorized representative, is hereby authorized to sign all lease contracts for the use and occupancy of stalls or spaces in the Center.

**SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE**

All other city ordinances, Executive Orders, and rules and regulations, which are inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby deemed amended, repealed, or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**

If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY**

This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 23rd day of April, 2019, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
