**ORDINANCE NO. 056-13**
**Series of 2013**

**AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING ST. PETER CHAPELS TO OPERATE A CREMATORIUM AT THEIR FUNERAL PARLOR AS AN ADDITIONAL SERVICE OR BUSINESS LINE LOCATED AT BARANGAY PANACAN, BURAWAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY**

Be it ordered by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING ST. PETER CHAPELS TO OPERATE A CREMATORIUM AT THEIR FUNERAL PARLOR AS AN ADDITIONAL SERVICE OR BUSINESS LINE LOCATED AT BARANGAY PANACAN, BURAWAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY"

--- [Page 3] ---

Ord No. 056-13
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 458, No. 4 (ix) of the Local Government Code of 1991 gives the Sangguniang Panlindhang the power and duty to regulate the establishment, operation and maintenance of funeral parlors and the burial of cremation of the dead, subject to existing laws, rules and regulations;
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - St. Peter Chapels Inc. is hereby authorized to establish, as an additional service or business line, a Crematorium at their existing Funeral Homes, located at Barangay Panacan, Bunsuan District, this City.
SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other ordinances and executive orders, or parts thereof previously enacted which are inconsistent herewith are deemed amended, repealed or modified accordingly;
SECTION 5. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect,
SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City,
ENACTED, November 5, 2013, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.
