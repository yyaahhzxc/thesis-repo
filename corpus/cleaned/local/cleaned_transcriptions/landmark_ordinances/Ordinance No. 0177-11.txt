Series of 2011
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0132-11, SERIES OF 2011, ENTITLED, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON HOUSING CONSTRUCTION UNDER THE RESETTLEMENT ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (R.A. No. 8954) BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY AND THE NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY (NHA) FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ONE HUNDRED (100) HOUSING UNITS IN THE EXISTING LOS AMIGOS RELOCATION SITE, LOS AMIGOS, DAVAO CITY, BY AMENDING THE SAME FROM ONE HUNDRED (100) HOUSING UNITS TO TWO HUNDRED (200) HOUSING UNITS AND THE AMOUNT DUE FROM THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY FROM TEN MILLION PESOS (P10,000,000.00) TO TWENTY MILLION PESOS (P20,000,000.00).

Ord No. 0177-11

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0132-11, SERIES OF 2011, ENTITLED, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON HOUSING CONSTRUCTION UNDER THE RESETTLEMENT ASSISTANCE PROGRAM TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS (LGUs) OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF DAVAO (MMDA) OR THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO (CGD) AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO (CGD) OR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ONE HUNDRED (100) HOUSING UNITS IN THE EXISTING LOS AMIGOS RELOCATION SITE, LOS AMIGOS, DAVAO CITY, BY AMENDING THE SAME FROM ONE HUNDRED (100) HOUSING UNITS TO TWO HUNDRED (200) HOUSING UNITS AND THE AMOUNT INVOLVED FROM TEN MILLION PESOS [P10,000,000.00] TO TWENTY MILLION PESOS [P20,000,000.00].

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - The Memorandum of Agreement on Housing Construction under the Resettlement Assistance Program approved per Ordinance No. 0132-11, Series of 2011, is hereby amended to provide for the construction of housing units to be constructed under the Resettlement Assistance Program from One Hundred (100) Housing Units to Two Hundred (200) Housing Units and the amount involved from Ten Million Pesos [P10,000,000.00] to Twenty Million Pesos [P20,000,000.00] in the Los Amigos Relocation Site, Los Amigos, Davao City, owned by the City Government of Davao.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ERACTED, October 4, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

LOS AMIGOS HOUSING CONSTRUCTION PROJECT
(200 Units Emergency Housing Assistance)
Los Amigos, Dapit Dibatang, Davao City

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement made and entered into by and between:

The NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY, a government corporation organized and existing by virtue of Presidential Decree No. 751 as amended, with principal office at NHA Bldg., Elliptical Road, Diliman, Quezon City, Metro Manila represented in this act by its General Manager, ATTY. CHITO M. CRUZ, and hereinafter referred to as "NHA";

and

The LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY, a government entity organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with principal office at the City Hall, San Pedro St., Davao City, represented in this act by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, and hereinafter referred to as the "CITY";

WITNESSETH, that

WHEREAS, pursuant to Executive Order No. 90, the NHA is mandated to address the housing needs of low-income and marginalized families particularly the lowest 30% of the urban population and for this purpose, the NHA seeks the active participation of the local government unit, the private sector and all other concerned agencies, national as well as local;

WHEREAS, an unfortunate incident, the City of Davao experienced severe flooding last June 26, 2011 that resulted in a displacement of a lot of residents and damage to a substantial number of residential structures in certain parts of the CITY;

WHEREAS, the NHA, upon the direction of the Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council (HUDCO), committed to extend assistance to the CITY and allocated funds to finance the construction of Two Hundred (200) housing units in the existing Los Amigos Relocation Site, Los Amigos, Davao City, in the amount of Five Million Pesos (Peso) or PHP 5,000,000.00 to be sourced from the Resettlement Assistance Program Funds to finance the abovementioned project undertaking;

WHEREAS, the Sangguniang Panlungsod, through Resolution No. 01169- 11 and 01425-11 dated July 12, 2011 and October 10, 2011, respectively authorized the City Mayor to enter into a Memorandum of Agreement for the construction of Two Hundred (200) housing units in the existing Los Amigos Relocation Site, Los Amigos, Davao City.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto agree as follows;

--- [Page 4] ---
**ARTICLE I THE PROJECT**

The agreement refers to the construction of Two Hundred (200) housing units in the existing Los Amigos Relocation Site, Los Amigos, Davao City, registered under the name of the City Government of Davao covered by TCT No. T-146-2012080415 and registered with the Registry of Deeds of Davao City, herein attached as Annex "A" and made an integral part hereof.

1.1 The eligible housing units shall be awarded to the identified beneficiaries who were victims of the flood in the area declared under a state of calamity, certified by the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office Division of the City Planning Office and confirmed by the Davao City Local Housing Board.

1.3 Project implementation shall commence within fifteen (15) days from date of release of the first tranche of the TWENTY MILLION PESOS (Pip 20,000,000.00) in the amount of FIVE MILLION PESOS (Pip 5,000,000.00) and duly received by the CITY.

**ARTICLE II PROJECT BENEFICIARIES**

2.1 Only beneficiaries who are duly endorsed and declared under Section 1.1 are entitled to and be awarded the housing units under this Agreement.

2.2 The rights of the project beneficiaries to individually acquire their housing unit shall be secured through the execution of a Conditional Contract to Sell (CCS) by and between the CITY and the beneficiaries;

2.3 Disposition of units, cost recovery and estate management shall be subject to restriction on disposition of lands prescribed under Article IV Section 14 of RA 7279.

**ARTICLE III PROJECT FINANCING AND FUND MANAGEMENT**

3.1 The housing construction cost of TWENTY MILLION PESOS (Pip 20,000,000.00) will be funded by NHA to be sourced from the Resettlement Assistance Program Funds, which will be released in four tranches, to wit:

First Tranche - Pip 3,000 M.
Second Tranche - Pip 5,000 M.
Third Tranche - Pip 5,000 M.
Fourth Tranche - Pip 5,000 M.
TOTAL - Pip 20,000 M.

3.2 The amount released and utilized by the CITY for the construction of the Two Hundred (200) units shall be recovered by NHA from the CITY within a maximum period of thirty (30) years at 6% per annum. Repayment of the total funds disbursed by the CITY, except not less than one (1) year from date of release of the funds. Failure on the part of the CITY to settle the scheduled quarterly amortization shall be covered by a delinquency interest of 1% of the amount due each month in accordance with the existing policy of NHA.

--- [Page 5] ---
**ARTICLE IV ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

4.1 The NHA shall:

4.1.1 Release to the CITY the amount of TWENTY MILLION PESOS (Phip 20,000,000.00) in four tranches with an initial amount of FIVE MILLION PESOS (Phip 5,000.00 M.) to correspond to the cost of fifty (50) housing units. Succeeding releases will be upon completion of the said units but not later than two (2) months for each release.

4.1.2 Provide technical assistance in beneficiary selection, relocation procedures, and other areas where assistance is required.

4.1.3 Receive liquidation report duly verified by the resident auditor of the CITY in accordance with existing government accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

4.1.4 Monitor the progress of the project.

4.2 The CITY shall:

4.2.1 Provide the land where the housing units shall be constructed.

4.2.2 Provide the cost of labor component of the construction.

4.2.3 Implement the Project in accordance with the approved project proposal.

4.2.4 Repay the NHA such amount as may be reflected in the quarterly amortization schedule prepared by NHA, copy of which is hereto attached as Annex "B."

4.2.5 Collect the monthly amortization from the project beneficiaries in accordance with the terms and conditions provided for under the Individual Contract to Sell.

4.2.6 Cause the issuance of transfer of ownership thereon to the beneficiaries only upon full payment of the housing unit.

4.2.7 Undertake estate management functions and disposition and sales documentation of housing units, and collection of fees from the identified beneficiaries and other material activities.

4.2.8 During the course of the Project implementation, the CITY shall submit to the NHA monthly financial and physical accomplishment/status reports. Upon completion of the Project, a Project Completion Report and an Inspection Report shall be submitted in accordance with existing rules and regulations.

4.2.9 Prepare liquidation report duly verified by the resident auditor of the CITY. The Certificate of Completion and all other necessary supporting documents to be submitted to NHA immediately upon completion of the Project shall be in accordance with existing government accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

--- [Page 6] ---
V. OTHER PROVISIONS

5.1 The parties represent that they have the requisite power and capacity to enter into this Agreement and to perform their obligations and undertakings according to the terms and conditions stated herein.

5.2 Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as establishing the relationship of employer and the employee among the parties or any of their respective staff. All parties shall at all times be personally and directly liable for the acts of all personnel under its employ and shall hold each party free and harmless from any and all claims or liabilities arising from the acts or conduct of its employees.

VI. EFFECTIVITY

6.1 This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect within thirty (30) days from the date of perfection of this Agreement.

6.2 This Memorandum of Agreement shall automatically expire upon full payment by the CITY to NHA of the fifty percent (50%) of the cost of housing units, full collection of project receivables, and settlement of all project obligations.
