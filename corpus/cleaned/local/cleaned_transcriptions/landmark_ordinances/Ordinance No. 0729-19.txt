ORDINANCE NO. 0729-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A TRANSFORMATIVE
PROGRAM FOR CHILDREN AT RISK

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 07-29-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "TRANSFORMATIVE PROGRAM FOR CHILDREN AT RISK ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao recognizes its obligations in securing the protection and welfare of every child in the City. It is one of the primary roles of the City Government to provide necessary intervention programs and other related services to Children-at-risk in relation to safeguarding their human rights, development, and well-being, and in relation to City Ordinance No. 0264-12, Series of 2012.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:

1. Children-at-risk (CAR) - refer to children who are vulnerable to and at risk of behaving in a way that can harm themselves or others, or vulnerable and at risk of living, pushed, and exploited to come into conflict with the law because of personal, family, and social circumstances, such as, but not limited to, the following:

a. Being abused by any person through sexual, physical, psychological, mental, economic or any other means and the parents or guardians refuse, unwilling or unable to provide protection for the child;

b. Being exploited sexually or economically;

c. Being abandoned or neglected after diligent search and inquiry where the parents or guardian cannot be found;

d. Coming from a dysfunctional or broken family or being without a parent or guardian;

e. Being an out of school youth;

f. Being street children;

g. Being a member of a gang;

h. Living in a community with a high level of criminality or drug abuse;

i. Living in a situation where armed-conflict is prevalent;

j. Committing vicious crimes.

2. Child - refers to a person under eighteen (18) years old and those above 18 but unable to protect themselves and/or physically or mentally incapable of doing so;

3. Intervention - refers to a series of activities which are designed to address issues that cause a child to come into conflict with the law or tend to make the vulnerability in committing offenses. It may take the form of an individualized treatment program which may include counseling, skills training, education, and other activities that will enhance the psychological, emotional, and psycho-social well-being of a child;

4. Parent Education - the various forms and alternative means of providing parents with information, skills, and support systems to assist them in their needs as their children's primary caregivers and educators. These include public and private parent education programs linked to center homes and media-based children education programs;

5. Transformative Approach - are programs and interventions that create opportunities for CAR to transform as an individual, create enabling community conducive for children, develop community partnership and volunteerism, enhance responsible parenting, and embrace community oriented programs;

--- [Page 3] ---
6. Children at Risk Transformative Community-Based Center (CARTC) — is the anchor of all community-based and psychosocial care and support program for CAR which will operate 24/7; it will serve as their transformative and transitional home.

SECTION 4. PREVENTION AND TRANSFORMATIVE PROGRAMS

1. The City Government of Davao through the City Social Services and Development Office shall provide programs such as child-parent education in order for the latter to enhance their capacity to raise their children in a healthy, loving, and nurturing environment and in a manner that is consistent with the child's normal growth and development.

2. Community-based centers for children-at-risk, under the program of Children at Risk Transformative Community-Based Center (CARTC), shall be established to provide immediate, appropriate, and holistic response to cases involving CAR. It will temporarily shelter CAR while providing psychological care and support with the involvement of the parents, Barangay Council for the Protection of Children, Non-Government Organizations, City Social Services and Development Office, and other support groups. With this end, it shall ensure a child-friendly, gender sensitive, and community-based approach that will prevent revictimization and risk of habitually committing offenses that may cause a child to be in conflict with the law.

SECTION 5. INTERVENTION — When the parties or competent authorities led by the City Social Services and Development Office decide upon thorough assessment that CAR must undergo the process of intervention, the following shall be taken into consideration:

a. Upon thorough assessment, it was found out that the family or relatives are the ones putting the child at risk;

b. Child is sexually or economically exploited;

c. When the child is physically, mentally, and emotionally abused;

d. When the child is abandoned or neglected;

e. There is frequency of petty offenses;

f. Poses a threat to the safety of the community.

SECTION 6. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS — An amount necessary to establish the facility and to carry out the initial implementation of this Ordinance shall be charged against the current year's appropriation of the Office of the City Mayor.

The next and succeeding funds needed for its continued operation shall be included in the Annual or Supplemental Budget of the City of Davao.

SECTION 7. TECHNICAL WORKING GROUP — For purposes of this Ordinance, a Technical Working Group shall be formed, composed of the following:

a. City Mayor's Office

b. Committee on Peace and Public Safety

c. Committee on Women, Children, and Family Relations

d. Davao City Police Office

e. Legal Aid Society of Davao

f. City Social Services and Development Office

g. Council for the Welfare of Children

h. Davao City Special Office for Children's Concern

i. Department of Education-Davao City

j. Representative from Non-Government Organization

**SECTION 8. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS -** The Technical Working Group shall, within thirty (30) days after the Ordinance has been approved, draft the necessary Implementing Rules and Regulations for the approval of the City Mayor of Davao.

**SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE -** Any ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY -** This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days upon approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation.

**ENACTED, on the 19th day of March 2019, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.**
