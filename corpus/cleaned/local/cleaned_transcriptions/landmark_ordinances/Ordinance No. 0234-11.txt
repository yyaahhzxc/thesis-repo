**ORDINANCE NO. 0234-11**
**Series of 2011**

**AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING NEW RATES FOR THE USE OF THE DAVAO CITY RECREATION CENTER (DCRC) AND OTHER AMENDMENTS TO THE PROVISIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 514, SERIES OF 1985**

Be it ordained by the Sanguaniang Panlungsoqd of Davao City in session assembled that:

--- [Page 2] ---
**Ord. No. 0234-11**

**SECTION 1.**
**TITLE:** This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING NEW RATES FOR THE USE OF THE DAVAO CITY RECREATION CENTER (DCRC) AND OTHER AMENDMENTS TO THE PROVISIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 514, SERIES OF 1985."

**SECTION 2.**
**DECLARATION OF POLICY** - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"For effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants, pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the City Government, and in this connection, shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

**SECTION 3.**
**AMENDMENTS** - The following are the new rates, as well as other proposed amendments to the provisions of Section 4 of Ordinance No. 514, Series of 1985, which shall now read as follows:

**SECTION 4.**
**RATES FOR THE USE OF THE CENTER** - The following rental rates shall be imposed for the use of the Almendras Gym - Davao City Recreation Center, to wit:

A. BOXING PROMOTIONS
B. STAGE AND HALL PRESENTATIONS
C. BASKETBALL
1. Basketball Leagues
2. Basketball Practices
D. FUNCTIONS
E. FACILITIES
1. Aircon
2. Game Clock
3. Plastic Chairs

The City Mayor may grant/give discounts or special rates to government agencies, charitable institutions or other institutions or persons deemed entitled thereto, for the use of the gym."

SECTION 4. CENTER'S FUND - The City Treasurer shall receive, to the credit of the center, all moneys and payments for rentals, and shall provide an account thereof:

SECTION 5. DISPOSITION OF INCOME - The revenue generated in the operation of the center shall be disposed of according to the following priorities:
A. 1st - to pay its contractual and statutory obligations to meet its essential current administrative operative expenses;
B. 2nd - to allocate the residue as a reserve, to be disbursed and utilized exclusively for the expansion and improvement of the center's facilities;

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, December 12, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
