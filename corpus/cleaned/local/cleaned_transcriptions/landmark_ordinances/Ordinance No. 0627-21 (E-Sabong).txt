ORDINANCE NO. 0627-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING ELECTRONIC SABONG (E-SABONG) FRANCHISEES TO BROADCAST LIVE AND DATA STREAMED COCKFIGHTING ACTIVITIES AND DERBIES AT DESIGNATED ESTABLISHMENTS IN DAVAO CITY AND TO ESTABLISH, OPERATE AND MAINTAIN OFF-SITE COCKPIT BETTING STATIONS WITHIN THE CITY OF DAVAO

Page 2 of 5
Ord. No. 0627-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING ELECTRONIC SABONG (E-SABONG) FRANCHISEES TO BROADCAST LIVE AND DATA STREAMED COCKFIGHTING ACTIVITIES AND DERBIES AT DESIGNATED ESTABLISHMENTS IN DAVAO CITY AND TO ESTABLISH, OPERATE AND MAINTAIN OFF-SITE COCKTIT BETTING STATIONS WITHIN THE CITY OF DAVAO".

It shall also be known by its short title "E-Sabong Ordinance of 2021".

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS.

a) E-Sabong is defined as the online/remote or off-site wagering/betting on live cockfighting matches, events, and/or activities streamed or broadcasted live from cockfight arena/s licensed or authorized by the Local Government Units having jurisdiction thereof;

b) Live Streaming - transmit or receive live video and audio coverage of an event over the internet (or through any other medium of communication);

c) Data Streaming - a sequence of digitally encoded coherent signals used to transmit or receive information that is in the process of being transmitted;

d) Cockfighting Derbies - is a blood sport between two cocks, or gamecocks, held in a ring called cockpit;

e) Cockpit - a place where cockfights are held;

f) Franchisee - an authorization granted by the Local Government of Davao City under Sections 2, 3, 129 of the Local Government Code, to an individual or group enabling them to carry out E-Sabong activities;

g) Sultada - is one round of cockfighting;

h) Plasada - the percentage of bets paid to the franchisee;

i) Admission Fees - any amount paid or consideration given for entrance, seats, tables, reserved or otherwise, and other similar accommodations, including charges for the use of facilities therein, irrespective of whether or not an admission ticket is issued in exchange for such admission fee or consideration.

SECTION 3. NATURE AND TERM OF OPERATION - Subject to the provisions of applicable laws, rules and regulations, a franchisee may broadcast live and data stream cockfighting activities and derbies at the appropriate designated establishments within the City of Davao provided that the cockfighting subject to broadcast shall only be done in a licensed cockpit, unless otherwise allowed in some other area, including the acceptance of bets through electronic means within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City.

The Commercial establishments where the franchisee intends to broadcast and live stream cockfighting activities and to establish, operate and maintain off-site cockpit betting stations shall be located and operated only within appropriate areas as prescribed by zoning laws and applicable ordinances. No establishment shall be operated and maintained within the radius of two hundred (200) linear meters from any existing school buildings, churches and public buildings.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY OF THE OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR. The franchisee shall apply and secure the required business permit to operate from the Office of the Mayor and the latter shall have the authority to require compliance of all existing laws, ordinances, rules and regulations similarly imposed to all business establishment and impose conditions thereof as are expressly or impliedly necessary for the protection of the welfare and safety of all persons going or transacting in said commercial establishments. The franchisee may appeal from such decision from the City Mayor's Office to the City Council for a Resolution of No Objection to operate and finally to be referred back to the City Mayor's Office for appropriate action;

SECTION 5. PROHIBITION OF SALE, LEASE, USURPT AND TRANSFER OF AUTHORITY TO BROADCAST. The authority to broadcast electronic sabong is non-transferable in character. As such, the franchisee shall not sell, assign, lease, grant thereunder to any person, natural or juridical, other than itself, nor engage with any corporation or individual without prior notice and approval of the Sanggunian Panglungsod.

SECTION 6. MANDATORY PAYMENT OF TAXES AND FEES PROVISIONS. The franchisee shall be liable to pay all taxes and other fees prescribed by the City of Davao. The assessment shall be without prejudice to the subsequent imposition as maybe adopted or hereafter required by law or ordinance. Failure on the part of the grantee to promptly and regularly comply with said tax obligations shall be a ground for the automatic revocation or cancellation of its permit or authority to broadcast.

SECTION 7. FRANCHISE TAX. There is hereby imposed a franchise tax, as provided for in Article V, Section 41, of Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, consisting of 87% of 1/2 of the gross receipts derived by the franchisee from the plada sa made through electronic means within Banday City), or One Hundred Thousand Pesos (Phip100,000.00) per event per day whichever is higher.

SECTION 8. AMUSEMENT TAX. There is hereby levied a tax to be collected from the operator of the local cockpit where the cockfighting activity is actually being conducted, or designated areas where such fights are broadcasted at a rate of 20% based on the gross receipts from admission fees. Further, an amusement tax is also hereby imposed to be collected from the franchisee of the actual cockfighting being broadcasted in an e-sabong system, the amount of Three Hundred Fifty Pesos (Phip350.00) per sultada.

SECTION 9. OBLIGATION OF THE FRANCHISEE. The herein franchises must regularly furnish the City Mayor or his duly authorized representative, an updated list of all its authorized agents/coordinators acting in furtherance of their operations and all accounts they are using in receiving bets from its players. They shall likewise ensure that all account funding and withdrawal shall be done only through legitimate channels, such as over-the-counter and online banking, e-Wallet systems, and through other legitimate money transfer services.

Further, the franchisee must also ensure that all its authorized agents must secure their own corresponding Mayor's Permit to operate and that no agent shall be allowed to commence operation without the necessary permit.

SECTION 10. TIME AND PLACE OF PAYMENT.—The franchise tax shall be paid to the City Treasurer's Office once in the first twenty (20) days of January or in quarterly installments within the first twenty (20) days of January, April, July and October of each year.

Further, the amusement tax herein imposed shall be due and payable to the City Treasurer's Office within the first ten (10) days of the month next following that for which they are due.

SECTION 11. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS.—In order to effectively and efficiently implement this Ordinance, including the collection of the taxes imposed herein, the Office of the City Mayor shall promulgate the necessary rules and regulations.

SECTION 12. OTHER GAMBLING ACTIVITIES.—All gambling games of any kind shall at any time be prohibited at the premises of the off-site business operation. The managers or lessee/s of such off-site business and the violators of these provisions shall be prosecuted and held criminally liable under the provisions of existing laws.

SECTION 13. PROHIBITIONS AGAINST MINORS.—No person less than twenty-one (21) years of age shall be allowed inside the premises of offsite cockfighting stations at any time. The operator, manager, or licensee shall be responsible for the strict compliance with these rules and any violation thereof is punishable by suspension or cancellation of the authority to broadcast without prejudice to criminal prosecution under existing ordinances or laws.

SECTION 14. ANTI-TRAFFIC MEASURES.—The Office of the City Mayor shall adopt such measure to prevent the use of fraudulent means or manipulation in all aspects of cockfighting. Persons employing fraudulent means or manipulation shall be prosecuted in accordance with the penal laws as may be warranted by the prevailing circumstances. Any breach and/or circumvention of any of the conditions set in this Ordinance shall be a cause for the suspension or revocation of the Issued e-Sabong Gaming License.

SECTION 15. IT DEVELOPMENT.—The operator must coordinate with the City Information Technology Center and create measures wherein the taxes under Section 8 will be automatically collected and paid upon the placement of the bet of the online better.

SECTION 16. NATIONAL GOVERNMENT.—The condition of the authority to broadcast shall at all times be subject to existing laws, rules and regulations issued by the National Government.

SECTION 17. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.—If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 18. EFFECTIVITY.—This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
