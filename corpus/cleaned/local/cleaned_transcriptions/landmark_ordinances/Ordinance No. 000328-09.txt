**ARTICLE I**

**TITLE OF THE ORDINANCE**

**SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE** This Ordinance shall be known as "The Tourism Development Code of Davao City".

**ARTICLE II**

**DECLARATION OF POLICY**

**SECTION 2 DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES** This ordinance shall observe the global code of ethics for tourism and shall be guided by the following principles: balance conservation, development, ecological integrity, and family-oriented while it protects its cultural heritage through the promotion of sustainable tourism.

To consciously promote gender sensitivity to all local & foreign tourists.

As a policy, no tourism-related establishment shall conduct business within the City of Davao without first securing a Mayor's permit or license.

Convention centers typically offer enough floor area to accommodate several thousand attendees and rent space for meetings such as corporate conferences, industry trade shows, entertainments, and concert halls;

ARTICLE IV
CHAPTER I - GENERAL ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS

SECTION 4 - CITY TOURISM OPERATIONS OFFICE - The City Tourism Operations Office shall recommend to the City Mayor for approval applications for business permit of any person, partnership, or corporation or other entities to maintain, operate, or engage in the business of, or related to tourism within the territorial limits of Davao City;

The objectives of these provisions are:

a) Insure the proper development of the tourist and hospitality industries bearing in mind the interest of the community and the economic implications of such;

b) To establish the City Tourism Operations Office as the body to administer and carry out the formal requirements of the ordinance and be responsible to the City Mayor in monitoring and making necessary recommendations to the Council for policy adjustment to meet the ongoing needs of the public and the industry;

c) To provide a flexible system as may be practicable and legal for the administration of this ordinance;

d) To provide a legislative framework which will minimize any violation of this ordinance.

SECTION 5 - REGISTRATION - No person, natural or juridical, shall keep, manage or operate any building, edifice or premises or a completely independent part thereof, for the purpose of engaging in the business of, or

CHAPTER III - REGULATION AND SUPERVISION OF THE OPERATION OF ACCOMMODATION ESTABLISHMENTS

SECTION 26 - SIGNBOARD - All tourism-oriented and tourism-related businesses shall keep a signboard displayed in a conspicuous place outside the establishment showing the name and style of the establishment;

SECTION 27 - GUEST REGISTRATION - No person shall occupy or be permitted to occupy a room in an accommodation establishment unless the personal circumstances and other particulars of the guests have been entered in the establishment registry book or card;

All motels shall keep a Motel Registry Book;

The motel management is required to maintain a separate logbook for the plate number of the vehicles or cars used by its customers or guests coming into or leaving the motels;

Section 28 - ENTRY OF PARTICULARS - Managers, operators, or their assistants shall require every guest seeking accommodation in the establishment to furnish the following minimum particulars to be entered in the register or card:

1. Full Name;
2. Particulars of number, date and place of issue of Residence Certificate, Class "A" or driver's license, or any identification card, passport or other travel documents;
3. The place of origin and permanent regular known address;
4. The probable duration of stay and intended destination;
5. Occupation and place of employment;
6. Nationality;
7. The hour and date of arrival.

The office, from time to time and as the need arises, may require other particulars to be entered in the register or card.

SECTION 29 - TIME OF ENTRY OF PARTICULARS - Before the guest is allowed to occupy the room, such particulars prescribed in the preceding section shall be entered forthwith in the register or card by such guest, or if he is unable to write, by the establishment keeper, manager, or clerk, and in either case, the entry shall be signed by the guest or, should he be unable with authenticated by his right thumb print. SECTION 31 - REGISTRATION OF VISITORS INVITED BY ESTABLISHMENT GUESTS - Any visitor accompanied by a guest to stay in his room shall register in a separate book for visitors, in which shall be entered the name, address, and other particulars mentioned in Section 28 hereof.

MINORS TO BE ACCOMPANYED BY PARENT OR GUARDIAN - No accommodation establishment shall accept for lodging or accommodation any person below eighteen (18) years of age unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.

SECTION 32 - ENTRY OF DATE AND TIME OF DEPARTURE - Upon the departure of the guest, the establishment keeper/ manager/ operator or assistant shall enter or cause to be entered in the register, Book the date and hour of their departure.

SECTION 33 - FALSE ENTRY IN ESTABLISHMENT REGISTER BOOK OR CARD - No manager shall enter or cause to be entered in the register or card any information, or particulars, which he knows or by the exercise of reasonable diligence, has ascertained such information to be false.

SECTION 34 - ROOM RATES - In addition to daily rates, motels may likewise impose wash-up rates. No guest who desires to be accommodated on a daily rate basis shall be refused. The rental rates shall be posted prominently at the reception counter and/or at the door of each room.

SECTION 35 - GUEST SUFFERING FROM DANGEROUS AND/OR CONTAGIOUS DISEASE - The establishment keeper, manager or operator shall immediately report to the City Health Office any guest, tenant, or member of the staff who is found to be suffering from a dangerous and/ or infectious disease. Thereupon, he shall carry out all precautions and directions given by the City Health Office to prevent the spread of the disease.

SECTION 37 - UNANNOUCED DISAPPEARANCE OR DEATH IN THE ESTABLISHMENT - The establishment keeper, manager or operator shall immediately report to the nearest police station the unannounced disappearance or death of any person booked in the accommodation establishment. To accomplish this end, they shall immediately report to the nearest police station the presence in the premises of any such undesirable person.

CHAPTER IV - ANNUAL INSPECTION

SECTION 40 - PERIODIC INSPECTION OF THE ESTABLISHMENT - When necessary and upon valid reason, the Office will make an inspection of the establishment and its immediate premises for the purpose of determining whether they are being kept or managed in a manner compatible with the standard set by this Ordinance for the class in which it has been registered and licensed, or whether it is being kept in an unclean and unsanitary condition.

SECTION 41 - HOURS OF INSPECTION - Inspections for registration may be done during the day but ordinary inspections to check whether or not they are complying with laws and ordinances may be done any time. Surprise inspections, even during night time, should be done especially in cases where there are reports of illegal activities.

SECTION 42 - ACCESS OF INSPECTION TEAM TO RECORDS AND PREMISES - The Inspection team shall have access to the registry, book or charts, the establishment building and all parts thereof, the facilities for cooking, and the right to interview any employees of the establishment and investigate any fact, conditions or matters which may be necessary to determine any violation to aid in arriving at just conclusion.

SECTION 43 - DEFECTS AND DEFICIENCIES FOUND DURING THE INSPECTION
- When certain defects or deficiencies have been found in the course of the periodic inspection, the Office shall give the establishment keeper, manager, or operator a period of two (2) weeks from the date of written notice to rectify the defects or deficiencies.

SECTION 44 - EXTENSION OF THE PERIOD
- The Office may, for good cause, extend the period within which to remedy the defects or deficiencies noted, but in no case shall the period of extension granted be more than one (1) month.

SECTION 45 - PENALTY FOR FAILURE TO REMEDY THE DEFECTS
- If the establishment management fails to remedy the defects or deficiencies noted, the Office shall recommend to the City Mayor the imposition of One Thousand Pesos (P. 1,000.00) but in no case shall the fine exceed the sum of Five Thousand Pesos (P. 5,000.00). Provided, that where the maximum fine impossible under this section has been reached and the establishment management has continued to fail to rectify the defects or deficiencies noted, the City Mayor upon recommendation of the Office, shall revoke or suspend the Certificate of Registration of the establishment for such period as may be justified under the circumstances.

SECTION 46 - UPGRAADING OR DOWNGRADING OF AN ACCOMMODATION ESTABLISHMENT
- A establishment may be upgraded or downgraded from one class to another as the facts may warrant.

SECTION 47 - UPGRAADING OF AN ACCOMMODATION ESTABLISHMENT
- Any establishment that has upgraded its facilities and services to, among others, comply with the requirements of a higher class, may apply for an upgrading to such higher class. This shall be as described in Section 15 hereof, shall evaluate the recommendations of the Office for its appropriate action.

SECTION 48 - DOWNGRADING OF AN ACCOMMODATION ESTABLISHMENT
- Where after due investigation it has been established that an establishment is being kept or managed in a manner not conforming with the standards set by this Ordinance for an establishment in which it is registered and permitted, the Office shall give notice within which time the establishment shall correct the established deficiencies. Upon the correction thereof being granted, the Office shall cancel the registration of the establishment from the classification and place it in a lower classification, or if warranted, exclude the same from the establishment classification. Further details shall be specified in the Implementing Rules and Regulations for this Code.

CHAPTER V - OPERATIONS AND MANAGEMENT OF ACCOMMODATION
ESTABLISHMENTS

SECTION 51 - MAINTENANCE AND HOUSEKEEPING - Maintenance of all
sections of the accommodation establishment shall be of acceptable standard
and shall be on a continuing basis, taking into consideration the quality of
materials used as well as its upkeep. Housekeeping shall be regularly
maintained in all areas of the accommodation establishment. Solid Waste
Management as prescribed in R.A. 9003 shall be strictly observed. Sanitation
measures shall be adopted in accordance with the standards prescribed on
sanitation, and the regulations of the City Health Office.

SECTION 52 - LIFEGUARD AND SECURITY - All accommodation
establishments with swimming pools should provide the services of a sufficient
number of well-trained lifeguards duly accredited either by the Philippine
National Red Cross (PNRC), the Water Life Saving Association of the Philippines,
or any recognized organization training or promoting safety objectives and
adequate security wherever there are guests.

SECTION 53 - FIRE FIGHTING FACILITIES - Fire-fighting facilities shall be
provided in accordance with the Fire Code of the Philippines.

SECTION 54 - ENTRANCE & EXIT GATES - Separate entrance and exit gates
shall be provided for motels.

SECTION 55 - DESIGNATED AREA - The owner of the accommodation
establishments or his duly authorized representative shall designate a portion
of the premises to be used exclusively for loading and unloading of guests, unless
otherwise designated elsewhere by rules and regulations specific to the area.

SECTION 56 - PROHIBITION ON PEDDLING - Accommodation establishment
owners shall prohibit ambulant vendors from peddling their wares within the
establishment in order to provide their guest a certain degree of privacy to
enable them to relax and enjoy their stay.

**SECTION 63 - ANNUAL INSPECTION OF THE RESORT**
The Office shall conduct annual inspection to determine whether the resorts are being kept and/or managed in accordance with the standards set by this Ordinance for the class for which they are registered and licensed.

**SECTION 64 - HOURS OF INSPECTION**
Inspections for registration may be done during the day but ordinary inspections to check whether or not they are complying with laws and ordinances may be done any time. Surprise inspections, even during night time, should be done especially in case where there are reports of illegal activities.

**SECTION 65 - ACCESS OF INSPECTORS TO RECORDS AND PREMISES**
The Office or its Inspection team shall have access to the resort's records and premises, the right to copy them, and to the person in charge, and investigate any fact, condition or matter which may be necessary to determine compliance with, or in aid of the enforcement of any provision of this Ordinance or circulars issued by the Office.

**SECTION 66 - DEFECTS AND DEFICIENCIES FOUND DURING THE INSPECTION**
When certain defects or deficiencies have been found in the course of the periodic inspection, the Office shall give the resort operator or manager a period of two (2) weeks from the date of written notice to rectify the defects or deficiencies.

**SECTION 57 - EXTENSION OF THE PERIOD**
The Office may, for good cause, extend the period within which to remedy the defects or deficiencies noted but in no case shall the period of extension granted be more than one (1) month.

**SECTION 58 - PENALTY FOR FAILURE TO REMEDY THE DEFECTS**

**SECTION 59 - UPGRAADING OR DOWNGRADING OF A RESORT**
A resort may be demoted or promoted from one classification to another class as the facts may warrant.

**SECTION 70 - DOWNGRADING OF A RESORT**
Where after due investigation it has been established that a resort is being kept or managed in a manner not conforming with the standards set by this Ordinance for a resort in which it is registered and permitted, the Office shall give notice within which to comply with the standards. If the resort fails to comply within the period granted, the Office shall cancel the registration of the resort from the classification and place it in a lower classification, if or warranted, exclude the same from the resort classification. Further details shall be specified in the Implementing Rules and Regulations for this Ordinance.

Regular and hygienic garbage disposal system shall be maintained.
