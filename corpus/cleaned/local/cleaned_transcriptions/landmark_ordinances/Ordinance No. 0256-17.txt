Ord No. 0256-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING AND PROVIDING AN ELECTRONIC OR ONLINE SYSTEM OF PAYMENT AS AN ALTERNATIVE MODE OF PAYMENT OF TAXES, FEES, CHARGES AND OTHER IMPOSITIONS DUE TO THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City of Davao recognizes the adoption of electronic transactions as an alternative to the traditional over-the-counter transactions to minimize human interaction between the transacting public and the local government employees in order to avoid graft and corruption and to reduce bureaucratic red tape. The use of modern technology will result to a more efficient collection of taxes, fees, charges and other impositions. It will also promote convenience to the transacting public and will expedite transactions in the government;

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY OF THE CITY MAYOR - In the absence of an internet-based electronic payment facility and gateway developed by the City Government of Davao, the City Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, any Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into with the Authorized Government Depository Bank (ADDB) that has an available internet-based electronic payment facility and gateway which will be used by the City Government in relation to the implementation of the electronic or online payment system;

Provided further that, it must be stated in every MOA that the use of the said payment facility and gateway by the City Government is free of charge and non-exclusive;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY OF THE CITY TREASURER - The Office of the City Treasurer is hereby authorized to recognize and accept payments made electronically, online or with the authorized banks or non-banks as an alternative to the traditional over-the-counter payments of the city cashiers;

SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE - Ordinances, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao;

ENACTED, August 22, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.
