ORDINANCE NO. 132
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING AND ESTABLISHMENT, CLASSIFICATION, ADMINISTRATION AND OPERATIONS OF GOVERNMENT OWNED/OPERATED PUBLIC MARKETS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO, CHARTERED UNDER AS THE "MARKET CODE OF DAVAO CITY".
WHEREAS, it is a declared policy of the City of Davao to provide within its territorial jurisdiction varied business opportunities to all kinds and classes of legitimate businessmen, spur the economy of the City and promote and enhance the economic well-being of its constituents;
WHEREAS, there is an urgent need to update the Tax Ordinances and/or other Ordinances and implementing rules and regulations of the City of Davao, governing operation of government owned/operated public markets in this City by making it more responsive to the declared policy and demand of time;
WHEREAS, there are on-going and future development projects for the improvement and expansion of existing City owned markets, some are made possible through World Bank a Loan and said projects are all designed to promote and increase the capabilities of the City Government to deliver the desired public services, ensure the safety, comfort and concern of vendors and the buying public as well;
WHEREAS, the attainment of the above envisioned objectives can only be achieved, if the administration Continued on Page -7-

CHAPTER I

GENERAL PROVISION

SECTION 1. - Coverage - This Ordinance shall govern
the establishment, classification, administration and opera-
tion of government-owned and/or operated public markets
in the City of Davao and the imposition and collection of
reasonable market rentals, fees and charges for occupancy
and use thereof;

SECTION 2. - Authority to Establish Public Markets -
The City of Davao, pursuant to existing law and legal
procedures, shall have the authority to establish, main-
tain and operate public markets within its territorial
jurisdiction;

**CHAPTER II
SUPERVISION AND CONTROL**

SECTION 5. - City Administrator - The City Administrator shall exercise direct and immediate supervision, administration and control over government-owned/operated public markets and the personnel thereof, including those whose duties concern the maintenance, upkeep, peace and order of the market premises except collectors in accordance with laws, local ordinances and other rules and regulations pertinent thereto.

SECTION 6. - Market Superintendent/Market Operations Officer - There shall be a Market Superintendent and Market Operations Officer who under the direct supervision and control of the City Administrator shall have supervision and control over government-owned/operated public markets as well as those that may still be established. SEC. 15. - Time for payment/penalties for delinquency - The monthly rentals for market stalls shall become due and payable within the first twenty (20) days of the month. of land belonging to the market. The cleaning of the
