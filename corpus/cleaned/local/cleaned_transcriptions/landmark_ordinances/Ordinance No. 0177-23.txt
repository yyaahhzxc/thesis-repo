Ord. No. 0177-23

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "APPROVING THE SUPPLEMENTARY ADJUSTMENTS TO THE SUBMITTED COMPREHENSIVE LAND USE PLAN (CLUP) 2019-2024" OR CONSISTENCY OF GEOGRAPHIC DATA AND FIGURES VOLUME I OF THE PLANNING PROCESS, PARTITIONED INTO THE FOLLOWING SECTIONS, TO WIT: A) EXISTING LAND USE PLAN; B) LAND USE TREND; C) DEVELOPMENT CONSTRAINTS; D) AVAILABLE LAND SUPPLY FOR DEVELOPMENT; E) ADDITIONAL AREA REQUIREMENT; F) PROPOSED LAND AND WATER USE, INCLUDING THE ADOPTION OF THE STRATEGIC AGRICULTURE AND FISHERIES DEVELOPMENT ZONE (SAFDZ) AND NETWORK OF PROTECTED AREAS FOR THE AGRICULTURAL AREAS WITHIN THE NPAAAD AND THE NETWORK OF PROTECTED AREAS FOR THE FISHERIES DEVELOPMENT ZONE (NPFZ), AND IN VOLUME II - THE COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE PARTICULARLY THE FOLLOWING SECTIONS, TO WIT: G) SECTION 12.3 REGULATIONS IN AGRICULTURAL ZONE: "AGRICULTURAL AREAS WITHIN THE NPAAAD AND SAFDZ SHALL NOT BE SUBJECT FOR RECLASSIFICATION"; H) SECTION 12.3.1 PROTECTION AGRICULTURE SUB-ZONE: "ALSO CONSIDERED IN THIS SUB-ZONE ARE IRRIGATED AND IRRIGABLE LANDS"

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - This Ordinance is enacted pursuant to the provisions of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, particularly Sections 447, 448, and 458 a.(27-9). "Authorizing the City, through the Sangguniang Panlungsod, to adopt a Zoning Ordinance subject to the provisions of existing laws" and in accordance with related laws such as, but not limited to, Commonwealth Act 141, Republic Act 8550, otherwise known as the Fisheries Code, as amended by Republic Act 10654, PD 705 or the Forestry Reform Code, PD 1067 or the Water Code, PD 1096 or the National Building Code, Republic Act 1012, otherwise known as National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act, Republic Act 9723 or the Climatic Change Act and Executive Order No. 72, and other related existing laws.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 21st day of March 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
