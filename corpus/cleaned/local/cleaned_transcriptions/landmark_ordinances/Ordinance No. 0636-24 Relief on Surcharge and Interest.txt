AN ORDINANCE GRANTING RELIEF ON SURCHARGES AND INTERESTS ON LOCAL TAX ON TRANSFER OF REAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP IN SUPPORT OF THE EXTENSION OF THE ESTATE TAX AMNESTY PROGRAM OF THE NATIONAL GOVERNMENT IN VIEW OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11956

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 2
Ord. No. 0636-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING RELIEF ON SURCHARGES AND INTERESTS ON LOCAL TAX ON TRANSFER OF REAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP IN SUPPORT OF THE EXTENSION OF THE ESTATE TAX AMNESTY PROGRAM OF THE NATIONAL GOVERNMENT IN VIEW OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11956".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall apply exclusively to local transfer taxes imposed upon transfer of real properties by succession, either testate or intestate, of decedent/s who died on or before 31 May 2022.

SECTION 3. GRANT OF RELIEF OR WAIVER OF SURCHARGE AND INTEREST - There shall be a waiver of the corresponding surcharge and interest imposed on late payment of Tax on Transfer of Real Property Ownership for transfer of estates of decedents who died on or before 31 May 2022.

SECTION 4. WHO MAY AVALI - The legal heir/s, administrator or executor may avail the relief upon full payment of the Tax on Transfer of Real Property Ownership within the period as provided for in this Ordinance.

SECTION 5. PERIOD OF AVAILMENT - The relief may be availed upon settlement of the Tax on Transfer of Real Property Ownership upon the effectiveness of this Ordinance until 14 June 2025.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other city Ordinances, Executive Orders and Rules and Regulations, which are inconsistent with this Ordinance, are hereby deemed amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days upon approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation in the City of Davao.

ENACTED, September 17, 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
