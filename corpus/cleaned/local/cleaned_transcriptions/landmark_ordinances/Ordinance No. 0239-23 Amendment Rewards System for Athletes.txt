AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0102-22, SERIES OF 2022, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING A REWARD SYSTEM FOR ATHLETES AND COACHES WHO BRING PRESTIGE AND HONOR TO THE CITY OF DAVAO IN THEIR OUTSTANDING PERFORMANCE IN REGIONAL, NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL YOUTH SPORTS COMPETITIONS" AND "TOURNAMENTS", APPROPRIATING FUNDS IN RELATION THERETO AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES"

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0102-22, SERIES OF 2022, ENTITLED 'AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING A REWARD SYSTEM FOR ATHLETES AND COACHES WHO BRING PRESTIGE AND HONOR TO THE CITY OF DAVAO IN THEIR OUTSTANDING PERFORMANCE IN REGIONAL, NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL YOUTH AND SPORTS COMPETITIONS AND TOURNAMENTS, APPROPRIATING FUNDS IN RELATION THERETO AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES"

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS - The following Sections of Ordinance No. 102-22, Series of 2022, are hereby amended:

Section 4 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:

"Definition of Terms. As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean as follows:
"Athlete" shall be construed as any person trained or skilled in sports, or games usually requiring skill, physical strength, agility, or stamina, who must be a Filipino citizen and a resident of the City of Davao. The term shall include all members of the official delegation of the City of Davao participating in an event or competition. Their names should be included in the official list of players prepared by the City Sports Office in coordination with the Philippine Olympic Committee, the Games and Amusements Board and the Philippine Sports Commission.

xxx*

Section 5 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:

"Section 5. Beneficiaries of Davao City Sports Excellence Incentives Program. All duly recognized athletes, trainers, and coaches from Davao City who are winners in any Regional, National or International competition or tournament sanctioned/acknowledged by the Philippine Olympic Committee, Games and Amusements Board and the Philippine Sports Commission, as the case may be, are hereby qualified under the Davao City Sports Excellence Incentives Program under the following guidelines:

a) An athlete, trainer or coach should represent Davao City as their LGU in the tournament or competition;

b) Only a one-time incentive per event/competition shall be given to the entitled athlete or coach regardless of the number of medals won; the incentive given is equivalent to the highest award won."

Section 6 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:

"Section 6. Regional Sports Competition. Regional sports competition shall refer to regular major competitions within the Davao Region and Mindanao-wide competitions such as the Betang Pinoy and other similar competitions, sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission and the Games and Amusements Board".

**Section 7 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:**

"Section 7. National Sports Competition. National sports competition shall refer to regular major national competitions such as: Philippine National Games, PhilsPilas National Games and Batang Pinoy National Championships held every year or any event sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission and the Games and Amusement Board."

**Section 8 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:**

"Section 8. International Sports Competition. International sports competition shall refer to regular major international competitions such as: Olympic Games, Asian Games, Southeast Asian (SEA) Games, Youth Olympic Games, Paralympic Games, Asian Para Games, Asian Games and Martial Arts Games, Asian Beach Games, and Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) Para Games; ASEAN school games, ARAFURA games and the BIMPPTT-EAGA games or any event sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission, the Games and Amusements Board and/or the Philippine Olympic Committee."

Section 9.2 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:
"Section 9.2. Only those competitions or tournaments sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission for regional and national sports competition, the Games and Amusements Board and/or the Philippine Olympic Committee for international sports competition, shall be qualified for the cash incentive program of this Ordinance."

Section 11.1 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:
"Section 11.1. For Athletes, the athlete:
a. Must be a Filipino citizen.
b. Must be a bona-fide resident of the City of Davao for at least two (2) years and must have duly represented the City of Davao in the sports competitions participated.
c. Must present a recommendatory letter or certification that the athlete was invited to join the event/competition.
d. Must be awarded with an honor for the City of Davao because of the athlete's achievements.
e. Must win a gold, silver and bronze medal in any sports event or competition recognized and sanctioned by the Philippine Sports Commission, the Games and Amusements Board, the Philippine Olympic Committee and the International Olympic Committee."

Section 12 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be read as follows:

Section 12. Documentary Requirements. The concerned athletes and their coaches shall also submit the following documents to the Davao City Sports Office to qualify for the cash award and incentive as contained in this Ordinance:

Section 12.1. For Athletes:
a. Certified true copy of Birth Certificate issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA);
b. Barangay Certification of residency or voter's affidavit or ID of the winner, or if in case a minor, voter's affidavit or ID of parents or guardians, showing that the athlete is a resident of Davao City;
c. Certificate of award, medal or trophy showing that the athlete has indeed won in the Regional, National or International competition/tournament;
d. Invitation Letter or Certificate inviting the athlete to join the competition/tournament that the athlete had won.

Section 12.2. For Coaches:
a. Certified true copy of Birth Certificate issued by Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA);
b. Barangay Certification of residency or voter's affidavit or ID showing that the coach is a resident of Davao City;
c. Certificate of award, medal or trophy showing that the Davao City athlete the coach has trained, has indeed won in the Regional, National or International competition/tournament;
d. Recommendatory letter or certification inviting the athlete that the coach had trained to join the competition/tournament that said athlete had won;
e. Certification from the City Sports Office that the Coach is the official coach and has personally trained or rendered service to the winning Davao City athlete or team. Such certification must be in writing.

Section 17 of Ordinance No. 0102-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended as follows:

"Section 17. Effectivity Clause. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its publication."

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - Should any provision of this Ordinance be declared by a court of competent jurisdiction to be illegal, unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not so affected, shall remain valid and effective.
