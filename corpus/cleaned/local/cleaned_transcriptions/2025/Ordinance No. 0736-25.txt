ORDINANCE NO. 0736-25
Series of 2025

AN ORDINANCE FOR THE PARTIAL TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC (HALF-LANE ONLY) OF THE JUNCTION OF ECOWEST DRIVE AND QUIPMO BOULEVARD GOING STRAIGHT PASSENGER JOHN ABUL COLLEGE, FROM THE JUNCTION OF QUIPMO BOULEVARD AND GUILLERMO E. TORRES STREET FOR NORTH BOUND AREA AND FROM THE JUNCTION OF GUILLERMO E. TORRES STREET AND QUIPMO BOULEVARD UP TO MACARTHUR HIGHWAY FOR WEST BOUND AREA ON FEBRUARY 1, 2025 FROM 10:30 AM UP TO 8:00 AM OF FEBRUARY 2, 2025 IN CONNECTION WITH THE NATIONWIDE ANNUAL 7-11 RUN SERIES DAVAO LEG

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE PARTIAL TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC (HALF-LANE ONLY) OF THE JUNCTION OF ECOWEST DRIVE AND QUIPMO BOULEVARD GOING STRAIGHT PASSING JOHN PAUL II COLLEGE UP TO THE JUNCTION OF GUILLERMO E. TORRES STREET FOR NORTH BOUND AREA AND FROM THE JUNCTION OF GUILLERMO E. TORRES STREET AND QUIPMO BOULEVARD UP TO MARTHUR HIGHWAY FOR WEST BOUND AREA FROM FEBRUARY 1, 2025 FROM 10:30 AM UP TO 8:00 AM OF FEBRUARY 2, 2025 IN CONNECTION WITH THE NATIONWIDE ANNUAL 7-11 RUN SERIES DAVAO LEG"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the request of Mr. Tomas E. Tan, Technical Race Coordinator-Davao Leg., there will be a temporary partial closure to vehicular traffic (half-lane only) of the junction of Ecowest Drive and QuiPMo Boulevard going straight passing John Paul II College up to the junction of QuiPMo Boulevard and Guillermo E. Torres Street for the North Bound Area and from junction of Guillermo E. Torres Street and QuiPMo Boulevard up to MARTHUR Highway for West Bound Area from February 1, 2025 at 10:30 am up to February 2, 2025 at 8:00 am in connection with the Nationwide Annual 7-11 Run Series Davao Leg.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, January 07, 2025, by unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

J. MELCHORD D. QUITAIN JR.
Vice Mayor
