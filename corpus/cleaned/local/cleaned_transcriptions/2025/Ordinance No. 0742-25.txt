Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "The Panigan-
Tamugan Sub-Watershed Ordinance of 2025."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The following are hereby declared
policies of the City:
a. To promote, improve, and safeguard the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed as the
main surface water source of the City's drinking water and is the largest
source of the City's surface water supply;
b. To effectively manage and protect the physical, biological, and socio-economic
attributes of the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed based on the assessment study
based on the masterplan as specified under Section 4 therein;
c. To ensure the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed's cleanliness, sustainable ecological balance, rich biodiversity, and improve the quality of life of the
community; and
d. To recognize that the watershed area is a recharged area for the City's
aquifers which are sources of the City's drinking water that must be protected,
conserved, maintained, and managed for the continued and full enjoyment of
the present and future generations.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For the purpose of this Ordinance, the
following terms are defined as follows:
a. "Critical Watershed" pertains to areas of land which show critical
degradation as to threaten their natural feature on which rainwater drains
from a river system to a common outlet such as a dam, lake or sea; or areas
where rain from a higher level of land flows down to a river, or stream then
to the Davao River, or where rainwater can drain as surface run-off via a specific
river or stream system to a common outlet point, which may be a dam, irrigation
system or a municipal water supply take-off point, or where a river or stream
discharges water into a larger river, stream or lake.
b. "Customary Laws" refer to a body of written and/or unwritten rules,
usages, customs and practices traditionally and continually recognized,
accepted and observed by respective ICCs/IPs;
c. "Environmentally Critical Areas (ECA)" refer to these areas declared by
law as national parks, watershed reserves, wildlife preserves and sanctuaries
and all others identified as such by Presidential Proclamation 2146,
specifically in Items B, 1-11;
d. "Groundwater" refers to subsurface water that occurs beneath a water table
in soils and rocks, or in geological formations.
e. "Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs)" refer to groups of people or homogenous societies identified under Republic
Act No. 8577, or The Indigenous Peoples Rights Act (IPRA) of 1997 generally
consisting of the Obu Manuvu and other tribes within the PTW.

f. "Motorized equipment" refers to any machine that uses or is activated by a motor, engine, or other power source. For the purpose of this Ordinance, motorized equipment shall refer to chainsaws, drilling machines, sawmills or pumping stations.

g. "Open dump" refers to a disposal area wherein the solid wastes indiscriminately thrown or disposed of without due planning and consideration for environmental and health standards (R.A. No. 9003).

h. "Protected Areas" refer to identified portions of land and water set aside by reasons of their unique physical and biological significance, managed to enhance biological diversity and protected against destructive human exploitation.

i. "PUSAKA" is the traditional Obu Manuve practice of sanctifying entities, living or non-living, as "precious" or "sacred" because it has become priceless from the perspective of a family, clan, or tribe.

j. "Settlement" refers to formal and informal communities or built-up residential areas where people prefer to live in and land areas classified, zoned, or converted for current or future residential or housing development purposes, including subsidized housing zones for the homeless and underprivileged.

k. "Surface Water" refers to waters (e.g., rivers, lakes, bays) which are open to the atmosphere and subject to surface runoff.

l. "Tourism and Recreational Activities" refer to activities people do for fun and enjoyment which shall refer to trekking or hiking, swimming, camping, mountain biking, running, canoeing, kayaking, rafting, tubing, for recreational purposes.

m. "Watershed Areas" are areas of land which rainwater drains via a river system to a common outlet such as dam, lake or the sea; an area where rain from the highest point flows down to a river and ultimately to the Davao Gulf or a tributary of the Davao Gulf; or an area where surface run-off via a specific stream of river system to a common outlet point, which may be a dam, irrigation system or municipal water supply take-off point, or where the stream or river discharges water into a larger river, stream or lake.

n. "Watershed Management" is the process of guiding and organizing uses of land and other resources in a watershed to provide desired goals and services without adversely affecting the watershed areas and water resources.

o. "Watershed Management Council (WMC)" refers to a body which issues policies and guidelines and sets the direction of collaboration in gathering, processing and evaluating information on watershed areas in Davao City and develops plans and strategies towards ensuring its health and sustainability.

SECTION 4. PANIGAN-TAMUGAN WATERSHED STUDY - The Panigan-Tamugan Watershed Assessment Study conducted by Ove Arup Partners Hong Kong Ltd. and their local partner, the Galerio Environmental Consultancy on August 17, 2023, provided a comprehensive assessment and management plan for the long-term protection of the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed and formulated similar studies to be conducted in the future for the preservation and protection of the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed.

SECTION 6. PANIGAN-TAMUGAN WATERSHED MANAGEMENT - The primary responsibility for the management, protection, maintenance, and preservation of the PTW shall be under the Watershed Management Council (WMC) pursuant to Ordinance No. 0310-07 or the Watershed Code.

SECTION 7. PROHIBITED ACTS - The prohibitions enumerated in the Watershed Code are deemed included herein, in addition to the specific prohibitions listed, to wit:

a. Land conversion to whatever classifications including, but not limited to, hotels, resorts, tourism, and recreational grounds;

b. Water drilling except those for the water system development conducted by Davao City Water District (DCWD) and other water operators as authorized/permited by NWRB and other governing agencies;

c. Open burning, open dumping of solid waste material is prohibited based on R.A. No. 9003 of 2000;

--- [Page 9] ---
Page 6 of 8
Ord. No. 0742-25

d. Aerial spray of prohibited pesticides in the Panigan-Tanugu Watershed (PTW);

e. Use of motorized cutting equipment, except for safety, disaster response, and disaster prevention

f. Exploitation of quarry resources and commercial sand and gravel resources that may affect the quality and quantity of water in the PTW

g. Use of pit privy toilets or toilets without septic tanks to prevent the fecal coliform count;

h. No new settlement, whether residential or commercial, shall be allowed within the boundary of the Panigan - Tanugu Watershed area, specifically in areas determined by the Water Management Council (WMC) where activities may affect the quality of water before the weir, except in Rural Settlement Areas after the weir.

SECTION 8. REGULATION - Existing households living within the PTW will remain in their area but no expansion or new construction is allowed unless only for repair and maintenance.

SECTION 9. SPECIFIC REGULATION. INDIGENOUS CULTURAL COMMUNITIES - Existing ethnic and cultural settlement/infrastructure and resettlement of Indigenous Peoples (IPs) communities shall be governed and regulated in accordance with their existing customary laws.

As to the identified PUSAKA areas within the PTW, its watershed conservation, forest protection, biodiversity and wildlife preservation, and ancestral land preservation shall be managed and regulated by the Obu-Manuvi tribe or as provided under R.A. No. 8371 or The Indigenous Peoples Rights Act of 1997 or other existing laws.

SECTION 10. MONITORING OF PTW - All activities within the Panigan-Tanugu Watershed shall be recorded and monitored by the Watershed Management Council (WMC) including the Watershed Multi-partite Monitoring Team (WMMT) and Barangay Watershed Management Council (BWMC) pursuant to Ordinance No. 0310-07 or the Watershed Code, together with other concerned national and local agencies. To ensure compliance with all the relevant provisions of this Ordinance, the following shall:

(a) Beginning 2025 and every 5 years thereafter, the WMC shall meet on the first Wednesday of March to review the contents of this Ordinance to see whether there is a need to revise, remove, add new provisions to make the Ordinance responsive to the needs of the city.

(b) On or before March 15, 2025, the WMC shall secure from DCWD and/or other resources an annual inventory of the water supply of the Panigan Tanugu River surface water to give proposals on how to improve and enhance the water supply to the WMC on the 3rd Tuesday of April.

(c) The WMC shall give the Mayor a copy of their report on or before March 15.

SECTION 11. FUNDING - The City shall provide sufficient funds amounting to Five Million Pesos ($5,000,000) for the purpose, as recommended by the WMC to the City Council, through appropriation either in the Annual Budget or in any Supplemental Budget, as the case may be.

**SECTION 12. PENALTIES -** Any violation of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be punished with imprisonment not exceeding one (1) year or a fine not exceeding Five Thousand Pesos (PHP5,000.00) or both at the discretion of the Court. If the violation is committed by a corporation, trust, firm, partnership, association or any other juridical person, the penalty shall be imposed upon the President, General Manager, and other guilty officer or officers of such corporation, trust, firm, partnership, association or entity; provided that in addition, a penalty of Five Thousand (5,000.00) Pesos shall be imposed on such corporation, trust, firm, partnership or association violating any provision of this Ordinance.

**SECTION 13. NO CONTEST PROVISION -** Any person cited for violation of this Ordinance who does not wish to contest and who is willing to pay voluntarily the fine imposed upon him/her or in case of inability to pay the fine is willing to render community service, such person shall be allowed to pay the said fine with the City Treasurer through the City Clerk of Courts. The City Treasurer may issue a warrant for the arrest of any person who qualifies and successfully avails of the "No Contest Provision" shall be exempted from liability under this Ordinance.

**SECTION 14. ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTION -** For purposes of enforcing the provisions of this Ordinance, any local government official or employee concerned who fails to comply with the rules and regulations promulgated relative to this Ordinance may be held administratively liable under existing rules and regulations.

**SECTION 15. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS -** The City Mayor, through an Executive Order, shall create and constitute a joint Executive Legislative Committee to formulate the Implementing Rules and Regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this Ordinance. Such rules and regulations shall take effect immediately after publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao.

**SECTION 16. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** In the event that any word, article, sentence, clause or paragraph of this Ordinance is declared invalid for any reason, other provisions thereof shall not be affected by such declaration and shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 17. REPEALING CLAUSE -** All ordinances as well as pertinent rules and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed and amended accordingly.

**SECTION 18. EFFECTIVITY -** This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after completion of its publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

**ENACTED, January 28, 2025, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
