Ordinance No. 0779-25
Series of 2025

An Ordinance Approving the Request of Oliver John R. Tiu, President of Ateneo de Davao Blue Knight Association (ABKA), for Full Exemption from the Payment of Amusement Tax on the Proceeds of Their Fundraising Event: The 2025 ABKA Cup Golf Tournament (ACGT), to be held on April 26, 2025, at APO Golf and Country Club, Talomo, This City

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0779-25

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF OLIVER JOHN R. TIU, PRESIDENT OF ATENEO DE DAVAO BLUE KNIGHT ASSOCIATION (ABKA), FOR FULL EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUNDRAISING EVENT:THE 2025 ABKA CUP GOLF TOURNAMENT (ANT), TO BE HELD ON APRIL 26, 2025, AT APO GOLF AND COUNTRY CLUB, TALOMO, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16, Chapter II, of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare".

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Section 66 (b) of the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City provides that, "(b) where the admission fees are collected in connection with the holding of operas, concerts, dramas, recitals, painting and art exhibitions, flower shows, musical programs, literary and oratorical presentations except pop rock, or similar concert not intended for profit and furthermore, not including film exhibition".

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - The aforementioned exemption shall be based on the system of computation adopted by the Business Tax and License Division of the City Treasurer's Office and their projected income statement, herein enumerated:

| NO. OF PARTICIPANTS | REGISTRATION FEE | TOTAL SALES | 5% TAX |
|---------------------|----------------|-------------|---------|
| 120                 | PHP 3,500.00    | PHP 420,000.00 | PHP 21,000.00 |

SECTION 5. TRANSMITTAL - The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is hereby directed to transmit a copy of the said Ordinance to the Executive Department immediately upon its approval.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 18th day of March 2025, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
