Ord. No. 0735-25

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF SAN PEDRO, C.M. RECTO, C. BANGUY, IÑIGO, PELAYO, BOLTON AND RIZAL STREETS ON JANUARY 11, 2025 FROM 9:00 P.M. UNTIL JANUARY 13, 2025, 7:59 A.M. FOR SET-UP (PARTIAL ROAD CLOSURE ONLY) AND ON JANUARY 12, 2025 (FULL ROAD CLOSURE). OF THE FULL STRETCH OF THE COASTAL ROAD FROM THE INTERSECTION OF SAN PEDRO STREET TO TULU DRIVE EXIT (TO BE USED FOR PARKING), AND FULL ROAD CLOSURE ALONG THE STREETS OF SAN PEDRO, C.M. RECTO, C. BANGUY, IÑIGO, PELAYO, BOLTON, RIZAL, ILUSTRE, BONIFACIO AND PALMA GIL FROM 8:00 A.M. UP TO 8:00 P.M. (FULL ROAD CLOSURE), IN CONNECTION WITH THE UPCOMING NATIONWIDE PEACE RALLY OF THE IGLESIA NI CRISTO (INC)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs xxx".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the upcoming nationwide Peace Rally, Rev. Macles V. Ivasco, District Manager, Iglesia ni Cristo, General Malvar, Davao City, requested for the temporary road closure along the streets on the dates and time specified herein.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 07th day of January 2025, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod there being a quorum.
