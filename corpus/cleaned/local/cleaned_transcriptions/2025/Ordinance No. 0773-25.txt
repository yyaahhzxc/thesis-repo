Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the herein Ordinance No. 0773-25, Series of 2025, informing you that the enactment of the same is well within the powers of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
The Sangguniang Panlungsod has the inherent power to enact, modify, amend or repeal an ordinance. Thus, in the case of Yakazi Torres Manufacturing, Inc. v. Court of Appeals, the Supreme Court held that:
The legislative power has been described generally as the power to make, alter, and repeal laws. The authority to amend, change, or modify a law is thus part of such legislative power.
IN VIEW OF THE FOREGOING PREMISES, it is recommended that the Ordinance be approved.

Ord. No. 0773-25
Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0582-24, SERIES OF 2024, PARTICULARLY THE LOCATION OF THE PROPERTY COVERED BY TCT NO. T-453112, INVOLVING A 320-SQUARE METER LOT, FROM LOT 11, BLOCK 34 TO LOT 15, BLOCK 12, BO. OBRERO, THIS CITY."
SECTION 2. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the amendment of Ordinance No. 0582-24, Series of 2024, relative to the correction of the location of a property located in Bo. Obreiro, this City.
SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - This Ordinance refers to the amendment of SP Resolution No. 02977-24 with its corresponding Ordinance No. 0582-24, both series of 2024, particularly Section (3) - Coverage, by changing the location of the property covered by TCT No. T-453112, which involves a 320-square meter lot, from Lot 11, Block 34 to Lot 15, Block 12.
SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
ENACTED, on the 11th day of March 2025, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
