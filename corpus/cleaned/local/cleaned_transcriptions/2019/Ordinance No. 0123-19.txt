ORDINANCE NO. 0123-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0171-14, SERIES OF 2014, OR "THE DAVAO CITY ADOPT-A-DOG ORDINANCE OF 2019" TO INCLUDE ITS APPLICATION TO CATS

--- [Page 6] ---
Page 2 of 7
Ord. No. 0123-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE – This Ordinance shall be known as “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0171-14, SERIES OF 2014, OR “THE DAVAO CITY ADOPT-A-DOG ORDINANCE OF 2019” TO INCLUDE ITS APPLICATION TO CATS”.

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS – City Ordinance No. 171-14, Series of 2014 or the Davao City Adopt-a-Dog Ordinance shall be amended as follows:

SECTION 1. TITLE – This Ordinance shall be known as “The Davao City Adopt-A-Dog And-Cat (DADC) Ordinance of 2019”.

SECTION 2. POLICY – It is the policy of the City of Davao to ensure the protection of dogs/cats from abuse; the promotion of adoption of dogs/cats, if practicable and necessary; and the rehabilitation of the same. It is likewise the duty of every pet owner to ensure that their pet dogs/cats are protected from harm and well cared for. The City will exhaust all means to save the life of a dog/cat and shall resort to euthanasia only as the last and desperate resort. Further, this Ordinance is not intended to hinder the anti-rabies program of the City, and in fact, the “Dog-Friendly” policy of the City of Davao must complement its rabies program.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.

A. Animal Welfare Non-Government Organization – Animal welfare non-government organization and other interested persons that advocate the welfare of animals, especially dogs/cats.

B. Dogs/cats unsafe for adoption or incapable of rehabilitation – Dogs/cats which aggressively attack a person causing physical injury or death or behave in a manner that a reasonable individual would believe poses an unjustified imminent threat of physical injury or death or provide signs that it is inclined to attack a person without provocation; a dog/cat afflicted with rabies infection or other contagious diseases, viruses or bacteria or a dog/cat with a severe medical condition that would pose danger to adopting individuals or terminally ill ones. The dog/cat size and weight shall be taken into consideration in determining the classification.

C. Dog/cat safe for adoption – Dogs/cats which are sociable or friendly, do not attack aggressively a person, do not pose an unjustified imminent threat of physical injury or death and give indication that it is liable to attack a person and in good health condition. The dog/cat size and weight shall be taken into consideration in determining the classification.

D. Dogs/cats safe for adoption but requires rehabilitation – Dogs/cats which are sociable or friendly, do not attack aggressively a person, do not pose as an unjustified imminent threat of physical injury or death and give indication that it is liable to attack a person but with physical and medical condition that shall require minor treatment. The dog/cat size and weight shall be taken into consideration in determining the classification.

--- [Page 7] ---
E. Euthanasia/Mercy Killing: Humane method of induction which does not result in a prolonged pain and suffering to end the life of a dog/cat.

F. Impounded animals: Stray dogs/cats whether registered or unregistered caught or impounded at the City Pound. Rescued dogs/cats are included in this definition.

G. Rescued dogs/cats: Any dog/cat which has been subjected to fighting, abandonment, neglect by its owner or severely maltreated by its owner, keeper, or persons other than the owner or keeper, shall be regarded as a rescued dog/cat for the purpose of rehabilitation.

H. Rehabilitated dog/cat: A dog/cat which has sufficiently regained its physical and medical condition which allows it to sufficiently perform its regular movement.

I. Stray dogs/cats: Any dog/cat leaving its owner's place or premise and no longer under the effective control of the owner. Registered and unregistered dogs/cats without owners shall be included in this Ordinance.

Section 4. Adoption of Dogs and Cats

A. If at the expiration of three (3) days from the time of impounding, an impounded dog/cat has not been claimed by the owner or keeper, the City Veterinary's Office (hereinafter referred to as "CVO" for brevity) may initially classify the dog/cat for purposes of permanent or temporary adoption to qualified persons, or euthanasia/mercy killing in accordance with law.

B. It shall be the duty and responsibility of the CVO to immediately, humanely, fairly, and with care, preferably in consultation with a government-registered animal welfare non-government organization, classify within three (3) days from impounding of the dog/cats whether a dog/cat is (a) safe for adoption, (b) safe for adoption but requires rehabilitation, and (c) dangerous for adoption or incapable of rehabilitation.

The CVO shall classify the dog/cat under (a) and (b) from the time of impounding up to the 10th day of impounding unless the CVO requires a longer period of rehabilitation. After impounding, the dog/cat shall be declared clear and placed for adoption. Impounded dogs/cats under (c) shall be euthanized in the most humane manner so as not to cause pain and suffering to the animal in accordance with law, unless Section 4F of this Ordinance is applied.

C. Any person who is qualified to adopt a dog/cat shall be allowed to adopt a dog/cat determined as safe and cleared for adoption preferably of his or her own choice.

D. After the 10th day of rehabilitation unless the CVO determines a longer period of rehabilitation and after the dog/cat has been cleared for adoption, the CVO shall clear the dog/cat for adoption and declare them safe for adoption including rescued dogs/cats under its care and supervision on permanent or temporary adoption to qualified caring and loving individuals only.

It shall exert utmost efforts so that the dogs/cats will be adopted by qualified individuals who will provide for adequate shelter, protection, love and care for the unclaimed or rescued dog/cat declared safe and cleared for adoption. Any person who is a resident of the City for six (6) continuous months of good conduct and over 18 years of legal age, has not been convicted of violation of the Animal Welfare Act, and has been in possession of the CVO, appears to have and actually has genuine concern for the welfare and protection for dogs and cats.

E. If the CVO determines that a dog/cat is safe for adoption but requires rehabilitation, the dog/cat shall immediately be placed under observation until the 10th day of impoundment and clearance, the CVO may then place the dog/cat under rehabilitation or for adoption by persons who shall volunteer to rehabilitate the dog/cat or shall volunteer to have the dog/cat rehabilitated through a private animal rehabilitation center or individual who specializes in rehabilitation of dogs/cats. Otherwise, the dog/cat shall be disposed of through euthanasia.

E.1. If the dog/cat is temporarily adopted, the adoptive person shall take care and custody of the dog/cat and may proceed with the rehabilitation of the dog/cat as provided by Section 5.E. The CVO shall determine the period of temporary adoption. Upon the expiration of the period of adoption, the CVO shall determine whether the dog/cat has been rehabilitated and qualified for adoption. If the dog/cat is declared as rehabilitated, the same shall be placed for permanent adoption. If the CVO determines that the same had not been rehabilitated, the dog/cat shall be disposed of through euthanasia.

E.2. The person who temporarily adopted the dog/cat shall have priority in the process of permanent adoption of the dog/cat once the latter is declared as rehabilitated.

F. A dog/cat determined to be dangerous for adoption or incapable of rehabilitation shall be immediately disposed of in accordance with law.

To ensure the safety of the public, all dogs/cats shall be presumed unsafe for adoption and incapable of rehabilitation if within three (3) days from impounding, the dogs/cats have yet to be classified in accordance with Section 4.B. of this Ordinance. However, in special instances where the dogs/cats are classified as unsafe for adoption or incapable of rehabilitation, the City Veterinarian may allow the adoption or of the dogs/cats to persons who are known or who claim to be knowledgeable in the field of dog/cat training or rehabilitation. Provided, the dog/cat shall be placed under observation up to the 10th day of impoundment unless the CVO determines a longer period of impoundment. Provided, the adopting persons are informed of the classification of the dog/cat, the reason for the classification, the risks which accompany in the adoption of such dogs/cats, and the persons who are responsible for the dog/cat so as to avoid future risk. Provided further, that the classification of the dogs/cats classified unsafe for adoption or incapable of rehabilitation under this provision are not due to rabies infection or other contagious diseases, bacteria, or viruses.

G. The CVO shall coordinate with animal welfare groups and other interested persons to promote, assist, and support in the adoption, and foster care of unclaimed or rescued dogs/cats, and exert all means possible to avoid the destruction of unclaimed or rescued dogs/cats safe for adoption by the City, and hand them to the public. Notwithstanding, it shall likewise be the duty of the CVO to ensure that any such initiative shall not unduly congest the City Pound and hinder the anti-rabies program of the City.

Furthermore, the CVO may, in a just and fair manner, permit the adoption of ten (10) dogs/cats a month unless it determines a different number of dogs/cats taking into consideration the following: the safety and security of the public, the effect on the city's anti-rabies program, if any, the number of dogs/cats impounded and qualified for adoption, and the estimated number of interested and qualified adopted individuals.

H. The CVO shall coordinate with and encourage participation of animal welfare groups and non-government agencies and other interested persons to help strengthen the adoption efforts of unclaimed or rescued dogs/cats especially if found unfit for adoption to willing and qualified individuals.

I. Temporary adoption is a system of adoption where a qualified person is permitted to have care and custody of a dog/cat for a period as determined by the CVO. Within the period of temporary adoption, the foster may likewise encourage the permanent adoption of the dog/cat under his custody. The adopting person shall return the dog/cat to the City Pound or its authorized agent within not more than seven (7) days after the expiration of the period of temporary adoption to other persons. If within one day from the return of the dogs/cats to the City Pound and after utmost efforts have been made to ensure that a dog/cat will be adopted, the dog/cat shall be subjected to euthanasia.

I.1. In case the temporary foster and other willing and qualified persons decide to permanently adopt the dog/cat, the temporary foster shall be preferred.

J. The CVO shall keep a record of the identity and maintain the whereabouts of the dogs/cats adopted and the individuals or persons adopting. In cases of change of address and contact details, the foster/adopter must inform the CVO.

K. All dogs/cats placed for adoption should be properly vaccinated, neutered or spayed, and registered by the CVO.

L. All adopting persons shall comply with the responsibilities of pet owners as provided by Section 5 of Republic Act 9482, including the mandatory rehabilitation of the dogs/cats placed for permanent adoption if the individual seeks rehabilitation of the dog/cat elsewhere.

M. The City Veterinary's Office shall ensure the proper and timely disposition of dogs/cats classified as unsafe for adoption or incapable of rehabilitation through means allowed by law to prevent endangerment of the public unless the CVO, in its discretion, acts in accordance with Section 4.F of this Ordinance.

Further, the City Veterinary's Office shall ensure the proper and timely disposition of dogs/cats classified as safe for adoption and safe for adoption but require rehabilitation which were unable to benefit from adoption with one day from placing the dogs and/or cats for adoption to prevent endangerment of the public.

N. The CVO shall provide adequate information and notice to the public as to the list of impounded dogs/cats classified as safe for adoption or safe for adoption but require rehabilitation who will be undergoing a period of observation for rabies infection before possibly being formally placed for adoption.

SECTION 5. REHABILITATION OF DOGS/CATS.

A. The City Veterinary's Office may provide for a dog/cat rehabilitation program at the City Pound which shall undertake the rehabilitation of dogs/cats qualified for adoption including rescued dogs/cats and other dogs/cats not impounded. The rehabilitation program shall provide for the following: determination of the physical and medical condition of the dogs/cats and determine if the same are capable of rehabilitation within the time provided by the program, the procedure for treatment of the dog's/cat's physical and medical condition.

B. The period of rehabilitation which shall not be more than five (5) days unless the CVO requires a longer period taking into consideration the following: the availability of funds, personnel, equipment, and facilities for the implementation of the program, the gravity of the physical and medical condition of the dogs/cats, and the number of dogs/cats placed under rehabilitation. If performed without causing danger to any individual, the rehabilitation may be undertaken during the period of confinement for observation for rabies virus contamination.

C. The CVO shall determine if a rescued dog/cat which has undergone rehabilitation and recovery procedure, has been rehabilitated. A dog/cat shall be considered as rehabilitated if the CVO has diagnosed the improvement of the physical and medical condition of the dog/cat and cleared the same for release.

D. As far as practicable, the CVO shall have a rehabilitation and treatment facility separate from the regular impoundment area for dogs/cats.

E. The CVO shall seek the cooperation and participation of animal welfare groups and other interested persons in the rescue, rehabilitation, and recovery of rescued dogs and cats. The CVO will allow the participation of the animal welfare groups and other interested persons in the information dissemination drive of the City on the existence of the Adopt-A-Dog-And-Cat Program of the City, including providing information on the possible violation of the Animal Welfare Act, and providing advice and recommendation to the CVO on the implementation of the rehabilitation program, if any.

Furthermore, animal welfare groups and other interested persons may be permitted to undertake the rehabilitation and recovery of the dogs/cats under the care and custody of the CVD, and undertake the rescue of stray or abandoned dogs/cats and turn over the same to the CVD or with the permission of the CVD to any person, individually or as a group, or volunteer to rehabilitate the rescued dog/cat in accordance with the provisions of this Ordinance.

F. The adopting persons may be charged with reasonable fees for the rehabilitation and treatment of the dogs/cats.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE – If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY – This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
