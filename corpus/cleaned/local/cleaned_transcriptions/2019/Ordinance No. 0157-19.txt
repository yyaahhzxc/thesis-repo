AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION – PHILMCHARTER, INVOLVING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A JOINT COMMITTEE FOR THE DELIVERY OF PROGRAMS AND SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN WITH PSYCHOLOGICAL PROBLEMS

--- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0157-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION – DAVAO CHAPTER, INVOLVING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A JOINT UNDERTAKING IN THE DELIVERY OF PROGRAMS AND SERVICES FOR WOMEN AND CHILDREN WITH PSYCHOLOGICAL PROBLEMS".

SECTION 2. LEGAL BASIS - Section 455, paragraph (b), sub-paragraph (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:

"SECTION 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.- xxx xxx xxx "(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall: xxx xxx xxx (vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the renewal of the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Mental Health Association – Davao Chapter, involving the establishment of a joint undertaking in the delivery of programs and services for women and children with psychological problems.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 17, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOWN ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This memorandum of agreement is entered into this 1st day of January by and between the following:

PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION, DAVAO CITY CHAPTER, a non-stock, non-profit corporation created in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at A. Fichon St., Davao City, represented by its Chairperson, MR. DOMINADOR A. LOPEZ, of legal age, Filipino Citizen and a resident of Davao City, herein referred to as the PMHA.

And

THE CITY OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit organized and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented by the City Mayor HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, Filipino Citizen, married, and resident of Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the City;

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, Section 34 of RA 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 recognized the non-government organizations as partners in the pursuit of local autonomy;

WHEREAS, Section 35 of RA 7160 authorizes the local government units to enter into joint ventures and such other cooperative arrangement with people and non-governmental organizations to engage in the delivery of certain basic services, capacity building and livelihood projects, and to develop local enterprises designed to improve productivity and income, diversify agriculture, spur rural industrialization, promote ecological balance and enhance the economic and social well-being of the people;

WHEREAS, both PARTIES recognize the urgency to address the prevalence of emotional and psychological conditions of the citizens, children in the streets, violence against women and children, drug addiction and other related social problems agreed to jointly undertake psychosocial intervention and other mental health programs for their holistic development;

WHEREAS, the PMHA has outlined programs and services to children and youth with emotional, behavioral, psychological and psychiatric disorders.

--- [Page 10] ---
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing, the parties have hereunto agreed as they hereby agree to establish a joint undertaking in the delivery of programs and services for women and children with mental and psychological problem under the following terms and conditions:

A. THE CITY OF DAVAO shall:

1. Grant the P120, 000.00 for Philippine Mental Health Association Davao Chapter to support the mental health related programs and services for the City of Davao.
2. Authorize the City Health Office to be in partnership with PMHA in the implementation of the programs and services.

B. THE PHILIPPINE MENTAL HEALTH ASSOCIATION DAVAO CHAPTER shall:

1. Provide a more comprehensive lecture/seminar/forum in relation with mental health issues such as, Caring For Your Mental Health, Stress Management, Understanding Depression, Internet Addiction among others by procuring a new set of Desktop Computer. This aims to capacitate every member of the community, and help them attain sound mental health and promote the prevention against mental disorders. The lecture/seminar/forum shall be given to different Barangay in the city of Davao, including government offices/centers such as DCTRCCD, CHO, Lingap Center, Davao City AIDS Council, GK Ray of Hope Village and other government agencies in need of mental health awareness;
2. Procurement of new set of Psychological Materials and provide Psychological Assessment following the standards and implementing procedures laid down by appropriate agencies to the wards under City Social Services and Development Office (CIC-Balay Danggupan, Paginrahawan Drop-in Center and Lamdag sa Kabataan) and clients from City Health Office (CHO) Reg. XI with free of charge as direct beneficiaries;
3. Provide necessary services to psychology students to different schools for mental health awareness and promotion as a leading promoter of mental health in Davao City through Progressive Youth Association of Mental Health (PyAMH - DVO) Program; and

--- [Page 11] ---
4. Render services to the people of Davao City in any means of service such as consultation/referral/coordination with different mental health practitioners (Psychiatrist, Developmental Pediatrician, Psychologists, Guidance Counselors) and other leading agency in mental health to address the needs of the people of Davao City to help them in combating their day to day struggles; and

5. Submit Quarterly the Liquidation Report reflecting the usage of funds in implementing the programs and services of Philippine Mental Health Association Davao Chapter.

C. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1. The Philippine Mental Health Association (PMHA) Davao Chapter is given a span of one (1) year from January 2019 to December 2019 for its full implementation pursuant to the goals of the program.

2. The guidelines issued by the Commission on Audit in the granting, utilization, accounting and auditing of funds released to Non-Government Organization/People's Organizations (NGOs/POS) as provided under COA Circular No. 2007-001 is hereby incorporated as an integral part of this Agreement.
