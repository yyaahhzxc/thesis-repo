Be it ordained by the Sanguaniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE INSTITUTE OF ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT, NATURAL RESOURCES, REGION 11 (DENR XI), THE COMMISSION ON HIGHER EDUCATION REGION, 11 (CHED XI), THE NATIONAL COMMISSION ON INDIGENOUS PEOPLES, REGION 11 (NCCIP XI), THE BARANGAY GOVERNMENT OF MALABOG, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, AND THE UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHEASTERN PHILIPPINES (USEP) RELATIVE TO THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE USEP MALABOG EXTENSION CAMPUS (UMECP) IN PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) and Section 25 (b) of the Local Government Code of 1991 or Republic Act No. 7160 provide that:

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.

X X X

(a) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:

X X X

1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:

X X X

(vi) Represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sanguaniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

X X X"

"Section 25. National Supervision over Local Government Units.

b) National agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation of said national projects.

X X X"

**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY -** The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to be entered into by and among the City Government of Davao, the Department of Environment and Natural Resources, Region 11 (DENR XI), the Commission on Higher Education, Region 11 (CHED XI), the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples, Region 11 (NCIP XI), the Barangay Government of Malabog, the Indigenous Cultural Community of Ati Tribe of Barangay Malabog, Paquibato District, and the University of Southeastern Philippines (USEP) relative to the establishment of the USEP Malabog Extension Campus (UMEC) in Paquibato District, this City.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY -** The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

for the Establishment of the USeP Malabog Campus (UMC)

in Paquibato District, Davao City

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Understanding made and executed by and between:

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a public corporation created under the Local Government Code of 1991, with its address at P.O. Box 200, St. Francis City Hall, Davao City, hereto represented by its Local Chief Executive, SARA Z. PEREZ-CARPIO, under Section 44(5)(v) of the Local Government Code of 1991 as couched under Resolution No. 0401-19 with its corresponding City Ordinance No. 0109-19, both series of 2019.

and

The DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL RESOURCES, REGION 11, a public corporation created under the law, with office address at Lanang, Davao City, hereto represented by its Regional Director, RUTH M. TAWANTAWAN.

and

and

The NATIONAL COMMISSION ON INDIGENOUS PEOPLES, REGION 11, a public corporation created under the law, with office address at E. Valerio Bldg., EB Lopez St., Sandawa Plaza, SIR New Matina, Davao City, hereto represented by its Regional Director, ATTY. GERONCIO R. AGUIO.

and

The BARANGAY GOVERNMENT OF MALABOG, a public corporation created under the law, with office address at Barangay Malabog, Paquibato District, Davao City, hereto represented by its Punung Barangay, MR. JESSIELITO C. AREJA.

and

The INDIGENOUS CULTURAL COMMUNITY OF ATA TRIBE OF BARANGAY MALABOG, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, with office address at Paquibato District, Davao City, hereto represented by its IPS Chairman, MR. JOSE AMBAN.

--- [Page 11] ---
The UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHEASTERN PHILIPPINES, a duly constituted Higher Educational Institution under the Philippine Law, located at F. Higlo St., Barrio Obrero, Davao City, herein represented by its University President, DR. LOURES C. GENERALAO

WITNESSETH:

In order to ensure sustainable development in Davao City, in particular, and the whole of the Philippines, in general, the herinabove PARTIES agree to cooperate and work together towards transforming conflicts in Paquibato District through the provision of basic social services for the communities within and around the area.

One of the priority areas for bringing about more positive peace in Paquibato District under the auspices of the Peace 911 Program of the City Government of Davao is the establishment of the USeP Malabog Campus (UMC), at Barangay Malabog, Paquibato District.

Now therefore, the PARTIES have agreed to execute this MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING under the following terms and conditions:

1. The PARTIES covenant to use their best endeavours to mutually cooperate so that quality yet accessible education shall be afforded to the partner communities and governed by the principles of:
   > Good Governance
   > Sustainable Development
   > As enunciated in E.O. 70, s2019
   > Conflict Sensitivity and Conflict Transformation (guided by Do-No-Harm & Do-Some-Good)
   > Cultural Sensitivity
   > Environmental Conservation,
   > Mutual Respect

2. The specific terms of any subsequent mutual cooperation shall be discussed and agreed upon in writing signed by the appropriate authorities of all the PARTIES hereto prior to the implementation of any particular program or activity for the establishment of the USeP Malabog Campus (UMC) thereof. Any such Agreement, Memoranda of Agreement, or Contract will become an integral part of this Memorandum of Understanding.

3. This Memorandum of Understanding will come into force on the date of signature by all PARTIES hereto.

--- [Page 12] ---
4. Any party may terminate its participation to this Memorandum of Understanding, subject to a prior notice of at least three (3) months in writing and served to the rest of the PARTIES hereto.

5. The PARTIES agree that they have separate financing responsibilities and neither party may call upon the other party for advance funding to meet any advance expenditure obligations.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed these presents on this 22nd day of November 2019, in Barangay Malabog, Paquibato District, Davao City, Philippines.
