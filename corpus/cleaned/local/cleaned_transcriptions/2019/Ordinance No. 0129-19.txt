ORDINANCE NO. 0129-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF HUAWEI TECHNOLOGIES INC., FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A 4-LEGGED SELF-SUPPORT TOWER SITUATED AT LANZONES STREET, BARANGAY TUGBOK PROPER, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY.

--- [Page 5] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF HUAWEI TECHNOLOGIES INC., FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A 4-LEGGED SELF-SUPPORT TOWER SITUATED AT LANZONES STREET, BARANGAY TUGBOK PROPER, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Article XII, Section 1 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) provides for request for additional allowable use, which reads:

"The uses enumerated in the preceding articles are general and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The barangay of Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other users not enumerated therein as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which have socio-economic and environmental significance to the community and interest of the municipality: a majority vote of the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution of this ordinance."

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST - The request of Huawei Technologies Inc., for additional allowable use for the installation of a 59.5-meter high, 4-legged self-support tower situated at Lanzones Street, Barangay Tugbok Proper, Tugbok District, this city, within a Major Commercial Zone and Water Resource Zone, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on December 03, 2019, by three-fourth (3/4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang, there being a quorum.
