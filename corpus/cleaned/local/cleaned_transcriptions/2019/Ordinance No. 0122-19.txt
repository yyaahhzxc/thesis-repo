Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject Ordinance, with the information that this office finds no legal infirmity therein. The grant of financial assistance to peoples and non-governmental organization is authorized under RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, to quote:

"SECTION 36 Assistance to People's and Non-governmental Organizations. A local government unit may, through its local chief executive and with the concurrence of the sanggunian concerned, provide assistance, financial or otherwise, to such people's and non-governmental organizations for economic, socially-oriented, environmental or cultural projects to be implemented within its territorial jurisdiction."

Viewed therefrom, this office recommends approval of the Ordinance and the execution of the Memorandum of Agreement.

Ord. No. 0122-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE ACTION FOUNDATION OF DAVAO INC. RELATIVE TO THE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE IN FAVOR OF ACTION FOUNDATION OF DAVAO INC. (BOYS TOWN DAVAO)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:

Section 455: Chief Executive Powers, Duties and Compensation

"(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for, and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Action Foundation of Davao, Inc. relative to the financial assistance in favor of Action Foundation of Davao, Inc. (Boys Town Davao).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 3, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement made and entered into by and between:

The City Government of Davao, a political subdivision of the government under the Republic of the Philippines, with office, address at the City Hall, represented herein by its Mayor HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE hereinafter referred to as the "LGU"

And,

Welfare Action Foundation of Davao, Inc., a non-stock, non-profit organization with office address at Ms.A, Davao City, presented herein by its President, Mr. Salvador A. Angala hereinafter referred to as "WAFDI"

WITHNESSETH, that:

WHEREAS, the mandate of WAFDI is to assist children of indigent families in Davao City and environs to attain education from grade school up high school by providing them board and lodging and financial assistance for clothing and school expenses;

WHEREAS, the resources of WAFDI are fully dependent on voluntary contribution from government and privately owned companies and association and private persons in their personal capacities;

WHEREAS, LGU recognizes the worthiness of the objectives of WAFDI in helping WAFDI's under privileged member of its city and operations within the city;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties agree to work together by:

A. The LGU shall:

1. Provide one (1) million pesos financial assistance annually from 2019-2021.

2. Enact an ordinance authorizing the city Mayor to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into and between the City Government of Davao and the Welfare Action Foundation of Davao, Inc. (Baystown Davao)

B. The WAFDI shall:

1. Provide the city all documents supports needed by all department to release the allocations
