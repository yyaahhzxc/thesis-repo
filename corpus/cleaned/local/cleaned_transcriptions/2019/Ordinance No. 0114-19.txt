Ord. No. 0114-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session, assembled,

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE SMOKE-FREE DECLARATION OF SUPPORT AND MEMBERSHIP TO THE SMOKE-FREE CITIES ASIA PACIFIC NETWORK."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - It is hereby declared to be the policy of the State to take a proactive role in the preventive health care of its citizens. Towards this end, the City Council of Davao shall enact this Ordinance as an acknowledgement and reiteration of its commitment to a smoke-free Davao and ultimately a smoke-free Philippines.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Smoke-free Declaration of Support and Membership to the Smoke-free Cities Asia Pacific Network (SCAN) of the Southeast Asia Tobacco Control Alliance.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, November 19, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

Smoke-Free Declaration
Pledge of Commitment
Smoke-Free Cities/Municipalities/Provinces

We acknowledge that:
• All tobacco products are harmful; scientific evidence has clearly and unequivocally established that tobacco use and exposure to tobacco smoke cause serious diseases, disabilities, and premature deaths in our communities.
• Tobacco is the only legal product that kills two third of all its regular users with six million tobacco-caused deaths globally plus 600,000 deaths from exposure to secondhand smoke.
• Tobacco is the leading cause worldwide (1.3 billion smokers) with 12% of the world's smokers (121 million) are in the ASEAN region.
• Tobacco use is a major public health problem that causes significant social and economic impact on individuals, families, and society; reducing smoking in our communities significantly increase household income and benefits the community and local economy;
• Addressing tobacco use is an important public health priority in the context of reducing the burden of non-communicable diseases and protection of the youth, women, and the elderly.
• Smoking is an epidemic created and sustained by the tobacco industry; because the tobacco industry is the vector of this epidemic, there is a need to be alert to any efforts by this industry to undermine tobacco control efforts.
• Designating indoor smoking areas or smoking rooms do not provide adequate protection, confuse the public about the dangers of safety, and increase the economic burden of businesses;
• The duty to protect individuals from tobacco smoke corresponds to an obligation of governments to enact and enforce regulations to protect individuals against threats to their fundamental rights and freedoms.

We welcome the:
• Commitment from local governments to lead local action to respond to the tobacco epidemic and secure the general welfare, health, social, economic and environmental benefits that come from reducing smoking prevalence by enacting and enforcing 100% smoke-free laws/regulations;
• Enforcement of this statement by the national government, Health Department and other government agencies, and partners.

Our Local Government commits to:
• Actively support national government action and local level initiatives to reduce smoking prevalence and health inequalities in our communities and to communicate continuously and effectively the evidence on the harms of tobacco use and exposure to tobacco smoke and the positive impact of a smoke-free environment;
• Work with our partners and local communities to address the causes and hazards of tobacco use; take immediate action to ban emerging tobacco products that will lure the youth into the habit of smoking;
• Act now and recognize that the tobacco epidemic is a serious health problem; therefore we have to strengthen our commitment to tobacco control strategies and ensure its sustained enforcement; implement programs and strategies in order to reduce smoking prevalence;
• Protect our health policies from the commercial and other vested interests of the tobacco industry and enforce that our officials, personnel, and work will not accept any partnerships, payments, services, research funds, or other forms of inducement from the tobacco industry;
• Publicly declare our commitment in reducing smoking in our communities by joining the guest member of the Smoke-Free Cities ASEAN Network (SCAN), the alliance of local government units working together towards the goal of a 100% smoke-free ASEAN;
• Actively participate in the SCAN and pledge ourselves to work together, share experiences, and support each other, towards our vision of a 100% smoke-free ASEAN.
