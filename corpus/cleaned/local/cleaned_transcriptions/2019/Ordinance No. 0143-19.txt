Ord. No. 0143-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH, RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF A MOBILE DENTAL CLINIC, INCLUDING ITS ACCESSORIES AND EQUIPMENT, IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Pursuant to section 455 of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

xxx xxx xxx

(vi) represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to accept and sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the Deed of Donation to be executed by the Department of Health, relative to the Donation of a Mobile Dental Clinic, including its Accessories and Equipment, in favor of the City Government of Davao.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 10, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Deed of Donation is entered into on the 2019 day of in Manila, Philippines, by the DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH, a National Government Entity duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, hereinafter referred to as "DOH," with office address at San Lazaro Compound, Rizal Avenue, Sta. Cruz, Manila, Philippines, herein represented by ABDULLAH B. DUMAMA, Jr., MD, MPA, CESO III, Assistant Secretary of Health-Mindanao Cluster, as the DONOR;

In favor of the CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, hereinafter referred to as DONEE with office address at City Hall, Davao City, Philippines, herein represented by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, City Mayor, as the DONEE.

In this Agreement, DOH and the City Government of Davao are individually referred to as "Party" and collectively as "Parties."

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the State recognizes its mandate to provide equitable and accessible quality healthcare for all Filipinos;

WHEREAS, the Health Facility Enhancement Program (HFEP) is one of the major pillars of "Kalusugan Pangkalahanan";

WHEREAS, "Kalusugan Pangkalahanan" envisions quality health care to be more accessible to the poor and vulnerable population by ensuring the availability of functional static and mobile health facilities, including mobile dental clinics;

WHEREAS, studies have shown that the ratio of government dentist to population is one (1) dentist for every 48,000 to 50,000, and that the prevalence of dental diseases continue to be high starting at childhood;

WHEREAS, to address the lack of dentists who will serve the dental health needs of all the municipalities and cities in the country, the DOH is investing in the acquisition of mobile dental clinics, including equipment, to cover the unserved communities;

WHEREAS, in CY 2015, the DOH has committed to provide mobile dental clinics, including equipment, as an initial response to improve dental health care coverage in the country;

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao plays a critical role in ensuring administrative support to augment public dental health services in the country; and

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao shall provide the necessary health human resource and logistics support for the operation and maintenance of the mobile dental clinic.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and subject to the conditions stipulated hereunder, the Department of Health hereby donates to the City Government of Davao one (1) unit of mobile dental clinic, including equipment.

--- [Page 10] ---
The Donee undertakes to:

1. Ensure that the mobile dental clinic is utilized in accordance with its purpose.
2. Ensure the availability of dentists and other personnel, drugs, medicines, and supplies, utilities, and other operational expenses for the operation of the mobile dental equipment.
3. Ensure the national use of the mobile dental clinic within the City to maximize its benefits to the residents needing dental service.
4. Ensure the proper maintenance, out of its own resources, of the aforesaid mobile dental clinic, including the equipment.
5. Designate the City Health Officer as the focal person who will be coordinating with the DOH regional office for the purposes of the program monitoring and evaluation.

The Donor reserves the right to revoke this Deed of Donation in case any of the aforesaid conditions are not faithfully complied with by the Donee. In case this Deed of Donation is revoked, the Donee shall surrender the mobile dental clinic, including all its accessories and the equipment, to the Donor. The Donee shall also surrender the key(s) to the mobile dental clinic upon receipt of the Notice of Revocation. Ownership of the said mobile dental equipment shall then vest to the Donor effective upon receipt by the Notice of Revocation. And the Donee shall have seven (7) days from receipt of the Notice of Revocation to transfer the possession of the mobile dental clinic, including all its accessories and the equipment, to the Donor.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties here under set their hand on the date and place above mentioned.
