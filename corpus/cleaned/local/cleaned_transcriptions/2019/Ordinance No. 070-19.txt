MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT made and executed this day of 2019 in by and among:

The RONALD McDonald House Charities of the Philippines, Inc., a non-stock, non-profit corporation duly recognized and existing under and by virtue of laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at 17th floor, Citibank Center Building, Paseo de Roxas, Makati City, represented in this act by its Vice President, MARIA MARGARITA B. TORRES, hereinafter referred as the RMHC;

and

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO with office address at Pichon Street, Davao City, represented herein by SARA Z. DUTERTE, City Mayor, hereinafter referred to as the CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO;

and

The BARANGAY DUMOY with office address at KM 12 Mc Arthur Highway, Dumoy, Davao City, represented herein by Barangay Captain JESSIE MAR Y. CULASTE, hereinafter referred to as BARANGAY;

Witnesseth, that:

WHEREAS, the quality of citizenship of any country depends to a large extent on the caring that children receive during the first three years of life whereby the child's basic character and attitudes are formed and preparedness for life are shaped;

WHEREAS, parents have the primary responsibility to care for and promote the total development of their children. Furthermore, under the 1991 Local Government Code, the primary role of local government units in the development and growth of communities rests in the latter thru the exercise of service and delivery functions, among them the delivery of health and welfare services and implementation of programs and projects on primary health care, maternal and child care;

WHEREAS, the City under the 1991 Local Government Code shall exercise supervision over their respective Barangays. The Barangays shall ensure and support the health, safety, moral well-being and enhance economic prosperity to its inhabitants through a child development program;

WHEREAS, the RMHC is a non-profit organization with a mission to support the Filipino children well being especially non-stock, their need for education, shelter and happiness. RMHC is registered with the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) as an Auxiliary Social Welfare and

--- [Page 13] ---
Development Agency (SWDA), particularly as a Resource Agency, that provides financial support and capability – building programs to various partners primarily for the benefit of disadvantaged children 6 years old and below as well as grade 1 students in Regions I - XII, CAR, CARAGA and NCR;
WHEREAS, the RMHC have pre-qualified and selected a site where "Ronald McDonald Bahay Bullit" Day Care Center, shall be constructed, and have coordinated with the City Government of Davao in order to secure its cooperation and coordination in the DAY CARE CENTER (DCC) project.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereby agree on the following terms and conditions, to wit:
1. RMHC hereby undertake to construct the "Ronald McDonald Bahay Bullit" Day Care Center (DCC) Project at Barangay Dumoy, Davao City. For this purpose, RMHC shall provide the plans, designs and specifications, as well as materials and manpower, to be used for the construction of the DAY CARE CENTER (DCC).
2. The DCC Project construction plans, designs and specifications, which are in accordance with the standard and uniform designs of day care centers that are being constructed and donated by RMHC, are hereto attached and made an integral part hereof as Annex A. The DCC shall be constructed in accordance of the said plans, designs and specifications.
3. The City Government of Davao and the Barangay agree to extend full coordination and cooperation in the securing necessary permits, licenses, clearances and documents necessary for the construction of the DCC;
4. The Parties hereby agree that RMHC has the exclusive right to decide on and to use color schemes for the painting of the structure of the DCC, to decorate the interior, exterior and premises of the DCC, to install or incorporate signages, billboards, marks and other similar representations indicating RMHC as sponsor for the construction of the said project, which shall be conspicuously displayed in the DCC.
The Parties hereby agree that all identifiable designs, trademarks, logos, visual materials and the like, whether written, visual or tangible form, that shall be introduced to, placed in, attached to, posted in or otherwise made visible in the DCC or its premises, whether or not actually used as part of the child development activities of RMHC, shall be considered as identifiable designs, trademarks, logos, visual materials and the like or belonging to RMHC. No other identifiable designs, trademarks, logos, visual materials and the like particularly those belonging or pertaining to other quick service restaurant, shall be introduced to, placed in, attached to, posted in or otherwise made visible in the DCC and its premises. The cost for introducing placing, attaching, posting or otherwise making visible such designs, trademarks, logos, visual materials shall be borne solely and exclusively by RMHC.

--- [Page 14] ---
13. The regular and ordinary upkeep of the DCC and its premises shall be the responsibility of the day care worker in partnership with the parents;

14. The parties agree that RMHC shall have the right to refer to the DCC project or to use pictures and other visual representations of the DCC, its facilities, activities, programs, or its premises in brochures, announcements, advertisements, whether published, televised aired through the radio, used in the internet or otherwise made public in or through any other form or medium for advertising purposes. It also agrees that RMHC shall have the right to include the DCC in its programs or activities, provided that the City Government of Davao and the Barangay is advised thereof.

15. Nothing in this Agreement shall create or be deemed to create any employer-employee relationship of any kind or nature between the parties and their respective employees and/or representatives. Moreover, nothing in this Agreement shall constitute or be deemed to constitute a partnership between the parties. Neither party shall have the right to create or assume an obligation of any kind, express or otherwise, for or on behalf of the other party in any manner whatsoever. Neither shall anything contained in this Agreement or documents executed hereunder be deemed to constitute the parties as agents or representatives of the other for any purpose or in any manner whatsoever. Neither party shall hold itself out or allow it to be held out as partner of the other.

16. Notwithstanding anything herein contained, the City Government of Davao and the Barangay hereby warrant that RMHC, including its stockholders, directors, officers, employees or agents, shall not in any manner be liable to the LGU, nor to any other person within the DCC or its premises in respect of:

a. Any damage, loss or destruction of any property or any injury due to any circumstances beyond RMHC's control (including but not limited to fire, flood, acts of God, escape of water, riot, civil commotion, curtailment of service, labor disputes, shortages of manpower, fuel materials, electricity or water, or other events of force majeure or fortuitous cause);

b. Any act, omission, default, misconduct or negligence of any contractor whose services have been secure for the construction of the DCC. Any such contractor shall not in any manner or for any purpose be deemed to be an agent or employee of RMHC.

c. Any damage, loss or destruction of any property or any injury arising out of the leakage or defect of the piping, wiring, sprinkler system or other apparatus or other structure in the DCC or its premises;

d. Any damage, loss or destruction of any property or any injury caused by persons in the DCC or its premises, whether or not such persons be present therein with the consent, knowledge.

--- [Page 15] ---
The City Government of Davao and the Barangay may display their respective seals or other visual representations within the DCC or its premises. Any cost incurred for this purpose shall be for their respective accounts.

5. RMHC shall undertake to complete the DCC on before . This period shall accordingly be extended should RMHC unable to or prevented from completing the construction on or before the said date due to causes beyond the control of RMHC, to causes without the fault of negligence of RMHC, or to events of force majeure or fortuitous cause.

6. Upon completion, RMHC shall turnover the DCC to the City Government of Davao and the Barangay for its operations on a date mutually agreed upon by the parties. It is hereby understood that subsequent to the turnover, the City Government of Davao and the Barangay shall have ownership of the DCC, and the City Government of Davao and the Barangay shall have joint and solidary liability for including undertaking necessary or ordinary repairs of the DCC;

7. The DCC shall not be used for purposes other than that for which it was constructed and established. Neither shall the structure of the DCC nor its appearance in any manner be altered or modified without the consent of RMHC;

8. The Barangay shall be solely and exclusively responsible for the administration, maintenance and general operations, including undertaking necessary or ordinary repairs, as well as obtaining and maintaining public utilities, such as water and electricity, of the DCC in accordance with the standards set forth in the minimum standards as provided under the Department of Social Welfare and Development Administrative Order 29, Series of 2004, "Standards for Day Care, other ECCD Centers and Service Provider";

9. Any cost incurred in connection with the administration, maintenance and general operations of the DCC shall be for the account of the City Government of Davao and the Barangay;

10. The City Government of Davao and the Barangay shall be responsible for any tax, charge or other similar fee imposed by, as well as for any permit, clearance or other similar document required by any government instrumentality, agency or body, or any private entity for continued operations of the DCC. Any cost or expense that shall be incurred in connection therewith shall be for the account of the City Government of Davao and the Barangay , as may be applicable;

11. The City Government of Davao and the Barangay will coordinate with the DSWD to be responsible for accreditation of the day care center and day care worker ;

12. The City Government of Davao shall provide the salary of the Day Care Worker who will be hired upon the recommendation of the Barangay;

and/or authority of the City Government of Davao their respective representatives, agents or employees;
e. Any nuisance made and/or suffered in the DCC and/or on its premises;
f. Any damage, loss or destruction of any property or injury, as well as any incident, occurrence, accident or any similar or analogous event's otherwise growing out of and/or caused by any failure on the part of the City Government of Davao and the Barangay, or any other entity or person to maintain, supervise, operate and/or manage the DCC Project, the DCC or its premises in a safe and secure condition;
9. Any damage, loss or destruction of any property or any injury caused by reason that the DCC or its premises is being maintained, supervised, operated and/or managed in violation or non-observance or non-performance of the appropriate laws, ordinances, rules and regulations and/or other issuances of government instrumentalities or agencies.
Insofar as may be applicable, this provision shall extend to include all representatives, agents or employees of the City Government of Davao and the Barangay, of any other person or entity.
