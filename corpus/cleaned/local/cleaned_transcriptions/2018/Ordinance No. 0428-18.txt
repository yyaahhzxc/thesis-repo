Ord. No. 0428-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF MAYOR'S PERMIT FEE, INTENDED FOR THE FARMER EXHIBITORS WHO WILL BE SELLING FRESH PRODUCE AND PROCESSED PRODUCTS IN ABREZZA AYALA MALLS, THIS CITY, FOR THE WHOLE YEAR ACTIVITY";

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption applies to the farmers exhibitors from Davao City who will be selling fresh produce and processed products and solely refers to the payment of Mayor's Permit Fee under Article 28, Section 234, Paragraph (a) of the 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao;

SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION - The exemption shall be from the approval of this Ordinance until the whole year of 2018 and that in no case shall the organizers be exempted from the compliance of the other provisions of the 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao and other applicable laws and ordinances;

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on March 9, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
