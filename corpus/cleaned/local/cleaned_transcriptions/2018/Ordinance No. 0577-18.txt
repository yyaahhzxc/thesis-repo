MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS.

This Agreement made and entered into by and among:

The DAVAO CITY LOCAL GOVERNMENT with office address at City Hall Drive, Poblacion District, Davao City represented herein by HON. MAYOR SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPHIO, and referred to hereinafter as the FUNDING AGENCY,

- and -

The NATIONAL DAIRY AUTHORITY, a government-owned and controlled corporation attached to the Department of Agriculture created by RA 7884, with office address at the NDA Bldg., BAI Compound, Visayas Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City, represented herein by its Administrator, MS. MARILYN B. MABALE, hereinafter referred to as the NDA.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, Section 16 of R.A. 7884, otherwise known as the National Dairy Development Act of 1995, provides that the government nutrition program requiring milk and dairy products shall be sourced from the small farmers and dairy cooperatives;

WHEREAS, for several years NDA has been implementing a milk feeding program nationwide for malnourished children in rural and urban communities aimed at sustaining livelihood and entrepreneurship opportunities for local dairy farmers and decreased prevalence of malnourished children;

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao will embark on a supplemental feeding among identified children beneficiaries in various barangays in Davao City

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties have hereto agreed and covenanted to execute this Program titled "EGG AND STERILIZED FRESH MILK FEEDING" under the following terms and conditions:

ARTICLE I The Program

1.1 The Program which shall be funded by the NDA and the FUNDING AGENCY aims to provide sterilized fresh milk to pre-identified Moderately Acute Malnourished (MAM) preschool children of Davao City on a continuing and regular basis for a period of 120 feeding days.

1.2 The FUNDING AGENCY has pre-identified 1,200 beneficiaries based on the criteria set by the City Health Office of Davao and the NDA.

ARTICLE III Roles and Responsibilities

3.1. On the Part of the FUNDING AGENCY.

a. Provide PHP 1,985,994.00 to be downloaded to the NDA for the supply of milk to 1,200 children beneficiaries for a total of 120 feeding days. The full amount shall be downloaded to NDA after complete delivery of the agreed number of milk pouches.
b. Responsible for the pre-feeding requirement of the program which includes but not limited to the following:
i. Deworming of beneficiaries/medical check-up of the beneficiaries (if needed).
ii. Orientation of implementers and target beneficiaries.
iii. Identification and validation of target beneficiaries based on the latest "Operation Timbang" (OPT).
iv. Providing a venue for the conduct of the orientation seminar.
v. Ensure the attendance of the program implementers as well as the feeding beneficiaries.
c. Ensure that the feeding program shall observe the following guidelines/protocols:
i. Feeding shall be for a period of 120 days, Mondays through Fridays.
ii. Feeding shall be done at the agreed feeding center, duly administered by the BNS/BHW under the general supervision of the Nutrition Officer.
d. Ensure that the feeding sites or delivery sites have appropriate storage areas for the milk pouches. These storage areas must be secured, free from rodents and other possible contaminants.
e. Undertake, in coordination with the NDA, the following activities/responsibilities:
1) Designate duly authorized representatives who shall take charge of receiving the milk during delivery and distributing the same to the intended beneficiaries using the milk pouches.
The authorized representative shall also be responsible for ensuring that milk will be properly stored and secured prior to distribution.
2) Assist the NDA thru its Nutrition Officers and BNS/BHW in the periodic monitoring and evaluation of the Program.
