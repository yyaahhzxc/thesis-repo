Ord. No. 0566-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE ROAD IN BETWEEN BOLTON STREET AND C.M. RECTO AVENUE (FRONTING THE CATHEDRAL ONLY) FROM 12:00 MIDNIGHT ON OCTOBER 15, 2018 UP TO 5:00 A.M. ON OCTOBER 16, 2018 AND FROM 5:00 P.M. ON OCTOBER 16, 2018, UP TO 8:00 A.M. ON OCTOBER 17, 2018, IN CONNECTION WITH THE VISIT OF THE INCORRUPT HEART OF PADRE PIO AT MINDANAO ARCHDIOCESE OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7169, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or final ceremonies, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs, or an undertaking of public works and highways; telecommunications and waterworks projects...

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The road in between Bolton Street and C.M. Recto Avenue, (fronting the Cathedral only) will be temporarily closed from 12:00 midnight on October 15, 2018 up to 5:00 in the morning on October 16, 2018 and from 5:00 p.m. on October 16, 2018 up to 8:00 a.m. on October 17, 2018, in connection with the visit of the Incorrupt Heart of Padre Pio in Mindanao Archdiocese of Davao;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED on October 9, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
