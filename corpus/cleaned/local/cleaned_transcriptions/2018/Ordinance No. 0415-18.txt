Series of 2018
AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT (SIA) FOR THE SOCIAL PENSION PROGRAM FOR INDIGENT SENIOR CITIZENS, A SUPPLEMENTARY TO THE GENERAL MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL PROTECTION PROGRAMS ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT (DSWD) XI AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO

--- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of Ord. No. 0415-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT (SIA) FOR THE SOCIAL PENSION PROGRAM FOR INDIGENT SENIOR CITIZENS, A SUPPLEMENTARY TO THE GENERAL MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON SOCIAL PROTECTION PROGRAMS ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT (DSWD) XI AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides, to wit:

"For effective, efficient and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Specific Implementation Agreement for the Social Pension Program for Indigent Senior Citizens, a Supplementary to the General Memorandum of Agreement on Social Protection Programs entered into by and between the Department of Social Welfare and Development XI and the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on March 6, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

**SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENTS**

**SOCIAL PENSION PROGRAM (SPP)**

This is a supplementary agreement to the Unified Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into on the day of _______ by and between the Department of Social Welfare and Development Field Office XI and the City Government of Davao.

I. **ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES**

A. **THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT - FIELD OFFICE XI**

1. Facilitate engagement with Local Government Unit (LGU) through signing of Memorandum of Agreement on the transfer of fund and implementation of Social Pension for indigent senior citizens.
2. Review and approve the quarterly updated list of eligible senior citizens submitted by the City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) and Office of the Senior Citizen's Affairs (OSCA) and the Local Chief Executive (LCE). The approved list of qualified senior citizens will be the basis for the quarterly release of funds to the Local Government Units (LGUs).
3. Monitor the quarterly submission of liquidation report verified by the Commission on Audit (COA).
4. Provide technical assistance to the LGUs through the City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) on the implementation of Social Pension.
5. Conduct regular monitoring and spot check to validate receipt of payment to the beneficiaries in all districts of Davao City.
6. Conduct orientation, advocacy activities, meeting as necessary with LGUs, Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA), Federation of the Senior Citizens Association of the Philippines (FSCAP), barangay senior citizens association.
7. Demand the refund of all unutilized/disallowed amounts at the end of the year.
8. Provide operational expenses to the LGU (direct implementers of the program).
9. Traveling expenses in the conduct of validation, pay-out, and monitoring of Social Pension to the beneficiaries.
10. The allowable amount for the reimbursement of traveling expenses shall be based on allocation per municipality in accordance with existing accounting and auditing rules and regulations per COA Circular N. 94-013.

B. **City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO)**

1. Facilitate preparation and approval of payroll and list of beneficiaries.
2. Coordinate with OSCA Head, FSAP members, senior citizens association in the conduct of validation and home visits to identified indigent senior citizens.
3. Submit monthly updates to the list of eligible senior citizens to the DSWD Field Office.
4. The City Treasurer of the LGU or the authorized representative of the LGU shall claim the check/s from the DSWD Field Office and issue an official receipt.
5. Ensure timely delivery of payment to the beneficiaries based on the approved list of beneficiaries/payroll.

--- [Page 10] ---
6. Facilitate creation of a Committee composed of OSCA, FSCAP, senior citizens organizations and other stakeholders to monitor the distribution of payment, status of the beneficiaries and attend/handle grievance / complaints.
7. Identify and designate staff/focal person for the Social Pension implementation.
8. Submit monthly accomplishment report on Social Pension to the DSWD.
9. Deposit the amount to LGU Trust Fund Account for DSWD.
10. Keep separate subsidiary records for the trust liability.
11. Submit the following documents to the LGU Chief/Author to DSWD Field Office within fifteen (15) days after the quarter pay-out: (1) Report of Check Issued RCI, (2) Report of Disbursement (RDC) in case there is cash advances, (3) Remit to the DSWD any unexpended balance at the end of the quarter.
12. The LGUs (thru the City Treasurer) shall take full responsibility in the proper disposition/disbursement of funds for the implementation of the programs in accordance with the existing budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations and shall liquidate the transfer of funds in accordance with Commission on Audit Circular No. 94-013.

DISENGAGEMENT, TERMINATION FOR CAUSE AND SANCTIONS

The Local Government Unit may request termination and this SIA and disengagement from the Social Pension Program (SPP) upon written notice to the other party specifying the reasons for the disengagement. The process of termination and disengagement shall follow Social Pension Program guidelines developed by the DSWD for the purpose.

Non-compliance or violation of the LGU to any of the provisions of this SIA, as determined by the DSWD after investigation, shall result in the imposition of the following sanctions, depending on the gravity of the offense: (a) warning or imposition of probationary status; (b) termination of SPP implementation; and (c) give rise to the filing of appropriate administrative and/or criminal actions against responsible offices of the Erring Party.

III. EFFECTIVITY AND AMENDMENTS

This agreement shall take effect upon signing of the parties and shall remain in full effect until the completion of all SPP implementation and/or the end of CY ______, unless otherwise revoked by any of the concerned parties.

Any modification on this Agreement will not be valid except when there is a written concurrence of BOTH PARTIES.
