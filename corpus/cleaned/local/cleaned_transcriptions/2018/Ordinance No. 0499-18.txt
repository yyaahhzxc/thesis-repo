Ordinance No. 0499-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2018, IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF FOUR MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P4,500,000.00), TO EXTEND TOTAL ASSISTANCE AT FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P500,000.00) EACH, TO OLONGAPO CITY, ZAMBALES; MACABEBE, PAMPANGA; MUNICIPALITIES OF CALUMPIT, PROVINCE OF NUEVA ECija; MUNICIPALITY OF CALUMPIT, PROVINCE OF BULACAN; MUNICIPALITIES OF MACABEBE AND SAN SIMON, PROVINCE OF PAMPANGA; MUNICIPALITIES OF ANAO, CAMILING AND MONCADA, PROVINCE OF TARIAC AND BALANGA CITY, PROVINCE OF BATAAN, FOR THE VICTIMS OF FLOODING BROUGHT BY SOUTHWEST MONSOON (HABAGAT) WORSENED BY TYPHOON INDY"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - In keeping with its mandates and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to judiciously utilize resources and put the same to proper use;

SECTION 3. BENEFICIARIES - The following areas are hereby designated as beneficiaries of the assistance, to wit:

| 1 | Olongapo City, Zambales | P | 500,000 |
| 2 | Licab, Nueva Ecija | P | 500,000 |
| 3 | Calumpit, Bulacan | P | 500,000 |
| 4 | Macabebe, Pampanga | P | 500,000 |
| 5 | San Simon, Pampanga | P | 500,000 |
| 6 | Anao, Tarlac | P | 500,000 |
| 7 | Camiling, Tarlac | P | 500,000 |
| 8 | Moncada, Tarlac | P | 500,000 |
| 9 | Balanga, Bataan | P | 4,500,000 |

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASIS - Pursuant to the provision of Section 324 (d) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, as amended by Republic Act 8185, "Five Percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as annual lump sum appropriations for relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction and other works or services in connection with calamities which may occur during the budget year. Provided, however, that such fund shall be used only within the area, or a portion thereof, of the local government unit or other areas affected by a disaster or calamity, as determined and declared by the local Sanggunian concerned";

Further, Section 5 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of R.A. No. 8185, states that:

Section 5. Allocation and Utilization of Five Percent (5%). Lump sum Appropriations for Calamity Fund

a) Allocation
xxx
"LGUs may also allocate/use a portion of the five percent (5%) Calamity Fund to other affected areas on condition that the said areas are declared as under a State of Calamity by the Sangguniang concerned";

b) Utilization
(2) A portion of the calamity fund may also be authorized to be used by the LGU concerned to provide financial assistance to other LGUs whose area or portion thereof had been declared under a state of calamity by its Sanggunian;

SECTION 5. USE OF FUNDS - The amounts herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures as approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to the existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval;
