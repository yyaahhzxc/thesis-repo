ORDINANCE NO. 0418-18
Series of 2018

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF MINDANAO TRADE EXPO FOUNDATION INC., FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF MAYOR'S PERMIT FEE IMPOSED ON DAVAO-BASED MICRO, SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTREPRENEURS DURING THE 81st ARAW NG DABAW CONGREGATION ON MARCH 11-15, 2018 AT ABREEZA MALL, J.P. LAUREL AVENUE, THIS CITY

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0418-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF MINDANAO TRADE EXPO FOUNDATION INC., FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF MAYOR'S PERMIT FEE IMPOSED ON DAVAO-BASED MICRO, SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTREPRENEURS DURING THE 81ST ARAW NG DABAW CELEBRATION ON MARCH 11-15, 2018 AT ABREEZA MALL, J.P. LAUREL AVENUE, THIS CITY";

SECTION 2. COVERAGE.- The exemption applies only to Davao-based exhibitors and solely refers to the payment of Mayor's permit fee under Article 26, Section 221, paragraphs a and d of the Revenue Code of 2005 and only covers Davao-based registered micro, small and medium enterprises;

SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION.- The exemption shall be from March 11-15, 2018 and that in no case shall the organizers be exempt from compliance of the other applicable laws and ordinances;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 6th day of March 2018, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

"For and in the absence of the Secretary"
