Ord. No. 0587-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND ON BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE BOARD OF INVESTMENTS FOR THE FACILITATION OF THE ENTRY OF INVESTMENTS AND PROMOTION OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared the policy of the City of Davao to encourage investments whether foreign or local consistent with the City's development plan towards sustainable economic growth. Towards this end, the City hereby enacts a flexible and dynamic policy of an encouraging investments to support its goal of becoming a prime investment destination in Mindanao.

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT - The Memorandum of Agreement refers to the agreement for the facilitation of the entry of investments and promotion of Davao City to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao, Republic of the Philippines, and the Board of Investment in Davao City.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Board of Investments.

SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 6th day of November 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) made and entered into by and between:

The BOARD OF INVESTMENTS (BOI), an attached agency of the DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY (DTI), with principal office located at Industry & Investments Bldg., 385 Sen. Gil Puyat Avenue, Makati City 1200, represented herein by the DTI Undersecretary for Investments, Hon. GEFEERNIO S. RODOLFO, hereinafter referred to as "DTI-BOI";

and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit, with principal office located at City Hall, 1st Floor, 1st St., Davao City, represented herein by its Mayor, SARA Z. DUERTER, who is duly authorized, and hereinafter referred to as "LGU-DAVAO".

WHEREAS, Section 15, Chapter 7, Title X, Book IV of Executive Order (EO) No. 292, otherwise known as the "Administrative Code of 1987," provides that the BOI shall be responsible for coordinating the formulation and implementation of short, medium and long term industrial plans as well as the formulation of policies and guidelines in accordance with national policies and priorities; register, monitor, and grant investment incentives to individual enterprises; formulate policies and guidelines aimed at creating an environment conducive to the expansion of existing investments or attracting prospective investments in the Philippines;

WHEREAS, Article 7(20) of EO No. 226, also known as the "Omnibus Investments Code of 1987," as amended, states that the BOI may enter into agreements with other agencies of government for the simplification and facilitation of systems and procedures involved in the promotion of investments, operation of registered enterprises and other activities necessary for the effective implementation of EO No. 226, as amended;

WHEREAS, Article 66, Book III of EO No. 226, aside from the incentives given under Article 39, Book I of said law as a means in promoting investments, provides that "regional or area headquarters [RHQs] and regional operating headquarters [ROHs] of a registered enterprise shall be exempted from all local taxes, fees, or charges imposed by a local government unit except real property tax on land improvements and equipment";

WHEREAS, Section 13(3) of Republic Act (RA) No. 7160, or the "Local Government Code of 1991," consistent with the State's policy to promote investments, states that "the taxing powers of provinces, cities, municipalities, and barangays shall not extend to the levy of x x [taxes] on business enterprises certified to by the Board of Investments as pioneer or non- pioneer for a period of six (5) and four (4) years, respectively, from the date of registration";

WHEREAS, the LGU-DAVAO issued Business Permit to authorize enterprises that will locate within its territorial jurisdiction to engage in business and conduct their activities therein;

WHEREAS, the LGU-DAVAO promotes investments through, among others, its Local Investments and Incentives Code (LIC) which lists down the priority activities and the available incentives to qualified investors/allocators;

WHEREAS, both Parties, consistent with Section 20, Article XII of the 1987 Constitution, in relation to Article 2(1) of EO No. 226, recognize the indispensable role of the private sector, encourage private enterprise, and provide incentives to needed investments that will provide significant employment opportunities, increase productivity, improve the technology of the people, and contribute to the continuous development of the economy, among others.

--- [Page 14] ---
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties have hereunto agreed that this MOA shall be binding upon the parties hereto and their respective assigns and further agreed as follows:

I. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE LGU-DAVAO

The LGU-DAVAO shall:
1. Furnish the DTI-BOI with a copy of new ordinances, guidelines, rules, and regulations governing the procedures and requirements for the issuance of Business Permit;
2. Provide DTI-BOI with an updated and relevant information such as but not limited to:
a. Investments projects for Joint Venture of the LGU-DAVAO;
b. Priority areas for development and promotion in the locality;
c. Local Incentives (as embodied in Local Investment Incentive Ordinance);
d. Cost of Doing Business (e.g. business permit; wage rate, land lease and/or selling rate; residential, office space, and building lease and/or selling rate; petroleum products price range; electricity rate, and water rate);
e. Economic Development Plan;
f. Available government/private owned land including zoning classification (e.g. agricultural, commercial, industrial, etc.);
g. Single contact person for investors;
h. Other information that may be needed by investors.

3. Allow authorized DTI-BOI Investments Assistance Center (AC) representatives or personnel to assist enterprises in the submission and follow-up of applications for Business Permit as well as in the enterprises' other business concerns with the City Government;

4. Recognize the exemption of regional or area headquarters (RHQs) and regional operating headquarters (ROHQs) of multinational companies from all kinds of local taxes, fees, or charges pursuant to Article 66 of EO No. 226;

5. Implement Section 133(g) of RA No. 7160, which exempts BOI-registered enterprises from business tax for a period of six (6) years for those with Pioneer status and four (4) years for those with non-pioneer status from the date of registration;

6. Provide such assistance to enterprises located within its territorial jurisdiction as may be necessary as the DTI-BOI's partner in its investments promotion endeavor.

II. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DTI-BOI

The DTI-BOI shall:
1. Assist in the promotion of the LGU-DAVAO as investment destination by providing potential investors with relevant information on the priority areas and available incentives to qualified investors/locators pursuant to the City/Municipality's LICC;
2. Enhance coordination with the LGU-DAVAO in the implementation of policies geared to promote ease of doing business in general and to hasten the process of starting a business in particular by disseminating a copy of the City's new ordinances, guidelines, rules and regulations governing the standards and requirements for the issuance of Business Permit to potential investors.

--- [Page 15] ---
3. Authorize DTI-BOI representative/s or personnel to assist in the submission and follow-up of enterprises' applications for Business Permit as well as in the enterprises' other business concerns with the LGU-DAVAO, and

4. Provide such other assistance to the LGU-DAVAO as well as its potential investors/locators may be requested relative to investments promotion and/or in furtherance of ease of doing business in the City/Municipality.

III. REPRESENTATION AND WARRANTIES

The parties represent that they have the requisite power, authority and capacity to enter into this Agreement and perform their duties, obligations and undertakings according to the terms and conditions set forth.

The parties shall comply with the pertinent laws, rules and regulations promulgated by the Republic of the Philippines.

This Agreement shall be binding upon the parties hereto and their respective assigns.

IV. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE

Any provision of this agreement subsequently declared illegal, invalid or unconstitutional by the court of law shall not affect the rest of other provisions which shall remain valid, subsisting and binding.

V. AMENDMENTS

No modification of this MOA or a part thereof shall be made except upon deliberation of the contested provision herein and execution of a written instrument duly signed by both parties thereto.

VI. EFFECTIVITY AND TERMINATION

1. This MOA shall take effect upon signing of the Parties thereto and shall remain effective unless sooner amended or revoked.

2. Any party to the MOA may terminate the same at any time during its implementation through written notice explaining the reasons thereof, thirty (30) days prior to the actual date of termination.
