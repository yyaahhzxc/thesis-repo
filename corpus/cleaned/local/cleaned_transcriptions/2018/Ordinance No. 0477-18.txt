Ord. No. 0477-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC THE STREETS ALONG SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL THROUGH C.M. RECTO AVENUE-ROMAS EXTENSION (ALONG MARCO POLO)-PONCIANO STREET-PALMA GIL STREET-LEGAPI STREET-MAGALLANES STREET-BONIFACIO MONUMENT ROTUNDA THEN BACK TO THE CATHEDRAL ON JUNE 28, 2018, FROM 3:00 P.M. ONWARDS IN CELEBRATION OF THE UPCOMING 170TH PAROCHIAL FIESTA OF SAN PEDRO CATHEDRAL PARISH".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs...".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The streets along San Pedro Cathedral through C.M. Recto Avenue-Romas Extension (along Marco Polo)-Ponciano Street-Palma Gil Street-Legapi Street-Magallanes Street-Bonifacio Monument Rotunda then back to the Cathedral will be temporarily closed on June 28, 2018, from 3:00 p.m. onwards in celebration of the upcoming 170th Parochial Fiesta of San Pedro Cathedral Parish;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 25th day of June, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
