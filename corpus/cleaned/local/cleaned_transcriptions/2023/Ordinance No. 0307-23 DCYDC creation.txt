Ord. No. 0307-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE DAVAO CITY YOUTH DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL ORDINANCE".

SECTION 2. POLICY STATEMENT - The State recognizes the vital role of the youth in nation-building and thus, promotes and protects their physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual, and social well-being, inculcates in them patriotism, nationalism, and other desirable values, and encourages their involvement in public and civic affairs.

Towards this end, the State shall establish adequate, effective, responsive and enabling mechanisms and support system that will ensure the meaningful participation of the youth in local governance and in nation building.

Thus, it is the policy of the City Government of Davao to encourage the youth sector to participate in local governance and in nation building and thus, establish effective, responsive, and enabling mechanisms for the youth to partake in the promotion of the general welfare of the youth constituents of the City.

This Ordinance shall be guided by the pertinent provisions of Republic Act No. 10742, otherwise known as "The Sangguniang Kabataan Reform Act of 2015", as amended by Republic Act No. 11768, which states that "to ensure wide and multi-sectoral youth participation in local governance, there shall be in every province, city and municipality a Local Youth Development Council ("LYDC").

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms are hereby defined:

(a) Davao City Youth Development Council (DCYDC) - shall refer to Davao City's multi-sectoral youth organization/council headed by the SK Pedersyon President and composed of representatives of youth and youth-serving organizations in the City;

(b) Davao City Youth Development Division (DCYDD) - the Local Youth Development Office (LYDO) of Davao City, herein referred to as the "DCYDD-LYDO", shall refer to Davao City's youth development office created in accordance with Republic Act No. 10742, otherwise known as "The Sangguniang Kabataan Reform Act of 2015", as amended, and as created by the applicable Ordinance of the City of Davao;

(c) Davao City Youth Development Plan (DCTYP) - shall refer to Davao City's plan as initially drafted by the SK Pedersyon, finalized by DCYCD and approved by the Sanggunian. DCTYP shall be anchored on the local development plans of the City Government of Davao and, as far as practicable, the Philippine Youth Development Plan (PYDP);

(d) Local Chief Executive (LCE) - shall refer to the City Mayor of Davao City;

(e) National Youth Commission (NYC) - shall refer to the National Youth Commission created under Republic Act No. 8044;

(f) Representative-members - shall refer to the representatives of the Youth Organizations or Youth-Serving Organizations elected as a sitting member of DCYDC;

(g) Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) - shall refer to the Sangguniang Kabataan of Davao City;

--- [Page 3] ---
(h) SK Pedersyon shall refer to the Pedersyon ng Sangguniang Kabataan of Davao City;

(i) Youth shall refer to those persons whose ages range from fifteen (15) to thirty (30) years old as defined under Republic Act No. 8044;

(j) Youth Organizations shall refer to those organizations whose membership and/or composition are the youth;

(k) Youth-Serving Organizations shall refer to those registered organizations whose principal programs, projects and activities are youth-oriented and youth-related.

ARTICLE II THE DAVAO CITY YOUTH DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL (DCYDC)

SECTION 4. CREATION - The Davao City Youth Development Council (DCYDC) is hereby created which shall assist in the planning and execution of projects and programs of the SK and the SK Pedersyon.

SECTION 5. COMPOSITION - (a) DCYDC shall be headed by the SK Pedersyon President and shall be composed of at least ten (10), but not more than twenty-one (21) members including the two (2) core members of the SK. The two (2) core members of the SK shall be composed of the President and the Vice-President of the SK Pedersyon.

(b) The two (2) core members of the SK shall be supported by at least eight (8) but not more than nineteen (19) representative-members from youth organizations or youth-serving organizations registered under the provisions of this Ordinance, provided that, all other incumbent SK officials shall not be eligible as members of DCYDC. The representative-members shall serve for three (3) years unless removed for cause.

(c) As far as practicable, youth organizations and youth-serving organizations shall include, but not limited to, those whose core advocacies involve peace, out-of-school youth, health, disaster, LGBTQI, Islam, faith, environment, community, PWD, indigenous peoples, women, economic and academic.

SECTION 6. QUALIFICATION OF THE REPRESENTATIVE-MEMBERS - A representative-member from a registered youth organization or youth-serving organization of DCYDC must be a citizen of the Philippines, a resident of Davao City for not less than one (1) year immediately preceding the day of his or her election to DCYDC, at least fifteen (15) years but not more than thirty (30) years on the day of his or her election to the council, able to read and write Filipino, English, or the local dialect, and must not have been convicted by final judgment of any crime involving moral turpitude.

SECTION 7. DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS - DCYDC shall discharge the following duties and functions:

1. Assist in the planning and execution of projects and programs of the Sangguniang Kabataan Pedersyon;

2. Serve as the core of the advocacy on youth participation in nation-building and youth empowerment;

3. Finalize the three (3) year Davao City Youth Development Plan (DCYDF) that is anchored in the development plans of the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Youth Development (PYDP);

--- [Page 4] ---
The DCYDP shall be initially drafted by the SK Pedersayon and shall be finalized by the DCLRC. This shall be submitted to the Local Chief Executive for inclusion in the Local Development Plan and subsequently endorsed by the Sanggunian Panglungsod of Davao City. These shall give rise to the following projects, activities, and initiatives that will promote and ensure the meaningful youth participation anchored on the nine (9) centers for youth participation namely: health; education; economic empowerment; social inclusion and equity; peace-building and security; governance; active citizenship; environment; and global mobility;

4. Monitor and evaluate the implementation of the Davao City Youth Development Plan;

5. Recommend policies to the local council;

6. Perform such other functions as may be prescribed by law or Ordinance.

The DCYDC shall formally convene within one hundred (100) days to discuss and formulate the DCYDP. As far as practicable, the DCYDC shall complete the DCYDP within one (1) year from the date of the initial convening or within the time limit as may be set by DILG and/or NYC.

SECTION 8. MEETINGS AND QUORUM - (a) The SK Pedersayon President shall regularly convene the DCYDC quarterly to conduct consultations with youth organizations and youth-serving organizations. The SK Pedersayon President, or in his/her absence, the SK Vice-President shall convene the council and shall preside over its meetings.

(b) The DCYDC shall schedule the quarterly regular meeting, and may schedule any meetings as often as needed. If the SK Pedersayon President or Vice-President fails to convene as scheduled, at least one-third (1/3) of the total membership of the DCYDC may call for the meeting. In all instances, a written notice shall be sent, either through traditional, non-traditional, or electronic means, to all members setting the date, time, place, and agenda of the meeting. A written notice shall be received at least two (2) days before the intended date of meeting.

(c) The presence of the majority of all members of the DCYDC shall constitute a quorum.

SECTION 9. ELECTION OF REPRESENTATIVE-MEMBERS - Within forty-five (45) days after the SK elections, the DCYDD-LYDO shall notify all presidents or authorized representative of youth organizations or youth-serving organizations duly registered under this Ordinance of the date, time, and venue of elections of the representatives of DCYDC. The elections may be made by consensus or secret balloting. They shall choose at least eight (8) but not more than nineteen (19) representatives who shall serve for a term of three (3) years in the DCYDC.

A youth organization or youth-serving organization shall only have one (1) representative-member in the DCYDC.

Should the representative-member fail to assume office, voluntarily resigns, dies, is permanently incapacitated, or is removed from office, the youth or youth-serving organization shall immediately submit to DCYDD-LYDO the name of a substitute representative who shall have all the qualifications and none of the disqualifications for the said office and shall assume the vacant position for the unexpired portion of the term.

The DCYDD-LYDO shall facilitate the election of the representative-members through election rules and regulations that it may promulgate taking into consideration the guidelines

**ARTICLE III**

REGISTRATION OF YOUTH ORGANIZATIONS AND YOUTH-SERVING ORGANIZATIONS

**SECTION 10. CONDITION PRECEDENT -** Youth organizations and youth-serving organizations intending to participate in the DCYDC must be qualified, duly registered, and verified under the provisions of this Ordinance. Local registration and verification of youth organization and youth-serving organization shall be a condition precedent for participation in the DCYDC.

**SECTION 11. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS -** All youth organizations and youth-serving organizations intending to participate in the DCYDC must have at least ten (10) members in good standing and must be operating within Davao City. They shall register with the Davao City Youth Development Division – the Local Youth Development Office (LYDO) of Davao City or DCYDD-LYDO. Youth and youth-serving organizations registered in other national agencies shall also register and may be recognized as registered organizations by DCYDD-LYDO, provided that, these organizations operate within Davao City.

**SECTION 12. LOCAL REGISTRATION AND VERIFICATION OF YOUTH ORGANIZATIONS AND YOUTH-SERVING ORGANIZATIONS -** (a) All youth organizations and youth-serving organizations intending to participate in the DCYDC shall submit a verified application with DCYDD-LYDO. The application must be signed by the President/Executive Director of the organization and shall include all pertinent information of such organization including, but not limited to, organization's name, official address, official e-mail, telephone and/or mobile numbers, list of officers and members, sector and/or advocacy or advocacys represented, registration information from NYC, if any, and other pertinent information or documents that DCYDD-LYDO may require. It shall also include a statement that:

(i) the organization intends to participate with DCYDD-LYDO;

(ii) the organization has at least ten (10) members in good standing;

(iii) has been in operation in Davao City for at least one (1) year;

(iv) has all the qualifications and none of the disqualifications to be registered as such organization;

(v) must be registered with the Youth Organization Registration Program (YORP) or if not yet registered, shall execute an undertaking that the organization shall register with YORP, with the assistance of DCYDD-LYDO, within three (3) months from the time of local application for registration;

(vi) should be elected as part of the DCYDC, it shall undertake to designate a representative-member who has all the qualifications and none of the disqualifications to become such member.

(b) Upon receipt of the application, DCYDD-LYDO shall verify the registration information provided by these organizations. After verification that the information given are correct, DCYDD-LYDO shall accord the organization, shall issue a certificate to such end, and shall entitle the organization to be elected to be part of the DCYDC. The DCYDD-LYDO shall also notify the YORP of its registrations and verified youth organizations and youth-serving organizations to NYC.

(c) The youth organizations and youth-serving organizations shall renew their registration with DCYDD-LYDO every three (3) years from the date of their accreditation. DCYDD-LYDO shall adopt a continuing registration procedure taking into consideration the budgetary and manpower requirements, the presence of youth and youth-serving organizations, and the ease of doing business policies of the City Government of Davao. The youth organization and youth-serving organization must remain in good standing by submission of its annual report with DCYDD-LYDO on or before the last working day of February of the succeeding year covered by such annual report.

ARTICLE IV
CAPABILITY-BUILDING AND ORIENTATION TOWARDS NATION-BUILDING AND EMPOWERMENT

SECTION 13. MANDATORY AND CONTINUING TRAINING - (a) For the purpose of emphasizing the role of the youth in nation-building and molding them to become better citizens with the values of patriotism, nationalism and honor as Filipino citizens, the representative members of the DCYDC shall undergo mandatory training programs before they can assume office. During their incumbency, they shall attend continuing programs in the areas of leadership, program and project development, sustainability, financial management, and accountability and transparency. In coordination with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DIILG), deliberate failure to attend said training programs shall constitute sufficient ground to disqualify said representative member or subject them to disciplinary actions.

(b) The Mandatory Training Programs shall include the following components:

(1) Philippine cultural history, political systems, ethics and ideologies;

(2) The Filipino youth and its role in nation-building; and (b) capability building on leadership, program and project development and sustainability, financial management, and accountability and transparency.

ARTICLE V
TRANSITORIAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 14. TRANSITORIAL PROVISIONS - (a) All registered youth and youth-serving organizations under existing executive orders, rules and regulations of existing Local Youth Development Office and/or by the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) of the City Government of Davao shall continue to be valid, subject to the provisions on registration renewal and continuing registration under this Ordinance.

(b) The representative members of the Local Youth Development Council created by virtue of Executive Order No. 16-2021 shall continue to hold office until such time their successors shall have been elected following the provisions of this Ordinance after the next Sanggunan Kabataan elections.

SECTION 15. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS (IRR) - The SK Pedersayan, together with CSWDO, shall issue the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) necessary for the effective implementation of this Ordinance within one hundred twenty (120) days from the effectiveness hereof.

SECTION 16. SEPARABILITY CAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining sections or provisions hereof not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.
