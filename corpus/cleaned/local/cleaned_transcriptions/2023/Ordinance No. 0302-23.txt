ORDINANCE NO. 0302-23
Series of 2023

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AUTHORITY TO GEM MASTER & SONS REALTY CORP. TO OPERATE A PRIVATELY-OWNED PUBLIC MARKET AT KM. 14, PANACAN, THIS CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0302-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AUTHORITY TO GEM MASTER & SONS REALTY CORP. TO OPERATE A PRIVATELY-OWNED PUBLIC MARKET AT KM. 14, PANACAN, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. PERTINENT PROVISIONS UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160 OR THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991

"Section 45B. Powers, Duties, Functions and Compensation.
(a) The Sangguniang Panlungsod, as the legislative body of the city, shall enact ordinances, pass resolutions, and appropriate funds for the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code and in the proper exercise of the corporate powers of the city as provided for under Section 22 of this Code, and shall:
xxx
xxx
xxx
(3) Subject to the provisions of Book II of this Code, enact ordinances granting franchises and authorizing the issuance of permits or licenses, upon such conditions and for such purposes intended to promote the general welfare of the inhabitants of the city and pursuant to this legislative authority shall:
xxx
xxx
xxx
(vii) Upon approval by a majority vote of all the members of the sangguniang panlungsod: grant a franchise to an person, partnership, corporation, or cooperative to do business within the city; establish, construct, operate, maintain ferries, wharf, markets, or slaughterhouses; or undertake such other activities within the city as may be allowed by existing laws *x*x"

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - Gem Master & Sons Realty Corp., is hereby granted authority to operate a privately-owned public market at Km. 14, Panacan, this City, pursuant to the provisions under Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, otherwise known as the "2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao", for a period of five (5) years and renewable for another five (5) years upon approval of the City Council.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 28th day of September 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
