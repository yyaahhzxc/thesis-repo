Ord. No. 0291-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE OF JAIME RIVERA FOR THE PROPOSED FUNERAL HOMES (J.D. RIVERA FUNERAL HOMES) LOCATED AT KM. 14, PANACAN, BUNAWAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Article XI, Section 42, Major and/or Innovative Projects - Request for Additional Allowable Uses, of the Integrated Zoning Ordinance of Davao City 2019-2028 provides for requests of additional allowable use, which reads:

"The Sanggunian Panlungsod, upon application of project proponent and upon, favorable recommendation by Local Zoning Board of Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not enumerated hereunder as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3/4 majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance:

d. Funeral Parlors/Memorial Chapel/Mortuaries
e. Memorial Parks/Cemeteries Crematorium
f. Ossuaries xxx"

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST - The request for Additional Allowable Use of Jaime Rivera for the construction of a funeral home (J.D. Rivera Funeral Homes) at Km. 14, Panacan, Bunawan District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on September 12, 2023, by a 3/4 majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
