An Ordinance Granting Legislative Authority to the City Mayor to Enter into and Sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and the City of Davao for the launching of the Joint Project, Ronda Palengke

Ord. No. 0366-23

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 4. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY (DTI) AND THE CITY OF DAVAO FOR THE LAUNCHING OF THE JOINT PROJECT, RONDA PALENGE."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 455, paragraph (b) sub-paragraph (vi) of RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provide:

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation. xxx

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:
xxx
xxx
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and the City of Davao for the launching of the Joint Project, Ronda Palenge.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILIT Y CLAUSE - The provisions of these Rules are hereby declared separable, and in the event that anyone or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval. ENACTED, on December 14, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
