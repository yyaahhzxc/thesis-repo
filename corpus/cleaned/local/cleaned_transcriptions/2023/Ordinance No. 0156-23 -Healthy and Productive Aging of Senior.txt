AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A COMMUNITY-BASED PROGRAM FOR HEALTHY AND PRODUCTIVE AGEING OF SENIOR CITIZENS AND PROVIDING FREE PACKAGE OF ESSENTIAL HEALTH, WELLNESS AND OTHER EQUITABLE SERVICES TO PROMOTE THE QUALITY OF LIFE OF THE OLDER ADULTS, AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREOF

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "HEALTHY AND PRODUCTIVE AGING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The Local Government of Davao City acknowledges the mandate of the 1987 Philippine Constitution, particularly Article II, Section IX, which states that "The State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health, and other social services available to all people at affordable cost. There shall be priority for the needs of the underprivileged, sick, elderly, disabled, women, and children."

Furthermore, Republic Act No. 9994 or the "Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010" provides that a Healthy and Productive Ageing Program for senior citizens shall be provided. This program primarily aims to promote quality life among older persons and contribute to nation building through providing equitable access to quality healthcare.

In addition, relevant laws such as Republic Act No. 11223 or the "Universal Health Care Act," particularly Section 6 (a) states that "Every Filipino shall be granted immediate eligibility and access to preventive, promotive, curative, rehabilitative, and palliative care for medical, dental, and hospital services, but not health insurance based on a population-based or individual-based health services." Moreover, Republic Act No. 10645 or the Act for the Mandatory PhilHealth Coverage for all Senior Citizens states that "All senior citizens shall be covered by the national health insurance program of PhilHealth."

Lastly, Department of Health Administrative Order (DOH-AD) 2015-0009 or the "National Policy on the Health and Wellness Program for Senior Citizens" further solidifies the various legal mandates and directs the Local Government Units on organizing the service delivery networks in managing and implementing the health and wellness program for senior citizens' activities, passage of necessary local legislation to implement the activities and provide counterpart funds for implementing their investment plan that supports the healthy and productive aging of the senior citizens (a.k.a. older adults).

The City also affirms the value of the Filipino tradition of caring for the older adults who also took care of us and in recognizing their contribution to the community as we look upon for their guidance in making Davao City a better place to live in.

The City recognizes the valuable contribution of the older adults, and commits to the improvement of their well-being by providing a "Healthy and Productive Ageing Program," a community-based program, exclusive for clients 60 years old and above, who are residents of Davao City. It shall cater basic health services needed by older adults and it shall be progressive and other health programs shall be integrated to meet the needs of the clientele.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS

a) Ageing - at the biological level, is a process resulting from the impact of the accumulation of a wide variety of molecular and cellular damage overtime, which leads to gradual decrease in physical and mental capacity, a growing risk of disease and ultimately death (WHD).

b) Geriatrician – is a physician who is specially trained to evaluate and manage the unique healthcare needs and treatment preferences of older people. He/She is a Board-Certified Internist or Family Physician who has additional training and certifications in geriatrics.

c) Healthy and Productive Ageing Program – focuses on promoting the health and well-being of older adults and alleviating the conditions of older persons who are encountering degenerative diseases. This program primarily aims to promote quality life among older persons who contribute to nation building through providing each one access to quality healthcare and provide the necessary services to promote the quality of life of older persons.

d) Home Visitation – a professional, purposeful interaction that takes place in the family's residence aimed at promoting, maintaining or restoring the health of the family or its members.

e) Medical Nutrition and Diet Therapy (MNDT) – a key component of holistic nutrition education and management. MNDT is defined as a "nutrition-based treatment provided by a Registered Nutrionist-Dietitian". It includes a nutrition diagnosis as well as therapeutic and counselling services to help manage certain health conditions/diseases. Further providing medical nutrition and diet therapy through the application of nutrition care process for the purpose of disease prevention, promotion and management that promotes nutritional health and well-being of individuals, groups, communities and population.

f) Nutritional-Dietician or a Registered Nutritional-Dietitian (RND) – a licensed professional in the field of nutrition and dietetics that provides medical nutrition and diet therapy to people with certain health conditions through the application of the Nutrition Care Process (RA No. 10862, or the Nutrition and Dietetics Law of 2016).

g) Senior Citizens or older adults or advance-aged adults – refer to the following: (a) any resident citizen of the Philippines at least sixty (60) years old; and (b) must be registered to their respective Office of Senior Citizen Affairs (OSCA), and (c) must have an OSCA ID upon avallment of services.

SECTION 4. COVERAGE – The services shall be made available to the senior citizens in Davao City.

SECTION 5. BENEFICIARIES AND QUALIFICATIONS – The services shall be provided to persons who must possess the following qualifications:

1. Must be at least sixty (60) years old; and
2. Must be a holder of an original Senior Citizens Card (OSCA ID) issued by the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) of Davao City.

In the absence of the OSCA ID, a birth certificate or any government issued valid ID may be presented as proof, for validation of the identity and age of the beneficiary.

In case there is absence of proof of identity and age of the beneficiaries, they will be referred to the OSCA representative within the center for further appropriate assistance as necessary.

**SECTION 6. SERVICES - The DCHPAC shall serve as a community-based program and provide free basic medical services such as, but not limited to, the following:**

**Medical Services:**

- Comprehensive Geriatric Screening
- Medical Consultation
- Medicines, supplies, logistics, and assistive devices
- Vaccination
- Health education and promotion
- Consultation and Education on Medical Nutrition and Diet Therapy
- Oral Health Services
- Dementia preventive and care
- Early screening for cancer
- Physical fitness exercise
- Health and wellness promotion, and disease prevention
- Counseling
- Telemedicine/Teleconsulting
- Philippine Package of Essential Non-Communicable Disease Services (PhilPEN)
- Individual based health service package for older adults
- Others

**As a holistic approach, it shall cater not only to medical or physical needs, but shall also encompass mental and psychological needs of the older adults. It shall also cater to older adult clients through referral and appropriate programming under the TUPAD or "Tulong Panhangapbuhay sa Ating Disadvantaged or Displaced Workers" program of the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE).**

**As a Community-based program, there should be joint efforts and responsibilities among the health personnel, program partners, agencies, patients, and their respective families, allied medical team, and other related persons, as mentioned in Sections 8, 9, 10 and 11 of this Ordinance, within the community to achieve the objective and proper implementation of this Ordinance.**

**Home Visitations shall be done, as deemed necessary, initiated by the Public Health Physician, Public Health Nurse, Rural Health Midwife and/or other appropriate public health professionals with assistance from the Barangay Health Workers (BHW), Barangay Health Volunteers, Barangay Nutrition Scholars (BNS), and Social Workers where the patient resides.**

**A Service Delivery Network shall be established for purposes of referring services not covered by this Ordinance to appropriate agencies.**

**Upon the commencement of its operation, the initial fund mentioned in Section 12 of this Ordinance shall be used for Medical, Dental, and Operational services.**

**SECTION 7. CENTRAL OUTPATIENT GERIATRIC CENTER AND IMPLEMENTING DISTRICT HEALTH CENTERS - The City Health Office (CHO) shall be responsible for the overall implementation of the Davao City Healthy and Productive Aging Program (DCHPAP) including the deployment of its personnel provided in Section 8 of this Ordinance.**

**The Sta. Ana Health Center as a core site for the implementation of the program which shall provide a package of essential health services to older persons of Davao City. It shall be equipped with laboratory facilities to respond to the needs of patients therein.**

**The program shall be used as the primary out-patient service provider for geriatric services of the entire Davao City.**

It shall cater cases referred to it coming from different Health Districts who needs to be seen, assessed, and managed by a geriatric medicine specialist/geriatrician.

Geriatric Training of the healthcare personnel shall be provided or done prior to its operation.

In completing the one-stop shop approach, the City Social Welfare and Development Office - Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (CSWDO-OSCA) will provide a desk and human resources representative of their office in DCHPAC. They will be the main referral for non-health/medical related matters of the older adult. Their services may include the following, but not limited to:

* Registration of the Senior Citizen (if not yet registered);
* Orientation on the benefits for Senior Citizens;
* Orientation on the use of their USCAs Records Booklet;
* Orientation on the services of USCAs and DCHPAC;
* Information desk regarding services related to Senior Citizens;
* Conduct of referral within LGU and NGA as appropriate;
* Others

All health centers and barangay health stations with trained personnel on geriatric shall implement the National Healthy and Productive Ageing Program.

As part of Davao LGU's commitment to create a Senior Friendly and Supportive Environment for older adults, public spaces such as parks, walking lanes steppers as well as facilities (stationary bikes) shall be provided to promote physical activities (indoor and outdoor) and ensure safety among them.

SECTION 8. HUMAN RESOURCES REQUIREMENT, DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - The Davao City Healthy and Productive Ageing Center (DCHPAC) to be established in Sta. Ana Health Center shall have the following personnel:

a) Public Health Physician - There shall be one (1) Physician assigned and shall have the following duties and responsibilities:
* Formulates Annual Healthy and Productive Ageing Operational Plan;
* Supervises the overall operations of the center;
* Assesses and manages patients;
* Formulates referral criteria and establish two-way referral system;
* Navigates referral of cases (teleconsult/face-to-face) from the health district of DCHPAC to DCHPAC;
* Conducts regular monitoring and evaluation of the program implementation in DCHPAC.

b) Geriatric Medical Specialist (Geriatrician) - There shall be one (1) Geriatrician hired as a consultant or under a Contract of Service (contract renewable yearly) with the following duties and responsibilities:
* Conducts medical consultation once or twice weekly in DCHPAC to attend to cases needing specialist's care and management;
* Evaluates and manages cases with complicated medical and social problems and those patients referred from the different health districts for further/specialized assessment and management;
* Acts as adviser to the Healthy and Productive Ageing Program of Davao City;
* Functions as resource speaker in Healthy and Productive Training and refresher courses for the health care providers.

c) Dentist – There shall be one (1) Dentist and shall make dental services daily with the following duties and responsibilities:
• Provides oral checkup and teachings to clients;
• Performs tooth extraction;
• Performs oral prophylaxis/dental cleaning and dental scaling to clients;
• Performs dental filling or tooth restoration to clients;
• Performs dental mold/cast preparation for dentures and facilitate provision of dentures, if deemed necessary;
• And other tasks deemed essential.

d) Nutritionist- Dietician – There shall be one (1) Nutritionist-Dietician assigned and shall have the following duties and responsibilities:
• Provides adequate and comprehensible Basic Nutrition Education to the clients
• Prepares meal plan for the client;
• Counsels the client on the importance of Food Diary and healthy food intake;
• Monitors the practice of actual prescribed diet by the patient through the Food Diary.

e) Nurse/s – There shall be three (3) Nurses to be assigned in DCHPAC and shall have the following duties and responsibilities:
• Implement the National Healthy and Productive Ageing Program which encompasses but not limited to the following:
• Vital Signs taking;
• Perform Geriatric screening on the patient;
• Refer clients if with positive screening findings to the physician for Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment;
• Carry out medical orders and medical prescription;
• Provide Health Education;
• Monitor the progress of the patient and refer accordingly;
• Facilitate both internal referral to other service providers within the center as well as external referral to other levels of care and other agencies and institutions (both government and private);
• Conduct home visitation, if deemed necessary;
• Prepare and submit reports to the City Health Office;
• Supervise infection control and safety in the facility; and
• Perform other functions and activities related to the program.

f) Medical Technologist – There shall be two (2) Medical Technologists with the following functions:
• Responsible for the licensing of the Medical Laboratory in the Center;
• Perform laboratory tests requested; and
• Prepare and submit reports.

g) Social Worker – There shall be one (1) social worker to be assigned with the following duties and responsibilities:

1. Ensures the provision of budget for the effective implementation of the program;
2. Ensures that the ordinances and policies are enforced;
3. Advocates for the Older Adults or Aged - A friendly and supportive environment built for the older adults;
4. Develops legislation and policies to promote health and wellness of the older persons and improve quality of life, in partnership with other stakeholders and sectors;
5. Provides and supports equitable health financing to develop, implement, sustain, monitor, and continuously improve quality health and social programs accessible to the older persons;
6. Provides guidance and assistance in developing standards to ensure safety and accessibility of all health facilities;
7. Helps enhance the capacity of health providers and other stakeholders, including senior citizens group in the implementation of health programs;
8. Helps strengthen coordination and collaboration among government entities, non-government organizations, partner agencies and other stakeholders involved in the implementation of programs for senior citizens;
9. Helps strengthen the health systems to make them responsive to the needs of senior citizens through the provision of acceptable, accessible, available, and effective health services across continuum of care; and
10. Helps strengthen evidence-based policy and decision making for aging and health;

SECTION 10. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF OLDER ADULT CLIENTS,
RESPECTIVE FAMILIES AND OTHER MEMBERS OF THE HOUSEHOLD - The duties
and responsibilities of older adult clients, their respective families and other members of the
household are as follows:
• The client's family members and other members of the household shall
accompany their client as they come for medical consultation;
• The family member or caregiver shall be provided with basic knowledge on
geriatric syndromes and other health concerns of older persons, including its
implications to health, prevention and management;
• The family member/caregiver shall be taught or trained on the standard of
care for older persons to ensure continuum of care at home for better health
outcomes for older persons;
• The family member/caregiver shall be partner in promoting health and quality
of life of the older adults; and
• A social worker, BHW/BRS shall assist, if deemed necessary, the senior citizen
living alone for medical consultation or availing of medical services.

SECTION 11. ALLIED MEDICAL TEAM - The DCHPAC may accept students of
allied health and medical professions approved by the City Health Office through a
Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to undergo exposure trainings and related-learning
experiences in the center as well as in the community to develop knowledge, skills, attitudes,
values and competencies required of their respective profession. Existing policies and
protocols relative to students' trainings and/or affiliations in the center shall be followed.

SECTION 12. BUDGET ALLOCATION - For the establishment of DCHPAC, the City
Health of Davao shall allocate an initial amount of Five Million Pesos ($5,000,000.00) for its
establishment.

SECTION 13. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby
declared separable and in the event that anyone or more of such provisions are
declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 14. REPEALING CLAUSE - All ordinances, resolutions, rules or
regulations which are inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Ordinance are
hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 15. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in at least three (3) newspapers of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, on the 24th day of January 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members
of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
