Ord. No. 0309-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE CREATION OF THE PROJECT IMPLEMENTATION TEAM RELATIVE TO THE HOUSING PROJECT AGREEMENTS ENTERED INTO BY THE CITY WITH THE NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY".

SECTION 2. PERTINENT PROVISIONS UNDER THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991 -

"Section 16. General Welfare - Every local government unit shall exercise the power expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage the development of public health facilities and services, scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.-
(a) The city mayor, as chief executive of the city government, shall exercise such powers and perform such duties and functions as provided by this Code and other laws.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. COMPOSITION - To ensure the implementation of all housing projects entered into with the National Housing Authority (NHA), the Housing Project Implementation Team (HPT) is hereby created to be composed of the following:

1) Office of the City Planning and Development Coordinator
2) Office of the City Engineer
3) Office of the City Building Official
4) City Environment and Natural Resources Office
5) Davao City Water District
6) Davao Light and Power Company
7) National Housing Authority
8) Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development

For housing projects involving the Indigenous People, the HPIT may include the:

9) National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP); and
10) The concerned IP Tribe covered by the project.

The City Planning and Development Office, as the lead Office of the project, is hereby authorized to add members to the HPIT as the need arises.

SECTION 4. FUNCTIONS - The HPIT shall have the following functions, but not limited to:
a. Ensure the implementation of the various housing projects;
b. Coordinate with all stakeholders to achieve the completion of the projects;
c. Perform all other acts necessary to carry out the abovementated functions.

SECTION 5. FUNDING - The HPIT may be provided with an appropriate operating fund as may be legally allowable.

SECTION 6. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the creation of the Project Implementation Team relative to the Housing Project Agreements entered into by the City of Davao with the National Housing Authority.

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 10th day of October, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
