Ord. No. 0261-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND MALAGOS DAIRY COOPERATIVE".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Malagos Dairy Cooperative.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 18th day of July 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
