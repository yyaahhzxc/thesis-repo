Ord. No. 0300-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND OUR LADY OF LOURDES DAVAO MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE (OLDAMULCO)".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Our Lady of Lourdes Davao Multipurpose Cooperative (OLDAMULCO).

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 26th day of September 2023, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
