SIERRA MADRE TO SIERRA MADRE FROM 7:00 A.M. ON DECEMBER 23, 2023 UNTIL 7:00 P.M. ON DECEMBER 25, 2023 IN CONNECTION WITH THE YEARLY CELEBRATION OF THE CHRISTMAS GIFT-GIVING ACTIVITY

Ord. No. 0360-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE STREETS AT CENTRAL PARK AND ROYAL VALLEY SUBDIVISIONS SPECIFICALLY, 1. CENTRAL PARK AVENUE FROM MC ARTHUR HIGHWAY GOING TO SIERRA MADRE, 2. CORDILLERA ROAD TO MATUTUM ROAD FROM CORNER SIERRA MADRE, 3. MATUTUM ROAD TO TAAL ROAD FROM CORNER CORDILLERA ROAD, 4. ORTIZ ROAD TO BAILER ROAD, 5. SIERRA MADRE TO BULUSAN, 6. ARAYAT TO KANLUA, AND 7. PINATUBO ROAD FROM SIERRA MADRE FROM 7:00 A.M. ON DECEMBER 25, 2023 UNTIL 7:00 P.M. ON DECEMBER 25, 2023 IN CONNECTION WITH THE YEARLY CELEBRATION OF THE CHRISTMAS GIFT-GIVING ACTIVITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the yearly celebration of the Christmas Gift-Giving Activity, the abovementioned roads at Central Park and Royal Valley Subdivision are temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on the time and date herein mentioned.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 11th day of December 2023, by unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
