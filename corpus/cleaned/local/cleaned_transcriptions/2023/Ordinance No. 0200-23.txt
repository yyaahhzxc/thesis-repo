Ord. No. 0200-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING FULL EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX TO FIRE AND ICE MEDIA AND PRODUCTIONS, INC. ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR CONCERT DUBBED 'BECOMING ICE THE 35TH ANNIVERSARY CONCERT' ON APRIL 28, 2023 AT THE SMX CONVENTION CENTER, DAVAO CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 192 and 458 (a) (2) (xii) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that local government units, through an ordinance duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or relief under such terms and conditions as they may deem necessary.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - The aforementioned exemption shall be based on the system of computation adopted by the Business Tax and License Division of the City Treasurer's Office and their projected income statement, herein enumerated:

| AMOUNT | NO. OF TICKETS | TOTAL | 5% TAX |
|--------|---------------|-------|-------|
| SVIP    | P5,000.00      | 50   | P250,000.00 | P12,500.00 |
| VIP     | P4,500.00      | 80   | P360,000.00 | P18,000.00 |
| VIP38L  | P3,500.00      | 420  | P1,470,000.00| P73,500.00 |
| PATRON  | P3,000.00      | 420  | P1,470,000.00| P73,500.00 |
| GOLD LEFT| P2,800.00      | 140  | P392,000.00 | P19,600.00 |
| SILVER  | P1,200.00      | 700  | P840,000.00 | P42,000.00 |
| GEN AD  | P1,000.00      | 900  | P900,000.00 | P45,000.00 |
|        |               | 2,510 |       | P254,600.00 |

Total worth of tickets is P5, 092,000.00 x 5% = P254,600.00

The total amount of amusement tax charged is TWO HUNDRED FIFTY-FOUR THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED PESOS (P254,600.00).

SECTION 4. TRANSMITTAL - The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is hereby directed to transmit a copy of the said Ordinance to the Executive Department immediately upon its approval.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 25th day of April 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
