SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
CREATING THE DAVAO CITY OFFICE FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES. Davao City is
committed to the promotion of culture and arts and adheres to the following national
principles and policies:

(a) Article XIV of the Philippine Constitution mandates that the state shall foster
the preservation, enrichment and dynamic evolution of a Filipino national culture based
on the principle of unity in diversity in a climate of free artistic and intellectual
expression. It further ensures that the arts shall enjoy the patronage of the state and it
shall conserve, promote, popularize the nation's historical and cultural heritage and
resources as well as artistic creations.

(b) Article V, Section 14(b) of Republic Act No. 10066, otherwise known as
the National Cultural Heritage Act of 2009, stipulates that local government units,
through their cultural offices, shall maintain an inventory of cultural property under its
jurisdiction.

(c) Philippine Development Plan 2017-2022, which includes the promotion of
Philippine Culture and Values, highlights the establishment of an office for culture and
the arts in the local government units, with a mandate to protect and promote local
cultural heritage and arts.

(d) The Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) requires all
local government units to comply with the following Essential Areas in Cultural Heritage
Promotion and Conversation: presence of a council or office for the promotion of culture
and arts; budget appropriated for the conservation and preservation of cultural
property; and availability of updated cultural property inventory in the local government
unit.

(e) Section 3(h) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local
Government Code of 1991, provides that local government units shall have a continuing
mechanism to enhance local autonomy not only by legislative enabling acts but also by
administrative and organizational reforms. Section 76 of the same Code further states
that every local government unit shall design and implement its own organizational
structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and
financial capability subject to minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil
Service Commission (CSC).

Heritage
The full range of our inherited traditions, monuments, objects, and culture. Most important, it is the range of contemporary activities, meanings, and behaviors that we draw from them. Culture
The set of distinctive spiritual, material, intellectual, and emotional features of society or a social group that encompasses, not only art and literature, but lifestyles, ways of living together, value systems, traditions, and beliefs. (UNESCO, 2001:3)
Art
Something that is created with imagination and skill and that is beautiful or that expresses important ideas or feelings. (Britannica)

Creative City
A city that identifies creativity as a strategic factor for sustainable urban development; placing creativity and cultural industries at the heart of its development plans at the local level and cooperating actively at the international level. (UNESCO Creative Cities Network)

SECTION 4. CREATION OF THE DAVAO CITY OFFICE FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS. The Office for Culture and the Arts shall be created within the City Government of Davao.
(a) The Office for Culture and the Arts shall be created as a Division under the Office of the City Mayor;
(b) The Office for Culture and the Arts shall be headed by a Development Management Officer IV with Salary Grade 22;
(c) A Development Management Officer III with Salary Grade 18 shall serve as assistant head of the Office for Culture and the Arts;
(d) The Office shall have the following Sections:
1. Cultural Heritage Section
2. Cultural Dissemination Section
3. Arts Section
4. Cultural Communities and Traditional Arts Section
5. Administrative Section

SECTION 5. POWERS, DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF THE OFFICE FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS AS A DIVISION UNDER THE OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR. The Division shall be the primary office dedicated to activating and sustaining Davao City through culture and arts and realizing its vision to become a "Creative City." Specifically, it will have the following functions:

--- [Page 4] ---
Page 4 of 15

(a) Promotes balanced and sustainable development by making sure creativity and cultural industries are mainstreamed in the City's development plans;

(b) Conserves and promotes the City's historical and cultural heritage;

(c) Preserves and integrates traditional culture and its diverse creative expressions as a dynamic part of the City's mainstream culture;

(d) Encourages and facilitates the organization of a network of culture and arts groups that will actively participate in the formulation and implementation of plans, programs, projects and activities related to culture and arts in the city;

(e) Advises the City Mayor on matters related to culture and the arts;

(f) Serves as the Secretariat of the Davao City Council for Culture and the Arts;

(g) Serves as the city government's liaison to the National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA) and other cultural agencies, international, national, and local;

(h) Administers the Davao City Endowment Fund for Culture and the Arts and coordinate with the allocation of resources from the city government in support of the artists and cultural groups which contribute significantly to the cultural legacy as means to extend artistic achievement including the development, protection, preservation and dissemination of the City's culture and the arts;

(i) Secures funds and generate resources from international and national sources and donors to support approved plans, programs, projects, and activities related to culture and the arts;

(j) Performs other functions as may be necessary for the promotion of Davao City's culture and the arts, including organizing, planning, and implementing the major festivals and celebrations of the city.

SECTION 6. DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF THE SECTIONS. The duties and functions of the three Sections under the Division shall be as follows:

(a) Cultural Heritage Section

1. Conducts and maintains an inventory of cultural property under the jurisdiction of the City of Davao in coordination with Museo Dabawenyo;

2. Supports, monitors, and systematizes the retrieval and conservation of artifacts of Davao culture and history in coordination with Museo Dabawenyo;

3. Encourages and supports the study, recognition, preservation, and promotion of local human cultural resources such as weavers, chanters, dancers, craftspeople and artisans;

--- [Page 5] ---

Ord. No. 0332-23

4. Supports and promotes the establishment and preservation of cultural and historical landmarks, monuments, names, and sites in the City;
5. Encourages and supports the creation and maintenance of museums, libraries, archives, galleries, and cultural centers by government and private sectors;
6. Promotes and supports the research and writing of Davao history from the perspective of the multi-cultural peoples of Davao;
7. Develops and supports programs, projects, and activities that promote Davao's history and culture;
8. Performs other functions as directed.

(b) Cultural Dissemination Section
1. Ensures the widest dissemination of artistic and cultural products among the greatest number within Davao City and beyond for their appreciation and enjoyment;
2. Promotes the creation and development of an intensified arts education program at all levels of the educational system, public and private, to ensure meaningful arts integration across the school curriculum;
3. Encourages and supports programs through publication, exhibition, production, performance, staging, and reproduction of original Davao and Mindanao creations;
4. Provides technical and financial assistance for cultural events and related activities such as festivals, competitions, seminars, and others in the City;
5. Promotes the popularization of information about artistic and cultural achievements and programs, in coordination with government agencies and non-government organizations and institutions;
6. Reorients tourism programs to become an instrument for popular education to promote Davao's history, cultural heritage, and creativity;
7. Undertakes systematic collection of data related to the state of cultural conditions in the City to serve as evidence-based inputs to the formulation of cultural policies and programs;
8. Creates and supports international and national cultural exchange, scholarships, and travel grants and other related programs.

(c) Arts Section
1. Promotes a culture of creativity and the exercise of freedom of expression in the City;
2. Promotes the interest and welfare of artists and cultural workers in the City;

--- [Page 6] ---

3. Proposes measures and recommends legislation to protect the intellectual and artistic rights and properties of Davao artists;
4. Engages with and encourages the active involvement of individual artists and artists' groups in making Davao a "Creative City," which shall cover, but will not be limited to, the following areas: visual arts, literary arts, architecture, dramatic arts, broadcast arts, musical arts, dance, and film.
5. Ensures the broad, inclusive, and equitable access of all local artists to support, services, and resources from the city government;
6. Develops a recognition program for artistic achievement through awards, incentives, and services to artists and cultural workers who contribute significantly to Davao City's cultural legacy;
7. Develops a local arts program for integration in formal education and for non-formal settings to nurture future generations of Davao artists;
8. Performs other functions as directed.

(d) Cultural Communities and Traditional Arts Section

1. Promotes, nurtures, and supports traditional artists and artisans in the City through establishment of schools of living tradition and cultural centers in cultural communities;
2. Encourages and supports cultural research on the City's folklore, dance, music, and crafts;
3. Develops training programs for students, teachers, and cultural workers to practice and perform traditional arts from the City's diverse cultural communities;
4. Engages with and encourages the active involvement of cultural communities and traditional artists in developing plans and programs that will include and mainstream traditional arts in Davao City's development plan towards making it a "Creative City;"
5. Ensures the broad, inclusive, and equitable access of all cultural communities and traditional artists to support, services, and resources from the city government;
6. Documents and helps preserve traditional arts from all of the City's cultural communities for future generations;
7. Promotes cultural sensitivity among residents of and visitors of Davao City to ensure that the rights and welfare of the City's diverse cultural communities are protected and respected;
8. Performs other functions as directed.

(e) Administrative Section

1. Provides assistance to other sections/units on all administrative matters;

2. Assists other sections in the preparation of documents and other official activities conducted by the office;
3. Performs others tasks that may be assigned by immediate superiors pursuant to the objectives of this Ordinance.

**SECTION 11. CREATION OF THE DAVAO CITY CULTURE AND ARTS COUNCIL:** In accordance with the issuance of revised Memorandum Circular No. 202-21 by the Department of the Interior and Local Government, entitled "Creation of the Local Culture and Arts Council" and in consonance with City Ordinance No. 0194-04, providing for, among others, the Creation of the Davao City Culture and Arts Council. The City Mayor shall constitute a multi-cultural body composed of members of the Davao City Council that shall adequately represent all classifications of the arts as well as the City's major cultural communities. The members of the council will serve coterminously with the City Mayor.

SECTION 13. CREATION OF BARANGAY CULTURE AND ARTS COUNCIL.
To ensure the broadest participation possible and to promote Barangay empowerment in culture and arts development, the Barangay Culture and Arts Council (BCAC) in all barangays in Davao City shall be created.

The BCAC shall be composed of government and private sector representatives with 40 percent coming from the government and 60 percent from the private sector.

The membership to the council shall be chosen collectively through a resolution of the Barangay Council and appointed by the Barangay Chair. A Barangay Committee on Culture and Arts shall likewise be created and an SK Chairperson to head it shall be designated by the Barangay Council.

The officers shall be elected by and among the members of the Council and they will be as follows:

Other Representatives as decided by the Barangay Council

The Chair shall sit in such capacity for the duration of his/her term as Barangay Chair and the rest of the members shall hold office co-terminus with the tenure of the Chair.

The main functions of the BCAC shall be as follows:
(a) Identify historical and cultural heritage sites, landmarks, monuments, and other cultural properties within the barangay;
(b) Undertake steps to promote and preserve the identified cultural heritage sites;

(d) Develop plans and programs that will promote culture and the arts in their barangay;

SECTION 14. THE CITY ENDOWMENT FUND FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS. A City Endowment Fund for Culture and the Arts (the Fund) is hereby established exclusively for Davao culture and arts programs, projects, and activities all over the city.

The private portion of the fund shall be raised from donations and other conveyances including funds, materials, property, and services by gratuitous title. Contribution to the fund shall be deductible for tax purposes.

No portion of the Fund shall be used for administrative and operational expenses of the Office for Culture and the Arts. All funds appropriated and raised under the Endowment Fund must go to programs, projects, activities, and grants to artists and cultural workers.

SECTION 15. APPROPRIATION. The City shall appropriate Ten Million Pesos (Pta10,000,000.00) for the first-year operation of the Davao City Office for Culture and the Arts and subsequent annual budget subject to the accounting rules and regulations.

SECTION 16. REPEALING CLAUSE. All other laws, decrees, orders, issuances, rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 17. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If, for any reason, any part of the provision of this Ordinance shall be held to be unconstitutional or invalid, or parts or provisions thereof which are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 18. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.
