Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT (DSWD) FOR THE 13TH CYCLE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE SUPPLEMENTARY FEEDING PROGRAM."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY- (1) Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:
"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
xxx xxx xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx xxx xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY- The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) for the 13th cycle implementation of the supplementary feeding program.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 28th day of November 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II)

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL PERSONS BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement ("MOA") made and entered into this day of 2023 in by and between:

The DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT ("DSWD"), a national government agency created pursuant to Republic Act No. 5417 (Social Welfare Act of 1968), through its FIELD OFFICE XI, with office address at Magaysay Avenue, corner Damaso Suazo Street, Davao City, and represented by its REGIONAL DIRECTOR, ATTY. VENESSA B. GOC-ONG, hereinafter referred to as the "DSWD"; and

The LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT OF DAVAO CITY with address at San Pedro Street, Poblacion District, Davao City herein represented by its LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE, SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the "LGU";

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Supplementary Feeding Program (SFP) intends to provide food in addition to the regular meals to target children ages 2-5 years old enrolled in the Child Development Centers (CDCs) and Supervised Neighborhood Play (SNPs) as part of the Development Component of the early childhood care and development (ECCD) program of the government;

WHEREAS, the Department of Education (DepEd) and the DSWD, in consultation with other National Government Agencies (NGAs), Local Government Units (LGUs), Non-Government Organizations (NGOs), and development partners, are mandated by Republic Act (RA) No. 11037, also known as "Masaysanang Pagkain Para sa Batang Pilipino Act," to promulgate their respective rules and regulations for the efficient and effective implementation of the National Feeding Program for Public Day Care Centers (PDCCs) or CDCs, public kindergarten and elementary schools, pursuant to Section 4 of the said law to address undernutrition among Filipino children;

WHEREAS, the DSWD, in coordination with the LGUs, shall implement an SFP for undernourished children with ages two to five (5) years which shall include the provision of at least one (1) fortified meal for a period of not less than one hundred twenty (120) days in a year under the Section 4(a) of RA No. 11037;

WHEREAS, the General Appropriation Act of 2023 provided that the target outcome indicator of SFP is 80% of malnourished children in CDCs and SNPs with improved nutritional status;

WHEREAS, the implementation of the SFP will help address the problem of hunger and food security among Filipino children and prevent a decline in the current nutritional status of the beneficiaries due to the limitation in the purchasing power of most families as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic's extended effects and food commodity inflation;

WHEREAS, Section 11 of RA No. 11321 otherwise known as the "Saajgo Saka Act" states that the National and Local Government Agencies shall directly purchase agricultural and fishery products from farmers and fisher folk cooperatives and enterprises provided that

--- [Page 5] ---
The said agricultural and fishery products are necessary in the performance of their respective mandates. The procurement by the national and local government agencies shall be exempt from the application of the bidding process prescribed under relevant government procurement laws provided that said agencies shall undergo a negotiated procurement under the applicable guidelines of the Government Procurement Policy Board;

WHEREAS, the DSWD adopted alternative modes of implementation such as distribution of dry rations, nutpacks, and nutrition with fresh milk to address issues on the continuous delivery of services despite the shift of the ECCD sessions from face-to-face to virtual learning;

WHEREAS, LGUs, as partners of the DSWD in the expeditious delivery of the social amelioration program, shall also assist in the implementation of SFP;

WHEREAS, by virtue of Sanggunining Resolution No., the LGU agrees to enter into a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with the DSWD for the implementation of the SFP, and further authorizes the LCE to represent the LGU and sign this MOA;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties agree to undertake the program under the following terms and conditions:

ARTICLE I

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES

Section 1. The DSWD shall:

a. Manage, coordinate, and supervise the regional implementation of the SFP;
b. Designate a Focal Person for the SFP to provide guidance and technical assistance to the Local Social Welfare & Development Officers, CDC/SNP workers, Day Care Workers Federation (DCWV) and Day Care Parent Group (DCPG) in the implementation of the Program;
c. Inform the LGU of the conduct of the 120 feeding days or, if needed, to conduct twice-a-day feeding implementation, subject to the availability of funds;
d. Determine the amount of Seventy Thousand Seven Hundred Fourteen Thousand Six Hundred Twenty-Two Pesos Only (Php 77,114,520.00) to the Province/City/Municipal LGU based on pertinent provision of Commission on Audit (COA) Circular 94-01 dated December 13, 1994, COA Circular No. 2006-002 dated May 31, 2016 and 2017-002 dated October 25, 2017;
e. Closely monitor the fund management, utilization, program implementation and support activities of the systems of the LGUS, DCWV, DCPG, SNP Parents Group, CDC, DCWV, or SNP Workers;
f. Consolidate the LGU report and submit monthly physical accomplishment and financial report on the utilization of funds to the DSWD Central Office-Program Management Bureau;
g. Conduct a regional evaluation at the end of the implementation period of each cycle.

Section 2. The LGU shall:

a. Update the database of children beneficiaries of the program and submit encoded master list of beneficiaries to the DSWD using the ECCD-IS;
b. Identify children-beneficiaries based on the Operation Tableau Results or may conduct anthropometric measurement of children beneficiaries in the Child Development Centers / Supervised Neighborhood Play / Feeding Extension Centers;

--- [Page 6] ---
c. Submit a project proposal and a signed master list of beneficiaries as the basis for funding allocation for the 13th cycle (Calendar Year 2023) and the 120 feeding days will be in the form of hot meals and alternative meals to be served during snack/meal to the children minimum of five (5) and maximum of seven (7) a day's week or the twice-a-day feeding provision for the identified beneficiaries, if necessary. The LGU shall include in the project proposal the mode of implementation they will use based on the type of food items to be procured.

d. Designate the Provincial/City/Municipal Social Welfare Development Officer (P/C/MSWD) who will act as the Focal Person for the feeding with the task of providing guidance, technical assistance, and support to the DCWF/DCSPG in coordination with the Provincial/City/Municipal Nutrition Action Officer (P/C/MNAO) and include concerns relative to the SFP in Local Nutrition Committee (LNC) meetings;

As the Focal Person, the P/C/M Social Welfare Development Officer shall:

d.1 Provide administrative and technical assistance in the operationalization of the SFP;
d.2 Oversee the activities of Parents Committee in each CDC, SNP, or in the assigned barangay;
d.3 Issue Official Receipt to the DSWD Field Office XI as acknowledgment of the fund transfer;
d.4 Ensure that funds intended for the DCWF/DCSPG and/or parents/guardians shall be solely for the latter's feeding operation;
d.5 Conduct the submission of all the required documents as basis for funding allocation; and
d.6 Maintain a separate subsidiary record for each account.

e. Purchase goods in accordance with RA No. 9184 (Government Procurement Reform Act), and resolutions, such as but not limited to the Government Procurement Policy Board Resolution No. 28-2021, as well as other applicable budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and regulations;
f. Prepare the cash advance for the respective District Heads to be released to the Child Development Workers in purchasing rice and rice commodities. Likewise the Child Development Workers should submit the exact amount of Official Receipt per beneficiary to the DSWD Field Office XI;
g. Lead the distribution of the procured items to the Child Development Centers (if applicable);
h. Implement the actual feeding in the Child Development Centers, Supervised Neighborhoods or Feeding Extension Centers;
i. Replacement of beneficiaries is allowed only if a child can still benefit the 90 days feeding program to gain benefits on their nutritional status.
j. Tap the City/Municipal Nutrition Committee to assist in monitoring and providing technical assistance to the implementers of the program. The City/Municipal Health Office may also assist in providing Vitamin A Supplementation of the DOH developed according to the target beneficiaries (i.e., children aged 6 months to 5 years).
k. Conduct Enhanced Parent Effectiveness Session and encouraged the parents to attend the nutrition education session and ensure application of learning in their home;
l. Provide cash or in-kind augmentation, whenever necessary, in the implementation of the feeding program at city, municipal, and barangay level if they catered beyond the target number of beneficiaries and other SF-related activities, as mandated by Section 7 of RA No. 11037;
m. In the event that there are savings which resulted from the procurement process, the LGU shall inform the DSWD who shall assess the SFP and shall determine and approve if such savings may be used for any of the following: a.) the extension of feeding program prioritizing the undernourished children beneficiaries; b.) the Page 3 of 5

--- [Page 7] ---
purchase of non-food items (cooking and eating utensils or anthropomorphic equipment) or c.) the return or remittance of the unexpendable savings;
n. Submit monthly Report of Checks issued and Report of Disbursements within thirty (30) days after the end of each month and submit full liquidation thirty (30) days after the completion of the program. The report shall be certified by the accountant and approved by the Head of Province/City/Municipal LGU in accordance with the Commission's Guidelines for the Implementation of the General Appropriations Act, 2007 Circular No. 2016-002 dated May 31, 2018, and 2017-002 dated October 25, 2017;
o. Return all the excess funds to the DSWD upon the completion of the project and within thirty (30) days after the completion of the program;
p. Consolidate and submit a monthly physical and financial accomplishment report on SPP implementation to the DSWD for consolidation and furnish the Provincial Office with a copy thereof;
q. Open and maintain the accounts maintained for inspection of any authorized representative of the DSWD and/or the auditor in-charge in accordance with existing COA regulations;
ARTICLE II ACCOUNTABILITY
This agreement shall be observed and complied with by the PARTIES upon receipt of the fund transferred to the LGU, and shall be implemented for 120 feeding days for the 13th Cycle within the applicable school year, and in accordance with the terms and conditions stipulated herein including the conduct of the twice-a-day feeding, if applicable. Failure on the part of any party to comply with the provisions hereof shall result in the issuance of administrative and/or criminal actions against responsible officers and employees of the erring party.
Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising from this MOA shall be settled amicably between the parties. In the event that the dispute remains unresolved the Parties shall submit the issue for arbitration and adjudication in accordance with the law.
ARTICLE III AMENDMENTS
The MOA may be amended or revoked only upon mutual agreement by both parties, provided that all amendments shall be covered by a Supplemental Agreement signed by both Parties, which shall be deemed incorporated as integral parts hereof.
ARTICLE IV SEPARABILITY CLAUSE
If any of the provisions contained in this Agreement shall be declared by any court of competent jurisdiction as invalid, illegal, or unenforceable in any respect, the validity, legality, and enforceability of the remaining provisions contained herein shall not in any way be affected or impaired.
ARTICLE V EFFECTIVITY
This MOA shall take effect upon signing by the PARTIES and shall remain valid and existing until the completion of the 120 feeding days for the 13th Cycle or until the end of the applicable school year August 2023 - June 2024, provided it shall not exceed the Extended Payment Period, as stated further under the applicable GAA General Provisions, unless otherwise revoked or terminated by either one of the parties.
