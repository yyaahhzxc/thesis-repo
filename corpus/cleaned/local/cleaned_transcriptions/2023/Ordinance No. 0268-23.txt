Ord. No. 026-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ALONG AURORA- GUERRERO STREETS ON JULY 29, 2023 IN CELEBRATION OF THE 32ND ARAW NG BARANGAY 24-C, POBLACION DISTRICT, DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7169, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or festive celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fair.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with their 32nd Araw ng Barangay 24-C, the Barangay Council of Barangay 24-C requested for a temporary road closure along Aurora-Guerrero Streets beside the Barangay Hall on July 29, 2023.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 25th day of July, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
