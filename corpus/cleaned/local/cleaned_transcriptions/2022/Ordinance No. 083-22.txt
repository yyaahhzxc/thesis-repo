AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE YEAR 2022 IN THE AMOUNT OF THIRY ONE MILLION PESOS ($31,000,000.00); OTHER FOLLOWING ACCOUNT EXPENSES, NAMELY: WELFARE GOODS FOR THIRTY MILLION PESOS ($30,000,000.00), FUEL OIL & LUBRICANTS FOR FIVE MILLION PESOS ($5,000,000.00); OTHER MAINTENANCE AND OTHER OPERATING EXPENSES (MODEE) AMOUNTING TO TWO MILLION PESOS ($2,000,000.00) AND TRAVELLING EXPENSES IN THE AMOUNT OF TWO MILLION PESOS ($2,000,000.00).

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2022 IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF THIRTY-NINE MILLION PESOS (P39,000,000.00); FOR THE FOLLOWING PURPOSES, NAMELY: FOOD & FEEDS FOR THIRTY MILLION PESOS (P30,000,000.00); FUEL OIL & LUBRICANTS FOR FIVE MILLION PESOS (P5,000,000.00); OTHER MAINTENANCE AND OTHER OPERATING EXPENSES (MOOE) AMOUNTING TO TWO MILLION PESOS (P2,000,000.00); AND TRAVELLING EXPENSES IN THE AMOUNT OF TWO MILLION PESOS (P2,000,000.00)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to adopt measures and adhere to the national principles and standards of humanitarian assistance in response to risk reduction and declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.

SECTION 4. SUMMARY OF BUDGETARY ALLOCATION - A portion of the Quick Response Fund (QRF) to be sourced out from the 5% Disaster Risk Reduction Management Fund of the City of Cagayan in the total amount of THIRTY-NINE MILLION PESOS (P39,000,000.00) FOR THE FOLLOWING OPERATING EXPENSES, NAMELY: WEAVING GOODS FOR THIRTY MILLION PESOS (P30,000,000.00); OTHER MAINTENANCE AND OTHER OPERATING EXPENSES (MOOE) AMOUNTING TO TWO MILLION PESOS (P2,000,000.00) AND TRAVELLING EXPENSES IN THE AMOUNT OF TWO MILLION PESOS (P2,000,000.00) shall be utilized to provide assistance to the identified local government units declared under State of Calamity due to earthquake, tropical depression and typhoon.

SECTION 5. LEGAL BASIS - Section 324 (d) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, as amended by Republic Act No. 8185, states that five percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as annual lump sum appropriations for relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction, and other works or services in connection with calamities which may occur during the budget year. Provided, however, that such funds may be allocated to the areas, or a portion thereof, of the local government unit or other areas affected by a disaster or calamity, as determined and declared by the local Sanggunian concerned.

Section 5 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 8185 likewise states that "LGUs may also allocate/ use a portion of the five percent (5%) Calamity Fund to other affected areas on condition that the said areas are declared as under the State of Calamity by the Sanggunian concerned." It further provides that "a portion of the Calamity Fund may be allocated/used by LGUs to other LGUs to provide financial assistance to other LGUs whose area or portion thereof has been declared under a state of calamity by its Sanggunian."

**SECTION 6. UTILIZATION OF FUNDS** - The amounts herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such item and expenditure approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government laws, rules, regulations, directives, policies, guidelines, and orders of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and Presidential directives.

**SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
