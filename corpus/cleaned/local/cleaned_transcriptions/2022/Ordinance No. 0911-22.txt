Ord. No. 0911-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ACCEPTING THE TURNOVER OF ROAD LOTS, ALLEYS AND OPEN SPACES OF LUZVILLE SUBDIVISION LOCATED IN BARANGAY PANACAN, BUNAANAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY, EXECUTED BY LS PROPERTIES, INC., REPRESENTED BY ARNOLD DAWA, IN FAVOR OF THE CITY OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the acceptance of the turnover of road lots, alleys and open spaces of Luzville Subdivision located in Barangay Panacan, Bunawan District, this City, executed by LS Properties, Inc., represented by Arnold Dawa, in favor of the City of Davao.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, March 22, 2022, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
