Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE DAVAO CITY MEETINGS,
INCENTIVES, CONVENTIONS, AND EXHIBITIONS (MICE)
BOARD, PRESCRIBING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "MICE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared that Davao City recognizes tourism as an indispensable component of the City's socio-economic development. It is likewise acknowledged that to establish Davao City as the prime hub and center for conventions and congresses, the City shall adopt policies and enact legislative measures for efficient and effective operations of the MICE Industry. This Ordinance intends to:

a) provide measures that will position Davao City as a top desired destination for regional, national, and international Meetings, Incentives, Conventions, and Exhibitions (MICE);

b) enact policies that will strengthen the Meetings, Incentives, Conventions, and Exhibitions (MICE) industry in the City; and

c) set the direction of the Meetings, Incentives, Conventions, and Exhibitions (MICE) industry;

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - Whenever used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean as herein set forth:

a) MICE shall refer to the Meetings, Incentives, Conventions, and Exhibitions events industry.

b) MICE events shall refer to MICE-related activities including social and business meetings, congresses, conventions, incentive trips, product presentations, business courses, conferences, and seminars.

c) Meetings shall mean any number of people coming together in one particular place for a particular activity, which can be a one-time event or recur regularly.

d) Incentives are typically given to employees as rewards. A non-business vacation with the aim of continued motivation for performance. Incentives may include incentive travel or trip.

e) Conventions are events that have specific objectives and exchanges of information.

f) Exhibitions are those where displayed products or services are the event's primary focus.

SECTION 4. MEETINGS, INCENTIVES, CONVENTIONS, AND EXHIBITIONS (MICE) BOARD OF DAVAO CITY - The Meetings, Incentives, Conventions, and Exhibitions (MICE) Board of Davao City, hereby created, to undertake systematic measures and plans appropriate or incidental to the efficient and effective provision of MICE-oriented and MICE-related services, activities and programs. Further, this Board shall be the primary planning and coordinating body in the development and promotion of Davao City as a MICE destination. This Board shall be known as the "MICE Board of Davao City."

SECTION 5. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS - The MICE Board of Davao City shall have the following powers and functions:

a) The MICE Board of Davao City shall be responsible in formulating MICE-related plans and programs, as well as rules and regulations that shall govern the operations of all MICE enterprises;

b) The MICE Board shall be a coordinating body for the development and promotion of the MICE Industry in Davao City;
c) To develop policies intended to support and encourage MICE stakeholders to engage in the conduct of MICE in the City;
d) To enlist the assistance of national agencies and private sector organizations, as may be necessary, useful, and incidental to the effective and efficient implementation of this Ordinance;
e) To conduct consultations with relevant private and government stakeholders to review the relevance of the provisions of this Ordinance;
f) To develop comprehensive local and global strategies that can increase the competitiveness of Davao City as a MICE destination;
g) To adopt mechanisms to market and set the direction of the MICE Industry in Davao City; and
h) To perform such other powers and functions as are necessary and incidental to the exercise and performance of its functions.
SECTION 6. COMPOSITION OF THE MICE BOARD OF DAVAO CITY.- The MICE Board of Davao City shall be composed of the following members, and hold their respective positions:
a) The City Mayor as Chairperson;
b) A Representative from the Private Sector as Vice-Chairperson;
c) City Tourism Officer, Davao City Tourism Operations Office (DCTOO) as Secretary of the MICE Board;
d) Chairman, Committee on Tourism and Beautification of the Sangguniang Panlungsod as Member;
e) Chairperson, Committee on International Relations of the Sangguniang Panlungsod as Member;
f) Chairperson, Committee on Finance, Ways and Means and Appropriations as Member;
g) President, Davao Tourism Association as Member;
h) Chairman for Tourism, Davao City Chamber of Commerce and Industry as Member;
i) President, Davao International Airport Authority as Member;
j) City Director, Davao City Police Office as Member;
k) Officer in Charge, Public Safety and Security Command Center as Member;
I) Regional Representative of the Department of Tourism XI as Member;
m) One Representative from each Primary and Secondary Tourism Sector;

The membership of the Board may be increased by the Sanggunian Panlungsod through the recommendation of the Board as it may deem necessary for the effective implementation of the provisions of the Ordinance.

SECTION 7. MEETINGS AND QUORUM OF THE BOARD. - The Board shall meet at least once every two (2) months or as often as may be necessary, the schedule of which it may decide. However, the City Mayor may call upon a special meeting as he/she may deem necessary.

The presence of at least a majority of its members shall constitute a quorum and a majority of the quorum of a meeting, validly held, shall be required for the Board to exercise its powers and perform its functions.

SECTION 8. APPROPRIATIONS. - To defray the expenses of the MICE Board of Davao City, the Technical Secretariat, and other expenses for the implementation of the provisions of this Ordinance, the necessary appropriations for its operation shall be chargeable to the Office of the City Mayor as the Chairperson of the MICE Board of Davao City.

SECTION 9. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE. - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable, and, if anyone or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE. - All ordinances, resolutions, rules or regulations which are inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect FIFTEEN (15) DAYS after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

ENACTED, June 14, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
