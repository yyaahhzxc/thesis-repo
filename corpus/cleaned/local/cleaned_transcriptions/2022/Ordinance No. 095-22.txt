Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE AMENDED DAVAO CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN (DCCRRMFIP) FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2022 IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SEVEN HUNDRED THIRTY-EIGHT MILLION, THREE HUNDRED THREE THOUSAND, FOUR HUNDRED FOUR PESOS AND 64/100 (P738,303,404.64).""

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to adopt measures and adhere to the national principles and standards of response methods to risk reduction and declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.

SECTION 3. AMENDMENTS - The Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund Investment Plan (DCCRRMFIP) for Calendar Year 2022 is hereby amended as follows:

SECTION 4. USE OF FUNDS - The amount herein appropriated for the projects
indicated therein shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget must conform with existing government budgeting,
accounting, and financial rules, regulations, policies, and guidelines, the Commission on Audit (COA), the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Government
Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances, and Presidential directives.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable, and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on November 22, 2022, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
