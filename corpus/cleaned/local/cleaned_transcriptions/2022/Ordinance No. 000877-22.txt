Ord. No. 0877-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "AN
ORDINANCE GRANTING THE PROPOSAL OF THE CITY PRICING COMMITTEE
UNDER RESOLUTION NO. 4, SERIES OF 2018, FOR THE PRICE
ADJUSTMENT SCHEME OF MINTAL RELOCATION AREA AND BO. OBRERO
RELOCATION AREA AS THE MOST APPROPRIATE AND RESPONSIVE TO THE
QUALIFIED BENEFICIARIES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Refers to the City Pricing
Committee Resolution No. 4, Series of 2018, Price Adjustment for Mintal Relocation and Bo. Obrero Relocation.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This shall apply to the actual possession of
qualified housing program beneficiaries from Mintal Relocation Area and Bo. Obrero Relocation Area.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of
this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or
provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on February 8, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

A. MINTAL RELOCATION AREA
I. Overview
By virtue of Ordinance No. 838 Series of 1993, the pricing scheme for the
Mintal Relocation was approved.
There are around 263 beneficiaries who have fully paid their lots and only
five beneficiaries have executed the Agreement to Sell.
There is a need to subject these areas for price adjustment or
amendment considering that it was almost 25 years ago since its approval.
