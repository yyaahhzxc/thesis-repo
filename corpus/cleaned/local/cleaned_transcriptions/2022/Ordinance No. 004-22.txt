AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE STREETS ALONG AND IN BETWEEN THE AREA OF CORNER PONCiano ST., -- PALMA GIL ST. TO PELAYO ST. (HALF-LANE ONLY) FROM 1:00 P.M. Ord. No. 004-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE STREETS ALONG AND IN BETWEEN THE AREA OF CORNER PONCIONA ST., PALMA GIL ST. TO PELAYO ST. (HALF-LANE ONLY) FROM 1:00 P.M. ON JULY 30, 2022 (SATURDAY) UP TO 9:00 A.M. ON JULY 31, 2022 (SUNDAY) IN CONNECTION WITH THE 2nd EDITION TULUSI ULTRA-MARATHON "STRIDE TO SURVIVE" FOR A CAUSE BY STRIDERS RUNNING CLUB OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or Fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fair or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and water projects.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The Davao City Striders Sports Clinic and Event Organizer in coordination with the Davao Striders Running Club will be holding its ultramarathon, half-marathon, and fun run for a cause on July 30-31, 2022, on the following routes;

For 65K Ultra Marathon - People's Park (exit area) - Pelayo St., turn left to E. Quirino Ave., going to Mc Arthur Hi-way - Ulas- Puan Toril (Gnall), going back to Crossing Bayabas, turn left to Manambalang Tapakapal Road, turn right to Libby Road - Semupenes St. (Bypass Puian), cross to Bukidnon Road - Bypass Ulas, then turn left to Davao City Diversion Road, turn right to Shime Hills - Santos Cuyogon Road, turn left to Gen. McArthur Hi-way, turn right to Davao City Diversion Road, turn right to Panacan Crossing - Davao City/Famangin O. Pardo Road - Sasa Lanang, turn right to J.P Laurel Ave. (Bjado), turn right to E. Quirino Ave., turn left to Pelayo St. - Palma Gil St. - People's Park (exit area only);

For 21k/10k/5k - People's Park (exit area), turn right to Palma Gil St. - Pelayo St., turn right to E. Quirino Ave., turn left to J.P Laurel Ave. - Duran Hotel (turning point for 5k), Bank of Commerce - Lanang Branch (10k turning point) then turn left going to C-One Km. 11, Sasa (turning point for 21k) - People's Park (exit area only).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, July 26, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
