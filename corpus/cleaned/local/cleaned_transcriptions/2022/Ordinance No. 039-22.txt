AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND, FROM THE GENERAL FUND OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2022, IN THE AMOUNT OF THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS ($300,000.00) AS FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE MUNICIPALITY OF CANDABA, PAMPANGA WHICH WAS DECLARED UNDER A STATE OF CALAMITY DUE TO SUPER TYPHOON KARDING

Ord. No. 039-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE – This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2022, IN THE AMOUNT OF THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P300,000.00) AS FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE MUNICIPALITY OF CANDABA, PAMPANGA WHICH WAS DECLARED UNDER A STATE OF CALAMITY DUE TO SUPER TYPHOON KARDING".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY – It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to adopt measures and adhere to the national principles and standards of humanitarian assistance in response to risk reduction and declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.

SECTION 3. BENEFICIARIES – The Municipality of Candaba, Pampanga, which was placed under State of Calamity, is hereby declared as beneficiary of the financial assistance and shall receive the amount of Three Hundred Thousand Pesos (P300,000.00).

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASIS – Section 324 (d) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Act of 1991, as amended by Republic Act No. 8185, states that "five percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as annual lump sum appropriations for relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction, and other works or services in connection with calamities which may occur during the budget year. Provided, however, that such fund shall be used only in the area, or a portion thereof, of the local government unit or other areas affected by a disaster or calamity, as determined and declared by the local Sanggunian concerned."

Section 5 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 8185 likewise states that "LGUs may also allocate/use a portion of the five percent (5%) Calamity Fund to other affected areas on condition that the said areas are declared as under the State of Calamity by the Sanggunian concerned." It further provided that "a portion of the calamity fund may also be authorized to be used by the LGU concerned to provide flood control measures in the areas of 1984 whose area or portion thereof had been declared under a state of calamity by its LGUg."

SECTION 5. UTILIZATION OF FUNDS – The amounts herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such item and expenditure approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and Presidential directives.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY – The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
