AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON THE INCLUSIVE DATES AND TIME LISTED HEREUNDER IN CONNECTION WITH THE REQUEST OF COCA-COLA AND GLOBE TELECOM, INC. FOR THE CELEBRATION OF THE 37TH KADAYAWAN SA DAVAO FESTIVAL (KADAYAWAN CONCERTS)

Ord. No. 007-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON THE INCLUSIVE DATES AND TIME LISTED HEREUNDER IN CONNECTION WITH THE REQUEST OF COCA-COLA AND GLOBE TELECOM, INC. FOR THE CELEBRATION OF THE 37TH KADAYAWAN SA DAVAO FESTIVAL (KADAYAWAN CONCERTS)"

| SPONSOR NAME | DATE | TIME AND ACTIVITY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| COCA-COLA | AUG 17, 2022 | * 9:00pm - Road Closure (Barricades) * 10:00pm - Ingress Roofing and Setup * 3:00am - Ingress Stage and Control Booth * 1:00pm - Ingress Technicals (Sounds and Lights and LED) * 6:00pm - Sound Check (if not, Aug 19 afternoon) * 7:00pm - Focusing and Programming * 9:00pm - Focusing and Programming |
| August 19, 2022 (Event Date) | 1:00pm - Rehearsals * 6:00pm - Standby * 7:00pm to 9:00pm - Concert Proper |
| August 20, 2022 | Egress ends at 2:00am to 3:00am (It would take a maximum of at least 6 hours to fully dismantle and clean the venue after the 9:00pm event) |
| GLOBE TELECOM INC. | August 20, 2022 | * Mounting of Concert Stage * Bestowance of barricades on the concert stretch * Supplementary security manpower from the registered security of Globe Telecom |

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or festive celebration, public rallies, and agricultural or industrial fairs."

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The area along Roxas Avenue will be temporarily closed on the specified dates and time herein mentioned in connection with the celebration of the 37th Kadayawan sa Davao Festival (Kadayawan Concerts).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other
