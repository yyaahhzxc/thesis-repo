Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be referred to as "AN ORDINANCE CONVERTING TWO (2) VACANT POSITIONS IN THE CITY VETERINARIAN'S OFFICE CONSISTENT WITH THE 2017 OMNIBUS RULES ON APPOINTMENTS AND OTHER HUMAN RESOURCE ACTIONS (REVISED JULY 2018) OF THE CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES. - Presented to the Honorable Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod for review and consideration, is a letter from Atty. Mark L. Layog, Acting City Administrator, proposing for the conversion of two (2) vacant positions in the City Veterinarian's Office, namely: Agricultural Center Chief (SG-18) and Computer Operator III (SG-12), into Veterinarian III (SG-19) under the Animal Husbandry and Disease Control Division, and Supply Officer I (SG-10) under the Administrative Division;

Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a better way of life, encourage the development of adequate and appropriate human, physical, and economic resources, provide adequate and appropriate scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among its residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

Section 76 of Republic Act No. 7160 empowers local government units to design and implement its organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil Service Commission (CSC).

Section 12 (2), Chapter 3, Title I (A), Book V of the Administrative Code of 1987 provides that the Civil Service Commission shall prescribe, amend and enforce rules and regulations for carrying into effect the provision of the Civil Service Law and other pertinent laws.

Civil Service Commission (CSC) Resolution No. 1701009 dated June 16, 2017 which was published in the Philippine Star on August 2, 2017 and took effect on August 18, 2017 and circularized through CSC Memorandum Circular No. 24, s. 2017, adopts and promulgates the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions (ORAORHA) to govern the preparation, submission of, and actions to be taken on appointments and other human resource movements.

Section 11 of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions (Revised July 2018) Nature of Appointment states that:

"Reclassification - A form of staffing modification and/or position classification action which is applied only when there is substantial change in the regular duties and responsibilities of the position. This may result in a change in any or all of the position attributes: position, title, level and/or salary grade. It is the

Department of Budget and Management (DBM) Budget Circular No. 2018-3 dated July 30, 2018 was issued to "amend the guidelines on reclassification of positions as part of the mandate of DBM to administer and maintain the Compensation and Position Classification System (CPCS) in the government";

"4.2. Reclassification may be applied in the following cases:

Section 12 of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions allows adjustments or movement of human resource without need of issuance of an appointment;
Section 1 of Republic Act No. 1080, entitled "An Act Declaring the Bar and Board Examinations as Civil Service Examinations" states, "The bar examinations and the examinations given by the various Boards of Examiners of the Government are declared as civil service examinations, and shall, for purposes of appointment to positions in the classified service the duties of which involve knowledge of the respective professions, except positions requiring highly specialized knowledge not covered by the ordinary board examinations, be considered equivalent to the first grade regular examination given by the Bureau of Civil Service to a professional who has learned the profession in college and the person has practiced his profession for at least two years, and as equivalent to the second grade regular examination if the profession requires less than four years of college study";

SECTION 4. CONVERSION OF POSITIONS UNDER THE CITY VETERINARIAN'S OFFICE - The two (2) vacant positions of Agricultural Center Chief 1 SG-18 under the Animal Husbandry, Disease Prevention and Control Division, and Administrative Assistant VI (Computer Operator III) SG-12 under the Administrative Division of the City Veterinarian's Office shall be abolished, and the following positions shall be created in the City Veterinarian's Office:

1. One (1) position of Veterinarian III SG-19 under the Animal Husbandry, Disease Prevention and Control Division of the CVO; and
2. One (1) position of Administrative Officer I (Supply Officer I) SG-10 under the Administrative Division of the CVO.

SECTION 5. QUALIFICATION STANDARDS (QS) AND DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - The following are the CSC required qualification standards and the duties and responsibilities of the positions:

A. Veterinarian III SG-19

Education Experience Training Eligibility
Doctor of Veterinary Medicine 2 years of relevant experience 8 hours of relevant training RA 1080 (Veterinarian)

Duties and Responsibilities
1. Assists Veterinarian IV in directing and supervising the implementation of policies, guidelines, rules and regulations with regard to castrating and immunizing animals, providing technical advice on the care of sick animals in the field or in the clinic, prescribing medications for treatment and improvement stocks through scientific breeding.
2. Plans, directs, coordinates and oversees the conduct of a technology transfer seminar on poultry and livestock management to promote good and healthy animals.
3. Provides the best possible care and technical advice to farmers on the treatment of sick and injured animals.
4. Oversees the monitoring and evaluation of strategies, initiatives and methodologies.
5. Directly supervises the treatment of animal diseases and conduct research on those that have an impact on breeding and production, posing a risk to the safety and security of food.
6. Supervises Veterinary Integrated Barangay Extension Services to far flung areas thereby promoting good animal health practices.
7. Do other functions as may be directed by the Department Head and/or supervisor.

**SECTION 6. AUTOMATIC REVIEW.** The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall automatically review this Ordinance once every three (3) years after its approval or whenever the need for such review arises.

**SECTION 7. APPROPRIATION.** The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall appropriate the necessary funds to cover the benefits and salaries of the positions stated in this Ordinance.

**SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE.** All Ordinances, resolutions, executive orders, memoranda, and other issuances in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.** Any provision of this Ordinance nullified by any court of law shall not affect the validity of the remaining provisions hereof not affected by the said judicial declaration.

**SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY.** The Ordinance shall take effect after the Sangguniang Panlungsod approves the pertinent resolution and Ordinance and the City Mayor signs for final approval.

**ENACTED**, on the 29th day of November 2022, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
