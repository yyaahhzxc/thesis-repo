AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION EXECUTED BY THE CITY DEVELOPMENT INC., IN FAVOR OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, INVOLVING THE ROAD, LOTS, ALLEY, PARKS AND PLAYGROUND, AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES OF ALTEZA GRANDE, LOCATED IN BARANGAY CATALUNAN GRANDE, TALOMO DISTRICT, THIS CITY

Ord. No. 01000-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION EXECUTED BY URBANEAST DEVELOPMENTS INC., IN FAVOR OF THE CITY OF DAVAO INVOLVING THE ROAD LOTS, ALLEY, PARKS, AND PLAYGROUND, AND COMMUNITY FACILITIES OF ALTEZZA GRANDE, LOCATED IN BARANGAY CATALUNAN GRANDE, TALOMO DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION AND POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of the Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government. And in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by Urbaneast Developments Inc. in favor of the City of Davao, involving the road lots, alley, park, and playground and community facilities of Altezza Grande Subdivision, located in Barangay Catalunan Grande, this City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 28th day of June 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present.
