Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXECUTIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH) AND THE CITY OF DAVAO FOR THE FORMATION OF THE CITY RESETTLEMENT IMPLEMENTATION COMMITTEE (CRIC) RELATIVE TO THE SAMAL ISLAND-DAVAO CITY CONNECTOR PROJECT".

**SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the Grant of Authority to the City Mayor to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum Of Understanding (MOU) by and between the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and the City of Davao for the formation of the City Resettlement Implementation Committee (CRIC) relative to the Sanal Island-Davao City Connector Project.**

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.**

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.**

**ENACTED, on the 5th day of December 2022, by a majority vote of the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
