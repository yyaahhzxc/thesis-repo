Ord. No. 0302-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panglungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE LOCAL DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN (DCDRRMFIP), FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2020, AS ENDORSED BY THE CITY DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL (CDC) IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SEVEN HUNDRED NINETY MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THIRTY-TWO THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED TWENTY-SEVEN PESOS AND 63/100 (P799,532,427.63);"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 2, paragraph (d) of Republic Act No. 10121, provides, to wit:

(d) Adopt a disaster risk reduction and management approach that is holistic, comprehensive, integrated and proactive in lessening the socio-economic and environment impact of disaster including climate and promote the involvement and participation of all sectors and all stakeholders concerned at all levels, especially the local community;

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF THE INVESTMENT PLAN. The Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund Investment Plan (DCDRRMFIP) for CY 2020, as prepared and approved by the Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council, shall be approved for the purpose of developing an all hazards planning approach that will be used for all hazards and/or emergencies or disasters and funding identified projects proposed by various sectors to enhance the readiness and responsiveness of the City government to lessen the impact of disasters in Davao City.

SECTION 4. BUDGETARY REQUIREMENT. The total amount of Seven Hundred Ninety Million Five Hundred Thirty-two Thousand Four Hundred Twenty-seven Pesos and 63/100 (P799,532,427.63) is hereby appropriated to fund the Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund Investment Plan (DCDRRMFIP) for CY 2020 (hereon attached as ANNEX "A");

SECTION 5. LEGAL BASIS. Pursuant to Section 458, par. (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991, it empowers the Sangguniang Panglungsod to adopt measures to protect the inhabitants of the City from harmful effects of man-made or natural disasters and calamities and to provide relief and assistance to victims during and in the aftermath of said disasters or calamities and their return to productive livelihood following said events;

SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS. The amounts herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panglungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to the existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission of Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances and Presidential directives;

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval; ENACTED, on the 7th day of July, 2020, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
