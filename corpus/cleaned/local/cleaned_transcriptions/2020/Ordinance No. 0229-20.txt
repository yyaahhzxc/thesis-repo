VETO MESSAGE
July 16, 2020

Repectfully returned to the Sangguniang Panlungsod, through the Office of the City Administrator, both the City, the subject Ordinance No. 0229-20 Series of 2020, with the information that same is being vetoed.

The subject ordinance imposes administrative fines against person, natural or juridical, who shall keep, manage or operate any building office or premises, or a complete independent part thereof, for the purpose of engaging in the tourism business without having obtained a business permit from the Business Permit and Licensing Office to operate the same, and a Certificate of Accreditation of the establishment from the Tourism and Promotion Department as mentioned in Section 1 and 4 of the subject ordinance.

One of the gravamen of the punishable act is that the offender is doing "tourism business." However, there is nothing in the ordinance that defines what constitute "tourism business," leaving the implementor in limbo.

The said defect cannot be cured in the implementing guidelines to be issued by the executive department. Lest, it will be unduly exercising the power of the legislative department.

It has been said that a statute or act suffers from the defect of vagueness when it lacks comprehensible standards that men of common intelligence must necessarily guess at its meaning and differ as to its application. It is repugnant to the Constitution in two (2) respects: (1) because it leaves the discretion unbridled in carrying out its provisions; and (2) it leaves law enforcers unbridled discretion in carrying out its provisions and becomes an arbitrary flexing of the Government muscle. (G.R. No. 225447, SAMAHAN NG MGA PROGRESIBONG KABATAN (SPARK), JOANNE ROSE SACE LIM, JOHN ARVIN NAUROBA BUENOS, RONEL BACCALAN, MARK DELOS REYES, and JAMES R. BACAS, Petitioners, vs. QUEZON CITY, as represented by his father, JULIAN VILLEGAS, Jr., Petitioners, vs. QUEZON CITY, as represented by MAYOR HERBERT BAUTISTA, CITY OF MANILA, as represented by MAYOR JOSEPH ESTRADA, and NAVOTAS CITY, as represented by MAYOR JOHN REY TIANGCO, Respondents.)

Hence, this VETO.

Finally, let copies of this VETO MESSAGE be furnished all members of the August Body for information and guidance.

ORDINANCE NO. 0229-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING PENALTIES TO ESTABLISHMENTS WITH BUSINESS PERMITS BUT NO TOURISM LICENSES, AS THOSE WITH LATE RENEWALS AND ESTABLISHMENTS THAT ARE OPERATING WITHOUT BUSINESS PERMIT AND TOURISM LICENSES

--- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0229-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. ACCREDITATION OF TOURISM ORIENTED/RELATED ESTABLISHMENTS - No person, natural or juridical, shall keep, manage or operate any building, edifice or premise, or a completely independent part thereof, for the purpose of engaging in the tourism business without having first secured a permit from the Business Permit and Licensing Office to operate the same, and a Certificate of Accreditation of the establishment from the Tourism and Promotion Department (TPD).

SECTION 2. DISPLAY OF CERTIFICATE OF ACCREDITATION - The Certificate of Accreditation shall always be displayed in a conspicuous place of the establishment, otherwise, it shall be presumed that no license or accreditation was issued to such establishment.

SECTION 3. NON-TRANSFERABILITY OF CERTIFICATE OF ACCREDITATION - The accreditation shall be non-transferable.

SECTION 4. PENALTIES - In case the management of any tourism-related establishment fails to obtain such license required under this Ordinance, the City shall impose the following administrative fines:
1. For establishments with Business Permits but without Tourism Licenses, a fine of One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) per month shall be imposed;
2. For establishments with Late Tourism Licenses, a fine of One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) per month shall also be imposed; and
3. For establishments operating without Business Permits and Tourism Licenses, a fine of Two Thousand Pesos (P2,000.00) per month shall be imposed.
In addition to the abovementioned, the City Mayor may suspend or revoke the licenses of the erring persons, corporations or establishments that violate this Ordinance.

SECTION 5. IMPLEMENTING RULES - The City Mayor, from time to time, may issue rules and regulations as he/she may deem fit and necessary for the effective implementation of this Ordinance.

SECTION 6. FUNDING - The funds necessary for the implementation of this Ordinance shall be taken from the devolved funds for tourism regulation, promotion and development. Succeeding funding requirements shall be provided for in the Annual Budget of the City Government.

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of these Rules are hereby declared separable, and in the event that anyone or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE - All existing Rules and Regulation Circulars issued by the Department of Tourism on the operations of tourism-related establishments subject of these Rules as well as local ordinances which are inconsistent with the provisions of these Rules and Regulations are hereby repealed and superseded accordingly.
