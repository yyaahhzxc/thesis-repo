Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. IIII. - This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS-XI (DPWH-XI), THE UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO PERTAINING TO THE USE OF THE DC-UP SPORTS COMPLEX TRAINING GYM, FACULTY AND STAFF HOUSING OF DC-UP SPORTS COMPLEX IN UP-MINDANAO CAMPUS, MINTAL, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY, AS QUARANTINE AREA FOR COVID-19 PATIENTS."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.- Section 22 (a) (5) and (c) and Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991, or Republic Act No. 7160, provides that:

Section 22. Corporate Powers.-
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this condition, shall;
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunining panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the renewal of the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and among the Department of Public Works and Highways-XI (DPWH-XI), the University of the Philippines and the City Government of Davao pertaining to the use of the DC-UP Sports Complex Training Gym, Faculty and Staff Housing of DC-UP Sports Complex in UP-Mindanao Campus, Mintal, Tugbok District, this City, as Quarantine Area for COVID-19 patients;

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL PERSONS BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement, made and entered into by and between:

DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS-
XI, a national government entity, organized and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address in Davao City, represented herein by Regional Director, ALLAN S. BORROMEO, Filipino, of legal age, residing in Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the "DPWH".

THE UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES, the national university of the Philippines, created by virtue of Act No. 1870, as amended, and re-organized and operating by virtue of Republic Act No. 9500, through its constituent unit, THE UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES MINDANAO, established by virtue of Republic Act No. 7889, with office address at Administration Building, Mintal, Tugbok District, Davao City, represented herein by Chancellor, LARRY N. DIGAL, Filipino, of legal age, residing in Davao City hereinafter referred to as the "UP-MIN".

-AND-

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit, organized and existing by virtue of laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with official address at City Hall Building, City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented by City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "LGU".

WITNESSETH: That

WHEREAS, the entire country is under the state of National Public Health Emergency by virtue of Proclamation No. 923 and calls for immediate decisive action of the government to contain the transmission of COVID-19, through healthcare, medical tests, treatment and quarantine, among others;

WHEREAS, COVID-19 remains a threat to public health and national security which can undermine the social, economic and well-being of the society;

WHEREAS, among the measures undertaken by the LGU to contain the spread of COVID-19 is the isolation of the patient and those under monitoring. For this purpose, the LGU is in need of available buildings or spaces that will serve as quarantine/isolation area.

WHEREAS, recognizing the threats brought about by the COVID-19 pandemic to the well-being of the Filipinos, the parties are in agreement to convert the DC-UP Sports Complex Training Gym and Faculty and Staff Housing in UP-Mindanao Campus, Mintal, Tugbok District, into an isolation or quarantine area to house the COVID-19 infected persons.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties hereto agree the following:

1. AREA, PURPOSE AND MANAGEMENT

1.1 The Parties hereby designate (1) DC-UP SPORTS COMPLEX TRAINING GYM and (2) FACULTY AND STAFF HOUSING, all located in UP-Mindanao Campus, Bago Oshiro, Tugbok District, Davao City, as Quarantine Area (hereinafter referred to as the "AREA"), primarily for the residents of Davao City who are affected with the pandemic, to be managed and operated by the LGU.

2. TERM / DURATION

2.1 This Agreement serves as a renewal of the contract which shall commence on 20 July 2020 for a term of three (3) months.

2.2 This Agreement may be renewed or extended for a similar period of three (3) months, under mutually acceptable terms and conditions, subject to the requirements of public health and the declared ECQ period. The LGU shall inform both the DPWH and UP Mindanao at least fifteen (15) days before the expiration of this Agreement of its intention to renew or extend.

3. THE UP-MIN

3.1 Allow the LGU and DPWH, upon prior notice, to introduce improvements in the AREA for the purpose of converting it into an isolation facility for COVID 19 suspects and probable cases.

3.2 Allow the entry of supplies, materials and personnel from the LGU, DPWH, Department of Health, upon prior notice, during the construction/conversion of Training Gym and Faculty and Staff Housing into isolation area while the AREA is operational as isolation facilities for COVID-19 suspects and probable cases.

4. THE LGU

4.1 Undertake to prepare and maintain the AREA for quarantine purpose following the guidelines issued by the Department of Health and other appropriate body.

4.2 Provide food, laundry, medical and other necessary services to the COVID-19 suspects and probable cases.

4.3 Keep and maintain the AREA in a clean, safe and sanitary condition.

4.4 Provide medical teams and support staff and ensure that all frontliners in the AREA shall use personal protective equipment (PPE) to prevent exposure to hazards that cause serious injury or illness, including but not limited to masks, gloves, etc.

--- [Page 10] ---
4.5 Assume all actual usage costs of consumption of all utilities and any and all services i.e., electric, telephone, water, security, etc., required in the AREA and pay for all charges for these utilities and services as they fall due.
4.6 Monitor and supervise the AREA to ensure the security, well-being, health of the frontliners and the patients.
4.7 Within fifteen (15) days after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, it shall cause the cleaning, sanitizing and disinfecting of the AREA. It shall surrender and return the premises in as good condition as the premises were actually found at the beginning of the use/occupancy;
4.8 It shall comply with the rules on waste management and ensure that no hazardous materials, toxic wastes and chemicals are left in the AREA upon expiration or termination of this Agreement.
4.9 Bear the cost of repairs necessary to preserve and maintain the FACULTY and STAFF HOUSING in such good, serviceable and fit condition during the effectiveness of this Agreement.
4.10 Not keep, deposit or store in the AREA any noxious, toxic or flammable materials or substances or any other substances or materials, which may constitute a fire, environmental or safety hazard.
5. THE DPWH
5.1 Bear the construction materials cost for the conversion of the Training Gym at the DC-UP Sports Complex;
5.2 Coordinate with the LGU through the City Engineer's Office on the design of the isolation facility to be set up in the Training Gym;
5.3 Allows the LGU to introduce improvement at the Faculty and Staff Housing for the purpose of converting it into an isolation facility for COVID-19 suspects and probable cases.
6. THIRD PARTY LIABILITY
6.1 The LGU during its occupancy of the AREA, shall hold the DPWH and UP-MIN free and harmless from any and all damages, liabilities or responsibilities to any person, natural or juridical, or to any property, arising out of or as a consequence of the use of the AREA by LGU, its agents, employees except when such damages, liabilities or responsibilities are due to the fault, or negligence of the DPWH or UP-MIN, its officers, employees, representatives and/or agents, in which case, they shall be liable for such loss, damage and/or injury.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties hereby affix their signatures, this day of at Davao City.
