AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF GLOBE TELECOM, INC., THROUGH EFB GEOSTRUKT, INC., FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A 50.0-METER HIGH, THREE-LEGGED TOWER SITUATED IN BARANGAY BIAO GUIANGA, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF GLOBE TELECOM, INC., THROUGH EFILCONSTRUCT, INC. FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A 50.0-METER HIGH, THREE-LEGGED TOWER SITUATED IN BARANGAY BLAD GUIGUANA, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Article XII, Section 1 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City provides for requests for Additional Allowable Use which reads:

"The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not enumerated therein as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a ¾ majority vote of all Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance".

ARTICLE XII, Section 1.3 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City provides the requirements that must be submitted in a request for Additional Allowable Use, to wit:

a. Barangay Council Resolution of No Objection;
b. Barangay Development Council Resolution favorably endorsing the project;
c. City Engineer's Office for drainage clearance;
d. City Environment and Natural Resources Office for solid waste management plan;
e. City Health Office for sanitation clearance;
f. City Assessor's Office for new tax declaration;
g. City Treasurer's Office for realty tax clearance;
h. Davao City Water District for certification of water supply availability;
i. Davao Electric and Power Company for certification of power supply availability;
j. Mines and Geosciences Bureau for certification for possible geohazard and recommended mitigating measures;
k. DENR-Environmental Management Bureau for waste treatment facilities and permit to discharge effluents;
l. Davao City Environmental Management Council/Task Force for projects within Water Resource Zone;
m. Barangay Watershed Management Council and City Watershed Management Council for areas in Conservation Zone;
n. Golf Construction and Development Committee (for golf course);
o. Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines and Philippine Air Force Command Davao City (for private landing strip, airports, helipads and heliports);
p. Davao Ports Authority (for ports and harbors);
q. National Power Corporation, Davao City (for power generation plant/station);

Article IV, Section 7.6, provides the following acts that are prohibited in Water Resource Zone (WRZ), viz:

7.6.1. No person shall drill a free-flowing or artesian well within the water resource area.

16.6.1. A water well dug for domestic purposes shall have a casing in excess of two (2) inches in diameter or a capacity of more than ten (10) gallons per minute (GPM) whichever is greater;

7.6.3. No person shall maintain a solid waste disposal system or garbage dump site within this zone;

7.6.4. No underground oil storage tanks shall be constructed within these areas;

7.6.5. No sanitary landfill or incinerator shall be located within water resource areas;

The application of agricultural fertilizers and pesticides or other chemicals may be prohibited that may cause pollution of surface or ground water or endanger the health and safety of the residents nearby;

7.6.7. Dumping, depositing or throwing of tailings or sediments from mining operations or chemical wastes from industries is strictly prohibited;

7.6.8. No cemetery or memorial park shall be located within these areas. Cemeteries and memorial parks existing in Water Resource Zone before the enactment of this Ordinance shall institute immediate corrective measures to prevent leakage and seepage of noxious substances and chemicals into the aquifers;

7.6.9. Activities or industries that deal with chemical and industrial products will not be allowed to prevent possible contamination;

7.6.10. No service station will not be issued locational clearance until the project proponents submit a safety measure plan to the zoning administrator to ensure that there will be no contamination of the ground water;

7.6.11. Introduction of Additional Allowable Use in accordance with Article XII, Section 1, is not allowed in this zone.

Article IV, Section 7.8, provides that the following are Special Requirements for development in Water Resource Zone (WRZ):

7.8.1. Special Permits: No Preliminary Approval and Locational Clearance (PALC), Development Permit (DP) and Building Permit (BP) shall be issued without clearances from Water Resource Management Council (Task Force), Davao City Water District (DCWDD), DENR - Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB), City Planning and Development Office (CPDO), and from DENR - Environmental Management Bureau.

While the area applied for falls within the Water Resource Zone, considering the nature of the development/construction applied for, the requirement to protect and ensure that the underground water will not be contaminated or extracted unlawfully is not present in this case.

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST – The request for Additional Allowable Use of Globe Telecom, Inc., through EFB Geostruct, for the construction of a 50.0-meter high, three-legged tower situated in Barangay Biao Guiana, Tugbok District, this City, is hereby approved.
