Ord. No. 0215-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST FOR EXCEPTION OF MR. RICKY PORRAS FROM ARTICLE IX, SECTION 2 OF THE COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY (2013-2022) FOR THE OPERATION OF A MINI GASOLINE STATION LOCATED IN RIVERSIDE CALINAN, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. SCOPE: The request for exception of Mr. Ricky Porras from Article IX, Section 2 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) for the operation of a Mini Gasoline Station located in Riverside, Calinan, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE: The Mini Gasoline Station project occupies a total area of SIX HUNDRED (600) square meters, more or less, and covered under Transfer Certificate of Title No. T-251506 located in Riverside Calinan, this City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, any part or provision not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect;

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 11th day of February 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
