AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE EXECUTED BY AND AMONG SAMAHAN NG MGA KOOPERATIBANG MAPAGKALINGA, INC. (SKMI), A NON-STOCK, NON-PROFIT CORPORATION COMPOSED OF FIFTEEN COOPERATIVES IN DAVAO CITY, THE CITY OF DAVAO DE ORO AND THE CITY OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF AN ENCLOSED CONCRETE KITCHEN FACILITY IN LOS AMIGOS EVACUATION CENTER, THIS CITY

--- [Page 11] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE EXECUTED BY AND AMONG SAMAHAN NG MGA KOOPERATIBANG MAPAGKALINGA, INC. (SKMI), A NON-PROFIT CORPORATION CONSISTING OF FIFTEEN (15) COOPERATIVES IN DAVAO CITY, THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE CITY SOCIAL SERVICES AND DEVELOPMENT OFFICE-DAVAO CITY, RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF AN ENCLOSED CONCRETE KITCHEN FACILITY IN LOS ANIGIOS EVACUATION CENTER, THIS CITY"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states:

Section 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be executed by and among the Samahan ng mga Kooperatibang Mapagkalinga, Inc. (SKMI), a non-profit corporation consisting of fifteen (15) Cooperatives in Davao City, the City Government of Davao and the City Social Services and Development Office-Davao City, relative to the construction of an enclosed concrete kitchen facility in Los Anigos Evacuation Center, this city.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, February 20, 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CONSIDERING THAT:

1. The City Government of Davao has a lineup of projects concentrating on the sustainability and development for the City of Davao in different Priority Areas in its DO30 Program for the years 2016 to 2019;

2. Among the priority areas of the DO30 Program is Disaster and Risk Reduction and Mitigation wherein it established Evacuation Centers in the different areas of the City, primarily managed by the CSSDO;

3. One such Evacuation Center managed by the CSSDO is the Los Amigos Evacuation Center at Tugbog District, Davao City that serves as temporary domicile for the victims of disaster, calamities and dislocation;

4. The Los Amigos Evacuation Center at Tugbog District presently has a presently a wooden kitchen facility with roofing which is open and utilizes firewood and have no fixed sink and food preparation facility;

5. In support of the Fire Prevention Month and for the clean, safe, orderly and efficient preparation and cooking of food and hot meals for the occupants at the Los Amigos Evacuation Center, SKMI has identified the Los Amigos Evacuation Center as its beneficiary of an enclosed concrete kitchen facility (hereinafter the "Project" for brevity);

6. SKMI supports and sustains the DO30 Program of the City of Davao, specifically the Priority Area of Disaster and Risk Reduction and Mitigation by undertaking the Project at Los Amigos Evacuation Center of the CSSDO, described in Annex "A" and attached hereto and made an integral part hereof;

HAVE REACHED THE FOLLOWING AGREEMENT:

ARTICLE 1: AIMS OF THE AGREEMENT

Parties undertake to provide the terms and conditions of the construction of the Project, including:

- The methods and procedures in the construction of the said Project.
- The right and undertakings of the Parties during and after the construction of the Project.

ARTICLE 2: PARTY OBLIGATIONS

ARTICLE 2.1: CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO OBLIGATIONS

2.1.1 The City Government of Davao shall designate the CSSDO as focal agency in the coordination meetings, monitoring, among others, composed of representatives from each Party during the construction of the Project;

2.1.2 The City Government of Davao shall allow SKMI to put the names and logos of the Cooperative Members of SKMI in approved areas or structures of the Evacuation Center;

--- [Page 15] ---
ARTICLE 2.2: CSSDO OBLIGATIONS

2.2.1 CSSDO shall be responsible for monitoring and supervising the construction of the Project, maintaining the coordination of all parties in this agreement throughout the construction duration of the Project;

2.2.2 CSSDO shall identify persons who can use the Project and take care of the Project like a good father of the family;

2.2.3 CSSDO shall monitor the viability and sustainability of the Project and make reports or recommendation to the City of Davao and to SKMI on any need for maintenance, improvement or repair;

ARTICLE 2.3: SKMI OBLIGATIONS

2.3.1 SKMI will link all parties of this Agreement in the efficient fulfillment of agreed rules and responsibilities in the construction of the Project.

2.3.2 SKMI will provide the fund, labor and materials in the construction of the Project.

ARTICLE 2.4: JOINT OBLIGATIONS

2.4.1 All Parties will be responsible for the security and safety of the laborers and all the occupants and visitors of the Evacuation Center during the construction of the Project.
