AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND SYCIP GORRES VENTURES (SGV) & CO., INC. Ord. No. 0276-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND SYCIP GORRES VELAYO (SGV) & CO., WITH THE LATTER OFFERING ITS EXPERTISE TO ASSIST THE CITY THROUGH THE DAVAO CITY INVESTMENT PROMOTION CENTER (DCIPC) IN COMING UP WITH THE "DOING BUSINESS IN DAVAO" PUBLICATION."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 45 (b) (vi) Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) between the City Government of Davao and Sycip Gorres Velayo & Co., with the latter offering its expertise to assist the City through the Davao City Investment Promotion Center (DCIPC) in coming up with the "Doing Business in Davao" publication.

SECTION 4 SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect.

SECTION 5 EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

ENACTED, on the 2nd day of June 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement ("Agreement") is executed this day of 2020 at Davao City by and between:

SYCIP GORRES VELAYO & CO., a general professional partnership duly organized and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines and a member firm of Ernst & Young Global Limited ("EY"), with business address at 6780 Ayala Avenue, Makati City, represented herein by its Country Managing Partner, Wilson P. Tan, and hereinafter referred to as "SGV";

and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit created and existing under Philippine laws with office address at City Hall Drive, San Pedro Street, Davao City, represented herein by Honorable Sara Z. Duterte, City Mayor, and hereinafter referred to as the "Davao City Government".

WHEREAS, SGV has known competence, technical knowledge, expertise and experience in promoting the Philippines to foreign investors as an investment destination, including through its publication of "Doing Business in the Philippines", an investment guide on doing business in the Philippines that provides an overview of the Philippines' social, political, and economic climate, and presents important information on the country's business environment; it will be useful for investors who have plans of setting up business in the country. It showcases not only relevant historical and statistical data on the economy but also lists and discusses key pieces of legislation that govern business transactions in the country;

WHEREAS, the Davao City Government intends to replicate the same concept of publication that will specifically showcase Davao City as a rich source of investment opportunities for both local and foreign investors because of its strategic location, skilled and highly educated human resources, diverse local products, excellent infrastructure, and peace and order situation that conducive to trade and commerce, plus reasonable local business tax rates, and efficient and business-friendly local government services in the City;

WHEREAS, SGV is able and willing to provide free technical assistance to the Davao City Government, in the conceptualization, design, coverage and publication of "Doing Business in Davao City" in printed and digital versions;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions hereinafter set forth in this Agreement, the Parties hereto agree as follows:

1. DEFINITION OF TERMS

Unless otherwise clearly required by the context, the following terms used in this Agreement shall have the respective meanings defined hereunder:

1.1 The term "Publication" or "Work Product" shall mean the "Doing Business in Davao City" publication, and its production in print or in any other medium such as but not limited to digital or electronic copy, or n CD or DVD format.

--- [Page 13] ---
1.2 The term "Services" shall mean the technical advisory services of SGV in relation to the "Doing Business in Davao City" publication.

2. PROVISION OF SERVICES

Subject to the terms detailed in this Agreement, SGV undertakes to assist the Davao City Government in coming up with the design and coverage of the "Doing Business in Davao City" publication as well as reviewing the information compiled relating to the process of setting up of business in the City, its local taxation, its current social, political and economic profile, demographics and other data related thereto. In particular, SGV agrees to:

A. Provide the outline and the report template;

B. Review the draft prepared by Davao City Government staff and suggest revisions or additions.

The Davao City Government agrees to:

A. Compile the data and documents and write the draft;

B. Review suggested revisions or additions by SGV and approve the draft; and

C. Print the publication.

SGV will provide the services described above to the Davao City Government as an independent contractor, agent, partner, or joint venturer. Neither the Davao City Government nor SGV shall have any right, power or authority to bind the other.

3. INTEGRITY OF CONTENT

The Davao City Government shall provide all the content and relevant information to be included in the content of the Publication. The Davao City Government shall be solely responsible for the veracity, accuracy and completeness of the information to be presented in the Publication or Work Product. The Davao City Government shall hold SGV free and harmless from any responsibility, and neither can SGV be made to accept or accepts responsibility for any errors, omissions, inaccuracies or damages arising from any inaccuracy in the information presented or arising from printing of the Publication, either in whole or in part.

In providing the services, SGV shall rely upon the relevant information provided by the Davao City Government and its understanding of the current Philippine tax laws, regulations, rules, and issuances of the relevant tax authorities before the completion of the services. Should such information provided by the Davao City Government, laws, regulations, rules, and issuances change, the information in the Publication or Work Product may need to change as well. SGV will not be responsible for updating the information therein, unless specifically requested to do so under a separate written agreement.

4. COSTS OF PUBLICATION

The costs of production and publication of the Work Product as well as all other costs related thereto such as printing and distribution, inter alia, shall be borne by the Davao City Government.

--- [Page 14] ---
As far as practicable, all materials and supplies and other expenses necessary and incidental to the said project should be provided for and procured by the Davao City Government prior to the commencement of the project.

5. COPIES FOR DISTRIBUTION AT THE LAUNCH

It is agreed that the Work Product may be reproduced and disseminated, in accordance with this Agreement, in either printed or CD/DVD form. It is further agreed that the Davao City Government will print at least three (3) copies of the Work Product at its own expense for distribution at the Cocktails-Reception to mark the launch of the Work Product. In addition, the Davao City Government may opt to distribute the Work Product in CD/DVD form to the net of attendees.

6. COPYRIGHT

SGV acknowledges that the Davao City Government shall own the copyright to the Work Product and subsequent updates or revisions prepared by the Davao City Government.

SGV may use data, software, designs, utilities, tools, models, systems and other methodologies and know-how ("Materials") that its owns in performing the services. Notwithstanding the delivery of the Work Product, SGV shall retain intellectual property rights in the Materials (including any improvements or knowledge developed while performing the services), and in any writing papers compiled in connection with the services.

7. TERMINATION

This Agreement may be terminated by either the Davao City Government or SGV without need of judicial intervention at any time by giving at least five (5) days' written notice to the other Party. Should either Party fail to comply with any of the provisions of this Agreement and not rectify such failure within five (5) days of having received a notice from the other Party to do so, then this Agreement shall be deemed terminated. Upon termination or cancellation, rescission of this Agreement, the authority granted by either Party with respect to each other is deemed terminated. Unless terminated earlier as provided in this Section 7, this Agreement shall terminate on the completion of the services.

8. WARRANTY

The Davao City Government hereby represents and warrants that it is duly authorized and empowered to execute, enter and perform this Agreement and that such action does not conflict with or violate any provision of law, regulation, policy, contract or other instrument to which it is a party or by which it is bound and that this Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation enforceable in accordance with its terms.

9. ANTI-CORRUPTION PROVISIONS

The Parties undertake that they:

1. Will fully comply with, and will procure that their officers, partners and employees fully comply with the Anti-Corruption Laws as defined below;

--- [Page 15] ---
2. Will use reasonable and proportionate efforts to procure that their agents and contractors fully comply with the Anti-Corruption Laws;
3. Will not do, or omit to do, any act that will cause the other Party to be in breach of the Anti-Corruption Laws;
4. Has in place, and shall maintain in place throughout the term of this Agreement, policies and procedures to ensure compliance with the Anti-Corruption Laws and will enforce them where appropriate;
5. Will notify and have notified those providing services for it, including its officers, partners, employees, agents and contractors, that it does not accept or condone the payment of bribes (including facilitation payments) on its behalf; and
6. Will promptly report to the other Party any request or demand for any undue financial or other advantage of any kind received by it in connection with the performance of the Services.
For the avoidance of doubt, Anti-Corruption Laws shall be understood by the Parties to mean any applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws and regulations, including Republic Act No. 3815 of the Revised Penal Code of the Philippines, or Republic Act No. 319, as amended, or the Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act of the Philippines.
Any Party in breach of the obligations in this Section 9 shall indemnify, keep indemnified and hold harmless the other Party and its partners, directors, officers, and employees, from and against all losses, damages, costs (including but not limited to legal costs and disbursements) arising from or incurred by reason of the breaching Party's breach of the Anti-Corruption Laws.
10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement, and any non-contractual matters or obligations arising out of this Agreement or the services, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, without giving effect to the conflict of laws rules.
11. SEVERABILITY
If one or more provisions of this Agreement shall be held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain valid and shall be given full force and effect.
12. WAIVER/AMENDMENTS
No waiver by a Party to any provision of this Agreement shall be binding unless made expressly and in writing. Any amendment or modification of this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
