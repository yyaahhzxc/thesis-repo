Ord. No. 0446-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE FOLLOWING STREETS ON SPECIFIED DATE, TIME AND PURPOSE AS INDICATED HEREIN IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THIS YEAR'S HOLY WEEK".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7166, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs";

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the request of Rev. Father Voltaire Dimol, Parish Administrator, San Pedro Cathedral Parish, the following streets are hereby temporarily closed on the following dates and time, to wit:

1) March 24 - 31, 2024 - Holy Monday - Easter Sunday
The temporary closure of the road in between Bolton St. and C.M. Recto Ave. (fronting the cathedral only) at the whole duration of the Holy Week and Easter celebration that is from March 24, 2024 @ 5:00 o'clock in the morning up to March 31, 2024 @ 8:00 o'clock in the evening for the installation and restoration of Steel Structures (Sogatán), for the Blessing of Fire and the traditional re-enactment of the Sugat/Encuentro/Salubong.

2) March 29, 2024 - Good Friday
The permission to use of the major thoroughfares of the city for the traditional Way of the Cross procession at 3:00 o'clock early morning onwards, starting from Felices Centrale to the Cathedral passing through Quimpo Boulevard - Rizal Extension - C. De Jesus St. (UIC) - Bonifacio St. - C.M. Recto Ave. - Roxas Extension (along Red Cross) - Ponciano St. - Bonifacio St. - Legaspi St. - Magallanes St. - Bonifacio Rotunda, And.

For the procession of the Santo Entero @ 3:00 o'clock in the afternoon onwards on Good Friday, from the Cathedral passing through C.M. Recto Ave. - Roxas Extension (along Red Cross) - Ponciano St. - Bonifacio St. - Legaspi St. - Bonifacio Rotunda and back to the Cathedral.

SECTION 4. SEPARARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 19th day of March 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
