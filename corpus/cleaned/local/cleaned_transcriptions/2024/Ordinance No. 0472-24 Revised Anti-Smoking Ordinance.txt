Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE REVISED COMPREHENSIVE ANTI-SMOKING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Pursuant to Republic Act (R.A.) No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991), the City Government of Davao shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within its respective territorial jurisdiction, it shall ensure and support the maintenance of peace and order, and the preservation of the comfort and convenience of its inhabitants.

Thus, it is the Policy of the City Government of Davao to ensure the prevention, suppression, and imposition of proper penalties for smoking, vaping, or engaging in similar activities in public places, selling the same to minors or allowing them to engage in such activities and any other acts using the same that are injurious to the health and welfare of the inhabitants of the City.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms are defined operationally:

1. Accommodation and Entertainment Establishments - refer to establishments that provide food, accommodation, drinks, merchandise, entertainment or other professional services, including but not limited to, restaurants, fast foods, eateries, hotels, lodges, inns, boarding houses, disco houses, videoke bars, resto bars, and movie houses, or any other place with pleasant environment and atmosphere conducive to comfort, healthful relaxation and rest, offering food and/or drinks, sleeping accommodation and recreation facilities to the public for a fee;

2. Centers of Youth Activities - refer to places such as play schools, preparatory schools, elementary schools, high schools, colleges and universities, youth hostels, parks and playgrounds, and other recreational facilities for minors;

3. Children Rescued - refer to minors rescued from any conceivable forms of danger, including substance use and abuse;

4. Electronic Device Systems (EDS) - refer to any device, such as electronic nicotine device system, electronic cigarette, vapes and other similar devices, whether or not it is used to deliver nicotine to the user, that resembles the outward appearance of real smoking products;

5. Enclosed or Partially Enclosed Public Place - refers to all places, which are enclosed by one or more walls or sides, whether covered by a roof or not, or even if open on all sides but is covered by a roof, regardless of whether the structure is permanent or temporary in nature, that are accessible or open to the public, whether or not by invitation or by payment, or all places for collective use, regardless of ownership or right to access including, but not limited to, gas stations, banks, malls, shopping/business arcades, town squares, terminals, airports, railway stations, bus terminals, stadiums, arenas, stadiums, gymnasiums/covered courts, funeral parlors, barber shops, cockpits, gaming

areas, recreational facilities, pedestrian overpass, indoor car parks, waiting sheds, sidewalks, and other places where people usually congregate;

6. Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health (IDLH) - describes hazards in which an acute exposure to the hazard poses a severe risk of immediate health damage, injury, or death. This is determined by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) or the City Health Office (CHO), with proper coordination with the FDA, in accordance with their existing policies and standards;

7. Minors - are persons who are below the age of majority or below the legal age of eighteen (18) years, or persons who are eighteen (18) years of age or older, but are incapable of taking care of themselves due to their mental incapacity;

8. Person-in-charge - refers to the president/manager, in case of a company, corporation, partnership or association, the owner/proprietor/operator, in case of a single proprietorship, of accommodation and entertainment establishments, whether tourism-creditted or not, or the administrator in case of government or private property, facility or building, workplaces, enclosed or partially enclosed public places, public buildings, public outdoor spaces, places of worship, hazardous areas, food preparation areas, health facilities of any kind, centers of youth activity or other facilities frequented particularly by minors, and the city/municipal schools superintendent, school president, dean or principal, in case of schools;

9. Public Conveyances - refer to any vehicle, whether mobile or stationary, used in the transport of passengers or available to the public as a mode of transport, such as, but not limited to, jeepsneys, buses, trains, vans, taxis, airplanes, ships, and other modes of water transportation, tricycles, motorbikes, and other public utility vehicles whether covered by a Certificate of Public Convenience or not;

10. Public Building - refers to any of the following:
   - A building or structure owned by the government or owned by a private person but used, rented, or occupied by the government or any of its instrumentalities;
   - Any building or structure used or controlled exclusively for public purposes by any department or branch of government, local government unit, or barangay without reference to the ownership of the building;

11. Public Outdoor Spaces - refer to outdoor spaces that are open to the public or places where facilities are available for the public or where a crowd of people gather or congregate regardless of ownership or right to access such as, but not limited to, parks, playgrounds, sports grounds or centers, gaming areas, gardens, resorts, beaches, pools, playgrounds, streets, sidewalks, parking areas, walkways, entrance ways, waiting areas, stairwells and the like;

12. Shisha - refers to a device or instrument which may have a single or multi-stemmed pipe for smoking, whether or not it delivers nicotine to the user, in which smoke is passed through a water basin before inhalation. It is also known as water pipe, sheesha, hookah, Nergeci, Argeci and Nargile;

13. Smoking/Vaping - refers to the lighting and/or puffing of any cigarette, cigar, tobacco product of any kind, form, or type, EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like, whether it contains nicotine or not. Possession of any lighted or/ or puffed cigarette, cigar or any tobacco product, or an actually operated EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like within the prohibited areas for smoking shall constitute "prima facie" evidence of smoking/vaping as prohibited under this Ordinance;

14. Tobacco Product - any product that consists of loose tobacco, whether or not it contains nicotine, and is intended for use in a cigarette, including any product containing tobacco, and intended for smoking/vaping or oral or nasal use; and

15. Workplaces - an area, permanent or temporary, in which a person performs duties of employment or work, regardless of whether the work is done for compensation or on a voluntary basis, and includes private offices, common area and any other area which is generally used or frequented during the course of employment or work. Company-owned vehicles used for transporting employees or guests or any vehicle used in the course of work are considered workplaces.

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - It shall be unlawful for any person to smoke/vape or allow the smoking/vaping of any tobacco product, or use EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like, in all forms of Public Conveyances, Government-owned Vehicles, Accommodation and Entertainment Establishments, Workplaces, Enclosed or Partially Enclosed Public Places, Public Buildings and Public Outdoor Spaces, Places of Worship, Hazardous Areas, Food Preparation Areas, Health Facilities of any kind, Centers of Youth Activities, and areas within one hundred (100) meters from any point of the perimeter of Schools, Public Playgrounds or other facilities frequented particularly by minors, except in duly designated smoking/vaping areas as defined in Section 5 hereunder, within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City.

It shall also be unlawful for any person to domestically manufacture or adulterate any tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like that are IDLH.

It shall also be unlawful for any person, natural or juridical, to direct, order, compel, request, or ask a minor to acquire or purchase from, possess, sell, offer or distribute any tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like to any other person and/or for any other person, and to allow, abet, tolerate or permit such minor to acquire, purchase, possess, sell, offer, distribute, use, consume, or smoke/vape any tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like.

SECTION 5. DESIGNATED SMOKING/VAPING AREA (DSVA) - refers to an outdoor space where a person is permitted to smoke and vape without violating this Ordinance, provided that, the same meets the following requirements, at the minimum:

i. There shall be no opening that will allow air to escape from the DSVA to the smoke/vape-free area of the building or conveyance, except for a single door equipped with an automatic door closer;

ii. It shall be located in or within ten (10) meters from entrances, exits, or any place where people pass or congregate, or in front of air intake ducts;

iii. The DSVAs shall not be larger than twenty percent (20%) of the total floor area of the building or conveyance, provided that, in no case shall such area be larger than five (5) square meters;
iv. No building or conveyance shall have more than one DSVA; Provided that, persons-in-charge have the option of establishing one (1) Designated Smoking Area (DSA) and one (1) Designated Vaping Area (VVA) therein, subject to the same standards under this Section, with a combined area not exceeding twenty percent (20%) of the total floor area of the building or conveyance;
v. Minors shall not be allowed inside the DSVAs;
vi. It shall have the following signages highly visible and prominently displayed:
a. "Smoking/Vaping Area" signage;
b. Graphic health warnings on the effects of using tobacco products, EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like;
c. Prohibition on the entry of minors, pregnant women, and lactating mothers.
vii. Other standards and specifications to better ensure a smoke/vape-free environment as may be prescribed by existing laws, rules and regulations and policies; Provided that, such standards and specifications are consistent with this Ordinance and persons-in-charge are given sixty (60) days to comply upon the effectiveness of this Ordinance.
Nothing in this Ordinance shall compel persons-in-charge to establish DSVAs nor prevent them from instituting more stringent measures in their buildings and establishments to better ensure a smoke/vape-free environment in their premises.
All DSVAs shall be covered by a Certificate of Compliance from the City Mayor's Office through the Anti-Vices Task Force (AVTF), created under this Ordinance.

SECTION 6. MANDATORY DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS - The person-in-charge, as defined in this Ordinance, shall:
1. Prominently display "NO SMOKING/VAPING" signs in the most visible locations in the area where smoking/vaping is prohibited (main accommodation area, function rooms, bars, cashiers, VIP rooms, and comfort rooms), in order to ensure that the public can easily view such signs and be notified.
2. The "NO SMOKING/VAPING" signs for establishments and for public areas, such as public parks and public playgrounds, shall be at least eight by eighteen (8x18) inches in size and the International "NO SMOKING" symbol (consisting of a pictorial representation of a burning cigarette, and EDS or Vape enclosed in a red circle with a red bar across it) shall occupy no less than sixty percent (60%) of the said signage. The remaining forty percent (40%) of the signage shall show the pertinent information/precautionary statement, as follows:
"STRICTLY NO SMOKING/VAPING"
Per Ordinance No.
Violators are fined up to _______
Report to _______

3. Furthermore, every establishment supplying, selling, distributing and/or offering any tobacco products, EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like, must put up a prescribed sign bearing the words "STRICTLY NO SMOKING/VAPING FOR MINORS", the letters of which shall not be less than three (3) inches tall and placed in a prominent area within the establishment.

4. Remove all ashtrays or any receptacles for dispensing cigarette refuse from Accommodation and Entertainment Establishments, Workplaces, Enclosed or Partially Enclosed Public Places, Public Buildings, other Public Outdoor Spaces, Places of Worship, Hazardous Areas, Food Preparation Areas, Health Facilities of any kind, Centers of Youth Activities, and areas within one hundred (100) meters from any point of the perimeter of schools, public playgrounds, or other facilities frequented particularly by minors.

5. Ensure that all the employees in the establishment are aware of the Ordinance and to provide a procedure for informing customers or clients of the provisions of this Ordinance.

6. Undertake all reasonable steps to ensure strict implementation of this Ordinance and to inform people to refrain from smoking/vaping any tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape and the like within their establishments, except in duly approved DSVs. The person responsible for his/her, duly authorized representative, including the employees concerned, shall immediately refer to the AVD, the deputy enforcers, or any law enforcer or the nearest police station, of the violation and the violator/s thereof.

7. In terms of sale and distribution of any tobacco products, EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like:

a. No person or establishment may sell or distribute any tobacco products, EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like to minors;

b. When the age of a person is in doubt, the owners, managers or employees of any of said establishments are mandated to require the presentation of such person's valid government-issued identification (ID) card, which shall be "prima facie" evidence of said persons;

i. Persons who fail to show their valid government issued ID cards, or who present such ID which are of questionable validity, shall be denied service and in the case of minors, shall be immediately referred to the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) for their rescue. Said children rescued under this Ordinance shall be subjected to and dealt with in accordance with the provisions of the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act of 2006 under Republic Act No. 9344, as amended by Republic Act No. 10630, and the Child and Youth Welfare Code of 1974 under Presidential Decree No. 603;

ii. Persons or establishments who fail to require the presentation of such valid government-issued ID cards shall be held liable under Section 9 of this Ordinance;

SECTION 7. TOBACCO PRODUCT, ELECTRONIC DEVICE SYSTEMS, SHISHA, VAPE, AND THE LIKE OF DOMESTIC MANUFACTURE: WHEN SALE OF, MAY BE PROHIBITED - Whenever a domestically manufactured or adulterated tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like is found by the AVTF to be unlicensed before the FDA, the same must be reported to the Food and Drug Administration (FDA), for proper action. Once the FDA issues an order, report, or advisory against any unlicensed domestically manufactured or adulterated tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like, the AVTF shall ensure that the Business Bureau prohibits the manufacturing and/or sale of the same and the revocation of the Business Permit and closure of the establishment.
In case an unlicensed domestically manufactured or adulterated tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like is discovered by the City Health Office (CHO) to be IDLH to a person after conducting laboratory tests, the same must be reported to the FDA for proper action and confirmation. When the FDA issues a confirmatory report against the unlicensed domestically manufactured or adulterated tobacco product, EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like, the CHO shall immediately refer the matter to the Business Bureau for purposes of prohibiting the manufacturing and/or selling of the same, revocation of the Business Permit and to order the closure of the establishment.

With the approval of the City Mayor's Office (CMO), AVTF shall also have the power to recommend amendments or revisions of any provision of this Ordinance.

After the publication of this Ordinance, AVTF, in coordination with the Business Bureau and other concerned agencies, shall conduct information dissemination to all persons and establishments. An ocular inspection shall then be conducted to all non-accredited tourism establishments in Davao City after the effectiveness of this Ordinance to determine compliance with the requirements of this Ordinance.

For tourism-accredited accommodation and entertainment establishments, the ocular inspection shall be conducted by AVTF, in coordination with City Tourism Operations Office (CTOO) and Business Bureau.

The AVTF, in coordination with City Health Office (CHO), City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDIO), and the Department of Education XI (DePEd XI), shall undertake educational awareness campaigns on the ill effects of all vices, information dissemination programs, capacity building programs, and vices cessation clinics.

The AVTF, in coordination with the City Information Office (CIO), shall conduct an information drive on the provisions of this Ordinance and educational awareness campaigns on the ill effects of all vices through an intensified tri-media campaign.

The AVTF, in coordination with the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO) and Davao City Police Office (DCPO-TEU), shall inspect the presence of signages in all public utility vehicles and strictly monitor its compliance.

In addition to the penalty imposed for the violation of any provision of this Ordinance, the same shall carry with it the confiscation and forfeiture of all tobacco products. EDS, Shisha, Vape, and the like, involved in the commission of the offense.

SECTION 10. SMOKING/VAPING CESSATION COUNSELING - Smoking/vaping cessation counseling is required after the administrative fine has been paid. The smoking/vaping cessation counselor shall attest that the violator has undergone counseling and shall sign his name in the citation ticket and attached the required counseling form. After complying with the said requirements, the violator shall return the citation ticket to the apprehending officer or to the VRU if the deputized enforcer is not a member of DCPO.

Notwithstanding the payment of the administrative fine herein provided, the failure of any person violating any of the provisions of this Ordinance to undergo the smoking/vaping cessation counseling shall still warrant the filing of case in court.

SECTION 11. CITATION TICKETS - A citation ticket shall be issued to violators of the provisions of this Ordinance. The citation ticket shall state, among others, the name and address of the violator, the specific violation committed, and the corresponding administrative penalty. There shall be three (3) copies of the citation ticket: the first copy shall be endorsed to the VRU, the second copy shall be given to the violator, and the third copy shall be retained by the apprehending enforcer.

Failure to pay any of the administrative fines per count shall result in an additional administrative fine of One Hundred Pesos (P100.00) a day per count; Provided, that the total amount of administrative fine per count shall not exceed Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00). Provided, further, that non-payment of the total administrative fines per count within thirty (30) working days from the time of apprehension or issuance of citation ticket or failure to comply with the prescribed smoking/vaping cessation counseling shall lead to the prosecution of the case in court, subject to the penalties provided herein, without prejudice to the prosecution of other actions.

SECTION 12. DEPUTIZED ENforcERS - The City Mayor, through the Vices Regulation Unit (VRU), may deputize and authorize representatives from Davao City Police Office (DCPO) to issue the corresponding citation tickets, and apprehend violators, and the same shall be responsible for the strict implementation of this Ordinance.

Barangay officials or representatives from PSSO - Civil Security Unit (CSU), Business Bureau, City Economic Enterprise (CEE), City Health Office (CHO), City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDDO), private sector, non-government organizations, and government organizations may also be deputized and authorized to monitor compliance with the provisions of this Ordinance and report violations to the proper apprehending officers.

SECTION 13. TRAINING AND DEPUTATION - All deputized enforcers shall be required to undergo training on enforcement before they are deputized and allowed to enforce this Ordinance. Only those who complete and pass the training shall be deputized as enforcers by the City Mayor.

SECTION 14. BARANGAY OFFICIALS - Barangay Officials in Davao City shall assist with the information dissemination on the salient features of this Ordinance, and shall properly coordinate with the Department of Education XI (Deed Ed XI), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) XI, Technical Education and Skills Development

SECTION 17. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS (IRR) - The VRU shall promulgate such rules and regulations, with the approval of the City Mayor, as are necessary to ensure the effective implementation of this Ordinance.

Absence of the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of this Ordinance shall not preclude the enforcement of the provisions of this Ordinance.

SECTION 18. FUNDING - An amount of Five Million Pesos (P5,000,000.00) shall be appropriated by the City Government of Davao in its Annual Budget or in any Supplemental Budget, as the case may be, to carry out the purposes of this Ordinance.

SECTION 19. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance, local issuances, or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

In this regard, Davao City Ordinance No. 0367-12, Series of 2012, otherwise known as "The New Comprehensive Anti-Smoking Ordinance of Davao City," as amended by Davao City Ordinance No. 037-19, Series of 2019, is hereby repealed.
