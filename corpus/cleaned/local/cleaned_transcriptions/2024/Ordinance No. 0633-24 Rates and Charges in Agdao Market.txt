Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING THE RATES AND CHARGES TO BE IMPOSED IN AGDAO FARMERS MARKET".

SECTION 2. IMPOSITION OF RATES AND CHARGES - The following rates and charges shall be collected from every person or entity who shall transact in Agdao Farmers Market.

COMMERCIAL SPACES

| Commercial Spaces (Ground Floor) | Daily Rate | PHP 45.00/m2 |
|----------------------------------|------------|-------------|
| Commercial Spaces (Third Floor) | with private comfort room | Daily Rate | PHP 40.00/m2 |
| Commercial Spaces (Third Floor) | with private comfort room | Daily Rate | PHP 35.00/m2 |

FUNCTION HALLS

| Function Hall | Rate | Rate/Hour | Capacity | Inclusions | Additional Charges |
|----------------|------|------------|----------|------------|--------------------|
| Agdao Farmers Market Function Hall 1 | PHP 32,000.00/ max. of 4 hours | PHP 8,000.00 | 300 | Chairs & Tables | Aicon (PHP 250.00 per hr/unit) |
| Agdao Farmers Market Function Hall 2 | PHP 32,000.00/ max. of 4 hours | PHP 8,000.00 | 300 | Chairs & Tables | Aicon (PHP 250.00 per hr/unit) |
| Agdao Farmers Market Function Hall 3 | PHP 32,000.00/ max. of 4 hours | PHP 8,000.00 | 300 | Chairs & Tables | Aicon (PHP 250.00 per hr/unit) |
| Agdao Farmers Market Function Hall 4 | PHP 32,000.00/ max. of 4 hours | PHP 8,000.00 | 300 | Chairs & Tables | Aicon (PHP 250.00 per hr/unit) |

**SECTION 3. TIME OF PAYMENT AND PENALTIES FOR DELINQUENCY**
All rates and charges imposed in Section 2 for rental of commercial spaces shall be due and demandable within the first twenty (20) days of the month. For function halls, fees shall be payable at the time of booking.

Failure to pay the rental fee for commercial spaces within the period required herein shall subject the lessee or occupant to a surcharge of a sum of twenty-five percent (25%) of the amount of the rental fee plus an interest of two percent (2%) per month, not to exceed thirty-six (36%) months.

**SECTION 4. COLLECTION**
The City Treasurer is responsible for the collection of the rates and charges imposed in Section 2. To ensure effective discharge of this responsibility, all collectors, including other personnel involved in the collection function at Agdao Farmers Market, shall directly be under the control and supervision of the City Treasurer, for collection performance and accountability.

**SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE**
All Ordinances or portions of Ordinances in conflict with this Ordinance, or inconsistent with the regulations of this Ordinance, are hereby repealed to the extent necessary to give this Ordinance full force and effect.

**SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is held to be unconstitutional or invalid by competent authority, such judgment or action shall not affect or impair other sections or provisions hereof.

**SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY**
This Ordinance shall take effect FIFTEEN (15) DAYS after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

**ENACTED**
on the 10th day of September 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
