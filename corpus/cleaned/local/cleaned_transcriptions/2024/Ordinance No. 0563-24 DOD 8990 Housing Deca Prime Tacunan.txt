AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION MADE AND EXECUTED BY 8990 HOUSING DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF ROAD LOTS, OPEN SPACES, AND THE IMPROVEMENTS TO THE APOLINARES RESORT RESIDENCES VILLAGE PRIME, LOCATED IN BARANGAY TACUNAN, TACUBO DISTRICT; THIS CITY

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0563-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT, SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION MADE AND EXECUTED BY 8990 HOUSING DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF ROAD LOTS, OPEN SPACES, AND THE IMPROVEMENTS OF DECA HOMES RESORT RESIDENCES PHASE PRIME, LOCATED IN BARANGAY TACUNAN, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY;

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Rule VII, Section 33 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 220 provides for donations of roads and open spaces, which reads:

"The owner or project developer shall donate the roads and open spaces found in the project to the local government of the area after their completion had been certified by the Commission and it shall be mandatory for the said local government to accept such donations."

Sections 22 (a) (5) (c) and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.

(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts;
and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the chief PRIME, in behalf of the local government unit without proper authorization by the sanguanig concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol of the city, municipal, or barangay hall."

"Section 455. Chief PRIME: Powers, Duties, and Compensation.

(b) For efficient, effective, and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city may shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sanguaning Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the grant of authority to the City Mayor to accept and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation made and executed by 8990 Housing Development Corporation relative to the donation of road lots, open spaces, and the improvements of Deca Homes Resort Residences Phase Prime, located in Barangay Tacunan, Tugbok District, this City.

**SECTION 4. AUTHORITY -** The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to accept and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation made and executed by 8990 Housing Development Corporation relative to the donation of road lots, open spaces, and the improvements of Deca Homes Resort Residences Phase Prime, located in Barangay Tacunan, Tugbok District, this City.

**SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY -** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED, on the 16th day of July 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
