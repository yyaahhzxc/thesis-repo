Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 4 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2024, COMPRISING THE BUDGET UNDER THE GENERAL FUND PROPER AND THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF FORTY-SEVEN MILLION NINE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (PHP47,900,000.00)."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Aligned with its mandate and in response to the community's needs, the City Government of Davao affirms its commitment to optimize available resources. It likewise emphasizes the responsible and prudent utilization of these resources, ensuring that the allocated amounts are justified and aligned with the intended purposes and mandates.

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS - The following amounts or so much thereof as may be, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Council of the City of Davao, not otherwise appropriated from any other source, for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2024, except as otherwise provided hereunder:

GARRY MANTILLO CPA, MBA, CFS, OMB

AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS, AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES - The Supplemental Budget No. 4 of the City Government of Davao for Calendar Year 2024 is funded from the available sources of funds in the amount of FORTY-SEVEN MILLION NINE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (PHP47,900,000.00), broken down as follows:

**SECTION 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS**
The amount herein appropriated for the projects indicated herein shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget must conform with existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules, regulations, policies, and guidelines of the Commission on Audit (COA), Department of Budget and Management (DBM), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and Presidential directives.

**SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS**
The use of funds under Supplemental Budget No. 4 of the City Government of Davao for Calendar Year 2024, utilized in violation of Title 5, Local Fiscal Administration, as embodied in RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, shall subject the erring officials and employees to disciplinary action or punishment as provided under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines. The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable; and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

**SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable; and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

**SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY**
The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect on the date of its enactment.

**ENACTED, December 23, 2024, by a unanimous vote of all Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.**
