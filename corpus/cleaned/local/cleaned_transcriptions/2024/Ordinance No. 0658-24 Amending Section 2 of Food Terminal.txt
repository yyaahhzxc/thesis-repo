SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances or portions of Ordinances in conflict with this Ordinance, or inconsistent with the regulations of this Ordinance, are hereby repealed to the extent necessary to give this Ordinance full force and effect.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is held to be unconstitutional or invalid by competent authority, such judgment or action shall not affect or impair the other sections or provisions thereof.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.
