Ord. No. 0460-24

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be referred to as "AN ORDINANCE
ALLOWING THE REQUEST OF THE HEIRS OF ROSARIO S. CALZADA,
REPRESENTED BY CELSA CALZADA-MIASIS, ATTORNEY-IN-FACT, FOR A
WRITTEN CONSENT AND AUTHORITY TO DISPOSE THEIR TITLED PROPERTY,
PARTICULARLY DESCRIBED AS LOT 17, BLOCK 22, LOCATED IN BO. OBRERO
DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. PERTINENT PROVISIONS UNDER THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT
CODE OF 1991 -

"Section 16. General Welfare. Every Local government unit shall exercise the
power expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers
necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance,
and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their
respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support,
among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, encourage and
support the development of the arts, sciences, and technology, enhance public
capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity, protect
justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order,
and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers Duties and Compensation -
(a) The city mayor, as chief executive of the city government, shall exercise such
powers and perform such duties and functions as provided by this Code and other
laws.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the
general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code
the city mayor shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all
bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of
the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the grant of legislative
authority to the City Mayor to allow the request of Ms. Rosario S. Calzada,
represented by Ms. Celsa Calzada-Miasis, Attorney-In-Fact, for written consent and authority to dispose their titled property, particularly described as Lot 17, Block 22, located in Bo. Obrero, Davao City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section of this
Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 22nd day of March 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
