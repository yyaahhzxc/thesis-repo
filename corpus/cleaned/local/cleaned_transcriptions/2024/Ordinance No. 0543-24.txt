Ord. No. 0543-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROADS (FULL CLOSURE) TO VEHICULAR
TRAFFIC OF SAN PEDRO SQUARE (CM RECTO-SAN PEDRO - RIZAL AND
BOLTON STS.) ON JUNE 29 (12:00 NOON) UNTIL 6:00 AM ON JUNE 30, 2024
IN CONNECTION WITH THE REQUEST OF DAVAO CITY TOURISM
OPERATIONS OFFICE IN CELEBRATION OF DUAW DAVAO 2024".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21. (c) of Republic Act No.
7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that: "any
national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual
emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the celebration of
Duaw Davao 2024, the City Tourism Operations Office requested for additional closure
of roads to vehicular traffic the San Pedro Square (CM Recto - San Pedro - Rizal and
Bolton Sts.) on June 29 (12:00 noon) until 6:00 am on June 30, 2024 in connection
with the request of Davao City Tourism Operations Office in celebration of Duaw Davao 2024.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 25th day of June 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
