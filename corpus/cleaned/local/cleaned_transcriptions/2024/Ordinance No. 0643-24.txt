Ord. No. 0643-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE STREETS ALONG SAavedra St., TO AGTON ST., ON OCTOBER 7, 2024 FROM 6:00 AM TO 10:00 AM IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE FEAST OF ST. ROSARIO PARISH."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the upcoming parochial fiesta, the Barangay Councilor of Toril Proper, Toril District, this City, requested for the temporary closure to vehicular traffic of the aforesaid streets on the specified date, time and purpose.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED on the 1st day of October 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
