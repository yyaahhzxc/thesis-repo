Ord. No. 0529-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF BOTH Portions of Roxas Avenue
(Fronting Ateneo de Davao University and Aldevinco Side) from
4:00 AM up to 11:00 AM on June 16, 2024, in connection with the
CENTRALIZED EIDUL ADHA CONGREGATIONAL PRAYER".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (C) of Republic Act No.
7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that: "any
national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual
emergency, or festa celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - Both portions of Roxas Avenue
(fronting Ateneo De Davao University and Aldevinco Side) will be temporarily closed from
4:00 am up to 11:00 am on June 16, 2024, in connection with the Centralized Eidul Adha Congregational Prayer.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 11th day of June 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
