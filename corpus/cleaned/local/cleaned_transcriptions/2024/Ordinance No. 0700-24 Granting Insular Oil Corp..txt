Ord. No. 0700-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE REQUEST OF INSULAR OIL CORPORATION TO REVERT
THE ZONE OF ITS AREA TO HEAVY INDUSTRIAL ZONE, LOCATED IN
BARANGAY SASA, DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - This Ordinance is enacted pursuant to the
provisions of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government
Code of 1991, dated 10 October 1991, particularly Sections 447, 448 and 458 a.2
(7-9), "Authorizing the City, through the Sangguniang Panlungsod, to adopt a
Zoning Ordinance subject to the provisions of existing laws" and in accordance with
related laws, Ordinance No. 0861-22, Series of 2022, as supplemented by
Ordinance No. 0861-23, Series of 2022, and An Ordinance Adding the Davao City
Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) 2019-2028.

SECTION 3. LEGAL BASIS - Article III - Definition of Terms, of the 2019-
2028 CLUP defines Commercial-2 Zone and Industrial-3 Zone, as follows:

(44) Commercial-2 Zone (C2-Z) - medium to high density commercial area
intended for trade, service and business activities performing
complementary/supplementary functions to the CBD.

(93) Industrial-3 Zone (I-3) - An area intended for heavy manufacturing of
production industries that are:
a. Highly pollutive/non-hazardous
b. Highly pollutive/hazardous
c. Highly pollutive/extremely hazardous
d. Pollutive/extremely hazardous
e. Non-pollutive/extremely hazardous

Article V - Zone Regulations. The 2019-2028 CLIP provides for the following
regulations and allowable uses/activities for "Industrial-3 Zone (I-3-Z)":

(31) Warehouse for high pollutive/hazardous industries

• Staff houses/quarters
• Offices

SECTION 4. PURPOSES.— The area of Insular Oil Corporation, located in Barangay Sasa, Davao City, classified as "Commercial-2," is hereby reverted to its previous zoning classification "Heavy Industrial Zone." A cursory reading of the definitions of the two zoning classifications under Article III would reveal that the area of business of Insular Oil Corporation is more in line with that of the Industrial-3 (I-3) Zone, it being primarily engaged in the production and supply of fuel in Davao City. Moreover, the allowable uses/activities listed under the zone regulations for highly pollutive/industries, and highly pollutive/extremely hazardous industries under Article IV, are more relevant to the nature of the business operations of the proponent.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY — This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 26th day of November 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
