Ord. No. 0578-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROADS TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC
PARTICULARLY ON CERTAIN SECTIONS OF DAVAO CITY COASTAL ROAD
AND THE DAVAO-COTABATO ROAD ON AUGUST 5-11, 2024 IN CONNECTION
WITH THE 2024 IRON MAN 70.3 DAVAO AS PER ATTACHED DOCUMENTS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No.
7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that: "any
national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an
actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial
fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and water
works projects."

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The Office of the City Mayor thru the
Office of the Vice Mayor requested for a road closure on August 5-11, 2024
in connection with the 2024 IRON MAN 70.3 DAVAO. Attached herewith is the
endorsement from the CTITMO identifying the specific date, time, place and type of
closure pertaining to the aforementioned event (Please see attached schedule).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections
or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force
and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 23rd day of July 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
