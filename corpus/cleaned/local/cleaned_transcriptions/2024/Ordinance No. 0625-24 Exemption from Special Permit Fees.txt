AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION TO ALL EXHIBITORS IN THE DAVAO AGRITRADE EXPO 2024 FROM THE PAYMENT OF SPECIAL PERMIT FEES COVERING THE PERIOD SEPTEMBER 19 TO 21, 2024, AT SMX CONVENTION CENTER, LANANG, THIS CITY

Ord. No. 0625-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION TO ALL EXHIBITORS IN THE DAVAO AGRITRADE EXPO 2024 FROM THE PAYMENT OF SPECIAL PERMIT FEES COVERING THE PERIOD SEPTEMBER 19 TO 21, 2024, AT SMX CONVENTION CENTER, LANANG, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. BASIS - The Sangguniang Panlungsod, as the legislative Body of the City may grant tax exemptions, incentives, or reliefs in accordance with Section 458 (a)(2)(i) of the Local Government Code of 1991. Further, Section 236, Article 28, of Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, or the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City provides that exemption on payment of Special Mayor's Permit may also be granted to programs conducted by educational, charitable, religious, and governmental institutions, NGOs duly registered/ accredited with the City Government.

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - All exhibitors who will participate in the Davao AgriTrade Expo 2024 (DATE) are hereby exempted from the payment of Special Permit Fees covering the period September 19 to 21, 2024.

SECTION 4. LIMITATIONS - The grant of exemption shall be limited only to the Special Permit Fees imposed by the City. Consequently, all other fees, impositions, and taxes shall be paid in accordance with law.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 3rd day of September 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
