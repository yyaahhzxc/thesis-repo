Ord. No. 0657-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE 10TH INFANTRY (AGILIA) DIVISION OF THE PHILIPPINE ARMY AND THE CITY OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE RENEWAL OF FIVE (5) CIVILIANS ARMED FORCES GEOGRAPHICAL UNIT (CAAFU) ACTIVE AUXILIARY COMPANY II (CAAC-II) COMPOSED OF SIX HUNDRED (600) CAAS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22 (a) (5) (c) and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, provide that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers -
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and
(b) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation -
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the 10th Infantry (AGILIA) Division of the Philippine Army and the City of Davao relative to the renewal of five (5) Citizens Armed Forces Geographical Unit (CAAFU) Active Auxiliary Company II (CAAC-II) composed of six hundred (600) CAAS.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

--- [Page 3] ---
**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED**, October 29, 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into this day of in Headquarters 10th Infantry (AGILA) Division, Philippine Army, Camp Gen Manuel T Yan Sr., Barangay Tubaran, Mawab, Davao de Oro, by and between:

The Local Government of Davao City, duly organized and existing under Philippine laws, with office address at City Hall Building, San Pedro Street, Davao City represented herein by City Mayor SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE hereinafter referred to as FIRST PARTY;

-and-

The 10TH INFANTRY (AGILA) DIVISION, PHILIPPINE ARMY stationed at Camp General Manuel T Yan Sr., Barangay Tubaran, Mawab, Davao de Oro represented herein by MGEN ALLAN D HAMBALA PA hereinafter referred to as SECOND PARTY.

WITNESSETH:

Whereas, the First Party requested the renewal of five (5) Citizens Armed Forces Geographical Unit Active Auxiliary Company II (CAAC-II) within its territory to supplement its efforts and further secure and ensure the peace and order situation in its locality, wherein the Task Force Davao is utilizing three (3) units of CAAC-II and the 1003rd Infantry (Raptor) Brigade is utilizing two (2) units of CAAC-II.

Whereas, the Second Party agrees to the request of the First Party for the services of the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th Davao City CAFGU Active Auxiliary Company Category - II CAAC-II composed of six hundred (600) CAAAs to secure Davao City, subject to the terms and conditions as agreed by both parties.

Wherefore, the parties bind themselves to the following terms and conditions:

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS:

1. Both parties agree to terms and conditions set forth by GHQ LOI 10-2005 Nr 10-2005 (Revision to LOI 09-2005 Tanggol Bayan "Delta"), Interim IRR to EO 264 s 1987, and other relevant laws and policies.

2. Both parties agree that a need for Threat Justification issued by the Regional Intelligence Coordinating Committee (RICC) shall be required in all activation and renewal of CAAC-II.

3. The activation of CAAC-II depends on military exigencies or as "dictated by military necessity."

--- [Page 5] ---
4. The Second Party reserves the right to recommend termination of the CAA-II services when the security situation warrants or in the event of violation of the provisions of the CAA Guidelines and this MOA.

5. The Second Party reserves the right to preserve the unit integrity of the CAA-I. The minimum strength in accordance with existing policies shall be maintained in any one location. To this end, the Second Party reserves the right to increase or decrease the number of CAA-II, as the need arises.

6. CAA-II should not be primarily utilized for manning checkpoints or the entry and exit gates of a Local Government Unit establishment, rather as support elements. The security guards or police officer, in the case of LGU, should be the principal actors in such situations particularly in frisking, search and inspections since these are law enforcement functions.

7. The CAA-I should in no way join any labor union in the Local Government Unit to avoid conflict of interest. Should this happen, his CAA-II service shall be terminated.

8. In all matters, when conflict arises between an AFP regulation and an LGU rule or regulation, the AFP regulation shall always prevail.

9. All supplies, equipment and other properties of the affected CAA-II that were procured and provided by the Local Government Unit shall be reverted back to the sponsoring Local Government Unit. However, all ammunitions shall be recalled and be surrendered to the AFP.

II. OBLIGATIONS OF THE FIRST PARTY:

1. The First Party shall be required to provide all appointed CAA-II for the following allowances and benefits, including but limited to:

a) Subsistence Allowance at least same as with a CAA-I;
b) Annual issuance of CCIE at least same as with a CAA-I;
c) Combat Incentive Pay in coordination with the sponsoring LGUs;
d) Hospitalization/Medical care for injuries and sickness incurred in line of duty;
e) Life insurance at least same as with a CAA-I;
f) Special Financial Assistance (SFA) as part of the sponsoring LGU policy;
g) Scholarship Program for dependents as part of the sponsoring LGU's policy;
h) Phil Health in coordination with LGU;
i) PAG-IBIG Membership and Monthly Contribution;
j) Social Security System (SSS) Membership and Monthly Contribution;
k) Separation Gratuity;
l) Death benefits at least same as with a CAA-I.

2. The First Party shall be responsible for any claim arising from personal injury, damages or death caused by CAA-II when such injury or death is incurred in the course of lawful performance of duty.

3. The First Party shall provide complete uniform and equipment (CCIE) to the CAA-II.

--- [Page 6] ---
4. The First Party must not, for all intents and purposes, employ CAA-II in the pursuit of any personal, political, economic, or any motive to wield power and influence.

5. First Party must shoulder the necessary expenses for the purchase of ammunitions during the training and ammunition cost during actual deployment.

6. In case of losses in government properties issued to CAAIs-II, an ad hoc investigation committee shall be immediately convened by the Second Party (AFP). The First Party being secured by the CAAC-II shall pay corresponding amount of the damages for lost government properties which depends on the following circumstances:

a) If after investigation by the Second Party, the loss is found to have been occasioned by the intentional breach of the First Party, such as, but not limited to, utilizing the CAAI-II other than their mandated purposes such as being guides, caradrones, personal security of VIPs, and utilization of CAAI-II outside authorized areas of deployment/employment in the absence of appropriate authority, the compensation to be paid by the First Party to the Second Party shall be the value of lost of property + an amount as may be reasonably determined by the ad hoc investigation committee in light of intentional breach.

b) If after due investigation by the Second Party, the loss is found to have been occasioned by the gross neglect of the First Party, the compensation to be paid by the First Party to the Second Party shall be the value of lost of property + an amount as may be reasonably determined by the ad hoc investigation committee in light of gross negligence.

c) If after due investigation by the Second Party, the loss was occasioned by negligence and not under the performance of the official function or in line of duty of the CAAI-II, no liability shall be accrued to the First Party."

7. The FIRST PARTY will provide the necessary billeting space or hutment for their CAAI-II and augmentation/response force within the strategic vicinity to ensure the security of the FIRST PARTY. Further, "off-site" facilities will be determined by the concerned SECOND PARTY (AFP) ground commanders.

III. OBLIGATIONS OF THE SECOND PARTY:

1. The Second Party shall provide CAAIC-II to the First Party anchored primarily on the protection of life and property within the territorial jurisdiction of Local Government of Davao City.

2. The Second Party, in consultation with the Peace and Order Council (POC) of the Local Government of Davao City shall screen volunteers of CAAI-II based on the qualification standards as prescribed in an appropriate AFP regulation.

3. The Second Party must ensure that appointed members for CAAIC-II must possess the following qualifications;

--- [Page 7] ---
a) Natural-born Filipino citizen.

b) Volunteer reservist.

c) Preferably belonging to the First Category Reserve/Age profile (16-35 years old).

d) Not more than 51 years old.

e) Bona fide resident of the community

f) Physically and mentally fit for combat duty.

g) Good moral character.

h) No criminal record

i) Nominated by the Barangay Peace and Order Council and/or Municipal Peace and Order Council through a Resolution.

4. The Infantry Division Commander shall initiate the recommendation to the Unified Command Commander.

5. The Second Party shall provide firearms to the CAAC-II during the conduct of the training up to their actual deployment.

6. The Second Party and/or its authorized representative shall exercise direct control and supervision over the CAAC-II assigned to the Task Force Deva, Sta. Ana Wharf, Brgy. Leon Garcia, Davao City and 1003rd Infantry (Raptor) Brigade, in accordance with the rules and regulations promulgated by the AFP.

7. In the event of imminent or actual attack on the LGU by insurgents, terrorists and lawless elements, the Second Party shall have the authority to muster the other CAAC-II under its operational control to secure Davao City.

IV. EFFECTIVITY

This agreement shall be valid between the parties, their successor-in-interest and their assigns, and shall take effect upon approval hereof and remain in force for one (1) year unless sooner terminated upon mutual consent of the parties or any violation of the provisions herein upon prior written notice.

Three (3) months prior to the expiration of this MOA, LGU or Non-Government Entity should signify its intent of renewal provided that the required justification by the RICC is validated and updated.

Further, upon the approval of this MOA, all succeeding contracts signed by the contracting parties emanating therefrom or for its further implementation shall be considered effective and valid.
