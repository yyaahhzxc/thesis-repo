ORDINANCE NO. 0485-24
Series of 2024

AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON APRIL 30, 2024 FROM 6:30 AM UNTIL 12:00 MIDNIGHT ON MAY 12, 2024 FROM SAN FRANCISCO STREET UP TO KAMAGONG STREET IN FRONT OF THE BARANGAY HALL OF BARANGAY BUHANGIN PROPER IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THEIR ARAW NG BARANGAY

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

--- [Page 2] ---
**AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON APRIL 30, 2024 FROM 8:30AM UNTIL 12:00 MIDNIGHT ON MAY 12, 2024 FROM SAN FRANCISCO STREET UP TO KAMAGONG STREET IN FRONT OF THE BARANGAY HALL OF BARANGAY BUHANGIN PROPER IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THEIR ARAW NG BARANGAY**

**SECTION 1. TITLE:** This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ON APRIL 30, 2024 FROM 8:30AM UNTIL 12:00 MIDNIGHT ON MAY 12, 2024 FROM SAN FRANCISCO STREET UP TO KAMAGONG STREET IN FRONT OF THE BARANGAY HALL OF BARANGAY BUHANGIN PROPER IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THEIR ARAW NG BARANGAY".

**SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY:** Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7168, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, square, or public place is hereby declared to be closed during an actual emergency, or a fiesta celebration, public rallies, and agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and waterworks projects xxx;

**SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE:** In connection with the upcoming Araw ng Barangay Buhangin Proper, Punong Barangay Omar Vincent S. Duterte requested for the closure to vehicular traffic of the aforementioned road on the specified date and time;

**SECTION 4. SEPARATIBILITAS CLAUSE:** If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY:** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 30th day of April 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head III).

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

CHIARITO M. SANTOS Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
