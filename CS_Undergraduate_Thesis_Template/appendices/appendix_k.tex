\chapter{Annotator Certificate of Contribution}
\label{app:annotator_certificate}

This appendix presents the official visual layout of the Certificate of Contribution and Commendation, designed in coordination with the Department of Computer Science and approved by the course professor for issuance to participating Sangguniang Panlungsod legal researchers upon completion of their annotation assignments during the post-study institutional deployment roadmap. The certificate is formally structured to serve as an institutional credential suitable for inclusion in the evaluators' official Civil Service 201 personnel records in recognition of their voluntary professional advisory assistance.
