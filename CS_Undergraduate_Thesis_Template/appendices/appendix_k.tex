\chapter{Annotator Certificate of Contribution}
\label{app:annotator_certificate}

This appendix presents the official \textit{Certificate of Contribution} awarded to the legal researchers and legislative staff members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City who participate in validating the Ground Truth benchmark dataset. In institutional coordination with the Sangguniang Panlungsod and in strict compliance with Ateneo de Davao University research ethics guidelines, this certificate formally recognizes each evaluator's voluntary service, legal domain expertise, and analytical contributions to this research study.

The certificate layout is structured specifically to serve as formal institutional documentation suitable for inclusion in the annotators' official Civil Service 201 personnel records. Each certificate is officially endorsed and signed by the Assistant Dean for Computer Studies, the Thesis Adviser, the Thesis Course Instructor, and the Student Proponents.
