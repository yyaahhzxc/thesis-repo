\section{Participant Information Sheet}
\label{sec:app_participant_info_sheet}

The following information sheet was presented to all prospective legal evaluators prior to obtaining written informed consent:

\begin{quote}
    \small
    \textbf{Project Title:} A Coarse-to-Fine Semantic Conflict Detection System for Ex-Ante Davao City Ordinances Using Information Retrieval and Natural Language Inference\\
    \textbf{Institutional Affiliation:} Department of Computer Science, School of Arts and Sciences, Ateneo de Davao University\\
    \textbf{Principal Investigators:} Ralph Paolo Dulce \& Yahyah Odin\\
    \textbf{Research Adviser:} Mr.~Adrian Ablazo\\
    \textbf{Course Professor:} Ma'am Grace Tacadao\\[6pt]
    \textbf{1. Purpose of the Study:}\\
    You are cordially invited to participate in an undergraduate research study conducted by senior Computer Science students of Ateneo de Davao University. This study evaluates an artificial intelligence decision-support prototype designed to identify potential semantic conflicts and statutory preemptions between proposed Davao City ordinances and superior national statutes or existing local legislation.\\[4pt]
    \textbf{2. Participant Role and Procedures:}\\
    As a participant, you will be requested to review paired statutory provisions (comprising a local ordinance draft provision and a candidate national law or prior ordinance provision) and assign a three-way consistency classification: (a)~\textit{Contradiction / Conflict}, (b)~\textit{Entailment / Alignment}, or (c)~\textit{Neutral / Independent}. Following the pairwise review, you will be invited to evaluate the prototype interface using a standardized 14-item questionnaire regarding system usability and perceived utility. The entire evaluation takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes.\\[4pt]
    \textbf{3. Voluntary Participation and Right to Withdraw:}\\
    Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any specific question, pause the evaluation, or withdraw your participation at any time without penalty or explanation. Your decision whether or not to participate will have no impact on your employment status, job evaluations, or professional relationship with the Sangguniang Panlungsod or Ateneo de Davao University.\\[4pt]
    \textbf{4. Risks and Benefits:}\\
    This evaluation carries no physical, legal, or occupational risks. The questions pertain strictly to the objective semantic consistency between statutory texts and your user experience with the software tool. Participants who complete the evaluation will receive an official Certificate of Contribution and Commendation issued by Ateneo de Davao University, structured for inclusion in their official Civil Service Form 201 records.\\[4pt]
    \textbf{5. Privacy and Data Security:}\\
    All collected feedback is strictly confidential. Personal names and email addresses are separated from response data and replaced with alphanumeric participant codes (\textit{e.g.}, \texttt{EVAL-01}). Aggregated statistical metrics will be reported in the academic thesis and potential scholarly publications, with zero individual attribution.
\end{quote}

\section{Data Privacy Compliance Protocol (Republic Act No.~10173)}
\label{sec:app_data_privacy_protocol}

Data handling across the human evaluation lifecycle adheres strictly to the statutory principles of the \textit{Data Privacy Act of 2012} (Republic Act No.~10173):

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Principle of Transparency:} Prior to data collection, participants receive explicit disclosure regarding the specific variables collected (years of legal drafting experience, pairwise conflict classifications, and Likert-scale usability scores).
    \item \textbf{Principle of Legitimate Purpose:} Collected data is utilized exclusively for determining inter-annotator agreement metrics (Fleiss' Kappa and Cohen's Kappa) and evaluating prototype system acceptance.
    \item \textbf{Principle of Proportionality:} No sensitive personal information---such as government identification numbers, residential addresses, political affiliations, or compensation data---is requested or recorded.
    \item \textbf{Pseudonymization and Cryptographic Protection:} All evaluation submissions are assigned a random hash identifier upon intake. Raw response rosters are stored on an encrypted, access-restricted partition (AES-256) accessible only to the principal investigators and thesis adviser.
    \item \textbf{Data Disposal Policy:} Upon the expiration of the three-year academic retention window mandated by university policy, all digital evaluation logs will be subjected to DoD 5220.22-M cryptographic zeroization.
\end{enumerate}
