\chapter[Evaluation Questionnaire]{Consistency Evaluation Questionnaire}
\label{app:usability_instruments}

This appendix presents the digital evaluation instrument administered to Sangguniang Panlungsod legal researchers, transcribed from the Google Forms questionnaire. The instrument includes the introductory statement, the data privacy terms, evaluator demographic details, and the pairwise evaluation response structure.

\hrule
\vspace{1em}

\begin{center}
    \textbf{\large SP Davao City -- Legal Consistency Ground Truth Evaluation}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Introductory Statement}

\noindent Magis Greetings!

\noindent We are Ralph Paolo Dulce and Yahyah Odin, senior Computer Science students from Ateneo de Davao University, under the advisory of Mr. Adrian ``Ogs'' Ablazo and course guidance of Ma'am Grace Tacadao. In institutional coordination with the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, we are conducting our undergraduate thesis entitled: \textit{``A Coarse-to-Fine Semantic Conflict Detection System for Ex-Ante Davao City Ordinances Using Information Retrieval and Natural Language Inference.''}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Purpose and Your Role:} Under RA 7160 and the \textit{Magtajas v. Pryce} doctrine, local ordinances cannot contradict national statutes. To aid Second Reading legislative review, our study develops an automated screening tool to flag potential conflicts in proposed Davao City Ordinances against national laws and enacted city ordinances before enactment. As SP legal researchers, your independent legal judgments serve as the authoritative gold standard, or essentially the answer key, to benchmark our system's accuracy.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Workload:} You are reviewing ONE batch of 70 short provision pairs (taking approximately 60 minutes). You will NOT be asked to review the full 350-item dataset. We deeply appreciate your valuable time, legal expertise, and service in supporting this research. Your insights will directly contribute to advancing data-driven decision support tools tailored to the legislative review workflow of the Davao City Council.

\noindent\textbf{Data Privacy Notice \& Informed Consent (R.A. 10173)}

\noindent Please read the ethical guidelines and data privacy terms governing this research evaluation:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Statutory Compliance \& Institutional Oversight:} In strict compliance with Republic Act No.~10173 (The Data Privacy Act of 2012), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the ethical standards of the Ateneo de Davao University Research Ethics Committee, this study guarantees the full confidentiality, privacy, and intellectual integrity of your responses.
    \item \textbf{Purpose \& Scope of Data Collection:} All collected data, including your professional background and legal ratings (Contradiction, Entailment, Neutral), will be utilized exclusively for academic research within this undergraduate thesis to benchmark the detection accuracy and inter-rater reliability of an automated statutory conflict screening system.
    \item \textbf{Strict Confidentiality \& Evaluator Anonymization:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=2pt]
        \item \textit{Anonymized Analysis:} All evaluations will be analyzed and presented strictly in aggregate form (e.g., domain accuracy rates, confusion matrices). No individual response will ever be singled out or publicly attributed to any researcher.
        \item \textit{Coded Identifiers:} For statistical tracking across the 350-pair benchmark, your responses will be processed using an anonymized alphanumeric identifier (e.g., Evaluator-01 to Evaluator-15).
        \item \textit{Voluntary Name Provision:} Providing your name in the subsequent demographic section is purely OPTIONAL. If provided, your name will be used strictly by the proponents and faculty to generate a Certificate of Contribution and Commendation endorsed for inclusion in your Civil Service 201 personnel records. If you choose to leave the name field blank, your evaluation will remain completely anonymous.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Voluntary Participation \& Right to Withdraw:} Your participation in this evaluation is entirely voluntary. You may pause, skip any question, or withdraw from this study at any stage without incurring any administrative, professional, or employment-related consequences, and without prejudice to your standing at the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City.
    \item \textbf{Data Retention \& Security Safeguards:} All raw submission data will be securely stored within password-protected, encrypted institutional storage accessible solely to the thesis proponents and their academic adviser. Data will be retained only for the duration required for thesis defense and institutional archiving, after which any identifying administrative logs will be permanently deleted.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Pairwise Consistency Evaluation Item Structure}

\noindent For each assigned item, evaluators read the Controlling National Statute provision and the corresponding Davao City Ordinance provision, then answer the following two standard questions:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=6pt]
    \item \textbf{Question 1: Legal Consistency Decision (Required):}\\
    \textit{Mark only one oval.}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
        \item[$\bigcirc$] Contradiction (Legal Conflict / Exceeds Authority)
        \item[$\bigcirc$] Entailment (Complies With / Directly Executes)
        \item[$\bigcirc$] Neutral (Independent / Unrelated Subject Matter)
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Question 2: Confidence Rating (Required):}\\
    \textit{Mark only one oval.}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
        \item[$\bigcirc$] 1 - Low
        \item[$\bigcirc$] 2
        \item[$\bigcirc$] 3 - High
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
