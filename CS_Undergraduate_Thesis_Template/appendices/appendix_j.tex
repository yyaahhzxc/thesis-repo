\section{Institutional Statement and Informed Consent (R.A. 10173)}
\label{sec:app_gforms_consent}

\begin{center}
    \textbf{SP Davao City --- Legal Consistency Ground Truth Evaluation}\\
    \small Ateneo de Davao University $\cdot$ Department of Computer Science
\end{center}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Research Context:} Under Republic Act No.~7160 and the landmark \textit{Magtajas v. Pryce Properties} doctrine, local ordinances cannot contradict national statutes. To assist Second Reading legislative reviews, this study develops an automated screening tool to flag candidate legal conflicts in draft Davao City ordinances before enactment. As Sangguniang Panlungsod legal researchers, your independent professional ratings serve as the authoritative ground truth benchmark to evaluate model accuracy.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Data Privacy Notice (Republic Act No.~10173):}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Statutory Compliance and Oversight:} In strict compliance with Republic Act No.~10173 (The Data Privacy Act of 2012) and the ethical standards of the Ateneo de Davao University Research Ethics Committee, this study guarantees full confidentiality, privacy, and intellectual integrity.
    \item \textbf{Purpose and Scope:} All submitted ratings (Contradiction, Entailment, Neutral) and qualitative notes are collected strictly for academic benchmarking within this undergraduate thesis.
    \item \textbf{Evaluator Anonymization:} Evaluations are analyzed in aggregate (e.g., domain accuracy, inter-rater reliability). Evaluators are assigned alphanumeric tokens (\texttt{SP-ANN-01} to \texttt{SP-ANN-15}). Name submission is optional and used solely for issuing official Certificates of Contribution for Civil Service 201 records.
    \item \textbf{Voluntary Participation:} Participation is voluntary. Evaluators may skip items or withdraw at any stage without prejudice to their institutional standing.
    \item \textbf{Data Retention:} Raw responses are stored in encrypted institutional repositories accessible solely to proponents and their adviser, and retained only for thesis defense verification.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Pairwise Consistency Evaluation Instrument}
\label{sec:app_gforms_items}

Each evaluation item presents a verified national statute section alongside an authentic or draft municipal ordinance provision. Below is the standardized question card format deployed in Google Forms:

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.97\linewidth}{
\textbf{Sample Item: GT-001 [Panel A, Item 1 of 70]}\\[4pt]
\textbf{NATIONAL STATUTE PREMISE (Republic Act No.~7581 \S6):}\\
\textit{``Section 6. Automatic Price Control. --- Unless otherwise declared by the President, prices of basic necessities in an area shall automatically be frozen at their prevailing prices or placed under automatic price control whenever that area is proclaimed or declared a disaster area or under a state of calamity\dots\ Unless sooner lifted by the President, price control of basic necessities under this section shall remain effective for the duration of the condition that brought it about, but not for more than sixty (60) days.''}\\[4pt]
\textbf{MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE HYPOTHESIS (Davao City Draft Clause \S4):}\\
\textit{``SECTION 4. MANDATORY LOCAL PRICE CEILINGS UPON CALAMITY. --- In accordance with local civil defense standards and the emergency management powers of the City Government of Davao, upon official declaration of a state of local calamity by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, the City Mayor shall enforce mandatory price ceilings across all commercial retail establishments, which price freeze shall remain in continuous legal effect for a mandatory period of one hundred eighty (180) calendar days from issuance.''}\\[6pt]
