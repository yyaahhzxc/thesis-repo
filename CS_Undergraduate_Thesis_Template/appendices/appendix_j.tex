\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Research Context:} Under Republic Act No.~7160 and the landmark \textit{Magtajas v. Pryce Properties} doctrine, local ordinances cannot contradict national statutes. To assist Second Reading legislative reviews, this study develops an automated screening tool to flag candidate legal conflicts in draft Davao City ordinances before enactment. As Sangguniang Panlungsod legal researchers, your independent professional ratings serve as the authoritative ground truth benchmark to evaluate model accuracy.

\vspace{0.5em}

\begin{enumerate}

\section{Pairwise Consistency Evaluation Instrument}
\label{sec:app_gforms_items}

Each evaluation prompt presents an authentic controlling national statute provision alongside a corresponding Davao City municipal ordinance clause. Evaluators are instructed to read both provisions carefully and submit two independent ratings per pair:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Question 1: Legal Consistency Decision (Required):}\\
    Select the legal relationship between the national statute (Premise) and the municipal provision (Hypothesis):
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
        \item[$\bigcirc$] \textbf{Contradiction} (Direct Legal Conflict / Ultra Vires Overreach / Violates Statutory Preemption)
        \item[$\bigcirc$] \textbf{Entailment} (Substantive Harmony / Direct Municipal Execution of Statutory Mandate)
        \item[$\bigcirc$] \textbf{Neutral} (Independent Regulatory Scope / Non-Intersecting Administrative Domain)
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Question 2: Confidence Rating (Required):}\\
    Indicate your professional certainty in the legal consistency classification selected above:\\
    \vspace{2pt}
    $\bigcirc$ \textbf{1 --- Low Confidence} (Ambiguous statutory authority / Highly debatable interpretation)\\[2pt]
    $\bigcirc$ \textbf{2 --- Moderate Confidence} (Clear statutory text with minor contextual nuance)\\[2pt]
    $\bigcirc$ \textbf{3 --- High Confidence} (Direct textual contradiction or verbatim alignment; clear precedent)
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.97\linewidth}{\textbf{Evaluated Pairwise Corpus Cross-Reference:}\\[2pt]
To preserve visual clarity and allow direct comparative analysis, the complete verbatim inventory of all 350 evaluated statutory-ordinance text pairs administered through this questionnaire across Panels A through E (Items GT-001 to GT-350) is compiled in Appendix~\ref{app:ground_truth_inventory}. In Appendix~\ref{app:ground_truth_inventory}, each evaluation item is presented in a two-column side-by-side format contrasting the controlling national statutory provision (Premise) against the Davao City municipal ordinance provision (Hypothesis). Each item corresponds directly to the sequential evaluation prompts administered via this instrument.}}
