\chapter{Administrative Correspondence and Transmittal Emails}
\label{app:administrative_emails}

This appendix compiles the formal institutional communications, administrative transmittals, and official referral letters exchanged between Ateneo de Davao University, the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, the Human Resource Management Office (HRMO), and the City Administrator's Office.

\section{Transmittal to the Sangguniang Panlungsod Secretariat}
\label{sec:app_email_sp_request}

The following formal communication was transmitted to the Sangguniang Panlungsod Secretariat requesting institutional partnership, access to digitized historical ordinance records under the Legislative Information Support System Program (LISSP), and participation of legal researchers:

\begin{quote}
    \small
    \textbf{Date:} September 15, 2026\\
    \textbf{To:} Ms.~Ma.~Theresa A.~Reyes, Acting Department Head II, Sangguniang Panlungsod Secretariat\\
    \textbf{From:} Ralph Paolo Dulce \& Yahyah Odin (Proponents); Mr.~Adrian Ablazo (Thesis Adviser)\\
    \textbf{Subject:} Formal Request for Academic Partnership and Dataset Access for Computer Science Undergraduate Thesis on Ex-Ante Conflict Detection in Davao City Ordinances\\[6pt]
    \textit{Dear Ms.~Reyes,}\\[4pt]
    Magis greetings! We are senior Computer Science students from Ateneo de Davao University conducting research on automated legal conflict detection for local legislation. We respectfully request permission to access digitized ordinance records from the LISSP archive and to engage the voluntary participation of fifteen (15) legal researchers to evaluate statutory preemption benchmarks\dots
\end{quote}

\section{Sangguniang Panlungsod Official Response and Referral}
\label{sec:app_sp_referral_letter}

On September 28, 2026, the Sangguniang Panlungsod Secretariat issued an official response recognizing the value of the research and referring administrative clearance to the City Administrator's Office via the Human Resource Management Office (HRMO):

\begin{quote}
    \small
    \textbf{Date:} September 28, 2026\\
    \textbf{From:} Ms.~Ma.~Theresa A.~Reyes, Acting Department Head II, City Council of Davao\\
    \textbf{Reference:} SP-ADM-2026-0928\\
    \textbf{Subject:} Endorsement and Referral to City Administrator via HRMO\\[6pt]
    \textit{Memorandum:}\\[4pt]
    Please be advised that while the Sangguniang Panlungsod commends the proponents for developing an ex-ante consistency review system for Davao City ordinances, formal approval for personnel participation of City Council legal researchers falls under the administrative purview of the City Administrator. Accordingly, this request is hereby referred to the Human Resource Management Office (HRMO) for routing and final executive clearance\dots
\end{quote}

\section{HRMO and City Administrator Routing Packet}
\label{sec:app_hrmo_routing_packet}

Following the SP referral, the proponents prepared the formal routing packet addressed to the City Administrator and HRMO Department Head, incorporating data privacy guarantees under Republic Act No.~10173 and ethical research clearances.
