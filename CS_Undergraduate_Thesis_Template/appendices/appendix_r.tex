\chapter{Attestation of Primary Authorship and AI Disclosure Statement}
\label{app:authorship_ai_disclosure}

This appendix presents the signed Ateneo de Davao University Computer Studies Cluster \textit{Attestation of Primary Authorship and AI Disclosure Statement}, executed in compliance with institutional academic integrity policies for the undergraduate thesis oral defense. The disclosure details the operational boundaries of assistive generative AI tools utilized exclusively for coding scaffolding, visualization parameterization, and LaTeX typesetting, affirming sole human intellectual authorship over all research formulations, legal methodologies, empirical experiments, and analytical findings.
