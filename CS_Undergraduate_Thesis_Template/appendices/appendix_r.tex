\chapter{Attestation of Primary Authorship and AI Disclosure Statement}
\label{app:authorship_ai_disclosure}

In compliance with the academic integrity mandates of Ateneo de Davao University, the Computer Studies Cluster, and international publication ethics standards established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE), the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), this appendix documents the formal disclosure of artificial intelligence (AI) assistance utilized throughout this undergraduate research investigation. The following sections articulate the operational scope of assistive AI tools, affirm the sole intellectual authority and oversight exercised by the human authors, delineate the technical boundary between assistive utilities and experimental research models, and present the official signed institutional attestation form.

\section{Operational Scope of AI Assistance}

To successfully execute an undergraduate research investigation of this empirical scale and document volume (spanning 17 publication-grade appendices, a dual statutory corpus of 27,357 enactments, and 178,494 searchable provisions), the proponents strategically leveraged modern generative artificial intelligence as an assistive laboratory partner. AI assistance was utilized across complementary functional roles: Google Antigravity (powered by Google DeepMind and Google Gemini models) as an interactive agentic pair-programmer and LaTeX typesetting copilot, Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic) as an adversarial simulated peer reviewer and defense-proofing critic, and Google Gemini Flash 3.8 as a syntactic drafting aid. The operational assistance rendered by these tools was structured across the following distinct functional dimensions:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.0em, itemsep=5pt]
    \item \textbf{Agentic Codebase Scaffolding and Pipeline Engineering (Google Antigravity / Gemini Models):} 
    Assisting as an interactive pair-programmer in writing modular Python boilerplate code across the \texttt{src/} and \texttt{scripts/} directories. This included scaffolding directory architectures, implementing automated command-line execution harnesses, configuring asynchronous batch evaluation wrappers for cloud VLM extraction, structuring data pipelines for dual-corpus JSONL compilation, and building the interactive offline web prototype demonstration interface (\texttt{demo/}).
    
    \item \textbf{LaTeX Typesetting and Document Build Engineering (Google Antigravity / Gemini Models):} 
    Diagnosing and resolving complex multi-pass LaTeX compilation diagnostics (\texttt{pdflatex} and \texttt{bibtex}), configuring dedicated full-page landscape plates (\texttt{pdflscape}) for high-density empirical tables, standardizing multi-column table alignments (\texttt{booktabs}, \texttt{tabularx}, and \texttt{longtable}), managing front-matter listings (Table of Contents, List of Figures, List of Tables, List of Listings, and List of Equations), and synchronizing the automated numbers registry (\texttt{thesis\_numbers.sty}) to maintain mathematical consistency across all manuscript chapters.
    
    \item \textbf{Scientific Data Visualization and Layout Optimization (Google Antigravity / Matplotlib / Seaborn):} 
    Assisting in configuring Matplotlib and Seaborn visualization parameters, publication color palettes, 300-DPI high-resolution rendering, and the vertical top-and-bottom structural stacking of two-panel empirical figures (Figures 3.2--3.8 and Figures 4.1--4.4) to guarantee visual legibility for physical hardcopy printing and bookbinding.
    
    \item \textbf{Adversarial Peer Review and Defense-Proofing Auditing (Claude 3.5 Sonnet -- Anthropic):} 
    Simulating rigorous external academic peer review and mock defense panel interrogation. The authors submitted compiled manuscript chapters and empirical drafts to Claude 3.5 Sonnet to stress-test scientific claims, conduct forensic checks on mathematical and statistical consistency (such as McNemar discordance calculations on $N=53$, Friedman ANOVA test rankings, Wilson score confidence intervals, and Bayesian low-prevalence screening formulas), identify unintended narrative overclaiming, and uncover methodological ambiguities before committee defense.
    
    \item \textbf{Editorial Refinement and Structural Prose Polish (Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Gemini Models):} 
    Serving as an assistive editorial tool to refine academic sentence transitions, eliminate formulaic cadence, purge conversational padding, and ensure stylistic cohesion across continuous academic paragraphs in strict accordance with departmental formatting directives.
    
    \item \textbf{Syntactic Hypothesis Phrasing and Enacting Clause Templating (Gemini Flash 3.8):} 
    Assisting in the syntactic drafting and formal enacting clause templating of candidate local ordinance clauses (as detailed in Section~3.5.3) based strictly on human-specified statutory premises and conflict mechanisms, ensuring clauses adhered to standard Davao City legislative syntax and DILG drafting manuals without introducing unverified facts.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Human Intellectual Authority, Decision-Making, and Oversight}

The proponents explicitly affirm that generative artificial intelligence was not granted autonomous control over any phase of this research, nor was it utilized to fabricate statutory texts, empirical metrics, benchmark annotations, or jurisprudential interpretations. The authors, Ralph Paolo Dulce and Yahyah Odin, served as the principal investigators, lead system architects, and ultimate decision-makers throughout the entire research lifecycle:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.0em, itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Critical Adjudication and Selective Implementation:} Every code implementation, algorithmic design choice, and prose modification suggested by AI models was critically evaluated, debugged, and verified by the authors. Suggestions that contradicted Philippine constitutional jurisprudence, local legislative practice, or departmental guidelines were rejected. All final code was executed and validated directly by the authors on the designated physical testbeds (Host~A: Intel Core i3-10105F and Host~B: AMD Ryzen 7 7435HS with RTX 5050 GPU).
    
    \item \textbf{Problem Formulation and Architectural Conception:} The primary research dilemma, five specific research objectives, cascaded coarse-to-fine system design, cost-sensitive asymmetric loss weighting ($C(\text{FN})/C(\text{FP}) = 5.0$), and calibrated decision threshold formulation ($\tau^* = 0.45$) were conceived, formulated, and directed exclusively by the human authors under the mentorship of thesis adviser Mr.~Adrian ``Ogs'' Ablazo and course instructor Ms.~Grace Tacadao.
    
    \item \textbf{Authentic Legal Corpus Acquisition and Curation:} The collection, physical archival scanning, OCR noise profiling, and structural segmentation of the 1,925 Davao City local ordinances (1950--2025) and 25,432 national statutory enactments (totaling 178,494 searchable provisions) were gathered directly from authentic public records (the Sangguniang Panlungsod LISSP archive and The Lawphil Project) without synthetic generation.
    
    \item \textbf{Ground Truth Benchmark and Precedent Formulation:} The 350-pair ground truth evaluation benchmark (comprising 198 unique instances across 22 authentic national statutes), the 11 landmark Supreme Court and RTC preemption cases, and the 6 full-length draft ordinances were conceived, verified, and cross-examined against authentic Philippine jurisprudence exclusively by the authors and formally verified by the thesis adviser.
    
    \item \textbf{Empirical Execution and Statistical Integrity:} All model training routines, zero-leakage premise-grouped cross-validation runs (\texttt{GroupKFold}), ablation experiments, latency profiles over 30 Monte Carlo iterations, and non-parametric hypothesis tests reflect genuine computational executions conducted by the authors.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Distinction Between Assistive AI and Experimental Research Subjects}

A fundamental technical distinction is maintained between assistive generative AI tools and the machine learning models under empirical investigation. Deep language representations (such as \texttt{DeBERTa-v3}, \texttt{ModernBERT}, \texttt{Legal-BERT}, and \texttt{all-MiniLM-L6-v2}) and multimodal vision-language models (\texttt{Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct}) evaluated in this study represent formal computational subjects and experimental artifacts of the thesis methodology. These models were executed strictly within sandboxed, offline environments with deterministic parameter seeds to investigate Character Error Rates, candidate retrieval recall, and deontic conflict classification, rather than acting as unverified generative text generators.
