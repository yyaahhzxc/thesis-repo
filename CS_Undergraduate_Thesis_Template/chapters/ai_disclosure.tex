In compliance with the academic integrity mandates of Ateneo de Davao University, the Computer Studies Cluster, and international standards established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE), the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), the proponents provide this formal disclosure detailing the extent, nature, and boundaries of artificial intelligence (AI) assistance utilized throughout the conduct of this undergraduate thesis research and the preparation of the accompanying manuscript.

\section*{Operational Scope of AI Assistance}

During the research and development lifecycle, the authors utilized Google Antigravity (incorporating Google DeepMind and Google Gemini models) as an interactive agentic pair-programming assistant, codebase orchestrator, and technical editorial aid. The operational assistance rendered by these tools was strictly confined to the following supplementary tasks:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.0em, itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Code Authoring and Pipeline Scaffolding:} Assisting in the drafting of routine Python boilerplate code, modular directory restructuring, asynchronous batch execution wrappers, and automated command-line execution workflows across the \texttt{src/} and \texttt{scripts/} directories.
    \item \textbf{Data Visualization and Graphical Layout:} Assisting in configuring Matplotlib script parameters, publication styling configurations, high-resolution rendering parameters, and the vertical top-and-bottom structural arrangement of two-panel empirical figures to ensure maximum legibility for hardcopy printing.
    \item \textbf{Manuscript Typography and LaTeX Typesetting:} Diagnosing and rectifying LaTeX compilation diagnostics, configuring dedicated full-page landscape plates (\texttt{pdflscape}) for dense empirical tables, standardizing multi-column table alignments (\texttt{booktabs}, \texttt{longtable}), formatting front matter listings, and resolving citation bibliography keys.
    \item \textbf{Grammatical Consistency and Editorial Polish:} Serving as an assistive proofreading and copyediting tool to refine academic sentence transitions, eliminate colloquial phrasing, and ensure stylistic cohesion across continuous paragraphs in accordance with departmental formatting directives.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Methodological Integrity and Intellectual Contributions}

The proponents explicitly affirm that generative artificial intelligence was not utilized to fabricate, simulate, or hallucinate any statutory texts, empirical metrics, benchmark annotations, or jurisprudential interpretations. All core intellectual contributions, scientific formulations, and empirical evaluations remain the exclusive work of the human authors, executed under the continuous guidance of the thesis adviser, Mr.~Adrian ``Ogs'' Ablazo, and course instructor, Ms.~Grace Tacadao:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.0em, itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Problem Formulation and Conceptual Design:} The primary research problem, five specific research objectives, coarse-to-fine cascaded architecture, and the hybrid retrieval and calibrated natural language inference methodology were conceived, formulated, and validated solely by the human proponents.
    \item \textbf{Corpus Curation and Archival Verification:} The collection, physical scanning, OCR noise filtering, layout perimeter cropping, and structural categorization of the 1,890 Davao City local ordinances (1950--2025) and 25,432 national statutory enactments were gathered directly from authentic public records (the Sangguniang Panlungsod LISSP repository and The Lawphil Project) without synthetic augmentation.
    \item \textbf{Ground Truth and Benchmark Establishment:} The 350-pair faculty adviser-verified ground truth benchmark across eight Macro Legal Domains, the 11 landmark Supreme Court statutory preemption cases, and the 10 full-length synthetic draft ordinances were manually compiled, verified, and cross-examined against authentic Philippine jurisprudence.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Execution and Statistical Analysis:} All model training procedures, 5-fold stratified cross-validation runs, ablation benchmarks, and non-parametric statistical evaluations (Friedman test, McNemar's test with Edwards' correction, and Wilcoxon signed-rank test) reflect genuine neural model executions conducted on local LGU simulation hardware (Host A: Intel Core i3-10105F, 8~GB RAM) and dedicated GPU environments.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Distinction Between Assistive AI and Experimental Artifacts}

A fundamental technical distinction is maintained between general assistive generative AI tools and the machine learning models under experimental investigation. Deep language representations (such as \texttt{DeBERTa-v3}, \texttt{ModernBERT}, \texttt{Legal-BERT}, and \texttt{all-MiniLM-L6-v2}) and multimodal vision-language models (\texttt{Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct}) evaluated in this study represent formal computational subjects and empirical artifacts of the thesis methodology. These models were executed strictly within sandboxed, offline environments with deterministic parameter seeds to investigate character error rates, candidate retrieval recall, and deontic conflict classification, rather than acting as unverified generative text generators.
