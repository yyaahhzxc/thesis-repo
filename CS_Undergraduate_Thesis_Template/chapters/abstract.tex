\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Under statutory preemption established in \textit{Magtajas v. Pryce Properties} and Section~5(a) of Republic Act No.~7160, local government ordinances in the Philippines are strictly subordinate to national statutes; local provisions contradicting national law are \textit{ultra vires} and void. In the Davao City Sangguniang Panlungsod, manual ex-ante consistency reviews impose heavy cognitive burdens upon legislative researchers due to extensive statutory volumes and optical character recognition noise in historical archives. To automate screening within standard municipal computing environments, this study develops an offline, coarse-to-fine semantic conflict detection architecture decoupling candidate retrieval from cross-attention inference. In Stage~1, hybrid retrieval fusing Okapi BM25 and dense bi-encoder embeddings via Reciprocal Rank Fusion isolates candidate provisions ($k \le 50$) across 27,357 enactments (178,494 provisions), achieving 100.0\% Recall@50 on a controlled candidate benchmark and 65.14\% under open-corpus retrieval. In Stage~2, candidate pairs are classified through a fine-tuned cross-encoder with calibrated decision thresholds ($\tau^* = 0.45$). On a 350-pair faculty adviser-verified benchmark across eight Macro Legal Domains, fine-tuned \texttt{DeBERTa-v3} achieved 88.30\% accuracy and a 0.9008 Conflict $F_1$-score, significantly outperforming supervised baselines (McNemar's $p = 0.0388$ uncorrected) while eliminating affirmative entailment bias. Algorithmic optimizations lowered full-document audit latency by 55.4\% (from 28.15~seconds down to 12.69~seconds, a $2.22\times$ speedup) on a quad-core CPU with a 1,416.4~MB memory footprint. An open-corpus full-document pilot stress test demonstrated 100\% document triage recall, while revealing that open-corpus candidate retrieval remains the primary operational bottleneck. An offline web prototype provides explainable preemption alerts, establishing a practical decision-support baseline for Philippine local legislative review.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Semantic Conflict Detection, Ex-Ante Legislative Review, Statutory Preemption, Natural Language Inference, Information Retrieval, Cross-Encoder, Local Ordinances, Sangguniang Panlungsod, Davao City.
