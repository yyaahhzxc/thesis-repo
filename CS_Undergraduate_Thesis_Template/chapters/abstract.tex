\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Under constitutional statutory preemption established in \textit{Magtajas v. Pryce Properties} and Republic Act No.~7160, local ordinances cannot contradict superior national statutes. In the Davao City Sangguniang Panlungsod, manual ex-ante consistency reviews impose heavy cognitive burdens on legislative researchers due to statutory volume and archival scanning noise. To automate screening within municipal computing constraints, this study presents an offline coarse-to-fine semantic conflict detection architecture decoupling candidate retrieval from cross-attention inference. In Stage~1, hybrid retrieval fusing Okapi BM25 and dense bi-encoder embeddings via Reciprocal Rank Fusion isolates candidates across 27,357 enactments, achieving 100.0\% Recall@50 on a controlled candidate benchmark and 65.14\% across the unassisted open corpus. In Stage~2, candidate pairs are evaluated through a fine-tuned \texttt{DeBERTa-v3} cross-encoder with calibrated decision thresholds. Under zero-leakage premise-grouped cross-validation across 22 authentic national statutes on a 350-pair adviser-verified benchmark comprising 198 unique instances, the model achieves 86.44\% accuracy and a 0.8945 Conflict $F_1$-score, consistently outperforming unadapted zero-shot and supervised transformer baselines. Algorithmic optimizations reduce full-document audit latency by 55.4\% to 12.69~seconds on an entry-level quad-core CPU with a 1,416.4~MB memory footprint. In an open-corpus pilot stress test across six full-length draft ordinances, the pipeline flagged all six defective drafts, surfacing two of seven planted conflicts alongside six false alarms while clearing 39 of 45 compliant sections at 86.67\% specificity, versus seven of seven under oracle candidate retrieval. An offline web prototype provides explainable preemption alerts, establishing a practical decision-support baseline and institutional roadmap for Philippine local legislative review.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Semantic Conflict Detection, Ex-Ante Legislative Review, Statutory Preemption, Natural Language Inference, Information Retrieval, Cross-Encoder, Local Ordinances, Sangguniang Panlungsod, Davao City.
