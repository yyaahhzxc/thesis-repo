\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Under constitutional statutory preemption established in \textit{Magtajas v. Pryce Properties} and Republic Act No.~7160, local ordinances cannot contradict superior national statutes. In the Davao City Sangguniang Panlungsod, manual ex-ante consistency reviews impose heavy cognitive burdens on legislative researchers due to statutory volume and archival scanning noise. To automate screening within municipal computing constraints, this study presents an offline coarse-to-fine semantic conflict detection architecture decoupling candidate retrieval from cross-attention inference. In Stage~1, hybrid retrieval fusing Okapi BM25 and dense bi-encoder embeddings via Reciprocal Rank Fusion isolates candidate provisions across 27,357 enactments; this stage attains 100.0\% Recall@50 on a controlled benchmark and 65.14\% across the unassisted open corpus. In Stage~2, candidate pairs are evaluated through a fine-tuned \texttt{DeBERTa-v3} cross-encoder with calibrated decision thresholds. Under zero-leakage premise-grouped cross-validation across 22 premise provisions from 17 statutes on a 350-pair faculty adviser-reviewed benchmark comprising 198 unique instances, the model achieves 86.44\% accuracy and a 0.8945 Conflict $F_1$-score. In an open-corpus triage pilot across six draft ordinances, the pipeline surfaced two of seven planted conflicts with six false alarms while clearing 39 of 45 compliant sections at 86.67\% specificity. In contrast, oracle candidate retrieval detected all seven planted conflicts, which confirms that Stage~1 candidate retrieval forms the operational constraint. Algorithmic optimizations reduce full-document audit latency by 54.9\% to 12.69~seconds on an entry-level CPU with a 1,416.4~MB memory footprint. An offline web prototype delivers explainable preemption alerts and establishes an ex-ante decision-support baseline for Philippine local legislative review.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Semantic Conflict Detection, Ex-Ante Legislative Review, Statutory Preemption, Natural Language Inference, Information Retrieval, Cross-Encoder, Local Ordinances, Sangguniang Panlungsod, Davao City.
