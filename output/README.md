# **Output Directory: Experimental Artifacts & Generated Results**

This directory stores all automated outputs, empirical evaluation matrices, benchmark summaries, and precomputed neural indices produced by the thesis codebase.

---

## **Directory Structure & Artifact Typology**

### **1. Empirical Evaluation Matrices & Screening Results (`.csv`, `.json`)**
Generated by evaluation and screening scripts in `scripts/`:
* `dual_retrieval_benchmark_results.json`: Stage 1 hybrid retrieval recall and MRR across 176,421 provisions.
* `stage1_model_screening_results.json`: Comparative screening metrics for Stage 1 candidate bi-encoders.
* `stage2_screening_results.json`: Cross-Encoder validation metrics and hyperparameter screening.
* `champion_models_sc_peak_matrix.csv` & `champion_models_sc_peak_summary.csv`: Finalist model performance on Supreme Court preemption cases.
* `multi_model_sc_aggregate_summary.csv` & `multi_model_sc_prediction_matrix.csv`: Prediction breakdown across legal conflict difficulty tiers.
* `sweep_350_benchmark_results.csv`: 350-pair ground truth evaluation sweeps across decision thresholds ($\tau^*$).
* `system_level_sc_benchmark_results.json` & `system_level_sc_summary_table.csv`: Joint cascaded hit rates and full-audit latency.
* `tier2_system_level_test_results.json`: Injected-fault full-document test results on 10 synthetic draft ordinances.

### **2. Corpus Preprocessing & Quality Census**
* `corpus_token_census.json`: Subword token length distributions across 176,421 provisions.
* `topic_summary.csv`: 6-class functional topic distribution across 1,925 local enactments.
* `cleaning_summary_report.csv`: OCR and VLM transcription anomaly audit log.
* `ocr_experiment_report.json`: Comparative CER, WER, and NED metrics across OCR engines.

### **3. Precomputed Neural Embeddings & Retrieval Indices**
* `demo_local_statutory_embeddings.npy` & `demo_local_statutory_meta.json`: Offline vector embeddings for the interactive prototype.
* `dual_corpus_embeddings_meta.json`: Provision metadata mapping for the 176,421 dual-statutory provisions.
* *(Locally generated / Git-ignored)*: `dual_corpus_embeddings_minilm.npy` (full 176k embeddings) and `dual_corpus_bm25.pkl` (inverted BM25 index).

### **4. Subdirectories**
* `visualizations/`: High-resolution (300 DPI) publication charts, attention saliency heatmaps, and empirical loss curve plots.
* `kaggle_multi_model_results/` & `kaggle_sc_results/`: Raw metrics and prediction tensors pulled from Kaggle headless cloud training.
* `ocr_experiment_transcripts/`: Raw OCR engine transcript comparisons for historical document analysis.
