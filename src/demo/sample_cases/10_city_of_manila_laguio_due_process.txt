REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES
CITY OF MANILA
CITY COUNCIL OF MANILA

Manila Ordinance No. 7783
AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE ESTABLISHMENT OR OPERATION OF MOTELS, INNS, AND KARAOKE BARS IN ERMITA-MALATE

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF MANILA, IN SESSION ASSEMBLED, THAT:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the 'Ermita-Malate Hospitality Zoning and Moral Rehabilitation Ordinance'.

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF URBAN POLICY - It is declared the policy of the City of Manila to revitalize the tourist district of Ermita-Malate and eliminate venues associated with illicit commercial activities.

SECTION 3. CLOSURE OF HOSPITALITY ESTABLISHMENTS - Prohibits the establishment or operation of motels, inns, and karaoke bars in the Ermita-Malate district, ordering their permanent closure or relocation within three (3) months without just compensation.

SECTION 4. REVOCATION OF BUSINESS LICENSES - The City Mayor and Bureau of Permits are directed to cancel all business permits previously issued to motels, inns, massage parlors, and sauna baths in the covered area.

SECTION 5. PENAL SANCTIONS - Failure to comply shall result in a fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) and summary eviction and physical sealing of premises.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance are separable; invalidity of any provision shall not affect the remaining parts.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon enactment.
