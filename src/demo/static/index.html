Davao City Ordinance
Conflict Detector

An automated system designed to detect any conflicts from proposed Davao City ordinances to a national statute or existing Davao City ordinance.

What is this system?

This system is a conflict checker for proposed Davao City ordinances. It checks against national statutes, including Republic Acts, Batas Pambansa, Presidential Decrees, Executive Orders, and Commonwealth Acts, spanning from 1901 to March 2026.

It also checks against Davao City's local ordinances, with landmark ordinances from 1950s to 2020s, as well as ordinances stored from 2021 to 2025.

The system functions in two steps: first, after receiving the proposed ordinance, it finds the most relevant national laws and local ordinances; after which it will compare each section of the proposed ordinance against each section of those top relevant laws and ordinances. If there is a contradiction, it will show the specific provisions that contradicted, along with its confidence level. Give it a try!
