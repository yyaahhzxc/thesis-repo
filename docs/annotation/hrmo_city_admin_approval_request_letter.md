The Department of Computer Science respectfully submits this formal request for institutional approval and clearance regarding an applied undergraduate research study conducted by our senior BS Computer Science students, **Ralph Paolo P. Dulce** and **Yahyah Odin**, entitled:

> **"A Coarse-to-Fine Semantic Conflict Detection System for Ex-Ante Davao City Ordinances Using Information Retrieval and Natural Language Inference"**

#### 1. Background & Referral from the Sangguniang Panlungsod
On September 2026, the researchers initially reached out to the Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (SP) to invite legislative researchers and legal staff to participate in evaluating a specialized legal-decision-support AI system. 

In a formal response dated **September 28, 2026**, signed by **Ms. Ma. Theresa A. Reyes**, Acting City Government Department Head II of the Sangguniang Panlungsod *(attached as Enclosure 1)*, the SP expressed appreciation for the objectives of the study and our initiative to involve legislative personnel. In accordance with City Government policy, Ms. Reyes formally advised our team to coordinate directly with the **City Administrator’s Office through the Human Resource Management Office (HRMO)** for proper clearance, evaluation, and administrative approval. We are therefore writing to officially seek this authorization.

#### 2. Practical Value to the City Government of Davao
Under the *Magtajas v. Pryce Properties* doctrine and Republic Act No. 7160 (*Local Government Code of 1991*), local ordinances must strictly align with national legislation and avoid conflicting with active municipal statutes. Manually cross-referencing newly drafted ordinances against more than 25,000 national laws and over 1,500 existing Davao City enactments is an intensive, time-consuming responsibility.

Our students are engineering an offline-capable, diagnostic AI system tailored to run on standard Local Government Unit (LGU) desktop computers. The system assists legislative drafters by automatically flagging potential conflicts (e.g., statutory preemption, penal sanction ceiling breaches, regulatory overreach, and internal policy contradictions) *ex-ante*—before an ordinance is submitted for First Reading or formal enactment.

#### 3. Nature and Scope of Personnel Participation
To scientifically validate the system's accuracy against authentic legal reasoning, the researchers require expert benchmark evaluations from qualified legislative researchers and legal personnel of the Sangguniang Panlungsod:
* **Target Participants:** Approximately fifteen (15) Legal Researchers or Legislative Staff Members assigned to ordinance drafting and legal review.
* **Low Workload & Asynchronous Format:** Evaluators will review a curated, batched evaluation set of **70 statutory pairs** via a streamlined online Google Form or private spreadsheet. Completing this task requires only **45 to 60 minutes**. Participants may complete this asynchronously at their convenience during off-peak hours without interrupting daily legislative duties.
* **Annotator Guidance:** Participants are provided with a concise, self-contained 1-page Codebook and Annotator Guidebook explaining the decision labels (*Contradiction*, *Entailment*, *Neutral*).

#### 4. Data Privacy, Confidentiality, and Ethical Safeguards
The research strictly complies with Republic Act No. 10173 (*Data Privacy Act of 2012*) and institutional research ethics standards:
* **Strict Anonymity:** Participants will be identified only through randomized study IDs (`SP-ANN-01` to `SP-ANN-15`). No personal or identifying information will be published.
* **Public Legal Records Only:** The study examines publicly accessible enacted statutes and ordinances. It does not solicit, access, or handle classified, confidential, or proprietary City Government documents.
* **Aggregate Reporting:** All responses will be analyzed strictly in aggregate form (Inter-Rater Reliability / Fleiss' Kappa) for academic calibration and model validation.

#### 5. Institutional Reciprocity and Contribution to Davao City
Upon the completion and defense of the thesis, the proponents will submit a full copy of the research findings, validated statutory benchmarks, and prototype software system to the City Government of Davao and the Sangguniang Panlungsod as a free, open-source technology contribution toward modernizing digital legislative drafting and local governance quality assurance.
