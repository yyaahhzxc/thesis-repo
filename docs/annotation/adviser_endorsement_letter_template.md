### SUBJECT: Request for Expert Legal Collaboration and Human Benchmark Annotation for Undergraduate Computer Science Thesis

Dear Atty. Guinto and Honorable Members of the Legal Research Staff:

Warm greetings from the Ateneo de Davao University!

The Department of Computer Science is currently supporting an applied undergraduate research study conducted by our senior BS Computer Science students, **Ralph Paolo Dulce** and **Yahyah Odin**, entitled:

> **"A Coarse-to-Fine Semantic Conflict Detection System for Ex-Ante Davao City Ordinances Using Information Retrieval and Natural Language Inference"**

#### Background and Purpose of the Study
Under the *Magtajas v. Pryce Doctrine* (G.R. No. 111097) and the *Local Government Code of 1991* (Republic Act No. 7160), local municipal ordinances must remain strictly consistent with national statutes. As legislative enactments grow in volume, manually cross-referencing drafted city ordinances against more than 25,000 national Republic Acts, Batas Pambansa, and Presidential Decrees presents significant cognitive demands. 

Our researchers are developing an AI-driven, diagnostic decision-support system designed to run on consumer-grade local government hardware. The system flags potential statutory contradictions—such as penal sanction ceiling breaches, regulatory preemption, and missing statutory exemptions—*before* an ordinance is formally enacted.

#### The Request for Expert Legal Collaboration
To scientifically evaluate and calibrate the underlying Natural Language Inference (NLI) logic models, the study requires a validated "Ground Truth" benchmark dataset reflecting authentic Philippine legal reasoning. Because interpreting local legislative preemption is the specialized operational competency of the Sangguniang Panlungsod legal staff, we formally request the participation of **fifteen (15) Legal Researchers or Legislative Staff Members** to serve as expert human evaluators.

#### Structure of the Evaluation Task
To respect the demanding schedule of your office and prevent cognitive fatigue, the study utilizes an optimized **batched distribution framework** (adapted from Ablazo, 2019):
1. **Manageable Workload**: The benchmark of 350 statutory pairs is partitioned into five independent blocks of **70 pairs per researcher**. Completing one block takes approximately **45 to 60 minutes**.
2. **Strict Anonymity and Independence**: Each participant will be assigned an anonymized research identifier (`SP-ANN-01` to `SP-ANN-15`) and will receive a private Google Sheet. Responses are strictly confidential and will be analyzed only in aggregate statistical form (Inter-Rater Reliability / Fleiss' Kappa).
3. **Clear Evaluation Decision Codebook**: Evaluators are provided with a 1-page quick-reference decision guide detailing the operational criteria for identifying *Contradiction*, *Entailment*, or *Neutral* states.

#### Institutional Commitment
The outputs of this collaborative annotation campaign will serve solely for academic benchmark calibration. Upon completion of the thesis defense, the validated findings, benchmark datasets, and prototype software will be presented and made available to the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City as an institutional contribution toward modernizing digital legislative drafting and ex-ante quality assurance.

We humbly request your favorable consideration and approval of this academic collaboration. Our researchers are available at your earliest convenience to present the evaluation materials and address any technical questions.
