ORDINANCE NO. 0230-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING THE DAVAO CITY SPECIAL NEEDS INTERVENTION CENTER FOR CHILDREN DIRECTING ITS OBJECTIVES AND PROVIDING FUNDS FOR THE PURPOSE

Ord. No. 0230-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE DAVAO CITY SPECIAL NEEDS INTERVENTION CENTER FOR CHILDREN."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City Government of Davao, pursuant to the mandate of the 1987 Philippine Constitution to promote the right of all citizens to quality education and services and to take appropriate steps to make education accessible to all and offer quality interventions for children with developmental delays and behavioral concerns. Thus, the City Government hereby commits to establish the Davao City Special Needs Intervention Center for Children to provide free intervention and special education to children with special needs.

SECTION 3. NAME OF INSTITUTION - The "DAVAO CITY SPECIAL NEEDS INTERVENTION CENTER FOR CHILDREN," to be hereinafter referred to as the "CENTER," is hereby named as such or in accordance with law and within the power and limitations heretofore specified.

SECTION 4. PURPOSES - The CENTER, which operates all year round, shall be established to provide educational and therapy services to children and youth with special needs. The CENTER addresses the concerns and skills deficit brought about by Intellectual Disability, Generalized Delay, Autism Spectrum Disorder, Communication Disorders, Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder, Learning Disabilities, Motor Disorders, Visual and Hearing Impairment, other Neuro-developmental Disorders and related behavioral, sensory and cognitive delays.

The state-of-the-art CENTER also offers the following programs and services to prepare the children and youth with special needs for success and high quality of life in a mainstream society:

A. Allied Medical Services:
a. Occupational Therapy (OT)
b. Physical Therapy (PT)
c. Speech Therapy
d. Aqua Therapy

B. Special Education Services
a. Early Intervention
b. Transition Program

The Center shall also operate as a Resource Center for Inclusive Education which shall support children with special needs integrated in regular schools, assist in the conduct of school-based in-service training, produce appropriate teaching materials and conduct appropriate assessment of children with special needs.

The CENTER shall also operate as a venue for support groups for parents who have children with special needs.

SECTION 5: DEFINITION OF TERMS.

A. Children with Special Needs - are gifted and fast learners and those who are disabled, impaired and handicapped children in need of special education as well as services and rehabilitation. He/She differs from the average child in:

a. Mental characteristics;

b. Sensory abilities;

c. Neuro-muscular physical characteristics;

d. Nutritional handicaps;

e. Neuro developmental disorder and/or

f. Has a developmental lag to such an extent that he/she requires modified school practices or special education services to develop to his/her maximum capability:

I. The Intellectual Handicapped - are those who have the substantial limitation in present functioning characterized by significant sub-average intellectual functioning, existing concurrently with related limitations in two or more of the applicable adaptive skill areas: communication, self-direction, home living social skills, community use, health and safety, functional academics, leisure and work.

II. The Hearing Impaired - are those who are deaf or hard-of-hearing. A deaf person is one whose hearing is non-functional for ordinary purposes in life. The hard-of-hearing has a sense of hearing, which although defective, is functional with or without a hearing aid.

III. Student/Children with Behavior Problems - are those who cannot adjust to the socially accepted norms or behavior and consequently disrupt their academic progress, the learning effort of their classmates and interpersonal relations.

IV. The Orthopedically Handicapped - are those whose impairment, permanently or temporarily, interferes with the normal functions of the joints, muscles or limbs.

B. Special instructional materials - may include, but is not limited to, a textbook in Braille, large type or any other medium or apparatus that conveys information to a student or otherwise contributes to the learning process.

C. Special Education - is basic education that takes into account the special needs of both disabled children and gifted children that requires a systematic and deliberate process for them to achieve functions and capacities that are consistent with and up to the highest level of his potential and capacity. It is also defined as any type of education tailored to meet the needs of children who cannot profit because of disabilities or exceptional abilities.

**SECTION 6. ADMISSION:** The CENTER shall have a system of admission which shall facilitate the enrollment of children with special needs regardless of gender, religious beliefs, status, or political affiliations. Provided, however, that admission shall be given to the bonafide residents of Davao City who belong to indigent families or low income families, subject to rules and regulations on admission and retention prescribed by the Council adopted by the City Social Welfare and Development Office.

**SECTION 7. SPECIAL NEEDS INTERVENTION CENTER FOR CHILDREN COUNCIL:** The overall governance of the CENTER shall be vested in a Special Needs Intervention Center for Children Council to be hereinafter referred to as the "COUNCIL," to be composed of persons of known probity and integrity, and with great interest in the continuing development of the City of Davao, and shall consist of seventeen (17) members, as follows:

a. The Davao City Mayor as Chairperson;

b. The City Social Welfare & Development Officer;

c. The City Finance Officer;

d. The City Treasurer;

e. The City Budget Officer;

f. The Chairperson of the Committee on Social Services;

g. The Chairperson of the Committee on Health;

h. The Chairperson of the Committee on Education, Science & Technology, Arts & Culture;

i. The City School's Division Superintendent of Davao City;

j. The Chief of Hospital of Southern Philippines Medical Center;

k. The Administrator of the Davao City Special Needs Intervention Center;

l. The representative of the Professional Organization of Occupational Therapists of Davao City;

m. The Representative of the Professional Organization of Physical Therapists of Davao City;

n. The representative of the Professional Organization of Speech Pathologists of Davao City;

o. The representative of the Association of Special Education Teachers of Davao City;

p. The representative of the Philippine Society for Developmental & Behavioral Pediatrics;

q. One (1) distinguished citizen of the community representing the parents of children with special needs to be appointed by the City Mayor;

The member of the Council appointed by the City Mayor shall serve for three (3) years, and thereafter, the Mayor will appoint his/her successor.

**SECTION 8. QUALIFICATIONS:** All members of the Council shall be Filipino citizens and residents of Davao City.

**SECTION 9. COMPENSATION:** Council Members shall serve without compensation, other than honorarium, for attendance in Council meetings and for official business, only duly authorized by the Board, subject to availability of funds.

**SECTION 10. PROHIBITION:** Members of the Council shall not have any business interest, directly or indirectly, in any aspect of the center operations.

SECTION 11. POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE COUNCIL
The Council shall have the following powers and duties, in consonance with existing laws:

a. To receive and appropriate such sums of money, properties, or materials as may be provided by an Ordinance of the City of Davao for the support of the CENTER;

b. To establish the necessary curricula, programs and projects responsive to the social and economic needs of children with special needs;

c. To recommend and to sit as a member of the Human Resource Merit Promotion and Selection Board (HRMPSB) in the selection for positions to be filled up by the Council on recommendation of the Center Administrator; namely: the Official of the Center, the teachers who have training in special education, intervention, therapy, support personnel, technical advisers and other Center personnel, and to remove them for just cause after an investigation and hearing shall have been conducted;

d. To prescribe rules for its own governance and to enact, for the governance of the CENTER, such general policies, rules and regulations which are consistent with its purpose as defined in Section 4 of this Ordinance;

e. To provide a compensation scheme, including direct and indirect fringe benefits for all CENTER personnel, and to fix their hours of work, duties and such other conditions and processes as it may deem proper under existing laws;

f. To receive in trust, tax free, all legacies, gift donations and grants of personal and real properties and other forms of donation, and to administer the same for the benefit of the CENTER in accordance with the instructions of the donor, or in default thereof, in such other manner as it may determine;

g. To act expeditiously on all academic matters referred to it by the Academic Division;

h. To provide a system of scholarships and fellowships, and to award the same to students, faculty, and the staff on the basis of merit and fitness;

i. To establish a system for the initial assessment, check-up and succeeding evaluations and follow ups of special needs children in partnership with the Child's Institute of the Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC), and any member of the Philippine Society for Developmental and Behavioral Pediatrics;

j. To provide a grievance machinery for any concern involving students, faculty, staff and employees in the CENTER;

k. To perform other things not inconsistent with law and as it may deem appropriate for the proper guidance and operation of the CENTER.

**SECTION 12. ACTIONS AGAINST THE COUNCIL**

Actions and processes against the Council shall be served to the Center Administrator or the Center Secretary thereof.

**SECTION 13. CREATION OF POSITIONS**

The Davao City Special Needs Intervention Center shall be composed of the following personnel:

One (1) DCSNICC Center Manager;

One (1) Administrative Officer II;

One (1) Physician/Pediatrician;

One (1) Nurse;

One (1) Anesthetist;

One (1) Optometrist;

One (1) Occupational Therapist;

One (1) Physical Therapist;

One (1) Speech and Language Therapist;

One (1) Psychologist;

One (1) Speech Teacher - Visual Impairment;

One (1) SPED Teacher - Hearing Impairment;

One (1) SPED Teacher - Intellectually Handicapped;

One (1) SPED Teacher;

Two (2) Auxiliary Teachers;

One (1) Administrative Aide VI;

One (1) Administrative Service Assistant;

One (1) Janitor;

**SECTION 14. RESPONSIBILITY FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION ADMINISTRATION**

Responsibility for special education administration shall devolve upon the Center Manager and Center officials.

There shall be an Administrative Section, an Allied Medical Services Section and a Special Education Section with the Center Manager as the Presiding Officer.

**SECTION 15. THE CENTER SECTIONS**

The Special Education Section shall consist of the Center Manager as the Presiding Officer and the teachers with the rank of instructor and above.

The Special Education Section shall be responsible for determining curricular offerings, courses of study, academic policies and rules and disciplines, requirements for admission and retention, subject to the approval of the Council upon the recommendation of the Center Manager.

The Administrative Section shall be headed by the Center Manager as the Presiding Officer, and the heads of all administrative matters, which shall be in support of the instruction, research and the extension services of the CENTER.

Allied Medical Services Section shall be responsible for the planning, implementation, execution and monitoring of therapeutic services for children with special needs.

**SECTION 16. THE SECRETARY OF THE CENTER**

There shall be a Secretary of the Center who shall serve as Council Secretary, responsible for all documentation, records, and information dissemination of all decisions of the Center Manager and the Council to ensure proper implementation, monitoring and evaluation.

**SECTION 17. CENTER POLICY:** The Council shall formulate policies that shall encourage students as well as parents to participate in the programs initiated by the City Government of Davao.

**SECTION 18. CURRICULUM:** The Curriculum of the Center shall be affirmed and approved by the Council. It shall conform to the standard requirements of DepEd and any changes/enrichment/enhancement thereto shall be implemented based on the approval of the Council.

**SECTION 19. COLLECTION OF FEES:** The Center shall not collect tuition fee, professional fee or any other fees from students/parents, other than those authorized by the Department of Education and the City Government of Davao.

**SECTION 20. FUNDING:** The City Government shall allocate funds from the City's General Fund to ensure the delivery of basic services of the Center. Further, additional funding requirements necessary for the smooth operation of the Center may be referred to the Local School Board (LSB) for appropriate action. The Center may receive funding from Non-Government Organizations (NGOs) such as donations, grants, etc.

**SECTION 21. ADDITIONAL COMPENSATION:** To attract competent, dedicated, and committed allied medical services personnel, teaching and non-teaching staff shall be for the account of the City Government's General Fund.

**SECTION 22. COUNCIL MEETING:** The Council shall meet regularly once in every quarter or in a special session as may be deemed necessary by the Chairperson and the Center Manager or any nine (9) of its members. A majority of the members shall constitute a quorum to transact business. All acts of the Council shall be collegial and no member may take action, on any matter, on his own.

**SECTION 23. GENERAL PROVISIONS:** The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall appropriate funds deemed necessary for the operation of the Center. The budget shall be under the account of the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), provided further that the Sangguniang Panlungsod may also appropriate additional funds for special programs as may it deem necessary to be undertaken by the Center outside of its regular budget.

**SECTION 24. EMBLEM, COLOR AND SEAL:** The Center shall adopt its emblem, color, seal, and other appurtenances consistent and reflective of special education and values that shall enhance the dignity of Filipino children with special needs.

**SECTION 25. REPORTS:** There shall be an annual report prepared by the Council to be submitted to the City Mayor, the Sangguniang Panlungsod, and the Department of Education (DepEd) setting forth the programs, conditions, needs, problems, and progress made by the Center.

**SECTION 26. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:** If for justifiable reasons, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid by a competent court, no other section or provision shall be affected thereby.

**SECTION 27. REPEALING CLAUSE:** Any resolution, ordinance, or any part thereof found inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance, are hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 28. EFFECTIVITY:** This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.
