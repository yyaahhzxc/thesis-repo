**ORDINANCE NO. 0171-11**

**Series of 2011**

**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE REVISED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT RECOMMENDED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND SYCIP GORRES VELAYO & CO., RELATIVE TO THE PUBLICATION OF "DOING BUSINESS IN DAVAO CITY" THAT WILL SPECIFICALLY SHOWCASE THE CITY AS A RICH SOURCE OF INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR BOTH LOCAL AND FOREIGN INVESTORS**

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0171-11

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE REVISED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND SYCIP QORES VELAYO & CO., RELATIVE TO THE PUBLICATION OF 'DOING BUSINESS IN DAVAO CITY' THAT WILL SPECIFICALLY SHOWCASE THE CITY AS A RICH SOURCE OF INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR BOTH LOCAL AND FOREIGN INVESTORS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vii) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economic governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of the Local Code, the City shall fully exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:
Represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf, all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT - The Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) refers to the instrument to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Sycip Qores Velayo & Co., relative to the publication of "Doing Business in Davao City".

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the revised Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Sycip Qores Velayo & Co., relative to the publication of "Doing Business in Davao City" that will specifically showcase the City as a rich source of investment opportunities for both local and foreign investors.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other section or provision shall be affected thereby;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, September 22, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Final Draft

This Agreement is executed this day of 2011 at Davao City by and between:

SYCIP GORRES VELAYO & CO., a general professional partnership duly organized and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with business address at 6760 Ayala Avenue, Makati City, represented herein by its Chairman and Managing Partner, Atty. Cirilo P. Noel, and hereinafter referred to as a "SGV".

and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit created and existing under Philippine laws with office address at City Hall Drive, San Pedro Street, Davao City, represented herein by Honorable Sara S. Duterte, City Mayor, and hereinafter referred to as the "City Government of Davao or City".

WHEREAS, SGV has the known competence, technical knowledge, expertise and experience in promoting in the Philippines to foreign investors as an investment destinations through its yearly publications of the "Doing Business in the Philippines", which is an investment guide that provides information in the Philippines that provides an overview of the Philippines' social, political, and economic climate, and presents important information on the country's assets and strategic positioning that can be useful for investors who have plans of setting up business in the country. It showcases not only relevant historic and statistical data on economy but lists and discusses key pieces of legislation that govern business transactions in the country today;

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao intends to replicate the same concept of publication that will specifically showcase the City as a rich source of investment opportunities for both local and foreign investors because of its strategic location, skilled and highly educated human resource, diverse local products, excellent infrastructure, and peace and order situation that is conducive to trade and commerce, reasonable local business tax rates and its efficient and business-friendly local government service in the City;

WHEREAS, SGV is able and willing to provide from technical assistance to the City Government of Davao in the conceptualization, layout, design, publication of the "Doing Business in Davao City" and its production in other mediums.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions hereinafter set forth in this Agreement, the Parties hereto agree as follows:

1. DEFINITION OF TERMS

Unless otherwise clearly required by the context, the following terms as used in this Memorandum of Agreement shall have the respective meanings defined hereunder:

1.1 The term "Publication or Work Product" shall mean the "Doing Business in Davao City" publication, and its production by any other medium such as but not limited to digital or electronic copy, in CD or DVD format.

1.2 The term "services" shall mean the technical consultancy and marketing services of SGV.

--- [Page 5] ---
2. PROVISION OF SERVICES

Subject to the terms detailed in this Agreement, SGV undertakes to assist the City Government of Davao in coming up with the layout and design of the "Doing Business in Davao City" as well as the updating of the information relating to the process of setting up of business in the City, its local taxation, its current social, political and economic profile, demographics and other data related thereto.

3. INTEGRITY OF CONTENT

The City Government of Davao shall provide all the content and relevant information to be included in the content of the Publication. The City shall be solely responsible as to the veracity, accuracy and completeness of the information to be presented in the Publication or Work Product. The City shall hold SGV free and harmless of any responsibility, and neither can the SGV be made to accept or accepts any responsibility for any claim of loss, whether imputed, potential, or actual, arising from any inaccuracy in the information presented or arising from printing of the Publication, either in whole or in part.

4. COSTS OF PUBLICATION

The costs of production and publication of the Work Product as well as all other costs related thereto such as printing and distribution, inter alia, shall be borne by the City Government of Davao. As far as practicable, all materials and supplies and other expenses necessary and incidental to the said project should be provided and procured by the City Government of Davao prior to the commencement of the project.

5. COPIES FOR DISTRIBUTION AT THE LAUNCH

It is agreed that the Davao City Version may be reproduced and disseminated, in accordance with this Agreement, in either printed or CD form. It is further agreed that City LGU will print at least three (3) copies of the Davao City Edition at its own expense for distribution at the Cocktails-Reception to mark the launch of the Davao City Edition. In addition, City LGU may opt to distribute in CD form the Davao City Edition to the rest of the attendees.

6. COPYRIGHT

SGV acknowledges that the City Government of Davao shall own the copyright of the Publication or the Work Product and all its future editions, forms and models of expression.

7. TERMINATION

This Agreement may be terminated by either the City Government of Davao or SGV without need of judicial intervention at any time by giving at least five (5) days written notice to the either party. Should either party fail to comply with any of the provisions of this Agreement and not rectify such failure within five (5) days of having received a notice from the aggrieved party to do so, the said agreement shall "annul" and "terminate." Upon termination, cancellation or rescission of this Agreement, the authority granted by either party with respect to each other is deemed terminated.

--- [Page 6] ---
8. SEVERABILITY

If one or more provisions of this Agreement shall be held invalid, illegal or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain valid and shall be given full force and effect.

9. WAIVER AMENDMENTS

No waiver by a party to any provision of this Agreement shall be binding unless made expressly and in writing. Any amendment or modification of this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.
