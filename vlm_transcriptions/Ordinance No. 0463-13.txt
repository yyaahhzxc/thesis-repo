**SECTION 1. TITLE**  
This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "TELECOMMUNICATION ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO."

**SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY**  
It is the policy of the City Government of Davao primarily:

(a) to promote a pro-competitive, responsive environment for telecommunications providers, in order to secure lower prices, better services, and faster access to new technologies for consumers;

(b) to remove barriers to the entry of duly authorized telecom service providers and to pursue the expansion and improvement of basic telecommunication infrastructure and services, including those provided by local and long-distance telephone companies and video, cable, and wireless companies such as cellular mobile telephone services;

(c) to ensure rational land use, through the zoning ordinance, for the location of communication infrastructure, for the protection of the providers and users, as well as the public in general while ensuring efficient and responsive communication services;

(d) to ensure the ability of the City Government of Davao to impose requirements necessary to protect the public safety, welfare, and safeguard the rights of consumers.

**SECTION 3. SCOPE OF APPLICATION**  
This Ordinance shall apply to telecommunications towers to be installed by telecommunications companies, their general contractors, and sub-contractors applying to construct towers for base stations, cellular, and other wireless communication facilities in the City of Davao. The installation of antennas and other similar facilities are not covered by this Ordinance.

**SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS**  
The following terms are defined as used in this Ordinance:

a. Cellular Mobile Telephone Service (CMTS) - a public radio telephone service which, by means of mobile, portable, or fixed terminal equipment, gives two-way access to the public switched telephone network and other mobile telephone stations.

b. Telecommunication tower - shall refer to a pyramid-like skeletal structure with a framework of angular steel, bolted or welded together, typically higher than its diameter and high relative to its surroundings, and used to install antennas or signal boosters for better radio and sound-wave reception and transmission. These are classified as:

(i) Self-Supporting Tower (SST) - refers to a tower that has three or more supporting steel legs, capable of supporting itself without the aid of any appurtenances.

(ii) Guyed Tower (GT) - referring to a tower consisting of a main monopole and supported by steel guy cables.

(iii) Lashed Tower (LT) - refers to a tower, whether self-supporting Tower (SST) or Guyed Tower (GT), constructed on an open lot.

c. Affected party - refers to any resident/s who is/are actually living within the radial distance of a proposed tower to be constructed. Any person residing outside of radial distance is no longer considered affected even if the said resident/s is/are living within the same barangay.

d. Radial distance - refers to that area equivalent to the height of the proposed tower measured from the base of the said tower.

e. Variance - a special locational clearance which grants a property owner relief from certain provisions of the Zoning Ordinance. This applies after telecommunication companies select sites that maximize communication distance and quality, requiring them to obtain variances for non-conforming uses from the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

SECTION 5. SITING OF TELECOMMUNICATION TOWER

a. A telecommunication company, upon selecting a site for their telecommunication tower that maximizes communication distance and quality, shall apply for variance for the chosen property if the property is privately-owned, through the City Planning and Development Office, with the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod;

b. The City Planning and Development Office shall regulate the issuance of the application for locational clearance for the construction of cellular tower based on the City's Zoning Ordinance.

SECTION 6. GUIDELINES IN SECURING LOCATIONAL CLEARANCE

The following guidelines in securing locational clearance shall be observed by the telecommunication company:

a. Secure barangay resolution/clearance endorsing the site and construction of telecommunication tower; provided however, the applicant shall submit a written conformity, through a document signed by the majority of the affected households/residents opposing the construction of the cellular tower, to the Barangay Council prior to the issuance of Barangay Clearance, provided further that the issuance of barangay clearance shall be subject to the requirements of the Barangay Council.

b. For proposed tower location without households/residents or uninhabited within One hundred (100) meter radius, the requirement for a signed consent by households/residents hereby waived, provided however that the telecommunication company comply other requirement as provided for in paragraph d of this Section.

c. If the location of the telecommunication tower is privately-owned, the owner of the property shall apply for variance through the City Planning and Development Office, with the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

d. If the location of the telecommunication tower is privately-owned, the owner of the property shall apply for business permit from real estate lessor.

e. If the construction of the telecommunication tower is to be undertaken by a contractor or sub-contractor other than the owner of the telecommunication company, both the contractor and sub-contractor shall secure business permit separately.

SECTION 7. CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY

a. The City Planning and Development Office shall inspect and validate existing uses within a radius of 200 meters, the building, and actual structures within a radius of 100 meters and submit verification/circular report using an approved form.

b. The following shall be submitted to the City Engineers' Office (Office of the Building Official) for the issuance of occupancy permit:

- Copy of the Sangguniang Panlungsod Resolution
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) from the Bureau of Fire Protection
- A certification from the insurance company duly authority by the Insurance Commission

With the issuance of occupancy permit, the City Engineer's Office shall provide endorsement letter to Davao Light and Power Company for the issuance of power connections.

Consequently, the applicant shall submit approved occupancy permit to the City Assessors' Office for assessment purposes and to the City Treasurer's Office.

SECTION 8. AUTHORITY TO CONSTRUCT - The applicant telecommunication company shall apply for an authority to construct its tower to the Sangguniang Panlungsod with the submission of all the permits, clearances, Certificates and Study/Reports issued by the aforementioned agencies in Section 6, together with a copy of its Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity (CPCN) or Certificate of Public Convenience (CPC) as the case may be, to the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

The insurance shall answer for or cover any and all damages that may be caused in case of the tower due to natural deterioration, or that which may be caused by fortuitous events like, strong winds, typhoons and other disastrous calamities.  

SECTION 12. STANDARDS, PROHIBITIONS, AND OTHER CONDITIONS  
a. Cellular towers may not be approved near structure of historic or cultural importance and areas designated for scenic corridors. The views of and vistas from such structures and corridors shall not be impaired or diminished by the placement of a cellular tower.  
b. The minimum set back from any property line, public and private road and road right of way, building, or structure, except for accessory buildings or equipment structures, for a cellular tower shall not be less than five (5) meters.  
c. No commercial message nor any other signs beyond safety warning and an identification sign shall be placed on or added to any tower facility.  
d. No lights, reflectors, flasers, daytime strobes, or steady nighttime light or other illuminating devices except beacon lights maybe affixed or attached to it in any way to any tower, and as may be required by the Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP). However, perimeter lights and other illuminating devices needed for the security and safety of the site may be affixed on the other parts of the facility.  
e. The cellular tower shall be painted international red and white for air navigation visibility in accordance with international standards in air safety.  
f. All towers shall be designed to withstand applicable wind load requirements as prescribed under the National Building Code and constructed of or treated with corrosion-resistance material. A regular maintenance schedule shall be followed.  
g. All new or existing cellular towers shall comply with all relevant National Telecommunication Commission (NTC) and Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP) standards and shall not interfere with public safety and

SECTION 13: DISPOSAL OF TELECOMMUNICATION TOWER -  

a. It shall be the sole responsibility of the telecommunications company to dispose properly the telecommunication tower on the following condition:  

[1] On the event of non-renewal of contract of lease with lot owner;  

b. It is prohibited for telecommunication company to transfer, assign, or retain the telecommunication facility to the lot owner;  

c. The telecommunication company shall observe laws, rules and regulations governing the disposal of telecommunication facility.  

SECTION 14: TRANSFER OF OWNERSHIP - Transfer of ownership of the facility shall be allowed provided that the assignee shall comply with the provisions of this Ordinance by signing a sworn statement to that effect prior to the approval of the City Council.  

SECTION 15: CREATION OF REGULATORY COMMITTEE - A City Telecommunications Regulatory Committee (CTRC) is hereby created to regulate and conduct the necessary investigation, monitoring and periodic inspection on all telecommunication towers located in the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao.

**E. Regulatory Functions of the City Telecommunications Regulatory Committee - The CTRC shall:**

a. Be the governing body over the application, registration, construction, and maintenance of cellular phone towers within the jurisdiction of the City of Davao.

b. Regulate the hardware associated with the construction of cellular phone towers based on the standards of Presidential Decree 1096 otherwise known as National Building Code of the Philippines;

c. Review siting proposals of telecommunication companies for their cellular phone towers to protect public safety, welfare, and safeguard the rights of consumers;

d. Consider "safety of life and property," "efficiency," "competitiveness," and the provision of services to the "largest number of feasible users" in considering the construction of cellular phone towers;

e. Assess the impact of the construction of a tower on the location, the dimensions necessary for seamless cellular phone service, their applications for variances for non-conforming uses, property values, and visual comfort;

f. Review the impact of the tower on the environment and following maintenance procedures for making the tower viable;

g. Regularly monitor the effects of radio frequency electromagnetic fields. By tracking these emissions, the City Telecommunications Regulatory Committee (CTRC) can monitor the health effects of radio frequency electromagnetic fields emitted from communication towers on humans and animals. It will be the responsibility of the CTRC that the radio frequency electromagnetic field emissions from communication towers are monitored by the Bureau of Health Devices and Technology of the Department of Health. The monitoring activities shall be conducted in coordination with the Bureau of Health Devices and Technology of the Department of Health, and the National Telecommunications Commission;

h. Conduct due diligence in determining the legitimate owner/s of the cellular phone tower, inclusive of real property, in an event of company buy-outs and mergers, in the interest of local taxation;

i. Perform periodic inventory of cellular towers, routine inspection of the cellular towers according to its structural integrity, in cooperation with the telecommunication companies, their general contractors, and subcontractors, and the National Telecommunications Commission.

**SECTION 17. REGULATORY FEE - A telecommunication company whose application for the construction and maintenance of cellular towers within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao has been approved shall hereafter be required to pay a regulatory fee of Fifty Thousand Pesos [P50,000.00] per tower, upon approval of this ordinance.**

Any cellular tower erected, structurally altered, determined to be unsafe, or devoted to any use carried on in violation of the provisions of this Ordinance is hereby declared public nuisance or nuisance per se, entitling the City to apply to the City Trial Court to refrain or abate such nuisance.

SECTION 19. APPEAL PROCEDURES - Any person affected by any decision, action, denial, or determination made by the interpretation of the provisions of this Ordinance may file an appeal with the City Council within fifteen (15) days from receipt of notice thereof.

SECTION 20. LIABILITY OF DAMAGES - The telecommunication companies and/or their general contractors and/or subcontractors shall be liable for the death of, or injuries suffered by, any person by reason of defective condition of the cellular phone towers under their ownership.

SECTION 21. REPEALING CLAUSE - All previous issuances, executive orders, ordinances, rules and regulations or parts thereof which are inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions of this Code are hereby repealed and modified accordingly.

SECTION 22. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any provision or section of this Ordinance is declared not valid by a court of competent jurisdiction or suspended or revoked by the Office of the President, such judgment or order shall not affect or impair the remaining provisions, sections, or parts which are not affected thereby and shall continue to be in force and effect.

SECTION 23. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall effect fifteen days (15) days after its approval and publication in a local community newspaper with daily frequency.
