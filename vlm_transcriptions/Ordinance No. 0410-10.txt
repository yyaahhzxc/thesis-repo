**ARTICLE I:**

**TITLE, PURPOSE AND DEFINITION OF TERMS.**

**SECTION 1 - TITLE:** This Ordinance shall be called and referred to as the "NEW DAVAO CITY TRICYCLE FOR HIRE (TTH) FRANCHISING AND REGULATORY CODE OF 2010."

**SECTION 2 - PURPOSE AND SCOPE:** Pursuant to Section 17 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, the City of Davao will exercise the devolved powers to grant franchises and regulate the operation of all motorized tricycles-for-hire within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City.

**SECTION 3 - DEFINITION OF TERMS:** As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall be understood, construed, and shall mean as follows:

(a) Motorized Tricycle-For-Hire (MTH): A motor vehicle composed of a motorcycle fitted with a single-wheeled side cab, or with a center cab operated to render services to the general public for a fee;

(b) Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP): The document granting franchise or license issued under this Ordinance to a person, natural or juridical, allowing him/it to operate motorcycles-for-hire over zones specified therein;

(c) Zone: A contiguous land area or block, say a subdivision or barangay, where a motorcycle-for-hire may operate without a fix origin and destination;

**ARTICLE II.**

**DAVAO CITY MTH FRANCHISING AND REGULATORY BOARD**

**SECTION 4 - CREATION OF THE DAVAO CITY FRANCHISING AND REGULATORY BOARD:** To effectively exercise and perform the devolved powers and functions relative to the administration and control of the operation of motorized tricycle-for-hire, a body called and known as the Davao City Motorized Tricycle-For-Hire Franchising and Regulatory Board is hereby created, and may be referred to as the MTH Board.

**SECTION 5 - COMPOSITION OF THE BOARD:** The Davao City MTH Franchising and Regulatory Board shall be composed of the following:

1. City Mayor - Chairperson;
2. City Administrator - Vice Chairperson;
3. City Legal Officer - Member;

SECTION 6. FUNCTIONS OF MOTORIZED-TRICYCLE FOR HIRE (MTH) BOARD - The Motorized-Tricycle for Hire (MTH) Board shall be tasked to perform the following functions:  

a. Oversee, administer and regulate the operation of all motorized tricycles for hire within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City. Thereupon, it shall exercise administrative supervision and control over the Transportation Regulation Division (TRD);  

b. Determine, fix, prescribe or periodically adjust fares or rates for the service provided in the particular zone or area after public hearing;  

c. Prescribe and recommend areas or zones of services;  

d. Fix, impose and periodically review and adjust, but not oftener than once every three (3) years, reasonable fees and other related charges in the regulation of MTH;  

e. Open, re-open or cause the closure of service routes upon petition or at the instance of the office (Transportation Regulation Division (TRD)) after due notice and public hearing;  

f. Conduct investigation and hearing of complaints for violations of this Code, rules, regulations, orders and resolutions issued by the Transportation Regulation Division (TRD);  

g. Submit to the City Mayor periodic and special reports relative to activities and operation of the MTH Regulatory Division;  

h. Perform all other activities and functions as may be provided by law, or as may be necessary, proper or incidental for the purposes and activities of this Code;

SECTION 8. SUPPORT STAFF - The Transportation Regulation Division (TRD) shall act as Secretary of the Board and shall respectively be supported by a technical and evaluation staff, a legal staff, and an enforcement staff, as the service requires and a common administrative staff;

**SECTION 9. - FUNCTIONS OF THE TRANSPORTATION REGULATION DIVISION (TRD).**

The following are the powers and functions of the Transportation Regulation Division (TRD):

a. To hear and decide uncontested MTOP Application;

b. To issue, amend, revise, drop, cancel, and revoke MTOP after due notice and hearings;

c. To conduct investigations and hearings of complaints for violations of this Code and other traffic laws, ordinances, rules, and regulations issued by the Davao City MTH Franchising Board;

d. To summon operators/drivers and witnesses to appear in any of the hearings conducted by the Transportation Regulation Division (TRD);

e. To coordinate and cooperate with other Government agencies with any aspect involving MTH services with the end view of effecting continuing improvement of such services;

f. To determine, fix, and prescribe summons or traffic citation tickets for citing violators of this code and traffic ordinance of the City of Davao;

g. To implement and enforce the rules and regulation requiring MTH operators to equip, install, and provide their units with such devices, equipment, facilities that may promote safety, protection, comfort, and convenience of the riding public;

h. To perform the functions and duties as may be provided by law or as may be necessary proper or incidental to the purposes and objectives of this Code.

**SECTION 10. - ADVISORY COMMITTEE.** An Advisory Committee should be created to be composed of the following:

1. Integrated District Tricycle Federation – Davao City (IDTF-DC) NGO;

2. Student Representative;

3. Transmission DC (NGO);

4. Riding Public (representative from the media) whose members shall be appointed by the City Mayor upon recommendation of the concerned group;

No honorarium shall be given to the Advisory Committee.

**ARTICLE 111. - RULES OF ENTRY**

**SECTION 11. - QUALIFICATION.** Only Filipino Citizens and partnerships, cooperatives or corporations with eighty percent (80%) Filipino equity, are qualified to be operators of MTH. However, no Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOIP) shall be granted unless the applicant is in possession of units with valid registration papers from the Land Transportation Office (LTO).

**(a) Qualification and Requirements for Cooperative:**

(1) Tricycle Transport Cooperatives shall be required to secure favorable endorsement from the MTH Board and the City Cooperative Development Office (CCDO) prior to their registration with the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA);

(2) Membership in a transport service cooperative shall be open to all operators/investors, drivers, allied workers, dispatchers, mechanics, and the like who are residents in one zone or area of operation. Operator – member of a tricycle transport cooperative shall not have more than five (5) individually-owned units whose franchise or Certificate of Public Convenience and management shall be transferred/shared with the cooperative through a management agreement. Provided that if any operator – member shall have acquired more than five (5) units during his membership, he shall cease to be a regular member and shall be deemed an associate or preferred member as defined in Article 26 of RA 9520, otherwise known as The Philippine Cooperative Code of 2008. No person shall be a member of more than one (1) transport service cooperative;

(3) For purposes of validity where the transport service cooperative shall engage in allied services or businesses related to transport operations to be patronized by member – operators and drivers, a minimum of fifty (50) members shall be required upon organization, at least fifteen (15) of whom shall be licensed drivers whose representation in the Board of Directors, together with the allied workers shall not be less than thirty percent (30%) of the total number of directors at any time;

**SECTION 12. - COMMON CARRIER'S INSURANCE.** - Grantee of MTOP must carry a common carrier's insurance sufficient to answer for any liability it may incur to passengers and third parties in case of accidents;

**SECTION 13. - DISPLAY AND CARRYING OF PHOTOCOPY OF THE MTOP.** The driver or operator shall always carry with him a photocopy of the MTOP, or display a copy of the same inside the vehicle, in front of the driver;

**ARTICLE IV. - FARES.**

**SECTION 14. - RATES.** The fares shall be established at a level that will provide the operator a reasonable return of profit, but still affordable to the general public. Until such time that the Board has prescribed the fare structure for the zone, the official fare shall be that authorized by the Land Transportation and Franchising Regulatory Board (LTFRB) on MTR operating in the City;

**SECTION 15. - STUDENT PRIVILEGE.** For students, however, with valid student's identification card, the discount authorized by the LTFRB on public utilities is hereby adopted;

**SECTION 16. - POSTING OF FARE TABLE.** The table of fares should be posted prominently at the tricycle terminal and in the conspicuous part of the vehicle, in front of the driver;

**ARTICLE V. - OPERATING CONDITIONS**

**SECTION 17. - PROHIBITION.** No tricycle should be operated on national highways utilized by 4-wheel vehicles greater than four (4) tons and where normal speed exceeds fifty (50) Kilometers Per Hour (KPH). However, the MTH Regulatory Board may provide exception, if there is no other alternative route;

SECTION 18. - ZONE OF ADJOINING JURISDICTIONS. - Zones must be within the boundaries of the City. However, existing zones transcending the City boundary shall be maintained, provided that operators serving said zone shall secure the corresponding MTOP.

SECTION 19. - COLOR FOR A ZONE. - A common color for tricycles for hire operating in the same zone should be imposed. Each unit shall be assigned and must bear an identification Number, aside from its LTO License Plate Number. The Identification Number shall be printed at the front, left, and right sides of the MTH.

SECTION 20. - SUSPENSION OR STOPPAGE OF SERVICES. - An operator wishing to stop services completely or to suspend services for more than one (1) month, shall report in writing such termination or suspension to the office herein called the City Franchising Division which originally granted the MTOP. Transfer to another zone may be permitted upon filing application with the City Franchising Division.

SECTION 21. - LIFE OF MTOP. - The MTOP shall be valid for three (3) years, renewable for the same period. Transfer to another zone, change of ownership or unit or transfer of MTOP shall be construed as an amendment to an MTOP and shall require appropriate approval of the City Franchising Division.

SECTION 22. - LICENSED DRIVER. - Operators shall employ only drivers duly licensed by LTO for motorized tricycles for hire.

SECTION 23. - LOAD LIMIT. - No MTH shall be allowed to carry more passengers or for goods than what it is designed for or more than what is authorized in the franchise.

SECTION 24. - AUTHORITY TO OPERATE. - A Motorized Tricycle for Hire (MTH) shall be allowed to operate like a taxi, service, i.e., services is rendered, but prior authority shall be secured from the MTH Franchising Division and the Office of the City Mayor and the corresponding taxes, fees and charges paid to the City Treasurer.

SECTION 25. - ROUTES AND ROUTE MARKINGS. - All Motorized Tricycles for Hire (MTH) shall bear the following route markings:

(i) Route Number - All motorized tricycles for hire shall display the route number on the front and rear portion of the vehicle. The lettering of the route number shall be in black with white background and not less than three (3) inches in length;

ARTICLE VI. - FRANCHISE AND OTHER FEES

SECTION 26. - FRANCHISE AND ACCESSORY FEES. - The following fees shall be imposed and collected upon filing of the application or approval thereof, as the case may be, by the City Government of Davao through the City Treasurer:

(a) Annual Franchise Fee or Superintendence Fee - To be collected from all holders of MTOP upon approval and every three (3) years thereafter, on or before the anniversary date of the MTOP;

(b) Filing Fee - To be collected upon application for MTOP based on the number of units;

(c) Fare Adjustment Fee for Fare Increases - To accrue upon approval of fare increases to be collected with the annual franchise fee;

(d) Filing Fee for Amendment of MTOP - To be collected upon application;

(e) Confirmation Fee - To be collected from all applicants to answer for documentation and/or postage expenses;

(f) Filing Fee for Opening or Closure of Service Routes - To be collected upon application;

(g) Certification Fee - For every certification on any document or act of the MTH Board or its division, a certification fee of Ten Pesos (Pip 10.00) shall be paid to the City Treasurer's Office;

(h) Legal Research Fee - The amount of Ten Pesos (Pip 10.00) to be paid to the City Treasurer's Office shall answer for all legal research work undertaken in connection with any application filed with the MTH Board. Such amount shall accrue to the Special Fund of the MTH Board;

(i) Special Permit Fee - The amount of Fifty Pesos (Pip 50.00) shall be collected from the operators who intend to bring their MTH unit outside of its route for personal use (upon written order) from the City Franchising Division.

XII. Exemption

Registered transport service cooperatives shall be exempted from the payment of franchise, CPC fees, and other accessory fees imposed on motorcycles owned by the cooperative. Provided that the following requirements shall be complied with:

1.) Secure Business Permit,
2.) Submission of Annual Report with audited financial statement to the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA);
3.) Submission of Tax Exemption issued by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR);
4.) Utilize a portion of the Cooperative Education and Training Fund (CETF) for cooperative education and transport operation seminar;

SECTION 29. - EXTENSION/RENEWAL OF MTOP - Failure to renew MTOP on the date stated in the franchise or failure to renew a delinquent registration, a surcharge of twenty-five percent (25%) of the franchise fee shall be paid, without prejudice to the payment of all lawful fees under this Code,
SECTION 30. - CONFISCATION OF DRIVER'S LICENSE - The license of the driver of the MTH unit that violates any provision of this Code shall be confiscated after a summons or Traffic Citation Ticket (TCT) is issued for violation thereof and the same shall be submitted to the Metropolitan Police for Hire (MTH) Franchising Division for disposition or appropriate action.
As used in this Code, a Traffic Citation Ticket (TCT) is a summons or citation issued to a person warning any of the provisions of this Code of the city, warning him to answer the charge against him within fifteen (15) days after his apprehension.
SECTION 31. - FORFEITURE OF MTH UNIT - An MTH unit operated in violation of this Code or any traffic ordinance of the City will be impounded and will be released only upon payment of the administrative fines, provided, however that in the event the vehicle remains unclaimed or the administrative fines unmet for more than ninety (90) days, the unpunished vehicle shall be forfeited in favor of the City Government and thereafter be sold at public auction.
SECTION 32. - PENAL CLAUSE - Violators of any provision of this Code shall upon conviction thereof be punished by imprisonment of not more than twelve (12) months or by a fine of P 5,000.00 or both such fine and imprisonment, at the discretion of the court: Provided, that if the violator is a corporation, partnership, association, cooperative, or any other juridical entity, its president, vice president, manager, or the officer responsible therefore, shall be penalized accordingly.
SECTION 33. - REVOCATION OR SUSPENSION OF MTOP/DRIVER'S LICENSE - In addition to the penalty heretofore provided, the court may revoke or suspend for a period not to exceed one (1) year, the MTOP or driver's license of the grantee/driver convicted of a violation of any section or provision of this Code.
SECTION 34. - DISPOSITION OF FINES - All fines/forfeitures collected for the violation of any provision of this Code shall be paid to the City, through the City Treasurer and deposited under the Special Fund of the City;
SECTION 35. - SPECIAL PERMIT TO TRAVEL OUTSIDE ROUTE - Special Permit to Travel Outside Route shall be granted to an MTH upon request, stating therein the date, origin, 2nd destination and the purpose of the said permit. In no case shall a special permit exceed three (3) days from the issuance thereof.
