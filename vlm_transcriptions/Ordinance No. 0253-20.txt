ORDINANCE NO. 0253-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE PENALIZING DISHONESTY OR UNTRUTHFULNESS IN THE DECLARATION OF PATIENTS WHO ARE EITHER CONFIRMED, SUSPECT OR PROBABLE COVID-19 CASE DURING THE PRESENT COVID-19 HEALTH CRISIS AND/OR PANDEMIC, IMPOSING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF

Ord. No. 0253-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE ANTI-PATIENT DISHONESTY AND NON-COOPERATION ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS

a.) Health Workers or Professionals - are persons who render health care services to patients seeking medical attention, such as doctors, nurses, medics, hospital and clinic aides, laboratory technicians and the like;

b.) Hospital Workers - are persons, not falling under the aforementioned definition, who likewise work or are employed in hospitals, clinics, laboratories and diagnostic centers dealing with COVID-19 patients, whether confirmed or not, including PUs or PUMs.

c.) Patients - are defined as:

i. Confirmed COVID-19 Infected Person - are persons tested positive for COVID-19 by duly authorized and accredited facilities by the Department of Health.

ii. Suspect COVID-19 Case - are persons, who are classified as such under the protocols and guidelines of the Department of Health.

iii. Probable COVID-19 Case - are persons who are classified as such under the protocols and guidelines of the Department of Health.

d.) Close Contacts - persons who may have come into contact with Confirmed, Suspect and Probable COVID-19 Infected Person, but who are not declared to be Confirmed, Suspect and Probable COVID-19 Infected Persons.

SECTION 3. DISHONESTY AND UNTRUTHFULNESS IN THE DECLARATION OF MEDICAL CONDITION - Any person who is required by any Hospital or Health/Medical facility such as clinics, laboratories, diagnostic centers, health centers, and the like, be it public or private, to accomplish and fill-out a medical/personal form or any similar document, shall state the truth and date of birth, personal identification information, and shall affirmatively declare that the said form or document and shall affix his signature at the same. Said person shall likewise observe honesty and truthfulness in answering questions propounded during interview by the health worker/professional or hospital worker.

SECTION 4. UNLAWFUL ACTS - It shall be unlawful for any person who is a Confirmed, Suspect and Probable COVID-19 infected person, to knowingly and deliberately make false and untruthful statements and/or withhold relevant information when asked or required to do so, such as but not limited to travel history, experience of symptoms, medical condition and other important data during the Health Crisis brought about by the COVID-19 outbreak as declared by the National Government.

Any omission of the required information aforementioned in Section 3 hereof, of which a reasonable person is expected to have personal knowledge shall be prima facie evidence of dishonesty, untruthfulness and constitutes a violation of this Ordinance.

**SECTION 5 PENALTY** - Any person who commits the unlawful acts under Section 4 hereof shall be liable to pay a fine in the amount of not less than Five Thousand Pesos (Pip 5,000.00) or by imprisonment of not less than thirty (30) days or both, at the discretion of the court.

**SECTION 6 SEPARABILITY CLAUSE** - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in force and effect.

**SECTION 7 EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED**, on the 16th day of April 2020, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
