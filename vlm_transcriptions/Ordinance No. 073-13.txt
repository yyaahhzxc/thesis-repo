ORDINANCE NO. 073-13  
Series of 2013  

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING BARANGAY LENGTH OF SERVICE BENEFITS/INCENTIVES TO ALL BARANGAY ELECTED OFFICIALS AFTER THEIR TENURE OF OFFICE AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that.

--- [Page 2] ---
**Page 2 of 3**

**Ord. No. 073-13**

**SECTION 1. TITLE:** This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING BARANGAY LENGTH OF SERVICE BENEFITS INCENTIVES TO ALL BARANGAY ELECTED OFFICIALS AFTER THEIR TENURE OF OFFICE AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR."

**SECTION 2. FINANCIAL INCENTIVES:** Elected Barangay Officials after their tenure of office shall be entitled to a financial incentive of Ten Thousand Pesos (P10,000.00) for Punong Barangay and Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) for Barangay Kagawad for every year of service.

**SECTION 3. APPOINTED BARANGAY OFFICERS:** Barangay Officials who served in an appointive status or capacity to fill up an elective position as such, shall also be entitled to the herein incentive. Provided, however, that they must have served at least one (1) year.

**SECTION 4. FULL TERM INCENTIVES:** The provisions of Sections 2 and 3 shall not apply in case said Barangay Officials have been elected for three (3) consecutive terms, in which event they will be entitled to receive a Plaque of Recognition in an appropriate ceremony as well as financial assistance as follows:

a) For Punong Barangay - Php 100,000.00;

b) For Barangay Kagawad - Php 60,000.00;

For purposes of giving the award under this Ordinance, Barangay Officials shall refer to the Punong Barangay and Barangay Kagawad who has not been declared guilty of any administrative or criminal offense by any government agency, quasi-judicial bodies or Courts of Justice, including those barangay officials with pending cases until such time that they will be cleared at the time of his/her term of office;

**SECTION 5. SUCCESSION OF OFFICE:** In cases where the highest ranking Barangay Kagawad shall succeed the position of Punong Barangay by virtue of operation of law pursuant to the Local Government Code of 1991, the longer period served either as Kagawad or Punong Barangay shall be taken into account in determining the benefit he shall be entitled to;

**SECTION 6. ASSESSMENT AND EFFECTIVITY:** The assessment of the herein benefit shall be after their tenure of office and upon submission of the requirements needed such as Certification from the Office of the Barangay Cultural Communities Affairs and Development (BCCAD) under the Office of the City Mayor, the Barangay Charter, the Barangay Code of Ethics, he/she served and other requirements which the City Treasurer may require;

**SECTION 7. BUDGETARY ALLOCATION:** An amount equivalent to one percent (1%) of the Annual General Fund as provided for in Section 308 of the Local Government Code is hereby appropriated;

**SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY:** This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and after due publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the locality.
