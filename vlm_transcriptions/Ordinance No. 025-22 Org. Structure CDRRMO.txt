Section 1. Short Title - This Ordinance shall be referred to as "An Ordinance Amending Certain Provisions of Ordinance No. 0966-22."

Section 2. Declaration of Principles and Policies

A. Section 77 of RA 7160 states: "The chief executive of every local government unit shall be responsible for human resources and development in his unit and shall take all personnel actions in accordance with the Constitutional provisions on civil service, pertinent laws, and rules and regulations thereof, including such policies, guidelines, and standards as the Civil Service Commission may establish; Provided, that the local chief executive may employ emergency or casual employees or laborers paid on a daily wage or piecework basis and hired through job orders for local projects authorized by the Sanggunian concerned, without need of approval or attestation by the Civil Service Commission; Provided, further, that the period of employment of emergency or casual laborers as provided in this Section shall not exceed six (6) months."

B. Section 458 (a) (1) (vii) and (viii) of the Local Government Code of 1991 authorizes the Sangguniang Panlungsod to determine positions, powers, duties, and salaries of officials and employees of the City;

C. Section 454 (c) (2) of RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, empowers the Sangguniang Panlungsod to create such other offices as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of the City Government;

D. Civil Service Commission (CSC) Resolution No. 1800692 (Revised Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions) dated July 3, 2018 approved amendments and additional provisions of CSC Resolution No. 1701009 of June 16, 2017 (Adoption and Pronouncement of 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions);

E. Joint Memorandum Circular (JMC) No. 2014-1 is the Implementing Guidelines for the Establishment of Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Offices (LDRRMOs) or Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committees (BDRCs) in Local Government Units issued to all Provincial Governors, City Mayors, Municipal Mayors, Punong Barangays, Members of the Sanggunian, Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Councils, Local Finance Committees, and all other national and local government officials concerned.

Section 3. Definition of Terms and Acronyms

RA 7160 Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, applies primarily to all local governments, provinces, cities, municipalities, and barangays in the Philippines. It also includes all offices, agencies, and bodies of the national government as far as their relationship to the local governments are concerned. The Code does not apply in the Autonomous Region of Muslim Mindanao (ARM). Being an Autonomous Region, it has its own local government code.
