ORDINANCE NO. 0311-09  
Series of 2009  
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 5 OF  
ORDINANCE NO. 143, SERIES OF 2005, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE USE OF CELLULAR PHONES, HANDHELD COMPUTERS, PORTABLE RADIO RECEIVERS (PARK AND OTHER SIMILAR ELECTRONIC DEVICES WHILE DRIVING  
Be it ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 5 OF ORDINANCE NO. 143, SERIES OF 2005, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE

--- [Page 3] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord No. 0311-09

USE OF CELLULAR PHONES, HAND-HELD RADIOS, PERSONAL DIGITAL ASSISTANT (PDA) AND OTHER SIMILAR ELECTRONIC DEVICES WHILE DRIVING,"

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 5 of Ordinance No. 143, Series of 2005, otherwise known as "An ordinance prohibiting the use of Cellular Phones, Hand-HeId Radios, Personal Digital Assistant (PDA) and other similar electronic devices while driving," is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Section 5. PENALITY CLAUSE

Any person who shall violate the provisions of this Ordinance shall be subject to the confiscation of his/her driver's license by the Traffic Management Group, other duly authorized traffic personnel, or any law enforcer. The confiscated license will be turned over to the Land Transportation Office (LTO) for renewal by the person upon payment at the City Treasurer's Office of the following:

a. fine of P500.00 on the 1st offense;
b. fine of P1,000.00 on the 2nd offense;
c. fine of P1,500.00 on subsequent instances.

All penalties are without prejudice to the application of the provisions of other existing applicable ordinances and laws."

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason any Section of the Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other Sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation in Davao City, and upon posting for three (3) consecutive weeks at conspicuous places at City Hall,

ENACTED, November 24, 2009, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

(Note: Councilors Dante A. Apostol Jr. and Leonardo R. Asila III were out when this ordinance was approved)

CERTIFIED CORRECT.
