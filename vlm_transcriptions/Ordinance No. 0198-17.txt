Ord. No. 0198-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0266-06/RESOLUTION NO. 02966-06, SERIES
OF 2006, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING THE
MUSEO DABAWENYO, PROVIDING FOR ITS PERMANENT HOME AND FOR
OTHER PURPOSES," PARTICULARLY SECTION 4 OF THE SAME, TO ALLOW THE
TRANSFER OF THE MUSEUM'S LOCATION FROM A. PICHON STREET TO PALMA
GIL STREET, WITHIN THE PREMISES OF THE PEOPLE'S PARK;

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - The provision to be amended is as follows:
Section 4 of Ordinance No. 0266-06/02966-06 reads:

Section 4. PERMANENT HOME - The old Court of First
Instance Building of A. Pichon Sr. Street shall be the permanent and
exclusive site of the Museo Dabawenyo, as per recommendation of
the City Planning and Development Office and as concurred by the
Office of the City Mayor without prejudice to the establishment of
other buildings in other places for the same purpose;

The amendment will read as:

Section 4. NEW HOME - The new site of the Museo
Dabawenyo shall be in a building to be constructed for that purpose
within the premises of the People's Park without prejudice to the
establishment of other buildings in other places for the same
purpose;

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or
provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections
or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on May 23, 2017, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

CHARITO N. SANTOS
