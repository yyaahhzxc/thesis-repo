A. Section 22 of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"SECTION 22. Corporate Powers. - (a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:

3) To have and use a corporate seal;
4) To acquire, hold, convey real and personal property;
5) To enter into contracts; and
6) To exercise such other powers as are granted to corporations, subject to the limitations provided in this Code and other laws";

B. Section 455 of Republic Act No. 7160 provides:

"SECTION 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(a) The City Mayor, as chief executive of the city government, shall exercise such powers and perform such duties and functions as provided by this Code and other laws.

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(c) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contacts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

F. There are eight broad categories that create and sustain peaceful societies. The Institute of Economics and Peace has identified the Following Eight (8) Pillars of Positive Peace as the most common characteristics of most peaceful societies. These pillars are the following:

* well-functioning government;
* low levels of corruption;
* acceptance of the rights of others;
* good relations with neighbors;
* sound business environment;
* high levels of human capital;
* equitable distribution of resources; and
* free flow of information.

The concept of Positive Peace acknowledges that ending direct violence alone does not guarantee durable peace. Ending direct violence is Negative Peace. For peace to endure, a society must build longer-term conditions that enable society to unify and flourish. This is called Positive Peace. Investing in Positive Peace improves resilience, mitigates risk of violence, and reduces the economic toll from conflict. The Eight Pillars of Positive Peace are highly interconnected and interact together to build a system. It therefore requires building upon each other to achieve the goal of Positive Peace. The Pillars of Peace interact with communities and other structures and institutions of the City. The establishment of Peace 911 - The Center for Peacebuilding will contribute to the achievement of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals of the United Nations, in particular, Goal 16 on building peace, justice and strong institutions, strengthening human rights, reducing discrimination and inequality, promoting gender equality, preserving natural resources and protecting the climate.

SECTION 4. AMENDMENTS TO ORDINANCE NO. 0891-22, SERIES OF 2022.
Sections 4 and 6 shall be amended and restated; Section 10 shall be amended and restated in its entirety, and Section 13 shall be rescinded.

A. Section 4.(E) of Ordinance No. 0891-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended and restated to read as "Reconstitution of the members of the City Peace 911 Council shall be done by the City Mayor every three (3) years."

B. Section 4.(F) of Ordinance No. 0891-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended and restated to read as "Composition of the members of Peace 911 sa Barangay shall be reconstituted every three (3) years by the City Peace 911 Council."

C. Section 6.(A) of Ordinance No. 0891-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended and restated to read as:

"A. The City Peace 911 Council shall be composed of:
1. The City Mayor or the duly authorized representative to act as chairperson;
2. Two (2) members from the Government;"

**D. Section 10 of Ordinance No. 0891-22 Series of 2022 shall be amended and restated as follows:**

"Section 10. Members of Peace 911 sa Barangay - Members of Peace 911 sa Barangay shall be constituted by the City Peace 911 Council.

A. The City Peace 911 Council shall identify eleven (11) sectors or groups related to Peacebuilding that will constitute the members of Peace 911 sa Barangay;

B. In addition to members coming from the identified eleven (11) sectors (Section 10.A), the following shall be the ex-officio members of Peace 911 sa Barangay:

1. The Punong Barangay;
2. One (1) Barangay Kagawad;
3. Two (2) personnel from the Barangay Functionaries;

C. Members of the Peace 911 sa Barangay shall be approved by the City Peace 911 Council;

D. Changes in the present membership of the Peace 911 sa Barangay shall be approved by the City Peace 911 Council."

E. Section 13 of Ordinance No. 0891-22 Series of 2022 is hereby rescinded.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.
