Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUCT BETWEEN DEPED - DAVAO CITY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE TEMPORARY USE OF THE FACILITIES OF DAVAO CITY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL FOR THE OPERATION OF THE CITY COLLEGE OF DAVAO

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0306-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUCT BETWEEN DepEd- DAVAO CITY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE TEMPORARY USE OF A PORTION OF THE FACILITIES OF DAVAO CITY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL FOR THE OPERATION OF THE CITY COLLEGE OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared the policy of the City of Davao to encourage advancement of education as it remains the most important key in enhancing the knowledge, abilities, attitude, and values of society in a challenging advanced economic and technological environment. It is the key to safeguard and secure sustainable development.

SECTION 3. DEED OF USUFRUCT - The Deed of Usufruct refers to the instrument to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and DepEd- Davao City National High School.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the Deed of Usufruct between DepEd- Davao City National High School and the City Government of Davao, relative to the temporary use of a portion of the facilities of Davao City National High School for the operation of the City College of Davao.

SECTION 5. REPEALING CAUSE - Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, July 7, 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
