Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

ARTICLE I
GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "AMENDMENT OF THE HALAL LANE ORDINANCE."

SECTION 2. PURPOSE - The purpose of this Ordinance is to amend Sections 4 (b), 14 and 16 of the Halal Lane Ordinance by recodifying it with the national law, Republic Act No. 10817, thereby recognizing the establishment of the Philippine Halal Export Development and Promotion Board, hereinafter referred to as the "Halal Board" as the empowered body to issue the Philippine Halal Logo and the guidelines on the proper use thereof.

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - Sections 4 (b), 14 and 16 of Ordinance No. 0520-16, Series of 2016, otherwise known as the "Halal Lane Ordinance" is hereby amended as follows:

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**  
If any provision of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance is declared unconstitutional or inconsistent with national laws, other unaffected provisions of this Ordinance shall remain in full force and effect.

Prosecution under this Ordinance is without prejudice to the prosecution under other national laws, regulations and issuances.

**SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE**  
Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules and regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY**  
This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao and its posting for three consecutive weeks at conspicuous places within the City of Davao;

ENACTED, March 20, 2018, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
