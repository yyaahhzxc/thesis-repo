AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE DAVAO CITY PUBLIC EMPLOYMENT SERVICE OFFICE (DC-PESO) AND INTEGRATING IT AS ONE DIVISION UNDER THE CITY MAYOR'S OFFICE  
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "DAVAO CITY PUBLIC EMPLOYMENT SERVICE OFFICE (DC-PESO) ORDINANCE OF 2012".  
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao advocates for the attainment of optimized human resources where human dignity is foremost; hence, it hereby declares its strong commitment to provide full and

equal employment opportunities for all Davaoeno's through the creation of Davao City Public Employment Service Office;

SECTION 3. DAVAO CITY PUBLIC EMPLOYMENT SERVICE OFFICE (DC-PESO) Defined - The Davao City Public Employment Service Office (DC-PESO) is a division under the City Mayor's Office which shall act as the coordinating and direct link to the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) and other local stakeholders in order to ensure the prompt, timely and adequate delivery of employment facilitation services to all residents of Davao City;

SECTION 4. FUNCTIONS OF THE DAVAO CITY PUBLIC EMPLOYMENT SERVICE OFFICE - The Davao City Public Employment Service Office (DC-PESO) shall perform the following duties and responsibilities, to wit:

a) Encourage employers to submit to the DC-PESO on a regular basis a list of job vacancies in their respective establishments in order to facilitate the exchange of labor market information between job seekers and employers by providing employment information services to job seekers, both local and overseas employment, and recruitment assistance to employers;

b) Coordinate with the Department of Labor and Employment for the technical supervision on the operation of the said office;

c) Provide persons with entrepreneurship qualities access to the various livelihood and self-employed program offered in the City by government and non-government organizations;

d) Coordinate with the different barangay in the City for the inventory of skilled workers and professionals and transmit information to them relative to job vacancies and other employment services to job seekers;

e) Encourage the barangay within the City to create Barangay Employment Assistance Center;

f) Provide employment or occupational counseling and values development activities;

g) Conduct pre-employment counseling and orientation to prospective local and overseas workers;

h) Provide reintegration assistance service to returning Filipino migrant workers;

i) Promote equal opportunity employment for qualified differently-abled persons;

In addition to the foregoing, the DAVAO CITY PUBLIC EMPLOYMENT SERVICE OFFICE shall undertake the following programs and activities:

a) Jobs Fairs - These shall be conducted periodically all over the City to bring together in one venue job seekers and employers for immediate matching;

b) Livelihood and Self-employment Bazaars - These will give clients information on the wide array of livelihood programs they choose to avail of, particularly in the rural areas;

c) Special Credit Assistance for Displaced Overseas Workers - This type of assistance will enable poor but qualified applicants to avoid opportunities for overseas employment;

d) Special Program for Employment of Students and out-of-School Youth (SSEOS) - This program shall endeavor to provide employment to deserving students and out-of-school youth coming from poor families during summer and/or Christmas vacations as provided for under Republic Act No. 7223 and its implementing rules, to enable them to pursue their education.

e) Work Appreciation Program (WAP) - This program aims to develop the values of work appreciation and ethics by exposing the young and first-time job seekers to actual work situations;

f) Workers Hiring for Infrastructure Projects (WHIP) - This program is in pursuance of Republic Act No. 6655 which requires construction companies, including the Department of Public Works and Highways and certified contractors, to hire at least thirty percent (30%) of skilled and fifty percent (50%) of unskilled labor requirements from the areas where the project is constructed/located;

g) Other programs/activities developed by Davao City Public Employment Service Office (DC-PESQ) and Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) to enhance provision of employment assistance to DC-PESO clients, particularly for the special groups of disadvantaged workers such as Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) and displaced workers;

SECTION 5. CREATION OF POSITION - The following positions are hereby created to compose the Davao City Public Employment Service Office (DC-PESO) organizational structure and staffing pattern, to wit:

1. One [1] Supervising Labor & Employment Officer - Salary Grade 22 (SG-22)
2. One [1] Senior Labor & Employment Officer - Salary Grade 19 (SG-19)
3. Four [4] Labor & Employment Officers II - Salary Grade 13 (SG-13)
4. One [1] Labor & Employment Officer I - Salary Grade 11 (SG-11)
5. One [1] Labor & Employment Assistant - Salary Grade 8 (SG-8)

SECTION 6. DAVAO CITY PUBLIC EMPLOYMENT SERVICE OFFICE ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN - The Davao City Public Employment Service Office (DC-PESO) shall be headed by a Supervising Labor and Employment Officer, otherwise known as PESO MANAGER with Salary Grade of 22 (SG-22), and four (4) Labor and Employment Officers II, otherwise known as Assistant PESO Manager with Salary Grade 19 (SG-19) in the Supervision of the following units:

- Labor Market Information Services Section
- Job Placement & Employment Facilitation Services Section
- Training & Career Guidance/Counseling Services Section
- Coordinative/Research & Technical Services Section
- Barangay PESO Coordinators

SECTION 7. DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF THE OFFICE OF THE
SUPERVISING LABOR AND EMPLOYMENT OFFICER AND OTHER SECTION - a) The following shall be the duties and functions of the Office of the Supervising
Labor & Employment Officer as:
1. Plan and supervise the implementation and administration of all FESO
programs and services under the Labor Market Information Services
Section/Job Placement & Employment Facilitation Services Section/Training and Career Guidance/Counseling Services Section and the
Coordination/Research and Technical Services Section;
2. Assigns workloads to subordinates in the division and monitors,
supervises the work performance of the same to produce performance
outputs by way of briefing, tutorial and coaching and/or issuance of
directives;
3. Coordinate with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE)
subjected for technical supervision on the program operation of PESO;
4. Ensure prompt, timely & efficient delivery of employment services
and provision of information on other DOLe Programs;

5. Perform such other duties and functions as may be directed by the City Mayor and other higher authorities;

b) The Office of the Senior Labor and Employment Officer shall perform the following:

1. Assist in the formulation of plans and in the implementation of all PESO programs and services including the supervision and administrative related functions of the PESO;
2. Represent the PESO Manager during his absence in meetings, conferences and similar activities;
3. Review all communications related to PESO operations;
4. Does other duties and functions as may be directed by the PESO Manager and other higher authorities;

c) The Labor Market Information Services Section shall perform the following:

1. Market and promote services through all forms of media, to all its labor market clients;
2. Regularly obtain a list of job vacancies from employers;
3. Generate and maintain an updated registry of skills;
4. Update list of jobseekers which can be accessed by all DC-PESO clients (employers) through internet;
5. Gather, collate and process data and information on employment trends, particularly labor supply and demand and disseminate such data or information to labor market clients;
6. Conduct a regular industry/employer's forum to assess satisfaction on DC-PESO services;
7. Participate actively in consultative meetings/trainings conducted by employers association, council and other institutions with related program on employment and livelihood;
8. Provide information to licensed or authorized recruitment agencies or recruiters for local and overseas employment;
9. Establish a strong network with other PESOs for job vacancy exchange purposes;

d) The Job Placement and Employment Facilitation Services Section shall take charge of the following:

1. Undertake active sourcing of vacancies;
2. Evaluate the qualifications of job seekers in relation to wage employment option;
3. Refer jobseekers for appropriate employment assistance and monitor the result of such referrals;

4. Institute measures to ensure that recruitment and placement services both for local and overseas are in accordance with existing laws, rules and regulations;

5. Initiate and organize job fairs;

6. Supervise and monitor job fairs conducted by private entities or educational institutions as provided in the DOLE guidelines;

7. endeavor to reach out qualified poor but deserving students and out-of-school youth to pursue their education by facilitating their employment pursuant to R.A. No. 7323, otherwise known as Special Program for Employment of Students and by providing opportunities for scholarship program;

8. Perform such other tasks related to its functions and undertake pertinent assignments and duties as may be assigned by the PESO Manager and/or higher authorities;

e) The Training/Career Guidance and Counseling Services Section shall undertake the following:

1. Conduct pre-employment counseling including values development and enhancement of work ethics, to prospective local and most especially to overseas jobseekers;

2. Administer & evaluate psychological tests or examinations;

3. Undertake employability enhancement trainings or seminars for job seekers as well as those intending to change careers or enhance their employability and conduct orientation and training to prospective local and overseas job seekers regarding rules and regulations on anti-illegal recruitment;

4. Perform such other tasks related to its functions and undertake pertinent assignments and duties as may be assigned by the PESO Manager and/or higher authorities;

f) The Coordinative/Research and Technical Services Section shall take charge of the following:

1. Conduct and/or coordinate with other institution in the conduct or researches and studies on labor and employment situation;

2. Coordinate closely with the Committee on Labor and Employment of the City Council of Davao City and other Labor and Employment agencies for policy recommendations;

3. Prepare monthly accomplishment report for submission to the Office of the City Mayor, Sanggunang Panlungsod and DOLE;

4. Monitor economic developments and key economic indicators affecting the City;

5. Provide support and coordinate with the TIPC in Labor Employment related activities;

6. Coordinate and maintain linkages with other GOs, NGOs, NGAs, and other institutions in undertaking other appropriate programs to promote the welfare of the OFWs and assist in gathering or tapping the skills and potentials of the returning OFWs;

7. Perform such tasks related to its functions and undertake pertinent assignments and duties as may be assigned by the PESO Manager and/or higher authorities;

g) Davao City Public Employment Service Office Barangay Coordinators shall perform the following:

1. Disseminate information up to the Barangay level on the programs and activities undertaken by the DC-PESO;

2. Coordinate with the Barangay for the conduct of Barangay unskilled, skilled and professional inventory;

3. Oversee the setting-up of DC-PESO Bulletin Board in every barangay in order to disseminate information relative to job vacancies and other DC-PESO programs;

4. Initiate training relative to entrepreneurship, barangay livelihood and other employment-related activities applicable to a certain barangay.

5. Such other functions assigned by the DC-PESO Manager;

SECTION 8. SELECTION OF EMPLOYEES FOR APPOINTMENTS - A person who will be appointed in the career service as part of the DC-PESO should meet the maximum qualification standards approved by the Civil Service Commission,

In determining the relevance of education and experience, reference shall be made to the duties and responsibilities attached to the positions and their previous engagement with the PESO programs;

Furthermore, selection of employees for appointment in this newly created division shall be open to all qualified men and women according to the principle of merit and fitness;

SECTION 9. FUNDING REQUIREMENT - Sufficient funds shall be allocated for the creation of DAVAO CITY-PESO and for the plantilla positions and staff under the aforementioned office;

The provisions of this Ordinance shall be implemented starting the 1st day of January 2013;

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason/s, any provision of this ordinance is declared invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 11.
