**RESOLUTION NO. 126**  
*A proposed Resolution fixing the boundaries of the different districts/barangays of Davao City, was presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body, by Councilor Antonio S. Castille.*  
*During the discussion of this item, Councilor Castille informed the Body that Davao City was divided into different districts by means of Ordinance No. 35, dated February 19, 1947, for present civil administration, thereby appointing deputy mayors in different districts. He pointed out that in the Ordinance which was approved by the Congress of the Philippines particularly Region XI, Davao City has three (3) districts, namely First District - Districts of Tuguegarao, Buhacan, and Pagalat; Second District - Districts of Bulungan, Bunawan and Pagalat; and Third District - Districts of Tugil, Tugbog, Calinag and Bagac. Whereas, in other cities, councilors are empowered to determine the boundaries of particular districts. In Davao City, it merely speaks of poblacion district and Talomo, and a question may arise as to what comprises Talomo District. He further clarified that under the present law, the Sangguniang Panlungsod is empowered to determine the boundaries of barangays and districts and settle disputes arising therefrom. He requested that these be amended or ordinance defining certain districts like Talomo, Buhangan, Bunawan and Pagalat.*
