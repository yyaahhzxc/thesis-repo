Ord. No. 0740-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 1.3, ARTICLE XII, OF ORDINANCE NO. 0546-13, SERIES OF 2013, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE 'COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY FOR 2013-2022'".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 1.3, Article XII, of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City for 2013-2022 shall be amended as follows:

1.3 REQUIREMENTS. The following requirements must be submitted in a request for additional allowable use:

a. Barangay Council Resolution of No Objection;
b. Barangay Development Council Resolution favorably endorsing the project;
c. City Engineer's Office for drainage clearance;
d. City Environmental Natural Resources for solid waste management plan;
e. City Health Office for Sanitation Clearance;
f. City Assessor's Office for new tax declaration;
g. City Treasurer's Office for realty tax clearance;
h. Davao City Water District for Certification of water supply availability (not applicable to letter I of Article XII, Section 1);
i. Davao Light and Power Company for certification of power supply availability (not applicable to letter I of Article XII, Section 1);
j. Mines and Geosciences Bureau for certification for possible geohazard and recommended mitigating measures;
k. DENR-Environmental Management Bureau for waste treatment facilities and permit to discharge effluents;
l. Water Resource Management Council/Task Force for Project within Water Resource Zone;
m. Barangay Watershed Management Council and City Watershed Management Council for areas in Conservation Zone;
n. Construction and Development Committee (applicable only to letter h of Article XII, Section 1 hereof);
o. Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines and Philippine Air Force Command Davao City (applicable only to letter j of Article XII, Section 1 hereof);
p. Philippine Ports Authority (applicable only to letter k of Article XII Section 1 hereof);
q. Department of Public Works and Highways Davao City (applicable only to letters c and o of Article XII, Section 1 hereof);
r. Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction Management Office for Hazard and Risk Assessment (applicable only for infrastructure developments stated in Article XII, Section 1 hereof)

**SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY:** This Ordinance shall take effect after approval.
