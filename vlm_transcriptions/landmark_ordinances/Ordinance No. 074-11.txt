**ORDINANCE NO. 074-11**

**Series of 2011**

**AN ORDINANCE PENALIZING ANY PERSON IN ACTUAL OR CONSTRUCTIVE POSSESSION OF ANY DOG FOUND DEFAECATING UPON ANOTHER PERSON'S PRIVATE PROPERTY OR ON COMMONLY-USED PROPERTIES SUCH AS ROADS, PARKS AND PLAYGROUNDS OR ON PUBLIC PROPERTIES**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE PENALIZING ANY PERSON IN ACTUAL OR CONSTRUCTIVE POSSESSION OF ANY DOG FOUND DEFAECATING UPON ANOTHER PERSON'S PRIVATE PROPERTY OR ON COMMONLY-USED PROPERTIES"

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 074-1
SUCH AS ROADS, PARKS AND PLAYGROUNDS OR ON PUBLIC
PROPERTIES":
SECTION 2. PENALIZED ACT - Any person in actual or constructive
possession of any dog found defecating upon another person's private
property, commonly used properties such as roads, parks and playgrounds
or on public properties shall be penalized unless the owner will cause the
immediate removal of all feces so deposited by such dog:
SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - The provisions of this Ordinance shall not
apply to a guide dog accompanying any blind person;
SECTION 4. PENALTY - Any person in actual or constructive
possession of any dog found defecating upon another person's private
property, on commonly-used properties such as roads, parks and playgrounds
or on public properties shall be penalized with an amount of One
Thousand Pesos [P1,000.00] for the 1st and each subsequent offenses;
SECTION 5. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS - Within
thirty (30) days after the approval of this Ordinance, the corresponding
implementing Rules and Regulations shall be promulgated;
SECTION 6. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section
or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid,
no other section or provision shall be affected thereby;
SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen
(15) days after its complete publication in a newspaper of general circulation in
Davao City;
ENACTED, February 22, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of
the Sangguniang Panlungsod present.
