**ORDINANCE NO. 0448-10**  
**Series of 2010**

**AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING MANDATORY REPRESENTATION FOR INDIGENOUS PEOPLES IN THE CITY COUNCIL OF DAVAO AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR**

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. **TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance Providing Mandatory Representation for Indigenous Peoples in the City Council of Davao and Providing Funds Therefor".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - This Ordinance is enacted pursuant to the provisions of R.A. 7160, otherwise known as the Revised Local Government Code of 1991 and RA 8371 or the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997, subject to the provisions of pertinent existing laws;

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:

a. NCIP - National Commission on Indigenous Peoples;
b. RA 8371 - Indigenous Peoples Rights Act of 1977;
c. RA 7160 - Local Government Code of 1991;
d. ICCS - Indigenous Cultural Communities;
e. IPs - Indigenous Peoples;
f. Mandatory Representation - Mandatory representation in local legislative councils;
g. TWG - Technical Working Group;

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - For the purpose of this Ordinance, Davao City has six (6) major Indigenous Peoples (IPs) needing representation in the City Council of Davao to ensure the advancement of their interests and aspirations, namely: Ata, Kalagan, K Iata, Matigalag, Tagabawa and Ubo Manabo who will be represented on a rotation basis per term in the same order of priority enumerated above;

SECTION 5. PURPOSE - This Ordinance is enacted for the following purposes:

1. Provide mandatory representation of the IPs in the City Council of Davao;
2. Recognize, promote, and protect the rights of the ICCs/IPs;
3. Ensure recognition and promotion of the customs and traditions of the ICC/IPs;
4. Provide mechanisms to enhance the ICCs/IPs social, economic, and political system to make them productive participants in progress and development.

SECTION 6. SELECTION PROCESS - A Technical Working Group (TWG) with members to be designated pursuant to RA 8371 and Section 6, Rule V of NCIP Administrative Order No. 1, Series of 1998, or the Implementing Rules and Regulations of RA 8371, shall be created by the City Mayor upon the effectiveness of the Ordinances, to develop the processes and mechanisms in the selection of the mandatory representative, subject to the approval of the City Council of Davao. Thereafter, a representative for the IPs and the ICCs shall be immediately chosen in accordance with the approved selection procedure;

SECTION 7. TERM OF OFFICE. The mandatory representative shall serve for three (3) years as provided in RA 7160.

SECTION 8. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS. The mandatory representative shall receive compensation, benefits, and other entitlements equal to the regular members of the legislative body and shall enjoy the same privileges enjoyed by them.

The mandatory representative shall have the same powers, duties, and functions, qualifications, and disqualification as provided in RA 7160 and other applicable laws with specific focus on the representation of the collective interest of the ICC/IES.

SECTION 9. FUND ALLOCATION. There shall be appropriated every year from the General Fund sufficient amount for the effective implementation of this Ordinance.

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. Should any section or provision of this Ordinance be declared by the Courts to be unconstitutional or invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of this Ordinance as a whole or any part thereof other than the part so declared to be unconstitutional or invalid.

SECTION 11. REPEALING CLAUSE. All ordinances, rules, and regulations in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, provided that the rights that are vested upon the effectiveness of this Ordinance shall not be impaired.

SECTION 12. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

ENACTED, June 22, 2010, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
