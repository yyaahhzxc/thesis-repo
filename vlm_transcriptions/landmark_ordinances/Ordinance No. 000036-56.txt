**ORDINANCE NO. 36**

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE ESTABLISHMENT, MAINTENANCE AND OPERATION OF BILLIARD AND POOL HALLS AND BOWLING ALLIES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.

By virtue of the powers granted to the City Council under Republic Act No. 938, entitled "An Act Granting Municipal or City Boards and Councils the Power to Regulate the Establishment, Maintenance and Operation of Certain Places of Amusement within their respective Territorial Jurisdiction," as amended by Republic Act No. 979 and 1224:

Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - No person shall conduct or operate a billiard or pool hall, or a bowling alley, where persons are permitted to play on a compensation for hire basis, without first securing a permit from the City Mayor and paying the license fees for such purpose to the City Treasurer.

SEC. 2. - No billiard or pool hall, or bowling alley shall be allowed to be maintained or operated within a radius of 50 lineal meters from any public building, school, public library, church or hospital.

This provision as to location shall not apply to those already licensed and operating at the time of the approval of this ordinance nor will the opening of any public building or other premises from which distance shall be measured prejudice any billiard or pool hall or bowling alley already then licensed and operating: Provided, however, that such billiard or pool hall or bowling alley established within 50 lineal meters from any school, hospital, public library or church shall be so constructed, as approved by the City Engineer, that the noise coming therefrom shall not disturb those in the school, hospital or church and, if such noise causes such disturbance then such billiard or pool hall or bowling alley shall not operate during school hours.

SEC. 3. - No billiard or pool hall, or bowling alley shall be open to the public, and no billiard, pool, or bowling games shall be allowed therein, except during the hours from 8:00 o'clock a. m. to 12:00 o'clock midnight daily: Provided, however, That on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve and on Saturdays, Sundays and days preceding official holidays, and the town fiesta, billiard or pool halls or bowling alleys may be open until 2:00 o'clock in the morning of the following day: Provided, Further, That any bowling alley located in any residential section of the city shall not be permitted to operate after 11:00 o'clock in the evening.
SEC. 4. - No person conducting or operating a billiard or pool hall shall admit minors less than sixteen years of age to participate in any game therein; and in the case of bowling alleys minors under twelve years of age shall not be allowed to participate in any game therein.
SEC. 5. - No intoxicating liquor of any kind except beer shall be sold within the premises of any billiard or pool hall, or bowling alley; neither shall any firearm or any deadly weapon be permitted to be carried within the premises thereof, except by peace officers in the due performance of official duties.
SEC. 6. - Any person who believes that a billiard or pool hall, or a bowling alley is established or located in any place not authorized or is not operating or conducting said establishment in accordance with the provisions of this ordinance may file a protest with the City Mayor who is hereby authorized after proper investigation to decide the case or cancel the permit and license.
SEC. 7. - It shall be the duty of the operator or concessionaire of any billiard or pool hall or bowling alley to see that these rules and regulations are properly observed, and that peace and order is maintained at all times within the premises thereof.

SEC. 8. - The City Mayor shall revoke any permit or license granted under this Ordinance upon satisfactory evidence that gambling or playing of any prohibited game has taken place within the premises of any billiard or pool hall, or bowling alley. Any violation of these rules and regulations shall likewise be sufficient cause for the revocation by the City Mayor of the permit granted and forfeiture of the license issued. Such revocation shall forfeit to the City all sums paid therefor.
SEC. 9. - Any violation of this ordinance shall be punished by a fine of not less than P100.00 but not more than P200.00, or by imprisonment of not more than 6 months or both such fine and imprisonment in the discretion of the Court. If the violation is committed by a firm or corporation, the manager or managing director of the person charged with the management of the establishment shall be criminally responsible therefor.
SEC. 10. - This Ordinance shall take effect on and after the 10th day following its passage.
