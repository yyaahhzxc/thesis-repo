Series of 2000  

AN ORDINANCE IMPLEMENTING A PUBLIC UTILITY  
REROUTING SCHEME FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO  
AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 38
Ord. No. 108

Be it ordained by the Sanggunan ng Pandulong in session assembled:

SECTION 1. - TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as the PUBLIC UTILITY REROUTING SCHEME FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

SECTION 2. - DECLARATION OF POLICY. The City of Davao is envisioned to be the gateway of Mindanao in terms of industrialization and eco-tourism as well as a center of development not only in Mindanao but also in the Bicol-Sagao region. This vision of the City requires that the City must be able to meet the demands of its population growth and urbanization.

The City of Davao envisions its transportation system to meet its growing urbanization and development by providing an environment-friendly and sustainable transportation network anchored on its efficient multimodal public transportation system and road networks.

SECTION 3. - OBJECTIVE. The Ordinance aims to implement a public utility rerouting scheme that will result in an efficient traffic circulation route to ensure smooth vehicular and pedestrian flow in the City.

SECTION 4. - SCOPE AND APPLICATION. This Ordinance shall cover all routes and shall apply to all Public Utility Jeepneys (PUJs), Public Utility Buses (PUBs) and Shuttle Buses (SBs) in the City.

SECTION 5. - DEFINITION OF TERMS:

1. Public Utility Jeepneys (PUJs) - are means of public transportation with franchises duly issued by the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) emanating from the north and south districts of the city as well as those circulating within the City Poblacion.

2. Public Utility Buses (PUBs) - are means of public transportation with franchise duly issued by the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) emanating from various origins and entering the City to load and unload passengers at the Davao City Overland Transport Terminal (ICOTT).

3. Shuttle Buses (SBs) - are means of public transportation with franchises duly issued by the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) emanating from specified points to transport passengers to specific points in the City Poblacion.

SECTION 6. - REROUTING SCHEME. For purposes of this Ordinance and in accordance with the Comprehensive Development Plan of the City as well as in line with the City's Transportation and Traffic Management Plan, all existing routes in City of Davao with franchise duly issued by the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) are hereby adjusted as follows:

SECTION 7. - ADOPTION. It is understood that this Ordinance shall be subject to adoption by the LTFRB in accordance with Section 5 (a) and (b) of Presidential Executive Order No. 202, dated June 19, 1987, defining its powers and functions to prescribe and regulate routes of service and zones of areas of operation of public land transportation services, particularly on motorized vehicles.
SECTION 8. - CONTINUING ADJUSTMENT. In line with the City's Transportation and Traffic Management Plan, the TMCB shall continue to periodically review the viability and effectiveness of the adjusted routes in close coordination with LTFRB and concerned government agencies as well as the Operators' and Drivers' Associations of the City and to recommend specific measures to be undertaken as basis for future amendment and/or revision of this Ordinance.
SECTION 9. - IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS. The Traffic Management and Control Board (TMCB) created and constituted through Executive Order No. 43, Series of 1994, amended by Executive Order No. 21, Series of 1995 and Executive Order No. 43, Series of 1996, shall cooperate with the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB), the concerned government agencies, the Chamber of the Committee on Energy, Transportation and Communication, as well as the Operators' and Drivers' Associations, to formulate the Implementing Rules and Regulations to make effective the provisions of Section 8 of this Ordinance within ninety (90) days after its approval.
It shall embody a mechanism for review and shall recommend specific measures to ensure the viability and effectiveness of the different routes.
Such rules and regulations shall take effect immediately on the day following compliance and completion of the posting and publication requirements prescribed by law.
SECTION 10. - PENALTIES. Any violation of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be subject to the penalties imposed by pertinent national laws, other existing memorandums orders, policies as well as rules and regulations of the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB).
