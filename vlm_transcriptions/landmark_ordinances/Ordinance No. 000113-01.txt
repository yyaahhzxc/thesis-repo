ORDINANCE NO. 117-01
Series of 2001

AN ORDINANCE FURTHER AMENDING THE ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO APPROVED UNDER ORDINANCE NO. 2374, SERIES OF 1994 AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Re: it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. - TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance Further Amending the Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City Government of Davao Approved under Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994 and for Other Purposes."

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 2
Ord. No. 113-01
SECTION 9. - AMENDMENT. The existing Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City Government of Davao is hereby further amended by changing the position titles or certain positions in various departments/offices of the City to conform with the Revised Index of Organizational Structure (RIO) issued from under Section 6(c) of Republic Act 6178, as amended by Republic Act 6179, dated March 18, 1994, list of which is hereto attached to form part of this Ordinance.
SECTION 10. - EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall commence to take effect on January 1, 2001.
ENACTED, January 16, 2001 by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
