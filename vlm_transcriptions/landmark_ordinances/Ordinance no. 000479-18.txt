Ord. No. 0479-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE SUPPRESSION OF A PORTION OF CAMUS STREET, THIS
CITY, FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE PROPOSED TEEN CENTER".

SECTION 2. SCOPE - The suppression of a portion of Camus Street with a total
area of Four Hundred Twenty-Five (425) square meters, more or less, shall be for
the purpose of constructing the proposed Teen Center.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, July 3, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
