**ARTICLE I: TITLE, POLICY AND DEFINITION OF TERMS**

**Section 1. Title - This Ordinance shall be known as the "COMPREHENSIVE URBAN SHELTER AND SERVICES DEVELOPMENT CODE OF DAVAO CITY," otherwise known as the SHELTER CODE OF DAVAO CITY.**

**Section 2. Declaration of Policy - It is the policy of the City Government of Davao City to promote a just, dynamic, and comprehensive land, security of tenure, and urban services development program, in cooperation with the people's organizations, non-government organizations, private sector, and other key agencies of the government, which shall:**

a. improve the conditions of the destitute and underprivileged citizens in the urban and urbanizing areas by means of making more accessible to them urban shelter and services at affordable cost;

b. secure and provide for the rational use and development of socialized housing sites;

c. institutionalize people's participation and representation in policy formulation and implementation of socialized housing and urban services programs.

**Section 3. Definition of Terms - For purposes of this Ordinance, the terms and phrases used shall mean or be understood as follows:**

a. Affordable Cost either to the most reasonable price of land and shelter based on the needs and financial capabilities of the community beneficiary/partners and appropriate financing scheme.

b. Balanced Housing Requirement refers to the mandate pursuant to Section 18 of the Urban Development and Housing Act (UDHA), whereby developers of proposed subdivision projects shall be required to develop an area for socialized housing equivalent to at least twenty percent (20%) of the total subdivision area or total subdivision project cost, at the option of the developer, within Davao City.

c. Census and Tagging Registration refers to the system of identifying and registering poor household partners/beneficiaries to serve as basis for prioritizing social housing projects targeted in the Local Comprehensive Shelter Plan.

d. Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) refers to the primary and dominant basis for zoning and use of land, applying a rational approach for allocating available land resources among competing user groups and for different functions, consistent with the development and zoning plan of the city.

e. Housing Backlog refers to the geographically identified urban poor households specifically located in depressed and blighted areas such as:

e.1. those in danger zones (road easements, river and creek sides, foreshore, esteros, canals, under the bridge and so on);

e.2. those that are for demolition and eviction with existing or threat of demolition orders from either the courts or LGU;

e.3. those affected by government infrastructure projects;

e.4. those in titled government or private lands and in public open spaces;

a.5. those that are living in the Area Priority Development (APD) areas
and those living in areas covered by Presidential Proclamations.

f. Idle Lands refer to non-agricultural lands in urban and urbanizable areas on which no improvements as herein defined have been made by the owner, as certified by the City Assessor.

g. Improvements refer to all types of development introduced by the landowner consistent to the tax declaration and approved land use.

h. Comprehensive Shelter Plan refers to a document, coherent to the City Development Plan, regularly updated by the Housing Board once every three (3) years, containing the following elements:

- an analysis of the housing situation and problems
- prioritization of social housing projects addressing the housing backlog
- identification, prioritization and zoning classification of social housing sites
- fund sourcing and financing for the prioritized projects
- appropriate organizational and management structure of the City Housing Office to implement the plan and programs of the Housing Board

i. Non-government Organizations (NGOs) refer to private organizations performing development services and duly registered with the appropriate registering agencies (Securities and Exchange Commission, Bureau of Rural Workers, Cooperative Development Authority) and other similar bodies.

j. Professional Squatters refer to individuals or groups who occupy lands without the express consent of the landowner and who have sufficient income for legitimate housing. The term shall also apply to persons who have been previously awarded home lots or housing units by the Government but who sold, leased or transferred the same to settle illegally in the same place or in another urban area, and non-bona fide occupants and intruders of lands reserved for socialized housing project. The term shall not apply to individuals or groups who simply rent land and housing from professional squatters or squatting syndicates.

k. Resettlement Action Plan (RAP) refers to the specific resettlement plan prescribed for involuntary relocation of informal settlers affected by demolitions and evictions issued by courts and the City Government.

l. Resettlement/Relocation Sites refer to areas identified by the local government and other appropriate national agencies which shall be used for the relocation of the underprivileged and homeless citizens affected by government infrastructure projects and with court order for eviction.

m. Security of Tenure refers to the degree of protection afforded (ownership, lease agreement, usufruct and other contractual arrangements) to qualified program beneficiaries against infringement or unjust, and arbitrary eviction.

n. Socialized Housing or Social Housing refers to housing programs and projects covering only house only, house and lot, undertaken by national or local governments or the private sector for underprivileged and homeless citizens, which shall include resettlement development, sites and services development, slum upgrading, financing, liberalized terms on interest payments, and such other benefits in accordance with existing laws, rules and regulations.

o. Squatting Syndicates refers to groups of persons engaged in the business of squatting housing for profit or gain.

p. Urban Areas refer to the built-up areas of the city with a population density of at least five hundred (500) persons per square kilometer.

ARTICLE II  
SOCIAL HOUSING PROGRAMS

Section 4. No Eviction and Demolition Policy. True to the mandates of improving the conditions of the underprivileged and homeless urban poor dwellers by the provision of urban housing and access to urban services at affordable cost, eviction and demolition as a matter of policy, shall be discouraged. It may, however, be allowed under the following situations:

1. When persons or entities occupy danger areas such as esteros, railroad tracks, garbage dumps, riverbanks, shorelines, waterways, and other public places such as sidewalks, roads, parks and playgrounds;

2. When government infrastructure projects with available funding are about to be implemented in any government or public property; or

3. When there is a court order for eviction and demolition.

Provided that the execution of eviction of underprivileged and homeless citizens and demolition of their structures be in accordance with the UPDHA's implementing rules, Executive Order No. 152, S. 2002, and other laws and regulations on the matter.

Section 5. Framework of the Social Housing Program. A framework for the Social Housing Program shall be formulated by the City Government of Davao, through the Housing Board, in cooperation with the national shelter agencies, non-government organizations, people's organizations, and the private sector pursuant to the provisions of R.A. 7160 and R.A. 7279 and consistent with and pursuant to the provisions of this Ordinance shall be adopted and implemented sixty (60) days from the effectiveness of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of this Ordinance.

Section 6. Coverage and Exemptions: The Program shall cover all urban and urbanizing areas within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City as defined by R.A. 7279 and all other lands which the City, through the Sangguniang Panlungsod, may certify and determine as suitable for socialized housing subject to existing national and local laws.

The following lands shall be exempt from the coverage of the Social Housing Program of Davao City:

a. Those included in the coverage of Republic Act No. 6657, otherwise known as the Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Law;
b. Those actually used for national defense and security of the State;
c. Those used, reserved or otherwise owned by the National Government, its agencies and instrumentalities, including government-owned or controlled corporations, or by the local government units. Provided, however, that the lands herein mentioned, or portions thereof, which have not been used for the purpose for which they have been reserved or set aside for the past ten (10) years from the effectivity of this Ordinance, shall be covered by this Ordinance;
d. Those used or set aside for parks, reserves for flyways and fauna forests and watersheds, and other areas necessary to maintain ecological balance or environmental protection, as determined and certified to by the proper government agency;
e. Those actually and primarily used for religious, charitable, or educational purposes including information technology parks, cultural and historical sites, hospitals and health centers, and cemeteries or memorial parks;
f. Those irrigated and irrigable lands;
g. Those areas identified as Strategic Agriculture and Fishery Development Zones (SAFDZ);
h. Those prime agricultural areas declared by the Department of Agriculture;
i. Those areas already declared as eco-tourism zones;
j. Those areas already declared as protected low and protected medium density residential zones, except those already occupied for Socialized Housing before the effectivity of this Code.

Section 7. Eligibility Criteria for Beneficiaries: Criteria for beneficiaries of the Davao City Social Housing Program are as follows:

a. Must be a Filipino citizen;
b. Must be of legal age;
c. Must be a resident of the City for at least one year;
d. Must be a registered voter of Davao City;
e. Must be an underprivileged and homeless citizen belonging to the identified backlog as reflected in the Comprehensive Shelter Plan of the City;
f. Must not own any piece of property in any location in the City or any other place in the country;
g. Must not be a professional squatter, as defined in Section 3 (j) of this Ordinance or a member of any squatting syndicate, as defined by Section 3 (o) of this Ordinance;

**9.1. Beneficiary Census and Tagging Registration.** Within one (1) year from the effectiveness of this Ordinance, the Housing Office, in coordination with the City Registration Committee as created by the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of RA 7279, shall identify and register all social housing beneficiaries within its jurisdiction pursuant to existing laws, regulations, and subsequent guidelines which the Housing Board shall formulate subject to the approval of the City Council of Davao.

**8.2. Land Inventory.** Within one (1) year from the effectiveness of this Ordinance, the City, through the City Housing Office and with guidelines formulated by the Board, shall conduct an inventory of the following lands and improvements thereon enumerated below:

a. Residential lands;

b. Government-owned lands: Whether owned by the government or any of its subdivisions, instrumentalities, or agencies, including government-owned or controlled corporations and subsidiaries;

The City Housing Office shall update the inventory of the foregoing lands and improvements once every three years.

**8.3. Land Identification.** After the inventory, the City, through the City Council, and in coordination with the National Housing Authority (NHA), the Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board (HLURB), the National Mapping Resource Information Authority (NMRIA), the Land Management Bureau Department of Environment and Natural Resources (LMB-DENR), and the Presidential Commission on Urban Poverty (PCUP), shall identify lands for socialized housing and resettlement areas for the immediate and future needs of the underprivileged and homeless in the urban areas, taking into consideration the degree of availability of basic services and facilities, the accessibility and proximity of job sites and other economic opportunities, and the actual number of registered beneficiaries.

**3.4. Urban Land Reform Program for Relocation.** The establishment of relocation or resettlement projects for poor communities affected by demolition or eviction is the core assistance under the Urban Land Reform Program.

No lands shall be appropriated for relocation purposes unless basic services or facilities are provided in cooperation with the private developers and concerned agencies. These basic services or facilities include, but are not limited to, the following:

a. Power and electricity and an adequate power distribution system;

b. Potable water;

c. Sewage facilities and an efficient and adequate solid waste disposal system;

d. Access to primary roads and transportation facilities.

To the extent feasible, social housing and resettlement projects shall be located near areas where employment opportunities are accessible. The government agencies dealing with the development of livelihood programs and grant of livelihood loans shall give priority to the beneficiaries of this Program.

**Section 11. Limitations on the Disposition of Lands for Socialized Housing**

No land for socialized housing, including improvements or rights thereon, shall not be sold, alienated, conveyed, or encumbered by any beneficiary of this Program within twenty (20) years from the issuance of the title except to qualified Program beneficiaries as determined by the government agency concerned.

Should the beneficiary unlawfully sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of his lot or any right thereon, the transaction shall be null and void. He shall also lose his right to the land, forfeit the total amortization paid thereon, and shall be barred from the benefits under this Ordinance for a period of ten (10) years from the date of violation.

In the event the beneficiary dies before full ownership of the land is vested on him, transfer to his heirs shall take place only upon their assumption of his outstanding obligations. In case of failure by the heirs to assume such obligations, that land shall revert to the Government for disposition in accordance with this Ordinance.

---

**Section 12. Balanced Housing Development**

The developers of subdivision projects, pursuant to the Urban Development and Housing Act (UDHA), shall be required to develop an area for socialized housing equivalent to at least twenty percent (20%) of the total subdivision area or total subdivision project cost, at the option of the developer, provided further that:

a. The area compliance should be within Davao City, specifically within the social housing sites identified in the Comprehensive Shelter Plan;

b. In case of monetary compliance, the same will accrue to the Davao City Socialized Housing Special Fund;

c. The beneficiary compliance should be the qualified beneficiaries prioritized in the Comprehensive Shelter Plan.

Developers of residential subdivision and residential condominium projects and/or proposed expansion of existing residential subdivisions and residential condominiums may opt to enter into development of new and/or existing settlement under the socialized housing programs, joint venture agreements with either the local government units or any of the housing agencies, slum upgrading or renewal of areas for priority development either through zonal improvement programs or slum improvement and resettlement program, participation in the community mortgage program, partnerships, contract to sell, accreditation of developers, land donation, and other feasible schemes.

No development permit application shall be approved unless the City Government, through the City Housing Land Use and Regulatory Unit (CHLRU), in coordination with the Local Housing Board, certifies to the compliance of the twenty percent (20%) Balanced Housing requirement.

The said amount shall be deposited to the Davao City Socialized Housing Fund mentioned in Section 21 hereof.

**ARTICLE III  
CREATION AND MANDATE OF THE DAVAO CITY HOUSING BOARD**

**Section 15. Creation and Mandate of the Housing Board - There is hereby created the Davao City Housing Board which shall formulate, develop, recommend, coordinate and monitor the implementation of social housing policies, plans and programs and administer the efficient discharge of the provisions of this Ordinance and the expeditious delivery of tenure security and urban services in accordance with R.A. 7279. For this purpose, the Mayor of Davao City shall convene the Housing Board within thirty (30) days from the effectiveness of this Ordinance.**

1. Act as a policy-making body and report to the City Housing Office.
2. Formulate the Local Comprehensive Shelter Plan which shall include among others, the social housing programs under Article III hereof within six (6) months from the constitution of the City Housing Board.
3. Assist in the updating of the residential land zoning classification of the current CLUP.
4. Recommend policies for the simplification and transparent processing of development permits, individualization of title, awards, effect the transfer of awards and/or titles in Presidential Proclamations, for social housing projects.

7. Formulate measures and monitor compliance to policies for the inventory and identification of lands for socialized housing, resettlement or relocation, based on site selection criteria, documentation requirements, and technical assistance under guidelines enacted for the purpose, with due regard to community participation in the selection and identification process of such lands.

ARTICLE IV  
BAVAO CITY SOCIALIZED HOUSING FUND  

Section 21. Davao City Socialized Housing Fund. Subject and pursuant to existing laws, auditing guidelines and regulations, there shall hereby be created a special account to be called "Davao City Socialized Housing Fund" to be used exclusively for social housing purposes, provided however that no expenses under housing funds shall be appropriated for the purpose under this ordinance without the matter being placed specifically in the budget approved by the City Council. The Housing Board shall be responsible for formulating guidelines on the allocation of such fund intended for the City's Socialized Housing Programs under Section 13 hereof. Aside from the annual budget allocated by the City for socialized housing programs, other sources of funds pursuant to ordinances or resolutions to be enacted by the City Council for the purpose may be generated from any of the following:  

a. Fixed percentage allocation based on the total loan share of the annual IRA/annual investment portfolio;  
b. Guarantees for social housing from the Home Guarantee Corporation and the Local Government Units Guarantee Corporation and other similar bodies;  
c. Imposition of equity share through savings as qualification for social housing availability of beneficiaries;  
d. Accessing SHFC financing for CMP projects within the city;  
e. Bond flotation guaranteed by assets and other forms;  
f. Retention of social housing project repayment refloors as part of a revolving fund for social housing and maintained as trust fund (Section 6);  
g. Tax arrears paid as land swap for social housing;  
h. Land, cash or bond sales contribution from housing developers in compliance to Section 18 of RA 7279;  
i. Fees for housing related activities, grants, donations, loans and partnership commitments;  
j. Share from the payment of development permits, locational clearances and land use conversion fees;  
k. Other modes feasible and not limited to the above;

ARTICLE V
THE CITY HOUSING OFFICE
Section 22. The City Housing Office (CHO). The City Housing Office shall be the implementing body of the Shelter Programs of the City Government. The formulation of the Comprehensive Shelter Plan (CSP) shall be done in coordination with the City Housing Office.

ARTICLE VI
TRANSITORIO COORDINATION WITH EXISTING LOCAL SPECIAL BODIES
Section 23. Transitory Coordination with Existing Local Special Bodies. The LHB upon its organization shall coordinate with other local shelter special bodies such as:
a. Committee Against Squatting Syndicates and Professional Squatters (CASSPS) - Identified syndicates and those with double enrollment of local and/or national financing programs shall be endorsed to the CASPSS for eviction proceedings. Their payments shall be forfeited and they will be substituted with qualified beneficiaries by the Housing Board.
b. Task Force on Resettlement and Relocation (TFRB) - Identified legitimate but excluded informal settlers by the TFRB shall be reconsidered subject to the process of the Local Comprehensive Shelter Plan.
c. City Registration Committee (CRC) - The census and tagging registration activity shall develop the alpha list of all the bona fide beneficiaries for socialized housing as identified by the CSP of the City. Designate beneficiaries shall be subjected to prioritization based on the CSP.

ARTICLE VII
FINAL PROVISIONS
Section 24. Separability Clause. If, for any reason, any provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid, the remaining provisions not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in force and effect.
Section 25. Effectivity. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and posting in three (3) conspicuous places and after publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City;
