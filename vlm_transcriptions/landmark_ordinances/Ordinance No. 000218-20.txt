ORDINANCE NO. 0218-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING TAX AMNESTY OR RELIEF FROM DELINQUENT REAL PROPERTY TAX

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "An Ordinance Granting Tax Amnesty or Relief from Delinquent Real Property Tax"

**Page 2 of 3**

**Ord. No. 0218-20**

**SECTION 2. AUTHORITY** - Section 192 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Local Government Units may, through ordinances duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or relief under such terms and conditions as they may be deemed necessary."

**SECTION 3. COVERAGE** - This Ordinance shall apply and cover all unpaid interests and penalties on delinquent real property taxes imposed on real properties within the City of Davao, including those which are yet to be declared by the City Assessor's Office.

**SECTION 4. THE KIND OF FINANCIAL RELIEF AND/OR AMNESTY** - There shall be a condonation of the interests, penalties, and surcharges of delinquent real property taxes.

Condonation of payment will only apply to assessed penalties and interests on delinquent back taxes relating to real properties as of December 31, 2019 and prior years.

**SECTION 5. GUIDELINES AND PRESCRIBED PERIOD OF PAYMENT OF TAXES TO AVALIABLE FINANCIAL RELIEF AND/OR AMNESTY**

a. All accrued penalties and interests shall be condoned only upon settlement of the basic and Special Education Fund (SEF) taxes within six (6) months from the effectivity of this Ordinance;

b. Payment of real property tax under this Ordinance shall be made in full, either in cash or manager's check;

c. Only after all delinquencies are settled may tax payments be credited to the current period;

d. The payment of real property tax for the current year shall be in accordance with Sections 19 and 19 of Ordinance No. 0291-17, entitled "The 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City";

e. No further tax amnesty shall be granted after the expiration of the tax amnesty period; instead, it shall be strictly enforced that real properties with delinquent taxes attached thereto shall be offered for sale at public auction to the highest bidder for the sole purpose of satisfying payment of accumulated delinquent taxes, with the corresponding penalties and interests, as provided by law.

**SECTION 6. FILING OF APPLICATION** - All taxpayers who want to avail of the tax amnesty or relief shall apply in a prescribed form to be issued by the City Treasurer's Office.

**SECTION 7. DURATION** - A delinquent real property taxpayer may avail of such financial relief and/or amnesty within six (6) months from the effectivity of this Ordinance. Thereafter, there shall be no grant of financial relief and/or amnesty to delinquent real property taxpayers.

**SECTION 8. PUBLIC AUCTION HELD IN ABYEYANCE** - No public auction of real properties due to non-payment of real property tax shall be held and initiated by the City Government of Davao during the amnesty period.

SECTION 9. INFORMATION DISSEMINATION - Aside from the publication of this Ordinance in a newspaper of general circulation, all Barangays within the City of Davao shall contribute to disseminating public information through posters, tarpaulins or streamers to be placed in at least three (3) conspicuous places within their respective areas of jurisdiction.
SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days upon approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation in the City of Davao.
