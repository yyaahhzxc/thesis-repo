Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 1, 2, AND 4 OF ORDINANCE NO. 0192-06, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "AN ORDINANCE FURTHER AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 110, SERIES OF 1986, AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO. 2021, SERIES OF 1994, AND ORDINANCE NO. 047-04, SERIES OF 2004, BY ALLOWING THE ESTABLISHMENT BY QUALIFIED TRANSPORT OPERATORS OR BY THE CITY OF DAVAO TRANSPORT TERMINALS FOR PUBLIC LAND TRANSPORT VANS WITH DULY ISSUED FRANCHISES OR CERTIFICATES OF PUBLIC CONVENIENCE AT THE OLD MATINA AND BAJADA AREAS OF DAVAO CITY";  

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVES  
1. To decongest the Davao City Overland Transport Terminal (DCOTT) specially Candelaria Street where the entrance of the DCOTT is found;  
2. To provide comfort and new transport accessibility and opportunities to the commuting public by providing new terminal sites for both buses and vans;  
3. To give qualified bus/van operators with duly issued franchises and Certificates of Public Convenience to have a choice of their own satellite terminals as long as they comply with the necessary requirements required by local and national laws;  
4. To generate more revenue for the city since more public buses and vans will be coming in and out of the city due to increase in number of trips.  

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - Sections 1, 2, and 4 of Ordinance No. 0192-06, are hereby amended as follows:

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASIS
SECTION 458 (a) (5) (v) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991.
The said provision of the Local Government Code, which refers to the Sanggunian Panlungsod's power, duties, functions and compensation, states to wit:
"(G) Approve ordinances which shall:
(v) regulate the use of streets, avenues, alleys, sidewalk, bridges, parks and other public places and approve the construction, improvement, repair and maintenance of the same; establish the bus and vehicle stops and terminals or regulate the use of the same by privately-owned vehicles which serve the public, regulate garages and the operation of conveyances for hire;"
SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
