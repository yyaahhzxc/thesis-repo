ORDINANCE NO. 156
AN ORDINANCE TO BAN AND/OR PROHIBIT THE USE, PLAYING, OPERATION AND MAIN-
TENANCE OF PINBALL MACHINES AND OTHER SIMILAR DEVICES.
Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, that:
SECTION 1. - Any ordinance and/or resolution to the contrary notwithstanding, it shall be unlawful for any person, entity and corporation to maintain, tolerate and/or allow the use, playing and operation of pinball machines and other similar devices in any place or establishment within the City of Davao.
SEC. 2. - Any violation of this ordinance shall be punished by a fine not exceeding Two Hundred Pesos ($200.00) or by imprisonment for a period not exceeding two (2) months, or both in the discretion of the court.
SEC. 3. - This Ordinance shall take effect on October 1, 1960.
