**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN REHAF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENTIAL ADVISER ON THE FACE PROCES FOR THE NATIONWIDE CONVERTED PEACEBUILDING PROGRAM FUNDEN ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF CONFLICT-AFFECTED AND PEACE AGREEMENT AREAS KNOWN AS "PAYAPA AT MASAGANANG PAMAYANAN (PAMANA)" OR "RESILIENT COMMUNITIES PROGRAM"**

Ord. 0245-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of the City of Davao, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENTIAL ADVISER ON THE PEACE PROCESS CONVERGED PEACE-BUILDING PROGRAM FOCUSED ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF CONFLICT-AFFECTED AND PEACE AGREEMENT AREAS KNOWN AS "PAYAPA AT MASAGANANO PAMAYANAN (PAMANA)" OR "RESILIENT COMMUNITIES PROGRAM".

SECTION 2: DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 455, (b)(i)(vi) of the Local Government Code provides, to wit;

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign contracts, agreements, instruments and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod provided for in this ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT. The Memorandum of Agreement refers to the instrument to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao as the SECOND PARTY and the Office of the Presidential Adviser on Peace Process as the FIRST PARTY FOR THE NATIONWIDE CONVERGED PEACE-BUILDING PROGRAM FOCUSED ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF CONFLICT-AFFECTED AND PEACE AGREEMENT AREAS KNOWN AS "PAYAPA AT MASAGANANO PAMAYANAN (PAMANA)" OR "RESILIENT COMMUNITIES PROGRAM".

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. The City Mayor is hereby granted LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENTIAL ADVISER ON THE PEACE PROCESS CONVERGED PEACE-BUILDING PROGRAM FOCUSED ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF CONFLICT-AFFECTED AND PEACE AGREEMENT AREAS KNOWN AS "PAYAPA AT MASAGANANO PAMAYANAN (PAMANA)" OR "RESILIENT COMMUNITIES PROGRAM".

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement made and executed in Davao City this day of , 2012 by and between:

THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENTIAL ADVISER ON THE PEACE PROCESS (OPAPP), a government agency with office address at the 7th Floor, Agustin I Bldg., F. Ortigas Road, Pasig City, represented herein by SECRETARY TERESITA QUINTOS- DELES hereinafter referred to as the FIRST PARTY;

-and-

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit (LGI) duly established under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with address at City Hall, Davao City, represented herein by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, City Mayor, hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARTY.

WITNESSETH: That-

WHEREAS, the Government through the OPAPP shall oversee the implementation of a nationwide converged peace-building program focused on the development of conflict-affected and peace agreement areas known as "Papaya at Masaganang Pamayan (PAMANA)" or Resilient Communities Program;

WHEREAS, PAMANA aims to reduce poverty and vulnerability in conflict-affected areas, to improve governance, and to empower communities and strengthen their capacity to address issues of conflict and peace through activities that promote social cohesion;

WHEREAS, the 1996 Final Peace Agreement between the Government of the Philippines (GPH) and the Moro National Liberation Front (MNLF) ushered in the constitution of Peace and Development Communities (PDCs) which were supported by three phases of the United Nations Multi-Donor Programme, the fourth of which is the Act for Conflict Transformation (ACT) for Peace Programme;

WHEREAS, the Peace and Development Communities (PDCs) demonstrate advanced capacities for peace and development;

WHEREAS, OPAPP recognizes the significant role of the Local Government Units in peace-building and conflict transformation due to their strategic advantage in the community that has direct knowledge of community issues and concerns;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the PARTIES have agreed and do hereby agree as follows:

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

a. This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) shall cover PDC PAMANA projects with a total Project Cost amounting to Three Million Two Hundred Thousand Pesos (PHP 3.2 M).

--- [Page 5] ---
b. These identified Projects shall be implemented within twelve (12) months from the signing of this Agreement in coordination with the concerned community stakeholders.

c. The SECOND PARTY through its Provincial Coordination Committee (PCC) shall oversee the overall PAMANA program implementation, provide technical and logistical support, and undertake monitoring and evaluation of the PAMANA program implementation, in consultation with the concerned community stakeholders.

d. The SECOND PARTY shall undertake the identified Projects in accordance with the TERMS OF REFERENCE attached herein as Annex A and make an integral part thereof.

e. Variation of the project design during the course of implementation should first seek the review and conformity of the FIRST PARTY and should only be limited to the total project cost. Variance orders resulting in an increase in the total project cost shall be borne by the SECOND PARTY.

2. SOCIAL MOBILIZATION and BARANGAY ASSEMBLIES

a. A barangay assembly shall be conducted in the Peace and Development Communities to identify viable, feasible, and sustainable projects of the community infrastructure that will enhance the economic situation of the community. The barangay assembly shall also establish a management and operational mechanism for the implemented projects.

b. The Federation of the United Mindanaowan Bangsamoro Women Multi-Purpose Cooperatives (FUMBWMPC) and the Peace and Development League (PDAL) shall assist in the conduct of barangay assemblies.

i. The FUMBWMPC shall coordinate, assist, and support the Peace and Development Advocates in the preparation for the conduct of barangay assemblies.

ii. The Peace and Development Advocates shall prepare for the conduct of the barangay assemblies through coordination with Barangay and municipal officials as well as most senior MNLF heads in the province.

iii. The Peace and Development Advocates shall conduct massive house-to-house information campaign on the reason and purpose of the conduct of the barangay assemblies.

iv. The Peace and Development Advocates shall engage the MNLF provincial formations on the preparations and information dissemination for the conduct of the barangay assemblies.

v. The PDALs and the FUMBWMPC shall submit a copy of the barangay assembly resolution or Sangguniang Barangay resolution to the PDC-PAMANA Coordinating Committee.

--- [Page 6] ---
c. The SECOND PARTY shall engage the concerned community/barangay in the conduct barangay assemblies. Towards this end, the SECOND PARTY should ensure that the barangay shall:

i. Provide the certified list of barangay assembly members

ii. Post the certified list of barangay assembly members in two conspicuous sites in the Barangay, preferably in the barangay hall.

iii. Send invitation for the Members of the barangay assembly

iv. Convene the barangay assembly.

v. Identify thru the barangay assembly, viable, sustainable and feasible projects and prioritize the same.

vi. Transmit to the Provincial Coordinating Committee the results of the barangay assembly as well as the priority list o requested projects

3. PROJECT FUNDS:

a. For the above-sad projects, the FIRST PARTY shall download the the SECOND PARTY the amount of Three Million Two Hundred Thousand Pesos (Php) 3,200,000.00) in tranches as stated in the TERMS OF REFERENCE (TOR).

b. The above-said amount, as allocated in the TOR, is not subject to any adjustment or revision because of price or currency fluctuations or the actual costs incurred by the SECOND PARTY in the performance of the activities under this MDA, unless otherwise agreed upon by the PARTIES.

c. The SECOND PARTY shall ensure that funds provided pursuant to this agreement shall be strictly used in accordance with the TOR, and none may be used for any purpose other than those expressly set forth therein.

d. The SECOND PARTY is allowed to utilize a maximum of five percent (5%) of the total project cost to cover part of the operational and administrative requirements of the Project. Any increase from the 5% allocation for operational and administrative requirements shall be shouldered by the SECOND PARTY.

e. The SECOND PARTY shall open and maintain a separate bank/full trust account under the name of the PAMANA-PDC Project in any stable bank, to facilitate transfer of funds, disbursement and effecting sound financial controls based on standard accounting guidelines. It shall maintain a cashbook for disbursing the funds received from the FIRST PARTY.

f. The SECOND PARTY shall furnish, complete and make available at all times all financial records and information which the FIRST PARTY and their authorized auditors shall require for auditing and liquidation purposes.

--- [Page 7] ---
g. The PARTIES herein agree that all funds, which remain unutilized after completion of activities/Project or in the event any phase or part of the Project are not carried out by the SECOND PARTY for any reason not attributable to the FIRST PARTY, or any bank interest gained from such funds, shall be promptly returned by the SECOND PARTY to the FIRST PARTY within fifteen (15) days from the occurrence of such event, even without need of demand from the FIRST PARTY prior to the said date, the same was mutually agreed to be used in another purpose in substitution and approval of the FIRST PARTY.

h. The SECOND PARTY acknowledges that the FIRST PARTY has made no actual or implied promise of funding except for the amount specified in the TOR. If any of the funds are returned to the FIRST PARTY or if this contract is rescinded, the SECOND PARTY acknowledges that the FIRST PARTY will have no further obligation to the former as a result of such return or rescission.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FIRST PARTY

The FIRST PARTY shall undertake the following:

a. Facilitate the release of funds to the SECOND PARTY in accordance with the TOR;

b. Review, monitor and certify the progress of the work and the satisfactory completion of outputs by the SECOND PARTY in accordance with the approved program of works, work and financial plans and other specifications;

c. Facilitate the verification and audit of the Second Party's financial reports and records; and,

d. Partner with FUMBWMPC that shall act as Third Party Monitor for the transparent and accountable implementation of projects.

5. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE SECOND PARTY

The SECOND PARTY hereby agrees to undertake the following:

a. Create a Provincial Coordination Committee (PCC) to oversee the implementation and monitoring of the projects;

b. Activate and implement the Project in accordance with the TOR;

c. Prepare the Project Design, Detailed Engineering Design and other documents as required for the Bidding duly approved by authorized executives of the LGU to be reviewed by the FIRST PARTY as to compliance with existing standards and regulations;

d. Ensure the appointment/designation of a qualified project manager/ engineer who will supervise in the implementation of the project for the entire project duration

--- [Page 8] ---
e. Ensure implementation of the project in accordance with the approved Program of Work (POW) and plans, and specifications

f. Open a trust account exclusively for the PAMANA-PDC project and deposit the amount received from the FIRST PARTY in the same trust account and maintain a separate subsidiary record for the funds released by the FIRST PARTY;

g. Observe applicable provisions and stipulations of RA 9184 in the implementation of projects and conform with COA Circular No. 94-013 on the Rules and Regulations in the Grant, Utilization and Liquidation of Funds Transferred to Implementing Agencies;

h. Accept and issue receipt therefore, the funds allocated for the project and ensure the judicious and proper use of funds in a manner that is consistent with the Commission on Audit (COA) Rules and Regulations;

i. Submit monthly progress reports to the FIRST PARTY as specified in the TOR;

j. Submit to the FIRST PARTY within ten (10) days after the end of each month and upon completion of the Project the Report of Checks Issued (RCI) and Report of Disbursement (RD) to report the utilization of funds, and duly signed by the Governor;

k. Put up project billboard(s) indicating the project title and status of fund utilization and conduct of various tests (material testing, etc.) relative to the project implementation and submit copy of results to OPAPP;

l. Secure of the right-of-way for the project whenever applicable

m. Secure Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) and adhere to its provisions and the other social safeguard measures

6. OWNERSHIP

a. The full ownership of the Project during implementation, including reports, studies, documents and written materials used for Project purposes, shall belong to the OPAPP and the Government of the Republic of the Philippines.

b. All identified stakeholders to the Project shall have access to all reports, studies, documents and written materials used for Project purposes.

c. Ownership of the actual output of the Project shall belong to the community and the local government including its maintenance and management, after a documented turnover.

7. OTHER PROVISIONS

a. The SECOND PARTY shall not be held responsible for any loss, change or delay on the completion of the Project due to fortuitous event or whatever cause beyond its control.

--- [Page 9] ---
b. THE SECOND PARTY shall solely bear full responsibilities in respect to life, health, accident, travel or any other coverage for any person under their employment, mandate or which may be necessary or desirable for the purpose of this MOA or for any personnel undertaking activities under this MOA.

c. THE SECOND PARTY shall be solely liable for claims by persons arising from its own acts or omissions in the course of performing this MOA and under no circumstances shall the FIRST PARTY be held liable for such claims.

d. The rights and obligations of the SECOND PARTY are limited to the terms and conditions of this MOA as far as this Project is concerned. Accordingly, the SECOND PARTY and personnel performing services on its behalf shall not be entitled to any benefit, payment, compensation, or entitlement except as expressly provided in the TORs.

8. TERMINATION

This MOA may be terminated by either party by giving Ten (10) days written to the other party and the SECOND PARTY shall promptly return any unutilized funds to the FIRST PARTY not later than the 15th day from receipt of such notice by the other party.

9. AMENDMENTS

No amendments or modification to this agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by duly authorized representatives of both parties.

10. EFFECTIVITY

The Agreement shall take effect immediately upon approval and signing by the contracting parties and shall remain in full force and effect until completion of the requirements detailed in the TOR, unless sooner terminated.

11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

The terms and conditions herein contained as well as those stated in the Annex above-aid constitute the entire agreement between the parties and supersede all previous communications whether oral or written between the parties hereto with respect to the subject matter hereof, and no other previous agreement or understanding varying or extending the same shall be binding upon either party hereto.

12. APPLICABLE LAW AND VENUE

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Philippines. Any action arising in connection with the implementation and/or interpretation of this Agreement shall be brought before the proper judicial and/or quasi-judicial courts of Pasig City.

2. Implementing Party

These Community Infrastructure Projects identified by Peace and Development Communities shall be implemented by the City Government of Davao.

3. Institutional Arrangement

The PARTIES agree to constitute a Provincial Coordination Committee (PCC), composed of representatives from: (I) Provincial Government, (II) OPAAPP, (III) Peace and Development Advocate/League, (IV) Federation of the United Mindanawan Bangsamoro Women Multi-Purpose Cooperatives (FUMBWMPC), and (V) Most Senior Military Officer in the Area. The PCC shall oversee the implementation and monitoring of the projects. Specifically it shall:

a. Coordinate, assist and support the conduct of community assemblies

b. Coordinate provincial programs, activities and funding releases for areas identified under PAMANA-PDC;

c. Mainstream PAMANA-PDC interventions into the plans and programs of concerned local government units;

d. Facilitate planning, review and endorse program and project interventions;

e. Coordinate with concerned agencies, development partners and CSOs, project management offices on the implementation of the PAMANA-PDC in the LGU;

f. Validate the results of the Barangay Assembly or Consultation as well as the priority list of projects submitted by the Barangay.

g. Monitor the implementation of the PAMANA-PDC in the province.

h. Submit monthly progress report on both expenditures as well as works performance.

i. Resolve issues and problems that may arise while the project is being implemented.
