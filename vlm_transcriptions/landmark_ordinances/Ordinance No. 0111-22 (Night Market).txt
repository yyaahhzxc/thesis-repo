Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance Amending City Ordinance No. 089-13, Series of 2013, Establishing and Regulating the Night Market Operations in the City of Davao, shall be called "NIGHT MARKET ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY, AS AMENDED";  

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS - Ordinance No. 089-13 is hereby amended, to read as follows:  

ARTICLE 1  

TITLE  
SECTION 1. This Ordinance establishing and regulating the Night Market in the City of Davao shall be called "NIGHT MARKET ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY, AS AMENDED".  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY  
a. Section 16 of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied there from, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced society, encourage the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."  
b. Section 31, paragraph (d) of the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Any city, municipality, or barangay may, by a duly enacted ordinance, temporarily close and regulate the use of any local street, road, thoroughfare, or any other public place where shopping malls, Sunday, flea or night markets, or shopping areas may be established where goods, merchandise, foodstuffs, commodities, or articles of commerce may be sold and dispensed to the general public."  

ARTICLE 2  

DEFINITION OF TERMS  
SECTION 1. As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall be construed, understood and shall mean as follows:  
a. MARKET - a private or public place, building or structure operated and dedicated to the service of the general public where basic commodities are displayed, offered for sale, and for profit;  
b. NIGHT MARKET - a government-regulated market conducted at specific time and duration of the night, located in a property, portions of designated places or streets

In the City of Davao legally and temporarily closed to vehicular traffic to accommodate the people indulged in the business of selling goods or offering of services to the public for profit, located outside the city public markets;

C. COLLECTOR - is the revenue collection clerk who is properly bonded representing the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) to collect the night market fees or charges and issue the corresponding receipt or cash tickets;

d. CUSTOMER - any person buying the goods displayed or patronizing the services of the awardees in the night markets of the City of Davao.

e. SECURITY OFFICER - is the Civil Security Officer, assigned to maintain peace and order in the market area, in coordination with the Public Safety and Security Office;

f. NIGHT MARKET SUPERVISOR - a personnel assigned or designated by the City Government of Davao to supervise the day-to-day operations of the night market;

g. DESIGNATED NIGHT MARK PLACES - these are officially declared locations which could be lands, portion of roads or streets legally and temporarily closed to vehicular traffic to serve as night markets in the City of Davao. Location can be expanded, relocated or transferred by the local government through a Resolution enacted by the Tangga sa Katipunan Management Team and upon approval of the City Mayor, as the need arises;

h. JURIDICAL PERSON - entities created by law, or by a group of individuals for a certain lawful purpose, susceptible of rights and obligations, or of being subject to juridical relations;

I. IMMEDIATE HEIRS - spouse or children of the regular awardee, provided that he/she is the head of the family;

J. HEAD OF THE FAMILY - pertains to the husband or wife, or an unmarried man or woman with one or both parents, or one or more legitimate, recognized natural, or adopted children dependent upon him or her for their chief support where such brothers, sisters, or children are less than twenty-one years of age;

K. REGULAR AWARDEE - refers to an interested person who has submitted an application and has been selected through raffling, thus, allowed to sell in the night market and allowed to display and sell goods or offer service thereto within the period set forth on the Ordinance, and has paid the corresponding fees also provided in this Ordinance. This term may be used interchangeably with "awardee" and "awardees" and "awardees";

L. RERVE AWARDEES - refers to additional persons selected during raffling, who shall be allowed to sell all space in the night market in the event that the number of regular awardees are insufficient to fill in the allocated number of stalls by reason of withdrawal, incapacity, disqualification or eviction due to revocation of award;

m. PHYSICAL INCAPACITY - refers to the inability of an awardee to occupy the space awarded to him/her by reason of total physical disability and death;

n. INCAPACITY - refers to disqualification other than physical incapacity, and revocation of award due to violation of this Ordinance;

o. READY-TO-EAT FOOD - refers to food which is already prepared prior to display and can be immediately consumed by a customer such as, turo-turo, sandwiches,

ARTICLE 3  

CREATION OF "TIANGGE SA KATAWAN MANAGEMEN TEAM"  

SECTION 1. There is hereby created a "Tiangge Sa Katawan Management Team" to be composed of the representative of the following offices:  
1. City Mayor's Office - as Head;  
2. City Administrator's Office;  
3. City Engineer's Office;  
4. Office of the City Planning and Development Coordinator;  
5. City Legal Office;  
6. Integrated Gender and Development Division;  
7. City Finance Office;  
8. City Environment and Natural Resources Office;  
9. City Economic Enterprise;  
10. Public Safety and Security Office;  
11. City Transport and Traffic Management Office;  
12. City Tourism Operations Office;  
13. SP Committee on Government Enterprises and Privatization;  
14. SP Committee on Trade, Commerce and Industry;  
15. SP Committee on Finance, Ways and Means, and Appropriations;  
16. SP Committee on Tourism and Beautification;  
17. City Health Office;  
18. Davao City Police Office;  
19. Task Force Davao; and  
20. City Social Services and Development Office.  

SECTION 2. The "Tiangge Sa Katawan Management Team" shall oversee and supervise the operations of the Night Market project of the city. It shall have the following duties and responsibilities:  
a. Formulate policies, rules, regulations, and guidelines for the operation and management of the night market;  
b. Conduct periodic review of the night market operations;  
c. Decide on the number of spaces for selling or display in various night markets and designate a number of reserve awardees to be allowed to sell in the night market in case of incapacity of the regular awardees;  
d. Oversee the drawing of lots and raffling of spaces allowed for selling and display and those that shall be subsequently declared vacant in the night market;  
e. Certify the results of the raffling;

f. Formulate such measures of actions as may be necessary in the resolution of problems, in connection with the displaying or selling in the Night Market;  
g. Decide on the revocation or cancellation of applications, eviction of awardees from the space he/she is allowed to sell on the ground/s of violation of specific provisions of this Ordinance and other relevant existing Ordinances;  
h. Designate other areas to be utilized for night market operations, as the need arises, subject to the approval of the City Mayor; and,

SECTION 3. The "Tiangge Sa Katawhan Management Team" shall convene and hold a regular meeting at least once a month, or as the need arises upon the call of its Head.  

SECTION 4. The City Economic Enterprise shall serve as the Secretariat of the Tiangge sa Katawhan Management Team.  

ARTICLE 4  
LOCATIONS OF THE NIGHT MARKET  

SECTION 1. The following roads shall be designated as Night Market areas in the City of Davao, to wit:  
a. From corner C.M., Recto/M., Roxas Avenue Road (right side) going to Quezon Boulevard, except its intersections;  
b. Road 1, Torill Public Market (half lane only)- right side coming from Makati St. to corner General Lao Street; and,  
c. All other areas within the City of Davao, as may be designated by the Tiangge sa Katawhan Management Team and as approved by the City Mayor.  

ARTICLE 5  
CLOSURE OF ROADS  

SECTION 1. In furtherance to the declared policy of the city, as embodied under Section 2, Article 1 of this Ordinance, the abovementioned roads shall be closed to vehicular traffic for night market operations, from 5:00 PM to 2:00 AM of the following day, from Mondays to Sundays. However, the abovementioned roads shall be closed to vehicular traffic starting at 4:00 PM in order to give way for the CTTMO and PSSO to commence the preparations for the night market.

**ARTICLE 6**

**INSTALLATION OF STRUCTURES, DISPLAY OF GOODS AND ITS REMOVAL**

**SECTION 1.** Awardees shall be allowed to sell and install their structures only in their designated area.

**SECTION 2.** The City Government, through the "Tiangge Sa Katawhan Management Team," shall provide the design of the collapsible structures and shall regulate the same to observe safety, orderliness, and aesthetics.

**SECTION 3.** All structures, materials, and goods displayed must be removed and cleared completely on or before closing time.

**SECTION 4.** No structures and goods or any part thereof shall be displayed before the designated time nor shall they be left within the allowed market site and/or along any streets, road-right-of-ways, and any government facilities or sites prior to the commencement of operations of the night market.

**SECTION 5.** The awardees are solely responsible for the safekeeping, maintenance, and installation of their stall, tents, or fixtures.

**ARTICLE 7**

**DESIGNATED TIME AND DURATION OF THE NIGHT MARKET**

**SECTION 1.** Awardees shall be allowed to open their tents and display their goods, etc., starting at 5:00 o'clock in the evening from Mondays to Sundays".

However, the Tiangge sa Katawhan Management Team may, through a Resolution enacted for the purpose, designate night markets to operate only for certain days and time of the week.

**SECTION 2.** Closing time shall not be later than 2:00 o'clock the following morning.

**ARTICLE 8**

**AVAILMENT OF SPACE IN THE NIGHT MARKET**

**SECTION 1.** The application to avail a designated space in the Night Market must be submitted to the "Tiangge Sa Katawhan Management Team," through the City Economic Enterprise, for evaluation.

**SECTION 2.** Only one (1) application can be submitted per head of the family. The applicant must decide which Section he/she intends to apply for.

The City Economic Enterprise is authorized to refuse acceptance of any application of individuals found to have applied for two (2) or more sections, or found to belong to the same immediate family.

**SECTION 3.** The following documents shall be submitted (original or certified true copy) by the applicant:

1. Duly-accomplished Application Form;
2. Marriage Contract, or if not married but head of the family, Birth Certificate of child/children or Notarized Affidavit alleging that he/she is the head of the family;
3. Voter's Certification/COMELEC ID/National ID;
4. Barangay Clearance;
5. Police Clearance;
6. For massage, NC2 Certification or Training Certificate duly issued by accredited Training Centers;
7. Health Certificate (to be complied if applicant is selected during raffling).

SECTION 4. Open raffling of applicants shall be conducted by the "Tiangge Sa Katawhan Management Team," for the selection of vendors who may sell in the Night Market areas. The result of the raffling shall be certified by the Tiangge sa Katawhan Management Team and submitted to the Office of the City Mayor for information.

SECTION 5. The selected awardees of the spaces at the Night Market shall be allowed to sell in the same area for a duration of three (3) months.

The said awardees shall be disqualified to sell in the same area of the night market for the next succeeding quarters within the year.

SECTION 6. All vendors selected during raffling must personally attend the Orientation conducted by the different agencies of the City Government of Davao on pertinent subjects, including but not limited to, Sanitation, Peace and Order, Proper Waste Disposal, Identification of Improvised Explosive Devices (IED), Identification of suspicious looking individuals and hotline numbers in case of emergencies, such as fires, earthquakes, looting, etc. Failure to attend the Orientation shall be ground for revocation of the award, subject to the procedures as provided in this Ordinance.

SECTION 7. The awardees shall only be allowed to sell in the allotted space upon payment of the Night Market Vending Fee in an amount equal to one (1) month, computed in accordance with the rate provided in this Ordinance. Succeeding monthly payments of the said fee shall be made on or before the fifth (5th) day of the following month.

SECTION 8. The vendors must commence doing business in the designated area on the day determined by the Tiangge sa Katawhan Management Team for him to sell in the space.

Failure to sell for three (3) consecutive days without justifiable cause shall be a ground for the immediate revocation of award.

The selected awardee who fails to sell without justifiable cause, as determined by the Tiangge sa Katawhan Management Team, shall be block-listed to participate in the raffling for all night markets and markets duly operated by the City Government of Davao for a period of one (1) year from the date of expiration of their award.

SECTION 9. In case of withdrawal, disqualification, and incapacity of the current awardee, the reserve awardee selected, based on order of priority during raffling, shall be allowed to sell in the subject space within the remaining period allocated for the quarter.

**ARTICLE 9  
QUALIFICATIONS OF AWARDEES IN THE NIGHT MARKET**

**SECTION 1.** Must be a natural person, of legal age (18 years old), a resident of Davao City, and the head of a family.

**SECTION 2.** Must have submitted all documents as may be required under Article 8, Section 3 of this Ordinance.

**SECTION 3.** Must not have an existing business registered within the City of Davao.

**SECTION 4.** Must not have any arrears due to the City Government of Davao.

**ARTICLE 10  
SANITATION**

**SECTION 1.** Awardees shall, at all times, be responsible for the sanitation of their premises and their immediate surroundings.

**SECTION 2.** Awardees must place their solid waste in disposable containers, properly kept at the side of the gutter of the road, its top opening tied or sealed to avoid scattering or spillage for convenient collection by garbage trucks.

**SECTION 3.** Awardees must maintain dryness of the area to avoid being filthy looking.

**SECTION 4.** Liquid waste must be placed in a container and disposed in the drainage canal.

**SECTION 5.** No solid waste shall be discharged to the drainage canals.

**SECTION 6.** Awardees must, at any time, grant access to City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) for the conduct of regular inspection of waste disposal in the areas designated as Night Market.

**SECTION 7.** Awardees must, at any time, grant access to the City Health Office (CHO) during the conduct of random inspection and testing of food for food vendors to ensure food safety and compliance to the provisions of Ordinance No. 078, otherwise known as the "Environmental Health and Sanitation Ordinance of Davao City".

**SECTION 8.** Violation of any sanitary laws and other related Ordinances, in connection with their selling as awardees, shall be a ground for revocation of award under this Ordinance.

**ARTICLE 11  
GOODS AND SERVICES NOT ALLOWED IN THE NIGHT MARKET**

**SECTION 1.** Livestock, heavy and bulky hardware, lumber, pungent and odorous goods.

**SECTION 2.** Services that emit loud noise and require heavy electrical load rated more than 500 watts.

**SECTION 3.** Alcoholic or intoxicating beverages.

**ARTICLE 12**

**PEACE AND ORDER**

**SECTION 1.** The City Government, through the Public Safety and Security Office-Civil Security Unit (PSSC-CSI), shall assign a Civil Security Officer to the areas designated as Night Market to maintain peace and order. Assistance from the Philippine National Police shall be called whenever necessary and a hotline number will be provided for emergency.

**SECTION 2.** In case of emergency or fire/conflict near the vicinity of the Night Market area, awardees must immediately evacuate their structures to clear the road for the passage of the fire fighting units and firemen. It shall be the duty of the Night Market Supervisors to inform the awardees of such precautions.

**SECTION 3.** The awardees must be vigilant and must observe peace and orderliness at all times.

**SECTION 4.** Illegal and suspicious-looking abandoned goods shall be confiscated in favor of the City Government for proper disposal and will not be returned to the owner.

**ARTICLE 13**

**FEES AND CHARGES**

**SECTION 1.** A Night Market Vending Fee for the privilege of selling in the designated Night Markets shall be collected by the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) at the rate of P10.00/sqm/day.

Further, an additional fee amounting to Php 10.00/day per awardee shall also be collected for the maintenance of the night market.

**SECTION 2.** The vendors shall only be allowed to sell in the night market upon payment of the aforementioned fees in an amount equal to one (1) month computed in accordance with the existing rate, as provided under the immediately preceding Section. Succeeding monthly payments for such imposition shall be made on or before the fifth (5th) day of the following month.

**SECTION 3.** The City Economic Enterprise and City Treasurer's Office shall ensure that all vendors in the Night Market must pay their tax obligation within the allowable period and in accordance with the prescribed manner as provided for in the Ordinance.

**SECTION 4.** Non-payment of the abovementioned fees within the period required shall be a ground for the revocation of award of the delinquent awardees and his/her subsequent eviction from his/her designated space in the Night Market.

**ARTICLE 14**

LIMIT OF AREA TO BE OCCUPIED

SECTION 1. The space available for each vendor shall be 3x3 meters for Food Section and 2x3 meters for Accessories and RTW Sections.

However, the Tiange sa Katawhan Management Team, upon recommendation of the City Engineer's Office, and through a Resolution enacted for the purpose, may amend the dimension/measurement of the area.

**ARTICLE 15**

ELECTRICAL AND LIGHTING FACILITIES

SECTION 1. No utility services or amenities shall be allowed to be installed by the vendors/occupants at their space in the Night Market without the written approval of the Tiange sa Katawhan Management Team.

SECTION 2. Use of generator sets as power supply is prohibited. Only rechargeable lights may be used.

**ARTICLE 18  
LIMITATIONS**

**SECTION 1.** Allowing another individual to sell in his/her designated area in the Night Market is strictly prohibited, except when an awardee dies or becomes physically incapacitated.

In the event that an awardee dies, or becomes physically incapacitated, his/her immediate heir may continue the privilege to sell, upon approval of the Tjiangsa Katawhan Management Team, provided he/she has submitted sufficient documentary proof of such incapacity, such as the following:

a. In case of death, Death Certificate of the awardee and Notarized Extradjudicial Settlement of Estate with Waiver of Rights in favor of the new heir-transferee in case of multiple heirs or Affidavit of Self-Adjudication in case of a sole heir; and,

b. In case of physical incapacity, medical certificate indicating that the awardee is physically incapacitated to sell at the night market.

**ARTICLE 19  
PENAL CLAUSE**

**SECTION 1.** Violations of this Ordinance shall be subject to the following administrative penalties, as follows:

a. First Offense: Warning;

b. Second Offense: Suspension for 1 week with payment of rental fee;

c. Third Offense: Revocation of Award

**ARTICLE 20  
SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**

**SECTION 1.** If for any reason, or reasons, any part or provision of this Ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**ARTICLE 21  
EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE**

**SECTION 1.** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
