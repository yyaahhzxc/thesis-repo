**Series of 2004**

**AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL BUSINESS**

**ESTABLISHMENTS WITH FIXED PLACES OF BUSINESS**

**OPERATIONS OR ADDRESSES TO REGISTER AND SECURE**

**A BUSINESS REGISTRATION AND IDENTIFICATION**

**PLATES AND YEARLY VALIDATION STICKERS**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION I. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known and referred to as,
"The BUSINESS REGISTRATION AND IDENTIFICATION PLATE AND
VALIDATION STICKER ORDINANCE OF 2004";

SECTION 2. SCOPE. Owners or operators of any business establishment with
fixed place of business operation or address, are hereby required to register and secure
a Business Registration and Identification Plate from the Business Bureau, Office of
the City Mayor.

SECTION 3. ISSUANCE OF THE BUSINESS PERMIT REGISTRATION
PLATE. A Business Permit is issued and the corresponding taxes and fees are paid, all operators or owners of any business establishments with fixed place of business operation or address shall be issued a corresponding Business Registration and
Identification Plate by the Business Bureau upon payment of the cost of the plate in the amount of One Hundred Fifty Pesos (P 150.00) at the City Treasurer's Office.

SECTION 4. PERIOD. The Business Registration and Identification Plate may
be used for a period of five (5) years from issuance thereof, replaceable every five (5)
years thereafter. However, the same shall be validated yearly with a printed sticker
which shall be clearly indicated and attached to the plate, likewise, to be issued by the Business Bureau, after payment of the cost of the sticker in the amount of Five Pesos (P5.00), at the City Treasurer's Office.

SECTION 5. FORM OF THE PLATE AND STICKER. The Business
Registration and Identification Plate and the corresponding Validation Sticker shall be in
the material and form prescribed by the Business Bureau, subject to the approval of the
City Mayor.

SECTION 6. POSTING OR DISPLAY AT PUBLIC VIEW. It shall be a
mandatory requirement that Business Registration and Identification Plates issued to all
owners or operators of business establishments with fixed business operation or address
shall at all times be posted or displayed for public view.

SECTION 7. SURRENDER OF BUSINESS REGISTRATION AND
IDENTIFICATION PLATE. When a person desires to retire his business or in case a
business establishment is closed, the person surrendering the same shall surrender the
Business Registration and Identification Plate and Validation Stickers to the Business
Bureau.

SECTION 8. PENAL CLAUSE. Any person or persons, natural or juridical
who shall violate any of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be penalized with
Administrative Fine which shall be imposed in accordance with the rules and regulations
implementing herein provided of this Ordinance, to be collected by the City Treasurer, as follows:

Provided, however, that should the violation be committed by any corporation, partnership or association, the General Manager or Resident Manager thereof shall become responsible and shall be charged with such violation and penalized accordingly.

SECTION 9. SEPARABILITy Clause. Any judicial declaration that a part of this Ordinance is unconstitutional or unlawful, shall not affect all the others which shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect on April 01, 2004, unless otherwise provided herein, after its complete publication in at least one (1) local newspaper of general circulation.
