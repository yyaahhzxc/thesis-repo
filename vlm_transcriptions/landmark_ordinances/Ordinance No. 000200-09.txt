ORDINANCE NO. 0200-09
Series of 2009

AN ORDINANCE DECLARING BARANGAY
MINTAL, TUGBOK DISTRICT, DAVAO CITY,
AS A JAPANESE HERITAGE SITE IN
RECOGNITION OF THE CULTURAL AND
HISTORICAL RICHES AND ATTAIN THE
PROPEL OF JAPAN AND DAVAO CITY

Be it Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

--- [Page 3] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0200-09

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "Mintal Japanese Heritage Site Ordinance".

SECTION 2. PURPOSE. - This Ordinance recognizes the cultural and historical bonds between the people of Japan and Davao City, specifically Barangay Mintal, which has been the primary residence of Japanese citizens during the World War II;

SECTION 3. GENERAL WELFARE. - Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and develop the development of the economy, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

SECTION 4. DECLARATION. - Barangay Mintal, Davao City, shall hereinafter be known as the Japanese Cultural Heritage Site of Davao City and structures depicting the same shall be endeavored to be erected in the site, especially in areas within the Barangay where Japanese edifices, artifacts or ruins are located;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - The provisions of this Ordinance are separable, and in the event that any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby;

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE. - Any Ordinance, Executive Orders and local issuances or Rules and Regulations or part thereof inconsistent with or in conflict with this Ordinance are hereby repealed and/or altered accordingly;

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, April 21, 2009, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod

(Note: Councillor Conrad C. Baluran was out when this ordinance was approved.)
