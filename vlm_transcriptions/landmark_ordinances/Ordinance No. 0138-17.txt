Ord. No. 0138-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. - TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0195-04, SERIES OF 2004, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "AMENDED REROUTING SCHEME OF DAVAO CITY", FOR THE ROUTE ADJUSTMENT OF ROUTE 1 TURNING POINTS ALONG CIRCUMFERNENTIAL ROAD AND LOPEZ JAENA STREET";

SECTION 2. - DECLARATION OF POLICY. - City Ordinance No. 0334-12, Series of 2012, "An Ordinance for the Comprehensive Transport and Traffic Code of Davao City", Section 152, second paragraph provides to wit:
SECTION 152. ROUTES OF PUBLIC UTILITY BUSES AND JEEPNEYS.
Without necessarily modifying their basic authorized routes, the City Transport and Traffic Management Office may adjust the turning points and terminal of public utility buses and jeepneys, prescribe their loading and unloading points, and/or require them to utilize passengers interchange terminals, if so required by an approved traffic improvement scheme.
