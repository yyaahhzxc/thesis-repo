Series of 2010

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO GRANT PRODUCTIVITY ENHANCEMENT INCENTIVE (PEI) TO ALL PERSONNEL OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DABAO AUTHORIZED UNDER ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER [AO] NO. 3 OF THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT DATED NOVEMBER 25, 2010, AND SHALL BE IMPLEMENTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE DEPARTMENTAL BUDGETARY MANAGEMENT PLAN (DBMP), CHARGEBLE AGAINST THE AVAILABLE SAVINGS FROM THE CURRENT OPERATING EXPENDITURE (COE) OF ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND OFFICES FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2010

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO GRANT PRODUCTIVITY ENHANCEMENT INCENTIVE (PEI) TO ALL PERSONNEL OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AUTHORIZED UNDER ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER (A.O.) NO. 3 OF THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT DATED NOVEMBER 25, 2010, AND SHALL BE IMPLEMENTED IN ACCORDANCE WITH BUDGET CIRCULAR NO. 2010-3, CY 2010 OF THE DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT (DBM), CHARGEABLE AGAINST THE AVAILABLE SAVINGS FROM THE CURRENT OPERATING EXPENDITURE (COE) OF ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND OFFICES FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2010."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use.

SECTION 3. LEGAL BASIS. The Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) for all employees/workers of the City Government of Davao shall be granted by virtue of Administrative Order No. 3 dated November 25, 2010, issued by President Benigno Simeon C. Aquino III, to be implemented consistent with the provisions of Budget Circular No. 2010-3 of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM).

SECTION 4. SOURCE OF FUND. The granting of Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) to all employees/workers of the City Government of Davao shall be sourced out of the savings from the Current Operating Expenditure (COE) of all city departments and offices for Calendar Year 2010.

SECTION 5. IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PRODUCTIVITY ENHANCEMENT INCENTIVE (PEI). The Productivity Enhancement Incentives (PEI) shall be granted to all personnel of the City Government of Davao in the amount authorized by the President of the Republic of the Philippines under Administrative Order No. 3 and shall be implemented in accordance with Budget Circular No. 2010-3, CY 2010 of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM).

SECTION 6. LIMITATION. The amount provided for the purpose shall be exclusively used for the granting of Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) to all employees/workers of the City Government of Davao, the release of which must conform with the existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations.
