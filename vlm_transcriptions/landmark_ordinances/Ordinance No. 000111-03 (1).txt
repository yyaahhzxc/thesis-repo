**An Ordinance Revising Article 12**

---

**Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:**

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0111-03

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REVISING ARTICLE 12 (PROFESSIONAL TAX) OF ORDINANCE NO. 230, SERIES OF 1991, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE TAX CODE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 5 of Article 12 of Ordinance No. 230, Series of 1991, as amended, is hereby further amended by incorporating therein paragraph (b) which shall read as follows:

xxx
Article 12, PROFESSIONAL TAX
Section 5.
xxx

b) "The City Treasurer or his duly authorized representative shall require from professionals their unexpired professional license card or professional identification card issued by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) or a certification from PRC that the Professional ID/License card is still in proper use before accepting payment for their professional tax for the current year."

xxx

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If any provision of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to any person or circumstances is declared invalid, the remainder of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected by such declaration.

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance or executive order issued by the City Mayor, or any provision therein, inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance, shall be modified or repealed accordingly.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect after ten (10) days of posting in the official board of the City Hall of Davao, the Sangguniang Panlungsod Building of Davao City and the Hall of Justice of Davao City as well as the completion of a publication of its gist or salient feature in a newspaper of general circulation within the City.

ENACTED, July 01, 2003 by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
