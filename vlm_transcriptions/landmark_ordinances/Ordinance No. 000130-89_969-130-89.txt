**RESOLUTION NO. 262**

The proposed ordinance authored by Councilor Benzate S. Bengay prohibiting the posting of any kind of propaganda materials in City streets and any public places in the City of Davao was presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body.

Councilor Victorico S. Advincia explained that it is the responsibility of the City Government to promote the general welfare for betterment of the community. He informed the Body that he had submitted that it is the constitutional right of some sectors of the citizenry to advertise and/or voice their rights. He also emphasized that it is the duty of every member of the state to regulate such constitutional rights to meet the aim of promoting such general welfare clause.

After careful deliberations, an motion of Councilor Gerardo C. Negreres, Senorfrano R. Alterado, Antonio S. Castille and Benzate S. Bengay, continued on page 2.

RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREIN RESOLVED, To Approve an Ordinance,
entitled:

ORDINANCE No. 130

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE PASTING OF ANY KIND OF PROPAGANDA
MATERIALS IN CITY STREETS AND IN ANY PUBLIC PLACE IN THE CITY OF
DAVAO.

Be it ordained by the Sangunang Panglungsod, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
PROHIBITING THE PASTING OF ANY KIND OF PROPAGANDA MATERIALS IN CITY
STREETS AND ANY PUBLIC PLACE IN THE CITY OF DAVAO";

SECTION 2.- The provisions of this Ordinance shall be applicable
within the entire territory and jurisdiction of the City of Davao;

SECTION 3.- It shall be unlawful for any person to paste and/
or allow to be pasted in City Streets and any public places any kind
of propaganda materials for the purpose of advertisement and/or
 airing grievances;

SECTION 4.- That any person found violating this Ordinance
shall be fined an amount of P100.00 for the first offense; P150.00
for the second offense; and P200.00 for the succeeding offense and/or
an infringement of six (6) months or both at the discretion of the
Court;

SECTION 5.- Any existing Ordinance or parts thereof which
are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby
 repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 6.- This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
