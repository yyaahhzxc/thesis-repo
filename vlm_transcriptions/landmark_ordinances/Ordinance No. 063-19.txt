Ord. No. 063-19
Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REPEALING SECTION 5 OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 065-90, SERIES OF 1990, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE SELLING OF AIRLINE, SHIPPING AND BUS TICKETS OUTSIDE THE TICKET OFFICES OR OFFICIAL BOOTH OR PLACES DESIGNATED FOR THE PURPOSE AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF".
SECTION 2. REPEALING CLAUSE - City Ordinance No. 065-90, Series of 1990, entitled "AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE SELLING OF AIRLINE, SHIPPING AND BUS TICKETS OUTSIDE THE TICKET OFFICES OR OFFICIAL BOOTH OR PLACES DESIGNATED FOR THE PURPOSE AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF," is hereby repealed.
SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.
ENACTED, on the 8th day of October 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
