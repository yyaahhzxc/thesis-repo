**SECTION 3. GUIDELINES**  
The guidelines for hereditary succession as laid down in Section 2 (C) paragraphs (I), (II), (III), (IV), (V), and (VII) of Omnibus Legal Opinion No. 586, Series of 2023, are as follows:

**Request for transfer of title of property through hereditary succession, where:**

I. The title has an annotation which reads "except by hereditary succession, the lot herein sold, conveyed or any part thereof, cannot be alienated, transferred or encumbered within five (5) years from the date of issuance of title without prior written consent and authority from the seller."

> The case of hereditary succession falls under the exception in the annotation. Thus, it may be allowed. The heirs are only required to comply with the minimum requirements set by law. The consent of the City Government is not required anymore.

II. The title has no annotation as to the period of prohibition:
> The property can only be transferred to the heirs of the awardee, subject to compliance of the requirements.

III. There is extra-judicial partition:
> Allowed. In fact, it is a prerequisite to effect transfer of property to the heirs of the awardee.

IV. There is waiver in favor of one or some of the heirs:
> Allowed. Extra-judicial with waiver must be executed by the heirs of the awardee to effect transfer of property to the transferee-heirs.

V. There is sale to another heir:
> Allowed. Extra-judicial with sale must be executed by the heirs of the awardee to effect transfer of property to the buyer-heir.

VI. There is donation to an heir. (Only direct heirs through Succession):
> Allowed. Extra-judicial with donation must be executed by the heirs of the awardee to effect transfer of property to the donee-heir.

**SECTION 4. GRANT OF AUTHORITY**  
This Ordinance allows the Davao City Housing Office (DCHO) to issue clearance, certifications for individuals acquiring social housing rights via hereditary succession.

SECTION 5. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the grant of legislative authority to the Davao City Housing Office (DCHO), formerly known as Office of the City Planning and Development Coordinator - Housing and Homeste Development Division (CPOH-HDH). The Ordinance shall be applicable to all properties subject to hereditary succession, eliminating the need to secure an SP Resolution as a requirement before the Registry of Deeds to effect the transfer of title pursuant to Omnibus Legal Opinion No. 586, Series of 2023 - Guidelines on Transactions Involving Transfer of Title of Property, Cancellation of Encumbrance and Grant of Legislative Authority (Socialized Housing).

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, all sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, April 16, 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
