ORDINANCE NO. 0287-12  
Series of 2012  

AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE FILM DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL OF THE PHILIPPINES (FDCP) AND THE DAVAO CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY, INC. (DCCCIII) RELATIVE TO THE DAVAO CITY INTERNATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL ON JUNE 25 TO 29, 2012

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of
Ord. No. 0287-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE FILM DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL OF THE PHILIPPINES (FDCP) AND THE DAVAO CITY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY, INC. (OCCII) RELATIVE TO THE DAVAO CITY INTERNATIONAL FILM FESTIVAL ON JUNE 25 TO 29, 2012."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:
(vi) Represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT. The Memorandum of Agreement refers to the instrument to be entered into by and among the City Government of Davao, the Film Development Council of the Philippines (FDCP), and the Davao City Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Inc. (OCCII) relative to the Davao City International Film Festival on June 25 to 29, 2012.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement, to be entered into by and among the City Government of Davao, the Film Development Council of the Philippines (FDCP), and the Davao City Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Inc. (OCCII) relative to the Davao City International Film Festival on June 25 to 29, 2012.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, May 08, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod present.

CERTIFIED CORRECT: 
CHARLITO M. SANTOS
Acting Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Asst. Department Head II)
