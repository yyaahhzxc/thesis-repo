AN ORDINANCE REPEALING ORDINANCE NO. 0307-20, SERIES OF 2020, AS AMENDED, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING THE MANDATORY WEARING OF FACE MASKS AT ALL TIMES IN PUBLIC PLACES DURING THE COMMUNITY QUARANTINE AND/OR AFTER THE STATE OF PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY IS LIFTED"

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0316-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REPEALING ORDINANCE NO. 0307-20, SERIES OF 2020, AS AMENDED, ENTITLED AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING THE MANDATORY WEARING OF FACE MASKS AT ALL TIMES IN PUBLIC PLACES DURING THE COMMUNITY QUARANTINE AND/OR AFTER THE STATE OF PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY IS LIFTED".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 25 (a) of the Local Government Code of 1991 or RA 7160, provides:

SECTION 25. NATIONAL SUPERVISION OVER LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS - (a) Consistent with the basic policy on local autonomy, the President shall exercise general supervision over local government units to ensure that their acts are within the scope of their prescribed powers and duties. The President shall exercise supervisory authority directly over provinces, highly urbanized cities, and independent component districts through the province and district to component cities and municipalities; and through the city and municipality with respect to barangays.

SECTION 3. REPEAL OF ORDINANCE No. 0307-20, SERIES OF 2020 - The said Ordinance still mandates the wearing of face masks in public transport by land, air, or sea, and provides for the penalty for violations during the state of public health emergency relative to the COVID-19 pandemic is hereby repealed in order to harmonize with Presidential Proclamation No. 297, Series of 2023, mandating the lifting of all prior orders, memoranda and issuances that are effective only during the state of Public Health Emergency to be deemed withdrawn, revoked or cancelled and shall no longer be effective.

SECTION 4. EFFECT OF THE REPEAL OF ORDINANCE No. 0307-20, SERIES OF 2020 - The authority to issue citation tickets and the subsequent collection of payment of penalty in relation to the implementation of Ordinance No. 0307-20, Series of 2020, is hereby revoked.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall come into effect immediately upon approval.
