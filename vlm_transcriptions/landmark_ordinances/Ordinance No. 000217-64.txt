**Republic of the Philippines**
**CITY OF DAVAO**
**Office of the City Council**

**ORDINANCE NO. 217**

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR PENALTIES AGAINST PERSONS FOR SNEAKING INTO ANY MOVIE HOUSE OR THEATER IN THE CITY OF DAVAO WHILE SEEING THE MOVIE.

Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

SEC. 1. It shall be unlawful for any person to sneak into any movie house and/or theater in the City of Davao while seeing the show.

SEC. 2. Any person violating the provision of this ordinance shall be punished by a fine of not exceeding Fifty Pesos ($50.00) or by imprisonment of not more than one (1) month or both, such fine and imprisonment at the discretion of the Court.

SEC. 3. Any provision of ordinance or ordinances inconsistent herewith is hereby repealed.

SEC. 4. This Ordinance shall take effect on or after the 10th day following its passage.

ENACTED: July 8, 1964.

**Signed:**  
**MILTON R. LUNETA**  
Vice-Mayor

**ATTENDED BY:**  
**GERONIMO G. LATUZILLA**  
City Council

**APPROVED:**  
July 1964
