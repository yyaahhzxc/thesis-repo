ORDINANCE NO. 0105-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING ANNUAL FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO ALL QUALIFIED SENIOR CITIZENS OF DAVAO CITY

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

**Ord. No. 0105-19**

**SECTION 1. TITLE**  
This Ordinance shall be known as "THE ANNUAL FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO ALL QUALIFIED SENIOR CITIZENS ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

**SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICIES**  
Article XV, Section 4 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution declares that it is the duty of the family to take care of its elderly members while the State may design programs of social security for them. In addition to this, Section 10 in the Declaration of Principles and State Policy provides that the State shall promote social justice in all phases of national development. Further, Article XIII of the Constitution provides that the State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to social development which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health and social services available to all people at affordable cost. There shall be priority for the needs of the elderly.

**SECTION 3. PURPOSE**  
This Ordinance aims to support the senior citizens in the cost of their maintenance for medicines and other expenses relative to their health.

**SECTION 4. AMOUNT OF FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE**  
Qualified Senior Citizens shall be entitled to a financial assistance in the minimum amount of One Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (PHP 1,500.00) annually. This provision shall be subject to review every two (2) years by the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

**SECTION 5. QUALIFICATIONS**  
The applicant must possess all the following qualifications, to wit:

a) Must be sixty five (65) years old within the calendar year starting 2020;

b) Must be a holder of an original Senior Citizen's Identification Card issued by the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) of Davao City;

c) Must be a registered voter of Davao City as evidenced by the list of registered voters issued by the Commission on Elections (COMELEC);

d) Must be a member of a duly recognized Senior Citizens Organization recognized by OSCA and CSSDO;

e) In case of permanent bedridden senior citizen due to illness who is not a registered voter, he/she must present a certification from CSSDO assigned in the barangay as to his/her condition.

**SECTION 6. GRIEVANCE COMMITTEE**  
1. Composition - The Grievance Committee shall be composed of the following:

a) OSCA Head or an authorized representative;

b) CSSDO Head or an authorized representative;

c) Davao City DilG Director or an authorized representative;

d) Chairperson, Committee on Social Services of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or any member in case of his absence;

2. Functions - The Grievance Committee will settle any qualification issued duly referred to it through a written complaint within a period of three (3) working days from the receipt thereof.

Section 7. ENFORCEMENT: The Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) of Davao City, the City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO), and the City DILG shall enforce this Ordinance.

The CSSDO shall take charge in the processing of the application and shall prepare the payroll of the qualified applicants.

Section 8. FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE DISTRIBUTION: The annual financial assistance to all qualified senior citizens of Davao City shall be given every second week of November starting from the year 2020. The financial assistance shall be distributed by CSSDO barangays on a date that will be announced by OSCA in coordination with the City Treasurer's Office, the City Social Services and Development Office, and the City Accountant's Office.

The financial assistance shall be claimed personally by the qualified recipient except in case of bedridden senior citizens who may claim through a relative within the second degree of consanguinity or affinity.

Section 9. BUDGETARY ALLOCATION: The budgetary requirements for the annual financial assistance to senior citizens shall be included in the annual budget of the City subject to the usual auditing and accounting rules and procedures.

Section 10. FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE FROM THE BARANGAY: The Barangay may likewise grant the same benefits to its Senior Citizens with funds to be taken from its Real Property Taxes (RPT) subject to the availability of funds.

Section 11. REPEALING CAUSE: All ordinances, resolutions, rules, and regulations whose provisions are inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

Section 12. APPLICABILITY CLAUSE: The provisions of existing ordinances, resolutions or executive orders which are not contradictory with this Ordinance are hereby adopted as an integral part of this Ordinance.

Section 13. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE: If for any reason, any section or provision hereof is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected shall continue to be full time and effect.

Section 14. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.

ENACTED, November 19, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (Local Legislative Staff Officer IV).
