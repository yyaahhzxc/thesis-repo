March 20, 2018
Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the attached Ordinance No. 0407-18, Series of 2018 entitled "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE SUPPRESSION OF LUNAR STREET EXTENSION (ABANDONED ROAD) LOCATED IN GSIS SUBDIVISION, MATINA, THIS CITY," informing you that this office finds the enactment of the ordinance free from any legal infirmity, citing Section 21 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, to quote:
"SECTION 21. Closure and Opening of Roads. -(a) A local government unit may, pursuant to an ordinance, permanently or temporarily close or open any local road, alley, park, or square facility for public use. Where the closure is due to causes of permanent closure, such ordinance must be approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of all the members of the sanggunian, and when necessary, an adequate substitute for the public facility that is subject to closure is provided."
VIEWED from the foregoing, it is recommended that the Ordinance be approved.

Ord. No. 0407-18  

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE SUPPRESSION OF LUNAR STREET EXTENSION (ABANDONED ROAD) LOCATED IN GSIIS SUBDIVISION, MATINA, THIS CITY."  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare (Section 16, Republic Act 7160);  

SECTION 3. SCOPE - The suppression of Lunar Street Extension shall be for the purpose of construction and/or repair of government buildings/facilities;  

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;  

ENACTED, on February 27, 2018, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
