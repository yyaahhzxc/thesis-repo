**Ordinance No. 0304-12 Series of 2012**

**An Ordinance Authorizing the City Mayor to Sign, For and in Behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Amended Memorandum of Agreement Creating the PhilHealth Capitation Fund**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT CREATING THE PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND".

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
PHILIPPINE HEALTH INSURANCE CORPORATION (PhilHealth)
and
THE CITY OF DAVAO

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement made and entered into by and among:
The PHILIPPINE HEALTH INSURANCE CORPORATION, a government corporation organized and existing by virtue of Republic Act 7875, as amended by Republic Act 9241, otherwise known as the National Health Insurance Act of 1995, duly represented herein by its Regional Vice President, Dennis B. Adre and hereinafter referred to as PhilHealth or the Corporation;
and
The CITY OF DAVAO a political entity organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, represented herein by its Honorable Mayor Sara Z. Duterte-Carpio and hereinafter referred to as the CITY.

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the Philippine Constitution mandates the State to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development, which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health and social services available to all citizens at affordable cost;
WHEREAS, the National Health Insurance Act of 1995, in recognition of the need to provide all citizens with the mechanism to gain financial access to health services, created the National Health Insurance Program (NHIIP) to serve as the means to help the people pay for the same;
WHEREAS, consistent with the vision and objectives of the State, as manifested in NHIIP's mandate and in local autonomy statutes, the parties herin acknowledge their joint responsibility to provide a basic package of needed personal health services for the social and economic well-being of the people of Davao City, specifically the marginalized and underprivileged through the implementation of the Sponsored (Indigent) Program;
WHEREAS, in order to set into motion an efficient health care financing and delivery program anchored on local government autonomy, the contracting parties hereby acknowledge the need to adopt separate mechanisms for the implementation of NHIIP's Regular In-Fill/In-Farmer and Outpatient Package (Phase I), and the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package (Phase II); and
WHEREAS, the parties hereto agree and declare their commitment to take the necessary steps to implement the principles and objectives provided for in this Agreement.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto have agreed, as they hereby agree and bind themselves, as follows:

Article I
DEFINITION OF TERMS

SECTION 1. For the purpose of this Agreement, the terms set below shall have their meanings indicated, to wit:

1.1 National Health Insurance Program (NHIIP) - refers to the compulsory health insurance program of the government instituted under Republic Act No. 7875 which aims to implement a universal health insurance system and provide universal access to affordable, acceptable,

--- [Page 5] ---
1.2 Sponsored (Indigent) Program - refers to the Indigent Sector Component of NHIP. It is the tool by which the State envisions to provide Medicare privileges to the indigent population as a supplement to its Medicare Program for the employed and individually paying sectors.
1.3 Health Center and/or Rural Health Unit (RHU) - refers to the health unit or health center's owned, administered, managed and financed by the local government unit, which is either attached to or directly supervised by the Office of the City Health Officer. Health centers and rural health units are used alternatively and interchangeably in this Agreement.
1.4 In-Patient & Outpatient Package (Phase I) - refers to the Medicare package covering hospital benefits for room and board, medicines, laboratory and professional fees, including existing outpatient benefits under the unified benefit schedule different from those provided by an accredited Rural Health Unit (RHU).
1.5 Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package (Phase II) - refers to the primary consultation and diagnostic services which are included in the Program's benefit package and administered, managed and/or delivered by accredited RHUs/Health Centers.
1.6 Capitalization - refers to the scheme or form of paying the RHU for specific services it provides for a particular period which is arrived at based on a pre-determined criteria under the condition that any savings from the agreed amount accrues to the benefit of the facility and that in case of insufficiency thereof, the particular services due to the covered beneficiaries shall be delivered at the cost of the RHU.
1.7 Philippine National Drug Formulary (PNDF) - refers to the essential drugs list for the Philippines which is prepared by the National Drug Committee of the Department of Health (DOH) in consultation with experts and specialists from organized professional medical societies, the academe and pharmaceutical industry, and which is updated every year.
Article III
UNDERTAKINGS OF PHILHEALTH
SECTION 2. The Corporation shall perform, with due diligence and efficiency, the following undertakings:
2.1 It shall collect funds from the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) to finance the national government premium subsidy for qualified beneficiaries enrolled in the Program who are residing within the respective jurisdictions of the LGUs herein represented.
2.2 It shall assist in the activities leading to the enrollment of Program beneficiaries based on the prescribed PhilHealth tool.
2.3 It shall assist in the conduct of public information campaign as provided for in Article 3 Section 3.Sub-section 3.3 of this Agreement.
2.4 It shall facilitate the accreditation of public health institutions and facilities under the jurisdiction of the local government units herein represented, in accordance with the accreditation standards set forth in RA 8785 as amended by RA 9241 and its Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations.
2.5 It shall ensure the prompt disbursement of funds for the payment of approved claims under the In-Patient Program or Program Phase I filed by the accredited health care providers or directly by the duly qualified beneficiaries. Provided, however, that it shall be liable to pay only up to the prescribed limit of benefits.

--- [Page 6] ---
2.6 It shall assist in the annual updating of the status of membership of current members by reasons of transfer of residence, death, change in economic and civil status and/or coverage under NPHP in any of its membership categories.

2.7 It shall collect from the Local Government Units herein represented the latter's counterparty annual premium contribution in accordance with Article III, Section 3, Subsection 3.2 hereof, and apply the same for the provision of benefits to qualified beneficiaries.

2.8 For the purpose of implementing the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package, the Corporation shall:

2.8.1 Facilitate accreditation of Rural Health Units/Health Centers/District Hospitals/other facilities in accordance with PhilHealth's accreditation guidelines;

2.8.2 Release the capitation amount to the accredited OPF Provider, through the Local Government Unit herein represented, in accordance with the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines, copy of which is hereto attached as "Annex A" and shall form an integral part thereof;

2.8.3 Monitor the implementation of the benefit package, consistent with the objectives of the NPHP, in coordination with the LGUs herein represented; and

2.8.4 Provide technical and other support services for the implementation and administration of the Outpatient Consultation Diagnostic Package.

Article III UNDERTAKINGS OF THE CITY

SECTION 3. The City shall perform, with due diligence and efficiency, the following undertakings:

3.1 It shall, through the Sangguniang Panlungsod, enact appropriate Ordinance/Resolution for the following purposes:

3.1.1 Adoption of and/or continued support to the National Health Insurance Program and its governing rules as set forth in RA 7875 as amended by RA 9241 and its Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations. Such support shall include active encouragement of participation by private and non-government organizations within its territorial jurisdiction, mandating compliance to NPHP rules by entities required to obtain LGU permits/clients, and instituting revolving funds for medicines in owned or managed hospitals, as may be warranted under existing rules/guidelines or similar future arrangements and/or NPHP issuances;

3.1.2 Grant of authority to the City Mayor to enter into an Agreement and/or interpose favorable modifications, revisions and/or amendments thereto;

3.1.3 Enrollment of the targeted number of indigent families identified from the National Household Targeting System for Poverty Reduction as validated by Philhealth, City Social Services and Development Office, Department of Social Welfare and Development;

3.2 It shall assist in the conduct of public information campaign aimed at appraising its constituents on the policies, rules and regulation relative to Program benefits, availingment procedures, and their corresponding rights and obligations under the same;

3.3 For the purpose of implementing the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package, the City shall:

3.3.1 Pass an Ordinance to set up a PhilHealth Capitation Fund for the capitation amount of THREE HUNDRED PESOS (P300.00) per family with paid premium.

--- [Page 7] ---
released by the Corporation to the City through the passage of an ordinance in accordance with the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines and the relevant provisions of Article VII hereof;
3.3.1 Oversee and supervise the implementation of the package through its Phil/Health Centers/accredited providers and the administration of the 20% corresponding capital fund released;
3.3.2 Upgrade or enhance the administrative and operational capabilities of its local health facilities to conform with PhilHealth's accreditation standards;
3.3.3 Ensure continued delivery of the services to the Program beneficiaries;
3.3.4 Submit to the Corporation or its instrumentality such reports as may be required for the purpose of monitoring/evaluation, research and program development;
3.3.5 It shall continuously strive for the expansion of Program coverage within its jurisdiction.

Article V PAYMENT OF PREMIUM CONTRIBUTIONS
SECTION 5. The City shall remit to PhilHealth its payment premium counterpart subject to the terms and conditions stipulated in Article IV of this Agreement.
Article VI OUTPATIENT CONSULTATION AND DIAGNOSTIC PACKAGE (Phase II)
SECTION 6. The Program shall provide the following benefits, referred to herein as Program Phase II, which shall be administered by the Rural Health Units and/or Health Centers in accordance with PhilHealth's accreditation standards and Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines.
6.1 Primary Consults with general/physicians; and

--- [Page 8] ---
6.2 Laboratory fees for:
6.2.1 Chest X-ray;
6.2.2 Complete Blood Count;
6.2.3 Fecalysis;
6.2.4 Urinalysis;
6.2.5 Sputum Microscopy.
6.3 Other preventive services as may be provided in the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines.
Additional benefits may be integrated into the program based on set criteria and in consideration of PhilHealth's financial capability.
The allotment of these benefits shall not be portable and the indigent members shall be assigned to specific providers. In the event that the Rural Health Unit is not accredited to provide the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package based on the set standards for accreditation, the transitory provisions stipulated in the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines shall take effect until otherwise become inapplicable.
Article VII CREATION OF PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND
SECTION 7. A Philhealth Capitation Fund (PCF) set up as provided for under 2.8 and 4.6 shall be released in accordance with the following, viz:
7.1 Initial release shall be subject to prior accreditation of the Rural Health Unit and the payment of premium contribution by the Local Government Units (LGUs);
7.2 The capitation amount shall be released by the Corporation to appropriate entities based on the mode of payment of the Local Government Units (LGUs);
7.3 Succeeding release of the capitation shall be based on the mode of payment of the Local Government Units (LGUs) and the submission of required monitoring/evaluation reports and payment of premium; the frequency of the submission of monitoring/evaluation reports shall be based on the mode of payment of the Local Government Unit (LGU);
7.4 The Corporation shall withhold the release of the subsequent PCFs due to any of the following:
7.4.1 Violation of government accounting and auditing rules and regulations on the disbursement and liquidation of the PCF;
7.4.2 Non-submission of the required reports under Subsection 7.3 hereof.
Article VIII DISPOSITION OF THE PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND
SECTION 8. The use, management, and disposition of the PCF shall be governed by the following rules:
8.1 The disbursement and liquidation of the PCF shall be in accordance with pertinent government accounting and auditing rules and regulations and to be audited accordingly by the local COA Auditors;
8.2 A separate depository account under Trust Fund Book of Accounts shall be maintained by the City for the purpose;
8.3 The following may be charged to PCF:

--- [Page 9] ---
8.3.1 All drugs listed in the Philippine National Drug Formulary (PNDF);
8.3.2 Equipment and supplies necessary/related to carry out the delivery of required services including but not limited to medical equipment and supplies, furniture and fixtures, appliances, office equipment, consumables and supplies;
8.3.3 Site improvement of health facility;
8.3.4 Referral fees for diagnostic services; and
8.3.5 Administrative cost equivalent to twenty percent (20%) of the total capital amount.
8.4 The Corporation through its Accreditation Department shall approve the acquisition and usage of new medical technological equipment, devices and modalities of treatment consistent with actual needs and current standards of medical practice and ethics with national health objectives after assessing the advantage and appropriateness of such purchase.
8.5 The Twenty percent (20%) mandatory provision for administrative cost shall be released to the Cities and Municipalities with accredited RHUs/Health Centers. It shall be divided as follows:
8.5.1 Fifty percent (50%) of the administrative cost shall accrue to the physician/s;
8.5.2 Twenty five percent (25%) shall accure to the other RHU personnel directly involved in the provision of services required thereof;
8.5.3 The remaining twenty five (25%) shall be utilized for other administrative cost such as office supplies, transportation expenses, etc.
8.6 The twenty percent (20%) mandatory provision for administrative cost, which shall be released to the City with accredited RHUs/Health Center, shall be treated as Trust Fund by the LGU.
8.6 Eighty percent (80%) of the total capital fund shall be held in trust by the City in accordance with the following:
8.6.1 The amount shall be utilized for bulk procurement of drugs and medicines listed in the PNDF and medical equipment and supplies based on the need assessment requisition issue voucher (RIV) submitted by the accredited RHUs/Health Centers to the City Government;
8.6.2 PNDF drugs and medicines purchased under the preceding section shall be released to the RHUs/Health Centers by the City as may be deemed necessary, preferably on a regular basis;
8.6.3 For accounting purposes, the price of drugs, medicines, equipment and supplies charged against the FCF of the City or Municipality shall be at its actual cost or winning bid price;
Article IX
PROVISION FOR PAYMENT OF PREMIUM CONTRIBUTION AND LATE REMITTANCES
SECTION 9. The corporation shall be guided accordingly by the following provisions pertinent to premium contribution payments and late remittances, viz:

--- [Page 10] ---
9.1 Payments of premium shall be remitted to PhilHealth (quarterly, semi-annually, or annually). The Province and/or the participating City shall remit its payment prior to the issuance of the PhilHealth IDs or renewal of membership;
9.2 For late remittances, the City are hereby given a grace period of thirty (30) calendar days from the date the payment is due within which to remit their premium contribution in accordance with Article IV, Section 4, Subsection 4.2 hereof, without thereby incurring penalty as prescribed in the Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations of RA 7875 as amended by RA 9241.
The corporation shall cause the non-renewal of membership, in the event of non-payment of premium contribution of the delinquent party, when due and demandable.
Article X REFERRAL SYSTEM
SECTION 10. In case of inability by the Rural Health Unit to deliver the required Chest X-ray service, the City must establish a referral system in accordance with PhilHealth's accreditation standards and Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines.
Article XI PORTABILITY OF BENEFITS UNDER PHASE I
SECTION 11. The indigent members and their beneficiaries may avail of the benefits provided for under the In-Patient and Out Patient Package from any health care provider duly accredited by PhilHealth.
Article XII MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
SECTION 12. The operationalization of the Program shall be pursued through the collaboration of the parties under the following circumstances:
1.2.1 The parties agree that the benefits under Phase I shall commence on the effectiveness date exhibited on the Sponsored Program IDs distributed to indigent beneficiaries.
1.2.2 The parties agree that the implementation of Program Phase II shall commence immediately on the Calendar Quarter following the compliance to all attendant requirements and procedures as set forth in the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines (Phase I).
1.2.3 The parties agree to continuously strive to improve its systems and procedures accordingly by enhancing, developing, and modifying its policies to be more responsive to the needs of NHI indigent members residing within the territorial jurisdiction of the LGUs herein represented.
ARTICLE XIII EFFECTIVITY
SECTION 13. The collaboration of the parties under this Agreement shall take effect this 1st day of October, 2011 and shall continue to be effective for as long as there are qualified indigent members residing within the territorial jurisdiction of the City, provided that the City Government has budget for the same, subject to the provisions of Article IV, Section 4 and Article XII hereof.
Article XIV REVISION OR AMENDMENT
Section 14 Any revision or amendment of this Agreement shall require the written consent of all parties hereto.
