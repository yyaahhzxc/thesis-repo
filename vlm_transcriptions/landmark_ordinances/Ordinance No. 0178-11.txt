Ord. No. 0178-11

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT RELATIVE TO THE MINERALOLOGY SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY PARK (MSTCP) TO BE ENTERED INTO BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO WITH VARIOUS NATIONAL AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT AGENCIES AND PRIVATE ENTITIES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this code, the City Mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the City Government, and in this connection, shall:

Represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf of all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT. The Memorandum of Agreement refers to the instrument to be entered into by the City Mayor, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, relative to the Mindanao Science and Technology Park Consortium;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement relative to the Mindanao Science & Technology Park Consortium ;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other section or provision shall be affected thereby;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, October 04, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
MARGARITO C. DELA CRUZ Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II)

WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, public knowledge, information and communications technology, and other technological innovations are considered modern day creators of wealth as they bolster labor productivity, increase per capita income and improve the quality of life;
WHEREAS, technological innovations require a critical mass of scientists and researchers as well as basic infrastructure for research, technology development and technology transfer;
WHEREAS, in the meeting of the Mindanao Science and Technology Park Consortium on February 15, 2011, the members see the need to forge a new Memorandum of Agreement (MCA) with the following adjustment:
* Change of name from Mindanao Science and Technology Community Consortium (MSTCC) to Mindanao Science and Technology Park Consortium (MSTPC);
WHEREAS, an area in Tugbok District in Davao City is proposed for designation as the Mindanao Science and Technology Park (MSTP), because of the presence of knowledge-based institutions that have a critical mass of scientists and researchers equipped with tools and facilities for innovative research activities;
WHEREAS, the said area is an ideal place for the stimulation and management of knowledge and technology among universities in the region, R&D institutions, companies and markets, and for the creation and growth of innovation-based companies through incubation and spin-off processes;
WHEREAS, the knowledge-based institutions in the area have the resources - human, land, infrastructure and equipment - that can be tapped to become the foundation for a modern S&T facility that can serve the island of Mindanao;
WHEREAS, given appropriate R&D programs, systems and procedures, funds and human resources, these institutions can collaborate to produce technological innovations beneficial to Mindanao's development;

WHEREAS, the presence of residential communities, schools, and a thriving economy with supportive local government units makes the area ripe for an initiative such as the MSTPCP in which the local community stand to gain directly from the benefits derived from scientific and economic activities generated in the Tugbok District.

WHEREAS, the Mintal and Tugbok area is designated as an academic zone for its rich Filipino-Japanese cultural heritage creating opportunities for diversified economies, promoting community pride and improving quality of life through understanding the people and the place.

WHEREAS, DOST XI is mandated to support local scientific and technological efforts, develop local capabilities to achieve technical self-reliance, promote public and private sector partnership in S&T activities and encourage the private sector to take a great role in R&D activities;

WHEREAS, BPI is mandated to develop and promote relevant technologies in tropical fruits and other horticultural crops; produce quality seeds and plant materials that increase farmers productivity and food security for sustainable farming enterprise towards global competitiveness;

WHEREAS, City Government of Davao aims to become a major growth pole in the international market by engaging the empowered and participative citizenry in resource based and market-driven economic activities within the context of balanced ecology, sustainable growth and equity-led development; provide Davao city with the necessary infrastructure, facilities, and services to support basic needs and utilities and urban basic services in order to attain the socio-economic uplift to the citizens of Davao; sustainable development; and transform Davao City into a modern and well-planned investment center in Mindanao and the Asia-Pacific Region, propelled by socially enlightened leaders and empowered citizenry, spiritually committed to attain sustainable growth and optimum development within the context of balanced ecology;

WHEREAS, DA XI as an implementing arm of the Department of Agriculture is mandated to operationalize the policy framework for agricultural and fishery development. Thus, spur and facilitate initiatives among the private sectors and stakeholders to propel sustainable economic growth in the pursuance of food security in the Philippines;

WHEREAS, DCCCLII will support organization that provides leadership and a strong voice for the business professionals who have joined together to improve the economic, civic and social welfare of Davao City;

WHEREAS, DENR XI is mandated to formulate and recommend integrated research programs, generate technologies and provides scientific assistance in the R&D of technologies relevant to sustainable use of natural resources; and provide technical assistance in the implementation and monitoring of research programs;

WHEREAS, DPWH XI is responsible for the planning, design, construction and maintenance of infrastructure, especially the national highways, flood control and water resources development system, and other works in accordance with national development objectives;

WHEREAS, DTI XI shall assist in coordinating, promoting and facilitating the trade, industry and investment activities relevant to the promotion of the consortium's identified and approved R & D projects;

WHEREAS, FIDA - BORFSS is mandated to promote the accelerated growth and development of the Philippine oil industry in all its aspects including research production, processing, marketing and trade regulation;

WHEREAS, NEDIA XI is mandated to formulate continuing, coordinated and integrated social and economic policies, plans and programs as well as to provide technical staff support as may be required;

WHEREAS, PCCA XI is mandated to formulate and promote a strategic and comprehensive development program for the coconut and other palm oil industry in all its aspects and to conduct research and extension works on farm productivity and process development for product quality and diversification;

WHEREAS, PSHS-SMC as an academic institution shall serve as the training center for teachers and students, and source of research on student abilities, teaching methods and strategies. It shall promote S&T awareness to the community and opportunities to a wider sector of the population in Mindanao by strengthening their participation in the National Competitive Examination which is conducted every year;

WHEREAS, UPMn is a state institution of higher learning and has a threefold function of teaching, research and extension services in the fulfillment of its goals and mission.

WHEREAS, UDBPH is a state university mandated to provide programs of instruction and professional training; promote advanced studies, research and extension services and progressive leadership in science, agriculture, forestry, fisheries, engineering and industrial fields and other courses needed in the socio-economic development of Mindanao;

WHEREAS, UM shall conduct researches and other scholarly activities that generate new knowledge and contribute to the improvement of the quality of life for all; engage in community services that foster self-reliance and empowerment among the marginalized community; and lead in the promotion and preservation of the cultural heritage of Mindanao and of the country as a whole;

WHEREAS, MSTCFI shall promote science education through the development and improvement of its component programs, projects and activities; provide technical assistance, and strengthen institutional linkages and collaboration for the purpose of promoting science education, technology generation and utilization toward the development of Davao City and Mindanao;

WHEREAS, RECIN Mindanao is mandated to establish or strengthen institutional linkages and collaboration in R&D, and provide technical assistance for the purpose of promoting science education, technology generation and utilization toward the development of Region XI;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises and mutual covenants, agreements and stipulations hereinafter set forth, the parties agree to jointly undertake:

ARTICLE I NAME, DOMICILE AND OBJECTIVES

Section 1. The name of the organization shall be Mindanao Science and Technology Park Consortium (MSTPC). It shall hold office at Department of Science and Technology Regional Office No. XI (DOST XI), Corner Friendship and Dumarianas Roads, Bajau, Davao City.

Section 2. The objectives of the Mindanao Science and Technology Park Consortium are to:

a. Advance collaboration among member agencies to work on programs and projects for effective and relevant research and technology transfer outputs;

b. Attract investors to tap MSTPC experts, facilities and services while attaining and maintaining internationally accepted ethical, scientific and environmental standards in all aspects of operation;

c. Promote Tugbok District as a prime destination in Mindanao for visiting scientists and researchers and as a venue for scientific researches and gatherings.

ARTICLE II MEMBERSHIP

SECTION 1. COMPOSITION. The MSTPC shall be composed of government, non-government and private sectors where mandates are in line with the purpose of the consortium. It shall be represented by the Regional Director/Heads of the agencies and their official designated representatives.

SECTION 2. QUALIFICATION FOR EXPANSION OF MEMBERSHIP. Any public, private or non-government organizations in Region XI which has the mandate, financial capability and resources to undertake S&T related development activities can become a member of the TPC. The Board of Directors shall approve the membership of the MSTPC based on approved guidelines.

SECTION 3. DUTIES OF MEMBER INSTITUTIONS. The member institutions shall have the following duties:

a) All parties agree to form part of the Mindanao Science and Technology Park Consortium;

b) All parties shall commit themselves to the mandate and purpose of the consortium for which it was established.

--- [Page 9] ---
c) All parties agree to the annual contribution of Php 5,000,000 in cash or in kind which may include but not limited to forms such as support or complementary manpower, technical expertise that may be needed by the consortium at time to time, supplies and materials, provision for office space, hosting of meetings and/or activities during the year 2011 to be used for the operation and maintenance of the consortium and shall be subject to change as approved by the policy making body;

d) All parties agree to commit the services of their research staff and experts to the consortium, wherever these may be needed;

e) All parties agree to share with other member-agencies necessary research facilities' (eg. library, equipment, laboratories) in the conduct of approved collaborative R&D and training projects of the consortium;

f) All parties shall initiate and cooperate in resource generation endeavors;

g) The member-agencies shall provide relevant information on the technological and research needs of the various sectors in Mindanao and in the promotion of generated mature technologies to effect transfer to and adoption by possible users;

h) Attend consortium meetings, and

i) Accept tasks as may be assigned by the Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson.

SECTION 4. TERMINATION OF MEMBERSHIP. In the event that a party would wish to terminate its membership in the consortium, a written notice should be submitted to the Board of Directors at least ninety (90) days prior to termination of membership.

ARTICLE III OFFICERS

SECTION 1. SELECTION PROCESS AND APPOINTMENT OF OFFICIALS. The Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, Public Affairs Officer, Auditor and Ex-officio member shall be selected among the members through election. They will serve as the policy-making body of the consortium.

SECTION 2. TERM OF OFFICE. All officers of the consortium shall hold office for two (2) years and until their successors are duly elected and qualified.

SECTION 3. VACANCY TO THE POSITION. In the event that any of the positions became vacant through resignation, retirement, reassignment, removal from office, death or permanent disabilities, an election shall be held on the next consortium meeting to fill-up the said positions. Incumbent officers are allowed to run for the said positions, of which case, if elected, the vacated post/position shall be subjected to election.

ARTICLE IV FUNCTIONS

SECTION 1. FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS.

a) Provide central direction, leadership and coordination of all S&T activities in the region;

b) Establish policies and guidelines in consultation with stakeholders in the identification of priority S&T programs and projects in the region;

c) Promote the development of research capacity, technology transfer and linkages on S&T;

d) Review and approve the consolidated work and financial plans of the TVQG;

e) Establish monitoring and evaluation mechanism to ensure long-term sustainability of the consortium;

f) Oversee the overall implementation of the MSTPC programs and projects.

--- [Page 10] ---
ARTICLE V

TECHNICAL WORKING GROUP (TWG)

SECTION 1. COMPOSITION. The M5TFC Technical Working Group (TWG) shall be composed of one representative from each of the member institutions.

SECTION 2. FUNCTIONS. The Technical Working Group (TWG) shall be created to assist the Board of Directors in the conceptualization, planning, implementation and evaluation of the various programs/projects and related activities of the consortium.

ARTICLE VI

MEETINGS

SECTION 1. QUARTERLY MEETING. The consortium shall meet at least once per quarter. The Board of Directors shall handle all urgent matters presented to the consortium before the scheduled meeting. This is without prejudice to holding consortium meetings in between the prescribed period when deemed necessary.

SECTION 2. SPECIAL MEETINGS. Special meetings of the members shall be called as the need thereof arises, by the Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson.

SECTION 3. LINING UP OF AGENDA ITEMS FOR DISCUSSION. All subject matters and concerns for deliberation during consortium meetings shall be channeled through the Secretariat.

SECTION 4. NOTICES. Notices of the time and place of the consortium meetings shall be given either personally or by special delivery mail, at least one (1) week before the date set for such meeting. The notice shall specify the subject matter, the likely purpose or purposes of the meeting. Hosting of meetings shall be rotated among member institutions so that the expertise and experience of all members and expertise shall be adopted.

SECTION 5. SIGNATORIES OF INVITATION LETTERS. The Chairperson of the consortium shall sign all letters of invitation and other communications pertinent to the conduct of consortium meetings, duly noted by the Secretariat. In the absence of the Chairperson, the Vice-Chairperson shall have the authority of signing same.

SECTION 6. MEETING PROPER. The Chairperson shall preside over all consortium meetings. In his absence, the Vice-Chairperson shall act as the presiding officer.

SECTION 7. QUORUM. The presence in a meeting of a simple majority of the consortium members or official designated representatives shall constitute a quorum. In the absence of regular members and/or their official designated representatives, only those alternate representatives already pre-identified by the regular member or official designated representatives shall be counted as present and can decide for and with the full number represented on any matter for decision by the consortium.

SECTION 8. DECISIONS. The decisions of the consortium shall be a majority of the members that constitute a quorum. Other matters and concerns that need immediate consideration and decision of the organization shall be handled by the Board of Directors through referrals whenever the members are not scheduled to meet.

SECTION 9. VOTING PROXY. Each member shall be entitled to one vote, and he/she may vote either in person or by proxy of the pre-identified alternate of regular members and/or official designated representatives.

SECTION 10. CONSORTIUM RESOLUTIONS. All decisions of the consortium shall be in the form of resolutions. The responsibility of the consortium Secretariat at the end of each meeting, the resolutions passed during that meeting will be retransmitted by the Secretariat to ensure that decisions were properly recorded. Resolutions shall be numbered consecutively every fiscal year indicating the number of the resolution, number of the resolution, and the year the resolution was passed. At the end of each fiscal year, a report on the actions taken and the extent of the action determined, i.e., whether these were carried out and the extent of such action. A report on the status of these resolutions shall be presented to the consortium for information during its next meeting.

--- [Page 11] ---
**SECTION 11. MINUTES OF CONSORTIUM MEETINGS.** All issues/agreements reached during the discussion shall form part of the minutes of the meeting. The said minutes of the consortium meetings shall be prepared by the Secretary, certified by the Secretary and attested by the Chairperson.

**SECTION 12. OFFICIAL RECORD OF CONSORTIUM MEETINGS.** The agenda of the proceedings of the consortium meetings shall be recorded in the minutes of the meetings. The minutes of these meetings and the records of implementing actions taken shall constitute the official records of the consortium meetings. The Consortium Secretary shall issue certified copies of any consortium meeting records or parts thereof to any interested party upon request of the interested party and written authorization of the Chairperson.

**ARTICLE VII FUNDS**

**SECTION 1. FUNDS.** The funds of the association shall be derived from the annual contribution of the member institutions, gifts and donations or grants received from various sources. The funds shall be used for the functioning of the consortium and its activities. The consortium shall open and maintain a deposit account and/or trust account in India as it is required under the policy making body. Pending registration with the Securities and Exchange Commission and/or any government regulating agencies, funds of MSTPC shall be deposited in this account, with the Consortium Chairperson and Treasurer as the official signatories to all bank transactions.

**SECTION 2. DISBURSEMENTS.** Disbursement of funds of the consortium shall be signed by the Treasurer and countersigned by the Chairperson of the MSTPC.

**SECTION 3. FISCAL YEAR.** The fiscal year of the consortium shall be from January 1st to December 31st of each year.

**ARTICLE VIII OWNERSHIP OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS**

**SECTION 1. OWNERSHIP OF PROJECT OUTPUTS.** All research outputs, collaborative works, publications and reports produced by virtue and pursuant to this Agreement shall be owned jointly and severally by all members of MSTPC and/or concerned funding agencies. As to UP/MnF, collaborative works among institutions shall be owned jointly by the partners of the Technology Transfer Act of 2008 and Article 4 Section 2(B) of the Proposed Revised UP IP Policies.

**SECTION 2. UTILIZATION OF RESEARCH OUTPUTS AND FINDINGS.** The MSTPC shall exert all efforts to ensure dissemination and utilization of the results of all researches undertaken by the consortium. Publication of research output shall be pursued through the MSTPC publication media, which will prejudice to the researcher's choice of publication, journals, provided that proper acknowledgment is made.

**SECTION 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS, ROYALTIES AND INNOVATION.** Ownership and right to innovative discoveries, royalties and intellectual property rights that may arise from the activities undertaken pursuant to this Agreement shall be governed by pertinent provisions of Republic Act No 8253 or Intellectual Property Code of 2002 and DOST Memorandum Circular No. 001 series of 2002, Japan respectively Policies and other applicable laws, rules and regulations.

**ARTICLE IX TAXES OR FEES**

**SECTION 1.** All taxes and fees shall be borne by MSTPC from its common fund and never by individual parties.

**ARTICLE X OTHER PROVISIONS**

--- [Page 12] ---
**SECTION 1: All parties concerned shall exercise full diligence, good faith and commitment to ensure compliance to the stipulations of this Agreement. Any problems, questions or conflicts that may arise from this Agreement shall be resolved through consultation.**

**SECTION 2: This Agreement is not intended to create a partnership or joint venture of any kind between or among the members of this Agreement. No member shall have authority to act in the name of or otherwise bind any other member.**

**ARTICLE XI**

**LEGAL BASIS**

**SECTION 1: This Memorandum of Agreement shall serve as the legal basis for member-agencies' participation, financial and other resource contributions to the consortium in accordance with approved Board resolution.**

**ARTICLE XII**

**AMENDMENT AND TERMINATION**

**SECTION 1: This Memorandum of Agreement may be amended, altered, modified, supplemented or terminated upon mutual consent of all parties concerned.**

**ARTICLE XIII**

**EFFECTIVITY**

**SECTION 1: This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing, except that as to UPMin, it shall take effect upon confirmation by the Board of Directors. It shall be effective for a period of five (5) years, without prejudice to renewal or extension as the herein parties may mutually agree.**
