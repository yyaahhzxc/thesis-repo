RESOLUTION NO. 365
Proposed Ordinance, entitled: "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL SELLERS AND DEALERS OF LIQUID PROFESSION (CAS) IN DAVAO CITY TO HAVE WEIGHING SCALES IN THEIR PLACE OF BUSINESS IN DAVAO CITY, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES," was presented for consideration and approbation by Councilor Valentinico C. Bancoz, Jr. He stated that the proposed ordinance requires a fine of P100.00 or an infringement of not more than one (1) month's license at the discretion of the Court upon violation thereof. He further stated that in case of non-compliance under this ordinance shall be a ground for the cancellation of license to a business per- mit to sell LPG in Davao City.

Councilor Donadorado B. Zulo, Jr., proposed an amendment to increase the fine to P200.00 instead of only P100.00. The amendment was accepted by the proponents.

Councilor Emperador M. Porras likewise proposed to amend the title of the Ordinance to read, "An Ordinance Regulating the Sale of Liquid Profeesion (CAS) for other purposes." The amendment was also accepted by the proponents.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councilor Tomas Monteverde, Jr., seconded by Councilors Donadorado B. Zulo, Jr., Pantaleon V. Falayo, Jr., and endorsed by Councilors Donadorado B. Zulo, Jr. and Emperador M. Porras, it was RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an Ordinance, entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 156
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE SALE OF LPG IN DAVAO CITY, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

Resolution No. 169, Ordinance No. 166,
series of 1981, cont'd.
SECTION 1.- All sellers and dealers of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) in Davao City contained in cylinders with contents of
5 lbs., 11 kilograms, 22 kilograms, 43 kilograms, 50 kilograms,
and any other weight required to indicate in the cylinder
the weight of the liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) contained therein
and the weight of the cylinder itself;
SEC. 2. - All LPG sellers and dealers in Davao City are
required to have a weighing scale in their places of business
duly certified by the Bureau of Standards, Ministry of Commerce
and Trade and duly inspected and scaled twice a year by the City
Treasurer, City of Davao, before they can be issued any license
or business permit to do business in Davao City;
SEC. 3. - Any LPG sold in Davao City by sellers or dealers
which is contained in cylinders must be weighed in the presence
of the customers before any sale is made;
SEC. 4. - Any person, firm or corporation found violating
any of the provisions of this Ordinance shall upon conviction be
punished by a fine of not more than P200.00 or an imprisonment
of not more than one (1) month or both at the discretion of the Court.
A conviction under this Ordinance shall be ground for the cancellation
of a license and business permit to sell LPG in Davao City. In
case a partnership or corporation violates the provisions of this
Ordinance, the Manager of said company in Davao City shall be
criminally liable therefor.
SEC. 5. - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
