Series of 2014

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING POLICIES, GUIDELINES AND
RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE OPERATION AND
MAINTENANCE OF THE PASALUBONG CENTER, A TOURISM-
ORIENTED PFOJECT OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO

Ord No. 0115-14

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING POLICIES, GUIDELINES AND RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE OF THE PASALUBONG CENTER, A TOURISM-ORIENTED PROJECT OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO";

SECTION 2. CONSTITUTION OF MANAGEMENT BOARD - There is hereby constituted a Pasalubong Center Management Board, to be composed of the following:

1. City Mayor as Chairperson, or his/her authorized representative;
2. Manager of the City Economic Enterprise as Vice Chairperson, or his/her authorized representative;
3. Members: Heads of the following departments/offices, or their authorized representatives:
a. City Planning Office;
b. City Environment and Natural Resources Office;
c. City Treasurer's Office;
d. City Agriculturist's Office;
e. City Information Office;
f. City Legal Office;
g. Sangguniang Panlungsod, Committee on Government Enterprises and Privatization;
h. Sangguniang Panlungsod, Committee on Tourism and Beautification.

The Board may from time to time recommend additional members to be appointed to the Board by the City Mayor in the interest of the service and for the effective discharge of its duties and functions.

SECTION 3. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD - The Board shall be vested with powers, functions and duties, as follows:

1. To formulate and adopt policy guidelines and standards in the formulation and administration of the said Pasalubong Center;
2. To promulgate policies, regulations governing the operation, administration and maintenance of the Pasalubong Center;
3. To conduct periodic review on the operations of the Center;
4. To conduct raffle and drawing of lots for the allocation and awarding of stalls or booths at the Pasalubong Center, and certify the results thereof to the appropriate City Mayor;
5. To decide on the revocation or cancellation of lease contract, closure of stalls/ space, or eviction of lessees from the stall/space for breach of contracts, or violation against the provisions of existing laws and ordinances;
6. To recommend such measures of action as may be necessary in the resolution of problems, in connection with the use and occupancy of stalls, or spaces in the center;
7. To recommend, as the need arises, for the creation of similar centers in the three (3) districts of the City;

8. To adopt from time to time a budget for the administration and operation of the center to be drawn against the budget of the City Economic Enterprise.
9. To do and perform any and all acts necessary, proper or incidental to the attainment of the purpose of the Center;
10. All policy action of the Board shall be subject to the approval of the City Mayor.

SECTION 4. MEETING AND QUORUM - The Board shall hold a regular meeting at least once a month. Special meetings may be convened upon the call of the Chairperson or by the majority of the members of the Board. The presence of a simple majority of all the members shall constitute a quorum.

In the absence of the Chairperson and Vice Chairperson, a temporary presiding officer shall be designated by the majority of the board constituting a quorum.

SECTION 5. ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT AND MANAGEMENT OF THE CENTER - The Pasalubong Center shall be operated and managed by the City Economic Enterprise under the direct supervision of the Board. The City Economic Enterprise shall be responsible in providing overall administrative and secretarial services to the Board.

However, the City Tourism Office shall, likewise, assign personnel to conduct and promote the city tourism programs and activities.

SECTION 6. BUDGETARY REQUIREMENTS AND SOURCE OF FUNDS - The budgetary requirements for the operation of the Pasalubong Center, including the corresponding compensation of personnel that may be assigned therein, shall be sourced out from the existing budget of the City Economic Enterprise.

SECTION 7. INVITATION TO ALL PROSPECTIVE QUALIFIED TENANTS - The Board shall cause to be published once a week for two consecutive weeks in a newspaper of local circulation, or posted in two conspicuous places an invitation to all prospective qualified persons or entities to lease the available stall/booth at the Center.

SECTION 8. WHO MAY QUALIFY AS TENANT - Any person or entity residing in Davao City, who is engaged in the business of producing locally made Davao City delicacies and food products, goods or other souvenirs items using local and indigenous raw material may apply as tenant of the Center.

SECTION 9. EVALUATION AND APPROVAL OF APPLICATION - The City Economic Enterprise, acting as the Center Management, shall evaluate the qualifications and products of all interested lessee-applicants based on the criteria, as prescribed under the implementing rules and regulations to be adopted by the Board, and submit the list of qualified applicants and their products for the approval of the Board.

Provided that, if the initial total number of applicants/tenants exceed the available number of stalls/boths, the Board shall allocate and award the same to qualified applicants through a raffle and certify the results thereof to the City Mayor for approval.  

SECTION 10. LOCALLY MADE PRODUCT SHOWCASE - Being a concept store, products presented shall adhere to categories or groupings as follows:  

a) Souvenir and gift items such as, but not limited to, handicrafts, handmade paper products, fashion accessories, souvenir shirts, home decorations, indigenous costumes and other native accessories;  
b) Fresh fruits and vegetables such as, but not limited to, snack foods, bottled jams and candies;  
c) Health, beauty and wellness products;  
d) Musical instruments, guitars, flutes, etc.;  
e) Fresh fruits and derivatives/preriprices;  
f) Flower arrangements, silk flowers and floral arrangements;  
g) Shell craft, wood craft and coconut cor; and  
h) Other Davao City locally made delicacies and food products, goods or other souvenirs items using local and indigenous raw materials.  

SECTION 11. RENTAL RATE - There shall be collected a monthly rental fee in the amount of Four Hundred Fifty Pesos (P 450.00) per square meter of an area or space occupied by the tenants of the Center, payable to the City Treasurer's Office on or before the 10th day of the following month. The above rental fee, however, does not include the cost of utilities, such as water and electric bills, the actual cost of which shall be for the account of the individual lessees.  

Failure to pay the rental within the period required herein shall subject the tenant/tenant to charge of twenty-five percent (25%) of the amount of the rental fee, plus an interest of four hundred forty-four (444) per annum on the unpaid amount from the due date until the rental fee is fully paid.  

SECTION 12. AUTOMATIC RENTAL RATE INCREASE - There shall be an automatic increase in rental fee of ten percent (10%) for every two years from the time of the approval of this Ordinance to cover the incremental operating cost of maintaining the Pasalubong Center.  

SECTION 13. AUTHORITY TO SIGN LEASE CONTRACTS - The Pasalubong Center Management Board, through its Chairperson, is hereby authorized to sign all lease contracts for the use of stalls or spaces in the center.  

SECTION 14. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be held to be unconstitutional or invalid by competent authority, such judgment or action shall not affect or impair the other sections or provisions hereof.

SECTION 15. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other city ordinances, Executive Orders, and rules and regulations, which are inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby deemed amended, repealed or modified accordingly.
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval hereof.
