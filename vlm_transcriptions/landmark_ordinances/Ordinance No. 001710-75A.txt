RESOLUTION NO. 2569
Prohibition of the fruits and vegetables vendors of the Bunkercham Public Market, dated August 4, 1974, recommending the banning and/or closure of juke boxes being operated with-in the said market, was presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body, the City Treasurer under its duties under Section 18 of the Public Markets Act as amended by the Committees on Public Markets under its 5th Indorsement. According to the market vendors, the operation of juke boxes inside the said market is causing the vendors to be financially burdened. Furthermore, it also violates the contract executed by and between the City Government and the Harfori Estate that the market building constructed therein is exclusively for fruits and vegetables only.
After a lengthy deliberation, on motion of Councilors Domingo E. Vidares, duly and jointly seconded by Councilors Donidero B. Zuza, Jr. and Benigno S. Bangoy, it was RESOLVED, To approve, as it is hereby approved, an Ordinance entitled:
ORDINANCE NO. 1710-A
AN ORDINANCE BANNING AND/OR PROHIBITING THE OPERATIONS OF JUKE BOXES WITHIN CITY PUBLIC MARKETS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND PROVIDING PENALTY THEREFOR.
Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

--- [Page 2] ---
Resolution No. 2569 and Ordinance No. 1710-A, series of 1975, continued:

SECTION 1. - It shall be unlawful for any person or persons, natural or juridical, to operate juke boxes inside the public markets of the City of Davao.

SEC. 2. - Any person, company, corporation or establishment violating this Ordinance shall be punished by a fine of not less than Twenty ($20,00) Pesos, but not more than Two HUNDRED Pesos ($200,00) or by an imprisonment of not less than three (3) months or both such fine and imprisonment at the discretion of the Court.

In case the violation has been committed by a company, corporation or establishment, the person in-charge of the management shall be responsible therefor.

SEC. 3. - Any provision of this Ordinance which is inconsistency herewith is hereby repealed.

SEC. 4. - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days upon approval.

APPROVED: June 1, 1975.

CARRILLO, Unanimously.
