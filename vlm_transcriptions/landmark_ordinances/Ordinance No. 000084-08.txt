ORDINANCE NO. 694-08 Series of 2008  

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 110, "AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING THE 'DAVO CITY OVERLAND TRANSPORT TERMINAL ORDINANCE', BY INCREASING THE TERMINAL AND RENTAL FEES THEREIN  

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "DAVO CITY OVERLAND TRANSPORT TERMINAL ORDINANCES";

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord No. 084-08

SECTION 2: DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 154, Article V of the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that the Local Government Units (LGUs) may fix the rates for the operation of public utilities owned, operated and maintained by them within their jurisdiction.

SECTION 3: AMENDMENT. Article 3, Section 8, paragraph 5, (a), (b) and (d) of Ordinance No. 110, Series of 1986, is hereby amended as follows:

| Type of Vehicles | Terminal Fee |
|------------------|------------|
| (a) Buses (per loading) | From To |
| 1. Baby buses & RJJs with destination outside the City limits with a maximum seating capacity of 20 passengers | Php2.50 | Php25.00 |
| 2. Medium buses with a maximum seating capacity of 40 passengers | 15.00 | 40.00 |
| 3. Large buses with a maximum seating capacity of over 40 passengers | 25.00 | 50.00 |
| (b) Public Utility jeepsneys with a passenger capacity of more than 20 passengers | Php2.50 | Php30.00 |
| (c) xxxxxxxxxxxx | | |
| (d) For rental of land use for refreshments, eatery and churchia concession spaces, with structures built by the concessionaires | Php6.00/ s.m / day | maximum Php3,000/mo. |

SECTION 4: APPROVAL - This Ordinance approves the amendment of Ordinance No. 110, Series of 1986, otherwise known as the "Davao City Overland Transport Terminal Ordinance".

SECTION 5: EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, April 1, 2008, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
