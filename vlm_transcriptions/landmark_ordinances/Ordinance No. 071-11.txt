**ORDINANCE NO. 071-11  
Series of 2011**

**AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL DRIVERS OF PUBLIC UTILITY VEHICLES (PUVs) IN THE CITY OF DAVAO TO SECURE AN OFFICIAL IDENTIFICATION CARD ISSUED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

**SECTION 1. TITLE**  
This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL DRIVERS OF PUBLIC UTILITY VEHICLES (PUVs) IN THE CITY OF DAVAO TO SECURE AN OFFICIAL IDENTIFICATION CARD ISSUED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT."

**SECTION 2. SCOPE OF APPLICATION**  
This Ordinance shall apply to all drivers of Public Utility Vehicles (PUVs) in the City of Davao, requiring them to attend a driver's license enforcement seminar on Safety, Driving, Road Safety, Road Courtesy, Discipline, Traffic Rules and Regulations, Traffic Ordinance, prior to the issuance and/or renewal of their driver's license;

**SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS -** The following terms are defined as follows:

1. **Public Utility Vehicle (PUV)** refers to Public Utility Jeeps (PJJs), buses, vans, taxis, tricycles, and other for hire public utility vehicles used in the transport of passengers, except those in transit and triskedraks;

2. **Identification Card (ID)** is a card issued by the City Mayor or his/her duly authorized representative indicating the complete name of the driver with his/her picture, address, control number, expiry date, and hot line number of LTO, LTFRB, and LTFRB. The driver must attend and complete the seminar hereinafter described. Such card must be prominently displayed inside the vehicle for all passengers to see and read;

3. Seminars are lessons on road safety driving, road courtesy, and discipline, traffic rules and regulations, ordinances pertaining to traffic and other topics may be deemed necessary. The City Government of Davao City, in cooperation with LTO, LTFRB, PHT, and other national and local agencies concerned about traffic regulations shall conduct the seminars free of charge during the first year of this ordinance. In the next succeeding years, the city government may charge reasonable fees for the seminar;

**SECTION 4. MANDATORY PROVISION**

4.a. All drivers of Public Utility Vehicles (PUVs) in Davao City shall, under this ordinance, undergo seminar on Safety Driving, Road Safety, Road Courtesy, Discipline, Traffic Rules and Regulations, and Traffic Ordinances prior to the issuance or renewal of their driver's license. Drivers of Public Utility Vehicles who have received and secured their driver's license shall be required to attend and undergo the herein-mentioned seminar and secure the said Identification Card (ID) as a precondition to drive any Public Utility Vehicle (PUV) in Davao City;

4.b. No driver shall be allowed to drive a PUV in Davao City without undergoing a seminar and securing the identification card (ID) herein required by this ordinance; provided, however, that all drivers of PUVs who have failed to attend and secure the identification card (ID) within thirty (30) days from the effectiveness of this ordinance, whether or not their driver's license is subject to renewal within the said thirty-day period;

**SECTION 5. BUDGET** - The City Government shall appropriate Two Million (P2,000,000.00) annually for the implementation of this Ordinance;

**SECTION 6. PENAL PROVISION** - The following unlawful acts shall be fined according to:

6.a. Failure to secure an official identification card issued by the City Government of Davao:

a. First offense and subsequent offenses - P1,000.00
