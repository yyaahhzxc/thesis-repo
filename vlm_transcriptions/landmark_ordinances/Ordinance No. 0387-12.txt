SECTION 1. TITLE OF THE ORDINANCE - This ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance further Amending Ordinance No. 0227-04, series of 2004, otherwise known as the Internet Cafe Ordinance of Davao City, as amended";

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms shall be defined as follows:

a. Accreditation - refers to the recognition accorded to an establishment or institution that meets the standard or criteria established by the Board;

b. Accreditation Fee - refers to an annual fee payable to the city government with respect to the accreditation of the internet cafe or Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) operating within the city;

c. Board - refers to the Internet Cafe Accrediting Board;

d. Class Hours - Prescribed school hours;

e. Citation Ticket - is issued by the Board, through the Business Bureau, against any violator, indicating the violation and the corresponding imposable fine under this Ordinance;

f. COIM - stands for Coin Operated Internet Machine, commonly known as "Piso-piso internet," which refers to standalone arcade system with a computer and internet connection which can be accessed through inserting any coin denominations or tokens;

g. Community Service - is a penalty imposed to the internet user who violated the ordinance for the second offense and subsequent thereto;

h. Cyber sex - shall mean the exchanging of sexual messages or chat or information via the Internet or sexual activity performed in cyberspace using web cameras or other computer peripherals by means of virtual reality equipment;

i. Fine - refers to the monetary penalty for the violation of this ordinance payable to the City Government through the City Treasurer's Office;

j. Filtering device - is a software that filters out viruses, bugs, and spam when you visit websites while the computer documents load;

k. Gambling - is any game or scheme, whether upon chance or skill, wherein wagers consisting of money, articles or value or representative of value are at stake or made;

l. Hacking - is the practice of modifying computer hardware and software to accomplish a goal outside of the creator's original purpose such as installing data loggers or any devices or software capable of storing passwords, emails or any private information of other users, accessing the internet inside the internet cafe, Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM), or other establishments offering internet access,

SECTION 3. Section 3 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 3. NATURE OF INTERNET CAFE, COIN OPERATED INTERNET MACHINE (COIM), AND OTHER ESTABLISHMENTS OFFERING ACCESS TO INTERNET

--- [Page 4] ---
This section herewith explains the nature and scope of Internet Cafe, COIM, and Other Establishments Offering Access to Internet.

3. A. INTERNET CAFE

3. A.1 Any enclosed business establishments, including those in residential areas, with a computer or internet capable units principally offering internet services, including but not limited to, e-mail, fax, online and network games, chatting, surfing, and other such services covered by the internet technology which are rented out for a fee;

3. A.2 Internet Cafe may offer a combination of services such as food and Internet, coffee or beverage, bar and Internet, and other combinations of similar nature; provided that no liquor or intoxicating drink shall be served in said establishment;

3. A.3 The following shall not be covered under this ordinance:

a. Educational institutions, government agencies, private homes and corporate entities using the internet through an Internet Service Provider (ISP) for access limited to their specialized users and not offered/open to public access;

b. Internet Cafe expanding to other lines of services such as offering short term courses which may run between one (1) to six (6) months and which may fall under computer programming, computer technology and data processing, etc., provided that such courses will not offer such courses from the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) and other appropriate government agencies, shall not be covered in so far as the computer units exclusively used and with respect to such activity;

c. Internet Cafe offering games without internet connection;

SECTION 4. There shall be incorporated after Section 3 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, a new section to read as follows:

SECTION 3-B. COIN OPERATED INTERNET MACHINE (COIM)

3. B.1 Any business establishment in public or residential areas such as, but not limited to, sari-sari stores, public markets or resorts which has a Coin Operated Internet Machine or "Piso-Piso Internet" which is placed outside such establishments or area and offered to public for profit;

3. B.2 Video Karera Machine is not a COIM;

SECTION 5. There shall be further incorporated after Section 3 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, a new section to read as follows:

SECTION 3-C. OTHER ESTABLISHMENTS OFFERING ACCESS TO INTERNET

3. C.1 Any business establishment such as, but not limited to, coffee shops, hotels, spa, parlors, restaurants and establishments offering access through Wifi or any other means of internet sharing to its customers,

3.C.2. A user that has its own private computer/gadget accessing internet through Wi-Fi or LAN shall not be covered unless the accessed information is publicly shown or made public.

SECTION 6. Section 4 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 4. GUIDELINES FOR THE OPERATION OF INTERNET CAFE, COIN OPERATED INTERNET MACHINE (COIM), AND OTHER ESTABLISHMENTS OFFERING ACCESS TO INTERNET

4.A. COMMON PROVISIONS TO ALL

Internet Cafe, Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM), and Other Establishments Offering Access to Internet shall have the following common provisions in their operation.

4.A.1. These entities shall not allow, promote and tolerate computer hacking in their premises;

4.A.2. These entities shall not install data loggers or any devices or software capable of storing passwords, emails or any private information of users accessing the internet inside the internet cafe establishments unless the consent of the users are given in writing nor shall they allow, promote and tolerate any party from doing the same in their premises;

4.A.3. Gambling within its premises, nudity, vulgar language, depiction of graphic sex and all other forms of lewd, obscene, and pornographic pictorial representation or material are prohibited from being sourced, accessed or provided by these entities and by all internet users;

4.A.4. Chatting for the purpose of sexual exploitation and experimentation with pedophiles or other sexual perverts shall be terminated by these entities or its management at any time;

4.A.5. These entities must provide a system for monitoring and blocking or filtering access to pornographic sites or contents approved by the board annually;

4.A.6. These entities must post in a conspicuous and visible area within the establishment a warning sign against accessing to pornographic sites, hacking and gambling. It must maintain a copy of this ordinance inside their premises and must post in a conspicuous and visible area a summary of this ordinance as may be drafted by the Board;

SECTION 7. There shall be incorporated after Section 4 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, a new section to read as follows:

SECTION 4.B. COMMON PROVISIONS FOR INTERNET CAFE AND COIN OPERATED INTERNET MACHINE (COIM)

Internet Cafe and Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) shall follow the following guidelines in their operation:

4.B.1. These entities are required to secure a Business Permit and clearance required by the local government to operate.

SECTION 8. There shall be further incorporated after Section 4 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, a new section to read as follows:

SECTION 4-C. INTERNET CAFE
Internet Cafe shall follow the following guidelines in its operation:
4.C.1. Selling or drinking of intoxicating drinks, and selling of cigarettes are prohibited within its premises;
4.C.2. Internet Cafe offering a combination of services such as research and games shall either provide headphones for every unit. Internet Cafe establishments are not allowed to play loud music;
4.C.3. Internet Cafe must not use enclosed cubicle Cubicle may only be allowed if it conforms with the specifications provided by the Board;
4.C.4. Internet Cafe shall install and maintain undetachable cameras or similar devices for every computer unit;
4.C.5. Internet Cafe shall maintain a good sanitary environment. It shall provide separate restrooms for male and female use;
4.C.6. Internet Cafe shall not allow entry to any person, who are or appear to be under the influence of liquor, prohibited drugs, carrying firearms or any deadly weapon within its premises;
4.C.7. In all instances where access to internet is allowed under this ordinance, the same shall be within the hours of 8 o'clock in the morning to 10 o'clock in the evening of any given day for minors, except when accompanied by a parent, adult, or relative or guardian. A warning sign for that purpose shall be posted in a conspicuous and visible area within and without the immediate premises of the internet establishments;
4.C.8. Internet Cafe offering combinations of other services is required to secure separate business permits;
4.C.9. Internet Cafe shall secure a permit from the Board before hosting gaming tournaments;

**SECTION 9.** There shall be furthermore incorporated after Section 4 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, a new section to read as follows:

**SECTION 4-D. COIN OPERATED INTERNET MACHINE (COIM)**

A Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) shall follow the following guidelines in its operation:

4.D.1. Any person or establishment who owns and operates any Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) or "Piso-Piso Internet" shall not store any pornographic material, nor allow, promote or tolerate any person from storing or downloading any pornographic material;

4.D.2. The operation of this shall be limited from 8 A.M. to 10 P.M. only;

4.D.3. The owner of the place where the COIM is placed is deemed the owner thereof;

4.D.4. Each unit of Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) placed or operated in different location shall have its own business permit and accreditation.

5.2. Accreditation shall be a mandatory requirement prior to the issuance of business permit or license to operate;

5.3. Procedures for the application of accreditation in general:

5.3.1 Application with the Board;

5.3.4 Final approval of accreditation or denial by the board;

**SECTION 12. Section 8 of Ordinance No. 0227-04, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**SECTION 8. FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD**

The Board shall perform the following functions:

A. Formulate policies and criteria to serve as guidelines for accreditation of Internet Cafes and Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) in the city and monitoring of other establishments offering access to internet;

B. Accredite annually Internet Cafes and Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) thirty (30) days prior to the application or renewal of business permit and after complying with all the necessary requirements as provided by the Board;

C. Conduct monitoring activities and do ocular inspections, through the Business Bureau and/or barangay concerned, in pursuance of its functions in accrediting Internet Cafés and Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) in Davao City and the barangay concerned shall issue a corresponding certificate to the duly accredited Internet Café and Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM) that have been complying with the provisions of this Ordinance;

D. Recommend to the City Mayor, through the Business Bureau, the closure and/or suspension of business permit of any Internet Café, Coin Operated Internet Machine (COIM), and other establishments offering access to internet found violating the Ordinance;

E. Issue a citation ticket, through the Business Bureau, with the assistance of the barangay concerned for any actual or confirmed violation;

G. Program annually the disbursement collected subject to the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod;

H. Approved uniform filtering system to be applied;

K. Coordinate with the barangay concerned by the AEC President in the creation of their respective barangay task force for the purpose of ocular inspections and monitoring activities and to report to the Board any violation of this Ordinance, without prejudice to any report made by any concerned citizen which will reach the Board;

L. Formulate guidelines and/or implementing rules and regulations for the proper implementation of some of the pertinent provisions of this Ordinance subject to the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
