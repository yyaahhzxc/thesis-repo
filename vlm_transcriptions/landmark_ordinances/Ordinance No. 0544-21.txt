SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be referred to and cited as "The City Water Resources Office (CWRO) Ordinance".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES.

1. This Ordinance promotes the following basic principles:
A. Equitable access to safe drinking water;
B. Safe drinking water is an essential basic human right;
C. Water resource quality must be maintained for the ecosystem;
D. Recognize diversity in different geographical locations of water resources;
E. Consistency of water resource management;
F. Integrated water resource management is a process which promotes the coordinated development and the management of water, land and related resources, in order to maximize the resultant economic and social welfare in an equitable manner without compromising the sustainability of vital ecosystems.

2. Republic Act No. 7160
A. Section 16 provides that "every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."
B. Section 76 empowers local government units to design and implement its organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil Service Commission (CSC).

ARTICLE II Organization  
SECTION 1. THE CITY WATER RESOURCES OFFICE (CWRO)  
1. The City Water Resources Office (CWRO) is hereby created subject to this Ordinance and pursuant to national and local laws;  
2. The CWRO shall ensure that water resources of the city are managed, used, protected, conserved, developed, and controlled for sustainability;  
3. The CWRO shall adhere to the provisions of the constitution, national and local laws and policies pertaining to water resources;  
4. The CWRO shall abide by the principle of equitable access to safe and clean drinking water;  
5. The CWRO shall recognize the relevance of water in transforming the economy and eradicating poverty;  
6. The CWRO shall employ integrated management system meeting the balance between development and the environment in a sustainable manner;

--- [Page 9] ---
7. The CWRO shall act as a coordinating body of all data, benchmarks, indicators, researches, development and testing all water-related programs, activities, studies and projects of the city.

8. The CWRO shall administer necessary policy and resource reforms with respect to the management of water.

9. The CWRO shall monitor, analyze and evaluate compliance of city goals relating to water.

10. The CWRO shall promote or encourage sustainable development goals in relation to water which shall include, but not limited to:

A. Safe and clean drinking water in the city;

B. Availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation;

C. Sustainable consumption and production patterns;

D. Conservation and sustainable use of the oceans, seas and marine resources for sustainable development;

E. Sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems;

F. Peaceful and inclusive societies for sustainable development.

11. The CWRO shall improve conservation of water and increase system efficiencies.

SECTION 5. POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE CITY WATER RESOURCES OFFICE - The City Water Resources Office (CWRO) shall have the following powers and duties:

**SECTION 9. THE OPERATIONS DIVISION OF THE CITY WATER RESOURCES OFFICE**  
The Operations Division of the City Water Resources Office (CVRO) shall be composed of specialists, engineers, technicians, assistants, community extension workers, and other related field personnel. All data and activities shall be reviewed by the Science, Technology, and Laboratory Division, and shall be recorded at the Information and Management Division. The division shall include, but not limited to, the following sections and the respective functions:

**A. Drinking Water and Groundwater Section**

1. Provides technical support to community water systems, classification of the community water system into Level I, Level II or Level III, and periodic sampling of community water systems source for analysis and evaluation by the Science Technology and Laboratory Division;
2. Assists in the administration and implementation of the by-laws and regulations of community water systems and sanitation associations;
3. Periodically gathers water samples and other related specimen from designated locations of drinking water sources and distribution points;
4. Locates, surveys, and geo-tags possible sources of drinking water and groundwater;
5. Monitors the quality and quantity of possible drinking water sources and groundwater in specific locations;
6. Assists in the implementation of national drinking water standards;
7. Protects underground sources of drinking water;
8. Accomplishes water resource inventory for drinking water and groundwater of the city;
9. Performs other tasks that may be assigned from time to time pertaining to drinking and groundwater.

**B. Surface Water and Marine Water Section**

1. Periodically gathers water samples and other related specimen from specific sources of surface water and marine water;
2. Monitors the quality and quantity of identified surface water and marine water;
3. Observes and records factors relating to risk management and assessment;
4. Gathers physical, chemical, biological, and other related characteristics for analysis and evaluation as specified;
5. Monitors and records specified characteristics of identified coastal waters receiving bodies of water from major and minor river systems;
6. Monitors dumping and vessel discharges in surface and marine waters;
7. Assists in reducing aquatic trash and debris;
8. Accomplishes water resource inventory for surface and marine water of the city;
9. Performs other tasks that may be assigned from time to time pertaining to surface and marine water.

7. Evaluates and recommends instruments and equipment for optimum, efficient and sustainable utilization of water;
8. Coordinates with other offices for the planning and design of applications for water sources;
9. Coordinates with the Operations Division for the needed physical and engineering requirements;
10. Coordinates with the Information Management Division for database storage and utilization, reports, and other needed software applications;
11. Performs other tasks relating to engineering and physical characteristics of water as may be assigned from time to time.

C. Research and Development Section

The Division may assign personnel to perform research and development studies of water sources. Research proposals may be submitted to the office from other agencies, offices, and other organizations. All researches done in the office must be stored in a database for use by the public.
