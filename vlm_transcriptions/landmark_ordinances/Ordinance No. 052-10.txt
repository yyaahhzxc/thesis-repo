**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GROW MICRO PROGRAM - MAGNEGOSYO TA DAVAO PROJECT TO THE EFFECTED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR AND EMPLOYMENT (DOLE)**

**Page 2 of 3**

**Ord No 052-10**

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GROW MICRO PROGRAM "MAGNEGOSYO TA DAY" PROJECT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR AND EMPLOYMENT (DOLE)."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 455 (b) (1) (u) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over all project programs, projects, services and activities of the City Government, and in this connection, staff: Represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations; and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT. The Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) refers to the instrument executed by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) Region XI for the implementation of the Grow Micro Program "Mag Negosyo Ta Day" Project for the benefit of informal sector workers;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) between the former and the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) Region XI for the implementation of the Grow Micro Program "Mag Negosyo Ta Day" Project for the benefit of informal sector workers;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other section or provision shall be affected thereby;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 10, 2010, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

MARGARITO C. DELA CRUZ Acting Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Assistant Department Head II)

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement entered into by and between:
The DEPARTMENT OF LABOR AND EMPLOYMENT (DOLE) XI, with office address at LDL Building, Bangoy Street, Davao City, represented in this act by its Regional Director, Atty. Jallo O. Dela Torre, hereinafter referred to as the First Party;
and-
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO with office address at City Hall of Davao, Davao City, represented in this act by its City Mayor, the Honorable Sara Z. Duterte, referred to as the Second Party.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, in the interest of service and in support of the government's thrust to reduce poverty through micro-entrepreneurial activity, DOLE RO XI has developed the sustainable partnership to the growth of informal sector through micro-enterprise development program briefly known as "Grow Micro Program" for the benefit of informal sector workers;

WHEREAS, the informal sector workers are considered to be economic units outside the scope of legal and social protection because they are not normally registered and with very low levels of income and productivity, such as ambulant vendors, alternative transport workers like drivers of tricycles, sandtricycles, school bus drivers, street vendors, street cleaners, street lighting plumbers, laundry workers, and the like who constitute nearly half of the country's employed;

WHEREAS, the program aims to bring about viable and sustainable business for informal sector workers to stimulate the local economy and alternative poverty for the marginalized constituents;

WHEREAS, the parties believe that with holistic support from the national and local governments, the informal sector can be mobilized as a significant contributor to Davao City's growth and development;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing, the parties hereto have agreed to enter into this Agreement and commit to do the following in line with their mandates, capabilities, program and services:

--- [Page 5] ---
I. ROLE OF PARTIES

The FIRST PARTY shall:

1. Provide program coordination, advocacy, promotion, monitoring and supervision of the "Grow Micro Program".

2. Chair the Project Management Team of the program.

3. Provide technical, managerial, logistical, and financial assistance in building their capabilities for Incubation Center management and in providing livelihood skills and assistance to the informal sector identified by the institution upon approval of a project proposal or business plan by the Project Management Team.

4. Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of the program in generating or creating jobs.

5. Determine, allocate and release to the Second Party the entire budget necessary for the "Grow Micro Program" which shall be distributed and apportioned by the latter to the different project proposals for capacity-building, enhancement, livelihood and other enterprise development projects submitted by the City Government of Davao and other accredited consults;

The SECOND PARTY shall:

1. Provide the space and facilities of the Micro Business Incubation Center which shall have the following minimum requirements:

a. An office space of at least 10x10 feet in size

b. Internet connected computer

c. TV and DVD player for film showing and sound system

d. Business books and entrepreneurship-related books, journals, manuals and magazines

e. Business information resource finder

f. At least 2 staff capable of providing training services on:

1. Business management and production skills

2. Productivity

3. Business advisory

4. Marketing strategies

5. Confidence building

6. And other trainings needed by the informal sector

g. Telephone for communication

h. Furniture and fixtures to receive clients comfortably

It may use an existing office space and facilities of an appropriate department, division, or office in the City Government of Davao.

2. Receive from the First Party the entire budget set aside, assigned, or allotted for the "Grow Micro Program", apportion the same to the different approved project proposals for capacity-building, enhancement, livelihood and other enterprise development project, and release or distribute said amounts under the Livelihood Assistance Program pursuant to the guidelines set forth under Executive Order No. 17 other than Executive Orders 18, 19, AND 20, SERIES OF 1997, THE RULES AND PROCEDURES IN THE GRANT OF ASSISTANCE UNDER THE LIVELIHOOD ASSISTANCE PROGRAM OF THE DAVAO CITY AND AMENDING ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO.2, DATED JULY 12, 1999 WHICH PRESCRIBES THE GUIDELINES, SYSTEMS AND PROCEDURES IN THE GRANT OF COOPERATIVE

--- [Page 6] ---
DEVELOPMENT LOAN ASSISTANCE AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES as amended by Executive Order No. 08;
3. Make arrangements for the availability of business mentors from the private sector or the Davao City Chamber of Commerce & Industry, Inc. who will serve as business incubation center resource persons;
4. Conduct rapid assessment and profiling survey of the Informal Sector Workers in Davao City;
5. Sustain the operations of the Micro Business Incubation Center when turned over to the City Government of DOLE XI;
6. Identify the project beneficiaries and endorse their proposals to the Project Management Team;
7. Submit regular financial and narrative reports to the Project Management Team;
8. Designate and appropriate City Government Department or Division or Office head to serve as Incubation Center Manager.
II. AMENDMENTS AND EFFECTIVITY
1. Any and all previous agreements between the parties or parts thereof which are inconsistent with this Memorandum of Agreement shall be subject to the mutual consent of the parties hereto;
2. Any modification or amendment to this Agreement shall be subject to the mutual consent of the parties hereto;
3. This agreement takes effect upon signing of the parties hereto.
