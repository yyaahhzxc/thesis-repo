Ord. No. 0134-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN THE PERFORMANCE COMMITMENT FOR HClIS (REVISION 2) AND PROVIDER DATA RECORD HEALTH CARE INSTITUTION OF TORIL HEALTH CENTER BIRTHING HOME FOR ITS APPLICATION FOR PHILHEALTH ACCREDITATION FOR MATERNITY CARE."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod of pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Performance Commitment for HClIs (Revision 2) and Provider Data Record Health Care Institution. The said documents are necessary for the application of Toril Health Center Birthing Home for Philhealth accreditation for its maternity care package;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 10th of January 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod present.

E. REGULAR SURVEYS / ADMINISTRATIVE INVESTIGATIONS / DOMICILIARY VISITATIONS ON THE CONDUCT OF OPERATIONS IN THE EXERCISE OF THE PRIVILEGE OF ACCREDITATION

33. That we shall extend full cooperation with duly recognized authorities of PhilHealth and any other authorized personnel and instrumentalities to provide access to patient records and submit to any orderly assessment conducted by PhilHealth relative to any findings, adverse reports, pattern of utilization (or any other information) that may be prejudicial or detrimental to the NHP, as an accredited HCI of the NHP that may be prejudicial or tends to undermine the NHP and make available all pertinent official records and documents including the provision of copies thereof; provided that our rights to private ownership and privacy are respected at all times.

34. That we shall ensure that our officers, employees and personnel extend full cooperation and due courtesy to all PhilHealth officers, employees and staff during the conduct of assessment/visitation/investigation/monitoring of our operations as an accredited HCI of the NHP.

35. That at any time during the period of our participation in the NHP, upon request of PhilHealth, we shall voluntarily sign and execute any new "Performance Commitment" to cover the remaining portion of our indemnity insurance coverage for the remainder of the term of the NHP if the case may be, as a sign of our good faith and continuous commitment to support the NHP.

36. That, unless proven to be a palpable mistake or excusable error, we shall take full responsibility for any inaccuracies and/or falsities entered into and/or reflected in our patients' records as well as in any omission, addition, inaccuracies and/or falsities entered into and/or reflected in claims submitted to PhilHealth by our institution.

37. That we shall comply with PhilHealth's summons, subpoenas, subpœnae, "duces tecum" and other legal or quality assurance processes and requirements.

38. That we shall recognize the authority of PhilHealth, its Officers and personnel and/or its duly authorized representatives to inspect, investigate, review, monitor, supervise, consult, review, and/or conduct administrative assessments at any reasonable time relative to the exercise of our privilege and conduct of our operations as an accredited HCI of the NHP.

39. That we shall comply with PhilHealth corrective actions given after monitoring activities within the prescribed period.

F. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

40. This we shall protect the NHP against abuse, violation and/or over-utilization of its funds and we shall not allow our institution to be a party to any act, scheme, plan, or contract that may directly or indirectly be prejudicial or detrimental to the NHP.

41. That we shall not directly or indirectly engage in any form of unethical or improper practices as an accredited health care provider such as but not limited to solicitation of patients for purposes of compensation under the NHP, the purpose and/or the end consideration of which tends unnecessary financial gain rather than promotion of the NHP.

42. That we shall immediately report to PhilHealth, its Officers and/or to any of its personnel, any act of illegal, improper and/or unethical practices of HCI of the NHP that may have come to our knowledge directly or indirectly.

43. That we shall allow PhilHealth to deduct or charge to our future claims, all reimbursements paid to our institution under the following, but not limited to: (a) during the period of its non-accredited status as a result of a gap in validity of our DOH LTO, suspension of accreditation, etc.; (b) downgrading of level, loss of license for certain services; (c) when NHI eligible, PhilHealth members and their dependents were made to pay out-of-pocket for HCI and professional fees, if applicable; (d) validated claims of under declaration of PhilHealth benefits.

Furthermore, recognizing and respecting its indispensable role in the NHP, we hereby acknowledge the power and authority of PhilHealth to do the following:

44. After the process and in accordance with the pertinent provisions of R.A. 7875 and its IRR, to suspend, shorten, pre-terminate, and/or revoke our privilege of participating in the NHP including the appurtenant benefits and opportunities at any time during the validity of the commitment for any violation of any provision of this Performance Commitment and of R.A. 7875 and its IRR.
