Ord. No. 0303-23  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE LESS PAPER SESSION SYSTEM THROUGH THE UPGRADING OF THE LEGISLATIVE INFORMATION SUPPORT SYSTEM PROGRAM (LISSP) OF THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD".  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Pursuant to Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, which states that:  

"Section 16. General Welfare - Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare."  

SECTION 3. LESS PAPER SESSION SYSTEM - This refers to the method of conduct of the session day wherein Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod will be able to access their agenda through their respective emails registered with the Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod. The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is required to produce only three (3) copies of the Agenda and all its attachments to reduce the utilization of print materials as a cost-effective and environment-friendly measure. The Agenda and all Committee Reports, attachments and items scheduled on a particular day shall be converted to digital copies which will be flashed on screen for the viewing benefit of everyone inside the session hall. All Committee Reports, attachments, correspondence, and all other matters for inclusion on a particular day shall be sent through electronic mail (email) which contains the Digital signatures of the respective Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.  

SECTION 4. THE LEGISLATIVE INFORMATION SUPPORT SYSTEM PROGRAM (LISSP)  
1. A Cloud subscription shall be procured to provide for the storage of all archived electronic files of the session's Agenda, attachments, and each shall be duly identified by date of the session day;  
2. The same shall be managed by the designated staff to be determined by the Secretary to the Sanggunian;  
3. The implementation of the LISSP will be in two (2) phases wherein 1st Phase includes the following:  
a. Conversion of status of all items included in the Agenda, for items referred to respective committees and the status of the items for committee hearings;  
b. Tracking of items, committee hearings and all other matters submitted to the SP Secretariat for Indorsement to the respective committees of the Sanggunian.

Phase 2 Implementation shall include the following:

a. Conversion of paper documents to digital form to be used during SP Sessions, preparation of Agenda, Committee Reports, Stenographic Notes, and other attached documents to a central Cloud Storage;

b. Access of the items in the Agenda through the cloud storage which shall be available to all SP Members and designated personnel of the Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod;

c. Use of Digital Signature shall be made by the SP Member through his/her designated personnel for submission of Committee Reports, communications and attachments, to be included in the Agenda;

d. The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall provide a system for the tracking of documents, retrieval and storage, monitoring reports, and feedback reports;

e. Create an archive for all approved resolutions and ordinances, and provide sufficient identification, for easy navigation and search.

SECTION 5. APPROPRIATION: To fully implement the "Less Paper Session System", acquisition, procurement and installation of the following items shall be included in the budget appropriation of the Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod:

1. (30) Units PORTABLE DEVICE;
2. (8) Units Laptops;
3. (1) document camera;
4. (6) Units Access Points;
5. Cloud Subscription;
6. (1) Unit New Generation Firewall;
7. Enterprise Broadband Internet Access;
8. Development Cost for implementation of LISSP Phase 2.

All recurring annual expenses shall be included in the Annual Fund Budget of the Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

SECTION 6. IMPLEMENTING RULES: The Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall formulate the necessary guidelines for the effective operation of the Less Paper Session Implementation any time after the effectivity of the Ordinance.

SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE: All resolutions, Ordinances, executive orders, rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
