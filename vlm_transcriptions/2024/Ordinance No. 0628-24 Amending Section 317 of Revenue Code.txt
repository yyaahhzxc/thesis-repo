Ord. No. 0628-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 317 OF ORDINANCE NO. 0291-17, SERIES OF 2017, ENTITLED 'AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE 2017 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO' FOR THE IMPOSITION OF RENTAL FEES FOR THE FIFTH LEVEL COMPARTMENTALIZED NICHES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 2 and 3 of Republic Act No. 9465 as amended by Republic Act No. 11032 also known as the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018, states:

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby declared the policy of the State to promote integrity, accountability, proper management of public affairs and public property as well as to establish effective practices, aimed at efficient turnaround of the delivery of government services and the prevention of graft and corruption in government. Toward this end, the State shall maintain transparency in the conduct of its business with qualified employees, and shall take appropriate measures to promote transparency in each agency with regard to the manner of transacting with the public, which shall encompass a program for the adoption of simplified requirements and procedures that will reduce red tape and expedite business and nonbusiness related transactions in government.

Section 3. Coverage. - This Act shall apply to all government offices and agencies including Local Government Units (LGUs), government-owned or controlled corporations and other government instrumentalities, whether located in the Philippines or abroad, that provide services covering business and nonbusiness related transactions as defined in this Act.

SECTION 3. ADOPTION - The amendment to Section 317 Imposition of Fees of Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, entitled "An Ordinance Amending the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao, otherwise known as the 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao" shall form part of this Ordinance by adding one level to the same:

FROM:

xxx

B. Compartmentalized Niches Rental Fee
- Ground Level: Php 7,000.00
- Second Level: Php 8,800.00
- Third Level: Php 7,920.00
- Fourth Level: Php 6,160.00

TO:
- Ground Level: Php 7,000.00
- Second Level: Php 8,800.00
- Third Level: Php 7,920.00
- Fourth Level: Php 6,160.00
- Fifth Level: Php 5,280.00
