ORDINANCE NO. 0719-24  
Series of 2024  

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 111, SERIES OF 1986, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 510, SERIES OF 1979, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING ANY PERSON FROM URBANIZING AND/OR DEFEACATING WITHIN THE PUBLIC VIEW IN ANY STREET, ALLEY, HIGHWAY, AND ANY PLACE WITHIN THE VICINITY OF ANY PUBLIC BUILDING IN DAVAO CITY AND PROVIDING PENALTIES IN CASE OF VIOLATION THEREOF

Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No. 0719-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "ANTI-URINATING AND/OR DEFEACATING WITHIN PUBLIC VIEW ORDINANCE OF 2024".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS - The following provisions shall be amended to read as follows:

"Section 2. PROHIBITED ACTS - It shall be unlawful for any person to urinate and/or defecate within the public view in any street, alley, highway, and other public places not intended for urination, and any place within the vicinity of any public building in Davao City.

Public places shall refer to streets and alleys, public parks, schools, buildings, malls, bars, restaurants, transportation terminals, public markets, spaces used as evacuation centers, government offices, public utility vehicles as well as private vehicles covered by app- blications for motor vehicle licenses and other recreational spaces such as, but not limited to, cinemas, halls, theaters, and spas. (Sec. 3, Republic Act No. 11313)

SECTION 3. PENAL CLAUSE - Any person violating Section 2 of this Ordinance shall, upon conviction, be punished by a fine of Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00) and/or imprisonment of not more than ten (10) days or both at the discretion of the Court.

SECTION 4. NO CONTEST PROVISION - A violator of this Ordinance who is cited for a violation and who does not wish to contest the offense and is willing to pay the imposed administrative penalty of Two Hundred Pesos (P200.00) shall pay the same to the City Treasurer's Office before the filing of formal charges with the proper court seated within three (3) days from apprehension, provided that the no contest provision can only be availed once.

All proceeds from the payment of the herein administrative penalty shall be apportioned as follows:

1. Sixty percent (60%) of the penalty collected per apprehension shall accrue to the apprehending office/agency;

2. Forty percent (40%) shall accrue to the General Fund of the City.

SECTION 5. IMPLEMENTING AGENCY - The following are directed to enforce and implement this Ordinance strictly:

a) Barangay officials duly appointed by the City Health Office (CHO) after undergoing the proper training and seminar;

b) Davao City Police Office (DCPO)."

SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE - All ordinances, rules, orders, and regulations or parts thereof contrary to or inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed.
