Ord. No. 0555-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
FOR THE PARTIAL TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC
(SINGULAR) ORDER OF THE STREETS ALONG DAVAO COASTAL ROAD
FROM JUNCTION ECONOMY DEVELOPMENT PARK TO JUNCTION BAGO
APLAYA-MC ARTHUR HIGHWAY SOUTHBOUND DIRECTIONS (DAVAO
SIDE) ON NOVEMBER 24, 2024, (SUNDAY) FROM 2:30 AM UP TO 8:00 AM
IN CONNECTION WITH THE ANDRES ULTRAMARATHON SEASON 3, IN
PARTNERSHIP WITH THE PHILIPPINE DRUG ENFORCEMENT AGENCY
(PDEA-DAVAO XI)". 

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act
No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that
any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during
an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial
fairssxx.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE - In connection with the
abovementioned request, Mr. Nick Pilapil, Race Director of Team Titans- Davaos
requested for the partial temporary closure to vehicular traffic of the aforesaid
roads on the time and date herein indicated;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections
or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force
and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately
upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 29th day of October 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
