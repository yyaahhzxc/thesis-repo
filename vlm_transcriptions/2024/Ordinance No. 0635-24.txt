Ord No. 0635-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF VEHICULAR TRAFFIC A PORTION OF THE COASTAL ROAD (HALF LANE ONLY) ON SEPTEMBER 28, 2024, FROM 8:00 PM UP TO 12:00 MIDNIGHT FOR THE PREPARATION AND SET-UP, AND ON SEPTEMBER 29, 2024, FROM 12:01 AM UP TO 8:00 AM ONLY FOR THE EVENTS PROPER, IN CONNECTION WITH THE REQUEST OF DR. ERWIN M. FALLER, PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINE PHARMACISTS ASSOCIATION, INC., CITY CHAPTER IN THEIR UPCOMING EVENT DUBBED AS, "PHARMA RUN 2024/RUN FOR HEALTH", IN CELEBRATION OF THE WORLD PHARMACISTS DAY 2024".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs xxx".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the upcoming "PHARMA RUN 2024: RUN FOR HEALTH" in celebration of the World Pharmacists Day 2024, Dr. Erwin M. Faller requested for a temporary road closure.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 10th day of September 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
