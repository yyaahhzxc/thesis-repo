ORDINANCE NO. 0710-24
Series of 2024

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ARTICLE 16 (OPERATIONS OF PEDAL-OPERATED TRICYCLES) OF ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, SERIES OF 2012, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "COMPREHENSIVE TRAFFIC CODE OF DAVAO CITY" BY INCLUDING THE DEFINITION OF THE WORD NON-CONVEX OR NON-CONVEX-OPERATED TRICYCLES UNDER ARTICLE 2, SECTION 5 (41) ("DEFINITION OF TERMS")

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ARTICLE 16 (OPERATIONS OF PEDAL-OPERATED TRICYCLES) OF ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, SERIES OF 2012, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "COMPREHENSIVE TRAFFIC CODE OF DAVAO CITY" BY INCLUDING THE DEFINITION OF THE WORD "NON-CONVENTIONAL PEDAL-OPERATED TRICYCLES" UNDER ARTICLE 2, SECTION 5 (41) (DEFINITION OF TERMS)".

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVES

1. As of the moment, "Non-Conventional Pedal-Operated 'Tricycles' type of public transport vehicle is not yet recognized considering that its specifications and terms are not yet included in the present Ordinance No. 0334-12, Series of 2012, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive Traffic Code of Davao City".

2. To make this plausible, there is need to amend Article 16 of Ordinance No. 0334-12, Series of 2012, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive Traffic Code of Davao City" (Operations of Pedal-Operated Tricycles) by including the word "Non-Conventional Pedal-Operated Tricycles" under Article 2, Section 5 (41) (DEFINITION OF TERMS);

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT

Article 16 of Ordinance No. 0334-12, Series of 2012, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive Traffic Code of Davao City" (Operations of Pedal-Operated Tricycles) to include the word "Non-Conventional Pedal-Operated Tricycle" under Article 2, Section 5 (41) (DEFINITION OF TERMS) is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Non-Conventional Pedal-Operated Tricycle - a vehicle having three (3) wheels and operated by means of a pedal and/or electricity, battery, or any other Non-Conventional means, to include a bicycle with sidecar".

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASIS

Section 54 of RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, states:

Section 54 - Approval of Ordinances. 

(a) Every Ordinance enacted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, shall be presented to the City Mayor, as the case may be. If the local chief executive concerned approves the same, he shall affix his signature on each and every page thereof; otherwise, he shall veto and return the same with his objections to the City Mayor, which will proceed to the Sanggunian. The Sanggunian concurrence shall override the veto of the local chief executive by two-thirds (2/3) vote of all its members, thereby making the ordinance or resolution effective for all legal intents and purposes.
