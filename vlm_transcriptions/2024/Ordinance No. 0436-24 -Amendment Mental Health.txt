Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 7 AND 8 OF ORDINANCE NO. 0721-19, SERIES OF 2019, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR A MENTAL HEALTH PROGRAM AND DELIVERY SYSTEM IN DAVAO CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES - The Human Resource Management Office (HRMO) made the following comments and recommendations:

1. The creation of Mental Health Division would affect the Organizational Structure and Organizational Functions of the City Health Office as a whole. As such, the creation must be conducted in coordination with the office concerned;

2. The functions of the proposed division must be clearly defined whether it would be coordinative/referal treatment or both. Section 6 of the proposed Ordinance would seem to indicate that it is both. In addition, provisions should be drawn as to when the city would come in with regard to the treatment of prospective patients;

3. Per record, death (hard-to-fill) positions which are highly specialized in nature as in the case of Psychiatrists have a very low turn-out of applicants. Further, under the Index of Occupational Services, the position of Psychiatrist does not exist. Hence, we can only use the equivalent position title of Medical Specialist III (SG-24);

A. Civil Service Commission (CSC) Resolution No. 1800692 (Revised Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions) dated July 03, 2018, approved amendments and additional provisions of CSC Resolution No. 1701009 of June 16, 2017 (Adoption and Promulgation of 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions);

B. Civil Service Commission (CSC) Memorandum Circular (MC) No. 19, Series of 1992, provides the "Guidelines and Standards in the Establishment of Organizational Structures and Staffing Patterns in Local Government Units";

C. DBM Budget Circular No. 2022-2 released on April 5, 2022, the subject of which is the "Index of Occupational Services, Occupational Groups, Classes and Salary Grades (IDS), CY 2022 Edition" states:

"2.0 As a Component of the Compensation and Position Classification System (CPCS), the Index of Occupational Services, Occupational Groups, Classes and Salary Grades (IDS for brevity) contains the list of classes or position titles existing in the government that are grouped by occupation. The IDS shall continually serve as the guide for national government agencies and certain GOCCs in determining the appropriate positions that will comprise their respective staffing pattern.

3.0 Conversely, in view of the peculiarity of its position classification plan, a separate IDS for local government units was issued under Local Budget Circular No. 137, dated July 13, 2021."

**SECTION 4. AMENDMENTS TO ORDINANCE NO. 0721-19 SERIES OF 2019**  
Sections 7 and 8 shall be amended and restated as follows:

A. Section 7 – The Mental Health Division under Ordinance No. 0721-19, Series of 2019, shall be amended and restated to read as:

Section 7. MENTAL HEALTH DIVISION – A Mental Health Division is hereby created under the City Health Office, to provide consistent and accessible mental healthcare services to those needing mental healthcare, counseling and treatment.

B. Section 8 – The Duties and Functions under Ordinance No. 0721-19, Series of 2019, shall be amended and restated to read as:

Section 8. DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS – The Mental Health Division shall:

1. Review and formulate guidelines in all matters relative to the operation of the Mental Health Awareness Program of the City;

2. Develop and implement Mental Treatment Plans and Programs;

3. Conduct regular monitoring and evaluation of clients undergoing treatment, care or counseling;
4. Conduct Capacity Building, Reorientation Training among the keyholders and staff, community health workers and others who are involved in the implementation of Mental Healthcare Programs of the division to improve the skills and to further enhance the delivery of services;
5. Conduct Research and Development activities in collaboration with academic institutions, mental health associations, and non-government organizations for the development of mental health services;
6. Promote and facilitate collaboration among sectors for the development and implementation of mental health related programs;
7. Create and implement mental health programs for government employees;
8. Create/Develop a database system for registration of persons with Mental, Neurological and Substance Abuse Disorder;
9. Implement other programs and projects relative to Mental Health Wellness as approved by the City Mayor;
10. Does other functions relative to the Mental Health Program of the City.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - Any provision of this Ordinance nullified by any court of law shall not affect the validity of the remaining provisions hereof not affected by the said judicial declaration.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

ENACTED, on the 5th day of March 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
