Ord. No. 0567-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION MADE AND EXECUTED BY 8990 HOUSING DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION IN FAVOR OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF ROAD LOTS, OPEN SPACES, AND IMPROVEMENTS OF DECA HOMES RESORT RESIDENCES PHASE 12, LOCATED IN BARANGAY TACUNAN, TUBGOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Rule VII, Section 33 of Batas Pambansa Blg. 220 provides for donations of roads and open spaces, which reads:

"The owner or project developer shall donate the roads and open spaces found in the project to the local government of the area after their completion had been certified by to the Commission and it shall be mandatory for the said local government to accept such donations"

"Sections 22 (a) (5) (c) and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provide, to wit:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:

(5) To enter into contracts; and

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.

**Ord. No. 0567-24**

**SECTION 3. COVERAGE AND AUTHORITY** - This Ordinance shall cover the grant of authority to the City Mayor to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation made and executed by 8990 Housing Development Corporation in favor of the City Government of Davao, relative to the donation of road lots, open spaces, and improvements of Deca Homes Resort Residences Phases 12, located in Barangay Tacunan, Tuguegarao District, this City.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE** - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED** on the 16th day of July 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
