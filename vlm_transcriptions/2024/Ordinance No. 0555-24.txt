Ord. No. 0555-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC FROM 3:00 PM ON JULY 20 UP TO 9:00 AM ON JULY 21, 2024 (SUNDAY) ALONG THE AREA OF CORNER PONCiano ST.- PALMA GIL ST. TO PELATO ST. IN CONNECTION WITH THE 4TH EDITION TDS 65K ULTRAMARATHON "STRIDE TO SURVIVE" FOR A CAUSE BY STRIDERS RUNNING CLUB OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any nation, city, or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fair or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and water works projects."

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The Davao City Striders Sports Clinic and Event Organizer in coordination with the Davao Striders Running Club will be holding its ultramarathon, half-marathon, and fun run events for a cause on July 20-21, 2024. In connection therewith, Mr. Richard P. Comajig, Race Organizer/President Team Davao Striders was requested for a road closure from the City Council.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 9th day of July 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang, there being a quorum.
