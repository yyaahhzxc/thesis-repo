Ord. No. 0717-24  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTING JANUARY AS BIBLE MONTH IN DAVAO CITY".  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of Davao City to recognize and celebrate the cultural and spiritual heritage of its people. In support of this commitment, Davao City hereby declares the month of January as Bible Month, an annual observance promoting the teachings, values, and wisdom of the Bible as part of Davao's cultural and moral development.  

SECTION 3. OBJECTIVES - The objectives of this Ordinance are as follows:  

a) To encourage reflection on the teachings of the Bible and its relevance in promoting peace, unity, and moral integrity in Davao City;  

b) To promote interfaith understanding and respect for religious diversity within the City;  

c) To inspire community engagement through organized activities that honor the Bible and encourage values formation across all sectors.  

SECTION 4. BIBLE MONTH ACTIVITIES - During the Bible Month, the following activities are encouraged across Davao City:  

1. Local Bible-believing institutions/organizations and schools shall be encouraged to hold special Bible studies, youth programs, and family-centered activities that emphasize values formation;  

2. The City Government, in partnership with the private sector, Bible-believing institutions/organizations, and NGOs, shall coordinate city-wide events such as Bible exhibits, poster-making contests, seminars, and reflections that promote unity, moral values, and other activities;  

3. All Barangay Councils in Davao City are encouraged to support and participate in Bible Month activities to foster a spirit of community, love, and service.  

SECTION 5. IMPLEMENTING AGENCY - The implementation of this Ordinance shall be under the Office of the City Mayor through the Moral Recovery Council, in coordination with the National Bible Ecumenical Team. These agencies shall encourage collaboration among educational, cultural, and Bible-believing institutions/organizations to ensure the effective observance of the Bible Month.  

SECTION 6. FUNDING - Funds shall be allocated for the effective implementation of this Ordinance and subsequent annual budget shall be necessary subject to the accounting rules and regulations.

**SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If, for any reason part of the provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the same shall not affect the validity of the other provisions hereof.

**SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE -** All previous ordinances, rules and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance shall be deemed repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY -** This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in any newspaper of general circulation in the City.

**ENACTED -** on the 5th day of December 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
