Ord. No. 0640-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE ESPLANADE PORTION (JOGGING AREA OF THE MAIN ROAD) ALONG COASTAL ROAD ON SEPTEMBER 28, 2024, (SATURDAY) FROM 4:00AM UP TO 8:00AM FOR FAMILY FUN RUN 2024 IN CELEBRATION OF THE 32ND NATIONAL FAMILY WEEK".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7169, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs xxx".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the upcoming 32nd NATIONAL FAMILY WEEK, Mr. Elder Bartolome Madriga, Area Seventy and Priesthood Adviser, DCCC, requested for the temporary closure of the aforementioned road for the specified purpose.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 24th day of September 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
