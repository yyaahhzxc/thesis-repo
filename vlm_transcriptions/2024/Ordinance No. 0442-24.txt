1st INDORSEMENT  
May 14, 2024  

Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the herein attached documents relate to the Ordinance No. 0442-24 Series of 2024 entitled "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO AND CI-PHILIPPINES IN RELATION TO THEIR INTENT TO COLLABORATE AND DONATE GIFT-IN-KIND TO THE SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT OFFICE TO AUGMENT ITS HEALTH AND HYGIENE PROGRAMS" with the intent to augment the health and hygiene programs of the Social Welfare and Development Office of the City of Davao. The enactment of the same is well within the realm of Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991.

It is further informed that the subject ordinance is a Memorandum of Agreement and not a donation. Thus, it is considered the view of this office that a Deed of Donation should be subsequently formalized, outlining precise specifications, including quantity and description of the goods to be donated, in favor of the city.

IN VIEW THEREOF, this office recommends the approval of the ordinance, and the Memorandum of Agreement be executed.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND CI-PHILIPPINES IN RELATION TO THEIR INTENT TO COLLABORATE AND DONATE GIFT-IN-KIND TO THE CITY SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT OFFICE TO AUGMENT ITS HEALTH AND HYGIENE PROGRAMS."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22 (a) (5) (c) and 455 (b)

(1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provide:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
5) To enter into contracts;
and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

"Section 455 Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor, hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and CI-Philippines in relation to their intent to collaborate and donate gift-in-kind to the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) to augment its health and hygiene programs.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS

This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into this day of 2023 in Davao City between and among:

The CI-Philippines, Inc. is non-stock corporation duly constituted under Philippine laws with principal office address at Block 10, Lot 19, Skylark Street, Zabarte Subd., Kaligayahan, Novaliches, Quezon City, represented by its Head and Chief of Programs, REBECCA J. FUELLOS.

And

The CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of Philippines, with principal office address at City Hall Bldg., San Pedro St., Davao City, herein represented by its City Mayor, HON. SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the LGU.

WITNESSETH:

Whereas, CI-Philippines, Inc. is affiliated with Children International, a global charitable organization based in Kansas City, Missouri, U.S.A., is duly registered by the DSWD;

Whereas, CI-Philippines, Inc. is a non-profit organization that supports programs on health and hygiene for marginalized, vulnerable individuals, families, and communities in collaboration with selected partners from the local government units, schools, public health facilities and non-profit organizations;

Whereas, The Parties have expressed their common interest and shared commitment in working collaboratively as development partners in the conduct of the above-mentioned undertakings that are aligned and consistent with existing laws.

Whereas, the City Social Welfare Development Office of Davao City, is willing to collaborate with CI-Philippines and avail of Gift-in-Kind donations to support and augment their health and hygiene programs.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and the terms and conditions herein set forth, The Parties have agreed to institutionalize its partnership under the program and commit to the following:

I. UNDERTAKINGS OF THE CI-PHILIPPINES, INC

CI-Philippines, Inc shall:

1. Provide available commodities as donations in support of health and hygiene programs use of commodities for humanitarian assistance, and for post-disaster response;

2. Ensure that the donated goods pass through Government Regulating Agencies such as the Department of Health, Food and Drug Administration, and other regulating agencies depending on the type of goods.

--- [Page 10] ---
3. Responsible for the shipping cost from point of origin to designated Philippine ports and processing/release from the Bureau of Customs.

4. Participate in the inventory, allocation, and distribution of goods to sub-partners and selected beneficiaries.

5. Conducts joint monitoring visits with the CSWDO-Davao City on selected beneficiaries.

II. UNDERTAKINGS OF THE PARTNER ORGANIZATION

Shall:

1. Submit the distribution plan to CI-Philippines for approval.

2. Shoulder the transportation cost from CIP's warehouse to the partner's designated temporary storage area.

3. Provide a safe, hygienic, and adequate storage facility for the temporary storage of the donated goods until distribution as per the approved distribution plan.

4. The donated goods must not be used for any political undertakings or personal interests.

5. During distribution, when there are several donors apart from Children International, the use of the Donor's logo should not be too close to each other. The logo shall not also be unreasonably big and shall not contain images or pictures of political personalities.

6. For the distribution of commodities, generic bags shall be used and shall not contain markings related to political personalities and parties or organizations.

7. Update the status distribution must be submitted to CI-Philippines Inc after 60 days upon receipt of goods.

8. Ensure that donated Gift-In kind items shall not be sold or offered for sale and that the same shall be distributed at no cost on the part of qualified beneficiaries.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereby affix their signatures this day of OCTOBER 1, 2023, in Davao City.
