ORDINANCE NO. 0397-24
Series of 2024

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH-DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT, THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT, AND PERHAPS MAE L. BARRERA, A PERSONNEL UNDER THE RURAL HEALTH MIDWIVES PLACEMENT PROGRAM (RHMP)

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No.: 0397-24  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH-DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT, THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT, AND FERLY MAE L. BARRERA, A PERSONNEL UNDER THE RURAL HEALTH MIDWIVES PLACEMENT PROGRAM (RHMP)".  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 445 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:  

"Section 445. Chief Executive; Duties and Compensation.  
Xxx XXX XXX  
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:  
Xxx XXX XXX  
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."  

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and among the Department of Health-Davao Center for Health Development, the City of Davao, and Ferly Mae L. Barrera, a personnel under the Rural Health Midwives Placement Program (RHMP).  

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.  

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.  

ENACTED, on the 25th day of January 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang, there being a quorum.
