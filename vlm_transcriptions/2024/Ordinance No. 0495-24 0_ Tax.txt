Ord. No. 0495-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING ZERO PERCENT (0%) TAX ON THE LOCAL BUSINESS TAX OF THE JAPANESE OFFICIAL DEVELOPMENT ASSISTANCE-FUNDED PROJECTS IN DAVAO CITY SUBJECT TO ALL LAWS AND EXISTING LEGAL RULES AND REGULATIONS".

SECTION 2. TAX RATE - A zero percent (0%) tax on the local business tax of the contractors and other independent contractors under Ordinance No. 0291- 17, as amended by Ordinance No. 0147-19, shall be imposed against a project proven to be funded by the Japan-Official Development Assistance.

SECTION 3. CONDITIONS - In order to be granted the privilege imposed in Section 1, the applicant must submit to the City Treasurer the following:

1. Confirmation from the Ambassador of Japan of the inclusion of the project in the current and updated list of Japan-ODA funded projects; and

2. Proof of payment of tax obligations prior to the enactment of this Ordinance.

The applicant may, in the absence of the above-stated documents, submit such other documents as may be prescribed by the City Treasurer in order to be granted the privilege as prescribed in Section 2.

The burden of proof lies upon the applicant to prove the factual basis of its claim for application under this Ordinance. In case of doubt, this Ordinance shall be strictly construed against the applicant.

SECTION 4. DUTY OF THE CITY TREASURER - The City Treasurer shall be responsible to:

1. Provide for an official list of documents necessary to prove the authenticity of statements presented by the applicant;

2. Ensure the inclusion of the projects to be exempt in the official list of Japan-ODA funded projects; and

3. Issue a clearance stating the fact that the applicant has no standing tax obligations with the City.

SECTION 5. REPORTING REQUIREMENT - Applicants granted the Zero Percent (0%) tax privilege shall submit an annual report to the City Treasurer, detailing the progress and financial status of the Japan-ODA funded project.

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances or portions of Ordinances in conflict with this Ordinance, or inconsistent with the regulations of this Ordinance, are hereby repealed to the extent necessary to give this Ordinance full force and effect.
