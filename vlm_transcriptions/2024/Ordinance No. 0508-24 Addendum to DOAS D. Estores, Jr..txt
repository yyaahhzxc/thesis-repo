that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR, THE ENTRER, AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE ADDENDUM TO THE DEED OF ABSOLUTE SALE AND EXTRAJUDICIAL SETTLEMENT OF ESTATE TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND DANilo B. ESTORES JR., RELATIVE TO THE PURCHASE OF A PARCEL OF LAND IN BARANGAY NEW CARMEN, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY, FOR THE DAVAO CITY SANITARY LANDFILL PROJECT."

**Ord. No. 0508-24**

**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY -** The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Addendum to the Deed of Absolute Sale and Extrajudicial Settlement of Estate to be executed by and between the City of Davao and Danilo B. Estores Jr. relative to the purchase of a parcel of land in Barangay New Carmen, Tugbok District, this City, for the Davao City Sanitary Landfill Project.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE -** If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, such sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY -** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED, on May 28, 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
