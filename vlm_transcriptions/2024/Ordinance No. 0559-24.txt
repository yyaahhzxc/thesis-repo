Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION MADE AND
EXECUTED BY 8990 HOUSING DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION RELATIVE TO
THE DONATION OF ROAD LOTS, OPEN SPACES, AND IMPROVEMENTS OF
DECA HOMES RESORT RESIDENCES PHASE 10, LOCATED IN BARANGAY
TACUNAN, TUBGOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Rule VII, Section 33 of the Batas
Pambansa Blg. 220 provides for donations of roads and open spaces, which reads:
The owner or project developer shall donate the roads and
open spaces (in the project to the local government of
the area after their condition had been certified by the
Commission and it shall be mandatory for the said local
government to accept such donations.
SECTION 22. Corporate Powers -
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have
the following powers:

shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial
capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

government, and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sanggunian Pangungod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the grant of authority to the City Mayor to accept and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation made and executed by 8990 Housing Development Corporation relative to the donation of road lots, open spaces, and improvements of Deca Homes Resort Residences Phase 10, located in Barangay Tacunan, Tugbok District, Davao City.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Donation (ODD) made and executed by 8990 Housing Development Corporation relative to the donation of road lots, open spaces, and improvements of Deca Homes Resort Residences Phase 10, located in Barangay Tacunan, Tugbok District, Davao City.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, July 16, 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
