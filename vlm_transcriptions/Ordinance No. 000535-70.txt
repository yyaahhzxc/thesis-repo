**ORDINANCE NO. 535**  

**AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT AND MAINTENANCE OF A PUBLIC POSED FOR MOTOR VEHICLES, APPROPRIATING THE IMPOUNDED REVENUE TO THE CITY ENGINEER, AND PROVIDING FOR THE CHARGES INCURRED IN CONNECTION WITH THE COLLECTION OF IMPOUNDING FEES THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.**  

**Be it Ordained by the City Council, City of Davao, that:**  

**SECTION 1.** This Ordinance shall be known as the "Motor Vehicles Impounding Ordinance of the City of Davao."  

**SECTION 2.** A public posed for motor vehicles is hereby authorized to be established and maintained under the supervision of the City Engineer in such localities as may be designated by the City Mayor.  

**SECTION 3.** The Police Department of the City of Davao shall cause to be taken up or impounded any and all motor vehicles abandoned or parked under any of the circumstances as enumerated and defined in Section 4 of this Ordinance. There shall be created the positions of a Poundkeeper and Assistant Poundkeeper and such number of employees and/or laborers as may be necessary for the proper maintenance and administration of the public pound. Upon being advised by the Police Department, it shall be the duty of the Poundkeeper to receive and impound any and all motor vehicles found abandoned or improperly parked under any of the circumstances enumerated and defined in Section 4. He shall keep a complete record of each motor vehicles impounded, showing the date of receipt, kind of motor vehicle, engine and chassis numbers and such other identifying description of said motor vehicles as he may deem necessary, furnishing the owner of the vehicle of such data and the manner and mode of disposal. He shall also keep a record of the amount collected on each motor vehicle in case of redemption or sale as provided for in this ordinance.

SECTION A. - For the purpose of this Ordinance, a motor vehicle shall be considered abandoned or improperly parked, if not being under the care of a responsible person, if it is found in any of the following conditions, to wit:
a) A motor vehicle abandoned or parked in such manner as to block, obstruct or interfere with the free movement of vehicular or pedestrian traffic;
b) A motor vehicle abandoned in the national highway in such a manner as part of the motor vehicle is parked in the cemented or asphalted portion;
c) A motor vehicle parked for repair and/or assembly work in any public street or place by motor vehicle repair shop and/or assembly plant shall be liable for impounding under this Ordinance, and
d) A motor vehicle parked or left on a public street or place for eight (8) hours for any reason not specified in the foregoing paragraphs.
If the vehicle cannot be towed under any circumstances without inflicting damage thereon, the Foundeeper shall cause the plate number of the motor vehicle to be removed and such plate number can only be reissued upon the payment of the impounding fees mentioned in Section 5,

A motor vehicle so impounded shall be held for the period of three (3) months from the date of its impounding, at the expiration of which time the same shall be sold as provided for in this Ordinance.

SECTION 6. - Forty-Sixth (46) hours after receipt of any motor vehicle in the Public Pound, the Poundkeeper shall advertise for two (2) consecutive weeks in two (2) weekly newspapers of general circulation in the city a notice in the following form:

**NOTICE VEHICLE**
**PENALTY ENJOYED**

Impounded in the Public Pound is the following described motor vehicles: (Here state the description of said motor vehicle and its registered owner, if known). Unless redeemed, said motor vehicles will be sold at public auction for cash to the highest bidder, said sale to be held at the Public Pound at the hour of ______ o'clock on the _______ th day of _______.

Poundkeeper

The date mentioned in said notice shall be at least three (3) months from the date the motor vehicle has been received for impounding. If said motor vehicle is not redeemed before said date, an authorized representative of the City Treasurer shall proceed to sell the same in accordance with said notice. Within twenty-four (24) hours after the receipt of an impounded motor vehicle, the Poundkeeper shall notify the person in whose name said vehicle is registered in the Land Transportation Commission, if known, of the fact that said motor vehicles has been impounded.

The owner and/or any authorized person redeeming the impounded vehicle shall reimburse the advertisement fee for the publication of the notice.

SECTION 7. - After the auction sale of an impounded motor vehicle, the Pontiffkeeper shall render to the City Treasurer a bill of the authorized fees and actual expenditures thereof to be credited to the proper account. The remaining balance of the amount received from the said sale after deducting the amount of the bill and all other expenses incident to the sale, if any, shall be refunded to the owner of such motor vehicle if said account is claimed within thirty (30) days from the date of sale; thereafter, said balance shall accrue to the general fund of the City.

SECTION 8. - No Pontiffkeeper or other employee in the public pound shall be allowed to participate, directly or indirectly, as a purchaser in the public auction sale of an impounded motor vehicle. Such sale to the Pontiffkeeper or other employees of the public pound shall be null and void.

SECTION 9. - It shall be the duty of the Pontiffkeeper or other employee of the Public pound to exercise due care in the towing of the motor vehicle as to prevent any damage to the same.

SECTION 10. - Repealing clause: This Ordinance repeals any ordinance(s) or parts thereof which are inconsistent with any of the provisions hereof.

SECTION 11. - Effectivity clause: This Ordinance shall take effect ten (10) days after publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.
