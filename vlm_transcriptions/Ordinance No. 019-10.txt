**ORDINANCE NO. 019-10**  
**Series of 2010**

**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO IMPLEMENT THE NEW RATES OF REPRESENTATION AND TRANSPORTATION ALLOWANCES (RATA) OF THE SANGUNGAN NG LUNGSOD (SLM) MEMBERS AND CITY GOVERNMENT DEPARTMENT HEAD (CGD) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DABAW EFFECTIVE JANUARY 2019 ON THE BASIS OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9970 (RA 9970) OR THE GENERAL APPROPRIATIONS ACT OF FISCAL YEAR 2010 (GAA FY 2010) AND TO COLLECT THE DIFFERENCE OF P280.00 PER MONTH CHARGEABLE AGAINST THE LUMPUSUM MISCELLANEOUS PERSONNEL BENEFITS FUND**

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord No. 019-10

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance authorizing the City Mayor to implement the new rates of Representation and Transportation Allowances (RATA) of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (SP) Members and City Government Department Head II (CCGD II) of the City Government of Davao effective January 2010 on the basis of Republic Act No. 9970 (RA 9970) or the General Appropriations Act of Fiscal Year 2010 (GAA FY 2010) and to collect the difference of P200.00 per month chargeable against the Ilipamap Misclassification Personnel Benefits Fund".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares its policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use;

SECTION 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS: The amounts herein provided shall be used to implement the increase in rates of RATA of the Sangguniang Panlungsod Members and the City Government Department Head II of the City Government of Davao from P6,300.00 to P6,500.00 per month effective January 1, 2010 on the basis of Sec 47 of Republic Act No. 9970 or the General Appropriations Act FY 2010 and LBC No. 2008-01 and must conform with existing accounting and auditing rules, regulations, policies and guidelines as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions thereof which are not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, October 5, 2010, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

CERTIFIED CORRECT.
