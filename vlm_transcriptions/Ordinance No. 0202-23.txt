AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE DAVAO CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN (DCDRMFP) FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2023 IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SEVEN HUNDRED THIRTY-NINE MILLION SIX HUNDRED NINETY-NINE PESOS AND 75/100 (P793,688,099.75)

Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No. 0202-23  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE DAVAO CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN (DCDRMFP) FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2023 IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SEVEN HUNDRED THIRTY-NINE MILLION SIX HUNDRED SIXTY THOUSAND NINETY-NINE PESOS AND 71/100 (P739,660,099.71)."  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to adopt procedures and adhere to the national principles and standards of programs, projects, and activities concerning pre-disaster preparedness, risk reduction, and recovery measures and declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.  

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF THE INVESTMENT PLAN - The Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund Investment Plan (DCDRMFP) for Calendar Year 2023 shall be approved to support the local government's pre-disaster preparedness measures, risk reduction activities, and relief and recovery programs.  

SECTION 4. BUDGET REQUIREMENT - The total amount of SEVEN HUNDRED THIRTY-NINE MILLION SIX HUNDRED SIXTY THOUSAND NINETY-NINE PESOS AND 71/100 (P739,660,099.71) is hereby appropriated to fund the DAVAO CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN (DCDRMFP) FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2023 (hereinafter attached as "Annex A").  

SECTION 5. LEGAL BASIS - Section 21 of Republic Act No. 10121 states that not less than five percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as the Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund (LDRRMF) to support disaster risk management activities such as, but not limited to, pre-disaster preparedness programs including training, purchasing life-saving rescue equipment, supplies and medicines, for post-disaster activities, and the payment of premiums on calamity insurance. The same law likewise provides that, of the amount appropriated for LDRRMF, thirty percent (30%) shall be allocated as Quick Response Fund (QRF) or stand-by fund for relief and recovery programs so that the situation and living conditions of people in communities or areas stricken by disasters, calamities, epidemics, or complex emergencies may be normalized as quickly as possible.  

SECTION 6. UTILIZATION OF FUNDS - The amounts herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to the existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.  

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
