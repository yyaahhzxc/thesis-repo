Ord. No. 0497-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BENEFIT OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS REGIONAL OFFICE-XI AND THE CITY OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE PERSONAL PROPERTIES TO BE USED SOLELY FOR THE CONSTRUCTION AND MAINTENANCE OF COVID ISOLATION FACILITIES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO, SPECIFICALLY DC-UP SPORTS COMPLEX TRAINING GYM AND THE FACULTY AND STAFF HOUSING IN UP-MINDANAO CAMPUS, MINTAL, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation (DOD) to be entered into by and between the Department of Public Works and Highways Regional Office-XI and the City of Davao relative to the personal properties to be used solely for the construction and maintenance of COVID isolation facilities in the City of Davao, specifically DC-UP Sports Complex Training Gym and the Faculty and Staff Housing in UP-Mindanao Campus, Mintal, this City;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, February 23, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT: 
CHARLTON N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
