AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "COMPREHENSIVE TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC CODE OF DAVAO CITY" BY INCLUDING ARTICLE XVI TO REGULATE THE CONSTRUCTION OF HIGH SPEED BUSES AND ANY PROTRUBERANCE ON ROADS WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY, PROVIDING PENALTIES THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
"COMPREHENSIVE TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC CODE OF DAVAO CITY" BY
INCLUDING ARTICLE XXV TO REGULATE THE CONSTRUCTION OF SPEED
HUMPS, SPEED BUMPS OR ANY PROTUBERANCE ON ROADS WITHIN THE
TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY, PROVIDING PENALTIES
THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS: The Comprehensive Transport and Traffic Code of
Davao City shall be amended to insert Article XXV, to wit:

"Article XXV - Regulation on the Construction of
Speed Humps, Speed Bumps or Any Protuberance"

SECTION 1. DEFINITION OF TERMS

a. Clearance - authorization letter issued by the City Transport and
Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City
Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTM) permitting an
application for the construction or installation of speed humps,
speed bumps or any protuberance;

b. Protuberance - something that bulges or extend beyond above surface;

c. Rumble Strips - a traffic-calming device or sleeper lines designed
to warn drivers to slow down by causing a tactile vibration and
audible rumbling of the vehicle;

d. Speed bumps - are aggressive traffic-calming devices which may be
parabolic or circular in shape used to lower the speed of motor
vehicles and commonly used in places where pedestrians and cars
share space closely such as, but not limited to, parking lots and
driveways;

e. Speed humps - are less aggressive traffic-calming devices used to
lower the speed of motor vehicles which may be parabolic or
circular in shape often used in local streets or connector roads.

SECTION 2. REGULATED ACTS - No person, natural or juridical, shall construct/install any speed humps, speed bumps or
place any protuberance on any road within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City without approval from the City Transport
and Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTM);

Speed humps, speed bumps or any protuberance shall only be
allowed along schools, hospital zones, private/public subdivisions and highly hazardous and densely populated areas except in main roads or highways.

**SECTION 3. ROLES OF THE BARANGAY AND THE TRAFFIC ENGINEERING AND INFRASTRUCTURE DIVISION (TEID) OF THE CITY TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT OFFICE (CTTMQ)**

**3.1 Roles of the Barangay Local Government Unit**

a. Submit to the Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMQ) an inventory of all speed humps, speed bumps, and protuberances within their territorial jurisdiction specifying the following:

- Designation, location and description, if it is permanent/ temporary;
- Mapped Location;
- Upon completion of inventory, submit to the CTTMO;

b. Process Barangay Clearance or Barangay Resolution for the application of construction of speed humps, speed bumps or protuberance, such as, but not limited to the following:

- Private subdivisions in the absence of homeowner's association;
- Public Subdivisions;
- Private/Public Schools/ Day Care Centers;
- Private/Public Hospitals and Clinics;
- Business Establishments;
- Any local resident.

For private subdivisions, a Resolution of No Objection shall be sought from the subdivision's homeowners association. In the absence of an association, the Resolution of No Objection shall be sought from the Barangay having jurisdiction over the subdivision.

**3.2 Roles of the Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMQ)**

a. Evaluate and approve or disapprove all applications for construction or installation of speed humps, speed bumps or protuberance;

b. Provide the basic standards, specifications and guidelines as to the construction, methodology and design of speed humps, speed bumps or protuberance and shall consider the following:

1. Road grade or slope design;
2. Proximity to curve;
3. Street condition;
4. Curbs;

5. Spacing between speed humps;
6. Loading and unloading zones;
7. Diversion potential;

1) Rumble Strips (specifying its construction methodology and design)
2) Warning signs such as slow down, humps ahead, intersection ahead etc. and other applicable signages.
e. Review all existing speed humps, speed bumps or protuberance and recommend for its modification, alteration or demolition.
SECTION 4. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS
4.1 Any person, natural or juridical, desiring to construct/place any kind of speed humps, speed bumps or protuberance on any road within this City shall submit an application to the Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO).
4.2 No Clearance shall be issued without the written recommendation of TEID and must comply with Sections 3 and 4 of this Ordinance.
4.3 Speed humps, speed bumps or any protuberance must not obstruct the flow of traffic, pose hazard to pedestrians and will not physically destroy public/private vehicles.
4.4 Any speed humps, speed bumps or protuberance shall have paved margins and warning signs installed with the specifications provided by the Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO).
4.5 The Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO) shall evaluate all existing speed humps, speed bumps or protuberance and shall make the proper recommendation for its demolition, if found to violate the provisions of this Ordinance; otherwise, the person who caused the construction thereof must comply with the requirements of this Ordinance.

All applications for construction of speed humps, speed bumps and protruberance must be acted upon within five (5) days from receipt of the same. If the application is approved, a Permit to Construct shall be issued specifying the design, materials and specification of the speed humps or speed bumps. If the application is disapproved, the Notice of Disapproval must be issued within the same period clearly specifying the grounds or reasons thereof.

The applicant may file a verified Motion for Reconsideration within a period of ten (10) days from receipt of the Notice of Disapproval specifying the grounds or reasons why the Notice of Disapproval shall be recalled or reconsidered. The Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO) shall resolve the said Motion for Reconsideration within a period of five (5) days from receipt of the same.

If the Motion for Reconsideration is denied, the applicant may appeal the Order of the CTTMO to the City Engineer's Office who must resolve the appeal within a period of Ten (10) days from receipt of the same. The decision of the City Engineer shall be final and executive and no appeal shall be allowed thereafter.

No application or fee shall be collected from the filing of application, or Motion For Reconsideration or Appeal.

SECTION 5. AUTHORIZED GOVERNMENT AGENCIES - No speed humps, speed bumps or protruberance shall be constructed on the national highway. The Department of Public Works and Highways may, however, in accordance with applicable laws, place rumble road devices on the national highways.

The Philippine National Police or the Armed Forces of the Philippines, for security reasons, may place speed humps, speed bumps or obstructions in appropriate areas where their camps or checkpoints are situated.

SECTION 6. PENALTY CLAUSE - A fine of Five Thousand Pesos ($5,000.00) shall be imposed on any person, officer of firm, corporation or association who will violate the provisions of this Ordinance.

The person or any responsible officer of the firm, corporation or association who shall construct unauthorized speed humps, speed bumps or protruberance may likewise be required to remove the same.

SECTION 7. ENFORCEMENT - This Ordinance shall be enforced and implemented by the Traffic Engineering and Infrastructure Division (TEID) of the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO).

**SECTION 8. ADJUSTMENTS** - Upon approval of this Ordinance, the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall perform the necessary adjustments on the numbering of sections and articles of the "Comprehensive Transport and Traffic Code of Davao City".

**SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE** - If, for any reason a provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, all other provisions hereof which are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE** - All Ordinances, resolutions, memoranda, orders and similar issuances, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, modified or amended accordingly.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a local newspaper of general circulation within the City of Davao.

**ENACTED**, on the 26th day of January 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
