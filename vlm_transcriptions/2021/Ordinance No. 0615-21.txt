Ord. No. 0615-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH) AND THE CITY OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE ROAD WIDENING WITH INSTALLATION OF TRAFFIC SIGNALIZATION ALONG DIVERSION EXTENSION TO TOKIL, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as Local Government Code of 1991, provides:

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
xxx xxx xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx xxx xxx
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:
xxx xxx xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and the City of Davao, relative to the Road Widening with Installation of Traffic Signalization along Diversion Extension to Tokil, this City;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 15th day of June 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement is made and entered into this day of 2021 in Davao City, Philippines by and between:

The DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS - REGIONAL OFFICE XI a government agency organized and established under Executive Order No. 124 in 1987, represented herein by its OIC-Regional Director REY PETER B. GILLE, duly authorized for the purpose and hereinafter referred to as the "FIRST PARTY".

-and-

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY, a government agency organized and established under Philippine Laws, represented herein by Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE, duly authorized for the purpose and hereinafter referred to as the "SECOND PARTY".

WITNESSETH THAT:

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY has been tasked to undertake the implementation of road widening of Davao City Division Extension to Toril, Davao City, which includes Installation of Traffic Signalization at Junction Davao City Division Extension to Toril Road and Davao-Bukidnon Road under the Regular Infrastructure Program FY 2019, identified as: Road Widening - Davao City Division Extension to Toril, Package 2, Davao City in the amount of Php 268,123,000.00

WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY, has agreed to accept the completed traffic signalization and commit to undertake the management, maintenance and expenses and other related works for said traffic signalization.

WHEREAS, the Parties hereby agreed to execute this MOA subject to the terms and conditions hereinafter set forth;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of the mutual covenants contained the parties hereby agreed as follows:

ARTICLE I - RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FIRST PARTY

Section I.1 Receive the Funds and ensure the prompt implementation of the Road Widening - Davao City Division Extension to Toril, Package 2, Davao City, which includes installation of traffic signalization at

--- [Page 10] ---
**Junction Davao City Diversion Extension to Toril Road and Davao-Bukidnon Road in accordance with approved Plans and Program of Works;**

**Section I.2**  
Immediately turn-over to the Second Party the Completed Traffic Signalization project to the latter's proper acceptance and maintenance.

**ARTICLE II - RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE SECOND PARTY**

**Section II.1**  
Acknowledge receipt of the traffic signalization project;

**Section II.2**  
Undertake the repair of any damaged part of the structure, one (1) year after the issuance of Certificate of Completion or Acceptance on the succeeding years;

**Section II.3**  
Shoulder payment of management and maintenance expenses.

**Section II.4**  
Ensure that NO VENDORS, VAGRANTS, BEGGARS, etc. will be allowed to occupy within the immediate vicinity of area where the traffic signalization will be installed after its turn-over by the FIRST PARTY.

**ARTICLE III - RESPONSIBILITIES AND WARRANTIES**

**Section III.1**  
The herein Parties represent that they have the requisite power, authority and capacity to enter into this agreement and perform their obligations to terms and conditions set forth;

**Section III.2**  
The parties also agreed to sign, execute and deliver such other Agreements and documentations in furtherance, or in the implementation of this agreement;

**Section III.3**  
Either of the parties to this MOA may initiate the termination of this MOA in writing, in the event of a default by the Other Party of any of its obligations;

**Section III.4**  
This MOA shall take effect upon approval thereof by the DPWH and shall remain in force and until the completion of the traffic signalization project unless mutually terminated earlier by the said Parties in writing;

**Section III.5**  
This agreement shall be binding upon the parties hereto and their respective successors in interest and assigns.
