Ord. No. 0517-21  

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE RENEWAL OF FUNDING ALLOCATION OF FIFTY MILLION PESOS (P50,000,000.00) MONTHLY FOR THE MALASAKIT PROGRAM FOR 2021".  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:  

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation. xxx  
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:  
xxx  
(xii) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."  

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, the Memorandum of Agreement between the Office of the President and the City Government of Davao relative to the renewal of funding allocation of Fifty Million Pesos (P50,000,000.00) monthly for the Malasakit Program for 2021.  

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.  

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.  

ENACTED, on the 16th day of March 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into between:

The OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT, with office address at Malacañang Palace, J.P. Laurel St., San Miguel, Manila, represented herein by the Executive Secretary SALVADOR C. MEDIALDEA, and hereinafter referred to as the OP;

and

The CITY GOVERNMENT DAVAO, a local government unit organized and existing under laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall, San Pedro St., Davao City, represented by its Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE;

WITNESSETH, that:

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao (the "City Government") constantly promotes programs that help alleviate the economic and health conditions of its constituents through making services accessible to less fortunate and indigent members of the communities;

WHEREAS, among the programs implemented by the City Government in order to address poverty focuses on the provision of medical assistance and fundamental needs of recognized and qualified beneficiaries;

WHEREAS, the continuing increase of the public beneficiaries shows the effectiveness of these programs thereby giving the City Government more opportunity to advance its implementation and strategy in this aspect for the general welfare of its people;

WHEREAS, one of these programs is the Lingap Para sa Mahirap Program (the "Lingap Program") that has, through the years, provided medical assistance and benefits to indigents;

WHEREAS, considering that the guidelines of the Lingap Program of the City Government of Davao has already been established, and taking cognizant of the pressing concern vis-à-vis the government's obligation to prioritize the health and well-being of the people and in order to extend medical assistance beyond Davao City constituents, the Office of the President will allocate Fifty Million Pesos (P 50,000,000.00) monthly for the Malasakit Program, to be sourced from the Socio-Olivic Project Fund (SCPFF);

WHEREAS, the OP and the City Government of Davao have agreed to expand the coverage of the subject medical assistance to entire Mindanao;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of the terms, conditions as they hereby agree and stipulate as follows:

--- [Page 12] ---
A. OBLIGATIONS OF THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT (OP)
1. The OP shall allocate the amount of Fifty Million (Pmp 50,000,000.00) pesos monthly for a period of one (1) year chargeable against the Socio Civic Project Fund (SCCF) for the Malasakit Program.
2. The OP - Office of the Deputy Executive Secretary for Finance and Administration (ODESFA) shall pay direct to the hospitals, funeral parlors and other establishments issued by Guarantee Letter upon receipt of billing statement and supporting documents.

B. OBLIGATIONS OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY (LGU-DAVAO CITY)
1. The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall utilize the funding solely for Malasakit Program in Mindanao thru issuance of Guarantee Letters. In no case shall the amount exceed P 50,000.00 net of Philhealth and other assistance for medical and P 30,000.00 for funeral.
2. The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall submit to the OP the Quarterly Summary of Guarantee Letter issued.

IN WITNESS THEREOF, the parties hereof through their duly authorized representatives have heretofore entered into this Agreement and affixed their signatures on the date and place herein above-mentioned.
