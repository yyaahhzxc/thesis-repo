Ord. No. 0468-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunian Pan lungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. IIIIE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0291-17, SERIES OF 2017, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "THE 2017 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO" PARTICULARLY ARTICLE 28, SECTION 234 THEREOF, BY ADJUSTING, REDUCING AND/OR LOWERING THE RATES OF EXHIBITORS/STALLS TO 50% OFF FROM P300.00 TO P150 PER DAY FOR EVERY SELLING EXHIBITOR AND FROM P150 TO P75 PER DAY FOR NON-SELLING EXHIBITORS DURING THE STATE OF PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY ISSUED BY THE PRESIDENT".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY TO ADJUST AND/OR LOWER THE RATES. Section 132 of the Local Government Code states, "The power to impose a tax, fee, or charge or to generate revenue under this Code shall be exercised by the Sanggunian of the local government unit concerned through an appropriate ordinance". Pursuant to such provision, the City Council has the power to adjust and/or lower the previous rates it has imposed;

SECTION 3. ADJUSTED RATES. There shall be an adjustment, reduction and/or lowering of the rates of exhibitors/stalls from P300.00 to P150 per day for every selling exhibitor and from P150 to P75 per day for non-selling exhibitors;

SECTION 4. PERIOD. The said adjustment, reduction or lowering of rates of the exhibitors and non-selling exhibitors shall only be applicable during the period covering the COVID Pandemic which shall end but before the lifting of the state of public health emergency brought about by COVID-19.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 19th day of January, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
