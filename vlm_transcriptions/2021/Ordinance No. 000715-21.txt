TINGCON

Ord. No. 071-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT UNDER THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROGRAM NAMELY: PIAPI UPGRAADING AREA AND PIAPI COMMERCIAL AREA; AND NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASES 1 AND 2, PARTICULARLY IDENTIFIED AS BLOCK 78, LOT 3, PIAPI UPGRAADING AREA, BARANGAY 21-C, DAVAO CITY, CONSISTING OF SIXTY-SIX (66) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF ALVAREZ C. TINGCON."

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Sale for the lot in the Slum Improvement and Resettlement (SIR) Program Namely: Piapi Upgrading Area and Piapi Commercial Area; and New Matina Sites and Services Phases 1 and 2, particularly identified as Block 78, Lot 3, Piapi Upgrading Area, Barangay 21-C, Davao City, consisting of Sixty-Six (66) square meters, more or less, in favor of Alvarez C. Tingcon.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 14th day of September 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
