AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF JOE CHOA SHI FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF EARTHILL QUARRY LOCATED IN BARANGAY WA-AN, BUHANGIN DISTRICT, DAVAO CITY

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0659-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE REQUEST OF JOE CHOA SHI FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE
USE FOR THE OPERATION of EARTHFILL QUARRY LOCATED IN BARANGAY
WA-AN, BUHANGIN DISTRICT, DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Article XII, Section 1 of the
Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) provides for Requests for
Additional Allowable Use, which reads:

"The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general zone
and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of
Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not
enumerated herein, such as, but not limited to, the following projects which are socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3/4 majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance."

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST - The application for Additional
Allowable Use of Mr. Joe Choa Shi for the operation of earthfill quarry located in
Barangay Wa-an, Buhangin District, this City, with a total area of Forty-four Thousand Four Hundred Ninety-nine (44,499) square meters, more or less, registered under Transfer Certificate of Title No. T-277970 of the Registry of Deeds of Davao City in the name of Ms. Janel C. Gaisano is hereby APPROVED. Provided, no permit for earthfill
quarry shall be issued without the Affidavit of No Objection of all concerned adjacent lot owners.

SECTION 4. OBLIGATION OF APPLICANT - The applicant and his or her
successors-in-interest have the obligation to comply with existing rules, laws and
Ordinances. The environment shall at all times be protected. Adverse environmental
impact for any use of the property shall be mitigated. Non-compliance with existing
rules, laws and ordinances shall cause the repeal of this Ordinance.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 21st day of July, 2021, by three-fourths (3/4) votes of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
