Order No. 0851-21  
Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE CONDUCT OF MSME TRADE FAIR (CHRISTMAS BAZAAR), IN PARTNERSHIP WITH THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, FROM DECEMBER 8 TO DECEMBER 31, 2021, EXEMPTING THE CONDUCT OF THE EVENT FROM THE PAYMENT OF SPECIAL MAYOR'S PERMIT FEE AND OTHER CHARGES".  
SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption for the conduct of MSME Trade Fair (Christmas Bazaar) solely refer to the payment of the Special Mayor's Permit Fee and other fees of the participating companies and the exhibitors of the said event.  
SECTION 3. PERIOD - The exemption shall be from December 8 until December 31, 2021.  
SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.  
ENACTED, December 14, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
