Be it Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 3 FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2021 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, CONSISTING OF THE GENERAL FUND PROPER, THE DEVELOPMENT FUND AND THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES, IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF THE THREE HUNDRED FIFTY NINETY-EIGHT THOUSAND SEVEN PESOS (P350,098,007.00)";  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time, ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use;  

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS - The following amounts or so much thereof, as maybe necessary, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Treasurer of Davao City, not otherwise appropriated for the operation of the City Government of Davao from January 1 to December 31, 2021, except as otherwise provided therein;  

SECTION 4. AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES - The Supplemental Budget No. 3 for Calendar Year 2021 of the City Government of Davao covers the Source of Funds in the total amount of THREE HUNDRED FIFTY MILLION NINETY-EIGHT THOUSAND SEVEN PESOS (P350,098,007.00), derived from reversion of current and prior years appropriations, to wit:
