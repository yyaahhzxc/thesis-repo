ORDINANCE NO. 0848-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE THE REMAINING BALANCE OF THE 30% QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2021, IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SEVENTY-SIX MILLION SIX HUNDRED THIRTY THOUSAND PESOS (P76,630,000.00) FOR PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES TO PREVENT, INTERVENE AND CONTAIN THE ESCALATION OF COVID-19 CASES IN DAVAO CITY, SUBJECT TO EXERCISE OF GOVERNMENT FINANCING, ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0848-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in Session assembled;

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE THE REMAINING BALANCE OF THE 30% QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) AND STAND-BY-FUND FOR THE AMOUNT OF SEVENTY-SIX MILLION SIX HUNDRED THIRTY THOUSAND PESOS ($76,630,000.00) FOR PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES TO PREVENT, INTERVENE AND CONTAIN THE ESCALATION OF COVID-19 CASES IN DAVAO CITY, SUBJECT TO EXISTING GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. In keeping with its mandates and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.

SECTION 3. LEGAL BASIS. Section 21 of Republic Act (RA) 10121 provides that:

"Of the amount appropriated for LDRRMF, thirty percent (30%) shall be allocated as Quick Response Fund (QRF) or stand-by fund for relief and recovery programs in order that situation and living conditions of people in communities or areas stricken by disasters, calamities, epidemics and other complex emergencies, may be normalized as quickly as possible."

SECTION 4. THE LIST OF PROGRAMS AND ACTIVITIES ARE THE FOLLOWING:

| PROGRAMS/PROJECTS/ACTIVITIES | IMPLEMENTING OFFICE | APPROPRIATION |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Enhance COVID-19 Responses * Drugs and Medicines Expense (P1,967,000.00) * Medical, Dental and Laboratory Supplies Expense (P37,041,700.00) * Other Supplies and Material Expense (P991,800.00) | City Health Office | P40,000,500.00 |
| 2. Assistance for the Victims of COVID-19 and Natural and Man-made Disaster * Welfare Goods Expense Food Packs (P32,782,000.00) Groceries (P3,847,500.00) | City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDD) | P36,629,500.00 |
| TOTAL APPROPRIATIONS |  | P76,630,000.00 |

SECTION 5. USE OF FUNDS. The amounts herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such item and expenditure approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.

**SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY.**

The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval:

ENACTED, on the 14th day of December, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
