AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND BROKENSCHIESE INTEGRATED HEALTH MINISTRIES, INC., RELATIVE TO THE EQUIPMENT FOR TESTING TO BE INSTALLED AT THE LATTER'S MOLECULAR BIOLOGY LABORATORY FACILITY

Ord. No. 0510-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR, AND IN REHABILITATION OF THE CITY OF DAVAO AND BROKENSHIRE INTEGRATED HEALTH MINISTRIES, INC., RELATIVE TO THE EQUIPMENT FOR TESTING TO BE INSTALLED AT THE LATTER'S MOLECULAR BIOLOGY LABORATORY FACILITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160 or the "Local Government Code of 1991" provides:

"Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therewith, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, improve the quality of life, develop self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation between the City of Davao and Brokenshire Integrated Health Ministries, Inc., relative to the equipment for testing to be installed at the latter's Molecular Biology Laboratory Facility.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 9th day of March 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
