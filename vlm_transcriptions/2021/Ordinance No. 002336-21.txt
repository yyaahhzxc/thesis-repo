WHEREAS, City Administrator Atty. Zuleika T. Lopez, by authority of the City Mayor, per Executive Order No. 18, dated July 19, 2019, certified the matter as urgent to be passed under Suspended Rules;

WHEREAS, COVID-19 remains a threat to public health and national security which undermines the social, economic and well-being of this society, and among the measures undertaken by the Local Government Unit (LGU) to contain the spread of COVID-19 is the isolation of the patients and those under monitoring;

WHEREAS, for this purpose, the LGU is in need of available buildings or spaces that will serve as quarantine/isolation area for those affected with the present pandemic;

WHEREAS, the Office of the City Legal Officer, in its Legal Opinion No. 480, Series of 2021, dated May 10, 2021, found no legal infirmity in the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) and recommended that the same be forwarded to the Sangguniang Panlungsod for the requisite legislative authority, and eventually to the City Mayor for her signature;

NOW THEREFORE, on motion of Councilor J. Melchor B. Quitain Jr., duly seconded by Councilors Dante A. Apostol Sr., Myrna G. L'Balado-Oritz, Nilo D. Abellera, Bai Hundra Cassandria, Quintine N. Ribulincia and Pilipino Braga, it is resolved, as it is hereby resolved, to enter into consultation with the authorized officers and officials to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the renewal of the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and among the Department of Public Works and Highways - XI (DPWH-XI), THE UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES, AND THE CITY OF DAVAO PERTAINING TO THE USE OF THE DC-UP SPORTS COMPLEX TRAINING GYM, FACULTY AND STAFF HOUSING OF DC-UP SPORTS COMPLEX IN UP-MINDANAO CAMPAIGN DISTRICT, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY, AS QUARANTINE AREA FOR COVID-19 PATIENTS FOR A PERIOD OF THREE MONTHS COMMENCING ON APRIL 20, 2021;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that copies of this Resolution be furnished the Office of the City Mayor through the City Administrator's Office, the Vice Mayor's Office, the City Health Office, the City Legal Office, the Department of Public Works and Highways - XI (DPWH-XI), THE UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES - Mindanao and all other concerned offices/departments, for their information, guidance and appropriate action;

ADOPTED, May 11, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

CHARITO N. SANTOS

Secretary to the Sanggunian Panlungsod
