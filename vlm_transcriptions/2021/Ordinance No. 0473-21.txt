Ord. No. 0473-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE CONTRACT LEASE TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN METRO DAVAO DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO INVOLVING ONE (1) DOORWAY, TWO (2) STOREY CONCRETE COMMERCIAL BUILDING UTILIZED AS GSO SUPPLY OFFICE AND STORAGE, LOCATED AT CITY Hall DRIVE, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:

Section 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation

xxx xxx xxx

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

xxx xxx xxx

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:

xxx xxx xxx

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Contract Lease to be entered into by and between Metro Davao Development Corporation and the City Government of Davao involving one (1) door, two (2) storey concrete commercial building utilized as GSO supply office and storage, located at City Hall Drive, this City.

SECTION 4. SEPARAPBLITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any portion or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, January 26, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

**RENEWAL OF CONTRACT OF LEASE**

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Renewal of Contract of Lease executed and entered into by and between:

METRO DAVAO DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION, represented by GEORGE U. LIM, of legal age, married, Filipino, and residing at San Pedro St., Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR,"

and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, represented by MAYOR SARA DUTERTE, as authorized by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod in its Resolution No. ______, series of ______, with principal address at City Hall Building, Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE."

WITNESSETH

THAT, for and in consideration of the payment of the rent and compliance of all conditions and covenants hereinafter contained, the LESSOR has agreed to lease, let and demise, unto the LESSEE, and the latter has agreed to accept, under lease, the premises known as (1) door two story concrete commercial building situated at City Hall Drive, Davao City, with a floor area of 350 square meters, more or less, under the following terms and conditions:

1. TERM - The lease period is for three (3) years starting January 1, 2021, and until December 31, 2023, based on the following terms and conditions as agreed upon by both parties.

In the event that the LESSEE desires to renew this contract under this paragraph, the LESSOR shall be notified at least three (3) months before the expiration of the lease.

2. RENTAL - The LESSEE shall pay the LESSOR a monthly rental in the amount of PESOS: SIXTY EIGHT THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED NINETY PESOS AND FORTY ONE CENTS only (P 68,490.41) for year 2021, SEVENTY ONE THOUSAND NINE HUNDRED FOURTEEN AND NINETY THREE CENTS only (P 71,914.93) for year 2022, and SEVENTY FIVE THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED TEN AND SIXTY EIGHT CENTS only (P 75,510.68) for year 2023, inclusive of twelve percent (12%) adjusted value added tax (VAT). A five percent (5%) penalty on the rental shall be effective in any subsequent renewal of this Lease Contract.

Without prejudice to the exercise by the LESSOR of its rights in Paragraph 12 and herein, the LESSEE shall pay to the LESSOR interest at the rate of twenty four percent (24%) per annum on any monthly rental not paid, to be computed from the date of delinquency.

--- [Page 13] ---
3. USES OF THE PREMISES - The LESSEE shall not use the leased premises for dwelling or sleeping purposes. The premises hereby leased shall be used exclusively by the LESSEE, strictly for the purpose of the use hereinafter described. The Lessor shall not divert the premises to any other uses without the prior consent of the LESSOR, it being expressly agreed that if, at any time during the existence of this lease and without the previous consent of the LESSOR, the said premises is used for any other purposes, the LESSOR reserves the right or choice to, (a) rescind this contract in accordance with Paragraph 17, (b) increase the rental, or (c) compel the LESSEE to stop its new activities.

4. IMPROVEMENT AND ALTERATIONS - The LESSEE shall not make any alterations, installations, additions or improvements of the leased premises, such as partitions, shades, swings, blinds, window guards, etc., without the consent of the LESSOR. It is further agreed that all such alterations, installations, additions or improvements, except movable furniture and fixtures, placed at the expense of the LESSEE and removable without defacing or injuring the leased premises, shall, if so elected by the LESSOR, be removed by the LESSEE at the expense of the LESSOR, and considered with the premises as part thereof, upon termination of the lease, without compensation to the LESSEE. If the LESSOR does not elect to retain such alteration, installations, additions or improvements, the premises shall be surrendered to their original condition at the expense of the LESSEE. If the LESSOR does not elect to retain such alteration, installations, additions or improvements, the premises shall be surrendered to their original condition at the expense of the LESSEE. If the LESSOR's opinion, there is just cause to warrant such refusal.

5. POWER CONSUMPTION AND CHARGES FOR UTILITIES - All electrical consumption and charges, fees and costs for telephone, water, and other utilities used by the LESSEE in the leased premises, shall be for its account. The LESSEE may, at its discretion, install a separate meter and convenience outlets and all electrical connections as it may deem necessary.

6. SIGNS AND ADVERTISEMENTS - To insure uniformity of design, the LESSEE shall not cause to be affixed, inscribed or painted, any notice, sign or other advertising medium, on any part of the building without the consent of the LESSOR.

The LESSEE shall not permit or give consent to any other person or entity to advertise, as if said person or entity owns, holds office, or is otherwise established, at the premises leased, or any part thereof, and unless the LESSOR agrees, to the contrary, only the name and character of the LESSOR as written in the contract of lease shall be placed in the building directory and advertised at or near the main door of the premises leased. No other sign or advertisement may be placed in the leased premises other than the place herein above indicated.

7. CARE OF THE LEASED PREMISES - The LESSEE shall maintain the leased premises in a clean and sanitary condition, free from noxious odors and disturbing noise, and upon expiration of the lease, shall surrender and return the leased premises and fixtures in as good condition, except ordinary wear and tear, as the leased premises were actually found at the beginning of the lease.

8. RULES, REGULATIONS, ETC. - The LESSEE will comply with any and all reasonable rules and safety regulations which may be promulgated from time to time by the LESSOR and made known to the LESSEE in writing, for the administration/use/occupancy of the leased premises, and with all the rules, regulations, ordinances and laws made by the health and duly constituted authorities of the City National Government, arising from or regarding the use, occupancy and sanitation of leased premises.

The LESSEE shall indemnify and hold harmless the said LESSOR against all actions, suits, damages and claims by whoever they may be brought or made by reason of the non-observance or non-performance of the said rules, regulations, ordinances, or laws, or of any covenants of this section, without prejudice to the right of the LESSOR to cancel this lease in accordance with the penal provisions hereinafter contained.

9. INSPECTION OF PREMISES - The LESSOR or its authorized agent shall, by previous arrangement with the LESSEE, and after presenting his/her authority, have the right to enter the leased premises at any reasonable time, to examine the same, subject to security requirements of the LESSEE, and during the last three (3) months of the term of the lease, to exhibit the leased premises to prospective tenants, except when this lease has been renewed as herein provided.

10. REPAIRS - The LESSEE shall make necessary repairs on the leased premises and maintain the same in good and tenable condition.

During the existence of this lease, the LESSEE shall pay for the cost of repairs, replacements, and/or restoration of damages caused on the leased premises by the herein LESSEE, its employees, representatives, agents, clients and visitors.

The LESSEE shall at all times, subject to prior arrangement, allow and extend access to the LESSOR to the leased premises for the purpose of making repairs, remodeling or repairing or to undertake all works necessary for the preservation, improvement or decoration of the building or any part thereof. No compensation or claims shall be allowed against the LESSOR by reason of inconvenience, annoyance or injury to the LESSEES' business that may arise by virtue of undertaking any work under this provision.

11. TERMINATION OF LEASE: Should the LESSEE opt not to renew the lease, the LESSEE agrees to return and surrender to the LESSOR the leased premises at the end of the term in the same condition as reasonable wear and tear will permit and without delay whatever, defaulting furniture, fixtures, articles and effects of any kind other than such alterations, additions or improvement which shall be subject to the provisions of Paragraph 5.

12. SUB-LEASE, TRANSFER OR LEASE: The LESSEE shall not assign or transfer its right in this contract nor sub-lease or sublet all or any part of the leased premises, without the prior written consent of the LESSOR and no right, title or interest thereto shall be conferred or vested in anyone other than the LESSEE without such written consent.

13. OCCUPATION OF THE LEASED PREMISES: After this lease has been terminated for any reason whatsoever, if the LESSEE continues the occupation of the leased premises with the consent of the LESSOR, said extension of lease shall be understood as running from the date of termination hereof under the terms and conditions herein stipulated and may be terminated by either party by giving six (6) days' written notice served upon the other party at least thirty (30) days prior to the date of such termination.

14. INFLAMMABLE AND EXPLOSIVE MATERIALS: The LESSEE shall not bring into or store, nor shall allow three persons to bring into or store, in the leased premises anything of a highly inflammable nature or explosive materials, nor cook or install any apparatus, machinery or equipment which may expose the leased premises to fire or increase the fire hazard of the property or change the insurance rate of the property or any other article which the LESSOR may reasonably prohibit, it being understood that should the LESSEE do so, such violation may cause the LESSOR to order for the immediate removal of the abovementioned articles from the premises.

15. INSURANCE: The LESSOR shall insure the property up to the extent of its insurable interest and against all perils it may choose to be covered; the Lessee may also insure at its expense its property/improvement brought into the leased premises against perils it may want insured against.

16. NON-WAIVER: The failure of the LESSOR to insist upon a strict performance of any of the terms, conditions and covenants hereof shall not be deemed a relinquishment or waiver of any rights or remedy that said lessor may own, nor shall it be construed as a waiver of any subsequent breach or default of the terms, conditions and covenants hereof, which shall continue to be in full force and effect. No waiver by the LESSOR of any of its rights under this contract shall be deemed to have been made unless expressed in writing and signed by the LESSOR.

--- [Page 16] ---
17. PAYMENT OF REALTY TAXES - It shall be the duty of the LESSOR to pay the realty taxes on the land where the property is constructed as well as the property itself.

18. DAMAGE TO LEASED PREMISES - In case of damage to the leased premises or its appurtenances by fire, earthquake, war, or any other unforeseen cause, the LESSEE shall give immediate notice thereof to the LESSOR. If the lease premises shall be damaged by fire or other cause without the fault or negligence of the LESSEE, its agents, employees, servants, or visitors, the LESSEE shall be responsible for the repair thereof. The LESSOR may require the LESSEE to assume such repairs at such time as it deems fit; however, if the building of the leased premises is destroyed to such extent as to make it untenable without the fault or neglect of the LESSEE, either party may demand the rescission of the contract. The LESSEE shall be fully liable to the LESSOR in all cases where damage of any nature or kind is caused by the fault or negligence of the said LESSEE, his employees, agents, servants, or visitors. No compensation or claim shall be allowed against the LESSOR by reason of any inconvenience, subordination, injury to the LESSEE's business, or out of necessity of repairing any portion of the building, in whatever way such necessity may arise.

19. LOAD - No portion of the floor in said premises shall be loaded in excess of 32 kilos (70 lbs.) per square foot, nor shall any safe or other article weighing in excess of 200 kilos (440 lbs.) be placed in or removed from the premises without the knowledge and consent of the LESSOR, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

20. INJURY OR DAMAGE TO THIRD PERSONS OR LEASE - The LESSEE hereby assume full responsibility for any and all damage which may be caused to persons or property while occupying the premises leased to the LESSEE and further binds himself to hold the LESSOR free and harmless from any such claims for injury or damage unless such injury or damage is due to the negligence of the LESSOR. The LESSOR shall neither be liable nor responsible for:

a. the presence of bugs, vermin, ant, anay, insects, if any, in the leased premises;

b. for the failure of the water supply and/or electric;

c. for any article delivered or left to any of its employees;

d. for any injury, loss or damage, which the LESSEE, his agents or employees may sustain in the premises due to any cause whatsoever, except when the cause of said injury, loss or damage is the negligence of the LESSOR.

e. for damages occasioned by or arising from, plumbing, gas, water, and/or other pipes or air-conditioning system, or the bursting, leaking or destruction of any cistern, tank, water closet, or water pipe in, above, upon or under premises, nor for any damages arising from acts, nor negligence of the LESSEE or its agents, employees, representatives, or any and all persons.

21. EXPROPRIATION - In the event that expropriation proceedings are instituted during the period of this lease by any instrumentality of the Government or by any other entity with authority to exercise such power, either party may rescind this contract shall be forfeited and the other party shall have the right to terminate this lease upon giving the other party thirty (30) days previous written notice thereof. In case of such expropriation, the LESSEE hereby unconditionally relieves and releases the LESSOR from any and all liability under this contract in connection with or arising out of such expropriation proceedings, without prejudice to whatever recourse the LESSEE may have against the expropriating entity on account of damage done or caused to its properties.

22. FAILURE TO SURRENDER - If said premises be not surrendered at the expiration of the term, the LESSEE shall pay, by way of penalty, a sum equivalent to the amount of the rentals for the leased premises for the whole period of delay. It is hereby agreed that such penalty shall likewise be without prejudice to the attorney's fees and other liabilities provided in this contract. The LESSEE shall furthermore hold the lessor harmless from any liability in respect to any and all claims made by any succeeding tenants or lessees against the LESSOR from the delay by the LESSOR in delivering possession of the premises to such succeeding lessee or lessee as such is occasioned by the failure of the LESSEE to surrender the premises on time.

23. DISTURBANCE - Disturbance or discontinue of the possession of the leased premises by the LESSEE by causes beyond the control of the LESSOR shall confer no right of any kind to the LESSEE as against the LESSOR.

24. LEASE EXCLUDES PORTION OF BUILDING - This lease does not extend to the outside portion of the building corresponding to or opposite the leased premises and the painting, gutting or affixing of business notices, signs or any other advertising medium in accordance with paragraph No. 5 of this contract shall not be construed as an extension of this lease to that portion outside of the building.
