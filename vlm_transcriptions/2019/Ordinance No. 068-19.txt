ARTICLE I
Preliminary Provisions

Section 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be referred to and cited as "An Ordinance Reorganizing the City Archives and Records Office, the City Mayor's Office Correspondence and Records Division, the City Health Office, the City Legal Office, and the City Administrator's Office-City Economic Enterprise."

Section 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES. - Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as, powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

Furthermore, Section 76 of the aforementioned law empowers local government units to design and implement its organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines prescribed by the Civil Service Commission (CSC).

Section 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS. - The following words, terms, phrases and acronyms shall be defined as follows:

| CARO | City Archives and Records Office |
| CMO | City Mayor's Office |
| CMO-CRD | City Mayor's Office-City Records Division |
| CHO | City Health Office |
| CLO | City Legal Office |
| CAO | City Administrator's Office |
| CAO-CEE | City Administrator's Office-City Economic Enterprise |
| Radiologist | A medical doctor who specializes in diagnosing and treating disease and injury, using medical imaging techniques such as x-rays, computed tomography (CT), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), nuclear medicine, positron emission tomography (PET), fusion imaging, and ultrasound. |

Reclassification
A form of staffing modification and/or position classification action which is applied only when there is substantial change in the regular duties and responsibilities of the position. This may result in a change in any or all of the position attributes: position, title, level and/or salary grade. Transfer

ARTICLE II
The City Archives and Record Office (CARO)
Section 4. TRANSFER OF ITEM NO. 85, ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER V (RECORDS OFFICER III) TO THE CITY ASSESSOR'S OFFICE AND REVERT ITS POSITION TITLE BACK TO LOCAL ASSESSMENT OPERATIONS OFFICER III (LAOO III) - The position of Administrative Officer V under the City Archives and Records Office is hereby laterally transferred to the City Assessor's Office reverting its position title back to Local Assessment Operations Officer III (LAOO III) without any loss of benefits or seniority rights to the employee who may have been appointed to the said position at the time of the approval of this Ordinance. Section 11 (c) of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018 states the provisions for transfer.

A. Assist the Local Assessment Operations Officer IV in establishing, installing record management system and procedures such as proper filing methods, classification of records, cross-reference of records and proper storage of assessment records;

Section 9. The appointment of any person to the said positions is subject to the provisions of pertinent civil service laws and regulations.

Section 10. REQUIREMENTS, DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES – The requirements for regular appointments as stated in the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018, shall be followed. The Human Resource Management Office may add other requirements in accordance with Civil Service Rules and Regulations and pertinent laws. The duties, responsibilities and functions of the newly created positions are as follows:

A. Information Technology Officer I
1. Act as security officer for the Network and Systems Development Operations and assist in the conduct of trainings;
2. Consult with and assist the other units in the office with regards to systems and procedures, record, control, and security;
3. Assist the Information Technology Officer II in the preparation of program/evaluation reports regarding project development, technical operations and other activities carried out by the office;
4. Perform other functions as may be directed by the Department Head and/or any other superior officers.

B. Computer Programmer III
1. Plan, design, and develop information systems to optimize application of Information Technology and ICT resources in the office;
2. Develop, implement, maintain and evaluate computerized systems in the office to ensure efficacy;
3. Take charge in the build-up and maintenance of a complete and up-to-date personnel information system to facilitate better record keeping and reportorial functions of the office with the aid of computers and software;

--- [Page 8] ---
4. Supervise the work of computer operators to maximize productivity through planning, organizing and establishing work assignment and schedules, targets and evaluation of their work;
5. Supervise and spearhead the maintenance of IT hardware and software resources in the office by planning, scheduling and conducting inventory trouble shooting and simple repairs;
6. Perform other related functions as may be directed by the Department Head and/or any other superior officers.
C. Computer Maintenance Technologist II
1. Perform preventive maintenance and basic corrective maintenance on the office's peripherals and data communication equipment system;
2. Assist in carrying out duties associated with the efficient and effective operation of a computer system of equipment directly associated with computers;
3. Maintain close coordination with users in the event of hardware malfunction;
4. Enforce standards and procedures for equipment operation data and installation, security and trouble-shooting;
5. See to that basic equipment used in service maintenance is working;
6. Coordinate with the supply section regarding spare parts and other requirements; and
7. Perform other functions as may be directed by the Department Head and/or any other superior officers.
D. Supply Officer I
1. Undertake the issuance of requested supplies and materials after the requisition have been approved by the supervisors concerned and submit periodic reports of the issues made for monitoring its utilization;
2. Check/Inspect supplies and materials that have been delivered and kept, and maintains records of its delivery;
3. Perform other functions as may be directed by the Department Head.
E. Clerk III
1. Prepare correspondence and attends personally to important and urgent matters, drafts and finalizes official communications;
2. Perform arithmetical computations such as percentage, ratios, and other calculations of equivalent difficulties;
3. Encodes letters of recommendation, transmittal and other related work;
4. Prepare reports of activities and accomplishments of subordinates;
5. Perform other related work.
F. Clerk II
1. Under supervision, prepare drafts for routine official letters and endorsements in connection with office regulations;
2. Prepare drafts, simple routine applications and requests for completeness and accuracy;
3. Perform other functions as may be directed by the Department Head.

ARTICLE III
The City Mayor's Office – Correspondence and Records Division (CMO-CRD)

Section 11. Change of Division Name – The Correspondence and Records Division of the City Mayor's Office shall be renamed to Administrative Services which would cater to the administrative concerns of the City Mayor's Office.

Section 12. REORGANIZATION – With the creation of the City Archives and Records Office (CARO) as a regular department by virtue of City Ordinance No. 396-18 pursuant to Republic Act No. 9470, all Records Officer and Bookkeeper positions including the functions were transferred to the City Archives and Records Office (CARO), thus, the CMO-CRD was left with only First Level positions without definite functions. The following position titles and the respective grade level and number of positions as listed below are due for reclassification:

| FROM | TO | No. of Positions |
|------|-----|----------------|
| Administrative Aide VI | Clerk III | 2 |
| Administrative Aide IV | Clerk II | 1 |

To create the position of Administrative Officer IV with the level of Administrative Officer II with Salary Grade 15 as head of the City Mayor's Office-Administrative Services, the position created must be in the Index of Occupational Services, Position Titles and Salary Grades (IOS) of the Organization, Position Classification and Compensation Bureau of the Department of Budget and Management edition of October 2018. The appointment of any person to the said position is subject to the provisions of pertinent civil service laws and regulations.

Section 13. REQUIREMENTS, DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER IV WITH THE LEVEL OF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER II – The requirements for regular appointments as stated in the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018 shall be followed. The Human Resource Management Office may add other requirements in accordance with the Civil Service Rules and Regulations and pertinent laws. The duties, responsibilities, and functions of the Administrative Officer IV with the level of Administrative Officer II are as follows:

A. Supervise and coordinate records and correspondence management such as document tracing, records filing, storage and maintenance for safeguarding and future reference;

B. Supervise and coordinate property and supply management such as supply/property procurement, supply utilization, property/equipment maintenance to give or service requests for purchase of materials and other equipment repairs;

C. Plan and supervise budget preparation, monitoring and fund control to oversee the flow of the financial operations of the office;

D. Supervise administrative support and general services to provide assistance for office operations;

E. Perform other functions as may be directed by the Department Head and/or any other superior officers.

ARTICLE IV
The City Health Office (CHO)

Section 14. RECLASSIFICATION OF MEDICAL OFFICER VI TO MEDICAL OFFICER IV
For the reclassification of the position of Medical Officer VI with Salary Grade 24 to Medical Officer IV with Salary Grade 23, the requirements for regular appointments prescribed in the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018 shall be followed. The Human Resource Management Office may add other requirements in accordance with Civil Service Rules and Regulations and pertinent laws. The duties, responsibilities and functions of Medical Officer IV are as follows:

A. Examine patients and diagnose ailments, treat diseases by prescribing medicines and other therapeutic means;
B. Perform surgical operation within limitations as determined by the Chief of Clinic or Head of Department;
C. Make daily rounds to observe progress and condition of patients;
D. Prepare clinical records of patients upon admission and discharge;
E. Attend and participate in clinical pathological conferences;
F. Attend to patients during emergencies;
G. Consult supervisors on difficult and complicated cases and assist in major surgical operations;
H. Perform other functions as may be directed by the Department Head and/or any other superior officers.

Section 15. ABOLITION OF FOOD AND DRUG INSPECTION SECTION
The Food and Drug Inspection Section of the City Health Office is hereby abolished. The function of the Food and Drug Inspection Section is covered by the Food and Drug Administration which is a national agency created by Republic Act No. 3720, as amended by Republic Act No. 9711.

Section 16. PERSONNEL OF FOOD AND DRUG INSPECTION SECTION
The two (2) personnel under the abolished Food and Drug Inspection Section shall be transferred to the Environmental Sanitation Section. Sanitation Inspector VI shall be transferred as Section Head of the Environmental Sanitation Section. Sanitation Inspector V shall be transferred as Assistant Head of the Environmental Sanitation Section. Section 11 (c) of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018 states the provisions for transfer.

Section 17. REORGANIZATION OF ENVIRONMENTAL SANITATION SECTION
The single position of Engineer III shall be reclassified under the Environmental Sanitation Section to create two (2) positions of Sanitation Inspector II. In Local Government Units, reclassification is allowed if the position is vacant as provided for in the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018.

Section 18. REQUIREMENTS, DUTIES, AND RESPONSIBILITIES
Requirements for regular appointments as stated in the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018, shall be followed. The Human Resource Management Office may add other requirements in accordance with Civil Service Rules and Regulations and pertinent laws.

The duties, responsibilities and functions of Sanitation Inspector II are as follows:

A. Inspect/Monitor various environmental sanitation aspects: water sources, food establishments, public places and households;

ARTICLE V
The City Legal Office (CLO) and the City Administrator's Office - City Economic Enterprise (CAO-EE)

Section 19. CITY LEGAL OFFICE - Item No. 38 (Attorney IV) from the Transportation and Regulation Division of the City Legal Office shall be transferred to the Law Enforcement Division in the same office. Section 11 (c) of the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments and Other Human Resource Actions, Revised 2018, states the provisions for transfer.

Section 20. CITY ADMINISTRATOR'S OFFICE - CITY ECONOMIC ENTERPRISE - The New Matina Public Market and Food Terminal Office under the City Economic Enterprise is hereby abolished and the budget allocation for Personnel Services as well as the functions of fifteen (15) employees are likewise transferred to Bankerohan and Agdao Public Market Offices also of the City Economic Enterprise.

ARTICLE VI
Other Provisions

Section 21. APPROPRIATION - The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall appropriate the necessary funds to cover the benefits and salaries of the positions stated in this ordinance.

Section 23. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other laws, decrees, order, issuances, rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

Section 24. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any provision of this Ordinance, or any part thereof, is declared invalid or unconstitutional, all other sections or provisions not affected thereby shall remain in force and effect.

Section 22. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, October 15, 2019, by unanimous vote of the members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
