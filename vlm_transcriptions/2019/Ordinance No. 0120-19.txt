Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN DAVAO FASHION AND DESIGN COUNCIL FOUNDATION, INC. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

The agreement is made and entered into this day of 2019, at Davao City, Philippines by and between:

The City of Davao, a local government unit organized and existing under the laws of the Philippines, with address at City Hall Building, San Pedro St., Davao City herein represented by its City Mayor, Sara Z. Duterte, hereinafter referred to as the FIRST PARTY:

-and-

The Davao Fashion and Design Council Foundation, Inc. (DFDCFI), a domestic corporation duly registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission under Registration No. CN201622965 with address at its current Secretariat, U3/GF Double D Bldg., University Avenue, Juna Subd., Matina, Davao City, Mindanao, Philippines, represented in this Agreement by its President, JOSE RHONIEL L. BATU, through Board Resolution No. 0006, Series of 2019, dated May 11, 2019, the machine copy of which is herein attached as ANNEX "B," hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARTY:

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, DFDCFI has since 2016 embarked on series of projects to promote, preserve and develop Davao City's distinct culture, heritage and identity through fashion and design trade, culture and tourism initiatives specifically the Annual Fashion Weekend, Davao Biennial Stellar Mindanao Heritage Fashion Design Competition and Annual Hab' Kadayawan Fashion Accessories and Apparel Design Competitions leading to a global platform called Davao Design World Series with a theme: "One Thread, One Color, One Vision" need to accomplish these objectives;

WHEREAS, THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO has committed to support DFDCFI's trade, culture and tourism initiatives through a grant aid in support and assistance to the promotion, preservation and development of Davao's cultural heritage through progressive fashion and design;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the PARTIES agree(s) as follows:

SECTION I. SUBJECT OF AGREEMENT

A. The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO located in 2019 (Phip 1,400,000.00) to cover the initial implementation of Davao Design World Series Projects and release said amount to DFDCFI.

B. That the CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO shall be acknowledged in all promotional collaterals and publications of the DFDCFI's Davao Design World Series Projects.

--- [Page 14] ---
Memorandum of Agreement

SECTION II. REPORTING AND MONITORING

DFDCFI shall provide the CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO with an annual narrative and financial report in the use of the said grant.

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, through its duly appointed representative, may monitor and review the use of grant funds at any time it so desires.

SECTION III. EFFECTIVITY

This agreement shall take effect upon signing by the PARTIES and shall supersede any previous agreements and arrangements. Any changes or amendments hereto will be agreed upon in writing by the PARTIES.
