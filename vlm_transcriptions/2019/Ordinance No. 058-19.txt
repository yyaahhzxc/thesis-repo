Ord. No. 058-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND RIZAL MEMORIAL COLLEGES (RMC) FOR THE AFFILIATION OF ITS BACHELOR OF SCIENCE IN PSYCHOLOGY STUDENTS TO UNDERGO ON-THE-JOB TRAINING AT THE LINGAP CENTER FOR MENTALLY CHALLENGED CHILDREN".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Rizal Memorial Colleges (RMC), relative to the on-the-job training of its Bachelor of Science in Psychology students at the Lingap Center for Mentally Challenged Children.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, October 1, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement made and entered into and between:

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, located at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented herein by Davao City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE, herein after referred to as FIRST PARTY;

and

THE RIZAL MEMORIAL COLLEGES, INC., a corporation existing under the Philippine laws, with office address at RMC Buildings, Poblacion 7-A at F. Torres & Lopez-Jaena Streets, Marlboro Heights represented by its Vice President for Administration, ATTY. ERNESTO RAFAEL V. ROBILLO, hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARTY;

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the first party supervises the operations of the LINGAP CENTER FOR MENTALLY CHALLENGED CHILDREN located in Tugbok, Davao City, the institution under the City Social Services and Development Office that provides health services to children and adults with special needs.

WHEREAS, the second party desires to send and expose its students from the PSYCHOLOGY PROGRAM to the LINGAP CENTER FOR MENTALLY CHALLENGED CHILDREN for the internship training in compliance with the requirements of the course.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the above premises and their mutual covenants hereinafter set forth, the parties have agreed as follows:

1. TERMS OF REFERENCE (TOR)

The parties hereby bind themselves to undertake a Memorandum of Agreement for the purpose of internship program/practicum where students put into actual practice the knowledge and skills learned inside the classroom. The internship shall cover a period of fifteen days (15) working days consisting of six (6) hours a day, a total of ninety (90) hours per student for the entire duration of the affiliation program.

1. ACCOUNTABILITY

A. Responsibilities of the First Party

a. Accept affiliates of the second party Fourth Year Students of Bachelor of Science in Psychology and allied courses to undergo the On-the Job Training/Practicum or Internship Program.
b. Ensure the availability of the area/office or department assignment.
c. Provide competent cooperating site supervisor who will guide, direct and orient the Second Party Psychology students on the institution's policies, rules and regulations. The cooperating site supervisor will oversee the Intern Affiliation and evaluate the performance of the practicum interns.
d. Provide safety and security to the Second Party within the premises of the First Party.
e. Issue certificate of training completion at the end of the internship to the students who have satisfactorily complied with all the requirements under the program.

--- [Page 10] ---
B. Responsibilities of the Second Party

a. Provide a Training Curriculum to the First Party with a list of the students who will undergo internship, schedule of activities, rotation plan and other necessary forms to be used by the cooperating supervisors in evaluating the practicum intern.

b. Provide a person-in-charge of the practicum interns who shall be the contact person between the first party and the second party.

c. The Coordinator shall regularly visit the center to monitor, evaluate and supervise the activities and training of the student interns.

2. DURATION OF AGREEMENT

The agreement will commence upon signing by both parties on November 1, 2018 and will expire on May 31, 2021 renewable upon the recommendation of both parties, unless terminated by one of the parties for just cause of termination, within 30 days from notice to the other parties.
