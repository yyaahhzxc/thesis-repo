Ord. No. 0136-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR, AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE INSTITUTE OF CIVIL ENGINEERS (PICE) DAVAO CITY CHAPTER".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of the Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Institute of Civil Engineers (PICE) Davao City Chapter.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 5, 2019, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT, made and entered into by and between:

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a government entity, represented in this act by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, of legal age, Filipino, married, and a resident of Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the FIRST PARTY;

-AND-

PHILIPPINE INSTITUTE OF CIVIL ENGINEERS (PICE), DAVAO CITY CHAPTER, is a private organization, duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address located at cor. Panganiban-Arellano Sts., Davao City, represented in this act by its President, ENGR. ARNEL L. VILLALBA, of legal age, Filipino, and a resident of Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARTY;

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, THE FIRST PARTY thru the CITY ENGINEER'S OFFICE (CEO) AND OFFICE OF THE CITY BUILDING OFFICIAL (OCBO) is conducting rapid assessment and evaluation of all private and public infrastructure projects within Davao City, such as, but not limited to district halls, public schools, health centers, roads and bridges and other infrastructure projects funded by the City Government of Davao or the national government that were affected by the recent earthquakes;

WHEREAS, the OCBO is the office responsible for the implementation of P.D. 1096 for the inspection of all buildings within the jurisdiction of the FIRST PARTY;

WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY, who is composed of Civil Engineers and Structural Engineers, offered their services to help the FIRST PARTY to assist in the evaluation and validation of the structural stability of the buildings as part of their social responsibilities;

WHEREAS, THE FIRST PARTY agrees to engage the voluntary services offered by the SECOND PARTY due to their expertise, who are willing to conduct evaluation and validation, and make recommendations;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto have agreed to enter into an agreement, subject to the following terms and conditions, to wit:

RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE FIRST PARTY:

1. That the FIRST PARTY shall provide and inform the SECOND PARTY in the conduct inspections whenever there is occurrence of calamity, such as, but not limited to, earthquakes, floods, typhoons and tsunamis.

2. The FIRST PARTY shall keep records of all inspections conducted by both parties;

--- [Page 13] ---
RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE SECOND PARTY:

1. That the SECOND PARTY allows the FIRST PARTY to use the following standard assessment forms provided by the former with the logos of the City Government of Davao, CEO, OCBO and PICE, Davao City Chapter, to wit:
a) Green - Inspected (Lawful occupancy is permitted)
b) Yellow - Restricted Use
c) Red - Off Limits (Do not enter and occupy)

2. The SECOND PARTY shall provide the list of PICE volunteers for the conduct of inspections;

3. The SECOND PARTY shall provide IDs for the PICE volunteers and submit the list to the City Engineer's Office;

4. The SECOND PARTY shall review the retrofitting plan proposed by the FIRST PARTY and make recommendations thereto; and,

5. The SECOND PARTY shall cause/provide seminars for the safety officers appointed/identified by the FIRST PARTY.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have heretofore affixed their signatures this 1st day of November, 2019 at Davao City, Philippines.

PHILIPPINE INSTITUTE OF CIVIL ENGINEERS (PICE), DAVAO CITY CHAPTER
(Second Party)
