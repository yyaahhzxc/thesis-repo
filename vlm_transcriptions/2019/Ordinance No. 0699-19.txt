MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement, made, entered into and executed this 2018, by and between:

THE CITY OF DAVAO, a political subdivision of the Republic of the Philippines, City Hall of Davao, San Pedro Street represented herein by its MAYOR, HON. SARA DUERTE- CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as the "LGU";

THE HOUSING AND LAND USE REGULATORY BOARD, the government agency tasked to implement the provision RA 7279 in relation to RA 1084 with office address at 5th floor Sunmerry IT Center, 1614 Quizon Ave., Quzon City represented herein by its CEO and Commissioner, ATTY. LLOYD CHRISTOPHER LAO, hereinafter referred to as the "HLURB";

HT LAND a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at HT Land Inc., 8/F MSY Building, Peaceadores Street, Cebu Business Park, Cebu City, represented herein by its Project Director, TEODORE GILBERT C. ANG, and hereinafter referred to as "HT LAND";

BELLAVITA LAND CORP., a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at SF Makati Stock Exchange (MSX) Building, Ayala Triangle Avenue, Makati City, represented herein by its Attorney-in-Fact, MAY P. RODRIGUEZ and KRISTIN MELISSA R. OFILADA, and hereinafter referred to as "BELLAVITA";

Each of HLURB, LGU, HT LAND, and BELLAVITA may be individually referred to as a "Party," and collectively as "Parties."

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, Section 18 in relation to Section 21 of Republic Act No. 7279 as amended by Republic Act No. 10854 (the "Revised UDHA"), and Board Resolution No. 965, Series of 2017, or the "Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations to Givey Sections 3, 18 and 20 of Republic Act 7279, otherwise known as the Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992, as Amended by Republic Act No. 10884, Otherwise Known as "Balanced Housing Development Program Amendments" (the "Revised IRR") requires the developers of proposed subdivision and condominium projects to provide an area for socialized housing and/or condominium equivalent to 15% and 5%, respectively, or the same equivalent based on the cost of their subdivision or condominium project;

WHEREAS, Section 18(b) of the Revised UDHA, in relation to Section 11 of the Revised IRR states that the balanced housing requirement may be complied with by participating in any socialized housing project approved by the HLURB by way of construction or contracting through a third party for the implementation of land development plans which shall include the construction of roads, drainage and open space facilities;

WHEREAS, the HLURB is the sole agency tasked to ensure the strict compliance aforementioned laws;

WHEREAS, the CITY OF DAVAO is the local government unit seeking socialized housing projects which will adequately address the city's need for affordable housing;

WHEREAS, the LGU is the owner of eight (8) parcels of land located at barangay Biso, Lila, Los Amigos with a combined area of one hundred thirty-five thousand and thirty-three (135,033) square meters, more or less, and currently covered by Transfer Certificates of Title: 145-2011014522 (Lot 75), 145-

--- [Page 11] ---
2011/01446 (Lct. 257-A), 146-201101451 (Lct. 78), 146-201101443 (Lct. 257-B), 146-201200415 (Lct. 254), 146-201101451 (Lct. 263), 146-201200416 (Lct. 80), CL 4295, (Lct. 262) issued by the Registry of Deeds for Davao City, herein referred to as the "Property". The LGU's acquisition of the said property is covered by six (6) Deeds of Absolute Sale attached hereto as Annexes "____" to "____", while the certified true copies of the aforementioned Transfer Certificates of Title are attached as Annexes "____" to "____". LGU intends to rehabilitate the socialized housing resettlement project within the Property in accordance with the details as set forth in Section I of this Agreement.

WHEREAS, HT LAND and BELLAVITA (collectively, the "INVESTORS") undertake to invest in a rehabilitation of a socialized housing project through TWINA BUILDERS AND SUPPLY, INC., a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines ("the Contractor"), for the implementation of land development plans incentivized compliance under the abovementioned laws, under Sections 10 and 11 of the Revised IRR. A copy of the Articles of Incorporation and By-Laws, and Contractor's License of the Contractor are attached as Annexes "____" and "____" to this MOA and made an integral part hereof.

NOW THEREFORE, in accordance with their respective mandate, the PARTIES agree as they hereby agree as follows:

SECTION 1. Project Description. – The project shall consist mainly of the land development, which shall include the construction of roads, drainage, and open space facilities, of the Property with an aggregate area of 135,033 sq.m., with land development cost of ONE HUNDRED NINE MILLION SIXTY FOUR THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED PESOS ($189,042,000.00), which shall be known as FUENTE DE LOS AMIGOS PHASE II (the "PROJECT"). A copy of the site development plan for the PROJECT is attached as Annex "____" to this MOA and made an integral part hereof.

SECTION 2. Socialized Housing Compliance Component. – In accordance with the existing laws and rules and regulations, HLURB shall accredit the PROJECT as a BALAII Filipino Housing Project. This shall qualify all participation made by the INVESTORS for incentivized compliance of the balance housing requirement under RA 7278 as amended by RA 10824.

SECTION 3. Participation of the INVESTORS with Specifications. – HT LAND shall invest the total amount of THIRTY MILLION FOUR HUNDRED FORTY THREE THOUSAND THREE HUNDRED NINETY NINE PESOS AND THIRTY SIX CENTAVOS ($38,443,399.36) which shall be utilized and allocated through the Contractor for the design, engineering, and construction of the land development for the PROJECT, which shall include the construction of roads, drainage, and open space facilities with a total value of developing a total area of 27,469 sq.m. of the PROJECT. A map with a geocoding of the specified total area is attached hereto as "Annex ____".

BELLAVITA shall invest the total amount of ONE HUNDRED FIFTY MILLION SIX HUNDRED TWO THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED PESOS AND SIXTY FOUR CENTAVOS ($150,602,800.64) which shall be utilized and allocated through the Contractor for the design, engineering, and construction of the land development of the PROJECT, which shall include the construction of roads, drainage, and open space facilities for 157,537 sq.m. of the PROJECT. A map with a geocoding of the specified total area is attached hereto as "Annex ____" and made an integral part of this MOA.

The INVESTORS shall remit the corresponding portion of its respective investment directly to the Contractor or its representative, in accordance with the progress billings from the Contractor. The Contractor shall directly advise the INVESTORS in writing as to the progress billings and status of the land development

SECTION 4. Socialized Housing Credit Computation. – The land development cost shall be used as basis for purposes of computing the advance credit value in favor of the INVESTORS’ or such INVESTOR’s Affiliate’s compliance with the balanced housing requirement in Section 18 of the Revised UDHA. In relation, Section 11 of the Revised IRR provides for incentivized compliance on participation in the rehabilitation of calamity-stricken communities under the BALAI Programs of the HUDCC. The computation of the advance credit value shall be based on the percentage (20%) of the total project cost of the socialized housing project in order to be credited the entire socialized housing project or program should be the basis for the computation for purposes of compliance.

On the basis of the foregoing, the amount of THIRTY EIGHT MILLION FOUR HUNDRED FORTY THREE THOUSAND THREE HUNDRED NINETY NINE PESOS AND THIRTY SIX CENTAVOS (P38,443,399.46) as participation of HT Land may immediately be used as advance credit of ONE HUNDRED NINETY TWO MILLION TWO HUNDRED SIXTEEN THOUSAND THREE HUNDRED NINETY SIX PESOS AND EIGHTY CENTAVOS (P192,216,996.80).

On the basis of the foregoing, the amount of ONE HUNDRED FIFTY MILLION SIX HUNDRED TWO THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED PESOS AND SIXTY FOUR CENTAVOS (P150,682,604.64) as participation of BELLAVITA may immediately be used as advance credit of SEVEN HUNDRED FIFTY THREE MILLION FOURTEEN THOUSAND THREE PESOS AND TWENTY CENTAVOS (P753,014,003.20). Notwithstanding the foregoing, HLURB recognizes the critical role of the INVESTORS in the PROJECT, and hereby agrees and expressly authorizes the immediate application of the advance credit values computed above, notwithstanding actual remittance of the aforementioned amounts which the Parties have agreed to be based on progress billings by the Contractor, upon the execution of this MOA, for any of its or its assignees’ projects located anywhere in the Philippines. HLURB warrants that the authorization granted hereunder may be used by the INVESTORS in lieu of a Compliance Certification to be subsequently issued as proof of compliance with Section 18 of the Revised UDHA. However, should the INVESTORS or its assigns’ request for a Compliance Certification, the HLURB shall issue the same.

Any of the INVESTORS may assign the advance credit values computed above to any of its Affiliates. For this purpose, Affiliate means any corporation, partnership, other firm or entity, controlling, controlling or by or under Common Control with the INVESTOR, as defined in the following: “Control” (including with control) means the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of any corporation, partnership, other or firm of association, whether through the ownership of 51% or more of the voting stock, by contract or otherwise. For BELLAVITA, these Affiliates include Ayala Land, Inc., Alveo Land Corp., Avida Land Corp., and Amaia Land Corp, as evidenced by the Secretary’s Certificates attached hereto as Annexes “____” to ______.

Any assignment of the credit values to the projects of the INVESTORS and their Affiliates shall be annotated on the Transfer Certificates of Titles, as described above, and the PROJECT’s Certificate of Registration to be issued by HLURB, in lieu of the annotation on the License to Set as set in Section 22 of HLURB Memorandum Circular No. 9, Series of 2018 (the “Guidelines”). Details of the annotation shall be in accordance with the provisions set in the Guidelines.

SECTION 5. Government Responsibilities – The Parties understand and recognize the significant and critical role of the land development as a component of the PROJECT. Thus, in addition to the development of the PROJECT, HLURB and LGU agree to render full cooperation and fulfill the following obligations to support developer parties construction of the land development:

i) Authorize the Contractor and its employees, agents, contractors, and representatives to mobilize and access the Project for the purpose of conducting the necessary technical studies and surveys, as well as all works and construction in furtherance of its obligations hereunder;

ii) Process and secure all required national and local permits, consents, certificates, and approvals;

iii) Provide and secure the final alignment and demarcations of the main roads, streets, and alleys, including right-of-way agreements with private land owners, if necessary, to enable the Contractor to lay down the necessary transmission lines and tapping points needed for the PROJECT;

iv) Provide adequate security measures for the project development and construction personnel of the Contractor during the development of land until full turn-over, as stipulated below;

v) Take-over, by itself or through assignment in favor of another government instrumentalty, the over-all operation, maintenance, and management of the system, including all of its component parts, upon completion of the same;

vi) Allow the immediate application by the INVESTORS of their respective advance credit values computed in accordance with Section 4 upon the execution of this MOA and submission of written request for the application of the same, for any of its or its assignee's projects located anywhere in the Philippines; and

vii) Issue a Certificate of Compliance with the balanced housing requirement in Section 18 of the Revised UDHA upon written request of the relevant INVESTOR.

SECTION 7. Force Majeure. – No Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in the performance of its obligations hereunder to the extent that such failure or delay shall be caused by or arise out of a “Force Majeure Event.” For purposes of this Agreement, a “Force Majeure Event” shall include, but not be limited to: (i) declared or undeclared war, armed hostilities, revolution, rebellion, insurrection, riot, public disorder, and the like; (ii) the expropriation of any part of the Site or which the PROJECT will be located; (iii) unforeseen rock formations in the geodetic layers of the identified deep well sites; and (iv) extreme and unforeseen weather conditions, fire, unusual flood, earthquake, and similar calamities or acts of God.

The Party invoking the existence of a Force Majeure Event shall immediately notify the other Party, specifying the nature and details of the Force Majeure Event, how it affects the Party’s ability to comply with its obligations hereunder, and a proposed mitigating action to address the same. Upon the cessation of such Force Majeure Event, the Parties shall immediately resume the performance of their obligations hereunder; and if the Force Majeure Event is found to have lasted in excess of 180 consecutive calendar days, the Parties hereto shall meet to discuss the basis and terms upon which this MOA can be continued or mutually terminated.

SECTION 8. Termination. – In the event of termination of this MOA prior to the completion of the land development and without fault on the part of any of the Parties, whether or not the PROJECT is fully implemented or not, the Parties agree that the value of the portion of the land development then constructed and installed, if not yet complete, shall be appraised independently and INVESTORS shall be credited with a proportion of the Credit Value for future utilization in any of its subdivision or condominium projects for a period of ten (10) years from the date that the proportional Credit Value has been finally determined.

SECTION 9. Indemnification. – HLURB and LGU shall hold INVESTORS, and its respective directors, officers, employees, agents, and representatives free and harmless from and against any and all claims, demands, actions, suits, damages payable to third parties by reason of any delay or failure of LGU to fully develop or to develop the housing units in accordance with the relevant provisions of law the housing units of the PROJECT or due to workmanship issues on the housing units of the PROJECT), unless such is caused by its fault, negligence, or willful misconduct of the relevant INVESTOR or its representatives in the fulfillment of its role as investors of said PROJECT.

SECTION 10. Complete Agreement. – This MOA contains the full and complete agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter, and it supersedes any and all other representation, understanding, or agreement, verbal or written, implied or express, previously made or entered into by them. Thus, this MOA may only be amended upon the written mutual agreement of the Parties.
