Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. Section 1 of Ordinance No. 0218-09, Series of 2009, is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "Davao City Park Systems Management Code".

SECTION 2. Section 2 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - It is hereby declared the policy of the local government of Davao City to establish and maintain free, publicly accessible, and safe green and public open spaces as an important component of a sustainable city. It shall ensure the protection and sustainability of the environment taking into consideration climate change adaptation in synergy with disaster risk reduction and management, principles of biodiversity and preservation of historical and cultural heritage in all development processes.

The City shall adopt the concept of Privately-Owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS) and shall also promote a participative, empowered, and environmentally conscious community by developing linkages and interactions between among local and national government agencies and stakeholders, multi-sectoral participation, under a shared environmental governance of mutual accountability and collaborative management.

SECTION 3. Section 3 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS. - As used in this Code, the following terms shall mean:

A. "Green Space" - An area set apart to be planted with trees and ornamental plants and/or to be developed for water pond purposes to promote urban biodiversity and climate change mitigation.
B. "Green Strip" - A long narrow area of land planted with trees, shrubs, ornamental plants which include, but not limited to, sidewalks and street islands.
C. "Park" refers to any public area of land set aside for aesthetic, educational, recreational or cultural use by the public in essentially urban surroundings, such as, but not limited to, public squares, plazas, promenades, center islands, heritage sites, historical sites, protected areas, conservation areas, and memorials.
D. "Permeable Paving System" is an architectural and engineering design that allows storm water or flood to seep through the surface material laid down on an area.
E. "Privately-owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS)" - are publicly accessible spaces in the form of plazas, terraces, atriums, small parks, and even snippets which are provided and maintained by private developers.

F. "Street Island" - a raised or marked-off area in the middle of the street or road designed as a guide for traffic, used as a safety island, or for separating lanes.

SECTION 4. Section 4 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 4. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY. - The Davao City Park Systems Management Code shall apply to all existing public parks, green spaces and green strips, as well as areas declared as Open Space Easement Zones and Parks and Recreation Zones within the entire territory and jurisdiction of the City. This shall also apply to all those above enumerated which may later be established in the implementation of this Code.

SECTION 5. Section 5 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 5. DAVAO CITY PARK SYSTEMS MANAGEMENT BOARD. - For the purpose of the implementation of this Code, the Davao City Park Systems Management Board (DCPSMB) is hereby created which shall henceforth be referred as the Parks Board.

The Park Systems Board shall be responsible for formulating plans, policies and courses of action for the standards, regulations and maintenance of all public parks, green spaces and green strips within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao.

SECTION 6. SECTION 6 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 6. COMPOSITION. - The Parks Board shall be composed of the following:

A. Chairperson - the City Mayor or his/her duly authorized representative;

B. Vice Chairperson/Secretary - the head of the City Environment and Natural Resources Office or his/her duly authorized representative;

C. Members:
I. The Chairperson of the Committee on Environment and Natural Resources of the Davao City Council;
II. The Chairperson of the Committee on Tourism of the Davao City Council;
III. The President of the Liga ng mga Barangay;
IV. The Department of Public Works and Highways;
V. The Department of Environment and Natural Resources;
VI. The City Tourism Operations Office;
VII. The City Planning and Development Office;
VIII. The City Engineer;
IX. The City Legal Officer;
X. The Interim Office of the City Architect;
XI. The City Transport and Traffic Management Office;
XII. A representative from Museo Dabaweno;
XIII. The Davao City Chamber of Commerce and Industry;
XIV. Two (2) representatives from non-government organizations who are members of environmental conservation or protection groups;

XV. A representative from the Philippine Institute of Civil Engineers, Inc.;
XVI. A representative from the United Architects of the Philippines.

The Secretariat shall record all the activities of the Board, issue the resolutions passed by the same and shall retain the possession of all documents and records.

The representatives from the association of engineers and architects and duly accredited non-government environmental organizations must be members of a group, association, or organization approved and registered by the local government pursuant to laws relating to associations and shall be chosen by the Parks Board Chairperson from a short list of candidates formally nominated by the members of the Parks Board.

SECTION 7. Section 7 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 7. TERM OF OFFICE. – All members shall be coterminous with the appointing authority, provided that they shall be eligible for reappointment after the expiration of their respective terms.

If the Chairperson or any member fails to complete his/her term, the person appointed to fill the vacancy shall serve only for the unexpired portion of the vacancy which he/she succeeds, provided, that such service of the unexpired portion shall not be counted as a term.

SECTION 8. Section 8 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 8. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS. – The Parks Board shall have the following powers and functions, to wit;

A. Formulate the Implementing Rules and Regulations of this Code;
B. Ensure the implementation of all the provisions of this Code and its Implementing Rules and Regulations;
C. Adopt guidelines for the use of parks for special occasions;
D. Devise policies and procedures for the unitary parameter of all public parks, green spaces and green strips in Davao City, providing for their proper preservation and regulation for the furtherance of the ecological balance of the community;
E. Propose ways and means to ensure the best and most desirable facilities that shall be placed in the City Parks;
F. Approved architectural design and structural models of public parks, green spaces and green strips prepared by the Parks and Playground Division of the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO), the City Planning and Development Office, the City Interim Office of the City Architect and the City Engineer’s Office;

G. Identify and map out street islands and existing parks that are open for possible adoption;

H. Conduct a continuing information and education campaign to generate public awareness and participation on the importance of establishing and sustainably managing public parks and open spaces.

SECTION 9. Section 9 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 9. ESTABLISHMENT OF NEW PARKS. - The City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) shall identify possible areas to be utilized as public parks, green spaces and green strips, subject to the approval of the Parks Board. The same shall be forwarded to the Sangguniang Panlungsod for the appropriate legislation formally allocating the identified areas.

All new parks shall incorporate a permeable paving system, apply disaster risk reduction and management techniques, promote local biodiversity, and ensure a safe and inclusive environment for Persons with Disability, families with children, senior citizens and other people with special needs in their engineering and architectural design.

SECTION 10. Section 10 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 10. DEVELOPMENT AND MAINTENANCE OF PUBLIC PARKS. - Public Parks situated within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City shall be principally developed, maintained, regulated and preserved by the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) through its Parks and Playgrounds Division, which should be coordinated with the Parks Board and other concerned agencies for the effective and proper maintenance of all parks in the City.

SECTION 11. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance which shall read as:

SECTION 11. NAMING OF PARKS. - Pursuant to Section 13 of Republic Act 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, all parks, playgrounds, plazas and the like within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City shall be named in consultation with the National Historical Commission of the Philippines (previously the Philippine Historical Commission) and subject to the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

Names with historical, cultural, or ethnic significance shall be encouraged. None of the parks, playgrounds, plazas and the like shall be named after a living person. A change of name of an existing park, playground or plaza may not be made unless for a justifiable reason and, in any case, not oftener than once every ten (10) years.

SECTION 12. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read as:

SECTION 12. PARK RULES AND REGULATIONS.

A. Park Schedule. All public parks in Davao City shall have the following regular hours of operation, unless a different park schedule is provided by the Parks Board upon its creation or upon its resolution for valid grounds.
5:00 AM - 10:00 PM MONDAYS-SUNDAYS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC

B. Prohibited Acts. To ensure the proper management and maintenance of all public parks, adopted parks or street-islands or green spaces and green strips, the following acts shall be prohibited, to wit:

i. Smoking tobacco, e-cigarettes, vape, and other similar acts;
ii. Bringing and consuming alcohol and/or illegal drugs;
iii. Bringing in of dangerous or hazardous materials, weapons (or similar objects), and glass containers;
iv. Defacing, destroying, and vandalizing of park property;
v. Gambling of any form;
vi. Any form of indecent or immoral acts;
vii. Littering, spitting, public urination and other forms of improper waste disposal;
viii. Picking of flowers, nailing of trees and/or any destruction or injury to plants in the parks;
ix. Climbing of trees, statues, sculptures, walls, or any park structure not intended for play or recreation;
x. Leafletting, distributing or posting of bills or any printed materials;
xi. Sleeping and lying down anywhere within the premises;
xii. Putting up billboards, advertisements or signs;
xiii. Staging of unauthorized events, such as, but not limited to religious activities, public gatherings, or unauthorized assembly;
xiv. Selling, vending, peddling or displaying of goods of any sort;

SECTION 13. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read:

SECTION 13. PENALTY. – Any violation of the provision of this Ordinance shall be punished as follows, without prejudice to the prosecution of other actions:
1st Offense – fine of P3,000
2nd Offense – fine of P5,000 and/or imprisonment of three months at the discretion of the court
3rd Offense – fine of P5,000 and/or imprisonment of one year at the discretion of the court

If the violation is committed by a corporation, trust, firm, partnership or association or any other juridical person, the penalty shall be imposed upon the President, General Manager and other guilty officer or officers of such corporation, trust, firm, partnership, association or entity.

SECTION 14. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read as:

SECTION 14. PRIVATELY-OWNED PUBLIC OPEN SPACES (POPOS). – The Privately-Owned Public Open Spaces Program is hereby established, in collaboration with private developers and entities,

Private developers and entities that will provide POPOS may be given incentives as approved by the Parks Board and the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

SECTION 15. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read:

SECTION 15. ADOPT-A-STREET ISLAND OR ADOPT-A-PARK PROGRAM. – The Adopt-A-Street Island and Adopt-A-Park Program are hereby established, allowing opportunities for companies, organizations, institutions, and private groups or individuals to adopt a street island or park through two options: (1) hands-on landscaping and gardening, or (2) sponsoring the beautification and maintenance.

Requirements and mechanics of these programs shall be defined in the Implementing Rules and Regulations of this Code which shall be formulated by the Parks Board.

SECTION 16. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read:

SECTION 16. FUNDS. – To ensure the effectiveness of the implementation of the Parks Code, any budgetary requirements needed for the operation and implementation of the Parks Code shall be taken from any available funds in the Annual Budget subject to the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

The Parks Board is authorized to secure other sources of funds for the management of public parks, green spaces and green strips, from grants and collaborations with private institutions or sectors, subject to existing laws and regulations. Such grants and donations shall be used only for the specified purpose for which they have been given and shall not be appropriated for any other programs unless a new agreement will be made. A trust fund will be set up for this purpose.

SECTION 17. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read as:

SECTION 17. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS. - The City Mayor, through an Executive Order, shall convene the Parks Board to formulate the Implementing Rules and Regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this Code. Such rules and regulations shall take effect immediately after publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

SECTION 18. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - In the event that any word, article, sentence, clause or paragraph of this Ordinance is declared invalid for any reason, other provisions thereof shall not be affected by such declaration and shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 19. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read as:

SECTION 19. REPEALING CLAUSE. - All other ordinances and pertinent rules and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance are likewise repealed and amended accordingly.

SECTION 20. A new section shall be included in the amended Ordinance and shall read as:

SECTION 20. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after completion of its publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.
