Ord. No. 0171-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE TOURISM INFRASTRUCTURE AND ENTERPRISE ZONE AUTHORITY (TIEZA) AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO CONSULTING SERVICES FOR THE PREPARATION OF THE TOURISM MASTER PLAN".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be executed by and between the Tourism Infrastructure and Enterprise Zone Authority (TIEZA) and the City Government of Davao relative to consulting services for the preparation of the Tourism Master Plan.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 17, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement made and entered into this day of 2019 in Philippines, by and between:

The TOURISM INFRASTRUCTURE and ENTERPRISE ZONE AUTHORITY, a government-owned and controlled corporation, with office address at 7th floor, Tower 1, Double Dragon, Meridian Tower, Diosdado Macapagal Ave. cor. EDSA extension, Pasay City, represented herein by its Chief Operating Officer, POCHOLO JOSELITO D. PARAGAS, hereinafter referred to as "TIEZA";

and

The CITY OF DAVAO a local government unit, represented herein by its Mayor, SARA DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as "CITY";

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, in accordance with its mandate to develop tourism infrastructure projects in the country, TIEZA shall fund the CONSULTING SERVICES FOR THE PREPARATION OF TOURISM MASTER PLAN FOR CITY OF DAVAO located at Davao City hereinafter referred to as the "SERVICES";

WHEREAS, TIEZA is desirous that the CITY undertake the CONSULTING SERVICES FOR THE PREPARATION OF TOURISM MASTER PLAN FOR CITY OF DAVAO for the City of Davao.

WHEREAS, the CONSULTING SERVICES FOR THE PREPARATION OF TOURISM MASTER PLAN FOR CITY OF DAVAO shall be implemented by the CITY through TRANSFER OF FUND for the purpose.

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants and undertakings hereinafter set forth, the parties hereto have agreed as follows:

--- [Page 10] ---
ARTICLE I: RESPONSIBILITY OF PARTIES

A. Consistent with the purposes of this Agreement, TIEZA shall:

1. Transfer the Fund to the CITY in the amount of EIGHT MILLION FOUR-HUNDRED NINE SEVEN THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED FORTY-NINE NINE AND 50/100 CENTAVOS (P8,497,849.50) which shall be exclusively used to procure a contract for CONSULTING SERVICES FOR THE PREPARATION OF TOURISM MASTER PLAN FOR CITY OF DAVAO through competitive public bidding and in accordance with Terms of Reference previously evaluated and approved by TIEZA.

The "Fund" shall be released in two (2) tranches after the execution of this Memorandum of Agreement (MOA), based on the following schedule and subject to conditions set out in this Agreement:

a) First release - SIX MILLION PESOS (P6,000,000.00), upon the submission by the CITY of the required procurement documents, including Notice of Award (NOA);

b) Second/Final release - TWO MILLION FOUR HUNDRED NINETY SEVEN THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED FORTY NINE PESOS AND 50/100 CENTAVOS (P2,497,849.50), as required by the City Council and upon presentation of the amount from the first tranche released and submission of accomplishment report duly signed by TIEZA Project Engineer stating that the services rendered is 100% completed.

3. For its part, the CITY obligates itself to:

1. Accept the Fund for the "SERVICES" and place the same in a trust account opened by the CITY specifically for the project.

2. Upon receipt of the CITY of the amount allotted for each tranche, the Treasurer of the CITY shall issue and submit to TIEZA corresponding Official Receipts acknowledging receipt of the amount.

3. Fully and properly liquidate the fund released in accordance with the existing and applicable government accounting and auditing rules. The liquidation report shall be supported by an accomplishment report duly signed by the TIEZA Project Engineer attesting to the reported percentage of accomplished or completion of the project by the contractor.

4. Ensure the completion of the project in accordance with the Terms of Reference, as evaluated and approved by TIEZA, without modification or alteration and shall not commence the procurement process or conduct of public bidding on project unless any modification to the Terms of Reference is made.

5. Submit certificate of completion and acceptance to TIEZA.
6. Return to TIEZA within ninety (90) days from the date of completion of the project, the unutilized or unexpended fund released by TIEZA. In no instance shall the fund released by TIEZA or any part thereof be used by the CITY for any other purpose other than for the completion of the project in accordance with the terms of the Agreement.

8. Furnish TIEZA copy of the Post Audit Report prepared by the COA Engineers in the CITY.
9. The CITY shall provide for its own account in the event that consultancy cost is insufficient to cover the actual cost and related expenses.

ARTICLE II: OTHER COVENANTS
1. This Agreement contains the entire understanding of the parties. No modification or alteration of this Agreement shall be considered as having been made unless executed in writing and duly signed by the parties hereto. If any part of this Agreement be declared null and void by a competent authority, the other parts not affected thereby shall remain valid and binding between the parties;
2. It is understood and agreed that should there be any violation of the terms and conditions of this Agreement by the CITY, the TIEZA shall be constrained from entertaining or considering future requests by the CITY on any project to be funded by the TIEZA.
3. The CITY shall submit to the TIEZA a Sangguniang Panglunsod Resolution authorizing the Mayor to enter/ sign this contract.
4. TIEZA may require the CITY to submit documents, reports, books, certificates, affidavits, and such other proof of the completion of the projects.
5. It is hereby understood and agreed that the CITY shall take up in its books of accounts the project in accordance with accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

--- [Page 12] ---
6. The provisions, requirements and conditions under R.A. 9184 and Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations are incorporated in this Agreement.
7. The CITY shall invite a duly authorized representative from TIEZA to attend the pre-procurement conference and opening of bids.
8. If the approved budget for the contract is less than the budgetary estimate approved by the TIEZA Board, the former shall prevail.
9. The request for release of the First Tranche and Second/Final Tranche of the project cost as well as the submission of the reportorial requirements and requisite documents to be submitted to the TIEZA through an endorsement or transmittal letter.
10. Should the CITY fail to liquidate the project cost within the prescribed period, the TIEZA shall exercise its right to file the necessary legal actions against the CITY to protect the interest of the Government.
11. The Parties are under obligation to ensure the security of all personal and/or sensitive personal information received and shared between and among themselves in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
12. In case of data privacy concerns involving the parties and this MOA, reference shall be made to the TIEZA Privacy Manual.
13. In such event, the Authority shall not terminate this Agreement, but will immediately enforce the necessary measures in case of breach provided for in the Data Privacy Act, its IRR and related issuances.
14. No modification or alteration of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by duly authorized representatives of the Parties hereto.
