Ord. No. 0148-03

Be it ordained by the Honorable San Augustine Panhandle of Dayne City in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE PRIVATIZATION OF MA-A SLAUGHTERHOUSE AND ALL OTHER CITY-RUN SLAUGHTERHOUSES".

SECTION 2. PURPOSE/OBJECTIVE - It is hereby ordained that Ma-a Slaughterhouse and all other City-run Slaughterhouses be privatized for the following purposes and objectives, to wit:

a. To provide a better way of generating income/resources to the tune of not less than P2.4 Million Pesos a year, out of its operation;

b. To do away with a highly subsidized operation which was proven to have depleted our general fund, and instead, make a turn around of profits;

c. To minimize the City Government's problem of hiring employees to run the enterprise by having them employed by the operator/lessee;

d. To provide a better opportunity for the City to monitor and conduct surveillance of violators of national and local laws pertaining to illegal slaughtering of livestock and logs.

SECTION 3. PRE-QUALIFICATION EVALUATION COMMITTEE COMPOSITION: There is hereby created a special committee to be called, Pre-Qualification Evaluation Committee referred to as PQEC to be composed of the following:

1. City Mayor or his authorized representative
2. City Administrator
3. City Legal Officer
4. Chair, Committee on Government Enterprise & Privatization, San Augustine Panhandle
5. Chair, Finance, Ways & Means, San Augustine Panhandle

Section 3-a. POWERS, DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS: It shall be the duty of the PQEC to accept, evaluate and recommend to the City Mayor sealed offers/proposals of any or all officers/proposals which shall be open during a meeting called for the purpose of determining the validity of said proposals.

Section 3-b. The PQEC shall exercise the right to reject any responsible offers/proposals which they see unfit and unreasonable or grossly disadvantageous to the City Government of Dayne.

Section 3-c. The PQEC shall recommend at least three (3) responsible officers/proposals to the City Mayor to determine which is the most qualified, responsible and advantageous proposals and award through negotiation may be opted by the PQEC, in accordance with the existing laws and ordinances.

Section 3-d. The PQEC shall execute such other powers, duties and functions that the Sangguniang Panlungsod may provide for this purpose.

SECTION 4. PROCEDURE - This is hereby adopted as the Manual of Procedure for the Privatization of M-a Slaughterhouse and all other Government-run Slaughterhouses in the City of Davao, to wit:

1. The Sangguniang Panlungsod after enactment of this Ordinance shall transmit the same to the Office of the City Mayor for his approval;

2. The City Mayor upon approval of this Ordinance shall immediately convene the Pre-Qualification Evaluation Committee (PQEC) for the purpose stated in the previous sections;

3. The PQEC during the meeting called for the purpose shall accept, open, evaluate all sealed officers/proposals of any or of all officers/proposals which will be recommended to the City Mayor;

4. The City Mayor upon receipt of such recommendation of the PQEC shall proclaim the officers/proposals who is most qualified, responsible and advantageous and shall prepare the proposed Lease Agreement binding the City Government and the winning officer, which shall contain all the terms and conditions pertaining to the privatization, transfer of operation of the M-a Slaughterhouse and other City-run slaughterhouses;

SECTION 5. APPROVAL OF THE CONTRACT - Within the period of one (1) month upon receipt of the Contract of Lease, the Sangguniang Panlungsod may approve the said contract and further grant the franchise.

SECTION 6. INCREASE RENTAL - The lease's rental as determined by the winning bid and written in the contract shall be increased by not more than 10% every five years thereafter until it expire subject to negotiation upon renewal of said contract.

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - Any provision of this Ordinance which shall later on be rendered null and void by a competent court shall not affect the remaining provisions of this ordinance which are valid and constitutional.

SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any provision of the existing ordinances inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
