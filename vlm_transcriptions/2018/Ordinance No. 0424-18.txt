Ord. No. 0424-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC THE STREET ALONG SAN PEDRO CORNER CROOKED ROAD AND BOLTON STREET ON MARCH 18, 2018 FROM 3:00 P.M. TO 12:00 MIDNIGHT FOR THE TUGTUGAN SA DAVAO (BATTLE OF THE BAND);"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.- Section 21 (c) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs"; 

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE.- The street along San Pedro corner Crooked Road and Bolton Street shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on March 18, 2018 from 3:00 P.M. to 12:00 Midnight as requested by the City Tourism Operations Office for the Tugtugan sa Davao (Battle of Band);

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.- If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 9th day of March, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
