Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE CITY ARCHIVES AND RECORDS OFFICE, AS AMENDED".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of the Local Government Code of the Philippines provides that "every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare."

Section 17 thereof also provides that "local government units shall likewise exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective provision of the basic services and facilities enumerated herein."

SECTION 3. CREATION OF OFFICE - The City Archives and Records Office is hereby created under the direct supervision and control of the City Mayor's Office. It shall be headed by a City Government Department Head II to be assisted by a City Government Assistant Department Head II. The Office shall be composed of four (4) divisions, as follows:

a. Administrative Division;
b. Records Management Division;
c. Archives Collection & Access Division; and
d. Archives Preservation Division.

SECTION 4. THE CITY ARCHIVES AND RECORDS OFFICE

a. The City Government Department Head II shall oversee the operation of the Office and its employees in planning, formulating and implementing a records management and archival program for the efficient creation, utilization, maintenance, retention, storage, preservation, conservation, protection and disposal of all City public records. He/she shall perform other related tasks as may be necessary from time to time.

b. The City Government Assistant Department Head II shall assist the City Government Department Head II in overseeing the operation of the Office and its employees. He/she shall perform other related tasks as may be necessary from time to time.

c. The Administrative Division shall:

1. Take charge and provide administrative support functions for the smooth, efficient and effective implementation of the Office's mandate;

2. Disseminate and implement rules, laws, policies and other legal issuances pertaining to personnel, budgeting, accounting and such similar areas affecting the Office, its employees and activities;

--- [Page 6] ---
3. Provide coordinative action with other offices, agencies and the general public; and

4. Do other related activities as may be necessary or required from time to time.

d. The Records Management Division shall:

1. Assist in planning, formulating and implementing a records management program and coordinate with City Departments/Offices for the efficient creation, utilization, maintenance, retention, storage, protection and disposal of all temporary City public records;

2. Do other related tasks as may be necessary or required from time to time.

e. The Archives Collection & Access Division shall:

1. Undertake and provide technical assistance in the gathering, proper storage, protection and availability of all permanent City public records;

2. Do other related tasks as may be necessary or required from time to time.

f. The Archives Preservation Division shall:

1. Undertake conservation and restoration of all permanent City public records;

2. Render technical assistance, training, and repair/rehabilitation of damaged records;

3. Perform other related tasks as may be necessary or required from time to time.

SECTION 5. BUDGET ALLOCATION - The City Budget Officer shall annually allocate funds for the operation of the City Archives and Records Office which will be sourced from the General Fund;

SECTION 6. PERSONNEL COMPLEMENT - The Department shall be composed of a total of ninety-one (91) positions to support its manpower requirements, as follows:

(a) Seven (7) positions to be newly created, to wit:

Section 7. AUTOMATIC TRANSFER OF BUDGETARY ALLOCATION. All budgetary allocations pertaining to Personal Services for the positions to be transferred to the City Archives and Records Office from the different City Departments and Offices as mentioned in Section 6(b) hereof shall be deemed automatically transferred to the said Office from the effectiveness of this Ordinance.  

Section 8. PERSONNEL TRANSITION. To effect the transfer of personnel from other Department to the City Archives and Records Office, (CARO), affected employees shall be given due and appropriate notice by the Local Chief Executive pursuant to Civil Service Rules and Regulations.  

Section 9. REPEALING CLAUSE. All ordinances, resolution, orders and parts thereof which are inconsistent with any provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.  

Section 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If, for any reason/s any part or provision of this Ordinance is held to be unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof that are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE CITY ARCHIVES AND RECORDS OFFICE, AS AMENDED".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of the Local Government Code of the Philippines provides that "every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare";

Section 17 thereof also provides that "local government units shall likewise exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective provision of the basic services and facilities enumerated under the Local Government Code".

Section 13 of Republic Act 9470 (or the "National Archives of the Philippines Act of 2007") provides that "All government offices shall each establish their archives and records office/units in coordination with the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) and the National Archives from their organic personnel within a year from the effectivity of this Act".

Article 30 of Rule 20, Title IV of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of RA 9470 further provides that "... It shall plan, formulate and implement a records management and archival administration program for the efficient creation, utilization, maintenance, retention, storage, preservation, conservation and disposal of public records including the adoption of security measures and vital records protection program for the agency".

Article 32 of Rule 20, Title IV of the same IRR also states, "... every LGU should establish an archival system to ensure the safety and protection of all public documents and records such as records of births, marriages, deaths and such other documents and records of public interest in the various offices and departments in the province, city or municipality and the barangay concerned".

SECTION 3. CREATION OF OFFICE - The City Archives and Records Office is hereby created under the direct supervision and control of the City Mayor's Office. It shall be headed by a Department Head and a City Government Assistant Department Head. For this purpose, the following divisions shall be created:

a. Administrative Division;
b. Records Management Division;
c. Archives Collection & Access Division; and
d. Archives Preservation Division.

SECTION 4. THE CITY ARCHIVES AND RECORDS OFFICE

a. The Chief Archivist shall oversee the operation of the Office and its employees in planning, formulating and implementing a records management and archival program for the efficient creation, utilization, maintenance, retention, storage, preservation, conservation, protection and disposal of all City public records. He/she shall do other related tasks as may be necessary from time to time;

3. Provide coordinate action with other offices, agencies and the general public;

ORDINANCE NO. 0396-18, SERIES OF 2018 ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE CITY ARCHIVES AND RECORDS OFFICE AS AMENDED

1ST INDORSEMENT

April 23, 2018

Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject ordinance, informing your end that the same is legally defective as shown hereunder:

SECTION 3. of the ordinance states that "CREATION OF OFFICE: The City Archives and Records Office is hereby created under the direct supervision and control of the City Mayor's Office. It shall be headed by a Department Head and a City Government Assistant Department Head. For this purpose, the following divisions shall be created: (underline supplied)

a. Administrative Division
b. Records and Management Division
c. Archives Collection and Access Division; and
d. Archives Preservation Division."

It can be gleaned from the first sentence that the intention is to create the office under the Office of the City Mayor and not separate department. Hence, it should be headed by a Division head. However, under the second sentence, it states that the office shall be headed by a Department Head and Assistant Department Head with four (4) divisions under them. This being so, the ordinance is contrary to law.

If the intention is to create a department, then it should not be placed under the Office of the City Mayor.

SECTION 6 of the ordinance which states that "CREATION OF POSITIONS. The following positions are hereby created in support to the manpower requirements of the City Archives and Records Office, to wit 'xxx' is likewise defective.

It is noted that the step for the salary grade of the four (4) Supervising Administrative Officers are different. It is mind boggling as to why the Supervising Administrative Officer of Administrative Division is given the SG 22/2, for Records Management Division is SG 22/8 but for Archives Collection and Access Division and Archives Preservation Division is SG 22/1. This is true with respect to the salaries. Basic is the rule that when a person is hired, he/she should start with step 1.

--- [Page 21] ---
Section 7, which states that "AUTOMATIC TRANSFER OF BUDGETARY ALLOCATION-All budgetary allocations of every position transferred in or detailed from various offices are deemed transferred to the City Archives and Records Office from the effectivity of this Ordinance" is vague and prejudicial to public welfare.

It is vague for it did not specify what positions are affected.

If what is contemplated are positions similar to those listed in Sections 6, then the ordinance becomes prejudicial to public interest. It must be stressed that the title "Administrative Aide 1V" includes driver, machine operator, Administrative Assistant 1V covers Computer Operator.

Paragraph 2 of Section 8: PERSONNEL TRANSITION-which states "All personnel movements shall conform with Section 235 of RA No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 is inapplicable. The said provision of law talks about levy on real property, to quote:

SECTION 235. Additional Levy on Real Property for the Special Education Fund.- A province or city, or a municipality within the Metropolitan Manila Area, may levy and collect an annual tax of one percent (1%) on the assessed value of real property with shall be in addition to the basic real property tax. The proceeds thereof shall exclusively accrue to the Special Education Fund (SEF).
