**SECTION 1.1 - INTRODUCTION**

The Clydeside will be known as "Anchorage" unless otherwise provided for in these By-Laws.

**SECTION 2.1 - POLICY**

The Clydeside will be operated by the Clydeside Management Committee (CMC) to ensure that the Clydeside is maintained in good condition and that the Clydeside is used for recreational purposes only.

**SECTION 3.1 - MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE**

The Clydeside Management Committee shall consist of a minimum of three members who shall be appointed by the Clydeside Board of Directors. The Clydeside Management Committee shall have the authority to make decisions regarding the management and operation of the Clydeside.

**SECTION 4.1 - FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT**

The Clydeside shall be managed in accordance with the financial policies established by the Clydeside Board of Directors. The Clydeside shall maintain an accounting system that accurately reflects all financial transactions.

**SECTION 5.1 - SECURITY**

The Clydeside shall be secured against unauthorized access and vandalism. The Clydeside shall have a security system in place to protect the Clydeside from theft and damage.

**SECTION 6.1 - LIABILITY**

The Clydeside shall be insured against liability arising from the use of the Clydeside. The Clydeside shall maintain adequate insurance coverage to protect against potential claims.

**SECTION 7.1 - RULES AND REGULATIONS**

The Clydeside shall be governed by the rules and regulations established by the Clydeside Board of Directors. These rules and regulations shall be posted at the Clydeside and shall be available for review by all users.

**SECTION 8.1 - PARKING**

Parking shall be available at the Clydeside. Parking fees shall be charged to users of the Clydeside.

**SECTION 9.1 - RESTRICTIONS**

Users of the Clydeside shall be subject to the restrictions set forth in these By-Laws. Users of the Clydeside shall not engage in any activities that may cause damage to the Clydeside or other users of the Clydeside.

**SECTION 10.1 - LIABILITY**

Users of the Clydeside shall be responsible for their own safety and shall be liable for any injuries or damages caused by their actions while using the Clydeside.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

ON

The Provision and Training on the Use of the Rapid Earthquake Damage Assessment System (REDAS) Software To the City of Davao As a Tool for Mainstreaming Disaster Risk Reduction into the Local Development Planning Process, Contingency Planning and Emergency Preparedness

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement, hereinafter referred to as THE AGREEMENT, is made and entered into by and between:

The City Government of Davao (CGD), with office address at Davao City Hall, represented by SARA DUTERTE-CARPIO hereinafter referred to as the CGD;

and

The Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology – Department of Science and Technology (PHIVOLCS-DOST), a government agency created under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at C.P. Garcia Avenue, UP Campus, Diliman, Quezon City, represented by its Officer-in-Charge RENATO O. SOLIDIM, JR., hereinafter referred to as PHIVOLCS- DOST;

With the

CGD and PHIVOLCS-DOST, hereinafter collectively referred to as the PARTIES;

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, PHIVOLCS-DOST is the agency mandated to monitor volcanic activities and earthquake occurrences and conduct studies leading to volcanic eruption risk mitigation and earthquake risk reduction;

WHEREAS, PHIVOLCS-DOST developed a software called Rapid Earthquake Damage Assessment System (REDAS) that aims to produce seismic hazard and risk maps minutes after the occurrence of a potentially damaging earthquake;

WHEREAS, REDAS can be used to conduct hazard and risk assessment at the City and City level;

WHEREAS, REDAS can be used as a tool for mainstreaming disaster risk reduction into the local development planning process, contingency and emergency planning;

--- [Page 10] ---
MQA for REDAS

WHEREAS, PHIVOLCS-DOST would like to share the benefits of REDAS to Philippine communities by providing the software, training, technical support and future upgrade of the REDAS software;

WHEREAS, CGD realizing the potential use of REDAS for disaster preparedness and emergency planning for its City and had signified its interest to avail of the REDAS software and undergo training of its selected staffs;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of these premises, the parties to this AGREEMENT have agreed as follows:

ARTICLE 1. Joint Undertaking

The CGD and PHIVOLCS-DOST agree to:

1. commit to undertake and pursue REDAS training to support and strengthen disaster management capabilities of communities in their City;
2. share their resources, expertise, and facilities, and to complement each other to their best effort in the implementation of the training;
3. share information and technology relevant to the project such as, but not limited to:
a. hazard maps produced and built-in on REDAS software;
b. exposure database
4. extend assistance and cooperation to one another in the attainment of the goals and objectives of this MCA.

ARTICLE 2. Duties and Responsibilities of CGD

The CGD shall:

1. encourage planning officers, MDRRMC members and local government unit officials to actively participate in the activities, and in particular to facilitate invitation of participants to the REDAS training;
2. provide personnel, equipment, and logistical support in the conduct of the REDAS training;
3. provide support for the attendance of their representatives;
4. ensure that appropriate hardware requirements such as PC and android devices are ready for use during the training;
5. share elements-at-risk data (roads, barangay data, schools, bridges, etc.) to PHIVOLCS-DOST for inclusion in the REDAS software.

ARTICLE 3. Duties and Responsibilities of PHIVOLCS-DOST

PHIVOLCS-DOST shall:

1. provide REDAS software to the City of Davao, CPDO, CDRRMC and concerned stakeholders involved in disaster preparedness endorsed by CGD.

--- [Page 11] ---
2. design training modules for REDAS, provide resource speakers, and to compile and provide training materials.
3. provide expertise, personnel and logistical support in the conduct of the REDAS training:
ARTICLE 4. Source of Funding
Training costs including but not limited to training venue, food, accommodation of participants, transportation costs and supplies and materials shall be co-shared by CGD and PHIVOLCS. PHIVOLCS shall provide the REDAS software package, the training, trainers, technical expertise, other training costs and future upgrades to CGD.
ARTICLE 5. Data Generation, Database and Software Management and Proprietaryship
1. All data provided by CGD shall become part of the REDAS software.
2. The REDAS software provided by PHIVOLCS at no cost shall be used by the CGD for mainstreaming risk reduction into the local development including disaster risk management, land-use and development planning. A corresponding end-user license agreement (EULA) for the REDAS software shall be signed by individual participant for each copy installed at individual computers.
3. Third-party users of REDAS through collaboration with CGD shall first secure written consent from PHIVOLCS-DOST.
4. The CGD risk database and PHIVOLCS' REDAS and its accompanying risk data are not for distribution and shall not be used for any personal transaction, private business of commercial nature or any other way not explicitly agreed upon by both PARTIES.
5. That the CGD shall abide by the rules and regulations pertaining to the usage of the accompanying freeware and shareware in the REDAS software (e.g. the Generic Mapping Tools, Ghostscript, ImageMagik, PFE, SQLite, Spatialite, MySQL, Quantum GIS, etc).
ARTICLE 6. Sustainability
The CGD shall make its best effort to demonstrate the usage of the REDAS software and of its modules in any of the provincial or municipal plans after the training. It will then inform PHIVOLCS about said usages for documentation purposes.
ARTICLE 7. Authorship
All PARTIES shall ensure that authorship of any article, poster and abstract, paper or similar work products arising from the use of primary data generated from the activities of the project shall include the appropriate members of the project.
ARTICLE 8. Disclaimer
The hazard and risk assessment results generated by REDAS are for indicative purposes only and shall not be used to replace assessment requiring site specific evaluation. PHIVOLCS-DOST shall not
