ORDINANCE NO. 0536-18
SERIES OF 2018

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGNE FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE EXECUTED BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE-REGIONAL OFFICE XI AND THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS-REGIONAL OFFICE XL RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF AGDAO FARMER'S MARKET

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0536-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIG, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE EXECUTED BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE-REGIONAL OFFICE XI AND THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS-REGIONAL OFFICE XI, RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF AGDAD FARMER'S MARKET".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b)(ii) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be executed by and among the City Government of Davao, the Department of Agriculture-Regional Office XI and the Department of Public Works and Highways-Regional Office XI, relative to the construction of Agdad Farmer's Market.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ERACTED: September 4, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at City Hall, Davao City, represented by its City Mayor, SARAJ T. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "CGDAVAO";
THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE through its REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, a government agency created and existing under the Republic of the Philippines with office address at F. Bangoy St., Davao City, represented by its OIC, Regional Executive Director ENGR. RICARDO M. ONATE, Jr., hereinafter referred to as "DA RFO XI";
and
The DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS REGIONAL OFFICE XI, a government agency of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at Gov. Chavez St. corner R. Magaysay Ave Davao City, represented by its Regional Director, ENGR. ALLAN S. BORROMEO, hereinafter referred to as "DPWH RO XI".

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, CG-Davao has identified infrastructure development and agriculture as among the Top 10 Priority Programs of the government;
WHEREAS, CG-Davao aims to rehabilitate Agdao Public Market, located at Brgy. Agdao Proper, Davao City, to provide better facilities and services to the public;
WHEREAS, CG-Davao sought the assistance of the Department of Agriculture for funding of the above-mentioned project;
WHEREAS, Department of Agriculture through DA RFO XI initially appropriated Two Hundred Million Pesos Only (Php 200,000,000.00) for the Construction of Agdao Farmer's Market (1st Phase), funded under its Locally Funded Project as per approved General Appropriations Act (GAA) FY 2018, RA# 10964, the budget in the amount of Six Hundred Million Pesos (Php 600,000,000.00) for the Phase II of the project has already been provided in the National Expenditures Program for submission to Congress for approval; while the Phase III (in the amount of Three Hundred Fifty Million (Php 350,000,000) for the proposed parking building will be submitted by the Department of Agriculture for corresponding budget allocation by the Department of Budget and Management;
WHEREAS, DA RFO XI has sought the assistance of DPWH RO XI in the review of plans and quantity estimates prepared by City Engineering Office, Davao City and prepare the cost estimates including the procurement and implementation of the Project;
WHEREAS, the DPWH RO XI will review the Project Plans and quantity estimates and prepare the Program of Work and Detailed Cost Estimates amounting to Two Hundred Million Pesos Only (Php 200,000,000.00) as the Project Cost;
WHEREAS, DPWH RO XI is willing to provide the personnel and expertise necessary for the completion of the project;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto agree as follows:

A. RESPONSIBILITIES OF CG-DAVAO
1. Prepare the Detailed Cost Estimates and Quantity Estimates;

--- [Page 8] ---
2. In coordination with DA RFO XI, evaluate the recommendations made by the DPWH RO XI officials for possible changes and improvement relative to any aspect in the plans and specifications;
3. Assign personnel to monitor and supervise the implementation of the Project;
4. Coordinate with DA RFO XI and DPWH RO XI to ensure the efficient implementation of the Project;
5. Accept the completed project upon full determination that work has been implemented in accordance with the approved plans and specifications;
6. Manage the operations of the Agdao Farmer's Market upon completion of the Project.

B. RESPONSIBILITIES OF DA RFO XI

1. Secure the necessary funding for the Construction/Rehabilitation of the Agdao Farmer's Market;
2. Execute Memorandum of Agreement between DPWH RO XI on the terms and conditions for the release of the Project Cost amounting to Two Hundred Million Pesos Only (Pip 200,000,000.00) in tranches;
3. Disburse the amount equivalent to 0.5% of the contract amount intended for the administrative cost in favor of the DPWH RO XI upon Issuance of Notice of Award to the winning bidder;
4. Assign technical personnel to monitor and coordinate with CG-Davao and DPWH RO XI personnel during the construction and rehabilitation of the project and to conduct the necessary inspection to ensure conformity to approved plans and specifications;
5. Prepare and submit monthly status reports on the project and such other reports as may be required by the Commission on Audit (COA);
6. Issue the usual Certificate of Acceptance and Turn-over; and
7. Turn-over to the CG-Davao the completed project.

C. RESPONSIBILITIES OF DPWH RO XI

1. Review of plans and quality assurance prepared by City Engineering Office, Davao City and prepare a progress report including the procurement and implementation of the Project;
2. Inform the winning bidder/contractor that the project will be supervised by DPWH RO XI, and monitored by DA RFO XI and CG-Davao;
3. Initiate, coordinate and supervise all activities with respect to the implementation of the project to ensure conformity with the plans and specifications and other provisions stipulated in the contract;
4. Consult with, advise, assist and recommend to DA RFO XI and CG-Davao all changes relating to any aspect in the plans and specifications. Changes should be made after prior coordination and upon approval of DA RFO XI and CG-Davao;
5. Conduct meetings with DA RFO XI officials and CG-Davao whenever necessary to discuss the progress of work and to promptly address problems affecting the implementation of the project;
6. Conduct jointly with the DA RFO XI officials and CG-Davao the final inspection of the Project, for the Issuance of the Certificate of Completion, and recommend acceptance of the project;
7. Submit to DA RFO XI the duly liquidated and certified Billings Statement, expert certified by the Accountant every time Billing Statement is given by the Contractor and return and/or reimburse the same, any and all amount not spent or used in the Project; and
8. Turn-over to DA RFO XI the completed project in accordance with the plans and specifications.

D. ON ALL CONTRACTING PARTIES

The CG-Davao, DA RFO XI and DPWH RO XI agree that meetings may be called by either party anytime during the project implementation to address problems and concerns that may cause delay in the project, and to provide remedial measures and solutions thereto.

E. DURATION OF THE AGREEMENT

This Agreement shall be effective and binding upon the herein parties upon the execution hereof, and to continue in full force and effect until completion of the Project.

F. AMENDMENTS

Either party may request changes to this MOA. Any changes, modifications, terminations, or amendments to this MOA which are mutually agreed upon by and between the parties to this MOA shall be incorporated by written instrument and effective when executed and signed by all parties to this MOA.
