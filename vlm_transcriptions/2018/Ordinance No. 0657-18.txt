AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF ARIEL ARADILLOS FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY LOCATED AT LASANG RIVER, BARANGAY PAÑALUM, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, THIS CITY

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF ARIEL ARADILLOS FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY LOCATED AT LASANG RIVER, BARANGAY PAÑALUM, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, THIS CITY."

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES OF DEVELOPMENT. Article XII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022), more particularly Section 1 thereof, "Request for Additional Allowable Use" provides that the uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general allowable uses may be extended to other uses within the jurisdiction of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of project proponents and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA). This Ordinance may allow other uses not enumerated therein under as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a ¾ majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance;

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE. The request of Ariel Aradillos for additional allowable use for the operation of sand and gravel quarry in Lasang River, Barangay Pañalum, Paquibato District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, all sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December, 2018, by three-fourths (4%) majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
