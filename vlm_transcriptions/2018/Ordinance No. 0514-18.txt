Ord. No. 0514-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ALONG ROXAS AVENUE-LEFT TURN TO C.M. RECTO AVENUE, RIGHT TURN TO BONIFACIO STREET, LEFT TURN TO PELAYO STREET, THEN LEFT TURN TO SAN PEDRO STREET ON AUGUST 18, 2018 FROM 1:00 A.M. TO 5:00 P.M. (FULL ROAD CLOSURE AND PARKING SIGNS) IN CONNECTION WITH THE 'INDAK-INDAK SA KADAYAWAN COSPLAY' KADAYAWAN PARADE ROUTE OF THE 2018 KADAYAWAN FESTIVAL".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertakings of public works and highways, telecommunication and waterworks projects.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the 2018 Kadayawan Festival events ('Indak-Indak sa Kadayawan/Cosplay Kadayawan'), the City Tourism Operations Office requested for the temporary closure to vehicular traffic along Roxas Avenue-left turn to C.M. Recto Avenue, right turn to Bonifacio Street, left turn to Pelayo Street, then left turn to San Pedro Street on August 18, 2018 from 1:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M. (FULL ROAD CLOSURE AND PARKING SIGNS)

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, August 7, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
