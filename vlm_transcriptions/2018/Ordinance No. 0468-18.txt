Ord. No. 0468-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panglungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND ON BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT, A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND HOLY CROSS OF DAVAO COLLEGE, INC., FOR THE INTERNSHIP PROGRAM OF BS PSYCHOLOGY STUDENTS WITH THE DAVAO CITY TREATMENT AND REHABILITATION CENTER FOR DRUG DEPENDENTS (DTCRCD);"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (v) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and each other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panglungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) by and between the City Government of Davao and Holy Cross of Davao College, Inc., for the internship program of BS Psychology students with the Davao City Treatment and Rehabilitation Center for Drug Dependents.

SECTION 4. NON-EXCLUSIVITY CLAUSE - This Memorandum of Agreement is non-exclusive and all other schools may enter into the said agreement.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, June 19, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement is made and entered into this 1st day of June, 2023, in the Philippines by and between:

The HOLY CROSS OF DAVAO COLLEGE, INC., a non-stock, non-profit corporation created under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, duly registered at the Securities and Exchange Commission with Registration Number 141489 dated 4 February 1957 and with principal address at 67 Sta. Ana Avenue, Davao City, 8000, represented by its Acting President, Dr. Ma. Iris Milleza hereinafter referred to as the ACADEME PARTNER;

- and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at City Hall, Davao City, represented by its City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as CITY GOVERNMENT;

WITNESSETH:

Whereas, the objective if the Psychology Program of HCDC is to expose the student to the different setting where psychology is practiced as a profession, e.g. school setting, industrial setting, community organization centers, hospitals (Camo 46. 2012, 2) and to help them appreciate concepts and principles they have learned through a hands-on training while preparing for their same time for their career choice.

Whereas, the City Government operates and maintains the DAVAO CITY TREATMENT AND REHABILITATION CENTER FOR DRUG DEPENDENTS (OCTRCCD), a residential facility for drug dependents, located at Pumk 7, Bago Oshiro, Tugbog District, Davao City;

Whereas, the OCTRCCD can provide the appropriate learning opportunities and work space for student to apply the principle in Psychology that they have learned in HCDC;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in the consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereby agree as follows:

SECTION 1. Responsibilities of HCDC, the Practicum Adviser

1. Provides guidance and supervision over the practicumers for the duration of the program;
2. Monitors the practicumers' performance through the Practicum Supervisor;
3. Provides all the necessary guidelines in the Practicum program;
4. Serves as the contact persons of the Practicum Supervisor;
5. Recommends sanctions and penalties to the Practicum Supervisor in case of misbehavior, misconduct and neglect of duty on the part of the practicumers.

In case of misbehavior, misconduct and neglect of duty on the part of the practicumers, the Practicum Supervisor shall immediately report to the Practicum Adviser the incident and circumstances involving such conduct and behavior with stern warning on the practicumers.

If another misbehavior, misconduct and neglect is committed, the Practicum Supervisor may cause the termination of this

--- [Page 12] ---
agreement/ practicum program with the concurrence of the Practicum Adviser, in such case, due process must be observed.
SECTION 2. Responsibilities of the City Government, through the DCTRCCD
1. Designates personnel to serve as Practicum Supervisor,
2. The Practicum Supervisor shall:
• Provides basic orientation on the nature and functions of Davao City Treatment and Rehabilitation Center for Drug Dependents;
• Assign tasks and works to the practitioners in relation to the nature of the assignment;
• Serve as the contact person of the Practicum Adviser in the workplace
• Evaluate the practicums according to their performance through Performance Appraisal Form;
• Certify and validate all relevant record, such as the Daily Time Record, and other records that practitioners shall submit to the Adviser; and
• Act as the disciplining authority in case of misbehavior, misconduct and neglect of duty in the part of the practitioner.
SECTION 3. Responsibilities of the Practicum
1. Performs the functions, duties and responsibilities assigned to them by the Practicum Supervisor for the furtherance of their understanding of the programs objectives;
2. Reports regularly to the place of work in accordance with the agreed time with the Practicum Supervisor; and
3. Compiles with all practicum guidelines as laid down in the practicum manuals.
SECTION 4 Other Terms and Conditions
1. The practicums are designed to start on the month of ______ and end on the month of ______ every first semester and on the month of ______ and end on the month of ______ every second semester;
2. The practicum hours are expected to render services for at least ______ hours a day expect when there is school's functions and when their presence in school is needed;
3. That Agreement shall take effect immediately after signing hereof for the shall year 2015-2019 renewable every five year unless sooner terminated/ revoked by other party in writing not less than thirty (30) days advance notice.
