AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGNS FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH REGIONAL OFFICE XI PERTAINING TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF TWO (2) HOSPITAL HEALTH UNIT BUILDINGS-SMALL MODEL II BE LOCATED IN THE TOWN OF TRUJILLO, BUKAD DISTRICT AND BARANGAY INDANGAN, BUHANGIN DISTRICT

--- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of 3

Ord. No. 0401-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b) subparagraph (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:

"SECTION 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. RESPONSIBILITIES - The City Government of Davao shall provide two suitable government-owned lots as permanent sites of the two new health facilities to be located in Barangay Tibungco, Bunawan District, this City, and Indangan, Buhangin District and ensure that the funds for necessary human resource, maintenance and operating expenses for the health facilities shall be provided;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Health Regional Office XI pertaining to the construction of two (2) Rural Health Unit Buildings-Small Model to be located in Barangay Tibungco, Bunawan District and Barangay Indangan, Buhangin District;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement is entered into this day of 2018, in by and between:

The DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH - Regional Office XI with office address at J.P. Llanes Avenue, Bataan City herein represented by DR. ABDULLAH B. DUMAMA, JR., MD., MPA, in his capacity as ASSISTANT SECRETARY OF HEALTH-MINDANAO CLUSTER AND REGIONAL DIRECTOR, hereinafter referred to as DOH-RO XI;

and

The LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT OF DAVAO CITY with office address at City Hall Drive, Public Service Division, Davao City herein represented by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO in her capacity as the CITY MAYOR, hereinafter referred to as the CLGU.

WHEREAS, in the pursuit of the Department of Health (DOH) of Philippine Health Agenda 2016-2022 "An Ilog for Health towards Health for All," through the improvement of access to quality health services for all Filipinos, the DOH provides financial assistance for the Construction of Rural Health Quads/Urban Health Centers through the Health Facility Enhancement Program (HFEP);

WHEREAS, the objectives of the HFEP are:

1. To improve primary health facilities to gate keep and deliver preventive health services; and

2. To improve the quality of Rural Health Units/Urban Health Centers so that they can comply with DOH licensing and PHILHEALTH accreditation requirements as service providers.

WHEREAS, the CLGU of Davao is a recipient of two (2) units of RHU Building - Small Model. The subject is part of the construction contract package with the Pacific Summit Construction Corp. Inc., which was procured by the Philippine International Trading Corporation (PITC).

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and stipulations herein set forth, the Parties hereto agree and do hereby agree as follows:

A. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CLGU

1. Provide a suitable government-owned lot as the permanent site for the proposed new health facilities to be located in Tibiaoan, Bataan District, and Indangan, Bahangin District respectively attached hereto document, etc.

2. Ensure that funds for necessary human resource and the sustained budget for the maintenance and other operating expenses for the health facility shall be provided.

--- [Page 10] ---
B. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DOH RO XI

1. Coordinate with the representatives of the DOH Central Office, PITC, the concerned CLGU, and the Contractor for the implementation of the project;

2. Supervise the progress of project implementation regularly;

3. Prepare evaluation and approval of the contractor's work accomplishment subject for approval of the DOH Central Office; and

4. Monitor the implementation of the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA).

C. PROJECT MONITORING, COMPLETION, TURN OVER AND ACCEPTANCE

1. Project implementation shall be monitored jointly or separately by the concerned stakeholders;

2. A coordination meeting shall be conducted on the need basis;

3. The completed project shall be subsequently turned over to the CLGU after acceptance from the Contractor, subject to the defect- liability provisions of RA 9185, as amended; and

4. The DOH RO XI shall submit monthly status reports of physical and financial accomplishments to the Health Service Delivery Cluster (HSDC), copy furnished the DOH Health Facility Development Bureau (HFDB) and the Finance Service, and quarterly report to the National Economic Development Authority (NEDA) and Office of the President-Project Monitoring Office.

D. AMENDMENTS

This contract shall take effect immediately upon signing hereof and may be amended or revoked only upon mutual consent of both Parties, provided that any amendment shall be entered by Supplemental Provisions signed by both parties which shall be incorporated as an integral part of the MOA.
