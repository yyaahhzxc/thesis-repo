Ord. No. 0604-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT COMPANY (NDC) FOR THE USE OF A 5-HECTARE PORTION OF NDC'S PROPERTY IN BARANGAY DALIAO, TORIL DISTRICT, THIS CITY, AS A SITE FOR DAVAO FOOD TERMINAL COMPLEX (DFTC)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (v) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all transactions, contracts, negotiations, and other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Amended Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and National Development Company (NDC) for the use of a 5-hectare portion of NDC's property in Barangay Daliao, Toril District, this City, as a site for Davao Food Terminal Complex (DFTC).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or null, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 27th day of November, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

SO THE PUBLIC MAY KNOW:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ("Agreement" by brevity) entered into and executed by and between:

The NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT COMPANY, a government-owned and controlled corporation organized and existing pursuant to P.D. 1648, as amended, with principal offices at NDC Building, 116 Tordesillas Street, Salcedo Village, Makati City, represented herein by its General Manager, MA. LOURDES F. REBUENO, hereinafter referred to as "NDC".

-and-

CITY OF DAVAO, a government instrumentality of the Republic of the Philippines with Office address at Room 4, City Hall, Davao City and herein represented by its Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE hereafter referred to as "CG-Davao".

Together hereunder referred to as the "Parties".

-WITNESSETH-

On July 30, 2015, the parties executed a Memorandum of Agreement (the "Original MOA") for the use by the CG-Davao City of a portion of NDC's Dilao, Toril, Davao City as the SITES of an Davao Food Terminal Complex (DFTC), duly recorded in the notarial work of Lizarr, Lorena C. Bagasol-Menalang as Document No. 133, Page 28, Book No. 1 Series of 2015, a copy of which is attached hereto as Annex "A" and made an integral part hereof.

On December 15, 2017, CG-Davao requested for specific amendments on the MOA in line with the local government's objective of empowering farmers and promoting competitiveness in the agricultural sector.

On February 12, 2018, the NDC Board approved the counterproposals presented by NDC.

On April 24, 2018, NDC and CG-Davao negotiated and agreed on the proposed amendments to the MOA.

On July 6, 2018, the NDC Board approved the amendments agreed upon by the parties.

ACCORDINGLY, the Parties hereby agree to amend the Original MOA as follows:

--- [Page 14] ---
Article I
Objectives of the MOA

This MOA is entered into by the Parties for the use by the CG-Davao City of a portion of NDC's Daliao, Toril, Davao City as the SITE of an Davao Food Terminal Complex (DFTC).

Article II
Scope and Timetable of the Project

2.1 This MOA shall be for a period of twenty-five (25) years, renewable upon mutual agreement of the parties and shall come into full force and effect only upon the DA's and/or CG-Davao's commencement of the bidding process relative to the construction of the DFTC on the SITE.

2.2 The CG-Davao shall notify NDC in writing of its desire to renew of this MOA at least twelve (12) months prior to its expiration. NDC shall have thirty (30) calendar days from receipt within which to submit its written reply. Failure by either of the parties to undertake said notifications within said respective periods shall be construed as an implied disagreement to renew the contract or shall constitute as a default by each of the parties which shall be governed by Art. IV hereof.

2.3 The terms and conditions of the renewed contract shall be subject to the mutual agreements of the Parties and approval of their respective policy making authorities.

Article III
Roles and Responsibilities of the Parties

3.1 Rights and Obligations of NDC

a. Provide the 5-hectare portion of its Daliao, Toril, Davao City property as the site ("SITE") of the DFTC, as specifically marked in the attached location map marked as Annex "A" hereof. CG-Davao shall, at its own cost, conduct a subdivision survey to determine the particular technical description of the SITE.

b. Allow the use by the CG-Davao of the SITE for twenty-five (25) years subject to the following conditions:

i. The SITE shall be used primarily for the DFTC and may be sub-leased, provided, the sublease is in accordance with, or complementary to, the purposes for which the DFTC program is undertaken and CG-Davao shall inform NDC of the sub-lease arrangement entered into.

ii. The operations of the SITE shall not be transferred to any party/parties without the written consent from NDC;

iii. In all other instances of sublease not covered by paragraphs (i) and (ii), the prior written consent of NDC must be obtained by CG-Davao.

--- [Page 15] ---
iv. NDC shall be allowed access by the CG-Davao to the SITE at any reasonable time of the day to inspect the premises. No provision of this agreement shall diminish the inherent right of NDC, being the owner, to develop, sell, assign and/or dispose of the Property, provided that the Property shall be utilized in accordance with the Master Plan for the Davao Food Complex (DFC).

3.2 Rights and Obligations of CG-Davao City

a. Own, operate and maintain the DFTC. However, trading operations may be undertaken by any farmer group/s duly authorized by the City Government, subject to the supervision of the latter. CG-Davao shall hold NDC and its officers and employees free and harmless from any liability for any injury, damage, accident, illness, and/or property loss, that may occur during the operation of the DFTC. In the event that NDC is sued or involved in any litigation for the aforesaid reasons, the CG-Davao shall, at its own expense defend NDC with counsel of NDC's choice and any judgement that may be rendered against NDC shall be for the account of CG-Davao.

b. As consideration for the use of the property:

i. Upon the commencement of this MOA, pay for and/or waive the Real Property Taxes (RPT) that are due from the site, the DFTC, including all other improvements erected on the site; or stated differently, the whole five (5) hectare portion including the improvements found therein.

ii. Provide for a relocation site and at its own cost, cause and complete the relocation of all informal settlers (approximately 25 households) residing within the site, or the 5-hectare portion as identified, within six (6) months from the date of the execution of this MOA, without prejudice to any assistance, financial or otherwise, that the NDC may be willing to extend as part of the herein obligation to relocate.

c. Complete the subdivision survey of the SITE from NDC's Property within six (6) months from the date of the date of the execution of this MOA.

d. Provide and ensure that all necessary local government support services in relation to the construction and operation of the DFTC as well as the DFC are delivered in an efficient and effective manner such as but not limited to the securing of building permits, business permits, and garbage collection services in order to ensure the smooth implementation of the project.

e. Pay the lease rate and the escalation rate as provided for in DENR Administrative Order No. 98-20 and Bureau of Lands Administrative Order No. 47 (formula for the minimum lease rate) starting on the eight (8) year from the commencement of the operations of the DFTC until the end of the lease period as agreed upon. In the event that the income derived by the CG-Davao from the DFTC is not sufficient to pay the minimum lease rate, the Parties hereby undertake to negotiate for a reasonable lease rate. Conversely, when DFTC generates income before the 8th year, CG-Davao will pay the lease as stated herein.

--- [Page 16] ---
Article IV
Default, Indemnity and Termination

Any Party shall be deemed in default upon the occurrence of any of the following instances:

(a) Non-performance of its obligations set forth in this MOA;
(b) Any representation or warranty made in this MOA is proven to be untrue, false, incorrect or misleading;
(c) An action or proceeding is instituted against any of the Parties herein or its properties giving rise to serious doubt as to its ability to perform its obligations under this MOA, such as when any of the Parties shall suspend or cease, threaten to suspend or cease its operations, or all or substantial portion of its assets shall be expropriated or threatened with expropriation by the Government;
(d) In case any Party fails to observe or perform any term, stipulation, or agreement contained herein, which breach or failure continues uncorrected for a period of thirty (30) calendar days after receipt of the written notice, specifying the breach and requiring it to be remedied. In this instance, the non-defaulting party shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement without prejudice to costs and/or damages which it might be entitled to.
(e) When any Party fails to perform any of its obligations under this MOA which materially and adversely affects the performance of the service;

4.1 Indemnity

The Party at fault shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the aggrieved Party, its officers, directors, employees, contractors, and agents from and against all damages, losses and reasonable expenses, including without limitation of reasonable legal fees, suffered or paid by the aggrieved Party as a result of any and all claims for personal injury, death or property damage, except economic loss, to third parties due to an event occurring during the term of this Agreement, whether directly or indirectly caused by the negligence or omission of the Party at fault or its agents or employees, except to the extent that it was caused by any act or omission of the aggrieved Party or the failure by the latter to take reasonable steps to mitigate the damage or harm. In the event such injury or damage results from the joint or concurrent negligent or intentional act or omission of the Parties, each shall be liable under this section in proportion to its relative degree of fault.

4.2 This Agreement shall be terminated on the following grounds:

(a) Upon written agreement by the Parties; or
(b) Upon the happening of any of the events described above as a default which materially and adversely affects the operation of the project or when such default renders the continuation of operations disadvantageous to any or all of the parties.

4.3 Termination Procedure & Surrender of the Site to NDC

(A) Upon the occurrence of an event of default, the non-defaulting Party may give a notice to the defaulting Party, specifying in reasonable detail the event of default and the date on which the non-defaulting Party proposes to terminate this Agreement. Unless otherwise provided herein, the MOA may be 27-

--- [Page 17] ---
terminated on any date specified by the non-defaulting Party in the Termination Notice.

(b) Within six (6) months from the date of termination or non-renewal of the MOA, the CG-Davao shall return and surrender the SITE including the improvements erected thereon to NDC, which improvements shall be owned by NDC, without obligation for NDC to reimburse the cost thereof.

(c) NDC shall have the right to enter and possess the SITE upon failure of the CG-
Davao to surrender the premises within said agreed period.

Article V
Entire Agreement

5.1 This MOA constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties, and may not be altered, amended, or modified except in writing, executed by the duly authorized representatives of each Party.

5.2 This MOA may be executed in one or more duplicate counterparts and when signed by each of the Parties shall constitute an original and a single binding agreement.

Article VI
Amendments and Approvals

No amendment or supplement to this MOA, and no approval or consent constituting an amendment, shall be effective for any purpose unless made in writing and signed by a duly authorized officer of both Parties.

Article VII
Compliance with Government Procedures and Approvals

It is agreed and understood that any business arrangement that may be considered by the Parties shall be made on the condition that both Parties, being government-owned and controlled corporations, are subject to certain legal requirements and procedures. Hence, the performance of any act or undertaking contemplated under or in implementation of this Agreement, including negotiation, the execution and delivery of any further or future contracts, will require prior government and corporate procedures and approvals for their validity and enforceability.

--- [Page 18] ---
Article VIII
Applicable Law and Interpretation

a) This MOA shall be governed by, interpreted, and construed in accordance with Philippine laws.

b) The Parties shall endeavor to resolve any dispute, claim or controversy which may arise out of or in connection with this Agreement or the application, implementation, validity, breach or termination thereof through negotiation or amicable consultation on cooperative basis. Such consultations shall commence upon the written notification to that effect by one Party to the other Party.

c) Any dispute, claim, controversy or disagreement arising out of or in connection with this Agreement that cannot be settled amicably within thirty (30) calendar days after a written notice to resolve such dispute, claim, controversy or disagreement is received by a Party, the same shall be settled in accordance with the provisions of Executive Order No. 292, otherwise known as the "Revised Administrative Code of 1987."
