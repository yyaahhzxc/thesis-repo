ORDINANCE NO. 0678-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE RESORTING TO INDIGENOUS PEOPLES' TRADITIONAL WAY OF CONFLICT RESOLUTION, WHEN INVOKED BY DISPUTING IP PARTIES, IN AN INDIGENOUS CULTURAL COMMUNITY IN DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 6  
Ord. No. 0678-19  

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "IP ALTERNATIVE DISPUTE RESOLUTION IN DAVAO".  

SECTION 2. PURPOSE - The purpose of this Ordinance is to preserve the rights of Indigenous Peoples who voluntarily invoke the traditional way of resolving their disputes through the Council of Elders in an Indigenous Cultural Community (ICC) duly recognized in Davao City.  

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - This Ordinance is enacted pursuant to the provisions of the following:  

R.A. 8371 OR THE "INDIGENOUS PEOPLES' RIGHTS ACT OF 1997"  

Section 15. Justice System, Conflict Resolution Institutions and Peace Building Processes. –The ICCs/IPs shall have the right to use their own commonly accepted justice systems, conflict resolution institutions, peace building processes or mechanisms and other customary laws and practices within their respective communities and as may be compatible with the national legal system and with internationally recognized human rights.  

REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7160 OR THE "LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991"  

Section 399. Lupong Tagapamayapa. (f) In barangays where majority of the inhabitants are members of the indigenous cultural communities, local systems of settling disputes through the Council of Elders shall be recognized without prejudice to the applicable provisions of this Code.  

SECTION 4. SCOPE - This Ordinance shall apply to Indigenous Peoples who voluntarily invoke the local or customary way of settling disputes (except those expressly prohibited law to be subject of compromise), as a secondary mode of dispute resolution, between any Indigenous Peoples belonging to a recognized tribe in Davao City and who are residents in areas with ancestral domains as listed in Resolution No. 02199-15, Series of 2015 (including those who have subsequently been qualified to have an IPMR) such as, but not limited to, the following:

SECTION 5. DEFINITION OF TERMS
1. Indigenous Cultural Community (ICC) - Refer to a group or homogenous societies identified by self-ascension and ascription by others, who have continuously lived as organized community on communally bounded and defined territory, and who have, under claims of ownership since time immemorial, occupied, possessed, and utilized such territories sharing common cultural traits, customs, Austhetics, traditions, and other distinct cultural traits, or who have, through resistance to political, social and cultural inroads of colonization, non-indigenous religions and cultures, become historically differentiated from the majority of Filipinos. (Rules and Regulations Implementing Republic Act No. 8371, otherwise known as the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997)
2. Council of Elders - This is a duly constituted group of indigenous elders recognized by the state whose functions constitutes preservation of the customs and tradition of the tribe, and provides insights and solutions to address conflict resolution and peace-building processes, spiritual practices, rituals and ceremonies. They lead the community in decision-making processes aimed at protecting and promoting the sustainable development of their ancestral domains. The Council of Elders of each tribe or each ICC has its own tradition for adjudication.
3. Customary Laws - Refer to a body of written and/or unwritten rules, usages, customs and practices traditionally and continually recognized, accepted and observed by respective ICCs/IPs.

**SECTION 6. TRADITIONAL CONCILIATION PROCEEDING:**
When two or more IP parties in dispute are residing in an ICC duly recognized in Davao City, the IP parties may resort to the traditional conciliation proceeding by the Council of Elders.

Provided that both IP parties voluntarily invoke the jurisdiction of the Council of Elders and where such customary law does not involve any cruel, degrading or inhumane conduct or punishment and does not include death penalties or excessive fines.

**SECTION 7. PROCEDURE:**
The commonly accepted local justice system, traditional conflict mechanism, peace building process and customary law and practice of each tribe/ICC within their respective communities shall govern the procedure or mechanism in the conflict resolution of IP disputes under this Ordinance.

**SECTION 8. ARBITRATION AGREEMENT:**
Agreements during the application of the traditional conciliation proceeding shall be made in writing signed by the parties in dispute and Council of Elders and shall be submitted to the Katarungang Pambanaragay for confirmation. It shall be binding between the IP parties and shall be accorded with due respect and due enforcement. The customary law agreements herein shall not be in violation of any laws, public policy, and/or public orders.

**SECTION 9. IN CASE OF INACTION:**
If the Council of Elders fails to act upon the complaint within fifteen (15) days upon submission of the conflict to its jurisdiction, the issue shall automatically be raised to the jurisdiction of the Katarungang Pambanaragay through the Lupong Tagapamayapa.

**SECTION 10. SANCTION FOR NON-COMPLIANCE:**
Where the member of the Katarungang Pambanaragay/Barangay Officials, after having been informed by the parties that they prefer the IP local justice system, unjustifiably intervenes and refuses to refer the matter to the Council of Elders, the concerned barangay member/s shall be administratively charged in accordance with applicable laws. On the other hand, the Council of Elders who refuse or fail to discharge their responsibility under this Ordinance, shall be sanctioned under the law. Such sanctions may be enforced by the NAPI in the exercise of its supervision, after due proceeding.

**SECTION 11. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:**
If any provision of this Ordinance or its application to any person or circumstance is declared unconstitutional, or inconsistent with national laws, the other unaffected provisions of this Ordinance shall remain in full force and effect.

**SECTION 12. REPEALING CLAUSE:**
Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules or regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY:**
This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its publication in a newspaper of general circulation, and its posting for three (3) consecutive weeks at any conspicuous places at City Hall.

**ENACTED, on the 8th day of January, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
