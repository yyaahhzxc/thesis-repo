Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both the City, the subject ordinance, informing your end that this office finds the enactment of the ordinance free from legal infirmity pursuant to Section 167 of Republic Act 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 to wit:
SECTION 167. Time of Payment. Unless otherwise provided in this Code, all local taxes, fees, and charges shall be paid within the first twenty (20) days of January or of each subsequent quarter, as the case may be. The Sanggunian concerned may, for a justifiable reason or cause, extend the time for payment of such taxes, fees, or charges without surcharges or penalties, but only for a period not exceeding six (6) months.
Viewed from the foregoing, it is recommended that the Ordinance be approved.

Ord. No. 0304-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING A THREE (3)-MONTH EXTENSION ON THE DEADLINE OF PAYMENT FOR THE THIRD QUARTERLY INSTALLMENT FOR REAL PROPERTY TAX AND ADDITIONAL LEVY ON REAL PROPERTY FOR THE SPECIAL EDUCATION FUND (SEF) FROM 30 SEPTEMBER 2020 TO 31 DECEMBER 2020".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY: Section 167 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that the Sangguniang concerned may, for a justifiable reason or cause, extend the time for payment of all local taxes, fees, or charges without surcharges or penalties, but only for a period not exceeding six (6) months.

SECTION 3. EXTENSION ON THE TIME OF PAYMENT: There shall be an extension on the time of payment for the Third Quarterly Installment for Real Property Tax and Additional Levy on Real Property for Special Education Fund (SEF) from 30 September 2020 to 31 December 2020.

SECTION 4. INTERESTS, SURCHARGES, AND PENALTIES: No interests, surcharges, and penalties shall be imposed to unpaid taxes, fees, or charges, if duly paid within the extended period of payment.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 7th day of July, 2020, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
