Ord. No. 0154-17

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN THE NAME OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT, A TRIPARTITE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA), TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, DEPARTMENT OF AGRARIAN REFORM PROVINCIAL OFFICE (DARPO) AND TAMUGAN AGRARIAN REFORM BENEFICIARIES COOPERATIVE (TARBECO), FOR THE PROJECT "LINKING SMALLHOLDER FARMERS TO MARKETS (LINKSFARM);"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 455, (b) (1) (v) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions such as in the execution of contracts, obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Tripartite Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and among the City Government of Davao, Department of Agrarian Reform Provincial Office (DARPO) and Tamugan Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries Cooperative (TARBECO), for the project "Linking Smallholder Farmers to Markets (LINKSFARM);"

SECTION 4. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE. - If any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval,

ENACTED, January 31, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all Members of the Sanggunian present.

Article V PROJECT MANAGEMENT

The overall project management and supervision shall be lodged to the DAR- CRS Project Steering Committee chaired by the DAR's Undersecretary for Support Services (SSoS), and co-chaired by CRS County Representative. Its members are the DAR's Undersecretary for Finance, Planning, and Administration Office (FPAO), the DAR's Research Director, and Senior Program Manager of CRB. The PSC shall meet on a semestral basis.

The PSC shall be assisted by two (2) Regional Technical Working Groups headed by the Director of BARBD with members composed of the concerned PARPO II, II/PEPD CARPOs from DAR, and the Deputy Program Manager and AE Consultant of CRS. The TWG shall meet every two (2) months at as need aites to ensure the smooth operation of the project.

The Site Working Group will be organized at the municipal level. It shall be composed of DAR MARPO/DF, LGU, CRS staff and as well as representatives from the SLCs and other relevant local institutions in the project site.
