Ord. No. 0155-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO
AND COOPERATE WITH THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE
MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG
THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
(DEPED) AND PHILIPPINE LONG DISTANCE TELEPHONE COMPANY (PLDT),
RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF INFOTECH OUTREACH PROGRAM
IN DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 455, (b) (1) (v) of the
Local Government Code of 1991 provides:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance
the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its
inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city
mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions
and sign in its behalf bonds, contracts, and obligations,
and such other documents as may be necessary for the
safety of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the
Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and among the City Government
of Davao, the Department of Education (DEPED) and Philippine Long Distance Telephone
Company (PLDT), relative to the implementation of Infotech Outreach Program in
Davao City;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections
or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force
and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately
upon approval.

ENACTED, February 7, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all Members of the
Sanggunian present.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement (the "Agreement") executed this in by and among:

PLDT INC., a corporation duly organized under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at Ramon Cajuangco Bldg., Matatac Avenue, Manila City, represented herein by its Chief Corporate Secretary, ATTY. JUAN R. ESPINOSA, and its Vice President/Chief of Affairs Head, MR. RAMON R. ISEBERTO hereinafter referred to as "PLDT";

The DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION-DAVAO CITY, a government office mandated to develop functionally-literate and educated Filipinos, with principal office in Davao City, represented by the Schools Division Superintendent, DR. MARIA INES C. ASUNCION, CESO V hereinafter referred to as "DEPED";

and

The DAVAO CITY LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT, with principal office at Davao City, represented by its Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO hereinafter referred to as "LGU";

Each, a "Party," and collectively, as "Parties";

WITNESSETH THAT:

WHEREAS, PLDT believes that Filipinos shall be at par with the rest of the world amidst this changing global lifestyle brought about by rapid modernization of the information and communication technologies and advocates that through education, such vision of Filipinos becoming globally competitive can be achieved;

WHEREAS, PLDT has been implementing the PLDT Infoteach Outreach Program as one mechanism to achieve this vision;

WHEREAS, DEPED implements programs geared towards pedagogically-based integration of modern information Communication Technology to teaching and learning in order to improve education in their area of responsibility;

WHEREAS, the LGU likewise implements programs to make its constituents globally competitive;

WHEREAS, pursuant to a Memorandum of Agreement signed on 29 Nov. 2016 by PLDT, UP Open University, and the UPOU Foundation (the "MOA"), for the implementation of the Infoteach Outreach Program, Davao City has been identified as one of the ten (10) areas to be covered by the program;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing and the covenants and stipulations herein, the Parties have agreed to collaborate for the Implementation of PLDT's Infoteach Outreach Program in DAVAO CITY, (the "PLDT Infotech-DAVAO CITY") under the following terms and conditions:

--- [Page 12] ---
I. PLDT Infoteach - Davao City Project Description

1. The Project shall have two components:
   a. Training Component
   b. PLDT Home/DSL Broadband Quiz Contest

2. The Training Component shall include two (2) teachers in the Training of Teachers and the training of approximately 300 teachers and students from fifteen (15) schools selected by the LGU.

3. The training will use training modules developed by UPOU for the 2016 Infotech Outreach Program and approved by PLDT. The training will be a combination of Face-to-Face and online instruction.

4. The second component of the Project is the PLDT Home/DSL Broadband Quiz, details of which will be disseminated by PLDT to DEPED and the LGU subsequent to this Agreement.

II. Responsibilities of DEPED

1. Recommend additional schools who will participate in the program
2. Recommend appropriate training venue
3. Recommend participants to the Training of Teachers
4. Provide other logistics and coordinate with the Project Management Team to facilitate implementation of the program in the area

III. Responsibilities of the LGU

1. Provide supervision needed to facilitate implementation of the project in the area.
2. Coordinate with DEPED and the Project Management Team to ensure successful implementation of the project in the area.

IV. Responsibilities of PLDT and Other Partners

1. PLDT and other partners shall implement the Infotech Outreach Program as stipulated in the MOA and in this Agreement.
2. All modules, learning tools, teaching aids, research/project outputs, discoveries and/or inventions/innovations collectively, "Works" produced by virtue of and pursuant to this MOA shall be owned by PLDT. As such, the Works may not be used without the prior written consent of PLDT.

V. Representations and Warranties

The Parties represent and warrant to each other that it has legal capacity and its representatives have been duly authorized to execute and deliver this Agreement and that the performance of its obligations hereunder have likewise been duly authorized and will not violate any contract, rights of third parties, or local or national laws.

VI. Relationship of the Parties

The Parties hereby acknowledge that nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating between or among them an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or agency.

VII. Term and Termination

--- [Page 13] ---
This Agreement shall be effective from January 6, 2017, and shall remain in force until June 8, 2017, unless sooner terminated by any of the Parties for cause. In case of termination, any ongoing activity under the Project shall first be completed before the effective date of termination.

PLDT reserves the right to terminate this Agreement with written notice to the other Parties thirty (30) days prior to the intended date of termination.

VIII. Limitation of Liability

In no event shall PLDT be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, even if PLDT has been advised of the possibility of such claims.

DEPED and LGU shall hold PLDT free and harmless from all claims of, and liabilities to, its employees, personnel, agents, representatives, participants or third parties arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, and to the extent permitted by law, shall indemnify PLDT against, any and all claims, losses or damages to PLDT and/or third parties arising from or resulting from:

a) any breach of DEPED and the LGU's undertakings, representations, warranties and covenants herein;

b) any liability or damage that may be incurred as a result of any breach of DEPED's and LGU's undertakings, representations, warranties and covenants herein.

IX. Miscellaneous Provisions

1. Cooperation among all Parties heren shall be based on principles of mutual benefit and respect for each other's independence.

2. All Parties may pursue other forms of cooperation within fields that are mutually acceptable and subject to the requirements of each research activity and other undertakings.

3. The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement does not, and cannot address all the circumstances and issues that may arise during its Term. The process undertaken by the Parties in accordance with this Agreement will be dynamic in nature, and may change over time. This Agreement is not binding upon the Parties except by mutual written agreement of the Parties.

4. In all instances, the Parties shall strive at every earnest efforts to settle the differences in the manner most beneficial to both Parties, with the end-view of pursuing the objectives of the Agreement. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved by negotiations in good faith. Failing which, the aggrieved Party may file appropriate action in the proper court of Makati City, to the exclusion of all other venues.

5. If any provision of this Agreement is legally adjudged invalid or unenforceable, the Parties agree that such invalidity, unenforceability, or illegality shall have the effect of nullifying other provisions hereof, which shall continue in full force and effect.
