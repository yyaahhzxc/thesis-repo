Ord. No. 0170 - 17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY OF DAVAO TO ENTER INTO AND SIGNE FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE-REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ONE (1) UNIT NURSERY (DOUBLE UP) LOCATED AT MALAGOS, BAGUO DISTRICT, THIS CITY FUNDED UNDER THE HIGH VALUE CROPS DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM, CALABARZON YEAR 2016";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sect. n 455, (b) (1) (vi) of R.A. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, tovides to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical go emanates the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the Memorandum of Agreement by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Agriculture-Regional Field Office XI for the construction of one (1) unit nursery (double up) located in Malagos, Baguio District, this City funded under the High Value Crops Development Program CY 2016;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, March 21, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Construction Of One (1) unit Nursery (Double Up)
located at Malagos, Baguio District, Davao City
funded under High Value Crops Development Program CY 2016

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement made and entered into by and between:

The DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE through its REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, a government agency created and existing under the Republic of the Philippines with the office address at F. Banguy St., Davao City, represented by its OIC-Regional Director ENGR. RICARDO M. ONATE, JR., hereinafter referred to as "DA RFO XI";

and

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government existing under the Philippine Law with office address at San Pedro St., Davao City represented by its Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "LGU";

WITNESSETH

Whereas, RA 6435 otherwise known as AGRICULTURE AND FISHERIES MODERNIZATION ACT or AFMA is aimed to strengthen the Agriculture and Fisheries sector through modernization, greater participation among the Local Government Units (LGUs), farmers and fisherfolks, non-government organizations, and people empowerment;

Whereas, the Department of Agriculture is the principal government agency mandated to develop the agriculture and fisheries in the country through the provision of adequate and appropriate support services;

Whereas, the Local Government Code of 1991 allows the Department of Agriculture (DA), a National Agency, to implement nationally funded projects jointly with the Local Government Units (LGUs);

Whereas, the HIGH VALUE CROPS DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (HVCDP) is one of the banner programs of the Department of Agriculture created to help address food security, poverty alleviation and sustainable growth in response to Republic Act 7900, an act to promote the production, processing, marketing and distribution of high value crops otherwise known as "HIGH VALUE CROPS DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 1995";

Whereas, the objectives of the HVCDP are to deliver the appropriate development support systems and facilities and harmonize development interventions in the banner production area(s)(s), farm households and identified convergence areas(s) base on the National Convergence Initiative (NCI) which was created through a joint Memorandum Circular No.1 of 1999 with the Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR) and the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR);

Whereas, the City Agriculture's Office as the agricultural arm of the LGU and as active partner of DA RFO XI supports and undertakes activities to promote the development of the agriculture in the countryside and has the administrative and technical

**1. The DA-RFO XI shall:**

a) Evaluate and review all project documents required from the LGU for approval;

b) Facilitate the procurement and the construction of the of the PROJECT as identified facility under the PROGRAM in accordance with Republic Act 9184 and other government accounting rules and regulation;

c) Conduct preparation of Detailed Designs, Program of Works and Detailed Estimates based on engineering standards;

d) Extend technical services as may be needed by the LGU relative to project implementation in close coordination with the City Agriculture's Office;

e) Conduct periodic monitoring and inspection/evaluation in partnership with City Agriculture's Office to ensure the progress and accomplishment of the PROJECT in accordance with approved specification before, during and after construction of the same;

f) In case of non-compliance, institute corrective measures and terminate this agreement in the case of non-compliance with the terms herein stipulated on the basis of the monitoring and evaluation findings that will be conducted periodically;

g) Facilitate the turn-over of the PROJECT to the LGU and ensure the due documentation of said turn-over.

**2. The LGU shall:**

a) Submit to DA-RFO XI through the City Agriculture's Office the requirements related to the PROJECT such as letter of intent requesting for the PROJECT, Sangguniang Panlungsod resolution authorizing the Mayor to enter into and sign for and on behalf of the LGU the Memorandum of Agreement with the DA-RFO XI, and endorsement or certification from CAO;

b) Provide lot for the project and submit to DA RFO XI certificate of land ownership (Certificate of Title/Deed of Donation/Deed of Sale) or Deed of

d) Have access to all records and data logbook of the PROJECT's operation and whenever requested allow DA RFO XI monitoring team to access the same, including vistorial records by GDA personnel under an approved office order;

**ARTICLE II GENERAL PROVISIONS**

If a dispute arises out of this contract, and if the dispute cannot be settled through negotiation, the parties agree first to try in good faith to settle the dispute by mediation before resorting to arbitration, litigation, or some other dispute resolution procedure (Alternative Dispute Resolution Clause).

**ARTICLE III EFFECTIVITY AND TERMINATION OF THE AGREEMENT**

This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect upon signing hereof of both parties; the implementation shall begin immediately, and shall continue to be valid within a period of Fifteen (15) years upon completion and turn-over of the PROJECT, or until written notice is given by either party thirty (30) days prior to the intended date of termination, or shall be terminated upon the satisfactorily fulfillment of all terms and conditions herein set forth. In case of termination, the termination shall be effective to the minimum duration of this agreement without any justifiable cause, DA RFO XI shall impose a fine in the amount equivalent to the damage incurred by the government due to the pre-termination of this contract, without prejudice to other legal remedies the agency may likewise avail of.

**ARTICLE IV MODIFICATIONS OR AMENDMENTS**

No modifications or amendments to this agreement and the approved project documents shall be allowed without the written consent of both parties involved. All amendments that will be appended to this agreement shall be in writing and must be delivered to the address of the parties indicated herein.
