SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR, TO SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE-REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI FOR THE 2016 PAMANA LIVELIHOOD-MUNICIPAL FISHERY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT IN THE AMOUNT OF TWO MILLION PESOS (PHP2,000,000.00) FOR THE MNLF COMMUNITY IN TAMO AMINA MUSLIN VILLAGE, PUROK 77, BANGAL, TALOMO DISTRICT, THIS CITY";

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the code mayor shall:
vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;"

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval;

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR 2016 PAMANA-LIVELIHOOD LOCATED AT THE CITY OF DAVAO

KNOWN ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement made and executed by and between:

THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE-REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, a government agency with address at Bangoy St., Davao City, represented herein by its OIC - Regional Executive Director, EMR. RICARDO M. ONATE Jr., hereinafter referred to as "DA-RFO XI";

and

The LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT OF DAVAO CITY, a Local Government Unit (LGI) in the Davao Region, duly established under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall, San Pedro Street, Davao City herein represented by its Mayor, HON. SAMUEL SUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the "LGD".

WITNESSETH -

WHEREAS, one of the key results of the President's Social Contract as embodied in the Philippine Development Plan ("PDP") for 2011 to 2016 is to attain a comprehensive, just and lasting peace and the rule of law;

WHEREAS, Government has adopted a dual strategy to attain a just and lasting peace of undertaking political settlements of the conflict and a complementary track called PAMANA Program aimed at addressing the root causes of conflict and issues that affect the peace process and narrow the gap between negotiating table and the ground;

WHEREAS, the PPAyapa at Masangang PamayaNan (PAMANA) Program is envisioned to provide the framework for intervention in conflict-affected and vulnerable areas to complement the government's peace negotiation efforts;

WHEREAS, the PAMANA is the convergence framework through which national government, the local government units and development agencies provide targeted interventions in identified conflict-affected areas (CAA) focused on responsible governance; improved delivery of basic services; sustainable development and economic reconstruction; and security and social cohesion through community-based initiatives, implementation of transparent and accountable mechanisms and capacity building of communities while ensuring that income generating activities and economic development are supported and employment opportunities provided;

WHEREAS, the PAMANA Program aims to (i) reduce poverty and vulnerability in conflict- affected areas; (ii) improve governance; (iii) empower communities and strengthen their capacities to address issues of conflict and peace through activities that promote social cohesion;

Page 1 of 7

--- [Page 10] ---
WHEREAS, THE PAMANA Framework is anchored on three complementary strategic pillars that define state interventions to achieve lasting peace. These are:

* Pillar 1 refers to interventions that support the establishment of the foundations of peace and the building of resilient communities through policy reform and development.
* Pillar 2 refers to micro-level interventions to promote the convergent delivery of services and goods focused on households and communities by both national and local agencies.
* Pillar 3 refers to meso-level interventions that address regional and sub-regional development challenges and will contribute to peace building.

WHEREAS, THE PAMANA Program shall be implemented by national line agencies in partnership with local government units (LGUs) through appropriate mandates as provided under their respective mandates;

WHEREAS, FOR FY 2016, the Department of Agriculture, along with partner NGOs, will implement PAMANA Pillar 3 projects anchored on the goal of high impact sub-regional economic integration of poor areas, thus addressing development issues beyond the immediate area;

WHEREAS, to enable the realization of Pillar 3 interventions, the Program Fund will be sourced out from the CY 2016 General Appropriations Act (GAA) allocated to Department of Agriculture-Regional Field Offices (DA-RFOs) and will be used exclusively to implement projects in areas affected by and vulnerable to armed conflict, as identified by the Office of the Presidential Adviser on Peace Process (OPAPP) through conflict-sensitive programming processes.

WHEREAS, PAMANA covers seven (7) geographic zones, in which target areas have been identified and prioritized based on various peace process lines which includes specifically for BANGSAMORO PEACE PROCESS;

WHEREAS, Local Government Units (LGUs), as provided under the Local Government Code of 1991 or Republic Act No. 7140, assume the primary responsibility for the provision of basic services and facilities and the improvement of quality of life of their constituents;

WHEREAS, the LGUs are recognized by the OPAPP to have a significant role in peace-building and conflict transformation due to their strategic advantage in the community having direct knowledge of community issues and concerns and both entities are committed towards the achievement of the PAMANA Program;

WHEREAS, both the DA-RPO VI and the LGU of Davao City are committed to the principles of transparency and accountability and to engage the general public through identified beneficiaries formed as an association/organization, in the attainment of good governance, through the proper implementation of government programs, activities and projects, and the timely delivery of services to the Filipino people;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises and of the mutual covenants stipulated hereinafter set forth, the PARTIES hereto agree into this Memorandum of Agreement to adhere to the following terms and conditions.

**ARTICLE I. GENERAL PROVISIONS**

a) This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) shall cover CY 2019/20 PANAMA-DA Fund Livelihood project in support to the BANGSAMORO peace process specifically for the MNLV Community in Taomo Anima Muslim Village, Purok 77, Bangui, Talomo District, Davao City, as identified by OPAPP with a total project allocation amounting to Two Million (P2,000,000.00) pesos only for the herein listed project referred to as the PROJECT, the breakdown are as follows:

| Project Title | Amount |
|---------------|--------|
| Municipal Fishery Development Project | Php 2,000,000.00 |

b) All applicable provisions of the Guidelines in the Management of the PANAMA-DA Fund, attached herein as ANNEX A, is made an integral part of this MOA;

c) Variation of the project design during the course of implementation should first seek the review and conformity of the DA-RFO XI and should only be limited to the total project cost. Any variation order resulting in an increase in the total project cost shall be borne by the LGU.

**ARTICLE II. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTNERS**

The DA-RFOXI shall:

a) Set deadline for the submission and completion of the project proposal by the LGU of Davao City for proper validation of pre-identified project with supporting documents. Failure to submit and/or complete the proposal on the set deadline will be a basis for not validating and releasing of funds.

b) Conduct project site/field validation, geo-tagging and feasibility validation of the project and verify the accuracy of submitted project proposal and supporting documents;

c) Complete the review and evaluation of the project's feasibility after receipt of the LGU proposal with complete supporting documents. If it passes the evaluation criteria, the DA-RFOXI shall issue a Certificate of Feasibility to the LGU;

d) Upon verification of satisfactory compliance of all requirements, the DA-RFOXI XI shall enter into a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with the LGU;

e) Within ten (10) days after the signing of MOADA RFO XI shall issue a Certificate of Feasibility to the LGU for the corresponding amount of the approved PANAMA subsidy, subject to the submission of a Certificate from a Government Bank that the LGU has opened or used a Special Trust Account for the PANAMA Program;

--- [Page 12] ---
f) Release in full disbursement to the Special Trust Account (STA) for PAMANA Program under the name of LGD of Danao City the required funds for the various projects listed above on the basis of approved project proposals in the total amount of Two Million (P2,000,000.00) pesos only.

g) Quarterly monitors and validates the project implementation of the PAMANA-DA funded project of LGD in close coordination with the OPAPP-PAMANA Area Manager but may at any time conduct a separate field monitoring on the progress/states of projects.

The City Government shall:

a) Conduct social preparation activities to ensure that the communities where the projects are to be implemented are well-informed about the projects and the Core Messages of PAMANA (ANNEX D), and are engaged in the implementation either as laborers, implementing partners, direct or indirect beneficiaries, citizen volunteers and/or volunteers, as applicable.

b) Meet the following criteria: 1) with technical capabilities and organizational capacity to implement the project; 2) without unpledged funds to DA-RFO XI under PAMANA Program; 3) Submit Seagullion Resolution authorizing LCE to enter and sign MOA for and in behalf of the LGU;

c) Prepare and submit Project Proposals and its enabling documents under Section VII of the MOA, which are to be submitted to the Management of the Mgaayong PampanNan or PAMANA-DA, and of which is accorded by the PAMANA peace and social cohesion standards;

d) Enter into MOA with DA RFO XIfor the release of funds;

e) Designate an appropriate fiscal personnel to supervise fiscal units/departments in the area to coordinate, facilitate and supervise the implementation of PAMANA project in the area to ensure the integration, coordination, facilitation and supervision of all activities/outputs;

f) Facilitate the compliance of supporting documents such as Deed of Donation (DD)or usufruct Certificate of Land Availability (CLA) for infrastructure projects, and other necessary documents;

g) Open or Use an Existing Special Trust Account (STA)for PAMANA Program in a government bank to facilitate its transfer and provide the DA-RFO XI a certification from the Government Bank and details of the account;

h) Issue a receipt corresponding to the amount of the PAMANA - DA Fund subsidy received;

i) Maintain Book of Accounts for the PAMANA - DA Fund and set up sound financial control based on government accounting rules and regulations;

j) Shoulders the incaration cost of the MOA;

k) Ensure that the implementation is competitive bidding, adhering to the provisions of RA 9184 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), as revised;

l) Oversee the physical implementation of their respective project to ensure that it is in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the MOA and Work and Financial Plan (WFP);

m) Implement cost recovery program for economic enterprises;

n) Be responsible for the settlement of the road right-of-way, whenever applicable.

**MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA)**

**Article I. Project Implementation**

1. **Project Title and Status:**
   - The project title and status shall be indicated in compliance with the PAMANA transparency and accountability guidelines.
   - The design of the billboards must adhere to the PAMANA Guidelines for Community Project Billboards, attached as Annex E.

2. **System Implementation and Procedures:**
   - Implement systems and procedures necessary to sustain the life of the project/facility.
   - Submit monthly and quarterly physical accomplishment and financial reports to the Regional DA-PAMANA Project Management Team and an updated geo-tagged data;
   - Maintain and operate the project/facility/enterprise in accordance with 3rd-party standards, technical, engineering, and environmental practices;
   - Make available to DA-RFO XI at least two times per year, all financial records and information required for auditing and liquidation purposes;
   - Submit the liquidation reports of PAMANA-DA Transfer Funded to LGU within one (1) month from receipt of such notice by either party;
   - Return the unexpended balance of the released portion of the funds or the pertinent portion related to the cancelled component, whenever applicable;
   - Release the full amount allocated to the project, including accrued interest earnings, in case of:
     1. Misappropriation of fund without prejudice to the filing of administrative and legal action against the responsible party;
     2. Non-utilization of fund within six (6) months from the date of receipt;
     3. Non-compliance of any provision stated in this Agreement;
   - Shall be liable for any fine relative to the procurement and performance of any contract is consistent with the applicable provisions of RA 9184 and laws on corruption and fraudulent practices;
   - Shall resolve all issues relative to any contract within the implementation of LGU project.

**Article III. Termination or Suspension**

This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) shall be implemented in accordance with the terms and conditions herein stipulated. This MOA shall be terminated by either DA-RFO XI or LGU upon filing of a final filing (15) days after receipt of such notice by either party, the LGU shall promptly return any unutilized funds to DA-RFO XI, not later than the 15th day from receipt of such notice by either party. Furthermore, DA-RFO XI reserves the right to apply and implement any of the following options:

- **Terminate/Cancel the Implementation of the Project:** 
  - When situations/circumstances would make it impractical for the project/s to continue to be carried out;
  - Upon request of the LGU;

- **Suspend Project Implementation:** 
  - During the conduct of review and monitoring activities, the DA determines that: (i) the procurement of any contract is inconsistent with RA 9184 provisions; (ii) LGU is performing unsatisfactorily in the implementation and Page 5 of 7
