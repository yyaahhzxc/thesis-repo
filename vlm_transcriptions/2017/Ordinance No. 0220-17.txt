Series of 2017  

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND CHINA FUND CORPORATION FOR DEVELOPMENT (CFD) IN RELATION TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF WATER PUMPING STATIONS LOCATED IN BARANGAYS TIBUNCO AND PAÑALUM, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT AND BARANGAY BANTOL, MARILONG DISTRICT, ALL OF THIS CITY

Ord. No. 0220-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY, A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT WITH THE CITY BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND CHINA FOUNDATION FOR PEACE AND DEVELOPMENT (CFPD), IN RELATION TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF WATER PUMPING STATIONS LOCATED IN BARANGAYS TIBUNGO AND PAALUAN, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT AND BARANGAY BANTOL, MARILOG DISTRICT, ALL OF THIS CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY: The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Memorandum of Agreement by and between the City Government of Davao and China Foundation for Peace and Development (CFPD). The Agreement pertains to the construction of Water Pumping Stations located in Barangays Tibungo and Paaluan and Barangay Bantol, this City;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 27th day of June 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

**MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT**

本合作备忘录由以下双方共同见证并签署：

This Memorandum of Agreement is entered into by and between party A and party B:

甲方：中国和平发展基金会（简称基金会）；由中国和平发展基金会作为基金会的全权代表

PARTY A: THE CHINA FOUNDATION FOR PEACE AND DEVELOPMENT, represented herein by _____________, _____________ and hereinafter referred to as the CFPD

乙方：达沃市政府；达沃市市长萨拉·杜特尔特（SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO）全权代表达沃市政府作为合作方代表

PARTY B: THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit, established and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, represent herein by HONORABLE SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, in her capacity as the City Mayor, and hereinafter referred to as the LGU;

见证如下

WITNESSETH

为增进中国与菲律宾两国人民的友好与交往，推动中菲民间交流与合作，中国和平发展基金会（简称基金会）与菲律宾达沃市政府经友好协商，由中国基金会出资200,000美元（贰佰万美元）用于在达沃市郊区的周边三个村修建饮水泵站及配套设施，主要包括深水井、水塔、动力室和相关设备等。项目建成后，该饮水项目将成为象征中国和菲律宾友谊的示范项目。

In view of enhancing people-to-people friendly exchanges and promoting civil communication and cooperation between China and

--- [Page 9] ---
The Philippines, China Foundation for Peace and Development (hereinafter referred to as CCFPD) intends to raise a fund amounting to US$200,000 to be allocated as construction funds in constructing water pump stations in three different Barangays in the City of Davao which shall consist of a deep well pumping station, power-engine room, and other related equipment. Such project will symbolize Philippine-China Friendship.

To ensure project quality and engineering progress, the Davao City Government agrees to establish a Project Monitoring Committee which shall be in charge of monitoring the said construction and shall provide the CCFPD and its local partner with necessary assistance and support in the execution of the said project.

Based on the above, both parties agree as follows:

PARTIES A: The CCFPD shall:

1. Raise and allocate a budget hereinafter referred to as FUND. The FUND committed is TWO HUNDRED US DOLLARS ONLY (US$200,000) which shall be used for the construction of a water pumping station located at Barangay Tibungco, Barangay Pañalum, Paquibato, and Barangay Bantol, Marilao, Davao City;

--- [Page 10] ---
2. Cooperate with the local partner to construct the water pumping station in accordance with the policies and guidelines set by the foundation;

3. Make corresponding payments to the local partner based on his accomplishment;

4. Ask the LGU to form a project monitoring committee and provide and submit to the CFFD a project status report of the construction of the project on a regular basis;

5. After the completion of the project, name it "Philippines-China Friendship Water Pump Station";

PARTY B: THE LGU OF DAVAO CITY shall:

1. As the recipient of the project, identify the site as the recipient of the project;

2. Form a project monitoring committee to supervise the construction and ensure the quality of the project;

3. Conduct periodic monitoring of the construction activities to ensure that the project is in accordance with the desired result and with the objectives and timetables embodied in the contract signed between the foundation and the local partner.

--- [Page 11] ---
4. Provide the CFPD with an English project supervision report, monthly project construction photos and video footage. Upon completion of 90% of the project and meeting the requirements for project completion, provide a payment certificate to the CFPD as proof of payment. The CFPD will issue a certification letter attesting that payment may be made to the local partner after completing 90% of the project and complying with the specifications provided in the project plan.

5. Assist the CFPD and its local partner in obtaining relevant qualification certificates to promote project development.

6. After the project is completed, organize a launch and turnover ceremony. Invite relevant personnel from both the Philippines and China, as well as Philippine media, to participate and promote the project to facilitate the establishment of friendly relations between the Philippines and China.

This agreement shall take effect from the date of signing by the parties and shall be terminated upon the satisfactory fulfillment of all terms and conditions embodied herein. Any modifications or amendments to the agreement shall be acceptable to all parties in writing after such modifications or amendments have been substantially discussed by the parties herein.
