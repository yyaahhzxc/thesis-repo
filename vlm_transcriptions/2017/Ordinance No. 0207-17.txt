**Series of 2017**

**AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO FILE APPROPRIATE ACTION FOR THE CANCELLATION OF LAND TITLES ISSUED TO PRIVATE PERSONS APPROVE, RESTORE, APPLICATIONS AND APPROVED SURVEYS INVOLVING LANDS SITUATED WITHIN THE EDEN SUMMER RESORT UNDER PROCLAMATION NO. 239**

Ord. No. 0207 - 17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO FULL APPROPRIATE ACTION FOR THE CANCELLATION OF LAND TITLES ISSUED TO PRIVATE PERSONS, APPROVED HOMESTEAD APPLICATIONS AND APPROVED SURVEYS INVOLVING LOTS SITUATED WITHIN THE EDEN SUMMER RESORT UNDER PROCLAMATION NO. 239";

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to sign a complaint for:
a. cancellation of land titles issued to private persons;
b. cancellation of approved homestead patents of private individuals;
c. cancellation of approved surveys;
involving lots situated within the Eden Summer Resort under Proclamation No. 239 for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao City to the Office of the Solicitor General/Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR);

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, June 20, 2017, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
