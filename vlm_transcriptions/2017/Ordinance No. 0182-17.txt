Ord. No. 0182-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE TECHNICAL EDUCATION AND SKILLS DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (TESDA) RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE BOTTOM-UP BUDGETING (BUB) PROGRAM ON SKILLS TRAINING";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 455, (b) (i) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents under authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE TECHNICAL EDUCATION AND SKILLS DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (TESDA) RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE BOTTOM-UP BUDGETING (BUB) PROGRAM ON SKILLS TRAINING;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, April 25, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) made and entered into this day of 20 in between:

The Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), with office at 616 Interior 2, Rimas Street, Aquino Subd., J.P. Laurel Ave., Davao City herein represented by its Regional Director, GASPAR S. GAYONA hereinafter referred to as TESDA;

and The City of Davao, a Local Government Unit (LGU) represented by its Local Chief Executive, SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as DAVAO CITY GOVERNMENT;

I. Project

WHEREAS, the Grassroots Participatory Budgeting (GPB) formerly known as the Bottom-Up Budgeting (BUB) primarily empowers the grassroots organizations and the local government units (LGUs) in identifying priority poverty reduction projects that will be funded by national agencies;

WHEREAS, GPB is an approach to preparing the budget proposal of agencies, taking into consideration the development needs of cities/municipalities as identified in their respective local poverty reduction action plans that shall be formulated with strong participation of basic sector organizations and other civil organization;

WHEREAS, the project is envisioned to ensure the implementation of priority poverty reduction projects as identified at the city/municipal level through a participatory planning and budgeting process;

WHEREAS, the project shall showcase convergence of efforts of National Government Agencies (NGAs), Local Government Units (LGUs) and Civil Society Organizations (CSOs);

WHEREAS, the Qualification Map (QM) for GPB is developed to include the physical and financial details of the training program and the period of its implementation which is contained within the prescribed validity period for CY 2016 per TESDA GPB Guidelines;

WHEREAS, the training shall be provided by the Technical Vocational Institutions (TVIs) with TESDA registered programs as identified by the Second Party;

WHEREAS, assessment and certification shall be mandatory to all training programs covered with Assessment Tools.

11. Duties and Responsibilities of the Parties:

A. Specific Duties of the Parties

The First Party shall:
1. Allocate the amount of Two Million Eight Hundred Forty-One Thousand Four Hundred Pesos only (P2,841,400.00) for the provision of skills training;
2. Provide the Davao City Government the list of institutions with registered programs;
3. Provide assistance in the preparation and signing of the Qualification Map for GBP Program;
4. Conduct, in coordination with the Technical Vocational Institution (TVI), the pre-training process such as: Youth Profiling for Starring Careers (YPHSC), Training Induction Program (TIP);
5. Ensure that all trainees undergo the mandatory assessment after end of the training program;
6. Pay the training cost directly to the TVET Providers and the assessment fees to the TESDA accredited Assessment Centers through the TESDA Provincial Offices, provided that all required supporting documents are complied with subject to government accounting and auditing rules and regulations;
7. Prepare and submit reports to all concerned agencies as reasonably required and necessary based on the implementing guidelines;

The Second Party shall:
1. Designate a Focal Person for the Bottoms-up Budgeting (BuB) Program;
2. Select a training provider among the list of institutions with registered programs provided by the First Party;
3. Identify, recruit, screen and endorse qualified beneficiaries;
4. Coordinate with TVI on the submission of Enrollment Report within thirty (30) calendar days after start of the program while the terminal report shall be submitted within fifteen (15) calendar days after the end of training period;
5. Motivate and nurture the trainees, together with the TVI, to maintain one hundred percent (100%) completion of the training program;
6. Assist in the monitoring of attendance and participation of trainees to qualify for completion of the training program which is set at eighty percent (80%);
7. Coordinate with TVI in conducting activity to mark the completion of every training program and report the same to the First Party;
8. Provide adequate Local Counterpart Contribution for the project implementation in terms of Manpower Services and other Technical Monitoring for the supervision and monitoring of the projects or for other needs related to the projects as prescribed by the JMC # 4, Series of 2013.

B. Joint and Solidary Duties of the Parties:
1. Promote and advocate the skills training program;
2. Prepare and submit Qualification Map (QM) based on the approved projects bearing the signatures of the Regional Director, Provincial Director, City/Municipal Mayor, TESDA Provincial Office Focal Person, LGU Focal Person and Training Provider;

--- [Page 10] ---
3. Collaborate and work together in the implementation of the program to achieve the desired results;
4. Ensure the institutionalization and sustainability of the program outputs and outcomes through the development and implementation of a sustainability plan to include mechanisms for sharing of approaches and social marketing strategy to ensure wider reach among other agencies/partners and beneficiaries subject to availability of funds; and
5. Institutionalize effective monitoring system/database to establish reliable status report (tracer reports) of the graduates.
II. Relation to the Parties
The Parties are independent of each other and not an agent representative or joint venture partner of each other. Neither party nor any of its personnel or officers will enter into any contract or commitment on behalf of the other party and each party will be solely responsible for making all payments and on behalf of its employees and approved subcontractors including those required by law. A party will not be liable for any debts or other liabilities of the other party.
IV. Representations and Warranties
Parties hereby represent and warrant that they have the power to enter into and perform and have taken all necessary actions to authorize the entry into and performance of the Agreement under and in accordance with the laws of the Philippines.
V. Applicable Laws
This agreement is contingent upon compliance with all applicable laws of the Philippines, particularly the Anti-Craft and Corrupt Practices Act (RA 3019) and the Code of Conduct and Ethics Standards for Public Officials and Employees (RA 6713). As such, the Parties agree that they will not, in connection with the transactions contemplated in this MOA, transfer anything of value, directly or indirectly, to any government official, employee of a government-controlled company, or political party or other private (non-government) persons or entities working on behalf of any government in order to obtain an improper benefit or advantage. No money paid or delivered to the Parties as sponsorship, compensation or otherwise has been or will be used to pay any bribe or kickback in violation of applicable laws. Parties agree to provide prompt certification of its continuing compliance with applicable relevant laws whenever reasonably requested by another party.
VI. Modification/Amendment
This MOA may be revised, amended or modified only through a written instrument duly executed and signed by the parties hereto and executed with the same formality as this agreement.
