Ord. No. 0193-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH-DAVAO REGION, RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF BOTTOM-UP BUDGETING PROJECT WITHIN THE CITY OF DAVAO."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b)(1)(vi) of the Local Government Code provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;"
SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT - The Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) will be executed by and between the City Government of Davao, represented by the City Mayor and the Department of Health-Davao Region, represented by Dr. Abdullah B. Dumana Jr., it embodies the implementation of Bottom-Up Budgeting Projects within the City of Davao;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the Memorandum of Agreement between THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH-DAVAO REGION. The agreement pertains to the implementation of Bottom-Up Budgeting Projects within the City of Davao;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on May 9, 2017, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement is entered into this day of 2016, in by and between

The DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH - REGIONAL OFFICE XI with office address at J.P. Laurel Avenue, Bajada, Davao City herein represented by DR. ABDULLAH B. DUMAHA JR., in his capacity as ASSISTANT SECRETARY OF HEALTH - MINDANAO CLUSTER AND REGIONAL DIRECTOR, hereinafter referred to as DOH RO XI;

and

The CITY OF DAVAO with office address at Davao City, Davao del Sur herein represented by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE in her capacity as CITY MAYOR, hereinafter referred to as the City Local Government Unit (CLGU).

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, in pursuit of attaining the Philippine Development Plan's goal of the inclusive growth and poverty reduction and promoting good governance at the local level, the Human Development and Poverty Reduction Cluster (HDPRC) through the Bottom-up Budgeting (BUB) Oversight Agencies shall implement the Bottom-up Budgeting (BuB) program.

WHEREAS, the BuB program seeks to increase citizen's access to local service delivery through a demand-driven budget planning process and to strengthen government accountability in local public service provision.

WHEREAS, the BuB oversight agencies and DOH as one of the participating agencies are tasked to ensure the implementation of priority poverty reduction projects as identified at the city/municipal level through a participatory planning and budgeting process.

WHEREAS, CY 2015 General Appropriation Act (GAA) 2015-10651 has appropriated FIFTY THREE MILLION EIGHTY NINE THOUSAND SEVEN HUNDRED FIFTY PESOS (Php53,089,750.00) for the Bottom-Up-Budgeting (BuB) projects implementation in Davao Region.

WHEREAS, of the total fund allotted to DOH-RO XI, the amount of ONE MILLION PESOS (Php 1,000,000.00) was earmarked for the Capacity Building for Doctors, Nurses, Midwives, PPOs & PPVs in the city of Davao.

WHEREAS, the CLGU of Davao is granted the authority to undertake the Capability Building Activity for the year 2015.

WHEREAS, in order to implement the Capacity Building for Health Human Resources under BuB in Davao City, the DOH finds it justifiable and reasonable to transfer the funds for the year 2015 in order for the CLGU to fast track the facilitation of documents and completion of the project.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and stipulations herein set forth, the Parties hereto agree and do hereby agree as follows:

--- [Page 10] ---
A. FUND SUPPORT AND UTILIZATION:
- 1. The funds shall be obligated by DOH RO XI on or before Dec 31, 2016.
- 2. The said funds shall be released to the CLGU as Fund Transferred.
- 3. Release of funds shall follow the conditions set forth in COOA circular 2012:001.
- 4. The CLGU shall fully utilize the fund within a year after the release.
- 5. Disbursement of funds shall be in accordance with existing accounting and auditing rules.

B. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CLGU:
1. Fund releases to CLGU will only be allowed if CLGU has complied with the following governance conditions in accordance with the pertinent special and general provisions in the GAA:
   - Compliance with the Good Financial Housekeeping component of the Seal of Good Local Governance FY 2014 Assessment.
   - Submission of the PFM Improvement Plan for 2015 for CLGU under the LGU PFM 2 Project or initiation of its implementation (for 2013 and prior) to the LGU PFM 2 Project Office as determined by the DBM Regional Office.
2. Attend meetings called by the DOH-RO XI.
3. Supervise and monitor the implementation of the project until its completion.
4. Submit (monthly/quarterly) status report of physical and financial accounts to the DOH-RO XI.
5. The CLGU shall submit Fund Utilization Report (FUR) to DOH-RO XI duly accredited by the Auditor, together with the following documents:
   - Report of Checks Issued and Report of Disbursements certified correct by the Accountant, approved by the Head of CLGU and duly audited by the Auditor.
   - Copy of Credit Notice issued by the Auditor of the CLGU.
   - Copy of OR issued for the refund of unexpended/unutilized balance of fund transferred.

C. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DOH:
1. Provide technical assistance in the preparations of training designs and assist in the conduct of the capacity building activity if warranted.
2. Approve Training/Activity Design.
3. Ensure that the activity was conducted/implemented.

PROJECT MONITORING, COMPLETION, TURN OVER AND ACCEPTANCE:
1. Project implementation shall be monitored jointly or separately by the concerned stakeholders.
2. A coordination meeting shall be conducted on the need basis.
3. Completion Report of the Capacity Building Activity shall be submitted by the CLGU to DOH-RO XI.
4. The DOH-RO XI shall status reports of the activity conducted to the Bureau of Local Health System Division - DOH, Manila copy furnished to NEDA and Office of the President-Project Monitoring Office.

AMENDMENTS:
This contract shall take effect immediately upon signing hereof and may be amended or revoked only upon mutual consent of both Parties provided that any amendment shall be covered by Supplemental Provisions signed by both Parties which shall be incorporated as an integral parts of this MOA.
