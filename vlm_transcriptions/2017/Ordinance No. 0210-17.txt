Ord. No. 0210-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE OF LOTS OF ROSELYN M. BARBARONA, DIANE MAE A. MANOLO, DESAN M. CHUCAS AND CLINTON KENT DELGADO, IN THE CITY ASSISTED PROJECT OF SANDAWA VILLAGE SETTLERS' ASSOCIATION, INC., LOCATED IN SITIO TAGNIG, BARANGAY BUHANGIN, THIS CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 states that "For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to law or ordinance, the City Mayor may sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao all business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the Deed of Sale of Lots of Roselyn M. Barbarona, Diane Mae A. Manolo, Desan M. Chucas and Clinton Kent Delgado, in the City-Assisted Project of Sandawa Village Settlers Association, Inc., located in Sitio Tagnig, Barangay Buhangin, Davao City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, June 20, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
