Ordinance No. 0269-09
Series of 2009

An Ordinance Banning the Use, Sale and Distribution of Sugar Substitutes Such as Cyclamates or Neotogen Also Known as "Magic Sugar" Among Food and Business Establishments, Food Vendors, Local Markets and Other Similar Food and Business Outlets in Davao City and for Other Purposes

--- [Page 3] ---
Page 2 of 4  
Ord. No. 0269-09  

By it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE BANNING THE USE, SALE AND DISTRIBUTION OF THE SUGAR SUBSTITUTE SODIUM CYCLAMATE OR NEOTOGEN ALSO KNOWN AS MAGIC SUGAR" AMONG FOOD AND BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS, LOCAL MARKETS AND OTHER SIMILAR FOOD AND BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS IN DAVAO CITY AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES WHICH SHALL HEREINAFTER BE REFERRED TO AS THE "MAGIC SUGAR ORDINANCE".  

SECTION 2: DECLARATION OF POLICY. The City Government of Davao herby declares the following as its policies, to wit:  

The health and well-being of its constituents is of utmost importance, thus, food additives and other substitutes must be monitored and regulated;  

The constituents must be protected from deceptive advertising and from the dangers of hazardous food products, additives or substitutes such as sodium cyclamate or Neotogen also commonly known in the market as "magic sugar".  

SECTION 3. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY. This Ordinance shall be applicable to all food establishments including, but not limited to, bars, cafes such as short-order cafes, cafeterias, catering kitchens, c cocktail lounges, coffee shops, commissaries, convenience stores, delicatessens, dry goods grocery stores, general industrial feeding establishments, luncheonettes, markets including fast food outlets, public food markets and wet markets, nightclubs, prepared-food takeout places, restaurants whether fixed or mobile, sandwich shops, soda fountains, taverns, stands such as roadside stands, food stands, "sur-surf" stores or similar places including ambulant food vendors and inferiordly, any place where food items which are sold for sale or for service on the premises or elsewhere and other establishments or operations including homes where food is processed, prepared, stored, served or provided for the public for a charge and those that are distributing, selling, purchasing and/or using "magic sugar" within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao;  

SECTION 4: DEFINITION OF TERMS. The following terms as used in this Ordinance shall be defined as follows:  

Ambulant Vendors - as used in this ordinance refer to moving or mobile vendors of food items such as juice with "magic sugar" additives;  

Food Establishments - as used in this ordinance refer to establishments such as, but not limited to, cafeterias, catering kitchens, coffee shops, commissaries, delicatessens, eateries, luncheonettes and food takeout places or food stands offering food or drinks with "magic sugar" additives;

Sugar - refers to a food addition which duplicates the effect of sugar in taste but often with less food energy. In other countries, sugar substitutes are often referred to as "sweeteners" (to the exclusion of sucrose). They are compounds whose whole sweetness is many times that of sucrose. Because the sensation of sweetness caused by these substitutes is often different from sugar, they can be used in complex and large mixtures in order to achieve the most natural sweet sensation.

Neotogen - refers to a trademark or brand name for "magic sugar" commonly used in food and drinks as additives or sugar substitute.

Sodium Cyclamate - refers to the scientific name of "magic sugar".

SECTION 5: PROHIBITED ACT - it shall be unlawful for any person to use Neotogen or "magic sugar" in the processing, preparation of food, and selling and distribution of the same for public consumption within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City;

SECTION 6: PENALTY PROVISION - Any person, food establishment or food vendor within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City that knowingly and willfully violates the provisions of this ordinance commits an offense and shall be punished at the discretion of the Court;

5.1 For the first offense, a violator shall be fined not exceeding One Thousand Pesos (PHP 1,000.00) and/or imprisonment not exceeding one (1) month at the discretion of the Court;

5.2 For the second offense, a violator shall be fined not exceeding Two Thousand Pesos (PHP 2,000.00) and/or imprisonment not exceeding two (2) months at the discretion of the Court;

5.3 For the third offense, a violator shall be fined not exceeding Three Thousand Pesos (PHP 3,000.00) and/or imprisonment not exceeding three (3) months at the discretion of the Court;

SECTION 7: SEVERABILITY CLAUSE - In the event that any word, article, clause or paragraph of this Ordinance is declared invalid for any reason, other provisions thereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 8: DEPEALING CLAUSE - All ordinances as well as pertinent rules and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed or amended accordingly;

SECTION 9: EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days following the completion of its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City;
