Ord No. 034-13

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled.

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE USE OF SOME STREETS NEAR THE MINTAL PUBLIC MARKET IN BARANGAY MINTAL, THIS CITY, EVERY SUNDAY FROM 6:00 A.M. TO 6:00 P.M."

SECTION 2. REGULATION OF USE - Pursuant to Sec. 21 (d) of the Local Government Code of 1991, the use of the following streets near the Mintal Public Market in Barangay Mintal shall be regulated, as follows:

1. One way traffic from corner Lantana Street to Santan Street to the National Highway (Sampaguita St.)
2. One way traffic from the National Highway to Bougainville Street to corner Rubia Street to Bougainville Street
3. No entry from Sampaguita Street to Santan Street
4. Right turn from Bougainville Street to Rubia Street
5. No entry from Bougainville Street to Mintal Public Market

SECTION 3. DURATION - The regulation of the aforementioned streets shall be effective only every Sunday starting from 6:00 A.M. until 6:00 P.M., until the end of December 2013. After the prescribed period, regular traffic shall resume.

SECTION 4. PUBLIC INFORMATION - The Barangay Council of Mintal in coordination with the Traffic Management Center shall put up the necessary signages and perform the necessary actions to help inform the public of the changes in the traffic flow in the affected streets by reason of this Ordinance;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason any Section of the Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other Sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any ordinance, local issuance or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval and publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

ENACTED, September 24, 2013, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.
