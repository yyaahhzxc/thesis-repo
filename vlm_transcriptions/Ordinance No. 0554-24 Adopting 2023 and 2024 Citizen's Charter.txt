Ord. No. 0554-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
ADOPTING THE 2023 AND 2024 CITIZEN'S CHARTER OF THE CITY
GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 2 and 3 of Republic Act
No. 9485 as amended by Republic Act No. 11032, also known as the "Ease of Doing
Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018", provide:
"Section 2. Declaration of Policy - It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to promote integrity, accountability, proper management of
public affairs and public property, as well as to establish practices
aimed at efficient turnaround of the delivery of government services
and the prevention of graft and corruption in government. Towards
this end, the State shall maintain honesty and responsibility among its
public officials and employees, and shall take appropriate measures
to promote transparency in each agency with regard to the manner of
transacting with the public, which shall encompass a program for the
adoption of simplified requirements and procedures that will reduce
red tape and expedite business and nonbusiness related transactions
in government."

"Section 3. Coverage - This Act shall apply to all government offices
and agencies, including Local Government Units (LGUs), Government-
Owned or Controlled Corporations and other government
instrumentalities within the Philippines and abroad, that
provide services covering business and nonbusiness related
transactions as defined in this Act."

SECTION 3. ADOPTION - The provisions of the Citizen's Charter 2023
Volumes I and II and 2024 Volumes I and II of the City Government of Davao are
hereby adopted in toto and shall form part of this Ordinance.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 9th day of July 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members
of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
