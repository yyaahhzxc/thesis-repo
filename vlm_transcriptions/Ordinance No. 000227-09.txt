SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "THE DAVAO CITY JAPANESE HERITAGE SITE DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL".

SECTION 2. PURPOSE. This Ordinance aims to recognize the cultural and historical bonds between the people of Japan and Davao City, specifically Barangay Minal, which has been the primary residence of Japanese citizens during the World War II.

SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Undeniably, the Filipino-Japanese community has been contributing considerably to the Philippine economy and to its culture. They have regarded this City as their home and the Philippines as their land.

As one of the most livable and peaceful cities in the world, the City Government of Davao sees the need to create the Davao City Japanese Heritage Site Development Council to assist in the active formulation and implementation of policies in an area that has great commercial, historical, economic and social significance to the City of Davao.

SECTION 4. TERRITORIAL BOUNDARIES. The boundaries of the Davao City Japanese Heritage Site shall cover the entirety of Barangay Minal of the City of Davao.

SECTION 5. CREATION OF THE COUNCIL. There is hereby created a multi-sectoral body to be known as the Davao City Japanese Heritage Site Development Council which shall actively participate in the formulation and implementation of the development plan for the site.

SECTION 6. COMPOSITION OF MEMBERSHIP. The Davao City Japanese Heritage Site Development Council shall be governed by an Executive Committee which shall be composed of the following:

- City Mayor, Chairperson
- Chairperson, Committee on International Relations
- Chairperson, Committee on Tourism and Beautification
- Chairperson, Committee on Education, Science & Technology, Arts and Culture
- Chairperson, Committee on Cultural Communities & Muslim Affairs
- City Planning and Development Coordinator
- City Environment and Natural Resources Officer (CENRO)
- Davao City Investment Promotion Center (DCIPC)
- Punong Barangay, Barangay Minal
- Head, City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)
- Head, Davao City Police Office (DCPO)
- Head, City Engineer's Office

Private Sector
- Three (3) from Business Sector
- Philippine Nikkei Jintokai, Incorporated
- Two (2) from Civic Organization

SECTION 7. TERM OF OFFICE. - The term of office of the Members of the Executive Committee shall be co-terminous with the City Mayor.

SECTION 8. FUNCTIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES. - The Davao City Japanese Heritage Site Development Council shall be tasked with the following functions and responsibilities to wit:

a. Plan, conceptualize and draft the Davao City Japanese Heritage Site Development Plan in the context of ensuring the sustainable development of the Site;
b. Identify the development goals, objectives, programs and projects of the Japanese Heritage Site Development Plan, and conduct feasibility studies;
c. Provide overall direction, coordination and supervision in the planning and implementation of the Davao City Japanese Heritage Site Development Plan;
d. Enlist the support of the different divisions, departments, branches and bureaus of the City of Davao, as well as Non-Government Organizations (NGOs) and private sector components of the private sector involved with the development of the Site;
e. Define and promulgate its Implementing Rules and Regulations for the effective administration of its internal operations;
f. Conduct itself and its operations in a manner consistent with national and local laws, as well as the policies and programs of the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 9. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS. - The Implementing Rules and Regulations of this Ordinance shall be formulated by the Davao City Japanese Heritage Site Development Council, through detailed consultations with the Government, business, academic and civic society representatives. The IRR shall be formulated within ninety (90) days after the effectiveness of this Ordinance.

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. The provisions of this Ordinance are separable, and in the event that any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 11. REPEALING CLAUSE. - Any Ordinance, Executive Orders and Local Laws or Rules and Regulations or part thereof, inconsistent with or in conflict with this Ordinance are hereby repealed or altered accordingly.

SECTION 12. EFFECTIVITY. - The Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.

ENACTED, June 16, 2009, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
