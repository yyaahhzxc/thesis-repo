**ORDINANCE NO. 053-13**  
**Series of 2013**

**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A HOSTEL AT THE DAVAO CITY OVERLAND TRANSPORT TERMINAL (DCOTT) PRESCRIBING ITS RENTAL RATES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

**SECTION 1. AUTHORIZATION AND ESTABLISHMENT**  
The City Government of Davao, thru the Office of the City Administrator, is hereby authorized to establish and operate a Hostel at Davao City Overland Transport Terminal (DCOTT) for the benefit of transient passengers or those waiting for the availability or departure of buses to their places of destination;

--- [Page 4] ---
**SECTION 2. DAILY RENTAL RATE** - The daily rental rate of the Hostel shall be TWO HUNDRED PESOS (P200.00) per person, computed from the time of registration until twelve noon (12:00 N.M.) of the following day, payable in advance with the DCOTT Unit of the Office of the Treasurer (DCOTT-CTO).

**SECTION 3. ADDITIONAL CHARGES** - Each renter shall only be allowed to bring with him/her one (1) piece of luggage and an additional Twenty Pesos (P20.00) shall be charged for each piece of excess luggage deposited with the Hostel management for safekeeping, similarly computed from the time of registration until twelve noon (12:00 N.M.) of the following day and payable in advance with the DCOTT-CTO.

**SECTION 4. ADMINISTRATION** - The DCOTT Administration Office (DAO) shall oversee the day-to-day operations of the Hostel; however, policies and guidelines affecting such operations shall continue to be the prerogative of and subject to the approval of the Davao City Bus Terminal Board (DCETB) in accordance with Ordinance No. 63, Series of 1985.

**SECTION 5. OPERATION** - The Hostel shall be open for 24 hours progressively from its first day of operations, including Saturdays, Sundays, and Holidays;

**SECTION 6. PERSONNEL** - The Hostel shall be manned by available plantilla personnel from the City Government and such number of other officers as may be required for operations. Funding for wages and other necessary expenses shall be made available and chargeable to the budgetary allocation of the DCOTT.

**SECTION 7. REPORTORIAL REQUIREMENTS** - The Davao City Bus Terminal Board (DCETB) shall submit to the City Mayor the implementing Rules and Regulations for Hostel management and operation for approval.

**SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE** - All other Ordinances or any part thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby deemed repealed, modified or amended accordingly.

**SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

**ENACTED** - October 29, 2013, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.

CERTIFIED CORRECT.
