ORDINANCE NO. 066-98/15/04  
Series of 2008  
AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL PRIVATE  
ESTABLISHMENTS OPERATING AND/OR DOING  
BUSINESS WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION  
OF THE CITY OF DAVAO TO PROVIDE ALL THEIR  
FEMALE EMPLOYEES/WORKERS DURING WORKING  
HOURS WITHIN THE CITY LIMITS AND ALLOW  
THEM TO USE THE SAME DURING THE PERFORMANCE  
OF THEIR WORK AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR  
VIOLATIONS THEREOF, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES  
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session  
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL PRIVATE ESTABLISHMENTS OPERATING AND/OR DOING BUSINESS WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF THE CITY OF DAVAO TO PROVIDE ALL THEIR FEMALE EMPLOYEES/WORKERS DOING WORK IN THEIR ESTABLISHMENTS WITH SEATS FOR USE DURING THE PERFORMANCE OF THEIR WORK AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS HEREOF, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: The policies of the Local Government of Davao City underlying the enactment of this Ordinance are hereby declared as follows:

1. To initiate, give meaning to, and set standards that will ensure the safety and health of women employees and workers in the light of Article 152 of the Labor Code.
2. To achieve better working conditions for women labor as part of reproductive health care approach at all levels of health care delivery, in relation to family planning and child bearing as cited in Section 47, Article V of the Women Development Code of Davao City;
3. To recognize the role of women in nation-building and to ensure equality before the law at all times of men or women as enunciated in Section 14, Article II of the Philippine Constitution;
4. To reinforce the mechanisms to protect working women by providing safe and healthy working conditions, taking into account their maternal functions and such facilities and opportunities that will enhance their welfare and enable them to realize their full potential in the service of the Nation as stated in Section 14, Article XIII of the Constitution;
5. To enhance and ensure the development of the health of women, which is hereby declared as a priority objective in consonance with Section 15, Article II of the Constitution;

SECTION 3. IMPLEMENTING GUIDELINES

1. The employer shall provide seats for their female employees while on duty or during prescribed working hours, and while in the performance of their work, provided that the same shall not affect, and will not compromise the worker's efficiency in performing their duties. In establishments where there is one chair shall be provided for every five (5) female employees or workers, and in the case of establishments whose number of female workers is below five (5), one (1) chair shall be provided to them;

2. The employer shall not deprive their female workers to take a seat and be seated at a reasonable time while in the performance of their work or duty, provided that, the same shall not compromise the employer's interest to provide efficient quality service to their clients or customers;

3. The employer shall not reprimand, suspend or discharge its/its employees, whether male or female, for having testified, or prevent any of its employees from testifying, if such testimony is given by any female employee or worker against the employer for any violation of the provisions of this Ordinance.

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS: For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms are hereinafter defined, to wit:

1. Female Employee/Worker - includes any females employed by an employer. It shall also apply to any female working within the business premises of an employer, other hired directly by the latter or supplied by an agency or directly contracted by the establishment from said agency.

2. Employer - Any person or entity who employs the services of others, or one for whom employees work and pays their wages or salaries. It includes private business establishments, public service establishments such as, but not limited to, mails, stores, pharmacies, factories, restaurants, hotels, inns and the like in whose premises the female employee or female worker as above defined, is assigned or works for;

3. Private sector/ Entity - Any person, establishment, company or entity engaged in any activity or business, whether for profit or not, other than the National Government, its agencies, its political subdivisions, or any Government Owned and Controlled Corporation;

SECTION 5. PROVISIONS FOR IMPLEMENTATION

1. The Integrated Gender Development Office (IGDO) of the City, created and tasked to implement the Women Development Code of Davao City and to act as the coordinating, regulatory and monitoring body of the City Government for the realization of programs related to women's rights and empowerment, hereby declares that the provisions of this Ordinance shall be strictly implemented and complied with by all private establishments operating within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City.

2. For this, the said office is mandated to perform the following functions, to wit:

a. To submit an annual report to the Office of the City Mayor and to the Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod of the City of Davao relative to the status of the implementation of this Ordinance;

b. To receive complaints in any form from any female employee or worker against her employer for any violation under this Ordinance and to investigate the same and make recommendations to the Office of the City Mayor and Sangguniang Panlungsod;

c. To promulgate rules and regulations, if necessary for the effective implementation of this Ordinance, in consultation with the representatives of the employers in the private sector and the female employees/ workers' association.

SECTION 6. PENAL PROVISIONS: Any employee who violates the provisions of this Ordinance shall, upon conviction, be penalized by a fine of not less than One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) nor more than Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00), or by imprisonment of not less than one (1) month nor more than six (6) months, or both such fine and imprisonment at the discretion of the court in the light of Section 438, Article III of the Local Government Code;

If the violation is committed by a juridical entity, the President, General Manager, or the individual entrusted with the administration thereof at the time of the commission of the violation shall be held responsible or liable for the violation;

SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE: All ordinances, rules and regulations, or parts thereof in conflict with or inconsistent with any provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If any portion or provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining portions or provisions not affected thereby shall remain to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect after its publication in a local newspaper of general circulation once a week for three (3) consecutive weeks and posting in at least two (2) conspicuous places not later than five (5) days after approval thereof, all in accordance with Section 59 of the Local Government Code of 1991;

ENACTED, January 29, 2008, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
