Ord. No. 0179-11

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION FOR THE PROJECT ENTITLED, SA PAARALAN KO PWEDE NG MAGREHISTRO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Section 455 (b) (1) (a) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this code, the City Mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the City Government, and in this connection, shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf of all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT. The Memorandum of Agreement refers to the instrument to be entered into by the City Mayor, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, relative to the project "Sa Paaralan Ko Pwede Ng Magrehistro".

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Amended Memorandum of Agreement between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Education for the project entitled, "Sa Paaralan Ko Pwede Ng Magrehistro".

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other section or provision thereof shall be affected thereby.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, October 04, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement is executed by and between:
THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a municipal corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, herein represented by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, Filipino, of legal age, married and a resident of Davao City, hereinafter referred to as THE CITY.
and
THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION, a department of the National Government duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, herein represented by the School Division Superintendent DR. HILLEN D. PAGUCIAN, Filipino, of legal age, married and a resident of Davao City, hereinafter referred to as THE SCHOOL.

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 418 paragraph b of Republic Act No. 2160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, the City Civil Registrar is responsible in the implementation of civil registration programs in the local government unit in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Registry Law (Act 3/23), the New Family Code of the Philippines (E.O. 209) and other pertinent laws, rules and regulations;

WHEREAS, it is also the mandate of the City Civil Registrar to develop plans and strategies and implement the same upon the approval of the City Mayor, particularly those that have to do with civil registry programs and projects which the City Mayor is empowered to implement as provided under Section 419 par. c of RA 7166;

WHEREAS, the project has made civil registration available in 11 public schools in Davao City as identified in the Memorandum of Agreement entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Department of Education on February 28, 2011 in Davao City and acknowledged before Notary Public and entered in Docket No. 243, Page No. 57, Book No. 152 Series of 2011.
