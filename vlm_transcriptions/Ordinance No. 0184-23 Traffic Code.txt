AN ORDINANCE AMENDING APPENDIX 4 (PUBLIC PAY-PARKING ZONES) OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE COMPREHENSIVE TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORT CODE OF DAVAO CITY IN ORDER TO CONVERT THE PORTION OF GENERAL LUNA ST. (FRONTING DAVAO DENCIA'S RESTAURANT) TO BE A PAY-PARKING ZONE

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0184-23

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING APPENDIX 4 (PUBLIC PAY-PARKING ZONES) OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE COMPREHENSIVE TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORT CODE OF DAVAO CITY IN ORDER TO CONVERT THE PORTION OF GENERAL LUNA ST. (FRONTING DAVAO DENCIA'S RESTAURANT) TO BE A PAY-PARKING ZONE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - In accordance with the Local Government Code of 1991, it is the policy of the City Government of Davao to promote transportation and shall endeavor to be proactively involved in the planning and management of an efficient, safe, environment-friendly, affordable and people centered sustainable City traffic and transport system.

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - The amendment shall read as follows: (Those highlighted words are the inserted amendments.)

APPENDIX 4
PUBLIC PAY PARKING ZONES

1. Streets Designated as Public Pay Parking Zones

xxx
xxx
xxx

d. General Luna St. (Fronting Davao Dencia's Restaurant)

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance, local issuance or rule inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified, accordingly.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITATY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation.

ENACTED, on the 28th day of March 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head II)
