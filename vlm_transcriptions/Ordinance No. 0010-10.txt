**ORDINANCE NO. 010-10 Series of 2010**

**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ARTICLE 23-A RULE VII OF ORDINANCE NO. 229A, SERIES OF 1994, AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO. 4603A, SERIES OF 1997 AND ORDINANCE NO. 36, SERIES OF 1999, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE RULES AND REGULATIONS IMPLEMENTING THE DAVAO CITY INVESTMENT INCENTIVE CODE AS AMENDED, AND FOR RELATED PURPOSES**

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

**SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance amending Article 23-A Rule VII of Ordinance No. 229A, Series of 1994, as amended by Ordinance No. 4603A, Series of 1997 and Ordinance No. 36, Series of 1999, otherwise known as the Rules and Regulations Implementing the Davao City Investment Incentive Code as Amended, and For Related Purposes;"**

--- [Page 3] ---
**Page 2 of 2**

**Order No. 010-10**

**SECTION 2. AMENDMENT. - Article 23-A Rule VII of Ordinance No. 2269-A, Series of 1994 as amended is hereby further amended to read as follows:**

*Article 23-A. DAVAO CITY CENTER FOR INVESTMENT PROMOTIONS (The Center). There shall be created an office known as the Davao City Center for Investment Promotions, which shall be staffed by one (1) Economist IV, three (3) Economist III, one (1) Economist II, one (1) Economist I, and one (1) Economic Researcher.

The center shall be attached as a regular division in the Office of the City Mayor.*

**SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE. - Any previous Ordinance or parts thereof, executive orders and other issuances, which are inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed or superseded accordingly;**

**SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY. - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.**

ENACTED, September 14, 2010, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod
