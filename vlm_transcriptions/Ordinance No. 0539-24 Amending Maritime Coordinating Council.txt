Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0362-17, SERIES OF 2017, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE MARITIME COORDINATING COUNCIL (MCC) OF DAVAO CITY, AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO 0116-19, SERIES OF 2019, TO INCLUDE UNDER SECTION 4 A PROVISION TO INCLUDE THE DAVAO CITY MARITIME POLICE STATION AND TO AMEND THE UNIT MANDATED AS THE VICE CHAIRPERSON OF THE MCC COMPOSITION;"  

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVE - This Ordinance aims to include potential members to be added in the Maritime Coordinating Council and make any other amendments deemed necessary.  

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - Section 4 (Composition of Maritime Council) of Ordinance No. 0362-17, Series of 2017, otherwise known as the "Maritime Coordinating Council of Davao City" shall be amended as follows:  

Section 4 (Power and Functions)  

The Coast Guard District, Southeastern Mindanao (CGDSEM) as the Vice Chairperson of the Council is hereby replaced with Coast Guard Station Davao (CGS); and the following agency/group shall be represented mainly:  

A. The Chairperson-City Mayor of Davao  
B. Vice Chairperson-Coast Guard Station Davao  
C. Secretariat-Public Safety and Security Command Office  
D. Members:  

1. Joint Task Force (JTF) Haribon;  
2. Davao City Police Office (DCPO);  
3. Regional Maritime Unit (RMU) XI;  
4. Naval Forces East of Eastern Mindanao (NFEM);  
5. Task Force Davao (TFD);  
6. Central 911;  
7. Tactical Operations Group XI;  
8. Philippine Ports Authority;  
9. Bureau of Customs XI;  
10. Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA) XI;  
11. National Intelligence Coordinating Agency XI;  
12. Legal and General Bureau;  
13. Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) XI;  
14. City Agriculturist's Office;  
15. Maritime Industry Authority XI;  
16. National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) XI;  
17. Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) XI;  
18. City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO);  
19. Bureau of Immigration XI;  
20. Peace and Public Safety Committee Chairperson of the Davao City Council;  
21. Davao Beach Hotel and Resort Owners Group;  
22. Davao Fisher Folks Group;

23. Chairperson, Committee on Environment and Natural Resources; and
24. Davao City Maritime Police Station.

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE.— All other Ordinances, local rules and regulations that are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.— If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY — This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 25th day of June 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
