ORDINANCE NO. 0440-10
Series of 2010
AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE ESTABLISHMENT
OF A CREMATORIUM AT THE DAVAO CHINESE
CEMETERY AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A CREMATORIUM
AT THE DAVAO CHINESE CEMETERY AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES;

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 2
Ord. No. 0440-10

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The establishment of a crematorium at the Davao Chinese Cemetery in Bacaca Road, Davao City, is hereby authorized subject to the approval of the appropriate regulatory bodies of the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval;

ENACTED, June 18, 2010, by majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian.
