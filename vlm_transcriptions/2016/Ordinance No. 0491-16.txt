ORDINANCE NO. 0491-16  
Series of 2016  

AN ORDINANCE SPECIFYING AND ALLOCATING THE TAX  
SHARING ON THE REMAINING SEVENTY PERCENT (70%) OF  
THE LOCAL BUSINESS TAX OF THERMA SOUTH INC.  
BETWEEN DAVAO CITY AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. CRUZ, DAVAO DEL SUR, AT 60%-40% IN FAVOR OF  
DAVAO CITY  

Be it ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE  
SPECIFYING AND ALLOCATING THE TAX SHARING ON THE REMAINING  
SEVENTY PERCENT (70%) OF THE LOCAL BUSINESS TAX OF THERMA SOUTH INC., BETWEEN DAVAO CITY AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. CRUZ, DAVAO DEL SUR, AT 60%-40% IN FAVOR OF DAVAO CITY";

**SECTION 2: DECLARATION OF POLICY**
Section 16. General Welfare
Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for an efficient and effective government and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare.

**SECTION 3: ALLOCATION OF TAX SHARING**
THE TAX SHARING ON THE REMAINING SEVENTY PERCENT (70%) OF THE LOCAL BUSINESS TAX OF THERMA SOUTH INC. BETWEEN DAVAO CITY AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. CRUZ, DAVAO DEL SUR, WILL BE 60% - 40% IN FAVOR OF DAVAO CITY.

**SECTION 4: SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5: EFFECTIVITY**
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED, February 2, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.**
