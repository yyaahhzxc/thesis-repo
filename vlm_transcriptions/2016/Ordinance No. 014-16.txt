**AUGUST 17, 2016**

Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject Ordinance, informing your end that the grant of tax exemption is well within the powers of the Sanggunian Panlungsod citing the herein provision of RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, to quote:

"SECTION 192. Authority to Grant Tax Exemption Privileges. — Local government units may, through ordinances duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs under such terms and conditions as they may deem necessary."

Hence, it is recommended that the subject ordinance be approved.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF MINDANAO TRADE EXPO
FOUNDATION INC., FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF MAYOR'S
PERMIT FEE IMPOSED ON DAVAO-BASED MICRO, SMALL AND
MEDIUM ENTREPRENEURS DURING THE MINDANAO TRADE EXPO TO BE HELD ON
AUGUST 12-21, 2016 AT ABREZZA AYALA MALL, THIS CITY";
SECTION 2. COVERAGE. The exemption applies only to the Davao-
based exhibitors and solely refers to the payment of Mayor's Permit Fee under
Article 26, Section 221 paragraph (d) of the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of
Davao;
SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION. The exemption shall be from
the date of this Ordinance up to August 21, 2016 only; and that in no case
shall the organizers be exempted from compliance with the other provisions of
the "2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao" and other applicable laws and
ordinances.
SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval.
ENACTED, August 9, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
