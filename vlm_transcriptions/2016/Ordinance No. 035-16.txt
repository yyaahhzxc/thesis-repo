Ord. No. 035-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FON AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND DAPSA FISHERFOLK ASSOCIATION (DAFA), FOR THE "FISHERY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM OF MUNICIPAL FISHERY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT" UNDER THE BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING PROGRAMS FOR CY 2014";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code, provides to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in for and in behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and Dapsa Fisherfolk Association (DAFA), Barangay 76-A, Davao City, for the Fishery Resource Management Program of Municipal Fishery Development Project under the Bottoms-up Budgeting Programs for CY 2014;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, September 13, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

For and in the absence of the Secretary:

Nilda C. Lagno
Acting Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod

DEED OF DONATION

ONE (1) UNIT 7.5 H.P. MOTORIZED 16 FEET X 24"X19" BANCA
5 UNITS NON MOTORIZED 12 FEET X 18"X14" BANCA AND
WEIGHING SCALE, MANUAL 10 KLS. CAPACITY

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS :

This deed, made and executed in the City of Davao, Philippines, by and between:

The City Government of Davao-Davao City, an LGU of the Republic of the Philippines with and
office address at San Pedro St., Davao City, represented by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE
and hereinafter referred to as DONOR;

and

The DAPSA FISHERFOLK ASSOCIATION (DAFA) located at Brgy. 76-A, Davao City, represented by
its President, LOPE N. ZALOS, hereinafter referred to as DONEE;

WITNESSETH

1. That the DONOR is the owner of 1 unit 7.5 H.P. motorized 16 feet x 24" x 19" banca; 5 units
12x18"x14" non-motorized banca and weighing scale amounting to Ninety four thousand
four hundred fifty (Php 94,450.00), which is more particularly described as follows:
1 unit motorized 16"x24"x19" banca with serial no. WM 150B0000250
(King Eagle brand)
5 units non-motorized 12"x18"x14"
1 unit weighing scale 10 kls. Capacity
@ Php 43,250.00
@ Php 10,000.00 each
@ Php 1,200.00

2. That the DONEE will use the aforesaid property under the project of the DONOR entitled:
"Fishery Resource Management Program" Municipal Fishery Development Project under
Bottom-Up Budgeting CY 2014.

3. That the DONOR, by obligatory authority, helps the DONEE by way of donating the above-
described property, thus, DONOR hereby transferand conveys, by way of donation, unto
the DONEE said property for the purpose of increasing fish productivity, increasing income of
Fishersfolk, free and clear from all liens and encumbrances and subject to terms and conditions
provided hereunder:

a. To safeguard the property subject of donation, the DONEE must insure the donated
property and shall shoulder all license fees and taxes that will be imposed on the use and
operation of the same;
b. The DONEE must utilize the donated property in accordance to its intended use and shall not
in any way sell, lease, mortgage nor subject the same to all forms of encumbrances nor
liens;

--- [Page 14] ---
c. In order to optimize the useful life of the property subject of donation, the DONOR, through its project monitoring team shall monitor the use of the donated property within the period of five (5) years from the date of the DONEE's acceptance of the donation;
d. That upon acceptance of the donation, the donated property will become the property of the DONEE and not of any of the DONEE's officers, members, heirs and assigns;
e. That the DONOR agrees that the DONEE will be responsible for the maintenance and repair of the donated property and that it may be the DONEE shall respect and honor all the terms and conditions of this Deed of Donation;
f. The DONEE must maintain a complete and detailed logbook of the donated property's operation including subsidiary ledger and, whenever allowed, request the DONOR's project Monitoring team and the COA Auditors (who are duly authorized to perform vistorial auditing to access the same);
g. That the DONEE shall accept to the peaceful return and surrender of ownership and possession of the donated property in case of non-compliance with any of the terms and conditions herein stipulated whereason paid DONEE must also pay/refund the DONOR for all the expenses of this Deed of Donation;
h. The DONEE shall likewise pay/refund the DONOR for all the expenses of this Deed of Donation in case of the dissolution of the former; and
i. The property donated herein shall automatically revert to the DONOR should the purpose for which the donation is made would cease to exist;
4. That the DONEE hereby accepts the foregoing donation, subject to all terms and conditions set forth herin, and at the same time expresses its profound gratitude for this act of liberality on the part of the DONOR, who by these presents also takes notice of acceptance.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have set their hand at the place first above written, on this day of 2016.
