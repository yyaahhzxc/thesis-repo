Ord. No. 0495-16  

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH), THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT (DILG) AND CITY GOVERNMENT OF THE DAVAO FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE SPECIAL LOCAL ROAD FUND (SLRF) UNDER R.A. 8794 (MVUC LAW) FOR ROAD SAFETY PROJECTS AND TRAFFIC SERVICES MAINTENANCE";  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b)(1) (vii) of the Local Government Code, provides to wit:  

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf any agreement, contract and obligation and submit other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".  

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT - This is an agreement among the DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH), the DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT (DILG) AND CITY GOVERNMENT OF THE DAVAO for the implementation of the Special Local Road Fund (SLRF) under R.A. 8794 or MVUC Law. This agreement pertains to road safety projects and traffic services maintenance;  

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and among the DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH), THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT (DILG) AND CITY GOVERNMENT OF THE DAVAO for the implementation of the Special Local Road Fund (SLRF) under R.A. 8794 or MVUC Law;  

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration continue to be in full force and effect;  

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

WHEREAS, pursuant to R.A. 8794, the Motor Vehicle User's Charge (MVUC) Law, under Section 1 which declares that "it is the policy of the State to provide for and ensure the adequate maintenance of national and provincial roads through sufficient funding for the purpose";

WHEREAS, Section 7 of the said Act, which states that "all monies collected under this Act shall be earmarked solely and used exclusively (1) for road maintenance and improvement of road drainage, (2) for the installation of adequate and efficient traffic lights and road safety devices, and (3) for air pollution control." It further provides that the funds collected shall be deposited in the Special Local Road Fund (SLRF) under the Special Local Road Fund (SLRF) which will be apportioned to provincial and city governments in accordance with the vehicle population and size of the road network under their respective jurisdictions and shall be used exclusively for the maintenance of local roads, traffic management and road safety devices. It also directs that the Special Road Improvement Fund, the Special Roads Local Funds and Special Road Safety Fund shall be under the DPWH;

WHEREAS, pursuant to Sections 3 and 5 of Executive Order No. 124 dated 30 January 1987, the DPWH shall decentralize its responsibilities to the fullest extent possible; and may delegate to any agency it determines to have adequate technical capability, any of its powers and functions. Consistent with this policy of decentralization, the DPWH shall assist the LGUs in developing the latter's capabilities by allowing them greater participation in the local road maintenance activities undertaken by LGUs;

WHEREAS, the DILG is an agency mandated to exercise general supervision over the Local Government Units (LGUs) and is tasked to strengthen the capability of LGUs in the delivery of devolved services relating to the maintenance and construction of local roads and to coordinate the activities undertaken by LGUs;

WHEREAS, the DPWH, in consideration of the DILG's mandate over LGUs has proposed that DILG extends its assistance to provinces and cities through the Special Local Road Fund for the maintenance of local roads;

**RELEASE/TRANSFER OF FUNDS:**
The following procedures shall be followed in the transfer of funds to LGUs:
1. DPWH-RO/DEO Office shall release the funds thru the issuance of Sub-Audit Advice (SAA) and NCO to the DPWH Regional Offices within one (1) week upon receipt of the SARO.
2. The DPWH-RO/DEO Office shall issue a Letter of Advice of Allocation (LAA) effecting the full transfer of allocations to the concerned LGU. There shall be no retention for engineering and administrative overhead for the DPWH. The LGUs will use the LAA as basis to proceed with procurement process.

3. Within one (1) week upon the approval of the MOA, the DPWH-RO shall release the cash advance in full to the LGUs. The LGU receipt of the funding check will be acknowledged through the issuance of Official Receipt (OR).

4. Final liquidation of cash advance shall be submitted by the LGU to the DPWH-RO within one (1) month upon final inspection of the projects. Any unexpended amount after the project completion shall be remitted to the account of the DPWH-DEO/RDO. The only allowed amount for retention is the bank maintaining balance for the ongoing projects. DPWH-DEO/RDO shall issue an Official Receipt (OR) to all funds returned by LGUs. The LGU shall be made fully accountable for all funds released by the DPWH.

5. Release of SRF to an LGU for new projects is subject to full liquidation prior to receipt of SLRF Cash Advances. LGUs unliquidated balances shall be refunded to the DPWH-RO/DEO Office within 15 days from the date of liquidation. All Liquidation Report of Expenditure shall be submitted to the Comptrollership and Financial Management Division (CFMD) of the DPWH-RO in the format required (see Annex A). Copy furnished the DILG-RO/DPOICO.

REPORT OF EXPENDITURE: Each LGU shall submit a Report of Expenditure in the required form (refer to annex A) at least once every six months. Upon receipt of the Report of Expenditure, the DPWH-RO/DEO Office shall issue an Official Receipt (OR) to DPWH-RO. Copy furnished the DILG-Regional Office through DILG Provincial Office. Any unexpended amount after the project completion shall be remitted to the DPWH-RO. The DPWH-RO shall issue an official receipt to the LGU remittance of the unused cash balance. The LGU shall be made fully accountable for all funds released to be by the DPWH.

**ARTICLE II: WORKING ARRANGEMENTS AND PROCEDURES FOR EXECUTING THIS AGREEMENT**

For the efficient and successful execution of this Agreement, the PARTIES hereby agree to undertake the following:

A. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF DPWH-RO/DEO:
1. Enter into a MOA with LGU and DILG-RO.
2. Upon receipt of the approved list of projects from LGU, the DILG (SAA) from DPWH Central office, the DPWH-RO to effect transfer of fund to the LGU through the issuance of Letter of Advice of Allocation (LAA).
3. Provide monthly status of fund releases and liquidations to DILG-RO.
4. Enter the approved projects into the accounting system of DPWH Road Specifications.
5. Conduct a final inspection of each of the completed projects together with DILG-RO and LGU shall and shall issue a Joint Certificate of Completion.

B. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DILG:
1. Inform Provincial and City Governments of the approval and corresponding release of SARO for their proposed projects.
2. Advise the LGUs to submit a list of projects with location and project cost corresponding to the approved allocation.
3. Coordinate with LGU, DPWH Field Offices, and LGUs for the smooth implementation of projects and help resolve issues and concerns on project implementation.
4. Provide technical assistance to LGUs and provide training for institutional capacity building on local road management.

C. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DPWH-RO/DEO:
1. Enter into a MOA with LGU and DPWH-RO/DEO.
2. Contract with LGU for the implementation of the approved projects.
3. Facilitate signing of MOA among DPWH, DILG, and
