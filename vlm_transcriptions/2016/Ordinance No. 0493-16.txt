Ord. No. 0493-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE SPECIFYING AND ALLOCATING THE TAX SHARING ON THE
REMAINING SEVENTY PERCENT (70%) OF THE LOCAL BUSINESS TAX
BEING PAID BY BLACK AND VEATCH (BVIP), CONTRACTOR/SERVICE
PROVIDER OF THERMA SOUTH INC. (TSIS'S) COAL PLANT LOCATED
BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. CRUZ,
DAVAO DEL SUR, AT 66.6% - 33.3% IN FAVOR OF DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16. General Welfare
Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those
necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or
incidental for its efficient and effective governance and those which are
essential to the promotion of the general welfare;

SECTION 3. ALLOCATION OF TAX SHARING - The tax sharing on
THE REMAINING SEVENTY PERCENT (70%) OF THE LOCAL BUSINESS TAX
BEING PAID BY BLACK & VEATCH (BVIP), CONTRACTOR/SERVICE
PROVIDER OF THERMA SOUTH INC. (TSIS'S) COAL PLANT LOCATED
BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. CRUZ,
DAVAO DEL SUR, SHALL BE 66.6% AND 33.3% IN FAVOR OF DAVAO CITY;
