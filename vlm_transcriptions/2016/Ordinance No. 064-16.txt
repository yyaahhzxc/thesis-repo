ORDINANCE NO. 064-16
Series of 2016

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF CHOCOLATE PROCESSING PROJECT IN BARANGAY MALABOG WITH MALABOG INTEGRATED ENTERPRISES DEVELOPMENT COOP. (MIEDCO), AS BENEFICIARY FUNDED BY THE BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2016

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF CHOCOLATE PROCESSING PROJECTS IN BARANGAY MALABOG, BARANGAY MALABOG INTEGRATED ENTERPRISES DEVELOPMENT COOP (MIEDCO) AS BENEFICIARY FUNDED BY THE BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2016";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.- Paragraph (b) (vi), Section 455, of Republic Act 7166, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY.- The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF CHOCOLATE PROCESSING PROJECTS IN BARANGAY MALABOG, BARANGAY MALABOG INTEGRATED ENTERPRISES DEVELOPMENT COOP (MIEDCO) AS BENEFICIARY FUNDED BY BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2016;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.- If any part of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid and in effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE.- This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, September 13, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement entered into by and between:

THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY, a government agency with office address at 361 Sert. Gil J. Puyat Avenue, Makati City and represented herein by its Regional Director for DTI Regional Office XI, MARIA BELENDA Q. AMBI, CESO V, and hereinafter referred to as "DTI".

-and-

The local government of the city of Davao, with office address at City Hall Drive, Davao City and represented herein by its Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, and hereinafter referred to as "Partner LGU".

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the DTI is identified as one of the participating agencies in the implementation of Bottom-Up Budgeting (BuB) Projects authorized under the General Appropriations Act for FY 2016;

WHEREAS, the partner LGU is identified as one of the recipient LGUs of the project that is included in the DTI's budget under approved project;

WHEREAS, DBJM-DILG-DSWD-NAPC Joint Memorandum Circular No. 7, dated 03 November 2015 provides the Policy Guidelines and Procedures in the Implementation of the Bottom-Up Budgeting Projects for FY 2016;

WHEREAS, Paragraph 5.4.2.1, JMC No. 7 states that Fund releases from implementing agencies to LGUs will be allowed if the beneficiary LGU has complied with the following governance conditions, in accordance with the pertinent special and general provisions in the GAA:

* Compliance with Good Financial Housekeeping component of the Seal of Good Local Governance (latest available assessment), and
* Proof of progress in the implementation of the LGU Public Financial Management (PFM) Improvement Plan, as assessed by the DBM Regional Office;

WHEREAS, Paragraph 5.11.2.1, of JMC likewise states that BuB projects, except those mentioned in Items 5.11.1, hereof, may be implemented by LGUs provided they meet the eligibility standards stated in Paragraph 5.4.2, subject to the government procurement law, and budgeting, accounting, auditing rules and regulations;

WHEREAS, partner LGUs has complied with the said governance conditions and has shown that it has the technical capabilities to implement the project. Attached hereto is the copy of the 2015 Seal of Good Housekeeping issued by the DILG, submission of the PFM Improvement Plan as determined by DBM Regional Office No. XI, completeness of the requirements for the implementation of the Project and the Resolution of the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City authorizing Mayor Sara Z. Duterte to enter into a MOA with all BuB participating agencies for their BuB Projects, Annexes A, B, C and D;

--- [Page 5] ---
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above stated premises, both parties herewith agree to undertake the herein commitments to implement approved BuB project, hereinafter referred to as the "Project", in accordance with the following terms, conditions and stipulations:

1. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTIES

A) RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DTI

1. Transfer to the LGU partner the sum of TWO MILLION PESOS ONLY (2,000,000.00) needed to implement the Project and transfer said amount to the LGU Partner in accordance with the attached work and financial/cash program. Provided, that said amount is inclusive of tax which shall be automatically withheld by DBM upon release of funds;

2. The said amount is broken down as follows:
- TWO MILLION PESOS (Php 2,000,000.00) for SHARED SERVICE FACILITY FOR CACAO PROCESSING at Brgy. Malabog, Paquibato District, Davao City;
3. Designate a BuB Focal person in the Regional Office to provide regular supervision and guidance towards the successful implementation and completion of the Project;
4. Submit quarterly progress reports through the online reporting system established by DBM on or before the following dates:
- April 21 - 1st quarter report
- July 21 - 2nd quarter report
- October 21 - 3rd quarter report
- January 21 - for the 4th quarter report and consolidated annual report of the current year;
5. Assist in the provision of technical assistance to the partner LGU and in the implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the Project (Paragraph 5.16 - use of the 3% M&E fund).

B) RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTNER LGU

1. Ensure that the design and cost of the Project are within the standard and do not exceed the cost of budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations are observed (Paragraph 5.5.4);
2. Receive the funds and ensure that the Project is implemented in accordance with approved project's work and financial plan;
3. Support the transportation and travel of CSO representatives to the LPRAT meetings, monitoring activities and other related activities (Paragraph 5.14.6);
4. Administer the disbursement of funds subject to accounting and auditing rules and regulations;
5. Submit progress reports and other documents as may be required by the DTI;
6. Designate a technical staff that will assist in the implementation of the Project;

--- [Page 6] ---
7. Conduct monitoring and evaluation of the Project in coordination with the DTI and complete the Project within the timelines as provided in the approved Project's work and financial plan;

8. Submit a liquidation report of the Project including the Audited Report of Disbursements or Statement of Receipts and Disbursements (SORD) signed by the City Accountant, COA, and the LCE.

II. OWNERSHIP OF MATERIALS & PROJECT OUTPUTS

1. After disbursement of fund as evidenced by SORD signed by the City Accountant, LCE and COA and upon submission of post activity report, ownership of the materials and project output shall be transferred to the responsibility of partner LGU and shall divest DTI of any liability relative thereto.

III. COMMON PROVISIONS

1. Attachments A to D shall be considered as integral parts of this MOA.

2. This Agreement for BuB Project may be revised or modified upon mutual consent, made in writing and signed by the parties.

3. The parties shall terminate the Agreement upon 10 days of notice on any of the following grounds:

- Situations/Circumstances would make it impossible for the Project to continue to be carried out;
- Breach of any of the terms of the Agreement; and
- Failure to perform or deliver by any of the parties a material portion of the agreed outputs or deliverables.

IV. EFFECTIVITY

The Agreement shall take effect upon the signing thereof by the parties and shall terminate after completion of the Project in accordance with the Work and Financial Plan.

**CERTIFICATION**

To Whom It May Concern,

This is to certify that the LGU of Davao City has technical capabilities to manage and implement the different projects funded by our office through the Bottom Up Budgeting based on the following set of criteria:

- There is a unit or office mandated or tasked to implement projects with at least one trained personnel and/or with experience in Shared Service Facility concerns dedicated to DTI BuB projects (please see attached LGU certification).
- The Local Government Unit of Davao City has a good track record of performance in project management and has been complementing with the DTI-Davao City Field Office in implementing various community-based trade and industry development initiatives.
- The LGU has the absorptive capacity to implement the project based on various previous engagements related to these projects.

This certification is being issued in connection with the implementation of the Shared Service Facility Project under the Bottom Up Budgeting.
