Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be referred to as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CERTAIN SECTIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 0873-22, SERIES OF 2022, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE CREATING PEACE 911 THE CENTER FOR PEACE BUILDING AS A DIVISION OF THE OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR" SUBJECT TO ALL LAWS AND EXISTING LEGAL RULES AND REGULATIONS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES - Memorandum Circulars and other issuances from the Department of Budget and Management, the Civil Service Commission, and other related laws that will affect the qualifications and functions of plantilla personnel, viz:

A. Section 22 of Republic Act 7160 provides:

"SECTION 22. Corporate Powers. -(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
1. To have continuous succession in its corporate name;
2. To sue and be sued;
3. To have and use a corporate seal;
4. To acquire and convey real or personal property;
5. To enter into contracts;
6. To exercise such other powers as are granted to corporations, subject to the limitations provided in this Code and other laws";

B. Section 455 of Republic Act 7160 provides:

"SECTION 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation. -
(a) The city mayor, as chief executive of the city government, shall exercise such powers and perform such duties and functions as provided by this Code and other laws.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

C. Department of Budget and Management (DBM) Memorandum Circular No. 2018-3, Series of 2018, dated July 30, 2018 states the purpose as "This Circular is issued to amend the Guidelines on the reclassification of positions as part of the mandate of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) to administer and 'maintain' the Compensation and Position Classification System (GPCS) in the government.";

**SECTION 5. AMENDMENTS TO SECTION 6 OF ORDINANCE NO. 0873-22, SERIES OF 2022.** - Section 6 "Functions and Qualifications of Plantilla Positions" stipulated in Ordinance No. 0873-22, Series of 2022, shall be amended.

**A. Section 8.C.1 shall be amended and restated to read as:**

**8.C.1 Administrative Officer IV (SG-15)**

**Qualifications:**

a. Bachelor's Degree relevant to the Job;
b. One (1) year relevant experience;
c. Four (4) hours relevant training;
d. Career Service (Professional) Second Level Eligibility.

**Functions:**

a. Supervises the administrative functions of the center relative to records management, personnel administration, procurement, supply and property utilization, budgeting, janitorial services, including maintenance and repair;
b. Plans, directs, and coordinates in the administration of fringe benefits and other employee services;
c. Oversees the training of the employees;
d. Undertakes the dissemination of latest issuances relative to the functions of the center;
e. Prepares necessary documents such as memoranda, emails, and reports for the Office-In-Charge;
f. Organizes and schedules appointments and meetings as approved by the Office-In-Charge;
g. Plans meetings and take detailed minutes;
h. Performs such other related functions that may, from time to time, be assigned by the immediate supervisor.

B. Section 8.C.2 shall be amended and restated to read as:

8.C.2 Administrative Aide IV (Clerk II) (SG-4)

Qualifications:
a. Completion of two (2) years studies in college;
b. No experience required;
c. No training required;
d. Career Service (Sub-professional) First Level Eligibility.

Functions:
a. Assists in performing frontline services to clients, receiving, recording, releasing and sorting new, renewal and special permit applications;
b. Scans permit files and attachments for easy reference and retrieval, creating metadata and uploading of e-records and files;
c. Assists in managing records management and endorses the same to other agencies;
d. Assists in drafting memoranda, emails, and reports needed by the Officer-In-Charge;
e. Assists in personnel administration;
f. Assists in procurement of products and services;
g. Assists in managing inventory of all office equipment and ensures its good condition;
h. Does other functions as may be directed by the immediate superiors.

C. Section 8.D.1 shall be amended and restated to read as:

8.D.1 Development Management Officer II (SG-15)

Qualifications:
a. Bachelor's Degree relevant to the job;
b. One (1) year relevant experience;
c. Four (4) hours relevant training;
d. Career Service (Professional) Second Level Eligibility.

Functions:
a. Formulates peacebuilding programs, trainings, and projects for Peace 911 focused areas;
b. Implements approved peace building programs, Projects, Programs and Activities (PPAs) and trainings and monitor the progress of the said PPAs and trainings;
c. Evaluates the development of the implemented plans, programs, projects and trainings and reports it to the Officer-in-Charge;
d. Improves existing PPAs and trainings and executes it with better program efficiency rates;
e. Collaborates with all the center's units, prepares annual budgetary requirements for the said PPAs and trainings and submit the same to the Division Head;
f. Does other related functions as may be directed by the immediate superiors.
