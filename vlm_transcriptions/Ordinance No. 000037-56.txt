**ORDINANCE NO. 27**

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE ESTABLISHMENT, MAINTENANCE AND OPERATION
OF BARS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.

By virtue of the powers granted to the City Council under Republic
Act No. 938, entitled "An Act Granting Municipal or City Boards and Commis-
sions the Power to Regulate the Establishment, Maintenance and Operation
of Certain Places of Amusement within their Respective Territorial Jurisdic-
tions," as amended by Republic Acts Nos. 979 and 1224;
Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - Definition
A "Bar" shall include any place or establishment whose principal business is the sale of alcoholic beverages
or liquors of any kind to be used or consumed within its premises. A bar that allows music or dancing within its premises shall be considered a night club, cabaret, dancing school, or dance hall, as the case may be, and shall be subject of the provisions of Ordinance No. 26 series of 1954.

SEC. 2. - Location
No bar shall be established within a radius of 100 linear meters from any public building, school, public library, church or hospital.

This provision as to location shall not apply to those already licensed and operating at the time of the approval of this Ordinance,
nor will the opening of any public building or other premises from which distance shall be measured prejudice any bar then licensed and operating:
Provided, however, that such bar established within 100 linear meters from any school, hospital, public library or church shall be so constructed, as approved by the City Engineer, that the noise coming therefrom shall not disturb those in the school, hospital or church, and, if such noise causes such disturbances then such bar shall not operate during school hours when near a school, or at night when near a hospital, or when there are religious services when near a church.

SEC. 3. - Building Requirements
Bars shall be well lighted at all time, leaving no dark corners and shall be maintained under good sanitary condition. There shall be no private rooms nor separate compartments.

SEC. 4. - Days and hours of operation. - No bar shall be open to the public except during the hours from 9:00 A. M. to 12:00 midnight daily, Provided, however, that on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve and on Saturdays, Sundays and days preceding official holidays, and the town fiesta, they may be open until 2:00 in the morning of the following day. In case a bar is also duly licensed to operate a regular restaurant, cafe, or refreshment parlor, or is operated in connection with a restaurant, cafe, or refreshment parlor, it may remain open before or after said hours to serve only meals, refreshments or non-intoxicating drinks: Provided, that any store, place, or establishment wherein alcoholic beverages or liquors of any kind are sold shall be considered a bar for the purposes of the limitation as to the days and hours of operation prescribed in this ordinance.

SEC. 5. - Restriction as to persons. - (a) Minors (persons under twenty-one years of age) shall not be admitted in bar employing hostesses. Intoxicated persons and persons carrying deadly weapons or firearms of any description except government officials performing their public function, shall not be admitted nor allowed to remain in any bar as customer, employee or under any capacity. (Amended by Ord. No. 1956)

(b) No woman shall be employed as hostess or waitress, in any bar unless she is at least twenty-one years of age and without first having obtained a written certificate from the City Health Officer that she is free from contagious or infectious diseases: Provided, That with the written consent of her parents or guardian a woman eighteen years of age, or more but below twenty-one years may be so employed. No hostess or waitress shall be allowed to continue working as such, upon discovery by the City Health Officer that she is suffering from any contagious or infectious disease or after conviction for any disorderly, immodest conduct, or violation of any provision of this Ordinance or Ordinance No. 26, series of 1954. The medical certificate required herein shall be obtained once every three months.

(c) No hostess, waitress, or any other female employee of a bar shall be allowed to remain within its premises after its closing hours.

SEC. 6. - Supervision: Bar shall be under the supervision of the City Mayor who is hereby charged with the enforcement of this Ordinance. Whenever public interest so requires, or at the request of the operator, the City Mayor may assign one or more policemen in any bar to maintain peace and order in or around its premises and enforce the provisions of this Ordinance and other city or sanitary regulations in the premises.

SEC. 7. - Requisites for the issuance of permits: Permits for the opening or operation of bars shall be issued by the City Mayor upon recommendation of the City Engineer and City Treasurer; Provided, That no permit for the opening of any bar shall be issued unless the applicant has complied with the provisions of this Ordinance.

SEC. 8. - Licenses: (a) In addition to the permit herein required, the operator shall obtain a license from the City Treasurer for the operation of a bar, which license shall be issued upon payment of the required license fee.

(b) The operator shall, before employing any waiter, waitress, or hostess, require each one of them to secure the necessary permit and/or health certificate and to produce evidence of payment of his or her license fee. In no case shall any waiter, waitress, or hostess be allowed to work in the aforesaid place of amusement unless he or she has provided himself with the papers herein required.

SEC. 9. - Complaints: Any person who believes that a bar is established or located in any place not authorized by this Ordinance, or is not operating or conducting said establishment in accordance with the provisions of this Ordinance, may file a protest with the City Mayor, who shall, after proper investigation, decide the case or cancel the permit and license.

SEC. 10. - Prices of drinks: The operator is required to post on a conspicuous place inside the hall a billboard showing the different kinds of drinks for sale and their respective prices for the information of the customers.

SEC. 11. - Revocation of permit: The City Mayor shall revoke

any permit or license granted under these regulations upon satisfactory evidence that gambling or playing of any prohibited game has taken or is taking place within the premises of any bar.

SEC. 12. Violation of the regulations: In case of violation of any of these regulations, the permit for the operation of a bar shall be withdrawn by the City Mayor and the license revoked. Such revocation of the permit and license shall operate to forfeit to the city all sums paid therefor.

SEC. 13. General Penal Provision: Any violation of this Ordinance shall be punished by a fine of not less than P100.00 but not more than P200.00 or by imprisonment of not more than 6 months or both such fine and imprisonment in the discretion of the court. If the violation is committed by a firm or corporation, the manager, managing director, or person charged with the management of the bar shall be criminally responsible therefor.

SEC. 14. This Ordinance shall take effect on and after the 10th day after its passage.
