**AN ORDINANCE FURTHER AMENDING THE DAVAO CITY INVESTMENT INCENTIVE CODE OF 1994, AS AMENDED**

---

Be it ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

**CHAPTER I. TITLE AND DECLARATIONS OF POLICY**

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "AN ORDINANCE FURTHER AMENDING THE DAVAO CITY INVESTMENT INCENTIVE CODE OF 1994, AS AMENDED".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY

To accelerate the sound development of Davao City in accordance with the City Comprehensive Development Plan (1996-2031) and the Philippines Development Plan (2011-2030), it is hereby declared to be the policy of the City of Davao to establish an investment climate that will encourage investments or expansion/diversification in Agri-business sector, Tourism and recreational facilities, Light Manufacturing and Assembly, Property Development, Health and Wellness, Water Supply and Sanitation, Food Processing, Environmental Protection or Green Projects, Information and Communications Technology, Production and Generation of New Sources of Energy, Transportation and Infrastructure, PPP Projects, and such other preferred areas of investments as may be deemed necessary. This policy shall be based upon the recommendation of an independent study group commissioned for the purpose.

The City of Davao welcomes and encourages domestic and foreign capital to establish enterprises that would utilize substantial amount of labor, raw materials and natural resources of the City.

It is further declared to be the policy of the City to promote the establishment and operation of non-government organizations to serve as active partners in achieving local autonomy.

SECTION 3. SCOPE OF APPLICATION

This Ordinance shall apply to all persons, juridical entities or enterprises, and non-government organizations and instrumentalities to the extent provided in the Code.

CHAPTER II. DEFINITION OF TERMS

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For the purpose of this Ordinance the following terms shall be construed to mean:

a. "Board" shall refer to the Davao City Investment Incentive Board (DCIIB) created under the Code;

b. "Center" shall mean the Davao City Investment Promotion Center, or in short, the DCPC, which is the office that will serve as the technical secretariat of the Board and its ancillary arm to implement the provisions of the Code;

c. "Code" shall refer to the Davao City Investment Incentive Code of 1994 promulgated pursuant to the Pangunangulang Pangunglaungan Resolution No. 6652 and Ordinance No. 2017 dated 13 May 1994 and approved by Mayor Rodolfo R. Duterte on June 15, 1994;

d. "Existing Establishments/Enterprises" refer to private business enterprises whose place of operation or production is presently located within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City;

e. "Expansion" shall include modernization and rehabilitation and shall mean increase of existing volume or value of production or upgrading the quality of the registered product;

f. "Local Personnel/Worker" refers to those workers or personnel who are residents of Davao City for at least six (6) months wherein proof of residency is through the presentation of a Barangay Clearance and/or the Community Tax Certificate from Davao City;

g. "Modernization" herein shall refer to any replacement or installation of machinery or the improvement of production processes, without or with techniques through the use of technological advances resulting in a substantive improvement in efficiency and/or quality, with or without any change in the rated capacity;

SECTION 7. APPOINTMENT OF PERMANENT REPRESENTATIVES
At the beginning of every year, members of the Board shall recommend the appointment of their permanent authorized representative by submitting to the Chairman a duly executed document designating therein the name of said representative. SECTION 8. MEETINGS AND QUORUM OF THE BOARD
The Board shall meet at least once every quarter unless otherwise provided for in this Code, on such a day as it may fix. The presence of at least a majority of its members shall constitute a quorum, and a majority of the quorum shall be required for the Board to exercise its powers and perform its functions.

SECTION 9. MANNER OF VOTING
In all meetings, regular members of the Board, on their absence, their duly appointed permanent representatives, are allowed to vote.

SECTION 10. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD
The Board shall be vested with the following powers and functions:

a. To review and approve qualified applications for the granting of fiscal incentives in accordance with the policies and guidelines provided for in this Code;

b. To commission an independent study group to conduct an economic and technical study for the purpose of identifying priority investment areas and/or activities to be promoted and to make appropriate investments and support measures which should be extended to attract investors into these areas and/or activities;

c. To adopt a short and medium-term investment promotion program which shall specify the list of priority investment areas and activities and corresponding incentives and support measures to be used to attract targeted investors as may be recommended by the Technical Secretariat;

d. To propose and recommend the necessary appropriations to the Sangguniang Panlungsang or secure additional funding from other sources in order to support the operations of the Davao City Investment Promotion Center to support the implementation of the Code;

e. To assist the assistance of national agencies and private sector organizations, as may be necessary, useful, and incidental to the effective and efficient implementation of this ordinance;

f. To establish trade and investment liaison offices in places to be determined by the Board as may be necessary to promote the City's business climate.

3) Light Manufacturing and Assembly
4) Property Development

9) Transportation and Infrastructure
10.) PPP Projects
SECTION 15. DETERMINATION OF ADDITIONAL INVESTMENT AREA Based on a result of a study conducted by an independent economic and technical research group commissioned to review the provisions of the Code, and in consultation with the Board, the Center may recommend investment areas for inclusion in the list of priority investment areas subject to approval by the Board in a meeting validly held shall then be adopted for implementation under the Dacca City Investment Code subject to the approval of the Sangamangal Puranagoodo.
Basis for inclusion of investment areas are:
1. It must generate high levels of employment;
2. It must feature a high degree of value-added;
3. It must create linkages with local industries;
4. It must not have deleterious effect on the environment.
SECTION 16. REMOVAL/DENIAL OF PREFERRED/PRIOITY INVESTMENT AREA - Upon the recommendation of the Center, the Board may, through a resolution, remove an area or activity from the list of preferred/priorities even when the removal is not within the approved of the Sangamangal Puranagoodo. Bases for the Removal of Preferred Investment Area are as follows:
1. Sufficient investments in the area or activity have been attained;
2. The continued extension of incentives or support measures for the specific investment area is no longer in the interest of Dacca City; and
3. The investment area or activity cannot sustain profitability within reasonable time and cost or may result in unforeseeable business climates.

CHAPTER VI. REGISTRATION OF ENTERPRISES
SECTION 17. QUALIFICATIONS OF A NEW ENTERPRISE
A. New enterprise which intends to avail of the incentives provided for in the Code must meet the following qualifications:
1. The new business enterprise must have complied with all the requirements mandated under existing local and national laws and the Constitution by and through presentation of certificates of registration issued by either the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) or Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) and other national government accrediting agencies, as the case may be.
2. The business enterprise place of operation or production and/or facility must be located within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City, provided said real property, where the place of operation, production and/or facility is located, has not been a subject of nor is a present subject of reclassification before the Enabling Presidential Decree;
3. The investment must be among the approved "preferred / priority areas of investments";
4. The investment must provide employment amongst local personnel/workers of Davao City as described under Section 4-G hereof;
5. The new proposed project of an enterprise must have a total project cost and direct employment of:
| Project Size | Project Cost | Employment Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small scale | P3-million to P 15-million | Not less than Ten (10) Persons |
| Medium-scale | Over P15-million to P100-million | At least Twenty (20) Persons |
| Large-scale | Over P100-million | At least One Hundred (100) persons |
Under exceptional cases and for justifiable reasons clearly cited, the Board may adjust the minimum employment requirement for certain projects.

2. The expansion must be among the approved "preferred/priority areas of investments";
3. The existing enterprise will undertake any of the following activities/projects:
a. Establish a branch within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City;
b. Expand its existing production capacity or construct new buildings and other civil works for the purpose of installation of new machinery and equipment or improvements thereof which will result in an increase in production capacity;
c. That the expansion shall have a total project cost and direct employment of:

**SECTION 19. APPLICATION PROCEDURE**  
All applicants must file with the Board through the Davao City Investment Promotion Center (DCIPC). The case shall be recorded in a registration book which date of official acceptance shall be reckoned from that date the proposals comply with all the application requirements as defined in this section and that the same are duly received by the Center.

Applications for incentives shall be acted upon by the Center within ten (10) working days from the official acceptance, and that the same shall be submitted to the Board with an additional ten (10) working days for their proper disposition. All applications will only be deemed approved or disapproved by the Board in a meeting duly held for that purpose.

CHAPTER VIII INCENTIVES PROVIDED TO REORGANIZED ENTERPRISES

SECTION 29. TAX INCENTIVES TO NEW PROJECTS - In addition to the incentives provided by law, the national government as well, as enterprises registered in the country shall be fully exempted from the payment of:
1. Business tax for a period of three (3) years starting from the date of the first sales activity as evidenced by the first invoice or official receipt, whichever comes first, issued by the business/enterprise; and/or
2. Basic real property tax of real properties used in the registered business, excluding borrowing share for two (2) years from the effectivity or accrual of the real property tax on the new tax declaration/assessment issued by the City Assessor's Office; or, starting from the date of the first sales activity as evidenced by the first sales invoice issued by the business/enterprise.

SECTION 24. TAX INCENTIVES TO EXPANSION PROJECTS - An expanding enterprise registered under the Code shall be exempt from the payment of:
1. Business tax increments (where assessment increments are solely brought about by the expansion or diversification) for a period of three (3) years starting from January of the succeeding year; and/or
2. Basic real property tax of real properties used in the registered business, excluding borrowing share for two (2) years from the effectivity or accrual of the real property tax on the new tax declaration/assessment issued by the City Assessor's Office.
Provided that the Board may reduce the period of incentive entitlement if the project under consideration does not meet the minimum employment requirement.

SECTION 26. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS - In order for enterprises to avail of the above incentives, the concerned projects/s must undergo certain events or activities specified in this Code, within six (6) months from the date of the issuance of their business permit. Investments in such preferred investment areas or activities whose period of operations is already beyond the above prescribed period shall no longer be qualified to apply for incentives.

Furthermore, the City Treasurer's Office is tasked to provide the Board with the list and amount of incentives that every project has enjoyed during the exemption period, as may be requested.

CHAPTER II. APPROPRIATIONS

SECTION 27. APPROPRIATIONS - To defray the expenses for Personal Services (P.S.), Monthly Operating and Overhead Expenses (MOOE), and Capital/Equipment charges necessary for the implementation of projects provided for in this Ordinance, funds shall be sourced through a Supplemental Budget from the General Fund and/or from the 20% Annual Development Fund. Appropriations for the succeeding years shall be included in the regular annual budget. Likewise, the Board and the Technical Secretariat shall be provided with necessary appropriations for its use in carrying out the functions charged to the Office of the City Mayor, as Chairman of the Davao City Investment Intensive Board, in order to carry out its objectives and mandates.

CHAPTER X. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

SECTION 28. VESTORIAL POWER OF THE BOARD AND DCPC - The Board, the DCPC or any duly authorized member thereof may, at any time, during office hours, inspect the premises, books of accounts and records of any enterprise covered by this Code, require it to submit reports regularly on prescribed forms and act on violation of any provision in this Code. Provided, a written notification is served to the grantees, stating the date and nature of inspection and the name of the inspecting officer. The DCPC or any duly authorized member thereof, is not precluded from making unannounced visits as it may become necessary.

a. Amendment of Articles of Incorporation or By-Laws, Articles of Partnership, or Articles of Cooperation, within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of submission of the said amendments with the Securities and Exchange Commission or Cooperative Development Authority;

b. Change of Directors within thirty (30) calendar days from the change;

CHAPTER XL FINAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 30. SANCTIONS FOR LATE SUBMISSION OF REPORTORIAL REQUIREMENTS. For late submission of the reportorial requirements, every registered enterprise shall, for each preferred/priority area of investment, be fined in accordance with the following:

For non and/or late submission of quarterly reports under Section 29, and other reports under the same section:
a. 1st violation - P 100.00 per day until the requirement has been fully complied with;
b. 2nd violation - P 500.00 per day until the requirement has been fully complied with;
c. 3rd violation - P 1,000.00 per day until the requirement has been fully complied with;
d. 4th violation - cancellation and revocation of the certificate of registration.

In all instances, an enterprise is only allowed to comply and submit any report or document within a period of thirty (30) days, otherwise, its certificate of registration, shall be subject to revocation by the Board.

SECTION 31. PENAL CLAUSE. Any violation of the provisions of this Code, existing laws, ordinances, rules and regulations, shall be ground for the cancellation or revocation of the registration of the business and the withdrawal of all the incentives granted under the Code.

The Certificate of Registration of a Business Enterprise, as provided in the Code and these Rules, may be canceled or revoked due to the following:

a. Violation of the provisions of the Code and these Rules;
b. Violation of existing local and national laws, ordinances, rules and regulations;
c. Failure to commence actual project development within one (1) year from approval of registration.

SECTION 22. APPROVAL - All decisions made by the Board on the applications shall be final and executory.
