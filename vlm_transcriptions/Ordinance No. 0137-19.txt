ORDINANCE NO. 0137-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO WITH THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY RELATIVE TO THE TRANSFER OF FUNDS FROM THE CITY GOVERNMENT TOWARDS THE SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT OF THE HIGH PRIORITY BUS SYSTEM PROJECT OF THIS CITY

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0137-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY (DTI) AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE TRANSFER OF FUNDS TO THE CITY GOVERNMENT INTENDED FOR THE SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT OF THE HIGH PRIORITY BUS SYSTEM PROJECT OF THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (v) of the Local Government Code provides, to wit:

"Section 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and the City Government of Davao relative to the transfer of funds to the City Government intended for the Social Development of the High Priority Bus System Project of this City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section, or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 5, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement is entered into by and between:

THE DEPARTMENT OF TRADE AND INDUSTRY, a national government agency, existing under and by virtue of laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at 361 S. Gil J. Puyat Avenue, Makati City, represented herein by its Undersecretary of the Regional Operations Group, BLESLIA A. LANTAYONA, and its Regional Director for Region X1, MARIA BELENA Q. AMBI, CESO 111, hereinafter referred to as "DTI."

and

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Philippines with principal office address at the City Hall, Davao City and represented herein by Its Acting Mayor, SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "LGU."

WHEREAS, pursuant to the General Welfare Clause stated in Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991), every local government unit (LGU) is mandated to exercise its power for the promotion of the general welfare like the comfort and convenience of its inhabitants;

WHEREAS, Section 17 of the above-said law mandated that LGU shall likewise exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective provision of the basic services and facilities, and in the case of Cities, to provide adequate transportation facilities;

WHEREAS, with the rising demand for an efficient and effective public transportation within its territorial jurisdiction, the City of Davao addresses the challenge by providing a new mode of transportation to its inhabitants, through the High Priority Bus System (HPBS), herein referred to as the HPBS Project;

WHEREAS, the HPBS Project is Davao City's response to the National Government's flagship program which envisions a restructured, modern, well-managed and environmentally sustainable transport sector wherein the commuters get to their destinations quickly, safely and comfortably;

WHEREAS, considering the HPBS is a comprehensive system reform that will entirely change the public land transportation industry in Davao City, present public utility transport operators and drivers and their families will surely be affected by the reforms to be undertaken by the LGU as some of them might be absorbed into the new system while others will be displaced;

WHEREAS, it is the responsibility of the LGU to assist the displaced drivers and operators by providing them and their affected families new opportunities to enable them to have a decent, sufficient and sustainable livelihood through the establishment of new enterprise;

--- [Page 8] ---
WHHERAS, pursuant to book IV, Title X of Executive Order No 292, S. 1987 (Administrative Code of 1987), which refers to Trade and Industry, Chapter 1, Section 1 thereof declared as a policy of the State, that it shall develop a self- reliant and independent national economy effectively controlled by Filipinos. It recognizes the indispensable role of the private sector, encourages private enterprise, and to provides incentives to needed investments;
WHEREAS, the Department of Trade and Industry is empowered by law to formulate and implement policies, plans and programs relative to the development and promotion of trade, industry and investments, and to provide incentives to accelerate the formulation and growth of enterprise;
WHEREAS, in the discharge of its above-said mandate, the DTI proposed to the LGU the grant assistance for the Social Development Program of the HBPS Project;
WHEREAS, the LGU welcomes the project of the DTI to support the Social Development Program of the HBPS Project through DTI's Livelihood Support (Seeding) Program and Entrepreneurship Development Fund which shall be applied for the livelihood restoration of the displaced public utility drivers, operators and their families;
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual covenants herein stipulated, the PARTIES do hereby bind themselves and agree to the following:
ARTICLE I PROJECT BRIEF
1.1 The project, which will be known as the "Displaced Drivers-Operators (DD-Ops) Livelihood Restoration Program (LRP)," shall be pursued under the Livelihood Support (Seeding) Program and Entrepreneurship Development Fund, whereby the DTI provides financial assistance to DD-Ops under the LGU's Social Developments Program of the HBPS project.
1.2 The DD-Ops LRP shall be undertaken during the implementation of THE HBPS Project and once the identified DD-Ops are immediately willing to avail of the benefits under the LRP.
1.3 The DTI shall allocate the amount of ONE HUNDRED MILLION PESOS (P 100,000,000.00) from the Livelihood Support (Seeding) Fund to be utilized exclusively for the DD-Ops LRP.
1.4 Only the legitimate DD-Ops, as duly identified both the DTI and the LGU, shall be qualified as beneficiaries of the LRP. The Criteria and Procedure for Selecting Beneficiaries are herein attached as Annexes A & B, and are made as integral part hereof;
ARTICLE II ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
2.1 The DTI shall:

--- [Page 9] ---
2.11 Provide and release to the LGU the amount of ONE HUNDRED MILLION PESOS (P 1000,000,000.00) needed for the implementation of the DD-Ops LRP subject to government accounting and auditing laws and rules and regulations.

2.12 Assist in the provision of technical Assistance to the LGU and in the implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the LRP.

2.2 The LGU shall

2.21 Receive the funds and ensure that the project is implemented in accordance with the program work and financial plan which are attached herein as Annex C is made as integral part hereof.

2.22 Administer the disbursement of funds subject to accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

2.23 Designate a dedicated technical staff that will assist in the implementation of the DD-Ops LRP.

2.24 Conduct monitoring and evaluation of the project in coordination with the DTI.

2.25 Submit progress reports, liquidation reports and other documents to DTI on monthly basis.

ARTICLE III EFFECTIVITY

This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing hereof by the parties and shall remain effective unless otherwise terminated or upon satisfaction of the objectives by which this Agreement has been forged.

ARTICLE IV GENERAL PROVISIONS

4.1 Disbursement and transfer of funds must strictly be in accordance with applicable government laws and rules on accounting and auditing. The concerned officials of both agencies shall ensure that the transfer is properly taken up in the books of both agencies; that the transferred funds are used only for the intended purpose; and that proper accounting and reporting is made of the utilization of the funds in accordance with COA Circular No. 94-013 dated December 13, 1994 and other applicable COA issuances.

Any unutilized amount of the transferred funds shall be returned to the DTI upon completion of the project.

4.2 Nothing herein is intended nor shall be construed as creating any exclusive arrangement among the parties. This Agreement shall not restrict any party from entering into any similar, equal or like agreements with other persons or entities.

4.3 The parties undertake to act in good faith with respect to each other's rights and obligations under this agreement and to adopt all reasonable measures to ensure the

realization of the objectives of this agreement promptly within the specified timelines and to ensure proper utilization of the project fund.

4.4 In the performance of their obligations under this agreement, the parties shall ensure the privacy and security of any and all confidential, privileged, personal, and/or sensitive personal information that the parties and their officers, employees, or agents may have in connection with the performance of their obligations hereunder in accordance with Republic Act 10173 or the "Data Privacy Act of 2012", its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances. Any violation of this clause and any of the provisions of the said law and issuances committed by the aforementioned persons shall be subject to corresponding sanctions, penalties, and/or fines under the said law without prejudice to any other applicable civil and/or criminal liability. This clause shall survive the termination or expiration of this agreement.

4.5 In the event of any dispute arising out of or related to this agreement, the same shall be settled amicably by mutual consultation between the parties. In case the parties fail to amicably settle their dispute, the same shall be resolved through the alternative modes of dispute resolution pursuant to RA 9285 or the "Alternative Dispute Resolution Act of 2004."

4.6 This agreement may be revised, amended or modified only through a written instrument duly executed and signed by all parties hereto and executed with the same formality as this agreement.

4.7 This agreement/contract must not be in conflict with existing laws, legal orders, procedures or rules and regulations, otherwise the pertinent provision(s) violating or conflicting with the same will be void. If any provision of this agreement/contract is held invalid or declared contrary to law, the validity of the other conditions or provisions shall not be affected.

ANNEX A

CRITERIA FOR BENEFICIARIES

Each operator and driver and/or per household are entitled to only one (1) slot of Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program.

1. For public utility vehicle operators:

1.a. Must suit the following necessary qualifications as beneficiary of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project: 1.a.a. Owner of the unit by virtue of franchise and deed of sale; 1.a.b. Registered at Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board Region XI; 1.a.c. At least one (1) year of operator service.

1.b. Must have voluntarily chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.

1.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.

2. For public utility vehicle drivers:

2.a. Must suit the following necessary qualifications as beneficiary of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project: 2.a.a. Registered at Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board Region XI and City Transport and Traffic Management Office; 2.a.b. Employed and declared by operator and/or cooperative; 2.a.c. At least one (1) year of driver service.

2.b. Must have voluntarily chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.

2.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.

3. For dependents of public utility vehicle drivers and operators:

3.a. Must be related up to fourth civil degree of affinity to public utility vehicle drivers and operators.

3.b. Must have voluntarily chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.

3.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.

ANNEX B

PROCEDURES OF SELECTING BENEFICIARIES

Procedure 1. The qualified beneficiaries under Annex A shall comply the following necessary documents:

1.a. Filled up Social Assistance Package (SAP) Form

1.b. Filled up Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) Livelihood Restoration Program (LRP) Form

1.c. Driver's License

1.d. Certificate of Public Convenience (CPC)

1.e. Other attachments to be determined and required by the Department of Trade and Industry.

Procedure 2. The documents complied by the beneficiaries under Procedure 1 shall be assessed and reviewed by the Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.

2.a. If complete, documents will be made ready for submission and recommendation to the Department of Trade and Industry.

2.b. If incomplete, attention of the applicant will be called for necessary clarification.

Procedure 3. The Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project shall submit the lists of the successful beneficiaries entitled of the Livelihood Restoration Program (LRP).

ANNEX C

PROGRAM OF WORKS FOR THE DISPLACED DRIVERS-OPERATORS (DD-OPS) LIVELIHOOD RESTORATION PROGRAM (LRP)

Project Objectives:

1. To support the City Government of Davao in the implementation of the High Priority Bus System (HPBS) project which aims to modernize public transportation and render convenience to public commuters thus the displacement of public utility vehicle drivers and operators which the provision of Livelihood Restoration Program of the Department of Trade and Industry is of necessity.

2. To provide basic entrepreneurial mind-setting skills through the conduct of offtraining workshops to the displaced public utility vehicle drivers and operators in order for them to become entrepreneurs integral upon and after full implementation of the project.

3. To provide livelihood seeding program to the displaced public utility vehicle drivers and operators through provisions of starter kit packages for them to earn an income and augment their basic needs integral upon and after full implementation of the project.

Amount Downloaded to Davao City: Php 100,000,000.00
Fund Source: DTI General Appropriations Act (CY 2019)
Target Implementation: 2020

Project Description Justification
DISPLACED DRIVER- OPERATORS LIVELIHOOD RESTORATION PROGRAM One of Davao City's visions is to become "A model city for the Philippines that has a safe and sustainable mode of transportation which enhances the livability and improved connectivity." To achieve the growth and development in the city, it is envisioned that transportation in the city should be a complete and integrated system, facilitating the movement both of people and goods.
In this regard, Davao City will engage the services of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) to support the Displaced Public Utility Vehicle Drivers and Operators, in sum, are the following:
(a) Guide its planning, programming, and implementation strategies to enable it to achieve its visions and goals, which among others include, the provisions of entrepreneurship skills to the displaced Public Utility Vehicle drivers and
(b) Provide livelihood seeding program to the displaced Public Utility Vehicle drivers and operators through provisions of starter kit packages for them to earn an income and augment their basic needs integral upon and after full implementation of the project.
Davao City has been the recipient of various study grants through the years and it has been the focus city in several international studies identifying it as one of the important metropolitan areas of the Philippines.
With the help of these services from the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) specifically the Livelihood Restoration Program (LRP), the Displaced drivers/operators (DD-OPS) shall have skills related to business and obtain equipment according to their preferred corporate in order to

ANNEX A

CRITERIA FOR BENEFICIARIES

Each operator and driver and/or per household are entitled to only one (1) slot of Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program.

1. For public utility vehicle operators:

1.a. Must suit the following necessary qualifications as beneficiary of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project: 1.a.a. Owner of the unit by virtue of franchise and deed of sale; 1.b.b. Registered as Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board Region XI; 1.c. At least one (1) year of operator service.

1.b. Must have voluntarily chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.

1.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.

2. For public utility vehicle drivers:

2.a. Must suit the following necessary qualifications as beneficiary of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project: 2.a.a. Owner of the unit by virtue of franchise and deed of sale; 2.b.b. Registered as Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board Region XI and City Transport and Traffic Management Office; 2.b.b. Employed and declared by operator or cooperative; 2.c. At least one (1) year of driver service.

2.b. Must have voluntarily chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.

2.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.

3. For dependents of public utility vehicle drivers and operators:

3.a. Must be related up to fourth civil degree of affinity to public utility vehicle drivers and operators.

3.b. Must have voluntarily chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.

3.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.

ANNEX C

PROGRAM OF WORKS FOR THE DISPLACED DRIVERS-OPERATORS (DD-OPS) LIVELIHOOD RESTORATION PROGRAM (LRP)

Project Objectives:

1. To support the City Government of Davao in the implementation of the High Priority Bus System (HPBIS) project which aims to modernize public transportation and render convenience to public commuters, thus mitigating the displacement of public utility vehicle drivers and operators which the provision of Livelihood Restoration Program of the Department of Trade and Industry is of necessity.

2. To provide basic entrepreneurial mind-setting skills through the conduct of training workshops for the displaced public utility vehicle drivers and operators in order for them to become entrepreneurs' integral upon and after full implementation of the project.

3. To provide livelihood seeding program to the displaced public utility vehicle drivers and operators through provisions of starter kit packages for them to earn an income and augment their basic needs integral upon and after full implementation of the project.
