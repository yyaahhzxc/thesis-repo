Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in  
session assembled that:

Ord. No. 085-08

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0195-04, SERIES OF 2004, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS 'THE PUBLIC UTILITY RECORCUTING SCHEME FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO' IN ORDER TO GRANT THE REQUEST OF ROUTE 10-B MULTI-PURPOSE COOPERATIVE FOR DEVELOPMENTAL ROUTE OF ROUTE 10*."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY:

a. Section 6 of Ordinance No. 0105-00, Series of 2000, amended by Ordinance No. 0195-04, Series of 2004, is hereby further amended pursuant to the recommendations of the Traffic Management and Control Board (TMCB), to include the adoption of Route 10-B Developmental Route, to read as follows:

ROUTE 10-B

GREGORI -Bankokhan Public Market
Turn Left -Dato Bago
Turn Left -Underpass
Turn Right -Fichon [Magallanes]
Turn Left -Recoto
Turn Right -Magallanes Avenue
Turn Left -Sales
Turn Left -Juan Lucas
Turn Right -Lizasda
Turn Right -Magallanes Avenue
Turn Left -Leon Garcia-through Agdao Overpass to Cabaguo Avenue
Turn Right -J. P. Laurel Avenue
Turn Right -Magallanes Avenue
Turn Left -San Pedro [Puncianan]
Turn Left -Roxas
Turn Right -San Pedro through San Pedro Extension
Turn Left -Maritor and back to origin;

SECTION 3. APPROVAL: This Ordinance grants the request of Route 10 Multi-Purpose Cooperative, for the passage of a legislative measure amending Ordinance No. 0195-04, Series of 2004, otherwise known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0195-04, SERIES OF 2004, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS 'THE PUBLIC UTILITY RECORCUTING SCHEME FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO' IN ORDER TO GRANT THE REQUEST OF ROUTE 10-B MULTI-PURPOSE COOPERATIVE FOR DEVELOPMENTAL ROUTE OF ROUTE 10*."

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval;
