**ARTICLE II**  
**DECLARATION OF POLICY**

**Section I. Declaration of Policy**  
(AIDS) is a disease that recognizes no territorial, social, political, and economic boundaries for which there is no known cure. The gravity of AIDS threat today demands strenuous national action. Thus, the City Government of Davao shall actively promote public awareness of AIDS through comprehensive, city-wide educational and information campaigns, organized by the local government down to barangay level.

The City of Davao shall conscientiously adhere to generally accepted principles of human rights and adopt gender and women perspectives in dealing with STI, HIV/AIDS even as it shall promote value formation and employ scientifically proven approaches. The information and education campaigns shall be carried out in all schools, training centers, workplaces, and communities.

**ARTICLE III**  
**DEFINITION OF TERMS**

**Section 1. Definition of Terms**  
As used in this Ordinance, the following terms are defined as follows:

a) Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) - shall mean a condition characterized by a combination of signs and symptoms caused by HIV contracted from another person and which attacks and weakens the body's immune system, making the afflicted individual susceptible to other life-threatening infections.

b) Accredited NGO or Institution - is a non-government organization (NGO) or institution accredited by the City Government of Davao.

c) Children - refers to persons below 18 years of age or those over but unable to fully take care of themselves or protect themselves from abuse, neglect, cruelty, exploitation or discrimination because of a physical or mental disability or condition.

d) Compulsory HIV Testing - refers to HIV testing imposed upon a person attended or characterized by the lack of or vitiated consent, use of physical force, intimidation or any form of compulsion.

e) Contact Tracing - refers to the method of finding and counseling the sexual partner(s) of a person who has been diagnosed as having sexually transmitted disease.

f) Entertainment Establishments - are business establishments which include bars, night clubs, beer houses, cocktail lounges, massage clinics, karaoke bars/along pub houses within Davao City.

g) Establishment's Policies - refer to the statement of provisions of work and health benefits for workers/employees.

h) Ethics of Care - embraces the universal values of liberty, justice, and gender equity. This calls for health care providers' attitudes that are non-judgmental, non-discriminatory, non-homophobic, but norms that observe the use of non-sexist language, recognition of clients' voice as legitimate, capacity for empathy and belief on clients' capacity for autonomy without coercion and intimidation.

i) Health Certificate - is a certification in writing using the prescribed form issued by the City Health Officer stating that the person applying for or renewing such certificate is permitted by the said Officer or his duly authorized representative to work in an establishment. This certification is issued to the person applying for or renewing such after passing the required physical and medical examinations, immunizations and other requirements as may be required by the City Health Officer or his duly authorized representative.

j) Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) - refers to the virus which causes AIDS.

k) HIV/AIDS Monitoring - refers to the documentation and analysis of the number of HIV/AIDS infections and pattern of its spread.

l) HIV/AIDS Prevention and Control - refers to measures aimed at protecting non-infected persons from contracting HIV and minimizing the impact of the condition of persons living with HIV.

m) Information Materials - shall mean any written and/or printed materials which educate the public.

n) Medical Confidentiality - refers to the relationship of trust and confidence created or existing between a patient or a person with HIV and his attending physician, consulting medical specialist, nurse, medical technologist and other health workers or personnel involved in any counseling, testing or professional care of the former, it also applies to any person who, in any official capacity, has acquired or may have acquired such confidential information.

o) Medical Practitioner - licensed Doctor of Medicine engaged in health work in hospitals, clinics, sanitaria, health stations, barangay health centers and the like.

p) Monitoring - is the process of routine and periodic measurement and/or determination of program input/activities, outputs and problems encountered during program implementation.

q) Peer Educator - is a trained STI, HIV/AIDS educator who is capable of influencing and developing healthy behaviors among his/her peers within the establishment.

r) Person with HIV - refers to an individual whose HIV Antibody Test indicates, directly or indirectly, that he/she is infected with HIV.

ARTICLE IV EDUCATION AND INFORMATION

Section 1. Comprehensive STI, HIV/AIDS Education. Comprehensive education and information strategies shall be designed, integrated, and adopted in various levels geared towards the purpose of providing substantial information / knowledge, improving attitudes and skills in the prevention and control of STI, HIV/AIDS, in both public and private spheres.

Section 2. Sexual Abstinence and Mutual Fidelity. Sexual abstinence and mutual fidelity shall be emphasized as the most effective measures for STI, HIV/AIDS prevention.

Section 3. Media Campaign on STI, HIV/AIDS Prevention. In partnership with the Davao City Information Office, the Philippine Information Agency (PIA), and the various media organizations, the Davao City AIDS Council (DCA) shall prepare and implement a media communications plan for a regular information dissemination campaign and advocacy.

**Section 4. STI, HIV/AIDS Gender Sensitive IEC Materials and Education Modules.** Information, education, and communication (IEC) materials shall be designed appropriate to the needs and culture of groups being targeted in the campaign. Literature on STI/HIV shall utilize the local lingo to encourage and facilitate easy understanding of terms and concepts. Modules on STI/HIV/AIDS prevention and control shall not in any way be used as an excuse to propagate birth control, sale, or distribution of birth control devices. Proper consultations shall be held among heads, leaders, and representatives of respective groups, organizations, institutions, and individuals who are targets for application of any course content, scope, and methodology.

**Section 5. STI, HIV/AIDS Information as a Health Service.** STI/HIV/AIDS education and information dissemination shall form part of the delivery of health services prescribed by the present law. Knowledge and skills of health workers shall be enhanced to include skills for proper information dissemination and education on STI, HIV/AIDS. It shall likewise be considered a civic duty of health providers in the private sector to make available to the public such information necessary to control the spread of STI, HIV/AIDS and to correct common misconceptions on HIV/AIDS related ethical issues such as confidentiality, informed consent, and the duty to provide treatment.

**Section 6. STI, HIV/AIDS Education in Schools.** The City of Davao, the Department of Education (DepEd), the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), and the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) shall take the lead in information dissemination and popularization on the nature of STI, HIV/AIDS and its prevention, utilizing standardized information provided by the Department of Health (DOH). All schools, colleges, universities, and other indigenous learning system including non-formal system of education shall be required to undergo seminar or training on STI, HIV/AIDS prevention and control to be supervised by the Department of Education, CHED, TESDA, or any accredited institution/organization, in coordination with the Department of Health (DOH), before they are allowed to teach on the subject.

**Section 7. STI, HIV/AIDS Education in the Workplace.** All employees of the City of Davao, regardless of their status, shall be required to undergo basic orientation on STI, HIV/AIDS to be conducted by the City Health Office (CHO) and the Human Resource and Development Office (HRDO) of the City of Davao. Managers, supervisors, and employees in private workplaces shall be provided with the standardized basic information and instruction on STI, HIV/AIDS which shall include topics on confidentiality in the workplace and attitude towards infected employees and workers. The Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) and its attached agencies shall undertake measures in the implementation and monitoring of the same.

**Section 8. STI, HIV/AIDS Education for Filipinos Going Abroad.** The City Government of Davao shall coordinate with the Department of Labor and Employment in the implementation of STI, HIV/AIDS education among Filipinos going abroad. Pursuant to Section 7 of R.A. 8304, STI, HIV/AIDS education seminar shall be part of the Pre-Employment and Pre-Departure Orientation Seminar among Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs).

**Section 9. STI, HIV/AIDS Education in Communities.** In partnership with the City Health Office, all barangays shall conduct educational and information campaigns on STI, HIV/AIDS prevention. The Community Affairs Officers, the Barangay Officials and volunteers, private citizens, members of selected government agencies, non-government organizations and church-based groups.

**Section 10. Information Campaigns for Tourists and Transients.** Materials on the causes, modes of transmission, prevention, and consequences of STI/HIV infection shall be adequately provided at all points of entry and exit, hotels and night spots in the city.

**Section 11. Compulsory STI, HIV/AIDS Education.** It shall be compulsory for all owners and/or managers of all establishments to attend seminars on STI, HIV/AIDS prevention conducted regularly by any of the agencies such as the City Health Office, the Davao City AIDS Council, and accredited NGOs, otherwise, no renewal of permit to operate shall be granted to said establishments for violation thereof. A certificate of attendance to STI, HIV/AIDS Seminar shall form part of the requirements for the issuance or renewal of Health Certificate and Permit to Operate.

**Section 12. Peer Educator Required.** The owner and/or manager of all establishments shall have at least one (1) peer educator within the establishment trained by the City Health Office or accredited Non-Government Organization to counsel STI, HIV/AIDS prevention campaign among the workers.

**Section 13. False or Misleading Information.** Misinformation on HIV/AIDS prevention and control through false and misleading advertising and claims in any of the media or the promotion of marketing of drugs, devices, agents or procedures without prior approval from the Department of Health, Bureau of Food and Drug (BFD) and the requisite medical and scientific basis including markings and indications in drugs and devices or agents, purporting to be a cure of full-safe prophylactic for HIV infection is prohibited and punishable under Republic Act 8504.

**ARTICLE V. DECLARATION OF AIDS AWARENESS MONTH**

**Section 1. Declaration of AIDS Awareness Month.** The month of May is hereby declared as AIDS awareness month to coincide with the commemoration day for persons living with HIV/AIDS and the International Day of Action on Women's Health. Likewise, AIDS awareness should culminate on the month of December being the World AIDS Prevention Day.

**Section 2. Advocacy Programs for the AIDS Awareness Month.** There shall be an advocacy program for the whole month of May for the dissemination of information for the prevention and control of STI, HIV/AIDS.

**ARTICLE VII**

FUNCTIONS OF THE REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND WELLNESS CENTER (RBWC)

Section 1. Renaming of the Social Hygiene Clinic to Reproductive Health and Wellness Center (RBWC) and defining its functions: The Social Hygiene Clinic under the City Health Office is hereby renamed as Reproductive Health and Wellness Center (RBWC), which shall perform the following functions:

a. Be in the frontline in the delivery of reproductive health care services;

b. Provide education and information dissemination activities on the promotion of reproductive health, prevention and control of STI, HIV/AIDS;

c. Monitor and evaluate cases handled;

d. Refer clients to other agencies of the City Government for cases that need other health and social services such as livelihood projects, alternative home, and scholarship;

e. Ensure that children with STHIV are given priority in treatment and are referred to concerned agencies for further assistance;

f. Conduct HIV Antibody Test on a voluntary and regular basis. Further, pre and post test counseling shall likewise be administered before any examination may be considered;

g. Perform such other duties and functions as may be prescribed by law or ordinance.

**Section 2. Strengthening of the Reproductive Health and Wellness Center:** The Davao City Government shall strengthen the Reproductive Health and Wellness Center through provision of adequate personnel, facilities, and budget. It shall ensure provision of comprehensive reproductive health services and sexual health education, ensuring efficacy and quality care and information.

**ARTICLE VIII**

ADHERENCE TO SAFE PRACTICES AND PROCEDURES, TESTING, SCREENING AND COUNSELING

Section 1. Safe Practices and Procedures: The City of Davao shall strictly adhere to the principles and guidelines of safe practices and procedures to protect any person from HIV transmission as mandated in Articles II and III of RA 8764. Procedures on testing, screening, and counseling shall observe the ethics of care and medical confidentiality in dealing with individuals having vulnerable conditions for contracting STI/HIV.

ARTICLE X HEALTH AND SUPPORT SERVICES

Section 1. Universal Access to STI Healthcare Services. Access to STI healthcare services/assistance shall be extended to all individuals regardless of sex, gender, age, social status, political belief, tradition, and religion.

Section 2. Adherence to Quality Health Care and Ethics of Care. Medical practitioners or other staff assisting or directly providing health services/examinations/education to clients shall adhere to the basic principles of ethics of care. They shall also be sensitive to gender and sexual orientation in their treatment of patients' clients, informing them of their rights, including the right to file charges for abuses committed against them.

Section 3. Special Care and Support Services for Pregnant Women with HIV/AIDS. The City of Davao shall provide special care and support services for pregnant women with HIV/AIDS.

Section 4. Health Care Plans for Persons with HIV. The City of Davao, in coordination with the Philippine National AIDS Council (PNAC), Department of Health (DOH) and the Commissioner of the Insurance Commission shall conduct a study on the feasibility of setting up a package of health care plans, and should the study warrant, implement the plan for persons with HIV.

Section 5. Special Welfare Fund for RHWC Clients. The City of Davao shall establish and maintain a welfare fund for the clients of RHWC, which shall come from internal check-up and from other sources of the government. Said amount shall exclusively accrue to the welfare fund of the RHWC clientele to be managed by the City Health Office, in partnership with the RHWC Board and other interested institutions.

ARTICLE XI HIV/AIDS Monitoring

Section 1. Monitoring Program and Reporting Mechanisms. The Davao City AIDS Council (DCAC) shall develop a comprehensive STI/AIDS monitoring program and reporting procedures on the magnitude and progression of HIV infection in Davao City. From the said guidelines, mechanisms to further implement provisions on monitoring of Article IV of Republic Act 8304.

**Section 2. Documentation.** STI/AIDS contact tracing and all other related health intelligence activities may be initiated by the Department of Health (DOH), the Davao City AIDS Council (DCAC), provided that these do not run counter to the general purpose of the Ordinance. The information collected shall remain confidential and classified and shall not be used in any way to become basis or qualification for any employment, school attendance, freedom of abode, or travel.

**Section 3. Submission of Work Policies.** It shall be compulsory for all owners and/or managers of all establishments to provide the City Government copies of the establishment's policies in accordance with labor laws and other related national and local policies.

**Section 4. Research Investigations.** All research investigations related to HIV/AIDS should observe strict adherence to the ethical guidelines set by the Philippine National AIDS Council.

**ARTICLE XIII DISCRIMINATORY ACTS AND CONFIDENTIALITY OF INFORMATION**

**Section 1. Discriminatory Acts.** Discrimination in any form and place of person perceived or suggested with HIV (i.e., employment process, promotion, assignment, or termination from work, schools, hospitals, health institutions, travel, and habitation) shall be unlawful. Any set of discrimination related to this Ordinance shall be penalized under Republic Act 8594.

**Section 2. Prohibition Against Divulging of Confidential Information of Persons with STI, HIV/AIDS.** Any person who divulges confidential information about the HIV status of a person shall be penalized under Republic Act 8594.

**ARTICLE XIII PENAL PROVISIONS**

**Section 1. First Offense.** A fine of P 2,000.00 and/or an imprisonment of one (1) month.

**b. Second Offense.** A fine of P 3,000.00 and/or an imprisonment of three (3) months.

**c. Third Offense.** A fine of P 5,000.00 and/or an imprisonment of six (6) months and/or cancellation of business permit.
