**RESOLUTION NO. 496**

As a Privileged Matter after the passage of the Rules of the Body, Councillor Antonio S. Castillio proposed the passage of a Resolution designating the yellow boxes painted on City Streets and National Highways in the City of Davao, designed by the Davao City Urban Transport Cum Land Use Study (DACUTINUS), as Loading and Unloading areas for public utility vehicles.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councillor Antonio S. Castillio, duly seconded by Councillor Tomas Monteverde, Jr., it was

RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an Ordinance entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 271

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 778, SERIES OF 1973.

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - The yellow boxes painted on City Streets and National Highways in the City of Davao, designed by the Davao City Urban Transport Cum Land Use Study (DACUTINUS), are hereby designated as Loading and Unloading areas for public utility vehicles;

SECTION 2. - Ordinance No. 778, series of 1973, is hereby amended accordingly to this effect.
