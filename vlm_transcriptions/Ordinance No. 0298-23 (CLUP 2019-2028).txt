Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0861-22, SERIES OF 2022, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE DAVAO CITY COMPREHENSIVE LAND USE PLAN (CLUP) 2019-2028, AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0546-13, SERIES OF 2013", SPECIFICALLY ARTICLE V, ZONE REGULATIONS, SECTION 12.6.16- URBAN ECOLOGICAL ENHANCEMENT SUB-ZONE (UEESZ);

SECTION 2. SECTION 12.6.16- URBAN ECOLOGICAL ENHANCEMENT SUB-ZONE (UEESZ) IS HEREBY AMENDED TO READ AS FOLLOWS:

12.6.16. Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone (UEESZ) - This refers to areas intended for massive greening program for ecological enhancement in major urban zone as precautionary and proactive approach to climate change adaptation and part of risk reduction management program against flooding, landslide and inundation as the edges and slopes of the ridge or hill are found to be highly susceptible to landslides; and therefore the same is hereby declared protected at all times and any development in the area must strictly comply with the requirements in Article XI, Section 63.2.

Allowable Uses/Activities

1. All uses/activities in Forest Zone (FZ).

2. Nature and Eco-tourism projects, the maximum allowable building footprint of which is 25% of the total UEESZ classified land area for such project. Amenities and facilities within the eco tourism project that can be allowed inside the UEESZ area shall be, but not limited to, the following: refreshment parlor, restaurants, outdoor sports gadgets rental/retail shop; elevated walkway trail, hanging bridges; view deck/observatory spaces; rappelling and rock climbing facilities; swimming pool; indoor sports facility; gymnasium, recreation hall; wellness facility; chapel or other similar structures already enumerated. At least 75% of the total land area of the eco-tourism park shall be devoted to greening or natural landscape and nature preservation activities.

3. Improvement and maintenance of all waterway easement.

4. Other greening programs which include, among others:

Enhancement of biodiversity projects

Partial off-site greening program in compliance with the required 10% or 15% More Green Space in development projects. The off-site green space shall be located outside the limits of the residential space, and should be classified as currently part of the UEESZ. It must be developed as a green space before donation and turn over to the city for upkeep and maintenance.

Green space developed as a direct and consummated land donation to the City Government of Davao's greening program which shall entitle the donor a minimum (floor space credit) multiplier of one (1); or a maximum multiplier of three (3), depending on the potentials of the donated property as Park or Green Space.

--- [Page 3] ---
The Floor Space Credit (FSC) is computed as total donated UEESZ land area in square meters multiplied by the approved factor of the joint committees of the City Parks Board and the Buildings and Structures Health Regulation Committee (BRC). The Floor Space Credits Certificate issued to the donor of the Buildings and Structures Health Regulation Committee (see City Ordinance No. 027-19, Series of 2019). The FSC in square meters can be used to offset billable floor spaces generated above the regular building height limit of vertical projects that are to be built in areas appropriately zoned for the use intended which is outside the UEE SZ. The full or partial benefits of the FSC Certificate can be transferred or reasigned by the grantee or the land donor to developers of vertical buildings that exceed the regular height limits set by the city.

Green space as POPOS or Privately-Owned Public Open Space which shall entitle the owner a Floor Space Credit equivalent or equal to the total area being developed as POPOS. An annotation to the land title stating that the area shall perpetually be utilized as POPOS and that repurposing the area to business activities that are no longer POPOS in nature shall mean cancellation of FSC Certificate (if not yet used in the FSC offsetting scheme) or shall result to the owner paying the full treasury for the monetary value of the FSC as used in the offsetting scheme for vertical buildings that are above the regular height limit. The benefit of the FSC obtained from a POPOS scheme is also a transferable document and instrument.

5. Reformation Development Projects

UEESZ's moderate risk to low risk landslide areas or a combination of moderate risk to low risk, and high risk to landslide features and who do not wish to include their lot or participate in any land consolidation activity for revenue generating Eco-Tourism Projects in the UEE Sub-Zone may exercise their right to construct a residential building, subject to all conditions stated in the Building Regulations.

7. Subdivision Development Projects

Developers intending to pursue subdivision project's outside the Shrine Hills area or in the 2019 expanded UEE SZ that is largely within moderate risk or lower risk to landslide, may be allowed to apply for permits and eventually proceed with the approved plans, provided A, B, and C of the building regulations of the UEE SZ section are followed, including the Floor Area Ratio limitations.

In case where the area to be developed is a combination of moderate risk to low risk, and high risk to landslide features, the high risk to landslide can only be designated as open space or green space in the subdivision development.

8. Utilities, Transportation, and Services

Water, power and telecom service installations and utilities and transport line improvement or rehabilitation within the UEE SZ shall be allowed provided A, B, and C of the Building regulations provided herein are followed accordingly.

9. Activities Approved under the Additional Allowable Use Mode

Any application for business construction and business activity that are not enumerated in this section shall have the option to apply under the Allowable Use Mode. Applicant is then required to fulfill building regulations under the UEE SZ.

**General Building Regulations:**

Unless otherwise provided, all other activities, as may be allowed within the moderate risk to low risk to landslide zone, that will involve the introduction of concrete implements/structures, shall be required to:

A. Implement Slope Protection Measures and/or Landslide Mitigating Measures, plans of which are duly signed by a Geologist and a Structural Engineer with both professionals submitting an Undertaking to oversee and supervise the implementation of the above-mentioned measures;

B. Submit a Certification duly signed by a Geologist or equivalent professional that the proposed construction activity shall not cause landslides or land washing to the adjacent properties which are not under the ownership of the applicant/owner;

C. Submit a Notified Undertaking duly signed by the applicant/owner that extreme caution will be exercised in the construction activities to avoid destructive land movements and should land movements that are attributable to the said construction activity result in physical damages to adjoining properties, the applicant will bear the cost of the repair, rehabilitation or restoration of the damaged property, as determined and decided in a proper court or venue or as agreed upon by concerned parties, whichever is applicable.

Additional Regulations for activities within Shrine Hills and all other areas similarly situated as determined by the Technical Working Group for Evaluation:

1. The applicant/landowner has no other property classified as residential for zoning purposes anywhere in Davao City. For this purpose, the applicant shall be required to submit an omnibus affidavit of such fact and undertake to voluntarily remove all improvements from the property subject of the application in case of misrepresentation.

The foregoing regulation shall not be applicable if the property owned by the applicant is determined by CDRMO as hazard-prone.

2. The design of the structure shall be such that:

- It will introduce engineering safety measures. The owner can use permeable and semi-permeable pavement for the parking area as may be desired, provided that the permeable or semi-permeable bricks are laid on top of the natural ground using levelling materials like sand or natural fiber.
- Balcony and/or view deck may be constructed which will not be included in the computation of FAR, provided that the flooring of the attached feature outside the main building is designed in such a way that it allows air and water to pass through it.

3. Landslide Protection and Surface Erosion Control Measures should be observed, to wit:

o Slope Maintenance: Given the high susceptibility to landslides and surface erosion of the fringes of Shrine Hills, a Slope Protection Measure Program must be established by the City in partnership with the land owners. In all cases, slope maintenance responsibility belongs to the owner of the land.

o Slope Maintenance Inspection: Regular inspection should be carried out to determine the conditions of a slope. These inspections can be subdivided into three (3) levels/categories:

Routine Inspection - shall be conducted by NGOs, POs, members of the Technical Working Group for Evaluation based on some general visual guidelines;

Engineering inspection - shall be conducted by a qualified geotechnical engineer hired for the purpose by the City Engineer's Office (CEO) and the City Engineer in accordance with engineering standards;

Regular Monitoring Process - shall be conducted by the members of the Technical Working Group for Monitoring.

The City may hire the services of a qualified engineering firm as may be necessary in the assessment of technical ways forward in providing mitigating measures.

Other Regulations:

The required Floor Area Ratio (FAR) for residential buildings in the UEE Sub-Zone shall be 0.5 to cover a maximum of twenty-five percent (25%) of the entire lot, provided that another 25% shall be planted with endemic/indigenous tree species and provided further that such planting area shall be maintained in accordance with the specifications set by the Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB).

Provided further that no residential building will be constructed within the high risk to landslides area. In case where the property is a combination of moderate risk to low risk, and high risk to landslides features, no residential building shall be constructed in the high risk to landslide area.

When public interest so requires, the Preliminary Subdivision Development Plan (PSDP) granted for subdivision projects in Urban Ecological Enhancement Sub-Zone as provided herein may be recalled or revoked by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, through a resolution, upon majority vote of all its members, after observance of due process of law.

For Subdivision Development with duly approved Development Permit, land development completion and Authority to Sell by HULURB or SUDPR prior to the latest amendment which changed the effective zone of the area. Such subdivisions projects shall be allowed to exist or to continue unless determined and declared by competent authorities as no longer suitable for habitation. Construction of units in these subdivision projects shall be required to fulfill building regulations A, B, and C, as provided herein when applying for a locational clearance and building permit which are over and above the regular documentary requirements.

Subdivision Development projects applying for PALC/DP as approved at the CHLURU-Level prior to the July 17, 2023 effectiveness of the 2019 Zoning Ordinance shall be evaluated at the City Council using the 2013 Zoning Ordinance, provided, the application is still within the one-year transition period, to commence upon the date of effectivity of the amendments. Otherwise, the 2019 Zoning (or latest amendment) shall be applicable in the evaluation process.
