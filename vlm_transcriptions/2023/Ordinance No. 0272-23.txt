AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND BIAO ARRAIN REFORM BENEFICIARIES COOPERATIVE

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

--- [Page 2] ---
**Page 2 of 2**

**Ord. No. 0272-23**

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance granting legislative authority to the City Mayor to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Biao Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries Cooperative."

**SECTION 2. AUTHORITY** - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Biao Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries Cooperative.

**SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

**ENACTED, August 08, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.**
