Ord. No. 0352-23

Be it ordained by the Sangugunang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING FULL EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX
TO SR. PAULINE GUILMETTE FOUNDATION, INC., FROM THE PAYMENT
OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR CONCERT FOR A CAUSE,
ENTITLED "GRATITUDE", TO BE HELD ON DECEMBER 22, 2023, AT THE
PHILIPPINE WOMEN'S COLLEGE, RSM EVENT CENTER, MATINA, THIS
CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 192 and 458 (a) (2)
(xii) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code
of 1991, provides that Local Government Units, through an ordinance duly approved,
may grant tax exemptions, incentives or relief under such terms and conditions
as they may deem necessary.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - The aforementioned exemption shall be based
on the system of computation adopted by the Business Tax and Licensing Division
of the City Treasurer's Office and their projected income statement, herein
enumerated:
