AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND GREENWAVE MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE

Ord. No. 029-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND GREENWAVE MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE."

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Greenwave Multipurpose Cooperative.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 26th day of September, 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang, there being a quorum.
