Ord. No. 0260-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND MANUEL GUIANGA AND SIRIB GROWERS AND EMPLOYEES MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE (MAGSIGE MPC)". 

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Manuel Guianga and Sirib Growers and Employees Multipurpose Cooperative (MAGSIGE MPC).

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 18th day of July 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of
the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
