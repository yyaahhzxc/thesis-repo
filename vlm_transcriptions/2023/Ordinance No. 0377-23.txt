Ord. 0377-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known and cited "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF FR. LEONARDO DUBLAN, JR. PARISH PRIEST, ST. JOSEPH PARISH, FOR TAX EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE TICKETS SOLD FOR THE UPCOMING BLOCK SCREENING OF THE MOVIE ENTITLED "GOMBURZA" ON DECEMBER 30, 2023 AT NCCC CINEMA 1 (1:00 AM AND 1:30 PM), THIS CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 192 and 458 (a) (2) (xii) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provide that Local Government Units, through an ordinance duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or relief under such terms and conditions as they may be necessary.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - The aforementioned exemption shall be based on the system of computation adopted by the Business Tax and License Division of the City Treasurer's Office and their projected income statement, herein enumerated:

| NO OF | AMOUNT | TOTAL | 5% TAX |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| TICKETS | P 400.00 | P 142,400.00 | P 7,120.00 |

SECTION 4. TRANSMITTAL - The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is hereby directed to transmit a copy of the said Ordinance to the Executive department immediately upon its approval.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 14, 2023, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
