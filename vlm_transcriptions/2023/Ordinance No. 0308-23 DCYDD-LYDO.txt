AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE DAVAO CITY YOUTH DEVELOPMENT DIVISION - THE LOCAL YOUTH DEVELOPMENT OFFICE (DCYDD-LYDO) OF DAVAO CITY, DEFINING ITS FUNCTIONS AND PURPOSES AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "DAVAO CITY YOUTH DEVELOPMENT DIVISION: THE LOCAL YOUTH DEVELOPMENT OFFICE (DCYDD-LYDO) OF DAVAO CITY ORDINANCE". It shall be briefly called as the "DCYDD-LYDO ORDINANCE".

SECTION 2. POLICY STATEMENT - The State recognizes the vital role of the youth in nation-building and thus, promotes and protects their physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual and social well-being, inclinates them patriotism, nationalism and other desirable values, and encourages their involvement in public and civic affairs.

Towards this end, the State shall establish adequate, effective, responsive, and enabling mechanisms and support systems that will ensure the meaningful participation of the youth in local governance and in nation building.

Thus, it is the policy of the City Government of Davao to encourage the youth sector to participate in local government and in nation building and thus establish effective, responsive, and enabling mechanisms for the youth to partake in the promotion of the general welfare of the youth community of this city. Such mechanisms shall be guided by the pertinent provisions of Republic Act No. 10742, otherwise known as the "Sanggunian Kabataan Reform Act of 2015", as amended by R.A. No. 11768, which states that "there shall be in every province, city, and municipality a Youth Development Office which shall be headed by a youth development officer with rank of at least division chief. Such may be put under the Office of the Local Chief Executive, the City Planning and Development Office, the City Social Welfare and Development Office, or in any other office deemed appropriate by the local government unit."

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms are hereby defined:

(a) Davao City Youth Development Council (DCYDC) shall refer to Davao City's multi-sectoral youth association headed by the SK Pedersayan President and composed of representatives of youth and youth-serving organizations in the City, as created by R.A. No. 10742, as amended by R.A. No. 11768 and as created by the applicable ordinance;

(b) Davao City Youth Development Division - The Local Youth Development Office (DCYDD-LYDO) shall refer to the Local Youth Development Office of Davao City created under this Ordinance and under R.A. No. 10742, otherwise known as the "Sanggunian Kabataan Reform Act of 2015", as amended;

(c) Davao City Youth Development Plan (DCYPD) shall refer to Davao City's plan as initially drafted by the SK Pedersayan, finalized by DCYDC and approved by the Sanggunian. The DCYPD shall be anchored on the local development plans of the City Government of Davao and, as far as practicable, the Philippine Youth Development Plan (PYDP);

(d) Local Chief Executive (LCE) shall refer to the City Mayor of Davao City;

(e) National Youth Commission (NYC) shall refer to the National Youth Commission created under Republic Act No. 8044;

ARTICLE II  
DAVAO CITY YOUTH DEVELOPMENT DIVISION - THE LOCAL YOUTH DEVELOPMENT OFFICE (DCYDD-LYDO) OF DAVAO CITY

SECTION 4. CREATION: The Davao City Youth Development Division - The Local Youth Development Office (DCYDD-LYDO) of Davao City shall be created as a division under the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO). The new division shall be referred to as "Davao City Youth Development Division - The Local Youth Development Office (DCYDD-LYDO)".

A. The DCYDD-LYDO shall be headed by a Youth Development Officer IV with an assistant, to be designated as Youth Development Officer III;

B. The DCYDD-LYDO shall be composed of three (3) sections namely:

1. Policy and Planning;
2. Administration and Finance;
and
3. Programs and Operations.

C. All of these sections shall be provided with staffing requirements, pursuant to Section 26 (c) of the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of R.A. No. 10742.

SECTION 5. FUNCTIONS OF THE DCYDD-LYDO: The DCYDD-LYDO shall have the following functions:

(a) In accordance with Article III of this Ordinance, register and verify youth and youth-serving organizations (subject to the Revitalized Youth Organizations' Registration Program (YORP) guidelines);

(b) Provide technical assistance to DCYDC in the formulation of DCYDP;

(c) Facilitate the election of the DCYDC representatives;

(d) Serve as secretariat to DCYDC;

(e) Conduct mandatory and continuing training of SK officials and DCYDC members in accordance with the programs jointly designed and implemented by NYC and the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG). The Local Youth Development Officer and/or their staff shall apply for accreditation with the National Youth Commission (NYC) to be able for them to conduct the mandatory and continuing training programs of SK officials and DCYDC members;

(f) Provide technical, logistical and other support in the conduct of mandatory and continuing training programs and to such other programs of NYC and DILG;

(g) Coordinate with NYC with regard to the youth programs of the City Government of Davao;

(h) In coordination with the City Information Office (CIO) of Davao City, perform information drive on the functions, duties and responsibilities, as well as the accomplishments and programs of DCYDC and DCYDD-LYDO, as the case may be;

(i) Perform other functions that the LCE, DILG, or NYC may require.

SECTION 6. FUNCTIONS OF THE SECTIONS IN DCYDD-LYDO - The duties and functions of the following sections under the DCYDD-LYDO shall be as follows:

A. Policy and Planning Section - This Section shall manage and oversee the operational and functional work for policy research, program development, and planning of DCYDD-LYDO.

B. Administration and Finance Section - This Section shall perform operational and functional work of administrative, record taking, keeping and preserving data pertaining to the functions and operations of DCYDD-LYDO and DCYDC, as well as all matters related to finances.

C. Programs and Operations Section - This section shall perform operational and functional works related to the operations, logistics, monitoring and evaluation of DCYDD-LYDO in line with the policies, planning, and programs of the office.

In addition to the requirements prescribed by the Civil Service Commission (CSC), the Youth Development Officer shall:

(1) Be preferably not more than thirty (30) years of age at the time of their appointment;
(2) Be of good moral character;
(3) Must have acquired experience in youth development affairs or involvement in youth or youth-serving organizations for at least three (3) years;
(4) Shall be appointed by the LCE, but in no case be within the fourth (4th) civil degree of consanguinity or affinity of the appointing authority.

SECTION 9. MANPOWER COMPLEMENT - The City Mayor shall be allowed to hire additional workers for the division as emergency, casual or contractual employees, pursuant to Section 77 of the Local Government Code, Republic Act No. 7277, and other pertinent laws.

ARTICLE III TRANSITORIAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 10. TRANSITORIAL PROVISIONS

(a) All records of registered youth and youth-serving organizations under existing executive orders, rules and regulations of existing Local Youth Development Office and/or by the CSWDO of the City Government of Davao shall be adopted by DCYDD-LYDO. The said records shall continue to be valid subject to the provisions on registration renewal and continuing registration under this Ordinance.

(b) The existing Local Youth Development Office personnel created by virtue of Executive Order No. 16-2021 shall continue to hold office until such time that their successors shall have been appointed following the provisions of this Ordinance.

SECTION 11. APPROPRIATIONS - The City Government of Davao through a proper appropriation ordinance may appropriate not less than one percent (1%) of its annual budget for DCYDD-LYDO, which shall be used for the implementation of DCYDP, convening of the elections of the SK Federasyon and DCYCO, election of participating qualified youth and youth-serving organizations, maintenance of operations and effective functioning. The appropriation necessary for the implementation of this Ordinance shall be included in the proposed Annual Budget of the City Government subject to availability of funds.

SECTION 12. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS (IRR) - The SK Pedersayan, together with CSWDO, shall issue the IRR necessary for the effective implementation of this Ordinance within one hundred twenty (120) days from its effectivity hereof.
