ORDINANCE NO. 0279-23
Series of 2023

AN ORDINANCE FOR THE DISSOLUTION OF CALINAN SLAUGHTERHOUSE AND TORIL SLAUGHTERHOUSE AND THE TRANSFER OF THEIR PERSONNEL TO THE NEWLY ESTABLISHED MALAGOS SLAUGHTERHOUSE WITH MEAT CUTTING AND COLD STORAGE FACILITIES AND THE MALAGOS HALAL SLAUGHTERHOUSE, UNDER THE OPERATION OF THE SLAUGHTERHOUSE, OFFICE OF THE CITY ADMINISTRATOR - CITY ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0279-23

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as: "AN ORDINANCE FOR
THE DISOLUTION OF CALIGAN SLAUGHTERHOUSE AND TORIL SLAUGHTERHOUSE AND THE TRANSFER OF THEIR PERSONNEL TO THE NEWLY
ESTABLISHED MALAGOS SLAUGHTERHOUSE WITH MEAT CUTTING AND COLD
STORAGE FACILITIES AND THE MALAGOS HALAL SLAUGHTERHOUSE, UNDER
THE OPERATION OF THE SLAUGHTERHOUSE, OFFICE OF THE CITY
ADMINISTRATOR - CITY ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES".

SECTION 2. PERTinent Provisions Under Republic Act No. 7160
OR THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991

"Section 45B. Powers, Duties, Functions and Compensation. - (a) The
sangguniang panlungsod, as the legislative body of the city, shall enact ordinances,
approve resolutions and appropriate funds for the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code and in the proper exercise of the corporate powers of the city as provided for under Section 22 of this Code, and shall:
xxx

(3) Subject to the provisions of Book II of this Code, enact ordinances granting
franchises and authorizing the issuance of permits or licenses, upon such conditions and for such purposes intended to promote the general welfare of the
inhabitants of the city and pursuant to this legislative authority shall:
xxx

(vii) Upon approval by a majority vote of all the members of the sangguniang
panlungsod: grant a franchise to any person, partnership, corporation, or
cooperative to do business within the city; establish, construct, operate and maintain ferries, wharves, markets or slaughterhouses; or undertake such other activities within the city as may be allowed by existing laws."

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the Caligan Slaughterhouse
and Toril Slaughterhouse and the Transfer of their Personnel to the Newly Established
Malagos Slaughterhouse, with Meat Cutting and Cold Storage Facilities and the Malagos
Halal Slaughterhouse, under the operation of the Slaughterhouse, Office of the City
Administrator-City Economic Enterprise.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY - If for any reason, any section or provision of this
Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 29th day of August 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
