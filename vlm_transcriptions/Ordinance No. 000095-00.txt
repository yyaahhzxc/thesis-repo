**ORDINANCE NO. 895**
**Series of 2000**

**AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL FOOD ESTABLISHMENTS SUCH AS, BUT NOT LIMITED TO, RESTAURANTS, CAFESEATS, FOOD OUTLETS, HOTELS, CATERERS, AND HOSPITALS AS WELL AS FOOD MANUFACTURERS OR PROCESSORS TO USE ONLY IODIZED SALT**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 095

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known and called as the "Davao City Iodized Salt Ordinance".

SECTION 2. POLICY - It is an expressed policy of every local government unit, particularly the City Government of Davao to protect and ensure public health and the general welfare of its constituents:

Therefore, pursuant to the provisions mandated under Republic Act No. 8172, otherwise known as "An Act for Salt Iodization Nationwide (ASIN)," the City of Davao hereby enacts this legislation because of its belief in the people's right to good health and because of its sense of responsibility to promote and enhance the same.

SECTION 3. APPLICABILITY - This Ordinance shall be applicable within the entire territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao and shall apply to all food establishments such as, but not limited to, restaurants, canteens, food outlets, hotels, caterers and hospitals as well as all food manufacturers or processors;

SECTION 4. REQUIREMENT FOR USE OF IODIZED SALT: All food establishments, food manufacturers or processors, as stated in Section 3 of this Ordinance, are hereby required to use iodized salt in the preparation of food for human consumption;

SECTION 5. INFORMATION DISTRIBUTION AND EDUCATIONAL CAMPAIGNS: The City Government of Davao under the supervision of the Davao City Health Office shall conduct informational campaigns on iodized salt materials as well as conduct seminars, and other related activities pertaining to information dissemination and educational campaign about Salt Iodization and the related provisions mandated by the ASIN Law or Republic Act No. 8172.

The Davao City Health Office shall coordinate and collaborate with the Department of Health (DOH), other concerned government agencies and interested non-government organizations (NGOs) in the said information dissemination and educational campaigns;

SECTION 6. THE ROLE OF THE SANITATION SECTION OF THE DAVAO CITY HEALTH OFFICE: The Sanitation Section of the Davao City Health Office shall serve as the inspection and monitoring body that shall ensure the use of iodized salt at all times in all food establishments and all food manufacturers or processors in Davao city.

The frequency of inspections and other concerns related to monitoring shall be determined upon and determined by the Sanitation Section of the Davao City Health Office. The Section shall, however, provide all food establishments, manufacturers or processors with the guidelines to be followed during inspections and monitoring for better coordination.

SECTION 7. PENALTY: Any person, whether natural or juridical, who violates the provisions of Section 4 in relation to Section 3 of this Ordinance shall be penalized as follows:

a) First Offense: A fine of not less than Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00) but not more than One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) or an imprisonment of not less than seven (7) days but not more than one (1) month, or both, at the discretion of the court;

b) For Second Offense: A fine of not less than One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) but not more than Three Thousand Pesos (P3,000.00) or an imprisonment of not less than one (1) month but not more than three (3) months, or both, at the discretion of the court.
