Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod ng Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FURTHER AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 153-A, SERIES OF 1990, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING PARKING FEES ON ALL VEHICLE OWNERS/DRIVERS USING CERTAIN STREETS IN DAVAO CITY AS PARKING SPACES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES", AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO. 1767, SERIES OF 1990, ORDINANCE NO. 301, SERIES OF 1991, ORDINANCE NO. 88, SERIES OF 1992, ORDINANCE NO. 100, SERIES OF 1993, AND ORDINANCE NO. 005-01, SERIES OF 2001 AND ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, SERIES OF 2012".

SECTION 2. PARKING FEES - Section 3 of the Ordinance No. 153-A, Series of 1990, as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

SECTION 3. PARKING FEES. There shall be collected the amount of TWENTY PESOS [P20.00] for each additional hour or fraction thereof and TEN PESOS [P10.00] for each additional vehicle owner or driver thereof, provided that the total fee shall not exceed TWO HUNDRED PESOS [P200.00] a day for a single vehicle. Furthermore, buses and trucks shall be charged ONE HUNDRED PESOS [P100.00] for the first two (2) hours or a fraction thereof and TWENTY PESOS [P20.00] for each additional hour or fraction thereof, provided that the total fee shall not exceed THREE HUNDRED PESOS [P300.00] a day for a single bus or truck. A one year ticket fixed at THREE THOUSAND PESOS [P3,000.00] per vehicle shall be allowed upon the issuance of the annual parking sticker, except buses, trucks, and the like which will be charged FIVE THOUSAND PESOS [P5,000.00] annually.

For motor vehicle and bicycle, a parking fee of FIVE PESOS [P5.00] and TWO PESOS [P2.00], respectively, for the first two (2) hours or fraction thereof and ONE PESO [P1.00] per hour for the succeeding hours or fraction thereof;

SECTION 3. PARKING MANAGEMENT UNIT. There shall be created a Parking Management Unit which shall be principally tasked to implement the provisions of this Ordinance. It shall be under the supervision of the Traffic Management Center.

SECTION 4. PARKING SIGNS & PAINTING MARKINGS - Section 7 of Ordinance No. 153-A, Series of 1990, is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 7. The Engineering Division of the Traffic Management Center (TMC) in coordination with the Parking Management that shall issue the design drawings, specifications, and appropriate traffic signs and pay painting marks and shall implement the installation of the same. In no case shall any traffic signs and parking space markings be placed on the city streets without authority from the Traffic Management Center.

SECTION 5. PARKING BAYS - Section 8 of Ordinance No. 153-A, Series of 1990, is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 8. The Traffic Management Center (TMC) shall properly designate parking bays for all kinds of vehicles on all designated streets and shall specifically designate separate and exclusive parking spaces for motorcycles and bicycles. The fee will be FIVE PESOS [P5.00] and TWO PESOS [P2.00], respectively, for the first two [2] hours or a fraction thereof; and ONE PESO [P1.00] per hour for the next succeeding hours or a fraction thereof.

The Traffic Management Center or TMC shall likewise designate sufficient parking spaces for Persons with Disability (PWDs) and shall be charged an amount Twenty Percent (20%) less than the regular rates. Bicycle racks shall also be provided to ensure the safety of parked bicycles.

Further, the vehicle of the owner/ driver shall be towed in case/ if the fails to pay his parking fee dues and the vehicle shall be deposited to the city garage until the violator pays his dues at the City Treasurer's Office upon presentation of the parking fee ticket and corresponding towage service fee.

SECTION 6. PARKING FEE ENforcERS - Section 9 of Ordinance No. 153-A, Series of 1990, is hereby amended to read:

SECTION 9. PARKING MANAGEMENT UNIT AND PARKING FEE ENforcERS. Parking Fee Enforcers who are under the direct supervision and control of the Office of the City Treasurer shall be charged with the duty of collecting the fees for the use of pay parking facilities, using any form of official receipts of the City Treasurer to be issued to the driver/owner of the motor vehicle. The Parking Fee Enforcer shall be required to post a cash bond of not less than FIVE THOUSAND PESOS [P5,000.00] in order to safeguard the funds that come into his/her possession. For this purpose, the Traffic Management Center will recommend to the City Mayor the hiring of not less than One Hundred Fifty [150] Parking Fee Enforcers.

SECTION 7. APPROPRIATION FOR EXPENSES - Section 11 of Ordinance No. 153-A, Series of 1990, is hereby amended to read:

SECTION 11. APPROPRIATION FOR EXPENSES. In order to immediately implement the provisions of this Ordinance, there shall be appropriated the sum of THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS [P3,000,000.00] from available sources. However, the Local Finance Committee may designate to defray the initial expenses necessary to effectively carry out the purpose of this Ordinance;

SECTION 8. PENALTIES. Any violations to the provisions of the preceding sections of this Ordinance shall be punished by a fine of not less than FIVE HUNDRED PESOS [P500.00], or an imprisonment of THREE [3] MONTHS or both upon the discretion of the Court. The following shall be held liable upon violation of the provisions of this Ordinance: If the provisions of the previous Ordinance penalizing any violation of its provisions inconsistent with the herein amendments shall be considered amended.
