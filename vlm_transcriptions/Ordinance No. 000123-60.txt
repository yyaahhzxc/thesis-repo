Title II - Additional Fees by Cinematographs, Theaters, Vaudeville companies, Dramatical Shows and Boxing Exhibitions:

SECTION 1: Additional Fees. In addition to the fees paid by cinematographs, theaters, vaudeville companies, dramatical shows and boxing exhibitions, there shall be collected from the places of amusement where are specified above, the amount of P 0.05 fees on the price of every admission ticket sold by such enterprises for each admission ticket sold during the week-end. *P 0.05

SEC. 2: Number of Revenue. It shall be the duty of every proprietor, lessee, purchaser, or operator of such cinematographs, the theaters, vaudeville companies, dramatical shows and boxing exhibitions to sell tickets serially numbered, and to furnish the City Treasurer's Office with copies of the statement he files with the Bureau of Internal Revenue showing the serial numbers of tickets sold during the week-end. The payment of the corresponding fees herein imposed shall be made to the City Treasurer every Monday following every week-end. The City Treasurer is hereby authorized to promulgate rules and regulations for the effective collection of the above-mentioned fees.

SEC. 3: Exception. Fees herein prescribed shall not be paid where the admission fee or charges are entirely collected for and in behalf of any charitable, religious association or educational institution, otherwise the fees herein prescribed will be imposed.

Title III - Graduated Tax on the Quarterly Sales or Receipts of Merchants engaged in Wholesale or Retail Business, Including Importers and/or Manufacturers:

TITLE X - DEFINITION OF TERMS
"Airline Establishment" includes all persons, natural or juridical who for compensation or profit, negotiate freight or other business for owners or places, or for the shippers or consignors, or consignees of freight, carried by planes.
"Air Cargo Service" includes all persons, natural or juridical company, corporation or association, etc., for air freight renders service for another, either as a collector or deliverer of its cargo.
"Arrastre" used in this Ordinance shall mean the operation of receiving, conveying and loading merchandise on piers or wharves.
"Boarding House" shall be defined as any house of the lesser class which accepts passengers' tent and provide them lodging and meals during their stay.
"Business Agents" (agents de negocios) includes all persons who acts as agents for others in the transaction of business with any public officer, as well as those who conduct collecting, ar advertising for another.
"Carinderia" shall be defined as any eatery or establishment where ready cooked foods are displayed for sale and served to the customers. "First Class Carinderia" is one with an accumulation of more than five (5) dining tables of an average size of one meter square. "Second Class Carinderia" is one with accommodations of less than (5) dining tables with an average size of one square meter.
