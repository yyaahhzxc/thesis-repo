Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE ISKOLAR NG TRIBU PROGRAM, SEEKING SUPPORT FROM THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, AND OTHER STAKEHOLDERS, PARTICULARLY THE HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS, AS AMENDED

Page 2 of 7
Ord. No. 0999-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE ISKOLAR NG TRIBU PROGRAM SEEKING SUPPORT FROM THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND OTHER STAKEHOLDERS, PARTICULARLY THE HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The State shall provide equal access to various cultural opportunities to Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs) through the educational system, public or cultural entities, scholarships, grants, and other incentives. Accordingly, the State shall guarantee the right of Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs) to the government's basic services which shall include, but not limited to, education, health, and infrastructure.

SECTION 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS - Iskolar ng Tribu Program shall have the following objectives:

A.) Long Term Objectives:

A.1. To motivate Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples to value and appreciate education as an empowering tool in promoting peace and community building;

A.2. To create competent IP professionals who would complete Tertiary Education and Training and who will become instructors in:

A.2.a. uplifting their own respective Indigenous Cultural Communities;

A.2.b. eliminating prejudice and discrimination to promote tolerance, understanding and good relations among Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples, and all segments of the society; and

A.2.c. respecting, recognizing, and protecting the rights of Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples in preserving their culture, traditions, and institutions.

A.) Short Term Objectives:

B.1. To address the lack of access to tertiary education among members of Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples;

B.2. To accommodate a minimum of one hundred (100) IP scholar applicants every year;

B.3. To facilitate the screening of IP scholar applicants and processing of their application documents, including the signing of their Contract of Agreement with the Indigenous Peoples' Mandatory Representative Office;

B.4. To facilitate the enrollment of qualified IP scholar applicants.

**SECTION 3: DEFINITION OF TERMS -** Whenever used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean as herein set forth:

C.1. **Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs)** - refer to a group of people or homogenous societies identified by self-ascertainment and ascription by other, who have continuously lived as an organized community on a communally bounded and defined territory, and who have, under claims of ownership since time immemorial, occupied, possessed customs, tradition, and other distinctive cultural traits;

C.2. **Higher Education Institution** - shall refer to organizations or educational institutions providing higher, post-secondary, tertiary, and/or third-level education;

C.3. **Tertiary Education** - shall refer to education pursued beyond Secondary Education or High School;

C.4. **Secondary Education** - shall refer to education after primary school and before Higher or Tertiary Education;

C.5. **Priority Courses** - shall refer to bachelor's degree courses which are primarily community-related;

C.6. **IPMR Office** - The sectoral representative of the eleven (11) ICCs/IPs of Davao City, particularly the Matigsalug, Kagan, Klata, Ata, Tagabawa, Obo Manuvu, Maguindanano, Maranao, Tausug, Sama and Ibanot of the Sangguniang Panlungsod. It facilitates the screening and recommendation of qualified IP applicants to the respective scholarship programs of Higher Education Institutions, and other stakeholders under the Iskolar ng Tribu Program.

**SECTION 4: WHO MAY APPLY -** Any member of the Indigenous Peoples/Indigenous Cultural Communities (IPs/ICCs) who has satisfactorily completed Secondary Education may apply for Iskolar ng Tribu Program.

**SECTION 5: SELECTION SHALL BE OF TWO LEVELS, AS FOLLOWS:**

A. **General Conditions** - The applicant must adhere to the following conditions:

1. Bona fide member of an ethno linguistic group in his/her community duly recognized by the City Government of Davao;
2. Favorable endorsement from at least two (2) recognized/acknowledged IPs from a recognized/acknowledged IP traditional leader of his/her ethno-linguistic group recognized by the City Government of Davao;
3. Willingness to sign an undertaking to serve his/her Indigenous Cultural Community after completion of his/her four-year course for a period of at least two (2) years as the case may be;
4. Taking priority courses needed in his/her community at a duly accredited Higher Education Institution in or near his/her domicile, upon request, with the consent of the community elders;
5. Must be physically and mentally fit as shown in his/her medical/psychological records; and
6. Must be a resident of Davao City.

B.) Specific Criteria. The applicant must pass the following criteria:
1. Must have finished Secondary Education with a general average of 85% and above with no grade lower than 80% in all higher subjects;
2. Must be of good moral character;
3. Must adhere to the standards set by the Scholarship Program of the Higher Education Institution, such as the grade requirement, the screening, and the enrollment process.

SECTION 6. (A) SCREENING PROCESS. There shall be a screening process of IP applicants to assess the veracity of their membership in their Indigenous Cultural Communities (ICC), Academic Records and Commitment to serve their communities after completion of their studies. The Selection Process shall be the following:

1. Application. The IP applicant shall submit his/her application form to the IPMR Office which includes the following documents:
a) Certificate of Confirmation of Tribal Membership from National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) or National Commission on Filipinos (NCFP) as the case may be;
b) 2 pieces latest 2x2 ID picture in Tribal Attire;
c) Academic Records issued by the School from which the IP applicant graduated Secondary Education;
d) If currently enrolled in Tertiary Course, current Transcript of Records;
e) Certificate of Good Moral Character from the School which the IP applicant graduated Secondary Education;

2. Nomination/Endorsement. The IP scholar applicant must have an Endorsement/Character Reference of at least two (2) respected/acknowledged IP traditional leaders of his/her ethnolinguistic group recognized by the City Government of Davao;

3. Inclusivity. The IP applicant must be a bona fide member of an ethnolinguistic group in his/her community duly recognized by the City Government of Davao. Thus, any IP applicant can apply for the program, but the bona fide members of the ethnolinguistic groups of Matagalsig, Kagan, Tagabawa, Klata, Ata, Obo-Manuvao, Magundanao, Maranao, Tausug, Sama, Irunan are given priority.

4. Selection Panel. The selection panel shall evaluate the application of the IP applicant and shall be composed of the following:
a) A competent member of the Academe;
b) The Indigenous Peoples' Mandatory Representative (IPMR);
c) A competent official from the EBSU-STEP.

5. Recommendation of Priority Courses. Upon careful scrutiny and assessment of the Selection Panel, priority courses shall be recommended to the IP applicant. The recommendation of IP scholar applicants who are currently enrolled in a Tertiary Course shall be subject to the discretion of the Selection Panel. The IP applicant is qualified to be recommended upon the unanimous approval of the Selection Panel.

(B). Flow of Recommendation of Priority Courses.
1.) With existing scholarship programs. After the recommendation of the selection panel of priority courses to the respective applicants, the IPMR office will facilitate with Higher Education institutions their enrollment vis-a-vis Scholarship Guidelines of the concerned Higher Education Institutions;
2.) Without scholarship program. The IPMR Office may recommend a qualified IP applicant for enrollment to the Higher Education.

SECTION 7. BENEFITS. The following benefits may be received by the IP scholar applicant upon admission into the Iskola ng Tribu Scholarship Program in partnership with the stakeholders, particularly with Higher Education Institutions:

a. In partnership with the Higher Education Institutions
a.1. Free Tuition Fees and Miscellaneous Expenses or Tuition Fees only, in accordance with the Scholarship Program of a Higher Education Institution, where the IP Scholar has been enrolled;
a.2. Other Benefits (vis-a-vis specific guidelines of the concerned HEIs)
a.2.1. Books and Allowances;
a.2.2. Uniform Allowances;
a.2.3. Lodging/Boarding.

b. From the City Government of Davao and/or other partners (vis-a-vis specific programs and services)
b.1. Free Tuition Fees and Miscellaneous Expenses or Tuition Fees only;
b.2. Books and Allowances;
b.3. Uniform Allowances;
b.4. Lodging/Boarding.

SECTION 8. DISQUALIFICATION/DISCONTINUANCE. The enrolled IP scholar may subsequently be disqualified on any of the following grounds after thorough investigation of the designated committee:
1. Tampering/falsification of any/all documents relative to his/her scholarship;
2. Violated any of the Rules and Regulations set forth by the IPMR Office stipulated in the signed Contract of Agreement between the enrolled IP scholar and the IPMR;
3. Violated any of the Rules and Regulations set forth by the HEI where the IP scholar has been enrolled stipulated in the signed Contract of Agreement between the IP scholar and the concerned HEI;
4. Becomes physically and mentally unfit to continue studies (as evidenced by a medical/psychological record) without prejudice to the differently abled;

5. Not able to maintain the grades required by the concerned Higher Education Institution corresponding to its Scholarship Program;
6. Shifted to another course or transferred to another school without the knowledge and approval of the IPMR Office or the concerned HEI;
and
7. Dropped from school without the knowledge and approval of the IPMR Office and the concerned HEI.

SECTION 9. ESTABLISHMENT OF COMMITMENT. The IPMR office shall encourage different Higher Education Institutions to create a Scholarship Program for Indigenous Peoples if there are none. If there are already existing, the IPMR office shall endorse qualified IP applicants corresponding to the priority courses offered under their Scholarship Program. Higher Education Institutions are enjoined to regularly communicate any scholarship offering to the IPMR office. To this end, Higher Education Institutions shall be encouraged to keep in constant communication with any available courses under their different Scholarship Programs to the IPMR Office.

SECTION 10. PRIORITY COURSES. The Priority courses shall be primarily community-related courses which shall include, but not limited to, Social Sciences, Agriculture, Forestry, Economics, Community Development, Anthropology, Education, Environmental Sciences, Hotel Restaurant Management, Tourism and Health-Related Courses, such as Nursing, Midwifery, Medical Technology, Pharmacy, Medicine, etc.

SECTION 11. MONITORING AND EVALUATION. The IPMR Office shall conduct monitoring and evaluation of IP scholars by coordinating with their respective Higher Education Institutions and shall create a progress assessment by semester. Also, the IPMR Office shall coordinate for possible community immersion programs of IP scholars to gauge the level of awareness of their tribal identity and strengthen their socio-cultural engagement, but in no case shall this affect their studies.

SECTION 12. APPLICATION. The implementation of Iskolar ng Tribu Program shall be without prejudice to the implementation of other Scholarship Programs of the City of Davao, including other offices and agencies. Also, this Ordinance may be supplemented by the Guidelines of Iskolar ng Tribu Program. The City Government of Davao shall appropriate funds for the Iskolar ng Tribu Program.

SECTION 13. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any part of provision of these guidelines are declared invalid or unconstitutional, any part of provision not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 14. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect FIFTEEN (15) DAYS after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

ENACTED, June 28, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
