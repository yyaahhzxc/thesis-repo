Ord. No. 0376-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
REPEALING CITY ORDINANCES NO. 1710-A, SERIES OF 1975, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS "AN ORDINANCE BANNING AND/OR PROHIBITTING THE
OPERATION OF JUKEBOXES WITHIN THE CITY PUBLIC MARKETS AND
 PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREOF".

SECTION 2. REPEALING CLAUSE - City Ordinance No. 1710-A, Series of
1975, otherwise known as "An Ordinance Banning and/or Prohibiting the
Operation of Jukeboxes Within the City Public Markets and Providing
Penalties for Violations Thereof", is hereby repealed.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after
posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3)
consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao,

ENACTED, December 14, 2017, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
