RESOLUTION NO. 1745
2nd Indorsement of the Committee on Laws and Ordinances,
dated July 2, 1979, recommending favorable action on the Pro-
posed Resolution and/or ordinance of Counselor Tomas Monteverde,
Jr., with amendments, re An Ordinance Prohibiting any Person
from Urinating and/or Defecating within the Public View in any
street, alley, highway and in the Vicinity of any Public Building
in Davao City and Providing Penalties in case of Violation thereof,
was presented for consideration and appropriate action
of the Body.
After careful deliberation, on motion of Counselor Tomas
Monteverde, Jr., duly seconded by Counselor Petronilo G. Singen,
and amended by Counselors Benigno V. Vilasazo, Bonifacio Tamayo
and the prosecutor, it was
RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREIN RESOLVED, To approved an Ordinance
entitled
ORDINANCE No. 910
AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING ANY PERSON FROM URINATING AND/OR
DEFECATING WITHIN THE PUBLIC VIEW IN ANY STREET, ALLEY, HIGH-
WAY AND ANY PLACE WITHIN THE VICINITY OF ANY PUBLIC BUILDING
IN DAVAO CITY AND PROVIDING PENALTIES IN CASE OF VIOLATION
THEOREM.
Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:
SECTION 1.- It shall be unlawful for any person to urinate
and/or defecate within the public view in any street, alley, high-
way and any place within the vicinity of any public building in

Davao City, except in such places as may be designated by the City Public Service Officer;

SEC. 2. Any person violating the provisions of this Ordinance shall upon conviction be punished by a fine of ten ($10.00) pesos for the first offense; a fine of twenty ($20.00) pesos for the second offense; and a fine of thirty ($30.00) pesos or imprisonment of not more than ten (10) days or both at the discretion of the Court for the succeeding violations;

SEC. 3. If the act is committed by a minor who is accompanied by a person of lawful age, the accompanying person shall be presumed to have caused the minor to violate the provisions of this Ordinance and said person shall be held liable therefor;

SEC. 4. This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.
