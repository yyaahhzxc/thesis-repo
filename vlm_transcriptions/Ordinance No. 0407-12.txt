**ORDINANCE NO. 0407-12**  
**Series of 2012**  

**AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL ESTABLISHMENTS, WHETHER COMMERCIAL, INDUSTRIAL OR FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS, WITH CAPITALIZATION OF NOT LESS THAN THREE MILLION PESOS (3,000,000.00), INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO, BANKS, PAWNSHOPS, RESTAURANTS, SHOPPING MALLS, HOTELS AND INNS, EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS, HOSPITAL AND MEDICAL INSTITUTIONS, GAMING COCKPITS (SAMAHAN NG PAGKUKULTURA AT TINDERAN) AND TERTIARY EDUCATION OF 45E AND SEA OPERATING WITHIN THE CITY OF DAVAO, TO INSTALL, OPERATE, AND MAINTAIN CLOSED CIRCUIT TELEVISION (CCTV) CAMERAS IN PUBLIC/COMMON AREA/S**

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE CCTV ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. PURPOSE - To help curb the rate of criminality and other illegal activities in Davao City and to ensure that the life, liberty and property of the Dabawenyo populace are safeguarded, protected, access to new technologies of safety measures, such as the installation of Closed Circuit Television (CCTV) cameras, is deemed necessary by the City Government of Davao.

Thus, this Ordinance is promulgated to prescribe the guidelines for the implementation of installation of Closed Circuit Television (CCTV) cameras in public/common areas by all business establishments, whether commercial, industrial or financial institutions operating within Davao City in order to facilitate compliance herewith.

SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF POLICY - As promulgated by the Constitution in Article II, Section 6 thereof, the maintenance of peace and order, the protection of life, liberty and property, and the promotion of the general welfare are essential for the enjoyment by all the people of the blessings of democracy.

Furthermore, the Local Government Code provides under Section 458 (a) (1) (ii) of its Implementing Rules that it is the power, duty and function of the Sangguniang Panlungsod to maintain peace and order by enforcing measures to prevent and suppress lawlessness, disorder, riot, violence, rebellion or sedition and impose penalties for the violation of the said ordinances.

The Local Government of Davao City, pursuant to the provisions of Section 16 of the same Code, shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental to its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare and shall have unassailable powers to ensure compliance and continuity of the Ordinance from all concerned establishments.

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following shall be defined as follows:

a. CCTV System - Closed Circuit Television (CCTV) is a television system in which signals are not publicly distributed. Cameras are connected to television monitors, which are placed in a limited area, and are commonly used in surveillance systems;

b. Commercial Establishments - an establishment used for commercial purposes, such as hotels and inns, bars, restaurants, private offices, fitness clubs, offices, retail stores, shopping malls, department stores, supermarkets, auto and boat dealerships, and other like establishments with common areas;

c. Financial Establishments - an establishment that focuses on dealing with financial transactions, such as banks, financing companies, trust companies, insurance companies, investments, loans and deposits, and money-changers and the like;

d. Industrial Establishments - an establishment where one or several people are employed in manufacturing, fabricating, altering, making up into another material for sale, use, or consumption irrespective of the products.

e. Public/Common Area - an indoor or outdoor area of commercial, financial, and industrial establishments, to which the public have access by right or by invitation, expressed or implied, whether by payment of money or not, which includes, but is not limited to, areas designated for public use, places used for putting on clothes provided, that indoor areas shall not include comfort rooms and other private rooms or areas designated for confidential use of clients such as safety deposit box areas and the like;

f. 30p - a progressive format and produces video at 30 frames per second (FPS). Progressive (non-interlaced) scanning mimics a film camera's frame-by-frame image capture;

g. 60p - a progressive format and is used in high-end High Definition Television (HDTV) systems;

SECTION 5. SCOPE - The provisions of this Ordinance shall cover all business establishments, whether commercial, industrial, or financial institutions as defined under this Act, with a declared minimum capital of Three Million Pesos (P3,000,000.00) operating within the City of Davao;

SECTION 6. REGULATION - No business establishments covered by this Ordinance with a declared minimum capital of Three Million Pesos (P3,000,000.00) operating within the City of Davao shall be allowed to operate without installing, operating, and maintaining a CCTV System. The CCTV cameras shall be installed in public/common areas/s of the business establishment;

SECTION 7. GUIDELINES - All business establishments covered by this Act with a declared minimum capital of Three Million Pesos (P3,000,000.00) operating within the City of Davao are hereby mandated to install and operate (1) CCTV system with a resolution of 480 lines, 640 x 480 pixels, with scanning/panning capability and with Infra Red (IR) range of 300 meters for monitoring both within and without its premises, particularly in its public/common areas/s. The owners and managers of the establishment shall assign an individual to observe the television monitor/s during business hours and immediately report and inform the owner/manager and the local authorities of any crime/s offense committed during the watch.

The Business Bureau of the City shall send duly authorized representatives to inspect the premises of all business establishments covered by this Ordinance prior to the issuance of their business permits;

In the meantime, existing establishment/s covered by this Act with duly issued permit/s shall be granted strictly a period of thirty (30) days from written notification from the Office of the Business Bureau to comply with the requirements of this Ordinance. An extension of another thirty (30) days may be granted under reasonable grounds, provided, that no further extensions shall be allowed thereafter;

--- [Page 4] ---
The Business Bureau, through its authorized representatives, shall inspect an existing establishment that already complied with this Ordinance at least twice a year without prior notice to ensure compliance with this Act.

Failure to comply with the herein guidelines shall subject the covered establishment to be penalized in accordance with the provisions of this Ordinance.

SECTION 8. MANDATE TO SAFE SURVEILLANCE TAPES, DISCS, OR MEDIA - The owners of the business establishments that have complied within this Ordinance are mandated to safe-keep all records of the surveillance tapes, discs, or media taken within their premises for at least thirty (30) days. Thereafter, the owners of the said business establishments may already discard the records of the surveillance tapes, discs, or media covering the period within the said business establishments, the owner shall allow the verification and duplication of the records of the surveillance tapes, discs, or media covering the period when the said crime was committed. The investigating authorities who are hereby enjoined to issue an acknowledgment receipt for the original tapes, discs, or media surrendered for investigation and/or prosecution purposes;

SECTION 9. PROHIBITED ACT AND PERAL CLAUSE - Business establishments violating any of the provisions of this Act shall suffer the following penalties, to wit:

1. Non-issuance of business permit for non-compliance of Section 6 and Section 7 (first paragraph) of this Ordinance for new establishments;

2. Non-renewal of business permit for non-compliance of Section 6 and Section 7 (first paragraph) of this Ordinance for existing establishments;

3. A fine of Five Thousand Pesos [P5,000.00] in case of failure to install and maintain the CCTV System after the affected establishment has been granted the period of thirty (30) days, including extension to comply with this Ordinance. An extension of another thirty (30) days may be granted under reasonable grounds, provided that no further extensions shall be allowed thereafter;

4. A fine of Three Thousand Pesos [P3,000.00] in case of failure to provide a person to observe the CCTV monitor/s and the imposition of the same fine for each commission of the same offenses thereafter;

5. A fine of Five Thousand Pesos [P5,000.00] in case of failure to save the records of the surveillance tapes, discs, or media and/or voluntary surrender of such surveillance tapes, discs, or media under Section 8 of this Ordinance;

In case of single proprietorship business, the owner shall be made liable for the violation of the Act;

In like manner, the members of the Board of Directors/Trustees and partners shall be made personally liable for the violation of this Act in instances involving juridical entities;

--- [Page 5] ---
In the absence of evidence to the contrary, the manager and/or operator shall be presumed to be the owner of the business under this Act.

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This ordinance shall take effect within fifteen days (15) days after official publication in any local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City;

ENACTED, December 4, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
