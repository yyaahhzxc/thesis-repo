AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND AMONG HEDCOR, INC., AND THE CITY OF DAVAO, DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE REQUEST OF HEDCOR, INC. --- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0167-19

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGNS FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO AND BE SIGNED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE REQUEST OF HEDCOR, INC. FOR THE CITY TO OPEN TWO TRUST ACCOUNTS PURSUANT TO THE PROVISIONS OF DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY CIRCULAR NO. DC2018-08-0021".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city may delegate and shall delegate to the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sanggunian Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and HEDCOR, Inc., for the establishment of two ($2) trust accounts, to authorize the Department of Energy (DOE) to download all accumulated Development and Livelihood Fund (DLF) and Restoration Watershed Management Health and/or Environment Enhancement Fund (RWMHEEF) directly to the City and enable HEDCOR to remit financial benefits directly to the City Government of Davao.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provisions of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 17, 2019, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

Secretary to the Sanggunian Panlungsod

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Between the Hedcor, Inc. and City of Davao

For the Establishment and Administration of Interest-bearing Current Accounts in accordance with DC2018-08-0031 of Hedcor, Inc.

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement (the "Agreement"), made and entered into by and between:

Hedcor, Inc., a private corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the Philippines, with principal office address at C. Bantog Street (formerly Ponciano Reyes St.), Davao City, duly represented by its President and COO, CARLOS RAMON ABOITIZ, hereinafter referred to as the "GenCo/ERD," and

Davao City, a local government unit of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at Davao City duly represented herein by its Mayor, SARA DUERTE-CARPO for Sangguniang under Resolution No. series of and hereinafter referred to as "HB."

GenCo/ERD and HB shall hereinafter be collectively referred to as "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

WITNESSETH: That

WHEREAS, Section 5 (i) of Republic Act No. 7638 (R.A. No. 7638), otherwise known as the "Department of Energy Act of 1992" mandates the Department of Energy (DOE) to devise ways and means of giving direct benefits to the province, city or municipality, specially the community and people affected and equitable and preferential to the region that hosts the energy resource and/or the energy-generating facility;

WHEREAS, Energy Regulations No. 1-94 (ER 1-94) is promulgated by the DOE on 1 August 1994 to operationalize the implementation of section 5 (i) of R.A. No. 7638;

WHEREAS, Energy Regulations ("ER") No. 1-94 implementing Section 5(i) of Republic Act No. 7638, otherwise known as the "Department of Energy Act of 1992," grant financial benefits to the host communities of the energy-generation company and/or energy resources;

WHEREAS, Section 66 of R.A. No. 9136, otherwise known as the "Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001" and Rule 29(A) of its Implementing Rules and Regulations (EPIRA-IRR) require all energy generation companies (GenCos) and/or energy resource developers (ERDs) to provide financial benefits equivalent to one centavo per kilowatt-hour (P0.01/kWh) of the total electricity sold of the generation facility to the region, province, city and municipality and barangay that host the general facilities and the power plant facility, as well as the establishment of corresponding Interest-bearing Current Accounts and the administration thereof by the DOE;

WHEREAS, in accordance with Republic Act No. 8371 (RA 8371) also known as "The Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997," the DOE promulgated Department Circular No. DC2018-03-0005 on 20 March 2018 recognizing the rights of the Indigenous Cultural Communities (ICCCs) and Indigenous Peoples (IPs) to their ancestral domain and the natural resources therein and provides for reasonable share in the development and livelihood fund (DLF) and for the reforestation, watershed management, health and/or environment enhancement fund (RWMEHEF) components of the ER 1-94 funds;

--- [Page 5] ---
WHEREAS, on 07 August 2018, DGF issued Department Circular No. DC2018-08-0021, amending for the purpose Rule 29(1A) of the EPR(A)-R6 to accelerate economic development and to have more effective and efficient utilization of the funds and to enforce the immediate provision of benefits to Host communities. In this regard, by virtue of the transition to an efficient distribution of funds, the Host Communities shall not withhold or delay issuance of the necessary permits required for the operations of the energy resource and/or energy-generating facility;
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 6 of DC2018-08-0021 the financial benefit shall be allocated in the following manner:
"6.2. Twenty-five percent (25%) of one-centavo per kilowatt-hour (P0.0025/kWh) for Development and Livelihood Fund ("DLF"); and Twenty-five percent (25%) of one-centavo per kilowatt-hour (P0.0025/kWh) for Reforestation, Watershed Management, Health and Environment Enhancement Fund ("RWMHEEF");
The DLF and RWMHEEF shall be allocated in the following manner:
6.2.1. Community and People Affected - 5%
6.2.2. Host Barangay/s - 20%
6.2.3. Host Municipality/ies or City/ies - 35%
6.2.4. Host Province/s - 30%
6.2.5. Host ICCs/IPs - 5%
6.2.6. Host Region - 5%
In the absence of Community and People Affected, funds allocated for the same shall form part of the fund for the Host Barangay.
In the absence of ICCs/IPs, the funds allocated for the same shall form part of the fund for the Host Region."
NOW, THEREFORE, in view of the foregoing, the Parties hereby stipulate and agree as follows:
Section I. Scope of Agreement
This Agreement shall cover the establishment and administration by the HB of separate interest-bearing Current Accounts for DLF and RWMHEEF (the "DLF and RWMHEEF Interest-bearing Current Accounts").
Section II. Responsibilities of the Parties
1. The GenCo/ERD shall:
1.1. Remit directly to HB its share of the DLF and RWMHEEF in the manner set forth in DC2018-08-0021;
1.2. The DLF and RWMHEEF shall be remitted quarterly based on the reported Electricity Sales and in accordance with the following:
a) The DLF and RWMHEEF accruing from the billing period 26 June 2017 to 25 December 2018, shall be directly remitted to the HB immediately after the establishment of the trust account by the GenCo/ERD, provided the HB has already established its own interest-bearing Current Accounts;
b) For the succeeding quarterly billing periods, the remittance shall be made within fifteen (15) calendar days after the end of each billing quarter.

--- [Page 6] ---
1.3. Establish separate Interest-bearing Current Accounts for the benefit of the ER 1-94 Funds in accordance with the 1st Advisory on the Implementation of DC2018-08-0021.

1.4. Keep financial benefits in the GenCo/ERD Interest-bearing Current Account if the HB does not resubmit the revised AWP and/or the AWP is not validated on/Before April 15 of each year.

2. The HB shall:

2.1. Be responsible for the opening and maintenance of the DLF and RWMHEEF Interest-bearing Current Accounts in accordance with DC2018-08-0021;

a) If HB has existing DLF and RWMHEEF Interest-bearing Current Accounts, it shall request the Authorized Government Depository Bank (AGDB) to adopt the account names stated below and subsequently issue a certification of the said accounts, its status and current available balance.

HB - GenCo/ERD - DLF (ER 1-94)

HB - GenCo/ERD - RWMHEEF (ER 1-94)

b) If HB does not have existing DLF and RWMHEEF Interest-bearing Current Accounts, it shall establish Interest-bearing Current Accounts specific for DLF and RWMHEEF, under the following account names:

HB - GenCo/ERD - DLF (ER 1-94)

HB - GenCo/ERD - RWMHEEF (ER 1-94)

All costs related to the opening of such Interest-bearing Current Accounts shall be considered as administrative expenses and shall be deducted from the DLF and RWMHEEF Interest-bearing Current Accounts.

2.2. Maintain the required balance in the DLF and RWMHEEF Interest-bearing Current Accounts (the "Maintaining Balancer"), which shall not be withdrawn by HB. The fees and charges relative to the maintenance of such Interest-bearing Current Accounts shall be considered as administrative expenses and shall be deducted from the DLF and RWMHEEF Interest-bearing Current Accounts.

2.3. Prepare and submit to the GenCo/ERD the AWP as provided in Section 10 of Circular No. DC2018-08-0021. The AWP shall be submitted in print and in electronic copy to the GenCo/and/or ERD not later than March 15 of every year. It shall identify the priority tangible projects under the DLF and RWMHEEF that can be implemented within the referred period. The HB shall post the validated AWP in its website and in absence thereof, in two (2) consecutive copies, which shall be supported by a Resolution from the Sanggunian for the LGUs, or Council for the Region and ICCPUs/IPs, certifying that the said list of tangible projects is in accordance with their respective COP, CLUP, RDP, RFFP, ADDSPD, and IPD, whichever is applicable. The AWP shall likewise be submitted with a Program of Works that will serve as supporting document.

2.4. Conduct minimum social preparation activities such as, but not limited to, community consultations and orientations in the preparation of the AWP;

2.5. Accept all remittances made by the GenCo/ERD as deemed certified by the DOE, subject to post audit rules and regulations;

--- [Page 7] ---
2.6. Upon receipt of Financial Benefits the HB must issue an Official Receipt (OR); 
2.7. With prior written notice to the HB/ [HB], the Genco may visit/view the tangible projects completed by the HB/[HB] for documentation purposes. The Genco's visit, however, shall not be deemed as an official inspection of the completed tangible projects, which shall remain the responsibility of the authorized government auditors; 
2.8. Upon written request, the HB/[HB] shall furnish the Genco with a written update on the status of implementation of the tangible projects under the AWP. Such request may only be made once every quarter; 
2.9. The HB/[HB] shall ensure that all tangible projects implemented under the AWP, and funded by ER 1-94 funds, have appropriate markers/signage stating that the project was made possible through the ER 1-94 Fund contribution of Hedcor, Inc. The marker shall be displayed in a conspicuous area where the project is located or implemented. 
Section IV. Effectivity of Agreement This Agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing by both Parties and remain valid and effective unless revoked, modified and/or amended. 
Section V. Amendments Any amendment or modification of this Agreement will only be effective upon written consent by the Parties.
