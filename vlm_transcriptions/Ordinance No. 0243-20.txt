RE: ORDINANCE NO. 0243-20, SERIES OF 2020 entitled "AN ORDINANCE EXTENDING THE DELAYING OF THE SECOND QUARTERLY INSTALLMENT FOR PAYMENT OF BUSINESS TAX; FIRST QUARTERLY INSTALLMENT FOR REAL PROPERTY TAX AND ADDITIONAL LEVY ON REAL PROPERTY FOR THE SPECIAL EDUCATION FUND; AND RENTAL FEES FROM MARCH TO JUNE FOR PUBLIC MARKETS, STATIONERY, ANA PORT, MAGSAYSAY PARK AND PASALUBONG CENTER IN DAVAO CITY, SERIES OF 2020 IN VIEW OF THE THREAT OF THE CORONA VIRUS DISEASE (COVID-19) IN THE PHILIPPINES"

1st INDOORSEMENT  
March 27, 2020

Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject Ordinance, finding the same free from legal infirmity citing Section 167 of RA 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, to quote:

"SECTION 167. Time of Payment. — Unless otherwise provided in this Code, all local taxes, fees, and charges shall be paid within the first twenty (20) days of January or of each subsequent quarter, as the case may be. The sanggunian concerned may, for a justifiable reason or cause, extend the time for payment of such taxes, fees, or charges without surcharges or penalties, but only for a period not exceeding six (6) months."

Hence, it is recommended that the same be approved.

Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE EXTENDING THE DEADLINE OF THE SECOND QUARTERLY INSTALLMENT FOR PAYMENT OF BUSINESS TAX; FIRST AND SECOND INSTALLMENT FOR REAL PROPERTY TAX AND ADDITIONAL TAX ON REAL PROPERTY FOR THE SPECIAL EDUCATION FUND; AND THE INSTALLMENT FROM MARCH TO JUNE FOR PUBLIC MARKETS, DAVAO CITY OVERLAND TRANSPORT TERMINAL (DCCOT), STA. ANA PORT, MAGSAYSAY PARK AND PASALUBONG CENTER TO JUNE 30, 2020 IN VIEW OF THE THREAT OF THE CORONA VIRUS DISEASE (COVID-19) IN THE PHILIPPINES

Ord. No. 0243-20  
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  
SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE EXTENDING THE DEADLINE OF THE SECOND QUARTERLY INSTALLMENT FOR PAYMENT OF BUSINESS TAX, FIRST QUARTERLY INSTALLMENT FOR REAL PROPERTY TAX AND ADDITIONAL LEVY ON REAL PROPERTY FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION FUND; AND RENTAL FEES FROM MARCH TO JUNE FOR PUBLIC MARKETS, DAVAO CITY OVERLAND TRANSPORT TERMINAL (DCOTT), STA. ANA PORT, MAGASYAAY PARK AND PASALUBONG CENTER TO JUNE 30, 2020 IN VIEW OF THE THREAT OF THE CORONA VIRUS DISEASE (COVID-19) IN THE PHILIPPINES."  
SECTION 2. AUTHORITY: Section 167 of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "the Sanggunian concerned may, for a justifiable reason or cause, extend the time for payment of all local taxes, fees or charges without surcharges or penalties, but only for a period not exceeding six (6) months."  
SECTION 3. EXTENSION OF TIME OF PAYMENT: There shall be an extension of the time of the payment for the following, to wit:  
1. Second Quarterly Installment for Local Business Tax from April 20, 2020 to June 30, 2020;  
2. First Quarterly Installment of Real Property Tax and Additional Levy on Real Property for Special Education Fund from March 31, 2020 to June 30, 2020;  
3. Rental Fees for Public Markets, Davao City Overland Transport Terminal (DCOTT), Sta. Ana Port, Magaysay Park covering the period from March to June 2020 from the 20th of the month to June 30, 2020;  
4. Rental Fees for Pasalubong Center covering the period from March to June 2020 from the 10th of the month to June 30, 2020.  
SECTION 4. INTEREST, SURCHARGES AND PENALTIES: No interest, surcharge and penalty shall be imposed on unpaid taxes, fees or charges, if duly paid within the extended period of payment.  
SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full force and effect.  
SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.  
ENACTED, on the 20th day of March 2020, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
