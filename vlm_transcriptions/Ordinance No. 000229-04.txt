ORDINANCE NO. 0229-04 (09/9/9)
Series of 2004

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO.
0209-04-A, SERIES OF 2004 BY EXTENDING
THE CATTIPAN VIA DACUDAO ROUTE FROM
BANGOT STREET TO LZADA STREET

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunan Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3  
Ord. No. 0229-04  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance amending Ordinance No. 0209-04-A, Series of 2004 by extending the Catitipan Via Dacudao Route from Bangoy Street to Lizanda Street";  

SECTION 2. REROUTING SCHEME - Section 6 of Ordinance No. 108 Series of 2000, which states that the Revolving Scheme for the purpose of this Ordinance is in accordance with the Comprehensive Development Plan of the City as well as in line with the City Transportation and Traffic Management Plan, the existing route in the City of Davao with franchise duly issued by the Land Transportation Franchising Regulatory Board (LTFRB) particularly the Catitipan via Dacudao Route is hereby adjusted as follows:  

Catitipan Via Dacudao Avenue Route  
Origin - Catitipan  
Through - C.P. Garcia Highway (Diversion Rd.)  
Turn Left - Buhangin Road  
Turn Right - Del Pilar St.  
Turn Left - Cabagatyo Ave.  
Through - L. Garcia St. (Carpenter)  
Turn Right - R. Magayawan Ave. (Iyaganon)  
Turn Right - Z. Balado St.  
Turn Left - Lizanda St.  
Turn Left - Cabagatyo St.  
Turn Left - Vinzon St.  
Turn Left - Dacudao Ave.  
Through - Buhangin St.  
Turn Right - C.P. Garcia Highway (Diversion Rd.)  
Origin - Catitipan  

SECTION 3. EXTENSION OF ROUTES - Ordinance No. 108, Series of 2000 is hereby amended by extending Catitipan Via Dacudao Route from Street Bangoy to Lizanda Street;  

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any provision of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to any person or circumstances is declared invalid, the remainder of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected by such declaration;  

SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinance or executive order issued by the City Mayor or any provision therein, inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance shall hereby modified or repealed accordingly;  

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall be effective ten (10) days after the posting of a copy of the same in the bulletin boards of the City Hall of Davao, the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, the Hall of Justice of Davao and its publication in a newspaper of general circulation".
