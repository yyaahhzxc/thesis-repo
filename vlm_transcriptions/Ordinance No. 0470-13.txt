**ORDINANCE NO. 0470-13 Series of 2013**

**AN ORDINANCE PROMOTING THE RIGHTS OF INDIVIDUALS ENGAGED IN MATERNAL BOXING COMPETITIONS FOR THE SAFETY AND PROTECTION OF MINORS FROM INJURIES AND TO WELFARE PROTECTION**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be cited as the "Boxers Welfare Ordinance of 2012"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The State values the dignity of the Filipino youth and guarantees their respect for their human rights, recognizing their vital role in nation building. As such, the State shall promote and protect their physical well-being primarily in the realm of sports as a means to foster patriotism and nationalism, promote social progress and promote total human liberation and development;

It is likewise the policy of the State to promote physical education and encourage sports programs, league competitions and amateur sports, including training for international competitions, foster self-discipline, teamwork and excellence for the development of a healthy and alert citizenry.

Section 16 of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary appropriate, incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential for the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdiction, the local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and advancement of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to freedom of speech, encourage and support the development of appropriate and significant scientific research, technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic property and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms shall be defined as follows:

a) "Minor Boxer" - an individual, male or female, below eighteen (18) years of age, who fights in a boxing match, contest, competition, or exhibition.

b) "Boxing Match or Contest" - an engagement in which the minor boxer strives earnestly in good faith to score. For purposes of this Ordinance, this shall be understood as a sport in which minor boxers fight and score points for scoring clean blows to the head and body above the belt with three [3] blows. Matches shall consist of two [2] to three [3] rounds of two [2] minutes each. It includes boxing competitions or exhibitions. This shall exclude professional matches.

c) "Manager" - shall mean any person, who directly or indirectly directs or administers the affairs of any minor boxer;

d) "Official Coach" - refers to the person, who supervises the minor boxer's training and development, accompanies the minor boxer during a boxing match, and who, for purposes of this Ordinance, may be regarded as a person exercising special parental authority while the said minor boxer is under his custody and care;

--- [Page 5] ---
e) "Organizer or 'Promoter' - refers to the person, accredited by the Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD), primarily responsible for organizing, promoting and producing an amateur boxing contest or exhibition. In the case of a corporate promoter, this shall include any officer, employee or stockholder thereof, who produces, arranges or stages any boxing contest or exhibition."

f) "Contact sparring" - shall mean practice boxing in which a boxer receives, lands or attempts to land blows from or another person as part of a practice or training exercise.

g) "Licensed Physician or Doctor" - shall mean an individual licensed in the Philippines to engage in the general practice of medicine and surgery.

h) "Clearance" - refers to the official documents by the City Health Officer or his authorized and qualified representative to the parent or guardian of a minor boxer, after compliance with the guidelines issued by this Ordinance as well as supplemental guidelines issued by the City Health Officer (CHO) relative to this Ordinance;

i) "Certificate of Fitness to Participate" - refers to the official documents attested to by the City Health Officer or his authorized and qualified government representative who is a licensed physician after having conducted a thorough medical examination on a minor boxer and has been satisfied that the minor boxer is physically fit to engage in a boxing contest, match or exhibition;

j) "Certificate of Actual Training" - refers to the official documents issued by the accredited or certified coach or trainer who has personally trained, supervised or managed the minor boxer. Said Certification shall state the extent of actual sparring, practice or exercise made by the minor boxer which is necessary and sufficient for the minor boxer to undergo prior to engaging in a boxing contest, match or exhibition;

k) "Official Trainers" - refers to the person, accredited by a legitimate boxing sport organization accredited or recognized by the National or Local Government;

l) "Legitimate boxing associations" - a boxing association accredited or recognized by the National or Local Government;

SECTION 5. CITY HEALTH OFFICER/TOURNAMENT PHYSICIAN

A. Duty of City Health Officer to issue Clearance. The City Health Officer or his authorized and qualified government representative, shall issue a certificate of fitness to participate attesting to the physical fitness and condition of the minor boxer to engage in a boxing contest or exhibition which shall be before the scheduled weigh-in but not more than one week before the fight;

B. The City Health Officer shall use the medical examination form as provided in ANNEX A attached in this ordinance, as a guide in determining

whether or not to issue a certificate of fitness to participate attesting to the medical fitness and condition of the applicant.

C. The City Health Officer shall institute a program that shall ensure that a certificate of fitness to fight shall be issued at a reasonable time on any given day of the week and before the scheduled boxing match of the boxer but not more than one (1) week before the scheduled fight of the boxer,

SECTION 6. CONTINUOUS PRESENCE OF A TOURNAMENT PHYSICIAN

No boxing contest, match or exhibition shall be conducted without the presence or attendance of a qualified doctor or physician continuously at the ringside and shall not leave the ringside until the end of the last bout,

SECTION 7. DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF A PHYSICIAN

A. The Physician shall terminate any boxing contest, match or exhibition if in the opinion of such physician, a minor boxer has received severe punishment or is in danger of serious physical injury;

B. In the event of any serious physical injury, the physician shall immediately render any emergency treatment necessary, recommend further treatment or hospitalization if required, and fully report the entire matter to the City Health Officer not later than twenty-four (24) hours from the time the minor boxer sustained such injury;

C. The physician may likewise enter the ring at any time during a boxing match, contest or exhibition and may terminate the same if in his expert opinion, the same is necessary to prevent severe physical injury to a minor boxer,

SECTION 8. TIME OF WEIGH IN: The time of the weigh-in shall not be less than six (6) hours before the scheduled boxing match.

SECTION 9. DAVAO CITY SPORTS DEVELOPMENT DIVISION - DUTIES, POWERS, AND FUNCTIONS

The Davao City Sports Development Division (CDSDD) of the Office of the City Mayor shall issue a "permit" for the conduct of all boxing matches, competitions, contests, and exhibitions;

The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall likewise provide for the reasonable requirements for the issuance of a permit to conduct boxing matches, competitions, contests and exhibitions three (3) months after the approval of this Ordinance. It shall include, but not limit itself, the names of the boxers who will compete in the boxing match, competition, contest, or event, and the net prizes to be given to the competing boxers;

No Permit shall be valid without the above-mentioned requirements;

The applicant for the permit shall also submit to the Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD), before the scheduled fight of each fighter, the following:

[1] "Certificate of Fitness to Participate" of a boxer issued by the City Health Office;
[2] "Parental Consent of the Parent or Guardian" of a minor boxer; and
[3] "Certificate of Actual Training" of a boxer issued by a certified or accredited coach or accredited trainer who has personally trained, supervised or managed the boxer for the intended date of the match or contest. The certification of actual training shall likewise indicate the number of hours and extent of actual training practice or exercise made or undergone by a boxer that is deemed necessary and sufficient to engage in a boxing match.

The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall institute a process by which an organizer or promoter shall be able to submit the following:

[1] "Certificate of Fitness to Participate" of a boxer issued by the City Health Office;
[2] "Parental Consent of the Parent or Guardian" of a minor boxer; and
[3] "Certificate of Actual Training" of a boxer issued by a certified or accredited coach or accredited trainer who has personally trained, supervised or managed the boxer for the intended date of the match or contest before the scheduled fight of the boxer at a reasonable time on any given day of the week.

SECTION 10. Only the organizers of the boxing match, competition, who must be of legal age, shall be allowed to apply for a permit from the Davao City Sports Development Division of the Office of the City Mayor. In case of a corporation, association, cooperative, or group, unorganized or organized, the officers shall be deemed as the organizers;

SECTION 11. The City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall provide the guidelines for the development, training and preparation of the boxers that shall ensure their safety and protection and the rules and regulations for the conduct of boxing matches, contests and competitions. It shall also provide for the other duties and responsibilities of promoters, organizers, referees, judges, physicians and other boxing officials, not provided by this Ordinance, that shall ensure the proper management of a boxing tournament, contests, or competitions and the safety and welfare of the boxers competing.

SECTION 12. The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall provide for the guidelines for the identification and mandatory use of proper safety gears such as boxing gloves, head guard, bandages, gun shields, and cup protector to be used by the minor boxers that shall ensure the boxers' safety and protection;

SECTION 13. Safety gears such as boxing gloves, headguard, bandages, gun shields, and cup protector to be used by the boxers in a boxing match, contest or competition shall only be approved by the Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD). Only boxing gloves, head guard, gun shields and cup protectors and other safety gears and equipment approved by the Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall be used by boxers,

--- [Page 8] ---
**SECTION 14.** The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall inform the organizer of their duty to ensure that every boxer shall be accompanied by his official coach or official trainer at all times during the duration of the match;

**SECTION 15.** The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall require the organizer to submit the names of the coach or trainer submitted by the manager of the minor boxers to the Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD). The names of the coach or trainer submitted by the organizer to the Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall be deemed the official coach or official trainer of the boxer;

**SECTION 16.** The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall specify the mandatory training requirements of a boxer before he can compete in boxing matches to ensure that the minor boxer is properly trained and prepared to compete in a boxing match. It shall mandate the number of hours of actual training practice or exercise by a boxer that is necessary and sufficient to engage in a boxing match, contest, or competition; regulate the conduct of sparring matches during training sessions and identify allowable sessions;

**SECTION 17.** The Davao City Sports Development Division shall accredit all coaches, trainers, referees, except those already accredited by legitimate boxing associations;

**SECTION 18.** The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall issue the requirements for the accreditation of coaches, trainers, referees, organizers and other boxing officials not accredited by legitimate boxing associations;

**SECTION 19.** The Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD) shall encourage the sport of boxing among young individuals and simultaneously protect their welfare and ensure their safety when participating in the sport.

**SECTION 20.** The Rules of the ABA/International Amateur Boxing Association (ANEXE B) shall serve as guidelines for the development, training, and preparation of the guidelines for the development, training, and preparation of minor boxers. They shall ensure their safety and protection and for the conduct of boxing matches, contests, and competitions including the conduct and management of a boxing match, competition, and contest;

**SECTION 21.** No boxer signed by a minor or boxer or his/her parent shall entrench the legal and moral responsibility of the promoter or organizer to attend to his/her medical needs, in the event of his unfortunate injury or death due to the fault or negligence of the promoter or manager or by individuals under the promoter's or manager's control or supervision;

**SECTION 22.** UNLAWFUL/PROHIBITED ACTS

a. It shall be unlawful for any person, organizer, or promoter to conduct any boxing match, contest, competition, or any other similar activity without

the presence or attendance of a licensed doctor or physician in every boxing match from start to end.

b. It shall be unlawful for organizers, promoters, or person to conduct any boxing match, contest, competition, or other similar activities without the presence or attendance of an accredited referee in every boxing match;

c. It shall be unlawful for any person, organizer, or promoter to conduct or hold any boxing match, contest, competition, or other similar activities without securing accreditation and proper permit from the Davao City Sports Development Division (DCSDD). For the purpose of conducting a boxing match, contest, or competition, or other similar activities shall include the conduct of fight outside a boxing ring, the use of fighting equipment which involves the use of fists, elbows, knees, legs, elbows, or any part of the bodies (knees, legs, elbows, etc.) for attacking the opponent;

d. It shall be unlawful for any person, coach, organizer, or promoter to allow, permit or force minor boxers to engage or participate in any boxing match, contest, competition, or other activities without being properly equipped with the Davao City Sports Development Division's (DCSDD) approved boxing safety gears such as boxing gloves, head guard, bandages, gum shields, and cup protector to be used by the boxers;

e. It shall be unlawful for any person, coach, trainer, organizer, or promoter to allow, influence, permit or force minor boxers to engage in a boxing match, contest, competition, or contact sparring;

f. It shall be unlawful for any person, trainer, coach, organizer, or promoter to allow, influence, permit or force minor boxers to engage in a boxing match without the required Certificate of Fitness to Fight issued by the City Health Office and parental consent if the boxer is a minor;

g. It shall be unlawful for any person, organizer, or promoter to allow, influence, entice, advise, permit or force minor boxers to engage or participate in any boxing match, contest, competition, or other similar activities without being accompanied by his official trainer or official coach;

h. It shall be unlawful for any person, organizer, or person to allow, influence, entice, permit, or force a boxer to compete in a weight class if the boxer exceeds the weight limit of the weight class he will be completing in at the time of the weigh-in unless the excess weight is not greater than half (1/2) kilo;

i. It shall be unlawful for any trainers or coach to issue a false or untruthful Certificate of Training to a boxer;

j. It shall be unlawful for any person to act as an official trainer, coach, or referees during a boxing match contest, or competition, without being accredited by the Davao City Sports Development Division or by any legitimate boxing association;

k. It shall be unlawful for any person to force, influence, or entice minors to conduct a boxing match, contest, or competition, which actually lead to the holding of a boxing match, contest, or competition without the proper permit from the Davao City Sports Development Division;

It shall be, likewise, unlawful for any person to conduct a sparring session not permitted by the Davao City Sports Development Division;

SECTION 23. PENALTIES

a. The penalty for the commission of the prohibited acts as enumerated in Section 22 (A-1) shall be punishable by an imprisonment of one (1) year and a fine of Five Thousand Pesos [P5,000.00] for every violation thereof;

b. When the offender is a corporation, partnership or association, the officer or employee thereof who is directly responsible for the violation of this provision shall suffer the penalty imposed upon groups, organized or unorganized, the persons or officers directly responsible for the violation of this provision shall be held liable;

SECTION 24. REPEALING CLAUSE - Ordinances, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 25. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason or reasons, any part or provision of this ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 26. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall be posted at prominent places at City Hall, as the case may be, for a minimum period of three (3) consecutive week and shall take effect after publication of the same in a newspaper of general circulation.
