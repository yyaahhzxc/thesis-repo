Ord. No. 0284-15

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO CITY MAYOR RODRIGO R. DUERTE TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO (CGD), THE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE PHILIPPINE COMMISSION ON WOMEN AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE LOCAL GENDER AND DEVELOPMENT RESOURCE PROGRAM IN DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (i) (vi) of the Local Government Code provides to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants, pursuant to Section 16 of the Code, the city may enter into and sign for and in behalf of the city in all its business transactions and sign in its name any contracts, obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT - The Partnership Agreement refers to the instrument to be executed by the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Commission on Women relative to the implementation of the Local Gender and Development Resource Program. The main goal of this program is to empower women and promote gender equality.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - City Mayor Rodrigo R. Duterte is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Partnership Agreement relative to the implementation of the Local Gender and Development Resource Program in Davao City.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ERECTED: February 10, 2015, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.
