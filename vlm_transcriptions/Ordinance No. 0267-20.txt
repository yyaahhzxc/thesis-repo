Memorandum of Agreement Re: La Vida Inn as Quarantine Area for Covid-19 patients

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL PERSONS BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement, made and entered into by and between:

LA DIVA INN, a corporation, organized and existing by virtue of the by laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address in J. Rodriguez Ave., Maa Crossing, Davao City, represented herein by its Chairman/President, SAMUEL UY, Filipino, of legal age, residing in Davao City hereinafter referred to as the "OWNER";

-and-

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit, organized and existing by virtue of laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with official address at City Hall Building, City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented by its City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "LGU";

WITNESSETH: That

WHEREAS, the entire country is under the state of National Public Health Emergency by virtue of Proclamation No. 92 and calls for immediate decisive action of the government to contain the transmission of COVID-19, through healthcare, medical tests, treatment and quarantine, among others;

WHEREAS, COVID-19 remains a threat to public health and national security which can undermine the social, economic and well-being of the society;

WHEREAS, among the measures undertaken by the LGU to contain the spread of COVID 19 is the isolation of the patient and those under monitoring. For this purpose, the LGU is in need of available buildings or spaces that will serve as quarantining/isolation area for those affected with the present pandemic, to include the frontiers.

WHEREAS, recognizing the threats brought about by the COVID 19 pandemic to the well-being of the Filipinos, the OWNER desires to assist and support the LGU to contain the spread of the said COVID-19 virus by offering LA DIVA INN, as isolation or quarantine area free of charge.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties hereto agree the following:

1. AREA, PURPOSE AND MANAGEMENT

1.1 The Parties hereby designate that the building of the OWNER known as LA DIVA INN located along J. Rodriguez Avenue, Maa Crossing, Davao City, as Quarantine Area (hereinafter referred to as the "AREA"), primarily for the residents of Davao City who are affected with the pandemic, to be managed and operated by the LGU.

--- [Page 12] ---
2. TERM / DURATION

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on 18 March 2020 coinciding with the actual entry and use of the AREA for a term of three (3) months.

2.2 This Agreement may be renewed or extended for a similar period of three (3) months, under mutually acceptable terms and conditions, subject to the requirements of public health and the declared ECQ period. The LGU shall inform the OWNER at least fifteen (15) days before the expiration of this Agreement of its intention to renew and extend.

3. THE OWNER

3.1 Make available and allows the LA DIVA INN as Quarantine Area for COVID 19 patients (suspect and probable cases) as determined beforehand by the LGU.

3.2 Allows the City Government of Davao to introduce improvements on the AREA for the purpose of converting it into an isolation facility and to bring beds, beddings and other fixtures inside the AREA as needed.

3.3 Allow the personnel of the LGU to have access to the AREA anytime of the day and night including the use of water and electricity.

3.4 Subject to prior notice, may enter the AREA during reasonable hours of the day for the purpose of inspection, maintenance and repair of any work necessary for the preservation, conservation, improvement of the AREA.

4. THE LGU

4.1 Undertake to prepare the AREA for quarantine purpose following the guidelines issued by the Department of Health and other appropriate body.

4.2 Provide food, laundry, medical and other necessary services to the patients.

4.3 Keep and maintain the AREA in a clean, safe and sanitary condition.

4.4 Provide medical teams and support staff to ensure that all frontliners in the AREA shall use personal protective equipment (PPE) to prevent exposure to hazards that cause serious injury or illness, including but not limited to masks, gloves, etc.

4.5 Assume all actual usage costs of consumption of all utilities and any and all services i.e. electric, telephone, water, security, etc. required in the AREA and pay for all charges for these utilities and services as they fall due.

4.6 Monitor and supervise the AREA to ensure the security, well-being, health of the frontliners and the patients.

4.7 Within fifteen (15) days after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, it shall cause the cleaning, sanitizing and disinfecting the AREA. It shall surrender and return the premises in as good condition as the premises were

--- [Page 13] ---
actually found at the beginning of the use/occupancy, ordinary wear and tear excepted.
4.8 It shall comply with the rules on waste management and ensure that no hazardous materials, toxic wastes and chemicals are left in the AREA upon expiration or termination of this Agreement.
4.9 Bear the cost of repairs necessary to preserve and maintain the AREA in such good, serviceable and fit condition during the effectivity of this Agreement.
4.10 Not keep, deposit or store in the AREA any noxious, toxic or flammable materials or substances or any other substances or materials which may constitute a fire, environmental safety hazard.
5. THIRD PARTY LIABILITY
5.1 The LGU during its occupancy of the AREA shall hold the OWNER free and harmless from any and all damages, liabilities or responsibilities to any person, natural or juridical, or to any property arising out of or as a consequence of the use of the AREA by LGU, its agents, employees except when such damages, liabilities or responsibilities are due to the fault, or negligence of the OWNER, its officers, employees, representatives and/or agents, in which case, the OWNER shall be liable for such loss, damage and/or injury.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties hereby affix their signatures, this day of May, 2020, at Davao City.
