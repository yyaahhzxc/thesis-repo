ORDINANCE NO. 0237-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE APPLICATION OF ACCENDO COMMERCIAL CORPORATION FOR THE RECLASSIFICATION/REDESIGNATION OF CONSERVATION LAND CONSISTING OF ONE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED SEVENTEEN THOUSAND TWO, ONE HUNDRED THIRTY-TWO (1,517,232) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, COVERED BY VARIOUS TITLES FROM TOURISM DEVELOPMENT ZONE (TDZ) TO PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT (PUD), PRESERVING THE 142-SQUARE METER IDENTIFIED AS CONSERVATION ZONE (CZ) COVERED BY TRANSFER CERTIFICATE OF TITLE NUMBER T- 402262, LOT NO. 18, BLOCK 1, BARANGAY MARAPANGI AND CATIGAN, FORIL DISTRICT, THIS CITY

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0237-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE APPLICATION OF ACCENDO COMMERCIAL CORPORATION FOR THE RECLASSIFICATION/REZONING OF A PARCEL OF LAND CONSISTING OF THE MENTIONED FIVE HUNDRED SEVENTEEN THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED THIRTY-TWO (1,517,232) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, COVERED BY VARIOUS TITLES FROM TOURISM DEVELOPMENT ZONE (TDZ) TO PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT (PUD), PRESERVING THE 142-SQUARE METER IDENTIFIED AS CONSERVATION ZONE (CZ) COVERED BY TRANSFER CERTIFICATE OF TITLE NO. T-405163, LOCATED IN BARANGAYS MARAPANGI AND CATIGAN, TORIL DISTRICT, THIS CITY"

SECTION 2. SCOPE - The property subject of reclassification is covered by various titles enumerated as follows: Transfer Certificate of Titles Nos. T-33189, T-421606, T-419912, T-417873, T-417874, T-405162, T-405163, T-419516, T-146-2017024056, T-146-2017024227, T-146-2017024228, T-146-2017013744, T-146-2016007961, T-146-2016007687 and T-146-2017013745. With the exclusion of the 142 square meters classified as Conservation Zone which is part of T-405163, the total area for reclassification is reduced to 1,517,232 square meters instead of the original area of 1,517,374 square meters.

SECTION 3. CLASSIFICATION - The existing zoning classification of the abovementioned property is Tourism Development Zone (TDZ) to be reclassified as Planned Unit Development (PUD).

SECTION 4. USE - The property being requested for reclassification is covered by the conceptual master plan for a proposed mixed-used area under the name "PROJECT STALLION".

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, any part or provision not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

Enacted, March 3, 2020, by three-fourths (3/4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
