MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is made and entered into and executed this day of 2020, at City of Davao City by and between:

DAVAO CITY, a public corporation duly established and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at Davao City duly represented herein by its City Mayor, the HONORABLE SARA DUERTE;

and

KABANG KALIKASAN NG PILIPINAS FOUNDATION INC. (KKPFII), also known as the World Wide Fund for Nature – Philippines (WWF-Philippines), an organization existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at the 4th Floor, JBD Plaza, #85 Mindanao Avenue, Brgy. Bagong Pag-asa, Quezon City, represented by its President and Chief Executive Officer, Mr. JOSE ANGELITO M. PALMA; Davao City and KKPFII are hereinafter collectively referred to as "Parties" and individually as "Party".

WITNESSETH: That—

WHEREAS, KKPFII, a national organization of the World Wide Fund for Nature (the "WWF"), is a non-profit, non-governmental organization engaged in the conservation and preservation of the environment in selected areas of the Philippines, including Davao Gulf where it is currently working with partners to establish a network of marine protected areas;

WHEREAS, DAVAO CITY is a local government unit and a highly urbanized city politically subdivided into 182 barangays, through which the Davao River flows and which is bounded on the east by Davao Gulf, which gulf is an important priority conservation site of the Coral Triangle with its rich marine biodiversity;

WHEREAS, KKPFII, together with other four WWF national offices in Asia, is implementing pilot projects that are aligned with the global movement for "No Plastic in Nature" initiative of the WWF network to mobilize and engage citizens to address major leakage points in cities through scalable actions and bring stakeholders together to share knowledge and provide solutions to reduce marine litter and microplastics;

WHEREAS, KKPFII, building upon its current marine conservation work in Davao Gulf, desires to work with cities and municipalities within the gulf such as Davao City through policy intervention, corporate engagement, entrepreneurial solutions and education and citizen science, to reduce plastic waste leaking into the Davao Gulf that is rich with marine biodiversity and sources of food and income of local communities;

WHEREAS, Davao City, through its Davao City Ecological Solid Waste Management Board, implements policies and programs to manage its solid wastes, as mandated by the general welfare clause of the Local Government Code and the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act;

WHEREAS, DAVAO CITY and KKPFII share a common objective of conserving the Davao Gulf and reducing plastic pollution to stop the leakage into riverways and ocean;

--- [Page 13] ---
WHEREAS, DAVAO CITY and KKPFI have agreed to work together as partners to reduce plastic pollution in the city and address leakage into Davao Gulf that affects the marine life, human health and livelihood of people in order to protect our oceans, our resources and our pledge towards sustainable development.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of the mutual covenants hereinafter set forth, the Parties hereby set forth its understanding to work together to reduce plastic pollution on the following terms and conditions:
ARTICLE I - PLASTIC SMART CITIES PILOT PROJECT
The parties shall cooperate and collaborate together to implement the Plastic Smart Cities Project that will run until June 2021 subject to renewal, depending on funds availability. Project intentions (e.g. livelihood, education and citizen science) to be determined and stipulated in an action plan based on the scoring study results – are designed to accomplish the main objectives of reducing plastic pollution in the city and stopping the leakage into Davao Gulf that is rich with marine biodiversity and a source of food and income of local communities.
ARTICLE II - ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
1. DAVAO CITY, through its Davao City Solid Waste Management Board, shall:
a) work together with KKPFI, to plan, identify and jointly implement scalable solutions relating to policy, business engagement, entrepreneurship, education and citizen science to reduce plastic pollution in Davao River and the Davao Gulf, including setting benchmarks which will guide project results and impact;
b) designate a focal person from the City Environment and Natural Resources Office who will work closely with KKPFI to oversee the implementation of project activities and with whom KKPFI can coordinate, communicate and seek assistance with to implement activities;
c) assist KKPFI in engaging other stakeholders in Davao City and the Davao Gulf such as but not limited to (a) the business and commercial sector to reduce single use plastic, including participating in KKPFI's Ayoko ng Plastik campaign, and redesign packaging materials; (b) the entrepreneurs and communities to develop innovative entrepreneurial solutions that are also income-generating for communities; (c) the public sector to co-vent and co-organize learning camps for education and behavior change campaigns; (d) government agencies such as on mapping open dumpsites in or near waterways, characterizing marine litter, etc.; (d) barangays and the sangguniang panglungsod, as well as the Davao City Ecological Solid Waste Management Board itself to formulate and implement appropriate policies in support of project objectives;
d) attend/send representatives to project or related meetings and stakeholders' presentation events,
e) participate in technical webinars and conference calls in relation to the project, as may be needed or appropriate;
f) endeavor to become part of the global initiative of Plastic Smart Cities (PSC) by signing up to the PSC Charter by June 2020;
g) perform such other acts deemed necessary in the effective and efficient implementation of this Project.

2. KKPFII shall:

a) work together with Davao City, including organizing a planning meeting, to plan, identify, and jointly implement scalable solutions relating to policy, business engagement, entrepreneurship, education, and citizen science to reduce plastic pollution into Davao River and the Davao Gulf, including setting baselines by which to assess for project results and impact;

b) designate a project manager who will liaise with Davao City, through its designated focal person, to ensure the smooth implementation of project activities;

c) in close coordination with Davao City, engage the various sectors such as the Davao City Chamber of Commerce, the Department of Education, and high schools students, the entrepreneurs and innovators as well as communities, to reduce single use plastic and redesign packaging materials, where feasible, to develop innovative technological and entrepreneurial solutions, and to organize learning sessions and workshops for members leading to youth action or citizen science in support of project objectives;

d) use data and information received from Davao City solely for purposes of project objectives;

e) include as government partner the name of Davao City in relevant Plastic Smart Cities campaign and press releases, if any;

f) support the Davao City's information, education activities in relation to the project by sharing outreach materials;

g) support Davao City's bid to become part of the global initiative of Plastic Smart Cities (PSC) as it signs up to the PSC Charter by June 2020;

h) perform such other activities deemed necessary in the effective and efficient implementation of the project.

ARTICLE III - EFFECTIVITY

This memorandum of understanding shall take effect upon signing hereof by the Parties and shall continue to be in full force and effect for a period of three years until June 2021, unless terminated, modified and/or renewed by mutual consent of the herein Parties.
