Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject Ordinance, with the information that this office finds no legal infirmity therein.

Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, authorizes the passage of supplemental budget in the following instances:
(1) Funds actually available to support the change;
(2) The change is covered by new revenue sources;
(3) Change is due to state of calamity in the area (Section 321 of RA 7160).

In the case at bar, the enactment of Supplemental Budget No. 1, which causes some changes the annual budget is covered with sufficient funds as certified by the City Treasurer.

In view thereof, approval of the ordinance is hereby recommended.

OSMUNDO P. VILLANUEVA JR.

SECTION 1. TITLE. SECTION 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS. The amounts herein appropriated for the projects indicated therein shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget shall conform with the existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules, regulations policies and guidelines as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential Directives;

SECTION 7. USE OF FUNDS. The use of funds under Supplemental Budget No. 1 of the City Government of Davao, for Calendar Year 2020, utilized in violation of Title 5, Local Fiscal Administration, as embodied in RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, shall subject the erring officials and employees to disciplinary action or to appropriate legal measures under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines. The use of funds under this Supplemental Budget shall conform with the existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules, regulations, policies and guidelines of the Commission on Audit (COA), Department of Budget and Management (DBM) and Procurement Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential Directives;

SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provisions hereof, which are not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY. The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
