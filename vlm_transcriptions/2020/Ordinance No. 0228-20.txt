AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND DAVAO LIGHT AND POWER COMPANY (DLPC) FOR THE POLE USAGE OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT'S CCTV SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM

Ord. No. 0228-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND DAVAO LIGHT AND POWER COMPANY (DLPC) FOR THE POLE USAGE OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT'S CCTV SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code, provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts and all obligations and such other document upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between Davao Light and Power Company and the City Government of Davao for the implementation of the above-mentioned project.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, February 18, 2020, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Know All Men By These Presents:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT is made and executed in Davao City by and between:

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO (hereinafter referred to as the "City"), a public corporation, being a local government unit and a political subdivision of the Republic of the Philippines, with a seat of government at the City Hall, San Pedro Street, Davao City Philippines and duly represented in this agreement by its local Chief Executive, the Hon. SARA Z. DUTERTE;

and

DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION SCHOOLS DIVISION OF DAVAO CITY with office address at Elpidio Quirino Avenue, Davao City, Philippines and duly represented in this agreement by its, Schools Division Superintendent, REYNALDO M. GUILLENA;

The City and DepEd shall be collectively referred to as the "PARTIES".

WITNESSETH that:

WHEREAS, the Local Government of Davao City plans to undertake several activities and initiatives to take action against barriers to equality, through this project entitled "PROTECTION AGAINST SEXUAL ABUSE, EXPLOITATION AND DISCRIMINATION";

WHEREAS, the Integrated Gender and Development (IGD) is a division office under the City Mayor's Office, which shall act as the coordinative, regulatory, and monitoring body of the Local Government of Davao City to focus on gender-sensitive projects and activities. It shall compliment gender-related functions of other agencies of the local government;

WHEREAS, the Department of Education (DepEd), a Schools Division Office of Davao City is a government unit that oversees programs and projects directed at providing basic education to Davao City;

--- [Page 8] ---
WHEREAS, Department of Education (DepEd) through its Curriculum Implementation Division in coordination with Learning Resource Management and Development System (LRMDS), entered into a Memorandum of Understanding that commits both parties in conducting the Calibration of the Child Protection Modules;

WHEREAS, research shows that cases on sexual exploitation and abuse specifically on rape has been continuously increasing over the years;

WHEREAS, there is a need to conduct research on the prevalence of rape and other forms of sexual abuses in Davao City;

WHEREAS, there is a need to campaign against sexual exploitation and abuse which will focus more on men and boys in the academic (faculty members and students) as well as those in the communities;

NOW THEREFORE, herein PARTIES agree to the following terms and conditions:

Article I. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES

IA. Obligation of the CITY

The CITY shall:

1. Provide expert writers and illustrators for the development and creation of module on the Training of Trainers (TOT) on sexual exploitation and abuse;

2. Assist in the calibration of the modules to ensure pedagogical fitness on the level of the learners;

3. Organize a Training of Trainers (TOT) model for proper delivery of the program;

4. Ensure that all learners are covered in the dissemination/awareness campaign of the program,

5. Develop a proposal to include the capacity building budget from Special Education Fund (SEF)/ Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE) for the capacity building of all

--- [Page 9] ---
DepEd officials, school heads, teaching and non-teaching, learners, parents and other stakeholders;

6. Create and distribute Information-Education Campaign (IEC) materials for the capacity building and prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse; and

7. Do other related tasks as mandated by laws, legal orders and decrees relevant to the program.

1B. Obligation of the DepEd

The DepEd shall:

1. Act as a coordinating body and spearhead the conduct of the TOT;

2. Implement the projects and activities mutually agreed by both parties;

3. Set up and ensure an effective referral service delivery network;

4. Conduct research in relation to sexual exploitation and abuse;

5. Conduct activities to advocate and provide awareness on rape and its preventive measures;

6. Develop and distribute Information-Education Campaign (IEC) materials for the capacity building and prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse; and

7. Do other related tasks as mandated by laws, legal orders and decrees relevant to the program.

Article II. Arbitration

In case of any dispute that may arise in connection with this Agreement, the Parties shall exert best efforts towards an amicable settlement. In the event such dispute is not resolved amicably within a period of thirty (30) days from the date of its occurrence, the Parties shall settle the same pursuant to the rules of Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), whenever applicable. That in the event of

--- [Page 10] ---
violation of any of the aforesaid conditions, the PARTIES shall be subject to arbitration and other legal remedies.

ARTICLE III. EFFECTIVITY, TERMINATION, PRETERMINATION

This Agreement shall take effect upon the signing thereof by both parties and shall remain in effect for a period of one (1) year. This Agreement may be renewed upon mutual agreement of the parties.

ARTICLE IV. AMENDMENTS/PARTIAL INVALIDITY

All amendments or revisions of any provision of this Agreement shall be upon the prior written mutual consent of all Parties subject to the execution of an appropriate instrument for the purpose.

If any of the provisions of this Agreement is declared by a competent court or quasi-judicial body to be null and void or unenforceable, the remaining provisions not affected by the defects shall nevertheless continue to be in full force and effect without being impaired or invalidated in any way.

ARTICLE V. NOTICES

All notices, requests, consents and other documents required under this Agreement shall be given or served to the other Parties either by personal delivery, by registered mail, through reputable courier services, properly addressed or sent as the case may be, to the Party or its designated agent/representative.

ARTICLE VI. ENTIRETY

The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement constitutes the entire Agreement between them and shall supersede all other previous communications or contracts, oral or written, between and among the Parties with respect to the subject matter thereof.
