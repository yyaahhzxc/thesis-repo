ORDINANCE NO. 064-02 / Series of 2002

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING RETAILERS, WHOLESALEERS, GROCERY STORES, CONVENIENCE STORES AND OTHER BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS FROM SELLING OR ALLOTTING TO MINORS, AGED 17 AND BELOW, CIGARETTES AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS AND ESTABLISHMENTS CIGARETTIES AND OTHER TOBACCO PRODUCTS, BEER, LIQUOR AND OTHER INTOXICATING BEVERAGES AND PROHIBITING ALL MINORS, AGED 17 AND BELOW, FROM BUYING, ORDERING, SERVING, SELLING OR ORDERING, SERVING, SELLING OR USING ANY OF THE ABOVE-RECORDED INTOXICATING BEVERAGES AS WELL AS BUYING, ORDERING OR SMOKING CIGARETTIES AND OTHER TOBACCO PRODUCTS FROM WHOLESALE, RETAIL, GROCERY OR CONVENIENCE STORES AND ALL OTHER BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance Prohibiting Retailers, Wholesalers, Grocery Stores, Convenience Stores and Other Businesses from Selling or Allowing to be Sold to Minors in their Repective Stores or Establishments Cigarettes and Other Tobacco Products, Beer, Liquor, Other Intoxicating Beverages and Prohibiting all Minors, aged 17 and below, from purchasing, serving, or drinking beer, liquor, coconut wine and other intoxicating beverages as well as buying, ordering or smoking cigarettes and other tobacco products from wholesale, retail, grocery or convenience stores and all other business establishments in The City of Davao".

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this definition, the term "All Other Business Establishments" shall mean and include Hotels, Restaurants, Bars, Cafés, Food Houses, Eateries, Refreshment Parlors, Beer Houses, Disco Joint/Hostels, Fun Houses, Amusement Centers, Motels, Inns, and such other similar establishments selling and serving beer, liquor, coconut wine and other intoxicating beverages as well as cigarettes and other tobacco products;

SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City Government to promote the general welfare of its inhabitants, particularly the minors, who should be protected and shielded from the bad influence of drinking and smoking which not only affect their health but also their well-being as future leaders and responsible citizens of the City. It is also the duly and responsibly parents to see to it that their children who are minors are not exposed to and are spared from such bad influences. In the same way that all responsible citizens of the City must do their share in upholding and protecting the welfare and interests of minors.

SECTION 4. PERSONS COVERED BY THE PROHIBITION - All minors, aged 17 and below, are prohibited from buying, ordering, serving or drinking beer, liquor, coconut wine and other intoxicating beverages as well as buying and smoking cigarettes and other tobacco products from wholesale, retail, grocery, or convenience stores and all other business establishments in the city;

SECTION 5. OTHER PROHIBITED ACTS - The following are also considered prohibited acts under the Ordinance:

a) All persons are prohibited from instructing, ordering, sending on an errand or co-opting minors, aged 17 and below, to buy, order or drink beer, liquor, coconut wine and other intoxicating beverages as well as to buy, order or smoke cigarettes and other tobacco products from wholesale, retail, grocery, or convenience stores and all other business establishments in the city;

b) All wholesalers, retail, grocery or convenience stores as well as all other business establishments in the city are prohibited from selling or serving beer, liquor, coconut wine and other intoxicating beverages as well as cigarettes and other tobacco products to all minors, aged 17 and below. For this purpose, all wholesalers, retail, grocery, retail, convenience stores as well as all other business establishments in the city are mandated to determine the age of customers by requiring them, whenever necessary, by reason of their identification cards, such as Driver's License, School ID, Employee ID, other Government-issued ID or Community Tax Certificate before selling or serving to them beer, liquor, coconut wine and other intoxicating beverages as well as cigarettes and other tobacco products.

SECTION 6. PENALTIES - Violators of this Ordinance shall be penalized as follows:  
First Offense  
A fine of P1, 000.00 shall be imposed or imprisonment of not less than 10 days or both at the discretion of the Court;  
Second Offense  
A fine of P3, 000.00 shall be imposed or imprisonment of not less than 20 days or both at the discretion of the Court;  
Third Offense  
A fine of P5, 000.00 shall be imposed or imprisonment of not less than 30 days or both at the discretion of the Court;  

Children nine (9) years old and below shall be exempted from the criminal liability herein provided for, while children over 9 years old but under 15 years old are not also criminally liable, unless it be proved that he acted with discernment.  

Minors found violating this Ordinance shall be rescued in accordance with the Child Welfare Code and other pertinent laws.  

In accordance with Section 5 (b) hereof, the following shall be held responsible for violations of this Ordinance:  
1. The President or General Manager or any person acting for and in behalf of the General Manager in case the wholesale, retail, grocery or convenience store or business establishment is a corporation, partnership or cooperative; and  
2. The owner or proprietor or any person acting for and in behalf of the owner or proprietor if the wholesale, retail, grocery or convenience store or business establishment is a single proprietorship;  

In addition to the foregoing penalties, the business permit of the retail, wholesale, grocery or convenience store or business establishment shall be cancelled by the City Mayor upon its commission of the Third Offense.  

SECTION 7. EDUCATION AND INFORMATION DISSEMINATION - All departments and offices of the City Government are mandated to conduct massive education and information drive in their respective departments and offices to disseminate the existence of this Ordinance for their proper information and guidance. Likewise, all public and private tertiary, secondary and elementary schools, colleges and universities operating in the city are mandated to conduct massive education and information drive in their respective campuses to inform their students of the existence of this Ordinance and to place the sign mentioned under Section 5 (b) hereof in their respective bulletin boards in full view of their students for their continued guidance.
