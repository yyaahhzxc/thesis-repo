SECTION 2. OBJECTIVES - This Ordinance aims to:
a) To attract more investors in the City;
b) To essentially boost the trust and confidence of traders and businessmen in the Local Government of Davao;
c) To encourage and motivate traders to increase capital and provide job opportunities to a greater number; and
d) To equitably provide growth to marginalized business sectors so as not to defeat the life of small retailers and investors in the city.

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - The following provisions of Section 69 of Ordinance No. 138-05 shall be read as follows:

"Section 69. Imposition of Tax.- There is hereby imposed on the following persons who establish, operate, conduct or maintain their respective business within the City a graduated business tax in the amount hereinafter prescribed:
(a) xxx
(b) xxx
(c) xxx
(d) On retailers:
Gross Sales/Receipts for the preceding year Rate of Tax Per Annum
More than P50,000.00 but not over P400,000.00 2%
In excess of P400,000.00 1 1/2%
However, barangays shall have the exclusive power to levy taxes on stores whose gross sales or receipts of the preceding calendar year does not exceed Fifty Thousand Pesos (P50,000.00) subject to existing laws and regulations.
(e) xxx
(f) xxx

SECTION 4. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY. - The provisions of this Ordinance shall be applicable only to Retailers with gross sales/receipts in excess of P400,000.00 to be imposed on essential and non-essential commodities.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If, for any reason or reasons, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other section or provision thereof, which are not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE. - All ordinances or parts of any ordinances inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly."
