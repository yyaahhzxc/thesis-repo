ORDINANCE NO. 0930-22
Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE PORTION (PARTIAL SUBSTRUCTURE CONSTRUCTION ONLY) OF DAVAO CITY COASTAL ROAD (TALOMO SECTION) - DAVAO - BUKIDNON ROAD (CATALUANAN PEQUEÑO - MINTAL - CALINAN SECTION) BY-PASS ROAD (VIADUCT) FROM APRIL 6, 2022 TO OCTOBER 4, 2022, IN PREPARATION FOR THE CONCRETE POURING OF THE FOUNDATION OF THE VIADUCT PROJECT.

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0930-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE PORTION (PARTIAL SUBSTRUCTURE CONSTRUCTION ONLY) OF DAVAO CITY COASTAL ROAD (TALOMA SECTION) - DAVAO - BUKIDNON ROAD (CATALUNAN PEQUEÑO - MINTAL - CALINAN SECTION) BY-PASS ROAD (VIADUCT) FROM APRIL 6, 2022 UNTIL OCTOBER 4, 2022 IN PREPARATION FOR THE CONCRETE POURING OF THE FOUNDATION OF THE VIADUCT PROJECT".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 23 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991", provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural fair or undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunication and waterworks projects".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The portion of the road (Partial Substructure Construction Only) of Davao City Coastal Road (Taloma Section) - Davao - Bukidnon Road (Catalunan Pequeño - Mintal - Calinan Section) - By-pass Road (Viaduct) will be temporarily closed from April 6, 2022 to October 4, 2022, in preparation for the concrete pouring of the foundation of the viaduct project.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, this 12th day of April 2022, by a unanimous vote of all Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
