Ord. No. 928-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND BEHOLD OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUCT TO BE EXECUTED AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE STATION BUHANGIN POLICE STATION 5, RELATIVE TO THE USE OF THE LATTER OF A PARCEL OF LAND LOCATED ALONG BUGHANGIN-CABATLAN ROAD COVERED BY "TCT NO. T-32512".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 22 (a) (5) and (c) and Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the "Local Government Code of 1991" provide that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every Local Government Unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the Local Government Unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod pursuant to law or Ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and on behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Usufruct to be executed and between the City of Davao and the Philippine National Police, Buhangin Police Station 5, relative to the use of the latter of a parcel of land situated along Bughangin-Cabatlan Road, covered by TCT No. T-32512.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

DEED OF USUFRUCT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This DEED is made and executed by and between:

The CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit of the Republic of the Philippines,
with principal office at City Hall Building, City Hall Drive, Davao City represented herein by the City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUERTE, hereinafter to as the RANTOR;

AND

The PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE, Buhangin Police Station 5, represented by
Station Commander RMajor Bennie T. Suaga, is a government entity duly organized
and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, hereinafter referred to as
the USUFRUCTURY.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the City of Davao is GRANTOR of the registered owner of a real
property located along Buhangin-Cabantian Road, under Transfer Certificate of Title No.
T-32512 with a total land area of 1,000 square meters.

WHEREAS, a portion of the subject property is currently being occupied and used as site for Police Station by the USUFRUCTURY;

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing, the GRANTOR
cedes, conveys, by way of usufruct unto the USUFRUCTURY, portion of the aforesaid parcel of land, under the following terms and conditions:

LAND AREA: The USUFRUCTURY shall be allowed the exclusive and uninterrupted use of the 300 Square Meters portion of land.

TERM: That this Contract of Usufruct shall be effective for a period of TWENTY-
FIVE (25) years from the time of the signing of this contract renewable upon the
consent of both parties. However, if the USUFRUCTURY ceases to use or operate for
which it is intended, this Usufruct is automatically terminated, and all the improvements
thereon shall be forfeited in favor of the ADMINISTRATOR.

PURPOSE: The property shall be used solely and exclusively as POLICE
STATION or Office.

MAINTENANCE: The USUFRUCTURY shall maintain the cleanliness of the land
in accordance with the existing rules and regulation and ensure the same in habitable
condition during the existence of Usufruct.

PROHIBITION: The USUFRUCTURY is strictly prohibited to construct
additional building structures unless approved by the GRANTOR, however, the
Usufructary is authorized to make repair, renovation on the current building, provided
that the same shall conform to the standard prescribed by law.

--- [Page 5] ---
**LIABILITY:** The USUFRUCTUARY shall be solely liable for damages or criminal liability arising from its negligence while in possession of the lot stated above subject of this usufruct.

**MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS:**

1. During the existence of the Usufruct, the USUFRUCTUARY is absolutely prohibited from alienating its rights on the usufruct, nor lease the same to any third person.

2. That any violation of the terms and conditions of this Usufruct is a valid ground for the termination of this Contract without any Court Order.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, we have heretofore affixed our signatures this day of , at Davao City, Philippines.
