ORDINANCE NO. 052-22
Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION TO KALAYAG CHILD CARE & YOUTH COMMUNITY CENTER INC. AND CBN ASIA FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX WITH REGARDS TO THEIR UPCOMING BENEFIT FILM VIEWING OF "OLIGASE" ON OCTOBER 25, 2022 AT GAISANO MALL OF DAVAO Cinema 2, CONSIDERING THAT THE PROCEEDS WILL BE DISTRIBUTED TO THE INDIGENOUS PEOPLES' (IP'S) SCHOLARS UNDER THE "ISKOLAR NG TRIBU PROGRAM"

Ord. No. 052-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION TO KALAYAG CHILD CARE & YOUTH COMMUNITY CENTER INC. (KCCYCI) AND CBN ASIA FOR THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX REGARDS TO THEIR UPCOMING BENEFIT FILM VIEWING OF "OLIGASE" ON OCTOBER 25, 2022 AT GAISANO MALL OF DAVAO CINEMA 2, CONSIDERING THAT THE PROCEEDS WILL BE DIRECTED TOWARDS THE INDIGENOUS PEOPLES' (IP's) SCHOLARS UNDER THE "ISKOLAR NG TRIBU PROGRAM".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Paragraph (a) Section 59 of Ordinance No. 156-05, Series of 2005, otherwise known as the "Revenue Code of the City of Davao" states that the tax herein imposed does not apply in the following cases, provided, that exemption, should first be obtained for this purpose from the Sangguniang Panlungsod: a) Where the admission fees are collected for and in behalf of the charitable, education or religious institution or associations which are declared by law or presidential proclamation as exempted from the payment of amusement tax on paid admission; provided, further, that such exhibition, show, performances and the like, shall be limited to three (3) days only in a calendar;

SECTION 3. PURPOSE - The genuine purpose of promulgating this legislative measure is to exempt Kalayag Child Care & Youth Community Center Inc. and CBN Asia from paying the amusement tax on their upcoming benefit film viewing in which the proceeds of the said film viewing will be for the benefit of the IP scholars under the Iskolar ng Tribu Program.

SECTION 4. TRANSMITTAL - Due to the urgency of the matter, the Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is hereby directed to transmit a copy of the said Ordinance to the Executive Department immediately upon its final approval.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 24th day of October 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

CHARITO SANTOS

Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
