Ord. No. 0123-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR LOAN FACILITY TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES OF DAVAO CITY MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE (GEDCMC)".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement for Loan Facility to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Government Employees of Davao City Multipurpose Cooperative.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 9th day of December, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
