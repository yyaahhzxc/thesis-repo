ORDINANCE NO. 0125-22
Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF MICHAEL MANALAYSAY, M.D., PRESIDENT OF ANAK KAUGNAON FOUNDATION, INC., FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF THE AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUNDRAISING ACTIVITY/MOVIE SCREENING ON DECEMBER 17, 2022 AT THE CINEMA OF SM LANANG PREMIER, J.P. LAURELLE AVENUE, DAVAO CITY, FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER. THE PROCEEDS OF SAID EVENT WILL BE UTILIZED AS ASSISTANCE TO INDIGENT PATIENTS OF THE PEDIATRICS DEPARTMENT OF THE SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0125-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF MICHAEL MANALAYSAY, M.D., PRESIDENT OF THE MANALAYSAY FOUNDATION, INC., FOR THE USE AND AMUSEMENT OF THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUNDRAISING ACTIVITY/MOVIE SCREENING ON DECEMBER 17, 2022 AT THE CINEMAS OF SM LANANG PREMIER, J.P. LAUREL AVENUE, AGDAAO, THIS CITY CONSIDERING THAT A PORTION OF THE PROCEEDS OF SAID EVENT BE UTILIZED FOR THE ASSISTANCE OF INDIGENT PATIENTS OF THE PEDIATRICS DEPARTMENT OF THE SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines, state in part that "every local government shall exercise the powers expressly granted, that necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare".

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Pursuant to Section 66, (a) of the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City, it provides that "(a)" When the admission fees are collected for and in behalf of the charitable, educational, or religious institutions or associations who are declared by law or presidential proclamation as exempted from the payment of amusement tax on paid admission, provided further, that such exhibition, show, performance and the like shall be limited to only three (3) days in a calendar year";

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other sections or provisions shall be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 09th day of December, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHIARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sanggunian Panlungsod
