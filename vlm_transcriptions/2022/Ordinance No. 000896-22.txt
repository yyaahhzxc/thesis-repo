Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:  

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE CONTRACT OF LEASE BETWEEN MINDANAO CHRISTIAN FOUNDATION COLLEGE AND SEMINARY, INC., AND THE CITY OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE CITY COLLEGE OF DAVAO (CCD) PROJECT, SUBJECT TO GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT (DBM), THE COMMISSION ON AUDIT (COA), THE PROCUREMENT LAW, AS WELL AS OTHER APPLICABLE LAWS, ORDINANCES AND PRESIDENTIAL DIRECTIVES."  

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 22 (a) (5) and (c) and Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provide that:  

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.  
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:  
(5) To enter into contracts; and  
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."  

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.  
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:  
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:  
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."  

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, the Contract of Lease between MINDANAO CHRISTIAN FOUNDATION COLLEGE AND SEMINARY, INC., and the City of Davao relative to the City College of Davao (CCD) Project, subject to Government Budgeting, Accounting and Auditing Rules and Regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law, as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances and Presidential Directives.

1. LEASED PROPERTIES. The subject of this contract is limited to the portion of lots aforesaid described with all its office buildings, furniture and fixtures, elementary and high school buildings grounds and school premises, chapel hall, clinic, covered court and other amenities related to the function of the school. The back portion of the lot immediately after the covered court is not part of the leased properties and shall remain exclusive for the function of the lessees' own use, worship, theology/religious activities whereof the lessee permits right of access only to students attending to its authorized personnel and future theology students subject to proper submission of identification.

2. PURPOSES. The leased properties shall be utilized by the LESSEE exclusively for the operation, management and maintenance of the City College of Davao (CCD).

3. TERM. That the term of this lease shall be for a period of three years (3) years to commence on until . Upon its expiration, the lease contract may be renewed. The written notice of intention to renew the lease shall be served to the LESSOR not later than one (1) month prior to the expiry date of the period herein agreed upon.

4. RENTAL RATE. The total monthly payable shall be One Hundred Forty-One Thousand Six Hundred Sixty-Six Pesos and 67/100 (P141,666.67) inclusive of 5% withholding tax and twelve (12%) VAT. The LESSEE will pay the LESSOR net of five (5%) percent withholding tax the amount of One Hundred Thirty-Five Thousand Three Hundred Forty-Two Pesos and 26/100 (P135,342.28). The withholding tax which is equivalent to Six hundredths of one percent (0.06%) of the rental rate of P141,666.67 shall be remitted by the LESSEE to the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR). The LESSEE shall provide the LESSOR a copy of the BIR Form 2307.

5. DEFAULT PAYMENT. In case of default by the LESSEE in the payment of the rent, such as when the checks are dishonored, it shall be given a period of thirty (30) days to settle its balance. In case of failure to settle its liability, the LESSOR at its option, may terminate this contract and eject the LESSEE.

6. SUB-LEASE. The LESSEE shall not directly or indirectly sublet, allow or permit the leased properties to be occupied and utilized in whole or in part by any person, form or corporation, neither shall the LESSEE assign its rights hereunder to any other person or entity, and no right of interests thereto or therein shall be conferred on or vested in anyone by the LESSEE without the LESSOR's written approval.

7. PUBLIC UTILITIES. The LESSEE shall pay for the telephone, electricity, cable TV, water, internet connection and other public services and utilities during the duration of the lease.

--- [Page 9] ---
8. FORCE MAJEURE. If whole or any part of the leased premises shall be destroyed or damaged by fire, flood, lightning, typhoon, earthquake, storm, riot, or any other unforeseen disabling cause of acts of God, as to render the leased properties during the term substantially unfit for use and occupation of the LESSEE, then the lease contract may be terminated without compensation by the LESSOR or by the LESSEE by notice in writing to the other.

9. REPAIRS AND REPLACEMENT. The LESSEE shall be responsible to make repairs due to normal wear and tear of the leased properties. Furthermore, damages to leased properties caused by LESSEE shall likewise be borne by the latter. If the same is beyond repair, a corresponding replacement, in the same quantity and quality, or payment equivalent to its market value shall be made.

10. RIGHT OF ENTRY. The LESSOR or its authorized agent shall, after giving due notice to the LESSEE, have the right to enter the premises in the presence of the LESSEE or its representative at any reasonable hour to examine the same or for any other lawful purposes which may be necessary.

11. IMPROVEMENTS. Any permanent improvement/s made by the LESSEE shall become a property of the LESSOR upon the expiration of the term and/or termination of this Lease, for whatever reason and without need of reimbursement to the LESSEE thereof.

12. LIABILITY FOR DAMAGES. The LESSEE, for the duration of the lease, shall hold the LESSOR free from any damages as a consequence of the use of the facilities and equipment by the LESSEE or his/her agent employees.

13. EXPIRATION OF LEASE. At the expiration of the term of this lease or cancellation thereof, as herein provided, the LESSEE will promptly deliver to the LESSOR the leased properties in good and tenable condition as the same is now, ordinary wear and tear expected and remove all of its properties in the leased premises within fifteen (15) days from expiration or cancellation thereof. Non-compliance with the terms of this clause by the LESSEE will give the LESSOR the right, at the latter's option, to suspend or deny the renewal of the premises and compel the LESSEE to pay rent therefor at the rate plus Twenty-Five (25%) percent penalty until the LESSEE shall have complied with the terms hereof. The same penalty shall be imposed in case the LESSEE fails to leave the premises within the aforementioned period from the expiration of the contract or termination for whatsoever.

14. PRE-TERMINATION. The LESSEE may terminate this contract for whatever reason one (1) month prior notice to the LESSOR. In such event, the LESSEE shall pay the rental fee corresponding only the month it has occupied the leased properties.
