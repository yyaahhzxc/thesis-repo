ORDINANCE NO. 086-22
Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING CITY GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES TO WEAR AN INDIGENOUS PEOPLE OR MORO INSPIRED ATTIRE AND/OR ACCESSORIES EVERY FIRST MONDAY OF THE MONTH TO CELEBRATE OUR LOCAL CULTURE

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 5

Ord. No. 086-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "WEARING OF IP OR MORO INSPIRED ATTIRE ORDINANCE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City of Davao shall ensure that its culture and tradition is appreciated, promoted, and preserved. To take this into fruition, the City shall ensure that its local culture shall showcase the beautiful culture and rich tradition of its eleven (11) tribes, by requiring city government employees to wear an Indigenous People or Moro inspired attire and/or accessories, every first Monday of the month.

SECTION 3. SCOPE - This Ordinance shall be limited to government employees of this City, regardless whether such employee holds office or employment in casual, contractual, temporary, holderover, permanent or regular capacity. This shall also apply to newly-hired employees, Job Order and Contract of Service employees. Moreover, those whose ethnicity is outside the domain of the eleven (11) tribes of Davao City may wear their respective ethnic attire. Finally, a genuine or authentic Indigenous People or Moro inspired attire, may be worn by the abovementioned government employees, provided that, a certification from the Office of the Cultural Community Affairs shall be secured from the said Office, agency or department, as required under Section 6 of Ordinance No. 087-17, Series of 2017, otherwise known as the "IP Attire Ordinance".

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS - Whenever used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean as herein set forth:

a) Certification - shall refer to any document evidencing that a prior consultation for the use of an Indigenous Peoples Attire has been secured by an office, agency or department from the Office of the Cultural Affairs.

b) City Government Employees - shall refer to those employed by the Davao City Government.

c) Indigenous Peoples Inspired Attire - shall refer to inspired garments, clothes or accessories which generally represent the five (5) tribes of the Indigenous People of Davao City, to wit: Bagobo, Klatu, Ata, Obo Manuvu, Matigalsagul and Bagobo-Tagabawa tribes.

d) Inspired Attire - shall refer to garments, clothes or accessories which generally represent the six (6) Moro tribes of Davao City, to wit: Tausug, Sama, Maranao, Maguindanaon, Ibanun and Kagan tribes.

e) Moro Inspired Attire - shall refer to inspired garments, clothes or accessories which generally represent the six (6) Moro tribes of Davao City, to wit: Tausug, Sama, Maranao, Maguindanaon, Ibanun and Kagan tribes.

f) Office or employment in casual or contractual capacity - shall refer to those whose employment in the government is in accordance with a special contract to undertake a specific work or job, requiring special or technical skills not available in the employing agency, to be accomplished within a specific period, which in no case shall exceed one (1) year and performs or accomplishes the specific work or job, under his own responsibility with a minimum of direction and supervision from the hiring agency. This term shall include Job Order employees.

g) Office or employment in Holdover capacity - shall refer to an employee who is entitled to hold his office until his successor shall have been duly chosen and shall have qualified.

h) Office or employment in permanent or regular capacity - shall refer to a permanent appointment which is extended to a person possessing the requisite qualifications, including the eligibility required, for the position, and thus protected by the constitutional guaranty of security of tenure.

i) Office or employment in Temporary capacity - shall refer to the issuance to an employee who meets all the requirements for the position to which he is being appointed except the appropriate civil service eligibility and shall not exceed 12 months.

j) Tribal Accessories - shall exclusively refer to the accessories of the eleven (11) tribes of Davao City.

SECTION 5. WORD AND PHRASE CONSTRUCTION - All words and phrases used in this Ordinance shall be construed and understood according to the common and accepted usage of the language.

SECTION 6. MANDATORY WEARING OF IP AND MORO INSPIRED ATTIRE - It shall be required for all City government employees in the City of Davao to wear an Indigenous Peoples Inspired Attire or Moro Inspired Attire and/or accessories based on the eleven (11) tribes of Davao City, every first Monday of the month to celebrate the local culture. City government employees who belong to any of the eleven (11) tribes in Davao City can likewise wear their authentic attire. City government employees whose ethnicity is outside the domain of the eleven (11) tribes of Davao City may likewise wear their respective ethnic attire. This shall be required for all city government employees whether holding casual, contractual, hold-over, permanent and/or temporary capacity.

SECTION 7. EXCEPTIONS - The following acts or employees shall be exempted from the implementation of this Ordinance:

7.1 Those who are regularly performing their duties and functions in the field, and those assigned in actual field operations;

7.2 When the nature of the work of the concerned employee demands that he or she wears a clothing other than what is imposed in this Ordinance;

7.3 Those who are prohibited to wear such attire by reason of their religious affiliation;

7.4 Those employees who have lost a loved one may wear their mourning clothes during their period of mourning; and

7.5 Pregnant female employees who need to wear their maternity clothes.

**SECTION 8. APPROPRIATE ATTIRE** - An Indigenous People or Moro inspired attire is appropriate when it does not diminish the value of the cultural heritage of the Indigenous People of the Moro tribes of the City. A prescribed pattern shall be incorporated into the inspired attire as approved by the eleven tribes, to be worn by the City government employees, such as but not limited to, their uniforms or office-issued shirts. The eleven (11) tribes shall likewise prescribe the accessories that may be worn by the City government employees, in lieu of the inspired attire.

This prescribed pattern and/or accessory are those commonly known to be used or are representation of each of the eleven tribes in Davao City, as approved by their Council leaders to be correct and appropriate in accordance with their tradition and practice.

**SECTION 9. PROHIBITED ATTIRE** - It is necessary that respect to the culture and tradition of the Indigenous People and Moro Tribes is maintained and preserved incessantly. Hence, the following attire shall be prohibited when performing important and official functions inside the workplace:

9.1 Those who are not part of the Indigenous People or Moro Tribes in Davao City cannot wear their authentic attire without securing first a certification from the Office of the Cultural Community Affairs under the Office of the City Mayor or the tribal community as provided under Section 6 of Ordinance No. 0307-17, Series of 2017, otherwise known as the "IP Attire Ordinance".

9.2 Those accessory or clothing when worn will disrespect or derogate the cultural heritage and tradition of the Indigenous People or Moro tribes in Davao City;

9.3 Clothing or garments relative to a tribe's belief or creed that are not allowed to be worn by anyone which are, but not limited to the following:

a) Hijab;

b) Taqiyah;

c) Abaya.

**SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE** - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable, and, if any one or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

**SECTION 11. REPEALING CLAUSE** - All Ordinances, resolutions, rules, or regulations which are inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 12. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect FIFTEEN (15) DAYS after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao.

**ENACTED**, on the 15th day of November 2022, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
