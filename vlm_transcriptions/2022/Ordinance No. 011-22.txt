Ord. No. 011-22  
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:  
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND DAVAO CITY HOMESITE AND HOUSING MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE".  
SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and on behalf of the City of Davao, the Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Davao City Homesite and Housing Multipurpose Cooperative.  
SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.  
ENACTED, on the 23rd day of August 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.
