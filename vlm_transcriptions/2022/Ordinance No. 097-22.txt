Ord. No. 097-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING AMUSEMENT TAX EXEMPTION TO THE ARCHDIOCESAN NOURISHMENT CENTER (ANC) DAVAO, A NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION, FOR ITS UPCOMING FUNDRAISING CONCERT TO BE HELD AT RSM EVENT CENTER, PHILIPPINE WOMEN'S COLLEGE ON NOVEMBER 30, 2022, CONSIDERING THAT THE FUNDS FROM THE PROCEEDS OF SAID CONCERT WILL BE UTILIZED FOR THE OPERATION OF THE CENTER."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines provides that, "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare." X.XX.

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Section 66(a) and (b) of the 2017 Nueva Ecija Code of Davao City provides that, "(a) where the admission fees are collected and paid in behalf of the charitable, educational, or religious institutions or associations who are declared by law or presidential proclamation exempted from the payment of amusement tax on paid admission, provided further, that such exhibition, show, performance and the like shall be limited only to three (3) days in a calendar year; (b) where admission fees are collected in connection with the holding of the operas, concerts, dramas, recitals, paintings and art except pop rock or similar concert not intended for profit and furthermore, not including film exhibition".

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other sections or provisions shall be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, November 22, 2022, by unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II)

ATTESTED:
J. MELCHOR B.QUITAIN JR.
