Ord. No. 0991-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE STREETS ALONG AND IN BETWEEN THE AREA OF CORNER PONCIANO ST., PALMA GIL ST. TO PELAYO ST. (HALF-LANE ONLY) FROM 1:00 PM ON JULY 16, 2022 (SATURDAY) UP TO 9:00 AM ON JULY 17, 2022 (SUNDAY) IN CONNECTION WITH THE 2nd EDITION TDS 65K ULTRAMARATHON "STRIDE TO SURVIVE" FOR A CAUSE BY STRIDERS RUNNING CLUB OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21(c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and waterworks projects.;;

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD - The Davao City Striders Sports Clinic and Event Organizer, in coordination with the Davao Striders Running Club, will be holding its ultramarathon, half-marathon, and fun run for a cause on July 16-17, 2022 on the following routes:

* For 65k Ultra Marathon - Peoples Park (exit area) - Pelayo St., turn left to E. Quirino Ave., going along McArthur Highway - Ulas - Puan - Toril (Gmail), going back to Crossing Bayabas, turn left to Manambulang Tagapkan Road. Turn right to Libby Road - Semupenes St (Bypass Puan), cross to Bukidnon road - Bypass Ulas, then turn left to Davao City Division Road, turn right to Shrine Hills - Santos Cuygan Road, turn left to Gen. MacArthur highway, turn right to Davao City Diversion road, turn right to Panacan Crossing - Davao City/Paranabo City Road Sasa - Lanang, turn right to J.P Laurel ave. (Bajada), turn right to E. Quirino Ave., turn left to Pelayo St. - Palma Gil St. - Peoples Park (exit area only);

* For 21k/10k/5k - Peoples Park (exit area), turn right to Palma Gil St. - Pelayo St., turn right to E. Quirino, turn left to J.P Laurel - Durian Hotel (turning point for 5k), Bank of Commerce - Lanang Branch (10K turning point) then turn left going to C-One Km. 11 (turning point for 21k) - Peoples Park (exit area only).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provision hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 21st day of June 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
