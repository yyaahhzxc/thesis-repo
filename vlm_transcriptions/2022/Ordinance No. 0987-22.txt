SECTION 1.— Section 3 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:  

Section 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.  

a) Agricultural Waste shall refer to waste from planting or harvesting of crops, trimming or pruning of plants and trees, and waste or run-off materials from farms or fields;  

b) Ambulant Vendor or Peddler shall mean any individual or person who sells any kind of goods, merchandise, commodity or service while walking, moving or traveling about, or who does not have a fixed stall, store or place of vending or service;  

c) Barangay shall refer to the Barangay Local Government Unit;  

d) Biodegradable shall refer to materials capable of being decomposed by biologicals;  

e) Biodegradable Waste shall refer to any waste that is capable of undergoing anaerobic or aerobic decomposition, such as food waste, yard waste and wet paper and carton;  

f) Bulky Wastes shall refer to waste materials which cannot be appropriately placed in separate containers because of either its bulky size, shape or other physical attributes. These include large worn-out or broken household, commercial, and industrial items such as furniture, lamps, bookcases, filing cabinets, and other similar items;  

g) Buy-back Center shall refer to a recycling center that purchases or otherwise accepts recyclable materials from the public for the purpose of recycling such materials;  

h) Checkout Bag is a carryout bag provided by a retail establishment or vendor at the point of sale or other point of departure for the purpose of transporting food or merchandise;  

i) City shall refer to the City Government of Davao;  

j) City ENRO shall refer to the City Environment and Natural Resources Office;  

k) Collection shall refer to the act of removing solid waste from the source or from a common storage point;  

l) Community Service shall refer to the service that benefits the local community. It shall be imposed to violators of this Ordinance as a penalty in itself or in lieu of fine imposed against him;

m) Composting shall refer to the controlled decomposition of organic matter by micro-organisms, mainly bacteria and fungi, into a humus-like product;  

n) Consumer Electronics Waste shall refer to special wastes that include worn-out, broken, and other discarded items such as cellular phones, radios, stereos, TV sets, computer parts and hardware, and all wastes emanating or as a result of a defective electronic device;  

o) Domesticated Animal shall refer to a household pet kept for companionship and enjoyment;  

p) Disposal shall refer to the discharge, deposit, dumping, spilling, leaking, or placing of any solid waste into or in any land;  

q) Ecological Solid Waste Management shall refer to the systematic administration of activities which provide for segregation at source, segregated transportation, storage, transfer, processing, treatment, and disposal of solid waste and all other waste management activities which do not harm the environment;  

r) Generation shall refer to the act or process of producing solid waste;  

s) Generator shall refer to a person, natural or juridical, who last uses a material and makes it available for disposal or recycling;  

t) Grocery store shall refer to a shop selling foodstuffs and household supplies among others;  

u) Hazardous Waste shall refer to solid waste management or combination of solid waste which because of its quantity, concentration or physical, chemical or infectious characteristics may:  

(1) cause, or significantly contribute to an increase in mortality or an increase in serious irreversible, or incapacitating reversible, illness; or  

(2) pose a substantial present or potential hazard to human health or the environment when improperly treated, stored, transported, or disposed of, or otherwise managed;  

v) Hazardous Waste Containment Facility shall refer to the disposal facility for household hazardous wastes;  

w) Littering From Vehicles is the act of indiscriminately scattering food leftovers, wrappers, plastic bottles and other similar wastes in streets by drivers, passengers or commuters;  

x) Materials Recovery Facility shall include a solid waste transfer station or sorting station, drop-off center, a composting facility, and a recycling facility;  

y) Mobile Garage Bin shall refer to the receptacles with wheels distributed to garbage collection points by the City Government of Davao for use as

bb) Open Dump shall refer to a disposal area wherein the solid wastes are indiscriminately thrown or disposed of without due planning and consideration for environmental and health standards;

i) Recyclable material shall refer to any waste material retrieved from the waste stream and free from contamination that can still be converted into suitable beneficial use or for other purposes including, but not limited to, newspaper, ferrous scrap metal, non-ferrous scrap metal, used oil, corrugated cardboard, aluminum, glass, office paper, tin cans and other materials as may be determined by the National Solid Waste Management Commission;

j) Recycling shall refer to the treating of used or waste materials through a process of making them suitable for beneficial use and for other purposes, and includes any process by which solid waste materials are transformed into new products in such a manner that the original product may lose their identity, and which may be used as raw materials for the production of other goods or services. Provided, that the collection, segregation and re-use of previously used packaging material shall be deemed recycling under this Ordinance;

kk) Residual Wastes shall refer to those wastes that remain after processing at a Materials Recovery Facility or that which remain after recovery of

II) Residual Containment Area shall refer to a temporary facility for the storage of residual wastes;

rr) Segregation at source shall refer to a solid waste management practice of separating, at the point of origin, different materials found in solid waste in order to promote recycling and re-use of resources and to reduce the volume of waste for collection and disposal;

tt) Solid Waste Management shall refer to the discipline associated with the control of generation, storage, collection, transfer and transport, processing, and disposal of solid wastes in a manner that is in accord with the best principles of public health, economics, engineering, conservation, aesthetics, and other environmental considerations, and that is also responsive to public attitudes;

v) Source Reduction shall refer to the reduction of solid waste before it enters the solid waste stream by methods such as product design, materials substitution, materials re-use and packaging restrictions;

w) Source Separation shall refer to the sorting of solid waste into some or all of its component parts at the point of generation;

(1) Waste identified or listed under Republic Act No. 6969 as hazardous waste of a solid, liquid, contained gaseous or semisolid form which may cause or contribute to an increase in mortality or in serious or incapacitating irreversible illness, or acute/chronic effect on the health of persons and other organisms;

SECTION 2. - Section 5 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:

**Section 5. USE OF RECYCLABLE, REUSABLE AND BIODEGRADABLE CHECKOUT BAGS AND CONTAINERS.** - All stores, as well as ambulant vendors in Davao City shall sell or provide only the following as checkout bags or containers to customers:

a) Recycled or recyclable paper bags;

b) Biodegradable plastic bags;

c) Reusable bags, such as cloth bags and other eco bags;

d) Bags made of indigenous materials, such as buri, abaca, anahaw, bamboo, and pineapple;

e) Used corrugated boxes or cartons.

Biodegradable plastic bags must be properly disposed of like any other solid waste material and should not be thrown in canals, water bodies, vacant lots, and other public places.

**SECTION 3.** - Section 6 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:

**Section 6. INDIVIDUAL PARTICIPATION.** - Shoppers or customers in all stores in Davao City are encouraged to provide for themselves reusable and recyclable shopping bags such as those enumerated in Section 5, when shopping or buying from stores. They are also encouraged to refuse a checkout bag from the store when buying small items (like a CD, candy, etc.) that do not require a bag.

All stores, unless necessary, shall not provide plastic checkout bags for purchases of small items that may be held by hand by any customer.

**SECTION 4.** - Section 8 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:

**Section 8. DISPOSAL OF CARCASSES OF ANIMALS.** - Carcasses of domestic animals should be disposed of by burying them at least two (2) feet under the ground. The disposal of carcasses of farm animals and other animals shall be subject to the guidelines set by the City Health Office and the City Veterinarian's Office.

**SECTION 5.** - Section 9 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:

**Section 9. DISPOSAL OF EXCRETA OF DOMESTIC ANIMALS.** - Excreta of domestic animals should be disposed of by burying and/or through toilet/septic tank. No animal excreta shall be thrown inside the mobile garbage bins. The disposal of excreta of farm animals and other animals shall be subject to the guidelines set by the City Health Office and the City Veterinarian's Office.

**SECTION 6. – Section 10 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 10. MANDATORY SEGREGATION OF SOLID WASTE.** - The segregation of waste at source by generators such as institutional, industrial, commercial, and agri-industrial establishments and households shall be mandatory.

The barangays through their Barangay Solid Waste Management Committee shall be responsible for ensuring that mandatory segregation of solid waste shall be complied with.

For this purpose, Barangay Ecological and Solid Waste Management Council (BESWMC) may seek the assistance of other individuals, entities, or groups on the community.

**SECTION 7. – Section 11 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 11. TYPES OF SOLID WASTE.** - Solid waste shall be segregated into the following categories:

- Biodegradable
- Recyclable
- Residual
- Special Waste

Recyclables may be further segregated into different types of materials including but not limited to paper, metal, plastic, bottles, and other similar items for disposal to buyers or processors.

**SECTION 8. – Section 13 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 13. WASTE RECEPTECLES IN VEHICLES.** - Public utility vehicles for land or water transportation are required to put up or install waste receptacles inside their vehicles. Private vehicles are encouraged to do the same.

Waste receptacles inside vehicles are only for temporary storage. If separate containers for different types of waste cannot be provided inside the vehicle due to space constraints, one receptacle may be used, provided that wastes in receptacle used should be segregated before disposal to collection points.

It shall be the duty of the drivers and/or operators of the vehicles to properly segregate and dispose the said wastes.

**SECTION 9. - Section 16 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 16. COLLECTION POINTS. - Unless otherwise allowed or required by the City ENRO, collection of solid waste by the City shall be through collection points. The Barangay in coordination with the City ENRO shall identify and designate collection points for residual and/or special waste and/or other waste to be collected by the City. Unsegregated solid waste shall not be disposed of in any designated collection points. Solid waste collection by the Barangay may be through house-to-house or establishment collection and/or through drop-off at the Barangay Materials Recovery Facility.**

**SECTION 10. - Section 17 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 17. MANDATORY SEGREGATED COLLECTION AND TRANSPORT. - Segregated collection and transport of solid waste shall be mandatory. There shall be different collection schedules for the different types of solid waste. The collection of different types of waste on the same schedule may be allowed, provided that separate collection vehicles or vehicles with compartments are used. The City ENRO shall conduct segregated collection on the following types of solid wastes:**

a) Biodegradable from government-owned markets and slaughterhouses;

b) Residual waste from City ENRO collection points;

c) Special waste from City ENRO collection points.

Barangays shall conduct segregated collection of the following types of solid wastes:

a) Biodegradables;

b) Recyclable wastes.

The Barangay may identify, organize, deputize, or designate volunteer solid waste segregators within their area of responsibility including waste pickers as volunteer partners in solid waste management.**

**SECTION 11. - Section 19 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 19. SCHEDULE OF COLLECTION BY THE CITY ENRO.**

--- [Page 11] ---
The City ENRO shall prepare the schedules for the segregated collection and transport of residual and special solid wastes from the different collection points. The collection schedule shall be thoroughly disseminated by the City ENRO to the Barangay and to concerned establishments. Disposal of solid wastes, unless otherwise allowed by City ENRO, shall be in accordance with these prescribed schedules. Barangays may promulgate rules within their areas of responsibility in order to facilitate observance of the prescribed schedules.

SECTION 12. - Section 20 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:

Section 20. MAINTENANCE OF COLLECTION POINTS. - The Barangay shall be responsible for the maintenance and cleanliness of the collection points and their surrounding area in their respective Barangays. In case of clustering of collection points, both barangays shall be responsible for the maintenance and cleanliness of the collection points and their surrounding area.

Waste Pickers shall be given an orientation by the Barangay and it shall be the responsibility of the Barangay to impose waste picking rules.

SECTION 13. - Section 21 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:

Section 21. TRANSPORT OF LARGE QUANTITIES OF RESIDUAL AND SPECIAL WASTES. - Unless otherwise allowed under paragraph 2 hereof, the City ENRO shall not collect large quantities of residual and/or special wastes with a volume of three (3) cubic meters or more from any source except public markets. The generators of such quantity shall be responsible for the transport of the same to the Solid Waste Management Facilities of the City.

Establishments or entities which generate large quantities of residual and/or special wastes with a volume of three (3) cubic meters or more may enter into arrangements with the City ENRO, the Barangay, and/or other private hauling services for the hauling of the same. Such arrangement may include the payment of fees for the services provided. In accordance with the City's Tax Revenue Code or a Barangay ordinance as may be allowed under the law.

The Barangay shall maintain a list of all establishments or entities which generate large quantities of residual and/or special waste with a volume of three (3) cubic meters or more within their area of responsibility based on available data, information, and assessments which shall be submitted to the City ENRO and regularly updated.

The City ENRO shall require establishments or entities which generate large

SECTION 14. - Section 22 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:  

Section 22. COVER FOR DUMP TRUCKS AND OTHER OPEN WASTE COLLECTION VEHICLES. - Government and private solid waste collectors shall completely cover dump trucks or any other open collection vehicles with nets, sacks or tarps to prevent wastes from being blown or strewn and to minimize odor during transport.  

Owners and drivers of dump trucks and other waste collection vehicles which caused littering on the roads/streets shall be responsible to clean up the same.  

SECTION 15. - Section 28 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:  

Section 28. MATERIALS RECOVERY FACILITY (MRF) AND BUY-BACK CENTER. - Individual Barangays or clusters of Barangays shall establish a Materials Recovery Facility (MRF).  

The City shall assist the Barangays in recycling or processing biodegradable and recyclable wastes into fuel, construction materials or into other uses.  

Barangays may designate establishments having a Solid Waste Management Certificate, such as junk shops and recyclers within their respective areas that will serve as their buy-back center.  

Commercial establishments and/or residential subdivision developments in the City shall maintain a Materials Recovery Facility (MRF).  

The barangays, commercial establishments, and/or residential subdivision developments shall endeavor to find and link up with buyers, processors or users of all types of recyclable materials.

**SECTION 16. – Section 30 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 30. GUIDELINES FOR MRF AND BUY-BACK CENTERS.**  
MRFs and buy-back center operators shall not be allowed to place or dispose of their waste along the sidewalk.

They shall follow guidelines on good housekeeping, sanitation, and zoning as prescribed by the City and other concerned agencies.

MRFs and buy-back centers shall store their recyclable waste materials clean and safe and keep their premises clean and tidy and shall always institute ways to control emission of foul odors. The said establishments should be properly constructed with a roof to prevent rainwater to accumulate within the recyclable materials.

**SECTION 17. – Section 31 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 31. DISPOSAL.**  
All residual and special waste shall be disposed in the sanitary landfill or technologically innovative facilities or other solid waste management facility of the City. Special waste shall be disposed in Hazardous Waste Containment Facilities.

**SECTION 18. – Section 32 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 32. DISPOSAL BY PRIVATE ENTITIES.**  
Private solid waste haulers providing services for establishments in the City may be allowed to dispose of the collected residual in the Solid Waste Management Facilities of the City, sanitary landfill, technologically innovative facilities, or other solid management facilities of the City for a payment of tipping fee. Special waste disposed in Hazardous Waste Containment Facility shall also be assessed with fees.

**SECTION 19. – Section 44 of City Ordinance No. 0361-10 is hereby amended to read as follows:**

**Section 44. PROHIBITED ACTS AND OMISSIONS.**  
The following acts and omissions are prohibited and shall be penalized:

1. Littering, scattering, throwing, and dumping of waste matters in public places, such as roads, sidewalks, canals, esteros, gutters, manholes, parks, public playgrounds, public school campuses, public buildings, establishments, riverbanks, coastlines, rivers, lakes, creeks, and seas, or causing or permitting the same. The throwing and dumping of waste matter in designated collection points outside of the prescribed

time schedule is also prohibited.

2. Littering from vehicles;

3. Leaving or abandoning construction materials or debris on the public road;

4. Dumping of human excreta in mobile garbage bins or collection points;

5. Dumping excreta or carcase of animals in mobile garbage bins or collection points;

6. Removal of mobile garbage bin from designated collection points without authorization from the Barangay;

7. The open burning of solid waste;

8. Non-segregation of solid waste;

9. Causing or permitting the collection of non-segregated or unsorted waste;

10. Mixing, causing or permitting the mixing of toxic and hazardous wastes (THW) from health care facilities, industries and other THW generators in the municipal waste collected by the City;

13. Operating without a Solid Waste Management Certificate as prescribed in this Ordinance;

14. Non-implementation of the Solid Waste Management Plan and Program submitted as part of the requirements for the issuance of the Solid Waste Management Certificate;

15. Refusing entry to the City ENRO or its duly authorized representative while in the exercise of its visitorial powers provided in this Ordinance;

16. Violation of any other provision of this Ordinance.

SECTION 20. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 21. EFFECTIVITY. The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect Fifteen (15) days after its publication.

ENACTED, on the 21st of June 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.
