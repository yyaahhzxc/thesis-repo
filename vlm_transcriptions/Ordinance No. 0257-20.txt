AN ORDINANCE WAIVING THE INTEREST, PENALTIES, AND
SURCHARGES INCURRED FOR NON-PAYMENT OF ALL LOCAL

DURING THE PERIOD OF COMMUNITY QUARANTINE AND

Ord. No. 0257-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known and cited as "AN ORDINANCE WAIVING THE INTEREST, PENALTIES, AND SURCHARGES INCURRED FOR NON-PAYMENT OF ALL LOCAL TAXES, FEES, CHARGES, AND FINES WHICH FALL DUE DURING THE PERIOD OF COMMUNITY QUARANTINE AND ENHANCED COMMUNITY QUARANTINE IN DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - Section 192, Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Local Government Units may through ordinances duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs under such terms and conditions as they may deem necessary".

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall apply to all local taxes, fees, charges, and fines which fall due within the period of Community Quarantine (CQ) and Enhanced Community Quarantine (ECQ) in Davao City; such as, but not limited to the following:

1. Amusement Taxes;
2. Citation tickets issued for violations of Ordinance No. 0334-12, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive Transport and Traffic Code of Davao City"; and
3. All other local taxes, fees, charges, and fines.

SECTION 4. WAIVER OF INTEREST, PENALTIES, AND SURCHARGES - In the event that the local taxes, fees, charges, and fines fall due during the period of the CQ and ECQ in Davao City, no interest, penalties, and surcharges shall be imposed therein by reason of non-payment of such local taxes, fees, charges, and fines;

Upon the lifting of the CQ and ECQ, the taxpayer shall be given a fresh period to pay such taxes, fees, charges, and fines without interest, penalties, and surcharges;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance shall not, in any way, repeal or modify the provisions of Ordinance No. 0243-20, Series of 2020, known as an "Ordinance extending the deadline of the second quarterly installment for payment of business tax, first quarterly installment for real property tax and additional levy on real property for the special education fund, and rental fees from March to June for public markets, Davao City Omnibus Transport Terminal (DOOTT), Sta. Ana Port, Mayapad Port, and Davao City Central Business Zone" in 2020 in view of the threat of the Corona Virus Disease (COVID-19) in the Philippines.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect retroactively from the date of commencement of Community Quarantine in Davao City.
