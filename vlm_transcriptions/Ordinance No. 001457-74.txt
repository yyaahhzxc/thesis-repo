**MEMORANDUM NO. 58**

**AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR THE LACKENING OR DEFEATING A BREED OF DOGS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO, PROVIDING FOR THE CUSTODIAN AND RE- FORMING OF THE SAME, COLLECTING OF FINE FOR IMPROVING VEHICLES THEREFOR AND PROVIDING FOR THEIR REFORMING WHEN NOT CLAIMED WITHIN A CERTAIN PERIOD AND PROVIDING FOR FINESHIP IN CASE OF VIOLATION THEREOF.**

**BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL, CITY OF DAVAO, THAT:**

**SECTION 1. - Every owner of dogs shall have his doggy, if ever three months ago, properly vaccinated with anti- rabies vaccine as prescribed in Section 2 hereof.**

**SECTION 2. - Vaccination for immunization of dogs is that which is prepared for use in inoculating chicken eggs and is composed of living but weakened rabies virus administered in a single dose of ten milliliters and injected intramuscularly, or such other vaccines of equal or greater potency as may be prescribed by the health authorities.**

**SECTION 3. - Every person whose dogs are properly vacci- nated shall for each dog, be issued a certificate of immu- nation, a metal tag and license certificate as follows:**

a) The certificate of immunization shall be issued by the City Veterinarian and/or his authorized representative to the owner whose dogs have been properly vaccinated;

b) The metal tag shall be issued by the City Health Officer who shall impress it with a serial number and the year of issue. The tag shall be worn by the dog at all times suspended in a conspicuous manner from a collar to identify that such dog has been duly immunized;

SECTION 4. - The City Veterinarian or his authorized representative shall perform the vaccination of dogs.
SECTION 5. - The City Health Officer may establish vaccination stations in the population and in different boroughs, preferably in the Health Centers and such stations shall be made available to the City Veterinarian or his authorized representative for the purpose of anti-rabies vaccination of dogs.
SECTION 6. - The issuing of immunization, placing of metal tag and licensing of dogs shall be done at three years interval, and the issuance of the certificate of immunization, metal tag and license certificate for dogs as mentioned in Section 3, shall be renewed at the end of the third year and the fee therefor paid.
SECTION 7. - Dogs shall be considered stray if they are not accompanied by its owner and found wandering in streets, places, parks, school premises and other public places.

**SECTION 11:** - Owners of dogs shall immediately report to the City Health Officer any dog bite incident and bring the dog to his Office for examination, if practicable.

**SECTION 12:** - The death or disappearance of a vaccinated dog shall be reported at once to the City Health Officer. In case of death, the dog tag shall be surrendered to the City Health Officer.

All dogs certified to have been immunized against rabies from the place of origin and if such dogs do not have any proof of immunization from the place of origin, they will be turned over to the custody of the City Veterinarian for immunization and shall be subject to the requirements of Sections 3 and 10 hereof.

SECTION 14. - The term "owner" shall mean the person in whose name the license certificate for the dog is issued; provided, however, that if the dog is not yet immunized or has received a metal tag and license certificate, the term "owner" may refer to the possessor or person in custody of the same.

SECTION 15. - The presence of the dog tag attached to the dog's collar shall not be a license for the owner to allow such dog to be stray.

SECTION 16. - Any person violating this Ordinance shall be punished by a fine not less than Two Thousand (2000) Pesos but not more than Two Thousand Six Hundred (2600) Pesos or an injunction of not more than two hundred (200) days or both such fine and injunction at the discretion of the court.

SECTION 17. - All previous ordinances or parts thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed.

SECTION 18. - This Ordinance shall take effect three (3) months after its approval.
