Office of the City Council
Ordinance No. 271
AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING THE CONSTRUCTION OF A BUILDING AND/OR DRILLING OF WELL, STREET & DWELLINGS OF M.M.T.-M.T. (25) MILLERS FROM THE PASTEURIZED MILK OF ALBAY, MA-A, OFF OF JAWA.
Be it Ordained by the City Council, City of Jawa, that:
SECTION 1. It shall be unlawful for any person to construct a building and/or drill wells within a radius of twenty-five (25) meters from the perimeter of the New Cemetery Site at Alager, Ma-a, this City.
SEC. 2. Any violation of this ordinance shall be fined by not more than: 250.00 or by deprivation of not more than one (1) month, or both such fine and imprisonment, at the discretion of the court.
SEC. 3. Any Ordinance or provision thereof, inconsistent with this ordinance is hereby repealed.
SEC. 4. This ordinance shall take effect on and after the 10th day following its passage.
Enacted: August 26, 1964.
