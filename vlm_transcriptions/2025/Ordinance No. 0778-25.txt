Ord. No. 0778-25

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF ARRIEL & REVELYN NENGASCA, REPRESENTING BUKAS LOGOB CATHOLIC CHARISMATIC COVENANT COMMUNITY-DAVAO DISTRICT, FOR FULL EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUNDRAISING EVENT: A BLOCK SCREENING OF 'SNOW WHITE' ON MARCH 23, 2025, AT SM CITY DAVAO CINEMA 4, ECOLAND, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16, Chapter II of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:

"Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare."

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Section 56 (b) of the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City provides that, "(b) where the admission fees are collected in connection with the holding of operas, concerts, dramas, recitals, paintings and art exhibitions, flower shows, musical programs, literary and oratorical presentations except pop rock, or similar concerts not intended primarily for profit and furthermore, not including film exhibitions."

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - The aforementioned exemption shall be based on the system of computation adopted by the Business Tax and License Division of the City Treasurer's Office and their projected income statement, enumerated herein:

| NO. | OF | AMOUNT PER TICKET | TOTAL SALES | 10% TAX |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 399 | PHP 600.00 | PHP 239,400.00 | PHP 23,940.00 |

SECTION 5. TRANSMITTAL - The Office of the Secretary to the Sanggunian Panlungsod is hereby directed to transmit a copy of the said Ordinance to the Executive Department immediately upon its approval.

SECTION 6. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, March 18, 2025, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
