SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES - Civil
Service Commission (CSC) Memorandum Circular (MC) Number 12, Series of 2022,
issued the 2022 Guidelines and Standards in the Establishment of Organizational
Structures and Staffing Patterns in Local Government Units pursuant to CSC Resolution
No. 2200373. Titles of positions in Local Government Units corresponding to the
same or similar positions description as those established in the latest BBA Circular
shall be used and adopted:

"The Secretary to the Sanggunian shall take charge of the
Office of the Secretary to the Sanggunian and shall:
xxx
(9) Take custody of the local archives and, where applicable, the local
library and annually account for the same";
B. Section 76 of Republic Act No. 7160
"Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern - Every local government
unit shall design and implement its own organizational structure and
staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and
financial capability, subject to the minimum standards and guidelines
prescribed by the Civil Service Commission.";
C. Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994
Section 1 of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, entitled "An Ordinance
approving the organizational structure and staffing pattern of the City of
Davao, and for other purposes" approved the attached volumes of
staffing pattern and organization structure of the city;
D. CSC Memorandum Circular No. 12, Series of 2022
CSC MC No. 12, Series of 2022, states the adoption of the 2022
Guidelines and Standards in the Establishment of Organizational
Structures and Staffing Patterns (OSSPs) in Local Government Units
(LGU's) pursuant to CSC Resolution No. 2200373, promulgated on
September 20, 2022:
1. Appropriate OSSPs of LGUs shall be determined and established in
accordance with Section 17 (Basic Services and Facilities) of
Republic Act No. 7160;

This is the manual covering the principles governing the position classification and compensation system in the Philippines.

**SECTION 4. AMENDMENT OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE DIVISION OF THE OFFICE OF THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSO IN SECTION 3 (ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO) OF ORDINANCE NO. 2374, SERIES OF 1994.**

Section 1 (Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City Government of Davao) of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, specifically the Administrative Division of the Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall be amended.

A. The Records and Correspondence Section under the Administrative Division shall have additional positions of four (4) Local Legislative Staff Employee II SG-4;

B. Additional position of Administrative Assistant V SG-11 under the Property and Miscellaneous Section of the Administrative Division shall be created.

**SECTION 5. AMENDMENT OF THE ARCHIVAL AND LIBRARY DIVISION OF THE OFFICE OF THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSO IN SECTION 3 (ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO) OF ORDINANCE NO. 2374, SERIES OF 1994.**

The Archival and Library Division of the Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall be amended, as follows:

A. The Division shall be headed by a Local Legislative Staff Officer V with Salary Grade 22;

B. The position title of the Assistant Division Head shall be Local Legislative Staff Officer IV with Salary Grade 19;

C. The Archival Services Section shall be headed by a Local Legislative Officer III with Salary Grade 16.

**SECTION 6. AMENDMENT OF THE RESOLUTIONS, ORDINANCES AND AGENDA DIVISION OF THE OFFICE OF THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSO IN SECTION 3 (ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE AND STAFFING PATTERN OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO) OF ORDINANCE NO. 2374, SERIES OF 1994.**

Section 1 (Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City Government of Davao) of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, specifically the Resolutions, Ordinances and Agenda Division of the Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall be amended by creating an additional section which shall be named as Information and Technology Section.

A. The newly created section shall be composed of one (1) Information Technology Officer I SG-19, one (1) Computer Programmer II SG-15, one (1) Senior Administrative Assistant II (Computer Operator IV) SG-14, two (2) Computer Maintenance Technologist I SG-11, one (1) Administrative Aide VI (Clerk III) SG-6, and one (1) Administrative Aide II (Utility Worker II) SG-3.

B. The functions of the Information and Technology Section include:

1. Planning, development, and implementation of Information Technology plans and programs;

2. Serves as a support function of the secretariat to achieve more effective and efficient legislation and operations;

3. Manages all information system and technology resources in accordance to the needs and priorities of the office;  
4. Develops, implements, and maintains software applications that respond to the common needs within the office;  
5. Operates and maintains audio and video equipment;  
6. Troubleshoots and resolves equipment and technical problems;  
7. Ensures positive client relations, specifically providing excellent service, punctuality, reliability, technical competence, and professionalism; and  
8. Performs other functions that the office may require pursuant to government rules, laws, and regulations.
