Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH (DOH) - DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT - REGION XI AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE PAYMENT OF HEALTH EMERGENCY ALLOWANCE TO PUBLIC HEALTH WORKERS IN THE CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22 (a) (5) and (c) and 455 (b) (1) (vii) of Republic Act 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provide that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

*Section 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government. And in this connection, shall;
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the amended Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the Department of Health (DOH) - Davao Center for Health Development - Region XI and the City Government of Davao, relative to the payment of health emergency allowance to public health workers in the city.

**AMENDMENT TO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT**

This Amendment is entered into by and between:

THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH (DOH) - DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT, a national government agency/Department of Regional health office with office address at DOH Compound, J.P. Laurel, Davao City, herein represented by ABDULLAH B. DUHAMA JR., MD, MPA, CESO I, in her official capacity as Undersecretary of Health and Concurrent Regional Director, and hereafter referred to as "FIRST PARTY";

and

The CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall Building, San Pedro Street, Davao City, Davao del Sur, herein represented by its Mayor, SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, and hereafter referred to as "SECOND PARTY";

WHEREAS, the purpose of this Amendment is to modify certain clauses set forth in the attached Memorandum of Agreement due to the request for extension of submission of liquidation report sought by the SECOND PARTY for its inability to fully liquidate the first tranche of downloaded funds, and the submission of a commitment letter to modify the period for which to utilize the funds and fully liquidate the same to which the FIRST PARTY interposed no objection, subject to strict compliance of the amendments made herein.

NOW THEREFORE, and in consideration of these mutual undertakings and covenants, the parties agree as follows:

1. Section 1 (A) shall read, "THE FIRST PARTY shall transfer funds to the SECOND PARTY in the amount of Php 527,866,406.25 for the grant of HEA to the eligible HCWs and non-HCWs, upon submission of the complete documentary requirements and subject to pertinent accounting rules and regulations. The transfer of funds shall be in two tranches, first by October 2024, in the amount of Php 188,267,281.25, covering May to December 2022, and the other by December 2024, in the amount of Php 339,599,125.00, covering July to December 2021, January to July 2023 and all approved HEA appeals."

2. Section 1 (B) (7) shall read, "Utilize the transferred funds for the intended purpose not later than March 15, 2025 and return to the FIRST PARTY any unutilized funds. No further extension of the said period shall be allowed;"

3. Section 1 (B) (8) shall read, "Submit a liquidation report on March 15, 2025, with the attached Reports of Checks Issued (RCI) and the Report of Disbursement (RD) certified correct by the Head of the Accounting Office and approved by the Head of the Implementing Agency. No further extension of the said period to submit a liquidation report shall be allowed."

This Amendment and the Memorandum of Agreement are incorporated into each other and, when read together, constitute one integrated document (the "HEA Memorandum of Agreement"). Any inconsistency, conflict, or ambiguity between this Addendum and the Memorandum of Agreement shall be resolved by giving precedence and effect to this Amendment.
