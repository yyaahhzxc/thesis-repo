Ord. No. 0789-25

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE PHILIPPINE STATISTICS AUTHORITY (PSA) AND CITY OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE COMMUNITY-BASED MONITORING SYSTEM (CBMS)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22(a), (5), and (c); and 455 (b) (v) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, provide:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.-
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
xxx
(5) To enter into contracts; and xxx
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sangguniang concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation - xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) by and between the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) and the City of Davao relative to the Community-Based Monitoring System (CBMS).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ADDENDUM TO EXTEND MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Addendum, executed by and between:

PHILIPPINE STATISTICS AUTHORITY, a national government agency created by virtue of Republic Act (RA) No. 10625 or the "Philippine Statistical Act of 2013" with office address located at PSA Complex, East Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City, duly represented herein by RANDOLPH ANTHONY B. GALES, Officer-in-Charge, with office address located at Anglo Bldg., J.P. Catagalpo Ave., Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the "FIRST PARTY,"

and

The CITY OF DAVAO, with office address located at City Hall Bldg., San Pedro St., Davao City, represented herein by SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, Local Chief Executive, and hereinafter referred to as "SECOND PARTY."

WITNESSETH, that:

WHEREAS, in 2024, simultaneous conduct of the Census of Population and Community-Based Monitoring System (2024 POPCEN-CBMS), under Republic Act Nos. 10625 and 11315, respectively, were led by the FIRST PARTY to update the mid-decade inventory of the population of the entire Philippines and social registries of national government agencies and LGUs for evidence-based project development and program implementation;

WHEREAS, despite the integration of two statistical activities, the POPCEN remains a PSA-funded and facilitated undertaking, while only CBMS operations in Local Government Units within the 1st to 8th income classifications are PSA-funded and facilitated;

WHEREAS, Highly Urbanized Cities shall fund their own CBMS implementation, and the FIRST PARTY shall assist them either by conducting CBMS activities on its behalf or by providing technical assistance to ensure data quality;

WHEREAS, in May 2024, the parties entered into an agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Original Agreement"), designating the FIRST PARTY to lead the implementation of the Community-Based Monitoring System (CBMS) on behalf of the SECOND PARTY within its locality;

WHEREAS, the parties hereby agree to extend the term of the Original Agreement and conduct additional activities in accordance with the terms set out herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual covenants contained in this Addendum, the parties agree as follows:

--- [Page 8] ---
I. EXTENSION

The Original Agreement will expire on 31 March 2025 (hereinafter referred to as "Original Term"). Except to the extent otherwise provided in this Addendum, the Original Agreement will be extended on the same terms and conditions until 31 December 2025, or until the completion of the activities set forth in this Extended Agreement, whichever comes first, which will begin immediately on the expiry of the Original Term.

II. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES

The parties agree to execute the following responsibilities during the period of the Extended Agreement, to wit:

1. Continue performing all duties, obligations, and responsibilities outlined in the Original Agreement;

2. Conduct the updated activities hereto attached as Annex 1.

III. CONFLICT OF PROVISIONS

The provisions of this Addendum and its annexes shall form part of the Original Agreement. Except to the extent otherwise amended in this Addendum and its annexes, all other terms and conditions of the Original Agreement shall remain the same, provided that in the event of a conflict between the provisions of this Addendum and its annexes and those of the Original Agreement, the terms and provisions of this Addendum and its annexes shall supersede those provisions of the Original Agreement with which they conflict.
