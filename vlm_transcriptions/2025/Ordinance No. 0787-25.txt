Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the attached Ordinance No. 0787-25, Series of 2025 entitled "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY PARTIAL CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC THE STREET ALONG AGTON FROM CORNER LAO STREET TO CORNER TORIL HEALTH CENTER, IN FRONT OF TORIL PARK ADJACENT TO THE DISTRICT HALL, ON MARCH 27, 2025, FROM 5:00 PM UP TO 11:00 PM, IN CONNECTION WITH THE UPCOMING POLICE ACTION" informing you that the subject measure is in accord with Section 2.1 of Republic Act 7163, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 to quote:
"SECTION 21. Closure and Opening of Roads. --- (a) A local government may, pursuant to an ordinance promulgated or temporarily close or open any local road, alley, park, or square falling within its jurisdiction: Provided, however, That in case of permanent closure, such ordinance must be approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of all the members of the sanggunian, and when necessary, an adequate substitute for the public facility that is subject to closure is provided.
Xxx
(c) Any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs, or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications, and waterworks projects, the duration of which shall be specified by the local chief executive concerned in a written order: Provided, however, That no national or local road, alley, park, or square may be closed for any purpose other than those of general or civic activities not officially sponsored, recognized, or approved by the local government unit concerned".
In view thereof, this office recommends the approval of the subject ordinance.

Ord. No. 0787-25

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY PARTIAL CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC THE STREET ALONG AND FROM CORNER LAD STREET TO CORNER TORIL HEALTH CENTER, IN FRONT OF TORIL PARK ADJACENT TO THE DISTRICT HALL, ON MARCH 27, 2025, FROM 5:00 PM UP TO 11:00 PM IN CONNECTION WITH THE UPCOMING PRAYER RALLY FOR PEACE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs xxx".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the upcoming Prayer Rally for Peace, PB Joseph Dumogho of the Barangay Council of Daliang, Toril District, this City, requested for the temporary partial closure to vehicular traffic along the specified street, date and time specified herein.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 25th day of March 2025, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
