WHEREAS, as reflected on the table above, the Office of the City Mayor got the highest allocation of P708,669,295.03 which is equivalent to 73.75% of the total appropriation. This is contributed to the allocation for Gratuity Pay for FY 2024 to all personnel hired under Job Order and Contractual Services in the total amount of P153,237,000.00, pursuant to Administrative Order No. 28, Series of 2024 and Local Budget Act No. 100, Series of 2024. The allocation for the Salary of the City Administrator of P454,388,928.00 has been reapportioned under the Peace and Order and Public Safety Program/1011-1 due to changes in priorities. Other appropriations included were coming from various units under the City Mayor's Office, i.e., Museo Dabawenyo, Davao City Treatment and Rehabilitation Center for Drug Dependents, Integrated Gender and Development Division, Davao City Office for Culture and the Arts, Barangay Secretariat, City Information Office, Sports and Tourism Promotion Program, and the City of Davao Police Department. These allocations are intended to be transferred to national government agencies, and augmentation to the current year's appropriation for Public Service Enhancement, Training and Educational Assistance Program. Likewise, adequate funds have been provided for Social Security and Insurance Expenses under the Statutory Obligations.

WHEREAS, the remaining amounts allocated to the City Environment, and Natural Resources Bureau and the Nationalization of P132,428,118.16 or 13.78% of the total budget. Said amount is intended to augment the current year's appropriation for the Workers' Program, as well as, the Garbage Collection (Final Disposal) of the said Offices.

WHEREAS, the remaining amounts were allocated to certain offices, especially to the Sangguniang Panlungsod and the Human Resource Management Office that have undergone organizational restructuring, pursuant to Ordinance No. 0785-25, Series of 2023 and Ordinance No. 0799-24, Series of 2024, respectively.

WHEREAS, after careful deliberation on the matter, the Committee on Finance, Ways and Means and Appropriations, favorably recommends the approval in toto, of the FY 2025 Supplemental Budget No. 1 of the City Government of Davao:

1. Part I - Statement of the Sources of Funds in the total amount of NINE HUNDRED SIXTY MILLION EIGHT HUNDRED SIXTY-THREE THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED SEVENTY-TWO PESOS and 27/100 (960,863,872.27), and Part II - Supplemental Appropriations with the same total amount of NINE HUNDRED SIXTY MILLION EIGHT HUNDRED SEVENTY-TWO PESOS and 27/100 (960,863,872.27), thereby leaving no unappropriated Balance; therefore;

2. Passage of an "ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO FY 2025 SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 1 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF NINE HUNDRED SIXTY-THREE THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED SEVENTY-TWO PESOS AND 27/100 (960,863,872.27);"

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO APPROPRIATE SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 1 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CONSISTING OF THE GENERAL FUND PROPER IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF NINE HUNDRED SIXTY MILLION EIGHT HUNDRED SIXTY-THREE THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED SEVENTY-TWO PESOS AND 27/100 (P960,863,872.27);

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time, ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use;

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS. The following amounts or so much thereof, as may be necessary, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Treasurer of Davao City, not otherwise appropriated from the operation of the City Government of Davao from January 1 to December 31, 2025, except as otherwise provided therein;

SECTION 4. AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES. The FY 2025 Supplemental Budget No. 1 of the City Government of Davao covers the total Sources of Funds in the total amount of NINE HUNDRED SIXTY MILLION EIGHT HUNDRED SIXTY-THREE THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED SEVENTY-TWO PESOS AND 27/100 (P960,863,872.27), which was made available by the City Treasurer of Davao City for the above named total amount of NINE HUNDRED SIXTY MILLION EIGHT HUNDRED SEVENTY-TWO PESOS AND 27/100 (P960,863,872.27), thereby leaving no Unappropriated Balance thereof;

**SECTION 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS.**  
The amount herein appropriated for the projects indicated therein shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

**SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS.**  
The use of funds under the FY 2025 Supplemental Budget No. 1 of the City Government of Davao, utilized in violation of Title 5, Local Fiscal Administration, as embodied in RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, shall subject the erring officials and employees to disciplinary action or to appropriate legal measures under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget must conform with existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules, regulations, policies, and guidelines of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), as amended by Presidential Decree No. 140. Also, the Government Procurement Reform Act (RA No. 9184) as amended by RA No. 12009 or the New Government Procurement Act, as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances and other Presidential Directives;

**SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.**  
If any section, any section or provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof, which have been affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect. Authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod to enact Ordinance shall take effect on the date of its enactment.
