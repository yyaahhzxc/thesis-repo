ORDINANCE NO. 44

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE USE OF MUNICIPAL WATERS OR OTHER CITY PRO-
PERTY NOT OTHERWISE NEEDED FOR PUBLIC USE WHICH MAY BE USED FOR LOG
POND OR FOR STORAGE OF LOGS AND IMPOSING A MUNICIPAL LICENSE FEE FOR
THE USE THEREOF.

Be it ordained by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - Regulating the use of Municipal Waters and Other
Properties.- Except as herein provided for, no portion of the Municipal
Waters within the jurisdiction of Davao City or any other City property
shall be used for storing and/or depositing logs, and provided further,
that such use shall not endanger life and property, and/or obstruct na-
vigation, public drainage, public highways, fire protection and sanita-
tion.

SEC. 2. - Fees. - For every one (1) hectare or fraction thereof
of any portion of any Municipal Waters within the jurisdiction of Davao
City, or any public property that will be used for storing and/or de-
positing logs, a license fee of Two Hundred Pesos (P200.00) per annum
payable quarterly shall be paid to the City Treasurer, after issuance
of permit by the City Mayor upon recommendation of the City Engineer,
City Health Officer and the Chief of the Fire Department.

SEC. 3. - Definition of Terms. - "Municipal Waters" under this
Ordinance shall mean rivers, creeks, waterways, swamps, mouth of rivers
and sea along the shoreline within the three (3) miles territorial limits.

SEC. 4. - Limitations of Permit. - (a) Where a permit is granted
for the use of the bank of the river, creek, or other internal waterways,
which is navigable or capable of navigation by water crafts of any size,
the permit shall be directed to the use of only one bank of said river,
creek or other internal waterways; provided, however, that the use of
such bank shall not in any way obstruct the navigation of such river or
cause impediment in case of flood that will endanger life and property.

(b) Persons to whom the use of any Municipal Water is granted shall
have the duty to see to it that the regulations are properly observed and

SEC. 4. - Penal Provisions. - Any person, corporation, partnership, organization who uses for storing and/or depositing logs any portion of any of the Municipal Waters within the jurisdiction of Davao City, or any other public property, without first securing a permit from the City Mayor and paying the municipal license fee as provided under Section 2 hereof, shall be fined not more than Two Hundred Pesos (P200.00) or imprisonment for not more than six (6) months or both such fine and imprisonment at the discretion of the Court.

SEC. 5. - Transitory Provision. - This Ordinance shall not be effective upon areas, the use of which as log ponds, had been previously granted by the City of Davao under administrative concession agreement; provided, however, that upon the expiration of such administrative concession agreements, said areas shall fall within the provisions of this Ordinance and its use as log ponds shall be subject to the provisions hereof.

SEC. 6. - Effectivity. - This Ordinance shall take effect ten (10) days after approval.
