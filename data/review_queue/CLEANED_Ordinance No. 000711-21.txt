WHEREAS, the Slim Improvement Resettlement (SIR) Project is a low-cost housing project of the City Government of Davao under a Memorandum of Agreement with the National Housing Authority (NHA);

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao is the registered owner of the properties covered by the Slim Improvement Resettlement (SIR) Project;

WHEREAS, the Slim Improvement Resettlement (SIR) Project is in line with the State's declared policy to provide affordable housing to qualified beneficiaries;

WHEREAS, a lot in the Slim Improvement Resettlement (SIR) Project was sold to MAXIMIANO M. ATUEL, a qualified beneficiary under the Code of Policies of the SIR Program;

WHEREAS, Maximiano M. Atuel is an awardee of a parcel of land located at Block 15 Lot 10, New Matina Sites and Services Phase I, consisting of an area of ninety-eight (98) square meters, more or less;

WHEREAS, a Certification was issued by Rodolfo C. Arsua of the City Planning and Development Office certifying that the applicant has paid in full the value of it in the amount of Twenty-Eight Thousand Five Hundred Eighty-Nine Pesos and 73.100 (P28,589.73). The final and full amount was paid on February 2, 2016 per Official Receipt No. 5747400;

WHEREAS, a certification was also issued by Mr. Rodolfo C. Arsua, Estate Management-In-Charge (Census Evaluator), certifying that the beneficiary possesses all of the qualifications and none of the disqualifications provided under Section 14 of Republic Act 7275, otherwise known as the "Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992" (U.D.H.A);

WHEREAS, in compliance with the individual requirements for the Documentation of the Deed of Sale, the following requirements were submitted by the beneficiary:

Latest 2x2 picture (2 copies)

Latest Residence Certificate or Cedula

Marriage Contract (Clear Photocopy)

Affidavit of No Landholding

Certificate of No Landholding (City Legal Office)

Latest Barangay Clearance

Full Payment Receipt;

NOW THEREFORE, on motion of Councilor Jesus Joseph P. Zozobrado III, duly seconded by Councilors Pier C. Braga and Mary Joselle D. Villafuerte, be it resolved, as it is hereby resolved, to ENACT AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING AND DIVULGING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND IN REHABILITATE THE CITY GOVERNMENT BUILDINGS AND LOT FOR THE LOT UNDER THE SLIM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROJECT IN NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES PHASE I, CONSISTING OF NINETY-EIGHT (98) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF MAXIMIANO M. ATUEL;
