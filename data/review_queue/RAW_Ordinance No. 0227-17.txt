ORDINANCE NO. 0227-17
Series of 2017
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS
OF ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, SERIES OF 2012,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "COMPREHENSIVE
TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC CODE OF DAVAO CITY"

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING PERTINENT PROVISIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 0334-12, SERIES OF 2012, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE "COMPREHENSIVE TRANSPORT AND TRAFFIC CODE OF DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVES. - This Ordinance aims to:
a. Stabilize the traffic flow in Davao City for all kinds of vehicles travelling in and outside the City;
b. Promote the regularity of business transactions through unhindered carriage and shipment of goods within and outside of Davao City at any hour of the day or any day of the week;

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT. - The following provisions of Ordinance No. 0334-12, Series of 2012, are hereby amended:

Section 148.

(a) The entry and exit of cargo trucks, gravel and sand trucks and other heavy cargo trucks with more than six (6) wheels and up, or whose gross vehicle weight exceed 4,500 kilograms, including tractor trailers, containerized haulers, or refrigerated vans shall not be allowed to travel on any and all city streets, highways, bridges, tunnels, underpasses, Special and Non-Working Holidays, and along streets specified as truck routes shown in Appendix VI. The use of truck routes shall be restricted to the hours of five in the morning (5:00 a.m.) to nine in the morning (9:00 a.m.), and from four in the afternoon (4:00 p.m.) to eight in the evening (8:00 p.m.).

The corresponding Appendix VI is likewise amended, as follows:

* Southbound to Northbound Route coming from General Santos City and Cotabato

* Southbound to Northbound Route coming from Cagayan and Bukidnon
From Catalunan Pequeño Proper up to corner Greenhilsa Subdivision;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, July 4, 2017, by an unanimous vote of all Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
