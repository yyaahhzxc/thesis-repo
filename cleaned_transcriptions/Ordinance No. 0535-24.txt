xxx

xxx

SISTER CITY AGREEMENT

THE CITY OF DAVAO,
REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES
AND
MUNICIPALITY OF KALIBO, PROVINCE OF AKLAN
REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES

The City of Davao, Republic of the Philippines, and Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, the Republic of The Philippines, herein to referred collectively as "the Parties";

ANTECEDENTS
1. The City of Davao and the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan have been active in seeking for potential local partners for development and mutual cooperation;
2. On November 9, 2022, a letter of intent from the Municipality of Kalibo, inviting the City of Davao to form a Sisterhood Relationship, to which the latter hereby accepts;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and their mutual covenant hereinafter set forth, the parties hereby agree on the following provisions:

Article 1. Objective of the Sister City Agreement
The objective of the agreement is to establish a Sister City Relationship to promote mutually beneficial cooperation and deeper understanding between the parties through an exchange of ideas in the fields identified in Article 2.

Article 2. Cooperation
The City of Davao and the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan intend to create and fortify an open communicative atmosphere to induce a heightened exchange of ideas, particularly in the following fields:

1. TOURISM
2. EDUCATION
3. BUSINESS, TRADE AND INVESTMENT
4. TECHNOLOGY
5. CULTURE

--- [Page 12] ---
**Article 3. Further Agreements**

It is understood that this Sister City Agreement shall serve only as a preliminary agreement of the Parties. The activities being contemplated herein, as well as the performance of each Party of this undertaking, or in furtherance of this Sister City Agreement, shall be subject to further definitive agreements.

**Article 4. Amendments and Approvals**

No amendment or supplement to this agreement, and no approval or consent constituting an amendment, shall be effective for any purpose unless made in writing and signed by a duly authorized officer of both Parties.

**Article 5. Effectivity, Validity and Renewal**

This agreement shall be effective and valid for five (5) years from the date of signing hereof. It shall be considered extended and/or renewed for another five (5) years, if no objection in writing from either side is served upon the other within thirty (30) days of the expiration of the initial five (5) year term.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Mayors of both parties have caused this agreement to be executed in duplicate by placing their signatures thereon, each party retaining one (1) copy thereof, respectively on , 2024.

**RESOLUTION NO. (01) Series of 2024**

A RESOLUTION TO ENACT AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO THE SISTER CITY AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO, PHILIPPINES AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF KALIBO, AKLAN, PHILIPPINES

WHEREAS, this resolution pertains to the proposed Sister City Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao, Philippines and the Municipality of Kalibo, Aklan, Philippines as partners for development and mutual cooperation;

WHEREAS, the Davao City International Relations Board (DCIRB) held a meeting on September 26, 2023, to discuss the proposed sisterhood agreement between the City of Davao and the Municipality of Kalibo, Aklan;

WHEREAS, Municipality of Kalibo, Aklan is located in the north-west of Panay. It is the main transportation hub for the resort island of Boracay. The Municipality is known for its Ati-Athlean Festival, the semi-urban and multi-awarded mangrove forest, the Bahkawan Eco-Park and Pilaña-weaving;

WHEREAS, Municipality of Kalibo, Aklan has a population of 92,982 as of 2020, and the total land area is 1,800 sq. km.;;

WHEREAS, the particular areas of cooperation that the two parties may explore include the exchange of knowledge and interest, business, tourism, cultural development, peace and order, agriculture, and industrial;

WHEREAS, the Davao City International Relations Board suggested to include Aklan's thriving manufacturing industries in the agreement to share best practices in industrialization;

WHEREAS, the DCIRB issued Resolution No. 4, Series of 2023, entitled: "A Resolution Favorably Endorsing the Sister City Agreement between the City of Davao and the Municipality of Kalibo, Aklan to the Davao City Council Committee on International Relations for the Legislative Authority for Mayor Sebastian Z. Duterte to sign the said agreement for and on behalf of the City of Davao";

WHEREAS, Legal Opinion No. 347, Series of 2024 was issued by the Office of the City Legal Officer (CLO) on May 10, 2024, finding the Agreement and the attached documents without legal infirmity and that the same shall be forwarded to the Sangguniang for the required authority pursuant to Section 455, subparagraph (vi) of paragraph (b) of R.A. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991;;
