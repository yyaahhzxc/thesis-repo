44. Computer Software (10991020)
This account is used for the purchase cost or capitalized development cost of computer software program for use in government operation. Development costs include cost of coding, testing, and cost to produce product masters.

45. Communication Network (10703060)
This account is used for the cost incurred in the purchase or construction or fair value, if acquired through donation or transfers without cost, of communication networks such as towers/transmitters/ transceivers and other communication/telecommunication facilities for public use or for income generating purposes.

46. Other Investments (10205990)
This account is used to record other deposits or investments.

COA Circular No. 2022-004 dated May 31, 2022, provides the policy for the increase on the capitalization threshold from P15,000.00 to P50,000.00 which is effective retrospectively from CY 2022 and onwards.

NOTE: Accounts herein included are those that the City Offices/Departments would usually need in the course of its operation. Other accounts may be referred to as "Other Funds" under the Accounts under the Philippine Public Sector Accounting Standards (PPSAS) per Commission on Audit (COA) Circular No. 2015-009 dated December 1, 2015.

BY AUTHORITY OF THE SECRETARY OF FINANCE AND MANAGEMENT

SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS
The use of funds under the Annual Budget of the City Government of San Fernando, Pampanga utilized in violation of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as Senate Local Government Code of 1991, shall subject the erring officials and employees to disciplinary action or appropriate legal measures under the jurisdictional authority of the Department of Finance conform with existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules, regulations, policies, and guidelines of the Commission on Audit (COA), Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.

SECTION 7. USE OF SAVINGS
The Local Chief Executive and the Presiding Officer of the Sangguniang Panlungsod are hereby authorized to augment any item in the approved Annual Budget for their respective offices from savings in other items within the same expense class of their respective appropriations as provided for in Section 336 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991."

SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE
The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable, and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.
