Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THE FUNDRAISING EVENT, A MOVIE SCREENING OF 'MOANA 2' TO BE HELD ON NOVEMBER 30, 2024 AT THE SM CITY DAVAO, ECOLAND, THIS CITY, REQUESTED BY BATCH 2027 PTA PHILIPPINE SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL SOUTHERN MINDANOAN CAMPUS (PSHS SMC), REPRESENTED BY ITS PRESIDENT, ATTY. FERDINAND C. TUGAS, FOR THE ONE HUNDRED TWENTY (120) SCHOLARS OF THE PHILIPPINE SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL SOUTHERN MINDANOAN CAMPUS (PSHS SMC)."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 192 and 458 (a) (2) (vii) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provide for the authority and function of the Local Government in granting such tax exemption, viz:

"Section 192. Authority to Grant Tax Exemption Privileges - Local government units may, through ordinances duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs under such terms and conditions as they may deem necessary."

"Section 458 - Powers, Duties, Functions and Compensation - (a) The Sangguniang Panlungsod, as the legislative body of the city, shall enact ordinances, approve resolutions and appropriate funds for the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code and in the proper exercise of the corporate powers of the city as provided for under Section 22 of this code, and shall:

(2)(vii) With the concurrence of at least two-thirds (2/3) of all the members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs to entities engaged in community growth-inducing industries xxx."

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - The exemption shall be based on the system of computation adopted by the Business Tax and License Division of the City Treasurer's Office and their projected income statement, herein enumerated:

| NO. OF | AMOUNT | TOTAL | 10% TAX |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| TICKETS | PER     | SALES | TICKET |
|        | P235.00 | P93,765.00 | P9,376.50 |

SECTION 3. TRANSMITTAL - The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is hereby directed to transmit a copy of the said Ordinance to the Executive Department immediately upon its approval.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
