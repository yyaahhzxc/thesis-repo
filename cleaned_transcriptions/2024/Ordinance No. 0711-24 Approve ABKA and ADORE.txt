Series of 2024

AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF THE ATENEO DE DAVAO BLUE KNIGHT ASSOCIATION (ABKA) AND THE ATENEO DE DAVAO ORGANIZATION OF RETIREES AND FORMER EMPLOYEES, INC. (ADORE), FOR FULL EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUNDRAISING EVENT: A BLOCK SCREENING OF THE LORD OF THE RINGS: THE WAR OF THE ROHRRRIM, TO BE HELD ON DECEMBER 11, 2024, AT SM CITY DAVAO CINEMA

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0711-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF THE ATENEO DE DAVAO BLUE KNIGHT ASSOCIATION (ABKA) AND THE ATENEO DE DAVAO ORGANIZATION OF RETIREES AND FORMER EMPLOYEES, INC. (ADORE), FOR FULL EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX ON THE PROCEEDS OF THEIR FUNDRAISING EVENT: A BLOCK SCREENING OF THE LORD OF THE RINGS: THE WAR OF THE ROHIRRIM, TO BE HELD ON DECEMBER 11, 2024, AT SM CITY DAVAO CINEMA".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16, Chapter II, of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare" xxx.

SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Section 66 (b) of the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City provides that, "(b) where the admission fees are collected in connection with the holding of operas, concerts, dramas, recitals, painting and at exhibitions, flower shows, musical programs, literacy and oratorical presentations except pop rock, or similar concerts not intended for profit and furthermore, not including film exhibition".

SECTION 4. COVERAGE - The aforementioned exemption shall be based on the system of computation adopted by the Business Tax and License Division of the City Treasurer's Office and their projected income statement, herein enumerated:

| NO. OF TICKETS | AMOUNT PER TICKET | TOTAL SALES | 10% TAX |
|----------------|------------------|------------|---------|
| 2,332           | PHP 215.00        | PHP 501,380.00 | PHP 50,138.00 |

SECTION 5. TRANSMITTAL - The Office of the Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod is hereby directed to transmit a copy of the said Ordinance to the Executive Department immediately upon its approval.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect immediately after approval.

ENACTED, on the 3rd day of December 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
