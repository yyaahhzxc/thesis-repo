Series of 2024

An Ordinance for the Temporary Closure to Vehicular Traffic of the Streets along Corner Aurora-Suazo St. to Corner Aurora-Ponce St., Barangay 25-C, poblacion district, this city, in connection with their 2nd Semeser of the Sangguniang Panlungsod Assembly on October 27, 2024 (SUNDAY) from 8:00 AM until 12:00 Noon in order to therein "TALAKAYAN SA BARANGAY, AKTIBONG DISKUSYON NG MAYPANAYAN TUNGO SA MASIGLA AT MAUNLAD NA BAGONG PILIPINAS"

Ord. No. 0550-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE STREETS ALONG CORNER AURORA-SUAZO ST. TO CORNER AURORA-PONCE ST., BARANGAY 25-C, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY, IN CONNECTION WITH THEIR 2nd SEMESTER BARANGAY ASSEMBLY ON OCTOBER 27, 2024 (SUNDAY) FROM 8:00 AM UNTIL 12:00 NOON WITH THE THEME 'PAG-ASA NG PAMBANGAY' ARTIKULADO NG PAMATAYAN TUNGO SA MASIGLA AT MAHULAD NA BAGONG PILIPINAS."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairsoon.

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE - In connection with their upcoming 2nd Semester Barangay Assembly, PB Lito Maquiligan requested for a temporary road closure of the roads herein mentioned.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 22nd day of October 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
