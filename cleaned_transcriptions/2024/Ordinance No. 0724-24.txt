**BOARD RESOLUTION NO. 018 (s. 2024)**

**CONFIRMING THE AUTHORITY OF THE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE DEVELOPMENT ACADEMY OF THE PHILIPPINES TO NEGOTIATE AND SIGN AGREEMENTS WITH OTHER ENTITIES IN THE FURTHERANCE OF THE ACADEMY'S GOALS AND OBJECTIVES**

WHEREAS, the field of development in its various aspects, individual and national, formal and informal, human and organizational, admit varied interventions and contributions from individual groups, associations, corporations, foundations, and other related entities, the dynamic and creative potential of which may not be contained nor circumscribed;

WHEREAS, the Development Academy of the Philippines (DAP) is cognizant of these capabilities from other individuals, groups, and entities which it would like to access through appropriate tie-ups, memoranda, contracts, and agreements;

WHEREAS, to achieve the mentioned objective, it is necessary to adequately empower the management of DAP to enter into such contracts or agreements;

WHEREAS, under Referendum No. 01 Series of 1999, the DAP Board of Trustees (BOT) authorized the President of the Academy to negotiate and sign agreements with other entities to further the Academy's goals and objectives;

WHEREAS, under Board Resolution No. 2018-026, the DAP BOT, acting as the Head of the Procurement Entity (HoPE), delegated to the DAP President and CEO (PCEO) the authority to approve Procurement Activities for purposes of economy and expediency, and increased the amount of the Approved Budget for Contract (ABC) of the procurement activities delegated to the DAP PCEO from Ten Million Pesos (PHP 10,000,000.00) to Fifteen Million Pesos (PHP 15,000,000.00);

WHEREAS, the DAP BOT subsequently increased the amount of the ABC of the Procurement Activities delegated to the DAP PCEO from Fifteen Million Pesos (PHP 15,000,000) to Twenty Million Pesos (PHP 20,000,000.00) by virtue of Board Resolution No. 008 (s. 2023);

WHEREAS, under Board Resolution No. 001 (s. 2024), the DAP BOT ratified the DAP Procurement Activities from 01 July 2022 to 13 November 2023;

WHEREAS, at present, there is no existing threshold amount as to the Client-Funded Projects to which the DAP PCEO was authorized to negotiate and enter into;

NOW, THEREFORE, it is hereby RESOLVED, that the DAP PCEO is hereby authorized to negotiate, enter into, and contract with any individual, group, corporation, foundation, partnership, association, or any other entity, domestic or foreign, for the purpose of procuring goods, services, or works, including but not limited to the performance, completion or contribution to any DAP program, project, or enterprise, or any portion thereof, in the appreciation and discretion of the DAP PCEO, to the best interest of the DAP towards the achievement of its goals and objectives.

1. Procurement Activities in the nature of: 1) goods and services; 2) infrastructure works; 3) consulting services; and 4) alternative methods of procurement.

Procurement Activities delegated to the DAP PCEO pertain only to the regular course of business, affairs, and property, including repairs and maintenance of existing facilities, but exclude the acquisition of real assets.

This comprehensive approach ensures the optimization of its organizational structure and staffing pattern, leading to the efficient management of existing public services in alignment with best governance practices.

II. Background

Davao City is a multi-cultural and highly urbanized city in the southernmost region of the Philippines. It has developed enormously over the years due to the combined efforts of its citizens. As the capital city of the Davao Region, Davao City acts as the gateway to Mindanao. It also serves as the entry point for the Brunei Darussalam – Indonesia – Malaysia Peninsula East ASEAN Growth Area (BIMP-EAGA), making it Mindanao's development hub.

To sustain this continuous growth, the City Government of Davao is committed to enhancing and standardizing its systems and processes to ensure the delivery of high-quality public services to its constituents. Since its last major reorganization in 1998, it has become necessary to reexamine the current organizational structure and staffing patterns to effectively manage existing public services and meet emerging demands. In line with the City's initiative to revitalize its bureaucracy, the City Government of Davao has sought technical assistance from expert service providers.

The Development Academy of the Philippines (DAP), aligned with its mandate to promote excellence in governance and public administration, welcomes the opportunity to assist the City Government of Davao in this endeavor through technical assistance in strategic rightsizing. This partnership aims to support Davao City in achieving its vision of becoming a globally livable city and a center of excellence in governance, investment, tourism, climate change adaptation, disaster resilience, and sustainable growth, driven by an empowered and engaged citizenry.

The partnership will also leverage the DAP's expertise in strategic foresight. This approach involves anticipating future trends, challenges, and opportunities to make informed decisions that align with the City's strategic objectives.

By integrating strategic foresight into the reorganization process, Davao City can future-proof its operations, ensuring that the new organizational structure and processes are resilient and adaptable to current needs and future requirements. This involves scenario planning, trend analysis, and stakeholder engagement to envision multiple futures and develop strategic responses.

Anchoring on DAP's extensive track record in organizational development and strategic foresight through successful collaborations with various partners such as the Department of the Interior and Local Government, the City Government of Orotqueita, and other local government units and government agencies, the partnership aims to deliver a comprehensive and innovative approach to reorganization. By harnessing insights and methodologies developed through these collaborations, DAP aims to foster a reorganization process that is industry-responsive and cost-effective.

Through technical assistance, the City Government of Davao, with the support of DAP, will be able to develop an optimized organizational structure and staffing pattern, streamline systems and processes, and implement consistent public service delivery practices, thereby promoting sustainable and resilient governance practices. These collaborative efforts will ensure that Davao City remains future-proof and at the forefront of urban development and public service delivery, effectively meeting the needs of its growing population and setting a benchmark for excellence in the region.

III. Objectives

GENERAL OBJECTIVE

The project aims to provide technical assistance to the City Government of Davao in the process of reorganization. This comprehensive approach ensures the optimization of its organizational structure and staffing pattern, leading to the efficient management of existing public services in alignment with best governance practices.

SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES

Specifically, the project seeks to:

1. Conduct an assessment of the current organizational structure, functions, and processes of the City Government of Davao.
2. Identify areas for improvement and provide recommendations for structural, functional, and operational enhancements.
3. Facilitate the development of a new organizational structure that integrates insights which optimize resource allocation and enhances operational efficiency.
4. Develop and deliver training programs to address competency gaps among city personnel, focusing on enhancing efficiency and effectiveness in service delivery.
5. Foster a culture of continuous learning and professional development, drawing inspiration from successful training initiatives in comparable government organizations.
6. Facilitate the establishment of key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure the impact of reorganization efforts, ensuring accountability and transparency.

**IV. Project Components**

The project will have three (3) phases – (1) Inception & Mobilization; (2) Implementation; and (3) Evaluation and Closure. The design for project activities and the overall project framework shall be finalized upon detailed discussion with the City Government of Davao (LGU Davao) project team to ensure that these are well-tailored to the organization's needs.

**1. Phase I: Inception and Mobilization**

The DAP shall form a project team and prepare the required technical and administrative documents including the detailed project proposal, Memorandum of Agreement, detailed work plan, and other project documents. The DAP shall also lead the project mobilization meetings as well as ensure that experts from various fields are onboarded.

With the experts and the LGU Davao team, the final framework for the project shall be crafted and agreed upon during the face-to-face inception meeting. This will serve as a guide for everyone in the implementation of the project. To ensure the organization and protection of data shared to and from the project teams, the indicative report will also detail the communication protocols and other data privacy measures for the project.

The indicative procedures for the Reauthorization Process indicated below will serve as a guide for the DAP and LGU Davao teams in the conduct of the project activities. The DAP team will work closely with the LGU Davao team in the conduct of activities for Steps 3 to 5. These shall lead to the creation of the deliverables of the project, particularly the new organizational structure and staffing pattern of the City Government of Davao.
