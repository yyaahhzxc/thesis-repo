Ordinance No. 0587-24
Series of 2024
An Ordinance Granting Legislative Authority to the City Mayor to Utilize a Portion of the Three Percent (30%) Quick Response Fund (QRF) out of the Five Percent (5%) Discretionary Tax Reduction and Management Fund (Calamity Fund) of the City Government of Davao for Calendar Year 2024 in the Total Amount of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000,000) as Financial Assistance to the City of Tanauan, Batangas, Declared under a State of Calamity due to Typhoon Kristine

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0687-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING EXECUTIVE AUTHORITY TO THE MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUNDS OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2024 IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P500,000.00) AS FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE CITY OF TANAUAN, BATANGAS, DECLARED UNDER A STATE OF CALAMITY DUE TO TYPHOON KRISTINE."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City Government of Davao to adopt measures and adhere to the national principles and standards of humanitarian assistance in response to risk reduction and declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.

SECTION 3. BENEFICIARY - The City of Tanauan, Batangas, declared under a State of Calamity, is hereby declared a beneficiary of a financial assistance in the amount of Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (P500,000.00).

SECTION 4. LEGAL BASIS - Section 324 (d) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, as amended by Republic Act No. 8185, states that five percent (5%) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as annual lump sum appropriations for relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction, and other works or services in connection with calamities which may occur during the budget year. Provided, however, that such fund shall be used only in an area, or a portion thereof, at the local government unit or other areas declared by a disaster or calamity, as determined and declared by the local Sanggunian concerned.

Section 21 of Republic Act No. 10121 likewise provides that of the amount appropriated for LDRRMF, thirty percent (30%) shall be allocated as Quick Response Fund (QRF) or standby fund for relief and recovery programs in order that situation and living conditions of people in communities or areas struck by disasters, calamities, epidemics, or other emergencies may be restored to normal quick possible upon the recommendation of LDRRMO and approval of the Sanggunian concerned, the LDRRM may transfer the said fund to support disaster risk reduction works of other LDRRMs which are declared under a state of calamity.

SECTION 5. UTILIZATION OF FUNDS - The amount herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such item and expenditure approved by the Sanggunian Panlungsod. All disbursements of funds shall be subject to government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances and Presidential directives.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
