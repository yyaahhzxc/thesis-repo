AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE TWO RIGHTMOST LANES OF CENTRAL PARK AVENUE (IF COMING FROM MCARTHUR HIGHWAY ULAS SIDE, THE 2 LANES NEAREST MERCO) AND 2 LANES OF CORDILLERA ROAD, FROM CORNER CENTRAL PARK AVENUE TO CORNER ARAYAR ROAD (IF COMING FROM CENTRAL PARK AVENUE) ON DECEMBER 23 FROM 7:00 AM UP TO DECEMBER 25, 2024, IN RELATION TO THE CONDUCT OF THE BANGKAL OPEN HOUSE/PAHALIPAY SA TAL

Ord No. 0731-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE TWO RIGHTEST LANES OF CENTRAL PARK AVENUE (IF COMING FROM MCArtHILLS HIGHWAY ULAS SIDE, THE 2 LANES NEAREST MERCO) AND 2 LANES OF CORDILLERA ROAD, FROM CORNER CENTRAL PARK AVENUE TO CORNER ARAYAT ROAD (IF COMING FROM CENTRAL PARK AVENUE) ON DECEMBER 23 FROM 7:00 AM UP TO DECEMBER 25, 2024, IN RELATION TO THE CONDUCT OF THE BANGKAL OPEN HOUSE/PAHALPAY SA TAAL."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that: "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, and agricultural or industrial fairs xxx."

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with their upcoming Pahalipay sa Taal, Mr. Vince Jul O. Malicay, Chief of Staff, Office of the City Mayor, requested for the temporary closure to vehicular traffic of the streets herein mentioned on date and time specified herein.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
