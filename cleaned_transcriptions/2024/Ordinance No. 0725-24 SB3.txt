Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 3 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2024, COMPRISING THE BUDGET UNDER THE GENERAL FUND PROPER, THE DEVELOPMENT FUND, AND THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF FOUR HUNDRED THIRTY-THREE MILLION NINE HUNDRED TWENTY-EIGHT THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED NINETY-TWO PESOS AND 87/100 (PHP 433,928,192.87)."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Aligned with its mandate and in response to the community's needs, the City Government of Davao affirms its commitment to optimize available resources. It likewise emphasizes the responsible and prudent utilization of these resources, ensuring that the allocated amounts are justified and aligned with the intended purposes and mandates.

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS - The following amounts or so much thereof, as may be necessary, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Accountant and the City Treasurer of Davao City, not otherwise appropriated from the operations of the City Government of Davao from January 1 to December 31, 2024, except as otherwise provided for in this ordinance:

SECTION 4. AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS, AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES - The Supplemental Budget No. 3 of the City Government of Davao for Calendar Year 2024 is funded from the available sources of funds in the amount of FOUR HUNDRED THIRTY-THREE MILLION NINE HUNDRED TWENTY-EIGHT THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED NINETY-TWO PESOS AND 87/100 (PHP 433,928,192.87), with broken down as follows:

**SECTION 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS**
The amount herein appropriated for the projects indicated herein shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget must conform with existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules, regulations, policies, and guidelines of the Commission on Audit, Local Fiscal Administration Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and Presidential directives.

**SECTION 7. USE OF FUNDS**
The use of funds under Supplemental Budget Title No. 3 of the City Government of Davao for Calendar Year 2024, utilized in violation of Title 5, Local Fiscal Administration, as embodied in RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Fiscal Code, shall be subject to disciplinary action and appropriate legal measures under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines. All disbursements and utilization of funds appropriated under this Supplemental Budget must conform with existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules, regulations, policies, and guidelines of the Commission on Audit (COA), Department of Budget and Management (DBM), Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and Presidential directives.

**SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable, and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

**SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY**
The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect on the date of its enactment.
