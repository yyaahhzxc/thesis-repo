Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 2 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2024, COMPRISING THE BUDGET UNDER THE GENERAL FUND PROPER, THE DEVELOPMENT FUND, AND THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SIX HUNDRED THIRTY-TWO Pesos and 47/100 (PHP 638,906,692.47)."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Aligned with its mandate and in response to the community's needs, the City Government of Davao affirms its commitment to optimize available resources. It likewise emphasizes the responsible and prudent utilization of these resources, ensuring that the allocated amounts are justified and aligned with the intended purposes and mandates.

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS - The following amounts or so much thereof, as may be necessary, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Accountant and the City Treasurer of Davao City, not otherwise appropriated from the operation of the City Government of Davao from January 1 to December 31, 2024, except as otherwise provided therein.

SECTION 4. AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS, AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES - The Supplemental Budget No. 2 of the City Government of Davao for Calendar Year 2024 is funded from the available sources of funds in the amount of Six Hundred Ninety-Eight Million Five Hundred Fifty-Four Thousand Two Hundred Thirty-Five Pesos and 43/100 (PHP 698,554,235.43), with breakdown as follows:
