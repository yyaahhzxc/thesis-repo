Ord. No. 0493-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE EXECUTED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE JURIMATU LAND REGISTERED UNDER THE NAME OF ROMEO S. SANTOS, COVERED BY TRANSFER CERTIFICATE OF TITLE NO. T-246814, LOCATED AT DEL PILAR ST., BARANGAY 14-B, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY, TO BE UTILIZED AS A PERMANENT SITE FOR THE BARANGAY FACILITIES, INFRASTRUCTURES, OR OTHER PUBLIC PURPOSES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of the Local Government Code of 1991 empowers every local government unit to exercise powers granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those essential to the promotion of the general welfare.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Sale executed by the City Government of Davao for the purchase of land registered under the name of Romeo S. Santos covered by Transfer Certificate of Title No. T-246814, located at Del Pilar St., Barangay 14-B, Poblacion District, this City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITy CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable, and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, May 14, 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
For and in the absence of the Secretary:

MA. THERESE A. REYES
Acting Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (Asst. Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod)

J. MELCHOR B. QUITAIN JR.
