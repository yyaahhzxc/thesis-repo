ORDINANCE NO. 0170-03
Series of 2003
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 110, SERIES OF 1970, PRESCRIBING THE SONG "TAYO AY DABAWENYO" AS THE OFFICIAL SONG OF DAVAO CITY.
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in Session assembled:

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0170-03

SECTION 1 - TITLE
This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance Amending Ordinance No. 110, Series of 1970, prescribing the song 'Tayo ay Dabawenyo' as the official song of Davao City".

SECTION 2 - DECLARATION AS OFFICIAL SONG
The song "Tayo ay Dabawenyo" was made official by Mr. Guillermo B. Anjiao of Sta. Ana Central Elementary School, then City Superintendent of City Schools, and declared as the official song of the City of Davao, the lyrics of which are as follows:

"Sa dalong timog ng Mindanao
May tang langusap na hinirang
Ang likas niyung kapansanan
Ay walaan kapansay.
Mga buodok, dagat, ipinapan
Laging sagana sa pagkain
At ang mgaagadang tanswim
Kailangan aliw.
Misyang Langsod ng Dabaw
Sa iyong paglabasby
Sa nidadg kaularan
Ikwaw ay paglilinghuran.
Tayo ay Dabawenyo
Na tapop at tototo
Pangarap sya matano
Kalawalahanin mo
Langsod na paraiso"

SECTION 3 - PLAYING OF TAYO BY DABAWENYO
The song "TAYO BY DABAWENYO" shall be sung in all official ceremonies in the City of Davao, such as convocations, programs, sign-on and sign-off speeches of local broadcast facilities, cinema houses and the like, and during public and private schools activities.

SECTION 4 - PENALITY CLAUSE
Any person, firm, or corporation who violate any provisions of this Ordinance shall upon conviction be punished for such violations:
4.a. A fine of up to P2,000 or imprisonment of not less than five (5) days or both upon discretion of the court on the first instance.
4.b. A fine of up to P4,000 or imprisonment of not less than ten (10) days or both upon discretion of the court on the second instance.
4.c. A fine of up to P6,000 or imprisonment of not less than thirty (30) days or both upon discretion of the court on the subsequent instance.

SECTION 5 - SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason any Section of the Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other Sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6 - REPEALING CLAUSE. Any ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 7 - EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen days (15) after publication.

ENACTED, December 9, 2003 by a majority vote of all Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
