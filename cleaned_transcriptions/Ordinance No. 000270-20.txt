AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE DAVAO CITY
ONE: STOP SHOP FOR CONSTRUCTION PERMITS (OSSCP)

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 6
Ord. No. 0270-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE DAVAO CITY ONE-STOP SHOP FOR CONSTRUCTION PERMITS (OSSCP)."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao recognizes the need to ensure that the processing, inspection, approval, and release of construction/building permits and certificates of occupancy in the city are without delay.

SECTION 3. ONE-STOP SHOP FOR CONSTRUCTION PERMITS (OSSCP) COVERAGE - A mechanism of coordination among the Office of the Building Official (OBO), the City Assessor's Office, the City Treasurer's Office and other concerned departments or offices at the local government level under the purview of the City Fire Marshall through coordination of related functions and through practical system of linkages.

OSSCP shall cover only simple applications for Building Permits and Certificates of Occupancy whose floor area shall not exceed one thousand five hundred (1,500) square meters, to wit:

a. Single dwelling residential building of not more than three (3) floors/storeys;

b. Commercial Buildings of not more than two (2) floors/storeys;

c. Renovation within an mall with issued building permit; and

d. Warehouse storing non-hazardous substance.

SECTION 4. OSSCP COMPOSITION - The One-Stop Shop for Construction Permits (OSSCP) shall be composed of the Davao City Mayor or her duly authorized representative as Chairperson; the City Administrator as Co-Chairperson and the City Building Official, the Zoning Officer/ the City Planning and Development Officer (CPDO), the City Assessor, the City Treasurer and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) as Members.

SECTION 5. OSSCP FUNCTIONS - The One-Stop Shop for Construction Permits (OSSCP) shall have the following functions:

a. Receive applications and documents for Building Permits and Certificates of Occupancy, either through online or physical submission;

b. Coordinate and facilitate the conduct of technical reviews by the concerned departments of the City of Davao and Local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP);

c. Facilitate the retrieval of documentary requirements for Building Permits and Certificates of Occupancy that will be part of the OSSCP backroom operation. For this purpose, backroom function is defined as the working arrangement among the Office of the City Building Official, the City Assessor's Office, the City Treasurer's Office, and the concerned departments and offices of the City of Davao and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) where the application and documentary requirements for Building Permits and Certificates of Occupancy are transferred and moved from one department to another review, evaluation and action without the physical interference of the applicant;

d. Implement a one-time assessment of fees and charges related to applications for Building Permits and Certificates of Occupancy in coordination with the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), the Zoning Officer, the City Assessor's Office, the City Treasurer's Office and the Office of the City Building Official (OBO);

e. Coordinate with the conduct of joint inspections by the Office of the City Building Official (OBO), the City Assessor's Office, and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP);
f. Receive payment of all related fees and charges, either through online or physical payment, in coordination with the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), and the Office of the City Building Official (OBO), and g. Release Building Permit or Certificate of Occupancy.
SECTION 6. BUILDING PERMIT PROCESSING TIME - The different offices involved in the OSSCP, functioned as one unit, shall evaluate simple Building Permit applications with complete documentary requirements, within a maximum period of five (5) working days. The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall process the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) within a maximum period of ten (10) working days and shall abide by the manual operations that will be developed in relation to Joint Memorandum Circular No. 2018-01 dated January 4, 2018 issued by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), and other supplemental manuals that will be separately prepared by individual OSSCPs. In case of non-compliance by the applicant, applications with deficiencies shall be issued a Comprehensive Correction Sheet and/or Notice of Disapproval by the Zoning Officer, City Building Official (OBO), and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), stating all deficiencies. For applications that satisfy the requirements of the Zoning Ordinance, the OSSCP shall release the Building Permit, together with the local BFP, OBO, and Barangay permits and the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC), after payment of appropriate fees and charges.
The Office of the City Building Official (OBO) shall provide the local BFP and the City Assessor's Office with the list of the issued Building Permits on a monthly basis (every 5th day of the month), for their reference, in anticipation of the applicant's future application for a Certificate of Occupancy.
SECTION 7. ASSESSMENT OF FEES - After the evaluation of the building permit application, the Zoning Officer, the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), and the Office of the City Building Official (OBO) shall simultaneously issue an Order of Payment that will be issued to the applicant, at the same time, specifying the fees that the applicant is obligated to pay in accordance with the provisions of the Zoning Ordinance, the National Building Code of the Philippines (NBCP), the Fire Code of the Philippines (FCP), and the City's Revenue Code. The Zoning Officer, the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) and the Office of the City Building Official (OBO) shall put in place the necessary measures to implement and enforce assessment of building-related fees and charges which will include the local fees for securing the locational clearance, barangay clearance, filing fees among others.
The fees for the issuance of the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) under the Fire Code of the Philippines shall be based on the estimated value of the building or structure declared by the owner or applicant duly notarized. In the event that there is substantial difference between the estimated value of the building or structure as declared by the owner or applicant from the amount indicated in the application for the building permit, the higher value will be adopted.

For this purpose, the estimated value of the building or structure shall be defined as the value of the construction installed or erected at a given location and may also be considered as cost of construction including architectural and engineering work and labor cost.

The Office of the Building Official (OBO) and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) may collect a filing fee, in addition to the NBCPC and FCP prescribed fees and charges, to cover costs incurred in assessing building permit and FSEC applications. Such fee shall be set following the guidelines for setting fees and charges issued by the Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGFC) under the Department of Finance.

SECTION 8. PAYMENT OF FEES - The Office of the Building Official (OBO) and the Local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall jointly implement a one-time payment of fees and charges related to the Building Permit applications. All related fees shall be paid by the applicant in a designated cashier area in the OSSCC where the City Government and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall assign a cashier to receive payments for applications for Building Permit and Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC), respectively.

SECTION 9. ISSUANCE OF BUILDING AND OTHER ANCILLARY PERMITS - The Office of the Building Official (OBO) shall issue the Building Permit and other ancillary permits, the Barangay Clearance, the Locational Clearance and the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) upon payment of the required fees by the applicant.

SECTION 10. CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY PROCESSING TIME - The different offices at the OSSCC shall evaluate all applications for Certificate of Occupancy, which include applications for Certificate of Electrical Inspection (CEI) and other related certificates, within a maximum period of five (5) working days from the receipt of an application provided that it meets the documentary requirements. The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall process the Fire Marshal's Certificate (FMIC) within a maximum period of three (3) working days. Applications with deficiencies that will be issued a Comprehensive Correction Sheet and/or Notice of Disapproval stating all deficiencies.

SECTION 11. ASSESSMENT OF FEES - After final inspection by the Office of the Building Official (OBO) and receipt of the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) status report, the Office of the Building Official (OBO) and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall simultaneously prepare an Order of Payment for applications that meet their requirements specifying the fees that the applicant is obligated to pay in accordance with the provisions of the National Building Code of the Philippines (NBCPC) and the Fire Code of the City of Manila (FCM). The two (2) orders of payment shall be issued to the applicant at the same time. For applications that fail, the Local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall assign a staff who shall be co-located with the City Assessor within the Office of the Building Official (OBO) or designated OSSCC.

The Office of the Building Official (OBO) and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) may collect a filing fee, in addition to the National Building Code of the Philippines (NBCPC) and the Fire Code of the Philippines prescribed fees/charges to cover for the cost incurred in evaluating applications for Certificate of Electrical Inspection (CEI) and Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC), the setting of which shall follow the guidelines for setting fees and charges issued by the Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGFC).

**SECTION 12. PAYMENT OF FEES**
The OBO and the local BPF shall jointly implement a one-time payment of fees and charges related to applications for Certificates of Occupancy. All related fees shall be paid by the applicant in a designated cashiering area in the OSSCP where the City Government and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall assign a cashier to receive payments for applications for Certificate of Occupancy and FSC, respectively.

**SECTION 13. ISSUANCE OF CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY AND OTHER CERTIFICATES OR CLEARANCES**
The Office of the Building Official (OBO) shall issue the Certificate of Occupancy together with the Certificate of Electrical Inspection (CEI), the Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSC), and other related certificates or clearances upon payment of the required fees by the applicant.

**SECTION 14. SYSTEM OF ONLINE PAYMENT**
The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) and the City of Davao shall establish a system of online payment of fees and charges related to certificates and other electronic payment scheme to facilitate the one-time assessment and payment of Construction/Building Permit, Certificate of Occupancy, and other related fees and charges.

**SECTION 15. INTEGRATION OF LAND REGISTRATION AUTHORITY IN THE OSSCP**
The City of Davao shall set up a Land Registration Authority extension office in the OSSCP that will generate the Original Certificate of Title (OCT) and Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) as part of their backroom operation and service to the public.

**SECTION 16. INTEGRATION OF DAVAO LIGHT AND DAVAO CITY WATER DISTRICT PROCESSES IN THE OSSCP**
The Davao Light and Power Corporation (DLPC) and Davao City Water District (DCWID) shall have a service kiosk near the OSSCP for the application for electrical and water connections.

**SECTION 17. SEPARAPBLITY CLAUSE**
If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 18. EFFECTIVITY**
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED, on the 19th day of May 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).**
