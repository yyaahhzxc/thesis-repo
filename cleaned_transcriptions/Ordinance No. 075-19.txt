Ord. No. 075-19
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
REPEALING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0143-.05, SERIES OF 2005, ENTITLED
"AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE USE OF CELLULAR PHONES, HAND-HELD
RADIOES, PERSONAL DIGITAL ASSISTANT (PDA) AND OTHER SIMILAR
ELECTRONIC DEVICES WHILE DRIVING" BY REASON OF THE ENACTMENT
OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10913 OR THE ANTI-DISTRACTED DRIVING ACT.
SECTION 2. REPEALING CAUSE - City Ordinance No. 0143-.05, Series of 2005,
entitled "AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE USE OF CELLULAR PHONES, HAND-HELD
RADIOES, PERSONAL DIGITAL ASSISTANT (PDA) AND OTHER SIMILAR
ELECTRONIC DEVICES WHILE DRIVING," is hereby repealed by reason of the enactment
of Republic Act No. 10913 or the Anti-Distracted Driving Act.
SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after
posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive
days in a local newspaper of general circulation.
ENACTED, October 15, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present.
