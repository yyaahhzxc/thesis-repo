Ord. No. 0225-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE OF SOLID SANDROCK AGGREGATES INC. FOR THE OPERATION OF STONE CRUSHING PLANT LOCATED AT PUROK 1, BARANGAY LAMPIANAO, THIS CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.- Article XII, Section 1 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City allows request for Additional Allowable Use, to wit:

"The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of the proposed respondent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not enumerated therein as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a ¾ majority vote of all Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance."

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST.- The application for Allowable Use of Solid Sandrock Aggregates Inc. for the operation of a stone crushing plant at Purok 1, Barangay Lampianao, this City, located in the Floodway Mitigation Sub-zone, covered by OCT No. P-14314 and OCT No. P-14315, with an aggregate area of Thirty Thousand Seven Hundred Eighty-eight (30,788) square meters, more or less, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. REGULATIONS.- Loading, unloading and/or transport of quarry materials to or from within the area subject of the grant for Additional Allowable Use under this Ordinance which passes through a barangay road shall only use 6-wheeler dump trucks with a capacity of ten (10) cubic meter load.

SECTION 5. MANDATORY REVIEW.- This Ordinance shall be subject to mandatory review by the Sangguniang Panlungsod after a period of three (3) years from its effectiveness and every three (3) years thereafter. The grant for Additional Allowable Use is deemed in effect unless this Ordinance is amended, repealed, modified or revoked accordingly.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Sangguniang Panlungsod may review this Ordinance before the expiration of the 3-year period herein provided.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.- If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
