RESOLUTION No. 10254
The Proposed Amended Zoning Ordinance of Davao City for 1996 was presented for consideration and appropriate action of the Body by
Councillor Victoria S. Advincula, Vice Leader.
Councillor Advincula manifested that the Body shall continue presenting amendments to the proposed ordinance which were discussed and approved in previous sessions. He invited the attention of the Members of the Body who made an observation to the effect that the draft coming from the City Development Council to the City Mayor's Office is not complete. The draft shows that there are many provisions which are no longer applicable. Before the presentation of the other amendments, Councillor Advincula manifested that the proposed ordinance will be submitted to the Ilocos City Mayor together with the Comprehensive Development Plan on December 27, 1995 has been subject to thorough studies and deliberations by the Members of the Committee and the entire Body as a whole during several regular and special sessions held for the purpose.

Councillor Advincula briefed the Members of the Body that this Zoning Ordinance is a regulatory measure which is enacted to identify the various land use districts or zones, designate the allowable uses therein and prescribe physical performance standards based on the approved Comprehensive Development Plan of the City of Davao which was adopted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod per Resolution No. 10105, with its corresponding Ordinance No. 397, series of 1976 for the City of Davao to achieve the development goals, objectives and policies contained in the said plan.

Councillor Advincula thereupon moved for the unaniMouses as enumerated and read on re-closure.

He likewise manifested that those proposed amendments already presented and recorded in the said draft shall be shifted into the unaniMouses Closing Ordinance of the City of Davao for 1996 and that the City Planning & Development Coordinator will make the necessary interpretations of these amendments already approved by the body as previously presented to the body and the ordinances which are hereby reflected in the Minutes of the Special Session.

Considering that there were no other amendments presented, Councillor Advincula proposed for its approval.

After careful deliberation, unaniMouses of Councillor Victorio S. Advincula, duly and jointly seconded by Councillors Antonio "Teresa, Shane Dolor, Sicilia, Valentina Ruchan, Igny, Dglobo-Oritz, Mariano A. Librado, Tomas Honteville III, co-sponsored jointly by Councillors Antonio "Teresa, Vicente, Donato, Donato Dayaghiang, Paz Pata, Alfredo Teresa and Shane L. Dolor it was

RESOLUO, IS IT BEHEWENY RESOLUO, To approve the amended Zoning Ordinance of Davao City, all the provisions of the draft of the amended Zoning Ordinance of Davao City for 1996 as submitted by the Honorable Mayor thru the City Development Council and the Zoning Administrator's Office, and the amendments already signed by the Body as contained in the minutes of the previous regular and special sessions, including the special session today, June 11, 1996.

RESOLUO, FURTHER, to approve an Ordinance, entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 4042

AN ORDINANCE REVISING THE ZONING REGULATIONS FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO AND PROVIDING FOR THE ADMINISTRATION, IMPLEMENTATION AND AMENDMENT THEREOF AND FOR THE REPEAL OF ALL ORDINANCES IN CONFLICT THERETHIN.

Whereas, to address the present and future development of the City, there is a need to revise the zoning regulation;

Whereas, in order to achieve these developments, a Comprehensive Development Plan was enacted to translate the City's vision into reality;

Whereas, the enactment of a Zoning Ordinance is necessary to regulate the use of the city's land resources in the implementation of the Comprehensive Development Plan of Davao City;

No: 1, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDERED AND enacted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, the herein Zoning Ordinance, providing for the administration, enforcement and amendments thereof; and for the repeal of all ordinances in conflict therewith.

ARTICLE I - TITLE AND PURPOSE

SECTION 1.-TITLE OF THE ORDINANCE. This shall be known as the Revised Zoning Ordinance of 1996 of the City of Davao.

**SECTION 9. VIOLATION AND PENALTY:** Any person who violates any provisions of this Ordinance shall, upon conviction, be punished by a fine of not less than P500.00 per day but not more than P1,000.00 per day or by imprisonment of not less than six (6) months but not exceeding one (1) year or both at the discretion of the Court. In case of violation by a corporation, partnership or association, the penalty shall be imposed upon the erring officer thereof.

**SECTION 10. SUPPLEMENTARY EFFECT OF OTHER LAW AND DECREES:** The provisions of this Ordinance shall be without prejudice to the application of the existing laws.

**SECTION 11. BUSINESS PERMITS AND/OR LICENSES:** As a supplementary provision to the City's Taxing Ordinance of 1974 as amended, no business/Mayor's permits and/or licenses of any kind shall be granted or issued in favor of any establishment situated/located in non-conforming area or in areas where such business or establishment is not allowed under the provision of this Ordinance particularly Article V, Section 2 to 7, inclusive.

**SECTION 12. REPEALING CLAUSE:** All laws, ordinances, orders, issuances, rules and regulations or part thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 13. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE:** This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
