Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF FLORIAN GOTTEIN AND ANTONIO PERALTA, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR AND CHAIRMAN OF THE EUROPEAN CHAMBER OF COMMERCE OF THE PHILIPPINES (ECCP) - SOUTHERN MINDANAO BUSINESS COUNCIL, RESPECTIVELY, FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF SIGNAGE FEES RESPECTIVELY, FOR THE 2nd ECCP GOLF CHALLENGE MINDANAO ON NOVEMBER 30, 2019 AT RANCHO PALOS VERDES GOLF AND COUNTRY CLUB, THIS CITY

Ord. No. 0108-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF FLORIAN GOTTEIN AND ANTONIO PERALTA, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR AND CHAIRMAN OF THE EUROPEAN CHAMBER OF COMMERCE OF THE PHILIPPINES (ECCP) - SOUTHERN MINDANAO BUSINESS COUNCIL, RESPECTIVELY, FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF SIGNAGE FEES COVERING NOVEMBER 15, 2019 TO NOVEMBER 30, 2019 FOR THE 2ND ECCP GOLF CHALLENGE MINDANAO ON NOVEMBER 30, 2019 AT RANCHO PALOS VERDES GOLF AND COUNTRY CLUB, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption shall cover all fifty (50) banners/streamers along the lamp posts in the major streets of the downtown area.

SECTION 3. PERIOD - The said banners/streamers shall be installed from November 15, 2019 to November 30, 2019 and will be removed on December 1, 2019.

SECTION 4. LIMITATIONS/CONDITIONS - The grant of the exemption from payment of signage fees shall be subject to the following limitations/conditions:

1. The ECCP is strictly mandated to install only one (1) banner/streamer per lamp post.
2. In no case shall said banner/streamer obstruct the view of the motorists;
3. In case ECCP fails to remove all fifty (50) banners/streamers within one (1) week after the event, the exemption granted by this Ordinance shall be nullified;
4. The ECCP shall closely coordinate with the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and the Davao Light Power Company (DLCP).

SECTION 5. EXEMPTION - The request of Florian Gottein and Antonio Peralta, Executive Director and Chairman of the European Chamber of Commerce of the Philippines (ECCP) - Southern Mindanao Business Council, respectively, for exemption from the payment of Signage fees covering the period November 15, 2019 to November 30, 2019 for the 2nd ECCP Golf Challenge Mindanao on November 30, 2019 at Rancho Palos Verdes Golf and Country Club, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, November 19, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
