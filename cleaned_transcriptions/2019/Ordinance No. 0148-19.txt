1st INDORSEMENT
March 2, 2020
Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject Ordinance, with the herein findings:
It is well-settled rule of statutory construction that statutes are to be considered as having only prospective operations, unless the intentment of the legislature to give them retroactive effects is expressly declared.
In the case at hand, the ordinance will retroact on the date the 2017 Revenue Code took effect as shown in Section 4, to quote:
"Section 4. EFFECTIVITY-The provisions of this Ordinance shall apply retroactively upon the effectivity of the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City."
Further, its retroactive effect finds support as to the fact that the measure appears to be curative in nature. It cures the unforeseen effect of market reclassification in 2005, which was not amended in Revenue Code of 2017 vis-à-vis the Average Monthly Income in 2005, which was carried over in the Revenue Code of 2017 as shown in Resolution No. 0554-19, Series of 2019.
Viewed therefrom, this office recommends the approval of the subject ordinance.

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0291-17, SERIES OF 2017, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE 2017 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO SPECIFICALLY SECTION 347 OF ARTICLE ELEVEN -REGULATION IN THE ESTABLISHMENT, CLASSIFICATION, ADMINISTRATION, AND OPERATION OF GOVERNMENT-OWNED/OPERATED PUBLIC MARKET."

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT: Article Eleven- Regulation in the Establishment, Classification, Administration and Operation of Government-Owned/Operated Public Market Particularly Section 347 of Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, Otherwise Known as the 2017 Revenue Code of the City Government of Davao, is hereby amended as follows:

ARTICLE ELEVEN- REGULATION IN THE ESTABLISHMENT, CLASSIFICATION, ADMINISTRATION AND OPERATION OF GOVERNMENT-OWNED/OPERATED PUBLIC MARKET

FROM:

Section 347. Classification of Public Markets. For the purpose of this Code, city-owned and/or operated public markets are hereby classified into:

Class A - Those with an average monthly income of P 450,000.00 or more during the preceding six (6) months.

Class B - Those with an average monthly income of P300,000.00 or more but not more than P 450,000.00 during the preceding six (6) months.

Class C - Those with an average monthly income of P 150,000.00 or more but not more than P300,000.00 during the preceding six (6) months.

Class D - Those with an average monthly income of less than P150,000.00 during the preceding six (6) months.

TO:

Section 347.Classification of Public Markets. For the purpose of this Code, city-owned and/or operated public markets are hereby classified into:

Class A - Those with an average monthly income of P2,000,000.00 or more during the preceding six (6) months.

Class B - Those with an average monthly income of P1,500,000.00 or more but not more than P2,000,000.00 during the preceding six (6) months.

Class C - Those with an average monthly income of P700,000.00 or more but not more than P1,500,000.00 during the preceding six (6) months.
