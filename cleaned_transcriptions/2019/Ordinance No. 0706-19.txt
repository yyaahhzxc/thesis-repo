ORDINANCE NO. 0706-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING THE RATES AND CHARGES
TO BE IMPOSED AT THE DAVAO FOOD TERMINAL COMPLEX

--- [Page 5] ---
Be it Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING THE RATES AND CHARGES TO BE IMPOSED AT THE DAVAO FOOD TERMINAL COMPLEX".

SECTION 2. IMPOSITION OF FEES - There shall be collected the following fees from every person who shall transact at the Davao Food Terminal Complex:

| Parking Fees | Amount |
|-------------|--------|
| Kind of Transportation | P |
| 1. Motorcycle | 10.00 |
| 2. Tricycle | 15.00 |
| 3. 4-Wheeler Vehicle | 20.00 |
| 4. 6-Wheeler Vehicle | 25.00 |
| 5. 8-Wheeler Vehicle | 30.00 |
| 6. 10-Wheeler Vehicle | 35.00 |
| 7. 16-Wheeler Vehicle | 40.00 |

The above mentioned rates shall apply for the first three (3) hours. Succeeding hours - P 10.00/hr. Overnight - P 100.00. Vehicle entry for tenants: P 500.00/year

Space/Stall Rental Fees
Rental fee for any space at DFTC | P 8.00 per sq.m. per day
Rental fee for all stalls at DFTC | P 12.00 per sq.m. per day

Fees for Commodities (fruits, vegetables and root crops)
a. Price range of P 50.00 kg, and above | P 1.00/kg
b. P 30.00 but not exceeding P50.00/kg | 0.50/kg
c. P 20.00/kg, but not exceeding P30.00- | 0.25/kg
d. Below P20.00/kg. | 0.10/kg

Washing Fee
Inside washing area | P 0.20/kg
Washer Drum | 100.00/100kg (for the first hour, still applies if less than 1 hour) and P7.50 for every succeeding hour

Dormitory Fee
Non-aircon | P 100.00/head/night (minimum of 10 heads/night)
Aircon | 200.00/head/night (minimum of 10 heads/night)

Training Hall Rental Fee | P 1,000.00/hour

--- [Page 6] ---
SECTION 3. TIME FOR PAYMENT/PENALTIES FOR DELINQUENCY - All rental fees for the stalls and spaces shall become due and demandable within the first twenty (20) days of the month. Failure to pay the rental fee within the period required herein shall subject the lessee/occupant to a surcharge of twenty-five percent (25%) of the amount of the rental fee plus an interest of two percent (2%) per month not to exceed thirty-six (36) months.

SECTION 4. COLLECTION - Collection of the abovementioned fees and charges shall be the responsibility of the City Treasurer. To ensure that such responsibility is properly and effectively discharged, all collectors, including other personnel involved in the collection function at the Davao Food Terminal Complex shall directly be under the control and supervision of the City Treasurer for collection performance and accountability.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other city ordinances, Executive Orders and rules and regulations, which are inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby deemed amended, repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval and publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the city of Davao.

ENACTED, on the 26th day of February, 2019, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
