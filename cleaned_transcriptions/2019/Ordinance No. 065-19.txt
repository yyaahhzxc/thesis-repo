Ord. No. 065-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 2 OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0332-68, SERIES OF 1968, BY INCREASING THE CIVIL PENALTIES TO THE MAXIMUM AND ALSO THE PENALTY FOR IMPRISONMENT THEREFOR".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 2 of City Ordinance No. 0332-68, Series of 1968 shall be amended as follows:

Sec. 2 - The following penalties shall be imposed on violators of this Ordinance:
First Offense: Php3,000.00 or one (1) month imprisonment or both at the discretion of the court.
Second Offense: Php4,000.00 or two (2) months imprisonment or both at the discretion of the court.
Third and subsequent Offenses: Php5,000.00 or three (3) months imprisonment or both at the discretion of the court.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITy CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, on the 8th day of October 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
