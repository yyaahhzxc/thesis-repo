Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the attached Ordinance No. 0715-19, Series of 2019 entitled "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE FOLLOWING STREETS WITH STATED INCLUSIVE ACTIVITIES THEREON, TILL SUCH TIME AS THE RECONSTRUCTION OR RECONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE R2AND ARAW NG DAVAO", informs you end that the same is free from legal infamy citing RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, to quote:
"SECTION 21. Closure and Opening of Roads: (a) A local government unit may, pursuant to an ordinance, permanently or temporarily close or open any local road, alley, park, or square failing within its jurisdiction. Provided, however, That in case of permanent closure, such ordinance must be approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of all the members of the sanguinary, and when necessary, an adequate substitute for the public facility that is subject to closure is provided.
xxx
(C) Any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs, or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications, and waterworks projects, the duration of which shall be determined by the local executive concerned in written order. Provided, however, That national or local road, alley, park, or square shall be temporarily closed for athletic, cultural, or civic activities not officially sponsored, recognized, or approved by the local government unit concerned".
IN VIEW THEREOF, it is recommended that the Ordinance be approved.

Ord. No. 0715-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE FOLLOWING STREETS WITH STATED INCLUSIVE ACTIVITIES, DATES, AND TIME AS HEREIN ENUMERATED IN CONNECTION WITH THE CELEBRATION OF THE 82ND ARAW NG DAVAO:

| DATE | ACTIVITY | AREA OF COVERAGE OF ROAD CLOSURE | TIME |
|------|----------|----------------------------------|------|
| March 9, 2019 | Hudyaka Drum and Bugle Corps Competition | Half Lane Closure along Palma Gil St. in front of Pasalubong Center | 2:00 PM - onwards |
| March 15, 2019 | Parada Dabawenyo (Civic Parade) | Half Road Closure (Ingress) a portion of San Pedro Square behind Rizal Park Agila Stage; | 2:00 PM - onwards |
| March 16, 2019 | Parada Dabawenyo (Parade Proper) | Full Road Closure (Parade Proper) from Roxas Avenue - left turn to C.M. Recto Avenue - right turn to Bonifacio St. - left turn to Pelayo St. - left turn to San Pedro St. | 3:00 A.M. until 2:00 P.M. |
| Beef Sharing Activity | A portion of C.M. Recto Avenue from Osmena Park until China Bank and a portion of San Pedro St. from Davao City Police Office, Camp Domingo Leonor to San Pedro Square | 4:00 A.M. until 4:00 P.M. |

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that: "any national or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, fesita celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fairs or an undertaking of public works and highways, telecommunications and water works projects".

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the 82nd celebration of the Araw ng Davao, the following streets with stated inclusive activities, dates, and time as herein enumerated will be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic, viz:
