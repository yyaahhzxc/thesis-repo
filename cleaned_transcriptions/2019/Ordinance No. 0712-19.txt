Ord. No. 0712-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED BY AND AMONG THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE CITY AGRICULTURE AND FISHERIES COUNCIL AND THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE-REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF BIOGAS DIGESTER LOCATED IN THE ANIMAL PRODUCTION CENTER, MALAGOS, BAGUIO DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Section 455, (b) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the City Government and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY: The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and among the City Government of Davao, the City Agriculture and Fisheries Council and the Department of Agriculture-Regional Field Office XI for the construction of Biogas Digester located in the Animal Production Center, Malagos, Baguio District, this City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, any sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 5th day of March 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

CONSTRUCTION OF BIOMAS DIGESTER AT DAVAO CITY
FUNDED UNDER NATIONAL LIVESTOCK PROGRAM CY 2018

KNOWN ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement made and entered into by and between:

The DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE through its REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, a government agency created and existing under the Republic of the Philippines with office address at F. Bangoy St., Davao City, represented by its OIC Regional Executive Director ENGR. RICARDO M. ONATE, JR. hereinafter referred to as "DA RO XI";

THE CITY AGRICULTURE & FISHERY COUNCIL with office address at City Hall Annex Building, Pichon St., Davao City represented by its Chairperson- ALLAN MACANSANTOS hereinafter referred to as CAFC.

and

DAVAO CITY, a local government unit created under existing laws of the Philippines with office address at City Hall Building, San Pedro St., Davao City represented herein by its Mayor: SARA Z. DUERTE herein after referred to as "LGU DAVAO CITY";

WITNESSETH

Whereas, Article II, Section 23 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides that the "State shall encourage non-government, community-based or sectoral organizations to promote the welfare of the people."

Whereas, RA 8457 otherwise known as AGRICULTURE AND FISHERIES MODERNIZATION ACT or APMA aims to strengthen the Agriculture and Fishery sector through modernization, greater participation among Local Government Units (LGUs), farmers and fisherfolks, non-government organizations, and people empowerment;

Whereas, the Department of Agriculture is the principal government agency mandated to develop the agriculture and fisheries in the country through the provision of adequate and appropriate support services;

Whereas, RA 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 allows the Department of Agriculture (DA), a National Agency, to implement nationally funded projects jointly with the Local Government Units (LGUs);

Whereas, the DA is implementing projects under LIVESTOCK PROGRAM in which the general objectives are development of agribusiness lands, reduction of production costs (e.g. wage goods) through productivity enhancement, increase livestock production, ensure availability of quality feed for livestock, improve the efficiency of livestock farmers, ensure the compatibility of practices with environmental standards, transform the livestock industry from a resource-based to a technology-based, work for the global competitiveness and provide a conducive policy environment;

Whereas, one of the components under LIVESTOCK PROGRAM is the AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERY MACHINERY, EQUIPMENT AND FACILITIES SUPPORT SERVICES wherein specific MACHINERY/YES, EQUIPMENT, and/or FACILITY/IES will be provided to Farmer Organizations (FOs) and/or Local Government Units (LGUs).

--- [Page 12] ---
Whereas, the City Veterinarian's Office (CVO) is the agricultural arm of the LGU and an active partner of DA RFO XI supports and undertakes activities to promote the development of the livestock and poultry industry in the countryside and has the administrative and technical capability to effectively assist in the implementation of the PROJECT;
Whereas, the LGU Davao City has signed its intent to receive the CONSTRUCTION OF BIOGAS DIGESTER with estimated project allocation from DA RFO XI amounting to FIVE HUNDRED TWENTY-FOUR THOUSAND PESOS (Php 524,000.00) as referred to as the PROJECT located at APC Manages, Bagao District, Davao City under the PROGRAM;
Whereas, the LGU DAVAO CITY is identified most in need of and qualified to receive/has the technical capability to operate and manage the PROJECT and is willing to collaborate in the implementation of the same.
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual covenant and premises hereinafter set forth, the parties hereto agree as follows:
ARTICLE I RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES
1. The DA RFO XI shall:
a) Verify and validate the technical requirements of the COOPERATOR for the project implementation;
b) Prepare the Detailed Engineering Design, Program of Works and Detailed Estimates based on engineering standards;
c) Perform supervision, management and the construction of the PROJECT as identified facility under the PROGRAM in accordance with Republic Act 9184 and other government accounting rules and regulation;
d) Extend technical services as may be needed by the LGU Davao City relative to project preparation and implementation in close coordination with the City Veterinarian's Office (CVO);
e) Conduct geo-tagging and regular monitoring and inspection/evaluation in coordination with City Veterinarian's Office (CVO) to ensure the progress and accomplishment of the PROJECT;
f) Report to the LGU Davao City and institute corrective measures and terminate the agreement in the case of non-compliance with the terms herein stipulated on the basis of the monitoring and evaluation findings that will be conducted periodically;
g) Facilitate the turn-over of the PROJECT to the LGU Davao City and ensure the due documentation of said turn-over.
2. The CAFC shall:
a) Participate in the project planning, programming, and budgeting including participation of farmer-beneficiaries;
b) Conduct regular monitoring/ assessment on the status of the project implementation in coordination with Provincial and City/ Municipal Agriculture's Office to ensure the progress and accomplishment of the project;
c) Render written report on their findings and recommend possible solutions to the LGU Davao City;
d) Keep records and maintain a database of all projects implemented in the Provincial and City/ Municipality.
3. The LGU shall:
a) Submit to DA RFO XI all the requirements related to the PROJECT;
b) Provide an equity in the form of land supported with proof of land ownership/donation/lease of lot/usurpation agreement (at least 25 years).

--- [Page 13] ---
c) Acknowledge receipt of ownership/possession of the PROJECT and be responsible in its operation and maintenance;
d) Provide utilities as may be required for the project implementation such as but not limited to electric and water supply that conforms to the facility requirement;
e) Maintain a complete and detailed logbook of the PROJECT's operation and any other requested allow DA RFO XI monitoring team to access the same; including vistorial audit by COA personnel under an approved office order;
f) Utilize the PROJECT in accordance to its intended use and shall not in any way sell, lease or mortgage the same;

ARTICLE II
GENERAL PROVISIONS

If a dispute arises out of this contract, and if the dispute cannot be settled through negotiation, the parties agree first to try in good faith to settle the dispute by mediation before resorting to arbitration, litigation, or some other dispute resolution procedure (Alternative Dispute Resolution Clause).

ARTICLE III
EFFECTIVITY AND TERMINATION OF THE AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect upon signing hereof of both parties; the implementation shall begin immediately and shall continue to be valid within a period of Twenty-Five (25) years or until written notice is given by either party thirty (30) days prior to the intended date of termination or shall be terminated upon the satisfactorily fulfillment of all terms and conditions embodied herein.

Any breach or violation by a party of its obligations under this Agreement shall entitle the other party to immediately rescind, cancel or terminate this Agreement, by giving the party in breach / violation of this Agreement written notice to that effect, without prejudice to the right of the aggrieved party to require the payment of damages.

ARTICLE IV
MODIFICATIONS OR AMENDMENTS

No modification or amendment to this agreement and the approved project documents shall be allowed without the written consent of both parties involved. All amendment(s) that will be appended to this agreement shall be in writing and must be delivered to the address of the parties indicated herein.
