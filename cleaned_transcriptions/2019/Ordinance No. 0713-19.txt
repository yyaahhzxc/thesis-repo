ORDINANCE NO. 0713-19
Series of 2019

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF MINDANAO TRADE EXPO FOUNDATION, INC., AND THE REGISTERED EXHIBITORS OF DAVAO – BASED MICRO, SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTREPRENEURS FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF MAYOR'S PERMIT FEE, IN CONNECTION WITH THE MINDANAO TRADE EXPO (MTE) ARAW NG DABAW ON MARCH 10-13, 2019, AT ABREZZA AYALA "MALI," J.P. LAUREL AVENUE, THIS CITY, IN CELEBRATION OF THE 52ND ARAW NG DABAW

--- [Page 5] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF MINDANAO TRADE EXPO FOUNDATION, INC., AND THE REGISTERED EXHIBITORS OF DAVAO-BASED MICRO, SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTREPRENEURS FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF MAYOR'S PERMIT FEE, IN CONNECTION WITH THE MINDANAO TRADE EXPO (MTE) ARAW NG DABAW ON MARCH 10-13, 2019, AT ABREZZA AYALA MALL, J.P. LAUREL AVENUE, THIS CITY, IN CELEBRATION OF THE 82ND ARAW NG DABAW".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption applies only to registered exhibitors of Davao-based Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and solely refers to the payment of Mayor's Permit Fee under Article 26, Section 221, Paragraphs a & d of the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao.

SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION - The exemption shall be from the approval of this Ordinance until March 10-13, 2019, and that in no case shall the organizers be exempt from compliance with the other provisions of the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao and other applicable laws and ordinances.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 5th day of March, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
