ORDINANCE NO. 0410-18
Series of 2018

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE DRINKING OR
CONSUMING OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES IN PUBLIC
STREETS, SIDEWALKS, PUBLIC PARKS AND
PLAYGROUNDS AND OTHER PUBLIC AREAS

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0410-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "The Sobriety Ordinance of Davao City";

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS:
a. Alcoholic beverages or intoxicating beverages - any drink which contains alcohol as an active agent, including but not limited to beers, liquors, spirits, wines, locally distilled alcoholic drinks such as, but not limited to tuba, lambarog, basi, etc., and cocktails.
b. Public streets, sidewalks, walkways, footbridges - Any public street, highway, bridge, thoroughfare, sidewalk, walkway, footbridge within the territorial jurisdiction of the city.
c. Public Parks and Playgrounds - Any park, plaza, playground, gym, cemetery, public sports, or passenger vessel waters, or other public service or recreational areas created, designated or maintained by the Barangay, City or municipality, or other public areas.
d. Other Public Areas - the following shall be considered as "other public areas" within the meaning of this Ordinance:
(1) private areas generally open to the public such as parking areas, green spaces, gardens, playgrounds, etc.
(2) uninhabited private places used without the permission of the owner such as beaches, empty lots, abandoned houses or buildings.

SECTION 3. GENERAL PROVISION - The consumption of alcoholic beverages in public streets, sidewalks, public parks, playgrounds, and other public areas is prohibited, subject to the provisions of the succeeding sections hereunder. The consumption of the same aboard any motor vehicle found or parked within the prohibited areas also constitutes a violation of this Ordinance;

SECTION 4. EXEMPTIONS - the following shall be exempted from the provisions of this Ordinance:
a) Customers of any establishment whose business is authorized or permitted by the City Government to use a portion or part of the street, sidewalk or public park;
b) Special occasions or festivities that shall be authorized or permitted by the City Government or the Barangay concerned;

SECTION 5. SPECIAL PERMIT - The Barangay Council is hereby authorized to issue a Special Permit to any person or entity that shall conduct special events within their Barangay Parks, Plazas, Gyms, or Streets. For Special Permit issued by the Barangay, the Barangay Council shall be authorized to impose a processing fee in accordance with the Local Government Code of 1991;

Events utilizing City-owned or maintained establishments, parks, gyms, or streets shall obtain their permit from the City Mayor's Office;

SECTION 6. PENALTY CLAUSE - Any violation of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be punished as follows, without prejudice to the prosecution of other actions:

*• 1st Offense – Fine of P3,000
*• 2nd Offense – Fine of P5,000 and/or imprisonment of three months at the discretion of the court
*• 3rd Offense – Fine of P5,000 and/or imprisonment of one year at the discretion of the court*

Provided that, if minors are caught violating the Ordinance, they shall be dealt with in accordance with the Juvenile Justice Law;

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE – If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE – All ordinances, rules and regulations or parts thereof in conflict with this Ordinance are hereby repealed, provided that the rights that were vested upon the affectivity of this Ordinance shall not be impaired;

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY – This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on March 6, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
