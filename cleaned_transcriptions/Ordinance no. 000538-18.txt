Ord. No. 0538-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE DECLARING BOTH SIDES ALONG THE MAIN ACCESS ROAD OF PUNTA DUMALAG, MATINA APLAYA, TALOMO DISTRICT, THIS CITY, AS NO PARKING AND TOW-AWAY ZONE".

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES OF DEVELOPMENT - Section 16-General Welfare of the Local Government Code, states that "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and through which it may exercise the powers conferred upon it by virtue of its respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among its residents, maintain peace and order and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants".

SECTION 3. NO PARKING AND TOW-AWAY ZONE - Both sides along the main access road of Punta Dumalag, Matina Aplaya, this City, starting from Feliz Beach Resort Entrance Gate Area up to the Sea Gull Resort Entrance Gate area shall be declared a NO PARKING ZONE 24 hours daily, and any vehicle, cart, object found obstructing the road and sidewalk both sides shall be towed away, removed, disposed accordingly by the City Transport and Traffic Management Office personnel at the owner's expense.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval and after publication in accordance with the Local Government Code of 1991, otherwise known as Republic Act No. 7160.

ENACTED, on the 11th day of September, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
