Ordinance No. 0417-18
Series of 2018

An Ordinance Providing a Special Incentive Package for Properties to be Developed and Utilized as Parking Spaces within the Territorial Jurisdiction of Davao City, as Amended

--- [Page 5] ---
Page 2 of 6
Ord. No. 0417-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "THE SPECIAL INCENTIVE PACKAGE FOR PARKING SPACES IN DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. PURPOSE - It is the purpose of this Ordinance to encourage property owners and/or developers to develop their properties as parking spaces for public use;

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall only apply to properties which conform to the following conditions:
1. Properties within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City duly classified as within the Commercial Zone as provided under the Zoning Ordinance of Davao City;
2. Properties developed and/or used exclusively as parking spaces/buildings;
3. Properties with updated payment of Real Property Taxes. A Certification from the City Treasurer's Office shall be necessary upon application in order to comply with this condition;
Non-compliance of any of the above conditions shall mean disqualification to the benefits provided by this Ordinance;

SECTION 4. EXCLUSIONS - This Ordinance, however, excludes those parking spaces required under the National Building Code for property or building owners/developers; Further, all existing parking spaces of establishments/malls in excess of what is required by law but passed through the approval of the Office of the Building Official prior to this Ordinance shall likewise be excluded.

No alteration of approved Building Plans shall be allowed in order to avail of the benefits provided in this Ordinance, except when circumstances fall under Section 5.2 of this Ordinance;

SECTION 5. INCENTIVE PACKAGE - There shall be an incentive package to qualified applicants whose properties shall be developed and used as parking spaces, for a fee or for free to the public.
1. Incentives for New Developed Parking Facilities:
a. Real Property Tax for Land: All lands qualified under Section 3 of this Ordinance shall be exempt from the payment of Real Property Tax from the moment that the same shall be used or converted as a parking space for a period of two (2) years.
b. Real Property Tax for Building: Should the property owner develop or construct parking building on a land qualified under Section 3 of this Ordinance, said building shall likewise enjoy exemption from payment of Real Property Tax for five (5) years from the date of its completion if it has been built to the standards provided. Provided that the order for said building to enjoy the benefits granted by this Ordinance, the following requisites must concur:

--- [Page 6] ---
Page 3 of Ord. No. 0417-18

i. The building must be exclusively used as a parking space for a period not less than five (5) years; should the property owner introduce another use to the structure prior to the five (5) year lock-in period, the benefit shall be forfeited and the Real Property Tax for both land and the building shall be assessed commencing from the time of the grant of the benefit up to the time that the building has been converted.

ii. The structure must be, at the minimum, a two-story building;

c. Business Tax for Parking Spaces. Payment of business tax shall be exempt for a period of five (5) years. In case of cessation of business prior to the five (5) year period, the benefit shall be forfeited and the business tax shall be assessed commencing from the time until the time that the business ceased to operate.

2. Incentives for improved/renovated parking lots and buildings in excess of what is provided under the National Building Code under the following conditions:

a. Real Property Tax for Renovated or Improved Parking Lots/Buildings. Properties qualified under this Section shall enjoy real property tax incentives such as:

1. The approved or mandatory parking space is retained;
2. A new or additional structure is built on the existing parking lot/area;
3. The new or additional structure complies with Section 4, paragraph (b);
4. The improvement will not diminish or decrease building plans already approved by the Office of the Building Official of this city;

b. Business Tax. The Business Tax for the lot/building qualified under Section 5 shall enjoy the same benefits provided under paragraph (c) of the same Section.

SECTION 6. FORFEITURE OF BENEFITS - The benefits provided by this Ordinance shall be forfeited the moment the parking lot is converted to another use by its owner. In case of land with a building, Section 5, paragraph b (i) applies;

SECTION 7. REGISTRATION AND APPROVAL PROCESS - All applications for the grant of benefits under this Ordinance shall be submitted to the City Mayor, through the Davao City Investment Promotion Center (DCIPC). This provision is an addition to the Powers and Duties of the Center as provided for under Chapter IV, Section 13-B of the Davao City Investment Promotion Code.

**A. Registration Requirements. All applications shall be deemed complete upon compliance of the following requirements:**

i. Payment of a non-refundable Filing Fee, as shown in item "C" below for property owners whose lands and/or buildings are used as parking spaces;

ii. Three copies of duly accomplished and notarized application form, copies of which can be secured from the Center or its web portal;

iii. Certified true copy of the title(s) from Register of Deeds;

iv. Declaration of Real Property Valuation secured from the City Assessor's Office as proof that the applicant is the owner of the property sought to be granted with the benefits provided by this Ordinance;

v. Lease Contract (if the operator of the parking building and parking lot is not the property owner);

vi. Business Plan or Project Study;

vii. Business Permit;

viii. Certification of Payment for Real Property Tax from the City Treasurer's Office as proof that the property has no tax delinquency;

**B. Approval. The Center, through its Officer-In-Charge, shall evaluate the application and once found qualified, shall recommend approval of the same through the Davao City Investment Incentive Board. In the event that the application fails to meet the requirements and conditions set forth in this Ordinance, the applicant shall be immediately disapproved. Such disapproval shall be communicated to the applicant in writing citing the reason thereof;**

i. All approved applications for registration shall be issued Certificates of Registration by the Center to be signed by the Chairperson of the Davao City Investment Incentive Board or such other officer as the Board may empower and designate for the purpose;

ii. It shall be the duty of the Center to facilitate information dissemination to all concerned local government offices and other entities of the newly approved application;

**C. Filing Fee. The following non-refundable filing fee based on the assessed value of the property shall be paid as follows:**

| Filing Fee | Assessed Value |
|------------|---------------|
| 1,000       | Up to P100,000  |
| 2,000       | More than P100,000 up to P500,000  |
| 3,000       | More than P500,000 up to P3,000,000  |
| 5,000       | More than P3,000,000 up to P5,000,000  |
| 10,000      | More than P5,000,000  |

**SECTION 8. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS** - For monitoring purposes, the following pertinent Sections of the Davao City Investment Incentive Code, as amended, shall also be adopted;

i. **SECTION 28. Visitational Power of the Board and DCIPC;**

ii. **SECTION 29. Submission of Reports and other Documents;**

iii. **SECTION 30. Sanctions for Late Submission of Reportorial Requirements;**

iv. **SECTION 31. Final Clause;**

v. **SECTION 32. Appendix;**

**SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE** - All Ordinances contrary to or inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

**SECTION 10. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS** - The Davao City Investment Incentive Board, in consultation with the City Assessor's Office, the City Treasurer's Office, the City Legal Office, and the Office of the Building Official, shall formulate the necessary rules and regulations to implement the provisions of this Ordinance within nine (90) days after its approval. The rules and regulations issued pursuant to this Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation;

**SECTION 11. REVIEW** - The DCIPC, in consultation with local regulatory offices, shall review the provisions of this Ordinance every five (5) years and recommend to the Davao City Investment Incentive Board to remove any or all of the incentives provided for parking buildings and lots, when it deems that sufficient parking facilities have been attained and where continued extension of incentives may place the interest of the City and the public in a disadvantageous position, subject to the approval of the Sangguniang Panlungsod. For this purpose, the reckoning date for the review to be undertaken shall commence on the date of the approval of this Ordinance;

**SECTION 12. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE** - If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the same shall not affect the validity and effectiveness of the other parts or provisions hereof;

**SECTION 13. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE** - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of local or general circulation and posting in at least two (2) conspicuous places in the City of Davao;

**ENACTED, on March 6, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
