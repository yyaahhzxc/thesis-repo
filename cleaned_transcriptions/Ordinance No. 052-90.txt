**RESOLUTION NO. 163**

The letter-request, dated July 21, 1989, of Mr. Rosario M. Borromeo, Jr., President, Rotary Club of Matina Beach, for permit to operate a tricycle transportation (Trisized) within the area of SIR, Escolad, Ateneo High School, Juna Subdivision and Times Beach, all of the City of Davao, was presented for consideration and approbation by Resolution No. 162 of Councilor Agosto P. Amante.

Be it Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that: SECTION 1.- Classification of Pedicabs (Pedal-operated tri- cycle) otherwise known as triskikade. Pedicabs shall be classified as for hire, commercial use or owner driven. SEC. 2.- Definition of Terms a) Pedicab (Pedal-operated tricycle) otherwise known as triskikade is a vehicle having three (3) wheels and operated by means of a pedal to include bicycle with sidecar; b) Pedicab for hire is being used in transporting pas- sengers, goods or both for a fee; c) Pedicab for commercial use is being used to transport commercial goods of its owner for delivery to customers; d) Private pedicabs is one being used by its owner for transport of his own goods and for personal use; SEC. 3.- Who are authorized to operate a) Only Filipino citizens, corporation or entity, 60% of the ownership of which is owned by Filipino citizens; b) The person, corporation or entity shall have an asset not less than $50,000.00. SEC. 4. - Registration
a) No person shall operate pedicab unless the same is
registered with the Traffic Division of the Davao
METRODILCOM, PC-IMP;
b) Registration of pedicab shall be done annually every
January;
c) Newly acquired pedicab shall be registered within
thirty (30) days from date of acquisition without pre-
judice to sub-paragraph (a) hereof;
d) Failure to register within the period specified above
shall make the owner of the pedicab liable to a 50%
surcharge;
e) No registration or renewal of registration shall be
allowed of units which are unfit for public use or not
roadworthy as determined by the Chief Traffic Division,
Davao METRODILCOM, PC-IMP;

SEC. 5. - Registration Fees
The annual rate of registration fees shall be as follows:
a) Pedicab for hire - P100.00
b) Pedicab for commercial use - P100.00
c) Private pedicab - P50.00
Provided, however, the owner/operator of the pedicab shall have
the option to register his newly acquired unit/units for a period of
three (3) years.
Payment of registration fees shall be made to the City Treasurer's
Office which shall issue a corresponding receipt for registration plate
for the current year.
Insurance with a time coverage of one year for property damage
and physical injuries shall be required upon registration of pedal-
operated pedicabs.

SEC. 6. Limitations of Operation
a) Pedicabs for hire shall not be allowed to operate in streets designated as off-limits by the Chief Traffic Division, Davao METRODIZON, PC-IPN;
b) That pedicabs for hire shall not be allowed along the national highways;
c) Only a person who is a holder of a driver's license issued by the LTO shall be allowed to drive a pedicab;
d) As far as practicable the operation of pedicab shall be limited within residential areas;
e) The rate of fees to be collected from pedicab passengers shall not exceed the rates approved by the LTRB on public utility jeepsneys in Davao City;
f) The homeowners' association of private subdivisions may prescribe some limitations regarding the operation of the pedicabs within said subdivisions;
g) The total number of passengers of a pedicab shall be limited to three (3) persons including the driver.

SEC. 7. Display of Plate Number and Carrying a Photostat Copy of the Registration
The registration plate number of a pedicab shall be prominently displayed at the rear portion of the pedicab clearly visible at a distance of 50 feet and the driver/operator shall always carry his photo copy of the registration papers.

SEC. 8. Compliance with all traffic rules and regulations.
The drivers or operators of pedicabs shall at all times comply with the traffic rules and regulations while operating their units;

SEC. 9. Light of Pedicabs
Every pedicab being operated at night shall be equipped with light on the front which shall emit a white light from a distance of Continued on Page 7

at least 200 feet to the front and with a rear red reflex mirror or reflectorized tape visible for a distance of 200 feet to the rear.

SEC. 10.- Limitation as to the number of pedicabs for hire.

The number of pedicabs for hire shall be determined every year by the City Government upon the survey and recommendation of the Chief Traffic Division, Department of Transportation, PC-INT;

SEC. 11.- Warning.

No person shall operate a pedicab unless it is equipped with a bell or other devices capable of giving a signal for a distance of at least 100 feet except sirena or whistle.

SEC. 12.- Penalty Clause.

The following administrative penalty shall be imposed upon the driver or owner/operator of the pedicab for the violations indicated hereunder:

No registration - P 250.00
No plate number - P 200.00
No light or rear red reflex mirror - P 200.00

Operating in not designated areas:

First offense - P 250.00
Second offense - P 500.00
Failure to comply registration paper - P 100.00

Violations of traffic rules and regulations as provided for in Ordinance No. 778, Series of 1973, as amended, and other traffic ordinances shall be dealt with in accordance with the penalties therein provided.

Pedicabs operating in violation of any provision of this Ordinance will be impounded and be released upon payment of the administrative fines.
