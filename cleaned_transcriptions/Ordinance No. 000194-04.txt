CHAPTER I: TITLE AND POLICY

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance Creating the Davao City Culture and Arts Council".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Article II, Section 17 of the 1997 Constitution declares as a policy that the State shall give priority to education, science and technology, arts, culture and sports to foster patriotism and nationalism, accelerate social progress, and promote full human liberation and development.

The 1987 Constitution further declares under the following Sections of Article 1:

Section 14. The State shall foster the preservation, enrichment and dynamic evolution of a Filipino national culture based on the principle of unity in diversity in a climate of free artistic and intellectual expressions.

Section 15. Arts and letters shall enjoy the patronage of the State. The State shall conserve, promote, and popularize the nation's historical and cultural heritage and resources, as well as artistic creations.

Section 16. All the country's artistic and historic wealth constitutes the cultural treasures of the nation and shall be under the protection of the State which may regulate its disposition.

Section 17. The State shall recognize, respect and protect the rights of indigenous cultural communities to preserve and develop their cultures, traditions and institutions. It shall consider these rights in the formulation of national plans and policies.

Section 18. (1) The State shall ensure equal access to cultural opportunities through the educational system, public or private cultural entities, scholarship grants, and other incentives, community cultural centers, and other public venues.

(2) The State shall encourage and support researches and studies on arts and culture.

Title 3, Chapter 3, Article 3, Section 438, Paragraph a-5, subparagraph XVL of R.A. 7160 declares that the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall establish a City Council whose purpose is the promotion of culture and arts, coordinate with government agencies and non-government organizations and subject to the availability of funds, appropriate funds for the support and development of the arts and culture. R.A. 7160 further provides that local Government Units shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture;

CHAPTER II: SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
SECTION 1. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall be applicable within the entire territory and jurisdiction of the City of Davao.

CHAPTER III: GENERAL PROVISIONS
SECTION 4. COMPOSITION - The Davao City Culture and Arts Council shall be composed of the following:

CHAIR - The Mayor of the City of Davao or his duly appointed representative;
CO-CHAIR - Representative from the private sector, duly appointed by the City Mayor;
MEMBERS - SP Chair, Committee on Education, Science and Technology, Arts and Culture;
SP Chair Committee on Tourism and Beautification;
City Tourism Officer;
ARTS COMMUNITY - One (1) Representative;
CULTURE COMMUNITY - One (1) Representative;
ACADEMIE - One (1) Representative;
BUSINESS SECTOR - One (1) Representative;

SECTION 5. APPOINTING POWER OF THE MAYOR - The members of the Davao City Culture and Arts Council, except for the Chairperson of the San Miguel Pangilngod's Committee on Education, Science and Technology, Arts and Culture, Chairperson of the San Miguel Pangilngod's Committee on Tourism and Beautification, the City Tourism Officer, and the City Planning and Development Officer, shall be appointed by the City Mayor.

The City Mayor, as Chief Executive, may appoint any public officer of the local Government Unit as his duly authorized representative in order to ensure the City's active participation. The authorized representative, being the mayor's alter ego, shall act as Chairman of the Council with all the powers and functions attached to the office.

SECTION 6. TERM OF OFFICE - Except for the City Mayor or his duly appointed representative, the Chairperson of the San Miguel Pangilngod's Committee on Education, Science and Technology, Arts and Culture, the Chairperson of the San Miguel Pangilngod's Committee on Tourism and Beautification, the City Tourism Officer, and the City Planning and Development Officer, the term of office of the Davao City Culture and Arts Council shall not exceed five (5) years, provided that the City Mayor is prohibited from reappointing any member of the Council for another term of three (3) years.

SECTION 7.
FUNCTIONS. The functions of the Davao City Culture and Arts Council shall be as follows:

a. Plan and promote cultural and artistic activities in the community with the end view of establishing Davao City as the Cultural Center of Mindanao.

b. Conduct a coordinated and collaborative planning for the City's cultural development programs with persons and groups to ensure a unified direction of activities and consideration for local needs and priorities; thereby effecting an environment of meaningful exchange and mutual support among its members.

c. Stimulate and implement artistic activities by encouraging collaboration between artists and individual choices of expression and autonomy of each artistic sector in planning and implementing their activities and encouraging the artists' articulations of social realities and priorities in their genres.

d. Develop linkages with national and international institutions such as the Cultural Center of the Philippines (CCP), the National Commission for Culture and Arts (NCCA) and other similar network of agencies and foundations which will support the activities of the Council.

e. Preserve and promote the traditional arts of Davao City by enhancing public awareness of the folk traditions of the City, being verily the cultural cradle of the indigenous communities and by acknowledging the significance of creative cultural expression as reflections of our cultural heritage.

f. Develop a program for local artists through training apprenticeship, grants and the promotion of their works and encouraging their freedom of creative expressions.

SECTION 8.
FUNDING: All expenses necessary and incidental to the effective implementation of the operations of the Davao City Culture and Arts Council shall be charged to an initial fund of Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (P: 500,000.00) of the Local School Board Fund and that subsequent funds made available for the purpose shall be made through the Annual or Supplemental Budget of the City.

SECTION 9.
OTHER SOURCE OF FUND: The Council may identify possible funding in coordination with government agencies and/or non-government organizations or institutions subject to regular auditing procedures.

CHAPTER IV: TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 10.
INTER-AGENCY COMMITTEE AND THE IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS: An inter-agency Committee is hereby created and mandated to formulate rules and regulations relative to the full implementation of this Ordinance within sixty (60) days from its effectiveness.

The Inter-agency Committee shall be composed of representatives from the Sangguniang Panlungsod's Committee on Education, Culture and Arts, the City Mayor's Office, the City Tourism Office, the academically accredited civic, business, arts and culture organizations, all of whom shall be appointed by the Mayor.
