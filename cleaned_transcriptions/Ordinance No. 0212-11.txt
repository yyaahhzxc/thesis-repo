**SECTION 2. ACCEPTANCE AND ADOPTION OF THE RULES**

The Rules for the Selection of the Mandatory Representative of the Indigenous Peoples to the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City crafted by the Integrated Working Group (IWG) constituted by virtue of Executive Order No. 1079, dated December 12, 2011, pursuant to Section 8 of Ordinance No. 0448- of Senate Bill No. 1356, hereby adopted including the amendments to Section 10 paragraph 3, which shall now read:

**RULES FOR THE SELECTION OF THE MANDATORY REPRESENTATIVE OF THE INDIGENOUS PEOPLES TO THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGNSOD OF DAVAO CITY**

**Pursuant to Section 16 of the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997 (RA 8371), Department of Interior & Local Government Memorandum Circular 2010-119 dated 20 October 2010 and Davao City Ordinance No. 0448-10, the following rules are hereby promulgated to set up processes and mechanisms to select and install the IP representative in legislative bodies.**

**SECTION 1.**

**Title:** This rule shall be known as "The Local Guidelines for the Selection of the IP Representative to the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City."

**Section 2. Operating Principles:**

In the implementation of these guidelines, the following shall be observed:

**1. Primacy of customary laws and practices:** Customary laws and practices shall prevail upon administrative mechanisms in the manner of selecting the IP representative to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (SP) of Davao City. It may also be taken into account in resolving disputes in the selection process.

**2. Consensus building refers to the process of arriving at a decision by the ICCs/IPs on issues and concerns affecting them by employing their own traditional decision-making processes.**

**3. Inclusivity and full participation:** The National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) shall ensure that all ICCs/IPs residing within Davao City shall actively participate in the selection process.

**4. Representation of the collective interests and aspirations of ICCs/IPs:** The IP mandatory representative to the SP of Davao City shall represent the general concerns and welfare of all the ICCs/IPs in Davao City.

**5. Sustainability of the Indigenous Peoples Consultative Body (IPCB):** The ICCs/IPs mandatory representative shall provide support in sustaining the constitution and operations of the IPCB at his/her level of representation.

**6. Capacity Building:** The skills and capacities of the IP mandatory representative to engage effectively and efficiently in mainstream governance as well as to preserve and exercise their traditional political structures in their respective communities shall be enhanced through training modules to be developed and regularly implemented by the NCIP in partnership with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), the City Government of Davao City, and other government agencies/philanthropic entities of the city.

**7. Gender equity and sectoral representation:** ICC/IP women and youth shall enjoy equal rights and opportunities in the selection process as provided in Section 27 of RA 8371.

**SECTION 3. Coverage:**

These guidelines shall be applicable to all the ICCs/IPs residing within the ancestral domains or resettled communities within the political jurisdiction or boundaries of Davao City. In cases where an ancestral domain or resettled community is overlapped by two (2) or more local government units (LGUs) or another entity, only the members of the ICCs/IPs whose residence are within the City of Davao may take part in the selection of IPs mandatory representatives for the City of Davao.

--- [Page 4] ---
I. Definition of Terms: When used in these rules, the following terms shall mean:

(a) ICC - Indigenous Cultural Communities

(b) Customary Law - refers to a body of written or unwritten rules, usages, customs, and practices traditionally and socially recognized, accepted, and observed by the respective ICCs/IPs.

(c) Mandatory Representation - is the authority to act on account of the ICCs/IPs and the ability to speak for their needs and desires.

(d) IP - refers to that group or community of Indigenous Peoples residing within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao and identified by:

1. Self-assertion and ascription by others;
2. Having lived, possessed, and claimed a territory therein since time immemorial, observing their own language, beliefs, culture, tradition, and socio-political institutions; or
3. On account of their descent though they may have come from domains outside the concerned political unit.

For Davao City, the six (6) major IP communities are as follows: Ata, Kagan, K'leta, Matigsalog, Tagabawa, and Uba Manuwu.

(e) Validation - is the process of checking the legitimacy of a certain claim/interest according to the oral declaration and the documents presented, pursuant to the guidelines of the NCIP. It is intended also to supplement information in order to reach a reliable decision.

(f) IPCB - Indigenous Peoples Consultative Body. This is a council to be found at the national, regional, and provincial levels, composed of traditional leaders, elders, and representatives of the indigenous peoples living in the respective indigenous communities in Davao City, constituted pursuant to Section 50 of R.A. 8371. The Consultative Body shall be the consultative arm of the NCIP to advise it on matters relating to the problems, aspirations, and interest of the Indigenous People's community.

Section 5. Powers, Duties, and Functions: The IP representative selected in accordance with these rules shall act as a regular member of the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City and shall have the same powers and functions corresponding to a regular member thereof as provided under the Local Government Code of 1991 and other applicable laws.

The IP representative, however, shall have the primary responsibility of adopting measures for the common interests of ICCs/IPs, ensuring support for the sustained constitution and operations of the IPCs in Davao City, maintaining the development and practice of ICCs/IPs, conflict resolution institutions, and peace building mechanisms and processes that are compatible with the local legal system and with internationally-recognized human rights.

--- [Page 5] ---
i) 6. Term of Office. The term of office of the ICC/IP mandatory representative in the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City shall be for a period of three (3) years from the date of assumption to office.

The six major Indigenous Peoples communities needing representation in the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City to ensure the advancement of their respective aspirations are the following: Ata, Yakan, K'latata, Matigsalud, Tagabog, and Tuguegarao. The selected IP representative coming from the said IP communities will represent the said communities in the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City on a rotation basis per term in the same order of priority enumerated above, which was arranged in consultation with the ICC/IPs.

Section 7. Minimum Qualifications. The ICC/IP representative shall have the following minimum qualifications:

(a) Natural-born Filipino citizen;

(b) Registered voter in the City of Davao;

(c) Bona fide ICC/IP by blood or consanguinity, as certified by the NCIP;

(d) Acknowledged leader of the ICCs/IPs of which he or she is a member;

(e) He or she must be a bona fide IP by blood or consanguinity, an acknowledged leader of the ICCs/IPs of which he or she is a member, and has continuously engaged in setting up a track record of services for ICCs/IPs as certified by the NCIP;

(f) Able to speak, write, and;

(g) Knowledgeable and practices the customary ways of the ICCs/IPs of which he or she is a member.

Section 8. Disqualifications. Any person suffering from or characterized by any of the following circumstances shall not be eligible to represent the ICCs/IPs:

(a) Convicted by final judgment by the council of elders of concerned ICCs/IPs for violating their customs and traditions, as validated by the NCIP;

(b) Sentenced by final judgment for an offense involving moral turpitude or for an offense punishable by one (1) year or more of imprisonment, within two (2) years after serving sentence;

(c) Removed from office as a result of administrative case;

(d) Convicted by final judgment for violating the oath of allegiance to the Republic of the Philippines;

(e) Fugitive from justice within the country;

(f) Permanent resident in a foreign country or has acquired the right to reside abroad and continues to avail of the same right;

(h) Insane or feeble-minded based on the findings of competent authorities.

Section 9. Compensation and Benefits. The compensation, benefits, and other entitlements of the ICC/IP representative shall be the same with that of the regular members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod as prescribed in R.A. 7100 and other applicable laws.

Section 10. Manner of Selection. The IP Representative shall be selected under the following steps:

--- [Page 6] ---
1. The ICC/IP shall inform the NCIP of its written intent to select its IP representative.
2. The ICC/IP then selects its IP representative in accordance with its customary law. Each of the six (6) indigenous tribes identified herein shall append to these said guidelines a written document of its customary law of selecting its respective IP representative.
3. The selection process shall be facilitated by the NCIP whose task is to validate and document the same, with the assistance of the City Government Office of Davao and the DILG.
4. Upon the selection of an IP Representative, the NCIP Regional Director shall issue a Certificate of Authorization (COA) upon the recommendation of the Provincial Officer.
5. The NCIP Regional Office shall submit to the DILG and to the City Government Office of Davao a copy of its issued COA of the selected representative, including the name of the appointee, with a copy furnished to the NCIP Central Office through the Office of Employment and Human Rights (OEHRR) for the information of the Sanguinolang Panglungsog of his/her assumption.
6. The selected IP representative shall take his oath or unswear any person authorized to administer oaths and shall inform the Sanguinolang Panglungsog of his/her assumption.

Section 11. Vacancy: In case of vacancy, the ICC/IP concerned shall select its replacement in accordance with Section 10 hereof. The vacancy before the end of the term of office of an incumbent shall be filled up by a new representative coming from the same tribe of said incumbent. The new representative shall only serve the unexpired term of the ICC/IP to which he/she belongs.

Section 12. Replacement of ICC/IP Representative: The ICC/IP mandatory representative may be replaced at anytime for loss of confidence by the ICC/IP to which he/she belongs.

A replacement may be initiated by the filing of a written intent to replace the IP representative to the IPCB by any member of the ICC/IP of the City of Davao. The ICC/IP shall endorse such written intent to replace the IP representative to the Tribal Council to which the IP representative belongs. The Tribal Council shall determine whether the intent to replace the IP representative is meritorious and shall decide accordingly. The decision of the Tribal Council must be signed by the majority of the members of the ICC/IP to which such IP representative belongs.

No written intent to replace an IP representative shall be initiated or entertained within one (1) year from the date of the IP representative's assumption to office or within one (1) year immediately preceding the expiration of the term of the IP sought to be replaced. The replacement of an ICC/IP representative may be done only once every term.

Section 13. Amendments: Any amendments to these guidelines may be initiated by any member of the ICC/IPs by a written proposal to amend the rules submitted to the IPCB. The latter shall then assist in the drafting of the amendment to these guidelines. The amended guidelines shall be adopted in the same manner as the approval of the same.

Supplementary Clause: In case of any insufficiency in the provisions of these rules, the existing law, rules and regulations shall be applied in a supplementary character.

--- [Page 7] ---
15. Separability Clause: In case any part of these rules shall be held invalid by a competent court, the remaining provisions not affected shall be given full force and effect.
Section 16. Effectivity: These local guidelines shall take effect immediately upon the approval of the majority vote of all Tribal Councils of the six major IP Communities in Davao City.
