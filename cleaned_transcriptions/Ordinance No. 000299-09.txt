ORDINANCE NO. 0299-09
Series of 2009
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 3 OF ORDINANCE SERIES OF 1986, OTHERWISE Known As "An Ordinance No. 0297-86" RELATING TO RESTAURANTS, FOOD ESTABLISHMENTS SUCH AS RESTAURANTS, FOOD STALLS AND OTHER RELATED OUTLETS SERVING BARBECUED MEAT SUCH AS PORK, BEEF, FISH OR ANY FOOD COMPARABLE THEREIN, THE USE OF POINTED STICKS OR SEWERS TO REMOVE THE POINTED STICKS COMPLETELY OR CUT THEIR SHARP ENDS BEFORE SERVING TO CUSTOMERS, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATORS THEREOF
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

--- [Page 3] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0299-09

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 3 OF ORDINANCE NO. 115, SERIES OF 1986, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS 'AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING PERSONS AND/OR BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS SUCH AS RESTAURANTS, FOOD STALLS AND OTHER RELATED OUTLETS SERVING BARBECUED MEAT SUCH AS PORK, BEEF, FISH OR ANY FOOD PREPARATION NEEDING THE USE OF BAMBOO STICKS OR SHEARS TO REMOVE THE POINTED STICKS COMBINED WITH A PENALTY FOR THE TAKING OF SUCH SERVINGS TO CUSTOMERS, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREOF".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT: Section 3 of Ordinance 115, Series of 1986, otherwise known as an Ordinance requiring persons and/or business establishments such as restaurants, food stalls and other related outlet serving barbecued meat such as pork, beef, fish, or any food preparation needing the use of bamboo sticks or shears to remove the pointed sticks completely or cut their sharp ends before serving to customers, and providing penalties for violation thereof, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Section 3 - Violations hereof shall subject the owners, proprietors or managers of business establishments mentioned under Section 1 hereof to the following:
for the first offense, a fine of P300.00
for the second offense, a fine of P500.00
for the third and subsequent offenses, a fine of P750.00, or
an imprisonment of one (1) month or both, upon the discretion of the court and closure of the business establishment with the cancellation of the business permit".

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If for any reason, any Section of the Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other Sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE: Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect 15 days following its publication in at least two [2] newspapers of general circulation in Davao City, and upon posting for three consecutive weeks at conspicuous places in the City Hall;
