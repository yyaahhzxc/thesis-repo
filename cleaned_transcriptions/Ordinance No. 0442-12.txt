AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEEDS OF SALE FOR THE LOTS IN THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) NEW MATINIA COMMERCIAL AREA PROGRAM
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that;

--- [Page 2] ---
**Page 2 of 2**

**Ord. No. 0442-12**

**SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEEDS OF SALE FOR THE LOTS IN THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) NEW MATIMA COMMERCIAL ARE PROGRAM;"**

**SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deeds of Sale for the lots in the Slum Improvement and Resettlement (SIR) New Matima Commercial Area Program;**

**SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.**

**ENACTED, December 13, 2012 by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.**
