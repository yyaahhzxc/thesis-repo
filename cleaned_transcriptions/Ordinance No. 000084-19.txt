Ord. No. 084-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REPEALING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0441-65, SERIES OF 1965, BY REASON OF R.A. 9514 AND R.A. 6541."

SECTION 2. REPEALING CLAUSE. - City Ordinance No. 0441-65, Series of 1965, otherwise known as "AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE USE OF THE ROOF GARDENS OF BUILDINGS WITHIN THE CITY OF DAVAO", AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO. 105, SERIES OF 1986, is hereby repealed by reason of R.A. 9514 AND R.A. 6541.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, October 22, 2019, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
