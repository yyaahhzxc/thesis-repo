**ORDINANCE NO. 0123.11**
**Series of 2011**

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE HONORABLE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON HOUSING CONSTRUCTION PROGRAM FOR THE ELEMENTARY EDUCATION PROGRAM TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY AND THE NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY (NHA), FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ONE HUNDRED (100) HOUSING UNITS IN THE EXISTING LOS AMIGOS RELOCATION SITE, LOS AMIGOS, DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0132-11

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that

SECTION I. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE HONORABLE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON HOUSING CONSTRUCTION UNDER THE RESSETTLEMENT ASSISTANCE PROGRAM TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY AND THE NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY (NHA), FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ONE HUNDRED (100) HOUSING UNITS IN THE EXISTING LOS AMIGOS RELOCATION SITE, LOS AMIGOS, DAVAO CITY;

SECTION 2. PROJECT: The Memorandum of Agreement Housing Construction Under the Resettlement Assistance Program refers to the construction of One Hundred (100) housing units at the existing Los Amigos Relocation Site, Los Amigos, Davao City, owned by the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY: The Honorable City Mayor is hereby authorized to sign for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT ON HOUSING CONSTRUCTION UNDER THE RESSETTLEMENT ASSISTANCE PROGRAM to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and the National Housing Authority (NHA);

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

EXECATED, July 12, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
MARGARITO C. DELA CRUZ
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head II)
