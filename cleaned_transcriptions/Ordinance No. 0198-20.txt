Ord. No. 0198-20

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

ARTICLE I - GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as the "CHARTER OF THE
CITY COLLEGE OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. LOCATION: The City Government of Davao will acquire a
permanent site to which school buildings and other facilities will be constructed.

SECTION 3. PURPOSE: The primary purpose of the College is to help promote
the interest and welfare of the people of Davao by providing them access and equitable
opportunities with relevant and responsive academic and technology-driven programs for
all deprived, disadvantaged and underserved learners who will be equipped with
knowledge, skills and values to compete and contribute meaningfully to the growth
and development of the city and to the world at a very minimal cost.

SECTION 4. THE CORPORATE POWERS OF THE COLLEGE: The City
College shall constitute a body corporate which shall be endowed with the attributes and
perpetual succession of a corporation and shall possess the powers which pertains to
a local College to be exercised in conformity with the provisions of this Charter.
Furthermore, the College shall possess the following corporate powers:

1. To have a continuous succession in its corporate name;
2. To have and use a corporate seal;
3. To exercise such powers as granted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of
Davao, subject to the limitations provided in this Ordinance and other laws.

ARTICLE II - BOARD OF TRUSTEES

SECTION 1. BOARD OF TRUSTEES: The governing body and corporate
powers of the City College shall be vested in and exercised by the Board of Trustees
(BOT) composed of the following:

1. The City Mayor shall automatically be the Chairperson of the BOT. He/She
shall be the Presiding Officer at every meeting of the BOT;
2. The College President shall be the Vice Chairperson of the BOT. He shall
act as the Presiding Officer of the BOT in the absence of the Chairperson;
3. The Commission on Higher Education (CHED) Regional Director of Region XI, as member;
4. The Chairperson of the Committee on Education, Science and Technology,
Arts and Culture of the Sangguniang Panlungsod as member;
5. The President of the Faculty Association as member;
6. The President of the Supreme Student Government as member;
7. The President of the Alumni Association as member;
8. The representative of the Association of Local Colleges and Universities
(ALCU) as member;
9. The Chairperson of the Davao City Chamber of Commerce and Industry,
Inc., as member;
10. A Representative from the Education sector as member;
11. The Indigenous People's Mandatory Representative (IPMR) of the
Sangguniang Panlungsod, as member.

SECTION 2. The Board of Trustees shall have a Board Secretary who shall be appointed by the Chairperson and whose term of Office shall be co-terminous with the Chairperson. He shall likewise serve as the Executive Committee Secretary.

SECTION 3. The Members of the Board of Trustees shall have the following term of office:

1. The Chairperson - during his/her term as City Mayor;

2. The Vice-Chairperson - for a term of four (4) years, subject to reappointment;

3. The Chairperson's Committee on Education, Science and Technology, Arts and Culture, Sanggunian Panlungsood - during his/her term as a Member of the Sanggunian;

4. The Schools Division Superintendent (SDS) - during his/her term as SDS of the Division of Davao City;

5. The Faculty Representative - one (1) year;

6. The Student Council Representative - one (1) year;

7. The Indigenous People's Mandatory Representative (IPMR) of the Sanggunian Panlungsood - during his/her term as IPMR.

ARTICLE III - EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE

SECTION 1. THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE - The Executive Committee, hereinafter referred to as the "EXECOM", is hereby created for the purpose of carrying out and exercising the regular functions and powers of the Board of Trustees and shall be composed of the following:

1. The President of the City College of Davao who shall be the Presiding Officer in every meeting of the EXECOM;

2. The Chairperson of the Committee on Education, Science and Technology, Arts and Culture of the Sanggunian Panlungsood;

3. The Faculty Representative of the CCD;

4. The Student Representative of the City College;

5. The Indigenous Peoples Mandatory Representative (IPMR) in the Sanggunian Panlungsood.

SECTION 2. All acts and resolutions approved by the EXECOM shall be ratified by the Board of Trustees. Important matters affecting the administrative and/or academic affairs of the College shall be reported to the Board of Trustees through the Chairperson.

ARTICLE IV - MEETINGS

SECTION 1. BOT REGULAR MEETINGS - The regular meeting of the Board of Trustees shall be held at least once every quarter at the City College of Davao or at any place in the City of Davao.

SECTION 2. BOT SPECIAL MEETINGS - The special meetings of the Board of Trustees shall be held at the City College of Davao or at any place in the City upon call of the Chairperson or the majority of the members of the Board of Trustees.

SECTION 3. EXECOM MEETINGS - The meetings of the EXECOM shall be at the City College of Davao or at any place in the City, once every quarter.

SECTION 4. EXECOM SPECIAL MEETINGS - The special meetings of the EXECOM shall be held at the City College of Davao or at any place in the City upon call of the President or the majority of the EXECOM Members.

SECTION 5. NOTICES - Notices of the time and place of the meetings of the BOT and EXECOM shall be given personally or by mail, at least 3 days before the dates set for such meeting.

SECTION 6. QUORUM - A quorum for any meeting of the Board of Trustees and the EXECOM shall consist of the majority of the members and a majority of such quorum may decide any question raised in the meeting.

SECTION 7. ORDER OF BUSINESS - The Order of Business of the meeting of the Board of Trustees and EXECOM members shall be as follows:

1. Roll Call;
2. Call to Order;
3. Approval of the Minutes of the Previous Meeting;
4. Ratification of All Acts and Resolutions Approved by the EXECOM (only for BOT Meetings);
5. New Business;
6. Other Matters.

SECTION 8. VOTING - Each member of the Board of Trustees or the Executive Committee (EXECOM) shall be entitled to one (1) vote and no proxy is allowed.

ARTICLE V - QUALIFICATIONS, VACANCY, SUSPENSION/REMOVAL AND COMPENSATION

SECTION 1. QUALIFICATIONS - All members of the Board of Trustees shall be citizens of the Republic of the Philippines with proven sense of responsibility, integrity and honesty.

SECTION 2. VACANCY - In case of vacancy in the Board of Trustees by reason of resignation, removal from office, death or any other incapacity of one or more of its members, such vacancy shall be filled-up by the appointment of the Chairperson of the Board of Trustees.

SECTION 3. SUSPENSION AND REMOVAL - A member of the BOT may be suspended or removed from office upon a majority vote of the BOT members for the following causes:

1. Incapacity;
2. Incompetence;
3. Dishonesty;
4. Conviction of a crime involving moral turpitude;
5. Any act detrimental to the interest and welfare of the City College of Davao.

**SECTION 4. COMPENSATION** - The members of the Board of Trustees/EXECOM shall serve without compensation except for a reasonable allowance or per diem as may be authorized by the Board of Trustees and as allowed by law.

**ARTICLE VI - POWERS AND DUTIES**

**SECTION 1. POWERS AND DUTIES** - Subject to existing laws, the following are the powers and duties of the Board of Trustees, in addition to the general administrative and corporate powers:

1. To enact rules and regulations not contrary to law as may be necessary to carry out the purpose and function of the College;
2. To receive and appropriate all pecuniary matters as may be provided, for the support of the College in the manner it may determine, in its discretion, to carry out the purpose and functions of the College;
3. To receive in legacy trusts, gifts, and donations of real and personal properties of all kinds, to administer and dispose the same when necessary for the benefit of the College, subject to limitations and instructions, of the donors, if any;
4. To fix the tuition fees and other necessary school charges, such as but not limited to matriculation fees, graduation fees, and laboratory fees after due consultation with the involved sectors:
   a. Such fees and charges including government subsidies and other income generated by the College shall constitute a special trust fund and shall be deposited in any authorized government depository bank, and all interests accruing thereof shall form part of the same fund for the use of the College.
   b. Any income generated by the college from tuition fees and other charges, as well as from the operation of auxiliary services and land grants, shall be retained by the college and may be disbursed by the BOT for instruction, research, extension, or other programs or projects of the College and that all fiduciary fees shall be disbursed for the specific purpose for which they are collected.
   c. If, for reasons beyond its control, the College shall not be able to pursue any project which funds have been appropriated and allocated under its approved program of expenditures, the BOT may authorize the use of said funds for any reasonable purpose, which in its discretion may be necessary and urgent for the attainment of the objectives and goals of the College.
5. To adopt and implement a scheme of payment of tuition fees and other school fees for greater access to poor but deserving students;
6. To approve the curricula, institutional programs, and rules of discipline drawn by the administrative and academic councils;

ARTICLE VII - SEARCH COMMITTEE FOR THE PRESIDENT; MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS AND VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT

SECTION 1. CONSTITUTION OF SEARCH COMMITTEE - The Board of Trustees shall constitute a Search Committee for the President ("SCP") which shall be composed of representatives from the Civil Service Commission (CSC), the Academe, Davao City Chamber of Commerce and Industry Inc. (DCCCI), the Association of Local Colleges and Universities (ALCU) and the Commission on Higher Education (CHEED). The SCP shall elect a Chairman and a Vice-Chairman from among themselves.

SECTION 2. FUNDS - The Board of Trustees shall appropriate adequate funds to finance the activities of the SCP.

SECTION 3. PROCEDURE FOR THE SEARCH - The Search Committee, so constituted, may devise its own rules and procedure for the search.

SECTION 4. NOTICE OF VACANCY - Within ten (10) days upon its constitution, the SCP shall cause the publication of the notice of vacancy for President in a newspaper of local or general circulation and by posting in conspicuous places in the city. The publication shall contain provisions for invitation of applicants and the deadline for submission of applications.

SECTION 5. QUALIFICATIONS FOR PRESIDENCY - The President of the City College of Davao must be a holder of an appropriate earned doctoral degree and must have at least five (5) years of relevant administrative experience.

--- [Page 8] ---
**SECTION 6. SELECTION OF QUALIFIED CANDIDATES**

Within a period of thirty (30) days after the deadline of submission of applications, the Search Committee shall screen the applicants and select therefrom the three (3) most qualified candidates within twenty-four (24) hours after the selection.

**SECTION 7. APPOINTMENT OF THE PRESIDENT**

The Board of Trustees shall appoint the President from the three (3) most qualified candidates submitted by the SCP.

**ARTICLE VIII - COLLEGE OFFICIALS**

**SECTION 1. The Officers of the CCD:**

1. **The College President**

The President, who is the Chief Executive Officer of the College, shall be appointed by the Board of Trustees for a term of four (4) years and may be reappointed upon approval of the Board.

Leadership in the college is vested in the President and he shall be the ex-officio head of the College faculty and personnel in all offices.

The general supervision of all academic, administrative, and research extensions of the College shall be under the Office of the President.

The President shall carry out the general policies laid down by the Board of Trustees and shall have exclusive power to act, direct, and assign the details of executive action.

The President determines and prepares the agenda of all meetings of the Board of Trustees, the Academic Council, and the Administrative Council; provided, however, that any member of the BOT shall have the right to suggest any matter for inclusion in the agenda.

He/She shall preside at commencement exercises and other public affairs of the College, and confer such degrees and honors as are granted by the BOT. All diplomas and certificates issued by the College shall be signed by him, the respective Program Heads, and the Registrar.

He/She shall be the official link of communication between the faculty, employees, and students of the College on one hand, and the Board of Trustees, on the other.

He/She shall appoint competent and qualified persons to fill all vacancies and new positions. He/She shall have the authority to fill vacancies temporarily and to make such arrangements as are necessary to meet all emergencies that may occur between the meetings of the BOT so that the work of the College will not be impeded.

In the exigency of the service, he/she shall have the power to transfer faculty members and employees from one program to another, subject to confirmation by the Board of Trustees, and in accordance with law.

He/She shall have the authority to recommend to the BOT the change of leave status of the faculty from teacher's leave to cumulative leave and vice versa.

--- [Page 9] ---
The President shall hold all the officers, faculty and employees of the College responsible for the full discharge of their respective duties; when necessary and in the interest of public service, he/she shall, after consultation with the Dean or Head concerned, initiate the appropriate proceedings of the separation from service of the erring faculty member or administrative support personnel.

He/She shall submit an annual report to the Board highlighting the accomplishments of the past year and the needs of the current year. He/She shall also present to the Board the proposed annual budget of the College.

He/She shall execute and sign on behalf of the College all contracts, deeds, and other documents necessary for the proper conduct of the business in the College. However, in recurring undertakings and transactions which have been fixed in accordance with the College's existing regulations and general laws of the land, he/she may direct through appropriate written instructions that approval in specified cases be made in his/her behalf by the officers of administration or heads of college's offices or units, subject to such safeguards as he/she may impose.

He/She shall have general responsibility over the enforcement of discipline in the College and maintenance of satisfactory academic standards in all program units.

He/She may disapprove or suggest modification on any action or resolution of any college faculty or administrative section or both, if in his judgment, the general interest of the College so requires. In the exercise of such authority, the President shall communicate in writing his/her decision to the body concerned, stating the reasons for his action. Thereafter, he/she shall accordingly inform the Board of Trustees.

He/She may invite, from time to time, eminent scholars and other persons who are experts in their chosen fields to conduct seminars or a series thereof. He/She may authorize the payment of honoraria for such service in accordance with existing policies.

He/She shall have such other powers as elsewhere provided by this Ordinance and CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) 32, Series of 2006, or as may be authorized by the Board. He/She may delegate in writing his/her specific functions to any other officer of the College.

He/She shall create or abolish departments or offices serving other production-related functions, each to be headed by a Program Head or Director, whom he shall appoint or designate and confirmed by the Board.

He/She shall be authorized, in case of his/her absence, to designate a ranking officer of the administration to act as Officer-in-Charge of the Office of the President, who shall carry out the management of the College affairs in his/her name according to his/her instructions and the policies of the Board of Trustees.

2. The Vice President

A. The Vice President for Academic Affairs (VPAA) shall be appointed/designated by the President and confirmed by the BOT. He shall be directly responsible to the President for carrying out all education policies, programs, and projects of the College and for supervising curricular, instructional, and other academic activities of the College. He/She shall preside in meetings of the Academic Council in the absence of the President. The duties and responsibilities of the VPAA are the following:

1. Assist the President in the day-to-day operation of the College, particularly, in the management and/or supervision of Heads of the Academic Program Units (APUs), Library Services, Student Academic Records, and other academic functions;

2. Advise the President on matters pertaining to academic affairs of the College;

3. Evaluate work performance of all Heads of APUs, Chief Librarian, Registrar, and any other Head of Office under his/her supervision and recommend to the President appropriate personnel action;

4. Sign as recommending approval, and/or initial on requisitions, vouchers, payrolls, travel requests, leaves, appointments, and designations, contracts, memoranda of agreement, and sign daily time records, basic communications, and similar papers and certified expenditures/expenses incurred by his/her department or office;

5. Evaluate and recommend development plans or projects submitted by APU Heads, Registrar, Librarian and other offices under higher supervision for inclusion in the annual institutional budget proposal;

6. Formulate and submit to the President for approval of the Board of Trustees, the guidelines and policies relative to academic programs and other activities of the College;

7. Review and submit to the President Institutional program action plans and other similar reports;

8. Review and submit to the President reports of accomplishments of every APUs;

9. Review the faculty profile of every APU and recommend action plan for faculty development and evaluate credentials of faculty members recommended for attendance to seminars, trainings and conferences;

10. Review the curricular programs and see to it that all general education courses offered are equivalent in description and credit units;
11. Review the faculty loading, faculty productivity and student-faculty ratio;
12. Sign as recommending approval on the hiring of contractual and/or emergency personnel and part-time instructors;
13. Coordinate with other officials of the College in order to achieve the institution's academic goals;
14. Act as Chairman ex-officio of all special academic committees of the College; and
15. Perform such other duties and functions that may be assigned by the President from time to time.

B. The Vice President for Administration (VPA) shall be appointed by the President and confirmed by the Board of Trustees. 2. Advise the President on matters pertaining to administrative, financial management and external affairs of the College;

5. Prepare and recommend to the President for approval of the Board the guidelines and policy proposals relative to administrative, student support services, and financial matters;
6. Prepare and recommend systems and procedures for the smooth coordination among offices and services and for the effective and efficient performance of different tasks and functions;

7. Perform such other functions that may be assigned to him/her by the President.

C. The Vice President for Research & Extension (VPRE) shall be appointed by the President and confirmed by the BOT. He/She shall be directly responsible to the President for research and extension and other development projects. The duties and responsibilities of the VPRE are the general supervision and coordination of all units under his/her office. These include the following:

1. Assist the President in the day-to-day operation of the College particularly in the management and/or supervision of all Research and Extension Services Initiatives.

2. Advise the President on matters pertaining to research and development and extension services of the College;

3. Evaluate work performance of all Coordinators under his/her supervision and recommend to the President appropriate personnel action;

4. Sign as recommending approval on all requests, vouchers, payrolls, travels, leaves, appointments and designations, contracts, memoranda of agreement and sign daily time records, basic communications, and similar papers and certified expenditures or expenses incurred by his/her department or office;

5. Prepare and recommend to the President, for approval of the Board of Trustees, the guidelines and policy proposals relative to research and extension matters;

6. Prepare and recommend systems and procedures for the smooth coordination among units/divisions and for the effective and efficient performance of different tasks and functions;

7. Perform such other functions that may be assigned to him/her by the President.

The Term of Office of the VPAA, VPA and VPRE shall be co-terminus with that of the President without prejudice to the revocation before the expiration of their respective terms.

3. The Secretary of the College

The Secretary of the College shall be appointed by the President and confirmed by the Board of Trustees. He/She shall act as the College Secretary.

The College President, however, upon his discretion, may appoint a personal secretary as a member of his/her personal staff.

He/She shall also be the Board Secretary and shall keep all the records and the minutes of the proceedings of the meetings of the Board of Trustees and shall communicate to the Chairperson, Vice Chairperson and members of the Board of Trustees notice of all regular meetings, special sessions and other undertakings of the Board.

**SECTION 2. SUSPENSION AND REMOVAL -** The aforementioned officers may be suspended or removed from office by a majority vote of the Board of Trustees for incapacity, incompetence, dishonesty, conviction of a crime involving moral turpitude or any act detrimental to the College, in accordance with applicable laws.

**ARTICLE IX - FACULTY**

**SECTION 1. COMPOSITION -** The Faculty of the City College of Davao (CCD) shall be composed of Deans, Professors, Instructors, Regular Lecturers or the Teaching Personnel of CCD. The Deans shall be the heads of the various colleges, who shall be appointed by the Board of Trustees, from among the nominees of the President of the CCD.

**SECTION 2. THE MEMBERS OF THE FACULTY -** The Members of the Faculty of the College shall be subject and/or covered by CHED and Civil Service laws, rules and regulations.

**ARTICLE X - NON-TEACHING PERSONNEL**

**SECTION 1. COMPOSITION -** The non-teaching personnel shall be composed of the City College of Davao employees performing non-teaching functions or those not included in the enumeration under Section 1, Article VIII hereof.

**ARTICLE XI - STUDENT**

**SECTION 1. STUDENT ADMISSION -** The City College of Davao shall give preference in the admission of student to bona fide residents of Davao City, graduates of ALS, Senior High School, Late High School Program (SHS LAP), IPs, subject to the qualifications/rules of admissions as may be determined by the Board of Trustees; provided, however, that no students shall be denied admission to the College by reason of age, sexuality, nationality, religious belief or political affiliation.

**ARTICLE XII - COLLEGE COUNCIL**

**SECTION 1. ACADEMIC COUNCIL -** There shall be an Academic Council with the President of the College as Chairperson and all members of the instructional staff with the rank of not lower than Assistant Professors as members (Republic Act No. 8252 Sec. 10).

The Academic Council shall have the power to review and recommend curricular offerings and rules of discipline of the college subject to the approval of the Board of Trustees. It shall set the requirements for admission of students as well as for graduation and the conferment of degrees subject to review and/or approval of the Board of Trustees, through the College President.

**SECTION 2. ADMINISTRATIVE COUNCIL -** There shall be an Administrative Council consisting of the President as Chairperson, the Vice-Presidents, Program Heads and Directors as members.

The Administrative Council shall review and recommend to the Board of Trustees policies governing the administration, management, and development planning of the college subject to review and/or approval by the Board of Trustees, through the College President.

**ARTICLE XIV - MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS**

**SECTION 1. INTERIM BOARD OF TRUSTEES -**

The Mayor shall constitute an Interim Board of Trustees on its initial operation, which shall carry out the powers and duties as stipulated in Article VI of this Ordinance.

The Interim Board of Trustees shall be composed of the following:

1. The City Mayor as the Chairperson;

2. The First College President;

--- [Page 17] ---
3. The Regional Director of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED)- Region XI;
4. The Chairperson of the Committee on Education, Science and Technology, Arts and Culture of the Sangguniang Panlungsod;
5. Representative from the Education Sector; and
6. The Indigenous People's Mandatory Representative (IPMR) of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
The Interim Board of Trustees, however, shall be deemed automatically dissolved once the regular Board of Trustees has been duly constituted.

SECTION 2. APPOINTMENT OF THE FIRST COLLEGE PRESIDENT - The City Mayor, as the Local Chief Executive, may elect to appoint a College President who is deemed competent and qualified to manage the operation of the City College with the concurrence of the Interim Board of Trustees.

SECTION 3. BOARD OF VISITORS - The City Mayor, Vice-Mayor and the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod ng Davao shall constitute the Board of Visitors of the City College of Davao with the following duties and functions:
1. To visit the College at any time they may deem proper;
2. To inspect and evaluate the Book of Accounts, properties, program of study, discipline and state of finances of the College;
3. To make reports and recommendations to the Sangguniang Panlungsod relative to the exercise of its duties and functions.

SECTION 4. REPORTS - The Board of Trustees shall file with the Commission, the City Mayor and the Sangguniang Panlungsod, an annual detailed report of the progress, condition and needs of the College.

SECTION 5. SUPERVISION AND REGULATION - The Commission on Higher Education (CHED) through its duly authorized representatives shall exercise reasonable supervision and regulation over the College in accordance with existing rules and regulations.

SECTION 6. FUNDING - The City Government of Davao shall upon determination of the required funds, appropriate the same to fund the first year of operation of the City College.

The City Government of Davao shall continuously appropriate funds for the operation and maintenance of the College, in addition to the miscellaneous fees which may be, from time to time, determined by the Board of Trustees, provided, however, that any or all funds allotted and accruing to the College shall be accounted under its name and account, and provided further, that any surplus or unappropriated amount at the end of each year shall remain with the coffers of the City College of Davao.
