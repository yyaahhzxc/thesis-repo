Ordinance No. 0697-24
Series of 2024

Enacting an Ordinance for the Temporary Closure to Vehicular Traffic, Particularly at the Davao City Coastal Road, from Crossing Bago Aplata to Crossing Tulip Drive every Sunday from 4:00 AM to 8:00 AM, starting on November 30, 2024, in connection with the Letter Request of Mr. Michael Denton P. Apurtadera, Officer-in-Charge, Davao City Sports Development Division, pertaining to the proposed Physical Fitness Program dubbed as "Car Free Sunday"

Ord No. 0697-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC, PARTICULARLY AT THE DAVAO CITY COASTAL ROAD, FROM CROSSING BAGO APLAYA TO CROSSING TULIP DRIVE EVERY SUNDAY FROM 4:00 AM TO 8:00 AM, STARTING ON NOVEMBER 30, 2024, IN CONNECTION WITH THE LETTER-REQUEST OF MR. MICHAEL DENTON P. APORTADERA, OFFICER-IN-CHARGE, DAVAO CITY SPORTS DEVELOPMENT DIVISION, PERTAINING TO THE PROPOSED PHYSICAL FITNESS PROGRAM DUBBED AS "CAR FREE SUNDAY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21 (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that: "any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fair."

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In connection with the proposed fitness program of the Davao City Sports Development Division, Mr. Michael Denton P. Aportadera requested for the temporary road closure of the Davao City Coastal Road from Crossing Bago Aplaya to Crossing Tulip Drive every Sunday from 4:00 am to 8:00 am, starting on November 30, 2024, for the said purpose.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of November 2024, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
