**RESOLUTION NO. 272**

**2nd Indorsement of the Committee on Laws and Ordinances, dated July 29, 1986, recommending the passage of an Ordinance amending Section 4 of the City Code, which provides as follows: "An Ordinance penalizing acts of littering, scattering or any careless disposal of waste materials, refuse and other insanitary substances in public places, in the City of Davao, and in public places in the City of Davao", particularly Sec. 4 thereof, by increasing the penalties for violations as provided, viz:**

For the first offense, a fine of from P2.00 to P50.00;

For each succeeding offense, a fine of from P5.00 to P100.00, but not more than P200.00, at the discretion of the Court.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councilor Nicanor A. Magno, July seconded jointly by Councilors Luis

RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an
Ordinance, entitled:
ORDINANCE NO. 105
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 98, SERIES OF 1957, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS AN ORDINANCE PENALIZING ACTS OF LITTERING, SCATTERING OR CARELESS DISPOSAL OF WASTE MATERIALS, REFUSE, AND OTHER INSANITARY THINGS IN STREET, PARKS, PUBLIC BUILDINGS AND OTHER PUBLIC PLACES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.
Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, that:
SECTION 1.- Section 4 of Ordinance No. 98, series of 1957, is hereby amended, the same to read as follows:
"Sec. 4.- Violations of this Ordinance shall be penalized by a fine of P50.00 for the first offense and a fine of not less than P100.00 for each succeeding offenses, but not more than P200.00, at the discretion of the Court."
SEC. 2.- Ordinance No. 98, series of 1957, is hereby amended accordingly to this effect;
SEC. 3.- This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
ENACTED: July 25, 1986.
