AN ORDINANCE ON THE COLLECTION OF CORRESPONDING
BARANGAY CLEARANCE FEE IN THE APPLICATION FOR ANY
BUSINESS-RELATED TRANSACTION

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0240-20

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ON THE COLLECTION OF CORRESPONDING BARANGAY CLEARANCE FEE IN THE APPLICATION FOR ANY BUSINESS-RELATED TRANSACTION".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: It is the policy of the City of Davao to establish effective practices aimed at the efficient turnaround in the delivery of government services, and expedite business and activity related transactions by integrating and institutionalizing simplified requirements and procedures in the application, issuance, and collection of barangay clearance and permit related to doing business in the City Government of Davao.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY: Section 11(f) of Republic Act No. 11032, otherwise known as the "Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018", requires that barangay clearances and permits related to doing business shall be applied, issued, and collected by the city, provided that, the share in the collections shall be remitted to the respective barangays.

SECTION 4. ASSESSMENT AND ISSUANCE OF BARANGAY CLEARANCE AND PERMITS-RELATED TO DOING BUSINESS: The corresponding Barangay Clearance and Permit related to doing business shall be assessed, collected, and issued by the City through the City Treasurer's Office and Business Bureau.

SECTION 5. COST OF BARANGAY CLEARANCE AND PERMIT RELATED TO DOING BUSINESS: The amount assessed and collected by the City shall be uniform for all Barangays in the amount of One Hundred Pesos (P100.00).

SECTION 6. REPORTING REMITTANCE: The City of Davao shall remit the Barangay Clearance Fee collected on behalf of the subject Barangay not later than seven (7) working days of the ensuing month with the list of the issued barangay clearances or permits.

SECTION 7. INFORMATION DISSEMINATION: Aside from the publication of the Ordinance in a newspaper of general circulation, all Barangays within the City of Davao shall contribute in disseminating public information through posters, tabulins, or streamers to be placed in at least three (3) conspicuous places within their respective areas of jurisdiction.

SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED on the 10th day of March 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
