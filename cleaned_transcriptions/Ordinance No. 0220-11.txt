ORDINANCE NO. 0220-11 Series of 2011
AN ORDINANCE CREATING A PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND FROM THE PROCEEDS OF THE OUTPATIENT CONSULTATION AND DIAGNOSTIC BENEFIT PACKAGE TO BE PROVIDED BY THE PHILIPPINE HEALTH INSURANCE CORPORATION (PhilHealth) FOR THE QUALIFIED INDIGENT FAMILIES OF THE CITY OF DAVAO UNDER THE INDIGENT SECTOR COMPONENT OF THE NATIONAL HEALTH INSURANCE PROGRAM (NHIP)

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. 0220-11

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as the "PhilHealth Capitation Fund Ordinance of Davao City."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. Pursuant to the constitutional mandate that the State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development, it is this City's policy to endeavor in making essential goods, health, and other services available to all people at affordable cost, especially to the underprivileged, sick, elderly, disabled, women, and children.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE. This Ordinance shall cover all PhilHealth accredited Health Centers and/or Rural Health Units in Davao City.

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY. The creation of a PhilHealth Capitation Fund from the proceeds of the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Benefit Package to be provided by the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) for the qualified indigent families of the City of Davao under the Indigent Sector Component of the National Health Insurance Program is hereby authorized.

SECTION 5. DEFINITION OF TERMS. The words and phrases used in this Ordinance shall mean as follows:

a. Capitation - refers to the scheme or form of paying the Rural Health Unit (RHU) for specific services, payments for a particular period will be made based on a predetermined fee schedule. In case of condition that any savings from the agreed amount accrues to the benefit of the RHU and that in case of insufficiency thereof, the particular services due to the covered beneficiaries shall be delivered at the cost of the RHU;

b. Health Center and/or Rural Health Unit - refers to the health unit/s or health center/s owned, administered, managed and financed by the local government unit which is either attached to or directly supervised by the City/Municipal Health Office. Health Centers and rural health units are used alternately and interchangeably;

c. Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package - refers to the diagnostic and primary consultation services which shall be included in the Program's benefit package and shall be administered, managed and/or delivered by Rural Health Units (RHUs);

d. Philippine National Drug Formulary (PNDF) - refers to the essential drugs list for the Philippines which is prepared by the National Drug Committee of the Department of Health (DOH) in consultation with experts and specialists from organized professional medical societies, the academic and the pharmaceutical industry, which is updated every year;

**SECTION 6. PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND.** The use, management, and disposition of the PhilHealth Capitation Funds (PCF) shall be governed by the following:

a. The PCF shall be released upon prior accreditation of identified Davao City Health Centers and the payment of corresponding premium contributions;

b. The disbursement and liquidation of the PCF shall be in accordance with pertinent government accounting and auditing rules and regulations;

c. A separate book of accounts shall be maintained by the local government unit;

d. The capitation fund shall be used only for the specified purposes stipulated in the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines as prescribed by PhilHealth;

e. Only drugs listed in the Primary Medical Care Drugs of the Philippine National Drugs Formulary (PNDF), medical supplies and equipment necessary to carry out the delivery of the required services, referral fees and provision for administrative cost not exceeding twenty percent (20%) of the PCF may be charged to the fund;

f. The twenty percent (20%) Administrative cost shall be divided among the health personnel of the Health Center and/or Rural Health Unit, fifty percent (50%) of which shall accrue to the physician/s while the remaining fifty percent (50%) to the other health staff.

**SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE.** Any existing Ordinance or any provision thereof inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed.

**SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.** If for any reason, any part or provision of this Ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, the other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE.** This Ordinance shall take effect within thirty (30) days from its publication in a newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ERECTED: December 13, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
PHILIPPINE HEALTH INSURANCE CORPORATION (PhilHealth)
and
THE CITY OF DAVAO

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Agreement made and entered into by and among:
The PHILIPPINE HEALTH INSURANCE CORPORATION, a government corporation organized and existing by virtue of Republic Act 7875, as amended by Republic Act 9241, otherwise known as the National Health Insurance Act of 1995, duly represented herein by its Regional Vice President, Dennis B. Adre and hereinafter referred to as PhilHealth or the Corporation;
and
The CITY OF DAVAO, a political entity organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, represented herein by its Honorable Mayor Sara Z. Duterte-Carpio and hereinafter referred to as the CITY.

WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the Philippine Constitution mandates the State to adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development, which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health and social services available to all citizens at affordable cost;
WHEREAS, the National Health Insurance Act of 1995, in recognition of the need to provide all citizens with the mechanism to gain financial access to health services, created the National Health Insurance Program (NHIP) to serve as the means to help the people pay for the same;
WHEREAS, consistent with the vision and objectives of the State, as manifested in NHIP's mandate and in local autonomy statutes, the parties hereto acknowledge their joint responsibility to provide a basic package of needed personal health services for the social and economic well-being of the people of Davao City, specifically the marginalized and underprivileged, through the implementation of the Sponsored (Injutent) Program;
WHEREAS, in order to set into motion an efficient health care financing and delivery program anchored on local government autonomy, the contracting parties hereby acknowledge the need to adopt separate mechanisms for the implementation of NHIP's Regular In-Patient and Outpatient Package (Phase I) and the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package (Phase II);
WHEREAS, the parties hereto agree and declare their commitment to take the necessary steps to implement the principles and objectives provided for in this Agreement.
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto have agreed, as they hereby agree and bind themselves, as follows:

Article I
DEFINITION OF TERMS
SECTION 1. For the purpose of this Agreement, the terms set below shall have their meanings indicated, to wit:
1.1 National Health Insurance Program (NHIP) - refers to the compulsory health insurance program of the government instituted under Republic Act No. 7875 which aims to implement a universal health insurance system and provide universal access to affordable, acceptable,

--- [Page 6] ---
efficient and effective healthcare services for all citizens of the Philippines. NHIP is alternatively known as the Medicare Program, established under Republic Act 6111.
1.2 Sponsored (Indigent) Program - refers to the Indigent Sector Component of NHIP. It is the tool by which the State envisages to provide Medicare privileges to the indigent population as a supplement to its Medicare Program for the employed and individually paying sectors.
1.3 Health Center and/or Rural Health Unit (RHU) - refers to the health unit/s or health center/s owned, administered, managed and financed by the local government unit, which is either attached to or directly supervised by the Office of the City Health Officer. Health centers and rural health units shall be accredited by the Department of Health.
1.4 In-Patient & Outpatient Package (Phase I) - refers to the Medicare package covering hospital benefits for room and board, medicines, laboratory and professional fees, including existing outlying benefits under the unified benefit schedule different from those provided by an accredited Rural Health Unit (RHU).
1.5 Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package (Phase II) - refers to the primary consultation and diagnostic services which are included in the Program's benefit package and administered, managed and/or delivered by accredited RHUs/Health Centers.
1.6 Capitation - refers to the scheme or form of paying the RHU for specific services it provides for a particular period which is arrived at based on a pre-determined criteria under the condition that any savings from the agreed amount accrues to the benefit of the facility and that in case of insufficiency thereof, the particular services due to the covered beneficiaries shall be delivered at the cost of the RHU.
1.7 Philippine National Drug Formulary (PNDF) - refers to the essential drugs list for the Philippines which is prepared by the National Drug Committee of the Department of Health (DOH) in consultation with experts and specialists from organized professional medical societies, the academe and the pharmaceutical industry, and which is updated every year.
Article II
UNDERTAKINGS OF PHILHEALTH
SECTION 2. The Corporation shall perform, with due diligence and efficiency, the following undertakings:
2.1 It shall collect funds from the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) to finance the national government premium subsidy for qualified beneficiaries enrolled in the Program, who are residing within the respective jurisdictions of the LGUs herein represented.
2.2 It shall assist in the activities leading to the enrollment of Program beneficiaries based on the prescribed Philhealth tool.
2.3 It shall assist in the conduct of public information campaign as provided for in Article 3, Section 3.ub-section 3.3 of this Agreement.
2.4 It shall facilitate the accreditation of public health institutions and facilities under the jurisdiction of the local government units herein represented, in accordance with the accreditation standards set forth in RA 7875 as amended by RA 9241 and its Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations.
2.5 It shall ensure the prompt disbursement of funds for the payment of approved claims under the In-Patient Program of the Program Phase I filed by the accredited health care providers or directly by the duly qualified beneficiaries. Provided, however, that it shall be liable to pay only up to the prescribed limit of benefits.

--- [Page 7] ---
2.6 It shall assist in the annual updating of the status of membership of current members by reasons of transfer of residence, death, change in economic and civil status and/or coverage under NHHIP in any of its membership categories.
2.7 It shall collect from the Local Government Units herein represented the latter's counterparty annual premium contribution in consonance with Article III, Section 3, Sub-section 3.2 hereof, and apply the same for the provision of benefits for qualified beneficiaries.
2.8 For the purpose of implementing the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package, the Corporation shall:
2.8.1 Facilitate accreditation of Rural Health Units/Health Centers/District Hospitals/other facilities in accordance with PhilHealth's accreditation guidelines;
2.8.2 Release the capitation amount to the accredited OPH Providers, through the Local Government Unit herein represented, in accordance with the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines, copy of which is hereto attached as "Annex A" and shall form an integral part hereof;
2.8.3 Monitor the implementation of the benefit package, consistent with the objectives of the NHHIP, in coordination with the LGUs herein represented; and
2.8.4 Provide technical and other support services for the implementation and administration of the Outpatient Consultation Diagnostic Package.
Article III
UNDERTAKINGS OF THE CITY
SECTION 3. The City shall perform, with due diligence and efficiency, the following undertakings:
3.1 It shall, through the Sangguniang Panlungsod, enact appropriate Ordinance/Resolution for the following purposes:
3.1.1 Adoption of and/or continued support to the National Health Insurance Program and its governing rules as set forth in RA 7852 as amended by RA 9241 and its Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations. Such support shall include advocacy for NHHIP participation by private and non-government organizations within its territorial jurisdiction, mandating compliance to NHHIP rules by entities required to obtain LGU permits/licenses, and the setting-up of revolving funds for medicines in owned or managed hospitals, as may be warranted under existing rules/guidelines or similar future arrangements and/or NHHIP issuances;
3.1.2 Grant of authority to the City Mayor to enter into an Agreement and/or impose favorable modifications, revisions and/or amendments thereto;
3.1.3 Enrollment of the targeted number of indigent families identified from the National Household Targeting System for Poverty Reduction as validated by PhilHealth, City Social Services and Development Office, Department of Social Welfare and Development;
3.2 It shall assist in the conduct of public information campaign aimed at apprising its constituents on the policies, rules and regulation relative to Program benefits, avalliment procedures, and their corresponding rights and obligations under the same.
3.3 For the purpose of implementing the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package, the City shall:
3.3.1 Pass an Ordinance to set up a PhilHealth Capitation Fund for the capitation amount of THREE HUNDRED PESOS (P300.00) per family with paid premium.

--- [Page 8] ---
released by the Corporation to the City through the passage of an ordinance in accordance with the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines and the relevant provisions of Article VII hereof;
3.3.2 Oversee and supervise the implementation of the package through its RPH/Health Centers/ accredited providers and the administration of the 20% corresponding capitalization fund related;
3.3.3 Upgrade or enhance the administrative and operational capabilities of its local health facilities to conform with PhilHealth's accreditation standards;
3.3.4 Ensure continued delivery of the services to the Program beneficiaries; and
3.3.5 Submit to the Corporation or its instrumentalities such reports as may be required for the purpose of monitoring/evaluation, research and program development.
3.3.6 It shall continuously strive for the expansion of Program coverage within its jurisdiction.

SECTION 5. The City shall remit to PhilHealth its payment counterpart subject to the terms and conditions stipulated in Article IV of this Agreement.
Article VI OUTPATIENT CONSULTATION AND DIAGNOSTIC PACKAGE (Phase II)
SECTION 6. The Program shall provide the following benefits, referred to herein as Program Phase II, which shall be administered by the Rural Health Units and/or Health Centers in accordance with PhilHealth's accreditation standards and Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines.
6.1 Primary Consults with generalphysicians; and

6.2 Laboratory fees for:
6.2.1 Chest X-ray;
6.2.2 Complete Blood Count;
6.2.3 Fecalysis;
6.2.4 Urinalysis;
and
6.2.5 Sputum Microscopy.

In the event that the Rural Health Unit is not authorized to provide the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package based on the set standards for accreditation, the transitory provisions in the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines shall take effect until otherwise becomes inapplicable.
Article VII
CREATION OF PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND
SECTION 7. A PhilHealth Capitation Fund (PCF) set up as provided for under 2.8 and 4.6 shall be released in accordance with the following, viz:
7.1 Initial release shall be subject to prior accreditation of the Rural Health Unit and the payment of premium contribution by the Local Government Units (LGUs);
7.2 The capitation amount shall be released by the Corporation to appropriate entities based on the mode of payment of the Local Government Units (LGUs);
7.3 Succeeding release of the capitation shall be based on the mode of payment of the Local Government Units (LGUs) and the submission of required monitoring/evaluation reports and payment of premium; the frequency of the submission of monitoring/evaluation reports shall be based on the mode of payment of the Local Government Unit (LGU);
7.4 The Corporation shall withhold the release of the subsequent PCFs due to any of the following:
7.4.1 Violation of government accounting and auditing rules and regulations on the disbursement and liquidation of the PCF;
and
7.4.2 Non-submission of the required reports under Subsection 7.3 hereof.
Article VIII
DISPOSITION OF THE PHILHEALTH CAPITATION FUND
SECTION 8. The use, management, and disposition of the PCF shall be governed by the following rules:
8.1 The disbursement and liquidation of the PCF shall be in accordance with pertinent government accounting and auditing rules and regulations and to be audited accordingly by the local COA Auditors;
8.2 A separate book of accounts shall be maintained by the City for the purpose;
8.3 The following may be charged to PCF: OLD MOA

--- [Page 10] ---
8.3.1 All drugs listed in the Philippine National Drug Formulary (PNDF);
8.3.2 Equipment and supplies necessary/related to carry out the delivery of required services including but not limited to medical equipment and supplies, furniture and fixtures, appliances, office equipment, consumables and supplies;
8.3.3 Facility improvement of health facility;
8.3.4 Referral fees for diagnostic services;
and
8.3.5 Administrative cost equivalent to twenty percent (20%) of the total capitation amount.
8.4 The Corporation through its Accreditation Department shall approve the acquisition and usage of new medical technological equipment, devices and modalities of treatment consistent with actual needs and current standards of medical practice and ethics with national health objectives after assessing the advantage and appropriateness of such purchase.
8.5 The twenty percent (20%) mandatory provision for administrative cost shall be released to the Cities and Municipalities with accredited RHUs/Health Centers. It shall be divided as follows:
8.5.1 Fifty percent (50%) of the administrative cost shall accrue to the physician/s;
8.5.2 Twenty five percent (25%) shall accrue to the other RHU personnel directly involved in the provision of services required hereof; and
8.5.3 The remaining twenty five (25%) shall be utilized for other administrative cost such as office supplies, transportation expenses, etc.
8.6 The twenty percent (20%) mandatory provision for administrative cost, which shall be released to the City with accredited RHUs/Health Center, shall be treated as Trust Fund by the LGU;
8.6.1 Eighty percent (80%) of the total capitation fund shall be held in trust by the City in accordance with the following:
8.6.1 The amount shall be utilized for bulk procurement of drugs and medicines listed in the PNDF and medical equipment and supplies based on the need assessment / requisition issue voucher (RV) submitted by the accredited RHUs/Health Centers to the City Government;
8.6.2 PNDF drugs and medicines purchased under the preceding section shall be released to the RHUs/Health Centers by the City as may be deemed necessary, preferably on a regular basis;
8.6.3 For accounting purposes, the price of drugs, medicines, equipment and supplies charged against the PCF of the City or Municipality shall be at its actual cost or winning bid price;
Article IX
PROVISION FOR PAYMENT OF PREMIUM CONTRIBUTION AND LATE REMITTANCES
SECTION 9. The corporation shall be guided accordingly by the following provisions pertinent to premium contribution payments and late remittances, viz:

--- [Page 11] ---
9.1 Payments of premium shall be remitted to PhilHealth (quarterly, semi-annually, or annually). The Province and/or the participating City shall remit its payment prior to the issuance of the PhilHealth ID or renewal of membership.
9.2 For late remittances, the City hereby grants a grace period of thirty (30) calendar days from the date the payment is due within which to remit its premium contribution in accordance with Article IV, Section 4, Subsection A, hereof, without thereby incurring penalty as specified in the Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations of RA 7875 as amended by RA 9241.
The corporation shall cause the non-renewal of membership, in the event of non-payment of premium contribution of the delinquent party, when due and demandable.
Article X REFERRAL SYSTEM
SECTION 10. In case of inability by the Real Health Unit to deliver the required Chest X-ray service, the City shall establish a referral system in accordance with PhilHealth's accreditation standards and Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines.
Article XI PORTABILITY OF BENEFITS UNDER PHASE I
SECTION 11. The indigent members and their beneficiaries may avail of the benefits provided for under the In-Patient and Out Patient Package from any health care provider duly accredited by Philhealth.
Article XII MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
SECTION 12. The operationalization of the Program shall be pursued through the collaboration of the parties under the following circumstances:
12.1 The parties agree that the benefits under Phase I shall commence on the effectiveness date exhibited on the Sponsored Program IDs distributed to indigent beneficiaries.
12.2 The parties agree that the implementation of Program Phase II shall commence immediately on the Calendar Quarter following the compliance to all attendant requirements and procedures as set forth in the Outpatient Consultation and Diagnostic Package Guidelines (Phase I).
12.3 The parties agree to continuously strive to improve its systems and procedures accordingly by enhancing, developing, and modifying its policies to be more responsive to the needs of NHI indigent members residing within the territorial jurisdiction of the LGUs herein represented.
ARTICLE XIII EFFECTIVITY
SECTION 13. The collaboration of the parties under the Agreement shall take effect the 1st day of October, 2011, and shall continue to be effective for as long as there are qualified indigent members residing within the territorial jurisdiction of the City, provided that the City Government has budget for the same, subject to the provisions of Article IV, Section 4 and Article XII hereof.
Article XIV REVISION OR AMENDMENT
Section 14 Any revision or amendment of this Agreement shall require the written consent of all parties hereto.
