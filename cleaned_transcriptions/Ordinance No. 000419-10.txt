Ordinance No. 0419-10
Series of 2010
An Ordinance Prohibiting the Nailing,
Posting, Hanging of Streamers, Posters
and Advertisements on Trees in Davao City

Ebat Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AV
ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE NAILING, POSTING, HANGING OF
STREAMERS, POSTERS AND ADVERTISEMENTS ON TREES IN DAVAO CITY".
SECTION 2 - DECLARATION OF POLICY. Article II, Section 16 of
the 1987 Constitution declares that the State shall protect and advance the
right of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with the
rhythm and harmony of nature. It is likewise the policy of the City of Davao
to protect and conserve growing trees and maintain the cleanliness and
beautification that the City is known for.
SECTION 3 - SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY. The provisions of this
Ordinance shall apply to all acts of nailing, posting, pasting, hanging or
attaching of any and all forms of streamers, posters, banners and other
advertising materials on trees and species of similar nature in the City of
Davao.
SECTION 4 - DEFINITION OF TERMS.
(a) Tree - is a perennial woody plant. It is most often defined as a
woody plant that has many secondary branches supported clear of
the ground on a single main stem or trunk;
(b) Poster - is an eye-catching printed paper announcement or
advertisement which is exhibited to publicize or promote a product,
event or idea;
(c) Streamer - a long narrow flag, banner or pennant and may be made
of cloth or tarpsulim;
(d) Banner - display poster designed to look like a flag, with an
imprinted advertising message.
(e) Other Advertising Materials - directs attention to a business,
profession, services or entertainment rendered, or to a commodity
sold or offered for public/private use;
SECTION 5 - PROHIBITED ACTS. It shall be prohibited to nail,
post, paste, hang or attach any streamer, poster, banner or any other
advertising material on trees and species of similar nature as defined in
Section 4, paragraph (a) found in parks, plazas, public roads, streets and
boulevards or any other public grounds in the territory and within the
jurisdiction of the City of Davao;

SECTION 6 - PERSONS LIABLE.
The following persons or individuals shall be deemed liable under this Ordinance:
a. Any one who actually and directly nails, hangs, places, attaches or posts the streamers, posters or advertising materials in trees, arches, similar nature found in the parks, plazas, public roads, streets and sidewalks or any other public grounds; and/or
b. Any person, association, organization or corporation that authorized or paid for the mailing, hanging or posting of such streamers, posters or other advertising materials. Furthermore, the name of the person, group, association, organization or corporation that authorized and/or paid for the mailing, hanging or posting of such advertising materials shall be prima facie evidence that they authorized and/or paid for the mailing, hanging or posting.

SECTION 7. - FINES AND PERTALIES.
Any violation of this Ordinance shall, upon conviction, subject the individual, group, association or organization to a fine and/or penalty of:
For the first offense: Phip 1,000.00 to Phip 2,000.00
For the second offense: Phip 3,000.00 to Phip 5,000.00
For the third offense: Phip 5,000.00 and imprisonment of not more than one (1) month or both at the discretion of the court.

SECTION 8. - SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.
If for any reason/s any provision of this Ordinance is rendered invalid or unconstitutional, such provisions not affected by such declaration shall remain to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 9. - REPEALING CLAUSE.
Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby modified or repealed accordingly.

SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY.
This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and compliance with the pertinent provisions of the Local Government Code on this regard.

ENACTED, May 10, 2010, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
