**and others, duly authorized by Councilors Jaime G. Rodriguez and Juan M. Santos, in the presence of the following members of the Council: Librero V. Lemus, Aristides M. Albuy, Ceasar G. Batilla and Presidente J. Mahloum, it was resolved that as it is hereby resolved, to approve an Ordinance entitled:**

**ORDINANCE No. 51**

**AN ORDINANCE RESERVING THE GENERATION OF VIDEO GAME MACHINES FOR COMMERCIAL PURPOSES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.**

**Rto Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:**

**SECTION 1.- ORDINANCE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE RESERVING THE GENERATION OF VIDEO GAME MACHINES FOR COMMERCIAL PURPOSES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO."**

**SEC. 2.- PERSON - As used in this Ordinance, "Person" shall mean both natural and juridical persons. Any person engaged in the business of offering the use of Video Games like "Family Computer," "Atari," and other similar contrivances to the public for a fee shall be governed by this Ordinance.**

**SEC. 3.- Before any person is allowed to engage in the business of offering the use of Video Games such as, but not limited to "Family Computer," "Atari," and/or other similar Video contrivances to the public for a fee, he shall first secure a permit from the Office of the City Mayor.**

**SEC. 4.- Upon the effectiveness of this Ordinance, no person shall be allowed to establish, maintain and/or operate Video Game Machines for commercial purposes within a distance of "one hundred (100) linear meters" from the gate of any elementary and secondary school. Existing Video Game establishments which are within the "one hundred (100) linear meter" distance from the gate of any Elementary or Secondary School shall be required to transfer their establishments to other places within a period of sixty (60) days from the effectiveness of this ordinance.**

SEC. 1. Children from 6 to 17 years of age shall not be allowed inside Video Game establishments from 7:00 o'clock in the morning to 5:00 o'clock in the afternoon during school days.
SEC. 2. Video Game establishments shall be required to issue admission tickets with serial numbers, indicating therein the name of the establishment and the admission price, the same to be registered with the City Treasurer's Office.
SEC. 3. TAX ON AMUSEMENTS - The City shall collect a tax equivalent to thirty (30%) per cent of the admission price which shall be due and payable within the first twenty (20) days of the following month due the issuance of the Video Game establishments and such tax shall be determined on the basis of a true and complete return of the amount of gross receipts derived during the preceding month. If tax is not paid within the time fixed herewithhere, the taxpayer shall be subject to 25% surcharges and 1.5% interest. In case of willful neglect to file the returns and pay the tax within the time required, or in case fraudulent returns is filed or false returns is willfully made, the taxpayer shall be subject to a penalty of fifty (50) percent of the correct amount of the tax due in addition to the interest and surcharges above mentioned.
SEC. 4. PENALTY - Any person who violates any or all of the provisions of Sections 3, 4, 5 and 6 of this Ordinance shall upon conviction, suffer a mandatory penalty of six months imprisonment or a fine of five thousand ($5,000.00) Pounds or both such fines and imprisonment upon the discretion of the court. Should the offense be committed by a judicial person, the Chairman, President, Director, Auditor or Manager responsible therefor, shall be the persons to be held responsible.
SEC. 5. Ordinance No. 1, Series of 1974, as amended is hereby further amended accordingly to this effect.
