RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an Ordinance entitled:
ORDINANCE NO. 116
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING FISHING AND/OR FISHERIES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.
Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Pambanguedano, City of Davao, that:
SECTION 1.- This Ordinance shall be known as the BASIC FISHERY ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY and shall be enforced in conjunction with other pertinent laws, decrees, orders, rules, and regulations on fishing and/or fisheries which have been promulgated or which may be promulgated regarding the matter;
SEC. 2.- Municipal waters. For the purpose of this Ordinance the words "municipal waters" include not only streams, lakes and tidal waters included within the City of Davao, not being the subject of private ownership and not comprised with the national parks, public forests, timber lands, forest reserves, or fishery reserves, but also marine waters included between two lines drawn perpendicular to the general coastline from point where the boundary lines of the City touch the sea at low tide and a third line parallel with the general coastline and three nautical lines from such coastline. Where the coastline of the City is so situated that there is less than six nautical miles from the opposite coastline which belong to another municipality, the third line shall be a line equidistant to the coastline of the City and the coastline of the opposite shore belonging to the different municipality.
SEC. 3.- License permit, fishery grant. Shall be unlawful for any person, cooperative, partnership, association or corporation to take or catch fish or other aquatic products by means of net, traps, or other fishing gears within the municipal waters of the City of Davao or by means of fishing boats or vessels three tons gross or less provided with the necessary license permit issued for the purpose by the City Treasurer of Davao. It shall be likewise illegal for any person, cooperative, partnership, association or corporation to operate fish corrals oryster culture beds, or take or catch "bangus" fry or fry of other species for propagation within the jurisdiction of the City of Davao without first securing a grant from the City of Davao as provided for by law and this Ordinance.

SEC. 4. Persons eligible for fishing and/or fishery privileges:

The following are, under this Ordinance, eligible for fishing and/or fishery privileges:

a) Citizens of the Philippines

b) Cooperatives, partnerships, associations or corporations duly registered or incorporated under the laws of the Philippines and authorised to transact business in the Philippines and at least sixty per centum (50%) of their stock belongs shallly to citizens of the Philippines.

It shall be unlawful for persons, cooperatives, partnerships, associations or corporations not qualified by law and this Ordinance to engage, personally or through other persons, in fishing, or catching or collecting aquatic products in the municipal waters within the jurisdiction of the City.

SEC. 11. - Committee on Auction A committee on auction is hereby or aided to compose of the City Treasurer, as Chairman, and two members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, as members. In the composition of the Committee on Auction, the office of the City Treasurer should be represented by the Treasurer. Membership in said committee is entirely within the discretion of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

SEC. 12. - Publication of Notice. The Committee on Auction shall advertise notice for sealed bids for exclusive fishery privileges in areas or zones of the municipal waters available for erecting fish corrals, or construction or operating oyster species for propagation by posting said notice in a newspaper published in the locality, if any, for a period of not less than fifteen (15) days.

SEC. 13. - Time and place of Auction. The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall, in a resolution, provide for a notice calling for sealed bids for the exclusive fishery privileges granted under Section 29 (a) of Presidential Decree No. 704, specifying the time and place of the auction, the amount to be deposited before an individual or an entity is entitled to participate, the procedure to be followed by the committees on Auction before any exclusive privilege is granted and the bond as guaranteed of good faith and for satisfactory compliance with the terms of the lease or grant. The bond shall be in cash or in real estate situated within the Philippines or by surety company authorized for the purpose in an amount not less than two years rental.

SEC. 14. - Municipal Concessions and leases concerning fisheries. No lease or concession granted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod under authority of an ordinance approved pursuant to Section 6 of Presidential Decree No. 704 concerning fishing or fisheries in streams, lakes, rivers, inland or municipal waters shall be valid and enforceable unless the Secretary of Natural Resources, upon recommendation of the Director of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources, approves the same.

SEC. 15. - Restriction on baby trawls using fishing boats of three (3) tons gross or less. Baby trawls using fishing boats of three (3) gross tons or less may operate in areas for (4) fathoms deep or more if authorized by existing municipal ordinance duly approved by the Secretary of Natural Resources.

SEC. 17. - Report of this caught. Any individual or entity who has obtained a fishery grant or license to take or catch fish in the municipal waters of the municipality shall submit to the City Treasurer of the municipality wherein he is fishing within ten (10) days for each month a monthly report in triplicate copies showing the kind, quantity and value of fish caught during the month. Provided, that this Sangunang Anayang shall furnish the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources, for statistical purposes on forms which shall be furnished by the Bureau such information and data on fishery matters as are reflected in such forms.

SEC. 20. Penalty. Any individual or entity found violating
any of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be punished for each
offense by a fine not less than two hundred pesos but not exceeding
one thousand pesos, or imprisonment of not less than two days but
not exceeding six months, or both such fine and imprisonment in the
discretion of the court.
SEC. 21. Repeal. Any existing Ordinance and regulations
or parts thereof which are in conflict with this Ordinance are
hereby repealed.
SEC. 22. Effectivity. This Ordinance shall take effect upon
the approval of the Secretary of Natural Resources.
