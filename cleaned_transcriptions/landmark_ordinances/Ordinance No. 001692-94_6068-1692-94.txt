ORDINANCE NO. 1692

AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE CITY FRANCHISING AND REGULATORY BOARD, PRESCRIBING ITS FUNCTIONS AND THE PROCEDURES FOR FRANCHISING AND REGULATING THE OPERATION OF MYSTERIOUS TRICYCLES FOR HIRE AND THE COLLECTION OF FEES AND CHARGES THEREFOR.

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

ARTICLE 1. - TITLE, PURPOSE AND DEFINITION OF TERMS

SECTION 1. - Title. This ordinance shall be called and referred to as the "DAVAO CITY TRICYCLES FOR HIRE (TFH) FRANCHISING AND REGULATORY CODE OF 1994."

SECTION 2. - Purpose and Scope. Pursuant to Section 17 of Republic Act No. 7168, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, the City of Davao shall exercise the devolved powers to grant franchises and regulate the operation of all motorized tricycle-for-hire within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City.

SECTION 3. - Definition of Terms. As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall be understood, construed, and shall mean as follows:

(a) Motorized Tricycle-for-Hire. A motor vehicle composed of a motorcycle fitted with a single wheeled side cab, or with a center cab operated to render services to the general public for a fee;

(b) Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit or MOPR. The document granting franchise or license to operate issued under this Ordinance, to a person, natural or juridical, allowing him/hit to operate motor-cycles-for-hire, over zones specified therein;

(c) Zone. A contiguous land area or block, say a subdivision or a barangay, where a motorcycle-for-hire may operate without a fixed origin and destination;

**ARTICLE II. - DAVAO CITY MTH FRANCHISING AND REGULATORY BOARD**

**SEC. 4. - Creation of the Davao City Franchising and Regulatory Board.** To effectively exercise and perform the devolved powers and functions relative to the administration and control of the operation of motorized tricycles-for-hire, a body called and known as the Davao City Motorized Tricycles for hire Franchising and Regulatory Board is hereby created, and may be referred to as the MTH Board.

**SEC. 5. - Composition of the Board.** The Davao City MTH Franchising and Regulatory Board shall be composed of the following:

a) The Board shall be composed of the following:
1. City Mayor - Chairman
2. City Administrator - Vice Chairman
3. City Legal Officer or Hearing Officer
4. Necessary number of support staff

b) An Advisory Committee should be created to be composed of the following:
1. IDPF-DC (NCO)
2. Student Representative
3. Transmission DC (NCO)

4. Riding Public (representative from the media) whose members shall be appointed by the City Mayor upon recommendation of the concerned group.

c) No honorarium fee shall be given to the Advisory committee.

**SEC. 6. - FUNCTIONS.** The MTH Board shall oversee, administer and regulate the operation of all motorized tricycles-for-hire within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City. In pursuance hereof, it shall exercise administrative supervision and control over the MTH Franchising and MTH Regulatory Division.

SEC. 17.- Posting of Fare Table - The table of fares should be posted prominently at the tricycle terminal and in the conspicuous part of the vehicle, in front of the driver.

ARTICLE IV. - OPERATING CONDITIONS

SEC. 18.- Prohibition - No tricycle should be operated on national highways utilized by 4-wheel vehicles greater than 4 tons and where normal speed exceeds 50 KPH. However, the MTF Regulatory Board may provide exceptions, if there is no other alternative route.

SEC. 19.- Zones of adjoining Jurisdictions - Zones must be within the boundaries of the City. However, existing zones transcending the City boundary shall be maintained; provided that operators serving said zone shall secure the corresponding MTCF.

SEC. 20.- Color for a Zone - A common color for tricycles-for-hire operating in the same zone should be imposed. Each unit shall be assigned and must bear an Identification number, aside from its ITO License Plate Number. The Identification Number shall be printed at the front, left, and right sides of the MTF.

SEC. 21.- Suspension of Stoppage of Service - An operator wishing to stop service completely or to suspend service for more than one month, should report in writing such termination or suspension to the office herein called the City Franchising Division, which office originally granted the MTCF prior thereto. Transfer to another zone may be permitted upon application filed with the City Franchising Division.

SEC. 22.- Life of MTCF - The MTCF shall valid for three (3) years, renewable for the same period. Transfer to another zone, change of ownership or unit or transfer of MTCF shall be construed as an amendment to an MTCF and shall require appropriate approval of the City Franchising Division.

SEC. 23.-- Licensed Driver. - Operator shall employ only drivers duly licensed by LITC for motorized tricycles-for-hire.

SEC. 24.-- Load Limit. - No Motorized tricycle-for-hire shall be allowed to carry more passengers and/or goods than what it is designed for or more than what is authorized in the franchise.

SEC. 25.-- Authority to Operate. - A motorized tricycle-for-hire shall be allowed to operate like a taxi, service, i.e., service is rendered upon demand and without a fixed route within a zone; Provided, that prior authority therefor shall be secured from the MHP Franchising Division and the Office of the City Mayor and the corresponding taxes, fees and charges paid to the City Treasurer.

SEC. 26.-- Routes and Route Markings. (a) All Motorized tricycle-for-hire bear the following route markings:

(1) Route Number. - All motorized tricycle-for-hire shall display route number on the front and rear portion of the vehicle. The lettering of the route number shall be in black and with white background and not less than three (3) inches in length.

(2) Route Marking. - The authorized route line shall be painted on the front and right side of the MHP. The lettering shall be in red with white background and shall not be less than one inch in length.

a) Annual Franchise Fee or Supervisory Fee. - To be collected upon approval and every three (3) years thereafter, on or before the anniversary date of the MHP from all holders of MHP.

SEC. 30. EXTENSION/RENEWAL OF MTCP. Failure to renew MTCP on the date stated in the franchisee or for renewal of a delinquent registration, a surcharge of twenty five (25%) percent of the franchise fee, without prejudice to all lawful fees under this code.

SEC. 31. CONFISCATION OF DRIVER'S LICENSE. The license of the driver of the MTH unit that violates any provision of this code shall be confiscated after a summons or Traffic Citation Ticket (TCT) is issued for any violation hereof and the same shall be submitted to the MTH Franchising Division for disposition or appropriate action.

As used in this Code, a Traffic Citation Ticket (TCT) is a summons or citation in writing issued to a person violating any of the provision of this code of the City, warning him to answer to the charge against him, at a place and a time within fifteen (15) days after his apprehension.

SEC. 32. FORFEITURE OF MTH UNIT. An MTH unit operated in violation of this code or any traffic ordinance of the City will be impounded and can be released only upon payment of the Administrative fines; Provided, however, that in the event the vehicle or the administrative fines shall remain unclaimed or unsettled more than 90 days, the impounded vehicle shall be forfeited in favor of the City Government and there after shall be sold at public auction.

SEC. 33.- PENAL CLAUSE - Any violation any provision of this code shall, upon conviction thereof, be punished by an imprisonment of not more than 12 months or by a fine of $5,000.00 or both such fine and imprisonment, at the discretion of the court; Provided, that if the violator is a corporation, partnership, association, or any other juridical entity, its president, manager, or the officer responsible therefor, shall be penalized accordingly.

SEC. 34.- REVOCATION OR SUSPENSION OF MTQ/DRIVER'S LICENSE. In addition to the penalty heretofore provided, the court may revoke or suspend for a period not to exceed one (1) year, the MTQ or driver's license of the granted/driver convicted of a violation of any section or provision of this code.

SEC. 35.- DISPOSITION OF FINES. All fines/forfeitures collected for the violation of any (Section or) provision of this code shall be paid to the City, through the City Treasurer and deposited under the Special Fund of the City.

SEC. 36.- Special Permits to Travel Outside Routes. Special Permits to Travel Outside Route shall be granted to an MPW upon request, stating therein the date, origin, destination and the purpose of said permit. In no case shall a special permit exceed 3 days from the issuance thereof.

SEC. 37.- Saving Clause - Subject to the provision of the Local Government Code of 1991 (R.A. 7160) and consistent with any future congressional legislation and/or court decision, the City preserves the right to pass or enact legislation to enforce the real intention of the code.

SEC. 38.- Rules and Regulations. The rules and regulations concerning routes, re-routings, and all details of the operation of MTW, and the conditions therefor, not otherwise included in this code, shall be prescribed by the DAVAO CITY MSH FRANCHISING AND RE-LATORY BOARD, and shall form part of this Code.
