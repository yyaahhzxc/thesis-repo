Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTING THE DAVAO CITY COVID-19 EMERGENCY RESPONSE POLICIES

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTING THE DAVAO CITY COVID-19 EMERGENCY RESPONSE POLICIES."

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall be imposed upon all persons, entities and establishments within the City of Davao, until the existing Community Quarantine or State of Public Health Emergency is lifted, or unless any part therein is earlier modified, withdrawn or extended.

SECTION 3. COMMUNITY QUARANTINE - The entire City of Davao is placed under Community Quarantine which shall have the following effects:

1. No person can go out of their residence, dwelling, and its immediate surroundings except those who will buy food or medicine, go to the hospital or in an emergency situation, and except as herein provided;

2. Imposing a social distancing measure of at least two meters between all persons in public places;

3. Face masks are mandatory when going out in public;

4. Suspension of all mass gatherings including those which are religious, educational, or recreational in nature;

5. The implementation of a flexible work plan or a work from home system for all public and private employment;

6. All public and private educational institutions should prepare for online enrollment procedures and the possibility of online classes for the months of April to July 2020;

7. All private hospitals in Davao City are not allowed to admit Persons Under Investigation (PUIs) and COVID-19 positive patients. The Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC) shall be the primary hospital for severe and critical COVID-19 PUIs and positive patients. Other centers available for mild PUIs shall receive patients referred to them by SPMC.

SECTION 4. LAND TRAVEL -

A. Non-essential travel - All non-essential travel to, from and in Davao City is suspended. Travel into, out of and within Davao City is allowed only for the following:

1. Going to and from work;

2. Delivery of essential goods and services;

3. Buying food, medicines and other essential goods, or availing of essential services;

4. Going to the hospital, dentist, pharmacy or for medical emergencies;

5. Law enforcement officers and those officially assisting in the fight against Covid 19;

6. Other analogous humanitarian considerations. Anyone can leave the city at any time but cannot come back during the quarantine period.

B. Public Transportation - 1. Jeepney drivers shall continue to work for the government by providing public transportation service, sanitation of jeepney units, implementation of social distancing and wearing of masks in public;

--- [Page 3] ---
2. Taxis shall accommodate a maximum of two (2) passengers only per trip.
Tricycles and triskads shall carry one (1) passenger only per trip;
There shall be a "no-mask, no ride" policy in the use of all kinds of public transportation;

C. Truck Ban - The schedule of the ban of trucks along the highways is hereby lifted, until later restored.

D. Cargo - No person or vehicle shall be allowed to enter Davao City except for vehicles with cargo and one (1) driver with no assistant. "Cargo" shall include animals, vegetables, and all other non-living things but not dead human bodies. The following are additional rules for cargoes:

1. The driver cannot disembark from the truck;
2. Cargo vehicle assistants may disembark at the checkpoint and wait for the truck to come back and leave Davao City;
3. Cargo vehicle final destination must be within Davao City;
4. Empty cargo vehicles may come in to pick up cargo inside Davao City with one (1) driver only and no assistant.

SECTION 5. SEA TRAVEL - All sea travel into Davao City is hereby suspended, except for sea cargo and government vessels. Vessels can unload cargo in any port in the City but all personnel and crew of the vessels are prohibited from disembarking from the vessels.

SECTION 6. AIR TRAVEL - Domestic and Foreign Air travel to and from Davao City at the Davao International Airport shall be suspended until the same is lifted by the City Government.

The following are exempted from suspension:

1. Cargo flights
2. Medical Evacuations
3. Weather Mitigation flights
4. Maintenance flights
5. Government and Military flights
6. Flights specifically allowed by the City Government;
7. Emergency landings:
a. As much as possible, no crew or passenger may disembark without prior notice;
b. Any crew or crew who disembark must be placed under isolation upon landing;
8. Recovery flights for expatriates, subject to prior clearance with the City Government;

SECTION 7. CLOSURE OF ESTABLISHMENTS - All establishments and offices must be closed for the duration of the Community Quarantine;

1. Exemptions: The following are allowed to remain open, but, whenever possible, a skeletal workforce arrangement must be implemented to create distancing between employees:

a. government offices,
b. groceries, supermarkets, and wet markets/palengkes,

SECTION 8. BUSINESS PROCESS OUTSOURCING (BPO) OFFICES - BPOs/Call Centers, per mandate of Department of Trade and Industry Memorandum Circular No. 20-08, Series 2020, have two (2) options only:

1. Skeleton workforce with social distancing and appropriate temporary accommodation arrangements within the vicinity and with shuttle service;
2. Work from home arrangements;

If BPOs/Call Centers cannot comply with either of the two options, it shall be closed down immediately.

SECTION 9. CURFEW - A daily curfew from 9:00 P.M. to 5:00 A.M. of the next day shall be imposed upon all persons within the City of Davao, until the existing Community Quarantine is lifted.

Exemptions from the curfew are:

1. Employees of establishments providing basic necessities, i.e., those employed in:
a. public markets, supermarkets, groceries, convenience stores,
b. hospitals, medical and dental clinics, pharmacies and drug stores,
c. food preparation and delivery services, water-refilling stations,
manufacturing and processing plants of basic food products and medicines,
d. banks, couriers, money transfer services,
e. power, energy, water and telecommunications supplies,
f. media personnel,
g. public transportation or the transportation of goods and services;
h. persons transiting to and from work;

provided those persons enumerated above shall present their necessary identification and certificate of employment;

2. Persons accessing basic necessities and emergency services such as:
a. Those buying food or medicines;
b. Those requiring immediate health services;
c. Those doing other essential activities covered by analogous medical and humanitarian reasons.

3. Authorized government officials and personnel of law enforcement agencies;

4. Persons officially assisting in the fight against COVID-19.

Any person not covered by the exemption who are outside their residences during the curfew hours are in violation of this Ordinance and shall be reprimanded and sanctioned accordingly.

SECTION 10. SPECIAL CURFEW FOR VULNERABLE SECTORS - All senior citizens, minors, pregnant women, sick and immune-compromised individuals within the City of Davao are mandated to remain in their homes under a twenty-four (24) hour curfew or home quarantine.

Those covered under this Ordinance may leave their households in case they are the sole head of the family capable of accessing necessary goods and services, to answer to medical emergencies, or to undergo routine medical procedures for chronic illnesses.

SECTION 11. LIQUOR BAN - A liquor ban from 5:00 P.M. to 8:00 A.M. of the next day shall be imposed upon all persons within the City of Davao, until the existing Community Quarantine is lifted.

No person or entity shall sell or serve liquor, alcoholic beverages, coconut wine and other nature wines and the like that causes intoxication within the period provided.

SECTION 12. FUNERALS - Funeral parlors and private and public cemeteries are mandated to impose a "family members only" wake and burial and at all times demand compliance and observance of two (2) meter distance between family members and frequent handwashing with soap and water.

**SECTION 13. PATIENT UNDER MONITORING (PUMs)**
- Asymptomatic patients who within 14 days prior, have recent travel history to identified areas with local COVID-19 transmissions, or have been exposed to other PUMs, must be quarantined at home or at barangay isolation units for 14 days to monitor the development of symptoms.

Any PUM who:
1. Refuses to comply with the home quarantine or isolation;
2. Fails to fully comply with the terms of the home quarantine or isolation;
3. Escapes or avoids home quarantine or isolation after being defined as a PUM;

shall be in violation of this Ordinance and shall be subject to government intervention, without prejudice to other criminal actions.

**SECTION 14. PATIENT UNDER INVESTIGATION (PUIs)**
- Patients who are exhibiting symptoms and who within 14 days prior, have recent travel history to identified areas with local COVID-19 transmissions or have been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case, or have been in close contact with other PUIs must refer themselves or the affected patient to the proper authorities.

Any PUI who:
1. Refuses to comply with the home quarantine or isolation;
2. Fails to fully comply with the terms of the home quarantine or isolation;
3. Escapes or avoids home quarantine or isolation after being defined as a PU;

shall be in violation of this Ordinance and shall be subject to government intervention, without prejudice to other criminal actions.

**SECTION 15. FAILURE TO DISCLOSE**
- Any person, who fails to immediately disclose the fact of their recent travel history to COVID-19 areas with local transmission, or exposure to known PUMs or PUIs, shall be in violation of this ordinance and shall be subject to government intervention, without prejudice to other criminal actions.

**SECTION 16. PENALTY CLAUSE**
A. Any violation of Sections 2 to 12 of this Ordinance shall be punished by:
- **1st Offense:** a Fine of P3,000
- **2nd Offense:** a Fine of P5,000 and/or imprisonment of not more than 1 year, at the discretion of the court

without prejudice to the prosecution of other criminal actions.

B. Any violation of Sections 13, 14, and 15 of this Ordinance shall be punished by a Fine of P5,000 and imprisonment of not more than 1 year, at the discretion of the court, without prejudice to the prosecution of other criminal actions.

Provided that if the offender is a minor, the laws and regulations of the Philippines concerning minors shall apply, but the parent or guardian of such minor shall pay the stated fines.

**SECTION 17. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
— If, for any reason, any Section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 18. REPEALING CLAUSE**
— Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

**SECTION 19. EFFECTIVITY**
— This Ordinance shall take effect 15 days upon approval and publication in a newspaper of local circulation in the City of Davao.
