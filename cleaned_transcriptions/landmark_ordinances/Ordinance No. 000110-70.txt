**4th City Council of Davao**
**20th Regular Session**
**Series of 1970**

**Republic of the Philippines**
**CITY OF DAVAO**
**Office of the City Council**
**ORDINANCE No. 110**

**AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING THE SONG "TAYO AT DARMAHNYO" INDICATED BY MR. C. B. ANAJAO OF THE STA. ANA CENTRAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL AND LYRICS BY PEDRO O. SAVICENTE, SUPERINTENDENT OF CITY SCHOOLS, AS THE OFFICIAL SONG OF THE CITY OF DAVAO.**

**BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council, City of Davao, that:**

**SEC. 1. - The song "TAYO AT DARMAHNYO" music by Mr. C. B. Anajao of the Sta. Ana Central Elementary School, this City; and lyrics by Pedro O. Savicente, Superintendent of City Schools, is hereby declared as the official song of the City of Davao, the lyrics of which are as follows:**

**"Sa daknog tingang ng Hindinaw
Nag iangung lungos na hinirang
Ang likas niyang kuyamunan
Ay waling kapayang."**

**Nga hundok, dazut, lupain
Laging sagana sa pagkain
At ang magagardang tanawin
Kadilan ay alit.**

**Niyang lupain ng labaw
Sa syatang labas
Sa nihinghiningan
Ikasay pagkiligkiran.**

**Dayo ay Darmahnyo
Sa tapat at totoco
Pangarap ay matano
Kaluhadhanan mo
Lungog na paimiso."**

**SEC. 2. - The song "TAYO AT DARMAHNYO" shall be sung in all official ceremonies of the City of Davao, such as conventions, programs, and the like, and in public and private school activities.**

**SEC. 3. - Any person, firm, or corporation violating any provisions of this Ordinance shall upon conviction be punished with a fine of not less than 100,000 or an imprisonment of not less than five (5) days or both upon discretion of the court.**
