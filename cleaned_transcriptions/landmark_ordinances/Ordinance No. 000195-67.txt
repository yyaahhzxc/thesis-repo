ORDINANCE NO. 195

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL MOVIE OR CINEMA HOUSES AND/OR THEATERS OR COLISEUMS TO PROVIDE THE PUBLIC WITH SAFE AND SANITARY DRINKING WATER AT ALL TIMES DURING SCREENING HOURS.

Be it Ordained by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - Movie or Cinema houses and/or theaters or coliseums operating within the City of Davao is required to provide facilities or contrivance whereby the public or its patrons can avail of a safe and sanitary drinking water at all times during screening hours.

SECTION 2. - Violation of this Ordinance shall be subject to a fine of not less than P50.00 but not more than P200.00 or an imprisonment of not less than one week but not more than one month, provided, that in the case of a movie or cinema house and/or theater or coliseum owned and/or operated by a partnership or corporation, the manager thereof shall be held liable; and provided further, that any movie or cinema house and/or theater or coliseum who continues violating this ordinance after one year from the date of the approval of this ordinance, it shall be the duty of the City Health Officer to recommend for the cancellation of its permit and the closure of the said movie or cinema and/or theater or coliseum concerned.

SECTION 3. - Any ordinance or provisions thereof, inconsistent with this ordinance is hereby repealed.

SECTION 4. - This Ordinance shall take effect ten (10) days after its approval.

DATED: May 28, 1967.
