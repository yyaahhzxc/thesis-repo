**ARTICLE 1**

**TITLE**

SECTION 1. This ordinance establishing and regulating the Night and Weekend Street Market in the City of Davao shall be called "NIGHT MARKET AND WEEKEND STREET MARKET ORDINANCE."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY.

a. Section 16 of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that "every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted to it, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, and incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective jurisdiction, they shall provide for the protection, development, and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, prevent and control crime, maintain law and order, and preserve the conflict of conventional moralities."

b. Section 21, paragraph (d) of the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Any city, municipality, or barangay may, by a duly enacted ordinance, temporarily close and regulate the use of any local street, road, thoroughfare, or any other public place where shopping malls, Sunday flea or night markets, or shopping areas may be established and where goods, merchandise, foodstuffs, commodities, or articles of commerce may be sold and dispensed to the general public."

**ARTICLE 2**

**DEFINITION OF TERMS**

SECTION 1. As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall be construed, understood, and shall mean as follows:

a.) MARKET - A private or public place, building, or structure operated and dedicated to the service of the general public where basic commodities are displayed, offered for sale, and for profit;

b.) NIGHT MARKET - A government regulated market conducted at specific time and duration of the night, located in government property, portions of designated places or streets in the City of Davao legally and temporarily close to vehicular traffic to accommodate people indulged in the business of selling goods or offer services to the public for profit, located outside the city public market;

c.) WEEKEND STREET MARKET - A government regulated weekend market conducted at specific time and duration of the day and night located in government property, portions of designated places or streets in the City of Davao legally and temporarily close to vehicular traffic to accommodate people indulged in the business of selling goods or offer services to the public for profit, located outside the city public market;

d.) OCCUPANTS - Refers to interested person or persons allowed to display and sell goods or offer services after an application has been approved and a definite space has been assigned on a temporary basis and has paid the ambulant vending fee in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Municipalities and Towns Act as provided in this ordinance.

e.) COLLECTOR - Is the revenue collection clerk who is properly bonded representing the office of the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) to collect the fees or charges and issue the corresponding receipt or cash tickets;

f.) CUSTOMER - Any person buying the goods displayed or patronizing the services of the occupants in the night and street market of the City of Davao;

g.) SECURITY OFFICER - Is the Civil Security Officer, assigned to maintain peace and order in the market area in coordination with the Public Safety Command Center;

h.) NIGHT MARKET SUPERVISOR - A personnel assigned by the City Government in this case, the City Mayor's Office-Drainage Management Unit (CMO-DMU) as head of the "Tiangge Sa Katawan Management Team" who shall implement the distribution or awarding of the space to the occupants, ensure that sanitation, security, peace and order are maintained, opening and closing time of the night market are observed as well as other related matter;

i.) WEEKEND STREET MARKET SUPERVISOR - A personnel assigned by the City Government in this case, the City Mayor's Office-Drainage Management Unit (CMO-DMU) as head of the "Tiangge Sa Katawan Management Team" who shall implement the distribution or awarding of the space to the occupant, ensure that fees are collected, sanitation, security, peace and order are maintained, opening and closing time of the weekend street market are observed as well as other related matter;

j.) DESIGNATED NIGHT MARKET AND WEEKEND STREET MARKET PLANNING UNIT - The designated locations which could be government lands, portions of roads or streets legally and temporarily closed to vehicular traffic to accommodate the night market and weekend street market of the Davao City Government. Location can be expanded, relocated or transferred by the local government as the need arises;

k.) JURIDICAL PERSON - A legal entity created by the law which is not a natural person, such as a corporation created under state statutes, having a distinct identity and legal rights and obligations under the law,

b. Conduct periodic review of the night and weekend street market operations;

c. Oversee the drawing of lots and raffling of spaces and those that shall be subsequently declared vacant in the night and weekend street market;

d. Certify the results thereof for the approval of the City Administrator;

e. Adjudicate the request of transfer of occupants from one space to another;

f. Recommend such measures of actions as may be necessary in the resolution of problems, in connection with the use and occupancy of spaces in the Night Market and Weekend Street Market;

g. Decide on the revocation or cancellation of Lease Contract, or eviction of vendors from the space he/she occupied on the grounds of violation of specific provision of this Code and other existing ordinances;

SECTION 3. The "Tangge Sa Katawan Management Team" shall convene and hold a regular meeting for at least once a month, or as the need arises upon the call of its Head.

ARTICLE 6
INSTALLATION OF STRUCTURES, PARAPHERNALIA, DISPLAY OF GOODS AND ITS REMOVAL

SECTION 1. Occupants must occupy and install their structures and paraphernalia only in the designated area;

SECTION 2. The city government, through the City Mayor's Office-Drainage Management Unit (CMO-DMU), as head of the "Tangge Sa Kaawawan Project Management Team," shall provide the design of the collapsible structures and shall regulate the same to observe safety, orderliness, and aesthetics;

SECTION 3. All structures, materials, paraphernalia and goods displayed must be removed and cleared completely on or before closing time;

SECTION 4. No structures, paraphernalia and goods or any part thereof shall be displayed before the designated opening time;

SECTION 5. No structures, paraphernalia, and goods or any part thereof shall be left within the allowed market site and/or along any streets, road-right-of-ways and any government facilities or sites;

ARTICLE 7
DESIGNATED TIME AND DURATION OF THE NIGHT MARKET AND WEEKEND STREET MARKET

SECTION 1. Opening shall start at 7:00 o'clock in the evening for the Night Market from Monday to Friday only;

SECTION 2. Opening shall start at 4:00 o'clock in the afternoon for the Weekend Street Market from Saturday and Sunday only;

SECTION 3. Closing time shall be at exactly 3:00 o'clock the following morning for the Night Market;

SECTION 4. Closing time shall be at exactly 12:00 o'clock in the evening for the Weekend Street Market;

SECTION 5. Occupants must occupy and install their structures and paraphernalia in the designated area assigned to them by the CMO-DMU, in accordance with the result of the raffle;

**ARTICLE 8**

**HOW TO AVAIL SPACE IN THE NIGHT MARKET AND WEEKEND STREET MARKET**

**SECTION 1. The application for a Night Market or Weekend Street Market Permit must be filed with the City Mayor's Office - Drainage Maintenance Unit (CMO-DMU) and approved by the majority of the members of the "Tingge Sa Katawan Management Team";**

**SECTION 2. While all natural persons who are not disqualified under Article 9 may apply for a space in the Night Market and Weekend Street Market, however, those applicant vendors who are related among themselves within the second degree either by affinity or consanguinity shall only be allowed to submit at least not more than two (2) applications for this entire family.**

**SECTION 3. The following documents shall be submitted (original or certified true copy) together with the application by the applicant:**

a) Community Tax Certificate;

b) Barangay Clearance;

c) Barangay Certification of Residency;

d) Health Clearance/Certificate;

e) Police Clearance.

**SECTION 4. In the approval of application and raffling of space in the Night Market or Weekend Street Market, priority shall be given to persistent and prevailing ambulant or sidewalk vendors residing in the city, as identified and validated by CMO-DMU, who sell their wares and merchandise along the sidewalks of the roads to be closed to serve as venue for this Project;**

**SECTION 5. All vendors with approved applications shall be briefed by the different agencies of the City Government of Davao on pertinent subjects, including but not limited to, Sanitation, Peace and Order, Proper Waste Disposal, Identification of Improvised Explosive Devices (IED), identification of suspicious-looking individuals, and the reporting of emergencies, such as fires, floods, and calamities, etc. A certificate of completion shall be issued to those an attendance after undergoing such briefing.**

**SECTION 6. Open raffling of the applicants shall be done by the CMO-DMU, with the supervision of the "Tingge Sa Katawan Management Team", for the selection of the ambulant and sidewalk vendors who will occupy the Night Market and Weekend Street Market areas after the briefing proper. The result of the raffling, together with the renumeration of the selected ambulant and sidewalk vendors, shall be submitted to the Office of the City Administrator for approval.**

**SECTION 7. The selected occupants of the spaces for the Night and Weekend Street Market shall occupy the area for the duration of three (3) months, subject to extension depending upon the impact of this Project.**

ARTICLE 9
WHO ARE DISQUALIFIED TO OCCUPY IN THE NIGHT AND WEEKEND STREET MARKET
SECTION 1. The following applicants or vendors shall not be permitted to apply or occupy any of the spaces in the Night and Weekend Street Market, to wit:
1. Juridical persons or entities;
2. Those who are minors;
SECTION 2. Any violation of the provisions of this Ordinance shall be a ground for disqualification and/or ejection;

ARTICLE 10
SANITATION
SECTION 1. Occupants are responsible for the sanitation of their premises;
SECTION 2. Occupants are obliged to place their solid waste in disposable container properly kept at the side of the gutter of the road, its top opening tied or sealed to avoid scattering or spillage for convenient collection by garbage trucks;

--- [Page 11] ---
Page 11 of 15
Ord. No. 089-13

SECTION 3. Occupants must maintain dryness of the area to avoid being filthy looking.

SECTION 4. Liquid waste must be placed in a container and disposed in the drainage canal.

SECTION 5. No solid waste shall be discharged to the drainage canals;

SECTION 6. The City Environment and Natural Resources (CENRO) shall conduct a regular inspection of the waste disposal in the area designated as Night and Weekend Street Market, and impose appropriate sanctions in accordance with existing law or ordinance;

SECTION 7. The City Health Office (CHO) shall conduct a random inspection and testing of food for food vendors in regards to its safety to human consumption and to see if it is in compliance with the provisions of Ordinance No. 089-13, otherwise known as the Environmental Health and Sanitation Ordinance of Denver City, are strictly followed and likewise impose appropriate sanctions and penalties for any violations thereof.

SECTION 8. Provision of portalets.

ARTICLE 11

GOODS AND SERVICES NOT ALLOWED IN THE NIGHT MARKET AND WEEKEND STREET MARKET

SECTION 1. Livestocks, heavy and bulky hardwares, lumber, pungent and odorous goods;

SECTION 2. Services that emit loud noise and require heavy electrical load rated more than 500 watts;

SECTION 3. Illegal goods shall be confiscated and will not be returned to the owner;

SECTION 4. Alcoholic or intoxicating beverages;

ARTICLE 12

PEACE AND ORDER

SECTION 1. While it is understood that the occupants are to avoid unruly behavior, however, the City Government through the Public Safety Command Center (PSCC) shall assign Civil Security Officer to the Night Market and Weekend Street Market area to maintain peace and order. Assistance of Police Officer shall be called when necessary and a hotline number will be provided for emergency.

SECTION 2. In case of emergency or fire conflagration near the vicinity of the Night Market and Weekend Street Market area, occupants must be alert and speedily execute their structures and valuable to clear the road for the passage of the fire fighting units and shall be held in the duty of the Night Market Superintendents to inform the occupants of such precautions;

SECTION 3. Illegal and suspicious looking goods that are abandoned shall be confiscated for proper disposal and will not be returned to the owner;

**ARTICLE 13
FEES AND CHARGES**

**SECTION 1. An Ambulant Vending Fee for the privilege of occupying and selling in the designated Night Market and Weekend Street Market shall be collected by the City Treasurer's Office (CTO), in accordance with the rates as imposed under the provisions of Chapter 5, Article 30 of Ordinance No. 0158-05, otherwise known as the "2005 Revenue Code of Davao City."**

**SECTION 2. The vendors shall only be allowed to occupy the allotted space upon payment of the said Ambulant Vending Fee in an amount equal to one (1) month's rent, computed in accordance with the existing rate, as provided under the immediately preceding Section. Subsequent monthly payment for such imposition shall be made on or before the fifth (5th) day of the following month.**

**SECTION 3. The City Treasurer's Office and City Mayor's Office Drainage Management Unit (CMO-DMU) shall see to it that all vendors in the Night Market and Weekend Street Market pay their tax obligation within the allowable period and prescribed manner.**

**ARTICLE 14
LIMIT OF AREA TO BE OCCUPIED**

**SECTION 1. The minimum space available to each vendor shall be one point five (1.5) meters by two (2) meters. However, the vendors on meritorious ground may request the CMO-DMU for a bigger space, but not to exceed six (6) square meters.**

**ARTICLE 19 PENAL CLAUSE**

**SECTION 1. Violations of this Ordinance shall be subject to the following penalties as follows:**

a. Occupants operating without an approved application shall be imposed a fine of Three Hundred Pesos [P300.00] and closure;

b. Non-payment of fees within the prescribed period shall subject the occupants to eviction and closure;

c. Non-operation of the occupant for three (3) consecutive nights shall be ground for the revocation of the occupant's Night Market Permit and Weekend Street Permit;

d. Violation of Sanitary Laws:

First Offense: Fine of Three Hundred Pesos [P300.00] and mandatory seminar (City Environment Office and City Health Office)

Second Offense: Fine of Five Hundred Pesos [P500.00] and/or Five (5) days community service

Third Offense: Fine of One Thousand Pesos [P1,000.00] and/or Ten (10) days community service

Succeeding Offense: Prosecution and closure of Operation

e. A fine of P50.00 per day per person shall be imposed against the vendor or his sales staff who fails to wear an identification card or the proper T-shirt coded uniform;

f. All other acts committed in violation against the provisions of this ordinance not specifically cited above shall be sanctioned accordingly with suspension or eviction as the circumstances warrant;
