Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, Ordinance No. 0264-12, Series of 2012 ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR A JUVENILE INTERVENTION PROGRAM (JIP) FOR YOUTH AND CHILDREN AT RISK (CAR) OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR", informing your that the same is legally allowed citing Section 18 of the Republic Act No. 9344, otherwise known as the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act of 2006, which states that:
"SEC. 18. Development of a Comprehensive Juvenile Intervention Program. - A Comprehensive juvenile intervention program covering at least a 3-year period shall be instituted in LGUs from the barangay to the provincial level.
The LGUs shall set aside an amount necessary to implement their respective juvenile intervention programs in their annual budget.
The LGUs, in coordination with the LPCP, shall call on all sectors concerned, particularly the child-focused institutions, NGOs, people's organizations, educational institutions and government agencies involved in delinquency prevention to participate in the planning process, implementation of juvenile intervention programs. Such programs shall be implemented consistent with the national program formulated and designed by the JJWC. The implementation of the comprehensive juvenile intervention program will be reviewed and assessed annually by the LGUs in coordination with the LPCP. Results of the assessment shall be submitted by the provincial and city governments to the JJWC not later than March 30 of every year."
In view thereof, approval of the ordinance is hereby recommended.

**ORDINANCE NO. 024-12**
**Series of 2012**

**AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR A JUVENILE INTERVENTION PROGRAM FOR CHILDREN IN CONFLICT WITH THE LAW (CJCL) AND CHILDREN AT RISK (CAR) OF THE CITY OF DAVAO CITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE AND SCOPE – This Ordinance shall be known as the "Juvenile Intervention Program Ordinance of Davao City".

It shall create an intervention mechanism to provide for various intervention programs and diversion programs for Children at Risk or in Conflict with the Law without resorting to judicial proceedings;

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The Amended Children Welfare Code of Davao City declares it as a policy for the local government to ensure that programs aimed at the achievement of goals for the survival, protection, participation and development of children are given utmost priority. It mandates the establishment of a comprehensive support system for the development of the children.

The City of Davao recognizes that the policy of the State in enacting the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act of 2006 or R.A. No. 9344 is aimed to address the different stages involving Children at Risk and Children in Conflict with the Law from prevention to rehabilitation and integration which mandates all local government units to institute a comprehensive juvenile intervention program and requiring its annual funding in their budget to implement the various programs;

The City of Davao shall ensure that the rights of every child, consistent with the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child, to survival, development, protection and participation which are interdependent and indivisible should be respected, promoted and protected so that every child can enjoy a full life;

The City of Davao recognizes its role in ensuring the full implementation of the policies on juvenile justice cognizant with the protected rights of every child as well as the averted rights under the United Nations Guidelines for the prevention of juvenile delinquency and the protection of children against violence, abuse or degrading correction or punishment measures at home, in school or in any other institution;

Pursuant to the said mandate, it is the policy of the City of Davao to provide the necessary intervention programs, diversion programs and related services to Children at Risk or in Conflict with the Law consistent with the intent of granting them human rights and legal safeguards without resorting to judicial proceedings.

The City Government of Davao also recognizes the obligations of the parents as stipulated in Chapter 3, Art. 46, DUTIES OF PARENTS in the Child and Youth Welfare Code where parents shall have the following general duties toward their children, to include extending to their child children the benefits of moral guidance, self-discipline and religious instruction, supervising their activities during childhood and adolescence, and acting promptly on any matter affecting their development and well-being among others;

SECTION 3. APPLICABILITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall be applicable to children found in conflict with the law pursuant to the applicable provisions of R.A. No. 9344. Whenever applicable, and for the best interest of the child, the provisions shall be also applicable to Children at Risk;

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS - The following terms as used in this Ordinance shall mean as follows:

(a) CHILDREN IN CONFLICT WITH THE LAW - refers to a child or children, alleged as, accused of, or adjudged as, having committed a criminal offense under Philippine laws, CICIL for short;

(b) CHILDREN AT RISK - or CAR, refers to a child who is vulnerable to and at the risk of committing criminal offenses because of personal, familial, social or economic factors under the instances mentioned under Section 4 (d) of R.A. No. 9344;

(c) Board - refers to the Juvenile Intervention and Diversion Programs Board;
(d) BCPFC - refers to the Barangay Council for the Protection of Children;
(e) Child - refers to a person under eighteen (18) years old and those above 18 but unable to protect themselves and/or physically or mentally incapable of doing so;
(f) CSSDO - refers to the City Social Services and Development Office of Davao City;
(g) DCSSOC - refers to the Davao City Special Office for Children's Concern, an office created under the Office of the City Mayor as mandated by Republic Act No. 0292-06 or the Amended Davao City Children's Welfare Code;
(h) Diversion - refers to an alternative, alternate-appropriate process of determining the responsibility and treatment with the law in a public custodial setting from which the child in conflict with the law is not permitted to leave at will by order of any judicial or administrative authority;
(i) Diversion Program - refers to the program that the Child in Conflict with the Law is required to undergo after she/he is found responsible for an offense without resorting to formal proceedings;
(j) DSWD - refers to the Department of Social Welfare and Development;
(k) Facility - refers to the Juvenile Intervention Facility, otherwise known as "Children's Village of Davao City";
(l) Intervention - refers to a series of activities which are designed to address issues that cause a child to come in conflict with the law or tend to make the vulnerability to committing offense, it may take the form of an individualized treatment program which may include counseling, skills training, education and other activities that will enhance the psychological, emotional and psycho-social well-being of a child;
(m) Juvenile Justice and Welfare Council (JJWC) - refers to the JJWC created under Republic Act No. 9344. The Council is created and attached to the Department of Justice and placed under its supervision. It is tasked to implement the law on Juvenile Justice and coordinate with the concerned agencies as mentioned in Title II, Section 5, R.A. No. 9344;
(n) Local Council for the Protection of Children (LCPC) - refers to the responsible members of the community as mentioned in Title III, Chapter I, Section 13 of Republic Act No. 9344 which includes representatives from the local legislative government and private agencies con- nected with the welfare of children; at the barangay level, this refers to the Barangay Council for the Protection of Children or BCPC;
(o) Women and Children Concerns Section (WCCS) - refers to the Women and Children Concerned Section of the Davao City Police Office (DCPO) Women and Children's Protection Desk.

SECTION 5. THE JUVENILE INTERVENTION AND DIVERSION PROGRAMS BOARD: There shall be created under this Ordinance a Board to be known as the "Juvenile Intervention and Diversion Programs Board" (referred to "BOARD") which shall have the authority to supervise and operate the operation of the facility and to carry out the purposes of this Ordinance. The Chairman and management of the said institution. The Board shall be headed by the City Mayor of Davao or his/her representative and with membership composed of representatives of the following departments or agencies designated by the concerned Bicameral, to wit:

(a) City Prosecution Office of Davao;
(b) City Social Services and Development Office;
(c) Sangguniang Kabataan Federation;
(d) Department of Education;
(e) Bureau of Jail Management and Penology;
(f) Commission on Human Rights;
(g) City Health Office;
(h) Philippine National Police;
(i) Davao City Committee on Women, Children & Family Relations;
(j) Two (2) Non-government organizations concerned with children's welfare;
(k) Department of Interior and Local Government [DILG];

The Chairperson and members of the Board shall all serve in ex officio capacity with no additional monetary benefit or remuneration of any kind;

The City Mayor shall be designated as the appointing authority for the above named representatives to the Board.

SECTION 6. DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD: The Board shall have the following duties and functions, to wit:

(a) To oversee the implementation of this Ordinance and serve as the supervising and/or governing board of the facility. The actual day-to-day operations of the facility shall be under the auspices of a facility supervisor who shall head the facility, qualified child psychologists and/or social workers, and to handle cases involving Children in Conflict with the Law or Child in Risk who are under the care of employees who shall all be appointed by the City Mayor. The qualifications of the facility supervisor, child psychologists and/or social workers and such other personnel shall be provided by the Board under pertinent rules and be issued by it to implement the provisions of this ordinance.

(b) To advise the City Mayor of Davao and the Sangguniang Panahonod on all matters relating to juvenile intervention program and diversion program and those relating to juvenile justice and welfare aimed at improving the services of the facility.

(c) To formulate and recommend policies and strategies, to collect relevant information and conduct continuing researches and support the evaluations and studies on all matters relating to juvenile intervention and diversion programs;

(d) To coordinate with the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Council (JJWC), the Local Council for the Protection of Children (LCPC) and other stakeholders as well as with the various agencies mandated under R.A. SECTION 7. PREVENTION, REHABILITATION AND DIVERSION PROGRAMS - The prevention, rehabilitation and diversion programs shall be subject to the following guidelines, as follows:

(1) There shall be community based programs that will answer the needs and issues of the community, life skills training and others,

(2) Referral of the child in conflict with the law to individual or family counseling, restitution and community service instead of filing a case against the child,

(3) Diversion can only be applied in cases where the imposable penalty for the crime is below six (6) years imprisonment. The end goal of diversion is disposing of the case involving a youthful offender without resorting to formal trial by the competent authority,

(4) If the imposable penalty is above six (6) years imprisonment or if the child does not admit to the commission of the crime or does not consent to diversion if involved in community services, the child shall be referred to the Family Court through the Social Services and Counseling Division/Department of Social Welfare and Development/Juvenile Intervention Facility or the "Children's Village of Davao City" who will act as intake department;

(5) Based on DSWD/Children's Village of Davao City evaluation, it shall recommend to the Family Court the issuance of the necessary orders for appropriate measures including referral to psychiatrist, psychologist for counseling and therapy, referral to diversion programs or require the child to be placed in a housing estate by the family court;

(6) Consistent with R.A. No. 9344 when the child is found guilty by the Family Court for the offense charged and the sentence is suspended without need of appeal, the findings and pre-disposition report prepared by the DSWD/Children's Village of Davao City shall be forwarded and submitted to the Family Court for appropriate consideration of disposition measures;

The program shall be designed under the guidance of the Head of the Davao City Special Office for Children's Concern (DCSCC) in close coordination with the concerned offices/agencies.

**SECTION 8. JUVENILE DELINQUENCY PREVENTION PROGRAM.** - There shall be a comprehensive community-based Children's Program designed by the DCSSOC in its work with children, particularly at risk (CAF) in order to minimize, if not totally prevent, commission of crimes among children. A special program will be created within the DCSSOC that will be appropriately formulated and implemented within the DCSSOC in close coordination with CSSDO, other agencies and child-focused non-government organizations.

**SECTION 9. CREATION OF DAVAO CITY JUVENILE MEDIATION PROGRAM WITHIN THE DCSSOC UNDER THE OFFICE OF THE CITY MAJOR.** - A Juvenile Mediation Program shall be designed to create a venue or vehicle for deliberation and determination of non-custodial measures in order to avoid the formal contact of the child with the justice system. It shall demonstrate the Davao City government's adherence to the principle of restorative justice when resolving conflicts between children and the law. The program shall provide an opportunity for the community to further facilitate a participatory process in order for the victim to obtain reparations, reconciliation of the offender, the offended, and the community and reassertion to the offender that he or she can be reintegrated into society. The principles of non-discrimination and inclusion, survival and development shall be applied to the operations of the program as well and developed strategies shall be activated by the offender, the victim and the community in prevention strategies.

The program shall be designed under the guidance of the Head of the Davao City Special Office for Children's Concern (DCSSOC) in close coordination with the Barangay Council's Lupon Tagapamayapa, Barangay Council for the Protection of Children (BCPC), Davao City Council for the Welfare of Children and the Davao City Police Office. A social worker shall be detailed in the Women and Children's Concern Section of the Davao City Public Office (DCPO) for close monitoring of Children in Conflict with the Law, ensuring the rights and needs of the children, the victim's compensation and the community's safety. The social worker will assist the barangay immediately convening a mediation conference with the child, WCCS, parents/guardians concerned and representatives of interested organizations to effectively render justice without resorting to formal court proceedings.

Any person who has lawfully taken into custody a Child in Conflict with the Law shall enroll the child to the Davao City Juvenile Mediation Program for appropriate case management without prejudice to Sec. 24 of Administrative Matter No. 02-1-18-SC 2002-02-28, or Rule 6 on Juveniles in Conflict with the Law.

**SECTION 10. BARANGAY-BASED DIVERSION PROGRAM.** - There shall be a Barangay-based diversion program, headed by the Punong Barangay with the assistance of the Local Social Welfare and Development Officer or other members of the barangay that dispose the case of a Child in Conflict with the Law over nine (9) years old and under fifteen (15) years of age without going through court proceedings. This program shall be provided to prevent a child from suffering the negative effects of court proceedings, while at the same time putting the offender to a rehabilitation process following restorative justice.

The extent of the diversion program in the barangay level shall include, but not limited to, the following:

1. Restitution of property;
2. Reparation of the damage caused;
3. Indemnification for consequential damages;

--- [Page 9] ---
4. Written or oral apology;
5. Care, guidance and supervision orders;
6. Counseling for the Child in Conflict with the Law and the child's family;
7. Attendance in trainings, seminars and lectures on:
a. Anger management;
b. Problem solving and/or conflict resolution skills;
c. Values formation;
d. Other skills which will aid the child in dealing with situations which can lead to repetition of the offense;
8. Participation in available community-based programs, including community service;
9. Participation in detention, detention, detention and life skills programs (Chapters 2, Section 31, [a], R.A. 9344 supplied);
SECTION 11. CREATION OF GENDER-FAIR DAVAO CITY CHILD AND PRE-TRIAL HOME - A separate holding and pre-trial home, to be known as the Gender-Fair Pre-Trial Home, shall be established, designed and operated by the DC SOCC in coordination with the BJMP providing rehabilitation services where a Child in Conflict with the Law may be physically restricted pending formal complaints or court disposition of the charge against the child;
A separate home for girls shall be established to promote and protect their rights pending trial approximating standards designed in international instruments involving persons deprived of liberty. The Gender-Fair Pre-Trial Home shall also accommodate children apprehended at the barangay level when circumstances would prevent the rescuing personnel from turning them over to their respective families.
The Pre-Trial Home/s shall also provide after care services for children who have been released as part of the reintegration process;
SECTION 12. CREATION OF THE JUVENILE INTERVENTION FACILITY, TO BE KNOWN AS "CHILDREN'S VILLAGE OF DAVAO CITY" - In order to provide for an institute to cater and provide for the needs of every Child in Conflict with the Law for intervention programs, diversion programs and other related activities, the Children's Village of Davao City is hereby created. Such facility shall be established and made operational within a period of one (1) year from the effectivity of this ordinance. The facility shall have the following functions and powers, to wit:
(a) To provide short-term residential care for Children in Conflict with the Law and/or those waiting court disposition of their cases or transfer to other agencies or jurisdictions, in proper cases;
(b) With child psychologists and/or qualified social workers under its regular staffing pattern, to provide immediate assessment and recommendation on Children in Conflict with the Law immediately after the arrest of a child and prior to or at the time of the conduct of an inquest assistance or upon referral to it by any authority which had the initial contact with the said child in order to determine how to legally proceed with the said child;

(c) In coordination with the Local Council for the Protection of Children (LCPC) and other stakeholders consistent with the intervention programs recommended by the Juvenile Justice and Welfare Council (JJWC) in coordination with the various agencies mandated under R.A. 9344, to implement and administer a comprehensive juvenile intervention program aimed at delinquency prevention through community-based services cognizant of the roles of the family, educational system, and mass media which respond to the special needs, problems, interests, and concerns of children and their families intended to provide alternatives to incarceration, assist children in Conflict with the Law and/or Children at Risk and other related measures designed to avoid their unnecessary contact with the formal justice system and other measures to prevent re-offending.

(d) To provide, implement and administer diversion programs for Children in Conflict with the Law without undergoing court proceedings subject to the conditions and applicable provisions provided under Chapter 2 of R.A. No. 9344;

(e) To provide training and disposition, as may be practicable, on miscellaneous learning and guidance to the children in its custody, including but not limited to, the following:

(i) Competency and life skills development;

(ii) Socio-cultural and recreational activities;

(iii) Vocational and computer projects;

(iv) Leadership training;

(v) Social services;

(vi) Home life services;

(vii) Spiritual enrichment;

(viii) Community and family welfare services;

(f) To coordinate with, and therefor assess, consider and implement the recommendations of the Local Council for the Protection of Children (LCPC) on juvenile intervention programs, diversion programs or other related youth services that may be undertaken in the facility;

SECTION 13. MANUAL OF OPERATIONS - Consistent with the provisions and objectives of this Ordinance as well as its implementing Rules, all agencies authorized to undertake prevention, rehabilitation, and diversion program shall have their own manual of operations for a comprehensive, speedy, and effective execution of this ordinance. A copy of the said manual is to be furnished to all concerned for their appropriate reference and guidance;

SECTION 14. APPROPRIATION - An amount necessary to establish the facility and to carry out the initial implementation of this Ordinance shall be charged against the current year's appropriation of the Office of the City Mayor. The money from succeeding funds needed for its continued operation shall be included in the annual/supplemental budget ordinance of the City of Davao;
