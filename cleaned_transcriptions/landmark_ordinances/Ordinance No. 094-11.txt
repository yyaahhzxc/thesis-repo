SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance amending Ordinance No. 0410-10, Series of 2010, otherwise known as 'New Davao City Tricycle for Hire (TTH) Franchising and Regulatory Code of 2010', particularly Section 27 of Article VI, Franchise and Other Fees, III A. (3) and VIII A. (7) by way of removing the required Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) Clearance from the said provisions".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS. - Section 27, Article VI of Ordinance No. 0410- 10, Series of 2010 is hereby amended by way of deleting the required Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) Clearance from Article VI, Section 27 thereof, as follows:

a. The fifth (5th) requirement under paragraph "III. Application for Extension of Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) Three Years";

b. The seventh (7th) requirement under the designated paragraph "VIII. Petition for Approval of Sale and Transfer of CFC, MTH, PA, Decision or Computerized Certificate, MTOP whichever is Applicable with Equipment".

SECTION 3. AMENDED PROVISION. - Section 27, Article VI of Ordinance No. 0410-10, Series of 2010, shall now read as follows as amended:

"SECTION 27. - Requirements and Schedule of Fees. - The following are the franchising requirements and schedule of fees:
