**ORDINANCE NO. 020-10**
**Series of 2010**

**AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO IMPLEMENT THE ADJUSTED RATES OF SPECIAL COUNSEL ALLOWANCE PAID TO LAWYER PERSONNEL INCLUDING THOSE DESIGNATED TO ASSUME THE DUTIES OF A LEGAL OFFICER AND THOSE IN THE LEGAL STAFF OF DEPARTMENTS, BUREAUS AND OFFICES IN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO EFFECTIVE JANUARY 2018 ON THE BASIS OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9978 (RA 9978) OR THE GENERAL APPROPRIATIONS ACT OF FISCAL YEAR 2018 (GAAP) 2010**

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord No. 020-10

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance
authorizing the City Mayor to implement the adjusted rates of Special Counsel
Allowance paid to lawyer-personnel including those designated to assume the duties of
a legal officer and those in the legal staff of departments, bureaus and offices of the City
Government of Davao effective January 2010 on the basis of Republic Act No. 9970 (RA
9970) or the General Appropriations Act of Fiscal Year 2010 (GA FY 2010).

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: In keeping with its mandate
and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its
policy to maximize the use of available resources and at the same time ensure that these
resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use.

SECTION 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS: The amounts herein provided shall
be used to implement the adjusted rates of Special Counsel Allowance (SCA) for the
lawyer-personnel including those designated to assume the duties of a legal officer and
those in the legal staff of the City Legal Office of Davao from P800.00 to P1,000.00 for
each appearance or attendance to hearing effective January 1, 2010, subject to the
provisions of Section 52 of Republic Act No. 9970 or the General Appropriations Act of
Fiscal Year 2010 and must conform with existing accounting and auditing rules, regulations,
policies and guidelines as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.

SECTION 4. SEPARARIBILITY CLAUSE: If any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof which are not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full
force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: The provisions of this Ordinance shall take
effect upon approval.

ENACTED, October 5, 2010, by a majority votes of all the Members of the
Sangguniang Panlungsod.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
MARGARITO C. DELA CRUZ
Acting Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Assistant Department Head II)
