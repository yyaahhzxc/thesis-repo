AN ORDINANCE TO FURTHER AMEND ORDINANCE NO. 0307-20,
SERIES OF 2020, AS AMENDED, TO ALLOW VOLUNTARY WEARING
OF FACEMASKS IN INDOOR AND OUTDOOR SETTINGS SUBJECT

PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY RELATIVE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. Section 1 of Ordinance No. 0307-20, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"SECTION 1. TITLE - "AN ORDINANCE TO FURTHER AMEND ORDINANCE NO. 0307-20, SERIES OF 2020, AS AMENDED, TO ALLOW VOLUNTARY WEARING OF FACE MASKS IN INDOOR AND OUTDOOR SETTINGS SUBJECT TO CERTAIN EXCEPTIONS DURING THE STATE OF PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY RELATIVE TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC".

SECTION 2. Section 2 of Ordinance No. 0307-20, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS. For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms shall be defined as follows:

a) Face Mask - shall mean a protective device that is firmly fastened over the face and covering at least the nose, mouth and chin which serves as the protection or barrier against possible infection from a disease such as COVID-19 and other airborne pathogens. These shall include surgical masks, cloth masks, bandanas, washable masks, N95 masks, among others;

b) Immuno compromised individual - shall refer to an individual with or who are:

i. Immuno deficiency state;
ii. HIV;
iii. Active cancer or malignancy;
iv. Transplant recipients;
v. Undergoing steroid treatment;
vi. Patients with poor prognosis/bed-ridden patients; and
vii. Other conditions of immunodeficiency as certified by a physician.

c) Senior Citizen or Elderly - shall refer to any resident citizen of the Philippines at least sixty (60) years old.

d) Status of vaccination - There are three (3) status of vaccination as follows:

1) Fully vaccinated individuals - a person shall be considered fully vaccinated:

i. After two (2) weeks or fourteen (14) days of getting a dose of an accepted single-dose vaccine with at least one (1) booster shot;

ii. After two (2) weeks or fourteen (14) days of getting a second dose of an accepted 2-dose series with at least one (1) booster shot;

iii. After two (2) weeks or fourteen (14) days of getting a second dose of any "mix-and-match" combination of accepted Covid-19 vaccines with at least one (1) booster shot.

2) Partially or incompletely vaccinated individuals - a person shall be considered partially or incompletely vaccinated if he/she was not able to take at least one booster after getting a first dose of any accepted Covid-19 vaccines as mentioned in the preceding paragraph.

3) Unvaccinated individuals - a person shall be considered unvaccinated if he/she was not able to take a dose of any accepted Covid-19 vaccines.

The abovementioned guidelines shall be without prejudice to the resolutions or implementing guidelines set forth by proper authorities.

e) Symptomatic individual - shall refer to a person showing symptoms suggestive of Covid-19 such as, but not limited to:

i. Fever;
ii. Dry Cough;
iii. Difficulty in breathing;
iv. Sore throat;
v. Fatigue;
vi. Loss of sense of smell and taste;
vii. Other symptoms as certified by a physician;

SECTION 3. Section 3 of Ordinance No. 0307-20, as amended, is hereby amended, with an additional Section (Section 3-A) to read as:

"SECTION 3. Voluntary wearing of face masks in indoor and outdoor settings; Exceptions: The wearing of face masks in indoor and outdoor settings shall be voluntary except in the following settings:

a. Healthcare facilities including, but not limited to, clinics, hospitals, laboratories, nursing homes and dialysis clinics;
b. Medical transport vehicles such as ambulance and paradiamic rescue vehicles;
c. Public transport by land, air or sea.

Mask wearing is still encouraged for the:

a. Elderly;
b. Individuals with comorbidities;
c. Immunocompromised individuals;
d. Pregnant women;
e. Partially or incompletely vaccinated individuals;
f. Unvaccinated individuals;
g. Symptomatic individuals."

**SECTION 3-A. Minimum Public Health Standards.** The Minimum Public Health Standards (MPHS) intended to effectively prevent and minimize the spread of Covid-19 in the country issued by Department of Health (DOH), the Inter-Agency Task Force for the Management of Emerging Infectious Diseases (ITF), and other proper authorities is hereby adopted and continue to be implemented.

**SECTION 4. PENALTY.** Any person caught in violation of this Ordinance shall be penalized as follows:

a) First Offense - P 500.00

b) Second Offense - P 2,000.00

c) Third Offense - P 5,000.00 or 1 month imprisonment or both.

**SECTION 5. ENFORCERS AND MODE OF APPREHENSION.** The Philippine National Police (PNP) and such other appropriate enforcers as commissioned by the City Mayor have the power to apprehend violators of this Ordinance and issue citation tickets in accordance with their standard operating procedure.

The enforcers shall order them to take necessary steps to comply with the mandates of this Ordinance such as wearing of face mask and observing physical distancing.

The citation ticket shall state, among others, the name and address of the violator, the specific violation committed and the corresponding administrative penalty.

The City Mayor may deputize Barangay Officials or representatives of the private sector, non-government organizations and government organizations in the strict implementation of this Ordinance, as the need arises.

**SECTION 6. NO CONTEST PROVISION.** A person liable or violator of this Ordinance who is apprehended or cited for violation and who does not wish to contest the violation and is willing to pay voluntarily the administrative penalty imposed upon him/her prior to the filing of formal charges with the proper courts shall be allowed to pay a penalty of Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00) with the City Treasurer's Office to avoid being criminally prosecuted within three (3) days from apprehension. Otherwise, the case shall be prosecuted in court. The no contest provision can only be availed once.

All proceeds from the payment of the herein imposed penalty shall be apportioned as follows:

1. Sixty (60%) of the penalty collected per apprehension shall accrue to the apprehending barangay;

2. Forty percent (40%) shall accrue to the General Fund of the City.

The share of the barangay shall be remitted directly to the barangay, without need of any further action, on a monthly basis within ten (10) days of the following month. Said amount shall be utilized for Covid-19 response and for other matters incidental thereto.

SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable and in the event that anyone or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances, resolutions, rules or regulations which are inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVENESS - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in at least three (3) newspapers of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, on the 29th day of November 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
