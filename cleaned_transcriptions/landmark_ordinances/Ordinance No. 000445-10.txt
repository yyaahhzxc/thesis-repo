ORDINANCE NO. 0445-10
Series of 2010
AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING AND PENALIZING THE
ACT OF DRIVING A MOTOR VEHICLE WHILE UNDER
THE INFLUENCE OF LIQUOR AND/OR PROHIBITED
DRUGS.
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in
session assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE PROHIBITING AND PENALIZING THE ACT OF DRIVING A
MOTOR VEHICLE WHILE UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF LIQUOR AND/OR
PROHIBITED DRUGS;
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 5 of Article II of the
1987 Constitution provides that "the maintenance of peace and order, the
protection of life, liberty and property, and the promotion of the general

welfare are essential for the enjoyment by all the people of the blessings of democracy;
Pursuant to the above-mentioned Constitutional provision, it is hereby declared the policy of the City Government to protect its people from hazards brought about by driving under the influence of liquor and/or dangerous drugs that foolishly endanger life and property;
SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS
3.1 Motor Vehicle - refers to anything that carries people or goods powered by a motor or engine;
3.2 Driving under the influence of liquor and/or dangerous drugs - refers to the act of operating a motor vehicle while under the influence of liquor and/or dangerous drugs when the driver's blood alcohol content level is 0.08 percent or more;
3.3 Dangerous Drugs - include those listed in the Schedules annexed to the 1961 Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs, as amended by the 1972 Protocol, and in the Schedules annexed to the 1971 Single Convention on Psychotropic Substances as enumerated in the attached annex of Republic Act 9105 or the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002;
3.4 Liquor - refers to any alcoholic or intoxicating beverage made either by distillation or by fermentation;
3.5 Field sobriety tests - refer to standardized tests used to determine if a driver is impaired. The test assesses balance, coordination and ability of the driver to divide his attention to more than one task;
3.6 Chemical tests - refer to breath, urine and blood tests to determine the blood alcohol content level and/or the positive indication of drugs;
SECTION 4. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall be applicable to all persons driving any motorized vehicle in all streets, roads and boulevards within the entire territory and jurisdiction of the City of Davao;
SECTION 5. PROHIBITED ACT - It shall be unlawful for any person to drive a motor vehicle while under the influence of liquor and/or dangerous drugs;
When a law enforcement officer has probable cause to believe that a driver is driving under the influence of liquor and/or dangerous drugs due to manifestations, including erratic driving, poor coordination, or the presence of the influence of alcohol, the officer shall conduct field sobriety tests. If the driver fails the field sobriety tests, the driver shall be required to undergo chemical tests;
SECTION 6. PENALTY
6.1 Any person found guilty of driving under the influence of liquor and/or dangerous drugs shall be penalized in the following manner;

6.1.a. A fine of P2,000.00 and completion of a counseling course on the 1st offense;
6.1.b. Imprisonment of not less than four (4) months to six years and a fine of P3,000.00 on the 2nd offense;
6.1.c. Imprisonment of not less than six (6) months to one (1) year and a fine of P5,000.00 on the 3rd and subsequent offenses;

In addition, the driver's license of the erring person shall be subject to confiscation by the law enforcement officer or other duly authorized traffic personnel. The confiscated license will then be turned over to the Land Transportation Office (LTO).

6.2 All penalties are without prejudice to the application of the provisions of other existing applicable ordinances and laws.

SECTION 7. LEAD IMPLEMENTING AGENCY - The Traffic Management Center (TMC) shall be the lead agency in the implementation of the provisions of this Ordinance;

In addition, the Traffic Management Center (TMC) in cooperation with the City Health Office (CHO), Land Transportation Office (LTO), the Philippine National Police (PNP), Department of Health (DOH) and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA), within thirty (30) days from the approval of this ordinance, promulgate the necessary rules and regulations, which may be limited to the following:

a. Provisions for apprehension;
b. Identify the necessary gadgets or test instruments to be used;
c. The procedures for testing;
d. Accreditation of counseling centers;
e. Procedure and guidelines for counseling;

SECTION 8. APPROPRIATION OF FUND - There shall be allocated an initial amount of One Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00) for the purchase of the necessary gadgets or test instruments to be used in the measurement of the blood's alcohol content and/or drug content of a person suspected to be driving under the influence of alcohol and/or drugs. The said amount shall be included in the Annual or Supplemental Budgets of the City. Provided, that the procurement of such gadgets or instruments shall be made in accordance with the existing laws and regulations on government procurement;

SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason any Section of the Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other Sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any ordinance, local issuances or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly;

SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation in Davao City, and upon posting for three (3) consecutive weeks at a conspicuous place at City Hall;

ENACTED, June 22, 2010, by majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian.
