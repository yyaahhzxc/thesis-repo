RECOGNITION NO. 1264
As a privileged matter after the suspension of the
Rules of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, this City, Councillor
Jesus M. Tinio, Jr., proposed that all owners of motorcycles be
required to install silencers to minimize the noise produced by
motorcycles. He proposed further that a penalty of P5.00 be imposed
on any violator of said ordinance.
After a careful deliberation, on motion of Councillor
Jesus M. Tinio, Jr., it was
RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREIN RESOLVED, To approve an
Ordinance entitled:
ORDINANCE NO. 728
AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL MOTORCYCLE OWNERS WITHIN THE
TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY TO INSTALL SILENCERS TO
MINIMIZE THE NOISE PRODUCED BY MOTORCYCLES IN ITS JURISDICTION
IN MOTION.
Se it Ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao,
that:
SECTION 1. - All motorcycle owners within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City are hereby required to install
silencers to their motorcycles to minimize the noise while
