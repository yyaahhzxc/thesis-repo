SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If any provision of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance is declared invalid, the remainder of this Ordinance or the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected by such declaration;

SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE. Any Ordinance or executive order issued by the City Mayor, or any provision therein, inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance is hereby modified or repealed accordingly;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall be effective ten (10) days after the posting of a copy of the same in the bulletin boards of the City Hall of Davao, the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City and the Hall of Justice of Davao City as well as the completion of a publication of its gist or salient features in a newspaper of general circulation within the City.
