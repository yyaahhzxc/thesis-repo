ORDINANCE No. 51

AN ORDINANCE ENABLING/USING THE INTERIM BUS TERMINAL, STANDARDS, RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE OPERATION AND ESTABLISHMENT OF AN INTERIM BUS TERMINAL AT THE BUS TERMINAL SITE AT SM MARTA, CITY OF DAVAO, DECLARED AND AUTHORIZED BY THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND GENELATION NO. 1, SERIES OF 1984, ENACTED ON FEBRUARY 16, 1984.

Be it ordained by the Sanggunan Pangulo, City of Davao, that SECTION 1. This Ordinance shall be known and referred to as "The Interim Bus Terminal Ordinance."

SEC. 2. Pursuant to the provisions of R.P. Bldg. 357, the Local Government Code, and of City Ordinance No. 1, series of 1984, the City Government of Davao shall establish, operate and administer, an interim bus terminal at the 1.7 hectare portion of the SIR Project Area at new Jatinas, Davao City, to be utilized as an integrated loading and unloading terminal, for all public utility buses;

SEC. 4.- All public utility buses (PUBs-whether large, medium or baby bus), with final or transient destinations, and/or points of departure in Davao City may unload and load their passengers at the said Interim bus terminal at New Marta, this City.
SEC. 5.- Other public utility vehicles (i.e., RUVs and RVs), may be allowed to make use of the said Interim bus terminal, under such guidelines, rules and regulations as may be promulgated by the City Government of Davao, thru the Interim Bus Terminal Management, and in collaboration and consultations with the Board of Transportation.
SEC. 6.- Public Utility Buses (PUBs) with franchises to serve special subdivisions or specific areas only within Davao City, shall be allowed to load and unload passengers at such places in the Subdivision, as the Office of the City Mayor shall designate;
SEC. 7.- The Interim Bus Terminal Management may request the assistance and shall coordinate with the Board of Transportation, Mindanao Office in Davao City, the Constabulary Highway Patrol Group (CHPG), and the Davao City Metropolitan/PC-LEP, Davao City, in the prosecution and implementation of the said traffic routing scheme, in the general interest and welfare.

ARTICLE V - CONCESSION FEE STRUCTURE

SEC. 11. The Interim Bus Terminal Management shall distribute thru raffle, the afore-cited refreshment, eatery and cuchiria concession areas, under such rules and regulations, and terms of lease as the Interim Bus Terminal Board may adopt and promulgate.

ARTICLE VI - MANAGEMENT ORGANIZATION AND STRUCTURE

SEC. 12.- There is hereby constituted an INTERIOR BUS TERMINAL BOARD, to be composed of three (3) members, to wit:
a) A representative of the City Mayor
b) A representative of the Treasurer
c) A representative of the Sangguniang Panlungsod

Which Board shall be vested with the power, function and responsibility to adopt requisite policy guidelines and standards, in the management and administration of said interior bus terminal, subject to the approval of the City Mayor.

SEC. 13.- The City's interior bus terminal shall be managed and administered by an Interior Bus Terminal Management, to be composed of the following personnel:

A. Bus Terminal Manager

B. Operational Division:

1) Administrative Division - consisting of one (1) Administrative Assistant and one (1) Clerk;

2) Maintenance and Upgrading Division - consisting of one (1) Civil Engineer, one (1) Foreman and three (3) Laborers;

3) Enforcement and Security Services Division - consisting of one (1) Head Security Officer, two (2) Senior Security Guard Officers and twenty four (24) Security Guards;

4) Financial Management Division - consisting of one (1) Bookkeeper, three (3) Collectors and two (2) Clerks.

SEC. 14.- The Bus Terminal Manager shall be hired, and appointed or designated by the City Mayor, and may be either a competent person engaged on contractual basis at the maximum monthly compensation of three thousand pesos ($3,000.00) or an incumbent official of the City Government, who shall perform the duties of the Bus Terminal Manager, in addition to his other duties.

SEC. 15.- The positions in the Administrative Division, Maintenance and Upgrading Division, Enforcement and Security Services Division, and Financial Management Division of the Bus Terminal Management Staff shall be filled up, by means of detail thereof, from the offices of the City Mayor, City Engineer, City Treasurer, and City Civil Security Officer; PROVIDED however that for the Enforcement and Security Services Division, additional security guards may be engaged, if the present Civil Security Staff of the City Government shall be inadequate to perform the enforcement and security services, for the efficient operation of the said interim bus terminal.
SEC. 16.- The Bus Terminal Manager shall have the following duties, functions and responsibilities, to wit:
a) Direct the day to day operation of the Bus Terminal;
b) Ascertain the security of the area and safety of clients including orderly allocation of space within the terminal for all vehicles using the facility;
c) Assist in the enforcement of PDR regulations and apply the times of departure of all vehicles in accordance with their certificates of convenience;
d) Maintain/upgrade the facilities particularly of the internal road system, parking/departure bays and structures, sanitation within the area of the terminal environment and the operation of utility;
e) Insure collection of revenue due the facility;
f) Prepare the necessary administrative and financial reports for the use of higher authorities including the operating budget of the organization;
g) Propose changes to innovate operating procedures including policies as fee structure, etc., for the successful operations of the terminal;
h) Serve as secretary to the Interim Bus Terminal Board, including the development of proposals for policy enactment;
i) Perform such other duties as shall be directed by the Interim Bus Terminal Board. Continued on page -5-.

SEC. 17. - The Administrative Division shall have the duty, function and responsibility, to take charge of all administrative records, in connection with the operation of the interior bus terminal;

SEC. 18. - The Maintenance and Upgrading Division shall have the duty, function and responsibility to maintain and upgrade the functional efficiency and utility of all buildings, road ways and other facilities of the interior bus terminal;

SEC. 19. - The Enforcement and Security Services Division shall have the duty, function and responsibility:

A) To man and supervise the traffic control devices placed at the Javana-Agusan Road/Provincial Road Junction at Panay Island (for passenger vehicles) and at the MaharArthur Highway/Diversion Road Junction, at Bangkal, Davao City (for South-bound vehicles);

B) To maintain security within the interior bus terminal by providing security personnel and to maintain peace and order, and provide security for vehicle operators, their employees operating vehicles within the interior bus terminal; and to enforce the rules and regulations which the bus terminal management shall promulgate with respect to the operation of said interior bus terminal;

SEC. 20. - The Financial Management Division shall have the duty, function and responsibility to collect, issue receipts and to keep all financial records of the operation of the interior bus terminal;

SEC. 21. - Upon the enactment hereof, the Offices of the City Engineer, City Treasurer and City Civil Security Officer, Davao City, shall submit to the City Mayor, the names of competent personnel in their respective offices, who may be placed on detail with the respective divisions of the Terminal Management to whom they appertain; and forthwith, the Office of the City Mayor shall issue the requisite orders, for the detail of said personnel, to the bus Terminal Manager- ment Staff;

SEC. 22. - All the collections of fees or charges, from the operation, management and administration of the Interim Bus Terminal established hereunder, shall constitute a trust fund, to be utilized for the operational expenses, improvements and other relevant administrative expenses for such bus terminal, without prejudice to such administrative adjustments as the City Mayor and/or the Sanggunian Panlungsod shall prescribe.

SEC. 23. - The City Mayor is authorized to re-structure and/or make such requisite administrative personnel and management adjustments, including the hiring of additional personnel, as he may deem fit and proper.

ARTICLE VII - FINAL INSTRUCTIONS

SEC. 24. - Any person or persons who shall violate any provision of this Ordinance, or who shall violate the rules and regulations promulgated under the authority of this Ordinance, shall be punished by a fine of not exceeding one thousand pesos ($1,000.00), or imprisonment not exceeding six (6) months, or both such fine and imprisonment in the discretion of the Court; provided however, that should the violation be committed by any public utility firm or entity, the General or Resident Manager thereof, shall become responsible and shall be charged and penalized with such violation; and further, the involved public utility firm or entity, shall be recommended to the Board of Transportation (BOT), or other government Office having supervisory powers over the same, for any appropriate disciplinary or administrative action, including suspension and/or cancellation of franchise, in view of the violation of the provisions of this Ordinance and its implementing rules and regulations.

SEC. 25. - Separability Clause - If for any reason, or reasons, any part or provision of this Ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, the other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected
