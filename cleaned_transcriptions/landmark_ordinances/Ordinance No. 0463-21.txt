Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE COOPERATIVE DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY RELATIVE TO THE COOPERATIVE MANAGEMENT INFORMATION SYSTEM (CMIS)

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of
Ord. No. 0463-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF
AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY
GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE COOPERATIVE DEVELOPMENT
AUTHORITY RELATIVE TO THE COOPERATIVE MANAGEMENT INFORMATION
SYSTEM (CMIS)".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY: The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into
and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of
Agreement relative to the Cooperative Management Information System to be entered
into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Cooperative Development
Authority;

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of January 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
