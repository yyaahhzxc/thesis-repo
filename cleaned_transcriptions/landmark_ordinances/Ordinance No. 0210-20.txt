ORDINANCE NO. 0210-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE MENTAL WELLNESS CENTER OF DAVAO CITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

--- [Page 5] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE MENTAL WELLNESS CENTER OF DAVAO CITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared the policy of the City Government of Davao to uphold the right of the people to mental health in compliance with the Mental Health Code of Davao City.

The World Health Organization (WHO) has also signaled the urgent need for countries to provide a network of community mental health services at its Global Forum for Community Mental Health (Geneva, 2007).

Towards this end, the City shall establish a halfway home for recovered, abandoned, homeless and neglected persons with mental disability and a separate community-based mental wellness center that will provide immediate access to psychiatric and psychological services to the community especially persons who are faced with a great deal of stress in their homes, community, schools, workplace and are struggling, suffering and experiencing anxiety, depression and emotional and behavioral stress.

SECTION 3. OBJECTIVES - The objectives of this Ordinance are as follows:

a. To establish a halfway home for recovered, abandoned, homeless and neglected persons with mental disability;

b. To establish a community-based mental wellness center that will cater to the citizens of the City;

c. To establish a venue not only for intervention but also for research and development, training and other collaborative activities in the field of mental health;

d. To incorporate the aspect of social and mental health services into an integrated establishment;

e. To employ health professionals who specialize in mental health and wellness whose services shall complement other health services provided by the state;

f. To aid various sectors in research towards a better understanding of the interplay between social, emotional, psychological, and physiological factors affecting mental health;

g. To keep homeless persons with mental disabilities off the streets and to encourage and build their capacities in order to accelerate their possibility of reintegration to society;

h. To make community mental health services more accessible to Davaeans with mental disabilities, especially those which are addressable by therapy and counseling;

i. To promote the importance of mental health and wellness through a multi-faceted approach that covers public information campaigns, counseling, prevention, treatment and intervention;

--- [Page 6] ---
**SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS**

a. Allied professionals: any trained or certified non-psychiatric physician, social worker, nurse, physical therapist, occupational therapist, counselor, priest, pastor, sun, trained or certified non-psychiatric person or non-physician.

b. Mental health professionals: those persons with formal education and training in mental health and behavioral sciences such as, but not limited to, psychiatrists, psychologist, psychiatric nurses or psychiatric social workers.

c. Legal representative: a person authorized to act for another person, including a guardian, trustee, administrator.

d. Legal guardian: a person who has legal authority to care for another person.

e. Mental health: a state of well-being in which an individual realizes his/her own coping mechanisms adequately with the normal stresses of life, displays resilience in the face of extreme life events, works productively and fruitfully, and is able to make a positive impact to the community.

f. Halfway home: a residence for individuals after release from institutionalization (e.g., for mental illness, drug addiction, or criminal activity) that is designed to facilitate their readjustment to private life.

g. Counseling: the provision of assistance and guidance in resolving personal, social, or psychological problems and difficulties, especially by a professional.

**SECTION 5. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT**

The City Government of Davao shall provide a suitable government-owned site as a permanent site for the Facility and shall ensure that the funds for necessary human resource, maintenance, and operating expenses for the health facilities shall be provided. The Facility shall have an adequate number of mental health professionals, workers, and allied professionals as deemed appropriate to ensure that each patient is provided with the proper medical and therapeutic intervention.

**SECTION 6. COMPOSITION OF THE MENTAL WELLNESS CENTER**

The Facility shall have two (2) components: the halfway home for recovered, alleviated, homeless, and neglected persons with mental disabilities (the halfway home), and the community-based residential facility (the wellness center), respectively. The proper names of both these components shall be determined as fit at a later time.

The halfway home is a post-recovery home that provides shelter to recovered persons with mental disabilities who are abandoned, neglected, and homeless and found living or staying in the streets. The current and future operating procedures shall be in accordance with the provisions of the Mental Health Code of Davao City, as well as the accepted standards in the practice. The City Health Office shall take the lead in the management and supervision of the Shelter, while the City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) shall provide for after-care services at the community level when the persons are reintegrated back to their respective families and communities.

--- [Page 7] ---
The Mental Wellness Center shall function as a community-based facility which will provide immediate access to various psychiatric and psychological services. This facility will be operated and managed by the City Health Office.

SECTION 7. CREATION OF OFFICES AND POSITIONS - The creation of offices and positions within the Facility shall be determined by the appropriate committees, officers and government instrumentalities, in accordance with law.

The hiring of physicians, specialists and staff for the Facility shall be done in accordance with the regulations of the Department of Health, the Civil Service Commission, and the City Government, with due regard to the qualifications as specified for each position.

The CSSDO and the City Health Office shall take the lead in the management and operations of the Mental Wellness Center.

SECTION 8. ADMISSION TO THE FACILITY - Due to the distinct nature of the Shelter from the Counseling Center, there shall be a different set of criteria for admission to each. It shall be laid down, however, that the Shelter shall cater to homeless persons with mental health problems who are residents of the World Jurisdiction of Davao City. The Counseling Center, on the other hand, shall cater to all persons living in Davao City, regardless of race, age, disability, gender, sexual orientation, creed, or political affiliation. As regards the specific admission protocols, the Implementing Rules and Regulations shall govern.

SECTION 9. CONFIDENTIALITY OF RECORDS - All patients under the care of the facility shall enjoy the right to confidentiality as provided for by law.

Only current and former patients may have access to their personal mental health records. Confidential and sensitive information may however be withheld from the patient and instead given to a legal guardian, next of kin, representative, or counsel.

Disclosure of patient records to government agencies may only be allowed pursuant to a lawful order from the courts. An exception in case of exigent circumstances may be allowed, as provided for by law.

SECTION 10. CONSENT TO COUNSELING - The consent of the person seeking the services of the Counseling Center shall be obtained freely, without threat or improper inducement and with full disclosure in a language that is understandable to him/her.

SECTION 11. APPROPRIATIONS - The City shall appropriate funds for the construction of the Facility, the salaries and benefits of the physicians, specialists, allied professionals, and staff.

SECTION 12. SEVERABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

--- [Page 8] ---
**SECTION 13. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances, Resolutions, Executive Orders, Memoranda and Administrative Regulations or part thereof in conflict or inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are thereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.**

**SECTION 14. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.**

**ENACTED, February 4, 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
