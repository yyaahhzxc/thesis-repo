**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 5, ARTICLE 7.**
**CEMETERY CHARGES, PARTICULARLY SECTIONS 298-313 OF THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF DAVAO CITY, AS AMENDED.**

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

**SECTION 1. TITLE**
This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 5, ARTICLE 7. CEMETERY CHARGES, PARTICULARLY SECTIONS 298-313 OF THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF DAVAO CITY, AS AMENDED."

--- [Page 2] ---
**SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS TO THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF DAVAO CITY, AS AMENDED - Chapter 5, Article 7 - Cemetery Charges, Sections 298-313 of the 2005 Revenue Code of Davao City is hereby amended to read as follows:**

FROM:

Section 298. Cemeteries. The provisions of this Article shall apply to all unclaimed, undisposed grave lots/ niches in all public cemeteries and all those that may hereafter be acquired by the City of Davao;

Section 299. Administration of Cemeteries. The administration, operation and maintenance of all public cemeteries within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao shall be under the control and supervision of the Head of the City Engineering Department, who shall be the Cemetery Administrator and shall see to it that the provisions of this Article are strictly enforced and complied with;

Section 300. Lot Classification. There shall be two sections of grave lots for lease in all public cemeteries;

Section 301. Lot Identification. For purposes of identification and reference, lots in any public cemetery of Davao City shall be numbered consecutively with the corresponding block numbers by the Cemetery Administration, in accordance with the Sketch Plan prepared by the City Engineer's Office.

Section 302. Imposition of Fees. There shall be collected the following rental fees for a period of ten (10) years for the rental of City Cemetery lots:

1. Fee for Lease Period of ten (10) years

a) Rental fee for Grave Lots
   * Adult Section
   * Children Section
   P2,000.00
   P1,000.00

b) Rental fee for Niches
   * Ground Level
   * Second Level
   * Third Level
   * Fourth Level
   P6,000.00
   P7,200.00
   P6,400.00
   P3,600.00

Section 303. Lease of Grave Lots. Lease of grave lots or cemetery spaces for burials at the Davao City public cemetery shall be per lot, in accordance with the above lot classification.

Grave lots must be leased according to the actual needs or numbers of deceased persons to be buried and shall in no case be leased, disposed of or possessed by reservation for future use; or used.

The full rental fee of the niches in accordance with the above niche classification, as the case may be, must be paid in advance or prior to their utilization or use;

Section 305. Perimeter Niches. There shall be constructed niches for lease along the perimeter fence, except the frontage thereof, of every existing public cemetery, if feasible, and in all public cemeteries to be hereafter established in accordance with the specifications indicated in the working plan prepared by the Office of the City Engineer.

---

**Section 306. Niche Classification**

There shall be two (2) sections of niches for less in all public cemeteries: one for children and one for adults.

---

**Section 307. Niche Identification**

For purposes of identification and reference, niches constructed along the perimeter fence in all public cemeteries shall be numbered consecutively with the corresponding block numbers in the Cemetery Administration, as indicated in the plan of the City Engineer's Office.

---

**Section 308. Time of Payment**

The fee shall be paid to the City Treasurer upon application for a burial permit prior to the construction thereof of any structure whether permanent or temporary, or to the interment of the deceased. Thereafter, the fee shall be paid within twenty (20) days before the expiration of the lease period.

---

**Section 309. Place of Tombstone**

The lessee is required to place a grave marker and/or tombstone on the niche or on the grave within three (3) months from the date of burial in the following form:

| Date of Birth | Date of Death |

---

**Section 310. Tombstones and Marker Requirements**

The grave marker and/or tombstone in any public cemetery in Davao City shall be made in accordance with the approved plans and specifications prepared by the Office of the City Engineer. In no case shall the construction of niches or installation of grave markers or tombstones be permitted if the same shall be made in accordance with plans other than those provided.

---

**Section 311. Authorized Persons to Sign Contracts of Lease**

The City Economic Enterprises, representing the City Mayor, is hereby authorized to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, all contracts of lease which shall, among others, include the name of the lessee, civil status, nationality, address, relationship to the deceased, name of the deceased relative, address, location, and price, and such other information as may be necessary.

---

**Section 312. Duties of the Cemetery Administrator**

It shall be the duty of the Cemetery Administrator or the Officer-In-Charge of the cemetery administration to ensure the strict compliance and observance of the above provisions before releasing the lots/niches for lease or the installation of the corresponding grave marker or tombstone therefor, unless, as the case may be,

---

**Section 313. Penalty**

Failure to pay the rental fee within the time provided by this Article shall subject the lessee to pay a surcharge of twenty-five percent (25%) and an interest of fourteen percent (14%) per annum based on the unpaid amount until the same shall have been duly paid.

---

**Section 314. Administrative Provisions**

(a) As used in this Article, City Cemetery shall refer to the lot owned by the City Government.

(b) A standard cemetery lot shall be three (3) meters long and one (1) meter wide or three (3) square meters.

(c) Except in cases allowed under existing laws and regulations, no person may be buried or interred, permanently or temporarily, other than in properly designated cemeteries or burial grounds.

(d) In addition to the burial permit, a certificate of death issued by the attending City Health Officer, or if no medical officer is available, by the City Mayor, Administrator, or any member of the Sanggunan Panhinggo shall be required.

(e) Any construction of whatever kind or nature in the public cemetery whether for temporary or perpetual use, shall only be allowed after the approval of a permit issued by the City Mayor, upon recommendation of the City Health Officer.

(f) If a leasee intends to renew the lease after its termination, he must inform the City Treasurer within thirty (30) days before the expiry date of the lease, and shall pay the corresponding fees.

(g) It shall be the duty of the City Treasurer to prepare and submit to the City Mayor a list of the lessees that are to expire five (5) days prior to the expiration date. The City Treasurer shall send a reminder to the lessee two (2) weeks prior to the expiration date of the lease.

(h) The City Treasurer shall keep a register in account of the cemetery, together with such additional information as may be required by the Sanggunan Panhinggo.

Section 304. Perimeter Niches and Ossuaries: There shall be constructed niches and ossuaries, along the perimeter fence of every existing public cemetery, if feasible, and in all public cemeteries to be established hereafter in accordance with the specification indicated in the working plan, program, or other plans and specifications approved by the concerned government agencies like the Office of the City Engineer.

Section 305. Niche Classification: There shall be two (2) sections of niches for lease in all public cemeteries: one for children and one for adults.

Section 306. Niche Identification: For purposes of identification and reference, niches constructed along the perimeter fence in all public cemeteries shall be numbered consecutively from left to right in the corresponding block numbers by the Cemetery Administration, as indicated in the approved plans.

Section 307. Time of Payment: The fee as provided herein shall be paid to the City Treasurer upon the application for a burial permit prior to the interment of the deceased. Thereafter, renewal fee shall be paid within twenty (20) days before the expiration of the lease period. The fee shall not be collected in a pauper's burial, upon the recommendation of the City Mayor.

Section 308. Place of Tombstone: There shall be Tombstones provided by relatives of dead persons containing the following data and/or information, viz: Name of the Deceased Date of Birth Date of Death

Section 309. Tombstone and/or Marker Requirement: The grave marker and/or tombstone in any public cemetery in Davao City shall be made in accordance with the approved plans and specifications prepared by the City Engineer's Office. In addition, shall all the construction of niches or installation of grave markers and/or tombstones be permitted, if the same shall be made in the form other than that specified hereof.

Section 310. Authorized Persons to Sign Contracts: The City Economic Enterprise, representing the City Mayor, is authorized to sign, for and on behalf of the City Government of Davao, all contracts of which shall, among others, include the name of the lessee, civil status, nationality, address, relationship to the deceased, name of the deceased relative, the lot or niche classification, price, and such other information or data as may be necessary.

Section 311. Cemetery Obituary: Public Cemeteries shall cause to install an obituary in location where it can easily be noticed by the public, the size of which is proportionate to the number of persons that may be accommodated in the cemetery, or in the area where the above obituaries are posted, before leasing the lots/niches for lease or the installation of the corresponding grave marker or tombstone therefore, as the case may be.

Section 312. Duty of the Cemetery Administrator: It shall be the duty of the Cemetery Administrator or the Officer-in-Charge of the cemetery administration to inspect the public cemetery, to determine whether the above provisions, before leasing the lots/niches for lease or the installation of the corresponding grave marker or tombstone therefore, as the case may be.

**Section 313. Penalty:** Failure to pay the rental fee within the time provided for in the Article shall subject the lessee to pay a surcharge of twenty-five percent (25%) and an interest of fourteen percent (14%) per annum based on the unpaid amount of the rental fee.

**In the case of Osmany, failure to pay rent within the prescribed period, the bone remains of the decedent shall be transferred, moved and/or placed to the common grave area, made for the purpose, except unless a request for a grace period pursuant to paragraph 2 of Section 307 has been made.**

**Section 314. Administrative Provisions:**

(a) As used in this Article, City Cemetery shall refer to the lot owned by the City Government.

(b) A standard cemetery lot shall be three (3) meters long and one (1) meter wide or three (3) square meters.

(c) Except in cases allowed under existing laws and regulations, no person may be buried or interred, permanently or temporarily, other than in properly designated cemeteries or burial grounds.

(d) In addition to the burial permit, a certificate of death issued by the attending physician or City Health Officer, or if no medical officer is available, by the City Mayor or City Administrator shall be required.

(e) Any construction of whatever kind or nature in public cemetery whether for temporary or perpetual use, shall only be allowed after the approval of the City Mayor, upon recommendation of the City Health Officer.

(f) In case a lessee intends to renew the lease after its termination, he must inform the City Treasurer within thirty (30) days before the expiry date of the lease, and shall pay the corresponding fees therefore.

(g) It shall be the duty of the City Treasurer to prepare and submit to the City Mayor a list of the leases that are to expire five (5) days prior to the expiration date. The City Treasurer shall send a reminder to the lessees of the expiration date, five (5) weeks prior to the expiration date of the lease.

(h) The City Treasurer shall keep a register in account of the cemetery, together with such additional information as may be required by the Sanggunang Panlungsod.

**SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY:** This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval and publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao.

**ERECTED, November 9, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunan present.**
