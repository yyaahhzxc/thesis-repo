ORDINANCE NO. 0624-24
Series of 2024

AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE DAVAO CITY
JUSTICE ZONE TOWARDS COORDINATED JUSTICE SECTOR
ACTIONS TO ENABLE ACCESS TO JUSTICE

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

ARTICLE I

GENERAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "INSTITUTIONALIZATION OF THE DAVAO CITY JUSTICE ZONE".

SECTION 2. STATE POLICY: It is the policy of the State to promote social justice in all phases of national development. It also values the dignity of every human person and guarantees full respect for human rights. It also upholds that no person shall be deprived of life, liberty, or property without due process of law, nor shall any person be denied the equal protection of laws.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance:

A. Justice Sector Coordinating Council (JSCC) - The governing body composed of the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of the Philippines, the Secretary of Justice, and the Secretary of the Interior and Local Government. JSCC determines the policy direction and approves recommended plans, actions and projects for the Justice Zones.

B. Justice Zone - It is an area or locality where a minimum number of inter-agency coordinate reforms, specified by the Justice Sector Coordinating Council (JSCC) Technical Working Group (TWG) on Processes and Capacity Building, are present, rendering that area or locality compliant, based on the selection and rating criteria established by JSCC. (Glossary, Philippine Development Plan 2023-2028). It exemplifies a locality where the Criminal Justice System is working to the people's satisfaction. It may refer to a municipality, city, province, or an entire region. It refers to a local area where the justice sector agencies identify justice issues and consider sector-based solutions to address them. It aims to invigorate coordinate strategies among the institutions, ensure that complex institutional reforms are effectively linked and sequenced, and communicate such measures to the different stakeholders of the justice sector, and the public in general. (JSCC Joint Resolution No. 02-2022).

C. Justice Zone Convenor - A representative of a justice sector agency who leads the Justice Zone administratively in the planning and implementation of Justice Zone programs and projects. The Convenor communicates and coordinates on behalf of the Justice Zone with JSCC, JSCC TWGs, other concerned and relevant agencies and institutions in their localities, including the local government unit.

ARTICLE II

THE DAVAO CITY JUSTICE ZONE

SECTION 4. OVERALL MANDATE - The Davao City Justice Zone (DCJZ) shall serve as the local platform for effective interagency coordination in the criminal justice system.

--- [Page 3] ---
**SECTION 2. COMPOSITION**

The Davao City Justice Zone shall be composed of the following institutions:

a. Executive Judge of the Davao City Regional Trial Court as the Convener;
b. The City Mayor as Co-Convener;
c. 1st Vice Executive Judge of the Regional Trial Court as Co-Convener;
d. Executive Judge of the Municipal Trial Court in Cities as Co-Convener;
e. A representative of the Public Attorney's Office (PAO) XI as Vice Convener;
f. A representative of the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) Field Operations Office-Davao City as Vice Convener;
g. Field/City/Regional offices of agency representatives, whichever may be applicable, including but not limited to, the following:

I. Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), and other national government agencies which are either separate from, attached to, or considered as line bureaus under it, such as the i) Philippine National Police-Davao City Police Office, ii) National Police Commission (NAPOLCOM), iii) Davao City Jails, and iv) National Bureau of Investigation (NBI);

II. Department of Justice (DOJ) which comprises of the City Prosecutor's Office, Public Attorney's Office (PAO), and the Parole and Probation Administration Region XI (PPA);

h. City Government, such as the City Mayor's Office, the Committee Chairperson on Civil, Political and Human Rights, the City Legal Office, the City Anti-Drug Abuse Council, the City Planning and Development Office, the City Health Office, the Davao City Treatment and Rehabilitation Center for Drug Dependents, the City Social Welfare and Development Office, PESO and the local barangay affairs office;

i. Government Line Agencies, such as, the Department of Health (DOH) Region XI, Drug Abuse Treatment and Rehabilitation Center (DATRAC), Department of Labor and Employment-Region XI, Commission on Human Rights-Region XI, Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) Region XI, Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA)-Region XI;

j. Civil Society representatives involved in the provision of access to justice to poor and marginalized individuals, such as, the Integrated Bar of the Philippines (IBP);

k. Academe involved in the provision of legal aid service;

l. Other institutions and offices that may be included in the Davao City Justice Zone with the concurrence of its members.

**SECTION 3. MEMBERSHIP**

Membership in the Davao City Justice Zone shall be institutional in nature, in recognition of possible changes in the representatives within member institutions.

Each member institution shall provide one principal and one alternate representative to the convener of the Davao City Justice Zone to ensure continuity of operation. The principal representative shall be the head of the institution and the alternate shall be a key person who can act in the absence of the principal representative.

**SECTION 4. CONVENOR:** The position of Convenor of the Davao City Justice Zone shall belong to the incumbent Executive Judge of the Regional Trial Court.

**SECTION 5. CO-CONVENORS:** The City Mayor or his duly authorized representative, the 1st Vice Executive Judge of the Regional Trial Court, and the Executive Judge of the Municipal Trial Court in Cities shall serve as Co-Convenors alongside the Convenor of the Davao Justice Zone.

**SECTION 6. VICE CONVENORS:** A representative of the Public Attorney's Office (PAO) Region XI and the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) Field Operations Office-Davao City shall serve as Vice Convenors of the Davao Justice Zone.

**SECTION 7. SECRETARIAT:** The Davao City Justice Zone shall be assisted administratively by a Secretariat, who shall not be a member of the core group.

**SECTION 8. MEETINGS:** Regular meetings of the Davao City Justice Zone shall be conducted bi-monthly. Special meetings may be called for urgent issues, upon the request of any member.

**ARTICLE III**

FUNCTIONS OF THE DAVAO CITY JUSTICE ZONE

**SECTION 1. POLICY FORMULATION:** The Davao City Justice Zone shall formulate measures aimed at improving the delivery of just, responsive, and accountable justice services in the city. Such measures may be raised for consideration of the Sanggunian, for appropriate local legislation and budgets, or to the Justice Sector Coordinating Council (JSCC). All such measures must conform and not contravene the policy directives set forth by JSCC, the policies of the member institutions, and existing laws and regulations.

**SECTION 2. PLANNING AND PROGRAMMING:** In accordance with policy directions and resolutions of the JSCC, the Davao City Justice Zone shall prepare plans, programs, and services with accompanying budgets aimed at operationalizing measures to improve the delivery of just, responsive, and accountable justice services in the city. Measures within the purview of the Davao City Justice Zone are, but not limited to, the following:

a. Davao City Swift Justice Program to improve efficiency in the administration of justice, addressing issues in the justice system, such as clogged dockets, and ensuring that all pillars of the justice system work together to promote justice by disposing cases expeditiously;

b. Improving access to justice of the poor and marginalized, especially women, children, indigenous peoples, people of diverse sexual orientation and gender identity and expression (SOGIE), among others;

c. Enabling the barangay justice system, as well as customary and traditional justice systems to uphold rights-based approaches and build links with the formal justice mechanisms;

d. Rehabilitation and reintegration of Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDL) into society, among others.

The City Justice Zone Plan will form part of the city's local development plan, such as, the Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP) and its Annual Investment Plan (AIP), to ensure that it has appropriate political and financial resources for implementation.

It shall be formulated alongside the local development planning process of the city, which shall be reviewed every three (3) years and updated annually. It shall be formulated based on the assessment of the justice issues and needs of the city, and in cognizance with national plans, such as, the Philippine Development Plan.

Resources for the implementation of the plan could be sourced from the local budget and the convergence budget of JSCC. External funding may be undertaken, but with the concurrence of the members of the Davao City Justice Zone.

SECTION 3. COORDINATION - The Davao City Justice Zone shall coordinate with relevant agencies, offices, and organizations to support the delivery of just, responsive, and accountable justice services in the city. It shall initiate referral pathways to link individuals as well as groups to services related to access to justice, such as, health, psychosocial support, case management, safety, and security, among others.

The Davao City Justice Zone may formulate Memoranda of Understanding (MOU) with specific institutions and organizations to create partnerships and strengthen justice service delivery, subject to the approval of the JSCC Principals.

It may also formulate policies, projects, and services as a result of coordination work within and among justice zone members and other justice actors.

SECTION 4. MONITORING - The Davao City Justice Zone shall utilize its regular meetings to monitor progress of measures as identified in its plan and agreements made in view of improving the delivery of just, responsive, and accountable justice services in the city. It shall harvest lessons learned in implementing justice-related policies, plans, programs and services, and good practices.

It shall develop annual reports to be submitted to the Sanggunian and the Mayor, and to JSCC containing accomplishments towards benchmarking best practices on Justice delivery as well as recommendations to improve the delivery of justice in the city.

ARTICLE IV

**SPECIFIC FUNCTIONS OF THE DAVAO CITY JUSTICE ZONE MEMBERS**

SECTION 1. CONVENOR - The Convenor shall have the following specific administrative functions:

a. Formulate the regular and special meeting agenda, in consultation with the justice zone members;

b. Preside in the conduct of regular and special meetings of the Davao City Justice Zone;

c. Initiate the formulation of plans, programs and services, as well as reports of the Davao City Justice Zone;

d. Initiate coordination with other agencies, offices and organizations within the city towards improved justice service delivery;

e. Lead in the advocacy with the City Government of Davao and JSCC of measures towards improved justice service delivery.

**SECTION 2. SECRETARIAT:**
The Secretariat shall have the following specific functions:
a. Disseminate regular and special meeting agendas amongst the justice zone members;
b. Document, disseminate, and safeguard the Minutes of regular and special meetings of the Davao City Justice Zone;
c. Alert the convenor and the members of the Davao City Justice Zone of local and national policies, and on plans, programs, and services that may affect the delivery of justice services in the city;
d. Support in the formulation of plans and reports of the Davao City Justice Zone;
e. Support in the coordination with other agencies, offices, and organizations within the city towards improved justice service delivery.

An honorarium shall be provided to the secretariat to be sourced from the Justice Zone Plan of the City.

**SECTION 3. MEMBERS:**
The Members shall have the following specific functions:
a. Participate in the formulation of regular and special meeting agendas;
b. Participate in the conduct of regular and special meetings of the Davao City Justice Zone, and provide updates in these meetings;
c. Support in the formulation of plans, programs, and services, as well as reports of the Davao City Justice Zone;
d. Support in the coordination with other agencies, offices, and organizations within the city towards improved justice service delivery;
e. Participate in the advocacy of the City Government of Davao and JSCC on measures towards improved justice and service delivery.

**ARTICLE V FINAL PROVISIONS**

**SECTION 1. APPROPRIATIONS:**
The amount necessary for the implementation of this Ordinance, which will cover the implementation of the programs and services of the Davao City Justice Zone, shall be supported by the City Government of Davao, to be included in the Local Development Plan. The budget appropriation will be based on the Work and Financial Plan of the Davao City Justice Zone with an initial budget amounting to Three Million Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (Phip3,500,000.00), which may be thereafter adjusted in the annual appropriations budget of the City. Additional funding may also be from other sources, subject to existing government budgeting, accounting, auditing rules, regulations and guidelines of the Commission on Audit, the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Government Procurement Act, as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and presidential directives.

**SECTION 2. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:**
If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE:**
All Ordinances, Resolutions, Executive Orders, Memoranda and Administrative Regulations or parts thereof in conflict or inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.
