Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of
Davao City that:

SECTION I. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE DAVAO CITY ANTI-LITTERING ORDINANCE OF 2009".

SECTION II: DECLARATION OF POLICY. - It shall be the declared policy of the City of Davao to adhere to and adopt a systematic, comprehensive and ecological solid waste management program of Davao City which shall include measures that will protect public health and environment. Towards this end, the City Government of Davao hereby sets the guidelines for the strict implementation of this Ordinance pursuant to Section 48 (1) of R.A. 9003, otherwise known as "The Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000".

SECTION III: PROHIBITED ACTS. - It shall be unlawful for any person/s to commit the following prohibited acts as follows: littering, throwing, scattering and dumping or failure to properly dispose of any refuse, fruit peelsings, pieces of paper, plastic wrappers and other waste materials into public places such as roads, sidewalks, canals, streets, parks, public playgrounds, public school campuses, public buildings, rivers, lakes, creeks, seas and other public places where public domain is provided by the city but no appropriate waste receptacles or garbage bins provided for the purpose.

SECTION IV: Fines and Penalties. - Any person/s who violates Section 3 of this Ordinance shall, upon conviction, be punished with fines of:

For the first offense: P500 to P1000;
For the second offense: P1000 to P2000;
For the third & succeeding offenses: P2000 to P5000;

SECTION V: COORDINATING OFFICE. - The City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO) shall be the lead coordinating office in the strict implementation of the provisions of this Ordinance. It shall be the duty of CENRO to provide garbage bins/receptacles in public places, such as streets, playgrounds, public buildings and other places of similar nature. Likewise, CENRO shall prepare any and all informative materials and programs related to the implementation of this Ordinance and shall continuously disseminate the same to the general public in print or broadcast through signs/ notices to be placed in strategic public places and locations in the city.

SECTION VI: DUTIES OF THE BARANGAY. - In addition to the personnel of various law enforcement agencies, the Pinamang Hervang together with the barangay kapangatad and barangay tambod, shall be deputized to apprehend violators of the provisions of this Ordinance;

**SECTION 7. ANTI-LITTERING FUND.**
Any and all fines imposed upon violators by the court in accordance with this Ordinance shall form part of and accrue to a special fund to be created under the City Treasurer's Office, to be called the "Anti-Littering Fund," which shall be administered by the Solid Waste Management Board of Davao City. The funds shall be used solely for the purpose of achieving the full implementation and desired results of this Ordinance.

**SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE.**
If for any reason or reasons, any part or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining parts or provisions thereof not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE.**
City Ordinance No. 95, Series of 1956, and Ordinance No. 105, Series of 1956, are hereby repealed, substituted and repealed accordingly to this effect.

**SECTION 10. EFFECTIVE DATE.**
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

**ERECTED, January 20, 2009, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council).**
