Ord. No. 0100-19

Be it ordained by the Sanggunan Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0418-83, SERIES OF 1983, BY INCREASING THE PENALTY FROM A FINE OF TWO HUNDRED PESOS (PHP200.00) OR AN IMPRISONMENT OF ONE (1) MONTH TO A FINE OF FIVE THOUSAND PESOS (PHP5,000.00) OR AN IMPRISONMENT OF ONE (1) MONTH OR BOTH AT THE DISCRETION OF THE COURT.

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 3 of City Ordinance No. 0418-83 is hereby amended as follows:

Section 3 - PENALTY PROVISION: Failure to comply with the provisions of this Ordinance after due notice shall be fined PHP5,000.00 or an imprisonment of one (1) month, or both, at the discretion of the Court.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, November 12, 2019 by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunan present, there being a quorum.
