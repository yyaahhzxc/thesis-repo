RESOLUTION No. 942
Letter of Dr. Leon M. Garcia, President, of the Theatre Operators Association of Davao City (TOADH) dated September 16, 1982, inviting
the body to consider the matter of the public showing of betamax films in
public places mentioned therein to the detriment of the moviethouse industry,
and recommending therein some remedial measures to protect the interest of said moviethouse industries, and to prevent the enforcement
in the future of the said municipal taxes, for which the Com-
mittees on Laws and Ordinances recommended that this matter be treated
by the body as a whole, was presented for consideration and appro-
priate action or the body by Councilor Antonio S. Castillito.
Councilor Castillito manifested that the public showing of betamax
films, tapes in some public establishments, such as restaurants,
cocktail lounges, etc., were causing the public to be initially ruined,
the moviethouse industry in the City of Davao. Councilor Castillito stated further that movie films which are scheduled for showing in
Davao now are being shown in other cities, thus decreasing the show-
ing in the aforementioned business establishments thus decreases enorm-
ously the number of movie-goers patronizing the moviethouses. What is
worst, Councilor Castillito was further, is that there is no law's
regulation prohibiting the showing of movies, films, and betamax films in
public places such as those enumerated above.
On the other hand, Councilor Castillito stressed that these business establishments showing betamax films do not pay the
municipal taxes due to the moviethouses theatres which pay the
regular assessment taxes due.
Councilor Castillito proposed for the passage of an Ordinance
regulating the showing of movie films, tapes and betamax films in
public places such as those enumerated above.
After careful deliberation, on motion of Councilor Antonio S.
Castillito, duly seconded by Councilor Pantaleon V. Palayao, Jr., and
sponsored by Councilor Jose A. Viscan, it was
RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, to approve an Ordinance entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 381
AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE PUBLIC SHOWING OF MOVIE FILMS, TAPESS OR BETAMAX FILMS IN PUBLIC ESTABLISHMENTS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO.
Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:
SEC. 1.- The public exhibition of motion pictures, tapes or betamax films in public places such as restaurants, cocktail lounges, pub-houses and the like which have not been previously approved and stamped by the Board of Censors for Motion Pictures is hereby prohibited;
SEC. 2.- Penal provisions. Any person or persons found violating the foregoing provisions shall suffer the following penalties:
a) First offense - a fine of P100.00;
b) Second offense - a fine of P200.00 and/or imprisonment for a period not exceeding one month;
c) Third offense - a fine of P200.00 and imprisonment for one (1) month including the cancellation of business permits.
In case of Corporation, Partnership and Association, the General Manager and/or President shall be held liable.
SEC. 3.- Any existing ordinance on the same subject matter is hereby deemed amended, cancelled and/or repealed accordingly.
SEC. 4.- This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
