Ord. No. 062-19

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 2 OF CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0299-09, SERIES OF 2009, BY INCREASING THE PENALTIES TO THE MAXIMUM."

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT - Section 2 of City Ordinance No. 0299-09, Series of 2009 shall be amended as follows:

For the first offense, a fine of P 3,000.00
For the second offense, a fine of P 4,000.00
For the third and subsequent offenses, a fine of P 5,000.00

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City.

ENACTED, October 8, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
