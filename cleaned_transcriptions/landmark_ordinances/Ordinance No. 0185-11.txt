Ord. No. 0185-11

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND OPERATION Smile Philippines RELATIVE TO THE OPERATION SMILE PROGRAM IN DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (i) (a) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the city government, and in this connection shall:
(a) Represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf, all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";
SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT - The Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) refers to the instrument to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Operation Smile Philippines relative to the Operation Smile Program in Davao City;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and Operation Smile Philippines relative to the Operation Smile Program in Davao City;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval;

ENACTED, October 11, 2011, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.

CERTIFIED CORRECT.
MARGARITO C. DELA CRUZ
Secretary to the Sanggunian Panlungsod
City Government Department Head

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

BETWEEN THE UNDERSIGNED:

Operation Smile Philippines, a non-profit making association, governed by the laws of the Philippines, founded on 1982, herein represented by MR. ROBERTO J. MANZANO in his capacity as President and Executive Director of Operation Smile Philippines, and duly authorized by the Operation Smile Philippines Board of Trustees, whose local office is located at Brokenshire Integrated Health Ministries, Inc. (formerly Brokenshire Hospital), Davao City, hereinafter referred as the "First Party"

and

The City Government of Davao, local government unit organized and existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address in the Davao City Hall, San Pedro Street, Davao City represented herein by MS. SARA Z. DUTERTE, Mayor of Davao City, hereinafter referred as the "Second Party"

CONSIDERING THAT

1) The President of the Republic of the Philippines encourages the private and public partnership to intensify the state's call for a more advanced and sophisticated means of providing health care services to its people;

2) Studies have shown that one (1) infant will be born with an orofacial cleft per 500 live births;

3) As of 2007, the population census of the National Statistical Coordination Board has projected a growth rate of 2.39% per annum for Mindanao. Assuming this rate will be constant, there will be more than 40,000 new cases of infants born with orofacial clefts per year.

4) It is estimated that only less than 900 of these infants receive treatment per year. There is then a tremendous number of backlog.

5) Although numerous governmental and non-governmental agencies are addressing this problem on a mission based approach, only Operation Smile Philippines provides a holistic treatment that encompasses medical, surgical, dental and speech therapy during its missions only.

6) The price of reconstructive surgery would cost about Forty Four Thousand Pesos (P44,000.00) per patient.

HAVE REACHED THE FOLLOWING AGREEMENT:

ARTICLE 1: AIDS OF THE AGREEMENT

Both parties agree to cooperate to provide a safe and effective reconstructive surgery for patients in Davao City born with facial deformities such as cleft lip and cleft palate.

--- [Page 5] ---
ARTICLE 2.1. OBLIGATIONS COMMON TO ALL PARTIES

Article 2.1.1 ADMINISTRATIVE AND PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT

2.1.1.1 The First Party will mobilize medical and non-medical volunteers to conduct free reconstructive surgery to Davao City patients with cleft lip and cleft palate.

2.1.1.2 The Second Party is responsible for supplying the human and material means in carrying out its task of finding patients afflicted with cleft deformities including the needs of its staff, patient, and parents/guardians e.g. food, transportation and other needs as deemed necessary to meet its aforementioned obligations.

2.1.1.3 The First Party shall be responsible for the assessment and evaluation of patients for possible surgical treatment of those born with cleft lip and cleft palate.

2.1.1.4 The First Party shall be responsible for the post-surgical treatment of the patients.

2.1.1.5 The Second Party shall ensure the safety and security of all personnel and volunteers during the Operation Smile Program.

2.1.1.6 Both parties are individually in charge of the recruitment, training, and administrative management of their personnel.

ARTICLE 2.1.1 FINANCIAL OBLIGATIONS

2.1.1.1 Each party shall bear individually the responsibilities towards its donors or other concerned parties for the implementation, follow-up, and evaluation of its part of the project.

ARTICLE 2.1.2 OPERATIONAL OBLIGATIONS

2.1.2.1 Both parties agree to name their respective focal persons in this undertaking. The focal persons are responsible for ensuring that the commitments of their respective institutions are materialized in the program and their respective deliverables are attained.

ARTICLE 3.1 OPERATION Smile Obligations

3.1.1 Operation Smile shall provide for and supervise over the medical and non-medical volunteers in the conduct of the free reconstructive surgeries;

--- [Page 6] ---
3.1.2 Operation Smile shall be responsible in the overall management of the program including but not limited to hospital arrangement, medical supplies, equipment, and personnel necessary for its operation.

ARTICLE 3.2 CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO OBLIGATIONS

3.2.1 The City Government of Davao shall designate the City Health Office as the focal agency in the implementation of this Program;

3.2.2 The City Government of Davao shall be actively represented by the City Health Officer in the Program as the Focal Person or any duly authorized representative;

3.2.3 The City Government of Davao shall actively initiate and lead in the information campaigns and other social mobilizations activities related to the Operation Smile Program;

3.2.4 The City Government of Davao shall properly refer screened patients for operation;

3.2.5 The City Government of Davao shall only provide Two Thousand Pesos (P 2,000.00) counterpart fund from the Lingap Para Sa Mahirap Program per Davao City patient with cleft lip and cleft palate to help defray the medical costs of Operation Smile Philippines chapter in Davao City. The said counterpart fund shall be paid through Operation Smile Philippines.

Article 4.1 DURATION OF THE CONTRACT

The present contract takes effect from the date of signature by the parties, and is effective until December 2013.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this Agreement on the day of , 2011 in Davao City.
