RESOLUTION NO. 10105
The Proposed Comprehensive Development Plan of Davao City for 1995-2003 as submitted by the Honorable City Mayor thru the City Planning and Development Coordinator which was under study by the City Planning Commission and the DPAP favorably recommended by the Committee on Urban and Socio Economic Planning and Development, Zoning, Human Settlement, Landed Estates and Real Properties, was presented for consideration and appropriate action by Councilor Cornelio W. Reza, III, Chairman of the said Committee.
Councillor Avilica manifested that the proposed Comprehensive Development Plan and the amended Zoning Ordinance of Davao City for 1995-2003 have undergone a series of committee public hearings, and have been discussed in several special sessions of the Body, whereby Continued on Page 2

all representatives of the different sectors of the City were invited and consulted to shed light on complete their ideas on how the Comprehensive Development Plan and the Amended Zoning Ordinance of Davao City should be to use, which is envisioned to cover the period from 1996 to reach until year 2021.

Several amendments were introduced by the Members of the Body to the Comprehensive Development Plan and the Amended Zoning Ordinance; however, Councilor Advincula averred that the approval of the Comprehensive Development Plan and the Amended Zoning Ordinance of Davao City is necessary for the City to approve the Development Plan and to update the existing Zoning Ordinance of Davao City being major component of SAGA-BIFP Polygon in two years. The existing Development Plan and Zoning Ordinance of Davao City could no longer accommodate the increasing demands of more land uses for establishments of industries, housing, institutions, and other land uses to meet the demands of a growing community to sustain its growing population.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councilor Victoria S. Advincula, duly seconded by Councilors Antonio M. Vergara, J. C. Mata, Reynaldo P. Reyes and Renita N. Ornullo and co-sponsored by Councilors: Bonifacio B. Pillitao, Shane L. Dolor, Alfredo R. Vergara, and David M. Dytaghunag, as amended, it was

RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve the Comprehensive Development Plan e' of the City of Davao to cover the years '996-2001, as amended;

RECOGNIZED, FURTHER, That the Comprehensive Development Plan for the years 1996-2001 submitted by the City Mayor thru the City Planning and Development Office and as induced to the Sanggunian Panlungsod on December 27, 1995, as further amended is approved and shall form part and constitute an integral part of this resolution comprising of pages 2-396;

RESOLVED, FINALLY, To approve an Ordinance, entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 3996

AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE COMPREHENSIVE DEVELOPMENT PLAN TO COVER THE YEARS 1996-2001, AS AMENDED, OF THE CITY OF DAVAO AND OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it Ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:
