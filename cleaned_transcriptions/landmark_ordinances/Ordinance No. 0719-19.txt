AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE PRICING SCHEME OF MARAPANGI HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT AS ENDORSED BY THE CITY PRICING COMMITTEE, OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR, AS THE MOST APPROPRIATE AND RESPONSIVE TO THE BENEFICIARIES OF MARAPANGI HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT, THROUGH ITS RESOLUTION NO. 2, SERIES OF 2017 AS AMENDED BY RESOLUTION NO. 3, SERIES OF 2018

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 071-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE PRICING SCHEME OF MARAPANGI HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT AS ENDORSED BY THE CITY PRICING COMMITTEE, OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR, AS THE MOST APPROPRIATE AND RESPONSIVE TO THE BENEFICIARIES OF MARAPANGI HOMES RESETTLEMENT PROJECT, THROUGH ITS RESOLUTION NO. 2, SERIES OF 2017 AS AMENDED BY RESOLUTION NO. 3, SERIES OF 2018".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Section 2 of Executive Order No. 45, Series of 2013, entitled "Reconstituting the Membership of Pricing Committee (SIR) Project and Urban Land Reform Program (ULRP) of the City," states that the committee shall have the power to determine fair market value, cost, interest and amortization charges for the acquisition, rehabilitation, socialization, housing program of the City of Davao such as but not limited to, Slum Improvement and Resettlement Program, Urban Land Reform Program, Relocation Sites, for sale and resale to the awardees/qualified buyers", as amended.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE: This Ordinance shall cover the approval of the Pricing Scheme for the Marapangi Homes Resettlement Project.

SECTION 4. PRICING SCHEME: The Pricing Scheme for Marapangi Homes Resettlement Project shall be implemented based on the following computation, viz.:

| Pricing/Lot | No. | Total Interest | Monthly | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | of Years | Rate (6% per annum) | Amortization Factor | Installation Price |
| P67.554.40 | 3 | P8,263.76 | P2,106.06 | P75,818.16 |
| P67.554.40 | 4 | P10,428.32 | P2,624.64 | P77,982.72 |
| P67.554.40 | 5 | P12,631.40 | P3,365.43 | P80,185.80 |

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of March, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
