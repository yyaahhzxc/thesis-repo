AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL TELECOMMUNICATION
COMPANIES, DAVAO LIGHT AND POWER COMPANY (DLPCC)
AND ALL OTHER PERSONS TO COMPLY WITH THE
UNDERGROUND CABLING PLAN OF THE CITY OF DAVAO

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL TELECOMMUNICATION COMPANIES, DAVAO LIGHT AND POWER COMPANY (D LPC) AND ALL OTHER PERSONS TO COMPLY WITH THE UNDERGROUND CABLEING PLAN OF THE CITY OF DAVAO";

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following terms are defined as follows:
a) Wires - refer to electrical and telecommunication wires;
b) Cables - an insulated conductor or group of conductors protected by a waterproof coat that are installed aerial or underground;
c) Installation - an assembly of power/telecommunication equipment in a given location, designed for coordinated operation, properly erected and wired;
d) Jurisdictional Area - refers to corporations, institutions and entities engaged in businesses mentioned in the Ordinance.
e) Telecommunications - any process which enables a telecommunications entity to relay and receive voice, data, electronic messages, written or printed matter, fixed or moving pictures, words, music or visible or audible signals or any control signals of any design and for any purpose by wire, radio or other electromagnetic, spectral or technological means;
f) Public Telecommunications Carrier (PTC) - shall refer to a duly enfranchised and NTC certified telecommunications carrier and/or entity duly authorized by law including the government to provide public telecommunications services (Reference: NTC MC No.9-7-93);
g) Service Interface - the conductors and equipment for delivering electric energy from utility to the wiring system of the premises served (Reference: 2005 Philippine Electrical Code, Part I, Volume 1);

**SECTION 4. PURPOSE**
- This Ordinance is enacted for the following purposes:
  a) To bolster the image of the City of Davao as internationally competitive, capable of attracting investments while ensuring sustainable development in its midst;
  b) To provide the City of Davao with a clear model of a sustainable and aesthetic response to the rising demands of progress and their impact to the environment;
  c) To enhance safety and security for the offices and establishments within the Central Business District including other known landmarks in the City of Davao;

**SECTION 5. COMPULSORY UNDERGROUND PASSAGE**
- All electric utility companies and public telecommunication carriers servicing the City of Davao, including but not limited to, others persons constructing, maintaining, installing aerial wires and cables shall place underground all electrical and telecommunication wires and cables within the area covered by this Ordinance;
- All building owners within the covered area shall provide service entrances compatible for underground electrical and telecommunication wires and cables;

**SECTION 6. PENALTY PROVISIONS**
- The following penalties shall be imposed on any or all violators of this Ordinance:
  a) After three (3) notices, any person or entity who violates this Ordinance, by maintaining, constructing or installing aerial antennas and towers not placed with underground wires and cables shall be fined the amount of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00);
  b) Upon the fourth and final notice, cancellation of his/her business permit;
  c) If the violation is committed by partnership, corporation, association, firm or other juridical entity, including government-owned or controlled corporation, the penalty shall be imposed on the president, general manager and each of the officers of these entities. In addition, the penalty shall also include the cancellation of its business license;

**SECTION 7. COSTS**
- The cost for placing the underground electrical and telecommunication wires and cables including the restoration of the excavated streets/ sidewalks shall be jointly shared among the electric utility companies and public telecommunication carriers as determined in the integrated design/ plan formulated by the Wires and Cables Technical Team;

**SECTION 8. WIRES AND CABLES TECHNICAL TEAM**
- To ensure that the objectives of the Ordinance are attained, there shall be created a Wires and Cables Technical Team through an Executive Order of the City Mayor;

**SECTION 9. FUNCTIONS**
- The Wires and Cables Technical Team shall have the following functions:
  a) Implement the integrated design/ plan within 30 days upon its creation;
  b) Establish coordinate and working relationship among members of the team;
  c) Supervise and monitor the full implementation of the Ordinance;

SECTION 11. CLEAN-UP OF EVERY PHASE AND RESTORATION – Within ninety (90) days after the full implementation of this Ordinance, all aerial wires, cables, and poles shall be removed by the respective electric utility company and public telecommunication carrier.

The streets/sidewalks that were destroyed as a result of the laying down of cables and wires shall be properly restored at the expense of Telecommunication Companies and Davao Light and Power Company;

SECTION 12. NOTICE TO THE PUBLIC – Upon the approval of this Ordinance, copies thereof shall be posted by the Secretary to the City Council at all the entrance of City Hall and the Sangguniang Panlungsod building and are given to all electric companies and public telecommunication carriers and all establishments within the area covered;

SECTION 13. EFFECTIVITY – This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and after publication of the full text of the Ordinance in a newspaper of general circulation in accordance with the existing laws and regulations therewith;
