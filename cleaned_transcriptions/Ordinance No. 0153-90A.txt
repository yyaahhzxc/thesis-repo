SEC. 3. Parking Fee - There shall be collected the amount of
Two ($2.00) Pesos for the first three (3) hours and Fifty (50.00)
Centavos per hour thereafter or a fraction thereof on vehicles other
than cargo truck, truck for hire and delivery truck which shall be
charged the amount of Five ($5.00) Pesos for the first two (2) hours
and Two ($2.00) Pesos per hour thereafter from the owners/drivers of
motor vehicles who avail of the city streets mentioned in Section 2
hereof as parking spaces; A one year ticket fixed at $500.00 per
vehicle shall be allowed with the issuance of the annual parking
sticker by the City Government, except cargo trucks and the like
which shall be charged P1,000.00 yearly;

SEC. 4. Collected Fees - Parking fees collected under this
Ordinance shall accrue to the City Government of Davao and form part
of the general fund;

SEC. 5. Time Coverage - This Ordinance shall be be observed and
enforced starting 6:00 o'clock in the morning until 9:00 o'clock in
the evening;

SEC. 6. Prohibition - In no instance should parking may be
designated or located in front of fire hydrants and fire stations;

SEC. 7. Traffic signs and pavement markings - The Traffic
Management and Traffic Control Unit shall prepare the details and
plan specifications and shall implement the installation of appro-
priate traffic signs and parking bay markings;

SEC. 8 - Parking Bay - The Traffic Management and Traffic Control Unit shall properly designate parking bays for cargo trucks, trucks for hire, delivery trucks and all other vehicles on all those designated/identified streets;

SEC. 9 - Parking Aides - The Traffic Management and Traffic Control Unit, for the purpose of this Ordinance, shall recommend to the City Mayor the hiring of Parking Aides who shall be charged with the duties of collecting the fees for the use of the pay parking facilities, using any form of official receipt to be issued to the driver and/or owner of the motor vehicle parked in said designated/ identified streets; Parking Aides shall be required to post a cash bond of $1,000.00;

SEC. 10 - Liability of Drivers and/or Owners - The drivers and/or owners of motor vehicles shall be liable jointly and severally for the non-payment of parking fees and for violation of any provisions of this Ordinance;

SEC. 11 - Appropriation for Initial Expenses - In order to immediately implement the provisions of this Ordinance, there shall be appropriated the amount of One Hundred Thousand ($100,000.00) Pesos from any sources of fund that the City Treasurer may designate to defray the initial expenses necessary to effectively and efficiently carry out the purposes of this Ordinance;

SEC. 12 - Penalty - Any person or persons responsible for the violation of any of the foregoing provisions shall be criminally prosecuted and upon conviction, be punished by a fine of not exceeding Five Hundred ($500.00) Pesos or (1) month imprisonment or both at the discretion of the court;

SEC. 13 - Repealing Clause - All other Ordinances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby deemed repealed, modified or amended, accordingly;

SEC. 14 - This Ordinance shall take effect after approval of the Department of Finance and publication of the same.
