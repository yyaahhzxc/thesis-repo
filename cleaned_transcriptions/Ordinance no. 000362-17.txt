ORDINANCE NO. 0362-17
Series of 2017

AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE DAVAO CITY INTER-AGENCY COORDINATING COUNCIL ON MARITIME SAFETY AND SECURITY (MARITIME COORDINATING COUNCIL), DEFINING ITS POWERS AND FUNCTIONS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0362-17

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in Session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. ALTERNATIVE TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the
"MARITIME COORDINATING COUNCIL ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES - The Davao City Government
shall endeavor to improve the quality of life of its residents by addressing the
challenges of criminality, terrorism, anarchy, insurgency, economic sabotage
and violent extremism through the maintenance of peace and order within its maritime
territory jurisdiction. It shall issue policies that would ensure interoperability
operations by all concerned government agencies charged to enforce maritime laws
and protocols within the maritime jurisdiction of Davao City;

SECTION 3. CREATION OF THE DAVAO CITY INTER-AGENCY
COORDINATING COUNCIL ON MARITIME SAFETY AND SECURITY - To carry
out the provisions of this Ordinance, there is, hereby, created the Davao City Inter-
Agency Coordinating Council on Maritime Safety and Security, hereinafter referred
to as the Maritime Coordinating Council;

SECTION 4. COMPOSITION - The Maritime Coordinating Council shall be
composed of:
a. The Chairperson - City Mayor of Davao
b. Vice Chairperson - Coast Guard District Southeastern Mindanao (CGDSEM)
c. Secretariat - Public Safety and Security Command Center (PSSCC)
d. Members:
i. Armed Forces of the Philippines Eastern Mindanao Command
ii. Davao City Police Office
iii. Regional Maritime Unit XI
iv. Naval Forces of Eastern Mindanao
v. Task Force Davao
vi. Central 911
vii. Tactical Operations Group XI
viii. Philippine Ports Authority XI
ix. Bureau of Customs XI
x. Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency XI
xi. National Intelligence Coordinating Agency XI
xii. Liga ng mga Barangay
xiii. Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources XI
xiv. City Agriculturist's Office
xv. Maritime Industry Authority XI;

SECTION 5. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS - The Maritime Coordinating Council
shall have the following powers and functions:

1. Implements measures to address all maritime safety and security
issues, conduct search and rescue operations, and enforce laws on
environmental protection in the maritime waters within the territorial
jurisdiction of Davao City in support of the concerned government
agencies;

2. Sustains and monitors the protection of the maritime environment and other issues and events that may affect the local safety and security situation of Davao City in support with the concerned government agencies and NGOs;

3. Conducts an instant and up-to-date City risk assessment on criminality, terrorism, maritime emergencies and smuggling happening in the maritime waters and coastal areas within Davao City and to take law enforcement actions relative thereto;

4. Develops plan to integrate and orchestrate law enforcement operations affecting public safety and security along Davao City coastal water;

5. Formulates and conducts trainings, seminars, workshops and other activities within and among concerned agencies for the proper implementation of the duties imposed in this Ordinance;

6. Collects and disseminates intelligence information among involved government agencies in order to reinforce and strengthen maritime law enforcement operations;

7. Analyzes and identifies target personalities, and determines the emerging threats and crimes that happened at the maritime waters of the City for possible law enforcement operations;

8. Recommends policies, plans, programs and projects for efficient interoperability operations among agencies involved in enforcing the duties imposed in this Ordinance;

9. Performs other duties and functions as may be directed.

SECTION 6. THE CHAIRPERSON. - The Chairperson shall be the Mayor of Davao City or his/her duly designated representative and shall exercise overall administrative and/or operational supervision and control to ensure the effective and efficient implementation of the policies laid down by the Maritime Coordinating Council;

The Chairperson shall also have the following functions:

1. Direct and supervise the operations and internal affairs of the council;

2. Establish the internal organization and administrative procedures of the council;

3. Recommend to the Maritime Coordinating Council the appointment, suspension, detail suspension, dismissal for cause of its subordinate officials and employees; and

4. Such other functions which the Maritime Coordinating Council shall deem necessary.

SECTION 7. MEETINGS. - The Maritime Coordinating Council shall meet regularly at least once every quarter or as often as necessary at the call of the Chairperson or by a majority of the members. A majority of the members shall constitute a quorum to do business;

SECTION 8. FUNDING. - The budget for the Maritime Coordinating Council and its entire operations will be under the Office of the City Mayor and shall be appropriated in the annual or supplemental budget. The members of the Council may be entitled to honoraria based on existing government accounting and auditing rules and regulations;

SECTION 9. ANNUAL REPORT --The Maritime Coordinating Council shall, within thirty (30) days after the end of every fiscal year, submit to the Mayor of Davao City and to Sangguniang Panlungsod an annual report on its accomplishments under this Ordinance, together with its plans and recommendations to improve and develop its capability to enforce the provisions of this Ordinance, and a complete accounting of transactions with respect to any funds received;

SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE - All other ordinances, and local rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly;

SECTION 11. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any provision of this Ordinance, or any part thereof, is declared invalid or unconstitutional, all other sections or provisions not affected thereby shall remain in force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, December 12, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
