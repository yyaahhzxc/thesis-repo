MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This AGREEMENT is made on the day of 2012,

BETWEEN

PHILIPPINE POSTAL CORPORATION, a government-owned and controlled corporation created by virtue of Republic Act 7354 with a business address at Liwasang Bonifacio, Manila, represented herein by its Postmaster General and CEO Mrs. MA. JOSEFINA M. DELA CRUZ, hereinafter referred to as PPC;

and

The City of Davao -LGU (City of Davao), having its postal and registered address at Ground Floor, City Hall Building, San Pedro Street, Davao City, represented herein by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE hereinafter referred to as City of Davao-LGU;

WITNESSETH THAT:

WHEREAS, City of Davao-LGU has requested PPC, to issue, circulate, and sell commemorative stamps and other philatelic products to propagate the objectives of the City of Davao-LGU for celebrating its 75th Year as Chartered City, known for its rich cultural tradition by celebrating the King of all Feasts, the Kadayawan Festivals; Davao City being known for its orchids particularly Waling-waling and the home of the highest mountain in the Philippines, the Mt. Apo which is the resting ground of the world-famous Philippine Eagle;

WHEREAS, PPC, being the sole government entity mandated under the laws of the Philippines to produce, print, circulate, and sell commemorative stamps and other philatelic products upon receipt of the letter of commitment from City of Davao-LGU, in the following quantities and for the corresponding prices:

1. City of Davao-LGU shall provide PPC with the designs to be depicted on the commemorative stamps. PPC shall print and deliver to City of Davao-LGU aside from the regular requirement of the Office for local and foreign stamp collectors and dealers, the commemorative stamps and other philatelic products upon receipt of the letter of commitment from City of Davao-LGU, in the following quantities and for the corresponding prices:

2. That the above commemorative stamps and other philatelic products shall be prepared by PPC and made available for circulation and distribution by PPC beginning on the exact date of issue, March 16, 2012.
3. Upon signing of this agreement, City of Davao-LGU shall pay in cash or by manager's check an amount equivalent to fifty percent (50%) of the value of the stamps, first day covers and other philatelic products ordered to be purchased by City of Davao-LGU. The fifty percent (50%) balance shall be paid upon approval of the final stamp designs by the PPC stamps committee.
4. That unless the total cost of the stamps and other related products ordered by City of Davao-LGU is paid, PPC shall have the authority to hold the printing of stamps and other philatelic products. Only upon payment of the full amount of the purchased stamps that the same shall be printed, delivered to City of Davao-LGU, circulated and/or sold to collectors and the mailing public.
5. The parties are independent contractors, and nothing in this Agreement shall be construed so as to constitute the parties as partners, joint ventures, agents, employees or representatives of the other for any purpose whatsoever.
6. If by reason of force majeure, either party (the "Frustrated Party") is delayed or unable, in whole or in part to perform or comply with any obligation or covenant of this Agreement, the Frustrated Party will be relieved of liability and will suffer no prejudice from failing to perform or comply during the continuance and the effect of the inability so caused from and after the happening of the event of Force Majeure, provided that it gives to the other party prompt notice of such inability and full particulars of the cause. If notice is not promptly given then the Frustrated Party will use reasonable efforts to perform after such notice is given. The Frustrated Party will use reasonable efforts to remove, so far as possible with reasonable dispatch, the cause of its inability to perform or comply. The Frustrated Party will give prompt notice of cessation of Force Majeure.
7. In the event that, due to Force Majeure, performance by a party is suspended the parties shall contact and/or meet each other immediately in order to agree upon the best solution in the circumstances regarding the arrangements set out on this Agreement.

Ord. No. 0263-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE PHILIPPINE POSTAL CORPORATION TO ISSUE, CIRCULATE, AND PRINT MEMORANDUMATIVE STAMPS AND OTHER PHILATELIC PRODUCTS FOR THE CELEBRATION OF DAVAO CITY'S 75TH ARAW NG DABAW ANNIVERSARY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vii) of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides in part, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this connection:

"Represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf, all bonds, contracts and obligations and such other documents upon authority of the Sanggunang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT - The Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) refers to the instrument to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the Philippine Postal Corporation (PPC).

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into with the Philippine Postal Corporation.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, March 6, 2012, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunan present.
