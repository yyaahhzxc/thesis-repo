**RESOLUTION NO. 1263**

**Proposed Ordinance of Commissioner Benigno D. Duno, Jr., requiring motorcycle drivers plying along City Streets and National Highways in the City of Manila to wear crash helmets while riding.** The said ordinance was recommended by the Committee on Traffic. The said ordinance is for the protection of the public from injuries sustained in traffic accidents involving motorcycle drivers who, because of lack of helmets, suffered serious head injuries which, in some instances, have resulted in death. It is proposed an amendment by requiring not only the drivers but also all riders to wear crash helmets of any kind and in any condition. The said amendment was referred to the court on any person violating the provision of the proposed ordinance.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Commissioner Benigno D. Duno, Jr., duly seconded by Commissioner Esteban Sarabia, it was

**RECOMMENDED AS IT IS DELIVERED. TO APPROVE AN ORDINANCE ISSUED.**

Resolutions No. 1389 and Ordinance No. 737, copies of 1970, continued.
ORDINANCE NO. 737
AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL MOTORCYCLE DRIVERS AND RIDERS OF ANY KIND WHO OPERATE THEIR MOTORCYCLES ALONG THE CITY STREETS AND NATIONAL HIGHWAYS WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY TO WEAR CRASH HELMETS OF ANY KIND WHILE OPERATING SAID MOTORCYCLES.
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANZPEZINENG PUNHANGUERO, CITY OF DAVAO, that:
SECTION 1. - All motorcycle drivers and riders plying along the City Streets and National Highways within the territorial jurisdiction of Davao City are hereby required to wear crash helmets of any kind while operating said motorcycles.
SECTION 2. - Any person violating the provision of this ordinance shall upon conviction be punished with a fine from PESO (P. 50) PER PIECE to PESO (P. 500) PER PIECE at the discretion of the Court.
SECTION 3. - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
