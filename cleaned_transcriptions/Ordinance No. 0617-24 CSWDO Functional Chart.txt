SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE FUNCTIONAL CHART OF THE CITY SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT OFFICE (CSWDO), SPECIFICALLY, SECTION 1 OF ORDINANCE NO. 2374-94, SERIES OF 1994".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES - The City Social Services and Development Office (CSWDO) is the local social welfare arm of the City Government of Davao mandated to provide basic social welfare programs and services to its disadvantaged citizenry. CSWDO was devolved and decentralized to the City Government of Davao on October 1, 1992, pursuant to Republic Act 7160 and City Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, which established the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO). The name of the office was changed from the City Social Welfare and Development Bureau to the City Social Welfare and Development Office in 2019.

A. Batas Pambansa Big. 337, Batasang Pambansa, February 10, 1983 "An Act Enacting a Local Government Code"

Chapter Four, Article Five: The City Social Services and Development Officer

Section 192. Appointment, Qualifications, Compensation, Powers and Duties

The City Social Services and Development Officer (CSWDO) shall be appointed by the city mayor, subject to civil service law, rules, laws and regulations. Qualifications of CSWDO are as listed and compensation is determined by law or Ordinance. Functions and duties are enumerated in this section.

B. Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, Article Thirteen, Section 483, "The Social Welfare and Development Officer", Section 483. Qualifications, Powers and Duties

The specific section of Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991) states the specific functions of the Social Welfare and Development Officer, and the section further elaborates that "The appointment of a social welfare and development officer is mandatory for provincial and city governments, and optional for municipal government."

C. Republic Act No. 7160, Title Four - Final Provisions, Section 534 Repealing Clause

Batas Pambansa Big. 337, otherwise known as the Local Government Code, Executive Order No. 112 (1987), and Executive Order No. 319 (1988), are hereby repealed.

Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, entitled "An Ordinance Approving the Organizational Structure and Staffing Pattern of the City of Davao, and for other purposes" approved the three (3) volumes of Report namely:

SECTION 4. AMENDMENTS TO THE FUNCTIONAL CHART OF ORDINANCE NO. 2374, SERIES OF 1994 - The attached Functional Chart of the City Social Services and Development Office in Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, shall be amended as follows:
1. The City Social Services and Development Officer in the attached functional chart of the City Social Services and Development Office in Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, shall be amended as City Social Welfare and Development Officer, and its functions and duties also amended.
Ordinance No. 2374 s. 1994
City Social Services and Development Officer
Performs functions enumerated under Section 192, Article Five ("5") of Batas Pambansa Art. 33, otherwise known as the Local Government Code, other laws, rules and regulations.
Amended To: City Social Welfare and Development Officer
Powers, duties and functions of a City Social Welfare and Development Officer is hereby enumerated under Republic Act No. 7160, Article Thirteen, and Section 483.

2. Assistant City Social Services and Development Officer in the attached functional chart of the City Social Services and Development Office of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, shall be amended to Asst. City Social Welfare and Development Officer.
3. The functions of the Administrative Division in the attached functional chart of the City Social Services and Development Office of Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994, shall be amended as stated below:

Ordinance No. 2374, Series of 1994

Directing and supervising administrative functions of the office relative to records management, personnel administration, supply and property utilization, janitorial services, maintenance and repair; Serves as the center of communication, formulates administrative policies so as to improve existing systems and procedures for effective and efficient delivery of services.
