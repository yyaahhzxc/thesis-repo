ORDINANCE NO. 0178-03
Series of 2003
AN ORDINANCE DECLARING RAMON MAGSAYSAI AVENUE, MONTEVERDE AVENUE, STA. ANA AVENUE, LEON GARCIA STREET AND THE WHOLE AREA BOUNDED BY THESE STREETS AS CHINATOWN OF DAVAO CITY
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 2
Ord No. 0178-03

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "DAVAO CITY CHINATOWN ORDINANCE";

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, as the legislative Body of the City, is empowered to enact ordinances for the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, specifically Section 16 of the said Code;

SECTION 3. TERRITORIAL BOUNDARIES. The boundaries covered by the Davao City Chinatown shall include portions of Barangay Nos. 2, 7-A, C and 3-O of the City of Davao, particularly encompassing the areas bounded by Ramon Magaysay Avenue (formerly Uyanguren), Monteverde Avenue, Sta. Ana Avenue and Leon Garcia Street;

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, December 16, 2003 by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
