**ORDINANCE NO. 048-10**
**Series of 2010**

**AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0159-05, SERIES OF 2005, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO," PARTICULARLY ARTICLE 30, SECTION 222 THEREOF, BY ADJUSTING, REDUCING AND/OR LOWERING THE RATES OF THE ARBITRARY FUND FROM "TWENTY-FIVE PESOS (P25.00) PER SQUARE METER PER DAY" FROM "TWENTY-FIVE PESOS (P25.00) PER SQUARE METER PER DAY" FOR THE PERIOD DECEMBER 7, 2010 TO JANUARY 2, 2011 ONLY, IN CONNECTION WITH THE "PASKO STREET MARKET," ONE OF THE ACTIVITIES OF PASKO FESTA SA DAVAO 2010.**

Be it ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

--- [Page 2] ---
**Page 2 of 3**

**Ord No. 048-10**

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance amending Ordinance No. 0158-03, Series of 2005, otherwise known as 'The 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao' particularly Article 30, Section 232 thereof; by adjusting, reducing, and/or lowering the rates of the ambulant vending fee (space occupied per square meter per day) from 'Twenty-five Pesos (P25.00)' per square meter per day" to 'Fifteen Pesos (P15.00)' per square meter per day" for the period December 7, 2010 to January 2, 2011 only, in connection with the "Pasko Street Market", one of the activities of Pasko Fiesta in Davao 2010.

**SECTION 2. AUTHORITY TO ADJUST AND/OR LOWER THE RATES** - Section 132 of the Local Government Code states that the Sangguniang Panlungsod has the power to impose tax, fee, or charge or to generate revenue through an ordinance. Consequently, the Sangguniang Panlungsod has the power to make adjustments, modifications, reductions, or lowering of fees;

**SECTION 3. ADJUSTED RATES** - There shall be an adjustment, reduction, and/or lowering of the rates of the ambulant vending fee (space occupied per square meter per day) from 'Twenty-Five Pesos (P25.00)' per square meter per day, as stated in Article 30, Section 232 of the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao, to 'Fifteen Pesos (P15.00)' per square meter per day.

**SECTION 4. PERIOD** - The said adjustment, reduction, or lowering of rates of the ambulant vending fee (space occupied per square meter per day) shall only be for the period December 7, 2010 up to January 2, 2011 on the occasion of the Pasko Fiesta in Davao 2010.

**SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE** - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, all sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

**SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY** - This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.

**ENACTED, December 7, 2010, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.**
