AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE MORAL RECOVERY PROGRAM IN THE CITY OF DAVAO

Ord. No. 0623-24

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. IIIIE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE MORAL RECOVERY PROGRAM IN THE CITY OF DAVAO".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City to strengthen the moral and spiritual fiber of the Davaeños rooted in Filipino culture and values that are pro-God, pro-people, pro-country, and pro-nature.

SECTION 3. OBJECTIVES -
1. To uphold the seven (7) core values of the City Government of Davao, i.e., attaining and sustaining optimum performance, quality service and ultimate client satisfaction, namely:
   1.1 Leadership;
   1.2 Good Governance;
   1.3 Service Excellence;
   1.4 Resiliency;
   1.5 Unity;
   1.6 Integrity; and
   1.7 Discipline.
2. To uphold the moral and spiritual values of government officials and employees.
3. To make Moral Recovery a part of the political agenda because government officials are expected to embody the lifestyle and the management of government, the qualities of voluntary simplicity, honesty, dedication, discipline, professionalism, active non-violence, deep sense of justice and an abiding faith of God. Then and only then can they carry out their duties faithfully and enunciate its vision of national greatness into a living and abiding reality.
4. To instill among the youth, through education and by role-modeling, the shared values of Discipline in the light of a "permissive society", upholding its Davao slogan "D.C.Plinado".
SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS
1. Agencies/Instrumentalities - are the units attached to the departments that operate and function in accordance with their respective characters, laws or orders creating them, except as otherwise provided in the Administrative Code.
2. Integrity Circles - are the basic operating units of MRP's structure and promotional mechanism organization whereby government and civil society are able to actively express create/promote/enhance/realize their members' values and integrity towards the achievement of the shared national vision. As such, they constitute MRP's basic operational structure to lay the necessary foundation of the moral recovery crusade for the Filipino core values infusion into the organizational culture, system and processes.

3. Institutionalization – refers to the Presidential Order to formally/officially recognize, accept and include the Moral Recovery Program vision, values, programs, strategies and structures in all government departments, offices, agencies, government owned and controlled corporations, and government offices and instrumentalities to formally allocate adequate and regular budget for this purpose.
4. Moral Recovery Officer (MRO) – refers to a person appointed or designated to initiate/convoke/inspire the formation of Integrity Circles or similar mechanisms, strategies and activities that would evoke mass action addressed at affecting personal change and lead to appropriate systematic and structural change within the organization.
5. Moral Recovery Program (MRP) – refers to the national campaign for moral renewal which includes the objectives, strategies and cumulative experiences generated in the course of implementing Presidential Proclamation No. 62, dated September 30, 1992. The proclamation calls for a moral recovery in the country and encourages active participation from all sectors of society. It recognizes the need for moral renewal to address issues such as graft and corruption, patronage politics, apathy, passivity, mendicancy, fictionalism, and lack of patriotism.
SECTION 5. DESIGNATION OF MORAL RECOVERY OFFICER (MRO)
The heads of government departments, offices, agencies, government-owned and controlled corporations, including non-government offices, shall designate a Moral Recovery Officer (MRO) to initiate, convoke or inspire the formation of Integrity Circles within the agency.
SECTION 6. CREATION OF THE MORAL RECOVERY COUNCIL
The Moral Recovery Council shall be created to carry out the provisions of this Ordinance, to be composed of government and non-government people, representing a cross-section of the Davao community, predominantly the inter-faith sector.
The City Mayor shall issue an Executive Order Creating the Moral Recovery Council directly under the Office of the City Mayor.

SECTION 8. FUNCTIONS OF THE MORAL RECOVERY COUNCIL (MRC)
1. The MRC shall prepare a "Moral Recovery Plan" after due consultation with the stakeholders;
2. The MRC shall formulate programs, projects and activities that would carry out its objectives;
3. The MRC, in coordination with HRMO, shall create "Integrity Circles" in all local government departments aimed at operationalizing the aims and objectives of the Moral Recovery Program;
4. The MRC, in coordination with HRMO, shall conduct training and seminars aimed at instilling the core values as envisioned in the Moral Recovery Program;
5. The MRC shall formulate its annual budget to be recommended to the City Mayor;
6. The MRC shall submit a Quarterly Report to the City Mayor and to the Sangguniang Panlungsod;
7. The MRC shall conduct other related duties/functions as expressed in their mandate.

SECTION 9. SECRETARIAT - The City College of Davao shall serve as the Secretariat tasked to document all the activities of the Moral Recovery Program and be the keeper of the records.

SECTION 10. MEETING - The Council shall meet regularly at least once a month or as often as necessary at the call of the Chairperson or by a majority of the members. A majority of the members present shall constitute a quorum.

SECTION 11. BUDGET - A budget may be provided, subject to existing government budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules, regulations, policies, and guidelines of the Commission on Audit, the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Government Procurement Reform Act, as well as other applicable laws, ordinances, and Presidential directives.

As per Executive Order No. 319, dated April 3, 1996, all government departments, offices, agencies, and Government-Owned and Controlled Corporations (GOCCs) shall allocate one percent (1%) of their Training Expenses appropriation for the implementation of Integrity Circles.

SECTION 12. FILIPINO VALUES MONTH - Per Proclamation No. 479, dated October 7, 1994, declaring the month of November of every year as the Filipino Values Month for a sustained Moral Recovery Program, the same may be further facilitated through vigorous and coordinated campaign measures, actively participated by Davaeños and all sectors of society.

SECTION 13. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, a part of the provisions of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the same shall not affect the validity of the other provisions hereof.

SECTION 14. REPEALING CLAUSE - All previous Ordinances, rules and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance shall be deemed repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 15. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, on the 3rd day of September 2024, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
