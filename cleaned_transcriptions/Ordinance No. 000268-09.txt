OFFICE OF THE CITY LEGAL OFFICER
Reference No. 1131-09
1st INDORSEMENT
October 7, 2009
Respectfully returned to the Office of the Sangguniang Panlungsod,
through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the hereto
attached Ordinance No. 0268-09 SERIES 2009 ENTITLED AN
ORDINANCE REMAINING NAME OF MONTEVERDE SPORTS
COMPLEX (PTA GROUNDS) LOCATED ALONG CAMUS STREET
BARANGAY 4-A, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY AS "PEOPLE'S
PARK", with the information that this office finds the same to be without
any legal infirmity and the enactment of the aforementioned ordinance is
well within the power of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, as provided for
under Section 13, paragraph (b) (5) of Republic Act 7160 otherwise
known as the Local Government Code of 1991.
Therefore, this Office recommends the approval of the same.
Respectfully submitted.
ATTY. MARISA A. GALLO, RSW
Legal Officer IV
