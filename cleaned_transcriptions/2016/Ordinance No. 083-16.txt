An Ordinance Granting Legislative Authority to the City Mayor to Sign the Deed of Conveyance to be Executed by the National Housing Authority (NHA) in Favor of the City of Davao for the Development and Covering Six (6) Lots Demarcated as Institutional Area of the New Matina Sites and Services Embraced in TIC Nos. Page 2 of 3
Ord. No 083-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN THE DEED OF CONVEYANCE TO BE EXECUTED BY THE NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY (NHA) IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO COVERING SIX (6) LOTS DENOTATED AS INSTITUTIONAL AREA OF THE NEW MATHAIA SITES AND SERVICES ENCRBED IN TCT NOS. T-224465, T-224466, T-224467, T-224470, T-224471 AND T-224472";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Paragraph b (1) (vi), Section 455, of the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. DEED OF CONVEYANCE - The Deed of Conveyance to be executed by the National Housing Authority (NHA) in favor of the City Government of Davao covering six (6) lots denoted as Institutional Area of the New Mathaia Sites and Services embraced in TCT Nos. T-224465, T-224466, T-224467, T-224470, T-224471 AND T-224472;

SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to accept and sign the Deed of Conveyance executed by the National Housing Authority (NHA) in favor of the City Government of Davao;

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval,

ENACTED, October 14, 2016, by a unanimous vote of the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO S. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head)
