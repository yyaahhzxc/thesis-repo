Order No. 0489-16

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO GLOBE TELECOMMUNICATIONS INC. TO INSTALL, MAINTAIN AND OPERATE A 42.0 METERS-HIGH SELF SUPPORT TOWER (SST) AT PUROK 28, BARANGAY MA-A, DAVAO CITY, SUBJECT TO THE COMPLIANCE OF THE REGULATORY FEE PROVIDED FOR IN ORDINANCE NO. 0463-13."

SECTION 2. LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY.- The Sangguniang Panlungsod hereby grants legislative authority to Globe Telecommunications Inc. to install, provide, maintain and operate a 42.0 Meters-High Self Support Tower (SST) at Purok 28, Barangay Ma-a, Davao City, subject to the compliance of the regulatory fee provided for in Ordinance No. 0463-13.

SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE.- All other laws, decrees, orders, issuances, rules and regulations which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 4. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE.- If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, such sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 26th day of January, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
