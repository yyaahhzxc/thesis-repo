Ord. No. 095-16

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF CURE PHILIPPINES, INC., FOR EXEMPTION
FROM SECTIONS 37, 41 AND 42 OF ORDINANCE NO. 092, SERIES OF 2000
(SIGNAGE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY) IN CONNECTION WITH THE EXHIBIT
"CRADEL OF VISION AND HOPE" ON NOV. 21-26, 2016, AT TEBOW CURE
HOSPITAL, DEC. 1-7, 2016 AT ABREZZA AYALA MALL AND DEC. 8-15 AT SM
CITY DAVAO;

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption solely refers to the payment
of applicable fees under Section 37, Chapter 10; and the exemption from
restricted areas for installation and display of any advertising and/or propagaand
materials under Section 41 and Section 42, Chapter 10, Ordinance No. 092, Series
of 2000, (Signage Ordinance of Davao City);

SECTION 3. - PERIOD - The exemption shall be from the passage of this
Ordinance up to December 15, 2016 only, and in no case shall the organizers be exempt
from compliance with other provisions of the Signage Ordinance of Davao City and other
applicable laws and Ordinances;

SECTION 4. - CONDITIONS

a. All limitations and conditions set forth in the Signage
Ordinance of Davao City and those additional Conditions
imposed by the City Engineer's Office (CEO), the
Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and
the Light and Power Company (LPC) in relation with the
installation and display of any advertising and/or
propaganda materials within the restricted areas must be
strictly complied with;

b. The City Government of Davao shall be recognized as event
partner in all programs, collaterals and banners;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, this 8th of November 2016, by a unanimous vote of the Members of the
Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
(City Government Department Head II)
