Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ACCEPTING THE DEED OF DONATION OF THE ROAD LOTS AND OPEN SPACES IN THE ELENA HEIGHTS SUBDIVISION II (ALTERED PORTION) LOCATED AT BARANGAY CALATULAN GRANDE, TALOMO DISTRICT, THIS CITY, EXECUTED BY URAYA LAND DEVELOPMENT, INC., REPRESENTED BY ITS VICE PRESIDENT, MR.
