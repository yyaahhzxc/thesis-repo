Ord. No. 002-16

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC ALONG A PORTION OF THE ROAD IDENTIFIED AS MONTIVERDE STREET (BETWEEN GOV. SALES AND GEMPESAW STREETS) FROM 5:00 PM TO 2:00 AM, ON JULY 22-23, 2016, RESPECTIVELY, AND FROM 2:00 AM OF JULY 24, 2016 UP TO 7:00 AM OF JULY 25, 2016 IN COMMEMORATION OF THE 19TH ARAW NG BARANGAY OF BARANGAY STA. ANA 27-C, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 21(c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:

"Any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or festive celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fair."

SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - In commemoration of the 19th Araw ng Barangay of Barangay Sta. Ana 27-C, Poblacion District, Davao City, the portion of the road identified as Monteverde Street (between Gov. Sales and Gempesaw streets) shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on the following separate dates:

July 22, 2016 - from 5:00 pm to 2:00 am of July 23, 2016;

July 23, 2016 - from 5:00 pm to 2:00 am of July 24, 2016; and

July 24, 2016 - from 2:00 am to 7:00 am of July 25, 2016;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, July 19, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.
