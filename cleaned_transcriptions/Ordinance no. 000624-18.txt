AN ORDINANCE DECLARING BARANGAY SIBULAN,

Ord. No. 0624-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE DECLARING BARANGAY SIBULAN, TORIL DISTRICT, THIS CITY, AS AGRICULTURAL ORGANIC ZONE AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLE AND POLICY - It is hereby declared as the policy of the City Government of Davao that:
1. The Local Government shall promote and protect the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them;
2. The Local Government shall ensure that the Dabawenyo have access to clean and safe food products free from harmful synthetic pesticides and fertilizers; and
3. The Local Government shall promote Organic Agriculture as a mode of agricultural practice to ensure that the health of the consumers as well as the producers of food products are protected.

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in the Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:
1. Buffer Zone: are defined areas on the boundary of an organic farm that has land use controls and allow only activities compatible with the protection of the organic farm. It serves as a barrier that surrounds or separates an organic farm from other conventional farms where plants are not designated as organic.
2. Conversion: is the process of transforming a traditional or conventional farm into an organic farm for a period of three (3) to five (5) years.
3. Conventional Farm: refers to farming systems which include the use of synthetic chemical fertilizers, pesticides, and other synthetic farm inputs.
4. Organic Agriculture: is a production system that sustains the health of soils, ecosystems, and people. It relies on ecological processes, biodiversity, and cycles adapted to local conditions, rather than the use of inputs with adverse effects. Organic Agriculture combines tradition, innovation, and science to benefit the shared environment and promote fair relationships among farmers, workers, and consumers.
5. Organic Farming: is a farming method that involves growing and nurturing crops without the use of synthetic-based fertilizers and pesticides.
6. Organic Zone: is a zone that will be protected from encroachment and contamination of chemical-based farm inputs, mining, including contamination of genetically engineered crops and other similar activities. This Zone shall be separate and distinct and shall not be confused with the Zones provided under the Davao City Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP).

**SECTION 4. DECLARATION OF BARANGAY SIBULAN AS ORGANIC AGRICULTURAL ZONE:** It is hereby declared that Barangay Sibulan, located at the third district of Davao City, is an Organic Agricultural Zone. As such, all synthetic and chemical-based farm inputs shall be prohibited within the said zone. Further, no genetically modified/engineered agricultural products shall be introduced therein.

**SECTION 5. BUFFER ZONE:** To preserve the integrity of the area as an organic agricultural zone and to prevent contamination from non-organic practices surrounding the area, a buffer zone shall be established.

**SECTION 5.1. A minimum of 40-meter buffer zone shall be established between the organic agricultural zone and non-organic agricultural areas near and/or adjacent to the former which shall be measured from the outermost boundary of the organic agricultural zone. Buffer Zones shall be planted with diversified plant species.**

**SECTION 6. FARM CONVERSION:** Non-organic farms within the organic agricultural zone shall have a maximum period of five (5) years from the date of approval of this Ordinance within which to convert their conventional farm to organic farm. No new conventional farm using synthetic chemicals shall hereafter be established within the organic zone.

**SECTION 7. INCENTIVES TO INDIVIDUALS, FARMERS AND SCHOOLS:** The City shall give incentives, awards, and financial support to individuals, farmers, and schools which pursue, promote, and practice organic farming. The City Agriculturist's Office shall formulate guidelines for developing incentive programs, award systems, and/or financial support.

**SECTION 8. IMPLEMENTING AGENCIES:** The City Agriculturist's Office (CAO) shall serve as the lead agency in implementing this order in collaboration with the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO), the Department of Agriculture Region XI (DA XI), the Department of Environment and Natural Resources Region XI (DENR XI), and Pesticides Authority Region XI (FFPA X I.) are hereby enjoined to give full compliance to the orders, and cooperation in the course of the implementation of the provisions of this order.

**SECTION 9. SUPPORT SERVICES:** Section 9 and Section 15 of the Organic Agriculture of Davao City 2009 or Ordinance No. 0384-10, Section 15 of 2010, state that provision of support services to organic farmers and other assistance as may be deemed necessary by the implementing agencies;

**SECTION 10. SIGNS:** The Barangay Sibulan LGU is hereby directed to provide appropriate signage in collaboration with the cooperative advocating organic agriculture and the City Agriculturist's Office indicating that the area is an organic agricultural zone, as well as the prohibited acts within the area.

**SECTION 11. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:** If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 12. EFFECTIVITY:** This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after completion of its publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.
