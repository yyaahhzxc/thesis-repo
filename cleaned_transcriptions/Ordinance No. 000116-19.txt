Ord. No. 0116-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0362-17, SERIES OF 2017, OR THE MARITIME
COORDINATING COUNCIL ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY, AS AMENDED".

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVE: This Ordinance aims to identify potential members to
be added in the Maritime Coordinating Council and make any other amendments as
deemed necessary;

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - Section 2 (Declaration of Principles) and Section 4
(Composition of Maritime Council) of Ordinance No. 0362-17, Series of 2017,
otherwise known as the "Maritime Coordinating Council of Davao City" shall be
amended as follows:

Section 2. Declaration of Principles

The Davao City Government shall endeavor to improve the
quality of life of its residents by addressing the challenges of
criminality, terrorism, anarchy, insurgency, economic sabotage violent
extremism and environmental protection through the maintenance of
peace and order within its maritime territory jurisdiction. It shall issue
policies that would enable interoperability operations by all concerned
government agencies charged to enforce maritime laws and protocols
within the maritime jurisdiction of Davao City.

Section 4 (Composition of Maritime Council)

Armed Forces of the Philippines East Mindanao Command is
hereby replaced with Joint Task Force Haribon; and the following
agencies/groups shall be represented mainly:

i. National Bureau of Investigation XI
ii. Department of Environment and Natural Resources XI
iii. Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction Management Office
iv. Bureau of Immigration XI
v. Peace and Public Safety Committee Chairperson of the
Davao City Council
vi. Davao Fishermen and Resort Owners Group
vii. Davao Fisher Folks Group
viii. Chairperson, Committee on Environment and Natural
Resources

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, such sections
or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 26th day of November, 2019, by an unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
