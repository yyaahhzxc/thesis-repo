**Ord. No. 030-07**

**SECTION 1. TITLE OF THE ORDINANCE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 89 (1) OF THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF DAVAO CITY".**

**SECTION 2. OBJECTIVE - This Ordinance aims to ensure equal and ample protection to local trade against foreign nationals doing business in the Philippines by setting up proper guidelines for monitoring and regulation of their business activities in order not to defeat public welfare through compliance of the requirements of health and sanitation, cleanliness and beautification, peace and order, zonification and the like in order not to defeat public service.**

**SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - Section 89(1) of the 2005 Revenue Code of Davao City will read, thus:**

**"1. For newly started business**

a) Location Sketch of the new business;

b) Paid-up capital of the business as shown in the Articles of Incorporation, if a corporation or partnership, or a sworn statement of the Capital invested by the owner or operator, if a sole proprietorship;

c) A Certificate stating to the tax exemption if the business is tax exempt;

d) Certification from the office in charge of zoning that the location of the new business is in accordance with zoning regulations;

e) Tax Clearance showing that the operator has paid all tax obligations in the City;

f) Barangay Clearance;

g) Three (3) passport size pictures of the owner or operator, or in cases of a partnership or corporation the picture of the senior or managing partners and that of the President or General Manager;

h) Health Certificate for all food handlers, and those required under this Code;

i) A birth certificate, marriage certificate or any valid identification.

Foreign establishments must also secure the foregoing permits and requirements before they can start operating their business together with the following additional requirements:**

a) Certified Photocopy of Passport

b) Certified Photocopy of Visa

c) Alien Employment Permit which may be submitted within ninety (90) days after issuance of the business permit

d) Alien Employment Permit from the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE).**
