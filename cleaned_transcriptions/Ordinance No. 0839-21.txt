Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod ng Davao City, in session assembled, that:

**SECTION 1. TITLE** - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0531-21, SERIES OF 2022, ENTITLED 'AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, ALL PROPERTY ACQUISITION DOCUMENTS RELATIVE TO THE ACQUISITION OF NINE (9) PROPERTIES LOCATED IN BARANGAY LANGUB, TALOMO DISTRICT, DAVAO CITY, REGISTERED UNDER THE NAME OF THE PHILIPPINE DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION (PDIC) WITH A TOTAL AREA OF 188,628 SQUARE METERS COVERED BY ITC NO. 146-2013010870 WITH 19,583 SQM, 146-2013010871 WITH 19,583 SQM, 146-2013010872 WITH 19,583 SQM, 146-2013010873 WITH 19,583 SQMETERS, 146-2013010874 WITH 19,583 SQMETERS, 146-2013010875 WITH 26,903 SQMETERS, 146-2013010876 WITH 20,893 SQMETERS, 146-2013010877 WITH 22,916 SQMETERS AND 146-2013010878 WITH 20,000 SQMETERS TO BE DEVOTED FOR HOUSING RESETTLEMENT."

**SECTION 2. PURPOSE** - The City of Davao intends to address the housing backlog to relocate informal settlers situated in danger areas as identified under Republic Act No. 7279 or the Urban Development and Housing Act. In its landbanking program, the City of Davao has officially expressed intention to acquire properties for the purpose of providing informal settlers with resettlement sites with basic services and facilities with access to employment and livelihood opportunities.

**SECTION 3. OFFER TO BUY** - The City of Davao, identified nine (9) properties owned by the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation located in Barangay Langub, Talomo District, Davao City. The City of Davao submitted an Offer to Buy said properties with the following details and amount of offer based on the market value reflected in the zonal valuation/real property tax declaration in the amount of One Thousand Two Hundred Fifty Pesos ($1,250.00) per square meter, totaling One Thousand Three Hundred Million Seven Thousand Six Hundred Twenty-Eight Pesos ($1,300,762,800.00). **SECTION 5. PURCHASE AGREEMENT**
In the negotiation with the representatives of the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation, the City Government of Davao requested for a three-year payment scheme with the following details:

1. The full purchase price shall be divided into three (3) installments with the alternative option of the full payment of the purchase price in the 2nd installment subject to the availability of funds;
2. If terms of purchase is pursued under a 3-year installment scheme, the 2nd payment shall be on or before March 2022 and the 3rd and final payment on or before March 2023;
3. Each installment shall be paid annually on the month of March.

Part of the purchase agreement is that all expenses such as the capital gains tax, documentary stamp tax, transfer tax, registration expenses, and other related expenses of the sale shall be borne by the City Government of Davao.

As an assurance that the City Government of Davao will be able to settle its financial obligation, the City Budget Office shall issue the availability of a continued appropriation and source of budget.

**SECTION 6. COVERAGE**
This Ordinance shall cover the approval of the purchase/sale agreement between the City of Davao and the Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation in the acquisition of the above-mentioned nine (9) properties until the purchase agreement and payments shall have been completed.

**SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
If for any reason, any section or provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY**
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

**ENACTED**, on the 14th day of December 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
