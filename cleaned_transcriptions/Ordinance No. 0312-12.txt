Ord. No. 0312-12

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AV ORDINANCE APPROVING THE USE/UTILIZATION AND/OR ALLOCATION OF PART OF THE SOCIAL HOUSING TRUST FUND BALANCE FOR THE CITY'S COUNTERPART FOR LARGE HOUSING PROJECTS OF THE CONSTRUCTION OF TWO HUNDRED (200) HOUSING UNITS WORTH P20 MILLION GRANT BY THE NHABASESETTLEMENT ASSISTANCE PROGRAM."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and, at the same time, ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use.

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS/OBJECT OF EXPENDITURE - The following amounts or to much thereof as may be necessary are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Accountant Office as in the Books of Account of the City Government of Davao under Trust Fund Davao City Socialized Housing Fund under IEP Current Account No. 1472-1037-56 as of April 30, 2012:

1. City Counterpart for Labor Component Cost for the Construction of Two Hundred (200) Housing Units worth P20 Million grant by the NHABASESETTLEMENT Assistance Program
P: 000,000.00
Leaving a balance of
P32,707,500.00

SECTION 4. SOURCE OF FUND - The fund balance in the Davao City Socialized Housing Fund under IEP Current Account No. 1472-1037-56 in the amount of P39,707,500.00 as certified by the City Accountant's Office to be available as of April 30, 2012 per Certification dated May 21, 2012.

SECTION 5. GENERAL PROVISIONS - The amounts herein appropriated for the projects indicated herein, shall be used specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod and must conform with existing government accounting and auditing rules, regulations, policies and guidelines as well as the other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.

The use of funds under this ordinance utilized in violation of Title 5, Local Fiscal Administration, as embodied in RA 7150, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, shall subject the erring officials and employees to disciplinary action or to appropriate legal measures under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full force and effect;
