ORDINANCE 006-01
Series of 2001

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 153, SERIES OF 1990, AS AMENDED BY ORDINANCE NO. 1767, SERIES OF 1990, ORDINANCE NO. 301, SERIES OF 1991, ORDINANCE NO. 687, SERIES OF 1992, ORDINANCE NO. 1062, SERIES OF 1993

Be it enacted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled;

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord.No. 006-01

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as, "An Ordinance Amending Ordinance No. 153-A, Series Of 1990, As Amended By Ordinance No. 1767, Series Of 1990, Ordinance No. 301, Series Of 1991, Ordinance No. 687, Series Of 1992, Ordinance No. 1062, Series Of 1993".

SECTION 2. AMENDMENT: The pertinent provision of Ordinance No. 153-A, Series of 1990, as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

SECTION 3. PARKING FEES: There shall be collected the amount of FIVE PESOS (P5.00) for the first two (2) hours or fraction thereof and TWO PESOS (P2.00) for every additional hour or fraction thereof, provided that the total fee shall not exceed TWENTY FIVE PESOS (P25.00) a day for a single vehicle. Furthermore, buses and trucks shall be charged TEN PESOS (P10.00) for the first two (2) hours or fraction thereof, and FOUR PESOS (P4.00) for every additional hour or fraction thereof, provided that the total fee shall not exceed FIFTY PESOS (P50.00) a day for a single bus or truck, for drivers and/or owners of motor vehicles who shall avail of the parking spaces mentioned in Section 2 hereof. A one year ticket fixed at ONE THOUSAND PESOS (P1,000.00) per vehicle shall be allowed upon the issuance of the annual parking sticker, except buses, trucks and the like which will be charged TWO THOUSAND PESOS (P2,000.00) yearly.

SECTION 4. ACCRUAL OF FEES - Parking fees collected under this Ordinance shall accrue to the City Government of Davao and shall form part of the General Fund of the City.

SECTION 5. TIME COVERAGE: This Ordinance shall be observed and enforced starting 7:00 o'clock in the morning until 5:00 o'clock in the afternoon daily except Sundays.

SECTION 6. PROHIBITIONS: It shall be unlawful for any driver and/or owner:

a. To cause non-payment of the use of the pay parking bays nor pay less than the amount equivalent to the actual number of hours;

b. To use parking tickets issued in one parking bay in a street for another street designated as pay parking zone, except in cases when the maximum amount per day has been paid;

c. To park vehicles outside the painted parking bays within a designated pay parking zone;

d. To park vehicles in places declared as no parking zones by any existing ordinance or law;

e. To park bigger and longer vehicles exceeding the length of one parking bay in any designated parking places;

SECTION 7. TRAFFIC SIGNS AND PAVEMENT MARKINGS: The Engineering Division of the Traffic Management Center (TMC) shall cause the preparation of the details and plan specifications of appropriate traffic signs and pay parking markings and shall implement the installation of the same.

SECTION 8. PARKING BAYS: The Traffic Management Center (TMC) shall properly designate parking bays for all kinds of vehicles on all designated streets specified in the Section hereof, as amended;

SECTION 9. PARKING FEE COLLECTOR: Ticket Checkers who are under the direct supervision and control of the Office of the City Treasurer shall be charged with the duties of collection of the fees for the use of pay parking facilities, using any form of official receipts of the City Treasurer to be issued to the driver and/or owner of the motor vehicle parked in said designated/identified streets. The Ticket Checker shall be required to post a cash bond of not less than SIX HUNDRED PESOS (P600.00) but not more than ONE THOUSAND PESOS (P1,000.00) in order to safeguard the funds that come into their possession. For this purpose, the Traffic Management Center will recommend to the City Mayor the hiring of not less than ONE HUNDRED (100) Parking Aides.

SECTION 10. NON-RESPONSIBILITY FOR LOSS OR DAMAGE: The City Government of Davao or its duly authorized representative shall not be responsible for the loss and/or damage of vehicles or any of its accessories or articles left therein;

SECTION 11. APPROPRIATION FOR INITIAL EXPENSES: In order to immediately implement the provisions of this Ordinance, there shall be appropriated the amount of TWO HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P200,000.00) from any source of funds that the City Treasurer may designate to defray the initial expenses necessary to effectively carry out the purpose of this Ordinance;

SECTION 12. LIABILITY OF DRIVERS OR OWNERS FOR DAMAGES: The vehicle owner or his driver shall be held for any damage caused by him or his driver to persons/s or any property/ies within the parking area. In all cases, it shall be understood that the vehicle owner or his driver shall strictly observe traffic rules and regulations in the designated parking areas;

SECTION 13. JOINT LIABILITY FOR NON-PAYMENT OF FEES: The drivers and owners of motor vehicles shall be liable jointly and severally for the non-payment of parking fees and for the violation of any provisions of this Ordinance;

SECTION 14. PENALTY: Any person or persons responsible for the violation of the foregoing provisions shall be criminally prosecuted and upon conviction, be punished by a fine not exceeding ONE THOUSAND PESOS (P1,000) or ONE (1) MONTH IMPRISONMENT or both at the discretion of the court;

SECTION 15. REPEALING CLAUSE: All other Ordinances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby deemed repealed, modified or amended accordingly.
