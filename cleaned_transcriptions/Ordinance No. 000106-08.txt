ORDINANCE NO. 0106-08
Series of 2008
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ARTICLE 13 OF
ORDINANCE NO. 2269, SERIES OF 1994,
OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "THE DAVAO CITY
INVESTMENT INCENTIVE CODE OF 1994",
AS AMENDED
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE AMENDING ARTICLE 13 OF ORDINANCE NO. 2269, SERIES OF
1994, OTHERWISE Known as "THE DAVAO CITY INVESTMENT INCENTIVE
CODE OF 1994, AS AMENDED".

Ord. No. 0106-08

SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS

2.1 Article 13 of Ordinance No. 2269, Series of 1994, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Article 13. Preferred/Priority Investment Areas: Incentives will be provided to new, expansion and diversification projects in the following areas (as amended by Ordinance No. 4603-A Series of 1997), to include:

INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGY (ICT) covering:

A Telecommunication facilities (infrastructure) such as:
i. Local exchange service (to include public calling offices/Alternative Communications Program);
ii. International gateway facilities;
iii. Any type of broadband service or infrastructure;
iv. Telecommunication projects involving satellites
    * Inter-exchange service
    * Global mobile personal communications
    * Trunk radio service
    * International satellite communications

B. Products and Services:
i. Contact/Call Centers
ii. Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)/ Off-shoring
iii. Computer software Programs and Applications Development

C. Infrastructures like IT Parks, Zones, Buildings or Centers;

SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE: Ordinance No. 2269, Series of 1994, otherwise known as the "Davao City Investment Incentive Code of 1994", as amended, is hereby further amended, and modified accordingly.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval,

ENACTED, July 1, 2008, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod
