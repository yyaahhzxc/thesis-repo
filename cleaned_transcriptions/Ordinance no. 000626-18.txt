ORDINANCE NO. 0626-18
Series of 2018

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2017 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, PARTICULARLY SECTION 82- TIME OF PAYMENT AND SECTION 93- TIME AND MANNER OF PAYMENT, AS AMENDED BY ACT NO. 11032, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "EASE OF DOING BUSINESS AND EFFICIENT GOVERNMENT SERVICE DELIVERY ACT OF 2018"

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
No. 0626-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2017 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, PARTICULARLY SECTION 82-TIME OF PAYMENT AND SECTION 95-TIME AND MANNER OF PAYMENT, IN CONFORMITY WITH R.P. ACT NO. 11032, HEREAFTER KNOWN AS, "EASE OF DOING BUSINESS AND EFFICIENT GOVERNMENT SERVICE DELIVERY ACT OF 2018."

SECTION 2. AMENDED PROVISIONS-The 2017 Revenue Code of the City of Davao, particularly Section 82-Time of Payment and Section 95-Time and Manner of Payment, shall be amended as follows:

2017 Revenue Code present provision:

Section 82. Time of Payment: The tax shall be paid once within the first twenty (20) days of January or in quarterly installments within the first twenty (20) days of January, April, July and October of each year. The Sangguniang Panlungsod may, for a justifiable reason or cause, extend the time for payment of such taxes without surcharges or penalties, but only for a period not exceeding six (6) months.

Proposed Amendment:
Section 82. Time of payment: The tax shall be paid once within the first Thirty-one (31) days of January or in quarterly installments within the first Thirty-one (31) days of January. The remaining quarterly installments are payable within the first Twenty days of April, July and October of each year. The Sangguniang Panlungsod may, for a justifiable reason or cause, extend the time for payment of such taxes without surcharges or penalties, but only for a period not exceeding six (6) months.

2017 Revenue Code present provision:

Section 95. Time and Manner of Payment: The fee for the issuance of a Mayor's permit shall be paid to the City Treasurer upon application before any business or undertaking can be lawfully begun or pursued and within the first twenty (20) days of January of each year in case of renewal thereof. For a newly started business or activity that starts to operate after January 20, the fee shall be reckoned from the beginning of the calendar quarter. When the business or activity is abandoned, the fee shall not be exacted for a period longer than the end of the calendar quarter. If the fee has been paid for renewal (if any), in its current quarter, and the business activity is abandoned, no refund of the fee corresponding to the unfulfilled quarter or quarters shall be made.

Proposed Amendment:
Section 95. Time and Manner of Payment/Renewal: The fee for the issuance of a Mayor's Permit shall be paid to the City Treasurer upon application before any business or undertaking can be lawfully begun or pursued and within the first Thirty-one (31) days of January of each year in case of renewal thereof. The renewal of business permit shall start every 2nd day of January for each year, unless the same falls on a Sunday or declared a holiday. (Pursuant to Sec. 11 (e) RA 11032)

--- [Page 3] ---
For a newly-started business or activity that starts to operate after the month of January, the fee shall be reckoned from the beginning of the calendar quarter. When the business or activity is abandoned, the fee shall not be exacted for a period longer than the end of the calendar quarter. If the fee has been paid for a period longer than the current quarter and the business activity is abandoned, no refund of the fee corresponding to the unexpired quarter of quarters shall be made.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions thereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December, 2018, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
