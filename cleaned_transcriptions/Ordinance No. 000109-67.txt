ORDINANCE NO. 109

AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING RULES AND REGULATIONS IN THE SALE,
DISPOSAL AND/OR ACQUISITION OF LOTS AND IN THE CONSTRUCTION
OF NICHES AND GRAVEMARKERS AND/OR TOMBSTONES AT THE DAVAO
CITY PUBLIC CEMETERY, M.A.4., THIS CITY.

Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - Disposal of graveyards or spaces for burials at the Davao City Public Cemetery shall be by lot.

A. - There shall be two kinds of lots for sale at la-a Public Cemetery, one lot for adults which shall measure 1.50 x 2.50 meters and lots for children which shall measure 1.00 x 2.00 meters;

SEC. 2. - Lots must be sold, disposed and/or ac-
quired consecutively in accordance to the actual need or actual numbers of deceased person to be buried and shall in no case be sold, disposed and/or acquired by re-
servation or for future use;

SEC. 3. - Lots for adults as mentioned in Sub-
section 1 of Section 1 hereof, shall be sold at P100.00; then children's lot shall be sold at P40.00 per lot;

SEC. 4. - Lots shall have been paid in full in accordance with Section 3 hereof, before the said lot could be utilized or used by its prospective owners;

SEC. 5. - For purposes of identification and reference, lots at the Davao City Public Cemetery shall be numbered consecutively with the corres-
ponding block number as indicated on the Sketch Plan of the City Engineer's Office, this City, referred to and known as Annexes "A" and "B," and shall be recorded and made an in-
tegral part thereof;

SEC. 6. - Construction of niches and grave-
makers and/or tombstones at the la-a Public Cemetery, in accordance with the approved plan and specification of the Office of the City Engineer, this City, referred to and known as Annexes "A" and "B," and shall be made an inte-
gral part thereof;

A. - In no case therefore, shall it be permitted that inscriptions in niches and gravemarker and/or tombstone, if any, con- construction of the same shall be made by the owner of the dead, other than that specified in Annexes "A" and "A-I," above-mentioned;

B. - Wording on the Tombstone shall be uniform in inscription thereof shall there- fore, be worded in the following manner:

| I | Date of Birth: |
| : | Name: |
| : | Date of Death: |

SEC. 7. - It shall be the duty therefore, of the official or officer in charge of the admin- istration of this cemetery to see to it that the above provisions shall be strictly followed and observed;

SEC. 8. - In addition to the purchase price of the land, if any, for the purpose that the gravemarker and/or tombstone shall be furnished by the city, there shall be col- lected from him (the owner of the dead) the amount equivalent to the following which shall be paid in advance:

Gravemarker and/or Tombstone:
Type 1 * * * * * P 12.00
Type 2 * * * * * 25.00
Type 3 * * * * * 250.00

as shown and indicated on the plan and speci- cation known as Annexes "A" and "A-I";

SEC. 9. - The owner of the dead is required to place the gravemarker and/or tombstone not more than three (3) months from the date of the burial;

SEC. 10. - Violation of any of the provisions of this ordinance shall be subject to a fine of not less than $100.00 or imprisonment of not less than 15 days, both fine and imprisonment at the discretion of the court;

SEC. 11. - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

ENACTED: April 4, 1967. Continued on Page 3.
