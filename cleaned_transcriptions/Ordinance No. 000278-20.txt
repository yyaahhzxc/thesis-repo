ORDINANCE NO. 0278-20
Series of 2020

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING RELIEF TO MITIGATE THE ADVERSE ECONOMIC EFFECTS OF THE CORONA VIRUS DISEASE (COVID-19)

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0278-20

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING RELIEF TO MITIGATE THE ADVERSE ECONOMIC EFFECTS OF THE CORONA VIRUS DISEASE (COVID-19)".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - Section 192, Republic of Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that "Local Government Units may, through ordinances duly approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs under such terms and conditions as they may deem necessary".

SECTION 3. INCENTIVES/RELIEF - Any person firm, or entity conducting business in the City of Davao is hereby granted the following incentives/relief in view of the COVID-19 pandemic:
1. Proportional Reduction of Garbage Fee for Calendar Year (CY) 2021;
2. Deductibility from Gross Sales/Receipts of the Items provided under Section 5 hereof;
3. Waiver of Rental Fees for those Real Properties under the City Economic Enterprise Closed during Community Quarantine (CQ) and Enhanced Community Quarantine (ECQ);
4. Suspension of Implementation of Final Tranche of Increase in Real Property Payment.

SECTION 4. PROPORTIONAL REDUCTION OF GARBAGE FEE FOR CY 2021 - Business establishments which were mandated to close during the period of CQ and ECQ may avail of proportional reduction in the annual garbage fee imposed under Section 293 of Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017.

The garbage fee shall be reduced based on the number of months the establishment was closed, provided such closure is within the period of CQ and ECQ.

The proportional reduction of the amount of garbage fee is considered as advance payment and shall be applied only during the renewal period for CY 2021.

SECTION 5. DEDUCTIBILITY FROM GROSS SALES/RECEIPTS - The following shall be deductible from the CY 2020 gross sales/receipts of business establishments and shall be applied during the CY 2021 renewal:
1. Waived rental fees granted by lessors to lessees, provided that a duly notarized Certification from the lessee evidencing such waiver is submitted;
2. Monetary value of contributions of establishments utilized as quarantine facilities; and
3. Monetary value of donations made to the City Government of Davao as aid to respond to COVID-19 situation;

The gross sales/receipts less the aforementioned deductible amount shall be the basis for the computation of business taxes for CY 2021.

SECTION 6. WAIVER OF RENTAL FEES - Rental fees for real properties under the City Economic Enterprise which are mandated to be temporarily closed during the period of CO/ECQ shall be deemed waived.

SECTION 7. SUSPENSION OF THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE FINAL TRANCHIE OF THE INCREASE IN REAL PROPERTY TAX PAYMENT - The implementation of the final tranche (3/3) of the increase in real property tax payment as provided for in Ordinance No. 0257-17, Series of 2017, otherwise known as "An Ordinance approving the Schedule of Market Values of All Lands and Base Unit Construction Cost as Basis in the 2018 General Revision of Real Property Assessments in Davao City and to Take Effect Beginning Calendar Year 2019" due on calendar year (CY) 2021, shall be suspended and moved to CY 2022.

SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, June 9, 2020, by unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
