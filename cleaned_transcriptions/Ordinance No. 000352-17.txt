Ord. No. 0352-17

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING PERTINENT PROVISIONS OF ORDINANCE NO. 0463-13, SERIES OF 2013 OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING REGULATION OF THE CONSTRUCTION AND MAINTENANCE OF CELLULAR TOWERS FOR MOBILE PHONE SERVICES IN THE CITY OF DAVAO";

SECTION 2. OBJECTIVE - This Ordinance aims to provide clearer guidance on telecommunication companies in the construction, maintenance, and operation of cellular towers for mobile services;

SECTION 3. AMENDMENT - The following provisions of Ordinance No. 0463-13, Series of 2013, otherwise known as "An Ordinance Providing for the Regulation of the Construction and Maintenance of Cellular Towers for Mobile Phone Services in the City of Davao":

Section 6. Guidelines in Securing Locational Clearance:
"g. Building Permit-After securing the required Sangguniang Panlungsod resolution allowing or interposing no objection for the telecommunication company to construct its tower, but prior to the issuance of building permit, the applicant shall submit the requirements as stated in paragraph f, subparagraphs (i) to (xii) in this Section, including the clearance to construct in accordance with Republic Act 927, provided however, that the issue of building permit shall be subject to the assessment and evaluation of the building plan by the Office of the Building Official.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, on the 28th day of November, 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
Charito M. Santos
