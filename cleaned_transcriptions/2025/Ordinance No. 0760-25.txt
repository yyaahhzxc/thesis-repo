Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,

that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT FIELD OFFICE XI AND THE CITY OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE RISK RESILIENCY PROGRAM THROUGH CASH-FOR-TRAINING AND CASH-FOR-WORK (RRP-CFTW)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22(a), 455(b)

(1) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991", provide:

"Section 22. Corporate powers

(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:

(5) To enter into contracts; and

(b) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall.

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunan panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Department of Social Welfare and Development Field Office XI and the City of Davao, relative to the Risk Resiliency Program through Cash-for-Training and Cash-for-Work (RRP-CFTW).

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) made executed this day of 2024 in the City/Municipality of by and between:

THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT FIELD OFFICE XI, a government office existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with principal office address located at R. Magaysay Avenue, corner Damaso Suazo Street, Davao City, represented by its Regional Director, MERLINDA A. PARAGAMAC, and herein referred to as the "DSWD FO XI" -and-

The CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at San Pedro St., Davao City, Davao del Sur, herein represented by its Mayor, SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE in his capacity as the City Mayor, herein referred to as the CLGU.

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, it is the National Government's policy to implement poverty alleviation projects in all regions;

WHEREAS, the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) is the primary government agency mandated to develop, implement, and coordinate social protection and poverty-reduction solutions for and with the poor, vulnerable, and disadvantaged;

WHEREAS, the DSWD provides additional cash-based interventions to support the economically-poor, and climate and disaster-vulnerable families in exchange for community work participated and capacity-building activities attended under its Risk Resilience Program through Cash-For-Training and Cash-For-Work (RRP-CFTW), pursuant to the provisions of DSWD Memorandum Circular No. 04, series of 2021;

WHEREAS, the RRP-CFTW is designed to strengthen climate change and disaster response actions by reducing vulnerabilities of the communities through interventions on food security, water sufficiency, adaptive and shock-responsive social protection, ecological and environmental stability, and knowledge and capacity development in line with policies contained in the Philippine Development Plan 2023-2028, National Climate Change Action Plan 2011-2025, National Framework Strategy on Climate Change, and National Disaster Risk Reduction Management Plan in achieving economic and social transformation for a prosperous, inclusive, and resilient society;

WHEREAS, the DSWD Field Office XI has been given the responsibility to spearhead the overall management and implementation of the RRP-CFTW to provide additional income support to poor families for a period of twenty days;

WHEREAS, the City of Davao in partnership with the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), shall implement Project LAWA and BINHI for a period of ten (10) years, ensuring its sustainability and continued impact on target beneficiaries;

WHEREAS, the City of Davao has a designated number of target eligible beneficiaries, and the program shall be implemented in accordance with the approved budget allocation for each year, as determined by the Work and Financial Plan (WFP) and available funding;

--- [Page 11] ---
WHEREAS, the implementation of the program shall adhere to the agreed-upon guidelines, ensuring effective service delivery and maximizing benefits for the intended recipients;

WHEREAS, implementation of prior approved projects and activities will continue in case of the assumption of new Local Chief Executive Officer-in-Charge due to election and/or emergencies.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and for a mutual consent in the understanding set forth, the Parties hereto have agreed, as they hereby agree as follows:

1. The DSWD Field Office XI shall:
a. Spearhead the project management at the regional level and provincial level;
b. Lead the conduct of meetings, workshops, planning activities, project implementation review, among others with the CLGU and/or TWG;
c. Coordinate with implementing CLGU and other local stakeholders for the following purposes:
- Notification on general guidance and/or implementing updates, as well as the conduct of technical assistance/orientation if deemed necessary;
- Co-lead the review of the project proposal;
- Co-lead the actual site validation for related activities in project areas;
- Review and submission of feedback/recommendations to the PGUs based on the review and site validation results;
- Further validation of the beneficiaries as deemed necessary and acceptance of materials;
- Endorse/recommend for approval to DRBM on project-related documents;
d. Conduct orientation, field monitoring and provision of technical assistance to the CLGU;
e. Undertake direct payment of cash assistance to the beneficiaries in close coordination with mobilization of concerned CLGU;
f. Consolidate and submit physical and financial accomplishment reports (project completion report after the completion of the project to the DRBM and FMS);
g. Coordinate with the regional counterpart of the Department of Agriculture for continuous provision of learning exchange and technical assistance.

2. The Local Government Unit shall:
a. Lead the implementation of the project and all related activities detailed in the proposal;
b. Through the City Social Welfare and Development Office under its Family/Community Welfare Program Section, organize a technical working group (TWG) composed of the following: (1.) City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO), (2.) City Planning and Development Office (CPDO), (3.) Barangay & Cultural Communities Office (BCCO), (4.) City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO), (5.) City Agricultures Office (CAGRO), and (6.) City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWD). CSWDO shall coordinate with the DSWD Field Office in the preparation, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and reporting of the RRP target and accomplishment;
c. Through the CSWDO to prepare, revise, and submit the project proposal, work plan, and project timeline to DSWD Office for review and assessment;
d. Pre-identify potential partner-beneficiaries for the implementation of related activities;
e. Provide resource augmentation of fingerlings, vegetable seedlings from CAGRO. The CENRO shall ensure the management of the City's natural resources and ensure the delivery of basic services from other LGU partners;
f. Through Barangay Local Government Units (BLGUs) and the CSWDO Direct Service Implementer, lead the identification and selection of potential partner-beneficiaries. The CSWDO shall lead validation process of potential partner-beneficiaries as well as the

--- [Page 12] ---
administration of the beneficiary profiling through Family Assistance Card in Emergencies and Disasters (FACED);
g. Mobilize the CLGU and communities or partner beneficiaries as deemed necessary;
h. Identify barangays as potential partner beneficiaries of the project;
i. Through CSWDO, after the latter's validation of potential partner beneficiaries and beneficiary profiling, submit master list of eligible partner beneficiaries to the FO;
j. Through CSWDO, conduct social preparatory activities with BLGUs and partner beneficiaries;
k. The Organize TWG composed of CSWDO, CDRRMO, CENRO, BCCAD, CEO, CAGRO shall conduct joint field monitoring with the DSWD on "pre", "during" and "post" implementation;
l. Through CSWDO, to prepare and submit post-implementation documentary requirements such as Daily Time Record, Work Accomplishment Report, Photo Documentation on before, during, and after activities;
m. Draft and pass relevant ordinances to ensure the sustainability of the project; and
n. To ensure continuous community support by providing support training and mentoring of different leaders of the associations beneficiaries.
3. Qualified Beneficiaries are the following:
a. Any individual whose family is part of farmers, fisher folks, and/or indigenous peoples sector classified as poor by either the DSWD's Listahanan or by the LWSDO's assessment except for government employees regardless of status of employment (permanent, contractual, casual, or unemployed) individuals who are regularly receiving home loans or allowances to climate resilient and climate vulnerable families in any National Government Agency (NGA), Government-owned and Controlled Corporations (GOCC), and LGUs.
b. Other climate-vulnerable and disaster-vulnerable poor families given with appropriate tasks commensurate to their capabilities and competency to perform work;
c. Only one member per family aged at least 18 years old at the date of participation who resides within the program's target areas shall participate, no more than one project per calendar year per beneficiary. For those who are unable to perform due to disability, sickness and other valid reasons, a minor member may be considered based on the assessment of the LGU provided that the minor will be given a task and fair volume of workload that can be handled by him/her based on the assessment of the LGU, without reduction of grant/assistance as stated in the DSWD Memorandum Circular No. 4, Series of 2021.
4. Type of Projects and Activities - Qualified beneficiaries may be engaged in the following Cash-For-Training and Cash-For-Work activities and related activities that are focused on the following:
a. Food security through planting of climate-resilient and disaster-resilient crops and fruit-bearing trees;
b. Water sufficiency through construction of water harvesting facilities;
c. Ecological and environmental preservation;
d. Other activities in line with an approved Local Climate Change Action Plan (LCCAP), Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Plan (LDRRMP), and other Local Development Plan.
5. Rate of Assistance - Beneficiaries shall be paid 100% of the Regional Daily Minimum Wage prescribed by the Department of Labor and Employment for twenty (20) working days, based on the approved Work and Financial Plan (WF/P) of DSWD FO XI.
6. Documentary Requirements - (1.) Project Proposal, (2.) Program of Work, (3.) Master list, and (4.) other reference materials. These will be facilitated and submitted by the City Social

--- [Page 13] ---
Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) to be submitted to the Department of Social Welfare and Development RXI.

7. Implementation Timeline - All approved projects and activities shall be implemented within the first semester of the implementation year. Any funds allocated for cash assistance that are not disbursed within the set timeline will be redirected to other LGUs to ensure efficient fund management.

8. Fund Liquidation - shall be liquidated by the CSWDO supported with the hereunder documentary requirements consistent with government accounting and auditing rules and regulations:
- Daily attendance sheet is admissible to record the actual engagement of CFTW beneficiaries in the community works;
- Photo Documentation of the community works undertaken before, during, and after the project implementation;
- Project Completion Report or Work Accomplishment Report; and
- Other internal control measures required by the DSWD.

9. Venue of Actions - any legal actions arising from or in connection with this Agreement must be lodged before the Proper courts in Davao City only;

10. Separability Clause - In case any provision herein or any part therein be declared void or unenforceable by competent authorities, the remainder unaffected by such declaration shall remain valid and binding between the parties, their successors and assigns.

This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing of the document by the official representative of the DSWD FO XI and the Local Government Unit of Davao City and shall remain in force until full utilization of the whole amount, unless sooner terminated in writing by both parties.

The duration of this Memorandum of Agreement shall be for ten (10) years only subject to renewal upon mutual consent of both parties unless sooner revoked by either party for which a thirty (30)-days prior written notice will have to be observed.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have heretofore affixed their signature on the date at the places first above written.
