Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI AND ITS ATTACHED AGENCIES, AND THE CITY OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE ESTABLISHMENT AND UPGRADING OF REGIONAL AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERIES BORDER CONTROL FACILITIES IN REGION XI."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 22 (a) (5) and (c) and 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provide that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers. -
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts; and
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanguanion concerned.
A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall;
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor, hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Department of Agriculture - Regional Field Office XI and its attached agencies, and the City Government of Davao, relative to the establishment and upgrading of regional agricultural and fisheries border control facilities in Region XI.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT, entered into by:
The DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI (DA - RFO XI), a government office created under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at F. Bangoy St., Davao City, represented herein by its Regional Executive Director, MALACARO D. GONZAGA, and hereinafter referred to as the DA- RFO XI;
and
The BUREAU OF ANIMAL INDUSTRY - VETERINARY QUARANTINE SERVICES XI (BAI- VQS XI), a government office created under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at Sasa Port, Sasa, Davao City, represented herein by its Regional Quarantine Officer, MARIA TERESA R. BACAYO, and hereinafter referred to as the BAI- VQS XI;
and
The BUREAU OF PLANT INDUSTRY - PLANT QUARANTINE SERVICES XI (BPI- PQS XI), a government office created under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at Sasa Port, Sasa, Davao City, represented herein by its Regional Quarantine Officer, FLOR DE JASMIN C. BAYO, and hereinafter referred to as the BPI- PQS XI;
and
The BUREAU OF FISHERIES AND AQUATIC RESOURCES XI (BFAR- XI), a government office created under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at Ramon Macasyagay Ave, Poblacion District, Davao City, represented herein by its Regional Director, RELLY B. GARCIA, and hereinafter referred to as the BFAR XI;

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY, a local government unit created under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall Bldg., San Pedro St., Davao City, represented herein by its Mayor, Honorable SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE hereinafter referred to as the Proponent Local Government Unit/LGU.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, DAVAO REGION, designated as Region XI, is located in the southeastern portion of Mindanao. It consists of 5 provinces, namely, Davao de Oro, Davao del Norte, Davao Oriental, Davao del Sur, Davao Occidental, and 1 highly-urbanized city, Davao City;
WHEREAS, it is located in the Southeastern portion of the Island of Mindanao surrounding the Davao Gulf. It is bounded on the North by the provinces of Surigao del Sur, Agusan del Sur, and Bukidnon, on the East by the Philippine Sea; and on the West by the Central Mindanao Provinces;
WHEREAS, in terms of annual growth in the production value in the agriculture and fisheries sector, it was recorded in Davao Region at 3.4 percent in CY 2022,

--- [Page 11] ---
WHEREAS, before the effects of African Swine Fever (ASF), Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza (HPAI) and Banana diseases that devastated the livestock and banana production in the region, Region XI flourished in its livestock, poultry, and banana sector in terms of national production when compared with other regions;

WHEREAS, following the struggle caused by the ASF that affected the region last January 2020 up to the present, and brought forth the decrease in hog production in CY 2021 and its continuous effect on the hog sector and its allied industries up to the present especially among the smallholder hog farmers;

WHEREAS, the threat of avian influenza which once affected the Davao Region last CY 2022, and incidences continuously reported in several neighboring regions of Davao, moreover, there is an influx of poultry reported entering Region XI from other regions in Mindanao due to an increase in the number of established dressing plants in Davao Region and closure of dressing plants in other regions as part of strategic developments of some companies involved in the poultry industry;

WHEREAS, in the agricultural crop sector, the continued incidence of banana diseases especially the fusarium will provoke a continuous threat to the banana industry up to the present;

WHEREAS, export commodities such as durian, coffee, and cacao, wherein Davao Region is among the top producers are also at risk of pests and diseases such as mealy bugs, scale insects, fruit rot, and root rot and other diseases that may cause significant losses to the economy and importing countries require strict compliance that the fruits and plants are pest and disease-free;

WHEREAS, to address the Illegal, Unreported, Unregulated Fishing (IUUF) activities in relation to trade and transport of fish and fishery products, aquatic wildlife, by-products, or derivatives, the BFAR-XI implements regulations and enforcement measures to ensure sustainable and legal fishing practices;

WHEREAS, the threat to aquatic animals through the introduction and spread of diseases may endanger the health of aquatic animals and the production of local fishermen/farmers. Moreover, the importance of Local Transport Permit (LTP) and Health Certificate (HC) in every transport of aquatic animals for traceability;

WHEREAS, the strategic geographical location of the region which also served as a hub for transport and shipments of products and by-products throughout Mindanao also denotes its vulnerability to the entry of various livestock, poultry, and agricultural diseases and pests such as the risk for ASF, Avian Influenza (AI) and other economically important and transboundary animal diseases (TADs) of animals and plants;

WHEREAS, at present, there are four (4) BI quarantine stations - located in Sasa Wharf in Davao, at the Davao International Airport, at the Davao Fish Port Complex, Toril, Davao City and in Mati, Davao Oriental and 4 BFI quarantine stations - located in Sasa Wharf in Davao, at the Davao International Airport, at the Davao Fish Port Complex, Toril, Davao City and in the Philippine Postal Office, Davao City; and 3 BFAR quarantine stations - located in Davao International Airport, at the Davao Fish Port Complex, Toril, Davao City, and in the BFAR-XI Regional Office, Davao City;

WHEREAS, there are no properly established in-land agricultural and fisheries border control facilities in the regional boundaries of Region XI;

WHEREAS, there are makeshift border control checkpoints established in several areas in the region to provide temporary sites for the evaluation of documentary requirements and inspections of vehicles transporting livestock and agricultural products and by-products, and fish and fish by-products.

WHEREAS, these are the identified Regional and Provincial boundaries in Davao Region that need to be secured, and border control facilities should be established;

--- [Page 12] ---
WHEREAS, with Region XI's vast land area, growing populace, and fast-paced movement, the existing number of makeshift border control checkpoints is deemed inadequate; thus, proper and well-equipped border control facilities should be established and existing checkpoints be upgraded to maximize its potential and attain its vision of securing Region XI from the threat of animal diseases and plant pests;

WHEREAS, it is on this premise that this project was conceptualized, anchored, and proposed. However, for the sustainability and maintenance of this project, collaboration through the identification of responsibilities with Local Government Units (LGUs) shall be implemented;

WHEREAS, pursuant to such cooperating agency's intention, the DA-NAFO, BAI-VQXL BPI-PQS XI, BFAR XI, and the LGU jointly pledge to extend full support and cooperation for the successful implementation of the project entitled "Establishment and Upgrading of Regional Agricultural and Fisheries Border Control Facilities in Region XI";

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises and their mutual covenant herein set forth, the parties hereby agree on the following:

Section 1. Objectives. The objectives of the Memorandum of Agreement are:

1.1 To strengthen regional border control measures in Region XI against the threat of plant and animal pests and aquatic diseases particularly transboundary diseases by providing quarantine checkpoints to monitor the incoming transport of animals and agricultural products, by-products, and other agricultural, and natural resources, and fish and fishery products in Region XI for any type of pest and disease;

1.2 To provide an area for checking of completeness of regulatory requirements for the transport/shipment of plants, animals, and other agricultural products and natural resources, and fish and fishery products and aquatic wildlife, by-products or derivatives to IUIF;

1.3 To provide support to DA agencies and LGU's mandate in the implementation of disease prevention, control, eradication and eradication of plant and animal diseases (terrestrial and aquatic), thereby, ensuring food security to its populace;

1.4 To strengthen the implementation of the regulatory agencies under the Department of Agriculture for the enforcement of food safety standards and regulations in the primary and post-harvest stages of food derived from animals including eggs and honey.

Section 2. Scope of Agreement. This Memorandum of Agreement covers the implementation of the establishment of the Regional Agricultural and Fisheries Border Control Facility with Common Service Facilities from DA-RFO XI to the City Government of Davao City intended to serve as a logistical support from BA-Regional Field Office XI to BFARM for the operationalization and maintenance of the established Regional Agricultural and Fisheries Border Control Facilities in Region XI in Brgy. Buda, Marilogo District, Davao City.

Section 3. Obligations of the Parties Concerned

3.1 Duties and Responsibilities of the DA-NAFO XI - The DA-Regional Field Office XI (RFO XI), shall have the following duties and responsibilities:

3.1.1 Provide funds under FOD: Livestock and facilitate the establishment of a Regional Agricultural and Fisheries Border Control Facilities

--- [Page 13] ---
3.1.2 Provide mobile disinfection system units or other similar facilities/equipment that would provide disinfection of vehicles, animal, or agricultural transport carriers incoming to the Davao Region.

3.1.3 Provide support to logistics, equipment, or facilities needed in the operationalization of the quarantine facilities (e.g. disinfectants, PPEs, etc).

3.1.4 Coordinate with national agencies involved in project implementation (e.g. DPWH, BAI, BFI, BPAR, AFP, PNP, DILG, DEER, etc)

3.2 Duties and Responsibilities of the BAI- VQX XI, BPI, PQS XI and BFAR XI -- The Bureau of Animal Industry - Veterinary Quarantine Services XI (BAI-VQX XI), Bureau of Plant Industry - Plant Quarantine Services XI (BPI-PQS XI) and Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources XI (BFAR XI), shall have the following duties and responsibilities:

3.2.1 Conduct monitoring of the established border control facilities

3.2.2 Facilitate deputation of deployed quarantine personnel from Local Government Units to authorize the conduct of inspection and other regulatory functions

3.2.3 Provide support to quarantine personnel deployed in the established border control facilities to conduct inspection and other regulatory functions

3.2.4 Conduct training, refresher courses, and other capability-building activities of the assigned quarantine personnel to capacitate in the conduct of duties and responsibilities

3.2.5 Provide support to the logistics, equipment, or other facilities for the operationalization of the border control facilities (e.g. disinfectants, PPEs, etc), subject to the availability of funds

3.3 Responsibilities of the Proponent Local Government Unit

3.3.1 Operationalization and maintenance of the border control facilities

3.3.2 Provide quarantine personnel in manning the border control facilities to conduct inspection and other regulatory functions

3.3.3 Facilitate the acquisition of right-of-way (ROW)

3.3.4 Support/facilitate site development

3.3.5 Coordination with collaborating agencies involved in the implementation (PNP, AFP, Lao agencies, etc)

Section 4. Modification or Amendments

No modification or amendment to this agreement and the approved project documents shall be allowed without the written consent of both parties involved herein. All amendment(s) that will be appended to this agreement shall be in writing and must be delivered to the address of the parties hereto.

Section 5. Effectivity and Duration

This agreement shall take effect upon its signing and shall be in force for twenty-five (25) years from the date of release of the fund as per useful, unless otherwise expressly extended or renewed in writing by the parties herein. The approval of the documents that aim to extend the life of the MOA shall be undertaken before the expiration of the original MOA.
