SECTION 8. USE OF SAVINGS - The Local Chief Executive and the Presiding
Officer of the Sangguniang Panlungsod are hereby authorized to augment any item in
the approved Annual Budget for their respective offices from savings in other items
within the same expense class of their respective appropriations as provided for in Section 336 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991".

SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby
declared separable, and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the
validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect on
January 1, 2024.

ENACTED, December 1, 2023, by unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
