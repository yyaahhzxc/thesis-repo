Ord. No. 0278-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION FOR REZONIFICATION OF SME POSTERITY HOLDINGS CORPORATION FROM LOW DENSITY RESIDENTIAL SUB-ZONE AND WATER RESOURCE ZONE TO MAJOR COMMERCIAL ZONE."

SECTION 2. COMMON REGULATIONS FOR BASE ZONES AND ALL SUB-ZONES.- Article VII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2019-2028) provides Common Regulations for Base Zones and All Sub-Zones:

"SECTION 24 entitled "REVIEW OF BASE ZONES" provides: Base zones are subject to review by the Zoning Review Committee every five (5) years from the enactment of this Ordinance. Any amendment thereof as recommended by committee shall be in accordance with provisions of Article VIII Section 64.

Any change of zone to another zone shall be considered as amendment of the zoning ordinance and must comply with the provision of Article VII Section 64."

"SECTION 64. AMENDMENTS TO THE ZONING ORDINANCE. Changes in the integrated ZO, as a result of the review by the LZR shall be treated as an amendment, provided that any proposed amendment to the Zoning Ordinance or provisions thereof shall be subject to public hearing and shall be carried out through a three-fourths vote of the Sangguniang Panlungsod. Any amendment shall take effect only after approval and authentication by HLURB or Sangguniang Panlungsod."

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the grant of the application for rezoning of SME Posterity Holdings Corporation, from Low Density Residential Sub-zone (R-1) and Water Resource Zone to Major Commercial Zone (C-2) located at Libby Road, Barangay Bago Galleria, Talamo District, Davao City, with the following Transfer Certificate of Title, to wit:

| TCT No. | Area | Current Zone Classification | Proposed Zonal Classification |
|---------|------|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|
| TCT No. 146-2013000781 | 10,000 sqm. | Low Density Residential Sub-zone (R-1) | Major Commercial Zone (C-2) |

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval. ENACTED, on the 29th day of August 2023, by three-fourths vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang, there being a quorum.
