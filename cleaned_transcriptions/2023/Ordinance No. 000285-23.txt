Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled:

SECTION 1. TITLE. - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO SUPPLEMENTAL BUDGET NO. 1 OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2023, CONSISTING OF THE FOLLOWING FUNDS AND EXPENDITURES, TO BE USED BY THE ECONOMIC ENTERPRISES IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF TWO BILLION FIVE HUNDRED NINETY-EIGHT MILLION EIGHT HUNDRED FORTY-NINE THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED SEVENTEEN PESOS AND 33/100 (P2,598,849,414.33), AS AMENDED".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to maximize the use of available resources and, at the same time, ensure that these resources are judiciously utilized and put to proper use.

SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS. - The following amounts or so much thereof, as may be necessary, are hereby appropriated out of the funds certified to be available by the City Accountant and the City Treasurer of Davao City, not otherwise appropriated from the operation of the City Government of Davao from January 1 to December 31, 2023, except as otherwise provided therein.

SECTION 4. AMOUNTS, SOURCES OF FUNDS, AND OBJECT OF EXPENDITURES. - The Supplemental Budget No. 1 of the City Government of Davao for the Calendar Year 2023 has an available source of funds in the total amount of TWO BILLION SIX HUNDRED EIGHTY-SEVEN MILLION NINE HUNDRED SIXTEEN THOUSAND EIGHTY-FIVE PESOS AND 71/100 (P2,687,916,085.71), with breakdown as follows:
