Ord. No. 0216-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF THE BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION-DAVAO CITY FIRE DISTRICT FOR AN AUTHORITY TO PURCHASE MOTOR VEHICLE (APMV) TO BE CHARGED AGAINST THE 20% LOCAL GOVERNMENT FUND SHARE FROM THE FIRE CODE REVENUE IN THE AMOUNT OF SIX MILLION SIX HUNDRED EIGHTY-SEVEN THOUSAND PESOS ONLY (Php 6,887,000.00) FOR THE IMPROVEMENT OF THE DAVAO CITY FIRE DISTRICT OFFICE AND ACTIVATION OF BAJAIDA FIRE STATION, SUBJECT TO GENERAL POLICY AND REGULATIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT (DBM), THE COMMISSION ON AUDIT (COA), THE PROCUREMENT LAW, AS WELL AS OTHER APPLICABLE LAWS, ORDINANCES, AND PRESIDENTIAL DIRECTIVES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Sections 5.4 and 5.6 of DILG Memorandum Circular 2022-105 states:

5.4 Sources of Funds for the Purchase of Motor Vehicles.
5.4.1. Funds for motor vehicle purchases maybe sourced from the following:
xxx
xxx
"5.4.1.1.3. Portion of the twenty percent (20%) LGU share generated from the collection of fire permits pursuant to the Fire Code Revenue under DOF-DBM-DILG Joint Circular No. 2015-01, dated February 12, 2015;
5.6 Documentary and Procedural Requirements for the Issuance of Authority to Purchase Motor Vehicles.
5.6.1. Prior to the issuance of authority to purchase motor vehicles, the following documents, in original and/or certified true copy, shall be submitted to the concerned DILG Municipality/City Provincial Field Office," and further states that;
5.6.1.3 Certified true copy of an Ordinance approving such purchase (i.e., first two and last pages of the said Ordinance and the page indicating the appropriated amount, per vehicle, to be purchased, and reflecting the name of the office the budget was appropriated), and current balance sheet of the LGU, showing the amount of funds available for the purchase of motor vehicles, such as, unspent DRRM fund balance from the previous year(s) and funds transferred/donated to the requesting-LGU from higher-level LGUs, government corporations, etc.";
SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION - The Project Design as approved by the City Budget provides for an appropriation of:

| Particulars | Expense Code | Appropriation | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motor Vehicles | 10706010 | Php 3,100,000.00 | Php 3,100,000.00 |

--- [Page 3] ---
This shall be taken from the available balance, certified by the Office of the City Accountant, in the amount of Six Million Six Hundred Eighty-Seven Thousand Nine Hundred Twenty-Four and 61/100 Pesos (PhP 6,687,924.61) in the Book of Accounts from the 20% LGU Share from Fire Code Revenue under Trust Fund as of July 31, 2022.

SECTION 4. APPROVAL - The request of the Bureau of Fire Protection - Davao City Fire District for an Authority to Purchase Motor Vehicle (APMV) to be charged against the 20% Local Government Fund share from the Fire Code Revenue in the amount of Six Million Six Hundred Eighty-Seven Thousand Pesos Only (PhP 6,687,000.00) for the improvement of the Davao City Fire District Office and activation of Bajada Fire Station is hereby approved.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 09th day of May 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
