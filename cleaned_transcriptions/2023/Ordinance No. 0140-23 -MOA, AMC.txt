Ord. No. 0140-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - "This Ordinance shall be known as 'AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR THE LOAN FACILITY TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND AGRICULTURISTS MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE (AMC)."

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement for the Loan Facility to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Agriculturists Multipurpose Cooperative.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 17th day of January 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
