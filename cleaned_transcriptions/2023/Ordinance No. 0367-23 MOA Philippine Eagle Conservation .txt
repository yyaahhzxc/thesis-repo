Ord. No. 0367-23

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND PHILIPPINE EAGLE CONSERVATION PROGRAM FOUNDATION INC. (PECCFI) RELATIVE TO THE FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO BE GIVEN BY THE CITY TO THE SAID FOUNDATION IN THE AMOUNT OF TWO MILLION PESOS (P2,000,000.00), IN SUPPORT AND ASSISTANCE TO THE CONSERVATION EFFORTS OF THE LATTER".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 36 of the Local Government Code of 1991 provides that "a local government unit may, through its local chief executive and with the concurrence of the sanggunian concerned, provide assistance, financial or otherwise, to such people's and non-governmental organizations for economic, socially-oriented, environmental, or cultural projects to be implemented within its territorial jurisdiction".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Philippine Eagle Conservation Program Foundation Inc. (PECCFI), relative to the financial assistance to be given by the City to the said foundation in the amount of TWO MILLION PESOS (P2,000,000.00), in support and assistance to the conservation efforts of the latter.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of this Ordinance are hereby declared separable, and if any or more of such provisions are declared invalid, the validity of all other provisions shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 14th day of December 2023, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
