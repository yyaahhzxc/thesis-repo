**SECTION 3. AUTHORITY**
The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into, sign for, and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) and the City of Davao for the installation of streetlights under the Construction/Concrete of Bypass Road at Jct. Mamay-La Verna-Davao City Diversion Project.

**SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE**
If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY**
This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

**ENACTED, on the 16th day of May 2023, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
