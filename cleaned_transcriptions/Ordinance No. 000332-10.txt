ORDINANCE NO. 332

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE DEFACING, SCRIBBLING AND/OR WRITING ON THE WALLS OF PUBLIC AND PRIVATE BUILDINGS, AND/OR FENCES EXPOSED TO THE PUBLIC VIEW, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES IN CASE OF VIOLATION THEREOF.

Be it Ordained by the City Council, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1.- It shall be unlawful to deface or scribble or write on the walls of public or private buildings, and/or fences exposed to the public view.

SEC. 2.- Any person found violating Section 1 of this Ordinance, shall upon conviction be subject to an imprisonment of not more than thirty (30) days or a fine of not more than Fifty Pesos (P50.00) or both in the discretion of the Court.

SEC. 3.- This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
