**ORDINANCE NO. 447**

"GARBAGE DISPOSAL AND COLLECTION ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, AND PRESCRIBING RATES OF PAYMENT THEREFOR:"

Be it Ordered by the City Council, City of Davao, in regular session assembled, the following ordinance:

SECTION 1. - This ordinance shall be known as the GARBAGE DISPOSAL AND COLLECTION ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO AND PRESCRIBING RATES OF PAYMENT THEREFOR.

SEC. 2. - The Provisions of this ordinance shall be applicable within the entire territory and jurisdiction of the City of Davao and shall be enforced by the City Public Service Officer.

SEC. 3. - Area of collection and disposal: The area of collection and disposal of garbage and other refuse shall include the population of the City of Davao and those covered by the barriers, sitios, subdivisions, settlements, or group of buildings, the feasibility of operation of the service of which shall be determined by the Department of Public Services duly approved by the City Council, through the recommendation of the City Mayor.

SEC. 4. - "Garbage defined:" The word "garbage" as used in this ordinance shall include the refuse of such animal or vegetable matter or food supplies as were intended for human food but have been neglected for such use, waste or used papers, pasteboards, woven materials, straws, wood, brush, metal, cans, broken glass or porcelain, loose or decayed materials, dirtlike substance, sweepings, and cuttings, and waste products or materials of manufacturing establishments and construction work, and any single waste product or material not exceeding one (1) dump truck load.

SEC. 6. - Special collection of refuse: Permit and additional fee required: Upon previous permits obtained from the Office of the City Mayor, daily collection of refuse from within the compound or premises of any industrial, commercial, business or other establishment may be allowed when it is inconvenient due to the nature of the refuse, for lack of sidewalk, or the narrowness, or the busy condition of the street, for the establishment to place their refuse receptacles at the sidewalk or at the street edge, subject, however, to the condition that such special collection service shall be charged only when applied for, the party or parties concerned shall pay an additional monthly service fee of five ($5.00) Pesos, other than the fee schedule and that the refuse shall be contained in appropriate receptacles.

SEC. 7. - Disposal: Time to place receptacles on sidewalks: It shall be the duty of the occupant of every building, premises or place of business to have receptacles for garbage or rubbish placed on the outer edge of the sidewalk each day, or at the edge of the street if there is no sidewalk, at such time, not earlier than one (1) hour before collection as may be directed by the Department of Public Services, provided that upon previous

Sec. 5. - Disposal prohibition. As soon as the garbage wagon or vehicles is loaded it must be driven at once to the specified place of disposal and no account be allowed to stand in the vicinity of any human habitation, schoolhouse, or place of assembly. All such materials must be disposed of within Twelve (12) hours after collection.

Human excretes shall not be disposed of through the garbage collection services of the Department of Public Services.

Sec. 11. - Mode of Payment: The garbage fee shall be paid to the City Treasurer annually and shall be payable within the first twenty (20) days of January, every year; otherwise, such charge of fifty per centum thereof shall be imposed, provided, however, the new applicants of business permits shall pay only proportionate garbage fee corresponding to the period when they established their business in Davie City; provided, further that those already granted business permit upon the effectiveness of this ordinance shall pay only the proportionate fee corresponding the last quarter of 1970.
No license or permit to do business in Davie City, or any renewal thereof may be issued to any person, firm or corporation unless the garbage fee mentioned in Section 9 is paid.
