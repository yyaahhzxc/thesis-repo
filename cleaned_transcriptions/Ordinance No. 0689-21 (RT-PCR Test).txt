ORDINANCE NO. 0689-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR MANDATORY
REVERSE TRANSCRIPTION POLYMERASE CHAIN
REACTION (RT-PCR) TESTING OF ALL F1, F2, AND F3
CONTACTS OF AN RT-PCR CONFIRMED POSITIVE CASE
OF COVID-19 IN DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
WHEREAS, the World Health Organization (WHO) has declared a Corona Virus Disease (COVID-19) pandemic;

WHEREAS, President Rodrigo Roa Duterte issued Presidential Proclamation 1021 extending the State of Calamity in the country until September 2021;

WHEREAS, despite the issuance of Executive Order (EO) No. 20-A or "An Order Providing for Mandatory Swabbing of all F1, F2 and F3 Contacts of an RT-PCR Confirmed Positive Case of COVID-19 in Davao City," there is a notable lack of cooperation from persons who are found positive of COVID-19 and persons identified as F1, F2 and F3 contacts;

WHEREAS, contact tracers face the difficulty of getting accurate information from persons who are found positive of COVID-19, and further, they also face resistance to mandatory swabbing from persons identified as F1, F2 and F3 contacts;

WHEREAS, during the Davao City Task Force (DCTF) COVID-19 Meeting on May 25, 2021, DCTF passed a Resolution requesting the Sangguniang Panlungsod to enact an ordinance penalizing persons identified as F1, F2 and F3 Contacts who refuse to be swabbed as a probable COVID-19 virus carrier and refuse to give out accurate information needed for contact tracing;

WHEREAS, there is a need to formalize the City's policy on mandatory swabbing of all F1, F2 and F3 Contacts to make its strict compliance necessary, as well as to impose penal sanctions upon erring individuals who refuse to be swabbed and give out accurate information for contact tracing;

NOW THEREFORE, on motion of Councilor Maria Belen S. Acosta, duly seconded by Councilors Dante L. Apostol Sr., Myma G. L'Dalado-Oritz, Mary Josepha D. Villafuerte, Pilar C. Braga, Nilo D. Abellera and Edgar R. Ibuwan Sr., it is resolved, as it is hereby resolved, TO ENACT AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR MANDATORY REVERSE TRANSCRIPTION POLYMERASE CHAIN REACTION (RT-PCR) TESTING OF ALL F1, F2 AND F3 CONTACTS OF AN RT-PCR CONFIRMED POSITIVE CASE OF COVID-19 IN DAVAO CITY;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that copies of this Resolution be furnished the Office of the City Mayor through the City Administrator's Office, the Vice Mayor's Office, the City Health Office (CHO), the Public Safety and Security Command Center (PSSC), the Davao City Police Office (DCPO), the City Legal Office, and all other concerned offices/departments, for their information, guidance and appropriate action;

ADOPTED, June 22, 2021, by unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
