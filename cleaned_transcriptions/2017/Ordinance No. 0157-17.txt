MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This Memorandum of Agreement (hereinafter the "Agreement") entered into by and among:

NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY, a government corporation organized and existing pursuant to the provision of Presidential Decree No. 757, with principal office at Elliptical Road, Diliman, Quezon City, represented in this Act by its General Manager, MR. MARCELINO P. ESCALADA, JR., hereinafter referred to as the "NHA";

-AND-

PILIPINONG MAY PUSO FOUNDATION, INC., a newly established non-stock non-profit charitable organization with office address at Unit 3601, Robinsons-Equitable Tower, ADB cor. Poveda Street, Ortigas Center, Pasig City, represented in this act its Chairperson, ROWENA KRISTINA AMARA E. VELASCO, hereinafter referred to as "PMPF";

SAN MIGUEL FOUNDATION, INC., a non-stock, non-profit organization duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at 40 San Miguel Avenue, Mandaluyong, Metro Manila, represented in this act by MR. RAMON S. ANG and hereinafter referred to as "SMF";

-AND-

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit created by virtue of Republic Act No. 4354, as amended, with office address at 2nd Floor City Hall, City Hall Drive, San Pedro St., Davao City represented herein by its Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter the "CITY OF DAVAO."

WHEREAS, pursuant to Executive Order No. 90, the National Housing Authority is the sole national government agency engaged in direct housing production mandated to meet the housing needs of low-income and marginal families and to focus its effort towards the housing needs of the lowest 30% of the urban population;

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Local Government Code, otherwise known as R.A. 7160 and the Urban Development and Housing Act of 1992 or R.A. 7279, Local Government Units are mandated to take a principal role in the provision of housing services to its constituents;

WHEREAS, Article VII, Section 29 of R.A 7279 provides for the following wherein its implementation has been a continuing engagement among the local government units and NHA:

--- [Page 9] ---
"Resettlement. - Within two (2) years from the effectiveness of this Act, the local government units, in coordination with the National Housing Authority, shall implement the relocation and resettlement of persons living in danger areas such as esteros, railroad tracks, garbage dumps, riverbanks, shorelines, waterways, and in other public places as sidewalks, roads, parks, etc., to the extent possible. The local government units, in collaboration with the National Housing Authority, shall provide relocation or resettlement sites with basic services and facilities and access to employment and livelihood opportunities sufficient to meet the basic needs of the affected families."

WHEREAS, the City of Davao, thru its City Council, passed City Ordinance No. 014-07 otherwise known as the Comprehensive Urban Shelter Services Development Code or the Shelter Code of Davao City which promotes just dynamic, comprehensive land tenure security and urban services development program in cooperation with the people's organization, non-government organizations, private sectors and other key shelter agencies;

WHEREAS, the Davao City Comprehensive Land Use Plan, 2013-2022 recommends the acquisition and development of more relocation projects identified livable areas to address the growing number of informal settlers exposed to disaster risks/hazards, increasing their level of vulnerability considering that the poorest have developed more perils;

WHEREAS, SMC, the corporate social responsibility arm of San Miguel Corporation (SMC), continues to invest on important social causes which include community building and disaster management;

WHEREAS, PMIF, in its quest to provide housing and livelihood assistance to qualified beneficiaries, will partner with SMC to render service, support and assistance in the implementation phase of the Housing Project in the City of Davao;

WHEREAS, to address the foreseen challenges in the housing sector in Davao City, there is a need for the construction of more housing units to improve the condition of the underprivileged and homeless through affordable social housing projects;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises, their mutual consent herein set forth, the parties have agreed to the following terms and conditions:

ARTICLE I

THE PROJECT

The project shall be known as "Biyay ng Pagbabago Residential Project" herein referred to as the "PROJECT" which involves the construction of Five Thousand (5,000) housing units in Davao City.

The Project shall generate a total of 5,000 housing units with climate resilient specifications to be provided by NHA for its intended beneficiaries.

The Project shall be implemented pursuant to the plans, specifications and costs mutually approved by the parties and shall be in accordance with the provisions of Batas Pambansa 220 as a Socialized Housing Project.

The construction of the Five Thousand (5,000) housing units shall be within Los Amigos and Lasang Resettlement Areas and shall be completed within the reasonable

--- [Page 10] ---
number of days determined by the Parties and shall be extended only due to force majeure and other meritorious reasons. The number of units per relocation area shall be: Three Thousand (3,000) housing units at Los Amigos and Two Thousand (2,000) housing units at Lasang.

ARTICLE II

OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES

A. OBLIGATION OF NHA

The NHA shall:

1. Extend assistance in the form of planning and design for house construction, funds for land development for the first phase of the project of at least Twenty Four Million Pesos (24,000,000.00), construction, monitoring and supervision, and estate management, specifically on award and documentation;

2. Prepare the land development plan in consultation with the Parties including the technical specifications factoring in climate resilient components, cost estimates and implementing schedules;

3. As a member of the Inter-Agency Committee, composed of NHA, PMPF, SMF and CITY OF DAVAO, monitor the progress of the project implementation until its completion for the subsequent conduct of joint final inspection and acceptance of the housing unit;

4. Cause the proper use and maintenance of open spaces with the project by planting trees and other vegetative cover;

5. Cause the construction of necessary community facilities in duly designated sites wherein the operation shall be agreed upon by the Parties.

B. OBLIGATION OF PMPF

The PMPF shall:

1. Create sufficient and sustainable livelihood programs for the housing beneficiaries of the PROJECT and other activities to rehabilitate the community as a whole;

2. Provide educational assistance to further the capacities of qualified beneficiaries;

3. Design training, capability-building, educational and livelihood programs for the beneficiaries, a copy of which shall be furnished to the SMF;

4. Identify qualified partners who will accomplish the training, capability-building, educational and livelihood programs for the qualified beneficiaries of the PROJECT;

--- [Page 11] ---
C. OBLIGATION OF SMF:

The SMF shall:
1. Cause the construction of Five Thousand (5,000) housing units in areas identified by the City Government in accordance with the development plan, specifications and cost estimates;
2. Provide a project work plan inclusive of schedule of fund releases and project milestones;
3. Spearhead the day-to-day construction and operation of the component houses;
4. In collaboration with CITY OF DAVAO, secure the location where the housing units will be constructed, including all documentation and certifications required;
5. Prepare reports on the progress of the project and the completion thereof, such reports shall be submitted every two (2) months to the NHA, CITY OF DAVAO and PMPF;
6. Release funds following the approved disbursement scheme with the financial disbursement report as attachment;
7. Upon completion thereof, turn-over to the CITY OF DAVAO the component houses, its improvements and all its appurtenant facilities, parts and structures

D. OBLIGATION OF THE CITY OF DAVAO

The CITY OF DAVAO shall:
1. Make available lands in Los Amigos and Lasang where the housing units are to be constructed.
2. Identify beneficiaries of the project who shall be coming from danger zones in Davao City as determined by the City Government thru the Inter-Agency Committee after the formulation of policies on beneficiary selection. The area shall be cleared of structures of informal settlers who are exposed to threats in extreme weather conditions;
3. Secure all the required permits and clearances for the Project;
4. Monitor progress of project implementation.

ARTICLE III

IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PROJECT

Immediately after the execution of this Agreement, the parties shall coordinate for the commencement of implementation of the project making available resources necessary and essential for the timely completion of the same.

--- [Page 12] ---
**ARTICLE IV CONDITIONS**

The following shall be strictly complied with for continued use of the housing units by the qualified beneficiaries.

1. The housing units may not be utilized for any commercial purposes and shall be restricted to residential use, unless commercial purpose is pursuant to a livelihood program implemented by PMPF;
2. The beneficiaries may not introduce improvements thereto to alter the total area utilized, nor may they introduce any other improvements other than that which is necessary for the preservation and maintenance of the said units, provided that any alteration to the structure shall be made with the express consent of the City of Davao and NHRA;
3. In the event of voluntary or involuntary termination of the right of usufruct, possession of the said housing unit shall revert back to CITY OF DAVAO.

**ARTICLE V AMENDMENTS/PARTIAL INVALIDITY**

All amendments or revisions of any provision of this Agreement shall be upon the prior written mutual consent of both parties subject to the execution of an appropriate instrument for the purpose.

**ARTICLE VI ENTIRE CONTRACT**

The parties acknowledge that this Agreement constitutes the entire Agreement between them and shall supersede all other previous communications or contracts, oral or written, between and among the parties with respect to the subject matter thereof.

The parties represent that they have the requisite power and capacity to enter into Agreement and perform their obligations and undertakings according to the terms and conditions stated herein.

All parties shall, at all times, he personally and directly liable for the acts of its personnel under its employ and shall hold each party free and harmless from any and all claims or shall hold each party free and harmless from any and all claims or liabilities arising from the acts or conduct of its employees.

This Agreement may be signed in any number of counterparts. Any single counterpart or a set of counterparts signed, in either case, by all the parties hereto shall constitute a full and original agreement for all purposes.

**ARTICLE VII EFFECTIVITY**

This Agreement shall take effect upon its signing.
