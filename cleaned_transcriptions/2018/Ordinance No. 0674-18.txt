The ordinance granted an exemption from amusement tax for the event held on December 15, 2018, from 5:00 P.M. to 12:00 A.M. The proceeds from the ticket sales will be used for the following:
(1) Purchase of items for donation to the Alma Mater (2,500 pieces of emergency kits);
(2) Donation to the Beneficiary Balay Pasilungan Foundation Davao; and
(3) Donation to the Stella Maris Academy Alumni Association Inc. for their beneficiary, the Indigenous Students of Malabog, with the information that no executive action is needed on the matter since the activity has already lapsed, thereby rendering the ordinance moot, for your appropriate action.

**SECTION 1. INTRODUCTION.**

This document is an amendment to the Articles of Incorporation of Bevco, Inc., filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Nevada on October 20, 2019, as amended by the following amendments:

**SECTION 2. AMENDMENT TO ARTICLES OF INCORPORATION.**

The articles of incorporation of Bevco, Inc. (the "Company") are hereby amended as follows:

**ARTICLE I. NAME AND LOCATION OF THE COMPANY.**

The name of the Company shall be changed from "Bevco, Inc." to "BEVCO, INC."

**ARTICLE II. DESCRIPTION OF BUSINESS.**

The business of the Company shall be changed from "manufacturing, marketing, and distributing beverage containers and related products" to "manufacturing, marketing, and distributing beverage containers and related products, including but not limited to, glass, plastic, metal, and ceramic beverage containers, as well as related products such as closures, caps, and other packaging solutions."

**ARTICLE III. CAPITALIZATION.**

The authorized capital stock of the Company shall be increased from $500,000,000 in shares of common stock, par value $0.0001 per share, to $1,000,000,000 in shares of common stock, par value $0.0001 per share.

**ARTICLE IV. BOARD OF DIRECTORS.**

The number of directors of the Company shall be increased from ten (10) to fifteen (15).

**ARTICLE V. OFFICERS.**

The officers of the Company shall include a new position of Chief Executive Officer ("CEO"), who shall be elected by the Board of Directors.

**ARTICLE VI. AMENDMENTS TO THE ARTICLES OF INCORPORATION.**

The Articles of Incorporation may be amended only by vote of the Board of Directors and by resolution of the shareholders of the Company, provided that any amendment must be approved by a majority of the votes cast at a meeting of the shareholders.

**SECTION 3. EFFECTIVE DATE.**

This amendment shall become effective upon filing with the Secretary of State of the State of Nevada.
