Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE AMENDED DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY YATAI INTERNATIONAL CORPORATION, IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, RELATIVE TO THE PROVISION OF A CHRISTMAS TREE INCLUDING A STEEL FRAME AND BASE, FLATBAR STEEL BALLS, STRIP LIGHTS, LED BULBS, RUBBER SOCKETS, AND A LIGHTED PANAFLEX SIGNAGE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi), Section 22 (a) (5) and (c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides to wit:

Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
(5) To enter into contracts;
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the Sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capitol or the city, municipal or Barangay hall.

Section 35. Linkages with People's and Non-Governmental Organizations. - Local government units may enter into joint ventures and such other cooperative arrangement with people's and non-governmental organizations to engage in the delivery of certain basic services, capability-building and livelihood projects, and to develop local enterprises designed to improve productivity and income, diversify agriculture, spur rural industrialization, promote ecological balance, and enhance the economic and social well-being of the people.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to accept and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the amended Deed of Donation to be executed by Yatal International Corporation, in favor of the City Government of Davao, relative to the provision of a Christmas Tree including a steel frame and base, flatbar steel balls, strip lights, LED Bulbs, Rubber Sockets, and a Lighted Panhala Signage.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 23rd day of October, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Deed is entered into by and between:

YATAI INTERNATIONAL CORPORATION, a corporation duly organized and existing under Philippine laws with office address at [address hereinafter represented by LEVY YAO, hereinafter called the DONOR].

-and-

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local governmental unit established by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, herein represented by its City Mayor, the HONORABLE SARA DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as the DONEE;

WITNESSETH:

The DONOR is the distributor of Omni lights and electrical products and has agreed to provide all the materials, lights, equipment, decorations as well as the labor for setting up a Christmas tree which shall be erected and displayed at Rizal Park, Davao City.

FOR AND IN CONSIDERATION or the liberality of the DONOR, DONOR by these presents transfers and conveys, by way of DONATION, unto the said DONEE, its assigns and successors-in-interests a Christmas tree, including the following:

Steel Frame & Base;
Flatbar Steel Balls;
Strip Lights, LED Bulbs, Rubber Sockets;
Lighted Panaflex Signage;

This Deed of Donation shall be subject to the terms and conditions set in the Memorandum of agreement executed by both parties on [date hereinafter attached as Annex "A"].

That the DONEE hereby accepts this donation of the above-described Christmas tree, and does hereby express its gratitude for the kindness and liberality of the DONOR.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have heretofore affix their respective signatures this [date] at Davao City, Philippines.

YATAI INTERNATIONAL CORPORATION Donor Represented by: LEVY YAO

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO Donee Represented by: HONORABLE SARA DUTERTE-CARPIO
