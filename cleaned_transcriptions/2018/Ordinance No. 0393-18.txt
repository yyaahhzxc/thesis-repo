ORDINANCE NO. 0393-18
Series of 2018

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR, TO SITER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE ALLOCATION OF THIRTY MILLION PESOS (P30,000,000.00) FOR THE "PAGKALINGA SA BAYAN" PROGRAM

--- [Page 6] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE ALLOCATION OF THIRTY MILLION PESOS (P30,000,000.00) FOR THE "PAGKALINGA SA BAYAN" PROGRAM."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1993 provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligation, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor hereby granted authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the Office of the President and the City Government of Davao for the allocation of Thirty Million Pesos (P30,000,000.00) for the "Pagkalinga sa Bayan" Program.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED: January 30, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into this day of by and between:

The OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT, with office address at Malacañang Palace, J.P. Laurel St., San Miguel, Manila, represented herein by the Executive Secretary SALVADOR C. MEDIALDEA, and hereinafter referred to as the OP.

and—

The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit in Region XI, with office address at San Pedro St., Davao City, Davao Sur, represented herein by MAYOR SARA Z. DUTERTE and hereinafter referred to as the LGU-DAVAO CITY, issued Executive Order No. 35 Series of 2016 An Order Strengthening the Davao City Lingap Para sa Maharip Program, Further Redefining the Program's Trust, Providing for the General Guidelines for all Processes, Allocating Funds Therefore, and For Other Purposes;

WITNESSETH, that:

WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao (the "City Government") constantly promotes programs that help alleviate the economic and health conditions of its constituents through making services accessible to less fortunate and indigent members of the communities;

WHEREAS, among the programs implemented by the City Government in order to address poverty focuses on the provision of medical assistance and fundamental needs of recognized and qualified beneficiaries;

WHEREAS, the continuing increase of the public beneficiaries shows the effectiveness of these programs thereby giving the City Government more opportunity to advance its implementation and strategy in this aspect for the general welfare of its people;

WHEREAS, one of these programs is the Lingap Para sa Maharip Program (the "Lingap Program") that has, through the years, provided medical assistance and benefits to indigents;

WHEREAS, considering the guidelines of the Lingap Program of the City Government of Davao has already been established, and taking cognizant of the pressing concern vis-à-vis the government's obligation to prioritize the health and well-being of the people and in order to extend medical assistance beyond Davao City constituents, the Office of the President will allocate Thirty Million Pesos (P30,000,000.00) monthly for the Pagkalinga sa Bayan Program, to be sourced from the Socio-Civic Project Fund (SCPF).

--- [Page 9] ---
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of the terms, conditions as they hereby agree and stipulate as follows:

OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT (OP)
1. The OP shall allocate the amount of Thirty Million (Php 30,000,000.00) pesos monthly for a period of one (1) year, chargeable against the Socio Civic Project Fund (SCPF) for the Pagkalinga sa Bayan Program.
2. The OP – Office of the Deputy Executive Secretary for Finance and Administration (ODESFA) shall pay direct to the hospitals, funeral parlors and other establishments issued by Guarantee Letter upon receipt of billing statement and supporting documents.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY (LGU-DAVAO CITY)
1. The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall utilize the funding solely for Pagkalinga sa Bayan Program in Mindanao particularly Region XI, XII and XIII thru issuance of Guarantee Letters. In no case shall the amount exceed P50,000.00 net of Philhealth and other assistance for medical and P30,000.00 for funeral.
2. The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall submit to the OP the Quarterly Summary of Guarantee Letters issued.

IN WITNESS THEREOF, the parties hereof through their duly authorized representatives have hereto entered into this Agreement and affixed their signatures on the date and place herein above-mentioned.
