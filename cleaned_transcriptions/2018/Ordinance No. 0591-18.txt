Ord. No. 0591-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY DAVAO SAN EL TRADING, INC. IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE DONATION OF FOUR (4) 40-FOOTER CONTAINER VANS WITH SERIAL NUMBERS REGU-5045330, REGU-5062153, REGU-5052160 AND REGU-5069883".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign, for its own bonds, contracts and obligations, and other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY: The City Mayor hereby grants legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by Davao San El Trading, Inc. in favor of the City Government of Davao, relative to the donation of four (4) 40-foot container vans with serial numbers REGU-5045330, REGU-5062153, REGU-5052160 AND REGU-5069883.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 13th day of November, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This DEED OF DONATION is entered into by and between:

DAVAO SAN ET TRADING, INC., d/b/a represented by its President, NESTOR D. MA., of legal age, Filipino, married, and a resident of Rolling Hills Subd., Baca, Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter called the DONOR

and

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, A/O, represented by Hon. City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, of legal age, Filipino and a resident of Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the DONEE

WHEREAS, the DONOR is the registered owner of certain Empty Dry Container Vans described as follows:

Make/Model/Type: FOUR (4) UNITS EMPTY DRY CONTAINER VAN 40 FOOTER with Serial Nos. REGU-5045330, REGU-5052160, REGU-5062153, REGU-506983;

That FOR AND IN CONSIDERATION of the love and affection which the DONOR has for the DONEE, the said DONOR by him presents transfers and conveys, with all the improvements thereon, free from all liens and encumbrances, by way of DONATION, unto the said DONEE, his heirs and assigns, his interest in the above-described container van unit;

That the DONOR does hereby state that the purpose of giving full effect to this donation, that he has reserved for himself in full ownership sufficient property to support his in a manner appropriate to his needs;

That the DONEE does hereby accept this donation of the above-described 4 units of dry container vans 40 footer acquired last January 2007 at a total amount of Eight Hundred Eighty Thousand Pesos Only (Pp 880,000.00), and does hereby express his gratitude for the kindness and liberality of the DONOR.
