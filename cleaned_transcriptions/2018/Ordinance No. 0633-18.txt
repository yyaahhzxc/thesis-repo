Ord. No. 0633-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF MARIA TERESA AGUION FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF COMMERCIAL SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY AT BARANGAY LAMPIANOQ, CALINAN DISTRICT AND TALANDANG, TUGBOK DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES OF DEVELOPMENT - Article XII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) more particularly Section 1 thereof, "Request for Additional Allowable Use" provides that the uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general zone and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not enumerated thereunder as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3⁄4 majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolutions and ordinance.

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE - The request of Ms. Maria Theresa Aguion for additional allowable use for the operation of commercial sand and gravel quarry at Barangay Lampianoq, Calinan District, and Talandang, Tugbok District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any Section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December, 2018, by three-fourths (3⁄4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.
