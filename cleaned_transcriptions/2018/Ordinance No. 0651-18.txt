Ord. No. 0651-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panglungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF ROSALYN C. BLANCO FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF COMMERCIAL SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY LOCATED IN LASANG RIVER, PAQUIBATO PROPER, PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES FOR DEVELOPMENT - Article XII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) more particularly Section 1 thereof, "Request for Additional Allowable Use" provides that the uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general zone and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor in-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panglungsod, upon application of the project owner, may grant additional allowable use through the Land Use Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not enumerated thereunder as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3/4 majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panglungsod through resolutions and Ordinance;

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE - The request of Rosalyn Blanco for additional allowable use for the operation of commercial sand and gravel quarry located in Lasang River, Paquibato Proper, Paquibato District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, December 12, 2018, by three-fourths (3/4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
