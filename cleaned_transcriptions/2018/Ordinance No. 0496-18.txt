Ord. No. 0496-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN FOR AND ON BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND PARKLANE WEST ACADEMY, INC. FOR THE ON-THE-JOB TRAINING OF HEALTHCARE SERVICES STUDENTS WITH THE DAVAO CITY TREATMENT AND REHABILITATION CENTER FOR DRUG DEPENDENTS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance, the purpose of which is the general welfare of the City and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) by and between the City Government of Davao and Parklane West Academy, Inc. for the On-the-Job training of Healthcare Services students with the Davao City Treatment and Rehabilitation Center for Drug Dependents.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ERACTED, July 31, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT is made and executed by and between:

PARKLANE WEST ACADEMY, INC., an educational institution duly organized and existing by virtue of Philippine Laws with principal office at 572 L. Ma. Guerrero St., Davao City, represented by its President HUGHES TRINIDAD A. TAN, hereinafter referred to as FIRST PARTY.

AND

THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO with principal address at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao City represented in this instance by CITY MAYOR SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as SECOND PARTY.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY is an educational institution offering Health Care Services NC II requiring the students to undergo an exposure and training in an affiliated health care / educational facility;

WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY is a Rehabilitation Center which provides beneficial and informative programs specially designed for drug dependent clients, as well as giving inputs and trainings to trainees/affiliates;

WHEREAS, SECOND PARTY agrees to be a partner training center of the FIRST PARTY in the delivery of concepts and practical teachings during the trainees On the Job Training;

THAT the parties hereto mutually agree and covenant as follows:

1. FIRST PARTY shall provide SECOND PARTY the list of trainees one (1) week before training starts;

2. SECOND PARTY shall only accept trainees with endorsement letter from FIRST PARTY, approved by its Program Director and Coordinator or duly appointed Clinical Instructor;

3. SECOND PARTY shall provide the trainees with general orientation of its programs, services, center's rules/guidelines and regulations;

4. FIRST PARTY trainees agree to abide the rules and regulations of SECOND PARTY which has the right to decline a particular trainee to continue the training for any violation incurred.

5. The FIRST PARTY trainees shall be allowed to handle in - house resident clients subject to the following rules :

Trainees are advised to keep the confidentiality of the handled clients.

Trainees assisting the assigned clients shall observe proper decorum.

Trainees shall refrain from giving negative and offensive remarks and harsh jokes about the clients at any time.

Loitering is not allowed while clients are on siesta time.

SECOND PARTY reserves the right to terminate student's affiliation if the trainee fails to comply with any of the rules and regulations stated above.

6. FIRST PARTY and its trainees shall be jointly liable for any damage on the property of SECOND PARTY and for any injury caused to the handled clients during the period of training;

7. FIRST PARTY shall provide a Clinical Instructor who shall be closely supervising the performance of the trainees.

8. Trainees will render a minimum of FIVE (5) hours per day for TEN (10) days of On the Job Training;

--- [Page 10] ---
9. FIRST PARTY will issue a Certificate of Training to the trainees after completion of the required numbers of duty hours, signed by the program head of both parties;

The parties agree to faithfully comply with this agreement and that any violation of any part of this agreement would be sufficient ground for the immediate termination of this contract.

This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect on August 03, 2018, for a period of Three (3) years renewable thereafter upon mutual agreement of the parties heren, unless terminated by one of the parties in writing, 30 days prior notice.
