ORDINANCE NO. 0641-18
Series of 2018

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF ISIDRO AM-IS FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF COMMERCIAL SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY, LOCATED IN BARANGAYS LAMPIANAO AND PANGYAN, CALINAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY

--- [Page 5] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF ISIDRO AM-IS FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF SAND AND GRAVEL QUARRY, LOCATED IN BARANGAYS LAMPIANAO AND PANGYAN, CALINAN DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. INNOVATIVE MODES OF DEVELOPMENT. Article XII of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2017-2022), more particularly Section 1 thereof, "Request for Additional Allowable Use" provides that the following modes of development, upon application on general zone and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The Sangguniang Panlungsod, upon application of project proponent and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment and Appeals (LZBA), may allow other uses not enumerated thereunder as it may deem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are of socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 4/5 majority vote of all members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance;

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST. The request of Isidro Am-is for additional allowable use for the operation of sand and gravel quarry in Barangays Lampianao and Pangyan, Calinan District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 12th day of December, 2018, by three-fourths (3/4) majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
