AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY LORETO GALVEZ NICOLAS IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE INVESTMENT OF 150-SQUARE METER LOT PORTION OF THE PROPERTY COVERED BY TCT NO. --- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0487-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ACCEPT AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY LORETO GALVEZ NICOLAS IN FAVOR OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, INVOLVING A 150-SQUARE METER LOT PORTION OF THE PROPERTY COVERED BY TCT NO. T-146-201609005 LOCATED AT PUROK 3B, MISIYA AREA, BARANGAY 19-B, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY, FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF HEALTH CENTER AND DAY CARE CENTER".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (v) of R.A. No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services and activities of the city government and in this connection, shall represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in his behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to accept and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Deed of Donation to be executed by and between Loreto Galvez Nicolas and the City Government of Davao, involving a 150-square meter lot portion of the property covered by TCT No. T-146-201609005 located at Purok 3B, Misya Area, Barangay 19-B, this City, for the construction of Health Center and Day Care Center.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, July 17, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

DEED OF DONATION

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Deed of Donation made and entered into by and between

LORETO GAIVEZ NICOLAS, of legal age, Filipino, married to Lolita S. Nicolas, with residence and postal address at Km. 3 J. P. Laurel Ave., Bajada, Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the DONOR;

-and-

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, Davao City, a Local Government Unit duly organized and existing by virtue and under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, represented by its City Mayor, Hon. Sara Z. Duterte - Carpio, hereinafter referred to as the BONEE,

WITNESSETH:

That the DONOR is the absolute owner of land covered and embraced by Title of the Registry of Deeds for Davao City bearing Title No. 146-201009005 with an area of TWO THOUSAND (2,000) square meters, more or less, situated at Purok 8B, Muslim Area, Barangay 19-B, Poblacion District Davao City;

WHEREAS, the DONOR is donating portion of the aforesaid property containing an area of ONE HUNDRED (150) square meters, more or less, to the BONEE, covered by and embraced in TCT No. 146-201009005 located at Purok 8B, Muslim Area, Barangay 19-B, Davao City,

WHEREFORE, for and in consideration of the love, affection and generosity of the DONOR has in favor of the BONEE, the DONOR hereby TRANSFER, CEDES, AND CONVEY by way of Donation unto the BONEE, his heirs, successors and assigns, the above-mentioned portion of described property, free from all liens and encumbrances, subject to the following condition:

That the donated portion of Lot shall be MAINTAINED, IMPROVED and DEVELOPED by the BONEE and shall be exclusively used for the construction of HEALTH CENTERS and DAY CARE CENTERS purposes;

WHEREAS, the BONEE hereby receives and accepts this Deed of Donation made in its favor by the DONOR and do hereby express its appreciation and gratefulness for the kindness and generosity of the DONOR;

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have heretofore set their signature this day of 2017 at Davao City, Philippines.
