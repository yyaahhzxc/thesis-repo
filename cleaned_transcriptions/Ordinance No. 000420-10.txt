ORDINANCE NO. 0429-10
Series of 2010

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 30, SERIES OF 1999, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING AND REVISING ORDINANCE NO. 142, SERIES OF 1955, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE ANTI-NUISANCE ORDINANCE" TO BE KNOWN AS THE "REVISED ANTI-NUISANCE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY, DEFINING NUISANCE, PROHIBITING THE SAME, FINISHING VIOLATION THEREOF AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES"

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Res No. 0420-10

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:

SECTION I. Section 3 of Ordinance No. 30, Series of 1998, is hereby amended to read as follows:

That the allowable noise level in general areas are as follows:

Category of Daytime Area Morning and Evening Nighttime
AA 50dB 45dB 46dB
A 55dB 50dB 45dB
B 65dB 60dB 55dB
C 70dB 65dB 60dB
D 75dB 70dB 65dB

SECTION 2. Section 4 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

"That the classification of the General Areas are as follows:

Class A - a section or contiguous area which requires quietness such as areas within 100 meters from school sites, nursery schools, hospitals, special homes for the aged and other health care institutions and places of worship;

Class B - a section or contiguous area that is primarily used for residential purposes;

Class C - a section that is primarily reserved as a light industrial area;

Class D - a section that is primarily reserved as a heavy industrial area;"

SECTION 3. Section 5 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:

"That the division of the 24-hour period shall be as follows:

Morning 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m.
Day Time 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Evening 6:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.
Night Time 10:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m.;

SECTION 4. Sections 3, 4, and 5 of the said Ordinance shall now be designated as Sections 6, 7, and 8, respectively."
