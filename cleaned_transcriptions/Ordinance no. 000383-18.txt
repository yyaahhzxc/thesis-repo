ORDINANCE NO. 0383-18
Series of 2018

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING THE FOOD SERVICE INDUSTRY WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY TO INCLUDE ONE-HALF (1/2) CUP OF COOKED RICE IN THEIR MENU, PROVIDING PENALTIES THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0383-18

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as and cited by its short title as the "DAVAO CITY RICE CONSERVATION ORDINANCE";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: It is hereby a policy of the City to adopt measures which shall ensure rice conservation and shall educate the consuming public not to waste rice to attain rice self-sufficiency;

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS:
A. Food Service Industry - businesses, institutions and companies responsible for any sale of meals, including but not limited to, restaurants, schools, offices, hospitals, cafeterias, catering operations, fastfood chains and other food related services;
B. Half-Cup Rice - shall refer to one-half cup of the serving of one cup order of rice as determined by the food establishment, provided that, the half-cup shall not be less than 80 grams (80g);
C. City - City Government of Davao;

SECTION 4. COVERAGE: All businesses, institutions and companies responsible for any sale of meals, including but not limited to, restaurants, schools, offices, hospitals, cafeterias, catering operations, fastfood chains and other food related services operating within the territorial jurisdiction of the City are hereby required to include one-half cup of cooked rice as an option in their regular menu with price set at one set of half cup.

Food service industry engaged in eat-all-you-can or buffet meals shall be exempt from this Ordinance unless they are serving plate-in or ala carte meals.

As a form of public information and awareness, such information shall be mandatory contained in the menu and made known by the attending service crew, waiter, waitress, or cashier to the customer before the latter commence ordering;

SECTION 5. PENALTIES: The food service provider found violating this Ordinance shall be penalized, as follows, for refusal to include one-half cup of cooked rice in their menu:

1st Offense - Fine of One Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (P1, 500.00);
2nd Offense - Fine of Three Thousand Pesos (P3, 000.00) and one month suspension of license or permit to operate;
3rd Offense - Fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) and permanent cancellation of license or permit to operate;

SECTION 6. THE IMPLEMENTING AGENCY: The City Health Office in coordination with the City Agriculturist's Office will be empowered and tasked to be the lead agency in the inspection of food establishments and for purposes of implementing this Ordinance;

SECTION 7. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS: The City Mayor is hereby authorized to create a joint Executive-Legislative Technical Working Group to formulate the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of this Ordinance within the period of three months from the approval of this Ordinance;
