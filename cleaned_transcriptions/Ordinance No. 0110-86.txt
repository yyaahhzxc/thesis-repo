**RESOLUTION NO. 468**

A letter from the Hon. Reye Y. Calopoy, dated September 9, 1986, addressed to the Floor Leader of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, requested that the items pertaining to the City's Terminal Ordinance (Ordinance No. 5), series of 1983, otherwise known as the "DAVAO CITY BUS TERMINAL ORDINANCE," for reasons enumerated therein, be presented for consideration and appropriate action by the body by Councilor Corazon N. Malayong.

Councilor Malayong was hesitant to accept the same calendared anew considering that it was already approved in principle.

Councilor Calopoy manifested however, that he had objections to the proposed amendments which were made during his views when due to an unexpected telephone interview with the media when the same was taken up. He also cast his doubt on the ruling of the Chair considering that no nominal voting was done before the vote. The objection involving some other members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall be considered only approved after the majority of the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod shall have cast their votes in the affirmative in a nominal voting, a re, requirement which was inadvertently missed for the said (Calopoy).

A nominal voting then followed whereby 9 members voted in the affirmative, 3 in the negative with no abstentions. This being 90, the Chair ruled that action LOST.

Councilor Jacinto T. Rubillar, Jr. immediately noticed for its reconsideration to which nobody offered objections. A 7-minute recess followed.

In the next nominal voting, the dissenters explained their apparent change of mind which was made through a compromise agreement, i.e., privatization of the City's bus terminal to which everybody was in full accord. All 12 Members voted in the affirmative on the proposed amendments.

The proposed amendments to Ordinance No. 63, series of 1955, are as follows:

1. Change of the words "jus Jornal" to "jus Jornal" in Transport Terminal, and accordingly, the Ordinance shall henceforth be the "jus Jornal City Overland Trans- port Terminal Ordinance";

2. Inclusion of Public Utility Jeepneys (RJUs) with destinations outside the territorial jurisdiction of the city to lead and unload passengers at the terminal;

3. Deletion of the "jus Jornal Fee Structure" from the Ordinance;

4. Definition of the powers, functions and responsibilities of the journal Board as well as increasing its membership from three (3) to five (5) members.

5. Sec. 6, Art. II should be amended to read as follows:

"Public Utility Jeepneys (RJUs) with franchises to serve special subdivision or specific areas only, within Jayao City, are exempted or not covered by the provisions of this ordinance."

6. Deletion of Sec. 12 with regard to the appropriation for the initial operating and maintenance expenses of the terminal as the same was already covered in a supplemental budget.

ORDINANCE NO. 110

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE No. 63, SERIES OF 1985, ENTITLED "AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING GUIDELINES, STANDARDS, RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE ORGANIZATION AND ESTABLISHMENT OF THE DAVAO CITY OVERLAND TRANSPORT TERMINAL AT NEW MAYINA, CITY OF DAVAO, DECLARED AND AUTHORIZED UNDER RESOLUTION NO. 7, AND ORDINANCE NO. 1, SERIES OF 1984, ENACTED ON MAY 10, 1985."

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1.- Ordinance No. 63, s. 1985, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING GUIDELINES, STANDARDS, RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE ORGANIZATION AND ESTABLISHMENT OF THE DAVAO CITY OVERLAND TRANSPORT TERMINAL AT NEW MAYINA, CITY OF DAVAO, DECLARED AND AUTHORIZED UNDER RESOLUTION NO. 7, AND ORDINANCE NO. 1, SERIES OF 1984, ENACTED ON FEBRUARY 15, 1984."

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

ARTICLE I - ORDINANCE POLICY AND OBJECTIVES

SEC. 1. This Ordinance shall be known and referred to as the "Davao City Overland Transport Terminal Ordinance."

SEC. 2. In accordance with the provisions of p.p. Bldg. 357, the Local Government Code, and of City Ordinance No. 1, series of 1984, the City Government of Davao shall establish, operate and administer, an overland transport terminal at the project area of the Davao City Overland Transport Terminal utilizing the integrated loading and unloading terminal, for all public utility buses (mini, medium and large) and Public Utility Jeepneys with destinations outside the territorial jurisdiction of the City.

**ARTICLE II - TRAFFIC ROUTING SCHEME**

**SECTION 5.** For a more effective and efficient utilization of the said overland transport terminal, the following traffic routing scheme is hereby established for all public utility buses and public utility jeeps with destinations outside the city limits, entering and leaving the central portion (i.e., population of Javao City-Agusan) of the Javao City Overland Transport Terminal, for the unloading and loading of passengers, as follows:

A. Northern Route (i.e., Javao City-Agusan, and other point north):

a. Outbound

1) Exit thru SIR Road gate of the terminal, then right to Quipao Avenue;

2) Cross the Balton Bridge, proceed to Quezon Blvd., then Leon Garcia St.;

3) Right turn at R. Castillo Street, oblique left turn at J.P. Laurel Avenue, thru Bass and Panacan;

4) Proceed to Northbound destination, via Javao-Agusan National Highway.

b. Inbound

1) Ingress Panasan junction to Davao- Agusan National Highway;

2) Oblique left turn at R. Castillo St., up to Junction Lapa-Lapa St.;

3) Left turn at Leon Garcia St., then to Quezon Blvd., to Quipao Avenue;

4) Left turn at Candalaria gate and enter Candalaria gate of overland transport terminal.

B. Southern Route (i.e., Davao City-Cotabato, and point south):

a. Outbound

1) Exit Candalaria gate of Overland Transport Terminal, then left turn at Pan-
dalaria, then left turn and at Quipao Avenue;

2) Left turn at Mariphas Highway, at Matina;

3) Proceed Southbound destination along Mariphas Highway.

SEC. 4. All public utility buses (hereinafter referred to as "buses"), including public utility jeeps with destinations outside Davao City, with final or transient destinations, and/or points of departure at Davao City shall unload and load their passengers, and/or serve Davao City Overland Transport Terminal, under such guidelines, rules and regulations as may be promulgated by the Davao City Overland Transport Commission.
SEC. 5. Other public utility vehicles (i.e., FULs with franchises with the City and JNR), may be allowed to make use of the said Davao City Overland Transport Terminal, under such guidelines, rules and regulations as may be promulgated by the Land Transportation Commission.
SEC. 6. Public Utility Buses (PUBs) with franchises with specific areas only, within Davao City are exempted or not covered by the provisions of this ordinance.
SEC. 7. The Davao City Overland Transport Terminal Management may request, the assistance and shall coordinate with the Land Transportation Commission in Davao City, the Constabulary Highway Police Service (CHPS) and the Bureau of Public Works (BUPW) in the prosecution and implementation of the said traffic routing scheme, in the general interest and welfare.
