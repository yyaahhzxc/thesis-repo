RESOLUTION NO. 4828:
In sessions duly assembled, upon motion of Councilor Bonifacio E. Militar, duly seconded by Councilors Pilar C. Braga, Caesar C. Cabading, Vas C. Lata, Antonio N. Vergara and Frances A. Rohillo, co-sponsored by Councilor Paz C. Veta, the foregoing resolution was adopted:
WHEREAS, there are women who are victims of economic dislocation especially those coming from the rural areas and who seek employment for overseas work in urban centers of population;
WHEREAS, there are unscrupulous recruiters coming to their aid with false promises of ready and prompt employment either abroad or in urban centers of population;
WHEREAS, due to their poor situation and the lack of proper knowledge in employment processes, they easily succumb to the recruiters' enticements;

WHEREAS, because of false promises of employment, other young girls are compelled to commit a life of prostitution and are forced to incur debts in order to pay for the services of the recruiters in order to survive and to help their families live;
WHEREAS, cohorts of evil designs, such as pimps, funhouse operators and customers jointly take advantage of the plight of these young girls who are victims of economic dislocation, by continuously committing them under forced circumstances, to a life of prostitution and other lewd activities in order to survive;
NOW THEREFORE, it is the moral and legal duty of the society for proper legislation to be enacted to protect these segments of the society who are victims forced into a life of prostitution and other lewd activities.
BE IT THEREFORE REQUERED, AS IT IS HEREBY RECOGNIZED, To approve an Ordinance entitled:
ORDINANCE NO. 1329
AN ORDINANCE PENALIZING RECRUITERS, PIMPS, FUNHOUSE OPERATORS AND CUSTOMERS WHO VICTIMIZE WHEN TO COMMIT A LIFE OF PROSTITUTION AND OTHER LEWD ACTIVITIES.
Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:
SECTION 1. - Definitions:
(a) Recruiter - refers to any person who engages in any act of enlisting, contracting, transporting, utilizing, hiring, or procuring women and includes referrals, contract services for the purpose of prostitution.
(b) Pimps - one who provides gratification for the lust of others.
(c) Prostitute - term applied to a woman who habitually indulges in sexual intercourse or lascivious conduct for money or profit.
