Ord. No. 064-19

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
REPEALING ORDINANCE NO. 1004-74, SERIES OF 1974, KNOWN AS 'AN
ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE LOOSENING OR LETTING ASTRY ANIMALS IN THE
POBLACION OF THE CITY OF DAVAO PROVIDING FOR THE CATCHING OF THE SAME
AND IMPOUNDING, COLLECTION OF FEES FOR KEEPING THEM DURING THE
IMPOUNDING AND PROVIDING FURTHER ITS DISPOSITION WHEN CLAIMED FOR
A CERTAIN PERIOD AND PROVIDING PENALTY THEREOF".

SECTION 2. REPEALING CLAUSE - Ordinance No. 1004-74, Series of 1974,
known as "AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE LOOSENING OR LETTING ASTRY
ANIMALS IN THE POBLACION OF THE CITY OF DAVAO PROVIDING FOR THE
CATCHING OF THE SAME AND IMPOUNDING, COLLECTION OF FEES FOR KEEPING
THEM DURING THE IMPOUNDING AND PROVIDING FURTHER ITS DISPOSITION WHEN
CLAIMED FOR A CERTAIN PERIOD AND PROVIDING PENALTY THEREOF" is hereby
repealed as the imposition of fees on impounding of astray animals is already
embodied under Section 124 of Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, or the 2017
Revenue Code of Davao City.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - The Ordinance shall take effect immediately after
posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3)
consecutive days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, October 8, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
