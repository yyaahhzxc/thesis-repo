Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS AND LOCAL TRANSPORTATION IN DAVAO CITY WITH QR PH DIGITAL PAYMENTS IN LINE WITH THE PALENG-QR PH PROGRAM AND PROVIDING INCENTIVES THEREOF WHICH THEY MAY ADOPT AND AVAIL

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "PALENG-QR PH PROGRAM IN DAVAO CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines, states that, "Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within this context, the powers of the LGU include, but are not limited to, the power to provide support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic property and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS

(a) Ambulant, Transient or Itinerant Vendor - refers to a vendor or seller who does not permanently occupy a definite place of the market but one who comes daily or occasionally to sell his/her goods.

(b) Account Opening Day or Account On-Boarding Day - is an activity wherein QR PH-participating Financial Service Providers (FSPs), together with personnel from Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), will assist covered users and their clients in opening an account, including the provision of QR Code printouts that can be readily displayed in their stores, terminals, and vehicles. The LGU will determine the designated days and places in the City (e.g., public markets, terminals, barangay halls) to conduct on-board activities.

(c) Basic Deposit Account (BDA) - refers to interest or non-interest bearing bank account with an initial minimum deposit of no more than One Hundred Pesos (Phip100.00), no minimum maintenance balance, no dormancy charges, maximum balance of Fifty Thousand Pesos (Phip50,000.00) and with simplified know-your-customer or KYC requirements. The list of banks authorized by BSP to offer BDA can be found on the BSP website: https://bit.ly/BankswithBDAs.

(d) Buyer - shall refer to a person who purchases goods, commodities or foodstuffs from a vendor or seller.

(e) City Public Market - refers to a public market maintained, owned and/or operated by the City Government within its territorial jurisdiction.

(f) Digital payment - refers to a monetary payment transaction between two (2) parties through a digital payment instrument in which both the payer and the payee use an electronic channel.

(g) Electronic Fund Transfer (EFT) - refers to transfer of funds between two (2) transaction accounts in the same or in different financial institutions supervised by the BSP, which are initiated and received using electronic devices and channels to transmit payment transactions.

(h) Electronic Money (E-money) - a monetary value as represented by a claim on its issuer, that is: a. electronically stored in an instrument or device; b. issued against receipt of funds of an amount not less in value than the monetary value issued; c. accepted as a means of payment by persons or entities other than the issuer; d. withdrawable in cash or cash equivalent; e. issued in accordance with Section 702 of the BSP's Manual of Regulations for Banks.

The list of e-money issuers (EMI) or entities authorized by BSP to offer e-money account can be found on the BSP website: https://bit.ly/BSPSupervisedEMIs.

(i) Financial Service Providers (FSP) - means a regulated financial institution which is licensed and supervised by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas. The term refers to deposit-taking institutions, non-bank financial institutions and the mobile financial service providers.

(j) Government-owned and/or Operated Public Markets - refer to those established out of public funds or those leased/acquired by any legal modes or means from private persons, natural or juridical, to be operated by the government either through its instrumentality, branch or political subdivisions.

(k) InstaPay - an electronic fund transfer facility offered by a participating bank or EMI to its clients to allow them to instantaneous transfers from their account to another held in other participating bank or EMI in the Philippines. The list of institutions participating in InstaPay can be found on the BSP website: https://bit.ly/InstaPayQRH.

(l) Market Rental Fee - refers to the fee paid to and collected by the City Treasurer for the privilege of using public market facilities.

(m) Motor vehicle - any conveyance designed to be self-propelled by engine, and includes any vehicles designed to be propelled by electric power obtained from overhead wires but not operated upon rails.

(n) Peddler - any person, who either for himself or on commission, travels from place to place to sell and sells his goods or offers to sell and deliver the same. Whether a peddler is a wholesale peddler or a retail peddler of a particular commodity shall be determined from the definitions of wholesale dealer or retail dealer as provided in the Revenue Code of the City of Davao.

(o) Peddler, Hawker or Hustler - includes any person, whether adult or minor, who peddles or hawkets goods, wares, merchandise, or any articles, in any manner, from place to place; or one who offers for sale, articles without any regular fixed and established place of business in Davao City.

(p) Public Market - refers to any place, building or structure of any kind recognized as such under existing laws or ordinances and those to be established upon recommendation of the local government concerned. It embraces all market stalls, tiendas, buildings, roads, subways, waterways drainage and other connections, parking spaces, and other appurtenances thereto.

(q) Public Market Vendor - any person, who, upon approval of this Ordinance, has been occupying one or more stalls in any public market, including privately owned but city-operated market with or without lease contract with the City of Davao.

(i) Public Utility Vehicle - any motorized vehicle with franchise from the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) and the City Government for tricycles used in conducting passengers such as, but not limited to, buses, vans, motorized tricycle-for-hire.

(s) QR Code - short for "Quick Response Code," a QR Code is a two-dimensional image-based bar code capable of holding large amounts of information. QR Codes store information using patterns of black dots and white spaces arranged in a square grid. It can be read by a smartphone camera that is equipped with a QR code reader. When personal and financial information and data are stored in an QR Code, it can be used to initiate payment transactions. A recipient of the funds shows his/her QR Code to the payer or sender who will scan the code to initiate the payment transaction.

(t) QR PH - QR PH is the national QR Code standard that allows a quick, low-cost, and easy method to buy, transfer, sell, and receive funds from other bank and e-money accounts in the Philippines. This is aligned with the European MasterCard Visa Co (EMVCo) standard, the global standard for secure payments, which unifies various domestic cashless payment schemes that make use of the QR Code as a form factor. The list of institutions participating in QR PH can be found on the BSP website: https://bit.ly/QRPhFAQs.

(v) Seller - shall refer to a person who sells goods, commodities, or foodstuffs other than inside a public market.

(w) Tricycle Operators and Drivers' Association (TODA) - the organization of tricycle operators and drivers in a given local government unit.

(x) Transaction Account - refers to a bank or e-money account held with a BSP-regulated financial service provider that can be used to store, send, and receive funds.

SECTION 4. SCOPE OF APPLICATION - The Ordinance shall cover the following:

1. All public market vendors;
2. All public transportation in the City of Davao City; and
3. All stores and business establishments including, but not limited to, sari-sari stores, ambulant vendors and hawkers.

SECTION 5. QR PH ENABLED PAYMENTS - All public market vendors, sari-sari store owners, public transportation, and other local merchants in the City of Davao are hereby advised to make QR PH payments available for their customers, in line with the Paleng-QR PH program.

Public transportation, public markets and business establishments, at their own discretion, may select as their alternative mode of payment from any QR PH-ready financial service providers (FSP) participating in the Paleng-QR PH program of the City.

The list of institutions participating in QR PH can be found on the BSP website: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Media_and_Research/Primers%20Fags/QR_PH_P2M_FAQs.pdf.

Provided, however, that nothing in this Ordinance shall prohibit payment in cash when, otherwise chosen by the patrons, consumers, buyers, passenger, or clients.

**SECTION 6. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

**a) LGU shall:**

1. Coordinate with key local stakeholders, such as the vendor associations, TODA, and the market superintendents, to ensure effective implementation of the program;

2. Invite FSPs to participate in the program and negotiate for deals/special offers to encourage target participants to open a transaction account and use QR code digital payment. The LGU can partner with as many participating FSPs as deemed appropriate;

3. Assist target participants (e.g., vendors, public utility vehicle operators, and tricycle operators) in the opening of accounts by, among others, providing additional relevant documents such as KYC certification to support the know-your-customer (KYC) requirements of FSP;

4. Organize a special "Account Opening Day" or "Account On-Boarding Day" in coordination with participating FSPs, to ensure ease and convenience of account opening by covered users and their clients;

5. Facilitate provision of free Wi-Fi connectivity in public and community markets, and TODA terminals, whenever feasible;

6. Coordinate with the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) in line with the latter's programs such as the Free Wi-Fi for All (FWiA);

7. The LGU, through the City Information Office (CIO), in partnership with BSP, shall develop its information, education and communication campaign (IECC) to promote and sustain the objective of the Paleng-QR Ph Program. This will cover, among others, a financial literacy program using appropriate channels on key topics, such as effective use of digital payments in business; financial services for micro and small enterprises; and financial consumer protection;

8. Disseminate through appropriate channels/methods (e.g., LGU's official social media pages) Paleng-QR Ph, financial inclusion and financial literacy materials developed by the BSP and other stakeholders.

**b) Financial Service Providers (FSPs)**

Participating FSPs shall:

1. Provide personnel, customer assistance booths, and other appropriate resources, including QR code printouts that can be readily displayed in their stores, terminals, and vehicles, deemed reasonable to ensure successful conduct of LGU's Paleng-QR Ph Program "Account Opening Day(s)" or "Account On-Boarding Day(s);"

2. Provide target users with appropriate materials on opening a transaction account, using QR Code payments and other digital financial services;

3. Provide resource speakers and contents, as requested, for the LGU's financial literacy program;
4. Assign competent contact person(s) to the LGUs for efficient and timely coordination on the program implementation;
5. Provide special offers for accounts opened under the Paleng-QR Ph program, such as free microinsurance coverage for a certain period (e.g., three months); t-shirts and other wearables; and special user rebates for a limited period;
6. Be accountable to the preservation of the privacy of the personal information provided by the stakeholders, as mandated under Republic Act 10173, otherwise known as the "Data Privacy Act of 2012";
7. Maintain a record on participating merchants under the Paleng-QR Ph program, and submit quarterly to the Sangguniang Panlungsod the records of the merchants with Paleng-QR.

Participation of FSPs in the program shall be non-exclusive.

c) Covered merchants and local transportation

Covered merchants and local public transportation may:
1. Open and maintain a QR Ph-ready transaction account. In the event the covered entity does not have an account, he or she is advised to open or create an account;
2. Explicitly display their QR Ph code in their store or vehicle where it is visible to customers and passengers;
3. Make QR Ph payments available as a method of payment;
4. Participate in the information campaign, financial literacy program, and other relevant activities that will be facilitated by the LGU and FSPs.

SECTION 7. INCENTIVE CLAUSE: All business owners, situated within the city public markets and government-owned/controlled public markets and regulated by the City, who will adopt the QR Ph digital payments, by setting up an account with the QR Ph-participating FSPs, during a designated Account Opening Day or Account On-Boarding Day, shall be granted a free one-day rental fee on stalls.

SECTION 8. COMPLIANCE MONITORING: The Business Permit and Licensing Office or the Business Bureau, with the assistance of the City Economic Enterprise (CEE) and the City Transport and Traffic Management Office (CTTMO), with the assistance of the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) and the Philippine National Police (PNP) are hereby asked to monitor compliance of the Ordinance.

SECTION 9. DATA PRIVACY CLAUSE: The confidentiality of the personal information of the stakeholders shall always be observed, as provided under Republic Act 10173 (RA 10173), otherwise known as the "Data Privacy Act of 2012."

Any violation of the provisions of the aforementioned law shall be dealt with according to the provisions covering the same.
