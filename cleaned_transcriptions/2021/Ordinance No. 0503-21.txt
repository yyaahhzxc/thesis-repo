Ord. No. 0503-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE REQUEST OF DAVID GAISANO FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR THE OPERATION OF EARTHILL QUARRY LOCATED IN BARANGAY WAAN, BUHANGIN DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Article XII, Section 1 of the
Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City provides for request for Additional
Allowable Use, which reads:

"The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general zone and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all inclusive. The Sangguniang
Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponent and upon,
favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment
and Appeals (LZBA), may grant additional allowable use thereunder as it may seem fit and proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are socio-economic and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3/4 majority vote of all the members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance."

SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST - The application for Additional
Allowable Use of David Gaisano for the operation of earthfill quarry located in Barangay
Waan, Buhangin District, this City, with a total area of Forty-Four Thousand Five Hundred (44,500) square meters, more or less, registered under Transfer Certificate of Title No. T-277973 is hereby APPROVED. No permit for earthfill quarry shall be issued without the Affidavit of No Objection of concerned adjacent lot owners.

SECTION 4. OBLIGATION OF APPLICANT - The applicant and his or her
successors-in-interest has the obligation to comply with existing rules, laws and ordinances. The environment shall at all times be protected. Adverse environmental impact of any use of the property shall be mitigated. Non-compliance with existing
rules, laws and Ordinances shall cause the repeal of this Ordinance.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 9th day of March 2021, by 3/4 vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
