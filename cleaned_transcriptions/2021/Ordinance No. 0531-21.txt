**On the paragraph pertaining to City of Davao as a party, "01" after the word "Philippines" must be deleted. Also, "Sanggunining Bayan" shall be changed to "Sanggunining Panlungsod" since the former refers to a legislative body of a municipality and Davao is not a municipality. **There shall be separate Acknowledgments for the two (2) parties. The Acknowledgment shall state that the agreement consists of four (4) pages, not five (5) as stated in the attached MOA.**

**To fast-track the execution of the TMFF, it is suggested that there be only one (1) witness for each party. On the part of the City Government of Davao, it is recommended that it be Atty. Zuleika I. Lopez, City Administrator.**

**BASED ON THE FOREGOING, this Office is of the view that after making the abovementioned corrections, the MOA is free from legal infirmity and the same may be forwarded to the Sanggunining Panlungsod for the requisite legislative authority for the City Mayor to sign the same.**

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENT:

THIS AGREEMENT is entered into and executed by and between:

THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT, a national government agency responsible for ensuring access to basic public health services to all Filipinos through the provision of quality health care and regulation of health goods and services, with office address at JP Laurel Avenue, Bajada, Davao City, herein represented by DR. ANNABELLE P. YUMANG, MCH, CSEO IV in her capacity as Regional Director, and hereafter referred to as "DCH"; and

THE CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, hereinafter referred to as CITY OF DAVAO with office address at the City Hall Drive, Pichon 3c, Poblacion District, Davao City, herein represented by HON. SARA G. OTRIFELTE in her capacity as City Mayor, per Sangguniang Panlungsang Resolution No. 5, 2021, and hereinafter referred to as "LGU".

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, Proclamation No. 92 (s. 2020) declared a State of Public Health Emergency in recognition of the Covid-19 public health event as threat to national security, and prompted a whole-of-government approach in addressing the Covid-19 outbreak in the country;

WHEREAS, Republic Act (RA) 11494 known as "Bayanito to Become as One Act" affirmed the existence of a continuing national emergency and grants government agencies a wide array of measures not only to continue and enhance its efforts against COVID-19, but also supports measures to accelerate, expand and enhance the capability of our health system through the construction of temporary treatment and monitoring facilities, field hospitals, dormitories for front liners, and for the expansion of government hospitals capacity all over the country;

WHEREAS, the appropriations were provided in the Quick Response Fund under General Appropriations Act (GAA) of 2020 for the Department of Health, which shall be used for the Construction of Temporary Treatment and Monitoring Facility (TTMF) for the purpose of responding to COVID-19 health emergency of the Local Government Units;

WHEREAS, the Department of Health thru the Quick Response Fund has appropriated the amount of THIRTY MILLION PESOS ONLY (PHP 30,000,000.00) for the implementation of infrastructure projects on the following health facilities, to wit:

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and stipulations herein set forth, the Parties hereto agree and do hereby agree as follows:

A. FUND RELEASES AND UTILIZATION

The funds shall be chargeable against 2019 and 2020 Quick Response Fund (QRF), issued under DOH SAA No. 2020-11-2935 and No. 2020-12-3344, respectively, and shall be obligated on or before February 26, 2021;

B. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CITY OF DAVAO:

1. Submit Sanggunan Panlungsod Resolution authorizing the City Mayor to enter into an agreement with the DOH-DCHD;

2. Ensure that the above mentioned documentary requirements is duly signed by concerned officials and submit the same to DOH-DCHD prior to procurement;

3. Submit Proof of Let Ownership to DOH-DCHD prior to procurement:

3.1 For new health facility, one of the following shall be considered:
a. Copy of title
b. Deed of donation
c. Usufruct agreement

3.2 For existing health facilities, proof of ownership can be in the form of:
a. City certification with tax declaration;
b. Certification from DENR stipulating that the land is a government property

4. Submit Geo-hazard Certificate for the proposed project's location and Site Development Plan indicating the proposed TMTF;

5. Submit Program of works with detailed unit price analysis, Approved Budget Cost and Construction Schedule, based on the DOH technical standards and prototype plans/drawing for the said infrastructure projects, for concurrence prior to procurement;

6. Conduct procurement of the project in accordance to the government procurement law or any latest available national government issuances on procurement applicable to the project;

7. Supervise implementation and speedily complete the project following the highest standards for materials control, safety and quality assurance;

8. Conduct joint final inspection of the project prior to issuance of certificate of completion and acceptance.

9. Secure the application of the necessary local permits;

10. Ensure that the facility shall be functional immediately after the turn-over and acceptance of the project and utilize in accordance to its purpose;

11. Ensure that funds will be provided for the continuing maintenance and operations of the including adequate human resource complement;

12. Submit monthly financial and physical accomplishment report and fund liquidation report within the second quarter of 2021; and

13. Book-up the cost of the infrastructure project in the respective Book of Accounts of the LGU and maintain a separate subsidiary ledger of all DOH fund releases/projects.

2. Ensure that utilization of funds follow the usual budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and regulations;

3. Provide technical assistance and facilitate the issuance of the certification f

1. Monitor progress of implementation and coordinate with the End-user on any request for Variation; if any;

2. Conduct final joint inspection of the project prior to issuance of certificate of completion and acceptance;

3. Operate the facility in consonance to the DOH Memorandum No. 2020-0186 entitled Interim Guidelines on the Operations of Converted Public and Private Spaces into Temporary Treatment and Monitoring Facilities for CoVid 19.

E. AMENDMENTS

This Memorandum of Agreement may be amended or revoked only upon mutual consent of all parties provided that all amendments shall be covered by Supplemental Provisions signed by all parties which shall be incorporated by way of reference as integral part of this MOA.

RESOLUTION NO. 0210-4
Series of 2021

A RESOLUTION TO ENACT AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF TEMPORARY TREATMENT AND MONITORING FACILITIES (TTMF).

WHEREAS, Section 15, Article II of the 1987 Philippine Constitution declares that the State shall protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them.

WHEREAS, Section 16 of R.A. 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 mandates, among others, that every local government unit shall exercise its powers to promote health and safety of its inhabitants;

WHEREAS, Proclamation No. 922, s. 2020 was issued declaring a State of Public Health Emergency throughout the Philippines due to COVID-19, enjoining all government agencies and local government units (LGUs) to render full assistance and cooperation and mobilize the necessary resources to undertake needed, urgent and appropriate response and measures in a timely manner, to curtail and eliminate the threat of COVID-19;

WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 11469, otherwise known as the Bayanihan to Heal as One Act, further declared the existence of a state of national emergency over the Philippines due to the COVID-19 pandemic, and authorized the President to adopt emergency measures in response to the same;

WHEREAS, Department of Interior and Local Government (DILG) Circular No. 2020-064, otherwise known as the Provincial/City/Municipal Special Care Facilities and Isolation Units Amid COVID-19 Pandemic, directs all local chief executives to identify government facilities that may be temporarily converted into units for isolation and quarantine facilities, and to immediately identify, designate and establish their respective COVID-19 special care facilities;

WHEREAS, the appropriation is provided in the Quick Response Fund under the General Appropriations Act (GAA) of 2019 and 2020 for the Department of Health, which will be used for the construction of Temporary Treatment and Monitoring Facilities (TTMF) intended to respond to the COVID-19 health emergency;

WHEREAS, the DOH, through the Quick Response Fund, has appropriated the amount of THIRTY MILLION PESOS (PHP 30,000,000.00) for the construction of three (3) TTMFs in the City, broken down into TEN MILLION PESOS (PHP 10,000,000.00) per facility;

MOA - TT/Construction | Office of Coun Villaflorite | Page 1 of 4

WHEREAS, the City Health Office (CHO) in its 8 February 2021 letter to the DOH Davao Center for Health Development (DOH-DCHD), made known the position of the City of Davao that the DOH-DCHD undertake the construction of the three (3) facilities, with the City only providing the already available lots situated in Brgy. Catigan, Brgy. Malagos, and Brgy. Lasang;
WHEREAS, the DOH Davao Center for Health Development (DOH-DCHD), in its 17 February 2021 to the CHO, declared its willingness and capacity to undertake the project, without violating relevant policy guidelines issued by the Department of Budget and Management (DBM);
WHEREAS, the Office of the City Legal Officer, through Legal Opinion No. 895-2020, recommended that after the necessary corrections which have since been performed, the MOA be forwarded to the Sangguniang Panlungsod for the requisite legislative authority.
NOW THEREFORE, he is received, as it is hereby resolved, A RESOLUTION TO ENACT AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF TEMPORARY TREATMENT AND MONITORING FACILITIES (TTMF).
RESOLVED FURTHER, that copies of this Resolution be furnished to the City Mayor's Office through the City Administrator's Office, the City Health Office, the Department of Health, and all their concerned offices/departments, for their information and guidance.
Enacted this day of March 2021 in the City of Davao, Philippines

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH (DOH) RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF TEMPORARY TREATMENT AND MONITORING FACILITIES (TTMF)

Ord. No. 0531-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND CONSTRUCT TEMPORARY TREATMENT AND MONITORING FACILITIES (TTMF)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b) sub-
paragraph (vi) of RA 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991 provides, to wit:
"Section 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.-

xxx xxx xxx

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

xxx xxx xxx

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the MOA to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and the Department of Health relative to the construction of Temporary Treatment and Monitoring Facilities (TTMF).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provisions of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, April 6, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
