Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO AND BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND MAMAY INN & RESORT DESIGNATING THE LATTER AS A QUARANTINE FACILITY TO BE MANAGED AND OPERATED BY THE CITY OF DAVAO FOR A PERIOD OF THREE (3) MONTHS FROM JULY 22, 2021".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 22 (a) (5) and (c) and Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991 or RA 7160 provides that:

"Section 22. Corporate Powers.
(a) Every local government unit, as a corporation, shall have the following powers:
xxx
(5) To enter into contracts; and
xxx
(c) Unless otherwise provided in this Code, no contract may be entered into by the local chief executive in behalf of the local government unit without prior authorization by the sanggunian concerned. A legible copy of such contract shall be posted at a conspicuous place in the provincial capital or the city, municipal or barangay hall."

xxx

xxx
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs, projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this connection, shall:
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sanggunian panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance".

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign the renewal of the Memorandum of Agreement to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Mamay Inn & Resort designating the latter as a quarantine facility to be managed and operated by the City of Davao for a period of three (3) months from July 22, 2021.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL PERSONS BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Agreement, made and entered, by and between:

MAMAY INN & RESORT, a corporation, organized and existing by virtue of the of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address in Mamay Road, Buhangin, Davao City, represented herein by its Manager, ARMAND L. DOBLAS, Filipino, of legal age, residing in Davao City hereinafter referred to as the "MANAGER";

and

CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit, organized in existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with official address at City Hall Building, City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented by its City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "LGU".

WITHNESSETH: That

WHEREAS, the entire country is under the state of National Public Health Emergency by virtue of Proclamation No. 922 and calls for immediate decisive action of the government to contain the transmission of COVID-19, through healthcare, medical tests, treatment and quarantine, among others;

WHEREAS, COVID-19 remains a threat to public health and national security which can undermine the social, economic and well-being of the society.

WHEREAS, among the measures undertaken by the LGU to contain the spread of COVID 19 is the isolation of the patient and those under monitoring. For this purpose, the LGU is in need of available building or spaces that will serve as quarantine/isolation area for those affected with the present pandemic, to include the frontliners.

WHEREAS, recognizing the threats brought about by the COVID 19 pandemic to the well-being of the Filipinos, the MANAGER desires to assist and support the LGU to contain the spread of the said COVID-19 virus by offering MAMAY INN & RESORT, as isolation or quarantine area.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties hereto agree the following:

--- [Page 8] ---
1. AREA, PURPOSE AND MANAGEMENT

1.1 The Parties hereby designate that the building of the MANAGER known as MAMAY INN & RESORT located along Mamay Road, Buhangin, Davao City, as Quarantine Area (hereinafter referred to as the "AREA"), primarily for the residents of Davao City who are affected with the pandemic, to be managed and operated by the LGU.

2. TERM / DURATION

2.1 This Agreement serves as a renewal of the contract which shall commence on 22 July 2021 for a term of three (3) months.

2.2 This Agreement may be renewed or extended for a similar period of three (3) months, under mutually acceptable terms and conditions, subject to the requirements of public health and the declared ECQ period. The LGU shall inform the MANAGER at least fifteen (15) days before the expiration of this Agreement of its intention to renew and or extend.

3. THE MANAGER

3.1 Make available and allows the MAMAY INN & RESORT as Quarantine Area for COVID 19 patients (suspect and probable cases) as determined beforehand by the LGU.

3.2 Allows the City Government of Davao to introduce improvements on the AREA for the purpose of converting it into an isolation facility and to bring beds, beddings, and other fixtures inside the AREA as needed.

3.3 Allow the personnel of the LGU to have access to the AREA anytime of the day and night including the use of water and electricity.

3.4 Subject to prior notice, may enter the AREA during reasonable hours of the day for the purpose of inspection, maintenance and repair of any work necessary for the preservation, conservation, improvement of the AREA.

4. THE LGU

4.1 Undertake to prepare the AREA for quarantine purpose following the guidelines issue by the Department of Health and other appropriate body.

4.2 Pay rental for the following areas:

a. First Area (Resort):
- 36 units airconditioned room at P 10,000.00/mo. Plus 12% VAT;

--- [Page 9] ---
5 units airconditioned Family Room at P 20,000.00/mo.
Plus 12% VAT;
1 unit airconditioned Superior Room at P 15,000.00/mo.
Plus 12% VAT

b. Second Area (Inn):
45 units airconditioned room at P 10,000.00/mo.
Plus 12% VAT;

c. Bulwagan (as command center) 1 unit at P 5,000.00/mo.
Plus 12% VAT;

d. Function Room (Stock/Isolation Room) 1 unit at P 10,000.00/mo.
Plus 12% VAT;

e. Note: Started on September 22, 2020
Lobby Area (stock room) 1 unit at P 10,000.00/mo.
Plus 12% VAT

4.3 Provide food, laundry, medical and other necessary services to the patients.

4.4 Keep and maintain the AREA in a clean, safe and sanitary condition.

4.5 Provide Medical teams and support staff and ensure that all frontliners in the AREA shall use personal protective equipment (PPE) to prevent exposure to hazards that cause serious injury or illness, including but not limited to masks, gloves, etc.

4.6 Assume all actual usage costs of consumption of all utilities and any and all services i.e. electric, telephone, water, security, etc., required in the AREA and pay for all charges for these utilities and services as they fall due.

4.7 Monitor and supervise the AREA to ensure the security, well-being, and health of the frontliners and the patients.

4.8 Within fifteen (15) days after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, it shall cause the cleaning, sanitizing and disinfecting the AREA. It shall surrender and return the premises in as good condition as the premises were actually found at the beginning of the use/occupancy, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

4.9 It shall comply with the rules on water management and ensure that no hazardous materials, toxic wastes and chemicals are left in the AREA upon expiration or termination of this Agreement.

--- [Page 10] ---
4.10 Bear the cost of repairs necessary to preserve and maintain the AREA in such good, serviceable and fit condition during the effectiveness of this Agreement.
4.11 Not keep, deposit or store in the AREA any noxious, toxic, or flammable materials or substances or any other substances or materials which may constitute a fire, environmental or safety hazard.
5. THIRD PARTY LIABILITY
5.1 The LGU during its occupancy of the AREA shall hold the MANAGER free and harmless from any and all damages, liabilities or responsibilities to any person, natural or juridical, or to any property, arising out of or as a consequence of the use of the AREA by LGU, its agents, employees except when such damages, liabilities or responsibilities are due to the fault, or negligence of the MANAGER, its officers, employees, representatives and/or agents, in which case, the MANAGER shall be liable for such loss, damage and/or injury.
