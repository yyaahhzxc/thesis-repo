AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF JACKBILT INDUSTRIES, INC., REPRESENTED BY ITS PRESIDENT AND COO, MR. FRANCISCO G. BALAGTAS, FOR EXCEPTION FROM ARTICLE IX, SECTION 2 OF THE COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY, 2011, FOR THE OPERATION OF A CONCRETE HOLLOW BLOCKS PLANT IN BARANGAY CALLAWA, BULANGIN DISTRICT, THIS CITY

Ord. No. 0611-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE REQUEST OF JACKBILT INDUSTRIES, INC., REPRESENTED BY ITS PRESIDENT AND COO, MR. FRANCISCO G. BALAGATAS, FOR EXCEPTION FROM ARTICLE IX SECTION 2 OF THE COMPREHENSIVE ZONING ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY (2013-2022) FOR THE OPERATION of CONCRETE HOLLOW BLOCKS PLANT IN BARANGAY CALLAWA, BUHANGIN DISTRICT, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. SCOPE - The request for exception from Article IX Section 2 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) of Jackbilt Industries, Inc., represented by its President and COO, Mr. Francisco G. Balagatas, for the operation of a Concrete Hollow Blocks Plant located in Barangay Callawa, Buhangin District, this City, is hereby approved.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE - The Concrete Hollow Blocks Plant project will occupy a total area of FIFTY-FOUR THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED SIX (54,106) square meters, more or less, covered by Transfer Certificate of Title No. 146-202020532 located in Barangay Callawa, Buhangin District, Davao City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any part or provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, any part or provision not affected thereby shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, June 15, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.
