AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND POLYCLINIC DAVAO INCORPORATED, FOR THE PROVISION OF LABORATORY PROCEDURES TO COVID-19 SUSPECTED PATIENTS IN DAVAO CITY

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0506-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND POLYCLINIC DAVAO INCORPORATED, FOR THE PROVISION OF LABORATORY PROCEDURES TO ASYMPTOMATIC COVID-19 SUSPECTED PATIENTS IN DAVAO CITY."

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides to wit:
"SECTION 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.- xxx xxx xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
xxx xxx xxx
(w) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;"
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City of Davao and Polyclinic Davao, Inc., for the provision of laboratory procedures to asymptomatic COVID-19 suspected patients in Davao City.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, March 09, 2021, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:

CHARITO N. SANTOS
Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOWN ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This agreement is entered into by and between:

POLYCLINIC DAVAO, INCORPORATED, a domestic corporation organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines and with principal office address at Santiago Artigas and Aurora Quezon St., Davao City, represented by its President, DR. MEREDITH P. COLINA, herein referred to as the "CLINIC".

CITY OF DAVAO, a 1st class highly urbanized city in Mindanao, represented by its Chief Executive, MAYOR SARA Z. DUTERTE, whose office is located at the City Hall, San Pedro St., District Poblacion of Davao herein referred to as the "LGU of Davao".

WITNESSETH: That

WHEREAS, the CLINIC represents and warrants itself as an established medical clinic and diagnostic center with professionally and technically qualified manpower and with adequate medical equipment and facilities capable and competent to perform all the medical services subject on the Agreement;

WHEREAS, the CLINIC has offered its services to the LGU of Davao to provide laboratory procedures to Asymptomatic COVID 19 suspect of Davao City isolated at the different quarantine facilities namely: RMC Gym, Palm Residences Inn, Sta. Ana Central Elementary School and Kapitan Tomas Monteverde Elementary School;

WHEREAS, the LGU of Davao has agreed to engage to provide the service of the CLINIC for the above mentioned purpose under the terms and conditions hereinafter set forth;

NOW THEREFORE, for and its consideration of the foregoing premises and of mutual covenants and stipulations hereinafter contained, the LGU of Davao and the CLINIC have agreed, and do hereby agree as follows:

1. The CLINIC shall provide adequate and effective medical services to the LGU of Davao as follows:
a) Complete Blood Count (CBC)
b) Chest X-ray

2. The LGU of Davao shall provide the necessary PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) of the CLINIC'S MOBILE TEAM and that of the patients.

3. This agreement shall be enforced from January 1, 2021 to December 31, 2021.

4. FEES. For the above mentioned service, the LGU of Davao shall pay the CLINIC the hereunder stated amount:

PACKAGE RATE FOR CBC & CHEST X-RAY: FIVE HUNDRED PESOS PER PATIENT (PHP 500.00/pax)

5. BILLING: Statement of Account (SQA) which includes the name of the patient, date, of examinations and itemized fees shall be forwarded to the LGU of Davao. All SQA shall be settled by the LGU of Davao and shall pay the CLINIC within thirty (30) days after receipt of complete billing requirements.

--- [Page 5] ---
6. CONFIDENTIALITY OF INFORMATION. Both parties agree and undertake to hold all data and information received under or an account of this Agreement in strict confidence and not to disclose such data or information to any person unless required by law existing in the Philippines and unless there is written permission from the other party.

7. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement signed by both parties hereto and no modification or amendment of this Agreement shall be binding upon either party without the prior written consent of the other party.

8. TERMINATION. This Agreement is subject to termination by either party on a thirty (30) days advance notice in writing.

9. THE CLINIC agrees to attend to and handle to the selected patients of the LGU of Davao, conscientiously and with best care, skill and ability, and shall at all times work to protect the interest of the LGU of Davao.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have set their hands this day of 14 APR 2021 at Davao City.
