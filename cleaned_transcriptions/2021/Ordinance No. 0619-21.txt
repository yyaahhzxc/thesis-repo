**MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT**

**Page | 2**

**Section 1: Purpose**

The purpose of this Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) is to establish a partnership between the DSWD and the LGU which is aimed at providing the constituents with the most appropriate recovery measures to cushion the impact of COVID-19 in its locality and to implement a social protection program that will address the needs of the poor and marginalized and promote inclusive growth in the LGU.

**Section 2: Objectives**

The DSWD and the LGU agree to engage in a partnership with the objectives of:

(i) assisting in the economic recovery among the poor and marginalized;

(ii) capacitating communities to protect themselves from the effects of plausible similar disasters in the future; and

(iii) institutionalizing the DROM/P that was committed by the National Government to be adopted as a strategy under the Policy Loan for Disaster Risk and Response Management (DPL-DRRM).

**Section 3: Responsibilities of the Parties**

**Section 3.1: The DSWD through the KALALAH-CIDSS KKB shall:**

1. Coordinate with the LGU through the Regional Inter-Agency Task Force on Emerging Infectious Diseases (RIATI-EID)/City Disaster Risk Reduction Management Council (CDRRMC) and/or Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction Management Council (BDRCM) on the introduction, formulation, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of KALALAH-CIDSS KKB programs in to be undertaken in the LGU.

--- [Page 18] ---
2. Provide additional COVID-19 data generated from the Rapid Assessment of COVID-19 Effects/Impact/72 hours after the assessment, necessary for informed decision-making by the LGU on the implementation of KALAHI- CDDSS KKB COVID-19 response project using the DROM/P;

3. In strict compliance to Department of Health (DoH) protocols, deploy staff who will serve as DSWD's main unit for coordination and support in the implementation of the COVID-19 Response Project through the DROM/P;

4. Provide technical assistance to enable the LGU to enhance the community and people's capacity for effective program implementation;

5. Work with the LGU in ensuring all DSWD Projects, Activities and Programs (PAPs) are implemented in a participatory and transparent manner, building greater social accountability of Government to citizens, while ensuring efficient and effective delivery of results; and

6. Require the Area Coordinating Team (ACT) to ensure that all reports involving financial and transaction documents from the Barangay Local Government Unit (BLGU) and LGU Accountant are submitted to the DSWD and resident Commission on Audit (COA) of the LGU, respectively. Details of financial and BLGU reports as well as transaction documents are enumerated in the Specific Implementation Agreement (SIA).

Section 3.2. The LGU shall:

1. Coordinate with the DSWD on its various PAPs, that are currently being implemented and/or are planned for implementation, in the LGU relative to COVID-19 impact mitigation and recovery;

2. Through its Local Chief Executive, take a strong leadership role in ensuring that the implementation of the PAPs shall be responsive/in accordance to the issued policies, guidelines and objectives of the National Government;

3. Provide relevant data to the DSWD bi-monthly, necessary for informed decision making by the DSWD on the implementation of its various PAPs in the LGU;

4. Create a City Coordinating Team hereinafter referred to as CCI, to serve as the body to coordinate technical assistance, monitoring, and resolution of issues in the implementation of the DSWD's PAPs in the LGU;

5. Ensure the involvement of the City Health Officer (CHO), being a member of the City IATF, in planning and decision-making to guarantee that all activities shall observe proper COVID-19 protocols;

6. Authorize at least one (1) Barangay Kapagwad, preferably the head of the Socio-economic Affairs Committee, to assume the role of mobilizing personnel such as Barangay Tenedo, Barangay Health Workers (BFW's) or a composite team to support KALAHI-CDDSS KSB activities at the Barangay level.

7. Include the KALAHI-CIDSS KKB Area Coordinator and three (3) Community Volunteers as members in the City Disaster Risk Reduction Management Council (CDRRCM) for the duration of the project implementation;

8. Adopt measures to ensure the safety and security of DSWD personnel who may be assigned to coordinate or facilitate implementation of the PAP's in the LGU;

9. Work with the SWSD in ensuring all PAP's in the LGU are implemented in a participatory and transparent manner, building greater social accountability of government to citizens while ensuring efficient and effective delivery of results.

10. Work with BLGU officials in ensuring the allocation on availability of a "bridge" fund to be used for the opening of community accounts; and

11. Require the LGU Accountant to submit the necessary financial reports, transaction documents to the resident Commissioner on Audit (COA) of the LGU as prescribed in COA Circular 2019-001 dated January 30, 2019 and ensure the submission of reports from the BLGU to the DSWD Field Office. These financial reports, transaction documents and BLGU reports are enumerated in the SLA;

Section 4: Voluntary Commitment

This MOA is a voluntary commitment between the parties, and does not obligate the parties to engage solely with the other, nor restrict any activity of each party to engage in partnership with other groups or agencies for the delivery and/or implementation of social protection interventions. No party shall have any right, power, or authority to create any obligation, express or implied, for and in behalf of the other party.

Section 5: Resources to be Provided

Each party shall be responsible for allocating and managing resources to cover costs of PAP's to implement the purpose of this agreement, and to cover activities and matters related to the fulfillment of each party's responsibilities as stipulated under Section 3 above. The specific amount of resources to be committed by both parties including where applicable, system for fund transfer shall be in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Section 6: Grievance Redress System

A grievance redress system (GRS) shall be jointly established or strengthened by the DSWD and the LGU to provide a venue for stakeholders to raise their concerns and complaints on any aspect of implementation of the PAP's. The LGU may explore its existing communication on redress approaches such as those in the Barangay Justice System and the Citizens' Complaint or Assistance Desk and promote the GRS of DSWD in the KALAHI CIDDS - KKB and Pantawid Pilipilang Pilipino Program.

Section 7: Effectivity, Amendment and Termination

This Agreement shall take effect upon signing by both parties until and may be extended by mere amendment in writing of this MOA and which should explicitly or tacitly cover the SIA. The agreement may be terminated by any party subject to a thirty (30) day written notice of termination.

Any amendments to this agreement shall be made in writing and affirmed by BOTH PARTIES.
