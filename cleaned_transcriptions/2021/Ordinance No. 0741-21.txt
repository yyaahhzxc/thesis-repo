**TERMS AND CONDITIONS FOR THE USE OF DEPED SCHOOL BY THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT AS A TEMPORARY QUARANTINE OR ISOLATION FACILITY OF LAST RESORT**

The Local Government Unit of the City of Davao with office address at City Hall Building, City Hall Drive, Davao City, and represented by SARA Z. DUTERTE, City Mayor, herein after referred to as "LGU," and the Local Government Unit of the City of General Santos with office address at City Hall Building, City Hall Drive, General Santos City, and represented by ALLAN G. FARNAZO, Director IV, Regional Director, hereinafter referred to as the "RC," the use of JOSE BASTIDA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL, with address at Tonti St., District 1, General Santos City, hereinafter referred to as the "School," as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility relative to the COVID-19 Public Health Emergency.

The request is made under the following premises:

The President issued Proclamation No. 922 dated March 8, 2020, "Declaring a State of Public Health Emergency Throughout the Philippines," in view of the COVID-19 outbreak.

Section 2 of Proclamation No. 922, a 2020, states that "all government agencies and LGUs are hereby enjoined to render full assistance and cooperation and mobilize the necessary resources to undertake urgent, urgent, and appropriate response measures in a timely manner to curtail and eliminate the Covid-19 threat."

The LGU needs a temporary quarantine or isolation facility relative to its response to the COVID-19 public health situation and it has no other available place or structure for use, than it is hereby requested that the School be used as a quarantine or isolation facility for ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS heretofore referred to as the "School," as a quarantine or isolation facility of the LGU, subject to OFFICE MEMORANDUM OM-SEC-2020-002.

The RO, acting upon the recommendation of the Schools Division Office, approved the request, in adherence to applicable guidelines of the Department of Health (DOH) and the World Health Organization (WHO), to use the School as a quarantine or isolation facility, considering by the LPU of the need to use the School as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility due to absence of other available facility, as certified by the provincial/ city/municipal health officer, as well as pursuant to the LGU's of a planned management of the facility under the supervision of the city/municipal health officers, subject to DOH and other relevant guidelines.

The approval of the RO is conditional upon the undertaking of the LGU for the safekeeping of all property and variables in the school premises during the operation of the facility, payment of all expenses incurred by the LGU in connection with the use of the School as a quarantine or isolation facility, the conduct of the general cleaning and fumigation, and repair and/or replacement of damaged or lost school facilities as a result of, and incidentally, the use of the School by the LGU.

--- [Page 13] ---
Fostering premises considered, the LGU covenants and binds itself to the following terms and conditions set by the DepEd in its use of the School as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility of last resort.

I. SCOPE

This Terms and Conditions (TAC) pertains to the use of JOSE BASTIDA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility of the LGU in relation to the COVID-19 outbreak during the period of Public Health Emergency under Proclamation No. 221, s. 2020. The School may only be used by the LGU for that purpose if classes are not being conducted therein.

Quarantine refers to the separation of persons who are not ill but who may be exposed to an infectious agent or disease, with the objective of monitoring symptoms and early detection of COVID-19 cases.

Isolation refers to the separation of ill or infected persons from others, so as to prevent the spread of infection or contamination.

II. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

A. The LGU shall:

1. Prepare the area approved by the RO to be used as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility of the School, and shall not require students/pupils and DepEd personnel to be engaged in the preparation of the School for this purpose. The LGU shall vacate the designated areas and remove education equipment and resources thereof, under the supervision and guidance of the School.

2. Use only the specified School area and facilities approved by the RO to be used as quarantine or isolation facility.

3. Take charge of the management and maintenance of the School used as a quarantine or isolation facility, including the provision of necessary supplies and equipment, and in accordance with the applicable guidelines of the DDOH and the WHO, and all other applicable laws, rules and guidelines.

4. Cordon off the identified quarantine or isolation facility from the rest of the School premises or facilities;

5. Provide measures to ensure the safety and security of the School.

6. Provide all resources and equipment required for the use of the School as a quarantine or isolation facility.

7. Ensure that the water supply will be sufficient and there will be no cooking in the School during its use as a quarantine or isolation facility.

--- [Page 14] ---
8. Ensure compliance with the sanitation and health standards applicable to the School before, during, and after its use by the LGU as a quarantine or isolation facility, including disinfesting the premises used and ensuring the use of required protective equipment.

9. Facilitate and provide for the general cleaning, maintenance, and upkeep of School premises, structures, facilities, equipment, and resources, and repair and replace them if damaged, destroyed, or otherwise rendered unusable by the use of the School as a quarantine facility. Such repair and replacement shall be completed prior to the return of the use of the School to the School Head.

10. Clean up and fumigate the School within a maximum period of one week after its use by the LGU as a quarantine or isolation facility. The School Head shall certify whether the School is fit for education use subsequent to the fumigation, in accordance with pertinent guidelines of the DOH and other applicable rules and usances.

11. Pay for utilities during its use of the School as a quarantine or isolation facility, and until it has returned the use of the School to the School Head for education use and the School Head has accepted the same.

12. Confirm that the School and facilities will be returned to their original or better state, compared to their condition upon commencement of its use as quarantine or isolation facility, by the LGU.

13. Prior to return by the LGU of the use of the School to the School Head, secure clearance from the School with respect to the LGU's compliance with the TAC.

B. The School shall:

1. Identify the School spaces/structures/comfort rooms and other facilities approved by the RO to be used as temporary quarantine or isolation facility. School areas beyond those approved by the RO shall be off limits to the LGU and users of the quarantine or isolation facility.

2. In preparation for the use of the School as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility, ensure proper storage and safeguarding of all learning and education equipment, structures, materials, and supplies, and provide for the removal of such items from the School upon request by the LGU in the vesting of designated School spaces/structures and removal of education equipment and enclosures thereof by the LGU, provided that all DepEd personnel involved in the preparation and implementation of the quarantine or isolation facility shall comply with all existing health precautions and social distancing protocols of the DOH and the WHC.

3. Prepare and implement work protocols that would not require DepEd personnel to report to the LGU for the performance of regular functions during the period the School is used as a quarantine or isolation facility.

4. Inform the LGU of any damage, destruction, or loss of School facilities and resources due to, or incidental to, the use of the School, if any, to serve as basis for repair, maintenance, and/or replacement by the LGU after use of the School as a quarantine or isolation facility.

--- [Page 15] ---
5. Have the authority to monitor and access the School, subject to strict observance of safety and health requirements applicable during the public health emergency.

C. The LGU and the School

1. The school shall make a School Inventory and record the condition of school premises, structures, facilities, equipment and resources immediately prior to use, after the use and during the quarantine or isolation facility by the LGU. The inventory prior to use shall be signed by both the School Head and Mayor and shall form an integral part of this TAC (See Annex A).

2. Any and all expenses relative to the preparation and operation of the School as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility as well as the cleaning, fumigation and restoration of the School to its educational use shall be shouldered by the LGU.

3. The School and LGU shall each designate focal person/s who shall be responsible for coordination between the Parties to ensure compliance with this TAC.

III. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1. The LGU shall use the School as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility only upon its showing to the RO that there is no other available space or structure which can be used for the purpose. The use of the School as a temporary quarantine or isolation facility at last resort is a temporary measure only and shall be terminated when the space or structure for the purpose become available, the LGU shall terminate its use of the School as a quarantine or isolation facility.

2. This TAC shall take effect upon its execution, and shall be in full force and effect for a period of 90 days from the date of its Public Hearing, hereinafter referred to as "Proclamation" No. 923, U. O., unless further extended by the LGU or the RO in writing, or sooner terminated by either the LGU or the RO upon fifteen-day written notice for valid reasons.

3. When warranted by compelling circumstances, the RO may amend, modify or supplement this TAC for valid reasons, upon prior written notice to the LGU.

4. The LGU represents and warrants that it has the required power and authority to make, deliver, and comply with the provisions of this TAC, and has taken all the necessary actions to duly and validly authorize the execution, delivery and performance of this TAC.

5. Should any provision of this TAC conflict with the provisions of this TAC, and other Agreements between the LGU and the DepEd through the Regional Officer, Schools Division Office, or the School, this TAC shall prevail.
