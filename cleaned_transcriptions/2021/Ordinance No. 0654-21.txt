Ord. No. 0654-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF DONATION BETWEEN THE
CITY OF DAVAO, PATAG RC ASSOCIATION, BARANGAY MABUHAY WOMEN'S
ASSOCIATION, PAFALUMA BREAD AND PASTRY ASSOCIATION (PBFPA), AND
GUMAMELA WOMEN'S AND RURAL IMPROVEMENT CLUB ASSOCIATION, FOR
THE DONATION OF FOUR (4) UNITS OF TRIKEBAYAN 3-WHEELED ELECTRIC
VEHICLES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of the Local
Government Code of 1991 provides:

"Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly
granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers
necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective
governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the
general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions,
local governments shall promote and protect the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation between the City of Davao, and Patag RC Association, Barangay Mabuhay Women's Association, Pafaluma Bread and Pastry Association (PBFPA), and Gumamela Women's and Rural Improvement Club Association for the donation of four (4) units of Trikebayan 3-Wheeled Electric Vehicles.

SECTION 4. SEPARAPABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, July 13, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
