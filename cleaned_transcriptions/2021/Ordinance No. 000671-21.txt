ORDINANCE NO. 0671-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE CREATING A TRUST FUND USING PHILHEALTH FUNDS AND OTHER FUNDS FOR THE USE OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, MARILOG DISTRICT HOSPITAL, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE FINANCIAL BUDGET LAWS AND ACCOUNTING PROCEDURES OF THE CITY, PROVIDING RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR ITS DISBURSEMENT AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 6
Ord. No. 0671-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE CREATING A TRUST FUND USING PHILHEALTH FUNDS AND OTHER FUNDS FOR THE USE OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, MARILOG DISTRICT HOSPITAL, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PERTINENT BUDGETING LAWS AND ACCOUNTING PROCEDURES OF THE CITY, PROVIDING RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR ITS DISBURSEMENT AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES".

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - The Definitions and Terms used in the revised Implementing Rules and Regulations of the National Health Insurance Act of 1995 (Republic Act No. 7875) are hereby adopted. As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:

1. PHILHEALTH (PHIC) - refers to the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation created under Republic Act No. 7875, otherwise known as the National Health Insurance Act of 1995

2. PHILHEALTH CLAIMS - refers to claims filed by the service provider with the PHIC to recover the costs/expenses incurred in the treatment of patients. There are two (2) PHIC claims separately, the PAY TO HCI claims and the PAY TO PROFESSIONAL FEE claims

3. MARILOG DISTRICT HOSPITAL TRUST FUND ACCOUNTS - refers to the PHIC, HCI and PF accounts, account for other hospital claims and fees, and donations

4. PROFESSIONAL FEES (PF) - refers to PHIC reimbursements for professional services rendered by health providers in the hospitals

5. HEALTH CARE INSTITUTION (HCI) - refers to health care provider institution

6. HMO - refers to the Health Maintenance Organizations

7. INSTITUTIONAL EXPENSES - refers to the expenses that shall be incurred for trainings needed for accreditations and for any other licensing requirements, emergency purchases or the unforeseen expenses that are not included in the Manual Of Operation.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE AND SCOPE OF APPLICATION - This ordinance shall cover the City Government of Davao Marilog District Hospital, a duly accredited service provider of the PHIC.

SECTION 4. CREATION OF THE MARILOG DISTRICT HOSPITAL TRUST FUND - There is hereby created a Marilog District Hospital Trust Fund. This hereby create three (3) accounts, which shall be maintained to draw accounts from the proceeds of the following:

1. PHIC claims paid to the Health Care Institution (MDH);
2. PHIC claims that are separately issued by PHIC to Professional Fees;
3. Claims from financial assistance, grants, subsidies, insurances, Health Maintenance Organizations (HMO), and co-payments.

The City Accountant's Office and other relevant offices are hereby tasked to cause the assignment of allotment class and account code pursuant to the recent government accounting system on all sum accruing to this fund. Accounts accruing from the different PHIC claims shall be treated separately and distinctly from each other as stipulated in the PHIC disbursement of claims.

All other charges, fees, and other income received from the management and operations not given to PHIC shall be remitted and deposited by the City Treasurer to the Marilog District Hospital Trust Fund. All Donations received by the Davao City Government intended for the development and operation of the Marilog District Hospital shall also be deposited by the City Treasurer to the said Account.

SECTION 5. PHILHEALTH CIRCULAR NO.0035, SERIES OF 2013:

A. COMPUTATION OF THE PHIC REIMBURSEMENTS

1. For medical case rates, the HCI fee and PF shall be seventy percent (70%) and thirty percent (30%) of the case rate amount respectively.
2. For procedure case rates, the following shall be the basis for computation except for specified cases:
   a. The PF shall be computed as RVU-Relative Value Unit x 56 x 1.5 except for specified case rates.
   b. The HCI fee shall be the remaining balance when the PF is subtracted from the case amount.

B. DISTRIBUTION OF THE PHIC REIMBURSEMENTS

1. The entire case rate amount, including the PF, shall be paid directly to the HCI concerned to the HCI (MDH) ACCOUNT and to the PROFESSIONAL FEE. The HCI shall act as the withholding tax agent for the PF.
2. The PF shall be distributed by the HCI within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of receipt of reimbursement. Policies and procedures on the distribution of PF shall be drafted and enforced by the HCI based on the agreements between the HCI and the Professionals. Reports of noncompliance to this provision shall be forwarded to the Health Care Delivery Management Division (HCDMD) and shall be included as a violation of the HCI to the Health Care Provider Performance Commitment of the PHIC.
3. The government HCI shall facilitate the payment of the pooled PF share to the health personnel.

All of the provisions and amendments there may be in PHILHEALTH CIRCULAR NO.0035 SERIES OF 2013: ACR POLICY NO. 2: IMPLEMENTING GUIDELINES ON MEDICAL AND PROCEDURE CASE RATES are hereby adopted.

SECTION 6. DISBURSEMENT OF THE MDH TRUST FUND - The fund created under this ordinance shall be disbursed in the manner that will serve exclusively and strictly for the following purposes:

A. Purchase of medical equipment,
B. Purchase of medical supplies,
C. Purchase of diagnostics and laboratory reagents and supplies,
D. Purchase of office supplies,
E. Purchase of vehicles,
F. Construction of hospital facilities,
G. Repair and maintenance of equipment and facilities,
H. Institutional expenses,
I. Honoraria for paid services/ specialists,
J. Incentives for individuals/ institution for the referral of patients,
K. Community extension services (corporate social responsibilities), and
L. Other related expenses.

The appropriation shall be based on the Annual Operation Plan and the Projected Procurement Management Plan of the hospital and on the Memorandum of Agreements between the hospital and the specialists, individuals or institutions for the services and referral incentives.

SECTION 7. DISBURSEMENT OF THE CLAIMS FROM FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE INSURANCES, HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATIONS (HMO), AND CO-PAYMENTS - The proceeds from these claims shall go to the General Fund of the City Government and shall be used for hospital needs, health programs of the City Health Office, and other health related activities. The professional fees covered under this section shall not be subjected for inclusion and distribution among the hospital personnel to the pooled amount from PHC.

The Marilag District Hospital's Governing Board through its administrator, shall submit to the City Treasurer its annual work and financial plan.

Provided that, all disbursements or withdrawals from the Marilag District Hospital Trust Fund shall be based on the approved annual work and financial plan passed by the Governing Board. Programs or projects not included in the work and financial plan may be funded through appropriate board resolution duly approved by the Marilag District Hospital Governing Board.

Provided further that all matters pertaining to collection, disbursement and withdrawal shall conform with the proper accounting and auditing procedure of the City Treasurer's Office and other proper offices of Davao City.

**SECTION 8. DISTRIBUTION OF PHIC PF POOLED AMOUNT - The**

provisions in this ordinance shall only cover the hospital personnel, individuals, and institutions involved in giving services within the hospital.

The pooled amount shall be distributed as follows:

1. Fifty percent (50%) shall be distributed among the doctors of which the Chief of Hospital shall be given two (2) shares and the rest shall be given one (1) share each.
2. The other Fifty percent (50%) shall be distributed among the other non-doctor personnel of the hospital, wherein three (3) shares shall be given to the Administrative Officer, three (3) shares to the Nurse Supervisor, two (2) shares for the nurses, head of the diagnostics and laboratory, operating room, delivery room, pharmacy, outpatient department, and other treatment units. The other hospital personnel shall be given one (1) share each.
3. The eligibility of the personnel to avail incentives under this provision shall take effect three (3) months after the effectivity of the employment and shall be terminated upon the end of the contract of the employee.

The distribution of this pooled amount, with the guidance of PHIC, shall be agreed upon by the personnel and shall be approved by the City Government of Davao.

Further, the following disbursement shall be subject to pertinent budgeting law, rules and regulations.

**SECTION 9. DISTRIBUTION OF MARILOG DISTRICT HOSPITAL'S OTHER FUND - All other income which will accrue to the Marilog District Hospital shall accrue to the General Fund of the City Government of Davao, including, but not limited to, the following:**

1.) PCSO Medical assistance;
2.) Lingap Medical assistance;
3.) DSWD (CMAP);
4.) OSAP;
5.) OP;
6.) Other income.

Further, the following disbursements shall be subject to pertinent budgeting laws, rules and regulations.

**SECTION 10. COMPUTATION AND DISTRIBUTION OF THE PHIC PF FOR MEDICAL AND PROCEDURE CASE RATES - The computation of procedure case rates shall be based on PHIC Circular No.0035, s. 2013. The PF of the physicians who are medical specialists and physicians who performed the procedure shall be given fifty percent (50%) of the PHIC PF and the other fifty percent (50%) shall be added to the PF pooled amount.**

**SECTION 11. HOSPITAL CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY (HCSR)**
The hospital shall formulate its Manual of Operation (MOP) for the management, accounting, and disbursement of the HCSR. The policies and procedures in the MOP shall be agreed upon by the general assembly of the hospital and approved by the City Government.

**SECTION 12. PROCESSING OF CLAIMS**
To expedite the claims, an Information Technology (IT) and E-Claims Processor Section with the desired personnel shall be established in the hospital.

**SECTION 13. SEPARATIBILITIY CLAUSE**
If, for any reason, a particular provision of the ordinance is declared unconstitutional or contrary to the existing law, other parts which are not affected shall remain effective, in force, and operational.

**SECTION 14. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE**
The ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its approval. Let a copy of this ordinance be posted at the City Hall and Sangguniang Panlungsod in compliance with the requirements provided for by law.

**ENACTED, on the 3rd day of August, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.**
