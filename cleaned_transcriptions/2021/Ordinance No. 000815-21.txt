**SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS:** The use of funds under the Annual Budget of the City Government of Davao, for Calendar Year 2022, utilized in violation of Title 5, Local Fiscal Administration, as embodied in Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991," shall subject the erring officials and employees to disciplinary action or to appropriate legal measures under the existing laws of the Republic of the Philippines;

**SECTION 7. USE OF SAVINGS:** The Local Chief Executive and the Presiding Officer of the Sangguniang Panlungsod are hereby authorized to augment any item in the approved Annual Budget for their respective offices from savings in other items within the same expense class of their respective appropriations as provided for in Section 336 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991."

**SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE:** If, for any reason, any Section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof, which are not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

**SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY:** The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect on January 1, 2022.

ENACTED on the 7th day of December, 2021, by a unanimous vote of the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
