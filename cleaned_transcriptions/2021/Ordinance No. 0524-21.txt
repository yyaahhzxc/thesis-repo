Ord. No. 0524-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS (NCCA) RELATIVE TO THE LATTER'S FINANCIAL SUPPORT TO THE FORMER FOR THE PROJECT ENTITLED 'DIRECTORY OF ARTISTS AND CULTURAL WORKERS.'"

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 255 (b) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991, states that:

"Section 25. (b) National Agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation of said national projects."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement between the City of Davao and the National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA) relative to the latter's financial support to the former for the project entitled "Directory of Artists and Cultural Workers."

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on March 23, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT is made and entered into this day of 2020 in Davao City, Philippines by and between:

DAVAO CITY: a local government unit (LGU) existing under Republic Act (RA) No. 3458, with principal office address at the Davao City Hall, Poblacion District, Davao, represented herein by its City Mayor, Sara Z. Duterte, hereinafter referred to as "the City";

-AND-

NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS: a government agency created and operating mainly under RA 7356, with office address at the NCCA Building, 533 General Luna Street, Intramuros, Manila, represented herein by its Executive Director, Al Ryan S. Alejandre, hereinafter referred to as "the NCCA";

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, Section 16 of the RA 7356, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, mandates every LGU to ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture within their respective territorial jurisdiction;

WHEREAS, in line with the foregoing, the Davao City Culture and Arts Council (DCCAC) was established through Sangguniang Panlungsod (SP) Resolution No. 03546-04 Series of 2004 and City Ordinance No. 019c-04 Series of 2004, to, among others, develop linkages with national institutions such as the NCCA to support the cultural activities of Davao City;

WHEREAS, the Davao SP passed Resolution No. 01593-20 Series of 2020 to favorably endorse to the NCCA the project of the City on the Directory of Artists and Cultural Workers;

WHEREAS, the NCCA, pursuant to its mandate, as prescribed in Section 12 of RA 7356, to encourage and support scholarly research into and documentation of significant cultural achievements and personalities especially in the literary, visual, and performing arts, in mass media, and in the various aspects of Filipino culture, has committed to extend financial support to the aforementioned Project up to not more than two thousand pesos (P120,000.00) only;

NOW, THEREFORE, the City and the NCCA hereby agree to perform and/or deliver the following for and into Davao City's Directory of Artists and Cultural Workers Project:

A. The City shall, through the DCCAC, shall:

1. Exercise overall control and supervision over the Project;

2. Approve the field researchers who will mobilize the Project;

3. Approve the action plan crafted by the field researchers, comprising of tasks that are not limited to, online collaboration with artists through research, survey, fieldwork, and comprehensive data collection within a period of more or less five (5) months;

4. Coordinate with the NCCA for the submission of documentary requirements for the preparation of the contracts and payment of the honoraria of the field researchers;

5. If found to be proper, approve the final output of the field researchers which will be the basis of the NCCA for the payment of their honoraria;

6. Find sources of funds for expenses incidental to the Project but are not covered by the herein-specified NCCA financial support;

--- [Page 5] ---
7. Ensure the continuous implementation of the Project phases until its completion;

and

8. Exercise other powers and functions necessary to the success of the Project.

B. The NCCA shall:

1. Prepare the contracts for the direct payment of the honoraria of the field researchers approved by the City with a total amount not exceeding two hundred thousand pesos (Phip200,000.00) only chargeable to NCCA Board Resolution No. 2021-243, Mobilization for the Strengthening of Local Culture and Arts Sector, subject to withholding tax and to existing government accounting and audit rules and regulations;

2. Process and release the honoraria of the field researchers upon submission of their final output approved by the City and acceptance thereof by the NCCA; and

3. Coordinate with the City, through the DCACC, for any and all purposes related to the Memorandum of Agreement and/or the Project.

C. Should any dispute arise from this Memorandum of Agreement the parties hereby agree to settle the same amicably.

D. This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing by both Parties and shall cease to be in force upon receipt of the field researchers of their respective honoraria.

WHEREFORE, the City and the NCCA have hereto affixed their signature this day of in Davao City, Philippines.
