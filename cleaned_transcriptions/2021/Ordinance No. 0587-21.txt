Ord. No. 587-21

Be it ordained by the Sanggunian Pan lungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY
OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BY AND BETWEEN THE
UNITED NATIONS HUMAN SETTLEMENTS PROGRAMME (UN-HABITAT) AND THE
CITY OF DAVAO, REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES, RELATIVE TO THE
IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PROJECT 'HEALTHY OCEANS AND CLEAN CITIES
INITIATIVE (HOCJ)'.

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act No.
7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 states:

"SEC. 16. General Welfare - Every local government unit shall
exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied
therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental
for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are
essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their
respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall
ensure and support among other things, the preservation and
enrichment of the environment, promote health, safety, enhance the right
of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the
development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and
technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic
prosperity, provide for and promote full employment among their
residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and
convenience of their inhabitants."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum
of Understanding by and between the United Nations Human Settlements Programme
(UN-HABITAT) and the City of Davao, Republic of the Philippines, relative to the
Implementation of the Project 'Healthy Oceans and Clean Cities Initiative (HOCJ)'.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, May 11, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

UN HABITAT FOR A BETTER URBAN FUTURE

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN THE UNITED NATIONS HUMAN SETTLEMENTS PROGRAMME AND CITY OF DAVAO, REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES

WHEREAS, the United Nations Human Settlements Programme (hereinafter referred to as "UN-Habitat", established by the General Assembly of the United Nations by its resolution 32/162 of 19 December 1977, transformed into a Programme by its resolution 56/206 of 21 December 2001, having its Headquarters in Nairobi, Kenya. UN-Habitat is the coordinating agency within the United Nations System for human settlement activities and in collaboration with governments is responsible for promoting and consolidating collaboration with all partners, including local governments, in achieving the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), in particular Goal 11 of "Making cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable", as well as the task manager of the human settlements chapter of Agenda 21 and focal point for the monitoring, evaluation and implementation of the New Urban Agenda adopted during the United Nations Conference on Housing and Sustainable Urban Development in Habitat III in Quito, Ecuador.

WHEREAS, the City of Davao, hereinafter referred to as the "City" as represented by its City Mayor, is a local government unit established under the laws and regulations of the Republic of the Philippines committed to reduce plastic waste leakages into the marine environment through the practice of reduce, reuse and recycle (3RAs) and other functional elements of proper solid waste management (SWM);

WHEREAS, UN-Habitat and the City have agreed to collaborate in the "Healthy Oceans and Clean Cities Initiative" (HOCCI), which is being supported by the People of Japan and for implementation by UN-Habitat at the Regional Office for Asia and the Pacific (ROAP) and in the Philippines, in cooperation with global, regional and national stakeholders, including the private sector, to contribute to the achievement of SDGs in the Philippines to reduce marine plastic pollution through improved data collection, enhanced SWM systems, and support to the implementation of the upcoming Philippines National Plan of Action for the Prevention, Reduction and Management of Marine Litter (NPOA-MRL);

WHEREAS, UN-Habitat and the City of Davao, hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as the "Party" recognizing of genuine, substantive cooperation and wishing to pursue such cooperation, have agreed to enter into this Memorandum of Understanding (hereinafter referred to as the "MOU") in a spirit of trust and cooperation;

NOW THEREFORE, the Parties hereto hereby agree as follows:

--- [Page 12] ---
**ARTICLE I Scope and Purpose**

1. The purpose of this MoU is to provide a framework of cooperation within which UN-Habitat and the City of Davao will collaborate under the "Healthy Oceans and Clean Cities Initiative" in the Philippines.

2. The collaboration between the Parties in Davao City will be implemented with a focus on reducing waste leakage and marine plastic litter by strengthening the engagement of local and national stakeholders, piloting more effective waste collection, recycling, and disposal systems, and increasing the livelihood options available for marginalized groups to reduce their plastic waste.

3. Subject to Article II, Clause (5) herein below, and to their respective regulations, rules, policies, practices, procedures, the Parties shall collaborate and work together to contribute to efforts to improve SWM systems in the City, reduce marine plastic pollution, and improve the overall socio-economic conditions of the people of Davao City.

4. The following shall be the primary results of the collaboration in the City of Davao:

(a) Enhanced data collection and analysis for marine plastic litter leakage;

(b) City/Province-level marine plastic litter action plan jointly developed by the Parties and relevant stakeholders in the City, including identified priority actions under HOCRCC;

(c) Waste management pilot projects implemented and documented;

(d) Establishment of a public-private partnership with support from the private sector, including, but not limited to, developing information materials and carrying out social marketing campaigns on plastic waste reduction and management, community- and youth-based education programs through various communication platforms, riverine and coastal cleanup drives, and/or alternative livelihood opportunities for women and the informal sector.

5. Engage communities and other local stakeholders to support the implementation of SWM and 3Rs initiatives and share the tools, processes, and good practices at the national and international levels.

**ARTICLE II General Responsibilities of the Parties**

1. The Parties agree to carry out their respective responsibilities in accordance with the provisions of this MoU. The Parties agree to join efforts and to maintain close working relationships in order to achieve the objectives of this MoU.

2. The Parties shall keep each other informed of all relevant activities pertaining to this MoU and shall hold consultations at any time any Party considers it appropriate.

3. The Parties shall refrain from any action that may adversely affect the interests of the other Party and fulfill their commitments with fullest regard to the terms and conditions of this MoU and the principles of the United Nations and UN-Habitat.

4. Each party shall nominate a focal point for this collaboration as stated under Article XI ("Notices"), clause (1) herein below.

5. The Parties agree that this MoU and any work plan agreed to hereunder are neither fiscal nor funding obligations documents. Any commitment to transfer anything of value involving reimbursement or leverage funds, goods or services by the Parties for any agreed activity will be outlined in separate agreements.

--- [Page 13] ---
agreements that will be made in writing by representatives of the Parties and will be independently authorized by an appropriate authority of the funding party consistent with the regulations, rules, policies and practice of the Parties. The Parties agree that this MoU does not provide such authority.

ARTICLE III

Avenues of Collaboration of the Parties

1. Subject to Article II, clause (5) herein above, the Parties will jointly collaborate on the implementation of the "Healthy Oceans and Clean Cities Initiative" in the Philippines, including:

(a) Advocate and implement the HOCCI project as an activity to prevent, reduce and manage marine plastic litter;

(b) Engage and support communities and other stakeholders in Davao City to demonstrate good practices in reducing plastic waste generation and leakage into the rivers and marine environment;

(c) Collaborate with and complement efforts of the national government and other stakeholders in advocating for the operationalization of marine litter reduction-relevant policies and programs and in the facilitation of the upcoming NAPs;

(d) Highlight and advocate all positive accomplishments in the City of Davao.

Article IV

Specific Responsibilities of the Parties

1. Subject to Article II, clause (5) herein above, the specific responsibilities of UN-Habitat are as follows:

(a) Implement donor approved projects in accordance with UN rules and regulations;

(b) Provide technical assistance to the City, together with other HOCCI project partners, to achieve the results enumerated in Article I, clause (4);

(c) Develop 3Rs-based information, education and communication materials for the project;

(d) Promote entrepreneurship, specifically among women's groups and the informal sector which would serve as the main source of income for members of the community to transform recycled plastic waste into new kinds of products;

(e) Coordinate with other UN agencies to ensure coherence with the UN principles and guidelines.

2. Subject to Article II, clause (5) herein above, the specific responsibilities of the City of Davao are as follows:

(a) Provide guidance to project management and project partners as member of the Project Management Committee (PMC);

(b) Take the lead in identifying, pursuing, and promoting local actions that reduce marine plastic litter and promote sustainable consumption and production;

(c) In relation to Article II, clause (4), organize and assign members of a city-level Technical Working Group, which shall take the lead in the planning, implementation, monitoring and reporting of project-related city work plans and initiatives, in close coordination with UN-Habitat.

--- [Page 14] ---
(d) Ensure the participation of relevant stakeholders and vulnerable communities to strengthen their capacities to manage solid waste with special attention to plastic waste;

(e) Take the lead in coordinating with relevant national government agencies in the region;

(f) Assist in the implementation of the project;

(g) Support international/national-level project activities that require city participation like PMC meetings, Expert Group Meetings, learning exchanges, forums, workshops and other events.

ARTICLE V Monitoring and Evaluation

1. The Parties shall maintain regular close consultations to monitor and review the progress of activities for each joint project that may be agreed upon.

2. The Parties will share with each other all relevant information and documents, including plans, policies, researches, reports and any other information related to the activities, outputs and finally impact of this collaboration.

3. The Parties may, wherever possible and appropriate, undertake joint missions with respect to the project, in consideration of the regulations and limitations under the COVID-19 New Normal.

4. The Parties shall keep the United Nations Resident Coordinator in the Republic of the Philippines fully informed of all actions undertaken by them in carrying out this MoU. UN-Habitat will utilize the capacity of the Habitat Programme Manager based in Manila, Republic of the Philippines, as necessary and appropriate for the effective implementation of the project.

ARTICLE VI Termination

1. This MoU may be terminated by either Party giving the other party a written notice of thirty (30) days prior to its intention to terminate. In the event of termination, the Parties will take the appropriate steps to bring activities under this MoU to a prompt and orderly conclusion.

2. The termination of this MoU shall not affect any other agreement already entered into by either Party.

ARTICLE VII Amendments

1. This MoU may be modified by written agreement between the Parties hereto. Any relevant matter for which no provision is made in this MoU will be settled by the Parties in keeping with the general objectives of the MoU and in a manner that is conducive to continued good relations.

--- [Page 15] ---
**Article VIII Dispute Settlement**

1. Any dispute arising between UN-Habitat and the City concerning the interpretation or application of this MoU, except for a dispute subject to Section 30 of the Convention or to any other applicable agreement, that is not settled through negotiation or another agreed form of settlement shall be submitted, at the request of either Party for final decision, to a tribunal of three arbitrators, one to be named by the Secretary-General of the United Nations, one to be named by the City of Buenos Aires, and the third, who shall be the chairman, to be chosen by the first two.

2. If either Party fails to appoint an arbitrator within sixty (60) days of the appointment by the other party, or if these two should fail to agree on the third arbitrator within sixty (60) days of their appointment, the President of the International Court of Justice may make any necessary appointments at the request of either Party.

3. Except as otherwise provided for by the Parties, the Tribunal shall adopt its own rules of procedure, provide for the reimbursement of its members and the distribution of expenses between the Parties, and take all decisions by a two-thirds majority. Its decision on all questions of procedure and substance shall be final and, even if rendered in default of one of the parties, be binding on both Parties.

**Article IX Privileges and Immunities**

1. Nothing in or relating to this MoU shall be deemed a waiver, express or implied, of any of the privileges and immunities of the United Nations, including UN-Habitat.

**Article X Use of the Name, Emblem or Media**

1. Neither Party will use the name or emblem of the other Party, or an abbreviation thereof, in connection with its business or otherwise, without the express prior written permission by a duly authorized representative of the Party in each case.

2. Neither Party has the authority, express or implied, to make any public statement on behalf of the other Party and all press releases issued in relation to this MoU shall be approved in writing in advance by the Parties before being issued.

**Article XI Notices**

1. Any notice required to be given by either Party under this Agreement shall be given in writing and shall be deemed given when actually received by the other Party, to the following addresses:

Conflict of Interest

1. The Parties hereto warrant that at the time of signing this MoU no conflict of interest exists or is likely to arise in the implementation of its obligations under this MoU.

(a) Immediately notify each other;

(b) Make full disclosure of all relevant information relating to the conflict; and

(c) Take such steps as reasonably required to resolve or otherwise deal with the conflict.

ARTICLE XVII

Legal Status of the Parties

3. The collaboration between the Parties under this MoU shall be on a non-exclusive basis.

ARTICLE XVIII

Entry into Force

1. This MoU shall enter into force upon signature by the authorized representatives of the Parties, being effective from the date of the latest signature and shall remain valid for a period of two years from the effective date of this MoU, unless earlier terminated by either Party in accordance with Article VI ("Termination") above.
