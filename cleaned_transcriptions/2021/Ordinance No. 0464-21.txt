Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office of the City Administrator, both this City, the herein Ordinance No. 0464-21, Series of 2021, informing you that the enactment of the same is well within the powers of the Sangguniang Panlungsang citing Section 16 of the Republic Act 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, to wit:
Section 16. General Welfare. - Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective jurisdictions, they shall promote, protect, encourage, and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological research and development, promote economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants.
It is further informed that the timely enactment of the subject ordinance is in harmony with the declaration of principles and state policies of the state under Article I Sections 13 and 17 of the 1987 constitution to quote as follows:
SECTION 13. The State recognizes the vital role of the youth in nation-building and shall promote and protect their physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual, and social well-being. It shall incline in the youth patriotism and nationalism, and encourage their involvement in public and civic affairs.
SECTION 17. The State shall give priority to education, science and technology, arts, culture, and sports to foster patriotism and nationalism, accelerate social progress, and promote total human liberation and development.
VIEWED FROM THE FOREGOING, it is recommended that the Ordinance be approved.

ORDINANCE NO. 0464-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE USE OF VIDEOKE AND KARAOKE MACHINES DURING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE BLENDED LEARNING MODALITY

--- [Page 7] ---
Page 2 of 4
Ord. No. 0464-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE USE OF VIDEOKOE AND KARAOKE MACHINES DURING THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE BLENDED LEARNING MODALITY".

SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the following terms are defined:
a. Karaoke Machine - a device that plays instrumental accompaniments for selection of songs to which the user sings along;
b. Videoke Machine - a device where the user sings along to pre-recorded instrumental versions of songs, the lyrics of which are displayed for the user's reference;
c. Blended Learning Modality - refers to a mix of online distance learning, modular distance learning, and TV/Radio-based instruction;
d. KTV establishment - a commercial establishment that offers enclosed and sound-proofed rooms for videoke machines for a fee.

SECTION 3. PROHIBITED HOURS FOR THE OPERATION OF KARAOKE AND VIDEOKOE MACHINES FOR PRIVATE USE. - The operation of Karaoke and Videoke machines for private use shall not be allowed between 7 o'clock in the morning until 5 o'clock in the afternoon from Mondays to Saturdays except Sundays, declared special holidays and legal holidays during the entire period of the implementation of the blended learning modality during the COVID-19 pandemic.

SECTION 4. LIMITATIONS AND EXEMPTIONS. - Section 3 of this Ordinance shall only apply to those Karaoke and Videoke machines for private use, be it indoor or outdoor. Provided, further, that Section 3 shall not apply for KTV establishments.

SECTION 5. PENALTY. - The person found violating the provisions of this Ordinance after due notice shall be penalized based on the following:
a. First Offense - a fine of One Thousand Pesos (Pip 1,000.00) and/or community service;
b. Second Offense - a fine of Three Thousand Pesos (Pip 3,000.00) and/or community service;
c. Third Offense - a fine of Five Thousand Pesos (Pip 5,000.00) and/or community service or by imprisonment of not less than one (1) day but not more than three (3) days or both, at the discretion of the court.

SECTION 6. ENFORCEMENT. - The Ordinance shall be in force and effect during the implementation by the Department of Education of the blended learning modality. Once the blended learning modality has been lifted by the Department of Education, the Ordinance shall no longer be enforced.

The provisions of this Ordinance shall be enforced by the Barangay Officials of each barangay. Any complaint involving the provisions of this Ordinance shall be brought before the Punong Barangay of the respective barangay and/or police personnel.

SECTION 7. ORDINANCE VIOLATION RECEIPT OR CITATION TICKET. - An Ordinance Violation Receipt, otherwise known as "Citation Ticket" shall be issued to the violators of this Ordinance. The Citation Ticket shall state the name and address of the violator, the specific violation committed and the provisions of the succeeding Section on the "No Contest Provision."

If the violation of this Ordinance is deemed to have been committed by a corporation, partnership or juridical entity, duly recognized in accordance with law, the chief executive officer, president, general manager, managing partner, or such other officer-in-charge, if known, shall be cited for the commission of the offense.

**SECTION 8. NO CONTEST PROVISION:** Any person cited for violation of this Ordinance, who does not wish to contest the violation and is willing to pay voluntarily the fine imposed upon him or her, or to voluntarily render community service under this Ordinance prior to the filing of a formal complaint before the Office of the City Prosecutor, shall be allowed to pay said fine with the City Treasurer's Office, or to render community service to avoid being criminally prosecuted. The availing of the "No Contest Provision" shall exempt an individual from criminal liability under this Ordinance.

For this purpose, the City Legal Office and the City Treasurer's Office shall provide the procedure for the availing of the "No Contest Provision," and establish a case inventory and recording system for all violations of this Ordinance.

Provided that, if a violator has already availed of the "No Contest Provision," he or she can no longer avail of the same and the corresponding complaint for any violation of this Ordinance shall be immediately filed.

**SECTION 9. SEPARABILITAS CLAUSE:** If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other sections or provisions shall be affected thereby.

**SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY:** This Ordinance shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its approval and publication in a newspaper of local circulation consistent with the provision of the Local Government Code of 1991.

ENACTED, January 12, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).
