AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR THE YEAR 2021 IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SIXTY-SIX MILLION FIVE HUNDRED-NINE-THREE THOUSAND SEVEN HUNDRED-THIRTY-SEVEN PESOS ($66,593,437.00). FOR PROGRAMS, PROJECTS AND ACTIVITIES RELATIVE TO COVID-19 RELATED RESPONSE, SUBJECT TO EXISTING GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS

Ord. No. 0693-21

Be it ordered by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A PORTION OF THE THIRTY PERCENT (30%) QUICK RESPONSE FUND (QRF) OUT OF THE FIVE PERCENT (5%) DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND (CALAMITY FUND) OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2021 IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SIXTY-SIX MILLION FIVE HUNDRED NINETY-THREE THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED THIRTY-SEVEN PESOS ($66,593,437.00) FOR PROGRAMS, PROJECTS AND ACTIVITIES RELATIVE TO COVID-19 RELATED RESPONSE, SUBJECT TO EASY CITY GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY: In keeping with its mandate and in response to the needs of the people, the City Government of Davao declares as its policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.

SECTION 3. LEGAL BASIS: Section 21 of Republic Act No. 10121 provides: "Of the amount appropriated for LDRMRF, thirty percent (30%) shall be allocated as Quick Response Fund (QRF) or stand-by fund for relief and recovery programs in order that situation and living conditions of people in communities or areas stricken by disasters, calamities, epidemics, and other complex emergencies, may be normalized as quickly as possible."

SECTION 4. LIST OF PROGRAMS, PROJECTS AND ACTIVITIES:

| Programs/Projects/Activities | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase of 1,000 units Oxygen Tanks with Regulator @12,000.00 each | P |
| Rental Expenses due Orchard Hotel (Payment of Actual Cost from May 30 to July 31, 2021 at P1.JM (used to house swabbies) | 3,720.00 |
| Lab Testing and Processing of RT-PCR Specimen Actual Cost for the month of July 2021 | 12,093.00 |
| Operational Costs of 7 Additional Vaccination Sites at P 1.M each until December 2021 | 7,000,000.00 |
| Social Welfare Goods Expenses - requested by CSWDD for relief stocking for disasters and emergency situations | 23,411,337.00 |
| City Agriculturist's Office-Provision of vegetable packs to families under lockdown | 1,000,000.00 |
| City Veterinarian's Office-Provision of Poultry meat to households under lockdown | 3,000,000.00 |
| Disaster Evacuation Kit for CDRMO | 4,370,000.00 |
| TOTAL PROPOSED APPROPRIATIONS | P |
|  | 66,593,437.00 |

**SECTION 5. USE OF FUNDS - The amount herein appropriated shall be used specifically for such items and expenditure approved by the Sangguniang Panlungsod. All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM), the Commission on Audit (COA), the Government Procurement Reform Act (RA 9184), as well as other applicable laws, ordinances and Presidential directives.**

**SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - The provisions of this Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.**

**ENACTED, on the 24th day of August 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, there being a quorum.**
