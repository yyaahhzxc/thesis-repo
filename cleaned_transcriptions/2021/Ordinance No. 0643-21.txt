AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY THE DAVAO REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION (RDC), PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (OPWH) – DAVAO CITY 2nd DISTRICT ENGINEERING OFFICE, AND THE BARANGAY COUNCILS OF GATUNGAN, BUNAWAN DISTRICT, FATIMA, MABUHAY, MALABOG, PANDAITAN, PARALAM, PAQUIBATO PROPER, PARADISE EMBAK, SALAPAWAN, SUMIMAO, AND TAPAK, ALL IN PAQUIBATO DISTRICT, RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION AND IMPLEMENTATION OF THE BARANGAY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (LGSP-BDP) OF THE NATIONAL TASK FORCE TO END LOCAL COMMUNIST ARMED CONFLICT (NTF-ELAC) PURSUANT TO EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 70, SUBJECT TO GOVERNMENT BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING, AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF AUDIT, THE PROCUREMENT LAW AS WELL AS OTHER APPLICABLE LAWS

Res. No. 02411-21

WHEREAS, this item pertains to the Memorandum of Agreement by and among the City of Davao, the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) – Davao City District Engineering Office, and the Barangay Councils concerned relative to the construction and implementation of infrastructure projects under the Local Government Support Fund to the Barangay Development Program (LGSP-BDP) of the National Task Force to End Local Communist Armed Conflict (NTF-ELCAC) pursuant to Executive Order No. 70;

WHEREAS, as perferred from the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA), the Retooled Community Support Program (RCSP) is a convergence mechanism for local governments, particularly at the barangay level, for the identification of issues and needed government intervention. One of the outputs of the RCS process is the enhancement of Barangay Development Plans, which will include priority projects identified by the barangay to be funded by the Local Government Support Fund-Support to the Barangay Development Program of the National Task Force to End Local Communist Armed Conflict (NTF-ELCAC);

WHEREAS, the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) – Davao City District Engineering Office is entrusted to implement these projects and the City of Davao shall be responsible for the transfer, to the former of the funds appropriated for the said project for its completion, subject to liquidation and accounting rules;

WHEREAS, Engr. Gina R. Santos of the City Planning and Development Office manifested that the resources needed to accomplish the City Government of Davao's responsibility stipulated in the MOA shall be shouldered by the City Engineer's Office;

WHEREAS, the City Legal Office in its Legal Opinion No. 128, dated May 26, 2021, found no legal infirmity to the revised Memorandum of Agreement and recommended that the same MOA involving the eighty-two (82) barangays be forwarded to the Sangguniang Panlalagod for the requisite legislative authority for the Mayor to sign the same;

WHEREAS, the eleven (11) Memorandums of Agreement (MOA) were drafted to cover the eighty-two (82) barangays;

NOW THEREFORE, on motion of Councillor J. Melchor B. Quitan Jr., duly seconded by Councillors Jesus M. Bonguyen, Maria Elena S. Acosta, Mary A. Villufuerte, Bali B. Baluran, Cassandra Dominique N. Advincula, Ramon Abellera, Nilo D. Abellera, Wilberto E. Algo, Pilar C. Braga, Dante L. Apostol Sr., and Conrad Co. Baluran; co-sponsored by Councillors Maria Belen S. Acosta, Bali Hundra Cassandra Dominique N. Advincula, Conrad Co. Baluran, Pilar C. Braga, Nilo D. Abellera, Jessica M. Bonguyen, Dante L. Apostol Sr. and Augusto Javier G. Campos III, be it resolved, as it is hereby resolved, to ENACT an ORDINANCE authorizing the CITY MAYOR to SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE ELEVEN (11) MEMORANDUMS OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE DRAFTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS (DPWH) – DAVAOCITY DISTRICT ENGINEERING OFFICE, AND THE BARANGAY COUNCILS CONCERNED RELATIVE TO THE CONSTRUCTION AND IMPLEMENTATION OF INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS UNDER THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT SUPPORT FUND TO THE BARANGAY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (LGSP-BDP) OF THE NATIONAL TASK FORCE TO END LOCAL COMMUNIST ARMED CONFLICT (NTF-ELCAC) (HEREINAFTER CALLED “THE PROJECT”) AND TO PROVIDE FOR THE BUDGETING, ACCOUNTING, AND AUDITING RULES AND REGULATIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF BUDGET AND MANAGEMENT, COMMISSION ON AUDIT, THE PROCUREMENT LAW AS WELL AS OTHER APPLICABLE LAWS
