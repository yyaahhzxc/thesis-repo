Ord. No. 0525-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sanggunian Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) BETWEEN THE CITY OF DAVAO AND THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT (DSDW) RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE SUPPLEMENTARY FEEDING PROGRAM FOR THE CHILDREN IN CHILD DEVELOPMENT CENTERS (CDCS) AND SUPERVISED NEIGHBORHOOD PLAY (SNP)".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 25(b) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the "Local Government Code of 1991" states:

"Section 25. (b) National Agencies and offices with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local government units concerned in the discharge of these functions. They shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation of said national projects."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority to enter into and sign, the Memorandum of Understanding between the City of Davao and the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSDW) relative to the implementation of the Supplementary Feeding Program for the Children in Child Development Centers (CDCs) and Supervised Neighborhood Play (SNP).

SECTION 4. SEPARABILIT Y CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 23rd day of March 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

KNOW ALL PERSONS BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Understanding made and entered into this day of 2021 at between and among:

The DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WELFARE AND DEVELOPMENT (DSWD), government agency created pursuant to Republic Act No. 5416, through its REGIONAL OFFICE XI, with the office address at Corner Suazo Street, Ramon Magaysay Avenue, Davao City and represented by its REGIONAL DIRECTOR, RAQUEL E. NUNEZ (hereinafter referred to as the "DSWD");

and

The LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT OF DAVAO CITY with address at San Pedro Street, Poblacion District, Davao City herein represented by its LOCAL CHIEF EXECUTIVE, SARA Z. DUTERTE, duly authorized to represent the LGU, (hereinafter referred to as the "LGU");

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the Supplementary Feeding Program (SFP) is the provision of food in addition to the regular meals to target children ages 2-4 years old enrolled in the Child Development Center (CDCs) and Supervised Neighborhood Play (SNPs) as part of the DSWD's contribution to the early childhood care and development (ECCD) program of the government;

WHEREAS, the Department of Education (DepEd) and the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), in consultation with other National Government Agencies (NGAs), Local Government Units (LGUs), Non-Government Organizations (NGOs), and development partners, mandated by Republic Act No. 11037, also known as "Masustansyang Pagkain Para sa Batang Pilipino," to promulgate their respective rules and regulations for the efficient and effective implementation of the National Feeding Program for Public Day Care Centers (PDCCs) or Child Development Centers (CDCs), public kindergarten and elementary schools, pursuant to Section 4 of the said law to address undernutrition among Filipino children;

--- [Page 5] ---
WHEREAS, the efficient and effective implementation of a national feeding program shall sustain the gains of good nutrition achieved through the delivery of early childhood care and development services consistent with RA 10410, otherwise known as the "Early Years Act of 2013" and quality basic education services under RA 10533, otherwise known as the "Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013,"

WHEREAS, the General Appropriation Act, FY 2020 Vol. 116, No. 1 stated that the target outcome indicator of SFP is 80% of malnourished children in Community Development Centers (CDCs) and Supervised Neighborhood Play with improved nutritional status.

WHEREAS, on 12 March 2020, the Inter-Agency Task Force on Emerging Infectious Diseases (IATF-EID) per Resolution No. 11, series of 2020, raised the COVID-19 threat to the highest alert level, Code Red Sublevel 2, due to the evidence of sustained community transmission of the disease. Concomitantly, President Rodrigo Roa Duterte issued Proclamation No. 922, Series of 2020, declaring a State of Public Health Emergency all throughout the Philippines and thereafter, placed the entire Island of Luzon, under enhanced community quarantine. The LGUs outside of Luzon have imposed localized community quarantine in their areas of jurisdiction consistent with the parameters of the pertinent IATF Resolution until 30 April 2020 when the President issued Executive Order No. 112, Series 2020 specifically identifying the LGUs under the enhanced and community quarantine;

WHEREAS, noting the limitation in the purchasing power of most families, SFP implementation during the implementation of community quarantine shall assist in addressing the issue of hunger and food security among Filipino children and prevent regression of the current nutritional status of the beneficiaries.

WHEREAS, DSWD Memorandum Circular No. 35, series of 2020, a supplemental guideline to Memorandum Circular No. 12, series of 2020, sanctioned the amended scheme of implementation of SFP during the community quarantine period as stringent social distancing measures prohibit the conduct of assemblies or mass gatherings which include feeding of children in the CDCs and SNPs;

WHEREAS, local government units (LGUs), as partners of the DSWD in the delivering programs and projects shall also assist in the implementation of SFP,

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, hereinafter set forth, the parties hereto agree to undertake the program under the following terms and conditions:

--- [Page 6] ---
I. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

1. DSWD shall:

a. Manage and coordinate/supervise the regional implementation of the SFP. Designate a Focal Person for the SFP to provide guidance and technical assistance to the Social Welfare & Development Officers, CDC/SNP workers, Day Workers Federation (DCWF) and Day Care Service Parent Group (DCSPG) in the implementation of the Program;

b. Coordinate and inform the LGU to conduct twice-a-day feeding implementation composed of hot/meal in combination of dry rations to boost the nutritional concerns of the children during the pandemic;

c. Act as Procuring Entity, identify the applicable procurement scheme and guarantee compliance with Republic Act No. 9184 or the Community Participation Procurement Manual (CPPM) for Program Against Hunger and Poverty (PAHP) of DSWD, DAR, and DA, whichever may be applicable;

d. In all cases, ensure the timely delivery of the goods to CDCs/SNPs with the assistance of the CMS/VDCC;

e. Consolidate reports and submit quarterly physical accomplishment and financial report, distribution of funds and quarterly accomplishment on nutritional status of children to Central Office-Program Management Bureau (PMB);

f. Coordinate to the P/C/M Local Nutrition Councils on the roles and responsibilities in the program related to its objective and mechanics;

g. Conduct a Regional evaluation at the end of the implementation period of each cycle.

2. LGU shall:

a. Submit a project proposal and masterlist of beneficiaries as the basis for funding allocation for the 11th cycle (CY 2021) and the conduct of twice-a-day feeding provision for the beneficiaries. The LGU shall include in the project proposal the mode of implementation they will use based on the SFP implementing guidelines;

b. Designate the P/CMSWDO as Focal Person for the feeding with the task of providing guidance, technical assistance and support to the DCWF/DCSPG in coordination with the Province/City/Municipal Nutrition Action Officer (P/C/MNAO) and include in LGU/MNC meetings SF concerns;

c. Tap the City/Municipal Nutrition Committees to assist in monitoring and providing technical assistance to the implementers of the Program monthly. The City/Municipal Health Office may also assist in providing Vitamin A Supplementation and deworming to the target beneficiaries (children 2-5 years old).

d. As the Focal Person, the P/CMSWDO shall be responsible in providing administrative and technical assistance in the operationalization of the SFP and overseeing the organization of Parents' Committees in each CDCs/SNPs or in the assigned barangay.

e. Ensure that the goods intended for the Program will be delivered timely.

f. Provide technical assistance to food production and backyard gardening in CDCs and in family homes.

g. Monitor the timely and correct weighing and height measurement of the children beneficiaries and record their nutritional status monthly for six (6) months; a consolidated report from all the CDCs/SNPs shall be submitted quarterly to the DSWD Field Office.

h. Conduct monthly profiling and updating of database of children beneficiaries including list of drop out for submission to DSWD on the 1st Friday of the succeeding month;

i. Submit a monthly documentation report (narrative/photo) of the implementation as may be required in audit(s);

j. Assist the DSWD and provide the counterpart cost in the delivery of goods from the municipality to the barangays;

k. If necessary, provide augmentation support, in cash or in kind, in the implementation of the Program at the city/municipal and barangay level if the clients catered go beyond the target number of beneficiaries and also in other activities related to the program;

l. P/CMSWDO to conduct Parent Effectiveness Sessions (PES) on nutrition education and motivation in nurturing their children;

m. Assist the DSWD in the monitoring of the Program implementation.

ii. ACCOUNTABILITY

The agreement shall be implemented as agreed upon in accordance with the terms and conditions stipulated herein. Failure on the part of any party to comply with the provisions of this Agreement will warrant its discontinuance and give rise to filing of appropriate administrative and/or criminal actions against responsible officers and employees of the Ering party.

iii. AMENDMENTS

The Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) may be amended or revoked upon mutual agreement of both parties, provided that all amendments shall be filed with the DSWD.
