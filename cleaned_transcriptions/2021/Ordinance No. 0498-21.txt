AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) AMONG THE METROPOLITAN DAVAO DEVELOPMENT COORDINATING COMMITTEE (MDDCC) MEMBER LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS, RELATIVE TO THE LGU'S FINANCIAL SUPPORT FOR THE MDDCC OPERATIONS

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0498-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE: This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA)
AMONG THE METROPOLITAN DAVAO DEVELOPMENT COORDINATING COMMITTEE (MDCCC) MEMBER-LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNITS, RELATIVE TO THE LGU'S FINANCIAL SUPPORT FOR THE MDCCC OPERATIONS".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455 (b) (1) (vi) of Republic
Act No. 7160 or the "Local Government Code of 1991" provides, to wit:

"Section 455 Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation.
xxx
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose
of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants
pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(1) Exercise general supervision and control over all programs,
projects, services, and activities of the city government, and in this
connection, shall:
xxx
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its
behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other
documents upon authority of the sangguniang panlungsod or
pursuant to law or ordinance."

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to enter into and sign the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) among
the Metropolitan Davao Development Coordinating Committee (MDCCC) Member-Local
Government Units, relative to the LGU's financial support for the MDCCC Operations.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 23rd day of February 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Know All Men by These Presents:

This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) made and entered into by and among:
The City Government of Davao, with principal office at the Davao City Hall, Davao City, represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the Honorable Z. Duterte, hereinafter referred to as Davao City;
The City Government of Tagum, with principal office at the New Tagum City Hall, Tagum City, Davao del Norte represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the Honorable Allan L. Reillon, hereinafter referred to as Tagum City;
The City Government of Panabo, with principal office at the Panabo City Hall, Panabo City, Davao del Norte, represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the Honorable Jose E. Relampagos, hereinafter referred to as Panabo City;
The City Government of the Island Garden City of Samal (IGACOS), with principal office at the IGACOS, Davao del Norte, represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the Honorable Al David T. Uy, hereinafter referred to as Island Garden City of Samal;
The City Government of Digao, with principal office at the Digao City Hall, Davao del Sur, represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the Honorable Josef F. Cagas, hereinafter referred to as Digao City;
The Municipal Government of Carmen, with principal office at the Carmen Municipal Hall, Davao del Norte, represented in this MOA by its Municipal Mayor, the Honorable Virginia J. Perandos, hereinafter referred to as the Carmen Municipal Government;
The Municipal Government of Sta. Cruz, with principal office at the Sta. Cruz Municipal Hall, Davao del Sur, represented in this MOA by its Municipal Mayor, the Honorable Jaime G. Lao, hereinafter referred to as the Sta. Cruz Municipal Government;
All of which entities together, shall be hereinafter referred to as the PARTIES;

WITNESSETH THAT:
WHEREAS, Davao City, along with its adjacent local government units (LGUs), shall combine, collaborate and cooperate in order to form a metropolitan conglomeration, extending to the cities of Tagum, Panabo and the Island Garden City of Samal, and the municipality of Carmen, all in the province of Davao del Norte; and the city of Digao and the municipality of Sta. Cruz, both in the province of Davao del Sur, to be known as Metropolitan Davao, for a joint venture to establish an integrated and coordinated system of urban infrastructure facilities and services to support the expanding residential, commercial, industrial and institutional activities therein.

Memorandum of Agreement 1

--- [Page 5] ---
WHEREAS, the Metropolitan Davao Development Coordinating Committee (MDDCC) was created through RDC XI Resolution No. 72, Series of 2017, as a transitory structure for inter-LGU cooperation, and shall serve as a mechanism for coordinated planning, investment programming, project facilitation and monitoring of the Metropolitan Davao development initiatives;
WHEREAS, consistent with the Local Government Code of 1991, the PARTIES have entered into this Memorandum of Agreement (the "AGREEMENT") and
HAVE AGREED TO PURSUE
Section 1. Metropolitan Davao. In accordance with existing laws, rules and regulations, the parties shall work together to realize the broadening initiatives and in building trust for joint efforts and cooperative ventures focused on the needs of Metropolitan Davao that include among others: 1) land use planning and support to economic development; 2) tourism promotion and management; 3) Davao Gulf coastal zone management and protection; 4) urban planning and development of facilities and services in the areas of public transport, housing, solid waste management and sewage systems, digital connectivity, water and energy and disaster risk reduction, public safety, and integrated flood control. These LGUs, known collectively as Metropolitan Davao, shall pursue the following initiatives:
1.1 Identify and integrate development plans/programs within the Cities of Tagum, Panabo and Samal, and the Municipality of Carmen in the Province of Davao del Norte; and the City of Digos and the Municipality of Sta. Cruz in the Province of Davao del Sur, and the City of Davao, in accordance with their respective municipal plans/provincial development plans/physical development plans and use plans/provincial Development and Physical Framework Plans;
1.2 Coordinate activities of all ongoing pre-investment, feasibility and detailed engineering studies and development programs along the aforecited areas of cooperation within the Metropolitan Davao.
Section 2. Budgetary Requirements.
The total amount of financial contributions by LGU-members is Two Million Six Hundred Seventy-Two Thousand Pesos (P2,672,000.00). The breakdown per LGU shall be as follows:

Memorandum of Agreement

--- [Page 6] ---
The financial contributions of the local governments of the Cities of Davao, Tagum, Panabo, Samal, Digos and the Municipalities of Carmen, Sta. Cruz shall comprise the funding requirements for CY 2021 and 2022 for the operations of Metropolitan Davao Development Coordinating Committee (MDDC) and other initiatives to develop Metropolitan Davao. The LGU members shall remit their annual contributions not later than the 30th day of June, for calendar year 2021, and on the 30th day of January in 2022.

In the interim, the National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) XI as Secretariat, shall act as trustee of the funds until the creation of the Metropolitan Davao Development Authority (MDDA) by the Philippine Congress. Disbursement of funds shall be based on an approved work and financial plan, and shall be subject to the existing government budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations.

It is understood that the approval for the funding support and requirements for the Metropolitan Davao development initiatives, including the appropriations necessary to implement approved projects and activities, shall be sought from the PARTIES' respective SANGGUNIANS in accordance with the provisions of the Local Government Code.

Section 3. New Component LGUs: If warranted and provided that they meet the minimum requirements for integration into the Metropolitan Davao Area, other cities and municipalities of Davao Region, as approved by the Regional Development Council, shall be annexed to the Metropolitan Davao. The new component LGUs shall forthwith pay their respective annual contributions as herein provided; the total amount for the first year to be pre-allocated based on the number of months remaining on the year joined.

Section 4. Interpretation: Differences arising from the interpretation(s) or implementation of this AGREEMENT shall be settled amicably through consultation or negotiation among the PARTIES.

Section 5. Amendment: This AGREEMENT may be altered, modified or amended in writing, by mutual consent of the PARTIES.

Section 6. Effectivity: This AGREEMENT shall take effect on the date of its signing and shall be in force until terminated in writing by mutual consent of the PARTIES, or until the creation of the MDDA or its equivalent.

In witness thereof, the PARTIES have caused these presents to be signed by their duly authorized representatives this 23rd day of June, Two Thousand and Twenty-One, in Davao City, Philippines.
