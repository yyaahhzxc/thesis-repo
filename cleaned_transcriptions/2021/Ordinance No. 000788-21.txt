ORDINANCE NO. 0788-21
Series of 2021

AN ORDINANCE GIVING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT IN THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROJECT, NEW MATINA UPGRAADING AREA, PARTICULARLY IDENTIFIED AS BLOCK 67, LOT 5, NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES, PHASE I, DAVAO CITY, CONSISTING OF EIGHTY FOUR (84) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF CASIANA L. PAUSAL.

--- [Page 2] ---
Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0788-21

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE CREATING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND ON BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF SALE FOR THE LOT IN THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT (SIR) PROJECT, NEW MATINA UPGRAADING AREA, PARTICULARLY IDENTIFIED AS BLOCK 67, LOT 5, NEW MATINA SITES AND SERVICES, PHASE I, DAVAO CITY, CONSISTING OF EIGHTY FOUR (84) SQUARE METERS, MORE OR LESS, IN FAVOR OF CASIANA L. PAUSA".

SECTION 2. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and on behalf of the City of Davao, the Deed of Sale for the lot in the Slum Improvement and Resettlement (SIR) Project, New Matina Sites and Services, particularly located at Block 67 Lot 5, Phase I, New Matina Sites and Services, Davao City in favor of Casiana L. Pausa.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITIY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, November 23, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

CERTIFIED CORRECT:
