AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE TERMS OF PARTNERSHIP (TOP) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH - DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT AND THE CITY OF DAVAO FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE UNIVERSAL HEALTH CARE ACT AND THE FOURMULA ONE PLUS FOR HEALTH

--- [Page 2] ---
Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN THE NAME OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE TERMS OF PARTNERSHIP (TOP), TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH - DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT AND THE CITY OF DAVAO FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE UNIVERSAL HEALTH CARE ACT AND THE FORMULA ONE PLUS FOR HEALTH".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 455, paragraph (b) sub-paragraph (vi) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides to wit:

"SECTION 455. Chief Executive: Powers, Duties and Compensation

xxx xxx xxx

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:

xxx xxx xxx

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the Sangguniang Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Terms of Partnership (TOP) to be entered into by and between the Department of Health - Davao Center for Health Care Act and the Formula One Plus for Health;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, March 9, 2021, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang present, there being a quorum.

Cherito N. Santos Secretary to the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Government Department Head II).

KNOW ALL MEN BY THIS PRESENTS:

This Terms of Partnership (TOP) is made and entered into by and between:

The Department of Health – Davao Center for Health Development, a regional government agency created and existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with principal office address at JF Laurel, Bajada, Davao City herein represented by the Director IV, ASSAABELLE P. YUMANG, MD, MCH, CEC/3 Jv., and hereinafter referred to as the "DOH/CHD"; and

The City of Davao, a Local Government Unit established and existing under the laws and regulations of the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address at JF Laurel, Bajada, Davao City herein represented by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, in her capacity as the City Mayor, as duly authorized by the Shugnangan Pandudilang Panlungsod under Resolution No. ______ dated ______, hereinafter referred to as the "CITY";

WHEREAS, Section 11, Article XII of the 1987 Philippine Constitution provides, among others, that the State shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive approach to health development, which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health and social services available to all people at affordable cost;

WHEREAS, Republic Act 7160 otherwise known as the 1991 Local Government Code mandated the devolution of the delivery of health services and facilities as one of the basic functions and responsibilities of all Local Government Units (LGUs) at all levels;

WHEREAS, Section 22 of Republic Act 11223 otherwise known as the Universal Health Care (UHC) Act 2019 mandated the National Government to make available commensurate financial and non-financial matching grants, including capital outlay, human resources for health and health commodities, to improve the functionality of province-wide and city-wide health systems, provided that the grant shall be in accordance with the approved province-wide and city-wide health investment plan;

WHEREAS, the Formula One Plus for Health with its tagline "Boosting Universal Health Care" is the strategic framework for Health Sector Reform to attain the goals outlined by the Philippine Government in the Philippine Development Plan, the Sustainable Development Goals, and The Ambusyon Natin 2040, specifically for the health sector, goals of better health outcomes, more equitable health care financing, and more responsive health system;

WHEREAS, the City of Davao has agreed to implement health sector reforms consistent with the Universal Health Care Act and the Formula One Plus for Health and has proven its capacity to utilize investment and sustain the reform process, among others;

WHEREAS, the Local Investment Plan for Health (LIPH) is the key instrument in forging the Department of Health (DOH) and Local Government Unit (LGU) partnership to carry out the Formula One Plus for Health, and technical assistance from the LGU shall be consolidated and matched with the DOH's own resources through the Local Investment Plans for health, and whereas the implementation of the LIPH is translated through the Annual Operation Plan;

WHEREAS, the City, in partnership with their component LGUs, have agreed to fund and implement the programs, projects, and activities under their respective LIPHS/AOPs through their own financing source, and through available funds and resources from the DOH, and development partners, coordinated with the DOH;

WHEREAS, the City of Davao has prepared its 2020-2022 LIPH, and 2021 Annual Operational Plan (AOP), which have been reviewed by the City and DOH-CHD Appraisal Committee, and modified as necessary following the recommendations of the above;

**ARTICLE I**

**1.1 Program Coverage**

This agreement shall cover the management and implementation of the approved 2021 AOP (Amendment), and shall focus on the achievement of improved local health system performance consistent with the UHC Act and Formula One Plus for Health.

**1.2 Roles and Responsibilities**

**A. The City Shall:**

1. Issue executive instruments or local regulations, whenever necessary, for the effective implementation of the UHC Act and 2020-2022 LPHI as embodied in the approved 2021 AOP, through the approved 2021 AOP;
2. Secure the participation and cooperation of its component LGUs in carrying out local initiatives or activities pursuant to the city's implementation plan under the UHC Act and 2020-2022 LPHI; and the approved 2021 AOP. This includes but is not limited to:
   - 1.1 Increase secured, better, and sustained financing for health from DOH, PhilHealth, and LGU appropriations;
   - 2.2 Assure the quality and affordability of health goods and services through DOH and PhilHealth accreditation;
   - 2.3 Ensure access to and availability of essential and basic health packages by matching its catchment population to appropriate Primary Care Provider Network and through effective referral systems;
   - 2.4 Enhance the performance of the health system, measured through accurate, sensitive, and timely epidemiologic surveillance systems and other monitoring and evaluation systems;
   - 2.5 Conduct proactive, effective, and evidence-based health promotion programs or initiatives;
   - 2.6 Provide timely, effective, and efficient preparedness and response to public health emergencies and disasters;
   - 2.7 Improve human resources for health management and development;
   - 2.8 Implement the health management framework/EMR;
   - 2.9 Mitigate public health threats and pandemic such as COVID19.
3. Allocate, release, and use, together with its component LGUs, funds that are necessary for the city-wide implementation of the approved 2021 AOP;
4. Be guided by the approved 2021 AOP in the disbursement of funds;
5. ARTICLE III Miscellaneous
3.1 Mutual Obligations
The DOH-CHD and the City agree to perform, fulfill, abide by, and submit to any and all of the provisions and requirements and all matters related, contained or expressed or reasonably inferred from this Agreement. All unobligated amounts from DOH support funds shall be applied according to modalities determined by the DOH-CHD and City subject to existing budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules. The City shall abide by the decisions of the DOH-CHD in this regard.
3.2 Duration and Effectivity
The DOH-CHD and the City agree that the provisions herein described shall cover activities from January to December 2021. This Agreement shall be terminated upon satisfactory fulfillment of all the terms and conditions embodied herein but not later than December 2021. Any modification or amendments to this Agreement as proposed by either party shall mutually be agreed upon in writing by all the parties hereto.
3.3 Notices
All notices called for by the terms of this Agreement shall become effective only at the time of receipt thereof and only when received by the parties to whom they are addressed.
