Ord. No. 0589-21

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE PARTIALLY SUPPRESSING 112.65 SQUARE METERS PORTION OF THE LOCAL ROAD RIGHT OF WAY LEADING TO THE ALTERNATE ACCESS ROAD OF MAGALLANES RESIDENCES FOR PERMANENT CLOSURE TO FORM PART OF THE PROPERTY TO BE UTILIZED AS SITE OF THE CITY HEALTH BUILDING".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Pursuant to Section 21 of Republic Act No. 7160, on the authority of the local government unit for the closure and opening of road, it states:

"Section 21. Closure and Opening of Roads - (a) local government unit may, pursuant to an ordinance, permanently or temporarily close or open any local road, alley, park or square falling within its jurisdiction; Provided, however, that in case of permanent closure, such ordinance must be approved by at least two-thirds (2/3) of all the members of the sanggunian, and when necessary, an adequate substitute for the public facility that is subject to closure is provided."

SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE - All ordinances, executive orders, rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 4. SEPARATIBILITy Clause - If any provision of this Act is held unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions not affected thereby shall continue in operation and remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.

ENACTED, on the 11th day of May 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
