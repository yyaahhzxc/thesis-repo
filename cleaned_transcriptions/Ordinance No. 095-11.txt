MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

Know All Men By These Presents:

This Memorandum of Agreement entered into by and between:

NCCC Cares, Incorporated with principal address at 3rd floor LTS Bldg., R. Magalaysay Avenue, Davao City, represented by the President, MR. LAFAYETTE A. LIM, hereinafter referred to as the "FIRST PARTY";

and

The City Government of Davao through the City Social Services and Development Office with principal address at , represented by its City Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE hereinafter referred to as the "SECOND PARTY";

WITNESSETH:

NCCC CARES is a non-stock, non-profit private organization geared toward implementing family and community development services as well as promoting the advocacy of uplifting and supporting the poor children in Davao City through education.

City Government of Davao acknowledges and continue supporting meaningful programs for the benefit of the children through mandating City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO), a department of the local government unit which enforce Social Welfare Programs in line with the City's standards to protect the rights of the poor & the disadvantaged to quality services.

NCCC CARES and the City Government of Davao agree to enter into a partnership whereby NCCC CARES will provide educational assistance to indigent children of the City referred by City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) through a fund grant applicable under the Educational Assistance Program of NCCC CARES and allowing City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) to facilitate activities as deemed necessary to both Parties in implementing the above mentioned partnership.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

NCCC CARES agrees to work with City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) in facilitating and implementing the aforementioned partnership with sets of responsibilities as defined herinafter.

NCCC CARES shall:

1. Conduct re-assessment through Program Coordinator of the applicants based on the requirements submitted by CSSDO to NCCC CARES office prior accepting the applicants.

2. Return to CSSDO the submitted requirements of the applicants after re-assessment were done.

3. Support the school obligations of the One Hundred (100) accepted applicants in relation to their studies in Elementary and Secondary Public Schools.

4. Provide payment for School obligations of the applicants up to Two hundred pesos (Ppp 200.00) for Elementary School obligations and Php 700.00 for High School. School obligations shall comprise only the PTCA/PTA/PGA, Miscegulation, Graduation fee. In case that schools do not impose/require collection of school obligations as supported by a letter duly signed by the principal/school head, amount shall be given to the beneficiaries. Furthermore, if school obligations paid for do not consume the amount of Php 200.00 for Elementary and Php 700 for High School obligation, the difference amount shall be given to the beneficiaries as well.

5. Provide student needs for school opening (e.g. school supplies, school bag, uniform, school shoes and the like) worth Pp 300 Gift Certificate both Elementary and High School (to be liquidated by receipts) immediately after approval of the Masterlist.

--- [Page 15] ---
6. Provide office supplies (in-kind) worth Five hundred (500) pesos for district office with 2-5 grantees or one thousand (1000) pesos for district office with 6-10 grantees being monitored as operational support good for one school year. This will be distributed during the announced General Assembly.

7. Ensure that any announcements made for the Educational Assistance Program (EAP) beneficiaries shall be channeled through CSSDO Main Office.

CSSDO shall:

1. Conduct interview, assess and screen applicants to be referred to NCCC Cares Educational Assistance Program. The referred applicants must be proven as indigent and has a passing grade in the Report Card as prescribed by the Department of Education.

2. Facilitate the completion of the requirements for new and old or renewing applicants. Requirements are the following:

2.1 New Applicant
- Filled up Application Form
- Photocopy of Form I37 or Report Card with at least 3rd quarter grades
- Photocopy of Birth Certificate/Affidavit of Late Registration
- Barangay Certificate
- One (1) recent 2x2 Picture

2.2 Old/Renewing Applicant
- Progress Report prepared by the Case Worker
- Report Card with at least 3rd quarter grades
- One (1) recent 2x2 Picture (taken within the last 6 months)
- Barangay Certificate (if his/her family transferred to another barangay)

3. Submit to NCCC Caree complete requirements with the Masterlist of Applicants on the 3rd week of April of the current year.

4. Through the assigned Case Worker and District Head, attend the General Assembly at NCCC Cares Office as scheduled.

5. Monitor and evaluate the grantee on his or her school performance, welfare, conduct and discipline while under the EAP. This includes keeping the files and documents of the grantee. Submit to NCCC Cares Office the Statement of Accounts for processing of request payments.

6. Pay out the payment to schools and return Official or Acknowledgment Receipts from the school.

8. Disseminate information and announcement to the grantees as released by NCCC Cares.

9. Terminate information and announcement to the grantees based on the following conditions:

- Grantee who did not enroll for the current year for reasons of lack of interest.
- Grantee who dropped out due to reasons of tardiness and disinterest in studying.
- Grantee who transfer to other schools outside NCCC Cares area of coverage.
- Grantee has graduated from high school.
- Two (2) failed grades in one subject.
- Parents or guardian are found to be financially capable.
- Grantee has transferred to a private school.
- Grantee has incomplete requirements.
- Grantee has received major scholarship or educational assistance.
- Grantee has not filed for renewal.

--- [Page 16] ---
10. Inform NCCC Cares about any changes in the assigned Case Worker who directly monitor the grantees.

11. Abide on the schedule and/or set deadlines by NCCC Cares.

The initial term of this Memorandum of Agreement shall be one (1) year from the date signed by all parties and shall remain in effect unless any party desires to renegotiate or cancel the Agreement.

This Agreement may be terminated at any time by any party by mutual consent or by giving thirty (30) days written notice to the other party.

Either Party shall not transfer or assign this MOA or of the any rights or obligations under this MOA without prior written consent of the other Party.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereto set their signatures this day of July 2010 in Davao City, Philippines.
