ORDINANCE NO. 0474-13
Series of 2013
AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING AND APPROVING THE
PRICING SCHEME FOR TIBUNGCO RELOCATION PHASE I AND PHASE II
Be it ordered by the Sanggunihan Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN
ORDINANCE PROVIDING AND APPROVING THE PRICING SCHEME FOR
TIBUNGCO RELOCATION PHASE I AND PHASE II".

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0474-13

SECTION 2. PROVISION AND APPROVAL - The following pricing scheme for Tibungco Relocation Phase I and Phase II is hereby approved:

Pricing Scheme of Tibungco Relocation Phase I and Phase II
Less Phase II Land Dev. and No Interest Charge for ten (10) years

Land area:
(200,200 sq m & 28,985 sq m)
Saleable Area:
(126,105 sq m & 61,379 sq m)

COST OF PROJECT (Phase I & II)
Raw Land: P16,016,880.00 & 4,214,700.00
Land Development: P21,323,181.91

ADD:
DLPC: 3,160,000.00
Survey: 551,176.22
Splitting (R.O.D): 21,323,181.91 ONLY

TOTAL: P45,765,938.13

COST PER SQUARE METER: P 317.82
LOTS GENERATED: 1,580
AVERAGE LOT SIZE: 93.55 SQ.M.
COST PER LOT @ 93.55 SQ.M.: P 29,732.18

MONTHLY AMORTIZATION WITH NO INTEREST CHARGE:
NO. OF YRS. TO PAY: 10
MONTHLY AMORTIZATION: P 247.77

under the following terms and conditions:

1. Each beneficiary shall sign a Lease Purchase Agreement (LPA) with the City Government of Davao, providing that the lot shall not be sold or encumbered within the payment period and within twenty (20) years from the issuance of the title in the name of the beneficiary.

2. The price is at Three Hundred Seventeen Pesos and 82/100 (P317.82) per square meter payable in ten (10) years without interest.

3. After the lapse of ten (10) years from the date of Lease Purchase Agreement, a penalty of one percent (1%) per month shall be imposed on the outstanding principal balance until the same is fully paid.

4. Failure on the part of the beneficiary to pay one (1) monthly installment on due date, a penalty of one percent (1%) shall be imposed on the amount due per month until the same is fully paid.

--- [Page 4] ---
5. Failure of the beneficiary to move onsite into the lot subject of Lease Purchase Agreement (LPA), the city shall cancel the contract and shall replace another beneficiary into the subject lot, unless the failure to move on site is due to reasonable ground.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval;

ENACTED, February 26, 2013 by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian present.
