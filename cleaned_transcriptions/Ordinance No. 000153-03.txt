ORDINANCE NO. 6153-83
Series of 2003

AN ORDINANCE RATIFYING THE FEES PRESCRIBED UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 949 ON FILING AND SERVICE FEES FOR PETITIONS TO CORRECT OR AMEND STATUTORY ERRORS AND CHANGE OF FIRST NAMES OR NICKNAMES IN BIRTH CERTIFICATES AND ALL OTHER CIVIL REGISTRY DOCUMENTS

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled:

--- [Page 2] ---
**Page 2 of 7**

**Ord. No. 0133-03**

**Section 1. Title:** This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE RATIFYING THE FEES PRESCRIBED UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9048 ON FILING AND SERVICE FEES FOR PETITIONS TO CORRECT CLERICAL OR TYPOGRAPHICAL ERRORS AND CHANGE OF FIRST NAMES OR NICKNAMES IN THE BIRTH CERTIFICATE AND ALL OTHER CIVIL REGISTRY DOCUMENTS."

**Section 2. Authority to Collect Filing and Other Fees:** The Civil Registrar of Davao City is hereby authorized to collect from every petition a filing fee in the following amount:

1. For the correction of clerical or typographical error: One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00);
2. For change of first name or nickname: Three Thousand Pesos (P3,000.00);
3. When the petitioner files a petition for the correction of a clerical or typographical error simultaneously with a petition for change of first name, the petitioner shall pay only the amount corresponding to the fee for the petition for change of first name.

An indigent petitioner as defined under Rule 2.7 of Administrative Order No. 1, Series of 2001 of the Office of the Civil Registrar General, National Statistics Office, shall be exempt from the payment of the aforementioned fees.

**Section 3. Effectivity:** This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days from its publication in a newspaper of local circulation.

This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, October 21, 2003, by a majority vote of all the Members of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.
