ACTION:
On Motion of Councilor Tomas Monteverde, Jr., duly seconded by Councilor Blasencio O. Amoros, co-sponsored by Councilors Valentinio C. Banson and Dionisio A. Whipple, the following resolution was adopted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of the City of Davao at its session assembled:

WHEREAS, the people have a right to assemble peacefully and air their grievances against the government or any person or entity and there is a need to establish a site for the purpose to be called "Freedom Park";

WHEREAS, the "Clifford Park" fronting the present site of the Post Office and the key Scouts of the Philippines Building, Davao City, bounded by C. M. Recto Avenue, E. Nagayagay Avenue and F. Reyes St., Davao City, is hereby designated as "Freedom Park";

WHEREAS, the "Freedom Park" may be used by the people without securing a permit from the City Government subject to certain conditions;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve an Ordinance, entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 23

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING THE "FREEDOM PARK," A SITE FOR THE PEOPLE OF DAVAO CITY TO ASSEMBLE PEACEFULLY, WHERE THEY CAN AIR THEIR GRIEVANCES AGAINST THE GOVERNMENT OR ANY PERSON OR ENTITY WITHOUT SECURING A PERMIT FROM THE CITY GOVERNMENT SUBJECT TO CERTAIN CONDITIONS.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

SEC. 1. - There is hereby established a "Freedom Park" in Davao City where the people can peacefully assemble to air their grievances against the government or any person or entity;

SEC. 2. - The site of the "Freedom Park" shall be the area fronting

the Davao City Post Office and the Boy Scouts of the Philippines
Building, bounded by C. M. Recto Avenue, R. Magatay Ave and
Ponciano Reyes St., Davao City, presently called "The Clifford Park";
SEC. 3. Any person, group, organization, or party may use the
"Freedom Park" without securing a permit from the City Government subject
to the following conditions:
a) That they shall inform the Office of the City Mayor of the
name of the person, the organization, group or party and
its officers including the time and date of the use;
b) That they shall police their own rank and file and maintain
peace and order within the site;
c) That they shall not obstruct nor block traffic along C. M.
Recto, R. Magatay Avenue and P. Reyes St., bounding the
area;
d) That the City of Davao nor any of its officers or agents
shall not be liable nor responsible for any criminal act or
acts that may be committed by those who participate in the
use of the "Freedom Park".
SEC. 4. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
CARRIED By a majority of ten (10) affirmative votes. Council
