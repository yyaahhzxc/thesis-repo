Ord. No. 0627-18

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 0291-17, SERIES OF 2017, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2005 REVENUE CODE OF THE CITY OF DAVAO" PARTICULARLY CHAPTER 2, SECTION 13, PARAGRAPH 4-DECLARATION OF REAL PROPERTY BY THE OWNERS OR ADMINISTRATOR; AND CHAPTER 2, SECTION 14, PARAGRAPH 7-DUTY OF THE PERSONS ACQUIRING REAL PROPERTY".

SECTION 2. AMENDED PROVISION -Ordinance No. 0291-17, Series of 2017, otherwise known as "An Ordinance Amending the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao", shall be amended as follows:

Chapter 2, Section 13, 4th paragraph Declaration of Real Property by the Owner or Administrator

Current Provision:
Failure of property owners or administrators to comply with Section 7 of this Revenue Code shall be subject to a pecuniary/administrative fine of one-fourth (1/4) of one percent (1%) of the assessed value of the property. The same shall only be imposed during the General Revision of real property assessments.

Amendment:
Failure of property owners or administrators to comply with the provision of this Revenue Code shall be subject to a pecuniary/administrative fine of one-fourth (1/4) of one percent (1%) of the assessed value of the property. The same shall only be imposed during the General Revision of real property assessments.

Chapter 2, Section 14, 7th paragraph Duty of the Persons Acquiring Real Property

Current Provision:
Failure of property owners or administrators to comply with Section 13 of this Revenue Code shall be subject to a pecuniary/administrative fine of one-fourth (1/4) of one percent (1%) of the assessed value of the property.

Amendment:
Failure of Property owners or administrators to comply with this provision shall be subject to a pecuniary/administrative fine of one-fourth (1/4) of one percent (1%) of the assessed value of the property.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
