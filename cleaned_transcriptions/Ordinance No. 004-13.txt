Page 2 of 3

Ord. No. 004-13

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in Session assembled that:

SECTION 1. All persons below legal age are hereby prohibited to order, drink or consume liquors, alcoholic beverages, coconut wine (tuba) and other nature wines in hotels, beer houses, disco bars, fun houses, motels, family ktv bars, restaurants, foot houses, stores, and the like, whether tourists accredited establishments or not. The prohibition under this Ordinance shall be subjected to in accordance with the provisions of the Juvenile Justice Act.

SECTION 2. All persons selling or serving liquors, alcoholic beverages, coconut wine and other nature wines and the like that cause intoxication within the establishments or in other public places such as, but not limited to, streets, plazas, parks, parking areas and other uninhabited spaces within the same period, that is beginning 1:00 o'clock in the morning (1:00 a.m.) until 8:00 o'clock in the morning (8:00 a.m.), are likewise prohibited.

The drinking and consumption of liquors, alcoholic beverages, coconut wine and other nature wines and the like that causes intoxication within the establishments or in other public places such as, but not limited to, streets, plazas, parks, parking areas and other uninhabited spaces within the same period, that is beginning 1:00 o'clock in the morning (1:00 a.m.) until 8:00 o'clock in the morning (8:00 a.m.), are likewise prohibited.

All business establishments shall stop selling or serving liquors, alcoholic beverages, coconut wine and other nature wines within the same period;

SECTION 3. Any violation of the provisions of the preceding sections of this Ordinance shall be punished as follows:
- 1st Offense - Administrative fine of Three Thousand Pesos (P3,000.00);
- 2nd Offense - Administrative fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) or Imprisonment of Three (3) Months or both upon the discretion of the Court;
- 3rd Offense - Administrative fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) and Imprisonment of One (1) year including Revocation of Business Permit

If the violation is committed by a corporation, partnership or association, the President and/or Manager thereof, shall also be personally liable.

If the violation is committed by a single proprietorship, the owner and/or manager of the said entity shall also be liable therefor;

SECTION 4. Any provisions of any existing Ordinance inconsistent with any provisions of this Ordinance are hereby modified and/or amended accordingly;

SECTION 5. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and after due posting and publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the locality for three (3) consecutive times;
