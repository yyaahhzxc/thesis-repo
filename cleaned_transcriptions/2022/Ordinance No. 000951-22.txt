Series of 2022

AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE ROAD ALONG IÑIGO STREET AND SAN PEDRO STREET FROM 4 MAY 2022 AT 9:00 PM UNTIL 5 MAY 2022 AT 11:59 PM (PARTIAL ROAD CLOSURE) AND 6 MAY 2022 (WHOLE DAY FULL ROAD CLOSURE) IN CONNECTION WITH THE CONDUCT OF ELECTION RELATED POLITICAL ACTIVITIES

Page 2 of 3
Ord. No. 0951-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF THE ROAD ALONG/THROUGH STREET AND SAN PEDRO STREET FROM 4 MAY 2022 AT 9:00 PM UNTIL 5 MAY 2022 (WHOLE DAY FULL ROAD CLOSURE IN CONNECTION WITH THE CONDUCT OF ELECTION RELATED POLITICAL ACTIVITIES".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY -
a. Section 21(c) of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991," provides that any national or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency, or fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fairs.
b. Section 33 of COMELEC Resolution No. 10730, provides that "In the last week of the campaign period, all independent candidates and parties shall be entitled to hold at least one public meeting or rally in the public plaza or place where public political meetings or rallies are usually held".
SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - Iligo Street and San Pedro Street shall be temporarily partially closed from 4 May 2022 at 9:00 Pm until 5 May 2022 at 11:59 Pm and temporarily fully closed on 6 May 2022 from 12:00am to 11:59pm for the conduct of public rally, Meeting de Avance, and other election related political activities by Huppong sa Tawang Lungsood (HTL).
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, this 4th day of May 2022, by an unanimous vote of all Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
