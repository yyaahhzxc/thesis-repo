Ord. No. 088-22

Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE REQUEST OF IRENE M. VILLASICA FOR A WRITTEN CONSENT AND AUTHORITY TO MORTGAGE A PARCEL OF LAND IN HER FAVOR COVERED BY TCT NO. T-146-2021001149, LOCATED IN SANDAWA VILLAGE, PHASE 1, SITIO TAGNIC, BARANGAY BUHANGIN, THIS CITY".

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the grant to Irene M. Villasica for a written consent and authority to mortgage a parcel of land in her favor covered by TCT No. T-146-2021001149 located in Sandawa Village, Phase 1, Sitio Tagnic, Barangay Buhangin, Davao City.

SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED, on the 15th day of November, 2022, by an unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
