Ord. No. 0916-22

Be it enacted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FURTHER AMENDING CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0218-09, SERIES OF 2009, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS 'THE DAVAO CITY PARKS SYSTEM ORDINANCE,'PARTICULARLY SECTION 12 THEREOF."

SECTION 2. Section 12, (B) of City Ordinance No. 0218-09, Series of 2019, otherwise known as "The Davao City Parks System Ordinance," states that:

"B. Prohibited Acts. To ensure the proper management and maintenance of all public parks, adopted parks or streets-islands or green spaces and green strips, the following acts shall be prohibited, to wit:

i. Smoking tobacco, e-cigarettes, vape, and other similar acts;

ii. Bringing and consuming alcohol and/or illegal drugs;

iii. Bringing in of dangerous or hazardous materials, weapons (or similar objects), and glass containers;

iv. Defacing, defacing, and vandalizing of park property;

v. Gambling of any form;

vi. Any form of indecent or immoral acts;

vii. Littering, spitting, public urination and other forms of improper waste disposal;

viii. Picking of flowers, nailing of trees and/or any destruction or injury to plants, trees, shrubs, bushes, grass, etc.

ix. Climbing of trees, statues, sculptures, walls, or any park structure not intended for play or recreation;

x. Leaflettting, displaying or posting of bills or any printed materials;

xi. Sleeping or lying down anywhere within the premises;

xii. Putting up billboards, advertisements or signages;

xiii. Staging of unauthorized events, such as, but not limited to, religious activities, public gatherings, or unauthorized assembly;

xiv. Selling, vending, peddling or displaying of goods of any sort;

xv. Feeding of animals;

xvi. Using of any socket for charging of any electronic gadgets and devices;

xvii. Playing of loud music, unless prior to an authorized park activity;

xviii. Entry of pupils and students during school hours when unaccompanied by parents or guardians;

xix. Blocking of entrances, exits, fire exits, handicap access areas, public walkways or roadways, obstruction of pedestrian traffic or otherwise interfering with the provision of services or use of park property."

SECTION 3. Section 12, (B) (xii) and (xiii) of the aforementioned Ordinance are hereby amended to read as follows:

"B. Prohibited Acts. To ensure the proper management and maintenance of all public parks, adopted parks or streets-islands or green spaces and green strips, the following acts shall be prohibited, to wit:

xxx xxx

xii. Putting up billboards, advertisements, or any form of similar structure;

xiii. Staging of unauthorized events, such as, but not limited to, religious activities, all forms of barangay activities or events public gatherings or unauthorized assembly, subject to the provisions of Batas Pambansa Blg. 880."

Further, sub-paragraph xx is hereby added to Section 12, (B) which shall read as follows:

"xx. Installation of signages or markers without prior approval from the Parks Board."

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - The provisions of these Rules are hereby deemed severable; and in the event that anyone or more of such provisions are invalid, the validity of all other provision shall not be affected thereby.

SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE - All ordinances, resolutions, rules, or regulations which are inconsistent with or contrary to the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

ENACTED, April 05, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
