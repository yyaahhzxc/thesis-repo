SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "Peace 911 Ordinance".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES
A. The City Government of Davao is committed to building and maintaining a peaceful society where its citizens have the capacity to lead long, happy lives which is ultimately the goal of all development.
Peacebuilding should therefore be a cross-cutting theme in any development plan or program. Peacebuilding is the process of increasing connectors so that equitable development can occur. On the other hand, development is the process of increasing capacities and decreasing vulnerability so that people's choices are expanded to enable them to lead long happy lives.
B. There are eight broad categories that create and sustain peaceful societies. The Institute for Economics and Peace has identified the following eight (8) Pillars of Positive Peace as the most common characteristics of most peaceful societies. These pillars are:
• well-functioning government;
• low levels of corruption;
• acceptance of the rights of others;
• good relations with neighbors;
• sound business environment;
• high levels of human capital;
• equitable distribution of resources; and
• free flow of information.
The concept of Positive Peace acknowledges that ending direct violence alone does not guarantee durable peace. Ending direct violence is Negative Peace. For peace to endure, a society must build longer-term conditions that enable society to unify and flourish. This concept is called Positive Peace. Investing in Positive Peace will result in mitigating factors of conflict such as economic conflict from conflict. The Eight Pillars of Positive Peace are highly interconnected and interact together to build a system. It therefore requires building capacities for peace not only of the government but also of local communities and other structures and institutions of the City. The establishment of Peace 911-The Center for Peacebuilding will contribute to the achievement of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals of the United Nations, in particular Goal 16 on building peace, justice, and strong institutions, strengthening human rights, reducing discrimination and inequality, promoting gender equality, preserving natural resources and protecting the climate.
C. Section 3(b) of Republic Act 7160 states that there shall be established in every local government unit an accountable, efficient, and dynamic organizational structure and operating mechanism that will meet the priority needs and service requirements of its communities while Section 3(f) states that there shall be a continuing mechanism to enhance local economy not only by legislative enabling acts but also by administrative and organizational reforms;
D. Section 76 of Republic Act 7160 states that every local government unit shall design and implement its own organizational structure and staffing pattern taking into consideration its service requirements and financial capability.

SECTION 4. CREATION OF PEACE 911—the CENTER FOR PEACEBUILDING—Peace 911—the Center for Peacebuilding is hereby created as a division under the Office of the City Mayor to serve as the peacebuilding office of the City of Davao.

A. Peace 911—the Center for Peacebuilding shall be created as a division of the City Mayor’s Office (CMO);

B. Peace 911—the Center for Peacebuilding shall adhere to the following vision and mission:

1. Peace 911—the Center for Peacebuilding envisions peace as embedded in justice, the building of right relationships and social structures, through respect for one another and commitment to nonviolence as a way of life;

2. The mission of Peace 911—the Center for Peacebuilding is to build peaceful communities through people’s participation in good governance, equitable economic development and cohesive social relations within the framework of positive peace;

C. Peace 911—the Center for Peacebuilding shall be otherwise called as Peace 911;

D. Peace 911- The Center for Peacebuilding shall be headed by a Development Management Officer IV with Salary Grade 22;
E. A Development Management Officer III with Salary Grade 18 shall be the assistant head of Peace 911- The Center for Peacebuilding;
F. Peace 911- The Center for Peacebuilding shall be composed of four (4) sections namely,
1. Administrative Services Section
2. Program Management Section
3. Research and Documentation Section, and
4. Partnership Management Section
SECTION 5. DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF PEACE 911 - The Center for Peacebuilding: The duties and functions of Peace 911- The Center for Peacebuilding are the following:
A. Mainstreams a peace perspective into the City's development plans and programs;
B. Serves as the implementing arm of all initiatives embodied under the peacebuilding program recommended by the Peace 911 General Committee for the City of Davao, as approved by the City Mayor;
C. Plans and implements approved peacebuilding programs, projects, and activities;
D. Serves as the gatekeeper of all programs and projects to be implemented within the Peace 911 focus areas, and as such, shall coordinate all efforts and initiatives of the involved communities, government, NGOs, the private sector and other partners;
E. Creates a single channel (convergence) for all services, programs and activities that will be introduced to the focus areas;
F. Coordinates with teaching and training institutions to enhance government and local capacities for peace;
G. Conducts researches/studies in all matters involving peace practice and other programs relative to peace;
H. Forms partnerships with various groups of stakeholders to form a community of practice in peacebuilding;
I. Coordinates with national and local agencies in the implementation of programs and projects;
J. Recommends to the City Mayor any actions/counter measures for all concerns and issues which may arise from time to time relative to the peacebuilding process and program implementation;
K. Acts as the secretariat of the Peace 911 General Committee;
L. Performs such other acts to ensure the implementation of peace objectives of the national government.
SECTION 6. FUNCTIONS OF THE SECTIONS IN PEACE 911- THE CENTER FOR PEACEBUILDING - Peace 911- The Center for Peacebuilding as a Division under the Office of the City Mayor shall be composed of four (4) sections with its respective functions, to wit:
A. The Functions of the Administrative Section are:
1. Provides assistance to other sections/units on all administrative matters;
2. Assists other sections in the preparation of documents and other official activities conducted by the office;
3. Performs other tasks that maybe assigned by immediate superiors pursuant to the objectives of this Ordinance.

B. The Functions of the Program Management Section are:
1. Plans and implements peacebuilding programs, projects, and activities;
2. Engages in innovative peacebuilding practices;
3. Coordinates with peace teaching and training institutions to enhance government and local capacities for peace;
4. Performs other tasks that may be assigned by immediate superiors pursuant to the objectives of this Ordinance.

C. The Functions of the Research and Documentation Section are:
1. Conducts researches/studies in all matters involving peace promotion and other programs relative to the peacebuilding process;
2. Conducts documentation on all matters involving peacebuilding processes and peace promotion programs including lessons that will enhance peace practice and to incorporate these into training programs;
3. Performs other tasks that may be assigned by immediate superiors pursuant to the objectives of this Ordinance.

D. The Functions of the Partnership Management Section are:
1. Builds a community of practice locally and regionally by building mechanisms for cooperation among all relevant stakeholders;
2. Promotes cooperative problem-solving and institutionalizing appropriate governance mechanisms for conflict resolution;
3. Performs other tasks that may be assigned by immediate superiors pursuant to the objectives of this Ordinance.

SECTION 9. APPOINTMENT: The positions in Peace 911-The Center for Peacebuilding shall be appointed by the City Mayor in accordance with the civil service laws and rules and qualification standards which the appointee must possess as set by the Civil Service Commission.

SECTION 10. HUMAN RESOURCE COMPLEMENT: The City Mayor shall be allowed to employ emergency personnel or laborers needed by Peace 911-The Center for Peacebuilding through Job Orders or Contract of Services based on the availability of budget pursuant to Section 77 of R.A. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, and subject to government rules and regulations on budgeting, accounting, and auditing.

SECTION 11. APPROPRIATION: The appropriation necessary for the implementation of this Ordinance shall be included by the City Budget Officer in the proposed Annual Budget of the City Government.

SECTION 12. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE: If, for any reason or reasons, any part or provision of this Ordinance shall be held to be unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or provisions thereof which are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 13. EFFECTIVITY: This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval.

ENACTED, February 02, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
