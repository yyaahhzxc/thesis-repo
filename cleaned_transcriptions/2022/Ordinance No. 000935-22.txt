Ord. No. 0935-22

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF MS. MARILOU W. AMPUAN, PRESIDENT OF THE
PHILIPPINE HALAL TRADE AND TOURISM EXPOSITION, FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE
PAYMENT OF THE SPECIAL MAYOR'S PERMIT FEES OF THE PARTICIPATING MSMES
DURING THE 7TH PHILIPPINE HALAL TRADE AND TOURISM EXPO ON MAY 27-29,
2022, AT THE ABREZZA MALL, J.P. LAUREL, BAJADA, DAVAO CITY."

SECTION 2. COVERAGE - The exemption solely refers to the payment of the
Special Mayor's Permit Fees of the participating MSMES during the 7th Philippine Halal
Trade and Tourism Expo.

SECTION 3. PERIOD - The exemption shall be on May 27-29, 2022.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, April 26, 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
