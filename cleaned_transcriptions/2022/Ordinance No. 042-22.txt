Ord. No. 042-22.
Be it ordained by the Honorable Sangguniang Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE EXEMPTING ST. FRANCIS XAVIER COLLEGE SEMINARY OF DAVAO (SFXCS), A NON-STOCK, NON-PROFIT, INSTITUTION, FROM THE PAYMENT OF AMUSEMENT TAX WITH REGARD TO THEIR UPCOMING BENEFIT CONCERT TO BE HELD IN HOLY CROSS GYM ON DECEMBER 1, 2022, FROM 7:00 PM TO 9:00 PM, CONSIDERING THAT THE PROCEDURES OF THE SAID CONCERT WILL BE DIRECTED TOWARDS THE OPERATION AND REPAIRS OF THE SEMINARY".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Section 16 of Republic Act 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of the Philippines, state in part that "every local government shall exercise the powers expressly granted to them, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare".
SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - Pursuant to Section 66, paragraphs (a) and (b) of the 2017 Revenue Code of Davao City which state, in part, that "(a)"Where the admission fees are collected for and in behalf of the charitable, educational, or religious institutions or associations who are declared by law or presidential proclamation as exempted from the payment of amusement tax on paid admission, provided further, that such exhibition, show, performance and the like shall be limited to only three (3) days in a calendar year"; and "(b)"Where admission fees are collected in connection with the holding of the operas, concert, dramas, recitals, paintings and art exhibitions, flower shows, musical programs, literary and oratorical presentations except pop rock, or similar concerts not intended primarily for profit and furthermore, not including film exhibition, St. Francis Xavier College Seminary of Davao is hereby exempted from the payment of amusement tax.
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereto not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
ENACTED, on the 11th day of October 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
