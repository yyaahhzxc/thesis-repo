MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Agreement entered into between:

THE CONSULATE GENERAL OF THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA IN DAVAO, with address at Acacia Street, Juna Subdivision, Matina, Davao City, represented herein by its Consul General, MR. LI LIN, hereinafter referred to as "FIRST PARTY";

- and -

THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT OF DAVAO CITY, a Local Government Unit with offices at City Hall Drive, Davao City, represented herein by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "SECOND PARTY".

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic has greatly affected the livelihood of many people including the local banana farmers in the agriculture sector who are now struggling to make a profit;

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY aims to help the people of Mindanao march towards recovery in the face of the pandemic;

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY expressed their plan to provide assistance to One Hundred (100) families of local banana farmers from Davao City to ensure a steady and continuous harvest that will become a reliable source of profit and enable the identified farmers and their families to rise to the economic challenges brought by the pandemic;

WHEREAS, among the socio-economic agenda of the present administration of the City Government of Davao is to promote rural and value chain development toward increasing agricultural and rural enterprise productivity and rural tourism;

WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY, through the City Agriculturist's Office, signed its commitment to collaborate with the FIRST PARTY in providing assistance to the identified one hundred (100) families of local banana farmers from Davao City who will receive seedlings, fertilizers and pesticides;

WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY has the technical capabilities/capacities to implement the project such as the presence of knowledgeable technical staff, good track record or performance on project implementation.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereby agree on the following:

--- [Page 5] ---
**ARTICLE I THE PROJECT**

Parties agree to and signify their intention to undertake the DONATION AND DISTRIBUTION OF SEEDLINGS, FERTILIZERS AND PESTICIDES AMOUNTING TO THREE MILLION FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (P 3,500,000.00) FROM THE CONSULATOREN GENERAL OF THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA IN DAVAO TO IDENTIFIED DAVAO CITY BANANA-CARDAVA FARMERS, hereinafter referred to as "PROJECT".

This PROJECT aims to provide assistance to local banana farmers and ensure a steady and continuous harvest that will become a reliable source of profit and enable the identified farmers and their families to rise to the economic challenges brought by the pandemic.

The PROJECT also aims to reduce poverty by generating profit within the family and moving towards economic stability with every harvest, and boost and promote the market of locally produced bananas and banana chips made from the harvested banana-cardava.

The PROJECT is pursuant to the City Government of Davao's socio-economic agenda, which is to promote rural and value chain developments toward increasing agricultural and rural enterprise productivity and rural tourism.

Specifically, the PROJECT aims to produce at least 900 kilograms of harvested matured bananas per tully grown hill within eighteen (18) months after its planting. Thereafter, the harvested bananas shall be sold to local entrepreneurs or business owners at a minimum price of PHP 8.00 per kilogram. Through continuous harvests and given a margin of error of 10% per harvest, each farmer shall generate a profit of at least PHP 7,200.00 per month.

**ARTICLE II RESPONSIBILITIES AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES**

1. The FIRST PARTY shall:

1.1 Prepare the program or project plan/proposal (Annex A);

1.2 Designate, together with the SECOND PARTY, the supplier who will carry out the purchase of banana seedlings, pesticides and organic fertilizers that will be distributed to the beneficiaries;

1.3 Purchase the banana seedlings, pesticides and organic fertilizers amounting to Three Million Five Hundred Thousand Pesos (P 3,500,000.00) together with the SECOND PARTY;

1.4 Assist in the marketing of the harvested banana fruits; and

1.5 Coordinate with project collaborators approved by both parties herein.

2. The SECOND PARTY shall:

2.1 Secure a Sangguniang Panlungsod Resolution/Ordinance authorizing the Local Chief Executive to enter and sign a Memorandum of Agreement for

--- [Page 6] ---
and in behalf of the LGU with the Consulate General of the People's Republic of China in Davao relative to the implementation of the said project;
2.2 Identify the one hundred (100) project beneficiaries from Davao City;
2.3 Designate, together with the FIRST PARTY, the supplier who will carry out the purchase of banana seedlings, pesticides and organic fertilizers that will be distributed to the beneficiaries;
2.4 Distribute to the identified beneficiaries the banana-cardava seedlings, pesticides and organic fertilizers donated by the Consulate General of the People's Republic of China;
2.5 Provide technical assistance to the identified beneficiaries, which may be comprised of assistance ranging from the land preparation to harvesting of the fruits, or any manner as determined by the SECOND PARTY to be necessary;
2.6 Assist the beneficiaries in the marketing of their produce;
2.7 Conduct seminars, training and meetings with the beneficiaries relating to the Project; and
2.8 Prepare project reports.
ARTICLE III
GENERAL PROVISIONS
1. Data Privacy
Personal data and/or technical information, shared between all parties are necessary for effective research and development and shall be in accordance with Philippine Technology Transfer Act of 2009 and the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and their Implementing Rules and Regulations.
It is expressly understood by all parties that the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information shall be upheld at all times and disclosure or transfer, consciously or unconsciously, to any party outside of this agreement, shall be prevented without the knowledge and written consent of both parties.
In view of this, all parties shall agree to hold in confidence any such information and shall undertake to prevent its disclosure or transfer, consciously or unconsciously, to any party outside of this agreement without the knowledge and written consent of all parties. It is expressly understood that in the course of this agreement, no person may be exposed to technical and/or personal information, and other information and data deemed as classified by either party.
2. Modification
Any modification of this agreement shall be made with the written authority of both parties.
3. Dispute Settlement
Any disagreement in the implementation of the plans and provision of this corporation or any agreement related thereto shall be settled amicably between the parties.

--- [Page 7] ---
4. Modification or Amendments

No modification or amendment to this agreement and the approved project documents shall be allowed without the written consent of both parties involved.

All amendment(s) that will be appended to this agreement shall be in writing and must be delivered to the addresses of the parties indicated herein.

5. Other Provisions

All parties hereby agree that in all matters relating to this MOA, whether during the subsistence or after its termination, including all matters concerning these provisions where any question, dispute or difference may arise, all parties shall opt to settle these issues and concerns on mutually beneficial arrangements, more so, in good faith.

ARTICLE IV PROJECT DURATION, EFFECTIVITY, TERMINATION OF THE AGREEMENT

The establishment of the project will cover for an estimated duration period of five (5) years upon approval of the project proposal and will continue until the completion of the five-year project duration and/or terminated by the approving body. Any extension of contract duration due to unforeseen events with justifiable grounds shall be approved by both parties involved. In case of failure or termination of the project due to force majeure or fortuitous event, the Parties may suspend the implementation of the Project upon mutual agreement.
