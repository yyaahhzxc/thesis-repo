**Resolution No. 101**

A resolution/ordinance of Councilor Alexis C. Almendras prohibiting the establishment of funeral parlors within the radius of two hundred (200) meters from residential areas, tourist centers, commercial buildings, hotels, motels, sports complexes, parks, playgrounds, churches, and other institutional buildings including cockpits within the City of Davao except those already established and existing, with the proposed fine of One Thousand (P1,000.00) pesos for violation thereof and six (6) months imprisonment be deleted. The original proponent offered no objection.

After careful deliberation, on motion of Councilor Victorio S. Advincula, as amended, with the co-sponsorship of Councilor Alexis C. Almedras, duly seconded jointly by Councilors Senforiano B. Alterado and Matute H. Feli, it was

RESOLVED, AS IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, To approve and Ordinance, entitled:

ORDINANCE NO. 09

AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE ESTABLISHMENT OF FUNERAL PARLORS/ MEMORIAL HOMES WITHIN THE RADIUS OF TWO HUNDRED (200) METERS FROM RESIDENTIAL AREAS, COMMERCIAL BUILDINGS, HOTELS, MOTELS, SPORTS COMPLEX, PARKS AND PLAYGROUNDS, TOURIST CENTERS, INSTITUTIONAL BUILDINGS, INCLUDING COCKPITS WITHIN THE CITY OF DAVAO.

Be it Ordered by the Sangguniang Panlungsod, City of Davao, that:

SECTION 1. - Sub-section 3.1 of Section 8, Article IV of Ordinance No. 363, Series of 1982, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"3.1 - Funeral Parlors/Memorial Homes with adequate off street parking spaces (see parking standards of PD 1096) provided that they shall be established not less than two hundred (200) meters from any residential structures, commercial buildings, hotels, motels, sports complex, parks and playgrounds, churches, other institutional buildings, tourist centers, and cockpits."

SECTION 2. - Ordinance No. 363, Series of 1982, is hereby further amended accordingly to this effect;

SECTION 3. - This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

ENACTED: January 22, 1991.

A vote of unanimity has been entered into the records.
