ARTICLE I.
TITLE OF THE ORDINANCE

Section 1. Short Title. This Ordinance shall be known as the "Davao City Comprehensive STI, HIV and AIDS Ordinance of 2013."

ARTICLE II.
DECLARATION OF POLICY

Section 1. Declaration of Policy. The Sexually Transmitted Infection, Human Immunodeficiency Virus and Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (STI, HIV and AIDS) are public health concerns that have wide-ranging social, political, and economic repercussions. As such, responding to the STI, HIV and AIDS epidemic is imbued with public interest, and thus the City Government of Davao commits itself to make actions to: (1.) prevent the spread of HIV and other STIs and AIDS; (2.) provide treatment, care, and support services to persons living with HIV and AIDS and mitigate any related social, psychological, and economic consequences, as follows:

1. The City Government of Davao shall establish policies and programs to prevent the spread of STI, HIV, and AIDS and deliver treatment, care, and support services to persons living with HIV in accordance with evidence-based strategies and approaches that follow the principles of human rights, gender equality, and meaningful participation of communities affected by the epidemic;

2. The City Government of Davao shall adopt a multi-sectoral and culturally-sensitive approach in responding to the STI, HIV, and AIDS epidemic by ensuring that local communities, civil society groups, and persons living with HIV and their families, are part of the response to the epidemic;

3. The City Government of Davao shall remove all barriers to STI, HIV, and AIDS-related services and shall eliminate the climate of stigma that surrounds the epidemic and the people directly and indirectly affected by it; and

4. The City Government of Davao shall consistently adhere to generally-accepted principles of human rights and adopt gender perspectives in dealing with STI, HIV, and AIDS as it shall even promote value formation and employ scientifically-proven approaches. The information and education campaigns shall be carried out in all schools, training centers, workplaces, and communities.

Therefore it affirms that:

1. The protection, promotion, and fulfillment of human rights as the cornerstone of an effective response to the epidemic;

2. The meaningful inclusion and participation of persons directly and indirectly affected by the epidemic, especially persons living with HIV is crucial in eliminating the virus;

3. Unless otherwise provided in this Ordinance, the confidentiality, anonymity, and non-compulsory nature of HIV testing and HIV-related testing shall always be guaranteed and protected by the City Government of Davao;

4. Policies and practices that discriminate on the basis of perceived or actual HIV status, sex, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, economic status, ethnicity, and disability are inimical to national interest and contrary to the rights and freedoms guaranteed in the Constitution, and the City Government of Davao shall eliminate these acts of discrimination.

**Article III**

**DEFINITION OF TERMS**

**Section 3. Definition of Terms:** As used in this Ordinance, the following terms are defined as follows:

a. **Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS)** - refers to a condition where a body's immune system is weakened due to HIV infection, making an individual susceptible to opportunistic infections;

b. **Anti-retroviral Treatment** - refers to the treatment that suppresses a retrovirus like HIV;

c. **Children** - refers to persons below 18 years of age or those over but unable to fully take care of themselves or protect themselves from abuse, neglect, cruelty, exploitation or discrimination because of physical or mental disability or condition;

d. **Community-based HIV Screening** - refers to a non-laboratory rapid screening procedure done outside a health facility by a trained member of a community-based organizations or groups;

e. **Compulsory HIV Testing** - refers to HIV testing imposed upon a person attended or characterized by the lack of consent, use of force or intimidation;

f. **HIV Counseling and Testing Site/Facility** - refers to DOH accredited HIV testing facility with DOH proficient Medical Technologist;

g. **Discrimination** - refers to any action taken to distinguish, exclude, restrict or show preference based on any ground such as sex, gender, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, economic status, disability, and ethnicity, whether actual or perceived, which has the purpose or effect of nullifying or impairing the recognition, enjoyment or exercise by all persons similarly situated, of all rights and freedoms;

h. **Entertainment Establishments** - are business establishments which include bars, nightclubs, beer houses, karaoke bars/sing-along pub houses and other similar establishments within Davao City;

i. **Ethics of Care** - health care providers embraces the universal values of liberty, justice, confidentiality, respect, gender equity and equality to clients;

j. **Faith-based Organization** - refers to a group of individuals united on the basis of their religious and or spiritual beliefs;

k. **Gender Identity** - refers to a person's internal and individual experience of gender that may or may not correspond with the sex assigned at birth;

l. **Health Certificate** - is a certification in writing using the prescribed form issued by the City Health Officer stating that the person applying for or renewing such certificate, is permitted by the said Officer or his/her duly authorized representative to work in an establishment. This certification is issued to the person applying for or renewing such, after passing the required physical and medical examinations, immunizations and other examinations as may be required by the City Health Officer or his duly authorized representative;

m. **HIV and AIDS Counselor** - refers to any individual trained by an institution or organization accredited by the Department of Health to conduct counseling on STI, HIV and AIDS, HIV prevention, human rights and stigma reduction;

n. **HIV and AIDS Monitoring** - refers to the documentation and analysis of the number of HIV infection and pattern of its spread;

Article IV
STI, HIV AND AIDS PREVENTION

Section 1. STI, HIV and AIDS Prevention. The City Government of Davao, through the Davao City AIDS Council (DCAC), shall promote and adopt a range of measures and interventions, in partnership with civil society organizations, that aims to prevent, halt, or control the spread of sexually transmitted infections (STIs) in the general population especially among high populations and vulnerable communities.

Section 2. STI, HIV and AIDS Awareness Campaign. The DCAC shall conduct whole year round activities on STI, HIV and AIDS Awareness Campaign which officially starts in January, highlighting the month of May, the International AIDS Candlelight Memorial and Mobilization (IACMM) and the month of December being the World AIDS Day prevention month and HIV summit.

Section 3. Media Campaign on STI, HIV/AIDS Prevention. In partnership with the Davao City Information Office, the Philippine Information Agency (PIA), and the various media organizations, the DCAC shall prepare and implement a media and communications plan for a regular information dissemination campaign and advocacy.

Section 4. Sexual Abstinence and Mutual Fidelity. Sexual abstinence and mutual fidelity shall be emphasized as the most effective measures for STI, HIV/AIDS prevention.

Section 5. Comprehensive STI, HIV/AIDS Education. Comprehensive education and information strategies shall be designed, integrated and adopted in various levels geared towards the purpose of providing substantial information/knowledge, improving attitudes and skills in the prevention and control of STI, HIV/AIDS, in both public and private sphere.

**Section 6. STI, HIV/AIDS Information as a Health Service.** STI, HIV/AIDS education and information dissemination shall form part of the delivery of health services practitioners, workers, and personnel. The knowledge and capabilities of all public health workers shall be enhanced to include skills for proper information dissemination and education on STI, HIV/AIDS. It shall likewise be considered a civic duty of health providers in the private sector to make available to the public such information necessary to control the spread of STI, HIV/AIDS and to correct common misconceptions on HIV/AIDS-related ethical issues such as confidentiality, informed consent, and the duty to provide treatment.

**Section 7. HIV and AIDS Education for Key Populations and Vulnerable Communities.** The Davao City AIDS Council through the City Health Office - Health Promotion and Development (HPD) shall coordinate with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE), the Bureau of Labor and Employment Laga ng mga Barangay, NGOs, people's organizations, and civil society, shall support and ensure STI, HIV and AIDS education programs, such as peer education and outreach activities, and community-based screening services to reach target key populations and vulnerable communities.

**Section 8. STI, HIV/AIDS Gender Sensitive IEC Materials and Education Modules.** Information, education, and communication (IEC) materials shall be designed appropriate to the needs and culture of groups being targeted in the campaign. Literature on STI/AIDS shall utilize the local lingo to encourage and facilitate easy understanding of terms and concepts. Modules on STI, HIV/AIDS prevention and control shall be gender-sensitive and shall be an essential component of any comprehensive STI/AIDS prevention program. Proper consultations shall be held among heads, leaders, and representatives of respective groups, organizations, institutions, and individuals who are targets for application of any course content, scope, and methodology.

**Section 9. Age-appropriate HIV and AIDS Education in Schools.** The Davao City AIDS Council through the City Health Office - HPD shall coordinate with the Department of Education (DepEd), Commission on Higher Education (CHED), and the Technical Education Skills and Development Authority (TESDA) to integrate basic and age-appropriate instruction on the causes, modes of transmission, and ways of preventing the spread of HIV and other sexually transmitted infections in subjects taught in public and private schools including non-formal and indigenous learning systems.

**Section 10. HIV and AIDS Education in the Workplace.** The Davao City AIDS Council through the City Health Office - HPD in coordination with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) and the Civil Service Commission (CSC) shall provide standard basic information and instruction on STI, HIV and AIDS, national and local HIV related laws and other related issues of stigma reduction and discrimination in the workplace, public and private, including the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) and the Philippine National Police (PNP).

**Section 11. Compulsory STI, HIV/AIDS Education.** It shall be compulsory for all owners/employers of all establishments to attend seminar on STI, HIV/AIDS prevention conducted regularly by any of the agencies such as the City Health Office, Davao City AIDS Council, and accredited Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs), otherwise, no renewal of permit shall be granted to said establishments for violation thereof. A certificate of attendance to STI, HIV/AIDS Seminar shall form part of the requirements for the issuance or renewal of Health Certificate and Permit to Operate.

**Section 12. HIV and AIDS for Filipinos Going Abroad.** The Davao City AIDS Council through the City Health Office - HPD shall coordinate with the DOLE and its attached agencies the Philippine Overseas Employment Administration (POEA) and Overseas Workers Welfare Administration (OWWA) to ensure that all overseas Filipino workers shall be provided with information on how to avoid STI, HIV, and AIDS-related services and facilities before the termination of their assignment.

**Section 13. HIV and AIDS Education in Communities.** The City Health Office through the Bureau of Labor and Employment - Bureau of Children (BOPC) and other NGOs shall spearhead Education and Information Activities for public awareness campaigns on STI, HIV, and AIDS through various channels, on evidence-based, gender-sensitive, age-appropriate, culturally-sensitive, and human rights-oriented prevention and control measures.

Section 14. STI, HIV, and AIDS Education among Out-of-School Youth. The City Health Office, through the HPDD and City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) and other accredited agencies shall provide trainings or seminars on STI, HIV, and AIDS and other related issues on Adolescent Reproductive Health (ARH) to all Out-of-School Youth and young people in coordination with Sanggunian Kabataan (SK) officials in the barangay. For this purpose, SKs shall integrate STI and HIV in their plans, programs, and activities.

Section 15. Information for Tourists and Transients. The HQ-HPD and City Tourism Office shall conduct Information Education on the cause, modes of transmission, prevention and consequences of HIV infection which will be adequately provided at all domestic and international points of entry and exit.

Section 16. Prevention, Treatment, Care and Support in Prison and Other Closed-Setting Institutions. The City Health Office-HPDD and RHWRC and other accredited institutions and organizations, in coordination with the Bureau of Prisons, shall ensure that all prisons, public and private rehabilitation centers, and other closed-setting institutions shall have comprehensive STI, HIV and AIDS prevention, Treatment, Care and Support program.

Section 17. Compulsory STI, HIV and AIDS Education for Marriage Applicants. The CSSDO in partnership with CHO through the Population Division and IGGD and other implementing agencies shall integrate the conduct of STI, HIV and AIDS, HIV testing and gender sensitivity education among marriage applicants.

Section 18. Media Campaign on STI, HIV and AIDS Prevention. The CIO, in partnership with CHO-HPDD in coordination with the Philippine Information Agency (PIA), and the various media institutions, shall implement a media and communications plan including STI, HIV and AIDS education among media practitioners and regular information dissemination campaign and advocacy.

Section 19: HIV Counseling and HIV Testing. The City Government of Davao through the City Health Office-RHWRC and other implementing agencies shall ensure that HIV testing is voluntary and confidential pursuant to existing national and local laws. The CSSDO in coordination with DOH accredited testing facilities shall ensure that proxy consent shall be provided to those minors who voluntarily submit themselves for HIV counseling and testing.

Section 20. Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission. The City Health Office through Maternal Neonatal Child Health and Nutrition (MCHN) shall integrate STI, HIV and AIDS Prevention Program to prevent mother-to-child transmission in its maternal and child health services and other health facilities.

Section 21. Prevention of Blood Related Transmission. The City Health Office in coordination with DOH Blood Program, PRC, Hospitals, Clinics and Laboratories shall adopt the guidelines of the Department of Health and the existing ordinance on donation of blood, tissue or organ, and surgical and other similar procedures shall adopt standard precaution.

Section 22. Standard Precaution. All health care providers implementing HIV program shall adopt standard precautions according to the existing DOH guidelines.

Section 1. Psychosocial Care and Support to Persons Living with HIV and Affected Persons. The City Government of Davao through the CHQ-RHWHC shall ensure availability and accessibility of HIV treatment to Persons Infected with STI, HIV and provide education on adherence to treatment. The City Government of Davao and other service providers shall develop psychosocial care and support programs to PLHIV and affected families.

Section 2. Care and Support for Overseas Workers Living with HIV.
The CSSDO in coordination with DOLE, OWWA, POEA, DOH, CHD, Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) and NGOs shall develop a stigma-free comprehensive reintegration and care and support program, including economic, social and medical support for overseas workers who have been repatriated or are about to be repatriated due to their HIV positive status.

Section 3. Special Care and Support Services for Pregnant Women with STI, HIV and AIDS.
The City Health Office through its Maternal Health programs in coordination with City Social Services and Development Office and other accredited agencies, institutions shall provide special care and support services for pregnant women with STI, HIV and AIDS and shall be integrated in the development plans and activities of the department.

Section 4. Special Care and Support Services for Children Infected with HIV and Persons Living with HIV (PHLHV) and AIDS.
The City Social Services Development Office in coordination with CHD, Maternal Health, Nutrition (MHN) program and other relevant agencies shall provide special care and support services for children of persons living with HIV and children infected with HIV.

Section 5. Special Care and Support Services for Abused Women, Men and Children.
The IGDD in coordination with the CHD, CSSDO, Public Attorney's Office and DCPO-WPCD Desk shall facilitate legal services to abused women, men, children and persons with disability infected with STI, HIV and AIDS.

Section 6. Health Care Plans for Person with HIV.
The CSSDO shall assess and refer PLHV to avail of health care plans through the agency's indigency program.

ARTICLE VI
ADHERENCE TO SAFE PRACTICES AND PROCEDURES, TESTING, SCREENING AND COUNSELING

Section 1. Safe Practices and Procedures.
The City of Davao shall strictly adhere to the principles and guidelines on safe practices and procedures, necessary to protect any person from HIV transmission as mandated in Articles II and III of RA 8504. Procedures on testing, screening and counseling shall observe the ethics of care and medical confidentiality in dealing with individuals having vulnerable conditions for contracting STI/HIV.

ARTICLE VII
HEALTH AND SUPPORT SERVICES

Section 1. Universal Access to STI Healthcare Services.
Access to STI healthcare services/assistance shall be extended to all individuals regardless of sex, gender, age, social status, political belief, tradition and religion.

Section 2. Adherence to Quality Health Care and Ethics of Care.
Practitioners or other staff assisting or directly providing health services, examinations, education to clients shall adhere to the basic principles of ethics of care. They shall also be sensitive to gender and sexual orientation in their treatment to patients/clients, informing them of their rights, including the right to file charges for abuses committed against them.

Section 3. Health Care Plans for Person with HIV.
The City of Davao, in coordination with the Philippine National AIDS Council, Department of Health and Commissioner of the Insurance Commission shall conduct a study on the feasibility of setting up a package of health care plans, and should the study warrant, implement the plan for persons with HIV.

Section 4. Special Welfare Fund for RHWCC Clients.
The City of Davao shall establish and maintain a welfare fund for the clients of RHWCC, which shall come from the smear check-up and from other sources of the government. Said amount shall exclusively accrue to the welfare fund of the RHWCC Clients to be managed by the City Health Office, in partnership with AIDS Council and other financial institution.

ARTICLE VIII
STI, HIV AND AIDS SURVEILLANCE AND MONITORING
Section 1. Monitoring, Documentation and Reporting Mechanisms. The Davao City AIDS Council shall develop a comprehensive STI, HIV, and AIDS monitoring in the implementation of the program and reporting procedures on the magnitude of and progression of HIV and AIDS cases, and on program response and in aid of policy legislation. In addition, it shall enhance mechanisms to further implement provisions on monitoring of Article IV of Republic Act 8504 (as amended).
Section 2. Research Investigations. The City Health Office and CPDO in coordination with the Department of Health, academic and research institutions shall conduct STI, HIV, and AIDS related researches and studies as basis in planning, policy making and advocacy as needed.
Section 3. Documentation. STI/AIDS contact tracing and all other related health intelligence activities may be initiated by the Department of Health, the XMM Council, provided that these do not run counter to the general purpose of this Ordinance. Any information gathered shall remain confidential and classified, and shall not be used in any way to become basis or qualification for any employment, school attendance, freedom of abode, or travel.
ARTICLE IX
STIGMA REDUCTION AND HUMAN RIGHTS
Section 1: Stigma Reduction, Acts of Discrimination and other Human Rights Violations. The City Government through the Davao City AIDS Council shall ensure implementation of STI, HIV and AIDS anchored to the ethical standards and in pursuit to the National AIDS Law and other related existing laws and instrumentalities.
ARTICLE X
PROTECTION OF PEER EDUCATORS, OUTREACH WORKERS, SOCIAL WORKERS AND HEALTH SERVICE PROVIDERS ON STI, HIV AND AIDS
Section 1. Implementers of STI, HIV and AIDS program shall abide by the rules and regulations mandated under its existing human rights laws and related local ordinances and existing policies approved by the DCAC, and shall not be subject to arrest and prosecution in the implementation of STI, HIV and AIDS program.
ARTICLE XI
CONFIDENTIALITY OF INFORMATION
Section 1: The Davao City AIDS Council shall ensure confidentiality in all matters pertaining to STI, HIV and AIDS as provided for in the National AIDS law and other related policy issuances.
Section 2. Disclosure to Sexual Partners. - Any person who tested positive to HIV is obliged to disclose his or her status and health condition to his or her spouse or sexual partner prior to engaging in penetrative sex or any potential exposure to HIV and may opt to ask help from health providers in case of disclosure.
Section 3. Civil Liability. - A person who has obtained knowledge of confidential HIV and AIDS information and has used such information to malign or cause damage, injury, or loss to another person may face liability under Articles 19, 20, 21, and 26 of the Civil Code.
ARTICLE XII
STI, HIV AND AIDS PLANNING, MONITORING AND EVALUATION
Section 1. HIV and AIDS 3-year Plan. The Davao City AIDS Council shall develop a 3-year STI, HIV and AIDS Plan and budgetary allocations.

2. City Health Officer - Co-Chair

4. City Social Services and Development Office - Member
5. Department of Education Davao City Division - Member

7. Integrated Gender and Development Division (IGGD) - Member
8. City Health Office - CIO - Member

b. Coordinative Body in establishing, mobilizing and strengthening linkages at the local, national and international agencies and other stakeholders involved in the STI, HIV/AIDS prevention programs;

c. Lead Group in undertaking information, education and communication campaigns on STI, HIV/AIDS prevention;

d. Monitoring Body in the implementation of RA 8504, this Ordinance and other policies approved and adopted by DCAC.

**ARTICLE XVI**

FUNCTIONS OF THE REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH AND WELLNESS CENTER (RHWC)

**Section 1. Renaming of the Social Hygiene Clinic to Reproductive Health and Wellness Center (RHWC) and providing its functions.** It shall perform the following functions:

a. Be in the frontline in the delivery of reproductive health care services;
b. Provide education and information dissemination activities on the promotion of reproductive Health, prevention and control of STI, HIV/AIDS;
c. Monitor the follow-up of cases handled;
d. Refer clients to other agencies of the Government for cases that need other health and social services such as livelihood projects, alternative home, and scholarship;
e. Ensure that children with STI/HIV are given priority in treatment and are referred to concerned agencies for further assistance;
f. Conduct HIV counseling, testing, and treatment on a regular basis. Further, pre- and post-test counseling shall likewise be administered before any examination may be considered;
g. Perform such other duties and functions as may be prescribed by law or ordinance.

**Section 2. Strengthening of the Reproductive Health and Wellness Center.** The Davao City Government shall strengthen the Reproductive Health and Wellness Center through provision of adequate personnel, facilities and budget. It shall ensure provision of comprehensive reproductive health services and sexual health education, ensuring ethics and quality care and information.

**ARTICLE XVI**

DECLARATION OF AIDS AWARENESS MONTH

**Section 1. Declaration of AIDS Awareness Month.** The month of May is hereby declared as AIDS awareness month to coincide with the commemoration day for Persons Living with HIV/AIDS and the International Day of Action on Women's Health. Likewise AIDS awareness should culminate on the month of December being the World AIDS Prevention Month.

**Section 2. Advocacy Programs for the AIDS Awareness Month.** There shall be an advocacy program for the whole month of May for the dissemination of information for the prevention and control of STI, HIV/AIDS.

ARTICLE XVII
PENAL PROVISIONS

Section 1. Any person found guilty of violating any provisions of this Ordinance shall be meted with the following penalties:
a. First Offense - a fine of P 2,000.00 and/or imprisonment of one (1) month.
b. Second Offense - a fine of P 3,000.00 and/or imprisonment of three (3) months.
c. Third Offense - a fine of P 5,000.00 and/or imprisonment of six (6) months and/or cancellation of business permit.

Section 2. Redress Mechanism. The DCAC in coordination with the City Legal Office, the Commission on Human Rights (CHR), and other related agencies, shall provide redress mechanisms for persons living with HIV to ensure that their civil, political, economic, and social rights are protected.

ARTICLE XVIII
APPROPRIATION

Section 1. Appropriation. An amount of not less than Two Million Pesos, Philippine Currency (P2,000,000.00) shall be appropriated for the implementation of this Ordinance including projects and operations of the Davao City AIDS Council, chargeable against the Annual Development Fund (ADF).

Section 2. Appropriation of Budget by DCAC Member Agencies. All DCAC member agencies shall appropriate a budget for STI, HIV, and AIDS prevention program.

ARTICLE XIX
IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS (IRR)

Section 1. Implementing Rules and Regulations. The City Mayor's Office together with the Davao City AIDS Council shall formulate and issue appropriate rules and regulations necessary for the implementation of this Ordinance within one (1) month but not more than one (1) year from approval thereof.

ARTICLE XX
FINAL PROVISION

Section 1. Separability Clause. If for any reason, any section or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections of provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

Section 2. Repealing Clause. All ordinances, local issuances, or rules inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance heretofore repealed or modified accordingly.

Section 3. Effectivity. This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval and publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

ENACTED, on the 8th day of January, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
