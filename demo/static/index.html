Legal Findings & Jurisprudential Context
Vertical Preemption (Magtajas Doctrine & RA 7160 §5(a))

Under Philippine constitutional law and Section 5(a) of the Local Government Code of 1991, municipal ordinances are subordinate to national legislation. As established in Magtajas v. Pryce Properties Corp. and Mosqueda v. PBGEA, an ordinance cannot forbid what a national statute expressly permits, nor permit what a statute forbids. Any local measure colliding with national law is ultra vires and void.

Horizontal Coherence (Davao City Local Codification)

Proposed local enactments must maintain statutory harmony with the 1,664 active Davao City local ordinances. Contradictory, overlapping, or uncoordinated local provisions risk unintentional implied repeals, departmental conflict, and legal ambiguity during administrative enforcement.
