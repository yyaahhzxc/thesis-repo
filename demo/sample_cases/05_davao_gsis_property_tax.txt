REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES
CITY OF DAVAO
OFFICE OF THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD

Davao City Real Property Tax Assessment on GSIS
DAVAO CITY MUNICIPAL REAL PROPERTY TAX ASSESSMENT ON GSIS PROPERTIES

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD OF DAVAO CITY, IN SESSION ASSEMBLED, THAT:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Measure establishes the Municipal Real Property Tax Assessment Mandate on Government-Owned Corporation Assets.

SECTION 2. ASSESSMENT MANDATE ON COMMERCIAL REALTY - Notices of assessment and warrants of levy are hereby issued imposing municipal real property taxes upon GSIS properties located in Matina and Ulas, Davao City, for outstanding municipal tax liabilities pursuant to the withdrawal of GOCC tax exemptions.

SECTION 3. LEVY AND REMEDIES - The City Treasurer is instructed to pursue all administrative remedies under the Local Government Code to enforce tax delinquencies against taxable properties.

SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Measure takes effect immediately upon passage.
