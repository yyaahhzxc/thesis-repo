REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES
CITY OF BATANGAS
OFFICE OF THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD

Batangas SP Resolution No. 210, Series of 1993
RESOLUTION REGULATING THE CABLE TELEVISION FRANCHISE AND SUBSCRIBER RATES OF BATANGAS CATV, INC.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD OF BATANGAS CITY, IN SESSION ASSEMBLED, THAT:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Resolution shall be known as the 'Batangas City Cable Television Regulatory Resolution of 1993'.

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF CONSUMER POLICY - The City declares its duty to protect city residents from excessive monthly charges imposed by commercial telecommunications providers.

SECTION 3. CABLE RATE REGULATION - The Sangguniang Panlungsod assumes authority to fix, regulate, and adjust subscriber rates and channel programming charges for commercial cable television services.

SECTION 4. MANDATORY RATE SCHEDULE - All cable TV concessionaires must submit rate adjustment applications to the City Council for public hearing and approval prior to implementation.

SECTION 5. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE - Unauthorized rate increases shall be subject to a fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) per month of unauthorized collection and revocation of Mayor's Permit.

SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY AND EFFECTIVITY - This Resolution shall take effect upon approval.
