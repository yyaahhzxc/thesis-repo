REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES
CITY OF CAGAYAN DE ORO
OFFICE OF THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD

Cagayan de Oro Ord. No. 3353 & Ord. No. 3375-93
AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE OPERATION OF CASINOS IN THE CITY OF CAGAYAN DE ORO

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE SANGGUNIANG PANLUNGSOD OF CAGAYAN DE ORO, IN SESSION ASSEMBLED, THAT:

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the 'Anti-Casino Ordinance of the City of Cagayan de Oro'.

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF MORAL POLICY - It is hereby declared the policy of the City to protect the morality and public welfare of its citizens from the pernicious influences of legalized gambling.

SECTION 3. PROHIBITION OF CASINOS - The opening, establishment, and operation of casinos and all forms of gambling are strictly prohibited and banned within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Cagayan de Oro.

SECTION 4. PENAL SANCTIONS - Any person or corporate entity operating a casino shall be punished by a fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) and imprisonment of one (1) year, plus immediate closure.

SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any provision is declared invalid, the remainder shall continue in effect.

SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE - All resolutions and ordinances inconsistent with this enactment are repealed.

SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval.
